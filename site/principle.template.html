#DIV/0!

Every spreadsheet user is familiar with the infamous division-by-zero error. A forbidden mathematical operation is treated as a boundary beyond which calculation cannot continue. Yet geometers have long known another approach: by adjoining a point at infinity, 1/0 can be given a meaningful interpretation, an idea that Riemann extended to the complex numbers. The Transport Principle generalises this insight and suggests that, when applied to spacetime, it transforms our understanding of what spacetime actually is.

Each numbered reference under a subtitle opens that result in the Physics Graph, with its statement, proof and machine check. The papers themselves are on Zenodo, in the Transport Papers community; the guide G00 tells the same story with the mathematics.

The founding idea is Leon Butler’s: that division by zero in a Clifford algebra should be read not as a prohibition but as a transport into a larger algebra. The mathematical development was carried out in extended collaboration with Claude (Anthropic), an AI system. The papers are independent work and have not been through journal peer review.
