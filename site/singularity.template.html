Every numbered reference under a subtitle opens that result in the Physics Graph, with its statement, proof and checks. The predictions board is read from the record when the site is built.

The founding conjecture, the direction of investigation and critical review are the author’s, Leon Butler. The mathematical development and drafting were produced in extended collaboration with Claude (Anthropic), an AI system. The papers are independent work and have not yet been through journal peer review: the Transport Papers on Zenodo.

The view is computed by tracing light rays through the Schwarzschild gravitational field in the Painlevé–Gullstrand gauge (Gauge Theory Gravity on a flat background), for an observer falling from rest far away, in the plane of a thin disc of hot gas orbiting the hole. The disc is drawn the way most depictions draw it (the film Interstellar among them): its inner edge at the last stable orbit, its glow shifted by the gas’s motion and by gravity, and the gas close to you left out as you fall past it, so the view stays clear. Sagittarius A*’s own gas is fainter and thicker; its light, bent by the hole, makes the ring the Event Horizon Telescope imaged.
