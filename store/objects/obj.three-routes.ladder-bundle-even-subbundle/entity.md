## formal
With collar data fixed (Fact \claimref{three-routes.imported-collar-data-coupling}), the sections $k$ and $k^{*} = \nO/2\nu$ of the completed Clifford sheaf are global over $\Sig$, and at each $x \in \Sig$ they generate the fibre algebra $\Aa_x \cong \Mat_2(\R)$-type mode factor of \cite[Cor.~4.5]{ButlerT4}. Write $\sigma(x) := k^{*}(x)k(x) - k(x)k^{*}(x)$, a global section with $\sigma(x)^2 = 1$, and $\alpha_x := \operatorname{Ad}(\sigma(x))$ for the fibrewise parity. The \emph{ladder bundle} $\Aa \to \Sig$ is the bundle with fibres $\Aa_x$; the \emph{even subbundle} $\Aa^{+} \subseteq \Aa$ has fibres the fixed algebras $\Aa_x^{+} = \{a \in \Aa_x : \alpha_x(a) = a\}$. The \emph{metric-level presheaf} assigns to each open $U \subseteq \Sig$ the algebra
\[
\mathfrak{M}(U) \;:=\; \Gamma(U, \Aa^{+}) \;=\; \bigl\{\, s : U \to \Aa^{+} \ \text{smooth},\ s(x) \in \Aa_x^{+}\ \forall x \in U \,\bigr\},
\]
with the restriction maps of sections.

## technical
Fix collar data. Then the sections $k$ and $k^* = \nO/2\nu$ of the completed Clifford sheaf are global over $\Sig$, and at each wall point they generate the fibre algebra $\Aa_x$, a $\Mat_2(\R)$-type mode factor. The element $\sigma = k^*k - kk^*$ is a global section with $\sigma^2 = 1$, and $\alpha_x = \operatorname{Ad}(\sigma(x))$ is the fibrewise parity. The ladder bundle $\Aa \to \Sig$ has fibres $\Aa_x$; the even subbundle $\Aa^{+}$ has fibres the parity-fixed algebras $\Aa_x^{+}$; and the metric-level presheaf assigns to each open $U \subseteq \Sig$ the algebra $\mathfrak{M}(U) = \Gamma(U, \Aa^{+})$ of smooth sections, with the restriction maps of sections.

## lay
At each point of the wall, the dying direction and its partner generate a small algebra of two-by-two matrices, a ladder that steps between them. Collecting these over the whole wall gives a bundle of algebras. A natural reflection splits each algebra into even and odd parts, and the even parts, assembled smoothly over regions of the wall, define the metric-level presheaf.

## one-liner
With collar data fixed, the ladder bundle over $\Sig$ has as fibres the algebras generated by $k$ and $k^*$, and the metric-level presheaf is the presheaf of smooth sections of its parity-even subbundle.
