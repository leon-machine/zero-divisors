## formal
Let $K_{\mathrm{geo}} := \sum_k \omega_k K_k$ generate the Killing boost flow of the boundary modes, the flow whose bivector at each point of $E$ is the curvature-selected $\sigma_r$ of Theorem \claimref{singularity.curvature-gauge-field-exact-shape-decoupling} and Corollary \claimref{singularity.matching-steps-4b-4c-join-equation}, with $\omega_k$ the mode's weight. A \emph{faithful} state satisfies the \emph{geometric modular condition} (G) if its modular automorphism group is the phase flow $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{JtK_{\mathrm{geo}}})$ generated by $K_{\mathrm{geo}}$, written with the boundary modes' complex unit $J = e_{2}e_{3}$ (\claimref{synthesis.transverse-bivector-is-the-complex-unit-of-the-radial-atom}), not its real rotor flow, up to a single constant reparametrisation. Faithfulness is part of the condition, not a consequence of it: a state has a modular automorphism group only if it is faithful, so (G) presupposes hypothesis (iii) of Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-descent} and cannot be used to derive it.

## technical
Let $K_{\mathrm{geo}} = \sum_k \omega_k K_k$ generate the Killing boost flow of the boundary modes, the flow whose bivector at each point of the singular boundary is the curvature-selected boost plane $\sigma_r$, with $\omega_k$ the weight of mode $k$. A faithful state satisfies the geometric modular condition (G) if its modular automorphism group is the phase flow $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{JtK_{\mathrm{geo}}})$ generated by $K_{\mathrm{geo}}$, $J = e_{2}e_{3}$ the boundary modes' complex unit, up to a single constant reparametrisation.

Faithfulness is part of the condition: only a faithful state has a modular automorphism group, so (G) presupposes the cyclic-and-separating hypothesis of modular descent and cannot be used to derive it.

## lay
Every faithful quantum state comes with its own internal clock, its modular flow. The geometric modular condition asks that this internal clock run exactly along the black hole's own time symmetry, the boost picked out by the curvature, up to one overall rescaling of time. It only makes sense for states that are faithful in the technical sense, so it cannot be used to prove that a state is faithful.

## one-liner
A faithful state satisfies the geometric modular condition (G) when its modular automorphism group is the phase flow $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{JtK_{\mathrm{geo}}})$, $J = e_{2}e_{3}$, of the boundary modes' Killing boost generator $K_{\mathrm{geo}} = \sum_k \omega_k K_k$, up to one constant reparametrisation.
