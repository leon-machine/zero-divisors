Conventions of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.transported-frozen-point}: $v = \tfrac12(e_{-} + e_{+})$, $w = \tfrac12(e_{+} - e_{-})$, so $Q(v) = Q(w) = 0$, $v\cdot w = \tfrac12$, $vw + wv = 1$, $\nO' = -w$, $\nInf' = 2v$, $\nO'\cdot\nInf' = -1$. A rung-plane vector is $y = -\alpha w + \beta v$ with $Q(y) = -\alpha\beta$. The conjugation $*$ of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-tower-commuting-copies-fock-module} has $v^{*} = w$; on the real rung plane it fixes $e_{+} = v + w$ and negates $e_{-} = v - w$, so $y^{*} = -\alpha v + \beta w$ and $\|y\|_{*} = y^{*}\cdot y = \tfrac12(\alpha^{2} + \beta^{2})$.

(1) $B_{s}$ multiplies $v$ by $s$ and $w$ by $1/s$ and fixes the ground, so it preserves $Q(v) = Q(w) = 0$, $v\cdot w = \tfrac12$ and the orthogonality of the rung plane to the ground: it is an isometry, and the Clifford relations of the pair, which depend only on these products, hold for $(B_{s}v, B_{s}w)$. For a ground vector $x$, $B_{1/\mu}X_{1}(x) = B_{1/\mu}(-w + x + Q(x)v) = -\mu w + x + (Q(x)/\mu)v = X_{\mu}(x)$. An isometry extends to an automorphism of the Clifford algebra and of its quadric, so each clause of Fact \claimref{transport.totality}, proved for the completion with its adjoined orthonormal pair, holds verbatim for the completion with the adjoined orthonormal pair $(B_{1/\mu}e_{+}, B_{1/\mu}e_{-})$, whose lift is $X_{\mu}$ and whose involution is $\iota_{\mu}[N] = [e_{+}^{(\mu)}Ne_{+}^{(\mu)}] = B_{1/\mu}\,\iota[B_{\mu}N]$. The boosts act on coefficients by $(\alpha, \beta) \mapsto (\alpha/s, s\beta)$; on the set $\alpha\beta \ne 0$ the orbits are exactly the level sets of $\alpha\beta$ with fixed signs of $\alpha$ and $\beta$, since $(\alpha', \beta')$ with $\alpha'\beta' = \alpha\beta$ and the same signs is reached with $s = \alpha/\alpha' > 0$. So an invariant function is a function of the norm $-\alpha\beta$ and the sign of $\alpha$, the $w$-coefficient (the sign of $\beta$ is then fixed by the norm). This is the rung-plane version of the first-rung statement Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.transport-constrains-frozen-point-only-through}(iii). With $Q(I) = \Lambda/3$, $c_{\mu} = \mu\nO' + \tfrac{\Lambda}{6\mu}\nInf' = -\mu w + \tfrac{\Lambda}{3\mu}v$ and $Q(c_{\mu}) = -\Lambda/3$.

(2) $\tfrac{d}{d\mu}c_{\mu} = -w - \tfrac{\Lambda}{3\mu^{2}}v$, and the bracketed product $\bigl(\tfrac{d}{d\mu}c_{\mu}\bigr)\cdot c_{\mu}$ is $(-w - \tfrac{\Lambda}{3\mu^{2}}v)\cdot(-\mu w + \tfrac{\Lambda}{3\mu}v) = -\tfrac{\Lambda}{3\mu}\cdot\tfrac12 + \tfrac{\Lambda}{3\mu}\cdot\tfrac12 = 0$. And $\tfrac{\partial}{\partial\Lambda}c_{\mu} = \tfrac{1}{3\mu}v$, with $\bigl(\tfrac{\partial}{\partial\Lambda}c_{\mu}\bigr)\cdot c_{\mu} = \tfrac{1}{3\mu}v\cdot(-\mu w) = -\tfrac16$, so that $\tfrac{\partial}{\partial\Lambda}(Q(c_{\mu})) = -\tfrac13$, as $Q(c_{\mu}) = -\Lambda/3$ requires. The first vector is tangent to the level set $\{y : Q(y) = -\Lambda/3\}$ at $c_{\mu}$, since the derivative of $Q(y)$ along it is $2\,\tfrac{d}{d\mu}c_{\mu}\cdot c_{\mu} = 0$; the second is not.

(3) At $\mu = 1$, $c_{1} = -w + \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}v$ is Proposition \claimref{standard-model.transported-frozen-point}(i); at $\mu = \Lambda/3$, $c_{\Lambda/3} = -\tfrac{\Lambda}{3}w + v = \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}\nO' + \tfrac12\nInf'$, the rung component of (ii). By (1), $c_{\mu} = B_{1/\mu}c_{1}$, so $c_{\Lambda/3} = B_{3/\Lambda}c_{1}$. For $\Lambda > 0$ put $H^{2} = \Lambda/3$; the map $\mu \mapsto H^{2}/\mu$ is an involution of $(0, \infty)$ sending $1$ to $H^{2} = \Lambda/3$, so it exchanges $c_{1}$ and $c_{\Lambda/3}$, and its only fixed point is $\mu = H$. (The conjugation norm $\|c_{\mu}\|_{*} = \tfrac12(\mu^{2} + H^{4}/\mu^{2})$, its invariance under this involution and its minimum $H^{2} = |Q(c_{H})|$ at $\mu = H$ are proved in the result filed alongside, synthesis.rung-unit-is-frame-boost(4); the script recomputes them as a control.)

The script works in the Clifford algebra of $\R^{2,2}\oplus\R^{1,1}$ built with GA.py (a first-rung plane holding $I = \nInf^{(1)} - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO^{(1)}$, the second rung, two further ground directions): it checks that $B_{s}$ preserves all products of generators and the pair relations, that $*$ commutes with $B_{s}$ only at $s = 1$, that $X_{\mu}(x) = B_{1/\mu}X_{1}(x)$ is null and that $e_{+}^{(\mu)}X_{\mu}(x)e_{+}^{(\mu)}$ is proportional to $X_{\mu}(x/Q(x))$ for three ground vectors and four units, the norm of $c_{\mu}$ for four values of $\Lambda$ (one negative), the conjugation norm as a control, the symmetry $\mu \mapsto H^{2}/\mu$, the identification of $c_{1}$ and $c_{\Lambda/3}$ with Proposition \claimref{standard-model.transported-frozen-point}(i), (ii), the orthogonality (2) and the value $-\tfrac16$.
