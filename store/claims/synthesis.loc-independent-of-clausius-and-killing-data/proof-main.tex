Read in Gauge Theory Gravity, the metrics below are derived metrics of gravitational fields on flat spacetime and the Ricci tensors are contractions of their field strengths (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}, clauses (i) and (iv)). \emph{(i)} If $\mathrm{Ric} = \Lambda g$ then $R = 4\Lambda$ and $S_{ab} = \Lambda g_{ab} - \Lambda g_{ab} = 0$, so $\eta = \tfrac14$. For $T = 0$ the balance of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.einstein-equations-from-c-loc-and-clausius}(2) reads $-\tfrac{\eta}{2\pi}\mathrm{Ric}(k,k) = -\tfrac{\eta}{2\pi}\Lambda\,g(k,k) = 0$ for null $k$, so (H1) holds at leading order, and (H3), (H4) are immediate. The statements listed in (i) are statements about these vacuum geometries, their Killing flows and their boundary modes; they refer to no local-horizon entropy other than through the calibration, and Theorem \claimref{synthesis.black-hole-data-do-not-fix-field-equations}(iii) already records that they are the same in every theory with these vacuum solutions. The calibration of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.c-loc-entropy-clause-curvature-independent-density}(ii) evaluates the density on the Schwarzschild horizon, where $S_{ab} = 0$, and returns $\tfrac14$; the de Sitter and Kerr cross-checks there also sit on $\Lambda$-vacua, as does the Schwarzschild-de Sitter family of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.sds-clausius-fixes-area-constant}. Clause (R$_{\mathrm{loc}}$) concerns the state of the boundary modes and not the entropy density, so $\mathcal{M}_{c}$ imposes it as stated; this is consistent because version 2 of the premise does not read the entropy as the von Neumann entropy of a fixed mode set (in version 1 the state clause would fix $\eta$ through the count, and a curvature-dependent $\eta$ would need a curvature-dependent mode density). The check computes the Riemann tensor of Schwarzschild-de Sitter at two points and of de Sitter at one from the metric (exact Taylor jets of the rational metric in $(t, r, \cos\theta, \phi)$) and confirms $R = 4\Lambda$, $S_{ab}S^{ab} = 0$, $\eta = \tfrac14$.

\emph{(ii)} The Einstein static universe (standard, cited from memory) is $\mathbb{R}\times S^{3}$ with $-dt^{2} + a^{2}(d\chi^{2} + \sin^{2}\chi\,(d\theta^{2} + \sin^{2}\theta\,d\phi^{2}))$; in stereographic coordinates on $S^{3}$ its metric is $-dt^{2} + 4a^{2}(dx^{2} + dy^{2} + dz^{2})/(1 + x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2})^{2}$, rational and diagonal. The check expands it in exact jets at two points (the origin and $(\tfrac12, \tfrac13, -\tfrac15)$), for $a = 2$ and $a = \tfrac32$, and finds $\mathrm{Ric}(e_{t}, e_{t}) = 0$, $\mathrm{Ric}(e_{t}, e_{i}) = 0$, $\mathrm{Ric}(e_{i}, e_{j}) = (2/a^{2})\delta_{ij}$ in the orthonormal frame, $R = 6/a^{2}$ and $S_{ab}S^{ab} = (3/(2a^{2}))^{2} + 3(1/(2a^{2}))^{2} = 3/a^{4}$; the spacetime is homogeneous, so these hold everywhere. Hence $\eta = \tfrac14(1 + 3c/a^{4})$, constant, and $\eta \neq \tfrac14$ since $c \neq 0$. Dust at rest has $T_{ab} = \rho\,u_{a}u_{b}$, $u = \partial_{t}$; $\nabla_{a}T^{ab} = \rho(u^{a}\nabla_{a}u^{b} + u^{b}\nabla_{a}u^{a}) = \rho(\Gamma^{b}{}_{tt} + u^{b}\Gamma^{a}{}_{at})$, and the check finds $\Gamma^{b}{}_{tt} = 0$ and $\Gamma^{a}{}_{at} = 0$, so the dust is conserved with $\rho$ constant ((H3)). A future null vector is, up to a positive factor, $k = e_{t} + n^{i}e_{i}$ with $n$ a unit vector; then $u\cdot k = -1$, $T(k,k) = \rho$ and $\mathrm{Ric}(k,k) = 2/a^{2}$. The defect of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.einstein-equations-from-c-loc-and-clausius}(2) is $(\kappa/2)(T(k,k) - (\eta/2\pi)\mathrm{Ric}(k,k))\lambda_{0}^{2}$; it vanishes iff $8\pi\rho = 4\eta\cdot 2/a^{2} = 2(1 + 3c/a^{4})/a^{2}$, the stated density, and it scales by the positive factor of $k$ squared, so it vanishes for every null $k$. The check confirms this exactly for four rational unit vectors $n$ at both points, with nullity of $k$ computed by GA, and that the same density fails the balance with $\eta = \tfrac14$. The terms of order $\lambda_{0}^{3}$ in that theorem come from gradients of $\mathrm{Ric}(k,k)$ and $T(k,k)$ along the generator, which vanish here by homogeneity, and (H2) asks only for leading order. On the integrated reading the extra first-order term of that theorem's step (6), $(k\cdot\nabla\eta)\lambda_{0}$, vanishes because $\eta$ is constant. So (H1) holds on every local Rindler patch on both readings. Steps (3) and (4) of that theorem, applied with $\eta$ constant on this spacetime, give $G_{ab} + \Lambda_{J}g_{ab} = 8\pi G_{e}T_{ab}$ with $G_{e} = 1/(4\eta)$; the $tt$ component, $G_{tt} = 3/a^{2}$, gives $\Lambda_{J} = 3/a^{2} - 8\pi G_{e}\rho = 3/a^{2} - 2/a^{2} = 1/a^{2}$, and the spatial components, $G_{ij} = -g_{ij}/a^{2}$, are then satisfied with $T_{ij} = 0$; the check confirms both. Under (Loc) the density at a point depends only on the flat tangent data there, so by Theorem \claimref{synthesis.c-loc-entropy-clause-curvature-independent-density}(i) it is one constant, which (i) fixes at $\tfrac14$ on the Schwarzschild horizon; $\mathcal{M}_{c}$ gives $\tfrac14$ there and $\tfrac14(1 + 3c/a^{4})$ here, so (Loc) fails in $\mathcal{M}_{c}$.

\emph{(iii)} By (i) and (ii), $\mathcal{M}_{c}$ satisfies (R$_{\mathrm{loc}}$), (H1) to (H4) on either reading, and every Killing-horizon and vacuum statement of the store, and violates (Loc). So (Loc) is not a consequence of them. (Whether it satisfies every other postulate of the store is a matter of reading those postulates; a survey of the postulates of the store finds none that refers to the entropy of non-Killing horizons or to the matter coupling of the field equations, the only two places where $\mathcal{M}_{c}$ departs from the store's intended reading.)

\emph{(iv)} By Theorem \claimref{synthesis.c-loc-entropy-clause-curvature-independent-density}(iv), the integrated reading and (H1) give $k^{a}\nabla_{a}\eta = 0$ for every null $k$ at every point; null vectors span each tangent space, so $\nabla\eta = 0$ and $\eta$ is constant on each connected spacetime. If $\eta$ depends only on the curvature scalars at the point and some point $q$ has the curvature scalars of a point $q'$ on a Schwarzschild, Schwarzschild-de Sitter or de Sitter horizon, then $\eta(q) = \eta(q')$. To see $\eta(q') = \tfrac14$: on Schwarzschild, and on Schwarzschild-de Sitter at fixed $\Lambda$, every curvature scalar is a function of $M/r^{3}$, and as $r$ ranges over one Schwarzschild spacetime $M/r^{3}$ takes every positive value, so every Schwarzschild spacetime meets the scalar values of every point of every other; since $\eta$ is a function of the scalars and constant on each spacetime, it takes one value on all of them, in particular on every horizon, and the identification of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.c-loc-entropy-clause-curvature-independent-density}(ii), stated up to $o(A)$ as $M \to \infty$, gives that constant as $\lim S/A = \tfrac14$ (for de Sitter, $\pi N^{2}$ for area $4\pi N^{2}$). Constancy gives $\eta = \tfrac14$ on the whole spacetime. Examples: every point of de Sitter space has the curvature of a point of its horizon; on the Schwarzschild-de Sitter family the horizon value of $M/r_{b}^{3} = (1 - \Lambda r_{b}^{2}/3)/(2r_{b}^{2})$ runs over $(\Lambda/3, \infty)$, so exterior points with $M/r^{3}$ in that range are calibrated. The Einstein static universe of (ii) has $\mathrm{Ric} \neq \Lambda g$ everywhere and contains no such point, consistent with (ii). On the differences reading, $\eta$ need not be constant: by step (6) of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.einstein-equations-from-c-loc-and-clausius} a varying $\eta$ is constrained only through the Bianchi step, so this confinement is not available there.
