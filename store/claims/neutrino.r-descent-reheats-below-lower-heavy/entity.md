## formal
$T_{\mathrm{desc}} \le \alpha = \min(\alpha, M_{3})$, with $\alpha$ on the branch of Remark \ref{rem:branch}. This is a reading of the dark-matter paper's own assumption (Fact \claimref{neutrino.imported-ceiling-under-hypothesis-u-arrow}: $T_{\mathrm{desc}}$ at the ceiling or below it) at "below"; under it no heavy state ever thermalised, the washout of $N_{u'}$ is Boltzmann-suppressed, the pair $N_{\perp}$ was never produced thermally, and the descent's $B - L$ is untouched. The natural justification, that the frozen phase's description dissolves above the rung scale, identifies $M_{3}$ with that scale, which is Hypothesis (U) and the valuation of Remark \ref{rem:valuation}, not a theorem; and (R) at $\alpha$ asks more of the descent than that, since $\alpha$ lies nearly two orders below $M_{3}$ on the branch taken. A derivation of $M$ and of $\alpha$ (successor problems (2) and (3)) would decide it.

## technical
Premise (R) states $T_{\mathrm{desc}} \le \alpha = \min(\alpha, M_{3})$: the descent reheats below the lower heavy neutrino scale, on the branch $\alpha \ll M_{3}$. It reads the dark-matter paper's assumption, reheating at the ceiling or below it, in its "below" form. Under it no heavy state ever thermalised, the washout of $N_{u'}$ is Boltzmann-suppressed, the pair $N_{\perp}$ was never produced thermally, and the descent's $B - L$ is untouched.

Its natural justification, that the frozen phase's description dissolves above the rung scale, identifies $M_{3}$ with that scale, which is Hypothesis (U) and a valuation, not a theorem; and (R) at $\alpha$, nearly two orders below $M_{3}$ on the branch taken, asks more of the descent than that. A derivation of $M$ and of $\alpha$ would decide it.

## lay
This is an assumption about the early universe: after the event the framework calls the descent, it never became hot enough to produce the heavy neutrinos. If it holds, those neutrinos could neither create nor erase the matter-antimatter imbalance. The paper notes that the assumption is not derived and asks more than its simplest justification would give.

## one-liner
Premise: the descent reheats below the lower heavy neutrino scale $\alpha$, so no heavy neutrino ever thermalised.
