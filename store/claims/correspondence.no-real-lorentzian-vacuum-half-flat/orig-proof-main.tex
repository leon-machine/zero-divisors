By Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-weyl-commutes-duality} the (real) Weyl operator $\mathcal{W}$ preserves the eigenspaces $\Lambda^\pm$ of Lemma \claimref{correspondence.lorentzian-duality-complex-structure}; write $\mathcal{W}^\pm$ for its restrictions. $\mathcal{W}$ is real, so it commutes with complex conjugation, which interchanges $\Lambda^+$ and $\Lambda^-$ (Lemma \claimref{correspondence.lorentzian-duality-complex-structure}); hence $\mathcal{W}^-$ is the conjugate operator of $\mathcal{W}^+$, and $\mathcal{W}^- = 0$ forces $\mathcal{W}^+ = 0$, forcing $\mathcal{W} = 0$ on $\Lambda^2_\C$ and so on $\Lambda^2$. Conformal flatness of a metric with $\mathcal{W} = 0$ is Weyl's theorem (Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-weyls-theorem}). The black-hole clause imports the nonvanishing curvature scale from the geometry paper.
