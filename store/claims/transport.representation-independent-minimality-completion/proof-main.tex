Let $s_i = \Phi(p_i)$ for the points of Lemma \claimref{transport.conformal-gram-matrix}. The chart condition gives $s_i \cdot s_j = M_{ij}$, so the pullback of $Q''|_{\mathrm{span}\{s_i\}}$ to $\R^{n+2}$ along $(c_i) \mapsto \sum c_i s_i$ is the quadratic form of $M$. Since $M$ is nondegenerate (Lemma \claimref{transport.conformal-gram-matrix}), the map is injective, the span $S$ has dimension $n+2$, and $Q''|_S$ is nondegenerate of signature $(p{+}1, q{+}1)$. A nondegenerate subspace splits off orthogonally, $\mathbb{V}'' = S \perp S^\perp$, whence $p'' = (p{+}1) + p(S^\perp) \geq p+1$ and likewise for $q''$. Equality of dimensions forces $S = \mathbb{V}''$ and the signature claim; the algebra statement is the functoriality of $\Cl$ on isometries.
