Every displayed relation is an instance of the single Clifford relation $\alpha\beta + \beta\alpha = 2\langle\alpha, \beta\rangle$ on vectors (\claimref{std-algebra.vector-squares}), as for any Witt basis (\claimref{std-algebra.witt-basis-car}): the $v$'s are pairwise orthogonal null vectors ($\langle v_i, v_j \rangle = 0$ for $i \neq j$ since the planes are orthogonal, and $v_i^2 = 0$ since $v_i$ is null), likewise the $w$'s, and $\langle v_i, w_j \rangle = \tfrac12\delta_{ij}$ by the hyperbolic normalisation within each plane and orthogonality across planes. A new rung's vectors are orthogonal to all previous generators, so the old relations are untouched.
