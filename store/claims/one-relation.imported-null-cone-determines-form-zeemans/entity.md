## formal
For a real nondegenerate quadratic form $Q$ of mixed signature on $\R^{n}$, $n \geq 2$, the null cone $\{Q = 0\}$ determines $Q$ up to a nonzero real scalar: in an orthonormal basis $e_{1}, \dots, e_{p}, e_{p+1}, \dots, e_{n}$, a symmetric bilinear form $S$ vanishing on the null vectors $e_{i} \pm e_{j}$ ($i \leq p < j$) has $S(e_{i}, e_{j}) = 0$ and $S(e_{i}, e_{i}) = -S(e_{j}, e_{j}) =: a$ for all such pairs, and vanishing on $3e_{i} + 4e_{k} \pm 5e_{j}$ and $5e_{i} + 3e_{j} \pm 4e_{l}$ for distinct positive $i, k$ and distinct negative $j, l$ then kills the remaining off-diagonal entries, so $S = aQ$; at $n = 2$ the first family alone suffices. For $n \geq 3$ every bijection of Minkowski space preserving the causal order is generated by Lorentz transformations, translations and dilations, so causal structure and conformal structure coincide; in $n = 2$ the two null coordinates can be reparametrised independently and the causal automorphism group is infinite-dimensional. \emph{Provenance:} the first statement is the elementary computation displayed, which the script performs on every signature up to $(3,3)$, and is the statement that the null cone fixes the conformal class \cite{HawkingEllis1973}; the null-locus criterion, that $\Cl(p,q)$ contains a null vector if and only if $pq \neq 0$, is the root paper's \cite[Prop.~3.3]{ButlerT1}; the second statement is \cite{Zeeman1964}. All three enter Lemma \claimref{one-relation.third-constant-conversion-constant-between-charts} as imports.

## technical
For a real nondegenerate quadratic form $Q$ of mixed signature on $\R^n$, $n \ge 2$, the null cone $\{Q = 0\}$ determines $Q$ up to a nonzero real scalar: a symmetric bilinear form vanishing on explicit null test vectors such as $e_i \pm e_j$ must equal $aQ$. The fact also records the series' null-locus criterion, that $\Cl(p,q)$ contains a null vector exactly when $pq \neq 0$.

For $n \ge 3$, Zeeman's theorem says every bijection of Minkowski space preserving the causal order is generated by Lorentz transformations, translations and dilations, so causal and conformal structure coincide; for $n = 2$ the two null coordinates can be reparametrised independently and the causal automorphism group is infinite-dimensional.

## lay
Knowing which directions are light-like fixes the spacetime metric up to an overall scale. In three or more dimensions Zeeman showed more: any map of flat spacetime that preserves which events can influence which is built from rotations, boosts, shifts and rescalings. In two dimensions this fails, since each light direction can be stretched independently.

## one-liner
Imported: a mixed-signature null cone fixes its quadratic form up to scale, and for $n \ge 3$ the causal automorphisms of Minkowski space are the conformal ones (Zeeman).
