\emph{(i) The algebra of face words.} The face operators are those of Theorem \claimref{families.skeleton-rank-one-plus-ordering-shift}, and the modes are bosonic (Fact \claimref{families.imported-squeezed-vacuum-bosonic-pairing}). The commutator identity in the proof of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.face-mass-form-unit-plus-gram}, $[\hat\Omega(c), \hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger}] = 2\,\mathrm{tr}(\Theta\bar\Theta) + 4a^{\dagger}\Theta^{\dagger}\Theta a$, with $\mathrm{tr}(\Theta_{k}\Theta_{l}) = \delta_{kl}$ and $4\Theta_{k}\Theta_{l} = \delta_{kl}\mathbb{1} - e_{l}e_{k}^{\mathsf T}\otimes\mathbb{1}$ (Proposition \claimref{synthesis.cumulants-at-fixed-second-moments-keep-the-floor}, Step 1), reads, coefficient by coefficient in $\bar{c}_{k}c_{l}$,
\[
[\hat\Omega_{l}, \hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}] \;=\; (2 + \hat{N})\,\delta_{kl} - \hat\rho_{lk},
\qquad \hat\rho_{lk} = \textstyle\sum_{A}a_{lA}^{\dagger}a_{kA},
\]
while the $\hat\Omega_{k}$ commute among themselves, and so do the $\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}$. The density moves a family label: $[\hat\rho_{lk}, a_{jA}^{\dagger}] = \delta_{kj}a_{lA}^{\dagger}$, so with $\hat\Omega_{j}^{\dagger} = \tfrac12\varepsilon_{jab}\varepsilon_{AB}a_{aA}^{\dagger}a_{bB}^{\dagger}$ and $\varepsilon_{AB}a_{lA}^{\dagger}a_{bB}^{\dagger} = \sum_{m}\varepsilon_{mlb}\hat\Omega_{m}^{\dagger}$ (the pair is antisymmetric in $A, B$, hence in $l, b$),
\[
[\hat\rho_{lk}, \hat\Omega_{j}^{\dagger}] \;=\; \textstyle\sum_{b,m}\varepsilon_{jkb}\varepsilon_{mlb}\,\hat\Omega_{m}^{\dagger} \;=\; \delta_{kl}\hat\Omega_{j}^{\dagger} - \delta_{jl}\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}.
\]
Since $\hat\rho_{lk}\ket{0} = 0$, $\hat\rho_{lk}$ acts on a monomial as a derivation: $\hat\rho_{lk}\hat\Omega^{\dagger m}\ket{0} = \sum_{j}m_{j}(\delta_{kl}\hat\Omega^{\dagger m} - \delta_{jl}\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega^{\dagger(m - e_{j})})\ket{0} = D\delta_{kl}\hat\Omega^{\dagger m}\ket{0} - m_{l}\hat\Omega^{\dagger(m - e_{l} + e_{k})}\ket{0}$. For $\hat\Omega_{l}$ induct on $D$: with $m = m' + e_{j}$ and the claim for $m'$ (degree $D - 1$, on which $\hat{N} = 2D - 2$),
\[
\hat\Omega_{l}\hat\Omega_{j}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega^{\dagger m'}\ket{0}
= \hat\Omega_{j}^{\dagger}\,D m'_{l}\,\hat\Omega^{\dagger(m' - e_{l})}\ket{0} + \bigl(2D\delta_{jl} - \hat\rho_{lj}\bigr)\hat\Omega^{\dagger m'}\ket{0}
= D m'_{l}\hat\Omega^{\dagger(m - e_{l})}\ket{0} + 2D\delta_{jl}\hat\Omega^{\dagger m'}\ket{0} - (D - 1)\delta_{jl}\hat\Omega^{\dagger m'}\ket{0} + m'_{l}\hat\Omega^{\dagger(m - e_{l})}\ket{0},
\]
which is $(D + 1)(m'_{l} + \delta_{jl})\hat\Omega^{\dagger(m - e_{l})}\ket{0} = (D + 1)m_{l}\hat\Omega^{\dagger(m - e_{l})}\ket{0}$, since $\hat\Omega^{\dagger m'} = \hat\Omega^{\dagger(m - e_{l})}$ when $j = l$. Then $\langle\hat\Omega^{\dagger m}0, \hat\Omega^{\dagger m''}0\rangle = \langle\hat\Omega^{\dagger m'}0, \hat\Omega_{j}\hat\Omega^{\dagger m''}0\rangle = (D + 1)m''_{j}\langle\hat\Omega^{\dagger m'}0, \hat\Omega^{\dagger(m'' - e_{j})}0\rangle$ for $|m''| = D$ (different degrees are orthogonal, being eigenvectors of $\hat{N}$), and induction gives orthogonality and $\|\hat\Omega^{\dagger m}\ket{0}\|^{2} = (D + 1)m_{j}\cdot D!\,(m - e_{j})! = (D + 1)!\,m!$. Finally $\hat{V}\hat\Omega^{\dagger m}\ket{0} = \sum_{k}(D + 1)m_{k}\hat\Omega^{\dagger m}\ket{0} = D(D + 1)\hat\Omega^{\dagger m}\ket{0}$. The check verifies all of (i) entry by entry on the twenty monomials with $D \le 3$.

\emph{(ii) The mass form.} Comparing the formulas of (i) for $\hat\rho_{lk}$ and for $\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}$ (which sends $\hat\Omega^{\dagger m}\ket{0}$ to $(D + 1)m_{l}\hat\Omega^{\dagger(m - e_{l} + e_{k})}\ket{0}$) gives the operator identity on $\mathcal{S}$
\[
\hat\rho_{lk} \;=\; \hat{D}\,\delta_{kl} - \hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}\,(\hat{D} + 1)^{-1}, \qquad \hat{D} = \hat{N}/2,
\]
$\hat{D}$ commuting with $\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}$. The mass form is $H_{kl} = \tfrac12\langle\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l} + \hat\Omega_{l}\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\rangle = \langle\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}\rangle + \tfrac12\langle[\hat\Omega_{l}, \hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}]\rangle$ (Proposition \claimref{synthesis.face-mass-form-unit-plus-gram}), so for a normalised $\psi \in \mathcal{S}$
\[
H_{kl} = \langle\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}\rangle + \delta_{kl} + \tfrac12\langle\hat{N}\rangle\delta_{kl} - \tfrac12\langle\hat{D}\rangle\delta_{kl} + \tfrac12\bigl\langle\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}(\hat{D} + 1)^{-1}\bigr\rangle
= \Bigl(1 + \tfrac{\langle\hat{N}\rangle}{4}\Bigr)\delta_{kl} + \Bigl\langle\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}\,\frac{\hat{N} + 3}{\hat{N} + 2}\Bigr\rangle .
\]
Write $f = (\hat{N} + 3)/(\hat{N} + 2)$, positive and a function of $\hat{N}$; as $\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}$ preserves $\hat{N}$, $G_{kl} = \langle\hat\Omega_{k}f^{1/2}\psi, \hat\Omega_{l}f^{1/2}\psi\rangle$, the Gram matrix of the vectors $\hat\Omega_{l}f^{1/2}\psi$, so $G \ge 0$ and $\bar{y}^{\mathsf T}Gy = \|\hat\Omega(y)f^{1/2}\psi\|^{2}$. Since $\hat\Omega(y)f(\hat{N})^{1/2} = f(\hat{N} + 2)^{1/2}\hat\Omega(y)$ and $f > 0$, this vanishes exactly when $\hat\Omega(y)\psi = 0$. Mixed states supported on $\mathcal{S}$ follow by linearity of $H$ in the density matrix. The trace: $\sum_{k}\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{k} = \hat{V} = \hat{D}(\hat{D} + 1)$ on $\mathcal{S}$ by (i), so $\mathrm{tr}\,G = \langle\hat{D}(\hat{D} + 1)(2\hat{D} + 3)/(2\hat{D} + 2)\rangle = \langle\hat{D}^{2}\rangle + \tfrac32\langle\hat{D}\rangle$ and $\mathrm{tr}\,H = 3 + 3\langle\hat{D}\rangle + \langle\hat{D}^{2}\rangle$, in agreement with the trace identity $\mathrm{tr}\,H = 3 + \langle\hat{N}\rangle + \sum_{k}\|\hat\Omega_{k}\psi\|^{2}$ of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.cumulants-at-fixed-second-moments-keep-the-floor}. A reading, not used in the proof: the map $\hat\Omega^{\dagger m}\ket{0} \mapsto \sqrt{(D + 1)!}\,z^{\dagger m}\ket{0}$ is an isometry of $\mathcal{S}$ onto the Fock space of three auxiliary bosons $z_{k}$, under which $\hat\Omega_{l} = \sqrt{\hat{D} + 1}\,z_{l}$ and $G_{kl} = \langle z_{k}^{\dagger}z_{l}(\hat{D} + \tfrac32)\rangle$, the one-body density of the face quanta weighted by $\hat{D} + \tfrac32$. The check verifies the displayed formula for $H$ entry by entry, the trace identity and the Hermiticity and positivity of $G$ on five non-Gaussian superpositions of up to five monomials with complex rational coefficients.

\emph{(iii) Source-built states.} By (i), on $\mathcal{S}$ the operator $\hat\Omega_{l}$ acts on polynomials in the $\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}$ applied to the vacuum as $(\hat{D} + 1)\,\partial/\partial\hat\Omega_{l}^{\dagger}$ (the factor taken at the degree acted on), so $\hat\Omega(n) = \sum_{l}n_{l}\hat\Omega_{l}$ acts as $(\hat{D} + 1)$ times the derivative along $n$. For $y \in \mathrm{span}_{\C}\{c_{R}, c_{I}\} = n^{\perp}$, the derivative of $\hat\Omega(y)^{\dagger} = \sum_{k}\bar{y}_{k}\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}$ along $n$ is $\sum_{k}n_{k}\bar{y}_{k} = 0$, since $n$ is real and orthogonal to $c_{R}$ and $c_{I}$; by the Leibniz rule the derivative of any polynomial in $\hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger}$ and $\hat\Omega(\bar{c})^{\dagger}$ (both in that span) vanishes, and $\hat{V}$ is a function of $\hat{D}$ on $\mathcal{S}$, so $\hat\Omega(n)\psi = 0$ for every state of the class, and for mixtures of such. By (ii), $Gn = 0$ and $Hn = (1 + \langle\hat{N}\rangle/4)n$, the least eigenvalue of $H$. The other two eigenvalues are $1 + \langle\hat{N}\rangle/4$ plus the eigenvalues of $G$ on $n^{\perp}$; if $G$ is singular there, both light eigenvalues equal the floor and the pair is degenerate (the single source, by (ii) exactly when $\hat\Omega(y)\psi = 0$ for some $y \in n^{\perp}$); otherwise $n$ is strictly the lowest and the pair is split. The trace identity of (ii) then gives $\lambda_{2} + \lambda_{3} = \mathrm{tr}\,H - \lambda_{1} = 2 + \tfrac52\langle\hat{D}\rangle + \langle\hat{D}^{2}\rangle$. The check works in the frame of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.real-structure-breaks-source-stabiliser-to-z2} at $a = 1$ and $a = \tfrac23$, on seven states each (the truncated single source, the real family at $\beta = 1, 2, -\tfrac12$, the two-word state, and two states with $\hat{V}$ and degree-three words, complex coefficients): it verifies $\hat\Omega(n)\psi = 0$, $Hn = (1 + \langle\hat{D}\rangle/2)n$, and that the compression of $H - h_{n}$ to $n^{\perp}$ is positive semidefinite (determinant $\ge 0$, trace $> 0$, in the basis $c_{R}, c_{I}$ with its Gram matrix), degenerate exactly for the single source; and it reproduces the stored values $h_{n} = \tfrac{32}{17}, \tfrac{145}{77}, \tfrac{505}{257}, \tfrac{55}{32}, \tfrac{190}{101}$ as $1 + \langle\hat{D}\rangle/2$ (for instance the real family at $\beta = 1$ has weights $1$, $16$, $60$ in degrees $0, 1, 2$, so $\langle\hat{D}\rangle = \tfrac{136}{77}$ and $h_{n} = 1 + \tfrac{68}{77}$). For the stored state $(1 + \hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger} + 2\hat\Omega(n)^{\dagger 2})\ket{0}$, which uses a word in $n$ itself, $\hat\Omega(n)\psi \ne 0$ and $h_{n} = \tfrac{1738}{199}$ lies above the floor, as (ii) requires.

\emph{(iv) What a selection rule has to fix.} In a source-built state, by (iii), $\lambda_{e} = 1 + \langle\hat{D}\rangle/2$ and the two heavier eigenvalues are $\lambda_{e} + g_{\pm}$, $g_{\pm}$ the eigenvalues of $G$ on $n^{\perp}$, with $g_{+} + g_{-} = \langle\hat{D}^{2}\rangle + \tfrac32\langle\hat{D}\rangle$. Under the mass-squared reading of the skeleton theorem, $g_{\mu}/g_{\tau} = (m_{\mu}^{2} - m_{e}^{2})/(m_{\tau}^{2} - m_{e}^{2})$ and $(\lambda_{\mu} + \lambda_{\tau})/\lambda_{e} = (m_{\mu}^{2} + m_{\tau}^{2})/m_{e}^{2}$. With the store's lepton masses (as in Proposition \claimref{synthesis.cumulants-at-fixed-second-moments-keep-the-floor}) the first is about $3.536 \times 10^{-3}$, and the second, about $1.2135 \times 10^{7}$, equals $2 + (\langle\hat{D}^{2}\rangle + \tfrac32\langle\hat{D}\rangle)/(1 + \tfrac12\langle\hat{D}\rangle)$, so it fixes the ratio $\langle\hat{D}^{2}\rangle/\langle\hat{D}\rangle$ near $6.07 \times 10^{6}$ for large $\langle\hat{D}\rangle$; for a state of one degree $D$ it fixes $D$ between $6.066 \times 10^{6}$ and $6.069 \times 10^{6}$ (from $D(2D + 3)/(D + 2) = (m_{\mu}^{2} + m_{\tau}^{2})/m_{e}^{2} - 2$ over the $\tau$ mass interval; under a linear reading the same equation with $m/m_{e}$ in place of $m^{2}/m_{e}^{2}$ gives $D \approx 1841$ and $g_{\mu}/g_{\tau} \approx 0.0592$). These numbers are arithmetic on measured masses, computed outside the check; they are a dictionary for the state a rule would have to select, not a prediction. The Gaussian class of the skeleton is a special case: its pair-number distribution has mean $2x$ and variance $2x(1 + x)$, so (ii) gives the light eigenvalue $1 + x = \cosh^{2}r$ and $\mathrm{tr}\,H = 3 + 8x + 6x^{2}$, the theorem's $2(1 + x) + (1 + 6x + 6x^{2})$; for states with the skeleton's second moments the floor $1 + \langle\hat{N}\rangle/4$ is the floor $\cosh^{2}r$ of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.cumulants-at-fixed-second-moments-keep-the-floor}, here reached on all of $\mathcal{S}$ without fixing the second moments. Remark on quasi-free states: a pure quasi-free state of zero mean is $\exp(\tfrac12 a^{\dagger}Ka^{\dagger})\ket{0}$ up to normalisation, fixed by its degree-two component; if it lies in $\mathcal{S}$, that component lies in $\mathrm{span}\{\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\ket{0}\}$, so the state is $\exp(\hat\Omega(v)^{\dagger})\ket{0}$ for some $v$, a polynomial in the single word $\hat\Omega(v)^{\dagger}$, and by (ii) its $G$ is annihilated on $v^{\perp}$ (since $\hat\Omega(y)$ differentiates along $y \perp v$ to zero), so its light pair is degenerate at the floor. On $\mathcal{S}$ every split of the light pair therefore needs a state outside the quasi-free class.
