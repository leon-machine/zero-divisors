The three displayed actions of \eqref{eq:action} are the three columns of $\iota$ in the basis $(\nO, \mathbb{V}, \nInf)$: $e_+ N e_+ = -\bigl(2t\,\nO + x + \tfrac12 s\,\nInf\bigr)$, and the overall factor $-1$ is projectively immaterial, as in Section \ref{sec:totality}. Preservation of the condition is the check $2\,(2t)(\tfrac12 s) = 2st$. The stratification of the ideal points is read off from \eqref{eq:incidence} with $s = 0$: nullity of $x$ is forced; if $x \neq 0$ and $t \neq 0$, scaling the class by $\tfrac{1}{2t}$ gives the unique representative with last slot $\tfrac12$, and Theorem \claimref{transport.totality}(3) exhibits every such class, and no other, as $\iota[X(x')]$ for the null vector $x'$; if $x \neq 0$ and $t = 0$ the class is the ray $[x]$ and only the direction of $x$ survives the projective quotient; and if $x = 0$ then $t \neq 0$, since $s = x = t = 0$ is not a class, leaving $[\nInf]$.
