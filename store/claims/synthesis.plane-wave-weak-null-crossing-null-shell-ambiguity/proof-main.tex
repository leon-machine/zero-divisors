The Christoffel symbols, geodesics and Schouten tensor below are those of the derived metric of the gravitational field, read as connection components, free-fall worldlines and the Schouten map of the field strength by Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}(ii), (iii) and (v). \emph{(1) The hypotheses.} The components of $g$ depend on $u$ alone and are continuous. Off $u_c$ the Christoffel symbols are computed from $\partial_u g$ only: $\Gamma^{u}{}_{bc} = 0$ (since $g^{ua} = \delta^a_v$ and nothing depends on $v$), so $\nabla du = -\Gamma^u{}_{bc}\,dx^b dx^c = 0$ and $du$ is a parallel null covector; the remaining nonzero symbols are $\Gamma^x{}_{ux} = \Gamma^y{}_{uy} = a_u/a$ (and their index-symmetric partners) and $\Gamma^v{}_{xx} = \Gamma^v{}_{yy} = a\,a_u$, all of them $a_u$ times a continuous function. Along a geodesic with $\nu = du(\gamma') \neq 0$, $u = u_c + \nu(s - s_c)$, the momenta $p_x = a^2 x'$, $p_y = a^2 y'$ are conserved and $v'$ is fixed by the normalisation $g(\gamma',\gamma') = \kappa$ as $v' = (\kappa + a^2(x'^2 + y'^2))/(2\nu)$, so $\gamma'$ is bounded and continuous; the parallel-transport equation $V' = -\Gamma(\gamma', V)$ then has a coefficient bounded by a multiple of $|a_u(u(s))|$, which is integrable in $s$ because $\int|a_u|\,du < \infty$, so it has a unique continuous solution across $s_c$ and a parallel frame along $\gamma$ exists. The Ricci tensor of $g$ off $u_c$ has the single component $\Ric^{\circ}_{uu} = -2a_{uu}/a$ in the convention in which the round sphere has positive Ricci curvature; the store's convention ($\Ric = \Lambda g$ for de Sitter space in signature $(1,3)$, the convention in which Fact \claimref{interior.imported-tractor-connection} is written) is $\Ric = -\Ric^{\circ}$, as the check confirms on de Sitter space in conformal time, where $\Ric^{\circ} = -3H^2 g$. So $\Ric = (2a_{uu}/a)\,du\otimes du$, $R = 0$ (since $g^{uu} = 0$), and the four-dimensional Schouten formula of Fact \claimref{interior.imported-tractor-connection} gives $\Sch = (a_{uu}/a)\,du\otimes du$. Hence $\Sch(\gamma',\cdot) = (a_{uu}/a)\,\nu\,du$ along $\gamma$: the decomposition of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.tractor-transport-weak-null-crossing} with $n = du$, $S = a_{uu}/a$, $R = 0$, read off $s_c$ as that theorem prescribes. Since $a$ is bounded above and below on compact subintervals and $ds = du/\nu$, the Tipler-type condition $\int|s - s_c||S|\,ds < \infty$ is $\int|u - u_c|\,|a_{uu}|\,du < \infty$.

\emph{(2) The removal scale.} With $u = u_c + \nu(s - s_c)$, $d^2(a\circ u)/ds^2 = \nu^2 a_{uu} = \nu^2 S\,a$, so $y = a\circ u$ solves $y'' = \nu^2 S y$; it is continuous and positive and $y' = \nu a_u$ is integrable, so it is admissible. By the proof of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.tractor-transport-weak-null-crossing}(2) its gauge is the change of splitting along $\gamma$ induced by the scale $e^{\omega}$ with $\omega = \log y$, which in the identification of a scale $\sigma$ with the metric $\sigma^{-2}g$ is $a^{-2}g = 2\,du\,dv/a^2 - dx^2 - dy^2 = \eta$, since $d\tilde u = du/a^2$. The metric $\eta$ is flat in the chart $(\tilde u, v, x, y)$, and the check confirms $a^{-2}g$ flat in the original chart for the model profile. The map $u \mapsto \tilde u$ has derivative $a^{-2}$, continuous and positive, so it is a $C^1$ change of coordinate with $C^1$ inverse, and in the new chart the conformal class of $g$ is that of $\eta$ on both sides, the smooth flat one. On each side the solutions of $y_{uu} = S y$ form a two-dimensional space spanned by $a$ and $a\tilde u$ (reduction of order: $(a w)_{uu} = S a w$ if and only if $(a^2 w_u)_u = 0$), so an admissible $y$ is $a(\alpha_\pm + \beta_\pm\tilde u)$, with $\alpha_+ = \alpha_- = \alpha > 0$ by continuity at $u_c$ (where $\tilde u = 0$). Its Wronskian against $a$ is $a\,y_u - a_u\,y = a^2(\alpha + \beta\tilde u)_u = \beta_\pm$.

\emph{(3) The ambiguity.} In the proof of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.tractor-transport-weak-null-crossing}(2), two admissible solutions $y_1, y_2$ give transports differing by $\exp(cN_n)$ inserted at the crossing, with $c$ the jump at $s_c$ of $\delta = r'/(\nu r)$, $r = y_2/y_1$. With $y_1 = a$ and $y_2 = y$, $r = \alpha + \beta_\pm\tilde u$, $r' = \nu\beta_\pm a^{-2}$, so $\delta = \beta_\pm/(a^2(\alpha + \beta_\pm\tilde u))$ and $c = (\beta_+ - \beta_-)/(\alpha\,a(u_c)^2)$. The scale of $y$ is the metric $y^{-2}g = \varphi^{-2}\eta$ with $\varphi = \alpha + \beta_\pm\tilde u$. On each side $\varphi$ is affine in $\tilde u$ and $\varphi^{-2}\eta$ is flat (the check verifies $(1 + 3w)^{-2}\eta$; a constant factor $\alpha^{-2}$ does not change flatness). For a general $\varphi(\tilde u)$ the only nonzero Ricci component is $\Ric^{\circ}_{\tilde u\tilde u} = 2\varphi''/\varphi$ (checked for a quadratic $\varphi$) and the scalar curvature vanishes, so in the store's convention $\Sch = -(\varphi''/\varphi)\,d\tilde u\otimes d\tilde u$; for the piecewise affine $\varphi$, $\varphi'' = (\beta_+ - \beta_-)\,\delta(\tilde u)$ distributionally, a null shell of strength $-(\beta_+ - \beta_-)/\alpha$ at $\tilde u = 0$, absent exactly when $\beta_+ = \beta_-$, that is when $c = 0$. The comparison is made in the chart $(\tilde u, v, x, y)$ because there the conformal class is smooth across $\tilde u = 0$, so its tractor connection is defined through the crossing, whereas in the original chart the Schouten tensor of $g$ is not defined at $u_c$ and the tractor connection of $[g]$ has no value there to compare with. Finally, the transport selected by $a$ is the one whose components in the splitting of $\eta$ are continuous across the crossing (its gauged variable $\hat x$ is continuous, and $\hat x$ is the tractor in the splitting of the scale of $a$). In the chart $(\tilde u, v, x, y)$ the conformal structure is smooth, its tractor connection in the splitting of the flat $\eta$ has bounded coefficients in an $\eta$-parallel frame, and its transport has continuous components in that splitting; the components in a fixed splitting are tensorial (a density, a one-form, a density) under the $C^1$ change of chart, and the $g$-parallel and $\eta$-parallel frames along $\gamma$ differ by a Levi-Civita change with coefficient a multiple of $a_u/a$, integrable, hence continuous across. So the transport of the smooth conformal structure is the one selected by $a$, and by uniqueness in (1) of the stored theorem no other.

\emph{(4) The model.} Put $t = (u_c - u)^{1/2}$ for $u < u_c$, so $a = 1 + t$, $du = -2t\,dt$, $a_u = -1/(2t)$, $a_{uu} = -1/(4t^3)$, $S = a_{uu}/a = -\tfrac14 t^{-3}(1 + t)^{-1} < 0$. Then $|a_u|\,du = dt$ (integrable), $a_u^2\,du = dt/(2t)$ (not integrable at $t = 0$), $|S|\,du = dt/(2t^2(1 + t))$ (not integrable), and $|u - u_c|\,|S|\,du = dt/(2(1 + t))$ (integrable). So the metric is continuous, its Christoffel symbols $a_u/a$ are integrable but not locally square-integrable, $S$ has a fixed sign and is not integrable, and the Tipler-type condition holds: Theorem \claimref{synthesis.tractor-transport-weak-null-crossing}(3) applies, and in the splitting of $g$ a parallel tractor has bounded components up to the crossing only if $\sigma = 0$ and $\ip{n}{\mu} = 0$ there. The check verifies the orders at $t = 0$ of the four integrands exactly.
