Let $W$ be a maximal isotropic (three-dimensional) subspace of $V_{\C}$ with a dual isotropic complement. The complexified ascended algebra acts irreducibly on $\Lambda^{\bullet}W$, and its two chirality halves are $\CS = \Lambda^{\mathrm{even}}W$ and $\CSm = \Lambda^{\mathrm{odd}}W$, each of dimension four. Every nonzero conformal spinor (twistor) $u \in \CS$ is a pure spinor: it is the Fock vacuum of the system of three fermionic ladder pairs built on its own annihilator space $W_{u}$, every state of the module is generated from $u$ by those pair operators, and $\CS$ (respectively $\CSm$) is what the even (respectively odd) products generate. Conformal spinors are states of the pair algebra, not its operators. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thm.~3.1]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.fock-model-complexified-ascended-algebra}) for the Fock model and the chirality halves; \cite[Thm.~5.3]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.twistors-are-states-three-ladder-pairs}) for the pure-spinor and Fock-vacuum statements; the tower paper \cite[Thms.~4.2, 4.3]{ButlerT3} for the iterated pair structure (one qubit per rung, Witt index as capacity) within which this sits.
