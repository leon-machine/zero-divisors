\emph{(i) The normal form.} By Proposition \claimref{synthesis.coherent-face-vector-real-slices}(i) the $S_{3}$-equivariant real structures are $\sigma_{x,y} = A\circ\overline{\phantom{v}}$ with $A = xP_{s} + yP_{d}$, $|x| = |y| = 1$, compatible with the bracket form exactly for $x, y = \pm1$. Choose a unitary $Q = x^{1/2}P_{s} + y^{1/2}P_{d}$ (any square roots); then on $P_{s}$ the product $Q^{\dagger}A\bar{Q}$ is $\overline{x^{1/2}}\,x\,\overline{x^{1/2}} = \bar{x}x = 1$, and likewise $\bar{y}y = 1$ on $P_{d}$, so $Q^{\dagger}A\bar{Q} = 1$; and for any unitary $M$ one has $M\sigma M^{-1} = (MAM^{\mathsf T})\circ\overline{\phantom{v}}$, since $\overline{M^{-1}w} = M^{\mathsf T}\bar{w}$. Hence $Q^{\dagger}\sigma Q = \overline{\phantom{v}}$, complex conjugation, and so does $M = FRQ^{\dagger}$ for every real orthogonal $F, R$, because $FRQ^{\dagger}A\bar{Q}R^{\mathsf T}F^{\mathsf T} = 1$. For $\sigma_{-1,1}$ the choice $Q = P_{d} - I_4 P_{s}$ is the quarter-turn of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.light-cone-koide-is-quarter-turn}; for $\sigma_{-1,-1}$, $Q = -I_4$; for $\sigma_{1,-1}$, $Q = P_{s} - I_4 P_{d}$. Now $Q^{\dagger}v = s_{0}(u' + w'f_{+})$ with $s_{0} = \bar{x}^{1/2}\kappa$ and $w' = \lambda e^{I_4\delta}$, $\delta = \chi + \arg(\bar{y}^{1/2}x^{1/2})$: for $\kappa = -I_4|\kappa|$ and $\sigma_{-1,1}$ this is $\delta = \chi - \pi/2$, the slice phase of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.coherent-face-vector-real-slices}(iii), and for $\sigma_{-1,-1}$ it is $\delta = \chi$. Up to the overall phase $s_{0}/|\kappa|$ the vector is $u' + \lambda e^{I_4\delta}f_{+}$, whose real and imaginary parts in family coordinates are $1 + \lambda\cos\delta_{k}$ and $\lambda\sin\delta_{k}$, $\delta_{k} = \delta + 2\pi k/3$ (Proposition \claimref{families.chart-computation-sign-twist-bracket-form} for $f_{+}$ and the Fourier lines). In the orthonormal frame $u = u'/\sqrt3$, $d_{1} = (2,-1,-1)/\sqrt6$, $d_{2} = (0,1,-1)/\sqrt2$ the doublet part of $e^{I_4\delta}f_{+}$ is $\sqrt{3/2}\,(d_{1}(\delta) + I_4\,d_{2}(\delta))$ with $d_{1}(\delta) = \cos\delta\,d_{1} - \sin\delta\,d_{2}$ and $d_{2}(\delta) = \sin\delta\,d_{1} + \cos\delta\,d_{2}$, the frame rotated by $\delta$ about $u$: the vector $(1, I_4)$ of the doublet plane is an eigenvector of every rotation of that plane. The rotation $R_{\delta}$ about the democratic axis by $-\delta$ (real orthogonal, commuting with $P_{s}$, $P_{d}$ and conjugation) carries it to $\sqrt{3/2}\,(d_{1} + I_4 d_{2})$, and the frame map $F$ (rows $u, d_{1}, d_{2}$) then gives
\[
M v \;=\; e^{I_4\alpha}\,|\kappa|\bigl(\sqrt3,\ \lambda\sqrt{3/2},\ I_4\lambda\sqrt{3/2}\bigr) \;=\; e^{I_4\alpha}\sqrt3\,|\kappa|\,(1, a, I_4 a), \qquad a = \lambda/\sqrt2 .
\]
Here $e^{I_4\alpha} = s_{0}/|\kappa| = \overline{x^{1/2}}\,\kappa/|\kappa|$ (for the quarter-turn $Q = P_{d} - I_4 P_{s}$ and $\kappa = -I_4|\kappa|$ it is $1$): the normal form is that of the line $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_4\}\,v$; its phase depends on $\sigma$ through $x$ and on the choice of square root, not on $\chi$, and it enters completions with fixed coefficients, since $1 + \hat\Omega(v)^{\dagger} + \hat\Omega(\sigma v)^{\dagger}$ is carried to $1 + e^{-I_4\alpha}\hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger} + e^{I_4\alpha}\hat\Omega(\bar{c})^{\dagger}$ with $c = \sqrt3|\kappa|(1, a, I_4 a)$ (as $\hat\Omega(e^{I_4\alpha}c)^{\dagger} = e^{-I_4\alpha}\hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger}$ and $M\sigma v = \overline{Mv}$), a relative phase $e^{-2I_4\alpha}$ between the two words; the symmetry statements of (ii) are insensitive to it. Neither $\sigma$ nor $\chi$ is left in the line. The check verifies, in exact arithmetic in $\Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt3)(I_4)$, for $\sigma_{-1,1}$ at $(a, \cos\chi) = (1, \tfrac35), (\tfrac23, \tfrac5{13}), (1, \tfrac5{13})$, for $\sigma_{-1,-1}$ and for $\sigma_{1,-1}$ at $(1, \tfrac35)$: $Q^{\dagger}A\bar{Q} = 1$, $M$ unitary, $MAM^{\mathsf T} = 1$, $|w'| = \lambda$, and $Mv = e^{I_4\alpha}\sqrt3(1, a, I_4 a)$ with $|e^{I_4\alpha}| = 1$. The consequence for completions: a state built from $\hat\Omega(v)^{\dagger}$, $\hat\Omega(\sigma v)^{\dagger}$ and $U(3)$-invariant words is carried by the Fock unitary $\hat{U}$ with $N(U) = M$ (every unitary is some $N(U)$, as $N(e^{I_4\theta}1) = e^{-2I_4\theta}$ and $N(U) = U$ on $SU(3)$) to the same construction on $(Mv, \overline{\phantom{v}})$, by Proposition \claimref{synthesis.light-pair-split-needs-broken-source-stabiliser}(i), the invariant words being unchanged; its mass form is $MHM^{\dagger}$, with the same spectrum; and the construction in the frame does not contain $\chi$. The same holds for words defined in the normal frame. It does not hold for words fixed in the family frame that are only $S_{3}$-invariant, such as the cubic word of (iv): $M$ depends on $\chi$ through $R_{\delta}$, so such a word is carried to a $\chi$-dependent word in the frame, and the spectrum can depend on $\chi$, which is the stored statement that only family-basis structure can see the channel phase. This is the mechanism of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.channel-phase-invisible-to-axially-covariant-completions}: the rotation about the democratic axis that shifts $\chi$ commutes with every equivariant real structure.

\emph{(ii) The residual stabiliser.} In the frame, $c = (1, a, I_4 a) = c_{R} + I_4 c_{I}$ with $c_{R} = (1, a, 0)$, $c_{I} = (0, 0, a)$, independent for $a > 0$. A unitary $N$ commuting with conjugation is real, hence real orthogonal; $Nc = c$ then gives $Nc_{R} = c_{R}$ and $Nc_{I} = c_{I}$, so $N - 1$ vanishes on the plane $\mathrm{span}\{c_{R}, c_{I}\}$ and $N = 1 + yn^{\mathsf T}$ for some $y$, with $n = c_{R}\times c_{I} = (a^{2}, -a, 0) \propto (a, -1, 0)$ the normal (the check computes the kernel $\{X : Xc_{R} = Xc_{I} = 0\}$ exactly and finds dimension $3$). Orthogonality, $N^{\mathsf T}N = 1$, reads $ny^{\mathsf T} + yn^{\mathsf T} + |y|^{2}nn^{\mathsf T} = 0$; contracting with a vector orthogonal to $n$ forces $y \parallel n$, $y = tn$, and then $2t + t^{2}|n|^{2} = 0$: $t = 0$ or $t = -2/|n|^{2}$. So the stabiliser is $\{1, R_{n}\}$, $R_{n}$ the reflection in the source's real plane; it is in the face action since $N(I_4 R_{n}) = \overline{\det(I_4 R_{n})}\,I_4 R_{n} = \overline{I_4^{3}\det R_{n}}\,I_4 R_{n} = \overline{(-I_4)(-1)}\,I_4 R_{n} = \overline{I_4}\,I_4 R_{n}$, that is $\det(I_4 R_{n}) = I_4^{3}\det R_{n} = I_4$ and $N = (-I_4)(I_4)R_{n} = R_{n}$. At $a = 1$, $n = (1, -1, 0)$ and $R_{n}$ is the transposition of the first two axes. Its commutant in $M_{3}(\mathrm{span}\{1, I_4\})$ is $\{X : X_{11} = X_{22}, X_{12} = X_{21}, X_{13} = X_{23}, X_{31} = X_{32}\}$, of complex dimension $5$ (the check solves $XR_{n} = R_{n}X$ over $\Q$ as a real linear system). If $\hat{U}\psi = \psi$ for $U = I_4 R_{n}$, then $H = R_{n}HR_{n}$ by covariance, so $Hn = h_{n}n$ and $H$ preserves $n^{\perp}$, where it is an arbitrary Hermitian form; states built from $\hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger}$, $\hat\Omega(\bar{c})^{\dagger}$ and $U(3)$-invariant words are invariant because $R_{n}c = c$, $R_{n}\bar{c} = \overline{R_{n}c} = \bar{c}$ ($R_{n}$ real) and $\hat{U}\ket{0} = \ket{0}$, by Proposition \claimref{synthesis.light-pair-split-needs-broken-source-stabiliser}(i). The plane $n^{\perp}$ is $\{\alpha c_{R} + \beta c_{I} : \alpha, \beta \in \mathrm{span}\{1, I_4\}\} \ni c$, and the Hermitian complement of $c$ in it is $m = c_{R} - I_4(|c_{R}|^{2}/|c_{I}|^{2})c_{I} = c_{R} - I_4(1 + 2/\lambda^{2})c_{I}$, since $|c_{R}|^{2} = 1 + a^{2}$ and $|c_{I}|^{2} = a^{2}$. The phase rotation $e^{I_4\phi}P_{c} + (1 - P_{c})$ fixes $c$ up to phase, lies in the $U(2)$ of the stored stabiliser result, and is not real for $\phi \notin \pi\mathbb{Z}$: the check exhibits it at $e^{I_4\phi} = \tfrac35 + \tfrac45 I_4$.

Carried back: $n_{\mathrm{orig}} = M^{\dagger}n$ has slice coordinates $Q^{\dagger}n_{\mathrm{orig}} = R_{\delta}^{\mathsf T}F^{\mathsf T}(a, -1, 0)^{\mathsf T} = au - d_{1}(\delta)$, real, with family coordinates $a/\sqrt3 - \sqrt{2/3}\cos\delta_{k} \propto a - \sqrt2\cos\delta_{k}$, because $(d_{1}(\delta))_{k} = \sqrt{2/3}\cos(\delta + 2\pi k/3)$. The projected vector of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.coherent-face-vector-real-slices}(iii) has coordinates $p_{k} = |\kappa|(1 + \lambda\cos\delta_{k})$, and $\sum_{k}(a - \sqrt2\cos\delta_{k})(1 + \lambda\cos\delta_{k}) = 3a - \sqrt2\lambda\cdot\tfrac32 = 0$ for $a = \lambda/\sqrt2$, using $\sum_{k}\cos\delta_{k} = 0$ and $\sum_{k}\cos^{2}\delta_{k} = \tfrac32$. At $a = 1$, $1 - \sqrt2\cos\delta_{k} = 2 - p_{k}/|\kappa|$, so $n \propto 2u' - p$, the reflection of $p$ through the democratic axis ($2(p\cdot u)u - p$ with $p\cdot u = \sqrt3|\kappa|$), and $\sum_{k}n_{k}^{2} = 3 + 2\cdot\tfrac32 = 6 = \tfrac23(\sum_{k}n_{k})^{2}$: a Koide vector. The check verifies the reality of the slice coordinates, the proportionality to $a - \sqrt2\cos\delta_{k}$ (with $\cos\delta_{k}$ read off $w'f_{+}$ exactly), both orthogonalities and, at $a = 1$, the reflection identity and $Q = \tfrac23$, in all five cases of (i).

\emph{(iii) The free block; computed.} The check works in the six-mode Fock space of the stored stabiliser check, with the face operators $\hat\Omega_{k} = \tfrac12\sum\epsilon_{kij}\epsilon_{AB}a_{iA}a_{jB}$ of Theorem \claimref{families.skeleton-rank-one-plus-ordering-shift} and the mass form in an arbitrary state of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.face-mass-form-unit-plus-gram}, exact complex-rational amplitudes, at $a = 1$. The eleven states are the truncated single source $(1 + \hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger} + \tfrac12\hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger 2})\ket{0}$; six two-word states with coefficients in $\Q(I_4)$ using $\hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger}$, $\hat\Omega(\bar{c})^{\dagger}$ and $\hat{V}$ (listed in the check); and the real family at $\beta = 0, 1, 2, -\tfrac12$. For each: $\hat{U}\psi = \psi$ with $U = I_4 P_{12}$ (the Fock map of the stored check), $H$ in the commutant of $P_{12}$, and $Hn = h_{n}n$ with $n = (1, -1, 0)$, $h_{n} = H_{11} - H_{12}$. In the basis $(1,1,0)/\sqrt2$, $e_{3}$ of $n^{\perp}$ the block is $B = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} H_{11} + H_{12} & \sqrt2 H_{13}\\ \sqrt2\bar{H}_{13} & H_{33}\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, and $h_{n}$ lies below both eigenvalues of $B$ exactly when $\det(B - h_{n}) > 0$ and $\mathrm{tr}\,B - 2h_{n} > 0$, above both when $\det(B - h_{n}) > 0$ and $\mathrm{tr}\,B - 2h_{n} < 0$. The five real coordinates $(H_{11}, H_{12}, \mathrm{Re}\,H_{13}, \mathrm{Im}\,H_{13}, H_{33})$ of the eleven forms have differences of rank $5$: the affine span is the whole commutant. The truncated single source has $h_{n} = \tfrac{32}{17}$, discriminant zero and $Hc \parallel c$ (the skeleton's alignment); the state $(1 + \hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger} + \tfrac23\hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger}\hat\Omega(\bar{c})^{\dagger})\ket{0}$ has $h_{n} = \tfrac{190}{101}$, three distinct eigenvalues and $Hc \not\parallel c$. In the real family, $\hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger} + \hat\Omega(\bar{c})^{\dagger} = \hat\Omega(c + \bar{c})^{\dagger} = \hat\Omega(2c_{R})^{\dagger}$ and $\hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger}\hat\Omega(\bar{c})^{\dagger} = \sum_{kl}\mathrm{Re}(\bar{c}_{k}c_{l})\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}^{\dagger}$ (the $\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}$ commute), so for real $\beta$ the amplitudes are real: the state is invariant under the antiunitary $\hat{J}$ that conjugates Fock amplitudes, which implements $\sigma$ in the frame ($\hat{J}\hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger}\hat{J} = \hat\Omega(\bar{c})^{\dagger}$, the $\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}$ being real). At $\beta = 0$ it is a single real source, degenerate by Proposition \claimref{synthesis.light-pair-split-needs-broken-source-stabiliser}(iv); at $\beta = 1, 2, -\tfrac12$ it is split, with $h_{n} = \tfrac{145}{77}$ ($B$: $\tfrac{521}{77}$, $0$, $\tfrac{229}{77}$), $\tfrac{505}{257}$ and $\tfrac{55}{32}$. A Gaussian state $\exp(\hat\Omega(x)^{\dagger})\ket{0}$ is $\hat{J}$-invariant exactly when $\hat\Omega(x)^{\dagger} = \hat\Omega(\bar{x})^{\dagger}$, that is $x$ real, and real sources leave the pair degenerate; the Gaussian class (Fact \claimref{families.imported-squeezed-vacuum-bosonic-pairing}) gains nothing from reality. In all eight split instances $\det(B - h_{n}) > 0$ and $\mathrm{tr}\,B - 2h_{n} > 0$: $n$ is the lowest eigenvector. The $\mathbb{Z}_{2}$-invariant word $(\hat\Omega(n)^{\dagger})^{2}$ (invariant since $\hat\Omega(R_{n}n)^{\dagger} = -\hat\Omega(n)^{\dagger}$) reverses this: $(1 + \hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger} + 2\hat\Omega(n)^{\dagger 2})\ket{0}$ has $h_{n} = \tfrac{1738}{199}$ above both eigenvalues of $B$. In exploration (work folder, not part of the check) $150$ random states with coefficients in $\Q(I_4)$ and words of length up to four in $\hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger}$, $\hat\Omega(\bar{c})^{\dagger}$, $\hat{V}$ gave $85$ split instances, all with $n$ lowest; no proof is offered, and the statement is recorded as an observation.

\emph{(iv) The cubic family word.} Under $\hat{U}$, $\hat\Omega(e_{k})^{\dagger} \mapsto \hat\Omega(Ne_{k})^{\dagger}$ (Proposition \claimref{synthesis.light-pair-split-needs-broken-source-stabiliser}(i)), so the word $\hat{W} = \hat\Omega_{1}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{2}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{3}^{\dagger}$ maps to $\prod_{k}\hat\Omega(Ne_{k})^{\dagger}$, which equals $\hat{W}$ exactly when $\{Ne_{k}\}$ is $\{d_{k}e_{\pi(k)}\}$ with $\prod_{k}\bar{d}_{k} = 1$ (the $\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}$ commute, so any permutation $\pi$ is allowed and $\hat\Omega(de)^{\dagger} = \bar{d}\hat\Omega(e)^{\dagger}$): the monomial group with unit product of phases. The check verifies invariance under the Fock maps of $N = \mathrm{diag}(I_4, -I_4, 1)$ and of a $3$-cycle with phases $(I_4, 1, -I_4)$, and non-invariance under a real rotation of the first two families. A monomial $N$ with $Nv = v$ needs $d_{k}v_{\pi^{-1}(k)} = v_{k}$, so $|v_{k}| = |v_{\pi^{-1}(k)}|$ for all $k$; by Proposition \claimref{families.rigidity-pairing-sector-one-event}, $v_{k} = \kappa(1 + \lambda e^{I_4\chi}\zeta^{k})$ and $|v_{k}|^{2} = |\kappa|^{2}(1 + \lambda^{2} + 2\lambda\cos(\chi + 2\pi k/3))$, three distinct numbers unless $\cos\chi_{j} = \cos\chi_{k}$ for some $j \ne k$, which happens exactly for $\chi \in \tfrac{\pi}{3}\mathbb{Z}$; then $\pi = 1$ and $d_{k} = 1$. The check computes the three moduli exactly at $\lambda = \sqrt2$ for $\cos\chi = \tfrac35$ and $\tfrac5{13}$ (distinct; only the identity permutation preserves them) and at $\chi = 2\pi/3$ (two coincide). The physical phase of the projection reading, $\delta = 0.222$, $\chi = \delta + \pi/2 \approx 1.79$, is not in $\tfrac{\pi}{3}\mathbb{Z}$ (nearest members $1.05$ and $2.09$). So a completion built from the source and the cubic word is covariant under nothing, and its mass form is unconstrained by symmetry; by Proposition \claimref{synthesis.channel-phase-invisible-to-axially-covariant-completions} it is also the only kind that can see $\chi$ at all.

\emph{Conclusion.} The stored result required a second face-space structure breaking the source's stabiliser. The two candidates it named do break it, to $\mathbb{Z}_{2}$ and to the trivial group, and in both cases what covariance then fixes is at most one eigenvector; the split is a function of the state's coefficients, which the series does not fix, and the real structure cannot even see the channel phase. Hence no construction of this kind produces $m_{\mu}/m_{e}$ with no second free number, and the open problem's remaining content is a rule selecting the non-Gaussian frozen vacuum.
