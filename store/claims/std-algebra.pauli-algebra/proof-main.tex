The Pauli matrices square to $1$ and anticommute pairwise, so by the universal property (\claimref{std-algebra.existence-uniqueness}) $e_k \mapsto \sigma_k$ extends to a homomorphism $\Cl(3,0) \to \Mat_2(\C)$. The images of the eight blades are linearly independent over $\R$ (the check computes the rank), and both algebras have real dimension $8$ (\claimref{std-algebra.blade-basis}), so it is an isomorphism. $\sigma_1 \sigma_2 \sigma_3 = \mathrm{i}\,1$ by direct multiplication. $I$ is central by \claimref{primer.centre} and squares to $-1$ by \claimref{primer.sign}, consistent with its image.
