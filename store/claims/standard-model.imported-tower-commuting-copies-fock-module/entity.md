## formal
The transport principle completes $\Cl(p,q)$ to $\Cl(p{+}1, q{+}1)$ by adjoining one hyperbolic pair, and iterates: the $k$-th rung is $T_{k} = \Cl(p{+}k, q{+}k)$ with adjoined null pairs $v_{j}, w_{j}$ satisfying $v_{i}v_{j} + v_{j}v_{i} = 0$, $w_{i}w_{j} + w_{j}w_{i} = 0$, $v_{i}w_{j} + w_{j}v_{i} = \delta_{ij}$. Inside $T_{k}$ the subalgebra $B_{k}$ generated by the $2k$ plane generators is $\Cl(k,k) \cong \mathrm{M}_{2^{k}}(\R)$, the twisted ground generators $x\,\omega_{1}\cdots\omega_{k}$ generate a commuting copy $A_{k} \cong \Cl(p,q)$, and multiplication $A_{k} \otimes B_{k} \to T_{k}$ is an isomorphism. The Chevalley action makes $\Lambda^{\bullet}\operatorname{span}(v_{1}, \ldots, v_{k})$ an irreducible $B_{k}$-module, one qubit per rung. On each complexified floor there is a conjugate-linear antiautomorphism $*$ under which the ladder pairs are mutually adjoint, $v_{j}^{*} = w_{j}$. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thms.~3.2, 4.2]{ButlerT3}; proved there.

## technical
The transport completes $\Cl(p,q)$ to $\Cl(p+1,q+1)$ by adjoining one hyperbolic pair, and iterates: the $k$-th rung is $T_k = \Cl(p+k,q+k)$, with adjoined null pairs satisfying $v_iv_j + v_jv_i = 0$, $w_iw_j + w_jw_i = 0$, $v_iw_j + w_jv_i = \delta_{ij}$. Inside $T_k$ the plane generators give $B_k \cong \Cl(k,k) \cong \Mat_{2^k}(\R)$, the twisted ground generators $x\,\omega_1 \cdots \omega_k$ give a commuting copy $A_k \cong \Cl(p,q)$, and multiplication $A_k \otimes B_k \to T_k$ is an isomorphism.

The Chevalley action makes $\Lambda^\bullet \operatorname{span}(v_1, \ldots, v_k)$ an irreducible $B_k$-module, one qubit per rung, and on each complexified floor a conjugate-linear antiautomorphism makes the ladder pairs mutually adjoint, $v_j^* = w_j$.

## lay
Each completion step adds a pair of directions that acts like the creation and destruction operators of one fermion. After k steps the algebra is the original one alongside an independent matrix algebra for the k fermion modes, the two commuting with each other. The fermion modes act on a state space that doubles with every step, one qubit per step.

## one-liner
The tower's $k$-th rung $\Cl(p+k,q+k)$ factors as a commuting product $\Cl(p,q) \otimes \Mat_{2^k}(\R)$, its null pairs satisfy the CAR as vectors and act on an irreducible Fock module, one qubit per rung, and are mutually adjoint on each complexified floor.
