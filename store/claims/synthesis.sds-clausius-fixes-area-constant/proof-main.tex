\emph{The family.} Write $g(r) := r f(r) = r - 2M - r^{3}/\ell^{2}$. Then $g(0) = -2M < 0$, $g$ has its maximum on $r > 0$ at $r = \ell/\sqrt{3}$ with value $\tfrac{2\ell}{3\sqrt{3}} - 2M$, positive exactly when $M < M_{N}$, and $g \to -\infty$; so for $0 < M < M_{N}$ there are exactly two positive zeros, $r_{b} < \ell/\sqrt{3} < r_{c}$, and since the zeros of $-\ell^{2}g$ sum to $0$ the third is $-(r_{b} + r_{c})$. Comparing coefficients in $-\ell^{2}g(r) = (r - r_{b})(r - r_{c})(r + r_{b} + r_{c})$ gives $\ell^{2} = r_{b}^{2} + r_{b}r_{c} + r_{c}^{2}$ and $2M\ell^{2} = r_{b}r_{c}(r_{b} + r_{c})$; in particular $r_{c}^{2} < \ell^{2}$. On the horizon locus $f(r_{h}) = 0$ the mass is $M = \tfrac12 r_{h}(1 - r_{h}^{2}/\ell^{2})$, a function of either radius, with $dM/dr_{h} = \tfrac12(1 - 3r_{h}^{2}/\ell^{2})$, positive on the black-hole branch and negative on the cosmological branch.

\emph{(i).} By (T), $4\pi T_{h} = |f'(r_{h})|$ with $f'(r_{h}) = 2M/r_{h}^{2} - 2r_{h}/\ell^{2} = r_{h}^{-1}(1 - 3r_{h}^{2}/\ell^{2})$ after substituting $2M = r_{h}(1 - r_{h}^{2}/\ell^{2})$; so $f'(r_{b}) > 0 > f'(r_{c})$. Hence $T_{h}\,d(\pi r_{h}^{2}) = \tfrac{|f'(r_{h})|}{4\pi}\,2\pi r_{h}\,dr_{h} = \pm\tfrac12(1 - 3r_{h}^{2}/\ell^{2})\,dr_{h} = \pm\,dM$, with the upper sign on the black-hole branch and the lower on the cosmological one. So $S_{h} = \pi r_{h}^{2} = A_{h}/4$ satisfies (Cl) on each branch, and since (Cl) fixes $dS_{h}/dr_{h}$ on a connected interval of $M$, every solution is $A_{h}/4 + c_{h}$ with a constant $c_{h}$, and every constant occurs. The same computation at $\Lambda = 0$ is $f'(2M) = 1/2M = 2\kappa$ with $\kappa = 1/4M$, the input of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.quarter-from-temperature}, which this extends; at $M = 0$ it is $4\pi T_{c} = 2/\ell$, so $(2\pi T_{c})^{2} = 1/\ell^{2} = Q(I_{\Lambda})$, the temperature of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.de-sitter-temperature-frozen-point-norm}.

\emph{(ii).} As $M \to 0$ on the black-hole branch, $r_{b} \to 0$ (from $2M\ell^{2} = r_{b}r_{c}(r_{b} + r_{c}) > r_{b}r_{c}^{2} > r_{b}\ell^{2}/3$, so $r_{b} < 6M$), hence $A_{b} \to 0$ and $S_{b} \to c_{b}$.

\emph{(iii).} As $M \to M_{N}$ the maximum of $g$ tends to $0$ and both zeros tend to the double zero $\ell/\sqrt{3}$ (at $r_{b} = r_{c} = r_{N}$ the relations above give $\ell^{2} = 3r_{N}^{2}$ and $M = r_{N}/3 = M_{N}$, with $f(r_{N}) = f'(r_{N}) = 0$). So $A_{b}$ and $A_{c}$ have the common limit $4\pi\ell^{2}/3$, and $\lim S_{b} = \lim S_{c}$ if and only if $c_{b} = c_{c}$.

\emph{(iv).} As $M \to 0$ on the cosmological branch, $\ell^{2} = r_{b}^{2} + r_{b}r_{c} + r_{c}^{2}$ with $r_{b} \to 0$ gives $r_{c} \to \ell$, so $S_{c} \to \pi\ell^{2} + c_{c}$, which is $\pi\ell^{2}$ under (Z) and (N). In Planck units $\ell^{2} = 1/H^{2} = M_{P}^{2}/Q(I_{\Lambda}) = N^{2}$ by Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-frozen-point-dictionary}, with $Q(I_{\Lambda}) = \Lambda/3$ checked on the frozen point $I_{\Lambda} = \ninf + (\Lambda/6)\nO$ by the script, in the normalisation $\nO\cdot\nInf = +1$ that the script uses ($\nO = \tfrac12(e_{+} + e_{-})$, $\nInf = e_{+} - e_{-}$); in T01's normalisation $\nO\cdot\nInf = -1$ the same vector has $Q(I_{\Lambda}) = -\Lambda/3$.

\emph{(v).} A common constant $c$ leaves (T) and (Cl) untouched by (i) and gives equal limits at the merger, so (N) holds; (Z) fails since $S_{b} \to c \neq 0$. As $M \to 0$, $S_{b} \to c$, so $c < 0$ makes $S_{b}$ negative for all small $M$; each boundary mode's thermal entropy is $s \ge 0$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.boundary-mode-thermal-entropy}: $s$ is even and decreasing in $|x|$ with limit $0$), so any sum of such entropies is nonnegative and nonnegativity alone excludes $c < 0$, while (Z) fixes $c = 0$ exactly. For orientation (the bound is computed in the result filed alongside, synthesis.euler-term-horizon-entropy-shift): since $r_{b} < \ell/\sqrt{3}$ on the family, a constant $c = -\pi\ell^{2}$ would give $S_{b} < -\tfrac23\pi\ell^{2}$ for every black hole of the family, the inequality the script checks. That the Gauss-Bonnet term of the action of Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector} contributes a constant of this kind rests on the Wald (Jacobson-Myers) entropy of an Euler term, which is proportional to the Euler characteristic of the horizon cross-section and so the same for every spherical horizon of the family (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-wald-noether-charge-entropy}(c), read as horizon entropy).

The script checks exactly, on five rational horizon pairs: both radii are zeros of $f$, the factorisation of $\ell^{2} r f(r)$, the bracketing by the Nariai radius, the mass function and its derivative as rational functions of $r$, the two Clausius identities, the inequalities $r_{b}^{2} < \ell^{2}/3 < r_{c}^{2} < \ell^{2}$ and the orientation bound of (v); the double zero at the Nariai merger; the $M = 0$ and $\Lambda = 0$ limits, the former against the frozen point's norm in the conformal model.
