Isotropy: $X(x)$ is null by Fact \claimref{pairing.imported-flat-conformal-model-twistor-incidence}, the lifts are null because $\ninf$ is null and orthogonal to $\ell$, $m$, and $\ip{X(x)}{\ell(x)} = \ip{x}{\ell} - \ip{x}{\ell}\ip{\nz}{\ninf} = 0$, likewise for $m$. The bivector identity is $\tfrac12[u\wedge v, w] = \ip{v}{w}u - \ip{u}{w}v$ with $u = e_{a}$, $v = \ninf$, $w = X(x)$ and $\ip{\ninf}{X} = 1$, $\ip{e_{a}}{X} = x_{a}$. The classes: $\ip{\Omega(dx)\cdot X}{\ell(x)} = \ip{dx^{a}(e_{a} - x_{a}\ninf)}{\ell(x)} = \ip{dx}{\ell}$ since $dx \perp \ninf$ and $\ninf \perp \ell(x)$; the same for $m$; and $\Omega(dx)\cdot\ell(x) = -\ip{dx}{\ell}\ninf$ is orthogonal to $m(x)$. A real $dx$ with $\ip{dx}{\ell} = \ip{dx}{m} = 0$ is orthogonal to $\ell, m, \mb$, hence to $\ell^{\perp}$, hence a multiple of $\ell$; the script checks the complex counterexample $dx = m$ and the ingoing $dx = \mb$ with classes $(0, -2, 0)$. Incidence is the expansion of $X(x + s\ell)$ with $Q(\ell) = 0$. The dimension is the rank of the three functionals on the fifteen basis bivectors of $\Cl(2,4)$. The script verifies each statement at four rational points and twenty random real displacements, the four named real ones and the two complex ones, the translation identity for each $a$, incidence at three values of $s$, and, for the dimension, that the common kernel of the three functionals has dimension twelve and coincides with the stabiliser on ten random kernel elements and on every basis bivector.
