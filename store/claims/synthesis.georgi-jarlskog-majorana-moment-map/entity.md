## formal
Under Hypothesis (S), on the five-mode module with the pairing $B$ of Lemma \claimref{standard-model.invariant-pairing-five-modes}, let $T = v_{1}v_{2}v_{3}v_{4}v_{5}$ be the Majorana element of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.majorana-element}, let $\omega = \sum_{i \le 3} v_{i} \wedge w_{i}$ be the pair-number generator, so that $B - L = -\tfrac23\,\omega$, and let $\chi$ and $T_{\mathrm{GJ}} = w_{4}(B - L)\chi$ be as in Proposition \claimref{families.doublet-direction-module-georgi-jarlskog-clebsch}. Define the moment map $\mu(T)$ as the orthogonal projection, for the trace form, of $[T, T^{\dagger}]$ onto the span of the bivectors of the ten, and write $\mu_{P}$ and $\mu_{R}$ for its components along the bivectors of the pair space and of the rung space, the split $\so(6) \oplus \so(4)$ that the slot/pair split selects (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.automorphisms-preserve-slot-pair-split-are}). Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $\mu(T) = \tfrac18\sum_{i \le 5} v_{i} \wedge w_{i} = \tfrac18 X$, with $X = 2T_{3R} - \tfrac32(B - L)$ the thirteenth generator of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor}(3); it has no component mixing the pair and rung spaces, and $\mu_{P} = \tfrac18\,\omega = -\tfrac{3}{16}(B - L)$, $\mu_{R} = \tfrac14 T_{3R}$;
\item[(ii)] for every polynomial $p$, the symmetric part of the mass form of $w_{4}\,p(\mu(T))$ on the sixteen is a multiple of the $10$ form: the unsplit moment map gives $|y_{e}| = |y_{d}|$ only;
\item[(iii)] $w_{4}\,\mu_{P} = -\tfrac{3}{16}\,w_{4}(B - L)$, the grade-three direction of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.grade-three-georgi-jarlskog-direction}, with antisymmetric mass form;
\item[(iv)] $w_{4}\,\mu_{R}\,\mu_{P} = \tfrac{3}{256}\,w_{4}(B - L)(\chi - 1) = \tfrac{3}{256}\bigl(T_{\mathrm{GJ}} - w_{4}(B - L)\bigr)$. Its mass form has symmetric part $\tfrac{3}{256}$ times the Georgi-Jarlskog form, ratio $|y_{e}/y_{d}| = 3$, and antisymmetric part $-\tfrac{3}{256}$ times the grade-three form, the two equal in modulus entry by entry; the operator annihilates the doublet sector $N_{R} = 1$.
\end{enumerate}
So the pair-sector $\uu(1)$ current that $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$ lacks is the Pati-Salam pair component of the moment map of the Majorana element, and one composite of the rung vector with the two components of that moment map carries both the $126$ term of the second family and the grade-three term between families, with equal moduli. The coupling constant of such a composite, and a dynamics selecting it, are not supplied.

## technical
Under Hypothesis (S), project the commutator of the Majorana element $T = v_{1}\cdots v_{5}$ with its adjoint onto the bivectors of the ten (its moment map). The result is one eighth of the thirteenth generator $X = 2T_{3R} - \tfrac32(B - L)$, with pair component $\tfrac18\omega = -\tfrac{3}{16}(B - L)$ and rung component $\tfrac14 T_{3R}$.

Functions of the full moment map, multiplied into the rung vector $w_{4}$, give only the relation of a single $10$, $|y_{e}| = |y_{d}|$. Split along Pati-Salam they give more: $w_{4}\mu_{P}$ is the grade-three ($120$) direction, and $w_{4}\mu_{R}\mu_{P} = \tfrac{3}{256}(T_{\mathrm{GJ}} - w_{4}(B - L))$ has as symmetric part the Georgi-Jarlskog $126$ form, with ratio $3$, and as antisymmetric part the grade-three form, with equal moduli. It acts only on the singlet sectors, the one-sided coupling of Pati-Salam. Clause (i) is the standard relation between the moment map of a weight vector and its weight, here the $\uu(1)_{X}$ of $\mathrm{Spin}(10) \supset SU(5) \times U(1)_{X}$, and is transport-absent; after it $T$ plays no further role, and (iv) is the identity $w_{4}T_{3R}(B - L) = -\tfrac14\bigl(T_{\mathrm{GJ}} - w_{4}(B - L)\bigr)$ among $w_{4}$, $T_{3R}$ and $B - L$. The series' missing factor is therefore the pair component of the moment map of an element it derives. A coupling constant, and a reason for the dynamics to take this composite, are not derived.

## lay
The series could not produce the term that makes the muon about three times heavier than the strange quark at high energy: it had the three ingredients but no way to multiply them together. This result shows that, under the series' slot-statistics hypothesis (S), one of those ingredients, the charge that distinguishes quarks from leptons, is exactly what the series' own heavy-neutrino element produces when it is squared and projected onto the symmetries of the Pati-Salam picture. Multiplying the Higgs direction by the quark-lepton piece alone gives only a pattern that couples different families; multiplying it by both of the element's Pati-Salam pieces gives the Georgi-Jarlskog factor of three, together with that pattern. What is still missing is the strength of this coupling, which nothing in the series fixes.

## one-liner
Under (S), the Majorana element's moment map is $X/8$; its Pati-Salam pair component is the $B - L$ current, and the rung vector times its two components gives the Georgi-Jarlskog $126$ form plus the grade-three form with equal moduli, while the unsplit moment map gives only the $10$ relation.
