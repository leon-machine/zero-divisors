Immediate from Lemma \claimref{sheaf.blade-basis-degenerate-forms-included}: over any open set on which $T^*M$ admits a frame, the blades $e_I$ are $2^n$ sections of $\shA$ whose values at each point form a basis of the fibre (the lemma applies at each $x$ separately, to the form $G_x$, with no orthogonality needed), so they are a frame of $\shA$; the structure functions are the polynomials of the lemma evaluated on the smooth functions $G(e_i, e_j)$.
