\emph{(i).} For a differentiable family of density matrices with $\rho_{0} = \rho_{I}$ faithful, $\frac{d}{dt}S(\rho_{t})|_{0} = -\Tr(\dot\rho\ln\rho_{I}) - \Tr\dot\rho$ (the derivative of $\Tr\,\phi(\rho_{t})$ for $\phi(x) = -x\ln x$ is $\Tr(\phi'(\rho_{0})\dot\rho)$, a standard consequence of the spectral calculus for a differentiable family of Hermitian matrices), and $\Tr\dot\rho = 0$. So $\dot S = \Tr(\dot\rho K_{F}) = \beta\Tr(\dot\rho H_{F}) + \ln Z_{F}\Tr\dot\rho = \beta\Tr(\dot\rho H_{F})$. Only the Fock-diagonal part of $\dot\rho$ enters, since $K_{F}$ is diagonal; the script checks $\dot S = \beta\,\dot E$ as an identity of log symbols on three traceless diagonal variations.

\emph{(ii).} For a state $\rho$ and faithful $\rho_{I}$, $S(\rho\|\rho_{I}) := \Tr\rho(\ln\rho - \ln\rho_{I}) = -S(\rho) + \Tr(\rho K_{F}) = -S(\rho) + \beta\Tr(\rho H_{F}) + \ln Z_{F}$, which rearranges to the display. Klein's inequality (the relative entropy of finite-dimensional density matrices is nonnegative and vanishes only at equality, by convexity of $x\ln x$; standard, cited from memory) gives the bound and its equality case. At fixed $\Tr(\rho K_{F})$ the entropy is therefore maximal exactly at $\rho_{I}$. Expanding $S(\rho_{t}\|\rho_{I})$ to second order shows it is $O(t^{2})$, since its first derivative at $0$ is $-\dot S + \Tr(\dot\rho K_{F}) = 0$ by (i): the first law is exact at first order and fails at second order by the relative entropy. Per mode, with $\phi(q) = -q\ln q - (1 - q)\ln(1 - q) - xq$, $x = \beta\omega_{j}$: $\phi'(q) = \ln((1 - q)/q) - x$, zero exactly at $(1 - q)/q = e^{x}$, i.e.\ $q = n_{j}$, and $\phi''(q) = -1/(q(1 - q)) < 0$; the script checks the identity $D(q\|n_{j}) = \phi(n_{j}) - \phi(q)$ term by term in log symbols, its vanishing at $q = n_{j}$, and the stationarity and concavity statements, at rational trial occupations.
