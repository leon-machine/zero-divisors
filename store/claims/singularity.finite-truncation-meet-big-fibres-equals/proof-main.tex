The meet: an element fixed by every $\operatorname{Ad}\sigma(x_j)$ is diagonal in the joint number basis, and conversely (Proposition \claimref{singularity.p-reduced-metric-level-reading-many}), and $\operatorname{range}(E_F)$ is that algebra, of dimension $2^N$. The join: the $\sigma(x_j)$ commute, are single-site in the Jordan-Wigner representation (the strings cancel in $v^{*}v - vv^{*}$), and generate the $2^N$ independent monomials $\prod_{j \in S}\sigma(x_j)$, $S \subseteq F$, each joint-diagonal; so the join is a subalgebra of the meet of the same dimension. Compatible evaluations of $\mathfrak{M}$ at the points of $F$ are exactly tuples in $\prod_j \Aa_{x_j}^{+}$ (every tuple occurs, by bump functions), and the algebra they generate is the join.
