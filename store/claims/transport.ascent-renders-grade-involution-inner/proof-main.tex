By Lemma \claimref{transport.decomposition-commutation}, $\nu a \nu^{-1} = \hat{a}\,\nu\nu^{-1} = \hat{a}$. Since $\omega$ has odd grade $n$, $\hat{\omega} = -\omega$ (\claimref{std-algebra.involution-properties}), whence $\varepsilon_\pm \mapsto \varepsilon_\mp$. The displayed sum lies in the ideal generated by $\varepsilon_+$ (both summands are multiples of $\varepsilon_+$ by ring elements), and equals $1$.
