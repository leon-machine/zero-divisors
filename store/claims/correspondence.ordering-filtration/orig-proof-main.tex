Reordering a word is a sequence of adjacent transpositions; transposing $Z^\alpha$ past $\bar Z_\beta$ changes the word by $\hbar\,\delta^\alpha_\beta$ times the word with both letters deleted (the relations; transpositions within the $Z$'s or within the $\bar Z$'s change nothing). (1): for degree $\le 2$ the deleted word is a scalar. (2): for degree $4$ each transposition produces $\hbar$ times a degree-$2$ word, whose own ordering ambiguity is central by (1); deleting one $Z$ and one $\bar Z$ from a balanced word leaves a mixed word $Z^\alpha\bar Z_\beta$, which is $q(E_{\alpha\beta})$ up to a central term by Lemma \claimref{correspondence.symmetrised-quadratics-palatial-algebra}(3); the $q$-computation is Lemma \claimref{correspondence.symmetrised-quadratics-palatial-algebra}(2). (3): by (1) the quadratic ambiguities are scalars, and degree-$3$ monomials reorder with $\hbar\times$(linear) discrepancies, which are vector-valued, not $\mathfrak{gl}(\T)$-valued; degree $4$ is the first with a $\mathfrak{gl}(\T)$-valued discrepancy.
