Write the rung's hyperbolic plane orthogonal to the Lorentz frame as $\mathrm{span}(e_{+}, e_{-})$ with $e_{+}^{2} = +1$, $e_{-}^{2} = -1$; the frozen point is $I = c\,e_{+} + d\,e_{-}$ with $c^{2} - d^{2} = \Lambda/3$, and the orthogonal vector in the plane is $w = d\,e_{+} + c\,e_{-}$ with $Q(w) = d^{2} - c^{2} = -Q(I)$ and $\ip{I}{w} = cd - cd = 0$. (i) For $\Lambda \neq 0$, $V$ is the section of $I^{\perp}$ of norm $-3/\Lambda$ orthogonal to the Lorentz frame (Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-frozen-point}), so $V$ is a multiple of $w$ and $Q(I)\,Q(V) < 0$ is the identity of Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-frozen-point-degenerate-algebras}. Orthogonal vectors anticommute in a Clifford algebra. (ii) $I^{\perp} = \{I,V\}^{\perp} \oplus \mathrm{span}(w)$ by dimension, and $e_{V}^{2} = \operatorname{sign}Q(V) = -\operatorname{sign}\Lambda$; so the five generators have signature $(1,3) + (0,1) = (1,4)$ for $\Lambda > 0$ and $(1,3) + (1,0) = (2,3)$ for $\Lambda < 0$, which are the two extensions of Corollary \claimref{odd-extensions.four-extensions-oriented-pair}. The five together with $e_{I}$ are an orthogonal basis of the rung's vector space, so they generate $\Cl(2,4)$. (iii) At $c = d$, $Q(I) = 0$ and $w = I$, so $I \in I^{\perp}$, the induced form has the one-dimensional radical $\mathrm{span}(I)$, and the Clifford algebra of the five generators has one null generator: $\Cl(1,3;1)$, with the ideal and its square as in Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-frozen-point-degenerate-algebras}. (iv) The Lorentz frame is orthogonal to both $e_{+}$ and $e_{-}$ and its signature does not depend on $c, d$. The script verifies (i) to (iv) on exact rational instances with $Q(I) = 3$, $-3$ and $0$: the orthogonality relations, the signatures $(1,4)$, $(2,3)$ and $(1,3;1)$ by exact congruence, the anticommutation, the generation of all sixty-four dimensions when $Q(I) \neq 0$, the nilpotent generator and its ideal in $\Cl(1,3;1)$, and the signature $(1,3)$ of the Lorentz frame in all three cases.
