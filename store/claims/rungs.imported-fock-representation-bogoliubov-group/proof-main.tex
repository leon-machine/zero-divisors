For a complex Hermitian space $E$ of dimension $n$, the Fock space $\Lambda^{\bullet}E$ is the irreducible spinor module of the complex Clifford algebra on $2n$ generators, its parity grading the chirality decomposition; the orthogonal transformations of the $2n$ Majorana generators preserving the canonical anticommutation relations form the compact $O(2n)$, implemented by $\mathrm{Pin}(2n)$, with $SO(2n)$ implemented by $\mathrm{Spin}(2n)$; those preserving the complex structure form $U(n)$, acting as $\Lambda^{\bullet}(g)$ up to a determinant phase. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Fact~2.11]{ButlerT12} (\claimref{standard-model.imported-fock-representation-clifford-algebra-bogoliubov}); \cite[Lecture~20]{FultonHarris1991}; \cite[Ch.~I, \S\S 2, 5]{LawsonMichelsohn1989}.

This restates \claimref{rungs.imported-fock-representation-bogoliubov-group-external}.
