\emph{Conventions.} As in Theorem \claimref{synthesis.field-strength-cancels-from-the-leading-null-singularity-of-hadamard-dirac-states-on-null-hypersurfaces}: physical vectors as in Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}, signature $(+,-,-,-)$, $D_{a}\psi = a\cdot\nabla\psi + \tfrac12\Omega(a)\psi$, $D = g^{\mu}D_{e_{\mu}}$; spinors are even multivectors, the operators below act by left multiplication, and the mass term and the unit $i$ act on the right, so they commute with every left factor (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-hadamard-parametrix-of-the-squared-dirac-operator-leads-with-van-vleck-root-times-spin-transport}(b)). The frame $\{k, n, f_{1}, f_{2}\}$ is parallel along the generators, $k\cdot n = 1$, $f^{c} = -f_{c}$, so $\nabla = nD_{k} + kD_{n} + f^{c}D_{f_{c}}$; $\Lambda = \tfrac12 nk$, $J$, $a$, $B = J'J^{-1}$ (self-adjoint: $\mathcal{H}$ is twist-free), $\theta = \mathrm{tr}\,B = a'/a$, $M(u, u')$ and $\Delta$ are as in that theorem, whose (i) gives $\sigma(X(u, y' + \eta), X(u', y')) = -\tfrac12 M(\eta, \eta) + O(|\eta|^{3})$ and $\Delta^{1/2}/\sqrt{\det M} = (a(u)a(u'))^{-1/2}$. Rotation gauge as in that theorem: the parallel frame has constant images on $\mathcal{H}$, so $\Lambda$ and $k$ are constant on $\mathcal{H}$, $\Omega(k) = 0$ there, and the $f\wedge n$ part of $\Omega(f_{c})$ is $B(f_{c})\wedge n$; the statements are gauge covariant (a rotor field $L$ changes the kernel to $L(x)(\cdot)\tilde L(x')$, and the limits below are supported at $x = x'$).

\emph{Step 0 (the radiated solutions).} By Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-dimock-dirac-car-algebra-on-a-gravitational-field}(b) (Dimock's reproducing formula, \cite{Dimock1982}) the anticommutator pairing is the conserved Cauchy pairing and $S$ is its reproducing kernel: $\Phi(x) = \int_{\Sigma}S(x, x')\,n\,\Phi(x')\,d\Sigma'$. On a null piece of an achronal surface, written with labels $(u, y)$, $n\,d\Sigma = k\,a\,du\,d^{2}y$: in coordinates $(u, v, y)$ adapted to the foliation, the pulled-back three-form of a current $j$ is $j^{v}|g|^{1/2}du\,d^{2}y$, $\nabla v = k/(k\cdot\ell)$ on the leaf (it is orthogonal to $k$ and $\partial_{y}X$) and $|g|^{1/2} = (k\cdot\ell)\,a$. So $\Phi^{\Psi}_{v}$ is the solution whose pairing with any smooth solution $\Phi$ is the flux pairing of $\Phi$ with the null data $\Psi$ on $\mathcal{H}_{v}$, and since $k\Lambda = k$, $k\Phi = k\Lambda\Phi$ only the canonical component enters. In the canonical normalisation $T_{v}^{*}\Phi = \sqrt{2a_{v}}\Lambda\Phi$ (flux $\int 2a|\Lambda\Phi|^{2}$ at $k\cdot e_{0} = 1$ in the adapted gauge) this says $\Phi^{\Psi}_{v} = T_{v}G$ with $G = \sqrt{2a_{v}}\,\Psi$, and $T^{*}T_{v}G = \sqrt{2a}\,\Lambda\Phi^{\Psi}_{v}|_{\mathcal{H}}$.

\emph{Step 1 (the propagator near the light cone).} $D^{2} + m_{0}^{2}$ is normally hyperbolic with the spin connection as its connection (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-hadamard-parametrix-of-the-squared-dirac-operator-leads-with-van-vleck-root-times-spin-transport}(b)). On the geodesically convex, causally convex $N$ its retarded and advanced fundamental solutions are $G_{\pm} = \tfrac{1}{4\pi}\theta(\pm(t - t'))\,[U\delta(\sigma) + V\theta(\sigma)]$, $U = \Delta^{1/2}R$, $V$ smooth (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-hadamard-parametrix-of-the-squared-dirac-operator-leads-with-van-vleck-root-times-spin-transport}(d); \cite{Friedlander1975}, \cite[Ch.~2]{BarGinouxPfaffle2007}; on the flat field $\tfrac{1}{4\pi}\theta(t)\delta(\sigma) = \theta(t)\delta(t - r)/(4\pi r)$). So $S = \mathcal{D}E$, $E = G_{+} - G_{-} = \tfrac{1}{4\pi}\mathrm{sgn}(t - t')[U\delta(\sigma) + V\theta(\sigma)]$, $\mathcal{D} = D + m_{0}\rho$ with $\rho$ the right action of the mass term. For $x \in \mathcal{H}$, $x' \in \mathcal{H}_{v}$, $v \neq 0$: $x \neq x'$, so the factor $\mathrm{sgn}(t - t')$ is locally constant on the causal support and $D$ does not act on it; a covector conormal to both $\mathcal{H}$ at $x$ and $\mathcal{H}_{v}$ at $x'$ would have to be the tangent of a null geodesic joining them, which is the generator of $\mathcal{H}$ through $x$ and stays in $\mathcal{H}$; so the pullback is defined \cite[Thm.~8.2.4]{Hormander1983}, and $\sigma|_{\mathcal{H}\times\mathcal{H}_{v}}$ has nonzero differential on its zero set (Step 2), so $\delta(\sigma)$ and $\delta'(\sigma)$ pull back by composition. With the foliation function $V_{f}$ nondecreasing along future causal curves, a pair with $x' \in J^{\pm}(x)$ has $\pm v \ge 0$; hence $\mathrm{sgn}(t - t') = -\mathrm{sgn}(v)$ on the support. The projected kernel of Step 0 is
\[
\Lambda\,\mathcal{D}_{x}\bigl[E\,k_{v}\bigr]a_{v} = -\mathrm{sgn}(v)\frac{a_{v}}{4\pi}\Bigl[N_{1}\delta(\sigma) + N_{2}\delta'(\sigma) + \Lambda(DV)k_{v}\,\theta(\sigma) + \Lambda(\nabla\sigma)Vk_{v}\,\delta(\sigma) + m_{0}\Lambda(U\delta(\sigma) + V\theta(\sigma))k_{v}\rho\Bigr],
\]
$N_{1} = \Lambda(DU)k_{v}$, $N_{2} = \Lambda(\nabla_{x}\sigma)Uk_{v}$, using $D(\phi U) = (\nabla\phi)U + \phi DU$ for scalar $\phi$.

\emph{Step 2 (Morse reduction).} Write $x = X(u, y' + \eta)$, $x' = X_{v}(u', y')$, $(u, u', y')$ in a compact set. At $v = 0$, $\eta = 0$ is a nondegenerate critical point of $\sigma$ in $\eta$ with value $0$ (the generator is null, $\sigma$ has no linear term, Hessian $-M$, and $M(u, u) = \bar JJ$ is positive at coincidence), uniformly: $M(u, u')$ is positive definite for all $u, u'$ in the compact set, since $M \ge 0$ by (S) and $\det M = \Delta\,a(u)a(u') > 0$ by Theorem \claimref{synthesis.field-strength-cancels-from-the-leading-null-singularity-of-hadamard-dirac-states-on-null-hypersurfaces}(i) ($\Delta > 0$: no conjugate points in the convex $N$). By the Morse lemma with parameters \cite[App.~C]{Hormander1985} there are smooth coordinates $\xi(\eta; u, u', y', v)$ on $|\eta| < r_{0}$, $|v| < v_{0}$ with $\sigma = \sigma_{*} - \tfrac12|\xi|^{2}$, $\sigma_{*}(u, u', y', v)$ smooth, $\sigma_{*}|_{v = 0} = 0$; by the envelope theorem $\partial_{v}\sigma_{*}|_{v = 0} = \partial_{v}\sigma|_{\eta = 0} = \nabla_{x'}\sigma\cdot\ell = (u' - u)\,k\cdot\ell$, since on one generator $\nabla_{x'}\sigma = (u' - u)k$ (affine parameter). So $\sigma_{*} = v\tilde b$, $\tilde b = (u' - u)\beta + O(v)$, $\beta = k\cdot\ell > 0$, and by the implicit function theorem $\tilde b = 0$ exactly on $u' = u + v\varrho(u, y', v)$, $\varrho$ smooth. For $|\eta| \ge r_{0}$, (S) and compactness give $\sigma \le -c < 0$ for small $|v|$: no contribution to $\delta(\sigma)$, $\delta'(\sigma)$, $\theta(\sigma)$. For smooth $g$ and $\gamma(s) = \langle g\rangle_{|\xi| = \sqrt{2s}}$ (circle mean, smooth in $s \ge 0$, $\gamma(s) = g(0) + \tfrac{s}{2}\Delta_{\xi}g(0) + O(s^{2})$):
\[
\int g\,\delta(\sigma_{*} - \tfrac12|\xi|^{2})\,d^{2}\xi = 2\pi\theta(\sigma_{*})\gamma(\sigma_{*}),\qquad
\int g\,\delta'(\sigma_{*} - \tfrac12|\xi|^{2})\,d^{2}\xi = 2\pi\delta(\sigma_{*})\gamma(0) + 2\pi\theta(\sigma_{*})\gamma'(\sigma_{*}).
\]
Pairing the kernel with smooth profiles $\bar F(u, y' + \eta)$, $\Psi(u', y')$ and taking $g_{i} = \bar F N_{i}\Psi\,|\det\partial\eta/\partial\xi|$, the leading part becomes
\[
-\mathrm{sgn}(v)\frac{a_{v}}{4\pi}\,2\pi\Bigl[\theta(\sigma_{*})\bigl(\gamma_{1} + \gamma_{2}'\bigr)(\sigma_{*}) + \delta(\sigma_{*})\gamma_{2}(0)\Bigr],
\]
smooth in $(u, u', y', v)$ apart from $\theta(\sigma_{*})$ and $\delta(\sigma_{*})$.

\emph{Step 3 (orders; proved).} On one generator $\nabla_{x}\sigma = -(u' - u)k$ and $R = 1$ (adapted gauge), so $N_{2}|_{\eta = 0, v = 0} = -(u' - u)\Delta^{1/2}\Lambda k k = 0$ ($\Lambda k = 0$). The part of $N_{2}$ linear in $\eta$ at $v = 0$ is $\Delta^{1/2}\Lambda wk + (\text{terms with }\Lambda k) = 2\Delta^{1/2}(w\cdot k)\Lambda$, $w$ the linear part of $\nabla\sigma$, by $\Lambda pk = \tfrac12 n\,(kpk) = (p\cdot k)nk = 2(p\cdot k)\Lambda$ (with $kpk = 2(p\cdot k)k$); and $w\cdot k$ is the linear part of $\partial_{u}\sigma$, zero. So $N_{2} = O(|\eta|^{2} + |v|)$, hence $\gamma_{2}(0) = g_{2}(\xi = 0) = O(v)$ (the critical point is $O(v)$ from $\eta = 0$), and $\gamma_{2}(0) = v\hat\gamma(u, u', y', v)$ with $\hat\gamma$ smooth. The tail and mass terms: $\Lambda(DV)k_{v}\theta(\sigma)$ and $m_{0}\Lambda Vk_{v}\rho\,\theta(\sigma)$ are bounded on a set of $\xi$-area $2\pi|\sigma_{*}| = O(|v|)$; $\Lambda(\nabla\sigma)Vk_{v}\delta(\sigma)$ and $m_{0}\Lambda Uk_{v}\rho\,\delta(\sigma)$ give $2\pi\theta(\sigma_{*})$ times circle means over $|\xi| = \sqrt{2\sigma_{*}} = O(|v|^{1/2})$ of functions vanishing at $\eta = 0$, $v = 0$ ($\Lambda kVk = 0$ and $\Lambda Rk = \Lambda k = 0$ on the generator, any $V$): all four tend to $0$ uniformly.

\emph{Step 4 (the limit).} As $v \to 0^{\pm}$, $\theta(\sigma_{*}) \to \theta(\pm(u' - u))$ off $u = u'$ and boundedly, and $\delta(\sigma_{*})\gamma_{2}(0) = \mathrm{sgn}(v)\,\delta(\tilde b)\,\hat\gamma = \mathrm{sgn}(v)\,\delta(u' - u - v\varrho)\,\hat\gamma/|\partial_{u'}\tilde b|$. By dominated convergence in $(u, u', y')$ the paired kernel tends to
\[
-\frac{a(u)}{2}\,\frac{\hat\gamma(u, u, y', 0)}{\beta}\,\delta(u - u') \;-\; \mathrm{sgn}(v)\,\frac{a(u')}{2}\bigl(\gamma_{1} + \gamma_{2}'\bigr)(0)\,\theta(\pm(u' - u)).
\]
\emph{Coincidence coefficient.} At $u' = u$, $\eta = 0$: $x' - x = v\ell + O(v^{2})$, $\nabla_{x}\sigma = -v\ell + O(v^{2})$, $U = 1 + O(v)$, $k_{v} = k + O(v)$, so $\partial_{v}N_{2} = -\Lambda\ell k = -2\beta\Lambda$; the Morse Jacobian there is $(\det\bar JJ)^{-1/2} = 1/a$. So $\hat\gamma(u, u, y', 0) = \bar F(-2\beta\Lambda)\Psi/a$ and the $\delta$ term is $\bar F\Lambda\Psi\,\delta(u - u')$: coefficient exactly $\Lambda$, from both sides, with no other input than $\Delta(x, x) = 1$, $R(x, x) = 1$ and the coincidence Hessian. (On the flat null plane the computation is exact for every $v$: $\sigma_{*} = v(u' - u)$, $N_{2} = -2v\Lambda$, $N_{1} = 0$, and the kernel is $\delta(u - u')\Lambda$; Theorem \claimref{synthesis.bulk-hadamard-states-restrict-to-the-generator-condition-on-flat-null-planes}.)
\emph{Step coefficient.} At $v = 0$ the critical point is $\xi = 0 = \eta$, $\eta = A\xi + O(\xi^{2})$ with $\bar AMA = 1$, Jacobian $1/\sqrt{\det M}$, and since $N_{2}$ and its gradient vanish at $\eta = 0$, $\gamma_{1}(0) + \gamma_{2}'(0) = \bar F[N_{1}(0) + \mathrm{tr}(M^{-1}Q)]\Psi/\sqrt{\det M}$, $Q$ the quadratic form of the $\eta^{2}$ part of $N_{2}$ at $v = 0$ ($\gamma_{2}'(0) = \tfrac12\Delta_{\xi}g_{2}(0)$). It remains to show $N_{1}(0) + \mathrm{tr}(M^{-1}Q) = 2\sqrt{\det M}\,(\partial_{u} + \tfrac12\theta)(\Delta^{1/2}/\sqrt{\det M})\Lambda$, with $d = \Delta^{1/2}$ on the generator:
(a) $N_{1}(0) = \Lambda[nD_{k} + kD_{n} + f^{c}D_{f_{c}}](dR)k$ at $\eta = 0$. $\Lambda k = 0$ removes $kD_{n}$; $D_{k}R = 0$ (transport along the generator, $\Omega(k) = 0$), so $\Lambda n(\partial_{u}d)k = 2(\partial_{u}d)\Lambda$ ($n\cdot k = 1$); the transverse part is $d\,\Lambda f^{c}(\partial_{f_{c}}R + \tfrac12\Omega(f_{c}))k$, the terms $\Lambda f^{c}(\partial_{f_{c}}d)k = 2(f^{c}\cdot k)(\partial_{f_{c}}d)\Lambda$ being zero.
(b) Connection: for a bivector $W$, $\Lambda fWk$ ($f \perp k, n$) keeps only the $n\wedge f_{d}$ components ($k\,f\,(k\wedge\cdot)\,k = 0$, $kf(f_{1}f_{2})k = 0$, $kf(k\wedge n)k = 0$), with $\Lambda f^{c}(f_{d}\wedge n)k = -\Lambda f^{c}nf_{d}k = \Lambda nkf^{c}f_{d}$; summing $\Omega(f_{c}) \ni B_{dc}f_{d}\wedge n$ gives $\tfrac12 d\,\Lambda f^{c}\Omega(f_{c})k = d\,\theta\,\Lambda$ for self-adjoint $B$ (an antisymmetric part would leave a multiple of $\Lambda f_{1}f_{2}$).
(c) $Q$: $N_{2} = \Lambda(\nabla\sigma)(d + O(\eta))(1 + R_{1} + O(\eta^{2}))k$, $R_{1} = (J\eta)^{c}\partial_{f_{c}}R$. With $\nabla\sigma = -(u' - u)k + p_{1}(\eta) + O(\eta^{2})$: the terms $\Lambda k(\ldots)$ vanish; $2(\nabla\sigma\cdot k)d\Lambda = 2d\,(\partial_{u}\sigma)\Lambda$ contributes $-d\,\eta\cdot\partial_{u}M\eta\,\Lambda$, so $\mathrm{tr}(M^{-1}Q) \ni -d\,\mathrm{tr}(M^{-1}\partial_{u}M)\Lambda$; and $\Lambda p_{1}R_{1}k$ with $p_{1} = f^{c}(-\bar J^{-1}M\eta)_{c}$ (from $\partial_{\eta}\sigma = -M\eta = \bar J\,\partial_{f}\sigma$ to first order) is the quadratic form $-\bar\eta MJ^{-1}TJ\eta$, $T_{cd} = d\,\Lambda f^{c}(\partial_{f_{d}}R)k$, whose $M^{-1}$-trace is $-\mathrm{tr}\,T$: it cancels the transport part of (a) exactly. The first-order transport therefore drops out, whatever it is.
Hence $N_{1}(0) + \mathrm{tr}(M^{-1}Q) = 2[\partial_{u}d - \tfrac12 d\,\mathrm{tr}(M^{-1}\partial_{u}M) + \tfrac12\theta d]\Lambda = 2\sqrt{\det M}(\partial_{u} + \tfrac12\theta)(d/\sqrt{\det M})\Lambda$ (computed exactly on generic jets below). With the canonical factors the step coefficient is $c_{0}$ of (i), and $(\partial_{u} + \tfrac12\theta(u))(a(u)a(u'))^{-1/2} = 0$. This proves (i).

\emph{Step 5 ((ii)).} By the flux contraction (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.dirac-states-restrict-to-null-hypersurfaces-through-the-flux-contraction-and-are-thin-layer-limits-under-the-generator-car}(i): the Dirac current is future causal, so the flux through a null piece is at most the norm), $\|T_{v}\| \le 1$ and, for smooth $\Phi$, $(\Phi, T_{v}G) = \langle T_{v}^{*}\Phi, G\rangle \to \langle T^{*}\Phi, G\rangle$; so $T_{v}G \rightharpoonup TG$ weakly as $v \to 0$ from either side, and $\langle F, T^{*}TG\rangle = \lim_{v}(TF, T_{v}G) = \lim_{v}\langle F, \sqrt{2a}\Lambda\Phi^{\Psi}_{v}\rangle = \langle F, G\rangle$ by (i) and Step 0. Here $\Phi^{\Psi}_{v}$ is smooth near $\mathcal{H}$ (its singularities lie on $\mathcal{H}_{v}$, Step 1), and $T^{*}$ acts on it by restriction, by the locality of the flux trace (a cut-off of its Cauchy data away from $J(\mathcal{H}_{c})$ does not change it near $\mathcal{H}_{c}$). By density and boundedness $T^{*}T = 1$ on $L^{2}(\mathcal{H}_{c})$; every leaf is a geodesic null hypersurface of the same kind, so (Iso) holds on all of them. Local Rindler horizons through $x_{0}$ are smooth null hypersurfaces with affinely parametrised generators near $x_{0}$ (Premise \claimref{synthesis.c-loc-local-horizon-premise}); a small piece has (S) (positive transverse Hessian near the diagonal) and lies in a local null foliation (the null hypersurfaces orthogonal to a family of parallel two-surfaces; standard, cited from memory).
\emph{Locality (a second argument, from causality and the flux contraction).} For $F$, $G$ supported at positive distance in the labels, split them by a fine partition of unity into pieces whose pairs are either transversally separated (then spacelike for small $|v|$ by (S) and compactness: $(TF_{i}, T_{v}G_{j}) = 0$ by the support of $S$, Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-dimock-dirac-car-algebra-on-a-gravitational-field}) or separated along the generators with a fixed order, $u < u'$ say; then for $v < 0$ no point of $\mathcal{H}_{v}$ near $X(u', \cdot)$ is causally related to $X(u, \cdot)$ (a limit point of such pairs would be a point of $\mathcal{H}$ causally related to and to the future along the generator, $J^{+}(x)\cap\mathcal{H}_{v} = \emptyset$ by monotonicity), so the one-sided limit from $v < 0$ is $0$. Both one-sided limits equal $(TF, TG)$, so $T^{*}T$ is local, commutes with multiplication by uniformly continuous functions (partition of unity at scale $\delta$, error $\le 2\|T^{*}T\|\omega(\delta)$), hence is multiplication by a bounded $m$, $0 \le m \le 1$. In Step 4 the limits from the two sides differ by $c_{0}$ times the whole function $1$ ($\theta(u' - u)$ against $\theta(u' - u) - 1$, up to the sign carried by $\mathrm{sgn}(v)$); locality forces $c_{0} = 0$ and $m = 1$. Read backwards, with the jet formula for $c_{0}$ of Step 4: $c_{0} \equiv 0$ means $(\partial_{u} + \tfrac12\theta(u))(\Delta^{1/2}/\sqrt{\det M}) = 0$, which with the coincidence value $1/a(u')$ integrates to $(a(u)a(u'))^{-1/2}$: the flux contraction, causality and the computed form of the step coefficient imply the stored identity.
\emph{Jump relation (a third route; argued).} $\Phi^{\Psi}_{v} = \Phi_{\mathrm{ret}} - \Phi_{\mathrm{adv}}$ with $\mathcal{D}\Phi_{\mathrm{ret}} = \mathcal{D}\Phi_{\mathrm{adv}} = k_{v}\Psi\,a_{v}\,\delta_{\mathcal{H}_{v}}$, the layer density being $\delta(V_{f} - v)|g|^{1/2}/(k\cdot\ell)$ in the label measure. Since $\nabla V_{f} = k_{v}/(k\cdot\ell)$ on the leaf, the jump of either solution across $\mathcal{H}_{v}$ satisfies $k_{v}[\Phi] = k_{v}\Psi$, hence $\Lambda[\Phi] = \tfrac12 n(k_{v}[\Phi]) = \Psi$. $\Phi_{\mathrm{ret}}$ vanishes below $\mathcal{H}_{v}$ and $\Phi_{\mathrm{adv}}$ above, so $\Lambda\Phi^{\Psi}_{v}$ is continuous across $\mathcal{H}_{v}$ and equals $\Psi$ there from both sides, and $\Lambda\Phi^{\Psi}_{v}|_{\mathcal{H}} \to \Psi$ as the leaf approaches $\mathcal{H}$, with no step term. This needs conormal regularity of $\Phi_{\mathrm{ret}}$, $\Phi_{\mathrm{adv}}$ up to $\mathcal{H}_{v}$ uniformly in $v$, which is not proved here; it is independent of the stored identity and confirms that the step coefficient vanishes on every geodesic null hypersurface.

\emph{Step 6 ((iii)).} (a) The Hadamard parametrix is $G^{+}_{\mathrm{par}} = c_{H}\,\mathcal{D}[U/\sigma_{+} + V\log\sigma_{+}] + \text{smooth}$, $\sigma_{+}$ the boundary value of $\sigma + i\epsilon(t(x) - t(x'))$, $\epsilon \to 0^{+}$, for the ordering $\omega(\psi^{*}(x')\psi(x))$ (the sign of the stored computation of $W_{0}$) (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-hadamard-parametrix-of-the-squared-dirac-operator-leads-with-van-vleck-root-times-spin-transport}(a), (c); Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-dirac-hadamard-form-propagates-and-adiabatic-states-are-hadamard}(a)). For $v \neq 0$ its pullback to $\mathcal{H}\times\mathcal{H}_{v}$ is defined (wave front set of Hadamard type, Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-microlocal-hadamard-condition}; the argument of Step 1) and does not depend on the time function (the bulk distribution does not). The $i0$ matters only at $\sigma = 0$; there $x' - x$ is null with $\ell$-component $v$, so it is future-directed for $v > 0$ and past-directed for $v < 0$, and the regulator $\epsilon(t(x) - t(x'))$ has the sign $-\mathrm{sgn}(v)$. In Morse coordinates the projected kernel is $c_{H}[N_{1}/(\sigma_{*} - \tfrac12|\xi|^{2} - i0\,\mathrm{sgn}\,v) - N_{2}/(\ldots)^{2}] + \ldots$, and with $\int_{0}^{T}\gamma(t)(s - t \mp i0)^{-1}dt = \gamma(s)\log(s \mp i0) + h(s)$, $h$ smooth, the leading part is
\[
2\pi c_{H}\Bigl[(\gamma_{1} + \gamma_{2}')(\sigma_{*})\log(\sigma_{*} - i0\,\mathrm{sgn}\,v) + \frac{\gamma_{2}(0)}{\sigma_{*} - i0\,\mathrm{sgn}\,v}\Bigr] + \text{smooth}.
\]
The log coefficient is the jet combination of the step coefficient of (i), zero at $v = 0$ and $O(v)$ for $v \neq 0$, so the log term is $O(v\log|v|)$ plus a bounded term (the $O(v)$ estimate uses $\gamma_{2}''$, that is four transverse derivatives of the profiles). The pole term is $v\hat\gamma/(v\tilde b - i0\,\mathrm{sgn}\,v) = \hat\gamma/(\tilde b - i0)$ for both signs of $v$, which tends to $\hat\gamma(u, u')/(\beta(u'-u) - i0) = (\hat\gamma(u, u)/\beta)(u' - u - i0)^{-1} + $ a bounded difference quotient. The $V\log\sigma$ and mass terms have numerators vanishing on the generator (Step 3), so they give $O(|v|\log|v|)$ plus bounded terms. Its coefficient against $(u - u' + i0)^{-1}$ is fixed by (i): $G^{-}_{\mathrm{par}}$ has the same Morse data with the opposite $i0$, and $(u - u' + i0)^{-1} - (u - u' - i0)^{-1} = -2\pi i\delta(u - u')$, so $G^{+}_{\mathrm{par}} + G^{-}_{\mathrm{par}} = S$ modulo smooth kernels and the coefficient $\Lambda$ of (i) give $\tfrac{i}{2\pi}\Lambda$: the limit is $W_{0}\otimes\id_{K} + r$ where, for each pair of transverse profiles, $r(u, u')$ is a bounded function of $(u, u')$ (a difference quotient of smooth functions plus bounded log integrals), uniformly for profiles bounded in $C^{4}$ with support in a fixed compact set (all bounds come from the smooth functions $\gamma_{i}$, $\hat\gamma$, $h$).
(b) By the flux contraction, the restricted two-point function is $W_{\mathcal{H},\omega}(F, G) = \lim_{n}\lim_{m}W_{\omega}(f_{n}, g_{m})$ over thin layers $f_{n}$, $g_{m}$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.dirac-states-restrict-to-null-hypersurfaces-through-the-flux-contraction-and-are-thin-layer-limits-under-the-generator-car}(iii-a); it needs only weak convergence), and with (ii) the joint thin-layer limit exists. A thin-layer pairing is $\int\int\rho_{n}(v_{1})\rho_{m}(v_{2})P(v_{1}, v_{2})\,dv_{1}dv_{2}$, $P$ the pairing between the leaves $\mathcal{H}_{v_{1}}$, $\mathcal{H}_{v_{2}}$; by (a) applied with base leaf $\mathcal{H}_{v_{1}}$ (all data smooth in $v_{1}$), $P$ is bounded near the diagonal and continuous off it with the same limit $W_{0} + r$ from both sides, so the thin-layer limit of $G^{+}_{\mathrm{par}}$ is the limit of (a), and $W_{\omega} - G^{+}_{\mathrm{par}}$ smooth gives the smooth pullback $D_{0,0}$. Bounded and smooth kernels have generator scaling degree at most $0$ (for bounded $r$, $\lambda^{\varpi}\int r(u_{0} + \lambda x, u_{0} + \lambda x')\phi \to 0$ for every $\varpi > 0$), which gives the clause of (HS). This is clause (b) of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.hadamard-scaling-limit-premise} for the flux-trace restriction; its statement on the $\epsilon$-regularised restriction rests on Theorem \claimref{synthesis.restricted-dirac-hadamard-parametrix-on-a-null-hypersurface-is-the-null-vacuum-plus-a-bounded-kernel}(iii). By that bound the $\epsilon$-regularised restriction is also $W_{0}\otimes\id_{K}$ plus a bounded kernel, so the two restrictions differ by a bounded kernel. That kernel contains a smooth term depending on the time function: at leading order the $\epsilon$-restriction of $U/\sigma_{\epsilon}$ carries $\log(i s)$, $s = (u - u')\bar\tau(u, u')$, whose $\partial_{u}$ gives $\partial_{u}\log\bar\tau$ besides $(u - u')^{-1}$ (on the flat null plane with $\tau = t + ct^{2}$ it is $c/(1 + c(u + u'))$), while the thin-layer limit there is exactly $W_{0}$; so the two restrictions do not agree identically.
