\emph{Setting.} The rates $\rho$ and $\rho_{\mathrm{H}}$ are Proposition \claimref{singularity.modular-flow-boundary-modes-holonomy-universal}(i), (ii), with $\tau$ and $S$ from Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-interior-collar-kasner-form}; that Fact also gives the exceptional set $\{\tau = 0\}\times\R_{t}\times S^{2}$ and the exact parallelism of the frame $(\partial_{\tau}, e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3})$ along $\partial_{\tau}$. The lines of constant $(t, \theta, \varphi)$ are the integral curves of $\partial_{\tau}$, so the $\tau$-line through a collar point $(\tau, t, \theta, \varphi)$ ends at the point $(t, \theta, \varphi)$ of $E$; Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-one-sided-completion-singularity} places the ladder pair exactly over $E$, which is what (E) carries.

\emph{(i)} In the $(\tau, t)$ plane write the metric as $-d\tau^{2} + a(\tau)^{2}dt^{2}$ with $a^{2} = A$ (the overall sign convention does not enter). The only Christoffel symbol needed is $\Gamma^{t}_{\tau t} = \tfrac12 g^{tt}\partial_{\tau}g_{tt} = a'/a$, so $\nabla_{\tau}(a^{-1}\partial_{t}) = (a^{-1})'\partial_{t} + a^{-1}\Gamma^{t}_{\tau t}\partial_{t} = 0$: the unit vector $e_{1}$ along $\partial_{t}$ is parallel along $\partial_{\tau}$, as is $e_{0} = \partial_{\tau}$ (the $\tau$-lines are geodesics), hence $B_{1} = e_{0}\wedge e_{1}$. The script checks the vanishing as an identity of rational functions for two rational choices of $a$, computing $\Gamma$ from $g_{tt}$. The Killing field $\xi = \partial_{t}$ has $\xi_{\flat} = a^{2}dt$ and $\nabla\xi = \tfrac12 d\xi_{\flat} = a a'\,d\tau\wedge dt = a'\,e^{0}\wedge e^{1}$, also checked as an identity. With $a^{2} = A(r)$ and $dr/d\tau = a$, $a' = A'(r)\,(dr/d\tau)/(2a) = A'/2$, of magnitude $\rho(r) = 1/(2r^{2})$, equal to $\kappa = \tfrac12$ at $r = 1$, so the Killing bivector on the slice $r$ is $\rho(r)B_{1}$; this is the rate of the stored Proposition, rederived. Carried along the $\tau$-line, whose frame is parallel, the bivector keeps its frame components, so the slice $r$ carries to the point of $E$ the bivector $\rho(r)B_{1}$, and the slice generators on the atom are $\rho(r)K$; the script checks in $\Cl(1,3)$ that the carried generators for $r = 1, \tfrac12, \tfrac14, \tfrac18$ are pairwise proportional and commute, while $B_{1}$ and the sphere boost $B_{2}$ do not commute. The Hubble parametrisation multiplies the Killing rate by $\tau/\sqrt{A}$, a function of $r$ only, giving $\rho_{\mathrm{H}}(r)K$. Since the rates depend on $r$ only and not on $(t, \theta, \varphi)$, on a finite $P \subset E$ the slice generator is $f(r)K_{P}$.

\emph{Status of (E).} Hypothesis (E) assumes the form $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{Jsf(r)K_{P}})$, $J = e_{2}e_{3}$ the atoms' complex unit, of each slice's flow on the $E$-atoms; clause (i) is the geometric evidence for that form (the Killing bivector is $\rho(r)B_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ is parallel along the carry), not a derivation of (E), which also assumes that the collar has no modes other than the carried $E$-atoms. Given (E), clauses (ii) and (iii) are reparametrisation.

\emph{(ii)} In the atom of Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-structure-completed-pair}, realised in $\Cl(1,1)$ with $v = \tfrac12(e_{0} + e_{1})$, $v^{*} = \tfrac12(e_{0} - e_{1})$, the script checks the CAR and $\sigma = v^{*}v - vv^{*} = e_{0}e_{1} = B_{1}$, so the generator carried in (i) is the boost generator $K = \sigma/2$ of the completed pair. On the matrix factor of the atoms at $P$ the flow $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{Jsf(r)K_{P}})$ has, by Theorem \claimref{singularity.kms-uniqueness-factor}, the unique KMS state at $\beta$ with density $e^{-\beta f(r)K_{P}}/\Tr(\cdot)$, which equals the density of $\omega_{P}$ exactly when $\beta f(r) = 2\pi$. This is clause (3) of Proposition \claimref{singularity.rate-rigidity-temperature-not-algebra-intrinsic}: a constant reparametrisation $s \mapsto f(r)s$ of the flow keeps its KMS states and rescales their inverse temperatures. Hence $\omega_{P}$ is the unique KMS state of the Killing flow of the slice $r$ at $2\pi/\rho(r) = 4\pi r^{2}$ and of its Hubble flow at $2\pi/\rho_{\mathrm{H}}(r)$, and a state KMS for one slice at $\beta$ is KMS for the slice $r'$ at $\beta f(r)/f(r')$. In particular the Killing value $4\pi r^{2}$ tends to $0$ at the exceptional set, so the value $6\pi$ of (iii) is specific to the Hubble normalisation. The script keeps $z = e^{\pi}$ as a formal variable, writes the density of $\omega$ as $\operatorname{diag}(z, z^{-1})/(z + z^{-1})$ and $e^{-\beta f K}$ as $\operatorname{diag}(z^{m}, z^{-m})$ with $m = \beta f/(2\pi)$, and verifies the KMS identity $\Tr(\varrho\,X e^{-\beta fK}Ye^{\beta fK}) = \Tr(\varrho\,YX)$ on all matrix units as identities of rational functions in $z$, for $m = 1$ at $r = 1, \tfrac12, \tfrac14$ (where $\beta/\pi = 4r^{2}$), and its failure for $m = 2$; on two points of $E$ it verifies the same for the product state and the generator $K_{1} + K_{2}$. For the Hubble value it computes $2/\rho_{\mathrm{H}}(r) = 6 - \tfrac{24}{5}r + \cdots$ as an exact truncated series, which with $\sqrt{1-r}/S(r) = 1 - \tfrac45 r + \cdots$ is $\beta_{\mathrm{H}}(r)/\pi = 6\sqrt{1-r}/S(r)$.

\emph{(iii)} The constant term of $\rho_{\mathrm{H}}$ is $|p_{1}| = \tfrac13$ (the Kasner exponent of Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-interior-collar-kasner-form}), so the limiting Hubble-normalised holonomy flow at the exceptional set is $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{JsK_{P}/3})$, and by (ii) with $f = \tfrac13$ the state $\omega_{P}$ is its unique KMS state at $\beta_{\mathrm{H}} = 6\pi$ ($m = 6 \cdot \tfrac13 / 2 = 1$, checked). The horizon's state is the state of the completed pair with rapidity period $2\pi$, the value of Premise (R) in Proposition \claimref{singularity.modular-flow-boundary-modes-holonomy-universal}(i), which under (E) is a state of the same atoms; it is therefore a KMS state of the $t$-line holonomy flow of every slice and of its limit at $E$, and by uniqueness the only one at those inverse temperatures. This is the conclusion of the conditional sentence of that Proposition, with its hypothesis met.

\emph{(iv)} Under (E) the flows of different slices differ by the constant factors $f(r)/f(r')$ on one algebra, so the running of $\rho_{\mathrm{H}}$ changes the inverse temperature at which $\omega_{P}$ is read and nothing else. If instead two slices $r_{1} \neq r_{2}$ carry independent atoms, the Killing-parametrised generator is $\rho(r_{1})K_{1} + \rho(r_{2})K_{2}$, and the product of period-$2\pi$ states would need $\beta\rho(r_{1}) = \beta\rho(r_{2}) = 2\pi$, impossible as $\rho$ is injective; the script checks for $r_{1} = 1$, $r_{2} = \tfrac12$ (ratio $4$) that the KMS identity fails for the exponent pairs $(1, 4)$, $(-1, -4)$, $(2, 8)$. This is the mechanism of the slice-product verdict of \claimref{synthesis.collar-kms-extension-three-flows}, and it is absent under (E).

\emph{Conclusion.} Under (E) the horizon's KMS state is, uniquely, the KMS state of every slice's $t$-line holonomy flow at the reparametrised inverse temperature, and at the exceptional set its Hubble-normalised inverse temperature is $2\pi/|p_{1}| = 6\pi$.
