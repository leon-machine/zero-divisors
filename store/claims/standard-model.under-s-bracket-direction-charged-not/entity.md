## formal
Assume Hypothesis (S), under which the sectors of the module are the species of the generation. Let $\Lambda^{2}(R_{2} \oplus W) = \Lambda^{2}R_{2} \oplus (R_{2} \otimes W) \oplus \Lambda^{2}W$ be the two-mode states of the module. Under $S(U(2) \times U(3))$ this is $(1,1)_{1} \oplus (2,3)_{1/6} \oplus (1,\bar{3})_{-2/3}$, the $10$ of $SU(5)$ as $e^{c} \oplus Q \oplus u^{c}$, and the slot bivector $\Lambda^{2}R_{2}$, the direction of the mass bracket (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.mass-bracket-charged-singlet}), is the $e^{c}$ direction: $T_{3} = 0$, $Y = 1$, electric charge $1$. Consequently: (a) a condensate in the bracket direction breaks electromagnetism, so the bracket's modulus is a mass parameter and not a vacuum expectation value, as Proposition \claimref{standard-model.mass-bracket-charged-singlet} says; (b) in a $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$-covariant coupling of two $16$s to a scalar, the $10$ of $SU(5)$ occurs in the $120$ and in the $126$ and not in the $10_{v}$ (Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-spinor-bilinears}), so a scalar in the bracket direction couples through the $120$, antisymmetric in generations and vanishing for one, or through the $126$, where it is the charged component just excluded; (c) hence no mass operator on a single generation of the module is generated by an order parameter in the bracket direction.

## technical
Under Hypothesis (S), the two-mode states $\Lambda^{2}(R_{2} \oplus W) = \Lambda^{2}R_{2} \oplus (R_{2} \otimes W) \oplus \Lambda^{2}W$ form $(1,1)_{1} \oplus (2,3)_{1/6} \oplus (1,\bar{3})_{-2/3}$, the $10$ of $SU(5)$ as $e^{c} \oplus Q \oplus u^{c}$. The slot bivector $\Lambda^{2}R_{2}$, the direction of the mass bracket, is the $e^{c}$ direction, with $T_{3} = 0$, $Y = 1$ and electric charge $1$.

So a condensate in that direction would break electromagnetism, and the bracket's modulus is a mass parameter, not a vev. In a $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$-covariant coupling of two $16$s to a scalar, the $10$ of $SU(5)$ occurs only in the $120$, antisymmetric in generations and vanishing for one, and in the $126$, where it is the charged component; hence no mass operator on a single generation comes from an order parameter in the bracket direction.

## lay
Assuming Hypothesis (S), under which the sectors of the state space are the particle species, this result identifies whose charges the mass bracket's direction carries: those of $e^{c}$, the charge-conjugate partner of the right-handed electron, with electric charge $+1$. A background field pointing that way would break electromagnetism, so the bracket's size is a mass parameter and it cannot play the Higgs's role. Known group theory of the unified group then shows it cannot give masses within a single generation either.

Under Hypothesis (S), the mass bracket's direction is the charged $e^{c}$ direction, so it is not a vev and generates no one-generation mass operator.
