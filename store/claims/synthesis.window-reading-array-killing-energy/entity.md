## formal
\emph{The window reading (adopted).} Units $\hbar = c = 1$. On a half-generator of a horizon in the scope of Premise \claimref{synthesis.n-plus-null-ground-state}, with surface gravity $\kappa$, Killing time $\tau$ and affine parameter $u = e^{\kappa\tau}$, let an array be an evenly spaced set of modes with weights $\omega_{k} = \kappa(k + \delta)$, $k \in \Z$, $0 \le \delta < 1$ (an s-wave array of Premise \claimref{singularity.q-joint-quasi-freeness-s-wave}, whose version 3 has $\delta = \tfrac12$). The array is read as one chiral complex component of the continuum of that light ray on a window of Killing time of length $2\pi/\kappa$ (its real-time recurrence window), and its Killing energy, in every state, is the Killing energy of the continuum over that window measured from the null ground state that (N$_{+}$) selects, that is, in the normal ordering at that state, in which $\langle T_{uu}\rangle = 0$.

\emph{Scope (mean energy only).} The reading equates the mean Killing energy of an array with that of the continuum over one window, and nothing else. The array's entropy at the Hawking temperature, $\tfrac12\ln 2$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.half-integer-weight-array-carries-half-ln2}), and the continuum's entropy rate times the window, $\pi/6$ per complex component, are different objects, and the reading does not equate them; the variance of the energy differs too (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.antiperiodic-array-against-the-light-ray-continuum-at-window-length-r}(iii), (iv), which gives both differences at every window length). The count of the series uses the array's entropy.

\emph{Scope (one thermal period, spacing $\kappa$).} The equalities of energy behind the reading (the sea against the boost ground state, the thermal energy above the sea against the continuum's boost-ordered value, the symmetric-ordered thermal energy against the null-ordered value $0$) hold at a window of exactly one thermal period: for the antiperiodic array on a window of $r$ thermal periods each holds exactly when $r = 1$, the fixed point of $r \mapsto 1/r$ of the array's partition function; and for the array of spacing $\kappa$ at a temperature other than the Hawking temperature, compared with the continuum's Gibbs family at that temperature (a comparison family, not a state selected by (N$_{+}$)), the energies differ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.antiperiodic-array-against-the-light-ray-continuum-at-window-length-r}(ii)). So the equalities are verified at the sea and at the Hawking state, and the reading is not an extensive identification of an array with a stretch of the continuum. They hold at the spacing $\kappa$, which is taken from (Q): with the spacing left free the same two equalities (sea and thermal) are passed also by the two halves of the stored array, of spacing $2\kappa$ and twists $\tfrac14$, $\tfrac34$, and by one independent array at twist $\tfrac13$ on a window of $\sqrt{2/3}$ thermal periods; with the parity clause of (Q) only the stored array and its halves remain, and with the spacing $\kappa$ or the particle-hole clause only the stored array (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.window-energy-tests-with-free-spacing-admit-twists-half-third-and-quarter}(ii), (iv)).

\emph{Scope (a fibre, not a subsystem).} Under the one-window Killing flow the continuum of a light ray in its thermal state at the Hawking temperature decomposes over the twist $\delta \in [0, 1)$, and fibre by fibre it is the array of twist $\delta$ in its Gibbs state; the array of (Q) version 3 is the fibre $\delta = \tfrac12$, on which the one-window flow is $-1$. It is not a Killing-invariant subsystem of the continuum: no closed subspace invariant under the boost generator has point spectrum. Subalgebras that are not invariant under the Killing flow can carry the same occupations, for any twist, and select none (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.light-ray-continuum-is-the-twist-integral-of-arrays-and-window-values-are-twist-averages}(i)). The antiperiodic condition and the spacing are inputs of (Q), not derived: the Killing generator compressed to one window has a self-adjoint realisation for every twist and selects none, the antiperiodic twist is the weight clause of (Q) version 3, which (N$_{+}$) does not give (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.light-ray-continuum-is-the-twist-integral-of-arrays-and-window-values-are-twist-averages}(ii)), and the slope $\beta c = 2\pi$, that is the spacing $c = \kappa$, is a choice of (Q) (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.q-splits-maximum-entropy-affine-weights}(iv)) that the energy equalities do not make either (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.window-energy-tests-with-free-spacing-admit-twists-half-third-and-quarter}).

\emph{What it is for.} It is the hypothesis of clause (iv) of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.null-ordering-of-counted-units-is-zeta-symmetric}, which with it decides the ordering question left open by Theorem \claimref{synthesis.counted-unit-energy-at-half-offset}: the boost ground state carries $-\kappa/24$ per complex component over one window measured from the null ground state, and an array's zeta-regularised symmetric sea energy $\tfrac{\kappa}{2}B_{2}(\delta)$ takes that value exactly at $\delta = \tfrac12$. Given the reading, (i) at the spacing $\kappa$ of (Q) the half offset of version 3 is singled out by (N$_{+}$), being the only offset at that spacing at which an array's own sea is consistent with the null ordering window by window; (ii) at that offset the zeta-regularised symmetric ordering is the physical ordering of the sea's energy; (iii) a counted unit of two arrays carries mean Killing energy $0$ at the Hawking temperature, the equality case of clause (v) of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy}; (iv) the constant of the hole's Hamiltonian in Theorem \claimref{synthesis.hole-mass-is-ground-energy-label}(iii) is all of $M$, so the ground-energy label carries all of $M$ and the counted units none. It does not fix the count unit (two arrays per registered unit remains a decision), the label's energy function, or which fields form the sea.

\emph{What it costs.} It is a reading of the arrays, not a consequence of (N$_{+}$): (N$_{+}$) cannot be posed on point-spectrum mode sets (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.positive-null-energy-gives-r-premises}(iv)), and the reading applies that theorem's discretisation reading, by which the point-spectrum wordings of the thermal premises are discretisations of the continuum statements, to energy. It also identifies the spectrum of an array, of spacing $\kappa$, with a twisted identification of real Killing time over one window, which the continuum does not supply: the continuum contains every twist as a fibre and selects none, and the twist and the spacing come from (Q) (the second scope statement above). The numbers it relates are equalities proved without it; without it the ordering is not decided.

\emph{Refutation condition.} The reading is false if any of the following is established: (a) a derivation, from the series' premises, of the Killing energy of an array (for example from a construction of the arrays out of the continuum modes of a light ray) that differs from the continuum's window energy measured from the null ground state; (b) a derivation that the arrays' offset at spacing $\kappa$ is not $\tfrac12$, since under the reading no other offset at that spacing is consistent with the null ordering window by window (at $\delta = 0$ the symmetric sea is $+\kappa/12$, not $-\kappa/24$; at other spacings further arrays pass the same equalities, Theorem \claimref{synthesis.window-energy-tests-with-free-spacing-admit-twists-half-third-and-quarter}(ii)); (c) a derivation, from the series' premises, that the counted units carry a nonzero mean Killing energy, or that the hole's energy $M$ is shared between the label and the counted units.

## technical
The series counts a black hole's entropy in units of two arrays, evenly spaced sets of modes on each light ray of the horizon, and the energy those units carry depends on where the zero of energy is put. The null ground-state premise (N$_{+}$) puts the zero of null energy at the state of the light rays it selects, but it is a statement about the continuum of each light ray and cannot be posed on an array. This reading bridges the two: an array is one stretch of a light ray's continuum, one recurrence window of Killing time $2\pi/\kappa$ long, and its Killing energy is the continuum's energy over that window, measured from the null ground state. With it, the stored equality of numbers becomes a decision of the ordering: the half offset of (Q) version 3 is the only offset, at the spacing $\kappa$ of (Q), at which an array's zeta-regularised sea takes the null ground state's window value, the symmetric ordering is the physical one, the counted units carry no mean Killing energy, and the whole of the hole's mass sits in the ground-energy label. The reading equates mean Killing energy only: it does not equate the array's entropy, $\tfrac12\ln 2$, with the continuum's entropy rate times the window, $\pi/6$ per complex component, nor the variances of the energy. Two further limits of scope are recorded. The energy equalities behind the reading hold at a window of exactly one thermal period, the fixed point of a duality of the array's partition function, and at the spacing $\kappa$ taken from (Q): at twist one half they fail at every other window length, and with the spacing left free the two halves of the stored array and one array at twist one third pass the same two equalities. And the array is the twist one half fibre of the light ray's continuum under the one-window flow, not a Killing-invariant subsystem of it; subalgebras that are not invariant can carry the same occupations for any twist and select none, so the antiperiodic condition and the spacing are inputs of (Q), not derived from the continuum or from (N$_{+}$). It is adopted, not derived, and would be refuted by a derivation of an array's energy that disagrees, by a forced offset other than one half at spacing $\kappa$, or by a derivation that the counted units carry part of the mass.

## lay
The series counts a black hole's entropy in small units on its horizon, and whether those units carry energy depends on where the zero of energy is placed. The series' premise about the state on the horizon's light rays places it, but only for the light rays as a whole, not for the counted units. This assumption connects the two: a unit's energy is the energy of the stretch of light ray it stands for. With it, the units carry no energy on average and the hole's mass sits in a single overall constant. It is a statement about average energy only, not about entropy. The agreement of energies behind it holds when the stretch is exactly one thermal cycle long and the unit's levels have the spacing the series assumes, not for other lengths; and the unit is not a piece cut out of the light ray but one of a continuous family of ladders into which the light ray decomposes, the choice of that one ladder and of its spacing being assumptions of the series. It is an assumption of the series, stated so that it can be refuted.

## one-liner
Adopted reading: an array of (Q) is one chiral component of a light ray's continuum on a Killing-time window $2\pi/\kappa$, and its Killing energy is the continuum's over that window measured from the null ground state of (N$_{+}$); given it, the half offset is singled out by (N$_{+}$) at the spacing of (Q), the counted units carry zero mean Killing energy and the ground-energy label carries all of $M$; the reading equates mean Killing energy only, not entropy; the energy equalities hold at exactly one thermal period and at spacing $\kappa$, and the array is the twist one half fibre of the continuum, not a Killing-invariant subsystem, its antiperiodic condition and spacing being inputs of (Q).
