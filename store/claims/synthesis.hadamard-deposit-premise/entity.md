## formal
\emph{Premise (HD), the deposit is Hadamard.} Units $\hbar = c = 1$. Let $(\hb, \Omega)$ be the descended gravitational field on flat spacetime (Definition \claimref{obj.synthesis.gtg-flat-spacetime-gauge-fields}), on the region after the descent, and suppose its derived metric is smooth and globally hyperbolic there. A \emph{Cauchy collar of the descent} is an open neighbourhood $N$, in that region and adjacent to the descent, of a Cauchy surface $\Sigma_{\varepsilon}$ of it. The deposited state $\omega_{d}$ is the state in which the descent leaves the free fields of the descended phase: for the fermion modes a gauge-invariant quasi-free state, the vacuum plus quanta (Proposition \claimref{synthesis.quasi-free-deposit-is-vacuum-plus-shot-noise}), and for each free scalar field of the descended phase the quasi-free state the descent sets (as in Theorem \claimref{synthesis.frozen-point-geometry-reads-descent-as-vacuum}). The premise: \emph{there is a Cauchy collar $N$ of the descent on which $\omega_{d}$ is Hadamard}, that is, for the free Dirac field of each mass $\mathrm{WF}'(\mathcal{G}^{(\pm)}) = C^{(\pm)}$ on $N \times N$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-microlocal-hadamard-condition}(b)), and for each free scalar field $\mathrm{WF}'(\Lambda) = C^{+}$ on $N \times N$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-microlocal-hadamard-condition}(a)).

\emph{Equivalent form, and the free data.} Let an \emph{admissible reference vacuum} on $N$ be, for a massless conformally covariant field, the conformal vacuum where the field is Weyl-flat (Hadamard there: Theorem \claimref{synthesis.weyl-flat-fields-need-no-scaling-limit-premise-and-robertson-walker-generators-see-the-schwarzian}(i) for the Dirac field), and, for a massive Dirac field on a Robertson-Walker collar, a homogeneous state adiabatic of infinite order with $M = ma$ smooth (Hadamard: Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-dirac-hadamard-form-propagates-and-adiabatic-states-are-hadamard}(c)). Since Hadamard two-point functions differ by smooth kernels (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-microlocal-hadamard-condition}), (HD) says that the two-point functions of $\omega_{d}$ differ from those of an admissible reference vacuum by smooth kernels on $N \times N$. The occupation (Bogoliubov) profile of $\omega_{d}$ over that reference is free data: (HD) fixes only its ultraviolet tail.

\emph{What it is for.} (1) \emph{Hadamard states on every later field.} By the propagation of the Hadamard form (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-propagation-of-hadamard-form} for the scalar field, Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-dirac-hadamard-form-propagates-and-adiabatic-states-are-hadamard}(b) for the Dirac field), $\omega_{d}$ is Hadamard on the whole domain of dependence of $\Sigma_{\varepsilon}$: every later field it determines, regions of nonzero Weyl curvature and holes formed by collapse included, up to any Cauchy horizon, where the domain of dependence ends (and where, on the Reissner-Nordstr\"om-de Sitter field, the stress tensor of every Hadamard scalar state generically diverges, Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-hadamard-stress-tensor-diverges-at-the-reissner-nordstrom-de-sitter-cauchy-horizon}). (2) \emph{The local form of (N$_{+}$) at every horizon.} On Weyl-flat regions every quasi-free Hadamard state of the massless Dirac field has the local form of (N$_{+}$) at every point of every local Rindler horizon, at the leading order at which those horizons are defined, with no further premise (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.weyl-flat-fields-need-no-scaling-limit-premise-and-robertson-walker-generators-see-the-schwarzian}(ii)); at points of nonzero Weyl curvature, and for massive fields, it follows by Theorem \claimref{synthesis.hadamard-scaling-limit-premise}, (HS) (formerly a premise), with Theorem \claimref{synthesis.bulk-hadamard-states-restrict-to-the-generator-condition-on-flat-null-planes}. So the open premise that the series' states are Hadamard on horizon generators is supplied, for the local form of (N$_{+}$), by (HD), the propagation Facts and (HS). (3) \emph{The collapse result's state.} For a hole formed by collapse inside that domain, the propagated deposit is Hadamard at the horizon, and its short-distance form on the ingoing sheet, the condition (N$_{+}^{\mathrm{coll}}$) of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.collapsing-hole-flux-from-ground-state-on-the-ingoing-sheet}(v), follows given (HS) at the horizon point (argued: that result works with the s-wave reduction of its modes). (4) \emph{The renormalised stress tensor.} On the same domain the expected stress tensor of the Dirac part of $\omega_{d}$ is defined and covariantly conserved (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-renormalised-stress-tensor-of-hadamard-dirac-states}); for the scalar fields the corresponding Hadamard point splitting is standard and not imported.

\emph{What it does not give.} (1) \emph{Which state.} The occupation profile over an admissible reference vacuum stays free. For the massless Dirac field on a Robertson-Walker collar, a homogeneous deposit diagonal over the conformal vacuum satisfies (HD) exactly when every moment of its occupation profile is finite, and the thermal deposit does (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.deposit-over-the-conformal-vacuum-is-hadamard-exactly-when-every-occupation-moment-is-finite}); the number and energy spread of the quanta do not decide it. For the massive Dirac field (HD) excludes the instantaneous vacuum at an instant where $ma' \neq 0$, every deposit diagonal over it, and every state carried through a descent across which $M = ma$ is not smooth (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.massive-dirac-instantaneous-vacuum-and-non-smooth-descents-are-not-hadamard}). Neither the wall's smoothness nor the descent's conditional expectation selects a state (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.radical-normal-tame-wall-is-a-regular-initial-surface-whose-smoothness-selects-no-state}), and no locally covariant choice of state exists. (2) \emph{The ground-state clause of (N$_{+}$).} Every Hadamard state passes the local form, thermal ones included; on a Robertson-Walker field the conformal vacuum is the ground state of the physical affine translations on every null generator only on de Sitter and flat fields (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.weyl-flat-fields-need-no-scaling-limit-premise-and-robertson-walker-generators-see-the-schwarzian}(iii)). (3) \emph{The spectrum, the deposit's infrared datum.} Relative to a homogeneous Hadamard reference, (HD) leaves the amplitude and index of the spectrum free on every bounded band, allows a power-law ratio for the conformally coupled field on no band unbounded toward short scales, and, by the infrared divergence (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-ford-parker-infrared-divergence}), a red law on no band reaching $k = 0$ for slice fields of nonzero mean; a red band needs ends $k_{\mathrm{IR}}$ and $k_{\mathrm{UV}}$ that (HD) does not fix (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.hadamard-condition-leaves-the-deposit-spectrum-free-on-bounded-bands}). (4) Anything beyond the domain of dependence of the collar. ((HS) at points of nonzero Weyl curvature or for massive fields, listed here before, is now Theorem \claimref{synthesis.hadamard-scaling-limit-premise}.)

\emph{Relation to the descent's normal form.} (HD) needs only a Cauchy collar on the descended side; the normal form of the descent is not part of it. Under the radiation-type descent, a stated hypothesis of the results that need it and not a postulate, the descent is conformally a radical-normal tame wall, the descended side is globally hyperbolic with Cauchy slices from any collar (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.radical-normal-tame-wall-is-a-regular-initial-surface-whose-smoothness-selects-no-state}(i), (iv)), the collar is Weyl-flat, the conformal vacuum is an admissible reference for the conformally covariant fields, and $M = ma$ is smooth at the origin, so the jump law of the massive result does not fire there. On the Kasner branch, Premises \claimref{synthesis.pre-descent-contracting-kasner-stage-premise} (P1) and \claimref{synthesis.pre-descent-state-carried-into-descended-phase-premise} (P2), the four-dimensional Kasner approach is Weyl-curved except at the Milne and isotropic points (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.kasner-field-is-weyl-flat-only-at-the-milne-and-isotropic-points}), the wall is a curvature singularity and no conformal vacuum is available as reference; there (HD) asserts that the state carried by (P2) into the descended phase is Hadamard on a collar after the descent.

\emph{Refutation condition.} The premise is false if either of the following is established: (a) a derivation from the series' premises that the deposited state fails the Hadamard condition on every Cauchy collar of the descent, for example that the descent deposits, for a massive Dirac field, the instantaneous vacuum where $ma' \neq 0$ or a state diagonal over it, or the tracial state (occupation one half at every momentum), or an occupation profile with an infinite moment over the conformal vacuum, or that $M = ma$ is not smooth across the descent (a kink or jump in the scale factor or its derivatives); (b) an observation of a relic free field of the descended phase whose occupation numbers over the vacuum fall off at large momentum no faster than a power, at arbitrarily large momentum, which no Hadamard state has.

\emph{Note (the spin-product selection, 2 November 2026).} One candidate for deriving (HD), selecting the deposit by the Dirac field's own spin product (the Fewster-Lang weighted spin-product state on a collar, the finite-time form of the fermionic projector state; Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-fewster-lang-spin-product-states-of-the-dirac-field}), is ruled out for massless fields: the spin product is odd under chirality, so the selected state is Hadamard on no open set of any collar on which its operator is injective, torsion included, with occupation one half over the conformal vacuum on Robertson-Walker collars for every weight; for massive fields it is Hadamard only with a smooth time weight (\claimref{synthesis.spin-product-state-of-the-massless-dirac-field-is-odd-under-chirality-and-never-hadamard}). The premise is unchanged.
## technical
The series' field theory on a gravitational field needs a state, and the store's chain from the descent to the later fields lacked one datum: that the state the descent deposits is of the regular, Hadamard kind. This premise supplies it, as an assumption: there is a thin collar after the descent, a neighbourhood of a Cauchy surface, on which the deposited state of the Dirac field and of the scalar fields is Hadamard. Equivalently, its two-point functions differ by smooth kernels from those of an admissible reference vacuum: the conformal vacuum for massless conformally covariant fields where the field is Weyl-flat, and an adiabatic state of infinite order, with the mass term $ma$ smooth, for massive Dirac fields. The occupation profile above that reference is left free; the premise fixes only its high-momentum tail.

With the imported propagation of the Hadamard form, for the scalar and the Dirac field, the deposited state is then Hadamard on every later field in the collar's domain of dependence, regions of nonzero Weyl curvature and collapsing holes included, up to any Cauchy horizon. On Weyl-flat regions the local form of (N$_{+}$) follows for the massless Dirac field with no further premise; elsewhere it follows by the scaling-limit theorem (HS). The collapse result's short-distance condition follows, argued, given (HS) at the horizon point, and the renormalised stress tensor of the Dirac field is defined and conserved.

It does not say which state is deposited (for the massless Dirac field any diagonal profile with all moments finite, the thermal one included; for massive fields not the instantaneous vacuum and not a descent with a kink); it does not give the ground-state clause of (N$_{+}$); and it does not give the spectrum, which stays the deposit's infrared datum: free on bounded bands, with ends at both large and small scales that it does not fix. The descent's normal form is separate: under the radiation-type reading, a stated hypothesis, the collar is Weyl-flat and the conformal vacuum is available; under the Kasner branch the state carried across the wall must itself be Hadamard after it. It would be refuted by a derivation that the descent deposits a non-Hadamard state, such as the instantaneous vacuum of a massive field, or through a non-smooth descent, or by a relic field whose occupations fall off only as a power at arbitrarily high momentum.

(Note, 2 November 2026: selecting the deposit by the Dirac field's own spin product is ruled out for massless fields, where the selected state is never Hadamard, so that candidate does not derive this premise, \claimref{synthesis.spin-product-state-of-the-massless-dirac-field-is-odd-under-chirality-and-never-hadamard}.)
## lay
Quantum fields in a gravitational field make sense only in well-behaved states, called Hadamard states, which look like empty space at very short distances. The series' account of the universe's earliest moment, the descent, leaves its fields in some state, but nothing in the series yet shows that state is well behaved. This premise assumes it: just after the descent the state is of the well-behaved kind. Since good behaviour, once present, is kept by the equations of motion, every later situation, including black holes formed by collapse, then has well-behaved states, which is what the series' horizon results and the energy of quantum fields need. The premise does not say which well-behaved state it is, how many particles of each energy were left behind, or what pattern of primordial ripples they carry; those remain open inputs.

## one-liner
Premise (HD): the descent's deposited state is a Hadamard state on a Cauchy collar of the descent (Dirac and scalar fields), equivalently a smooth-kernel difference from an admissible reference vacuum (conformal vacuum for massless fields, adiabatic of infinite order with $ma$ smooth for massive ones), the occupation profile staying free; with the propagation Facts it gives Hadamard states on every later field, the local form of (N$_{+}$) (premise-free on Weyl-flat regions for the massless Dirac field, given (HS) elsewhere), the collapse result's short-distance condition and the renormalised Dirac stress tensor; not which state, not the ground-state clause, not the spectrum; refuted by a non-Hadamard deposit derived from the series' premises or a relic power-law ultraviolet tail.
