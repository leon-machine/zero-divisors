\emph{Setting.} In the sourcing rule of Lemma \claimref{dark-matter.projection-every-towers-content-dilutes-ones} a tower's content is homogeneous along every direction it has and contributes its energy per unit of shared three-volume. Take comoving numbers $N$ conserved. Our quanta have density $n_{b} \propto a^{-3}$ and cell radius $r_{b} = (V_{3}n_{b})^{-1/3} \propto a$. A three-dimensional tower's sheets contain two shared directions and are comoving, one per unit comoving length along the third; its own density is $n_{3} \propto a^{-2}$ and its cell radius $r_{3} = (V_{2}n_{3})^{-1/2} \propto a$. A five-dimensional tower with hidden scale $b \propto a^{\beta}$ has own density $n_{5} \propto (a^{3}b)^{-1}$ and cell radius $r_{5} = (V_{4}n_{5})^{-1/4} \propto a^{(3 + \beta)/4}$. The ball volumes are constants and drop out of every ratio of epochs.

\emph{(i)} The clauses are those of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.unit-ball-rule}. $r_{3}/r_{b}$ is constant and $r_{5}/r_{b} \propto a^{(\beta - 1)/4}$. The check verifies $(r_{3}/r_{b})^{6} = n_{b}^{2}/n_{3}^{3}$ constant and $(r_{5}/r_{b})^{24} = n_{b}^{8}/n_{5}^{6} = a^{6(\beta - 1)}$ times its value at $a = 1$, at the scale factors $1, 2, 3, \tfrac72$ and $\beta \in \{0, \tfrac12, 1, 2\}$, and that only $\beta = 1$ gives a constant. R2 sets $r_{5} = r_{3} = r_{b}$ in one unit; at two epochs this needs $\beta = 1$. The sheet spacing is a comoving length, so it grows as $a$ like $r_{3}$, and R3's ``one radius apart'' is preserved for the three-dimensional towers whichever reading is taken.

\emph{(ii)} With R3 standing, a five-dimensional tower contributes $\rho = m\,n_{5}\,r_{5}$ per unit shared volume, so $\rho^{4} \propto n_{5}^{3} \propto a^{-3(3 + \beta)}$, that is $\rho \propto a^{-3(1 + w)}$ with $1 + w = (3 + \beta)/4$, an effective equation of state read from the dilution exponent; no pressure is derived. The check verifies $\rho^{8}a^{24(1 + w)}$ constant at the sample scale factors for each sample $\beta$, and that $\rho^{8}a^{24}$ (dust) is constant only for $\beta = 1$; the effective $w$ is $-\tfrac14, -\tfrac18, 0, \tfrac14$ at $\beta = 0, \tfrac12, 1, 2$. For $\beta = 0$ the ratio of this projected density to ours goes as $a^{-9/4}/a^{-3} = a^{3/4}$, so between $1 + z$ and today it changes by $(1 + z)^{3/4}$; the integer part of $(1090^{3})^{1/4}$ is $189$ and that of $100 \cdot 2^{3/4}$ is $168$. At the unit-ball value, itself a fit to the observed ratio (Propositions \claimref{dark-matter.unit-ball-rule} and \claimref{dark-matter.status-rule}), the five-dimensional shares are $2 \cdot \tfrac{8}{3\pi}$ of the total $\tfrac{11}{3} + \tfrac{16}{3\pi}$, a fraction $\tfrac{16}{11\pi + 16}$, whose thousandths part is $316$ at both ends of $\tfrac{333}{106} < \pi < \tfrac{355}{113}$. (Observational reading, from memory and not used: the CMB and large-scale structure bound a dark-matter equation of state to $|w| \lesssim 10^{-3}$, and the dark-to-baryon ratio inferred at last scattering agrees with late-time cluster baryon fractions to about ten percent, so a component of a third of the dark matter with $w = -\tfrac14$ is excluded by orders of magnitude.)

\emph{(iii)} With R2 and R3 fixed at the deposit, the counted thickness is a fixed length along the hidden direction, $t \propto b$, and $\rho = m\,n_{5}\,t \propto a^{-3}$ for every $\beta$; the check verifies $\rho^{2}a^{6}$ constant for each sample $\beta$. This is the lemma's dilution, applied to the counted slab instead of the whole hidden extent.

\emph{(iv)} Under (iii) with $\beta = 0$, the counted thickness today equals its value at the deposit, which R3 set to the deposit-epoch cell radius, equal by R2 to ours then: $t_{0} = r_{b}(a_{\mathrm{dep}}) = d_{b,0}\,a_{\mathrm{dep}}$ with $a_{0} = 1$. Entropy conservation gives $a_{\mathrm{dep}} = (g_{s,0}/g_{s,\mathrm{dep}})^{1/3}\,T_{0}/T_{\mathrm{desc}} \le T_{0}/T_{\mathrm{desc}}$, since the entropy degrees of freedom do not increase toward the present. With $d_{b,0} < 1.02$ m (the bracket of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.thickness-not-gravitational-quantity}), $T_{0} = 2.7255$ K $< 2.35 \times 10^{-13}$ GeV and $T_{\mathrm{desc}} \ge 10^{-3}$ GeV, $t_{0} < 2.397 \times 10^{-10}$ m, and the $40$ micrometre bound of Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-inverse-square-law-short-range} exceeds it by a factor whose decimal order is $5$. Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.thickness-not-gravitational-quantity} lists today's baryon spacing and the de Sitter radius as the extents the series has; under co-expansion today's counted thickness is indeed of order $d_{b,0}$ (the cell radius grows with $a$ from the deposit on), but with a static hidden direction it is $t_{0}$, which that comparison does not reach. Which towers are populated, R1 and the value of the thickness are not touched; the statement is about the consistency of R2 and R3 across epochs only.

\emph{(v)} In the setting of the lemma the mass densities of a three-dimensional tower, of this world and of a five-dimensional tower in their own spaces go as $a^{-2}$, $a^{-3}$ and $(a^{3}b)^{-1}$, whatever their quanta are. Premise E (\claimref{dark-matter.e-equal-mass-per-unit-ball}) at a length $d$ is $\rho_{2}V_{2}d^{2} = \rho_{3}V_{3}d^{3} = \rho_{4}V_{4}d^{4}$. The first equality holds at $d = \rho_{2}V_{2}/(\rho_{3}V_{3}) \propto a$ and the second at $d = \rho_{3}V_{3}/(\rho_{4}V_{4}) \propto b$, so the two lengths, equal at one epoch, stay equal exactly when $b \propto a$. Under the deposit reading the shares are fixed at the descent and dilute as dust, as in (iii), which used no property of the quanta. Clause (iv) took the counted thickness at the deposit to be this world's cell radius then, by R2; E does not relate its length to that radius. The script checks the two exponents and the constancy of the ratio of the two lengths at the sample exponents.
