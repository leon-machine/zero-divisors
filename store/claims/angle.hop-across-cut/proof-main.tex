The one-particle block has trace $7$ and determinant $-144$, eigenvalues $16$ and $-9$, and the eigenvector for $-9$ satisfies $16u = 12v$, giving $(u, v) = (\tfrac35, \tfrac45)$. The script checks the block, the sector energies, the eigenvector, the norm, the parity, both marginals by restriction to the mode's algebra, and the two eigenvector statements. The generator lies outside the supplied class because every supplied generator commutes with every $N_{x}$ (Theorem \claimref{singularity.class-relative-no-go-no-supplied}), while $[N_{0}, a^{\dagger}_{0}a_{1} + a^{\dagger}_{1}a_{0}] = a^{\dagger}_{0}a_{1} - a^{\dagger}_{1}a_{0} \neq 0$.
