On a region where the gravitational field is a pure dilation $\hb = \sigma$ of the flat background in some gauge (so the Weyl part of the field strength vanishes; derived metric $\sigma^{-2}\eta$) the ambient bundle is trivial: its fibre is one fixed vector space $V$ with the ambient pairing, parallel sections are constant vectors, and the canonical tractor at the point $x$ is $X(x) = n_{0} + x - \tfrac12 Q(x)\, n_{\infty}$, with $n_{0}, n_{\infty}$ null, $\ip{n_{0}}{n_{\infty}} = +1$, $x$ in their orthogonal complement, and $Q(x) = \ip{x}{x} = g(x,x)$ the flat quadratic form, so that $Q(X(x)) = 0$ identically. The scale function of a constant vector $V$ is its $\sigma$-slot in the flat scale, $\sigma_{V}(x) = \ip{X(x)}{V}$. The complexification of $V$ is $\Lambda^{2}\CS$ for $\CS$ the conformal spinor space (twistor space; the chirality half of the ascended Dirac module), with pairing $\ip{U}{U'} = \varepsilon(U \wedge U')$ for the volume form $\varepsilon$ on $\Lambda^{4}\CS$ that the identification fixes; a bivector is simple iff its norm vanishes; the mixed slot carries $Q(x) = -2\det x$ in the chart's components \cite[Thm.~4.2(2)]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.incidence-relation-chart}); a conformal spinor splits as $Z = (\omega, \pi)$ with $\omega$ in the two-plane $\Som$ whose Pl\"ucker bivector is $n_{\infty}$ and $\pi$ in the two-plane $\Spi$ whose Pl\"ucker bivector is $n_{0}$. The action of $\mathfrak{sl}(\CS)$ on $\Lambda^{2}\CS$ by $A\cdot(u \wedge v) = Au \wedge v + u \wedge Av$ is a Lie algebra isomorphism onto $\mathfrak{so}(\Lambda^{2}\CS, \varepsilon)$, and the infinity bivector of a scale (the infinity twistor) is the image of its scale tractor: for a vacuum with $\Lambda = 0$ it is covariantly constant and simple, the Pl\"ucker bivector of the infinity line. \emph{Provenance:} the flat model as a fixed vector space with the point embedding is \cite[\S4.2]{ButlerT6}, the scale function $\sigma = \ip{X}{I}$ recovering $g = \sigma^{-2}\eta$ is used in \cite[Lem.~4.8]{ButlerT6}, and the general form of the dictionary (parallel sections are constant vectors, with $\sigma$-slot $\ip{X(x)}{V}$) is \cite[\S2]{BEG1994}; in the form needed here it is elementary and is proved in one paragraph inside Lemma \claimref{mass.de-sitter-scale-frozen-point}; the chirality half is \cite[Thm.~3.1]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.fock-model-complexified-ascended-algebra}); the identification $V_{\C} = \Lambda^{2}\CS$ with its pairing, the normalisation of $\varepsilon$, the simplicity criterion, and the equivariance of the identification are \cite[Thm.~3.4]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.spinor-identification}); the chart, the identification of the incidence relation with the completion's own chart, the spin isomorphism, and the infinity bivector are \cite[Thm.~4.2, Cor.~4.8, Prop.~5.2]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.spin-representation}, \claimref{correspondence.one-statement-three-languages}), in that paper's sign conventions, machine-verified there. The spinor bracket $\br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}} = \varepsilon^{AB}\pi^{1}_{A}\pi^{2}_{B}$, the null momentum $p_{AA'} = \pi_{A}\bar\pi_{A'}$ of a conformal spinor, and the conformal generators as Hermitian quadratics $\bar{Z}_{\alpha}Z^{\beta}$ in the conformal spinor variables are classical \cite{PenroseRindler1986, Hughston1979}.

This restates \claimref{mass.imported-flat-model-twistor-chart-infinity-external}.
