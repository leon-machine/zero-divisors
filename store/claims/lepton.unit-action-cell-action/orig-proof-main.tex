(1) Both actions send $e_{i} \otimes f_{a}$ to $Ae_{i} \otimes Bf_{a}$. (2) Under the cell action a mixing $A$ sends $\bigwedge_{a \in S}(e_{i} \otimes f_{a})$ to $\bigwedge_{a \in S}(Ae_{i} \otimes f_{a})$, which is the image of $(Ae_{i})^{\otimes k} \otimes f_{S}$ under the projection $(\C^{2})^{\otimes k} \otimes \Lambda^{k}\C^{3} \to \Lambda^{k}(\C^{2} \otimes \C^{3})$; as $Ae_{i}$ ranges over $\C^{2}$ the tensors $v^{\otimes k}$ span $S^{k}\C^{2}$, so the span is $S^{k}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{k}\C^{3}$, of dimension $(k+1)\binom{3}{k}$, which is $9$ at $k = 2$ and $4$ at $k = 3$, exceeding $\dim R_{k} = 2\binom{3}{k}$ ($6$ and $2$) so $R_{k}$ is not preserved. Its $SU(2) \times SU(3)$ content is $(k+1, \Lambda^{k}\C^{3})$, that is $(3,\bar{3})$ and $(4,1)$, the representations Fact \claimref{lepton.imported-tensor-ledger-contains-no-lepton} lists in degrees two and three; and the weight is $k$, one per cell. Under the unit action $A$ acts once on the slot vector, so the span is $\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{k}W = \Con_{k}$ with the weight of Proposition \claimref{lepton.content-constituent-space}. The script confirms the ranks: the $SU(2)$-orbit of a fixed pair subset $S$ under the cell action has rank $k + 1$ ($2, 3, 4$ for $k = 1, 2, 3$) and under the unit action rank $2$. (3) A subrepresentation would have to be preserved by the cell action; by (2) $R_{2}$ and $R_{3}$ are not, and by Fact \claimref{lepton.imported-tensor-ledger-contains-no-lepton} the ledger contains no $(2,1)$ at all. The reading of the ledger as the Fock space of pairs is the definition of the ledger, and is what Fact \claimref{lepton.imported-twistor-state-pair-system-excitations} gives on two twistors; what (2) adds is the exact point at which (C) departs from it: on a twistor with two or more pairs excited, the ledger lets a mixing of slots act on each pair separately, and (C) lets it act on the twistor once. Fact \claimref{standard-model.internal-symmetry-ladder} is silent on excited twistors, so it decides neither way; that is why (C) is a hypothesis.
