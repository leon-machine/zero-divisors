Read in Gauge Theory Gravity: the metric below is the derived metric of a gravitational field $(\hb, \Omega)$ on flat spacetime (Definition \claimref{obj.synthesis.gtg-flat-spacetime-gauge-fields}), and the manifold is its invertible region.

Substitute $(1, 0, \rho)$ into \claimref{interior.imported-tractor-connection}: the slots are $(\,-0,\; g_{ab}\rho - \Sch_{ab},\; \nabla_a \rho\,)$. Parallelism therefore requires $\rho$ constant and $\Sch_{ab} = \rho\, g_{ab}$; by Lemma \claimref{interior.metric-parallelism-trace-schouten-condition}(ii) this forces $\Ric_{ab} = \tfrac{R}{4}g_{ab}$ with $\rho = \tfrac{R}{24}$, i.e.\ the Einstein condition with $\Lambda = \tfrac{R}{4} = 6\rho$. Conversely, for Einstein $g$ with $\Ric = \Lambda g$: $R = 4\Lambda$ and $\Sch_{ab} = \tfrac12(\Lambda - \tfrac{4\Lambda}{6})g_{ab} = \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}g_{ab}$, so $(1, 0, \tfrac{\Lambda}{6})$ is parallel. The norm is $2 \cdot 1 \cdot \tfrac{\Lambda}{6} = \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}$.
