The source states that this matrix-factor theorem is the finite-dimensional case of a standard imported result: ``Theorems \claimref{state-selection.kms-uniqueness-factor}, \claimref{state-selection.rate-rigidity-temperature-not-algebra-intrinsic} and \claimref{state-selection.partner-purification-are-canonical} are proved here in the matrix factor, which is the setting Step 4d works in; the general versions are the imported Facts \claimref{state-selection.imported-general-statement}, and in the genuine field-theoretic setting the horizon algebra is a type III$_1$ factor, where the Gibbs form is unavailable and the KMS condition must be used directly.'' The general version is Fact \claimref{state-selection.imported-general-statement}: a factor state is extremal, and by Connes' cocycle theorem two faithful normal states with the same modular automorphism group on a factor are equal.
