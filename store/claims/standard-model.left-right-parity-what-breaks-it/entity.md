## formal
Under Hypothesis (S), let $D = \gamma_{5}\gamma_{9}$, one pair Majorana times one slot Majorana. Then $D^{2} = -1$ and $D = \exp(\tfrac{\pi}{2}\gamma_{5}\gamma_{9})$, a half-turn generated by a slot-pair mixing bivector; conjugation by $D$ exchanges $\su(2)_{L}$ and $\su(2)_{R}$ and maps the eight states of $Q$ and $L$ onto the eight singlet states; and $D$ anticommutes with $\chi$. Among the $210$ grade-four elements of the Clifford algebra of the ten, the Pati-Salam singlets form a one-dimensional space, spanned by $\gamma_{6}\gamma_{7}\gamma_{8}\gamma_{9}$, the product of the four slot Majoranas, which acts on the module as $\chi$. Hence a vev along the rung parity breaks $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ to Pati-Salam and, the rung parity being odd under $D$, breaks left-right parity at the same scale.

## technical
Under Hypothesis (S), $D = \gamma_{5}\gamma_{9}$, one pair Majorana times one slot Majorana, satisfies $D^{2} = -1$, so $D = \exp(\tfrac{\pi}{2}\gamma_{5}\gamma_{9})$ is a half-turn generated by a slot-pair mixing bivector. Conjugation by $D$ exchanges $\su(2)_{L}$ and $\su(2)_{R}$ and maps the eight states of $Q$ and $L$ onto the eight singlet states, and $D$ anticommutes with the rung parity $\chi$.

Among the $210$ grade-four elements of the Clifford algebra of the ten, the Pati-Salam singlets form a one-dimensional space spanned by $\gamma_{6}\gamma_{7}\gamma_{8}\gamma_{9}$, which acts on the module as $\chi$. So a vev along the rung parity breaks $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ to Pati-Salam and, since $\chi$ is odd under $D$, breaks left-right parity at the same scale.

## lay
In the Pati-Salam picture there is a symmetry that exchanges left-handed and right-handed matter. Under Hypothesis (S), the paper's hypothesis about the state space, this exchange is a half-turn that mixes one pair direction with one slot direction. The only Pati-Salam invariant of a certain kind is the rung parity, and the exchange flips its sign. So giving the rung parity a background value breaks the large symmetry down to Pati-Salam and breaks left-right symmetry at the same energy.

## one-liner
Under Hypothesis (S), left-right parity is the half-turn $D = \gamma_{5}\gamma_{9}$, and a vev along the rung parity, the unique Pati-Salam singlet of the $210$, breaks $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ to Pati-Salam and $D$ at one scale.
