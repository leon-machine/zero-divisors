Read in Gauge Theory Gravity: the metric $g$ below is the derived metric of the gravitational field $(\hb, \Omega)$ on flat spacetime, the manifold is the invertible region, and the tractor connection is the flat-space $\Cl(2,4)$ gauge field of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.gtg-tractor-gauge-field-reproduces-tractor-connection}; the index computation below is its reading in the splitting of $g$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}).

The ambient bundle carries the canonical connection, whose expression in the scale $g$ is
\begin{equation}
\nabla_{a}(\sigma, \mu_{b}, \rho) = \bigl(\nabla_{a}\sigma - \mu_{a},\;\; \nabla_{a}\mu_{b} + g_{ab}\,\rho - P_{ab}\,\sigma,\;\; \nabla_{a}\rho + P_{a}{}^{b}\mu_{b}\bigr),
\end{equation}
and it preserves \eqref{eq:tractormetric}. On a vacuum Einstein background with cosmological constant $\Lambda$, in these conventions $P_{ab} = (\Lambda/6)\,g_{ab}$ and $J = 2\Lambda/3$, the bundle carries a distinguished parallel section $I$, the frozen point: in its own scale $I = (1, 0, \rho_{I})$ with vanishing middle slot, $\nabla I = 0$ is equivalent to the vacuum equations, and
\[
\rho_{I} = \frac{J}{n} = \frac{\Lambda}{6}, \qquad Q(I) = \frac{2J}{n} = \frac{\Lambda}{3}.
\]
(The geometry paper is four-dimensional; the general-$n$ form of $\rho_{I}$ is the middle slot of \eqref{eq:tractorconn} for the section $(1, 0, \rho_{I})$, $g_{ab}\rho_{I} = P_{ab}$, traced.) $I$ is null exactly when $\Lambda = 0$; for $\Lambda > 0$ its stabiliser is $SO(1,4)$ and the induced form on $I^{\perp}$ has signature $(1,4)$. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Fact~4.1, Prop.~4.4, Thm.~4.5, Thm.~4.7, Prop.~4.10]{ButlerT6} (\claimref{interior.scale-tractor-leaves-cone}, \claimref{interior.stabiliser-rotates-parabolic-reductive}, \claimref{interior.non-null-stabilisers-section-they-act}, \claimref{interior.imported-tractor-connection}, \claimref{interior.covariant-form-vacuum-equations}) (Prop.~4.4 for the stabiliser algebra and the signature of $I^{\perp}$, Prop.~4.10 for the group). The sign of the norm is the series' convention, fixed there and machine-verified; \cite[Rem.~4.9]{ButlerT6} records why that sign is not free: a norm of the opposite sign could not carry the de Sitter stabiliser the same paper establishes. The relative sign of the Schouten slots in \eqref{eq:tractorconn} against the literature is convention-dependent and is fixed internally in the geometry paper \cite[Lem.~4.8]{ButlerT6} (\claimref{interior.sign-fixed-internally}); every formula of this paper is written in the convention of \eqref{eq:tractorconn}.
