(1): the map $x \wedge y \mapsto \bigl(z \mapsto \eta(x,z)\,y - \eta(y,z)\,x\bigr)$ sends bivectors to $\eta$-antisymmetric endomorphisms, is injective (a nonzero bivector moves some vector), and both spaces have dimension six, so the bivector algebra of the exceptional fibre \emph{is} $\mathfrak{so}(1,3)$, with the commutator matching the Clifford bracket; the script checks closure, dimension, and the bracket exactly. The residual freedom for $p_2 = p_3$ is the $O(2)$ of the degenerate pair. (2) is the dictionary: both sides attach the same exponents to the same eigendirections, by Theorem \claimref{sheaf.weights-are-forced-are-kasner-exponents} here and by definition there. (3): the action of $B_w$ sends $\hat e_0 \mapsto \sum_i p_i \hat e_i$ and $\hat e_j \mapsto p_j\, \hat e_0$, so it vanishes on the spatial vectors orthogonal to $\hat p := \sum_i p_i \hat e_i$ and squares to $\sum_i p_i^2$ times the identity on the boost plane $\operatorname{span}\{\hat e_0, \hat p\}$: the characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^2\,(\lambda^2 - \sum_i p_i^2) = \lambda^4 - (\sum_i p_i^2)\lambda^2$, and the constraint $\sum p_i^2 = 1$ normalises it (the script confirms the polynomial at two rational Kasner points). The ladder identities $K = N - \tfrac12$ and $K^2 = \tfrac14$ are verified on all $64$ ambient states (Section \ref{sec:verification}).
