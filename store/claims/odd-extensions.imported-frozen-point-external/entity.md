## formal
The results from outside the series on which \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-frozen-point} draws, as cited there: S. W. MacDowell and F. Mansouri, Unified geometric theory of gravity and supergravity, Phys. Rev. Lett. 38 (1977) 739.; K. S. Stelle and P. C. West, Spontaneously broken de Sitter symmetry and the gravitational holonomy group, Phys. Rev. D 21 (1980) 1466..

## technical
The outside sources for the frozen-point construction: MacDowell and Mansouri's formulation of gravity as a gauge theory of the de Sitter or anti-de Sitter group broken to the Lorentz group, and Stelle and West's form of it, in which the breaking is carried by an internal vector $V$ of fixed square $Q(V) = -3/\Lambda$.

In that form the vector $V$ selects a Lorentz subgroup, and the de Sitter connection splits into a Lorentz connection and a translation part that plays the role of the vierbein.

## lay
Gravity can be written as a gauge theory of a larger symmetry group that is then broken down to the familiar symmetry of spacetime. MacDowell and Mansouri introduced this idea, and Stelle and West recast it with a fixed internal vector doing the breaking. The series' frozen-point construction rests on their work.

## one-liner
The frozen-point construction draws on MacDowell and Mansouri's de Sitter gauge formulation of gravity in the form given by Stelle and West.
