The product form is Theorem \claimref{singularity.kms-uniqueness-factor} applied to $K_{\mathrm{geo}}$, whose exponential factorises because the $K_k$ commute. A product state has $S(\varrho) = \sum_k S(\varrho_k)$, so $C(\varrho) = 0$. A product of diagonal matrices is diagonal in the joint number basis, hence a fixed point of $\EN$, and the deficit theorem gives $S(\EN\varrho) - S(\varrho) = 0$. The last clause is Corollary \claimref{singularity.deficit-carried-quanta-correlations}, which identifies the deficit with $C(\varrho) - C(\EN\varrho)$. The script verifies the fixed-point and factorisation clauses in exact arithmetic against an entangled even control failing both.
