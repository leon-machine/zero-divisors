The product form is Theorem \claimref{state-selection.kms-uniqueness-factor} applied to $K_{\mathrm{geo}} = \sum_k \omega_k K_k$, whose exponential factorises because the $K_k$ commute. (1) For a product state $S(\rho) = \sum_k S(\rho_k)$, so $C(\rho) = 0$. (2) A product of diagonal matrices is diagonal in the joint number basis, hence fixed by the pinching $\EN$ of \cite[Def.~9.1]{ButlerT5}, and the deficit theorem gives $\rel{\rho}{\EN\rho} = S(\EN\rho) - S(\rho) = 0$. (3) is (1) and (2) with \cite[Cor.~9.4]{ButlerT5}, which identifies the deficit with $C(\rho) - C(\EN\rho)$.
