(i) The images square to $1$ and $-1$ as required ($\mathbf{q}_k^{2} = -1$) and anticommute: $\gamma_0$ against $\gamma_k$ by the sign pattern of the blocks, $\gamma_j$ against $\gamma_k$ because distinct quaternion units anticommute. By \claimref{std-algebra.existence-uniqueness} they define a homomorphism into $\Mat_2(\HH)$, a real algebra of dimension $16$; the $16$ blade images are independent (computed), so by \claimref{std-algebra.blade-basis} it is an isomorphism. This agrees with \claimref{primer.classification-real-clifford-algebras} for $p - q = -2 \equiv 6$. Inside the algebra the same isomorphism is read off from the displayed matrix units: $\tfrac12(1 \pm \gamma_0)$ are complementary idempotents, $J$ anticommutes with $\gamma_0$ and squares to $1$, so $\epsilon_{ij}\epsilon_{kl} = \delta_{jk}\epsilon_{il}$; $J$ and $\gamma_0$ commute with the even spatial bivectors $\mathbf{q}_k$; $J\mathbf{q}_k = \gamma_k$; and the sixteen products $\epsilon_{ij}\mathbf{q}$ ($\mathbf{q} \in \{1, \mathbf{q}_1, \mathbf{q}_2, \mathbf{q}_3\}$) are independent (a direct computation of their blade coefficients), so $x = \sum_{ij}\epsilon_{ij}x_{ij}$ with $x_{ij} \in \HH$ and $x \mapsto (x_{ij})$ is the displayed assignment. (ii) The same argument gives a homomorphism to $\Mat_4(\C)$ with real-independent blade images, hence injective; the images are also complex-independent, so their complex span has dimension $16 = \dim_\C \Mat_4(\C)$. (iii) $\sigma_k^{2} = \gamma_k\gamma_0\gamma_k\gamma_0 = -\gamma_k^{2}\gamma_0^{2} = 1$; for $j \ne k$, $\sigma_j\sigma_k = \gamma_j\gamma_0\gamma_k\gamma_0 = -\gamma_j\gamma_k\gamma_0^{2} = -\gamma_j\gamma_k$, which is antisymmetric in $j, k$. $\sigma_1\sigma_2\sigma_3 = I_4$ is computed. The $\sigma_k$ are even, their eight products are independent (computed) and $\dim \Cl^{+}(1,3) = 8$ (\claimref{std-algebra.z2-grading}), so the homomorphism $\Cl(3,0) \to \Cl^{+}(1,3)$, $e_k \mapsto \sigma_k$, is an isomorphism.
