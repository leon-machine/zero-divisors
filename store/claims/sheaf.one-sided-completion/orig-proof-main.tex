Take collar data with $\mu\nu > 0$ (Definition \claimref{obj.sheaf.collar-data-coupling}) and $h = G \perp H + \kappa$; the equality $h = G \perp H$ outside the collar is built in. Nondegeneracy on $\Mp$, in three regions.

\emph{On $\Sig$}: Lemma \claimref{sheaf.wall-gram}(4) gives nondegeneracy with signature $(p{+}1, q{+}1)$ directly.

\emph{In the collar, physical side}: here $\lambda > 0$, so by Lemma \claimref{sheaf.determinant-identity-blow-up-expansion}(2) there is a locally uniform constant $C$ with $s \geq 1/\lambda - C$; shrinking the collar (to a possibly variable-width collar, using local uniformity) until $1/\lambda \geq 2C$ on its physical side gives $s \geq 1/(2\lambda) > 0$ there. Then $1 + 2\mu\nu\beta^2 s \geq 1$, so by Lemma \claimref{sheaf.determinant-identity-blow-up-expansion}(1) $\det h \neq 0$: nondegenerate.

\emph{Outside the collar}: $h = G \perp H$, nondegenerate of signature $(p{+}1,q{+}1)$ since $G$ is nondegenerate of signature $(p,q)$ on $\Mo \cap \Mp$.

For the signature on all of $\Mp$: the signature of a continuous nondegenerate form is locally constant, and every point of $\Mp$ is connected within the nondegeneracy region $\Mp$ to either $\Sig$ or the exterior of the collar (follow the transversal), where the signature is $(p{+}1,q{+}1)$ by the first and third cases. The final sentence of the theorem is then Proposition \claimref{sheaf.local-freeness-constant-rank} applied to $(T, h)$ over $\Mp$: a locally free sheaf of algebras of rank $2^{n+2}$ with every fibre the Clifford algebra of a form of signature $(p{+}1, q{+}1)$.
