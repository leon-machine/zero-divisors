\emph{The split.} Let $A$, $B$ be complementary sets of modes, $\Cl(A)$, $\Cl(B)$ their sub-Clifford algebras, $d_{A}$, $d_{B}$ the dimensions $2^{|A|}$, $2^{|B|}$, $E_{A}$ the projection onto the blades of $\Cl(A)$, and $\Gamma_{A} = \prod_{k \in A}p_{k}$, of square $1$, anticommuting with the generators of $\Cl(A)$ and commuting with $\Cl(B)$. (a) The generators $y\Gamma_{A}$, $y$ a generator of $\Cl(B)$, keep their squares, anticommute with each other and commute with $\Cl(A)$; by Theorem \claimref{tower.commuting-copies} (ground algebra $\Cl(B)$, the planes of $A$, whose volume elements are the $-p_{k}$) they generate an algebra $B'$ commuting with $\Cl(A)$ with $\Cl(A) \otimes B'$ the whole algebra, an ordinary tensor product. (b) Blades are orthogonal for $\langle xy\rangle_{0}$, so $\langle E_{A}(x)y\rangle_{0} = \langle xy\rangle_{0}$ for $y \in \Cl(A)$: $d_{B}E_{A}$ is the partial trace of that tensor product over $B'$. Likewise the partial trace over $\Cl(A)$ is $d_{A}$ times the projection onto the blades of $B'$, which are the even blades of $\Cl(B)$ and $\Gamma_{A}$ times its odd blades; the latter and the odd blades of $\Cl(B)$ are odd, so on an even element this projection is $E_{B}$. (c) Hence for an even state the reductions $d_{B}E_{A}(\rho)$, $d_{A}E_{B}(\rho)$ are the two partial traces of an ordinary tensor product, and if the state is pure they have the same nonzero spectrum (Schmidt decomposition). Evenness is needed: $\tfrac14(1 + e_{1})(1 + e_{2}p_{1})$ is pure with reductions of purity $1$ and $\tfrac12$. (d) Even elements of the algebras of disjoint sets of modes commute, a product of blades from disjoint sets is a blade, scalar only if every factor is and in $\Cl(A)$ only if every factor is; so for such elements $x_{j}$, $\langle\prod x_{j}\rangle_{0} = \prod\langle x_{j}\rangle_{0}$, $E_{A}(\prod x_{j}) = \prod E_{A}(x_{j})$, and traces of powers of products multiply. (e) The pair state with amplitudes $c$ on $\ket{00}$ and $s$ on $\ket{11}$ of modes $k$, $k'$ is $\tfrac14(1 + p_{k}p_{k'})(1 + rp_{k}) + cs(w_{k}w_{k'} + v_{k'}v_{k})$, $r = c^{2} - s^{2}$ (expand $\psi\Omega\psi^{\dagger}$, $\psi = c + s\,w_{k}w_{k'}$, $\Omega = v_{k}w_{k}v_{k'}w_{k'}$, $\dagger$ the anti-automorphism exchanging $v$ and $w$); it is even, and its blades in the algebra of mode $k$ are $\tfrac14(1 + rp_{k})$. The state of the eviction theorem is a product of such pair states, so, reading the pairs of that theorem as these pair states, it is a pure even state, fixed by $x \mapsto \tfrac12(x + \Gamma x\Gamma)$, the total-parity form of the single-mode average of Theorem \claimref{singularity.descent-parity-average}. The last check function verifies (a) to (e) on three and four modes.

\emph{(i).} By Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}(1) the window state is the product of its members' thermal marginals, which, reading the pairs of that theorem as the pair states of (e), is on the split $\rho_{L} = \prod_{k \in W(t)}\tfrac12(1 + r_{k}p_{k})$ by (d) and (e), and the joint state of window and radiation is pure and even, so by (c) $\rho_{R}$ and $\rho_{L}$ have the same nonzero spectrum and $\operatorname{tr}\rho_{R}^{2} = \operatorname{tr}\rho_{L}^{2}$. The purity of the product is the product of the purities by (d). In the algebra of mode $k$, with trace $2\langle\cdot\rangle_{0}$ and $p_{k}^{2} = 1$, $\rho_{k}^{2} = \tfrac14(1 + r_{k}^{2}) + \tfrac12 r_{k}p_{k}$, so $\operatorname{tr}\rho_{k}^{2} = \tfrac12(1 + r_{k}^{2})$, which with $c_{k}^{2} + s_{k}^{2} = 1$ is $c_{k}^{4} + s_{k}^{4}$; zero weight ($c_{k}^{2} = s_{k}^{2} = \tfrac12$, $r_{k} = 0$) gives $\tfrac12$. The script computes $2\langle\rho^{2}\rangle_{0}$ for an element of square $1$ (the vector $e_{3}$ of $\Cl(3,0)$ standing for $p_{k}$) at $c^{2} = \tfrac12, \tfrac23, \tfrac57$ and the purity of the threefold Kronecker product of the matrices; the last check function computes the same in the algebra of the modes, with $p_{k} = f_{k}e_{k}$.

\emph{(ii).} At zero weight each window member is maximally entangled with one radiated quantum (Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}(4), maximally entangled idealisation). After $j$ radiated quanta, write $w = |W(t)|$; by clauses (3) and (4) of that theorem, $w = j$ before the first eviction, and afterwards the window is held at the capacity bound, so $w = \min(j, n_{\mathrm{cap}})$; an evicted pair has both halves in the radiation, where they form a pure pair. So $\rho_{R}$ is the product, in the sense of (d), of $w$ maximally mixed modes with $j - w$ pure even pairs, of purity $2^{-w} = \max(2^{-j}, 2^{-n_{\mathrm{cap}}})$, since $\min(j, n_{\mathrm{cap}})$ is the smaller exponent. It is one of the two terms of $2^{-j} + 2^{-n_{\mathrm{cap}}}$, never their sum, because both terms are positive. The script builds the state vector for every $1 \leq j \leq 6$ and $0 \leq w \leq j$ (radiation register of the $j$ quanta and the $j - w$ evicted partners, window register of the $w$ members, amplitudes in integers), forms $\rho_{R}$ by the exact partial trace, and confirms $\operatorname{tr}\rho_{R}^{2} = 2^{-w}$; it then runs a stage sequence with $n_{\mathrm{cap}} = 6 - j$ and confirms $\max(2^{-j}, 2^{-n_{\mathrm{cap}}})$ at each $j = 0, \ldots, 6$.

\emph{(iii).} First the abstract tensor product. For the unitarily invariant measure on unit vectors of a space of dimension $D = mn$, the second moment is $\langle\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}^{\otimes 2}\rangle = (\mathbb{1} + F)/(D(D + 1))$, with $F$ the swap of the two copies (Schur's lemma on the symmetric square). Since $\operatorname{tr}\rho_{A}^{2} = \operatorname{tr}[(\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}^{\otimes 2})(F_{A} \otimes \mathbb{1})]$ with $F_{A}$ the swap of the two $A$ factors, $\langle\operatorname{tr}\rho_{A}^{2}\rangle = (\operatorname{tr}F_{A} + \operatorname{tr}FF_{A})/(D(D + 1))$. On the basis $\ket{a_{1}b_{1}a_{2}b_{2}}$, $F_{A}$ sends it to $\ket{a_{2}b_{1}a_{1}b_{2}}$ and $FF_{A}$ to $\ket{a_{1}b_{2}a_{2}b_{1}}$, so $\operatorname{tr}F_{A} = mn^{2}$ and $\operatorname{tr}FF_{A} = m^{2}n$, and $\langle\operatorname{tr}\rho_{A}^{2}\rangle = mn(m + n)/(mn(mn + 1)) = (m + n)/(mn + 1)$. The script counts the diagonal entries for six pairs $(m, n)$. Then $1/m + 1/n - (m + n)/(mn + 1) = (m + n)/(mn(mn + 1)) > 0$, and $(m + n)/(mn + 1) > 1/\min(m, n)$ because $\min(m, n)(m + n) > mn + 1$ for $m, n \geq 2$ (with $m \leq n$: $m^{2} + mn > mn + 1$). Now the even states. A pure even state is a projector of rank one commuting with the involution $\Gamma$, so its vector lies in an eigenspace of $\Gamma$, a parity sector, of dimension $D/2$; in the tensor product of (a), $\Gamma = \Gamma_{A} \otimes \Gamma_{B}$, and the sector is spanned by the basis vectors $\ket{ab}$ with the parities of $a$ and $b$ equal (or, for the other sector, opposite). By (c) the reduction to $A$ is the partial trace of that tensor product, so $\operatorname{tr}\rho_{A}^{2}$ is again the expectation of $F_{A} \otimes \mathbb{1}$, and Schur's lemma on the symmetric square of the sector gives the second moment $\Pi^{\otimes 2}(\mathbb{1} + F)/(\tfrac{D}{2}(\tfrac{D}{2} + 1))$, $\Pi$ the projector on the sector. The trace of $F_{A}$ on the square of the sector counts $(a_{1}, b_{1}, a_{2}, b_{2})$ with $a_{1} = a_{2}$ and both $b$ of the parity fixed by $a_{1}$: $m(n/2)^{2}$; that of $FF_{A}$ counts those with $b_{1} = b_{2}$: $n(m/2)^{2}$. So $\langle\operatorname{tr}\rho_{A}^{2}\rangle = \tfrac14 mn(m + n)/(\tfrac14 mn(mn + 2)) = (m + n)/(mn + 2)$. Then $1/m + 1/n - (m + n)/(mn + 2) = 2(m + n)/(mn(mn + 2)) > 0$, $(m + n)/(mn + 2) < (m + n)/(mn + 1)$, and with $m \le n$, $m(m + n) > mn + 2$ since $m^{2} \ge 4$. The last check function counts the two traces on the sector for six pairs $(m, n)$. (The same value is the case $n = 2$ of the sector formula for every index, clause (iv) of the stored result on stabiliser-family averages and the parity sector, which is not needed here.)

\emph{(iv).} At $j = n_{\mathrm{cap}}$, (ii) gives $2^{-j} = 1/m$ with $m = 2^{j}$ and the two-saddle value $2/m$, ratio $2$, so $S_{2}$ differs by $\ln 2$; (iii) with $n = m$ gives $2m/(m^{2} + 1)$, whose ratio to $1/m$ is $2m^{2}/(m^{2} + 1) = 2 - 2/(m^{2} + 1) \in (2 - 2/m^{2}, 2)$, so its $S_{2}$ lies below the eviction value by $\ln 2 - \ln(1 + m^{-2})$, which is in $(\ln 2 - m^{-2}, \ln 2)$ since $0 < \ln(1 + u) < u$. On a sector, $2m/(m^{2} + 2)$ has ratio $2m^{2}/(m^{2} + 2) = 2 - 4/(m^{2} + 2) \in (2 - 4/m^{2}, 2)$ to $1/m$, so its $S_{2}$ lies below the eviction value by $\ln 2 - \ln(1 + 2m^{-2}) \in (\ln 2 - 2m^{-2}, \ln 2)$. Off the crossing the ratio of $2^{-j} + 2^{-n_{\mathrm{cap}}}$ to its larger term is $1 + 2^{-|j - n_{\mathrm{cap}}|}$. The script checks the ratios at $m = 2, 4, 8$ from the computed Haar purity (at $m = 16$ from the formula) and along the stage sequence of (ii).

\emph{(v).} By Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}(1), $S_{R}(t) = \sum_{k \in W(t)}h_{k}$, and $h_{k}$ is the von Neumann entropy of member $k$'s thermal marginal, so $S_{R}$ is the thermal entropy of the window with no correction term at any weight. The deficit of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.eviction-deficit-against-page-deficit} is $\delta_{\mathrm{ev}} = C - S_{R}$ with $C = |W(t)|\ln 2$, the maximal entropy of $|W(t)|$ two-level members; it equals the thermal entropy exactly when every $h_{k} = \ln 2$, that is at zero weight, where $h_{k} = s(0) = \ln 2$. Under the weights $\kappa(k + \tfrac12)$ every member has $|x_{k}| = (2k + 1)\pi \geq \pi$, so the stored clause (iii) applies with $x_{*} = \pi$ and its linear deficit is a deficit against $C$. The Page-typical branch of Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-page-average-entropy-subsystem} is compared against $\ln m$, the logarithm of the dimension, which for a window at zero weight is $C$. $\square$
