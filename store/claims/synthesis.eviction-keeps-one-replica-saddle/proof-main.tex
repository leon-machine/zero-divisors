\emph{(i).} By Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}(1) the window state is $\rho_{L} = \bigotimes_{k \in W(t)}\operatorname{diag}(c_{k}^{2}, s_{k}^{2})$, and the joint state of window and radiation is pure, so $\rho_{R}$ and $\rho_{L}$ have the same nonzero spectrum (Schmidt decomposition) and $\operatorname{tr}\rho_{R}^{2} = \operatorname{tr}\rho_{L}^{2}$. The purity of a tensor product is the product of the purities, since $\operatorname{tr}(A \otimes B)^{2} = \operatorname{tr}A^{2}\operatorname{tr}B^{2}$. In $\Cl(3,0)$, with the matrix trace equal to $2\langle\cdot\rangle_{0}$ and $e_{3}^{2} = 1$, $\rho_{k}^{2} = \tfrac14(1 + r_{k}^{2}) + \tfrac12 r_{k}e_{3}$, so $\operatorname{tr}\rho_{k}^{2} = \tfrac12(1 + r_{k}^{2})$, which with $c_{k}^{2} + s_{k}^{2} = 1$ is $c_{k}^{4} + s_{k}^{4}$; zero weight ($c_{k}^{2} = s_{k}^{2} = \tfrac12$, $r_{k} = 0$) gives $\tfrac12$. The script computes $2\langle\rho^{2}\rangle_{0}$ in $\Cl(3,0)$ at $c^{2} = \tfrac12, \tfrac23, \tfrac57$ and the purity of the threefold Kronecker product of the matrices.

\emph{(ii).} At zero weight each window member is maximally entangled with one radiated quantum (Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}(4), maximally entangled idealisation). After $j$ radiated quanta, write $w = |W(t)|$; by clauses (3) and (4) of that theorem, $w = j$ before the first eviction, and afterwards the window is held at the capacity bound, so $w = \min(j, n_{\mathrm{cap}})$; an evicted pair has both halves in the radiation, where they form a pure pair. So $\rho_{R}$ is the tensor product of $w$ maximally mixed qubits with $j - w$ pure pairs, of purity $2^{-w} = \max(2^{-j}, 2^{-n_{\mathrm{cap}}})$, since $\min(j, n_{\mathrm{cap}})$ is the smaller exponent. It is one of the two terms of $2^{-j} + 2^{-n_{\mathrm{cap}}}$, never their sum, because both terms are positive. The script builds the state vector for every $1 \leq j \leq 6$ and $0 \leq w \leq j$ (radiation register of the $j$ quanta and the $j - w$ evicted partners, window register of the $w$ members, amplitudes in integers), forms $\rho_{R}$ by the exact partial trace, and confirms $\operatorname{tr}\rho_{R}^{2} = 2^{-w}$; it then runs a stage sequence with $n_{\mathrm{cap}} = 6 - j$ and confirms $\max(2^{-j}, 2^{-n_{\mathrm{cap}}})$ at each $j = 0, \ldots, 6$.

\emph{(iii).} For the unitarily invariant measure on unit vectors of $\C^{D}$, $D = mn$, the second moment is $\langle\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}^{\otimes 2}\rangle = (\mathbb{1} + F)/(D(D + 1))$, with $F$ the swap of the two copies (Schur's lemma on the symmetric square; the value is Lubkin's, Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-haar-average-purity}). Since $\operatorname{tr}\rho_{A}^{2} = \operatorname{tr}[(\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}^{\otimes 2})(F_{A} \otimes \mathbb{1})]$ with $F_{A}$ the swap of the two $A$ factors, $\langle\operatorname{tr}\rho_{A}^{2}\rangle = (\operatorname{tr}F_{A} + \operatorname{tr}FF_{A})/(D(D + 1))$. On the basis $\ket{a_{1}b_{1}a_{2}b_{2}}$, $F_{A}$ sends it to $\ket{a_{2}b_{1}a_{1}b_{2}}$ and $FF_{A}$ to $\ket{a_{1}b_{2}a_{2}b_{1}}$, so $\operatorname{tr}F_{A} = mn^{2}$ and $\operatorname{tr}FF_{A} = m^{2}n$, and $\langle\operatorname{tr}\rho_{A}^{2}\rangle = mn(m + n)/(mn(mn + 1)) = (m + n)/(mn + 1)$. The script counts the diagonal entries for six pairs $(m, n)$. Then $1/m + 1/n - (m + n)/(mn + 1) = (m + n)/(mn(mn + 1)) > 0$, and $(m + n)/(mn + 1) > 1/\min(m, n)$ because $\min(m, n)(m + n) > mn + 1$ for $m, n \geq 2$ (with $m \leq n$: $m^{2} + mn > mn + 1$).

\emph{(iv).} At $j = n_{\mathrm{cap}}$, (ii) gives $2^{-j} = 1/m$ with $m = 2^{j}$ and the two-saddle value $2/m$, ratio $2$, so $S_{2}$ differs by $\ln 2$; (iii) with $n = m$ gives $2m/(m^{2} + 1)$, whose ratio to $1/m$ is $2m^{2}/(m^{2} + 1) = 2 - 2/(m^{2} + 1) \in (2 - 2/m^{2}, 2)$, so its $S_{2}$ lies below the eviction value by $\ln 2 - \ln(1 + m^{-2})$, which is in $(\ln 2 - m^{-2}, \ln 2)$ since $0 < \ln(1 + u) < u$. Off the crossing the ratio of $2^{-j} + 2^{-n_{\mathrm{cap}}}$ to its larger term is $1 + 2^{-|j - n_{\mathrm{cap}}|}$. The script checks the ratios at $m = 2, 4, 8$ from the computed Haar purity (at $m = 16$ from the formula) and along the stage sequence of (ii).

\emph{(v).} By Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}(1), $S_{R}(t) = \sum_{k \in W(t)}h_{k}$, and $h_{k}$ is the von Neumann entropy of member $k$'s thermal marginal, so $S_{R}$ is the thermal entropy of the window with no correction term at any weight. The deficit of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.eviction-deficit-against-page-deficit} is $\delta_{\mathrm{ev}} = C - S_{R}$ with $C = |W(t)|\ln 2$, the maximal entropy of $|W(t)|$ two-level members; it equals the thermal entropy exactly when every $h_{k} = \ln 2$, that is at zero weight, where $h_{k} = s(0) = \ln 2$. Under the weights $\kappa(k + \tfrac12)$ every member has $|x_{k}| = (2k + 1)\pi \geq \pi$, so the stored clause (iii) applies with $x_{*} = \pi$ and its linear deficit is a deficit against $C$. The Page-typical branch of Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-page-average-entropy-subsystem} is compared against $\ln m$, the logarithm of the dimension, which for a window at zero weight is $C$. $\square$
