The radiation contains $R_k$ for every injected pair $k$, and the window contains $L_k$ for $k \in W(t)$ only. By the support constraint $R_k = L_k$ (Lemma \claimref{singularity.parity-protection}), two global configurations that agree on every radiation qubit agree on every window qubit, so tracing out the radiation leaves no off-diagonal entries: $\rho_L$ is diagonal, with entries the probabilities of the window configurations. A window configuration is a choice of $b_k \in \{0, 1\}$ for $k \in W(t)$, and its probability is $\prod_{k \in W(t)}(c^{(k)}_{b_k})^{2}$ times the sum over the remaining pairs' configurations, which is $1$; the controlled-$Z$ phases square away. So the distribution is the product of the members' thermal distributions, its Shannon entropy is $\sum_{k \in W(t)}H(c_k^{2})$, its collision probability is $\prod_{k \in W(t)}(c_k^{4} + s_k^{4})$, and $S_R = S_L$, $\tr\rho_R^{2} = \tr\rho_L^{2}$ by purity of the global state. The Phase I identity is the first two rows of the table; $C = |W(t)|\ln 2$ and $H(c_k^{2}) \leq \ln 2$ with equality only at $c_k = s_k$ give the Phase II clause; and $W = \varnothing$ at completion gives $0$. The script computes the exact spectrum of $\rho_L$ at every stage, verifies the entropy identity as an identity of formal logarithms, and decides the strict inequalities by exact rational bounds on the logarithms of the primes involved.
