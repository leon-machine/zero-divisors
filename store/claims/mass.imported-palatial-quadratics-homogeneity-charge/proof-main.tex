The symmetrised quadratics $q(M)$ in the palatial oscillators $Z^\alpha$, $\bar Z_\beta$ (twistor oscillators: the conformal spinor coordinates and their conjugates) represent $\mathfrak{su}(2,2)$ with $[q(M), q(M')] = \hbar\, q([M,M'])$, and, in that paper's realisation $\bar{Z}_{\beta} = -\hbar\partial_{\beta}$, the symmetrised identity quadratic $q(\mathrm{id})$ acts on functions of homogeneity $h$ as $-(h+2)\hbar$ (Penrose's $-2s-2$, proved in the series as an exact identity in the proof of that theorem); the lemma represents all of $\mathfrak{gl}(4,\C)$, of which $\mathfrak{su}(2,2)$ is the real form used here. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Lem.~5.5, Thm.~5.7]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.symmetrised-quadratics-palatial-algebra}, \claimref{correspondence.shared-core-ordering-dictionary}). Used here only in Remark \ref{rem:quantum}, to place the classical computation of Section \ref{sec:join} against its quantisation.
