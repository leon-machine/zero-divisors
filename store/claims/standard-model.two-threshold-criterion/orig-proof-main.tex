Subtracting the three one-loop equations pairwise gives $a_{1} - a_{2} = x(b_{12} + f\Dl_{12} + f'\Dl'_{12})$ and $a_{2} - a_{3} = x(b_{23} + f\Dl_{23} + f'\Dl'_{23})$; their ratio is $R$, which is the displayed line after clearing the denominator, and $x$ is then read from the first. The system has three equations in four unknowns, so its solution set is one-dimensional, and the constraints are the triangle and $x > 0$. The reductions are immediate substitutions, and a shift $\Dl \to \Dl + c(1,1,1)$ leaves $\Dl_{12}, \Dl_{23}$ unchanged. The script verifies the level-set identity on each of four families at six points of the line, $f'$ solved from $f$, where $r(f, f') = R$ exactly, and at the points shifted off the line by $\pm\tfrac14$ in $f'$, where it is not; the two reductions against the one-threshold closed form of Theorem \claimref{standard-model.one-threshold-exact-criterion} (the full content's $f = 1.0116$), and inertness at both thresholds for three values of $c$.
