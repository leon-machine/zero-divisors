Read in Gauge Theory Gravity: in the case of a gravitational field $(\hb, \Omega)$ on flat spacetime, the metric $g$ below is its derived metric on the invertible region, and the tractor connection is the flat-space $\Cl(2,4)$ gauge field of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.gtg-tractor-gauge-field-reproduces-tractor-connection}; the index computation below is its reading in the splitting of $g$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}); the argument is the same on a general conformal manifold.

\emph{The curvature on the Einstein region.} Work in the scale $g$ of $I$, with the connection of Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-frozen-point-geometry},
\[
\nabla_a(\sigma, \mu_b, \rho) = \bigl(\nabla_a\sigma - \mu_a,\ \nabla_a\mu_b + g_{ab}\rho - P_{ab}\sigma,\ \nabla_a\rho + P_a{}^b\mu_b\bigr), \qquad P_{ab} = \tfrac{\Lambda}{6} g_{ab},
\]
and $I = (1, 0, \Lambda/6)$, which is parallel (for every $\Lambda$, including $\Lambda = 0$, where $I$ is null, $g$ is Ricci flat and $P = 0$): $(0 - 0,\ 0 + \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}g_{ab} - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}g_{ab},\ 0) = 0$. Apply $\nabla_c$ to $\nabla_a(\sigma,\mu,\rho)$ (Levi-Civita on the form index, which drops out on antisymmetrising because it is torsion free) and antisymmetrise in $c, a$. Top slot: $[\nabla_c, \nabla_a]\sigma - (\nabla_c\mu_a - \nabla_a\mu_c) - (\nabla_a\mu_c - \nabla_c\mu_a) - (g_{ac} - g_{ca})\rho + (P_{ac} - P_{ca})\sigma = 0$. Middle slot: the $\rho$-derivative terms cancel in pairs, the $\sigma$-derivative terms cancel in pairs, the term $-(\nabla_c P_{ab} - \nabla_a P_{cb})\sigma$ is the Cotton tensor, zero since $P$ is a constant multiple of $g$, and what remains is $[\nabla_c, \nabla_a]\mu_b + (g_{cb}P_a{}^d - g_{ab}P_c{}^d + P_{cb}\delta_a^d - P_{ab}\delta_c^d)\mu_d$, the Riemann tensor acting on $\mu$ corrected by the Kulkarni-Nomizu product of $P$ and $g$; with $R_{cabd} = W_{cabd} + \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}(g_{cb}g_{ad} - g_{cd}g_{ab})$ on an Einstein space ($P = \tfrac{\Lambda}{6} g$, so the Kulkarni-Nomizu product of $P$ and $g$ is the constant-curvature part) the correction cancels the constant-curvature part and the middle slot is $\pm W_{ca b}{}^{d}\mu_d$ (the overall sign depends on the curvature convention and plays no role below). Bottom slot: the $\mu$-derivative terms cancel in pairs, $(\nabla_c P_a{}^b - \nabla_a P_c{}^b)\mu_b$ is again a Cotton term and vanishes, $(P_{ca} - P_{ac})\rho = 0$ and $(P_c{}^bP_{ab} - P_a{}^bP_{cb})\sigma = 0$ since $P \propto g$. So the curvature is $\Omega_{ca}(\sigma, \mu, \rho) = (0, \pm W_{cab}{}^d \mu_d, 0)$.

\emph{Kernel.} If $I'$ is parallel then $\Omega_{ca} I' = 0$ for all $c, a$, so its middle slot $\mu$ satisfies $W_{cabd}\mu^d = 0$. The condition that $W$ has no kernel vector is open (it says that the linear map $v \mapsto W_{cab d}v^d$ has rank four, and rank is lower semicontinuous), so it holds on a neighbourhood $U$ of $x_0$, which we take connected and inside the Einstein region, and there $\mu \equiv 0$. The parallel transport equations on $U$ then read $\nabla_a\sigma = 0$, $g_{ab}\rho - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}g_{ab}\sigma = 0$, $\nabla_a\rho = 0$: so $\sigma = c$ is constant and $\rho = \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}c$, that is $I' = cI$ on $U$. The difference $I' - cI$ is parallel and vanishes on $U$; parallel sections of a bundle with connection on a connected manifold are determined by their value at one point, so $I' = cI$ on $M$.

\emph{Consequences.} The Einstein scale of a parallel tractor is its top slot in any scale (the almost-Einstein correspondence recorded in the cited Fact), so $\sigma' = c\sigma$, the metrics are $g' = \sigma'^{-2}g_{\text{ref}} = c^{-2} g$, their zero sets coincide, and $Q(I') = c^2Q(I) = c^2\Lambda/3$. For the reversed signature the argument applies verbatim: the class $[-g]$ has the same Levi-Civita connection and the same Ricci tensor, its Weyl tensor with lowered indices is the negative of that of $g$ (so it has the same kernel vectors), and in an Einstein scale its tractor connection has the same form with the same conclusion. For the towers, apply the statement to each $I_r$ with $I = I_{r_0}$ for one tower; $c_r = \pm 1$ is equivalent to $Q(I_r) = Q(I)$, and then all towers' metrics coincide (the sign of $c_r$ drops out of $\sigma^{-2}$).

\emph{Examples.} At a point of a Schwarzschild or Schwarzschild-de Sitter exterior, in a static orthonormal frame, the Weyl tensor is purely electric with tidal tensor $E = (M/r^3)\operatorname{diag}(-2,1,1)$ (standard, cited from memory; the cosmological constant enters only the Ricci part, so the Schwarzschild case $\Lambda = 0$ and the Schwarzschild-de Sitter case $\Lambda > 0$ have the same Weyl tensor). The check assembles $W$ from $E$ with $B = 0$, confirms the Weyl symmetries, and finds that $v \mapsto W_{abcd}v^d$ has rank four: no kernel vector, for $M/r^3 = 1$ and $2/5$. It finds the common kernel of the six $\Omega_{ab}$ on the tractor fibre to be two-dimensional, spanned by the $\sigma$ and $\rho$ slots, as the argument requires. For a type N Weyl tensor (transverse traceless $E$ and $B$ with the null direction $(1,1,0,0)$) it confirms that this null vector is a kernel vector and the common kernel is three-dimensional; for $W = 0$ it is six-dimensional, the case of vanishing Weyl part (conformally flat derived metric) in which parallel tractors are not unique. The algebraic step on $U$ is checked as a linear system at $\Lambda = 3/7$ and at $\Lambda = 0$: the middle slot equation has rank one with kernel $(1, \Lambda/6)$.
