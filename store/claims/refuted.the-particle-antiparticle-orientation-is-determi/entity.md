## formal
The particle-antiparticle orientation is determined by the charge-zero condition

## technical
The refuted expectation is that the charge-zero condition fixes the one absolute bit the Standard-Model derivation left open, the particle-antiparticle orientation. A $\Z_{2}$ covariance argument on three premises (CPT conjugation is an isomorphism of every kinematic structure, Hypothesis (S), and, for the last step, the trichotomy's exhaustiveness) shows that whatever the structures determine is conjugation-invariant, while the orientation is not. The charge-zero condition fixes all relative structure; the absolute bit is initial data, and it is the Witt flag, the same choice as the arrow of time.

## lay
The series' derivation of the Standard Model left one yes-or-no choice open: which member of each pair counts as the particle and which as the antiparticle. It had been expected that a neutrality condition would settle it. The paper shows that it cannot, because the relevant laws treat both choices alike, so the choice is part of the universe's starting conditions and is the same choice as the direction of time's arrow.

## one-liner
Refuted: the charge-zero condition cannot fix the particle-antiparticle orientation, which a CPT covariance argument on three premises shows to be initial data, identified with the Witt flag and the arrow of time.
