## formal
Fix a basis $\rho_1, \dots, \rho_r$ of $R$. In $\Cl(p,q,r)$ the radical generates the two-sided ideal $J = \sum_i \Cl \cdot \rho_i$, with
\[
\dim J = 2^m - 2^{m-r}, \qquad J^{\,r+1} = 0, \qquad J^{\,r} \neq 0 ;
\]
in particular $J^2 = 0$ precisely when $r = 1$, the corank-one case established at sheaf level in \cite[Thm.~3.1]{ButlerT4}. In any completion, the image of the radical generates the \emph{unit} ideal of the ambient Clifford algebra, and each radical basis vector spans a hyperbolic plane with a partner vector of the completion.

## technical
Fix a basis $\rho_1,\dots,\rho_r$ of the radical $R$. In $\Cl(p,q,r)$ the radical generates the two-sided ideal $J = \sum_i \Cl\cdot\rho_i$, spanned by the blades that contain at least one radical letter, so $\dim J = 2^m - 2^{m-r}$. Radical letters have $Q(\rho_i) = 0$, anticommute with every vector and are never contracted away, so $J^{r+1} = 0$, while the nonzero blade $\rho_1\cdots\rho_r$ shows $J^r \neq 0$; in particular $J^2 = 0$ exactly when $r = 1$.

In any completion each $\rho_i$ has a partner $\varphi_i$ with $Q(\rho_i,\varphi_i) = 1$, which can be chosen null, so $\operatorname{span}\{\rho_i,\varphi_i\}$ is a hyperbolic plane. The Clifford relation $\rho_i\varphi_i + \varphi_i\rho_i = 2$ then places $1$ in the ideal generated by $\rho_i$: the image of the radical generates the unit ideal of the ambient algebra.

## lay
In a degenerate geometric algebra the degenerate directions generate a family of elements that multiply to zero once enough of them are combined, the number needed being one more than the number of degenerate directions. None of these elements can be inverted, and no enlargement changes that. Once the space is completed, however, each degenerate direction gains a partner, and the two together produce the number one, so the obstruction disappears at the level of the whole algebra rather than by giving any single element an inverse.

## one-liner
In $\Cl(p,q,r)$ the radical generates a nilpotent ideal of dimension $2^m - 2^{m-r}$ and nilpotency index exactly $r+1$, and in any nondegenerate completion it generates the unit ideal, each radical basis vector spanning a hyperbolic plane with a partner.
