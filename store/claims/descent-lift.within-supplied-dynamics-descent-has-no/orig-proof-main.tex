A Hermitian quadratic element is $X = \sum_{i,j} h_{ij}a^{\dagger}_{i}a_{j} + \sum_{i<j} (p_{ij}a^{\dagger}_{i}a^{\dagger}_{j} + \bar{p}_{ij}a_{j}a_{i}) + \lambda$, and $X\ket{0} = \sum_{i<j} p_{ij}a^{\dagger}_{i}a^{\dagger}_{j}\ket{0} + \lambda\ket{0}$ with the states $a^{\dagger}_{i}a^{\dagger}_{j}\ket{0}$ independent; if $X$ stabilises the vacuum ray then $p = 0$, and $X$ is a combination of hops and Cartan elements, which conserve the total number. (The stabiliser of the ray in the complexified algebra also contains the pair annihilations, which are not Hermitian; the script checks that the Hermitian element $a_{2}a_{3} + a^{\dagger}_{3}a^{\dagger}_{2}$ built from one carries the vacuum to $\ket{11}$.) Every admissible generator therefore conserves the total number, since the supplied class is joint-number-diagonal, and Theorem \claimref{descent-lift.lift-costs-one-rung-per-mode}(v) excludes a lift; the same theorem gives the necessity clause. Adding the pairing rotation gives Theorem \claimref{descent-lift.lift-costs-one-rung-per-mode}(iii) and (iv). The restriction to quadratic elements is needed: the quartic element $iN_{0}(a^{\dagger}_{2}a^{\dagger}_{3} - a_{3}a_{2})$ is Hermitian, even, and kills the joint vacuum, but does not conserve the total number, and its rotation at $\pi/2$ is the unitary $(1 - N_{0}) + N_{0}(P^{R}_{\mathrm{odd}} + a^{\dagger}_{2}a^{\dagger}_{3} - a_{3}a_{2})$, which carries $\psi \otimes \ket{00}$ to the two-rung occupation copy of Corollary \claimref{descent-lift.parity-alone-forces-second-rung}, a lift. It is a pairing on the rungs conditioned on the system's occupation; the script checks each of these statements, the unitary being entered in closed form. What every lift needs is the non-conservation of number; among quadratic generators that is a pairing.
