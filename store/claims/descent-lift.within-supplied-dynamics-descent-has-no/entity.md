## formal
Let the admissible generators on the system and any number of rungs be the Hermitian elements of the singularity paper's supplied class together with the Hermitian quadratic elements, in $\mathfrak{so}(2(\nu + k))$, that stabilise the joint vacuum ray: all hops and Cartan elements, on the system, on the rungs and between them. Then no unitary generated by admissible elements realises a lift of the descent from the rungs' vacuum. A lift exists in the ascent by $\nu$ rungs when a pairing rotation is added to the admissible generators, and never without a generator that fails to conserve the total number; the lift of Theorem \claimref{descent-lift.lift-costs-one-rung-per-mode}(iv) places the pairing on the rungs.

## technical
Take as admissible generators, on the system and any number of rungs, the Hermitian elements of the supplied class together with the Hermitian quadratic elements of $\mathfrak{so}(2(\nu + k))$ that stabilise the joint vacuum ray, namely all hops and Cartan elements. A Hermitian quadratic element that stabilises the vacuum ray has zero pairing part, and the supplied class is joint-number-diagonal, so every admissible generator conserves total number.

Since a lift needs a generator that does not conserve number, no unitary generated by admissible elements realises a lift of the descent from the rungs' vacuum. Adding a pairing rotation gives a lift in the ascent by $\nu$ rungs, with the pairing on the rungs, and no lift exists without some generator that fails to conserve total number.

## lay
The framework supplies a particular set of allowed dynamics, all of which conserve particle number. Within that set, and starting from empty rungs, the information the descent discards can never be stored reversibly. Allowing one extra ingredient, a rotation that creates pairs of particles, makes it possible, so the one-way nature of the descent and the exclusion of pair creation are two views of one fact.

## one-liner
With only supplied-class generators and vacuum-stabilising quadratic generators the descent has no lift, and every lift needs a generator that fails to conserve total number.
