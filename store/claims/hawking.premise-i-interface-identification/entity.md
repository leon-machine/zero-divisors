## formal
The horizon-sector state feeds the exterior modes in the standard way, i.e.\ the occupancy of each near-horizon channel is carried to the corresponding asymptotic channel by the exterior propagation.

## technical
Premise (I) states that the horizon-sector state feeds the exterior modes in the standard way: the occupancy of each near-horizon channel is carried by the exterior propagation to the corresponding asymptotic channel. It is the interface between the state on the horizon algebra and the propagation through the exterior.

## lay
This premise assumes that whatever the horizon state puts into each outgoing channel is carried out to distant observers in the ordinary way. It is an assumption, not a theorem of the framework.
