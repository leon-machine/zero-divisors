\emph{The norm inequality.} Write $\lVert\cdot\rVert$ for the operator norm; for a complex symmetric matrix it is the largest Takagi value, and $\lVert M_{R}^{-1}\rVert = 1/\alpha$ with $\alpha$ the smallest Takagi value of $M_{R}$ (in the Takagi form $M_{R} = U^{\mathsf{T}}DU$, $U$ unitary, $D$ diagonal non-negative, $M_{R}^{-1} = U^{-1}D^{-1}U^{-\mathsf{T}}$ and unitaries preserve the norm). Submultiplicativity and $\lVert M_{D}^{\mathsf{T}}\rVert = \lVert M_{D}\rVert$ give $m_{3} = \lVert m_{\nu}\rVert \le \lVert M_{D}\rVert^{2}/\alpha$. The operator norm is at most the Frobenius norm, and a $3 \times 3$ matrix with entries of modulus at most $e$ has Frobenius norm at most $3e$. Hence $\alpha \le 9e^{2}/m_{3}$. A rank-one Dirac matrix with a single entry in the row of the lightest heavy state attains $\lVert m_{\nu}\rVert = \lVert M_{D}\rVert^{2}/\alpha$.

\emph{The untuned fit.} Proposition \claimref{standard-model.fits-record-level} lists representative fits under Premise (EW); for the fit with $10$s, $126$ and $120$ in which the second family's factor three comes through mixing with the third and nothing cancels, a largest Dirac neutrino entry of $0.04$ GeV at $M_{I}$, with $m_{3}$ run to $M_{I}$ in the Standard Model. We take $e \le 0.045$ GeV (the rounding) and, rather than compute the running, allow $m_{3}(M_{I}) \ge 25$ meV, half the record's lower end $49.4$ meV. Then $\alpha \le 9e^{2}/m_{3} = 7.29 \times 10^{8}$ GeV, and $M_{I}/\alpha > 26$ for $M_{I} = 1.9 \times 10^{10}$ GeV, the two-loop intermediate scale of the chain with the $(15,2,2)$ (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.chain}(4)).

\emph{The seeding requirement.} With rate per volume $c\,T^{8}/M_{P}^{4}$ (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-massless-graviton-production-rate}, $M_{P} = 2.435 \times 10^{18}$ GeV), entropy density $s = (2\pi^{2}/45)g_{s}T^{3}$ and $H = \sqrt{\pi^{2}g_{*}/90}\,T^{2}/M_{P}$ in radiation domination, the comoving yield obeys $dY/dT = -c\,T^{8}M_{P}^{-4}/(sHT)$, so $Y = c\,T_{\mathrm{desc}}^{3}/(3\kappa_{s}M_{P}^{3})$ with $\kappa_{s} = \sqrt{\pi^{2}g_{*}/90}\,(2\pi^{2}/45)g_{s}$, which at $g_{*} = g_{s} = 100$ lies in $(145.2, 145.3)$ for $\pi > 333/106$; this is the yield of T14's Appendix A. Allowing the factor $2$ on the yield that the rate's provenance absorbs, and quanta of mass $m \le T_{\mathrm{desc}}$, the mass per unit entropy of the relics is at most $2c\,T_{\mathrm{desc}}^{4}/(3\kappa_{s}M_{P}^{3})$. The remainder, $\Omega_{c}h^{2} - \Omega_{b}h^{2} \ge 0.1188 - 0.02252 = 0.09628$ (Planck 2018, one standard error each), has mass per unit entropy $(\Omega h^{2})(\rho_{c}/h^{2})/s_{0} > 3.508 \times 10^{-10}$ GeV. So $c\,T_{\mathrm{desc}}^{4} \ge A/2$ with $A = 3\kappa_{s}M_{P}^{3} \times 3.508 \times 10^{-10}$ GeV, evaluated with $\kappa_{s} = 145.2$: $A > 2.2068 \times 10^{48}$ GeV$^{4}$. With $T_{\mathrm{desc}} \le \alpha$ (Premise \claimref{neutrino.r-descent-reheats-below-lower-heavy}, $\alpha$ read as the fit's lightest heavy mass) this needs $c \ge A/(2\alpha^{4}) > 3.9 \times 10^{12}$, and $A/2 > (1400\,\alpha)^{4}$, so at $c \le 1$ the required temperature exceeds the cap more than $1400$-fold.

\emph{The tuned rows.} With entries $2.2$, $2.9$, $4.0$ GeV and $m_{3} = 49.4$ meV the bound gives $\alpha < 8.9$, $15.4$, $29.2 \times 10^{11}$ GeV; with the running allowance each fourth power exceeds $A/2$, so the crude bound does not decide those rows even at $c = 1$.

The check verifies the trace form of the inequality on exact instances (for real diagonal $M_{R}$ and integer $M_{D}$, $\mathrm{tr}\,m_{\nu} \le \lVert M_{D}\rVert_{F}^{2}/\alpha$, with equality exactly on the rank-one instances), $\kappa_{s}$ from its formula, and every number above in rational arithmetic.
