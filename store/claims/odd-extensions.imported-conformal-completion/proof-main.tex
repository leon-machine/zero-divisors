A shadow of signature $(t,s)$ has conformal completion with ambient of signature $(t+1, s+1)$, one rung up its own tower; for this world the ambient is $(2,4)$ and its conformal spin group acts on the conformal spinor space $\CS \cong \C^{4}$ (twistor space), whose complex unit is $I_6$, through $\Cl^{0}(2,4)$. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Fact~2.1, Thm.~3.4]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.imported-forced-completion-fibre-quadric}, \claimref{correspondence.spinor-identification}); \cite[Fact~2.11]{ButlerT12} (\claimref{standard-model.imported-fock-representation-clifford-algebra-bogoliubov}).
