## formal
The quanta of every populated tower carry the same mass $m$.

## technical
The premise sets the mass of the quanta of every populated tower to one common value $m$. It is one of three premises added to the sourcing rule to state the unit-ball rule for the dark-to-baryon ratio, and the series does not supply it.

## lay
This assumption says the basic particles in every hidden sector weigh the same. The paper needs it for its formula for the amount of dark matter and does not derive it.
