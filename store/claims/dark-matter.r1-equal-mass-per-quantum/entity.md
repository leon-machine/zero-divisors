## formal
The quanta of every populated tower carry the same mass $m$.

## technical
The premise sets the mass of the quanta of every populated tower to one common value $m$. With R2 it implies Premise E, equal mass per unit ball, which is what the dark-to-baryon total uses; since the decision of 19 November 2026 the total rests on E and not on R1, and R1 with R2 is one model of E, the particle model. R1 remains a premise of the stored results that use a quantum's mass by itself. The series does not supply it.

## lay
This assumption says the basic particles in every hidden sector weigh the same. The formula for the amount of dark matter was first stated with it, and no longer needs it: the formula needs only that every sector holds the same mass per unit ball, whatever its particles weigh. The assumption is kept for the results that do use a particle's mass, and it is not derived.

## one-liner
Premise: the quanta of every populated tower carry the same mass $m$.
