The weights are commutators with $y$ and $2T_{3}$: $[y, v_{a}] = +3v_{a}$ for $a = 4, 5$ and $-2v_{a}$ for $a \le 3$, with the opposite signs on the $w_{a}$, so $Y = \pm\tfrac12$ on rung vectors and $\mp\tfrac13$ on pair vectors; $[2T_{3}, v_{4}] = v_{4}$, $[2T_{3}, v_{5}] = -v_{5}$, and $2T_{3}$ annihilates every pair vector; the $\su(3)$ generator $v_{1}w_{2}$ carries $v_{2}$ to $v_{1}$ and $w_{1}$ to $-w_{2}$ and annihilates every rung vector. The script checks each commutator exactly, and lists the one-mode states as $(1,0)$ twice with $Y = \tfrac12$ and $(0,1)$ three times with $Y = -\tfrac13$, the $5$. That the mode vectors span the complexified ambient space of $\Cl(2{+}2, 4{+}2)$ is Facts \claimref{standard-model.imported-tower-commuting-copies-fock-module} and \claimref{standard-model.imported-fock-model-twistor-space}: the pair vectors span $V_{\C} = W \oplus \overline{W}$ and the rung vectors the two adjoined hyperbolic planes.
