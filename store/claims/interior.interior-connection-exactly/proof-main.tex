The computation below is written for the derived metric of the interior gravitational field; by Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}(ii) its Levi-Civita connection forms, evaluated on the frame and assembled into bivectors, are the rotation-gauge field $\Omega$ on the physical frame vectors, so it computes $\Omega$ directly. With orthonormal coframe $\theta^0 = d\tau$, $\theta^i = a_i dx^i$, dual to the frame of \claimref{interior.exact-parallelism-convergence-rate}, the first structure equation $d\theta^a = -\omega^a{}_b \wedge \theta^b$ determines the connection uniquely (torsion-free, metric). Computing: $d\theta^1 = (\partial_\tau a_1)\,d\tau \wedge dt = \sigma_1\,\theta^0 \wedge \theta^1$, and likewise for $\theta^2$; while $d\theta^3 = \partial_\tau(a_3)\,d\tau\wedge d\varphi\,+\,\partial_\theta(r\sin\theta)\,d\theta \wedge d\varphi = \sigma_3\,\theta^0\wedge\theta^3 + \frac{\cot\theta}{r}\,\theta^2 \wedge \theta^3$. Matching term by term gives $\omega^i{}_0 = \sigma_i\,\theta^i$ (no sum) and $\omega^3{}_2 = \frac{\cot\theta}{r}\,\theta^3$, with all other independent components zero; uniqueness of the Levi-Civita connection closes the argument. Evaluating on the unit vectors and assembling into bivectors in the sign of the Definition ($D_a M = a \cdot \nabla M + \Omega(a) \times M$: the form $\omega^i{}_0 = \sigma_i\,\theta^i$ gives the boost term $-\sigma_i B_i$, and $\omega^3{}_2$ gives $+\frac{\cot\theta}{r}R_{23}$) gives the display. The last sentence is the standard statement that the induced connection on the constant-$\tau$ spheres is that of $S^2$ of radius $r$, whose coefficients in an orthonormal frame scale as $1/r$; the $\cot\theta$ divergence at the poles is an artifact of the spherical frame, removable by a change of patch.
