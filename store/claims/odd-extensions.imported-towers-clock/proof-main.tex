The $(1,1)$ ascent $\Cl(p,q) \to \Cl(p+1,q+1) \cong \Mat(2, \Cl(p,q))$ preserves $p - q$; the real Clifford algebras are periodic modulo eight; the eight residues label eight parallel towers. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thms.~3.2 and 3.6]{ButlerT3} (\claimref{tower.rigidity-clock-frozen}, \claimref{tower.commuting-copies}); \cite[Fact~2.5]{ButlerT14} (\claimref{dark-matter.imported-eight-towers-mod-8-clock}).
