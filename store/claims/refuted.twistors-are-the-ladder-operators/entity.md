## formal
Conformal spinors (twistors) are the ladder operators

## technical
Refuted. The corrected statement: conformal spinors are the states of three ladder pairs: the two-level architecture.

## lay
This entry records a claim that the programme rejects: that the four-component spinors of the conformal algebra are themselves the ladder (creation and annihilation) operators of the completed algebra. The result that replaces it shows that these spinors are states instead. Each of them is the vacuum of its own three ladder pairs, and the ambient vectors are the operators that act on it.
