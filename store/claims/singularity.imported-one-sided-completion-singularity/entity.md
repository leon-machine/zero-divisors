## formal
Adjoin the trivial hyperbolic plane $(\nO, \nInf)$ to $\shW$ and couple it to the dying direction by the coupling construction of \cite[Def.~4.1]{ButlerT4}, whose standing hypothesis $w_1(\shL) = 0$ (the dying line is orientable) is met here, the radical line being trivialised globally by $k = d\Phi$ (clause (5) of Fact \claimref{sheaf.schwarzschild-degeneration-boundary-tame}), taking the normalised transversal $u = \partial_t/m$ (so that $k(u) = 1$, as that construction requires), coupling constants satisfying $\mu\nu < 0$, and bump supported inside the horizon. Then the ambient form $h$ is nondegenerate of constant signature $(2,4)$ at every point of $\Mt$, the boundary $E$ included; the subalgebra generated by $\shW$ is canonically the degeneration sheaf; and the ladder pair
\[
k^* = \frac{\nO}{2\nu}, \qquad k k^* + k^* k = 1, \qquad k^2 = (k^*)^2 = 0
\]
exists exactly over $E$. The ascent $\Cl(1,3) \to \Cl(2,4)$, i.e.\ $\Mat_2(\HH) \to \Mat_4(\HH)$, is thereby performed at the Schwarzschild singularity, localised on $E \cong \R \times S^2$. Moreover no shadow wall forms anywhere in the manifold.
\emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thm.~8.12]{ButlerT4}, verbatim up to the recorded notational alignment, with one clause added here: the parenthetical $w_1(\shL) = 0$ and its discharge by $k = d\Phi$ are not in the source's statement, which invokes \cite[Def.~4.1]{ButlerT4} with that hypothesis standing; the clause records that the hypothesis holds on the Schwarzschild data, by clause (5) of Fact \claimref{sheaf.schwarzschild-degeneration-boundary-tame}, so that the import is applied within its hypotheses.

## technical
On the blown-up Schwarzschild interior, adjoin a trivial hyperbolic plane $(\nO, \nInf)$ to $\shW$ and couple it to the dying direction by the coupling construction, with the normalised transversal $u = \partial_t/m$ (so $k(u) = 1$), coupling constants with $\mu\nu < 0$, and a bump supported inside the horizon. The construction's standing hypothesis $w_1(\shL) = 0$ holds, since the radical line is trivialised globally by $k = d\Phi$.

Then the ambient form $h$ is nondegenerate of constant signature $(2,4)$ at every point of $\Mt$, the boundary $E$ included; the subalgebra generated by $\shW$ is canonically the degeneration sheaf; and the ladder pair $k^* = \nO/2\nu$, with $kk^* + k^*k = 1$ and $Q(k) = Q(k^*) = 0$, exists exactly over $E$. This performs the ascent $\Cl(1,3) \to \Cl(2,4)$ at the singularity, localised on $E \cong \R \times S^2$, and no shadow wall forms anywhere in the manifold.

## lay
The centre of a non-rotating black hole is where the geometry loses a direction. This result, carried over from the sheaf paper, adds two extra directions and couples them to the lost one, with couplings of opposite sign switched on only inside the horizon, so that the enlarged geometry is non-degenerate everywhere, the centre included. The new partner for the lost direction exists exactly at the centre, and the repair creates no new defect anywhere else.

## one-liner
Coupling a hyperbolic plane to the dying direction, with $\mu\nu < 0$ and a bump inside the horizon, completes the Schwarzschild degeneration to a form of signature $(2,4)$ on the whole blown-up manifold, with the ladder pair supported exactly on the singularity and no shadow wall.
