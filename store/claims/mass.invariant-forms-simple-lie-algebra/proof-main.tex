Let $B_{1}$ be the Killing form, nondegenerate by Cartan's criterion \cite[\S5.1]{Humphreys1972}, and $B_{2}$ invariant. Then $B_{2}(A, \cdot) = B_{1}(\phi A, \cdot)$ defines a linear $\phi$, and invariance of both forms makes $\phi$ commute with the adjoint action; the adjoint representation of a simple algebra is irreducible, so $\phi$ is a scalar by Schur's lemma \cite[\S6.1]{Humphreys1972}.
