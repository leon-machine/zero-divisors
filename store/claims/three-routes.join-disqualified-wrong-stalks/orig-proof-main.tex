(i) For $y \neq x$ the odd generator $k(y)$ is fixed by $\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma(x))$, since the two points' ladders commute up to the grading and $\sigma(x)$ acts trivially on the $y$-factor's occupation-preserving part; concretely, in the joint representation $\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma_x)(v_y) = v_y$. So $J(U)$ contains every generator $k(y)$, $k^{*}(y)$ for every $y \in U$ (each lies in the fixed algebra of any \emph{other} point), and these generate the ledger. (ii) The element $v_y \in J(U)$ is odd at $y$: $\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma_y)(v_y) = -v_y$. A sheaf on $\Sig$ with stalks $C^\infty_x \otimes \Aa_x^{+}$ has every section even at every point of its domain, and $v_y$ is not; so no restriction structure can present $J$ as such a sheaf's sections. The script verifies both computations on two modes: $\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma_x)$ fixes $v_y$, $\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma_y)$ negates it.
