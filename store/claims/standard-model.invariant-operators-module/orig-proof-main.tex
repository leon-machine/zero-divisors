The Cartan subalgebra of $\mathfrak{s}(\uu(2) \oplus \uu(3))$ is the traceless diagonal matrices on $\C^{2} \oplus \C^{3}$, so the weight of an occupation state is its five occupation numbers modulo $(1,1,1,1,1)$; the only two states with a common weight are the vacuum and the filled state, both of weight zero. An operator commuting with the Cartan is block-diagonal in weights, and the script imposes commutation with the ten off-diagonal generators $v_{a}w_{b}$, $a \ne b$, on such operators by exact sparse elimination, finding a solution space of dimension $14 = 10 \cdot 1^{2} + 1 \cdot 2^{2}$ on the module and $6$ on the even half, where the filled state, of odd total mode number, is absent. The chirality of the two singlets is the parity of $j + k$, $0$ and $5$. The last sentence is the content of ``commutant'': the six scalars are free parameters of any invariant operator, and nothing in Facts \claimref{standard-model.imported-tower-commuting-copies-fock-module} to \claimref{standard-model.imported-grip-area-casimir-form} or in Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor} constrains them.
