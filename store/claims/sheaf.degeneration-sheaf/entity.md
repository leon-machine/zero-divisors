## formal
Let $(M, G)$ be a tame corank-one degeneration (Hypotheses \claimref{sheaf.tameness}), $\shA = \Cl(T^*M, G)$, and write $A(x)$ for the fibre at $x$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Over $\Mo$, each fibre $A(x)$ is isomorphic to the nondegenerate Clifford algebra of the local signature; on the interior of $\Mp$ this is $\Cl(p,q)$, on the far side $\Cl(p{-}1,q{+}1)$. In the physical case these are $\Cl(1,3) \cong \Mat_2(\HH)$ and $\Cl(0,4) \cong \Mat_2(\HH)$.
\item Over $x \in \Sig$, the two-sided ideal $J(x)$ of $A(x)$ generated by the radical line $\shL(x)$ is
\[
J(x) \;=\; A(x)\,k \;=\; k\,A(x), \qquad J(x)^2 = 0, \qquad \dim_\R J(x) = 2^{n-1},
\]
for any spanning $k \in \shL(x)$, and the quotient is canonically the Clifford algebra of the induced form:
\[
A(x)/J(x) \;\cong\; \Cl\bigl(T^*_xM / \shL(x),\ \overline{G}_x\bigr),
\]
where $\overline{G}_x$ is nondegenerate of rank $n-1$ and signature $(p{-}1, q)$.
\item The fibrewise ideals assemble: the multiplication map $\shA|_\Sig \otimes \shL \to \shA|_\Sig$ has image a smooth subbundle $\shA|_\Sig \cdot \shL$ of rank $2^{n-1}$, and its pushforward
\[
\shJ \;:=\; i_*\bigl(\shA|_\Sig \cdot \shL\bigr) \qquad (i : \Sig \hookrightarrow M)
\]
is a finitely presented sheaf of $\mathcal{O}_M$-modules and of $\shA$-bimodules, supported exactly on $\Sig$, with fibre $J(x)$ at $x \in \Sig$ and fibre $0$ elsewhere. The associated ideal sheaf of $\shA$ is the kernel
\[
\shK \;:=\; \ker\bigl(\shA \longrightarrow i_*\bigl(\shA|_\Sig \,/\, \shA|_\Sig \cdot \shL\bigr)\bigr),
\]
a sheaf of two-sided ideals of $\shA$ whose restriction to $\Sig$ realises $J(x)$ fibrewise and whose quotient $\shA/\shK$ is the pushforward of the quotient bundle of (2).
\end{enumerate}

## technical
Assume $(M, G)$ is a corank-one degeneration satisfying the tameness hypotheses, with wall $\Sig$ and $\shA = \Cl(T^*M, G)$. Off the wall each fibre is the nondegenerate Clifford algebra of the local signature: $\Cl(p,q)$ on the physical side and $\Cl(p{-}1,q{+}1)$ beyond the wall, both $\Mat_2(\HH)$ in the physical case. At $x \in \Sig$ a spanning radical covector $k$ anticommutes with every covector, so $k\,a = \hat a\,k$ for the grade involution, and the two-sided ideal it generates is $J(x) = A(x)\,k = k\,A(x)$, with $J(x)^2 = 0$ and $\dim_\R J(x) = 2^{n-1}$. The quotient is canonically $A(x)/J(x) \cong \Cl\bigl(T^*_xM/\shL(x), \overline{G}_x\bigr)$, nondegenerate of rank $n-1$ and signature $(p{-}1, q)$.

These fibrewise ideals form a smooth subbundle of rank $2^{n-1}$ over $\Sig$. Its pushforward $\shJ$ is a finitely presented sheaf of $\mathcal{O}_M$-modules and of $\shA$-bimodules supported exactly on $\Sig$, and the kernel $\shK$ of restriction to $\Sig$ followed by projection is a sheaf of two-sided ideals of $\shA$ realising $J(x)$ fibrewise on $\Sig$, with quotient $\shA/\shK$ the pushforward of the quotient bundle.

## lay
Where the geometry degenerates along a wall, assuming it does so in the controlled way the paper calls tame (one direction losing its length, transversally, along a smooth wall), the algebra attached to each point changes character. Away from the wall it is an ordinary Clifford algebra. On the wall, the direction that has lost its length generates a part of the algebra whose products all vanish, an infinitesimal part of exactly half the size, and removing it leaves a smaller ordinary Clifford algebra. These parts fit together smoothly along the wall into a well-behaved algebraic object living only on the wall.

## one-liner
For a corank-one degeneration satisfying the tameness hypotheses, the Clifford fibre at each wall point carries a canonical square-zero ideal of dimension $2^{n-1}$ generated by the radical, with quotient the Clifford algebra of the induced nondegenerate form, and these ideals assemble into sheaves supported exactly on the wall.
