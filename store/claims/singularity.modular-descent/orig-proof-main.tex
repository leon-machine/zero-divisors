The pair algebra is the $2 \times 2$ factor generated by $(v, v^*)$; its commutant in the doubled system is the partner copy, and hypotheses (i) and (iii) say exactly that the state vector is cyclic and separating for the pair factor. By Lemma \claimref{singularity.schmidt-forcing-proved-outright}, hypothesis (ii) forces
\[
|\Omega\rangle = \cos\theta\,|00\rangle + \sin\theta\,|11\rangle, \qquad \cos\theta\sin\theta \neq 0 ,
\]
and by Lemma \claimref{singularity.modular-theory-matrix-factor-proved-outright} (with $n = 2$, $\lambda_0 = \cos\theta$, $\lambda_1 = \sin\theta$) the modular operator of $|\Omega\rangle$ is $\Delta = \rho \otimes \bar\rho^{\,-1}$ with $\rho = \operatorname{diag}(\cos^2\theta, \sin^2\theta)$ and modular flow $\sigma_t = \operatorname{Ad}(\rho^{it})$ on the pair factor, KMS identity included. So far the state is Gibbs for $K$ at an inverse temperature $\beta$ determined by $\tan^{2}\theta = e^{-\beta}$, and nothing in (i) to (iii) constrains $\theta$: this is the residue Section \ref{subsec:selection} takes up. It remains to normalise, and this one step is an import. Assign the mode its frequency by scaling the boost generator: the physical generator of the flow is $\omega K$, $K = N - \tfrac12$ (Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-structure-completed-pair}(4)), and Proposition \claimref{singularity.plane-rate} converts rapidity to Schwarzschild time at rate $\kappa$. The modular group of Lemma \claimref{singularity.modular-theory-matrix-factor-proved-outright} runs at parameter $t$ with generator $\log\Delta$; demanding that modular parameter $2\pi$ correspond to the geometric flow of $\omega K$ over one period $2\pi/\kappa$ of rapidity-time forces $\log\rho = -(2\pi/\kappa)\,\omega N + \text{const}$, i.e.
\[
\rho \;\propto\; e^{-(2\pi/\kappa)\,\omega N}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\tan\theta = e^{-\omega/2T}, \qquad T = \frac{\kappa}{2\pi} ,
\]
since $\rho = \operatorname{diag}(\cos^2\theta, \sin^2\theta)$ and $N = \operatorname{diag}(0,1)$. The partial trace over the partner is then $\rho_{\text{mode}} = \operatorname{diag}(1, e^{-\omega/T})/Z$: Fermi-Dirac at exactly $\TH$, manifestly KMS for the flow of $K$ by the last clause of Lemma \claimref{singularity.modular-theory-matrix-factor-proved-outright}. What the hypotheses do and do not force: if (ii) fails, Lemma \claimref{singularity.schmidt-forcing-proved-outright}'s eigenvector argument fails and the Schmidt basis need not be the number basis, so the reduction need not be diagonal in $N$ and then is not a Gibbs state of $K$ (take $\Omega = c\,|{+}\rangle|0\rangle + s\,|{-}\rangle|1\rangle$ with $|{\pm}\rangle = (|0\rangle \pm |1\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and $c \neq s$, cyclic and separating, whose reduction $\tfrac12\begin{psmallmatrix} 1 & c^{2} - s^{2} \\ c^{2} - s^{2} & 1\end{psmallmatrix}$ has nonzero coherence); but the converse fails, since $\Omega = c|01\rangle + s|10\rangle$ with $cs \neq 0$ is pure, cyclic and separating and not a $G$-eigenvector, yet reduces to $\operatorname{diag}(c^{2}, s^{2})$, Fermi-Dirac at exactly $\TH$ when $s^{2}/c^{2} = e^{-\omega/\TH}$. If (iii) fails, one Schmidt coefficient vanishes and the reduction is pure, not thermal at any finite temperature. The script checks both examples. In general, conditions (i)-(iii) are the hypotheses of Tomita-Takesaki theory, and the identification of modular flow with the boost for horizon-localised algebras is the Bisognano-Wichmann-Sewell mechanism \cite{BW1976, Sewell1982}; the chain's contribution is that, within the class of real rotor flows the geometry supplies, the flow is algebraically \emph{forced} (Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-structure-completed-pair}(5), with the scope stated there) rather than assumed, and that the single-mode instance is proved outright above rather than quoted.
