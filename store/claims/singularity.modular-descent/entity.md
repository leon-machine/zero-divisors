## formal
Let the ascended algebra of the horizon pair carry any state that is (i) pure, (ii) invariant under the boost flow generated by $\sigma$, and (iii) entangling across the two ends of the pair (cyclic and separating for the one-sided subalgebra). Then the descended state, the partial trace over the partner, which for such a state coincides with the restriction under the pinching $E$ of Proposition \claimref{singularity.descent-what-it-erases} (Proposition \claimref{singularity.pinching-against-partial-trace-scope-coincidence}(i); the coincidence is exactly single-mode, Proposition \claimref{singularity.pinching-against-partial-trace-scope-coincidence}(ii)), is KMS for the phase flow $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{it\sigma/2})$ of $\sigma$ at some inverse temperature $\beta \in \R$ in rapidity, and the hypotheses leave $\beta$ free, its sign included: nothing in (i) to (iii) forces $\beta > 0$ (the descended form of Theorem \claimref{singularity.descended-form-state-independent} allows the closed range $[-\infty, +\infty]$, and hypothesis (iii) removes exactly the endpoints), and positivity arrives with (R). If the modular parameter is normalised to the geometric period, one modular period being one period $2\pi/\kappa$ of rapidity-time (the Bisognano-Wichmann normalisation, which Section \ref{subsec:selection} names as premise (R) and proves irreducible), then $\beta = 2\pi$ in rapidity and, by Proposition \claimref{singularity.plane-rate}, the physical temperature is
\[
T \;=\; \frac{\kappa}{2\pi} \;=\; \frac{1}{8\pi M} \;=\; \TH ,
\]
exactly the Hawking temperature. For the CAR pair, the reduced density matrix per mode of frequency $\omega$ is then the Fermi-Dirac matrix $\operatorname{diag}(1, e^{-\omega/T})/Z$ with occupancy $1/(e^{\omega/T} + 1)$. The hypotheses are sufficient, not necessary: a state violating (ii) or (iii) need not descend thermally, and the projection cannot make it do so, but some such states do (the proof exhibits one), and Theorem \claimref{singularity.descended-form-state-independent} explains why no hypothesis on the state can be necessary for the \emph{form}.

## technical
Let the ascended algebra of the horizon pair carry a state that is (i) pure, (ii) invariant under the boost flow generated by $\sigma$, and (iii) entangling across the two ends of the pair, that is cyclic and separating for the one-sided subalgebra. Then the descended state, the partial trace over the partner, is KMS for the phase flow $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{it\sigma/2})$ of $\sigma$ at some inverse temperature $\beta \in \R$ in rapidity, and the hypotheses leave $\beta$ free, its sign included; positivity arrives only with (R).

Under the geometric normalisation (R), one modular period equal to one period $2\pi/\kappa$ of rapidity-time, one gets $\beta = 2\pi$ in rapidity, and the rapidity-to-time conversion gives the physical temperature $T = \kappa/2\pi = 1/8\pi M = \TH$. For the CAR pair the reduced density matrix of a mode of frequency $\omega$ is then the Fermi-Dirac matrix $\operatorname{diag}(1, e^{-\omega/T})/Z$. The hypotheses are sufficient, not necessary: some states violating (ii) or (iii) still descend thermally, and some do not.

## lay
Suppose the quantum state of a horizon mode and its partner is pure, unchanged by the boost, and genuinely entangled across the pair. Then what is seen on one side is thermal, although these conditions alone do not fix the temperature, not even its sign. Adding the premise that ties the state's intrinsic time to the geometric period of the horizon gives exactly the Hawking temperature, with a Fermi-Dirac occupation for each mode. The conditions are enough for this conclusion but are not required for it.

## one-liner
A pure, boost-invariant, entangling state of the horizon pair descends to a KMS state of the phase flow $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{it\sigma/2})$ at a free $\beta$, and under the geometric normalisation (R) to the Fermi-Dirac state at exactly $\TH = \kappa/2\pi = 1/8\pi M$.
