## formal
The framework forbids uncaused structure: a construction may not carry distinctions (partitions, assignments, orderings) that nothing in the framework determines. \emph{Provenance and level:} this is the structural principle used to order the family faces, applied here at the declared level; it is a premise of the derivation below, not a theorem, and is flagged as such.

## technical
The principle of indifference forbids uncaused structure: a construction may not carry distinctions, such as partitions, assignments or orderings, that nothing in the framework determines. Concretely, if the framework supplies no datum that singles out one partition $n = n_1 + \dots + n_k$ of a set of modes over another, a construction may not rely on such a partition.

It is a premise, not a theorem, applied at the declared level and flagged wherever it is used. It is the principle used to order the family faces, and here it is the premise of the derivation that selects the principal embedding for the boundary modes.

## lay
This is an assumption, not a result: the theory may not build in distinctions that nothing in it explains. If the theory gives no reason to split things into groups, or to label one of them first, then no construction is allowed to do so. The series uses this rule repeatedly and marks each place where it does.

## one-liner
A construction may not carry distinctions that nothing in the framework determines; this is a declared premise of the series, not a theorem.
