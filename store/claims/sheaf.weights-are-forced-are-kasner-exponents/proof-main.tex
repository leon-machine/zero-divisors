The cometric entries are those of the derived cometric of the Schwarzschild gravitational field, read through Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}(i). The diagonal cometric entries have $r$-orders $(-1, +1, -2)$; a rescaling shifts these to $(2a_r - 1,\, 2a_t + 1,\, 2a_\theta - 2)$, and each regime's stated profile prescribes the target order of every slot ($(0, 1, 0)$ for the degeneration regime of \claimref{sheaf.schwarzschild-degeneration-boundary-tame} with the Killing-time slot dying, $(0,0,0)$ for the exceptional regime of \claimref{sheaf.exceptional-algebra-black-hole}), determining each weight by the invertible linear relation displayed.
