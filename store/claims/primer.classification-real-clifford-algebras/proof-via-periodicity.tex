The table is part (iii) of \claimref{std-algebra.periodicity}. There the base cases $\Cl(k,0)$ and $\Cl(0,k)$ for $k \le 8$ are computed from the tensor rules, and every $\Cl(p,q)$ is reduced to one of them by $\Cl(p+1,q+1) \cong \Mat_2(\Cl(p,q))$ and by the period-eight rule $\Cl(p+8,q) \cong \Cl(p,q+8) \cong \Mat_{16}(\Cl(p,q))$. Reading the result by $p - q \bmod 8$ gives exactly the types stated here, with the matrix size fixed by the dimension $2^{p+q}$. This derivation supplies the induction that the main proof leaves to the reader.
