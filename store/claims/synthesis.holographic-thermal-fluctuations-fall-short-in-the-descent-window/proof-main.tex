\emph{(i)} At $R = 1/k$, $\langle\delta\rho^{2}\rangle_{R} = T^{2}C/R^{6} \propto R^{2\tau + \gamma - 6} = k^{6 - \gamma - 2\tau}$, so $P_{\Phi} = 8G^{2}k^{-4}\langle\delta\rho^{2}\rangle_{R}$ (the conversion of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-string-gas-thermal-spectrum-and-conversion}) $\propto k^{2 - \gamma - 2\tau}$. Since $P_{\Phi}$ is the dimensionless spectrum and $\Phi = \tfrac23\zeta$ is fixed outside the Hubble length in the radiation era (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-large-scale-potential-and-sachs-wolfe-relations}), the index of $\zeta$ is $n_{s} - 1 = 2 - \gamma - 2\tau$. The cases: $(\gamma, \tau) = (2, 0)$ gives $0$; $(3, 0)$ gives $-1$; $(2, -1)$ gives $2$. Against $n_{s} - 1 = -0.0351 \pm 0.0042$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-planck-2018-lcdm-parameters}), $0.0351/0.0042 = 8.357$. Solving $2 - \gamma - 2\tau = -0.0351$ gives $\gamma + 2\tau = 2.0351$, and at $\gamma = 2$, $\tau = 0.01755$. The area-law exponent $\gamma = 2$ is the scaling of the horizon entropy with area in Proposition \claimref{singularity.area-law-test}; its use for a heat capacity of a sub-horizon region is the hypothesis of the mechanism, not a result of the series.

\emph{(ii)} With $C = \lambda A/4G = \lambda\pi R^{2}/G$, $P_{\Phi} = 8G^{2}k^{-4}T^{2}\lambda\pi R^{2}/(GR^{6}) = 8\pi\lambda G T^{2}$ at $R = 1/k$, independent of $k$, with $G = 1/M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^{2}$. With $3.14159 < \pi < 3.1416$ and $M_{\mathrm{Pl}} = 1.22089 \times 10^{19}$ GeV, at $T = 7.3 \times 10^{8}$ GeV, the ceiling of the window for the descent's reheating temperature under Premise (R) set by Proposition \claimref{synthesis.untuned-fit-caps-lightest-heavy-neutrino}, $8.9 \times 10^{-20} < P_{\Phi}/\lambda < 9.0 \times 10^{-20}$. Solving $8\pi T^{2}/M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^{2} = \tfrac49 A_{s}$ gives $T^{2}$ between $(7.4 \times 10^{13})^{2}$ and $(7.5 \times 10^{13})^{2}$ GeV$^{2}$, above $(10^{5} \times 7.3 \times 10^{8})^{2}$. At the nucleosynthesis floor $T = 1$ MeV, $P_{\Phi} < 10^{-42}\lambda$.
