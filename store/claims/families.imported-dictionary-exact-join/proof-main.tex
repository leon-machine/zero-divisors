Mass is conformal weight read against the frozen point: a density of weight $w$ coupled to the frozen point $I$ obeys the Klein-Gordon equation with $m^{2} = -w(w+3)\,\Lambda/3 = -w(w+3)H^{2}$. For the composite of two conformal spinors (twistors) the deformed bracket is $B_{\Lambda} = \br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}} + \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}[\omega^{1}\omega^{2}]$ and the exact join is
\[
m^{2} \;=\; 2|B_{\Lambda}|^{2} + \frac{\Lambda}{3}\,C, \qquad C \ge 0,
\]
with $C$ the internal $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ Casimir of the pair, vanishing exactly when the Gram matrix of the two spinors is scalar; the massless composites for $\Lambda > 0$ are exactly those with $B_{\Lambda} = 0$ and Gram matrix $h = 0$ (a scalar Gram matrix of a massless composite is zero, \claimref{mass.exact-join}(5)), and there are composites with $B_{\Lambda} = 0$ and $m^{2} = \Lambda C/3 \ne 0$. The induced weight of a composite heavier than $3H/2$ lies on the principal series, $w = -\tfrac32 \pm I_4\nu$ (numbers are read in $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_4\}$) with $\nu = m/H - \tfrac98 H/m + O((H/m)^{3})$: the mass sits in the imaginary part. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thm.~3.1, Thm.~5.6, Prop.~5.9]{ButlerT11} (\claimref{mass.mass-weight-dictionary}, \claimref{mass.exact-join}, \claimref{mass.weight-composite}), with $C$ defined at \cite[eq.~(9)]{ButlerT11} and read in \cite[Rem.~5.10]{ButlerT11}.
