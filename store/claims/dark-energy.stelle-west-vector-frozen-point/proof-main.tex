The proof is in Gauge Theory Gravity: flat spacetime with the gravitational field $(\hb, \Omega)$ on its invertible region (physical vectors, derived metric and $D$ as in Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}), and tractors as vectors of $\Cl(2,4)$. That the tractor connection is a $\Cl(2,4)$ gauge field is not assumed: the next paragraph derives it from the display of Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-frozen-point-geometry} alone, and uses neither that Fact's sentence naming Theorem \claimref{synthesis.gtg-tractor-gauge-field-reproduces-tractor-connection} nor that theorem, which uses the present result. Adjoin to the spacetime vectors the null vectors $\nO$, $\nInf$ with $\nO \cdot \nInf = 1$, orthogonal to spacetime, and write a tractor as $Y = \sigma\,\nInf + \mu + \rho\,\nO$ with $\mu$ a physical vector, so that $\nInf \cdot Y = \rho$, $\nO \cdot Y = \sigma$, $Q(Y) = 2\sigma\rho + Q(\mu)$ and $\ip{Y}{Y'} = Y \cdot Y' = \sigma\rho' + \rho\sigma' + \mu \cdot \mu'$, the tractor metric \eqref{eq:tractormetric}. For a background vector $a$ write $A = \hb^{-1}(a)$, the physical frame vector; the derived metric is $g(a, b) = A \cdot B$, so $g_{ab}$ is the flat product of physical vectors and the Schouten tensor is the Schouten map $\Sch$ of the field strength. For a bivector $B$ and a vector $Y$, $B \times Y = B \cdot Y$, and $(u \wedge w) \cdot Y = u\,(w \cdot Y) - w\,(u \cdot Y)$.

\emph{The connection as a gauge field.} Put $\Theta(A) = -\nInf \wedge A + \nO \wedge \Sch(A)$ and $\Omega_{T}(a) = \Omega(a) + \Theta(A)$, $D^{T}_{a}Y = a \cdot \nabla Y + \Omega_{T}(a) \times Y$. Then
\[
(-\nInf \wedge A) \cdot Y = -(A \cdot \mu)\,\nInf + \rho A, \qquad (\nO \wedge \Sch(A)) \cdot Y = (\Sch(A) \cdot \mu)\,\nO - \sigma\,\Sch(A),
\]
and $\Omega(a)$, a spacetime bivector, annihilates $\nO$ and $\nInf$ and acts on $\mu$ as the rotation part of $D_{a}\mu = a \cdot \nabla \mu + \Omega(a) \times \mu$. So the slots of $D^{T}_{a}Y$ are $(a \cdot \nabla\sigma - A \cdot \mu,\ D_{a}\mu + \rho A - \sigma\,\Sch(A),\ a \cdot \nabla\rho + \Sch(A) \cdot \mu)$: with $\mu_{a} = A \cdot \mu$ this is the connection of Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-frozen-point-geometry} slot by slot, and the computation uses nothing else. In the frozen point's scale $\Sch = \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\,\mathrm{id}$ (the Fact), so
\[
\Theta(A) = -\nInf \wedge A + \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\,\nO \wedge A = A \wedge \bigl(\nInf - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO\bigr) = A \wedge W .
\]

\emph{(i)} $Q(X) = Q(\nO) = 0$; $\ip{X}{I} = \nO \cdot (\nInf + \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO) = 1$; $Q(I) = 2\cdot\tfrac{\Lambda}{6} = \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}$; so $V = X - \tfrac{3}{\Lambda}I = -\tfrac{3}{\Lambda}\nInf + \tfrac12\nO$, the slots $(-3/\Lambda, 0, \tfrac12)$; $\ip{V}{I} = 1 - \tfrac{3}{\Lambda}\cdot\tfrac{\Lambda}{3} = 0$; $Q(V) = 2\cdot(-\tfrac{3}{\Lambda})\cdot\tfrac12 = -\tfrac{3}{\Lambda} = -\ell^{2}$; and $W = V/Q(V) = \nInf - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO$, the slots $(1, 0, -\Lambda/6)$.

\emph{(ii)} $X = \nO$ has constant slots, so $D^{T}_{a}X = (A \wedge W) \cdot \nO = A\,(W \cdot \nO) - W\,(A \cdot \nO) = A$, since $W \cdot \nO = \nInf \cdot \nO = 1$ and $A \cdot \nO = 0$: $D^{T}_{a}X = \hb^{-1}(a) = e_{a}$, the soldering form. $I$ is parallel in its scale (the Fact; directly, $(A \wedge W) \cdot I = A\,(W \cdot I) - W\,(A \cdot I) = 0$ as $W \cdot I = \tfrac{\Lambda}{6} - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6} = 0$), and $\ip{X}{I}$, $Q(I)$ are constants, so $D^{T}_{a}V = D^{T}_{a}X = e_{a}$. Finally $\ip{e_{a}}{I} = A \cdot I = 0$, $A$ being a spacetime vector.

\emph{(iii)} By the two displays, $\Omega_{T}(a) = \Omega(a) + \hb^{-1}(a) \wedge W$ with Schouten map $\tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\mathrm{id}$, and $W = V/Q(V)$ by (i), so the bivector is $e_{a} \wedge V/Q(V)$. On a tractor with $\mu = 0$, $\Omega(a)$ acts as zero and $D^{T}_{a} = a \cdot \nabla + (e_{a} \wedge W)\times$; on a general tractor $D^{T}_{a}$ is $D_{a}$ on the $\mu$ slot ($a \cdot \nabla$ on the other two) plus the same bivector.

\emph{(iv)} The bracket of the action convention is the commutator of the operators $Y \mapsto B \cdot Y$, which by the Jacobi identity of the commutator product is $B_{1} \times B_{2}$. For vectors, $(u \wedge w) \times (y \wedge z) = (w \cdot y)\,u \wedge z - (w \cdot z)\,u \wedge y - (u \cdot y)\,w \wedge z + (u \cdot z)\,w \wedge y$. With $u = e_{a}$, $w = z = W$, $y = e_{b}$, and $e_{a} \cdot W = e_{b} \cdot W = 0$, only the second term survives: $[e_{a} \wedge W, e_{b} \wedge W] = -Q(W)\,e_{a} \wedge e_{b}$, and $Q(W) = 2\cdot 1\cdot(-\tfrac{\Lambda}{6}) = -\tfrac{\Lambda}{3}$.

The check confirms in exact arithmetic the slot form of $\Omega_{T}$ for a generic Schouten map and rotation-gauge bivector, the operator identity of (iv), and (i) to (iv) on flat-sliced de Sitter fields.
