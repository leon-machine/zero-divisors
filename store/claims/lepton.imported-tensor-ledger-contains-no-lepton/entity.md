## formal
The tensor ledger $\Lambda^{\bullet}(\C^{2} \otimes \C^{3})$, the Fock space generated by the six cells $e_{i} \otimes f_{a}$ with the automorphisms acting cell by cell as $\Lambda^{\bullet}(A \otimes B)$, decomposes under $SU(2) \times SU(3)$ as $(1,1) \oplus (2,3) \oplus [(3,\bar{3}) \oplus (1,6)] \oplus [(4,1) \oplus (2,8)] \oplus [(3,3) \oplus (1,\bar{6})] \oplus (2,\bar{3}) \oplus (1,1)$, graded by cell number, which is also the abelian weight; no $(2,1)$ occurs; and the states that fill a whole row, $\bigwedge_{a}(v \otimes f_{a})$ for $v \in \C^{2}$, are cubic in $v$ and span $S^{3}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{3}\C^{3} = (4,1)$, of weight $3$. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Prop.~5.1 and Prop.~5.3(2)]{ButlerT12}.

## technical
The tensor ledger $\Lambda^{\bullet}(\C^{2} \otimes \C^{3})$ is the Fock space of the six cells $e_{i} \otimes f_{a}$, with the automorphisms acting cell by cell as $\Lambda^{\bullet}(A \otimes B)$. Under $SU(2) \times SU(3)$ it decomposes, graded by cell number (which is also the abelian weight), as $(1,1) \oplus (2,3) \oplus [(3,\bar{3}) \oplus (1,6)] \oplus [(4,1) \oplus (2,8)] \oplus [(3,3) \oplus (1,\bar{6})] \oplus (2,\bar{3}) \oplus (1,1)$, and no $(2,1)$ occurs.

The states that fill a whole row, $\bigwedge_{a}(v \otimes f_{a})$, are cubic in $v$ and span $S^{3}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{3}\C^{3} = (4,1)$, of weight $3$, a quadruplet and not a doublet.

## lay
If the states of the structure of two spinor states are counted cell by cell, as ordinary multi-particle states of its six cells, no state has the symmetry content of a lepton doublet. The obvious candidate, one spinor state's whole row filled, turns out to transform as a four-component object rather than a doublet. So the lepton does not live in this state space.

## one-liner
The cell-by-cell tensor ledger contains no $(2,1)$, and its filled-row states form the quadruplet $(4,1)$ of weight $3$.
