Throughout, under Hypothesis (S) (\claimref{standard-model.hyp-s-slot-statistics}), states are the occupation states $e_{S}$, $S \subseteq \{1, \dots, 5\}$ even, and a state has slot number $j = |S \cap \{4, 5\}|$ and pair number $k = |S \cap \{1, 2, 3\}|$; the species are $\nu^{c}$ $(0,0)$, $u^{c}$ $(0,2)$, $Q$ $(1,1)$, $L$ $(1,3)$, $e^{c}$ $(2,0)$, $d^{c}$ $(2,2)$, with $B - L = 1 - \tfrac23 k$ and $\chi = -(-1)^{j}$ (\claimref{standard-model.proton-decay-only-through-slot-pair}).

\emph{(i).} $\su(3)$ is spanned by the $v_{a}w_{b}$, $a \neq b$ pair modes, and their commutators; $\su(2)_{L}$ by $v_{4}w_{5}$, $v_{5}w_{4}$ and $\tfrac12(N_{4} - N_{5})$ (\claimref{standard-model.automorphisms-preserve-slot-pair-split-are}). They preserve $j$ and $k$, so each species is invariant. On a species $\su(3)$ acts on the pair factor $\Lambda^{k}\C^{3}$, irreducible, and $\su(2)_{L}$ on the slot factor $\Lambda^{j}\C^{2}$, irreducible, so each species is irreducible under $\mathfrak{g}$, of dimension $\binom{2}{j}\binom{3}{k}$: $6, 3, 3, 2, 1, 1$. Their hypercharges $\tfrac16, -\tfrac23, \tfrac13, -\tfrac12, 1, 0$ are distinct, so they are pairwise inequivalent and the commutant is spanned by the six projections. The check computes the commutant as an exact kernel, without this argument: it has dimension $6$ and is diagonal.

\emph{(ii).} $B(e_{S}, e_{U}) \neq 0$ only for $U$ the complement of $S$ (\claimref{standard-model.invariant-pairing-five-modes}). For $a = v_{i}$, $i$ a slot mode, $a e_{T} = \pm e_{T \cup \{i\}}$ when $i \notin T$, so the term pairs $T$ of type $(j, k)$, $j \le 1$, with $S$ of type $(1 - j, 3 - k)$: $\nu^{c}$ with $L$ and $u^{c}$ with $Q$. For $a = w_{i}$ it pairs $(j, k)$, $j \ge 1$, with $(3 - j, 3 - k)$: $e^{c}$ with $L$ and $d^{c}$ with $Q$. For $X$ diagonal the condition of conservation is $x_{S} + x_{T} + h_{a} = 0$ on each such pair:
\[
x_{Q} + x_{u} + h_{v} = 0, \quad x_{L} + x_{\nu} + h_{v} = 0, \quad x_{Q} + x_{d} + h_{w} = 0, \quad x_{L} + x_{e} + h_{w} = 0 .

Four independent conditions on eight numbers leave four; with $h_{w} = -h_{v} = -h$ they leave three, parametrised by $(x_{Q}, x_{L}, h)$: $x_{u} = -x_{Q} - h$, $x_{d} = -x_{Q} + h$, $x_{\nu} = -x_{L} - h$, $x_{e} = -x_{L} + h$. $Y$ is $(\tfrac16, -\tfrac12, \tfrac12)$, $n_{B}$ is $(\tfrac13, 0, 0)$, $n_{L}$ is $(0, 1, 0)$, and these are independent. \emph{(iii).} $T e_{T} \neq 0$ only for $T = \emptyset$, the state $\nu^{c}$, and $T e_{\emptyset}$ is the full state, which $B$ pairs with $e_{\emptyset}$; $T$ is a fixed element of the algebra, the direction of a vacuum value and not a field, so it is given no charge, $h_{T} = 0$; this is consistent for hypercharge, since $T$ commutes with $\mathfrak{g}$ (\claimref{standard-model.majorana-element}(2)). \emph{(iv).} $[N_{b}, v_{b}] = v_{b}$, $[N_{b}, w_{b}] = -w_{b}$, so every product of two mode operators is an eigenvector of $\mathrm{ad}$ of every $\sum_{b} c_{b}N_{b}$; $B - L$ and $Y$ are of this form on the sixteen. $n_{B}$ is not carried: the slot-pair generators anticommute with $\chi$ (\claimref{standard-model.proton-decay-only-through-slot-pair}), so they change $n_{B} = \tfrac14((B - L) + \chi)$ by amounts that differ between states. \emph{(v).} $4T_{3L}^{2} = (N_{4} - N_{5})^{2}$ is $1$ on the states with $j = 1$ and $0$ otherwise: six states of $Q$ and two of $L$, so $A_{2} = 6x_{Q} + 2x_{L}$. $(N_{1} - N_{2})^{2}$ is $1$ on the states containing exactly one of the pair modes $1, 2$: four of the six states of $Q$, two of the three of $u^{c}$, two of $d^{c}$, none of $L$ (which has $k = 3$) or of the singlets with $k = 0$; so $A_{3} = 4x_{Q} + 2x_{u} + 2x_{d}$, which on the solutions of the four Dirac conditions is $-2(h_{v} + h_{w})$: zero with one doublet, and $8$ on the unit, for which also $A_{2} = 6 + 2 = 8$.
