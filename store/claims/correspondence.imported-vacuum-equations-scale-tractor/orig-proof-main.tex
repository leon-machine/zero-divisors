On a region where the gauge field is invertible, the vacuum field equations are exactly equivalent to the covariant constancy of the null scale tractor $I_g$; the scale tractor of a scale $g$ is the section $I_g = (1, 0, \rho_g)$ in the splitting of that scale, with $\rho_g$ fixed by the scalar curvature of $g$; for an Einstein metric with $\mathrm{Ric} = \Lambda g$ it is parallel with squared norm $\ip{I_g}{I_g} = \Lambda/3$, and conversely a parallel section of the form $(1,0,\rho)$ forces $\rho$ constant and $g$ Einstein with $\Lambda = 6\rho$. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thm.~4.5, Thm.~4.7]{ButlerT6}.
