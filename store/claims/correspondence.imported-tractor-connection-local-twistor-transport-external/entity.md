## technical
This entry collects the outside sources behind the imported tractor connection and the conformal spinor connection (local twistor transport): Penrose and Rindler (volume two, chapter six) for the index conventions; Bailey, Eastwood and Gover, and Curry and Gover, for the tractor connection; Penrose and MacCallum, and Dighton, for conformal spinors (local twistors) and their transport. It asserts only what those works establish.

## lay
A list of the published works from which the series takes its two standard connection formulas for conformal geometry. Nothing new is claimed here; the entry records where the borrowed results come from.

## one-liner
The literature sources for the tractor connection and the conformal spinor connection: Penrose and Rindler, Bailey, Eastwood and Gover, Curry and Gover, Penrose and MacCallum, and Dighton.
