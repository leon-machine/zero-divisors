The sector of a single conformal spinor (massless, free) is $\Sp = \Lambda^{\bullet}W$ with the pair-slot factor acting as $\Lambda^{\bullet}(\lambda^{-2}b)$: its grade-$k$ states transform as $\Lambda^{k}\C^{3} = 1, 3, \bar{3}, 1$ with weight $-2k$, that is $(1)_{0}, (3)_{-1/3}, (\bar{3})_{-2/3}, (1)_{-1}$. For a gauge group with a $U(1)$ factor acting on Weyl fermions listed in one handedness, the four anomaly-cancellation conditions are those of \cite[Fact~2.7]{ButlerT12} (\claimref{standard-model.imported-anomaly-coefficients}); a fermion of the opposite handedness enters as its conjugate, with the conjugate representation and the opposite hypercharge. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Prop.~5.3(3), Fact~2.7]{ButlerT12} (\claimref{standard-model.quarks-leptons-two-level-fermion-content}); \cite[\S 22.4]{WeinbergQFT2}.
