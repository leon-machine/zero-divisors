## formal
The neutrino mass is set by the cosmological term of the deformed bracket

## technical
Refuted. The corrected statement: The term lies below the flat term and never dominates below the horizon; the mass comes from the seesaw on the empty conformal spinor (the state with no pair mode occupied), whose Majorana scale is a premise.

## lay
One idea was that the tiny cosmological constant, entering the framework's mass formula, could set the neutrino mass. The paper shows this effect is far too weak for anything smaller than the observable universe, so the idea is refuted. Neutrino masses come instead from the seesaw, with its heavy scale taken as a premise.
