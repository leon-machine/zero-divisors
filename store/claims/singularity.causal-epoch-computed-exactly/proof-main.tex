Read in Gauge Theory Gravity, a geodesic of the static metric below is a worldline whose velocity $\hb^{-1}(x')$ is $D$-parallel (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}, clause (iii)). For a radial geodesic of the static metric with conserved energy $\mathcal{E} = f\,dt/d\lambda$ per unit mass, the normalisation gives $(dr/d\lambda)^{2} = \mathcal{E}^{2} - f$, so $dt/dr = \mathcal{E}/\bigl(f\sqrt{\mathcal{E}^{2} - f}\bigr)$; at $\mathcal{E} = 1$, $\sqrt{1 - f} = \sqrt{2M/r}$ and $|dt/dr| = \sqrt{r/2M}\,\bigl|r/(r - 2M)\bigr|$, which is the stated integrand for $r < 2M$. Substituting $r = 2Mu^{2}$ turns the integral into $4M\int_0^{\sqrt{1-\varepsilon}} u^{4}/(1 - u^{2})\,du$, and the integrand identity $u^{4}/(1 - u^{2}) = -u^{2} - 1 + 1/(1 - u^{2})$ gives the antiderivative $-u^{3}/3 - u + \tfrac12\ln\frac{1+u}{1-u}$, checked by differentiation; the closed form follows. For the expansion, $u = \sqrt{1 - \varepsilon} = 1 - \varepsilon/2 + O(\varepsilon^{2})$ gives $\tfrac12\ln\frac{1+u}{1-u} = \tfrac12\ln\frac{4}{\varepsilon} + O(\varepsilon) = \tfrac12\ln\tfrac1\varepsilon + \ln 2 + O(\varepsilon)$ and $u + u^{3}/3 = \tfrac43 + O(\varepsilon)$, so $\Delta t = 2M\ln\tfrac1\varepsilon + 4M(\ln 2 - \tfrac43) + O(\varepsilon)$. Near the horizon $dt/dr \approx 1/f$ for every $\mathcal{E}$, which is why the logarithm's coefficient is universal. Both the integrand identity and the substitution are verified exactly in the deposited script, as identities of rational functions, and the closed form is checked against the integral by exact rational Riemann bounds at the rational endpoint $\varepsilon = 1999 \cdot 10^{-6}$.
