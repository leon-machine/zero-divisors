In the three-argument form of the gravitational action the frozen point enters subject to the Stelle-West constraint $Q(I) = \Lambda/3$, every fluctuation of the constrained pair $(I, V)$ is a gauge transformation (unitary gauge $\delta I = \delta V = 0$ exists and is complete), and the second variation of the action involves only the connection. The action therefore carries no equation of motion for $\Lambda$: the norm is fixed by a constraint, not evolved by a field equation. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Prop.~5.4]{ButlerT10} (\claimref{dark-energy.point-infinity-has-no-physical-fluctuation}); covariant constancy of the frozen point of norm $\Lambda/3$ (\claimref{dark-energy.imported-frozen-point-geometry}) is \cite[Prop.~4.2(i)]{ButlerT10} (\claimref{dark-energy.vacuum-region-registration-condition}) and \cite[Thms.~4.5, 4.7]{ButlerT6} (\claimref{interior.scale-tractor-leaves-cone}, \claimref{interior.covariant-form-vacuum-equations}).
