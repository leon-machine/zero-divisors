The mirror tower is a copy of ours, populated at $\Omega_{b'} = 0.995$ to $1$ times $\Omega_{b}$. Its temperature ratio $\xi = T'/T$ lies between the gravitational floor, $5.2 \times 10^{-4}$ (the source prints $2.8$ to $3.2 \times 10^{-4}$, the value at the production coefficients first imported; the present value is that of \claimref{dark-matter.stored-asymmetry-charge-not-number}(iii)), and the hot-deposit estimate, about $4 \times 10^{-3}$; its baryon-to-photon ratio is $\eta' = \eta/\xi^{3}$; on the expected (burning) branch it is helium, on the alternative it is protons. As neutral atoms its elastic cross-section per mass is of order $2 \times 10^{8}$ (hydrogen) or $5 \times 10^{7}$ (helium) $\cmg$, and inside a halo it is a plasma, collisional and dissipative like our gas. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thm.~4.3, Props.~4.6 and 4.7, Prop.~6.2]{ButlerT14} (\claimref{dark-matter.stored-asymmetry-charge-not-number}, \claimref{dark-matter.mirror-share-one-how-fragile}, \claimref{dark-matter.mirror-tower-copy-one}, \claimref{dark-matter.radiation-audit-what-theorem-closes-what}).
