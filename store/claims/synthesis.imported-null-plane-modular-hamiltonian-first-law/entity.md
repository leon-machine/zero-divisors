## formal
Units $\hbar = c = 1$. Let $\mathcal{N}$ be a null plane of Minkowski space, with affine parameter $u$ along its generators and transverse coordinates $y \in \R^{2}$, let $\omega$ be the vacuum of a free quantum field, and for $u_{0} \in \R$ let $\mathcal{A}(u_{0})$ be the algebra of the field restricted to the half $\{u > u_{0}\}$ of $\mathcal{N}$ (the horizon algebra of Wall). Write
\[
K(u_{0}) \;=\; \int d^{2}y\int_{u_{0}}^{\infty}(u - u_{0})\,T_{uu}(u, y)\,du
\]
for the boost energy on the generators beyond the cut $u = u_{0}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{(Null-plane modular Hamiltonian.)} The restriction of $\omega$ to $\mathcal{A}(u_{0})$ is KMS at temperature $1/2\pi$ for the null dilations about $u_{0}$ generated by $K(u_{0})$: its modular Hamiltonian is $2\pi K(u_{0})$, local on $\mathcal{N}$ and a sum over the generators with no cross terms. For a cut $u = \gamma(y)$ the same holds with $u_{0}$ replaced by $\gamma(y)$ under the transverse integral. \emph{Provenance:} \cite{Wall2011}, Sec. 2.4, eqs. (28) to (31), from Sewell's form of the Bisognano-Wichmann theorem with positivity of the null translations in place of the spectrum condition, the hypotheses verified explicitly for the free scalar (Sec. 3) and stated for free fields of spin $\tfrac12$, $1$ and $2$ (Sec. 4); \cite{CasiniTesteTorroba2017}, eqs. (1.5), (3.6), which record it as proved for free fields by \cite{Wall2011} and derive it for conformal field theories; \cite{FLPW2016} for the first-order deformation of a planar cut.
\item[(ii)] \emph{(Araki's relative entropy.)} For normal states $\rho$, $\sigma$ of a von Neumann algebra the relative entropy $S(\rho\,\|\,\sigma) \in [0, \infty]$ is defined, vanishes only for $\rho = \sigma$, and does not increase under restriction to a subalgebra; for density matrices it is $\Tr\rho(\ln\rho - \ln\sigma)$. \emph{Provenance:} \cite{Araki1975}; \cite{Wall2011}, Sec. 2.5, eq. (38) and its monotonicity.
\item[(iii)] \emph{(First law for entropy differences.)} Let $\sigma$ be a faithful normal state of a von Neumann algebra with modular Hamiltonian $H_{\sigma}$ (its modular operator is $\Delta_{\sigma} = e^{-H_{\sigma}}$). For a normal state $\rho$ with $S(\rho\,\|\,\sigma) < \infty$ and finite $\Delta\langle H_{\sigma}\rangle = \rho(H_{\sigma}) - \sigma(H_{\sigma})$, the entropy difference
\[
\Delta S \;:=\; \Delta\langle H_{\sigma}\rangle - S(\rho\,\|\,\sigma)
\]
is the von Neumann entropy difference whenever both entropies are finite (for density matrices, $S(\rho\,\|\,\sigma) = \Delta\langle H_{\sigma}\rangle - \Delta S$ with $H_{\sigma} = -\ln\sigma$), and on a type III algebra, where the entropies themselves are infinite, $\Delta S$ is defined in this way. Hence $\Delta S \le \Delta\langle H_{\sigma}\rangle$, with equality only for $\rho = \sigma$, and along a family $\rho_{\epsilon}$ with $\rho_{0} = \sigma$ on which $S(\rho_{\epsilon}\,\|\,\sigma)$ is differentiable, $\Delta S = \Delta\langle H_{\sigma}\rangle$ at first order in $\epsilon$: the first law of entanglement. For the vacuum on $\mathcal{A}(u_{0})$, by (i), $H_{\omega} = 2\pi K(u_{0})$ and $\Delta S = 2\pi\,\Delta\langle K\rangle - S(\rho\,\|\,\omega)$. \emph{Provenance:} \cite{CasiniTesteTorroba2017}, eqs. (1.1) to (1.3); \cite{Wall2011}, Sec. 2.5.
\end{enumerate}

## technical
These are known results of quantum field theory on a null plane. For a free field in its vacuum, the algebra of the field on the part of a null plane beyond a cut is thermal at temperature $1/2\pi$ for the dilations of the light rays about the cut, so its modular Hamiltonian is $2\pi$ times the boost energy $\int(u - u_{0})T_{uu}$ on the generators, ray by ray. Wall proved this from axioms of the horizon algebra, via Sewell's form of the Bisognano-Wichmann theorem with the positivity of null translations in place of the spectrum condition, and verified the axioms for the free scalar, stating them for higher spins; Casini, Teste and Torroba record the formula as proved for free fields and derive it for conformal theories, and Faulkner, Leigh, Parrikar and Wang give its first-order form for deformed cuts. Araki's relative entropy, defined for any pair of normal states of a von Neumann algebra, is nonnegative, zero only for equal states and monotone under restriction. With the modular Hamiltonian it defines the entropy difference of an excited state, $\Delta S = 2\pi\Delta\langle K\rangle - S(\rho\,\|\,\omega)$, the only meaning entropy has on these type III algebras; at first order in a perturbation the relative entropy drops out and $\Delta S = 2\pi\Delta\langle K\rangle$, the first law of entanglement. In the store the KMS property of (i) for the chiral fermion on one generator, in the store's conventions, is the computation of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.positive-null-energy-gives-r-premises}(ii).

## lay
A quantum field in empty space, looked at only on the part of a light-like surface beyond some cut, looks thermal: it has a temperature with respect to stretching the light rays away from the cut. The quantity that generates this stretching is a weighted total of the field's energy flowing along the rays, and it plays the role that energy plays for an ordinary hot system. A standard measure of distance between quantum states, Araki's relative entropy, then gives the entropy change of any disturbance of the field, and for small disturbances the entropy change equals that energy change divided by the temperature. These are established results of quantum field theory, imported here as facts.

## one-liner
For a free field in its vacuum, the algebra on a null plane beyond the cut $u = u_{0}$ has modular Hamiltonian $2\pi\int(u - u_{0})T_{uu}$, and with Araki's relative entropy the entropy difference of an excited state is $2\pi\Delta\langle K\rangle - S(\rho\,\|\,\omega)$, so $\Delta S = 2\pi\Delta\langle K\rangle$ at first order (Wall 2011; Casini, Teste and Torroba 2017; Faulkner et al. 2016).
