The framework forbids uncaused structure: a construction may not carry distinctions (partitions, assignments, orderings) that nothing in the framework determines; it is a structural premise of the series (\claimref{scrambling.indifference-structural-premise-series}), not a theorem, and is flagged as such wherever it is used. The reading this paper makes of it, stated here so that it can be examined: the instrument equates weights along symmetry orbits and forbids selections nothing causes; it never equates norms between inequivalent irreducible components, which no symmetry relates. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Principle~5.4]{ButlerT8}, with the principality of the fuzzy sphere \cite[Thm.~5.5]{ButlerT8} as the use of it that the proof there requires; the reading is this paper's.
