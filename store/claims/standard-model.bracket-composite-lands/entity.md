## formal
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the unital algebra generated, inside the endomorphisms of the five-mode module, by the frozen point $I = av_{1} + bw_{1}$, its lifted rung components $c_{4}$ and $c_{5}$, the bracket $b = v_{4}v_{5}$ and its conjugate $b^{\dagger} = w_{5}w_{4}$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $\dim \mathcal{A} = 32$, and $\mathcal{A} = \Cl_{R} \oplus I\,\Cl_{R}$ where $\Cl_{R} \cong \Cl(2,2)_{\C}$ is the sixteen-dimensional algebra of the two rung modes.
\item[(ii)] The colour-singlet part of $\mathcal{A}$, the commutant of $\su(3)$ acting on the pair modes, is exactly $\Cl_{R}$.
\item[(iii)] The odd elements of $\Cl_{R}$ of hypercharge $-\tfrac12$ and $T_{3} = -\tfrac12$ form the two-dimensional space spanned by $w_{4}$ and $w_{4}N_{5}$.
\item[(iv)] The mass form of $w_{4}N_{5}$ is one half the mass form of $w_{4}$. Hence every symmetric doublet mass form produced by $\mathcal{A}$ is a multiple of the $10$ form, with $|y_{e}| = |y_{d}|$ under Hypothesis (S), which names the species; in particular the neutral composite $b^{\dagger}v_{5} = -w_{4} + w_{4}N_{5}$ has mass form $-\tfrac12$ times the $10$ form.
\item[(v)] The $126$ direction $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$ is not in the span of the forms $\mathcal{A}$ produces, and the pair bivector $\sum_{i} v_{i} \wedge w_{i}$ is not in $\mathcal{A}$.
\end{enumerate}

## technical
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the unital algebra on the five-mode module generated by the frozen point $I = av_{1} + bw_{1}$, its lifted rung components $c_{4}, c_{5}$, the bracket $b = v_{4}v_{5}$ and its conjugate $b^{\dagger} = w_{5}w_{4}$. Then $\dim\mathcal{A} = 32$ with $\mathcal{A} = \Cl_{R} \oplus I\,\Cl_{R}$, where $\Cl_{R} \cong \Cl(2,2)_{\C}$ is the algebra of the two rung modes, and the colour-singlet part of $\mathcal{A}$ is exactly $\Cl_{R}$. The odd elements of $\Cl_{R}$ with $Y = -\tfrac12$ and $T_{3} = -\tfrac12$ are spanned by $w_{4}$ and $w_{4}N_{5}$, and the mass form of $w_{4}N_{5}$ is half that of $w_{4}$.

Hence every symmetric doublet mass form produced by $\mathcal{A}$ is a multiple of the $10$ form, with $|y_{e}| = |y_{d}|$ under Hypothesis (S); the neutral composite $b^{\dagger}v_{5}$ has $-\tfrac12$ times the $10$ form. The $126$ direction $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$ is not in the span of these forms, and the pair bivector $\sum_{i} v_{i}\wedge w_{i}$ is not in $\mathcal{A}$.

## lay
The framework's mass-making objects, the cosmological scale vector and the mass bracket, can be multiplied together in many ways. This theorem shows that every relevant mass pattern they produce is of one simple type, which under the structural Hypothesis (S), the one that names the particles, gives the electron and the down quark couplings of equal size. The different pattern needed to tell those two apart is out of reach of these objects.

## one-liner
The algebra generated by the frozen point, its rung lifts and the bracket produces only multiples of the $10$ mass form, never the $126$ direction, so that under Hypothesis (S) $|y_{e}| = |y_{d}|$.
