\emph{Setting.} Under hypothesis (B) the joint algebra is the CAR algebra over $\ell^{2}(J) \oplus \ell^{2}(J')$ (Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-quasi-free-states-car-algebra}), its windows are $\mathfrak{A}_{F} \otimes \mathfrak{A}'_{F'}$, and the joint boost dynamics is the quasi-free flow of $h \oplus h'$ with $h' = u h u^{*}$. The absence of a cross term is what Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.superselected-hence-dark-exactly-stable} supplies (the towers interact only through the shared metric); that Proposition holds given P1 to P3 and tameness, so the result below holds given them too, and what it avoids is only the application of P2 to the deposited energy per charge. On a window the generator is $H_{F} + H'_{F'}$ with $[H_{F}, H'_{F'}] = 0$, and each window is invariant under the dynamics (Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.boundary-mode-algebra-windows-boost-dynamics}).

(i) Let $\omega$ be any state that is KMS at $\beta$ for the joint flow. Since windows are invariant under the dynamics, the restriction of $\omega$ to a window is KMS there, and by Lemma \claimref{hawking.gibbs-states-are-kms-conversely-finite} it is the unique such state, the Gibbs state $e^{-\beta(H_{F} + H'_{F'})}/\tr$, which factorises as $e^{-\beta H_{F}} \otimes e^{-\beta H'_{F'}}$ up to normalisation. So both towers' window states are Gibbs at the same $\beta$, every mode of the mirror has the Fermi-Dirac occupation of its partner, $n'_{j} = n_{j} = (e^{\beta\omega_{j}} + 1)^{-1}$, and $\xi = 1$. A state is fixed by its window restrictions, so $\omega$ is the quasi-free state with symbol $(1 + e^{\beta(h \oplus h')})^{-1} = A_{I} \oplus u A_{I} u^{*}$, the product of the two I-states at one $\beta$ (Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state}; no quasi-free assumption is needed, since quasi-freeness follows); that it is KMS on the full algebra is Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-kms-passes-inductive-limits}. P2 is not applied to the deposit at any step.

(ii) The product of the two Gibbs factors at $\beta$ and $\beta'$ commutes with $H_{F} + H'_{F'}$, each factor commuting with its own tower's generator and, by the absence of a cross term, with the other's; so the state is invariant under the joint flow on every window and hence on the full algebra. (A cross term, for instance a hopping $a^{*}_{j}a'_{j} + a'^{*}_{j}a_{j}$ between partner modes, would break this invariance whenever $\beta \neq \beta'$; the check verifies that too.) If it were KMS at some $\beta''$ for the joint flow, uniqueness on windows would make it the Gibbs state at $\beta''$, forcing $\beta = \beta'' = \beta'$ on any mode of nonzero frequency. It is the Gibbs state at $\beta$ of $H_{F} + (\beta'/\beta)H'_{F'}$, that is, KMS at $\beta$ for the flow that runs the mirror's modes at rate $\beta'/\beta$.

(iii) The exchange of the towers through $u$ maps $\omega_{\beta} \otimes \omega'_{\beta'}$ to $\omega_{\beta'} \otimes \omega'_{\beta}$, so the half-sum is invariant, and it is invariant under the joint flow by (ii). For one mode $a$ of this tower and its partner $a'$, the half-sum gives $\omega(N N') = n_{\beta}n_{\beta'}$ while $\omega(N)\omega(N') = \bigl(\tfrac12(n_{\beta} + n_{\beta'})\bigr)^{2}$, unequal when $\beta \neq \beta'$; every cross two-point function, anomalous ones included, vanishes, so a quasi-free state would factorise these by Wick's theorem, and the half-sum is not quasi-free. By uniqueness on windows it is KMS for the joint flow at no $\beta$, the Gibbs state at any $\beta''$ being a product. Each component has temperature ratio $\beta/\beta' \neq 1$.

\emph{The reading under (M).} If T14's $\xi$ (Proposition \claimref{dark-tower-matter.mirrors-temperature-p2}) is the ratio of these KMS temperatures and the descent's deposited state lives on the two algebras, then (i) gives $\xi = 1$ and $\Delta\Neff = 7.45$ for a descent in equilibrium for the shared clock, without applying P2 to the deposit, and the cap $\xi < 0.448$ of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.open-channels-their-costs} can be met only by a joint state outside equilibrium, which (ii) keeps stationary. This paragraph is conditional on (M), which the store does not supply.

The checks verify (i) to (iii) exactly on a window of two modes per tower (frequencies $\omega$ and $2\omega$, Gibbs parameters as exact rationals), realising the modes in $\Cl(4,4)$ with the scalar part as normalised trace and the KMS identity tested as $\rho A = \tau_{J\beta}(A)\rho$, $\tau_{J\beta}(A) = e^{-\beta H}Ae^{\beta H}$, on the generators ($J = e_{2}e_{3}$ the complex unit of the boundary modes of the radial plane, \claimref{synthesis.transverse-bivector-is-the-complex-unit-of-the-radial-atom}), and verify that a cross term between the towers destroys the stationarity used in (ii), the obstruction being exactly the occupation gap $n_{\beta} - n_{\beta'}$ of the two partner modes. A separate check confirms the exact content of the numbers quoted under (M): $7.447$ rounds to $7.45$, exceeds the cap $0.3$ by more than a factor $24$, and $7.447\,\xi^{4} \le 0.3$ holds at $\xi = 0.448$ and fails at $0.449$.
