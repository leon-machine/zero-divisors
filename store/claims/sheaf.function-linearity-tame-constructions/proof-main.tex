(1) By inspection of each construction. The ideal $J = \shA k$ is generated by multiplication; the coupling $\kappa$ and the ambient form $h$ are built pointwise from $G$, $k$, $u$, $\beta$, $\mu$, $\nu$ by tensor operations; the Clifford algebra of a quadratic module is a functor (\claimref{std-algebra.functoriality}) applied fibrewise, and Lemma \claimref{sheaf.blade-basis-degenerate-forms-included} shows its blade frames are frames over $C^\infty$; the ladder pair is a pair of sections of the ambient module; the exceptional rescaling multiplies sections by powers of a defining function; and the weighted module is spanned over $C^\infty(\Mt_u)$ by named one-forms. No derivative of a coefficient function enters at any stage: derivatives appear in this paper only in the analysis of the log obstruction (Section \ref{subsec:smoothstructure}), which concerns the function algebra, not the constructions over it.

(2) Composition: $(D \circ D')(fs) = D(fD's) = f(D \circ D')(s)$. Sums and function multiples: immediate. Tensor operations: a $C^\infty$-bilinear pairing of $C^\infty$-linear data induces a $C^\infty$-linear map on the tensor product over $C^\infty$; Clifford multiplication is $C^\infty$-bilinear because the structure constants in a blade frame are functions of the Gram entries (Lemma \claimref{sheaf.blade-basis-degenerate-forms-included}). Submodules and quotients: a $C^\infty$-linear map preserving a $C^\infty$-submodule restricts and descends $C^\infty$-linearly, and each named submodule (the ideal, the even part, $\shW$) is $C^\infty$-spanned, so preservation is checked on spanning sections.

(3) A $C^\infty$-linear endomorphism $D$ of a bundle is tensorial: if $s(x) = 0$ then locally $s = \sum_i f_i t_i$ with $f_i(x) = 0$ and so $(Ds)(x) = \sum_i f_i(x)(Dt_i)(x) = 0$, whence $(Ds)(x)$ depends only on $s(x)$ and $D$ is a bundle map; on a line bundle a bundle map is multiplication by a function. That the quantifier cannot be widened is shown by any nonzero derivation: $d$ composed with a contraction is an endomorphism of sections that differentiates coefficients, and nothing in this paper constructs one.
