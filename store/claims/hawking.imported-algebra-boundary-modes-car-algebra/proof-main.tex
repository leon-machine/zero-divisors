For even transport charge, the completed complexified limit of the tower of iterated completions is $\Cl(p,q)_\C \otimes \CAR$, the ground algebra tensored with the CAR algebra of countably many fermionic modes, and the mode operators are the adjoined null pairs themselves ($a_j = v_j$, $a_j^\dagger = v_j^*$, with the Fock adjointness). Here the \emph{transport charge} is the mod-8 invariant the tower's completions advance and the \emph{lineage} is the tower of iterated completions itself, both as in \cite{ButlerT3}; the physical lineage has charge six, so the theorem applies. \emph{Provenance:} the tower paper \cite[Prop.~4.1, Thms.~5.3 and 5.6]{ButlerT3} (\claimref{tower.algebraic-limit-even-charge}, \claimref{tower.car-limit-even-charge}, \claimref{tower.ladder-pairs-satisfy-car-vectors}).
