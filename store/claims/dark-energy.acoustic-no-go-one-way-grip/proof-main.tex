In the conformal vacuum, Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-conformal-orthogonality-sugawara-construction} adds the orthogonality of quasi-primaries of unequal dimension, which makes the overlap with every probe of dimension other than two vanish whatever its charges; the two arguments together leave $T$ as the unique quasi-primary with nonzero overlap. In the thermal state the dimension argument is not available: $\varphi_\beta(T) \neq 0$ is already a nonvanishing pairing between dimensions two and zero, and for a charged ensemble ($\varphi(J_0) \neq 0$, which breaks $C$) the energy-charge covariance $\varphi(L_0 J_0) - \varphi(L_0)\varphi(J_0)$ is a nonzero thermodynamic derivative. This is why the hypothesis is the symmetry of the state and not the vanishing of two-point functions by dimension, and why the $C$-symmetric charge-zero sector is named. What remains after the symmetries are used is the $C$-even neutral part of the algebra, which for the $u(1)$ current algebra of Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-s-wave-current-algebra} is generated as an algebra by the normal-ordered bilinears of the currents; $T$ belongs to it, and so do the quartic and higher $C$-even neutral products, which in the thermal state, where the dimension argument is unavailable, can overlap $T$.
