\emph{(i).} By Theorem \claimref{synthesis.boundary-mode-thermal-entropy}, $s(x) = \ln(1 + e^{-x}) + x\,n$ with $n = 1/(e^{x} + 1)$, so $s(x) - x\,n = \ln(1 + e^{-x}) > 0$: in normal order $\beta E = x\,n < s(x)$. In the symmetric order of Proposition \claimref{hawking.modular-structure-completed-pair}(4), $\tfrac12\sigma = N - \tfrac12$, so $\beta E = x(n - \tfrac12)$, and $n - \tfrac12 = (e^{-x} - 1)/(2(1 + e^{-x})) = -\tfrac12\tanh\tfrac{x}{2}$, which has the sign opposite to $x$; hence $\beta E \le 0 < s(x)$. Summing over the counted modes and using $\sum_{j}s(x_{j}) = S = A/4$, $\beta E < S$ under either ordering, and $TS = (1/8\pi M)(4\pi M^{2}) = M/2$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy}(v)). At $x = 0$ both $x\,n$ and $x(n - \tfrac12)$ vanish. The check verifies the two sign identities for rational $q = e^{-x}$ and, with rational enclosures of $\pi$, $e^{2\pi}$ and $\ln(1 + e^{-2\pi})$, that $2\pi n(2\pi) < s(2\pi) \in (0.013577, 0.013578)$ and $n(2\pi) < \tfrac12$.

\emph{(ii).} The mode operators of $m$ completed pairs obey the canonical anticommutation relations (Proposition \claimref{scrambling.boundary-mode-space-matrix}); the check builds them in $\Cl(m,m)$ for $m = 1, 2, 3$, forms the vacuum $\prod_{j}v_{j}v_{j}^{*}$, confirms that every $v_{j}$ annihilates it, and finds that the $2^{m}$ monomials $v_{j_{1}}^{*}\cdots v_{j_{k}}^{*}$ applied to it are linearly independent, so $\dim\mathcal{H}_{m} = 2^{m}$. With all weights zero the Killing generator $\sum_{j}0\cdot K_{j}$ vanishes, so the counted modes' Killing excitation energy is zero in either ordering. At zero weight the gluing condition of (R) reads $n = 1 - n$ for every period (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.boundary-mode-thermal-entropy}: the one mode at which (R) fixes the occupancy without a temperature), so every marginal has $n = \tfrac12$; the state $2^{-m}\mathbf{1}$ has these marginals and entropy $m\ln 2$. With $M = \mu(m)$, $4\pi M^{2} = m\ln 2 = S$, and $A/4 = 16\pi M^{2}/4 = 4\pi M^{2}$, so $S = A/4$ with every counted mode at zero weight: the equality case of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy}(ii). The microcanonical entropy of $H$ at the eigenvalue $\mu(m)$ is $\ln\dim\mathcal{H}_{m} = m\ln 2 = 4\pi M^{2}$ ($\mu$ is strictly increasing, so the eigenvalue has the one sector). Along the family $dS/dM = 8\pi M$ and $T(M) = 1/8\pi M$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.quarter-from-temperature}), so $dS = dM/T$, the Clausius relation, with $S(0) = 0$; and $2TS = 2(1/8\pi M)(4\pi M^{2}) = M$. One added counted mode changes $S$ by $\ln 2$ and so $M$ by $T\ln 2$ to first order. $H$ is a function of $\hat m$, which is constant on each $\mathcal{H}_{m}$ and so commutes with every mode operator; the check confirms the thermodynamic identities for several rational stand-ins of $\pi$ and $M$, and that the zero-weight generator vanishes.

\emph{(iii).} $\mu(m)^{2} = m\ln 2/4\pi < m/16$ since $\ln 2/4\pi < 1/16$ (checked with rational enclosures), so for $m = k^{2}$ the exponent of $2^{m}e^{-\beta\mu(m)}$ is at least $k^{2}\ln 2 - \beta k/4$, which grows without bound in $k$ for every $\beta > 0$ (checked to be increasing and large past $k = \beta$ for $\beta = 1, 10, 1000$); the series diverges and no Gibbs state of $H$ exists. From $T = 1/8\pi M$, $dT/dM = -1/8\pi M^{2}$, so $dM/dT = -8\pi M^{2}$ (the check confirms the sign and the exact finite difference).

\emph{(iv).} With the enclosures of (i), $2\pi n(2\pi)/s(2\pi) \in (0.8625, 0.8627)$; a counted set mixing zero-weight modes and modes at $x = 2\pi$ has $\beta E = \sum_{j}x_{j}n_{j} \le 0.8627\sum_{j}s(x_{j}) < S$.

The model assumes the area law in the form of $\mu$: it shows that the hypotheses of the two theorems are jointly satisfiable at the equality case, the zero-weight case of the canonical reading filed alongside (synthesis.hole-mass-is-ground-energy-label), and names a carrier (a central label), but it derives neither $\mu$ nor the identification of the series' boundary modes with its sectors.
