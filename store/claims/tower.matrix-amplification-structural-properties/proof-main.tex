Parts (i) and (ii) are \claimref{std-algebra.morita-amplification}(i) and (ii); the computation runs as follows. (i) Let $z = (z_{ij})$ be central. The entries of $z E_{kl} = E_{kl} z$ are $(z E_{kl})_{ij} = z_{ik}\,\delta_{lj}$ and $(E_{kl} z)_{ij} = \delta_{ik}\, z_{lj}$; equality for all $i, j, k, l$ forces $z_{ik} = 0$ for $i \neq k$ and $z_{kk} = z_{ll}$, so $z = a \cdot 1_m$ with $a := z_{11}$, and commuting with $b \cdot 1_m$ for all $b \in A$ forces $a \in Z(A)$. The converse inclusion is clear. (ii) A nonzero two-sided ideal $I$ contains some $x$ with an entry $x_{kl} = a \neq 0$; then $E_{ik}\, x\, E_{lj} = a E_{ij} \in I$ for all $i, j$, so $I \supseteq \Mat_m(AaA) = \Mat_m(A)$, since $AaA = A$ by simplicity. (iii) and (iv) are regroupings of indices and entrywise splittings.
