$c_0^a$ for the three schemes of \claimref{scrambling.scheme-robustness-cutoff} is $2^{a/3}$, $2^{2a/3}3^{a/6}$, $2^{-a/3}3^{2a/3}$; their ratios $2^{a/3}3^{a/6}$ and $2^{-2a/3}3^{2a/3}$ are not $1$ for real $a \neq 0$, so a count carrying $r_c^a$ takes different values in different schemes, and a scheme-dependent count cannot equal the scheme-independent $\SBH$ in all of them; for rational $a$ the Kretschmann constant $48^{a/6} = 2^{2a/3}\,3^{a/6}$ is rational exactly when both exponents are integers, that is when $a \in 6\mathbb{Z}$, and is otherwise irrational (at $a = 3/2$, for instance, it is $2\cdot 3^{1/4}$). The Schwarzschild interior has one dimensionful parameter, $M$, so a cutoff-free radius is a fixed multiple of $M$; the horizon is the distinguished one, with residue $\tfrac14$ (\claimref{scrambling.residue-each-convention}(i)), and $r_* = M$ is the one at which the residue is $1$, the sphere's cell count there being $\SBH$ itself; the script displays both as arithmetic. The argument excludes the cutoff; it does not rank the two.
