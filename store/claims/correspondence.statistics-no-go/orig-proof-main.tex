In a representation on a nonzero finite-dimensional space $U$, take the trace of $[Z^1, \bar Z_1] = \hbar\,\mathrm{id}_U$: the left side has trace zero (the trace of any commutator of endomorphisms), the right side $\hbar\dim U \neq 0$. For the embedding statement: a unital embedding $\mathcal{P} \hookrightarrow A$ into a finite-dimensional algebra composes with the left regular representation of $A$ to give a finite-dimensional faithful representation of $\mathcal{P}$, which does not exist. The contrast with the fermionic side is instructive and exact: the CAR relation $ff^* + f^*f = 1$ is an \emph{anti}commutator, to which the trace argument does not apply, and indeed the ladder pairs lie in the $64$-dimensional $\Cl(2,4)$ (Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-ladder-pairs-fock-modules-over}). Bose statistics forbids finite dimensions; Fermi statistics is built from them.
