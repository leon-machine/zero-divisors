Let $V = \R^{2,4}$ be the ambient space of the conformal completion, $V_{\C} = W \oplus \overline{W}$ with $W$ a maximal isotropic subspace, $\dim W = 3$. The complexified algebra $\Cl(V_{\C}) \cong \mathrm{M}_{8}(\C)$ acts irreducibly on $\Lambda^{\bullet}W$, whose chirality halves are $\CS = \Lambda^{\mathrm{even}}W$ and $\CSm = \Lambda^{\mathrm{odd}}W$, the conformal spinor space and its opposite chirality (twistor space and its dual); there is a canonical isomorphism $V_{\C} \cong \Lambda^{2}\CS$ of $\mathrm{Spin}$-modules under which the quadratic form is $P \mapsto \varepsilon(P \wedge P)$ for a volume functional $\varepsilon$ on $\Lambda^{4}\CS$, and a vector is null exactly when its bivector is decomposable. In the chart $\CS = \Som \oplus \Spi$, $Z = (\omega^{A}, \pi_{A'})$, the bivector of $\Som$ is the point at infinity $n_{\infty}$. The opposite chirality is the conjugate of the conformal spinor space: the reality structure pairs $\CS$ with $\CSm$ and induces on $\CS$ the invariant Hermitian form (the twistor form) of signature $(2,2)$ preserved by $\mathrm{Spin}(2,4) \cong SU(2,2)$. The $\mathrm{Spin}$-invariant bilinear pairing of the module with itself is the top-degree pairing, pairing $\CS$ with $\CSm$ (degrees summing to three), twisted by the reversal sign on one argument. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thms.~3.1, 3.4, Prop.~4.5]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.reality-conjugation-twistor-form-factor}, \claimref{correspondence.fock-model-complexified-ascended-algebra}, \claimref{correspondence.spinor-identification}); proved there for three modes; the five-mode case is proved in Lemma \claimref{standard-model.invariant-pairing-five-modes} below.
