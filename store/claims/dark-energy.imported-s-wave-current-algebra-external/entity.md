## formal
The results from outside the series on which \claimref{dark-energy.imported-s-wave-current-algebra} draws, as cited there: A. Pressley and G. Segal, Loop Groups, Oxford University Press (1986)..

## technical
This entry records the one ingredient from outside the series on which the s-wave current algebra fact draws: the boson-fermion correspondence of loop-group theory. In the form used, the charge-zero sector of the fermionic Fock space of a chiral fermion with a filled sea is canonically the bosonic Fock space of its current algebra, with the fermions recovered as vertex operators.

## lay
A standard result of mathematical physics says that a one-dimensional system of fermions with a filled sea can be described equally well as a system of bosons. This entry is the citation of that result, which the current algebra entry uses to identify the two descriptions.

## one-liner
The boson-fermion correspondence, cited from Pressley and Segal, identifies the charge-zero fermionic Fock space with the bosonic Fock space.
