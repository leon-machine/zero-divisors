## formal
In a two-dimensional conformal field theory: the two-point function of quasi-primary operators of unequal scaling dimension vanishes in the conformal vacuum; and the stress tensor of a current algebra is the Sugawara bilinear of the currents, the dimension-two member of the vacuum family. \emph{Provenance:} textbook \cite[Ch.~4-5 for the two-point functions, Ch.~15 for the Sugawara construction]{DiFrancesco1997}.

## technical
Two textbook facts of two-dimensional conformal field theory. In the conformal vacuum the two-point function of quasi-primary operators of unequal scaling dimension vanishes. And the stress tensor of a current algebra is the Sugawara bilinear of the currents, the dimension-two member of the vacuum family.

## lay
In two-dimensional theories with full conformal symmetry, operators that scale differently cannot be correlated in the vacuum. For a theory built from currents, the stress tensor is a product of two currents. Both are standard results taken from the textbook literature.

## one-liner
In two-dimensional conformal field theory, quasi-primaries of unequal dimension are orthogonal in the conformal vacuum, and a current algebra's stress tensor is the Sugawara bilinear of its currents.
