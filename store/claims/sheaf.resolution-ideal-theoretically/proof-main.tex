In $(\mathbb{W}, Q)$ the vector $k$ is null but no longer radical ($Q$ is nondegenerate), so there is a null partner: pick $v$ with $Q(k, v) \neq 0$, rescale so that $Q(k,v) = \tfrac12$, and set $k^* := v - Q(v,v)\,k$; then $Q(k^*, k^*) = Q(v,v) - 2\,Q(v,v)\,Q(k,v) = 0$ and $Q(k, k^*) = Q(k,v) = \tfrac12$ (this is the hyperbolic-pair construction of \cite[\S 2.2]{ButlerT1}, reproved here in one line). The Clifford relation (\claimref{std-algebra.vector-squares}) then gives in $\Cl(\mathbb{W}, Q)$ the identity
\[
k\,k^* + k^*\,k \;=\; 1 .
\]
Both summands lie in the two-sided ideal generated by $k$; hence so does $1$.
