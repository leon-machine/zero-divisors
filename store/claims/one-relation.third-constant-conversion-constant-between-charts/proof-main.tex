(i) The null cone is $\{Q = 0\}$, preserved by isometries by definition; it contains a nonzero vector iff $pq \neq 0$ (the root paper's null-locus criterion, Fact \claimref{one-relation.imported-null-cone-determines-form-zeemans}); the script checks that $Q/c^{2} = \mathrm{diag}(1, -c^{-2}, -c^{-2}, -c^{-2})$ has signature $(1,3)$ at $c = 1, 10, 1000$ and that at $c = \infty$ the form is $\mathrm{diag}(1, 0, 0, 0)$, of rank one with radical the simultaneity hyperplane. (ii) is Witt's extension theorem (Fact \claimref{one-relation.imported-witts-extension-theorem}), which the dark-matter paper imports and Proposition \claimref{one-relation.two-capacities-three-consequences} uses: two hyperbolic planes are isometric, so the isometry between them extends to an isometry of $(V, Q)$. The script takes three rational null pairs of $(1,3)$, $n \cdot m = -1$, spanning three pairwise distinct hyperbolic planes, and computes that their anisotropic complements all have signature $(0,2)$, the witness the theorem needs; the theorem itself is imported. (iii) The rotor, the exponential of a boost bivector (\claimref{std-algebra.bivector-exponential}), has its identities computed exactly in $\Cl(1,1)$ at $\lambda = e^{\eta} = 4$, $\cosh\eta = \tfrac{17}{8}$, $\sinh\eta = \tfrac{15}{8}$: $R\tilde{R} = 1$, $Re_{0}\tilde{R} = \cosh\eta\,e_{0} + \sinh\eta\,e_{1}$ with norms preserved, $Rf_{\pm}\tilde{R} = \lambda^{\pm 1}f_{\pm}$, the null lines the fixed lines of every boost, and the velocity $\tanh\eta = (\lambda^{2} - 1)/(\lambda^{2} + 1) = \tfrac{15}{17}$; the sign convention matters, the opposite sign in the rotor reversing the boost, and the script fixes it as stated. (iv) The retraction $(a, b) \mapsto (a, tb)$, $t \in [0, 1]$, on $\{Q > 0\}$ written as $|a|^{2} - |b|^{2} > 0$ in an orthonormal basis, deformation-retracts it onto $\{|a| = 1, b = 0\} \cong S^{p-1}$; $S^{0}$ has two components, $S^{k}$ for $k \geq 1$ one, and $S^{-1}$ is empty. The script checks the component counts on sample points: on the signatures $(1,0)$ to $(3,2)$ and $(0,3)$ it counts the classes of a union-find over rational sample points of $\{Q > 0\}$ joined by exact segments with certified $Q$-minima, compares the count with the $S^{p-1}$ count entered as a literal, certifies two classes as distinct by the sign of the time coordinate, and checks the disjunction against $\min(p, q) = 1$ on seven signatures up to $n = 5$; the proof of the count is the retraction above, not the script; the cone-determines-form clause of Fact \claimref{one-relation.imported-null-cone-determines-form-zeemans} is also checked, on the signatures up to $(3,3)$, as the one-dimensionality of the space of symmetric forms vanishing on the rational null vectors. The exponent determinant is arithmetic the script performs, together with the statement that no monomial in $\hbar$ and $G$ alone has the dimension of a length, a time, or a mass. Nothing here derives a value for $c$: its number is a unit convention, and in the Planck system all three are; the only derivable things are dimensionless ratios, which the capstone examines.
