The computations are short enough to print. In the rescaled frame, using Theorem \claimref{sheaf.schwarzschild-degeneration-boundary-tame}'s Gram: $G(\rho, k/\sqrt r) = (G(\rho,\rho) + m\,G(\rho,\dtPG))/\sqrt r = ((m^2 - r) - m^2)/\sqrt r = -\sqrt r \to 0$, and $G(k/\sqrt r, k/\sqrt r) = g_k/r = -1$ exactly, so the limit Gram is $\mathrm{diag}(m^2, -1, -1, -1)$. For the residue, invert the $(\rho, \dtPG)$ block $\begin{psmallmatrix} m^2 - r & -m \\ -m & 1 \end{psmallmatrix}$, of determinant $-r$: the inverse is exactly $-\tfrac1r \begin{psmallmatrix} 1 & m \\ m & m^2 - r \end{psmallmatrix}$, so $r\,G^{-1} \to -\begin{psmallmatrix} 1 & m \\ m & m^2 \end{psmallmatrix} = -\,k \otimes k$ with $k = \rho + m\,\dtPG$, a rank-one matrix, while the angular block contributes $r \cdot O(1) \to 0$. All of this is confirmed as rational-function identities in the script; the invariance clauses are Lemma \claimref{sheaf.invariant-form-t3} and Theorem \claimref{sheaf.metrics-residue-square-root-twist}, applied one-sidedly per Remark \ref{rem:boundarytame} and read in $N^*_rE$ per the Conventions.
