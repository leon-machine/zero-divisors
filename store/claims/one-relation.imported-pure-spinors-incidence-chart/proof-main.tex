The complexified ascended algebra $\Cl(2,4)_{\C}$ acts irreducibly on the Fock space $\Lambda^{\bullet}W$ of a maximal isotropic $W \subset \C^{6}$, with the conformal spinor space $\CS$ (twistor space) its even half; a nonzero conformal spinor $u$ has annihilator $W_{u} = \{v : c(v)u = 0\}$ of dimension exactly three, so every conformal spinor is the Fock vacuum of its own three-pair system, and $W_{u}$ determines $u$ up to scale. This is Cartan's purity theorem in the case $2\nu = 6$ (Fact \claimref{one-relation.imported-cartans-purity-theorem-thouless-form}; \claimref{std-algebra.pure-spinors}(iii) at $k = 3$, read over $\C$): every Weyl spinor of $\mathrm{Spin}(6, \C)$ is pure, the vacuum orbit being open in $\PCS = \mathbb{P}(\CS) = \mathbb{P}^{3}$ by the exceptional isomorphism $\mathrm{Spin}(6, \C) \cong SL(4, \C)$, which is the transitivity on which the twistor correspondence (of the conformal spinor space with spacetime) rests; it is a low-dimensional coincidence and not a generic property of Fock vacua, purity becoming one quadratic condition at $2\nu = 8$ (Remark \ref{rem:quadric}). A point of conformally compactified spacetime is a null vector of $\C^{6}$, equivalently a simple bivector of $\Lambda^{2}\CS$, that is, a line of the projective spinor space $\PCS$; the point embedding $X(x) = n_{0} + x - \tfrac12 Q(x)n_{\infty}$, with $\ip{n_{0}}{n_{\infty}} = 1$, is null identically, with the slot coefficient $-\tfrac12$ the only value for which it is, and the incidence relation is the completion's own chart. The completion's fibre quadric double covers the real slice of the Klein quadric. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thm.~3.1, Thm.~3.4, Thm.~4.2, Thm.~5.3]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.fock-model-complexified-ascended-algebra}, \claimref{correspondence.twistors-are-states-three-ladder-pairs}, \claimref{correspondence.incidence-relation-chart}, \claimref{correspondence.spinor-identification}); \cite[Fact~2.3, Rem.~5.2]{ButlerT11} (\claimref{mass.imported-flat-model-twistor-chart-infinity}).
