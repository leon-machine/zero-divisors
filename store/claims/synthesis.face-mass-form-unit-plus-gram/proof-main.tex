The modes are bosonic (Fact \claimref{families.imported-squeezed-vacuum-bosonic-pairing}), $[a_{p}, a_{q}^{\dagger}] = \delta_{pq}$. Fix $c \in \C^{3}$ and write $\Theta = \Theta(c)$, a complex symmetric $6 \times 6$ matrix since each $\Theta_{k}$ is real symmetric, and $\hat\Omega = \hat\Omega(c) = \sum_{pq}\Theta_{pq}a_{p}a_{q}$, so $\hat\Omega^{\dagger} = \sum_{rs}\bar\Theta_{rs}a_{s}^{\dagger}a_{r}^{\dagger}$. From $[a_{p}a_{q}, a_{s}^{\dagger}a_{r}^{\dagger}] = \delta_{qs}\delta_{pr} + \delta_{qr}\delta_{ps} + \delta_{pr}a_{s}^{\dagger}a_{q} + \delta_{ps}a_{r}^{\dagger}a_{q} + \delta_{qr}a_{s}^{\dagger}a_{p} + \delta_{qs}a_{r}^{\dagger}a_{p}$ and the symmetry of $\Theta$,
\[
[\hat\Omega, \hat\Omega^{\dagger}] \;=\; 2\,\mathrm{tr}(\Theta\bar\Theta) \;+\; 4\,a^{\dagger}\,\Theta^{\dagger}\Theta\,a ,
\]
with $a^{\dagger}Xa = \sum_{pq}a_{p}^{\dagger}X_{pq}a_{q}$. Now $\mathrm{tr}(\Theta\bar\Theta) = \sum_{kl}c_{k}\bar{c}_{l}\,\mathrm{tr}(\Theta_{k}\Theta_{l}) = |c|^{2}$, because $\mathrm{tr}(\Theta_{k}\Theta_{l}) = \tfrac14\mathrm{tr}(E_{k}E_{l})\,\mathrm{tr}(\varepsilon\varepsilon) = \delta_{kl}$ (Theorem \claimref{families.skeleton-rank-one-plus-ordering-shift}, proof), and $\langle a^{\dagger}\Theta^{\dagger}\Theta a\rangle = \sum_{p}\|(\Theta a)_{p}\psi\|^{2}$ with $(\Theta a)_{p} = \sum_{q}\Theta_{pq}a_{q}$. Contracting the mass form with $c$,
\[
\bar{c}^{\mathsf{T}}Hc \;=\; \tfrac12\bigl\langle\hat\Omega^{\dagger}\hat\Omega + \hat\Omega\hat\Omega^{\dagger}\bigr\rangle
\;=\; \langle\hat\Omega^{\dagger}\hat\Omega\rangle + \tfrac12\bigl\langle[\hat\Omega, \hat\Omega^{\dagger}]\bigr\rangle
\;=\; |c|^{2} + \|\hat\Omega\psi\|^{2} + 2\sum_{p}\|(\Theta a)_{p}\psi\|^{2} \;=\; |c|^{2} + \|Wc\|^{2},
\]
using $\sum_{kl}\bar{c}_{k}\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}c_{l} = \hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger}\hat\Omega(c)$ and $\sum_{kl}\bar{c}_{k}\hat\Omega_{l}\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}c_{l} = \hat\Omega(c)\hat\Omega(c)^{\dagger}$. Since $W$ is linear in $c$, polarisation gives $H = \mathbb{1} + W^{\dagger}W$, whose entries are the displayed $\delta_{kl} + \langle\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}\rangle + 2\langle a^{\dagger}\Theta_{k}\Theta_{l}a\rangle$ (the $\Theta_{k}$ being real). Mixed states follow by linearity of $H$ in the state, $H = \sum_{i}p_{i}(\mathbb{1} + W_{i}^{\dagger}W_{i})$. (i) and (ii) are immediate: $\bar{c}^{\mathsf{T}}(H - \mathbb{1})c = \|Wc\|^{2} \ge 0$, vanishing exactly when both components of $Wc$ vanish, and the identity commutes with every unitary of face space. (iii): $\hat\Omega_{k}\psi = \omega_{k}\psi + (\hat\Omega_{k} - \omega_{k})\psi$ with the second vector orthogonal to $\psi$, so $\langle\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}\rangle = \bar\omega_{k}\omega_{l} + K_{kl}$, and $K$ and $\mathcal{N}$ are Gram matrices of the vectors $(\hat\Omega_{l} - \omega_{l})\psi$ and $((\Theta_{l}a)_{p}\psi)_{p}$. In the Gaussian class the theorem's computation gives $\langle\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}\rangle = 2x(2 + 3x)\bar{n}_{k}n_{l}$ and $\bar\omega_{k}\omega_{l} = 4x(1 + x)\bar{n}_{k}n_{l}$, so $K = 2x^{2}P_{[\bar v]}$, and its ordering term $c^{2}\delta_{kl} + x\bar{n}_{k}n_{l} = \delta_{kl} + 2\mathcal{N}_{kl}$ gives $2\mathcal{N} = x(\mathbb{1} + P_{[\bar v]})$.

The script works in the unnormalised monomial basis of the Fock space, where every amplitude is a complex rational: it checks $\mathrm{tr}(\Theta_{k}\Theta_{l}) = \delta_{kl}$ and the symmetry of $\Theta_{k}$, and verifies $H_{kl} = \langle\psi|\psi\rangle\delta_{kl} + \langle\hat\Omega_{k}\psi, \hat\Omega_{l}\psi\rangle + 2\sum_{p}\langle(\Theta_{k}a)_{p}\psi, (\Theta_{l}a)_{p}\psi\rangle$ entry by entry, exactly, on four states including non-Gaussian superpositions of up to five monomials with complex rational coefficients.
