Unitarity: $\|D_{t}f\|^{2} = e^{-\kappa t}\int|f(e^{-\kappa t}U)|^{2}dU = \int|f|^{2}$, and $D_{s}D_{t} = D_{s+t}$, so $t \mapsto D_{t}$ is a strongly continuous unitary group; by the CAR functor (Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.continuum-mode-space}) $a(f) \mapsto a(D_{t}f)$ is a quasi-free one-parameter group of $*$-automorphisms, a time evolution in the sense of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.phase-flow-is-time-rotor-flow-kms-continuation}. That it is the boost is the relation $\Delta^{it}U(a)\Delta^{-it} = U(e^{-2\pi t}a)$ of Fact \claimref{angle.imported-theorems-deliver-their-hypotheses}: with modular parameter $s$ corresponding to Killing time $t = -2\pi s/\kappa$ (rapidity $\kappa t$), the boost rescales null translations of the affine parameter by $e^{\kappa t}$, and on functions of $U$ this is $D_{t}$ (the sign of $t$ selects which of the two null directions is stretched; the statement is for the stretched one).

(i) Take $\delta = 1$ (the general case by scaling $U$). For $e^{\kappa t} = m$, $(D_{t}\chi_{j})(U) = m^{-1/2}\chi_{j}(U/m)$, which is $m^{-1/2}$ on $[mj, mj + m)$ and zero elsewhere, i.e.\ $m^{-1/2}\sum_{l=0}^{m-1}\chi_{mj+l}$. Iterating, at $e^{\kappa t} = m^{n}$ the support is $m^{n}$ consecutive cells with amplitude $m^{-n/2}$ each; setting $m^{n} = N$ gives $\kappa t = \ln N$. The script checks this exactly for $m = 4$ (amplitude $1/2$), $n = 1, \dots, 4$, two starting cells, and the flow property $D_{\ln 4}D_{\ln 4} = D_{\ln 16}$, on functions constant on unit cells, together with isometry on a generic cell-constant vector.

(ii) The CAR give $\{a(f), a^{*}(g)\} = \langle g, f\rangle$. By (i), $\langle\chi_{k}, D_{t}\chi_{j}\rangle$ is $e^{-\kappa t/2}$ for the $e^{\kappa t}$ cells in the support and zero otherwise, and the sum of squares is $\|D_{t}\chi_{j}\|^{2} = 1$. For even observables the same overlaps bound the commutators, for example $[a^{*}(D_{t}\chi_{j})a(D_{t}\chi_{j}), a^{*}(\chi_{k})] = \langle\chi_{k}, D_{t}\chi_{j}\rangle\,a^{*}(D_{t}\chi_{j})$, of norm at most $e^{-\kappa t/2}$. The script checks that every overlap with a fixed cell is $0$ or $2^{-n}$.

(iii) For $e^{\kappa t} = m$ and the indicator $1_{i}$ of $[m^{i}, m^{i+1})$, $D_{t}1_{i} = m^{-1/2}1_{i+1}$, and $\|1_{i+1}\|^{2} = m\|1_{i}\|^{2}$, so the normalised indicators satisfy $D_{t}\hat{1}_{i} = \hat{1}_{i+1}$. Checked for $m = 4$, $i = 0, \dots, 3$.

(iv) For $j \ge 1$ the cell $j$ is not among $mj, \dots, mj + m - 1$, so $\langle\chi_{j}, D_{t}\chi_{j}\rangle = 0$: the flow is not diagonal in the cell basis, and its generator moves occupation between cells (a hopping generator in the sense of Premise \claimref{singularity.l-boundary-mode-locality}, which excludes such generators for the ladders at points of $E$, not on a horizon generator). Checked for three cells.

Comparison with the stored results. Assumption \claimref{scrambling.spreading-law-chaos-bound-saturated} derives $t_{*} = \kappa^{-1}\ln N$ from amplitudes growing as $e^{\kappa t}$, and Theorem \claimref{synthesis.phase-flow-is-time-rotor-flow-kms-continuation} shows that on boost-eigenmode windows that growth is the rotor flow's, an imaginary-time rate. Here the real-time phase flow reaches the same count by spreading amplitudes that shrink. Both readings agree that no local anticommutator grows under quadratic dynamics, so neither supplies chaos-bound saturation; what (i) adds is that the count itself is available in real time, in the affine-cell basis of the horizon.
