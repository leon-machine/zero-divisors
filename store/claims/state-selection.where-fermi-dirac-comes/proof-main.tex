The value $R_{2\pi} = -1$ is Proposition \claimref{state-selection.euclidean-partner-curvature-selected}(2). Conjugation by $-1$ is trivial, so the even sector, which is where the metric-level observables live by Theorem \claimref{singularity.descent-parity-average}, is periodic. On ladder words the rotor acts factor by factor, each ladder generator taken as a vector of the spinor module, where $-1$ acts as $-1$, so a word of odd length changes sign: this factorwise action is the grading automorphism, $+1$ on $\Aev$ and $-1$ on $\Aod$ (the conjugation action of $-1$ is trivial on both sectors); the antiperiodicity of odd correlation functions around the modular period is the standard consequence, and the Matsubara reading of that antiperiodicity is the Fermi-Dirac factor. The last step is the standard reading and is flagged as such rather than proved here.
