Nullity: $G(n_\pm, n_\pm) = -1 + 1 = 0$ by Proposition \claimref{sheaf.rain-duality}(2). Components: clause (1) of the duality. The gauge map $L(\dtPG) = \dtPG$, $L(dr) = dr - \beta\,\dtPG$, angular fixed, satisfies $\eta(La, Lb) = G(a,b)$ on all coordinate pairs, which fixes the conventions internally; inverting, $L^{-1}(\dtPG \pm dr) = (1 \pm \beta)\dtPG \pm dr = \pm\bigl(dr + (\beta \pm 1)\dtPG\bigr) = \pm\,n_\pm$. All identities exact, at two masses (Section \ref{sec:verification}).
