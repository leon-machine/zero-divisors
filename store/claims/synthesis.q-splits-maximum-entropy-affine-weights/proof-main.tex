\emph{(i)} Work on a finite window, realised by Jordan-Wigner on $(\C^{2})^{\otimes |W|}$. For an even state the single-mode marginal on mode $k$ is $\operatorname{diag}(1 - \langle N_{k}\rangle, \langle N_{k}\rangle)$, since the odd one-point functions vanish; for $\varrho \in \mathcal{C}$ it is $\operatorname{diag}(1 - n_{k}, n_{k})$ with $0 < n_{k} < 1$ (Corollary \claimref{singularity.chains-state-class-stability}(1)). The product $\sigma = \prod_{k}\bigl((1 - n_{k})(1 - N_{k}) + n_{k}N_{k}\bigr)$ is diagonal in the joint number basis and faithful, so
\[
\log\sigma \;=\; \sum_{k}\bigl(\log(1 - n_{k})\,(1 - N_{k}) + \log n_{k}\,N_{k}\bigr),
\]
an element of $\operatorname{span}\{1, N_{k}\}$. Every $\varrho \in \mathcal{C}$ has $\tr\varrho = 1$ and $\tr(\varrho N_{k}) = n_{k}$, the same values as $\sigma$, so $\tr(\varrho\log\sigma) = \tr(\sigma\log\sigma) = -\sum_{k}S(\sigma_{k}) = -S(\sigma)$, whence $\rel{\varrho}{\sigma} = -S(\varrho) - \tr(\varrho\log\sigma) = S(\sigma) - S(\varrho)$; and $S(\sigma) = \sum_{k}S(\varrho_{k})$ because the marginals of $\varrho$ and $\sigma$ agree, so this is $C(\varrho)$ in the notation of Corollary \claimref{singularity.deficit-carried-quanta-correlations}. The key identity has the same shape as in Theorem \claimref{singularity.deficit-theorem-cost-descent-exactly}, with $\sigma$ in place of the pinched state, and as there nonnegativity and the equality case are Klein's inequality \cite[\S 11.3]{NielsenChuang}: $C(\varrho) \geq 0$ with equality if and only if $\varrho = \sigma$. This gives (b) $\Leftrightarrow$ (d) $\Leftrightarrow$ (e). (a) $\Leftrightarrow$ (b): by Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-quasi-free-states-car-algebra} the gauge-invariant quasi-free state with diagonal symbol $\operatorname{diag}(n_{k})$ is, on a window, exactly the product $\bigotimes_{k}\operatorname{diag}(1 - n_{k}, n_{k})$, which is $\sigma$. (b) $\Leftrightarrow$ (c): $\sigma$ is the product of the Gibbs states of $\omega_{k}K_{k}$ at the common $\beta$, that is $e^{-\beta K_{\mathrm{geo}}}/\tr e^{-\beta K_{\mathrm{geo}}}$, whose modular group is the phase flow of $K_{\mathrm{geo}}$ reparametrised by $-\beta$ (Theorem \claimref{singularity.kms-uniqueness-factor}), so it satisfies (G); conversely Theorem \claimref{singularity.exact-equilibrium-over-determines-boundary-modes} shows that a state satisfying (G) is this product, and Corollary \claimref{singularity.chains-state-class-stability}(3) records that it is the only member of $\mathcal{C}$ doing so. The same Corollary (3) gives members of $\mathcal{C}$ with $C(\varrho) > 0$, so (P) and (R) alone do not imply (Q-qf). The script builds, on three modes with rational occupations, the product state, verifies that it is Gibbs for $K_{\mathrm{geo}}$, that its two-point matrix is $\operatorname{diag}(n_{k})$ and that every four-point function factorises by Wick's rule; it then builds $\varrho = \sigma + \tfrac{1}{10}(N_{0} - \tfrac12)(N_{1} - \tfrac12)$, verifies that it is positive definite (all pivots positive), lies in $\mathcal{C}$ with the same two-point matrix, violates Wick's rule at $\langle N_{0}N_{1}\rangle$, and has the same values of $1$ and $N_{k}$ as $\sigma$, which is what the identity $\tr(\varrho\log\sigma) = \tr(\sigma\log\sigma)$ uses.

\emph{(ii)} The flow acts by $e^{itK_{\mathrm{geo}}}\psi_{k}^{\dagger}\psi_{l}e^{-itK_{\mathrm{geo}}} = e^{i(\omega_{k} - \omega_{l})t}\psi_{k}^{\dagger}\psi_{l}$, the constant in $K_{k}$ dropping out. Hence $e^{itK_{\mathrm{geo}}}a_{m}e^{-itK_{\mathrm{geo}}} = \sum_{k}e^{i(\omega_{k} - \omega_{k+m})t}\psi_{k}^{\dagger}\psi_{k+m}$. The bilinears $\psi_{k}^{\dagger}\psi_{k+m}$, $k \in \Z$, are linearly independent (their one-particle images, under the injective map $X \mapsto \hat{X}$ of Proposition \claimref{scrambling.boundary-mode-space-matrix}, are distinct matrix units), and for $m \neq 0$ the bilinears on different diagonals are independent as well. So the image of $a_{m}$ is a multiple of $a_{m}$ for all $t$, and more generally lies in the span of the currents, exactly when $\omega_{k} - \omega_{k+m}$ does not depend on $k$. For $m = 1$ this says $\omega_{k+1} - \omega_{k} = c$ for all $k$, that is $\omega_{k} = ck + d$; conversely affine weights give $\omega_{k} - \omega_{k+m} = -cm$ for every $k$ and $m$, so $\lambda_{m} = cm$. The script checks the one-particle criterion $[\operatorname{diag}(\omega), S_{m}] \in \C S_{m}$ for all shifts $S_{m}$ on an eight-site window, for an affine law (true) and for three non-affine ones (a single displaced weight, a quadratic law, a staggered offset; all false), and checks on a four-mode Fock space that the second-quantised commutator $[\hat{h}, \hat{S}_{m}]$ equals $\widehat{[h, S_{m}]}$ and equals $-3m\,\hat{S}_{m}$ for the affine law $\omega_{k} = 3k - 1$.

\emph{(iii)} Write $n(x) = (e^{x} + 1)^{-1}$. For all real $x, y$, $n(x)(1 - n(y)) = e^{y}/((e^{x} + 1)(e^{y} + 1))$ and $n(y) - n(x) = (e^{x} - e^{y})/((e^{x} + 1)(e^{y} + 1))$, so $n(x)(1 - n(y)) = (n(y) - n(x))/(e^{x - y} - 1)$ whenever $x \neq y$. With $x = \beta(c(k + m) + d)$ and $y = \beta(ck + d)$, $x - y = \beta cm$ for every $k$, so
\[
n_{k+m}(1 - n_{k}) \;=\; \frac{n_{k} - n_{k+m}}{e^{\beta cm} - 1} .
\]
Summing over $-K \le k \le K$ telescopes: $\sum_{k=-K}^{K}(n_{k} - n_{k+m}) = \sum_{k=-K}^{-K+m-1}n_{k} - \sum_{k=K+1}^{K+m}n_{k}$. As $K \to \infty$ the first sum tends to $m$ and the second to $0$, since $1 - n_{k} \le e^{\beta(ck + d)}$ and $n_{k} \le e^{-\beta(ck + d)}$; the limit is $m/(e^{\beta cm} - 1)$, independent of $d$. By Wick's rule for the quasi-free state, $\langle a_{-m}a_{m}\rangle = \sum_{k}n_{k+m}(1 - n_{k})$, as in Theorem \claimref{singularity.bosonisation-bridge-s-wave-sector}(3), so $\langle a_{-m}a_{m}\rangle/m = (e^{\beta cm} - 1)^{-1}$, the Bose-Einstein occupancy at frequency $cm$ and inverse temperature $\beta$, for every affine law. The script represents $e^{\beta c}$ and $e^{\beta d}$ by rationals $b = 3$ and $D \in \{1, \tfrac52, \tfrac27\}$, verifies the termwise identity, the exact telescoped value of every finite-window sum for $K = 5, 10, 20$, and the explicit edge bound $|S_{K} - m/(b^{m} - 1)| \le (mDb^{-K+m-1} + mb^{-K-1}/D)/(b^{m} - 1)$, which tends to zero.

\emph{(iv)} The map $\psi_{k} \mapsto \psi_{k_{0} - k}^{\dagger}$ preserves the CAR, so it is a Bogoliubov automorphism. With the flow $\alpha_{t}(\psi_{k}) = e^{-i\omega_{k}t}\psi_{k}$, the two composites send $\psi_{k}$ to $e^{-i\omega_{k}t}\psi_{k_{0}-k}^{\dagger}$ and to $e^{i\omega_{k_{0}-k}t}\psi_{k_{0}-k}^{\dagger}$, so the map commutes with the flow exactly when $\omega_{k_{0} - k} = -\omega_{k}$ for all $k$, which for affine weights is $ck_{0} + 2d = 0$. A Bogoliubov map carries a gauge-invariant quasi-free state to the gauge-invariant quasi-free state with transformed symbol (Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-quasi-free-states-car-algebra}, used in the same way in Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-common-weight-particle-hole}(iii)); here the transformed symbol is $n'_{k} = 1 - n_{k_{0} - k}$, and $1 - n(-x) = n(x)$ shows that $n' = n$ at a given $\beta \neq 0$ exactly when $\omega_{k_{0}-k} = -\omega_{k}$. Relabelling $k \mapsto k + r$ changes $d$ by $cr$ and $k_{0}$ by $-2r$, so the classes are $k_{0}$ even, reducible to $d = 0$, and $k_{0}$ odd, reducible to $d = c/2$. In the first class $\omega_{0} = 0$ and $n_{0} = \tfrac12$; the weights are integer multiples of $c$, so $\alpha_{2\pi/c}$ is the identity. In the second class the weights are odd multiples of $c/2$, so $\alpha_{2\pi/c}(\psi_{k}) = e^{-i\pi(2k+1)}\psi_{k} = -\psi_{k}$, the parity automorphism, and no weight vanishes. With the offset class fixed, the occupations depend on $c$, $d$ and $\beta$ only through $\beta\omega_{k} = y k$ or $y(k + \tfrac12)$, $y = \beta c$. Under (R), $\beta = 2\pi/\kappa$, and (Q-w) has $c = \kappa$, so $y = 2\pi$ and the recurrence time $2\pi/c$ equals $\beta$. The script checks, with $b = 4$ standing for $e^{y}$, that $n_{-k} = 1 - n_{k}$ for $D = 1$, $n_{-1-k} = 1 - n_{k}$ for $D = 2$ ($D^{2} = b$), that neither holds for $D = 3$, that the reflections map the one-particle generators $\operatorname{diag}(k)$ and $\operatorname{diag}(k + \tfrac12)$ to their negatives, and the integrality statements behind the two recurrences.

\emph{(v)} Second quantisation $X \mapsto \hat{X}$ is a Lie map (Proposition \claimref{scrambling.boundary-mode-space-matrix}), injective on one-particle operators, so $\hat{h}$ commutes with $(\id \otimes J_{a})^{\wedge}$ exactly when $h$ commutes with $\id \otimes J_{a}$. For $h = \operatorname{diag}(\omega_{k,a})$ the $(k,a),(k,a+1)$ entry of $[h, \id \otimes J_{+}]$ is $(\omega_{k,a} - \omega_{k,a+1})(J_{+})_{a,a+1}$, and the principal $J_{+}$ has all superdiagonal entries nonzero, so commutation forces $\omega_{k,a}$ to be independent of $a$; conversely $\operatorname{diag}(\omega) \otimes \id$ commutes with $\id \otimes X$ for every $X$. Applying (ii) to the currents of each channel $a$ gives $\omega_{k} = ck + d$. The symbol $\operatorname{diag}(n_{k}) \otimes \id$ commutes with every $\id \otimes u$, so the state is rotation invariant. Restricted to one level $k$, the modes $\psi_{k,a}$ carry the principal representation of Theorem \claimref{scrambling.principality-indifference-sphere} and the generator acts as $\omega_{k}N_{\mathrm{tot}}^{(k)}$, which is the form Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-common-weight-particle-hole}(i) proves necessary; its clause (iv) concerns weights $\kappa m$ indexed by the magnetic label of one principal window, which is the identification of the array index with the magnetic index, not made here. In the class $d = 0$ the level $k = 0$ has $\omega_{0} = 0$ and symbol $\tfrac12\id$, which is maximally mixed and invariant under the particle-hole map of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-common-weight-particle-hole}(iii), and by Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy}(ii) zero weight is its equality case. The script checks, for the principal triples on $\C^{2}$ and $\C^{3}$ and three array levels, that $\operatorname{diag}(-1, 0, 1) \otimes \id$ commutes with $\id \otimes e, f, h$, that the diagonal generators commuting with $\id \otimes e$ form a space of dimension three (one weight per level), that $\tfrac12 h$ does not commute with $e$, and, on the four-mode Fock space of two levels of spin $\tfrac12$, the second-quantised commutations.
