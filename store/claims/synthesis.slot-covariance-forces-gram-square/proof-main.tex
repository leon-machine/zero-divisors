(i) By Theorem \claimref{synthesis.many-twistor-join}(3), $B_{\Lambda} \mapsto U^{\mathsf T}B_{\Lambda}U$ and $h \mapsto U^{\dagger}hU$. Write the antisymmetric $B_{\Lambda}$ as $B_{ij} = \varepsilon_{ijk}\beta_{k}$. Then $(U^{\mathsf T}BU)_{ij} = \sum_{p,q}U_{pi}U_{qj}\varepsilon_{pqm}\beta_{m}$, and the cofactor identity $\sum_{p,q}\varepsilon_{pqm}U_{pi}U_{qj} = \det(U)\sum_{k}\varepsilon_{ijk}(U^{-1})_{km}$ gives $\beta \mapsto \det(U)U^{-1}\beta$. The map $U \mapsto \det(U)U^{-1}$ is onto $U(3)$: for $V \in U(3)$ pick $\mu$ with $\mu^{2} = \det V$ and put $U = \mu V^{-1}$, so that $\det U = \mu^{3}/\det V = \mu$ and $\det(U)U^{-1} = V$. For a permutation matrix $P$, $\det(P)P^{-1} = \operatorname{sgn}(P)P^{\mathsf T}$, the sign twist of Proposition \claimref{families.chart-computation-sign-twist-bracket-form}(1). That the slot mixings form exactly $U(3)$ is Proposition \claimref{standard-model.internal-symmetry-ladder}.

(ii) Since $R_{U}$ runs over all of $U(3)$ by (i), covariance says that the tensor $A$ is invariant under $U(3)$ acting on $\bar V \otimes V \otimes V \otimes \bar V$ (indices $k$, $l$ of the form, $m$ and $n$ of $\bar\beta_{m}\beta_{n}$), with $V = \C^{3}$ irreducible. The invariant tensors pair each $V$ index with a $\bar V$ index: $\delta_{km}\delta_{ln}$ and $\delta_{kl}\delta_{mn}$, so $M = a\,\bar\beta\beta^{\mathsf T} + b\,|\beta|^{2}\mathbb{1}$, and Hermiticity makes $a$, $b$ real. The check confirms the count independently: the infinitesimal covariance conditions for the nine generators of $\uu(3)$, solved in exact Gaussian-rational arithmetic, have a two-dimensional solution space. The diagonal is $M_{kk} = a|\beta_{k}|^{2} + b|\beta|^{2}$; the face-wise condition $M_{kk} = 2|\beta_{k}|^{2}$ for all configurations, at a configuration with $\beta_{k} = 0 \neq \beta_{l}$, gives $b = 0$, and then $a = 2$ (equivalently, the covariance conditions together with a zero diagonal have only the zero solution). The face-wise diagonal is the bracket term of the two-spinor join of face $k$, Theorem \claimref{synthesis.many-twistor-join}(2) at $N = 2$, whose $B^{ij}_{\Lambda}$ is $\beta_{k}$. The trace is $2|\beta|^{2} = 2\sum_{i<l}|B^{il}_{\Lambda}|^{2}$, the bracket part of the same Theorem at $N = 3$. For any $c \in \C^{3}$, $\bar c^{\mathsf T}M c = 2|\beta^{\mathsf T}c|^{2} \ge 0$, every $2 \times 2$ minor of $\bar\beta\beta^{\mathsf T}$ vanishes, and $M\bar\beta = 2|\beta|^{2}\bar\beta$.

(iii) The sign-twisted permutations are generated by $-P_{(12)}$ and the cyclic $P_{(123)}$; the invariance conditions for these two, solved exactly, leave a fourteen-dimensional space of tensors, of which the zero-diagonal ones form a nine-dimensional subspace, so the covariant forms with the face-wise diagonal are the Gram square plus that subspace, a nine-dimensional affine space. The tensor of $\operatorname{diag}(|\beta_{k}|^{2})$ is invariant under them (the permutations permute the $|\beta_{k}|^{2}$ and the sign squares away) and fails the $\uu(3)$ conditions; likewise $(\J - \mathbb{1})|\beta|^{2}$, since $P\J P^{\mathsf T} = \J$. At $\beta = (1, 0, 0)$, $2\bar\beta\beta^{\mathsf T} + c(\J - \mathbb{1})$ has the principal minor $\begin{psmallmatrix} 0 & c \\ c & 0 \end{psmallmatrix}$ of determinant $-c^{2} < 0$, so it is indefinite for $c \neq 0$. At the democratic direction $\beta \propto (1,1,1)$ the face-wise form is a multiple of the identity.

(iv) For a $2 \times 2$ block, $C(h_{k}) = \tfrac12\tr h_{k}^{2} - \tfrac14(\tr h_{k})^{2} = \tfrac14(h_{ii} - h_{jj})^{2} + |h_{ij}|^{2}$, and summing over the three faces against $C_{3} = \tfrac12\tr(h^{2}) - \tfrac14(\tr h)^{2}$ of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.many-twistor-join}(2) gives $\sum_{k}C(h_{k}) - C_{3} = \tfrac14\sum_{i}h_{ii}^{2}$; with the bracket parts this is the displayed sum. Each of the six listed forms is covariant: $\bar h \mapsto U^{\mathsf T}\bar h\bar U = \overline{R_{U}}\,\bar h\,R_{U}^{\mathsf T}$, the determinant phases cancelling. Suppose $a\bar\beta\beta^{\mathsf T} + b|\beta|^{2}\mathbb{1} + \alpha_{1}\bar h^{2} + \alpha_{2}(\tr h)\bar h + \alpha_{3}\tr(h^{2})\mathbb{1} + \alpha_{4}(\tr h)^{2}\mathbb{1}$ had diagonal $2|\beta_{k}|^{2} + \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}C(h_{k})$. All diagonal entries are real, so this is a real linear system for $(a, b, \alpha_{1}, \ldots, \alpha_{4})$, one equation per face and configuration. On the twelve configurations of the check (four composites, three nonzero values of $\Lambda$) its coefficient matrix has rank six and its augmented matrix rank seven, so it has no solution. At $\Lambda = 0$ the same system has rank six and is solved by $a = 2$ and all other coefficients zero, consistent with (ii). By hand: comparing the coefficients of $h_{ii}h_{jj}$, $h_{kk}^{2}$, $h_{ii}^{2}$, $h_{kk}h_{ii}$ and $|h_{ki}|^{2}$ in the $k$-th diagonal entry forces $\alpha_{4} = -\tfrac14$, $\alpha_{3} = \tfrac12$, $\alpha_{2} = \tfrac12$, $\alpha_{1} = -\tfrac34$ and then $\alpha_{1} + 2\alpha_{3} = 0$, which fails.

The closing sentence collects (ii) and (iv); the covariance it applies to the mass law is Premise \claimref{families.slot-covariance-of-mass-law}. Two remarks, not part of the proof. First, the internal $U(2)$ of each pair of slots (the two-spinor case of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.internal-symmetry-ladder}, under which that face's bracket picks up $\det$) is a subgroup of the slot $U(3)$, and the Lie algebras of the three pairwise $U(2)$ together span $\uu(3)$, so covariance under each pair's $U(2)$ is the same hypothesis as covariance under $U(3)$. Second, the same argument applies verbatim to the face operators $\hat\Omega_{k}$ of the families paper's six-mode face algebra under the passive mixing $a_{iA} \mapsto \sum_{j}U_{ji}a_{jA}$, since they are built from the same antisymmetric $\varepsilon$-contraction: the unique covariant normal-ordered face form with face-wise diagonal $\langle\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{k}\rangle$ is $\langle\hat\Omega_{k}^{\dagger}\hat\Omega_{l}\rangle$, the normal-ordered part of the mass form of Theorem \claimref{families.skeleton-rank-one-plus-ordering-shift}; this operator transcription is argued, not separately machine-checked.
