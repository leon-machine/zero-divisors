The statement was corrected on 2026-10-23: the untwisted ladder bundle exists exactly when $w_1(\shL) = 0$, but $\mathfrak{M}$, built from $\operatorname{Ad}\sigma$, glues on a twisted ladder bundle as well (as the even-condition clause below already notes), so the closing claim that $\Aa^{+}$ exists only in the $\shL$-twisted sense holds for the ladder bundle and not for its even part. Locality and gluing are inherited from the sheaf of sections of $\Aa$, because membership in $\mathfrak{M}(U)$ is a fibrewise condition: a family of sections $s_i \in \mathfrak{M}(U_i)$ agreeing on overlaps glues to a unique section $s$ of $\Aa$, and $s(x) \in \Aa_x^{+}$ holds at each $x$ because it holds for whichever $s_i$ is defined there. The restriction maps exist because the ladder bundle is a bundle of algebras with smooth ($C^\infty$-linear) structure maps, the machinery's linearity (the note's Fact 1.3(ii), \claimref{sheaf.function-linearity-tame-constructions}): nothing in the construction differentiates, so restriction is restriction of coefficient functions. The equivalence with $w_1(\shL) = 0$ is the chain stated: without a global $k$ the ladder is defined only up to the sign $(k, k^{*}) \mapsto (-k, -k^{*})$, under which $\sigma$, quadratic in the ladder, is unchanged, so the even condition and the even part $\Aa^{+}$ are well defined; it is the ladder bundle itself that exists only in the $\shL$-twisted sense of \cite[\S 4]{ButlerT4}; the untwisted statement used by the chain is exactly the collar-data case (\claimref{three-routes.imported-collar-data-coupling}, collar data existing exactly when $w_1(\shL) = 0$).
