\emph{Step 1 (the matrices).} Proposition \claimref{standard-model.fits-record-level} records four representative fits under Hypothesis (S), Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-clebsch-structure-seesaw}, Premise (EW) and the Pati-Salam $v_{R}$; their coupling sets $(Y, Z, f, h, w_{u}, w_{d}, c_{u}, c_{\nu}, c_{d})$ are stored digit for digit in the Standard-Model paper's script (Part M, obs:anglefits) \cite{ButlerT12}, and the check copies them as decimal strings, read as exact rationals with complex numbers as pairs. As in that script, Premise (EW) is imposed by setting $Y_{33} = 3w_{u}f_{33}$, and the Dirac mass matrices are $M_{u} = Y + w_{u}f + c_{u}h$, $M_{\nu} = Y - 3w_{u}f + c_{\nu}h$, $M_{d} = Z + w_{d}f + c_{d}h$ by the Fact. Since $h$ is antisymmetric, $(M_{\nu})_{33} = Y_{33} - 3w_{u}f_{33} = 0$ exactly, which the check confirms.

\emph{Step 2 (exact singular values).} For each matrix $M$ the check forms $A = MM^{\dagger}$, verifies that its trace, sum of principal two-by-two minors and determinant are real, and brackets the largest root $\sigma_{\max}^{2}$ of $\det(t - A)$ by Sturm sequences over $\Q$: exactly one root above $\mathrm{lo}^{2}$ and none above $\mathrm{hi}^{2}$. This gives (i) for $M_{u}$ and $M_{d}$ (the fits' targets $m_{t}(M_{I}) = 92.45$ GeV and $m_{b}(M_{I}) = 1.3651$ GeV, the latter met to the fits' two-percent tolerance in the third set, $1.36493$) and (ii) for $M_{\nu}$. The record-level table's largest $|M_{\nu}|$ entries ($0.04$, $2.9$, $4.0$, $2.2$ GeV) are consistent: each lies between $\sigma_{\max}/3$ and $\sigma_{\max}$.

\emph{Step 3 (comparison).} With $m_{t} = 92.45$ and $m_{b} = 1.3651$ GeV, each upper bracket times $18$ is below $m_{t}$; the first set's upper bracket times $2.2$ is below $m_{b}$, and the other three lower brackets exceed $2.2\,m_{b} = 3.003$; and the largest lower bracket exceeds $90$ times the smallest upper one. This is (iii). Hypothesis (U) asks the neutrino's leading Dirac value to equal $m_{t}$ (top reading) or to be split like $m_{b}$ (bottom reading) at the ceiling; at $M_{I}$ every set fails both, while satisfying everything listed in Step 1 and reproducing the fitted data (the record-level proposition), so none of those premises, singly or together, implies (U) at $M_{I}$.

\emph{Step 4 (the ceiling; argued).} Between $M_{I}$ and the ceiling the Yukawa couplings run; at one loop the running of the ratio of the neutrino's leading Dirac value to the bottom's is driven by gauge couplings and the large Yukawa couplings (top), which are common to the four sets, while the neutrino Dirac couplings themselves, at most $5.005/92.45 < 0.06$ of the top's, contribute at relative order their square. So the ratio is multiplied by a factor that is the same for the four sets up to such small corrections; sets that differ by a factor above $90$ at $M_{I}$ cannot all meet one value at the ceiling, and at most one can meet the bottom reading. The top reading would need the common factor to exceed $18$. This step is argued at the level stated and not computed.

\emph{Remark (computed in floating point, not part of the check).} In the set without a $120$, $\hat{f} = f/f_{33}$ has $\hat{f}_{22} \approx 0.0247 + 0.0109I_{4}$ and $\hat{f}_{23} \approx -0.0171 - 0.0063I_{4}$, so $|\hat{f}_{23}|^{2} \approx 3.3 \times 10^{-4}$ is much smaller than $|\hat{f}_{22}|$; in the two-three block of $M_{\nu} = M_{u} - m_{t}\hat{f}$ (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.neutrino-dirac-matrix-charged-fermions}) the eigenvalues solve $\ell^{2} - (r - \hat{f}_{22})\ell - \hat{f}_{23}^{2} = 0$ in units of $m_{t}$, $r = m_{c}/m_{t}$, so the leading value is set by the $126$'s second-family entry and a rank-one model $\hat{f}_{22} = \hat{f}_{23}^{2}$ does not describe the fit.
