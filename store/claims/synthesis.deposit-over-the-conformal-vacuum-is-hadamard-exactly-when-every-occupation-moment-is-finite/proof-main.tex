Units $\hbar = c = 1$; $\Cl(1,3)$ with $e_{0}^{2} = 1$, $e_{i}^{2} = -1$; the unit imaginary is $I_{4} = e_{0}e_{1}e_{2}e_{3}$ ($I_{4}^{2} = -1$, checked). Matrix forms are parenthetical readings of left multiplication.

\emph{Set-up.} The field $\hb(b) = b/a(t)$ has $\hb^{-1}(b) = a\,b$, so its derived metric is $g(u, v) = a^{2}\,u\cdot v$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}(i)), conformally flat, so the Weyl part of its field strength vanishes (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-conformal-covariance-of-massless-fields-and-local-conformal-flatness}(a)); $a$ is smooth and positive on the collar by hypothesis. The massless Dirac equation is conformally covariant with weight $\tfrac32$: $\psi = a^{-3/2}\chi$ solves it on $(\hb, \Omega)$ exactly when $\chi$ solves the flat massless equation (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-conformal-covariance-of-massless-fields-and-local-conformal-flatness}(b); the conformal transfer of null geodesics and wave-front sets is proved for every conformal factor in Theorem \claimref{synthesis.weyl-flat-fields-need-no-scaling-limit-premise-and-robertson-walker-generators-see-the-schwarzian}(i)), and the map is unitary for the conserved current, so the CAR algebras of $\psi$ and $\chi$ are identified. The flat massless vacuum of $\chi$ has (matrix reading, as in the stored kernels of Proposition \claimref{field-theory.second-quantisation}; the phases are numbers read in $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$) $\omega_{c}(\chi(x)\chi^{\dagger}(y)) = \int\frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}}\Lambda_{+}(\hat p)\,e^{-I_{4}p\cdot(x - y)}$ and the other ordering with $\Lambda_{-}$, where $p = |\vec p|(e_{0} + \hat p)$ is the null momentum, $\hat p$ the unit spatial vector ($\hat p^{2} = -1$) and $\Lambda_{\pm}(\hat p) = \tfrac12(1 \pm \hat p e_{0})$. The check verifies on Pythagorean directions that $(\hat p e_{0})^{2} = 1$, that $\Lambda_{\pm}$ are idempotent, complementary, mutually annihilating and self-adjoint for the Dirac adjoint ($e_{0}\tilde\Lambda e_{0} = \Lambda$), with scalar part $\tfrac12$, that $\Lambda_{+} = pe_{0}/(2p_{0})$ and $p\Lambda_{+} = 0$ on the null shell: orthogonal projectors of norm one onto the solutions.

\emph{The deposit's remainder.} In the quasi-particle modes of $\omega_{c}$ (annihilators $a_{\vec p}$ for particles, $b_{\vec p}$ for antiparticles), the deposit has $\omega(a^{\dagger}a) = n_{+}$ and $\omega(bb^{\dagger}) = 1 - n_{-}$ mode by mode, with no coherences and no pairing (gauge invariance and diagonality). Hence each remainder is a superposition of plane waves,
\[
R^{(\pm)}(x, y) = a(x)^{-3/2}a(y)^{-3/2}\sum_{h}\int\frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}}\Bigl(\epsilon^{\pm}_{+}\,n_{+}(p)\,\Pi_{+}\,e^{-I_{4}p\cdot(x - y)} + \epsilon^{\pm}_{-}\,n_{-}(p)\,\Pi_{-}\,e^{+I_{4}p\cdot(x - y)}\Bigr),
\]
summed over the helicities $h$, with signs $\epsilon = \pm 1$ fixed by the ordering and projector weights $\Pi_{\pm}$ of the form $\Lambda_{\pm}(\pm\hat p)$ (times a helicity projector), of norm one. The factor $a(x)^{-3/2}a(y)^{-3/2}$ is smooth and nonvanishing, so smoothness and boundedness of $R^{(\pm)}$ are those of the integral.

\emph{(i)} This is standard, and is proved for every conformal factor in Theorem \claimref{synthesis.weyl-flat-fields-need-no-scaling-limit-premise-and-robertson-walker-generators-see-the-schwarzian}(i): the flat null lines with affine parameter $\tilde\lambda$, $d\tilde\lambda = a^{2}d\lambda$, are the affine null geodesics of the derived metric (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}(iii)) and carry the flat covectors, so the relation $\sim$ and the sets $C^{\pm}$ of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-microlocal-hadamard-condition} are the same for the derived metric and the flat one. The flat massless vacuum satisfies $\mathrm{WF}'(\mathcal{G}^{(\pm)}) = C^{\pm}$ (Fact (b) for the flat field), and multiplication by the smooth nonvanishing $a(x)^{-3/2}a(y)^{-3/2}$ does not change a wave-front set (standard). So $\omega_{c}$ is Hadamard on $(\hb, \Omega)$.

\emph{(ii), sufficiency.} This is the standard flat-space criterion (rapid decrease of the occupation numbers), carried to the descended field by conformal covariance; the argument is recorded for completeness. For a null momentum $|p_{\mu}| \le p$ in every component, so every derivative of order $j$ of the integrand in $(x, y)$ is bounded by $p^{j}(n_{+} + n_{-})$ times a constant; if $M_{0}, \dots, M_{j} < \infty$ (the factor 2 in $M_{k}$ counts the helicities), dominated convergence gives remainders of class $C^{j}$, and if every $M_{k}$ is finite they are smooth. A smooth kernel has empty wave-front set, so $\mathrm{WF}'(\mathcal{G}^{(\pm)}_{\omega}) = \mathrm{WF}'(\mathcal{G}^{(\pm)}_{\omega_{c}}) = C^{\pm}$ and $\omega$ is Hadamard by (i).

\emph{(ii), necessity.} Suppose $\omega$ is Hadamard; by Fact (b) its remainders relative to the Hadamard $\omega_{c}$ are smooth. Restrict the ordering $\omega(\chi^{\dagger}(y)\chi(x))$ to a timelike line $x = y + \tau e_{0}$ and take the scalar part of the spin trace: $\phi(\tau) = \int e^{-I_{4}\nu\tau}\,d\mu(\nu)$ in the frequency $\nu$, where particles sit at $\nu = p$ with weight $+n_{+}$ and antiparticles at $\nu = -p$ with weight $-n_{-}$ (the sign of reordering $bb^{\dagger}$ against the vacuum), both times the positive spin trace. The measure $\mu$ is signed, but $\nu\,d\mu$ is positive: $p \cdot n_{+} \ge 0$ and $(-p)(-n_{-}) \ge 0$, and for every odd power $\nu^{2m+1}d\mu$ weighs particles and antiparticles alike by $p^{2m + 1}n_{\pm}$ (checked, with the even powers shown to mix signs). So $I_{4}\phi'$ is the Fourier transform of the positive tempered measure $\nu\,d\mu$. A positive tempered measure whose Fourier transform is continuous near $0$ is finite, and if the transform is of class $C^{2m}$ near $0$ the measure has a finite moment of order $2m$ (Bochner's theorem and the moment lemma for characteristic functions, Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-bochner-theorem-moment-lemma-and-whitney-even-functions}(a), (b); the step uses that $(z + z^{-1} - 2)^{m} = (-1)^{m}|z - 1|^{2m}$ for unit $z = c + sI_{4}$, so the $2m$-th symmetric differences at coincidence are of one sign, checked on Pythagorean phases). Hence a remainder of class $C^{2m+1}$ along the line, for which $I_{4}\phi'$ is of class $C^{2m}$, gives $\int p^{2m + 1}(n_{+} + n_{-})\,d^{3}p < \infty$, that is $M_{2m+1} < \infty$, and smoothness gives every odd moment; the even ones follow from $p^{2m} \le 1 + p^{2m+1}$ and $n_{\pm} \le 1$ on $p < 1$.

\emph{(iii)} The thermal deposit has the Fermi-Dirac occupations of Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state} in the comoving momenta. With $y = e^{-p/T} \in (0, 1]$, $n_{\mathrm{FD}} = y/(1 + y) \le y$ (checked on samples), and $\int_{0}^{\infty}x^{k}e^{-x}dx = k!$: the antiderivative is $-e^{-x}P_{k}(x)$ with $P_{k} = \sum_{j \le k}\tfrac{k!}{j!}x^{j}$, and $P_{k} - P_{k}' = x^{k}$, an exact polynomial identity (checked for $k \le 15$, with the factorials $0!, \dots, 7!$ and the bounds $16(k+2)!$ for $k \le 3$ asserted). So $M_{k} \le 2\cdot 2\cdot 4\pi\int_{0}^{\infty}p^{k + 2}e^{-p/T}dp = 16\pi(k + 2)!\,T^{k + 3}$ (two helicities, particles and antiparticles), finite for every $k$; by (ii) the thermal deposit is Hadamard. The exact moments are $16\pi(k + 2)!\,\eta(k + 3)T^{k+3}$ with $1 - 2^{-s} \le \eta(s) \le 1$ (alternating series, standard). The same bound holds whenever $n_{\pm} \le C_{N}(1 + p/T)^{-N}$ for every $N$.

\emph{(iv)} For $n = (1 + p/T)^{-6}$, substitute $q = u/(1 + u)$, $u = p/T$: $\int_{0}^{\infty}u^{2 + k}(1 + u)^{-6}du = \int_{0}^{1}q^{2 + k}(1 - q)^{2 - k}dq$, which is $\tfrac{1}{30}$, $\tfrac{1}{20}$, $\tfrac15$ for $k = 0, 1, 2$ (exact polynomial integrals, checked). So the number density, the mean energy $\tfrac{1/20}{1/30}T = \tfrac32 T$ and the energy spread $c = \tfrac{(1/5)(1/30)}{(1/20)^{2}} = \tfrac83$ are finite, and the shot term $(3c_{k}k^{3}/2\pi^{2}\bar n)^{1/2}$ of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.quasi-free-deposit-is-vacuum-plus-shot-noise}(iv) is finite ($c_{k} \le c(1 + k/\langle\omega\rangle)^{2}$ as there). For $k = 3$ the integrand is $q^{5}(1 - q)^{-1}$; on the piece $[1 - 2^{1 - j}, 1 - 2^{-j}]$ of length $2^{-j}$, $(1 - q)^{-1} \ge 2^{j - 1}$ and $q^{5} \ge (1 - 2^{1 - j})^{5}$, so each piece contributes at least $\tfrac12(1 - 2^{1-j})^{5} \ge \tfrac{1}{10}$ for $j \ge 3$ and the integral diverges (checked over forty pieces); since $u^{2 + k} \ge u^{5}$ for $u \ge 1$, every $M_{k}$ with $k \ge 3$ is infinite. By (ii) the remainders are of class $C^{2}$ and, since $M_{3} = \infty$, not of class $C^{3}$ along a timelike line; the deposit is not Hadamard. So the number of quanta and their energy spread, the data of the stored shot-noise bound, do not decide the Hadamard property. The profile is a valid occupation, $0 < n \le 1$.

\emph{(v)} With $\|\Pi_{\pm}\| = 1$, $|R^{(\pm)}(x, y)| \le a(x)^{-3/2}a(y)^{-3/2}(2\pi)^{-3}\sum_{h}\int d^{3}p\,(n_{+} + n_{-}) = (2\pi)^{-3}M_{0}\,a(x)^{-3/2}a(y)^{-3/2}$, the stated bound, finite when $M_{0}$ is, whether or not the higher moments are. Under the scaling about $x_{0}$ with weight $\tfrac32$ per leg, $(S_{l}R)(x, y) = l^{3}R(x_{0} + lx, x_{0} + ly)$, a remainder bounded by $C$ near $x_{0}$ is bounded by $l^{3}C \to 0$. The scaling limit of $\omega_{c}$ is the flat massless vacuum in the local inertial coordinates $a(t_{0})(x - x_{0})$: its kernel is $a(x)^{-3/2}a(y)^{-3/2}$ times the flat one, which is homogeneous of degree $-3$ (standard; computed in Theorem \claimref{synthesis.weyl-flat-fields-need-no-scaling-limit-premise-and-robertson-walker-generators-see-the-schwarzian}(i)). So the scaling limit of $\omega$ is the same, even when $\omega$ is not Hadamard; for Hadamard states this is that result's clause (i).
