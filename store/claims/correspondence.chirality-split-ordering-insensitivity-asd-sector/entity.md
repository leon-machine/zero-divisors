## formal
Let $\mathfrak{l} := \Lambda^2 (W_{1,3})_\C \cong \mathfrak{so}(1,3)_\C \subset \mathfrak{so}(2,4)_\C$ be the complexified Lorentz subalgebra of the splitting, and $\mathfrak{l} = \mathfrak{g}^+ \oplus \mathfrak{g}^-$ its decomposition into the $\pm I_7$ eigenspaces of the duality operator of Lemma \claimref{correspondence.lorentzian-duality-complex-structure}. Here the complex scalars are $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_7\}$, $I_7 = I_6e$ the central pseudoscalar of the odd extension $\Cl(3,4) = \Cl(2,4)_\C$ (\claimref{std-algebra.complexification-is-the-odd-extension}), so $\Lambda^2(W_{1,3})_\C = \Lambda^2W_{1,3} \oplus I_7\Lambda^2W_{1,3}$, and the Lemma is read with this complexification (its proof uses only that the unit is central with square $-1$). Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item each of $\mathfrak{g}^\pm$ is a subalgebra isomorphic to $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\C)$, and $[\mathfrak{g}^+, \mathfrak{g}^-] = 0$: the self-dual and anti-self-dual sectors commute;
\item (\emph{ordering insensitivity of the ASD sector}) Let $\mathcal{A}^- \subset \mathcal{P}$ be the unital subalgebra generated by $q(\rho(\mathfrak{g}^-))$, and call $q(\rho(\mathfrak{g}^+))$ the \emph{googly slot}. Then: (a) every ordering discrepancy of a word in elements of $q(\rho(\mathfrak{g}^-))$ lies in $\hbar\,\mathcal{A}^-$, and for a word of length two it is exactly $\hbar\,q(\rho([P, Q])) \in \hbar\,q(\rho(\mathfrak{g}^-))$; (b) $\mathcal{A}^-$ commutes with the googly slot, $[\mathcal{A}^-, q(\rho(\mathfrak{g}^+))] = 0$; (c) under the adjoint action of $\mathfrak{g}^+$ through $q\circ\rho/\hbar$, $\mathcal{P}$ is a locally finite $\mathfrak{g}^+$-module, the googly slot is a copy of the adjoint representation inside it, and every element of $\mathcal{A}^-$ (in particular every discrepancy of (a)) has zero component in the adjoint isotypic subspace: reorderings within the anti-self-dual sector never source the googly slot, at quartic order or at any order.
\end{enumerate}

## technical
The complexified Lorentz subalgebra $\mathfrak{l} = \Lambda^2(W_{1,3})_\C \cong \mathfrak{so}(1,3)_\C$ splits into the $\pm I_7$ eigenspaces $\mathfrak{g}^\pm$ of the duality operator, $I_7$ the central unit of $\Cl(3,4) = \Cl(2,4)_\C$. Each is a subalgebra isomorphic to $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\C)$, and $[\mathfrak{g}^+, \mathfrak{g}^-] = 0$: the self-dual and anti-self-dual sectors commute.

In the palatial algebra $\mathcal{P}$ let $\mathcal{A}^-$ be the unital subalgebra generated by $q(\rho(\mathfrak{g}^-))$, and call $q(\rho(\mathfrak{g}^+))$ the googly slot. Every ordering discrepancy of a word in $q(\rho(\mathfrak{g}^-))$ lies in $\hbar\,\mathcal{A}^-$, and for two letters it is exactly $\hbar\,q(\rho([P,Q]))$; $\mathcal{A}^-$ commutes with the googly slot; and under the adjoint action of $\mathfrak{g}^+$ through $q\circ\rho/\hbar$, $\mathcal{P}$ is locally finite, the googly slot is a copy of the adjoint representation, and every element of $\mathcal{A}^-$ has zero component in the adjoint isotypic subspace. So reorderings within the anti-self-dual sector never source the googly slot, at quartic or any other order.

## lay
The symmetries of spacetime, extended to complex numbers, fall into two halves that do not interact, called self-dual and anti-self-dual. In Penrose's palatial algebra, a noncommutative algebra of operators built on the conformal spinor coordinates, changing the order of operators built from one half only ever produces more operators from that same half. Such reorderings therefore never feed into the slot where the other half's information, the so-called googly data, would sit, however many operators are involved.

## one-liner
The complexified Lorentz algebra splits into commuting self-dual and anti-self-dual copies of $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\C)$, and ordering discrepancies within the anti-self-dual sector of the palatial algebra stay in that sector and never source the googly slot.
