If $q = 0$ or $p = 0$ the form is definite and anisotropic: $Q(v) = 0$ forces $v = 0$. If $p, q \geq 1$, then $v = e_1 + e_n$ satisfies $v^2 = 1 - 1 + (e_1e_n + e_ne_1) = 0$ (\claimref{std-algebra.orthogonal-anticommute}); the solution set of the homogeneous quadratic $Q = 0$ minus the origin is a cone of dimension $n-1$. Non-invertibility in every extension is Lemma \claimref{transport.noinverse}: from $v^2 = 0$, $vw = 1$ gives $v = v^2 w = 0$.
