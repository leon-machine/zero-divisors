## formal
Under the action as written, with $\Lambda$ fixed before variation, $N = M_{P}/H$ is a parameter of the laws (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.under-action-written-norm-parameter-laws}). The series adopts the Henneaux-Teitelboim promotion of the Stelle-West norm to the value of a Lagrange-multiplier field with $d\Lambda = 0$ as an equation of motion (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-henneaux-teitelboim-promotion}). Under the promoted action $N$ is initial data of the descent, dated to the origin: the norm mode is one global pair $(\Lambda, \tau)$ with no local mode, $N$ is constant on each solution and takes every positive value across the solution space (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.promoted-norm-one-global-pair}), and on the Clausius route alone it is initial data even without the promotion (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.einstein-route-makes-n-initial-data}(i)). The weak scale's constant of the laws is the D-term level, strictly $\xi G$ (Premise \claimref{synthesis.rung-unit-second-free-number}, version 3), so $v/M_{P}$ is the law up to relative order $(H/v)^{4}$, $v/H = (v/M_{P})N$ is initial data, and $Gv^{2}N^{2} = (v/H)^{2}$ is an identity, not a law (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.einstein-route-makes-n-initial-data}(iii), Theorem \claimref{synthesis.constant-d-term-level-fixes-weak-scale-against-planck-under-promotion}). \emph{Note (register, 2026-11-01).} Until version 5 this sentence read that the rung unit $v/H$ stays a parameter of the laws, so that $v/M_{P}$ is initial data and $Gv^{2}N^{2} = (v/H)^{2}$ holds as a law; the stored D-term action with a constant level, read under the promotion, makes $v/H$ initial data instead, and the register was restated accordingly. The time $\tau$ conjugate to the promoted norm is that of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.promoted-norm-weyl-clock-flat-weight}: for the frozen-point action it advances by the Euler-density excess, the integral over the background region of a density built from the field strength $\mathcal{R}(B)$ of the gravitational field on flat spacetime (Definition \claimref{obj.synthesis.gtg-flat-spacetime-gauge-fields}), with measure $\det(\hb)^{-1}d^{4}x$, which in vacuum is the integral of the square of the Weyl part $\mathcal{W}$ and stands still on the de Sitter field, and with matter runs on every FLRW gravitational field with dust or radiation; it is not monotone, changing sign for dust at $8\pi G\rho = \Lambda$, so it is a record of the history and not a time ordering it, and by an argued reading its advance is dominated by the earliest, hottest epoch. Why $\Lambda$, and so $N$, is small is outside the series: the promotion makes it initial data, the only measure it names, the Liouville density per unit $\tau$, is flat in whichever function of the norm is promoted (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.promoted-norm-one-global-pair}), and a normalisable measure over $\Lambda$ would need a further number.

\emph{Beyond one flat chart.} The compact spatial slices on which the promoted norm mode is one global pair $(\Lambda, \tau)$ are global and are not carried by a single gravitational field $(\hb, \Omega)$ on flat spacetime; the statements above that hold on the invertible region are in GTG, and the compact slices are read on the manifold assembled from the derived metric's charts.

\emph{Note (the Hubble unit).} Here $H$ is the de Sitter rate of $\Lambda$, $H^{2} = \Lambda/3$, that is $H_{0}\sqrt{\Omega_{\Lambda}}$, the $H$ of $N = M_{P}/H$; some texts quote $v/H$ with $H_{0}$, which differs from it by the factor $\sqrt{\Omega_{\Lambda}}$, of order one ($v/H \approx 2.1 \times 10^{44}$ against $v/H_{0} \approx 1.7 \times 10^{44}$; $\ln(v/H)$ changes by about $0.2$). No number here depends on the choice.

\emph{Note (register, 2026-11-01).} $N$ is now registered as a free input of the series by Premise \claimref{synthesis.number-n-registered-free-input}, as the D-term level $\xi G$ is by Premise \claimref{synthesis.rung-unit-second-free-number}; that premise carries the statement above of what kind of number $N$ is, unchanged, and this entity's placement of it stays a conjecture.

## technical
Under the action as written, with the frozen point's norm fixed before variation, $N = M_{P}/H$ is a parameter of the laws. The series adopts the Henneaux-Teitelboim promotion of that norm to a Lagrange-multiplier field, under which $\Lambda$ is constant on each solution but set by the solution, so $N$ becomes initial data of the descent, dated to the origin; on the Clausius route alone $\Lambda$ is already an integration constant. The weak scale's constant of the laws is the D-term level (registered premise, version 3), so the weak scale in Planck units, $v/M_{P}$, is the law up to a relative correction of order $(H/v)^{4}$, the rung unit $v/H$ is initial data, and $Gv^{2}N^{2} = (v/H)^{2}$ is an identity rather than a law (the register until 2026-11-01 held $v/H$ as the law).

The promotion adds a time conjugate to the norm. For the series' own action it advances by the Euler-density excess, an integral of the gravitational field strength over flat spacetime: in vacuum the Weyl-squared integral, which stands still on the de Sitter field, and with matter a quantity that runs on every cosmology with dust or radiation, changes sign for dust when the matter density equals the vacuum density, and (by an argued reading) advances fastest in the hot early universe. It is therefore a record of the history rather than a clock ordering it. Why the cosmological constant is small is declared outside the series. Its compact spatial slices, on which the norm mode is one global pair, are global: no single gravitational field on flat spacetime carries them, so that part is read on several charts of flat spacetime, each carrying its own gravitational field, glued on overlaps by displacement and rotation gauge transformations.

## lay
The huge number N, the ratio of the Planck mass to the Hubble scale, appears in all the framework's smallness puzzles. With the action as originally written, N is a fixed constant of the laws of physics. The framework now adopts a known modification that makes the cosmological constant a quantity set by the particular universe, so N becomes a fact about the universe's history, fixed at its beginning. The weak scale measured against the Planck mass is a constant of the laws, so its ratio to the Hubble scale becomes a historical fact too. The modification brings a new kind of cosmic time, which runs whenever matter is present but can run backwards, so it records the history rather than ordering it. Why the cosmological constant is as small as it is, the framework leaves outside its scope.

## one-liner
Under the action as written $N$ is a parameter of the laws; under the Henneaux-Teitelboim promotion, which the series adopts, $N$ and $v/H$ are initial data while $v/M_{P}$ (the level $\xi G$) is the law and $Gv^{2}N^{2} = (v/H)^{2}$ an identity, the conjugate time is the Euler-density excess (running with matter, not monotone), and the smallness of $\Lambda$ is outside the series.
