The relation $\{e_a, e_b\} = 2g_{ab}$ on a Witt basis is the canonical anticommutation relations, the Fock module being supplied by the pure-spinor construction. A real quadratic space of signature $(p,q)$ has real capacity $\min(p,q)$ and complex capacity $\lfloor (p+q)/2 \rfloor$; for $(1,3)$ these are one and two, the complex reading being the Dirac spinor as a two-mode Fock space. A vacuum at a point of spacetime is an extra datum, a null direction at the point, which the reading must supply. Every Weyl spinor of $\mathrm{Spin}(2\nu, \C)$ is pure for $2\nu \leq 6$, so at $\nu = 2$ and $\nu = 3$ every parity-definite state is the vacuum of some Witt decomposition. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Props.~3.1, 3.2, 4.1(3), Fact~2.10]{ButlerT16} (\claimref{one-relation.imported-cartans-purity-theorem-thouless-form}, \claimref{one-relation.two-presentations}, \claimref{one-relation.spacetime-manifold-vacuum-lines}, \claimref{one-relation.two-capacities-three-consequences}); the capacity count is \cite[Thm.~4.3]{ButlerT3} (\claimref{tower.witt-index-quantum-capacity}).
