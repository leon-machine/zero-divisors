\emph{Relations.} By Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-structure-completed-pair}, $\sigma_{j} = 2N_{j} - 1$ with $N_{j} = v_{j}^{*}v_{j}$. Since $v_{j}N_{j} = v_{j}(1 - v_{j}v_{j}^{*}) = v_{j}$ and $N_{j}v_{j} = v_{j}^{*}v_{j}v_{j} = 0$, we get $v_{j}\sigma_{j} = v_{j}$ and $\sigma_{j}v_{j} = -v_{j}$. For $k \neq j$, $\sigma_{k}$ is a product of two odd elements of mode $k$, each anticommuting with $v_{j}$, so $\sigma_{k}$ commutes with $v_{j}$. The decomposition $H = H_{0}^{(j)} + \sigma_{j}H_{1}^{(j)}$ exists and is unique because $\mathcal{D}$ is spanned by the monomials $\sigma_{S}$, $S \subseteq \{1, \dots, n\}$, linearly independent, and those containing $\sigma_{j}$ are $\sigma_{j}$ times those not containing it; $H = H^{*}$ makes $H_{0}^{(j)}, H_{1}^{(j)}$ self-adjoint.

\emph{(i)} $Hv_{j} = H_{0}^{(j)}v_{j} + \sigma_{j}H_{1}^{(j)}v_{j} = v_{j}H_{0}^{(j)} + \sigma_{j}v_{j}H_{1}^{(j)} = v_{j}H_{0}^{(j)} - v_{j}H_{1}^{(j)}$ and $v_{j}H = v_{j}H_{0}^{(j)} + v_{j}\sigma_{j}H_{1}^{(j)} = v_{j}H_{0}^{(j)} + v_{j}H_{1}^{(j)}$, so $\operatorname{ad}_{H}(v_{j}) = -2v_{j}H_{1}^{(j)}$. Since $H_{1}^{(j)}$ commutes with $H$ (both lie in the abelian $\mathcal{D}$), $\operatorname{ad}_{H}(v_{j}Y) = \operatorname{ad}_{H}(v_{j})Y$ for $Y \in \mathcal{D}$, and induction gives $\operatorname{ad}_{H}^{m}(v_{j}) = v_{j}(-2H_{1}^{(j)})^{m}$. Summing the exponential series in finite dimension, $e^{JtH}v_{j}e^{-JtH} = \sum_{m}\tfrac{(Jt)^{m}}{m!}\operatorname{ad}_{H}^{m}(v_{j}) = v_{j}e^{-2JtH_{1}^{(j)}}$. The script builds four modes in $\Cl(4,4)$, checks $v\sigma = v$ and $\sigma v = -v$, and verifies the closed form of $\operatorname{ad}_{H}^{m}(v_{0})$ for $m = 1, \dots, 5$ with a field on mode $0$, chain couplings $\sigma_{0}\sigma_{1}$, $\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}$, $\sigma_{2}\sigma_{3}$ and a field on mode $3$, with and without a three-body term $\sigma_{0}\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}$.

\emph{(ii)} $e^{-2JtH_{1}^{(j)}}$ is a polynomial in the $\sigma_{k}$, $k \in N(j)$, so the evolved operator involves only modes in $\{j\}\cup N(j)$. For $l \notin \{j\}\cup N(j)$, $v_{l}$ anticommutes with $v_{j}$ and commutes with every $\sigma_{k}$, $k \neq l$, hence anticommutes with $v_{j}e^{-2JtH_{1}^{(j)}}$; likewise $v_{l}^{*}$. An out-of-time-order correlator $\omega(X(t)^{*}W^{*}X(t)W)$ with $X = v_{j}$ and $W \in \{v_{l}, v_{l}^{*}\}$ then equals $-\omega(X(t)^{*}X(t)W^{*}W)$ and $X(t)^{*}X(t) = e^{2JtH_{1}}N_{j}e^{-2JtH_{1}} = N_{j}$, so it is constant in $t$. The script checks that $\operatorname{ad}_{H}^{m}(v_{0})$ never involves mode $3$, involves mode $2$ exactly when the three-body term is present, and, with the exact unitary $U = \cos\theta + J\sin\theta\,\sigma_{0}\sigma_{1}$ ($\cos\theta = \tfrac35$, $\sin\theta = \tfrac45$, Gaussian-rational coefficients), that $Uv_{0}U^{-1}$ anticommutes with $v_{3}$ and $v_{3}^{*}$ and not with $v_{1}$.

\emph{(iii)} With $H_{1}^{(j)} = h_{j} + \sum_{k}g_{jk}\sigma_{k}$ the terms commute and $\sigma_{k}^{2} = 1$, so $e^{-2JtH_{1}^{(j)}} = e^{-2Jh_{j}t}\prod_{k}(\cos 2g_{jk}t - J\sin 2g_{jk}t\,\sigma_{k})$. Expanding, $v_{j}(t) = e^{-2Jh_{j}t}\sum_{S}c_{S}v_{j}\sigma_{S}$ with $c_{S} = \prod_{k \notin S}\cos 2g_{jk}t\prod_{k \in S}(-J\sin 2g_{jk}t)$. In the trace inner product, $(v_{j}\sigma_{S})^{*}v_{j}\sigma_{T} = N_{j}\sigma_{S}\sigma_{T}$, whose trace vanishes unless $S = T$ (the $\sigma_{k}$, $k \neq j$, are traceless, independent and commute with $N_{j}$), so the $v_{j}\sigma_{S}$ are orthogonal of equal norm and $|c_{S}|^{2}$ is the weight on $S$; these weights sum to $\prod_{k}(\cos^{2} + \sin^{2}) = 1$ and the mean of $|S|$ is $\sum_{k}\sin^{2}2g_{jk}t \le |N(j)|$, a finite sum of periodic functions, hence almost periodic, and zero at $t = 0$. For $n - 1$ couplings equal to $g$ it is $(n-1)\sin^{2}2gt$, first equal to $(n-1)/2$ at $2gt = \pi/4$. The script checks, with all-to-all couplings of mode $0$ to modes $1, 2, 3$ and $(\cos\theta, \sin\theta) = (\tfrac35, \tfrac45)$, that the exact $U = \prod_{k}(\cos\theta + J\sin\theta\,\sigma_{0}\sigma_{k})$ is unitary, that $Uv_{0}U^{-1} = v_{0}\prod_{k}(\cos 2\theta - J\sin 2\theta\,\sigma_{k})$ with $(\cos 2\theta, \sin 2\theta) = (-\tfrac{7}{25}, \tfrac{24}{25})$, and that the size weights sum to $1$ with mean $3\sin^{2}2\theta = \tfrac{1728}{625}$.

\emph{(iv)} With $H = 2\sigma_{0}\sigma_{1} + (v_{1}^{*}v_{2} + v_{2}^{*}v_{1}) + (v_{2}^{*}v_{3} + v_{3}^{*}v_{2})$ the script finds the highest mode in $\operatorname{ad}_{H}^{m}(v_{0})$ to be $1, 2, 3$ for $m = 1, 2, 3$: a hop carries support one mode per order.

\emph{Consequence.} Proposition \claimref{singularity.number-diagonal-dynamics-models-scrambling-derived} puts every generator (L$_{E}$) admits in $\mathcal{D}$. By (i) to (iii) no such generator moves support along a chain of couplings. Support does grow onto the directly coupled modes, and with all-to-all couplings the mean size reaches half the window at $t = \pi/(8g)$ for every $n$, a time that does not depend on the number of modes. No exponential growth occurs: $e^{-2JtH_{1}^{(j)}}$ is a finite sum of terms $e^{-2J\lambda t}P_{\lambda}$ over the eigenvalues $\lambda$ of $H_{1}^{(j)}$ with spectral projections $P_{\lambda}$, so every matrix element of $v_{j}(t)$, every matrix element of a commutator $[v_{j}(t), W]$ and every out-of-time-order correlator of $v_{j}(t)$ with a fixed $W$ is a finite combination of $e^{J\omega t}$ with real $\omega$, hence bounded and almost periodic, and the commutator norm stays below $2\|W\|$. The expansion step of Proposition \claimref{scrambling.spreading-law-chaos-bound-saturated}, an amplitude at mode distance $d$ growing as $e^{\kappa t}$, needs support at distance $d \ge 2$, which by (ii) is zero in real time; for quadratic generators Theorem \claimref{synthesis.phase-flow-is-time-rotor-flow-kms-continuation}(iii) already shows the unitary flow is trigonometric, and the present result removes the non-quadratic diagonal generators as well.
