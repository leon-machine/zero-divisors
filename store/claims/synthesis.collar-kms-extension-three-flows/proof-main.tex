\emph{Rates.} The rates $\rho(r) = |A'|/2 = 1/(2r^{2})$ and $\rho_{\mathrm{H}}(r) = \tau|A'|/(2\sqrt{A}) = \tfrac13 S(r)(1-r)^{-1/2} = \tfrac13 + \tfrac{4}{15}r + \cdots$ are Proposition \claimref{singularity.modular-flow-boundary-modes-holonomy-universal}(i), (ii), with $\tau$ and $S$ from Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-interior-collar-kasner-form}. Since $d\tau/dr = \sqrt{r/(1-r)}$ and $\tau(0) = 0$, $S(r) = \sum_{n}\binom{2n}{n}4^{-n}\tfrac{3}{2n+3}r^{n} = 1 + \tfrac{3}{10}r + \tfrac{9}{56}r^{2} + \cdots$, with positive coefficients; the script checks $\tfrac{d}{dr}(\tfrac23 r^{3/2}S) = r^{1/2}(1-r)^{-1/2}$ as truncated series to order $8$ and the first coefficients against the Fact. Then $\rho_{\mathrm{H}}/\rho = \tau/\sqrt{A} = \tau\sqrt{r/(1-r)}$, a product of two positive strictly increasing functions on $(0,1)$; $\rho$ is strictly decreasing and $\rho_{\mathrm{H}}$ strictly increasing ($S$ and $(1-r)^{-1/2}$ have positive coefficients), so no two of $\rho$, $\rho_{\mathrm{H}}$, $1$ are proportional on any two slices.

\emph{(i)} For the Hubble flow the slices lie in $(0,1)$, where $\rho_{\mathrm{H}}$ is finite. Under (C) the algebra on the chosen slices is a matrix factor (a tensor product of matrix factors), and a flow of profile $f$ is $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{JtK_{f}})$ with $K_{f} = \sum_{k}f(r_{k})K_{k}$. By Theorem \claimref{singularity.kms-uniqueness-factor} its unique KMS state at $\beta$ has density $e^{-\beta K_{f}}/\Tr e^{-\beta K_{f}} = \bigotimes_{k}e^{-\beta f(r_{k})K_{k}}/\Tr(\cdot)$, the $K_{k}$ commuting, which is the product of slice Gibbs states with rapidity periods $\beta f(r_{k})$. If profiles $f$ and $g$ share a KMS state at $\beta, \beta' \neq 0$, the two Gibbs densities coincide, and the population ratio on slice $k$, $e^{\beta f(r_{k})} = e^{\beta' g(r_{k})}$ ($K_{k}$ having eigenvalues $\mp\tfrac12$), forces $\beta f(r_{k}) = \beta' g(r_{k})$ for all $k$, so $f/g$ is constant on the slices; equivalently the modular group of a faithful state on a factor is unique (Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-general-form}). The script checks the product form and the KMS identity on all matrix units of two slices with profile $(1,2)$ at $e^{\beta/2} = 2$, and that the population ratios $4$ and $16$ admit no common KMS state for the profile $(1,3)$, which would need $16 = 4^{3}$.

\emph{(ii)} Rapidity period $2\pi$ on the horizon slice fixes $\beta\rho(1) = 2\pi$, so $\beta = 4\pi = 2\pi/\kappa$, and the period on the slice $r$ is $4\pi\rho(r) = 2\pi/r^{2}$, which exceeds $2\pi$ for $r < 1$ and diverges as $r \to 0$, the slice Gibbs state tending to the projection onto the ground state of $K$. Its local inverse temperature in the Hubble-normalised parameter of the slice is the period divided by $\rho_{\mathrm{H}}(r)$: $4\pi\rho/\rho_{\mathrm{H}} = 4\pi\sqrt{A}/\tau = 4\pi\sqrt{(1-r)/r}\cdot\tfrac{3}{2}r^{-3/2}S^{-1} = 6\pi\sqrt{1-r}/(r^{2}S(r))$; the script computes $\sqrt{1-r}/S(r) = 1 - \tfrac45 r + \cdots$.

\emph{(iii)} The Hubble-flow KMS state at $\beta_{\mathrm{H}} > 0$ has period $\beta_{\mathrm{H}}\rho_{\mathrm{H}}(r)$ on the slice $r$. As $r \to 1$, $\tau \to \tau(1) = \pi/2 > 0$, $|A'| \to 1$ and $\sqrt{A} \to 0$, so $\rho_{\mathrm{H}} \to \infty$ and the period diverges; no such state has period $2\pi$ on slices near the horizon, and on the horizon slice itself the Hubble normalisation is undefined. At $\beta_{\mathrm{H}} = 6\pi$ the period is $6\pi\rho_{\mathrm{H}} = 2\pi S(r)(1-r)^{-1/2} = 2\pi(1 + \tfrac45 r + \cdots)$, computed by the script.

\emph{(iv)} The rapidity flow at $\beta = 2\pi$ gives period $2\pi$ on every slice, in particular on the horizon slice. Its local inverse temperatures are $2\pi/\rho_{\mathrm{H}}(r) \to 2\pi/|p_{1}| = 6\pi$ as $r \to 0$ and $2\pi/\rho(r) = 4\pi r^{2}$; by (i), since the profile $1$ is proportional to neither $\rho$ nor $\rho_{\mathrm{H}}$, it is KMS for neither of those flows.

\emph{The stored conditional.} Proposition \claimref{singularity.modular-flow-boundary-modes-holonomy-universal}(ii) asserts that if the completed pair's KMS state extends to a KMS state of the $t$-line holonomy flow on the collar, its Hubble-normalised inverse temperature at the exceptional set is $6\pi$. The $t$-line holonomy parametrised by Killing time is the Killing flow; under (C), by (ii), the extension exists and its local Hubble-normalised inverse temperature is $6\pi\sqrt{1-r}/(r^{2}S(r)) \to \infty$, not $6\pi$, so the conditional fails on that reading. Parametrised by the Hubble-normalised parameter, by (iii) no extension exists and the conditional is vacuous. The value $2\pi/|p_{1}| = 6\pi$ in that Proposition's derivation divides a rapidity period $2\pi$ held fixed on the slices near the exceptional set by the limiting Hubble rate, which is the local value of the state of (iv).

\emph{Conclusion.} By (iii) no KMS state of the Hubble-normalised holonomy flow extends the completed pair's state; by (ii) its unique extension along the Killing holonomy flow has infinite local Hubble-normalised inverse temperature at the exceptional set; and by (iii), (iv) the value $6\pi$ belongs either to the Hubble-KMS state with period $2\pi$ at the exceptional set, which is not the horizon's state, or to the slice-wise state as a local value. The two differ at first order in $r$ by the running $\tfrac45 r$ of $3\rho_{\mathrm{H}}$, and the Hubble flow is excluded at the horizon by the divergence of $\rho_{\mathrm{H}}/\rho$ there.
