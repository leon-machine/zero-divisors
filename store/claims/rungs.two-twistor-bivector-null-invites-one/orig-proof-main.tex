Decomposability is the form of $Z^{i} \wedge Z^{j}$; nullity of decomposable bivectors is Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-spinor-identification-witt-data-line}; the mass identity is Lemma \claimref{rungs.mass-bracket-frozen-points-grip-slot}. Whether a given complex null bivector lies on the real cone is the $\Sigma$-nullity of its line (Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-spinor-identification-witt-data-line}), which a massive pair need not satisfy; the argument does not need it, since it is the real quadratic space $\R^{2,4}$ that is completed, and $\R^{2,4}$ is nondegenerate of mixed signature, so Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-totality-minimality-completion} applies to it exactly as to $\R^{1,3}$ in Corollary \claimref{rungs.fact-null-momenta-invite-one-rung}. One rung above $\Cl(2,4)$ has module $\Lambda^{\bullet}W \otimes \Lambda^{\bullet}(\C v) = \Sp \otimes \C^{2}$ and $T_{1} \cong A_{1} \otimes B_{1}$ with $B_{1} \cong \Cl(1,1) \cong \mathrm{M}_{2}(\R)$ acting irreducibly on the qubit and commuting with the twisted ground algebra $A_{1}$ (Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-tower-one-qubit-per-rung}); after complexification $A_{1} \otimes \C \cong \mathrm{M}_{8}(\C)$, so the commutant of $A_{1}$ in $\mathrm{End}(\Sp \otimes \C^{2}) = \mathrm{M}_{8} \otimes \mathrm{M}_{2}$ is $1 \otimes \mathrm{M}_{2}(\C)$, whose unitary group is $U(2)$. The rung's vectors $v, w$ are odd for the mode-number grading, so they anticommute with $(-1)^{N}$, and the even part of $\mathrm{M}_{2}(\C)$ is spanned by $1$ and $vw$, which commute. At two rungs the new modes span $\C^{2}$ and $U(2) \subset U(5)$ acts on $\Lambda^{1}R_{2} = \C^{2}$ within a fixed chirality (Theorem \claimref{rungs.rung-spinor-module-mixings}(2)). The script checks the nullity of all six pairwise bivectors of a four-twistor instance, and, on the four-mode Fock space, that the twisted ground generators generate $\mathrm{M}_{8}(\C)$, that the rung's $\{1, v, w, vw\}$ commutes with them, that $v$ and $w$ anticommute with chirality, and that the even part is abelian.
