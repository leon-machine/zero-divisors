(i) $|P(k)| \le \int|C(r)|\,d^{3}r$. A covariance satisfies $|C(r)| \le C(0)$ (the Gram matrix of $\zeta(\vec x)$ and $\zeta(\vec x + \vec r)$ is positive semidefinite). With support in $r \le R$, $\int|C|\,d^{3}r \le \sigma^{2}\cdot\tfrac{4\pi}{3}R^{3}$, so $\Delta^{2}(k) \le k^{3}\sigma^{2}\tfrac{4\pi}{3}R^{3}/(2\pi^{2}) = \tfrac{2}{3\pi}\sigma^{2}(kR)^{3}$. With $|C| \le \sigma^{2}e^{-r/\xi}$, $\int|C|\,d^{3}r \le 4\pi\sigma^{2}\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{2}e^{-r/\xi}dr = 8\pi\sigma^{2}\xi^{3}$ (antiderivative $-\xi(r^{2} + 2r\xi + 2\xi^{2})e^{-r/\xi}$), so $\Delta^{2}(k) \le \tfrac{4}{\pi}\sigma^{2}(k\xi)^{3}$. For compact support $P$ is moreover an even entire function of $k$, $P(k) = \sum_{j}(-1)^{j}k^{2j}m_{2j}/(2j+1)!$ with $m_{2j} = 4\pi\int r^{2j+2}C\,dr$, so as $kR \to 0$ the local index $d\ln\Delta^{2}/d\ln k$ tends to $3 + 2j_{0}$, $j_{0}$ the first nonvanishing moment, unless $C \equiv 0$: $n_{s} - 1 \to 3$ or more. This is the white-noise form of the causality argument, standard in cosmology (from memory; not imported, since the step above is self-contained).

(ii) $f(a) = \dot a^{2}/H_{0}^{2}$ has $a^{3}f'(a) = g(a) = -2\Omega_{r} - \Omega_{m}a + 2\Omega_{\Lambda}a^{4}$, convex in $a$, with $g(0) = -2\Omega_{r} < 0$ and $g(\tfrac12) < 0$, hence $g < 0$ and $f$ decreasing on $(0, \tfrac12]$; $f'' > 0$ on $(0, \infty)$, so on $[\tfrac12, 1]$, $f \le \max(f(\tfrac12), f(1)) = f(1) = 1$. A mode $k > a_{0}H_{0}$ outside at the descent ($k < a_{d}H_{d} = \dot a_{d}$) enters at the unique $a_{e} \le \tfrac12$ with $\dot a(a_{e}) = k$ (since $\dot a(\tfrac12) < H_{0} < k$), stays inside while $\dot a$ decreases, and has $k > H_{0} \ge \dot a$ on $[\tfrac12, 1]$. The present-day inputs are the record's (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-planck-2018-lcdm-parameters}: $\Omega_{m} = 0.315$, and $\Omega_{r} = 9.2 \times 10^{-5}$ from $T_{0}$ with three relativistic neutrino species); any decelerating content before the Lambda era (more relativistic species, a matter era of any duration) only strengthens the monotonicity. $k_{*}/a_{0}H_{0} = 0.05 \times 299792.458/67.4 = 149896229/674000 \in (222, 223)$.

(iii) For radiation domination from $T_{d}$ with entropy conservation, $a_{d}/a_{0} = (T_{0}/T_{d})(g_{s}(T_{0})/g_{s}(T_{d}))^{1/3}$ and $H_{d} = \pi\sqrt{g_{*}/90}\,T_{d}^{2}/M_{P}$ (reduced Planck mass, $g_{s} = g_{*}$ at $T_{d}$). The check bounds $\rho^{6}$ below with $\pi > 3$ and exact rational record inputs ($T_{0} = 2.7255$ K and $H_{0} = 67.4$ km/s/Mpc from Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-planck-2018-lcdm-parameters}, the reduced Planck mass $M_{P} = 2.435 \times 10^{18}$ GeV, $g_{s}(T_{0}) = 43/11$, $g_{*} = 10.75$ at $1$ MeV and $106.75$ at $10^{12}$ GeV): $4.96 \times 10^{7} < \rho(1\ \mathrm{MeV}) < 5.21 \times 10^{7}$, $7.27 \times 10^{22} < \rho(10^{12}\ \mathrm{GeV}) < 7.63 \times 10^{22}$ (with $\pi < 22/7$ for the upper brackets, about $12$ and $47$ e-folds above the pivot after dividing by $222$). Put $R = K/(a_{d}H_{d})$, so $k_{*}R = K k_{*}/(a_{0}H_{0}\rho)$. By (i), $A_{s} \le \Delta^{2}(k_{*}) \le \tfrac{2}{3\pi}\sigma^{2}(k_{*}R)^{3}$ gives $K^{3} \ge \tfrac{3\pi}{2}A_{s}\rho^{3}(a_{0}H_{0}/k_{*})^{3}/\sigma^{2}$; with $\sigma^{2} \le 1$, $A_{s} \ge 2.0 \times 10^{-9}$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-planck-2018-lcdm-parameters}: $(2.100 \pm 0.030) \times 10^{-9}$) and $\pi > 3$ the check gives $K > 460$ and $K > 6.8 \times 10^{17}$, and the clustering version $K' > 250$ at $1$ MeV. $\rho$ is proportional to $T_{d}$ at fixed $g_{*}$ (up to $g_{*}^{1/2}g_{s}^{-1/3}$), and so is $K$. A smaller variance makes the requirement stronger, $K \propto \sigma^{-2/3}$. The premise that the pivot's amplitude is the deposit's is the passive reading: under the dynamics (L$_{E}$) permits, the boundary modes' occupations are conserved after the descent (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.quadratic-flows-cannot-thermalise-descent}), so the deposit is not reprocessed by relaxation; in the descended phase an adiabatic $\zeta$ is conserved outside the Hubble length (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-superhorizon-zeta-conservation}), and by (ii) the pivot is outside it from the descent until it enters. The lowest reheating temperature allowed by nucleosynthesis is $1.8$ MeV for radiative and $4$ to $5$ MeV for hadronic decays (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-bbn-reheating-temperature-floor}); $1$ MeV is used as a weaker bound.

(iv) By Theorem \claimref{synthesis.de-sitter-temperature-frozen-point-norm} the frozen point's horizon has $T_{\Lambda} = \sqrt{\Lambda/3}/2\pi$, the Hubble rate of the frozen phase being $H = \sqrt{\Lambda/3}$; for the observed $\Lambda$, $H_{\Lambda} = H_{0}\sqrt{\Omega_{\Lambda}}$. In that phase a mode leaves the Hubble length when $k = aH_{\Lambda}$; for the pivot this is $a = (k_{*}/a_{0}H_{0})/\sqrt{\Omega_{\Lambda}} \in (268, 269)$. The flat-slicing late-time boundary $x_{0} \to 0$ of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.frozen-phase-spectrum-not-red} is therefore reached by the pivot mode only in the far future. Whether the frozen phase at the descent is this stage depends on the single frozen point and the dating, which are premises and an observation-level reading respectively; (iv) is stated under that hypothesis only.

Not covered: active sources after the descent (scaling seeds), which can produce flat spectra causally but give incoherent acoustic peaks (from memory); a deposit with $\sigma^{2} > 1$, which is not a perturbation of FLRW.

The check verifies the constants $(4\pi/3)/(2\pi^{2}) = 2/(3\pi)$ and $8\pi/(2\pi^{2}) = 4/\pi$, the antiderivative identity at three values of $\xi$, the $2 \times 2$ Gram condition, the bounds on $\rho^{6}$, $k_{*}/a_{0}H_{0}$ and $K^{6}$ in exact rationals, and the convexity and monotonicity of $f$ for (ii) and the exit scale of (iv).
