A tower of dimension $n$ has density $\frac{M_{\mathrm{tot}}}{|\Sph^{n}|\,\ell^{n}}$ on its sphere (by \claimref{dark-matter.d-democracy-total-mass}); through a thickness $\ell$ of the direction we cannot see, or with sheets $\ell$ apart (by \claimref{dark-matter.r3-one-cell-per-unshared-direction}), that becomes a density per unit of our four-measure, and the share is
\[
f \;=\; \frac{M_{\mathrm{tot}}}{|\Sph^{n}|\,\ell^{4}} \Bigg/ \frac{M_{\mathrm{tot}}}{|\Sph^{4}|\,\ell^{4}} \;=\; \frac{|\Sph^{4}|}{|\Sph^{n}|} . \qedhere
\]
