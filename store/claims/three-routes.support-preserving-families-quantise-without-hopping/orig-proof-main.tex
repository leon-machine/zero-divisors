The block-diagonal form of $d\Gamma(a)$ is the definition of second quantisation applied to a bundle map, each summand supported at one point; each summand is a polynomial in that point's ladder and commutes with every $N_{x_j}$, its own included when $a_{x_j}$ is expressed in the number basis, and the others trivially. The cross-component statement is the elementary computation $\psi^{\dagger}_x\psi_y\,|0_x 1_y\rangle = |1_x 0_y\rangle$; the script performs both computations as exact matrix identities on two modes, with the controlled-$Z$ generator $\sigma_x\sigma_y$ verified to conserve both occupations, since cross-point \emph{diagonal} products are not hopping and were never excluded.
