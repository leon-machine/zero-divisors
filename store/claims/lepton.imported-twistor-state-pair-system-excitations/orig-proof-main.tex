Let $W$ be a maximal isotropic subspace of the complexified ambient space $V_{\C}$ of the completion $\Cl(2,4)$, with a dual isotropic complement $U'$. The complexified ascended algebra acts irreducibly on the eight-dimensional Dirac module $\Sp := \Lambda^{\bullet}W$, whose chirality halves are $\T = \Lambda^{\mathrm{even}}W$ and $\T' = \Lambda^{\mathrm{odd}}W$. Every nonzero twistor $u \in \T$ is the Fock vacuum of the three fermionic ladder pairs built on its annihilator space $W_{u}$, and the map $\Lambda^{\bullet}U' \to \Sp$, $\xi \mapsto c(\xi)u$, is a linear isomorphism: every state of the module is generated from $u$ by its own pair operators, the even products landing in $\T$ and the odd ones in $\T'$. Twistors are states of the pair algebra, not its operators. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thms.~3.1, 5.3]{ButlerT9}; \cite[Fact~2.2]{ButlerT12}.
