Work in $\Cl(1,3)_{\C} \cong \Mat_{4}(\C)$, a matrix factor, with the frame $*$-structure of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.phase-flow-is-time-rotor-flow-kms-continuation}(i): $e_{0}^{*} = e_{0}$, $e_{j}^{*} = -e_{j}$ ($j = 1, 2, 3$), so $B_{i}^{*} = e_{i}^{*}e_{0}^{*} = -e_{i}e_{0} = B_{i}$ and $R_{jk}^{*} = e_{k}^{*}e_{j}^{*} = e_{k}e_{j} = -R_{jk}$. Hence $K = \tfrac12(B_{b} + iB_{c})$ is $*$-symmetric and $e^{itK}$ is unitary, and the rotors $\exp(\theta R_{23}/2) = \cos(\theta/2) + \sin(\theta/2)R_{23}$ are unitary.

(i) The Hubble-normalised rates of Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-interior-collar-kasner-form} are diagonal on $(e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3})$ with entries $\tau\sigma_{1} = -\tfrac13 - \tfrac{4}{15}R + O(R^{2})$ and $\tau\sigma_{2} = \tau\sigma_{3} = \tfrac23 - \tfrac{2}{15}R + O(R^{2})$, limits $(-\tfrac13, \tfrac23, \tfrac23)$. A rotation $M$ of the frame is compatible with the collar's geometry at a point only if $MPM^{\mathsf T} = P$ for the rate tensor $P$. For a rotation by angle $\theta$ in the $(e_{1}, e_{2})$ plane, the $(1,2)$ entry of $MPM^{\mathsf T}$ is $\sin\theta\cos\theta\,(p_{1} - p_{2})$ and the $(1,1)$ entry is $p_{1}\cos^{2}\theta + p_{2}\sin^{2}\theta$, so invariance forces $\sin\theta = 0$ whenever $p_{1} \ne p_{2}$; here $p_{1} - p_{2} = -1$ (and $-1 - \tfrac{2}{15}R$ at first order). Rotations of the $(e_{2}, e_{3})$ plane fix $P$ since $p_{2} = p_{3}$ (to all orders shown). These are the isotropy rotations of the $SO(3)$ acting on the sphere factor of the collar. The script checks both statements on rational rotations at two values of $R$ and at the limit, together with $\sum p_{i} = \sum p_{i}^{2} = 1$.

(ii) By Proposition \claimref{singularity.kms-uniqueness-factor}, a state $\omega = \Tr(\varrho\,\cdot)$ is $(\alpha,\beta)$-KMS iff $\varrho = e^{-\beta K}/\Tr e^{-\beta K}$, which is faithful. For a unitary $U$, $\omega\circ\operatorname{Ad}(U) = \omega$ iff $U^{-1}\varrho U = \varrho$. If $\varrho$ is invariant under $\exp(\theta R_{23}/2)$ for all $\theta$, then differentiating at $\theta = 0$ gives $[R_{23}, \varrho] = 0$, and then $[R_{23}, K] = 0$, because $K = -\beta^{-1}\log(\varrho\,\Tr e^{-\beta K})$ is a function of $\varrho$ (functional calculus of a positive invertible matrix) and $\beta \ne 0$. Conversely $[R_{23}, K] = 0$ gives $[R_{23}, e^{-\beta K}] = 0$ and invariance. Since $R_{23}$, $B_{b}$ and $B_{c}$ lie in the real algebra, $[R_{23}, K] = \tfrac12([R_{23}, B_{b}] + i[R_{23}, B_{c}])$ vanishes iff both real commutators vanish. The script computes, by exact rank, that the bivectors commuting with $R_{23}$ form the two-dimensional space $\operatorname{span}\{B_{1}, R_{23}\}$ (indeed $[R_{23}, B_{1}] = 0$, $[R_{23}, B_{2}] = 2B_{3}$, $[R_{23}, B_{3}] = -2B_{2}$, and $R_{12}$, $R_{13}$ are rotated into each other). Its boost part is $\R B_{1}$ and its rotation part $\R R_{23}$, which gives the equivalence. For $K = \tfrac12 B_{1}$, $(B_{1}/2)^{2} = \tfrac14$ gives $e^{-\beta K} = \cosh(\beta/2) - \sinh(\beta/2)B_{1}$, which commutes with $R_{23}$; for $B_{2}$ the element $\cosh(\beta/2) - \sinh(\beta/2)B_{2}$ is carried by the isotropy rotation by $\theta$ to $\cosh(\beta/2) - \sinh(\beta/2)(\cos\theta\,B_{2} + \sin\theta\,B_{3})$ (in the script's sign convention), which differs from it when $\sinh(\beta/2)\sin\theta \ne 0$. The script checks invariance and non-invariance exactly for three rational points $(\cosh, \sinh)$ of the hyperbola and three rational rotations each.

(iii) The $SO(3)$ isometries act transitively on the sphere and carry the frame at one point to the frame at another, $e_{1}$ to $e_{1}$ (they commute with $\partial_{t}$ and $\partial_{\tau}$). A family of states carried into itself therefore has at each point the transported state of one point; by (ii) and Proposition \claimref{singularity.kms-uniqueness-factor} that is the $B_{1}$-Gibbs state of one $\beta$.

(iv) The script computes that the common centraliser of $R_{12}, R_{13}, R_{23}$ in the bivector span is zero (the bivectors carry the representation $\R^{3} \oplus \R^{3}$ of $SO(3)$, boosts and rotations, with no invariant vector); the argument of (ii) then excludes every nonzero $K$. By (i) $R_{12}$ and $R_{13}$ are not symmetries, so this stronger invariance is not a requirement of the collar.

\emph{Remark.} The three limiting boosts generate $\mathfrak{so}(1,3)$ and do not commute (the script checks $[B_{1}, B_{2}] = -2R_{12}$ in the commutator convention $xy - yx$), so on one algebra carrying all three flows a faithful KMS state selects one of them (Proposition \claimref{singularity.modular-flow-boundary-modes-holonomy-universal}(iii)); the present result says which one the isotropy permits. Whether the slice states extend along the $r$-direction is untouched: that is hypothesis (C) of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.collar-kms-extension-three-flows}.
