## formal
Units $\hbar = c = 1$. Let $(\hb, \Omega)$ be a gravitational field on four-dimensional flat spacetime (Definition \claimref{obj.synthesis.gtg-flat-spacetime-gauge-fields}) whose derived metric $g$ is smooth and globally hyperbolic on a region $U$ of its invertible region (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}), on which gauge-invariant quasi-free Hadamard states of the Dirac field exist. Let $\mathcal{H} \subset U$ be a null hypersurface generated by affinely parametrised null geodesics $u \mapsto X(u, y)$, labels $y$ in an open set of $\R^{2}$, with physical tangent $k$, considered on a geodesically convex neighbourhood of a cut of $\mathcal{H}$. Along each generator take a physical null frame $\{k, n, f_{1}, f_{2}\}$ parallel along $k$, $k\cdot n = 1$, and let the screen plane be the span of $f_{1}, f_{2}$, identified along the generator by the frame, with the positive product $-w\cdot w'$. Let $J(u)$ be the linear function from label displacements to the screen plane (the screen part of $\partial_{\eta}X$), $a(u) = |\det J(u)|$ the area density of $\mathcal{H}$ in its labels, $B = J'J^{-1}$ the expansion-shear function (expansion $\theta = \mathrm{tr}\,B$), $T(w) = -(\mathcal{R}(w\wedge k)\cdot k)_{\perp}$ the tidal function of the field strength along $k$ (so $J'' = -TJ$), $A(u, u')$ the Jacobi map ($A'' = -TA$, $A(u') = 0$, $A'(u') = 1$), and $\bar X$ the adjoint for the screen product. Let $\sigma$ be the Synge function of $g$ and $\Delta$ its Van Vleck determinant; boundary values carry the $I_{4}0$ of a time function (an infinitesimal shift of the spacetime separation, written in scalar form as a shift of $\sigma$ by a number: the square of a shifted vector is a number, Theorem \claimref{std-algebra.complexification-is-the-odd-extension}(iii); every value, the null-vacuum kernel and the logarithms included, is a number read in $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$, with the field theory's unit $I_{4}$) (the independence of the bulk distributions from the time function: Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-hadamard-parametrix-of-the-squared-dirac-operator-leads-with-van-vleck-root-times-spin-transport}(c); the $\epsilon$-regularised restriction of (ii) depends on it by a smooth kernel, so it is defined modulo smooth kernels, note below).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{(Transverse Hessian and Van Vleck factor; proved, computed.)} For $x = X(u, y' + \eta)$, $x' = X(u', y')$, $u \neq u'$, $\sigma(x, x') = -\tfrac12 M(u, u')(\eta, \eta) + O(|\eta|^{3})$, $M$ the Hessian of $-\sigma$ in the labels, with
\[
M(u, u')(\eta, \eta') = (u - u')\,\bigl(J(u')\eta\bigr)\star\bigl(A(u, u')^{-1}J(u)\eta'\bigr),\qquad \Delta(X(u, y'), X(u', y')) = \frac{(u - u')^{2}}{\det A(u, u')},
\]
$\star$ the screen product (in matrix reading $M = (u - u')J(u')^{T}A^{-1}J(u)$), hence
\[
\frac{\Delta^{1/2}}{\sqrt{\det M}} = \bigl(a(u)\,a(u')\bigr)^{-1/2}.
\]
The tidal function (its trace, the Ricci term, and its trace-free Weyl part), the expansion and the shear of $\mathcal{H}$ change $\Delta$, $\det M$ and $a$ but cancel from this ratio. Exactly computed on two plane-wave fields crossed by null hypersurfaces with both parts of $T$, the expansion and the shear nonzero, one with $J$, $A$ and $T$ not commuting.
\item[(ii)] \emph{(The leading restricted Dirac kernel; computed at leading order.)} Let $\psi$ be the free Dirac field of mass $m_{0} \ge 0$ on $(\hb, \Omega)$ and $\omega$ a gauge-invariant quasi-free Hadamard state (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-microlocal-hadamard-condition}(b)), whose two-point functions near $\mathcal{H}$ are the Dirac operator applied to a Hadamard parametrix of its square plus a smooth kernel (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-dirac-hadamard-form-propagates-and-adiabatic-states-are-hadamard}(a)), with leading coefficient $U = \Delta^{1/2}R$, $R$ the spinor transport rotor along the geodesic (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-hadamard-parametrix-of-the-squared-dirac-operator-leads-with-van-vleck-root-times-spin-transport}(a), (b)). With $\Lambda = \tfrac12 nk$ the canonical projector and the canonical generator field $\psi_{\mathcal{H}}(u, y) = \sqrt{2a(u, y)}\,\Lambda\psi(X(u, y))$, labels as multiplicity space $K = L^{2}(\R^{2}, d^{2}y)\otimes V_{\Lambda}$ as in Theorem \claimref{synthesis.bulk-hadamard-states-restrict-to-the-generator-condition-on-flat-null-planes}, and the restriction computed as the $\epsilon \to 0$ limit of the transverse-smeared regularised kernel, the term of $\omega(\psi_{\mathcal{H}}^{*}(u', y')\psi_{\mathcal{H}}(u, y))$ coming from the coincident-label coefficient of $U/\sigma$ and of the form $c(u, u')(u - u' + I_{4}0)^{-1}\delta^{2}(y - y')$ is
\[
W_{0}(u, u')\,\delta^{2}(y - y')\,\Lambda,\qquad W_{0}(u, u') = \frac{I_{4}}{2\pi}(u - u' + I_{4}0)^{-1},
\]
the flat field's null vacuum (that theorem's (ii) at $m_{0} = 0$), with no correction from the field strength, the expansion, the shear or the mass. It comes only from the projected Dirac operator, $\Lambda\,p\,e_{0}\Lambda = (p\cdot k)\Lambda$, acting along $k$, where $R = 1$ in the parallel frame and the canonical factor $(a(u)a(u'))^{1/2}$ times $\Delta^{1/2}/\sqrt{\det M}$ is $1$ by (i). The same derivative and the transverse part of the projected Dirac operator also give log-order terms, $-\pi\theta(u)(\log\epsilon + \log(-I_{4}s))\delta^{2}(y - y')$ and connection and first-order transport terms; the projected transverse connection carries the expansion only ($\Lambda f^{a}\Omega(f_{a})e_{0}\Lambda = \theta\Lambda$ when $\Omega(f_{a})\cdot k = B(f_{a})$; exact), and none of these terms has a derivative along $k$. That no other term contributes at the order of $(u - u' + I_{4}0)^{-1}\delta^{2}$, and that the $\log\epsilon$ terms cancel, is part of the argued bound of (iv).
\item[(iii)] \emph{(State independence; proved, given the imported Facts and the characteristic-data embedding of the generator field, cited from memory.)} For two gauge-invariant quasi-free Hadamard states the restricted kernels differ, in each $K$-component with compactly supported transverse profiles, by a smooth function of $(u, u')$, and $\lambda R(u_{0} + \lambda x, u_{0} + \lambda x') \to 0$ at rate $\lambda$ for every smooth $R$. So whether the generator scaling limit of the restriction at a point of $\mathcal{H}$ is the null vacuum, and whether the restriction satisfies the generator microlocal condition of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.microlocal-condition-on-a-generator-gives-the-local-null-ground-state}(i), is the same for every such state: a property of $(\hb, \Omega)$ and $\mathcal{H}$.
\item[(iv)] \emph{(What it leaves of (HS)(b).)} Write the restricted kernel as $W_{0}\otimes\id_{K} + R_{\omega}$. By (iii), at a point $x_{0} = X(u_{0}, y_{0})$ of $\mathcal{H}$, clause (b) of Premise \claimref{synthesis.hadamard-scaling-limit-premise} holds for every gauge-invariant quasi-free Hadamard state of the Dirac field if and only if, for one of them, $\lambda R_{\omega}(u_{0} + \lambda x, u_{0} + \lambda x') \to 0$ in each $K$-component with compactly supported transverse profiles (proved). By (ii), $R_{\omega}$ has no coincident-label term of the order of the null singularity with a field-strength coefficient; it collects the higher orders of $\sigma$ and $U$ in the label difference, the spinor transport off the generator, the log-order terms of (ii), $V\log\sigma$, the mass term and the smooth part. That these give generator kernels of scaling degree below one, uniformly in the transverse profiles, with the $\log\epsilon$ terms cancelling, is argued and not proved here: it is the one ingredient (HS)(b) still needs. (Here (HS)(b) is the clause (b) of version 2 of the premise; the premise has since been restated as this bound, posed on the restricted parametrix. Its bulk clause (a) is Theorem \claimref{synthesis.hadamard-dirac-states-scale-to-the-flat-massless-vacuum-at-every-point-of-a-gravitational-field}.)
\end{enumerate}
Nothing in (i) to (iii) uses vanishing expansion or shear at $x_{0}$: the conclusions are the same for every geodesic null hypersurface through $x_{0}$ tangent to a given null plane, so the choice of local Rindler horizon does not matter. The computation uses a rotation gauge in which the frame parallel along a generator is the constant background frame ($\Omega(k) = 0$ along it); this is compatible with the gauge $\hb(x_{0}) = 1$, $\Omega(x_{0}) = 0$ of (HS) at $x_{0}$ when $B(x_{0}) = 0$, and the results are gauge-covariant in any case. Clause (b) scales the generator parameter at fixed transverse profiles, so it tests every generator of the cut $u = u_{0}$ in the profiles' support, not $x_{0}$ alone; it needs the Hadamard property near that cut, which the states of (HS), Hadamard on $U$, have.

## technical
Clause (b) of the scaling-limit premise (HS) says that near a point of a gravitational field, the state of the Dirac field on the light rays of a local horizon looks like the null vacuum, the state the series' horizon temperature needs. Its stated way to fail is a curvature term at the order of the null singularity. This result shows that the coefficient of that singularity carries no such term, on every null hypersurface built from null geodesics, whatever the field strength along the light rays and however the rays converge or shear.

The singularity of a Hadamard state across a null hypersurface comes from points on nearby light rays: integrating over the transverse separation turns the leading term $\Delta^{1/2}/\sigma$ into a delta function in the transverse labels with coefficient $\Delta^{1/2}/\sqrt{\det M}$, $M$ the transverse Hessian of $-\sigma$. Both $\Delta$ and $M$ are fixed by the Jacobi map of the light ray, and their ratio is exactly the inverse square root of the area densities at the two points, which the normalisation of the light-ray field removes (proved). The Dirac projector keeps only the derivative along the ray for the term singular as the inverse separation, so its coefficient is the flat one (computed at leading order, with the standard leading Hadamard coefficient imported from the literature); log-order terms carrying the expansion also appear, and their cancellation is argued. Because two Hadamard states differ by smooth kernels, clause (b) is a property of the field and the hypersurface, the same for all Hadamard states (given the characteristic-data embedding of the light-ray field, cited from memory). What remains is a bound on the lower-order terms of the Hadamard parametrix restricted to the hypersurface, argued by power counting and not proved here. (Note added later: that bound, with the cancellation of the logarithms, is proved by a later result in the store for the regularised restriction, which depends on the time function only by a smooth kernel; a second later result supplies the light-ray embedding, so the state independence no longer rests on a cited step.) The exact checks use plane-wave fields crossed by a null hypersurface, where the Synge function and the Jacobi maps are rational, one of them with non-commuting maps. The result is transport-absent.

## lay
The series assumes that, looked at closely along the light rays that make up a small horizon, any reasonable quantum state of the electron field resembles empty space, even where gravity is present. One way this could fail is if gravity's tidal stretching of the light rays left a mark on the state at the shortest distances. This result shows that, at the leading order, it does not: the tidal effects change how light rays spread and how distances between them are measured, but the two changes cancel exactly. It also shows that the assumption is either true for all reasonable states or for none, so it is really a question about gravity's geometry, not about which state the world is in. Smaller correction terms needed a technical estimate, argued here and proved by a later result.

## one-liner
On any null hypersurface generated by null geodesics of a gravitational field, Weyl and Ricci field strength along the generators, expansion and shear cancel exactly from the coefficient of the leading null singularity of a restricted Hadamard Dirac kernel ($\Delta^{1/2}/\sqrt{\det M} = (a(u)a(u'))^{-1/2}$, proved), which at leading order in the canonical normalisation is the null vacuum (computed, with the leading Hadamard coefficient imported as a Fact); Hadamard states differ there by smooth kernels, so (HS)(b) is state-independent and reduces to one remainder bound on the parametrix, argued here (and proved by a later result, cited in a note).
