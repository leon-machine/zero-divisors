By (L) of \claimref{singularity.rate-models-kinematic-inputs-l-u} the state space at every stage is $H_{L(t)} \otimes H_{R(t)}$, with the exact dimensions of (C): there is no third tensor factor. An eviction is the transfer of one mode's factor from the $L$ side of the decomposition to the $R$ side (in physical terms, the mode leaves the shrinking boundary mode space and its content is carried with it into the radiation sector; in the finite-mode model of Section \ref{sec:model} this is implemented literally). A unitary step (U) preserves the global state, hence all its correlations; since the factor carrying them lands in $R$, the correlations of the evicted mode remain in $L \cup R$, which is the assertion. What this proof does \emph{not} establish, and what carries the physical content, is the exhaustiveness of (L)'s two sectors for real evaporation: that no remnant or third sector exists dynamically. That claim is exactly the closure-completeness programme, argued from the tower's floor-one completeness \cite[Cor.~6.2]{ButlerT3} and flagged as the conjectural step; here it lies inside (L), where it is visible, rather than inside an unexamined axiom. One candidate third sector is excluded by theorem rather than by hypothesis: the completion at the black hole forms no shadow wall anywhere in the physical closure, the conserved quantity exiting the manifold through the blown-up boundary \cite[Thm.~8.12, Rem.~8.13]{ButlerT4}, so no degeneracy-generated sector hides inside the two-sector decomposition. The dynamical no-remnant clause of (L) remains the programme's.
