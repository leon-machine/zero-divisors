On the real slice a single conformal spinor carries null momentum $p_{AA'} = \pi_{A}\bar\pi_{A'}$, and a system of two conformal spinors with $p = \pi^{1}\bar\pi^{1} + \pi^{2}\bar\pi^{2}$ has $m^{2} = p^{2} = 2|\br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}}|^{2}$, with $\br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}} = \varepsilon^{AB}\pi^{1}_{A}\pi^{2}_{B}$. In the flat chart of $\CS$ the frozen point is $I_{\Lambda} = n_{\infty} + (\Lambda/6)\,n_{0}$, so at $\Lambda = 0$ it is $n_{\infty}$. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Prop.~4.1, Lem.~5.1]{ButlerT11} (\claimref{mass.de-sitter-scale-frozen-point}, \claimref{mass.mass-symplectic-area-two-twistor-formula}); \cite[Fact~2.5]{ButlerT12} (\claimref{standard-model.imported-frozen-point-dictionary}).
