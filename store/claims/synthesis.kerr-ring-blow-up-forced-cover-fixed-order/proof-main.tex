The Kerr-Schild coordinates are labels of the flat background, $\eta$ its fixed product, and $g^{-1} = \eta^{-1} + 2H\,k \otimes k$ the derived cometric $\hb^{T}(u) \cdot \hb^{T}(v)$ of the Kerr gauge field $\hb(b) = b + H(b \cdot k)k$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}(i)). \emph{(1) Smooth frame.} In Kerr-Schild coordinates $x + Iy = (r + Ia)\sin\theta\,e^{I\varphi}$, $Z = r\cos\theta$ (one sign convention for the $a$-terms; the other reverses the ring tangent below and changes nothing else), the null covector of Fact \claimref{interior.imported-kerr-schild-form-kerr-gauge} is $k = dt + \bigl(r(x\,dx + y\,dy) + a(y\,dx - x\,dy)\bigr)/(r^2 + a^2) + (Z/r)\,dZ$, null for $\eta$ because $(x^2 + y^2)/(r^2 + a^2) + Z^2/r^2 = \sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$. On the double cover, $r = \operatorname{Re} z$ and $\cos\theta = -\operatorname{Im} z/a$ are smooth functions of $z$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.kerr-ring-double-cover-data}(1)), $\sin\theta = (1 - \cos^2\theta)^{1/2}$ is smooth near the ring where $\cos\theta = 0$, and so $x, y, Z$ and the coefficients $r/(r^2 + a^2)$, $a/(r^2 + a^2)$, $Z/r = \cos\theta$ are smooth functions of $(\varphi, z)$; hence $k$ is smooth up to the ring, where $r = 0 = \cos\theta$ and $k = dt + (y\,dx - x\,dy)/a$ with $x^2 + y^2 = a^2$: the time direction plus a unit tangent of the ring. Raising with $\eta$, $k = (1, v)$ with $v$ a smooth unit spatial field; $\ell := \tfrac12(1, -v)$ is smooth, null, with $\eta(k,\ell) = 1$, and the $\eta$-orthogonal complement of $\mathrm{span}(k,\ell)$ is a smooth rank-two bundle of spacelike vectors, locally trivialised by an orthonormal pair $e_1, e_2$.

\emph{(2) Forced weights.} In the frame, $\eta^{-1} = k\otimes\ell + \ell\otimes k - e_1\otimes e_1 - e_2\otimes e_2$, and by Proposition \claimref{interior.unipotency-across-kerr-family}(2) $g^{-1} = \eta^{-1} + 2H\,k\otimes k$ exactly. Rescaling the frame vectors by $\rho^{a_k}, \rho^{a_\ell}, \rho^{a_1}, \rho^{a_2}$ multiplies the entries as stated, and with $H = M\cos\psi/\rho$ the $kk$ entry is $2M\cos\psi\,\rho^{2a_k - 1}$. A finite nonzero limit of the $k\ell$ entry needs $a_k + a_\ell = 0$; if $a_k + a_\ell > 0$ the $(k,\ell)$ block tends to a matrix whose second row vanishes, and if $a_k + a_\ell < 0$ it diverges, so nondegeneracy forces $a_k + a_\ell = 0$; likewise $a_1 = a_2 = 0$. The $kk$ entry is finite for $2a_k \ge 1$ and keeps a nonzero limit (for $\cos\psi \neq 0$) only for $a_k = \tfrac12$. With these weights the rescaling is the boost $k \mapsto \rho^{1/2}k$, $\ell \mapsto \rho^{-1/2}\ell$, which preserves $\eta^{-1}$ and multiplies $k\otimes k$ by $\rho$, so the rescaled cometric is $\eta^{-1} + 2M\cos\psi\,k\otimes k$ for every $\rho > 0$; by (1) it extends continuously to $\rho = 0$ with value $\eta^{-1} + 2M\cos\psi\,k_0\otimes k_0$. In the frame the $(k,\ell)$ block is $\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} s & 1 \\ 1 & 0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ with $s = 2M\cos\psi$, of determinant $-1$ for every $s$, so the determinant is that of $\eta^{-1}$ and the signature is $(1,3)$ throughout. The comparison with Theorem \claimref{sheaf.weights-are-forced-are-kasner-exponents}(2), whose radial and time weights are $\tfrac12$ and $-\tfrac12$, is by inspection.

\emph{(3) The cover is forced.} Downstairs, $w = z^2$ gives $\rho = |w|^{1/2}$ and, on a branch, $\psi = \tfrac12\arg w$, so rescaling the frame by $|w|^{b_k}$ on $k$ (and $|w|^{-b_k}$ on $\ell$) multiplies $2H\,k\otimes k$ by $|w|^{2b_k}$ and gives the coefficient $2M\cos(\tfrac12\arg w)|w|^{2b_k - 1/2}$; in terms of the cover's exponent, $b_k = a_k/2$. Going once round the ring changes $\arg w$ by $2\pi$, which is the deck transformation $z \mapsto -z$, $\psi \mapsto \psi + \pi$, under which $H$ is odd (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.kerr-ring-double-cover-data}(3)); so $f(\vartheta) := \cos(\tfrac12\vartheta)$, $\vartheta = \arg w \in [0, 2\pi)$, has the one-sided limits $1$ and $-1$ at $\vartheta = 0$. Let the exponent $b_k(\vartheta)$ depend on the direction arbitrarily, and let $A$ be the set of directions with $b_k(\vartheta) = \tfrac14$ (on the others the limit of the Kerr term is $0$ or infinite). A continuous finite limit requires $b_k \geq \tfrac14$ except possibly at the zero of $f$, and the function $f\cdot 1_A$ to be continuous on the circle. Wherever a boundary point $\vartheta_0$ of $A$ has $f(\vartheta_0) \neq 0$, $f\cdot1_A$ jumps there; the only zero of $f$ on the circle is $\vartheta = \pi$, and at $\vartheta = 0$ the values $\pm 1$ from the two sides already differ, so $0$ is not in the closure of $A$ from either side unless $f\cdot1_A$ jumps there. Hence the boundary of $A$ lies in $\{\pi\}$ and $A$ avoids a neighbourhood of $0$, so $A \subseteq \{\pi\}$ and the limit of the Kerr term is zero in every direction.

\emph{(4) The data.} $\cos\psi = 0$ exactly at $\psi = \pm\pi/2$, where $z$ is purely imaginary, $r = 0$ and $\cos\theta \neq 0$: the two faces of the disc (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.kerr-ring-double-cover-data}(2)); $\cos(\psi + \pi) = -\cos\psi$. With $\mathfrak M = -Mz^{-3}$, $\rho^3\mathfrak M = -Me^{-3I\psi}$, whose phase turns by $-6\pi$ as $\psi$ goes once round, and the deck transformation multiplies it by $e^{-3I\pi} = -1$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.kerr-ring-double-cover-data}(4)).

\emph{The check.} At four rational Kerr-Schild points (two of them close to the ring) it verifies that $k$ is $\eta$-null, that $\ell = \tfrac12(1, -v)$ is null with $\eta(k,\ell) = 1$, that the boost $B$ preserves $\eta^{-1}$ and gives $Bg^{-1}B^{T} = \eta^{-1} + 2H\lambda^2\,k\otimes k$ for three rational $\lambda$, that $\det(\eta^{-1} + s\,k\otimes k) = \det\eta^{-1}$, that $H|z|^2 = Mr$, that a search over all $5^4$ weight vectors with entries in $\{-1, -\tfrac12, 0, \tfrac12, 1\}$, computing each rescaled cometric entry as a rational function of $s = \rho^{1/2}$ from $H = M\cos\psi/\rho$, finds exactly one with a finite, nondegenerate limit that keeps the Kerr term, namely $(\tfrac12, -\tfrac12, 0, 0)$, and that the deck map fixes the base point while reversing $H|z|^2$.
