The mirror tower is a copy of this one: every input to the Standard-Model chain (the conformal spin group $\mathrm{Spin}(2,4) \cong \mathrm{Spin}(4,2)$, the conformal spinor module $\CS$ (twistor module) with its helicity form, the reversion form $h$, the complexified isotropic structure, the mass formula of two conformal spinors) is invariant under signature reversal, because over $\C$ the ambient carries no signature and $\Cl^{0}(2,4) \cong \Cl^{0}(4,2) \cong \Mat(4,\C)$. The residue is a soldered invariant, not an algebraic one, and the one metric superselects the tower charge. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thms.~3.5 and 4.3, Prop.~4.1]{ButlerT14} (\claimref{dark-matter.superselected-hence-dark-exactly-stable}, \claimref{dark-matter.mirror-tower-copy-one}, \claimref{dark-matter.residue-soldered-invariant-not-algebraic-one}).
