\emph{Step 1 (the twin map).} If $P_{12}XP_{12} = -X$ then $P_{12}(XP_{12})P_{12} = P_{12}X = -XP_{12}$, so the twin is again $P_{12}$-odd, and $(XP_{12})P_{12} = X$, so the map is an involution. If $X$ is symmetric, $(XP_{12})^{\mathsf{T}} = P_{12}X = -XP_{12}$, so the twin is antisymmetric, and conversely. The explicit forms follow by permuting the first two columns: $X_{s}(s,b)P_{12} = X_{a}(s,b)$ and $X_{a}(c,e)P_{12} = X_{s}(c,e)$. Each type is two-dimensional (for $P_{12}$-odd $X$: $X_{11} = -X_{22}$, $X_{13} = -X_{23}$, $X_{31} = -X_{32}$, $X_{12} = -X_{21}$, $X_{33} = 0$; symmetry forces $X_{12} = 0$ and $X_{31} = X_{13}$, antisymmetry forces the diagonal to vanish and $X_{31} = -X_{13}$). With $o = (1,-1,0)$ and $v$ in the even plane, $P_{12}v = v$ and $P_{12}o = -o$, so $o^{\mathsf{T}}XP_{12}v = o^{\mathsf{T}}Xv$ (the entries $x$ are kept) and $v^{\mathsf{T}}XP_{12}o = -v^{\mathsf{T}}Xo$ (the entries $y$ are reversed). The check verifies all of this on rational samples, and that the twins of the samples span the antisymmetric type.

\emph{Step 2 (the sector matrices).} In the textures of Lemma \claimref{neutrino.breaking-relation}, $\J P_{12} = \J$ and $EP_{12} = E$ (the columns of $\J$ are equal, and $E$ has only its third column nonzero), so $M(X)P_{12} = \J + \delta(E + XP_{12}) = M(XP_{12})$ for every $\delta$. For the charged leptons $M(XP_{12})M(XP_{12})^{\mathsf{T}} = M(X)P_{12}P_{12}^{\mathsf{T}}M(X)^{\mathsf{T}} = M(X)M(X)^{\mathsf{T}}$, so the masses and left singular vectors are the same; the right singular vectors are permuted, and they do not enter the PMNS matrix. For the neutrinos, $\Pi$ and $\J$ commute with $P_{12}$, so $P_{12}M_{R}^{-1}P_{12}^{\mathsf{T}} = M_{R}^{-1}$ (with $M_{R}^{-1} = \Pi/\alpha + (\J/3)/M_{3}$, the two being complementary projectors) and $m_{\nu}$ is unchanged as a matrix. The PMNS matrix, built from the charged leptons' left states and the eigenvectors of $m_{\nu}$, is therefore unchanged up to the sign conventions of its columns. The check verifies the matrix identities exactly for four values of $\delta$ on both sides of zero (including the far root's $\delta = -0.286$), sixteen mixed stages $X = X_{s} + X_{a}$, and three seesaw pairs (including $M_{3}/\alpha = 530.7$ and $66$, the far and near roots of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.theta23-at-s3-s2-direction}). Nothing here uses the Lemma's expansion: (ii) holds at all orders.

\emph{Step 3 (consequence for the sum rule).} Proposition \claimref{synthesis.solar-angle-light-plane-sum-rule}(i) records $y_{s} = x_{s}$ for symmetric and $y_{s} = -x_{s}$ for antisymmetric $X_{s}$ at the Lemma's order; Step 1 shows the second is the twin of the first, and Step 2 that twins are indistinguishable at every order. So the two one-type cases of that proposition's clause (iii) are one case. A mixed stage $X = X_{s} + X_{a}$ has the twin $X_{s}P_{12} + X_{a}P_{12}$, again mixed, with $x$ kept and $y$ reversed, so $|y|$ and $|x|$ (and in the Lemma's two-by-two reading $|y_{\nu}/x_{\nu}|$) are twin invariants: the lopsidedness required there is a property of the relative weight of the two types within the sector.

\emph{Remark (exploration; computed, floating point, not certified, not part of the claim).} With the exact textures diagonalised numerically and $\sin^{2}\theta_{12}$ maximised by sequential quadratic programming over one-type (by Step 3, symmetric) stages, under $m_{e}/m_{\mu} \in [0.0047, 0.0050]$, $m_{\mu}/m_{\tau} \in [0.0585, 0.0605]$, $m_{2}/m_{3} \in [0.165, 0.18]$, $m_{1}/m_{2} \le 0.01$, $|U_{\tau 3}|^{2} \in [0.39, 0.55]$ and $\sin^{2}\theta_{13} \le 0.03$: with $\delta_{c} \in [0.2, 0.4]$, $|\delta| \le 1$ and second-stage entries in $[-1, 1]$ the maximum found was $0.154$, with the box edge $\delta = -1$ active (so the figure depends on the box: $|\delta| \le 2$ gives about $0.22$). With $\delta_{c} < 0$ allowed the bound fails: at $(\delta_{c}, \delta, \log_{10}M_{3}/\alpha, s_{e}, b_{e}, s_{\nu}, b_{\nu}) = (-0.25765, -0.998454, 1.823155, -0.179772, -0.06823, 0.386632, 0.482973)$ the constraints hold (several at their edges) with $\sin^{2}\theta_{12} = 0.310$, inside the measured range, and other points reach $0.87$. Proposition \claimref{synthesis.theta23-at-s3-s2-direction} fixes $\delta_{c} = +0.286$, so the question is open on that branch; nothing here decides it.

\emph{Scope.} Steps 1 to 3 are exact and hold for any $P_{12}$-even unbroken texture and any $P_{12}$-commuting Majorana matrix, at every order. They do not decide whether one-type stages are excluded beyond the Lemma's order, and they do not say which sector carries the lopsided stage or derive its weights; and they are statements about the textures of the breaking lemma, not about the Pati-Salam elimination, where the Dirac matrices carry independent family matrices.
