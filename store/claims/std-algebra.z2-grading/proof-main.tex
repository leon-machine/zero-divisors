$\Cl^{\pm}$ are the $\pm 1$ eigenspaces of the grade involution, an automorphism by \claimref{std-algebra.involution-properties}; if $\hat{x} = \epsilon x$ and $\hat{y} = \epsilon' y$ then $\widehat{xy} = \epsilon\epsilon' xy$. The dimension count is $\sum_{k \text{ even}} \binom{n}{k} = 2^{n-1}$ by \claimref{std-algebra.grade-decomposition}. For the finer statement take orthogonal basis blades $e_J$, $e_K$ with $|J| = j$, $|K| = k$: their product is a multiple of $e_{J \triangle K}$ and $|J \triangle K| = j + k - 2|J \cap K|$, with $|J \cap K|$ between $0$ and $\min(j, k)$ and $j + k - 2|J \cap K| \ge |j - k|$.
