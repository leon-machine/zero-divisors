By the GMC the state is KMS for the boost's phase flow $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{itK})$ at some $\beta$, one for all modes (\claimref{singularity.universality-temperature-under-g}), and by Theorem \claimref{singularity.kms-uniqueness-factor} it is the corresponding Gibbs state; the KMS condition says its correlation functions are analytic in the strip of width $\beta$ and periodic, with the graded sign (the state being even by (P), \claimref{singularity.p-univalence}), under translation by $i\beta$ in the flow parameter. Under the continuation $\lambda \mapsto i\theta$ of Proposition \claimref{state-selection.euclidean-partner-curvature-selected}(3), read on spinors as in its clause (4), that translation acts on each chiral half as the rotor of the second member of the curvature-selected pair, by the angle $\theta = \beta$, in one sense on one half and the opposite sense on the other; on vectors it is no real rotation, so the period is read in this action on spinors. By Proposition \claimref{state-selection.euclidean-partner-curvature-selected}(2) the conjugation action of the rotation closes at $2\pi$ and at no smaller angle, so a continuation that is single valued and regular at the fixed locus of the flow, which is premise (R) in the form T05 states it (\claimref{singularity.r-regularity-fixed-locus}: on each mode the two orderings of every ladder correlator are glued after one period of the regular Euclidean section), forces $\beta = 2\pi$ in rapidity: a value $\beta \neq 2\pi$ makes the continued correlation function multivalued around the fixed locus, equivalently gives the Euclidean section a conical excess or deficit there. The conversion to Killing time is \cite[Prop.~6.2]{ButlerT5} (\claimref{singularity.plane-rate}). Faithfulness: the GMC presupposes it, since a state has a modular automorphism group only if it is faithful, so hypothesis (iii) is an input of this argument and not an output. That $\beta = 2\pi$ is finite and nonzero, so that the Gibbs matrix has full rank, is consistent with it but derives nothing, the argument having started from the modular group. Hypothesis (iii) is discharged without the GMC by Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-discharge-hypothesis-iii}, from T05's (R) on each mode. The note's own wording of (R), regularity of the continued two-point functions at the fixed locus, is satisfiable and contentful on the boundary-mode algebra only when the two orderings are glued, and then on the whole window it adds vanishing inter-mode two-point functions, which this argument does not use (\claimref{synthesis.state-selection-r-and-le-against-t05}, (i), (ii) and (iv)); T05's (R) is the premise assumed here.
