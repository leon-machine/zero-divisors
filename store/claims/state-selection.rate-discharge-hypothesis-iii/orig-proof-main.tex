By the GMC the state is KMS for the boost flow at some $\beta$, and by Theorem \claimref{state-selection.kms-uniqueness-factor} it is the corresponding Gibbs state; the KMS condition says its correlation functions are analytic in the strip of width $\beta$ and periodic, with the graded sign, under translation by $i\beta$ in the flow parameter. Under the continuation $\lambda \mapsto i\theta$ of Proposition \claimref{state-selection.euclidean-partner-curvature-selected}(3), that translation is a rotation of the curvature-selected plane by the angle $\theta = \beta$. By Proposition \claimref{state-selection.euclidean-partner-curvature-selected}(2) the conjugation action of the rotation closes at $2\pi$ and at no smaller angle, so a continuation that is single valued and regular at the fixed locus of the flow, which is premise (R), forces $\beta = 2\pi$ in rapidity: a value $\beta \neq 2\pi$ makes the continued correlation function multivalued around the fixed locus, equivalently gives the Euclidean section a conical excess or deficit there. The conversion to Killing time is \cite[Prop.~6.2]{ButlerT5}. Finiteness: $\beta = 2\pi$ is finite and nonzero, so the Gibbs matrix has full rank, hence the state is faithful, hence cyclic and separating for the mode factor, which is hypothesis (iii); the two excluded boundary cases are $\beta = \infty$, the state with the ladder unoccupied, and $\beta = 0$, the tracial state.
