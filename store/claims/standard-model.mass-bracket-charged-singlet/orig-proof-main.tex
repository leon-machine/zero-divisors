The bracket is the determinant of the $\pi$-components in the two twistor slots: $\br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}} = \det(\pi^{i}_{A})$ regarded as a $2 \times 2$ matrix with slot index $i$ and spinor index $A$. A mixing $U$ acts on the slot index, multiplying the matrix by $U$ on the left, hence the determinant by $\det(U)$. An $SU(2)$ element has unit determinant and fixes the bracket; the abelian factor $e^{i\theta}\,\id$ has determinant $e^{2i\theta}$ and rotates the phase; the modulus is invariant. Vanishing on the massless locus is Fact \claimref{mass.mass-symplectic-area-two-twistor-formula}. The determinant transformation law is checked exactly by the script on Gaussian-rational instances. The last sentence anticipates Section \ref{sec:content}: the bracket is the $\det$ of the twistor-slot factor, $\Lambda^{2}\C^{2}$, on which $(A, B) = (\lambda^{3}a, \lambda^{-2}b)$ acts by $\det(\lambda^{3}a) = \lambda^{6}$, an $SU(2) \times SU(3)$ singlet of weight $6$, so $Y = 6/6 = 1$; $B$ acts trivially, each twistor being the vacuum of the pair system $B$ mixes (Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-pair-systems-two-chirality-halves}). The script confirms $\det(\lambda^{3}a) = \lambda^{6}$ on a unit-modulus Gaussian rational $\lambda$ and reads the weight $6$ off the hypercharge generator on $\Lambda^{2}\C^{2}$.
