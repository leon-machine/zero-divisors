Clauses (ii) and the even-dimensional type statement of (iv) are the published classification of the real Clifford algebras by quadratic forms over $\mathbb{F}_{2}$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-real-clifford-algebras-as-twisted-group-algebras-and-the-arf-invariant}). By clause (a) of that Fact, $\Cl(p,q)$ is the twisted group algebra of $\mathbb{F}_{2}^{n}$ with twisting function $f(x, y) = \sum_{j < i}x_{i}y_{j} + \sum_{i > p}x_{i}y_{i}$, so $e_{S}e_{T} = \pm e_{S \triangle T}$ and $e_{S}^{2} = (-1)^{f(S,S)}$ with $f(S, S) = \binom{|S|}{2} + |S \cap N| \bmod 2$, which is $q(S)$, while the polar form $f(S,T) + f(T,S) = |S|\,|T| + |S \cap T| \bmod 2$ records anticommutation: this is (ii). By clauses (b) and (c), the isomorphism type of $\Cl(p,q)$ is determined by the equivalence class of $q$, and for nondegenerate polar form the Arf invariant separates the real from the quaternionic matrix algebras: this is the even-dimensional type statement of (iv). The radical (iii), the Gauss-sum formula and its values in (iv), and the odd-dimensional case are proved in derivation main only, whose proof is self-contained and does not use the Fact; this route records that the statements it covers are classical and restated.
