All clauses are identities in the blade basis, checked by the script (Parts A and B) on rational vectors. For (ii): $\cosh(i\pi/2) + B\sinh(i\pi/2) = iB$, whose reverse is $-iB$, so the twisted action is $x \mapsto (iB)x(-iB) = BxB$; $B$ anticommutes with $e_{t}$ and $e_{r}$ and commutes with $e_{\theta}$, $e_{\varphi}$, and $B^{2} = 1$. For (iii): $(\nInf a)^{2} = -\nInf^{2}a^{2} = 0$ since $a$ anticommutes with $\nInf$, so $T_{a}$ is invertible with the stated inverse and is $R_{u}$ with $u = \tfrac12 a$. Expanding $T_{a}yT_{a}^{-1} = y + \tfrac12[\nInf a, y] - \tfrac14\nInf a\,y\,\nInf a$ and using $\nInf^{2} = 0$, $\nInf a = -a\nInf$ and $\nO\nInf + \nInf\nO = -2$: for $y = \nInf$ every correction vanishes; for $y = x$ in the base the commutator term gives $(x \cdot a)\nInf$ and the last term vanishes; for $y = \nO$ the commutator term gives $a$ and the last term $\tfrac12 Q(a)\nInf$. Summing with the coefficients of $X(x)$ gives $\nO + (x + a) + \tfrac12(Q(x) + 2x\cdot a + Q(a))\nInf = X(x + a)$, in the sign convention $\nO \cdot \nInf = -1$.
