## formal
Inside $\Lambda^2 V \cong \mathfrak{so}(2,4)$, in the notation of Proposition \claimref{interior.stabilisers-flat-model} with $W = \operatorname{span}(e_0, e_1, e_2, e_3)$, the Lie algebra generated by the three boosts $\{B_1, B_2, B_3\}$ is exactly $\Lambda^2 W \cong \mathfrak{so}(1,3)$: six-dimensional, containing all three rotations, and containing no component in $\nInf \wedge W$, $\nO \wedge W$, or $\R(\nInf\wedge\nO)$.

## technical
In $\Lambda^2 V \cong \mathfrak{so}(2,4)$, let $W = \operatorname{span}(e_0, e_1, e_2, e_3)$ be the orthogonal complement of the null pair $\nInf, \nO$, and take the three boosts $B_i = e_0 \wedge e_i$. The bracket rule $[u\wedge v,\ v\wedge w] = Q(v)\,u\wedge w$ for orthogonal frame vectors, with brackets of wedges sharing no factor vanishing, gives $[B_1, B_2] = -R_{12}$ and cyclically, so all three rotations appear, and brackets of rotations with boosts return the boosts.

So the Lie algebra generated by the boosts is exactly $\Lambda^2 W \cong \mathfrak{so}(1,3)$: six-dimensional, containing all three rotations, and with no component in $\nInf \wedge W$, in $\nO \wedge W$, or along $\nInf \wedge \nO$.

## lay
Among the symmetries of the series' six-dimensional ambient space, take the three boosts, the transformations that change velocity along each spatial axis. Repeatedly combining them produces exactly the six Lorentz transformations of ordinary spacetime and nothing else. In particular, boosts alone never generate a translation or a rescaling.

## one-liner
The three boosts generate exactly the six-dimensional Lorentz algebra $\Lambda^2 W \cong \mathfrak{so}(1,3)$, with no component outside it.
