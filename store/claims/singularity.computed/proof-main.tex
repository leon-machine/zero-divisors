The components below are those of the covariant derivative $D$ in the static frame, read through Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary} (clauses (ii) and (vi)). The static observer has four-velocity $u = \xi/\sqrt f$ with $\xi = \partial_{t_s}$. Its acceleration $A^\mu = u^\nu \nabla_\nu u^\mu$ has the single component $A^r = \Gamma^r_{tt}\,(u^t)^2$, with $\Gamma^r_{tt} = -\tfrac12 g^{rr}\partial_r g_{tt} = -\tfrac12(-f)(f') = \tfrac12 f f'$ and $(u^t)^2 = 1/f$, so $A^r = f'/2$. Its proper magnitude is $\sqrt{-g_{rr}}\,|A^r| = f'/(2\sqrt f)$, and the redshifted magnitude is $\sqrt f \cdot f'/(2\sqrt f) = f'/2$, finite and radius-independent in its limit. By Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-surface-gravity-redshifted-acceleration}, $\kappa = f'(2M)/2 = 1/4M$. The conversion quoted in Proposition \claimref{singularity.plane-rate} follows: one period $2\pi$ of rapidity is $2\pi/\kappa = 8\pi M$ of Schwarzschild time.
