In the trace normalisation of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-one-loop-running-trace-ratio}(b) the generators of $\su(2)_{R}$ and $\su(4)$ have the norm of $T_{3L}$, so the $\su(4)$ generator along $B - L$ is $T_{BL} = \sqrt{3/8}\,(B - L)$, and $Y = T_{3R} + \sqrt{2/3}\,T_{BL}$. Hence $\alpha_{Y}^{-1} = \alpha_{2R}^{-1} + \tfrac23\,\alpha_{4}^{-1}$, and $a_{1} = \tfrac35\,\alpha_{Y}^{-1}$ gives the first relation; equivalently, the two weights are $\tr T_{3R}^{2}/\tr Y^{2} = \tfrac35$ and $\tfrac14\tr(B - L)^{2}/\tr Y^{2} = \tfrac25$. The other two are the embeddings $\su(2) = \su(2)_{L}$ and $\su(3) \subset \su(4)$. The script computes the four traces on the module and the two weights.
