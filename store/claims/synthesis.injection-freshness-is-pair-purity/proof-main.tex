\textbf{(i).} Let $\varrho_{WR} = |\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$. Write $\varrho_{WRX}$ in a spectral decomposition $\sum_a p_a|\Phi_a\rangle\langle\Phi_a|$ and each $|\Phi_a\rangle$ in a Schmidt decomposition across $WR \,|\, X$, $|\Phi_a\rangle = \sum_i \lambda_{ai}|e_{ai}\rangle|f_{ai}\rangle$. Then $\varrho_{WR} = \sum_a p_a\sum_i\lambda_{ai}^{2}|e_{ai}\rangle\langle e_{ai}|$ is a convex combination of the pure states $|e_{ai}\rangle\langle e_{ai}|$; a pure state is an extreme point of the state space, so every $e_{ai}$ with $p_a\lambda_{ai} > 0$ equals $\psi$ up to phase. By orthonormality of the Schmidt vectors each $\Phi_a$ with $p_a > 0$ then has a single Schmidt term, $|\Phi_a\rangle = |\psi\rangle|f_a\rangle$ up to phase, and $\varrho_{WRX} = |\psi\rangle\langle\psi| \otimes \sum_a p_a|f_a\rangle\langle f_a| = \varrho_{WR} \otimes \varrho_X$.

\textbf{(ii).} If $\varrho_{WRX} = |\Phi\rangle\langle\Phi|$ and $\varrho_{WRX} = \varrho_{WR}\otimes\varrho_X$, then $1 = \tr\varrho_{WRX}^{2} = \tr\varrho_{WR}^{2}\,\tr\varrho_X^{2}$ with both factors in $(0, 1]$, so $\tr\varrho_{WR}^{2} = 1$ and $\varrho_{WR}$ is pure.

\textbf{(iii).} Write $\psi = \sum_{ij}a_{ij}|ij\rangle$ with coefficient matrix $A$. Then $\varrho_W = AA^{\dagger}$ and $\varrho_R = (A^{\dagger}A)^{T}$. Under (M), $AA^{\dagger} = D := \operatorname{diag}(c^{2}, s^{2})$ and $A^{\dagger}A = D$ (the transpose of a real diagonal matrix is itself). So $A$ commutes with $D$: $AD = AA^{\dagger}A = DA$. If $c \neq s$, $D$ has distinct eigenvalues and $A$ is diagonal, $A = \operatorname{diag}(a_{00}, a_{11})$ with $|a_{00}|^{2} = c^{2}$, $|a_{11}|^{2} = s^{2}$; after an overall phase this is (TF). Conversely (TF) is pure. If $c = s$, $AA^{\dagger} = \tfrac12\id$, so $\sqrt2A$ is any unitary and $\psi$ is any maximally entangled vector; $A = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0 & 1\\ 1 & 0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ gives $(|01\rangle + |10\rangle)/\sqrt2$, which has no $|00\rangle$ or $|11\rangle$ component. Invariance of the pair state under the pair's boost flow, hypothesis (ii) of Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-descent}, supplies the missing input: a flow generated by $N_W - N_R$ (up to constants) fixes $|00\rangle$ and $|11\rangle$ and gives $|01\rangle$, $|10\rangle$ the charges $\mp 1$, so an invariant pure pair lies in $\operatorname{span}\{|00\rangle, |11\rangle\}$ and with maximally mixed marginals has the form (TF) with $c = s$. Purity alone does not select it.

\textbf{(iv).} For $|\Phi\rangle = c|0000\rangle + s|1111\rangle$ (order $W, R, X_1, X_2$): $\langle\Phi|\Phi\rangle = c^{2} + s^{2} = 1$ and $\varrho = |\Phi\rangle\langle\Phi|$ is pure; both components have even occupation ($0$ and $4$), so $\varrho$ commutes with the total parity and is even, (P). Tracing out, $\varrho_W = \varrho_R = \operatorname{diag}(c^{2}, s^{2})$, (M), and $\varrho_{WR} = c^{2}|00\rangle\langle00| + s^{2}|11\rangle\langle11|$, even and diagonal in the number basis, with $\tr\varrho_{WR}^{2} = c^{4} + s^{4} < (c^{2} + s^{2})^{2} = 1$: (Pure) fails, hence (TF) fails, and by (ii) (F) fails, as also seen directly: $\varrho \neq \varrho_{WR}\otimes\varrho_X$ since the left side is pure and the right side is not. The marginal data of Corollary \claimref{singularity.chains-state-class-stability} (even states with thermal single-mode marginals) are satisfied by the pair, so the cited results leave (Pure) open, as that Corollary's clause (3) says of correlations in general.

\textbf{Consequence.} Clause (I) as stated asserts (TF) and (F). The pair form is taken from Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-descent}, whose hypothesis (i) is that the pair's state is pure, that is (Pure): the theorem presupposes the purity of the pair rather than supplying it. By (iii) and (i), (TF) implies (F) for $c \neq s$; by (ii), under (U), (F) implies (Pure), which with (M) and (iii) gives (TF). So the clause carries one assumption, which (iv) shows independent of (M), (U) and (P).

\textbf{Check.} With $(c, s) = (\tfrac35, \tfrac45)$ all amplitudes are rational and every statement is verified exactly over Fractions: (i) on a product with a mixed $X$; (iv) on the sixteen-dimensional model, including purity, evenness, the marginals, $\tr\varrho_{WR}^{2} = \tfrac{337}{625}$ and the failure of factorisation; (iii) by enumerating real pure states on a rational grid and finding the four sign choices of $\operatorname{diag}(\pm\tfrac35, \pm\tfrac45)$ as the only ones with both marginals thermal; and the zero-weight pair, using the density matrix $\tfrac12(|01\rangle + |10\rangle)(\langle01| + \langle10|)$.
