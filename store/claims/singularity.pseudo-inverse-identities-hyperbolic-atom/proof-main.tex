$v v^* v = v(1 - v v^*)^{\vphantom{*}} = v - v^2 v^* = v$ using \eqref{eq:car} and $v^2 = 0$; mirror for $v^*$. In the model: $v^2 = \tfrac14(e_0^2 + e_1^2) = \tfrac14(1 - 1) = 0$ (the cross terms cancel by anticommutation, \claimref{std-algebra.orthogonal-anticommute}), likewise $(v^*)^2 = 0$, and, by \claimref{std-algebra.vector-squares}, $v v^* + v^* v = 2\,(v \cdot v^*) = \tfrac12\bigl(e_0^2 - e_1^2\bigr) = \tfrac12(1 + 1) = 1$.
