Conventions: the chart's complex unit is $I_{6}$, the complex unit of the conformal spinor space $\CS$, acting on conformal spinors by right multiplication; numbers formed from conformal spinors (values of the Hermitian form, components, traces) are read in $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{6}\}$, and the bar is the conjugation that reverses $I_{6}$.

(1) Take a basis $e_{1}, \dots, e_{5}$ of $I^{\perp}$ and $e_{6} = I$; then the reciprocal basis has $e^{6} = I/Q(I)$, and $e^{1}, \dots, e^{5}$ is the reciprocal basis of $e_{1}, \dots, e_{5}$ within $I^{\perp}$. In $\tfrac12 \sum_{\alpha,\beta} M_{e_{\alpha}\wedge e_{\beta}}M_{e^{\alpha}\wedge e^{\beta}}$ the terms with no index equal to $6$ sum to $C_{2}(I^{\perp})$; the terms with exactly one index $6$ give $\tfrac12\sum_{a \le 5}\bigl(M_{e_{a}\wedge I}\,M_{e^{a}\wedge I} + M_{I\wedge e_{a}}\,M_{I\wedge e^{a}}\bigr)/Q(I) = Q(I)^{-1}\sum_{a} M_{e_{a}\wedge I}\,M_{e^{a}\wedge I}$; the term with both indices $6$ vanishes since $I \wedge I = 0$. Now $M_{a\wedge I} = \ip{P}{a}$, and $P \in I^{\perp}$, so $\sum_{a}\ip{P}{e_{a}}\ip{P}{e^{a}} = Q(P)$ (in components $g^{ab}M_{a6}M_{b6} = Q(P)$). Hence $C_{2}(\Lambda^{2}\CS) = C_{2}(I^{\perp}) + Q(P)/Q(I)$.

(2) \emph{The moment element.} Let $\mathsf{M} := \sum_{i}(Z^{i}I_{6})\otimes(\bar{Z}^{i}\cdot\,)$ be the endomorphism $W \mapsto \sum_{i}Z^{i}\,(\bar{Z}^{i}\cdot W)\,I_{6}$ of $\CS$, so that $M_{A} = \tr(\mathsf{M}A)$ by \eqref{eq:moment}, and let $\mathsf{M}_{0}$ be its traceless part; since every $A_{\alpha\beta} := A(e_{\alpha}\wedge e_{\beta})$ is traceless, $M_{\alpha\beta} = \tr(\mathsf{M}_{0}A_{\alpha\beta})$.

\emph{An invariant form.} Put
\[
\Phi(A, B) := \tfrac12 \sum_{\alpha,\beta}\tr\bigl(A\,A(e_{\alpha}\wedge e_{\beta})\bigr)\,\tr\bigl(B\,A(e^{\alpha}\wedge e^{\beta})\bigr)
\]
on $\mathfrak{sl}(\CS)$, so that $C_{2}(\Lambda^{2}\CS) = \Phi(\mathsf{M}_{0}, \mathsf{M}_{0})$. It is symmetric, and invariant: under $A \mapsto \gamma A\gamma^{-1}$ the matrices $A_{\alpha\beta}$ transform by the rotation of the ambient basis induced by $\gamma$ (Fact \claimref{mass.imported-flat-model-twistor-chart-infinity}), and a sum over a basis paired with its reciprocal basis does not depend on the basis, so is unchanged by that rotation. By Lemma \claimref{mass.invariant-forms-simple-lie-algebra}, $\Phi(A,B) = \kappa\tr(AB)$ for a constant $\kappa$.

\emph{The constant.} Evaluate at $A = B = \mathsf{E} := A(n_{\infty}\wedge n_{0}) = \operatorname{diag}(-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12,\tfrac12,\tfrac12)$, the grading element, in the basis $n_{\infty}, n_{0}$, $x$-slot. For a translation or special conformal generator $\tr(\mathsf{E}A_{\alpha\beta}) = 0$, the blocks being off-diagonal (Lemma \claimref{mass.chart-form-spin-isomorphism}). For a rotation of the $x$-slot: it annihilates $n_{\infty}$ and $n_{0}$, and an $A$ with $A\cdot n_{\infty} = 0$ has no $\Som \to \Spi$ block (the mixed component of $Ae_{1}\wedge e_{2} + e_{1}\wedge Ae_{2}$ is that block applied to $e_{1}, e_{2}$) and traceless $\Som$-block (the $n_{\infty}$-component is $\tr_{\Som}A$), and likewise from $n_{0}$; so $\tr(\mathsf{E}A_{\alpha\beta}) = -\tfrac12\tr_{\Som}A + \tfrac12\tr_{\Spi}A = 0$. Finally $\tr(\mathsf{E}\,A(n_{\infty}\wedge n_{0})) = \tr \mathsf{E}^{2} = 1$ and $\tr(\mathsf{E}\,A(n_{0}\wedge n_{\infty})) = -1$. The reciprocal of $n_{\infty}$ is $n_{0}$ and that of $n_{0}$ is $n_{\infty}$ (in components $g^{\infty 0} = g^{0\infty} = 1$, $g^{\infty\infty} = g^{00} = 0$), so the only surviving terms of $\Phi(\mathsf{E},\mathsf{E})$ are $(e_{\alpha}, e_{\beta}) = (n_{\infty}, n_{0})$, paired with $(e^{\alpha}, e^{\beta}) = (n_{0}, n_{\infty})$, and $(n_{0}, n_{\infty})$, paired with $(n_{\infty}, n_{0})$, each giving the product $(+1)(-1) = -1$, so $\Phi(\mathsf{E},\mathsf{E}) = \tfrac12(-1-1) = -1 = \kappa\tr \mathsf{E}^{2} = \kappa$.

\emph{The value.} Hence $C_{2}(\Lambda^{2}\CS) = -\tr(\mathsf{M}_{0}^{2})$. Now $\tr\mathsf{M} = I_{6}\sum_{i}\bar{Z}^{i}\cdot Z^{i} = I_{6}\tr h$ and $\tr\mathsf{M}^{2} = -\sum_{i,l}(\bar{Z}^{i}\cdot Z^{l})(\bar{Z}^{l}\cdot Z^{i}) = -\tr(h^{2})$ ($h$ is Hermitian), so $-\tr(\mathsf{M}_{0}^{2}) = -\tr\mathsf{M}^{2} + \tfrac14(\tr\mathsf{M})^{2} = \tr(h^{2}) - \tfrac14(\tr h)^{2}$.

(3) Combine (1), (2) and \claimref{mass.exact-join}: $C_{2}(I_{\Lambda}^{\perp}) = \tr h^{2} - \tfrac14(\tr h)^{2} - C - 6|B_{\Lambda}|^{2}/\Lambda$, and $\tr h^{2} - \tfrac14(\tr h)^{2} - C = \tfrac12\tr h^{2} = \tfrac14(\tr h)^{2} + C$ by \eqref{eq:gram}. The script forms both Casimirs from the exact moment components in adapted bases and confirms (1), (2) and (3) on four pairs, together with $\tr\mathsf{E}^{2} = 1$.
