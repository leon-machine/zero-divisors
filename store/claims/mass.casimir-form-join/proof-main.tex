Conventions: the chart's complex unit, written $i$ below, is $I_{6}$, the complex unit of the conformal spinor space $\CS$, and the bar is the conjugation that reverses it.

(1) Take a basis $e_{1}, \dots, e_{5}$ of $I^{\perp}$ and $e_{6} = I$; then $g^{a6} = 0$ for $a \le 5$ and $g^{66} = 1/Q(I)$. In $\tfrac12 g^{\alpha\alpha'}g^{\beta\beta'}M_{\alpha\beta}M_{\alpha'\beta'}$ the terms with no index equal to $6$ sum to $C_{2}(I^{\perp})$; the terms with exactly one $6$ in $(\alpha,\beta)$ need exactly one $6$ in $(\alpha',\beta')$ in the matching position, giving $\tfrac12\bigl(g^{ab}g^{66}M_{a6}M_{b6} + g^{66}g^{ab}M_{6a}M_{6b}\bigr) = g^{66}g^{ab}M_{a6}M_{b6}$; terms with $\alpha = \beta = 6$ vanish since $M_{66} = 0$. Now $M_{a6} = M_{e_{a}\wedge I} = \ip{P}{e_{a}}$, and $P \in I^{\perp}$, so $g^{ab}\ip{P}{e_{a}}\ip{P}{e_{b}} = Q(P)$. Hence $C_{2}(\Lambda^{2}\CS) = C_{2}(I^{\perp}) + Q(P)/Q(I)$.

(2) \emph{The moment element.} Let $\mathsf{M} := i\sum_{i}Z^{i}\otimes(\bar{Z}^{i}\cdot\,)$ be the endomorphism $W \mapsto i\sum_{i}Z^{i}\,(\bar{Z}^{i}\cdot W)$ of $\CS$, so that $M_{A} = \tr(\mathsf{M}A)$ by \eqref{eq:moment}, and let $\mathsf{M}_{0}$ be its traceless part; since every $A_{\alpha\beta} := A(e_{\alpha}\wedge e_{\beta})$ is traceless, $M_{\alpha\beta} = \tr(\mathsf{M}_{0}A_{\alpha\beta})$.

\emph{An invariant form.} Put
\[
\Phi(A, B) := \tfrac12\, g^{\alpha\alpha'}g^{\beta\beta'}\,\tr(AA_{\alpha\beta})\,\tr(BA_{\alpha'\beta'})
\]
on $\mathfrak{sl}(\CS)$, so that $C_{2}(\Lambda^{2}\CS) = \Phi(\mathsf{M}_{0}, \mathsf{M}_{0})$. It is symmetric, and invariant: under $A \mapsto \gamma A\gamma^{-1}$ the matrices $A_{\alpha\beta}$ transform by the rotation of the ambient basis induced by $\gamma$ (Fact \claimref{mass.imported-flat-model-twistor-chart-infinity}), and the contraction with $g^{\alpha\alpha'}g^{\beta\beta'}$ is invariant under rotations of the basis. By Lemma \claimref{mass.invariant-forms-simple-lie-algebra}, $\Phi(A,B) = \kappa\tr(AB)$ for a constant $\kappa$.

\emph{The constant.} Evaluate at $A = B = \mathsf{E} := A(n_{\infty}\wedge n_{0}) = \operatorname{diag}(-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12,\tfrac12,\tfrac12)$, the grading element, in the basis $n_{\infty}, n_{0}$, $x$-slot. For a translation or special conformal generator $\tr(\mathsf{E}A_{\alpha\beta}) = 0$, the blocks being off-diagonal (Lemma \claimref{mass.chart-form-spin-isomorphism}). For a rotation of the $x$-slot: it annihilates $n_{\infty}$ and $n_{0}$, and an $A$ with $A\cdot n_{\infty} = 0$ has no $\Som \to \Spi$ block (the mixed component of $Ae_{1}\wedge e_{2} + e_{1}\wedge Ae_{2}$ is that block applied to $e_{1}, e_{2}$) and traceless $\Som$-block (the $n_{\infty}$-component is $\tr_{\Som}A$), and likewise from $n_{0}$; so $\tr(\mathsf{E}A_{\alpha\beta}) = -\tfrac12\tr_{\Som}A + \tfrac12\tr_{\Spi}A = 0$. Finally $\tr(\mathsf{E}\,A(n_{\infty}\wedge n_{0})) = \tr \mathsf{E}^{2} = 1$ and $\tr(\mathsf{E}\,A(n_{0}\wedge n_{\infty})) = -1$. With $g^{\infty 0} = g^{0\infty} = 1$ and $g^{\infty\infty} = g^{00} = 0$ the only surviving terms of $\Phi(\mathsf{E},\mathsf{E})$ are $(\alpha\beta, \alpha'\beta') = (\infty 0, 0\infty)$ and $(0\infty, \infty 0)$, each contributing $-1$, so $\Phi(\mathsf{E},\mathsf{E}) = -1 = \kappa\tr \mathsf{E}^{2} = \kappa$.

\emph{The value.} Hence $C_{2}(\Lambda^{2}\CS) = -\tr(\mathsf{M}_{0}^{2})$. Now $\tr\mathsf{M} = i\sum_{i}\bar{Z}^{i}\cdot Z^{i} = i\tr h$ and $\tr\mathsf{M}^{2} = -\sum_{i,l}(\bar{Z}^{i}\cdot Z^{l})(\bar{Z}^{l}\cdot Z^{i}) = -\tr(h^{2})$ ($h$ is Hermitian), so $-\tr(\mathsf{M}_{0}^{2}) = -\tr\mathsf{M}^{2} + \tfrac14(\tr\mathsf{M})^{2} = \tr(h^{2}) - \tfrac14(\tr h)^{2}$.

(3) Combine (1), (2) and \claimref{mass.exact-join}: $C_{2}(I_{\Lambda}^{\perp}) = \tr h^{2} - \tfrac14(\tr h)^{2} - C - 6|B_{\Lambda}|^{2}/\Lambda$, and $\tr h^{2} - \tfrac14(\tr h)^{2} - C = \tfrac12\tr h^{2} = \tfrac14(\tr h)^{2} + C$ by \eqref{eq:gram}. The script forms both Casimirs from the exact moment components in adapted bases and confirms (1), (2) and (3) on four pairs, together with $\tr\mathsf{E}^{2} = 1$.
