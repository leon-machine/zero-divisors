Conventions as in the statement (store sign of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}). The check computes every curvature below from the gauge field, as exact Taylor jets of $\hb^{-1}$ (order two) about background points $(0, 0, 0, r)$ at $M = \tfrac12$ ($2M = 1$), at $r = \tfrac14, 1, \tfrac94, 4, 9$ (inside, on and outside the horizon), with the torsion-free $\Omega$ solved from the jets; the general $M$ follows by the scaling $x \mapsto 2M x$, under which $I$ scales as $(2M)^{-4}$ and $\hat g$, $\hat R$ as stated.

\emph{(i).} The check finds $\mathcal{R}(b) = 0$ (Ricci-flat) and $2\langle\mathcal{R}(a\wedge b)\mathcal{R}(\partial_{b}\wedge\partial_{a})\rangle = 48M^{2}/r^{6}$ at the five radii (tabulated), equal to the square of the Weyl part since the Ricci part vanishes. For general $r$ this is the standard Kretschmann scalar of the Schwarzschild metric (cited from memory); the samples on both sides of the horizon are a check of it in the store's sign, not a proof of the general formula.

\emph{(ii).} Let $C^{a}{}_{bcd}$ be the Weyl tensor of $g$, of type $(1,3)$. Under $g \mapsto \Omega^{2}g$ it is unchanged (conformal invariance of the Weyl tensor), and $I = C^{a}{}_{bcd}C^{e}{}_{fgh}\,g_{ae}g^{bf}g^{cg}g^{dh}$ has one metric and three inverse metrics, so $I \mapsto \Omega^{2 - 6}I = \Omega^{-4}I$. The check confirms the weight $-4$ exactly for the two nonconstant factors $\Omega = 2M/r$ and $\Omega = (2M/r)^{3/2}$ (tabulated). Hence $\hat g := (2M)^{3}(I/48M^{2})^{1/2}g = (2M/r)^{3}g$ satisfies $\widehat{\Omega^{2}g} = \Omega^{-2}\cdot\Omega^{2}\hat g = \hat g$. If $\xi$ is a conformal symmetry on $W$, its flow $\varphi_{s}$ satisfies $\varphi_{s}^{*}g = \Omega_{s}^{2}g$ and carries the Weyl tensor of $g$ to that of $\varphi_{s}^{*}g$, so $\varphi_{s}^{*}I = I(\varphi_{s}^{*}g) = \Omega_{s}^{-4}I$ and $\varphi_{s}^{*}\hat g = (2M)^{3}(\Omega_{s}^{-4}I/48M^{2})^{1/2}\Omega_{s}^{2}g = \hat g$: $\xi$ is a Killing field of $\hat g$. With $\Omega = (2M/r)^{3/2}$ the check finds the Weyl square of $\hat g$ equal to $12 = 48M^{2}/(2M)^{6}$ at three radii.

\emph{(iii).} The check computes the scalar curvature of the gauge field $(2M/r)^{3/2}\hb^{-1}$ (whose derived metric is $\hat g$) at the five radii: $\tfrac{99}{8}, 9, \tfrac{27}{8}, -\tfrac92, -27$, equal to $(27M - \tfrac92 r)/(2M)^{3}$, with every difference quotient $-\tfrac92$. (Independently: for $\hat g = \Omega^{2}g$ with $g$ Ricci-flat, $\hat R = -6\,\Omega^{-3}\Box_{g}\Omega$ in the sign where spheres have positive curvature, and $\Omega = (2M/r)^{3/2}$, $\Box_{g}\Omega = r^{-2}\partial_{r}(r^{2}(1 - 2M/r)\partial_{r}\Omega)$ give the same affine function.) A Killing field $\xi$ of $\hat g$ preserves $\hat R$, so $\xi(r) = -\tfrac{2(2M)^{3}}{9}\,\xi(\hat R) = 0$: $\xi$ is tangent to every hypersurface $r = \mathrm{const}$ in $W$.

\emph{(iv).} Use the outgoing coordinates $(u, r, \vartheta, \varphi)$ of the derived metric ($u = t - r$ with $t$ the background time of the gauge), in which $g = (1 - \tfrac{2M}{r})du^{2} + 2\,du\,dr - r^{2}d\sigma^{2}$, $d\sigma^{2}$ the unit round metric, and $\hat g = (2M/r)^{3}g$. On $W \cap \{r = r_{0}\}$ with $r_{0} \neq 2M$ the induced $\hat g$ is the product $(2M/r_{0})^{3}\bigl((1 - \tfrac{2M}{r_{0}})du^{2} - r_{0}^{2}d\sigma^{2}\bigr)$ of a line and a round two-sphere, nondegenerate. Since $\xi$ is tangent and Killing for $\hat g$, its restriction is Killing for this product. Write $\xi = \xi^{u}\partial_{u} + Y$ with $Y$ tangent to the spheres. The $uu$ component of the Killing equation gives $\partial_{u}\xi^{u} = 0$; the $AB$ components (sphere directions) say $Y$ is a Killing field of the round sphere for each $u$; the $uA$ components give $(1 - \tfrac{2M}{r_{0}})\partial_{A}\xi^{u} = r_{0}^{2}\,\partial_{u}Y_{A}$, so $\partial_{u}Y$ is a gradient field on the sphere; differentiating in $u$, $\partial_{u}^{2}Y = 0$ and $\partial_{u}Y$ is a Killing gradient field of the round sphere, which is parallel and hence zero (the round sphere has no nonzero parallel field); then $\partial_{A}\xi^{u} = 0$. So on each shell $\xi = \alpha(r)\partial_{u} + \sum_{j}c_{j}(r)L_{j}$, the Killing algebra of a line times a round sphere. Now the full Killing equation of $\hat g$ on $W$: $\hat g_{rr} = 0$ and $\hat g_{ru} = (2M/r)^{3}$, $\hat g_{rA} = 0$, $\xi^{r} = 0$, so $(\mathcal{L}_{\xi}\hat g)_{rr} = 2\hat g_{ru}\partial_{r}\xi^{u} = 0$ gives $\alpha' = 0$, and $(\mathcal{L}_{\xi}\hat g)_{rA} = \hat g_{AB}\partial_{r}\xi^{B} + \hat g_{ru}\partial_{A}\xi^{u} = \hat g_{AB}\partial_{r}Y^{B} = 0$ gives $c_{j}' = 0$. So $\xi = \alpha\partial_{u} + \sum c_{j}L_{j}$ with constants on $W$ minus the horizon, and on $W$ by continuity ($\partial_{u} = \partial_{t}$ at fixed $r$ and the $L_{j}$ are smooth across $r = 2M$, and $W$ minus the horizon is dense in $W$). These fields are Killing symmetries of the field: the gauge field does not depend on $t$ (checked: every coframe jet has vanishing $t$ derivative) and is rotation-covariant.

\emph{(v).} The physical vector $K = \hb^{-1}(e_{0})$ of $\partial_{t}$ has $Q(K) = 1 - 2M/r$: zero on $r = 2M$, positive outside, negative inside (checked at $r = 1, 4, \tfrac14$). On $r = 2M$ the check finds $(K\cdot D)K = D_{e_{0}}K = -\tfrac12 K = -\kappa K$ with $\kappa = 1/(4M)$, so $K$ is tangent to the null generators and not affinely parametrised, with inaffinity of magnitude $\kappa$; off the horizon $(K\cdot D)K$ is not along $K$ (checked). Along a generator of the past horizon $K = \partial_{u}$, and an affine tangent $h K$ needs $dh/du = \kappa h$, so an affine parameter $\lambda$ satisfies $d\lambda/du = c\,e^{-\kappa u}$, $\lambda = -(c/\kappa)e^{-\kappa u}$ up to an additive constant fixed by the bifurcation edge at $\lambda = 0$ (the boost $\beta_{t}$ of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.null-vacuum-on-a-horizon-generator}(i), which acts on the affine parameter as the dilation at rate $\kappa$), and the time translation by $\tau$ is $\lambda \mapsto e^{-\kappa\tau}\lambda$, generated by $-\kappa\lambda\,\partial_{\lambda}$. By (iv) any conformal symmetry restricts to an open piece of the horizon as $-\alpha\kappa\lambda\,\partial_{\lambda} + \sum c_{j}L_{j}$. If this were $\partial_{\lambda}$ on an open piece, the rotation part would vanish (it moves the generator) and $-\alpha\kappa\lambda = 1$ on an open interval of $\lambda$, impossible. The future horizon is the image of the past one under the time reflection $t \mapsto -t$, which carries the gauge to the ingoing one ($k = e_{0} - \hat{x}$, coordinate $v = t + r$); there $(K\cdot D)K = +\kappa K$, $d\lambda/dv = c\,e^{\kappa v}$ and the time translation is $\lambda \mapsto e^{\kappa\tau}\lambda$, and the argument is the same. The statement is symmetric under the reflection. So no conformal symmetry on any open set meeting the horizon restricts to the affine translations.

\emph{(vi).} In Proposition \claimref{synthesis.positive-conformal-energy-gives-positive-null-translations} and Theorem \claimref{synthesis.de-sitter-horizon-translation-is-conformal-null-rotation} the positivity of a translation along a horizon is obtained by exhibiting it as a limit of conjugates of $Z$ in a unitary representation of $\mathrm{Spin}^{+}(2,4)$ acting by conformal symmetries of the field (the null translation of Minkowski space; the conformal null rotation $\tfrac12 nI$ for de Sitter, whose Weyl part vanishes, which the check confirms as a control: Weyl square zero, no invariant metric). A representation of the conformal symmetries of the Schwarzschild field near a piece of its horizon is, by (iv), a representation of the time translation and rotations, whose action on the generators is the dilation and the rotations of (v); none of its elements, nor any limit of them in the Lie algebra (the algebra is four-dimensional and closed), acts on the generators as the affine translation. The statement concerns the geometric action of the field's conformal symmetries; an abstract unitary group on the generators' modes is not excluded, and that is where the positivity must sit. A conformal map of a region of vanishing field strength cannot carry a translation onto the horizon either, since it would be a conformal symmetry of the field near the horizon. So the positivity in case (a) of Premise \claimref{synthesis.n-plus-null-ground-state} is not supplied by the conformal structure; it is a condition on the state of the generators' modes, which the affine translations act on as automorphisms of the null-surface algebra but not as symmetries of the field.
