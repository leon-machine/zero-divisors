Read in Gauge Theory Gravity, the line element below is the derived metric of the Schwarzschild field in its synchronous gauge, and the frame sectional curvatures are the eigenvalues of the curvature operator $\mathcal{R}(B)$ on the frame planes (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}, clauses (i) and (iv)). \emph{(i)} By Proposition \claimref{interior.kasner-form-interior} the interior is $ds^{2} = d\tau^{2} - A\,dt^{2} - r^{2}\,d\Omega^{2}$ with $A = 2M/r - 1$ and $d\tau = A^{-1/2}dr$. Write $a = \sqrt A$ for the $t$ scale factor and $b = r$ for the sphere's, and a dot for $d/d\tau = \sqrt A\,d/dr$. Then $\dot b^{2} = A$, so $a = \dot b$, $\dot a = \ddot b = \tfrac12\,dA/dr = -M/r^{2}$, and $\ddot a/a = d\ddot b/dr = 2M/r^{3}$. For a metric of this Kantowski-Sachs form the frame sectional curvatures are, up to one overall sign fixed by the signature convention, $\ddot a/a$ for the $\tau t$ plane, $\ddot b/b$ for the two $\tau$-sphere planes, $\dot a\dot b/(ab)$ for the two $t$-sphere planes and $(1 + \dot b^{2})/b^{2}$ for the sphere plane, and the frame Riemann tensor is diagonal on these six planes (the metric is diagonal and depends on $\tau$ only, with the sphere's own curvature in the last entry). Substituting: $\ddot a/a = 2M/r^{3}$, $\ddot b/b = -M/r^{3}$, $\dot a\dot b/(ab) = \ddot b/b = -M/r^{3}$, $(1 + A)/r^{2} = 2M/r^{3}$, which is $(2, -1, -1, -1, -1, 2) \times M/r^{3}$. The three Ricci combinations $2 - 1 - 1$ vanish (vacuum), and the Kretschmann scalar is $4\sum K^{2} = 4 \cdot 12\,M^{2}/r^{6} = 48M^{2}/r^{6}$. An algebraic curvature scalar is a frame-invariant polynomial in the frame components, all of which are fixed rational multiples of $M/r^{3}$, so it is a polynomial in $w = 2M/r^{3}$; setting it to a mass-independent value selects, on each branch, a mass-independent value $\lambda$ of $w$, that is $r_c^{3} = 2M/\lambda$. At $\lambda = 1$ this is (C1); the Kretschmann criterion is $12w^{2} = 1$, $\lambda = 12^{-1/2}$. The script computes all six curvatures as exact rational functions of $r$ at five rational masses and checks the multiples, the vacuum combinations and the Kretschmann identity.

\emph{(ii)} For the Kasner metric $d\tau^{2} - \sum_i \tau^{2p_i}dx_i^{2}$ the same formulas with $a_i = \tau^{p_i}$ and flat factors give $\ddot a_i/a_i = p_i(p_i - 1)/\tau^{2}$ and $\dot a_i\dot a_j/(a_ia_j) = p_ip_j/\tau^{2}$. With the forced weights $(-\tfrac13, \tfrac23, \tfrac23)$ of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.weights-are-forced-are-kasner-exponents} (the Kasner exponents of Fact \claimref{sheaf.kasner-data-interior}) these are $4/9, -2/9, -2/9$ and $-2/9, -2/9, 4/9$ over $\tau^{2}$, that is $(2, -1, -1, -1, -1, 2) \times 2/(9\tau^{2})$. Hence $w = \lambda$ is $\tau = \tfrac23\lambda^{-1/2}$ at leading order, and $\tau = 1$ is $\lambda = 4/9$. The script checks the six Kasner numbers and the identity $4/(9\tau^{2}) = 2M/r^{3}$ on rational points.

\emph{(iii)} By Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-induced-geometry-interior-slice} the slice's metric is $|f(r_c)|\,dt^{2} + r_c^{2}d\Omega^{2}$ with constant $|f(r_c)|$, invariant under $t \mapsto t + c$ and under $SO(3)$ on the sphere. Translations are transitive on $\R$ and $SO(3)$ is transitive on $S^{2}$ (the script exhibits, for several rational unit vectors $v$, a rotor $1 + ve_3$ carrying $e_3$ to $v$ exactly), so $\R \times SO(3)$ is transitive on the slice, with compact stabiliser $SO(2)$. A Radon measure on a homogeneous space $G/K$, $K$ compact, invariant under $G$, is unique up to a positive constant (the Haar-Weil theorem, cited from memory); the Riemannian volume is one such measure, so any invariant density is $\nu$ times it, which is the form of (C2).

\emph{(iv)} On the locus $2M = \lambda r_c^{3}$, $2M/r_c - 1 = \lambda r_c^{2} - 1 = \lambda r_c^{2}(1 - r_c/2M)$ because $1/(\lambda r_c^{2}) = r_c/(\lambda r_c^{3}) = r_c/2M$.
