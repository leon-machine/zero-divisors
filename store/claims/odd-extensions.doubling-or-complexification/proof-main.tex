(i) With $\omega$ central (Lemma \claimref{odd-extensions.volume-element-odd-dimension}) and $\omega^{2} = 1$ the elements $P_{\pm} = (1 \pm \omega)/2$ are central, idempotent, orthogonal and sum to one, so the algebra is the direct sum of the ideals $P_{\pm}\Cl$. The map $x \mapsto P_{\pm}x$ restricted to $\Cl^{0}$ is injective, since $\omega$ is odd and $x - \omega x$ can vanish for even $x$ only if $x = 0$, and surjective onto $P_{\pm}\Cl$ because $P_{\pm}y = P_{\pm}(y_{0} \pm \omega y_{1})$ for $y = y_{0} + y_{1}$ split by parity, with $\omega y_{1}$ even; and $P_{\pm}$ is central, so the map is an algebra isomorphism. Finally $\omega = \omega' e_{n}$ with $e_{n}$ commuting with $\omega'$ (it anticommutes with each of the $n - 1$ factors, an even number, \claimref{std-algebra.orthogonal-anticommute}), so $\omega^{2} = \omega'^{2}e_{n}^{2}$ and $\omega^{2} = 1$ forces $\omega'^{2} = e_{n}^{2} =: s \in \{\pm 1\}$; then $P_{\pm}\omega' = (\omega' \pm s\,e_{n})/2$ and $P_{\pm}e_{n} = (e_{n} \pm s\,\omega')/2 = \pm s\,P_{\pm}\omega'$, so $e_{n}$ acts on the two summands as $+s\,\omega'$ and $-s\,\omega'$. (ii) With $\omega^{2} = -1$ central, $\Cl$ is an algebra over the field $\mathrm{span}\{1, \omega\} \cong \C$, and the $\R$-linear map $\Cl^{0} \otimes_{\R} \mathrm{span}\{1, \omega\} \to \Cl$, $x \otimes (a + b\,\omega) \mapsto ax + b\,\omega x$, is an algebra homomorphism, injective by the parity argument, and surjective by dimension ($2 \cdot 2^{n-1} = 2^{n}$, \claimref{std-algebra.z2-grading}). (iii) For $e_{n}^{2} = -1$ the elements $e_{i}e_{n}$, $i < n$, anticommute pairwise and square to $-e_{i}^{2}e_{n}^{2} = e_{i}^{2}$, so they generate a copy of $\Cl(p,q)$ inside $\Cl^{0}(p, q+1)$, of the full dimension $2^{n-1}$; for $e_{n}^{2} = +1$ they square to $-e_{i}^{2}$ and generate $\Cl(q,p)$. The script verifies (iii), and the identities behind (i) and (ii) (the sign pairing $P_{\pm}e_{n} = \pm s P_{\pm}\omega'$, the dimensions and the spanning, but not the idempotent identities and not the algebra-map properties, which are argued above), over every odd signature of dimensions three and five, and (i) and (ii) in detail on the four extensions of Corollary \claimref{odd-extensions.four-extensions-oriented-pair}: the idempotents, the two ideals of dimension sixteen, the action of $e_{5}$ as $\pm\omega'$ on them, and the spanning of all thirty-two dimensions by $\Cl(p,q) + \omega\,\Cl(p,q)$ in the complexified cases.
