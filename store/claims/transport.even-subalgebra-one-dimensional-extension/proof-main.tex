The images are even. Their squares are $(e_i\nu)^2 = e_i\nu e_i\nu = -e_i^2\nu^2$, which is $e_i^2$ for $\nu^2 = -1$ and $-e_i^2$ for $\nu^2 = +1$; for $i \neq j$, $(e_i\nu)(e_j\nu) = -e_ie_j\nu^2$ and $(e_j\nu)(e_i\nu) = -e_je_i\nu^2 = e_ie_j\nu^2$, so distinct images anticommute. By the universal property (\claimref{std-algebra.existence-uniqueness}) the assignment extends to a unital homomorphism from $\Cl(p,q)$ (respectively $\Cl(q,p)$) into the even subalgebra. It is surjective: for $i \neq j$, $(e_i\nu)(e_j\nu) = -\nu^2 e_ie_j = \mp e_ie_j$, so every even blade of the extension not involving $\nu$ is, up to sign, a product of images, and every even blade involving $\nu$ is $e_I\nu$ with $|I|$ odd, which is $\pm(e_{i_1}\nu)(e_{i_2}\nu)(e_{i_3}\nu)\cdots$ after pairing all but the last factor. Both sides have dimension $2^n$, so the surjection is an isomorphism. The script confirms the relations, the evenness, and the dimension on $(1,3)$ and $(2,0)$ with both signs of $\nu^2$.
