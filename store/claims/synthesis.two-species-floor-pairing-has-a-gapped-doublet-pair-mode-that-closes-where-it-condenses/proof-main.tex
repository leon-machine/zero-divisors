\begin{proof}
\emph{Setting.} The tower planes generate the CAR with $v$ creating and $w$ annihilating (Fact \claimref{field-theory.imported-tower-limit}): $\{w_{i}(b), v_{j}(c)\} = \delta_{ij}\delta_{bc}$, all other anticommutators zero. Every operator below is a sum of products of an even number of mode operators except where said, so it commutes with the parity $\Gamma$, which anticommutes with each $v$ and $w$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.kernel-bivector-is-the-unit-of-hermitian-boson-fields-on-the-tower}(iv)). Commutators of bilinears follow from $[AB, CD] = A\{B, C\}D - AC\{B, D\} + \{A, C\}DB - C\{A, D\}B$ for mode operators $A, B, C, D$. The check realises one block in the Clifford algebra of three tower planes and a kernel plane with GA's product, and the floors in GA's exterior model of the CAR (creation by wedge, annihilation by contraction), as in the stored pairing checks.

\emph{(i).} $S^{*}S$ and $B_{\alpha}^{*}B_{\alpha}$ move a pair between blocks, so they conserve each $N_{i}$. With the identity above, $[t_{-}, B_{1}^{*}] = \sum_{b}[v_{2}w_{1}, v_{3}v_{1}] = -v_{2}v_{3}$ summed, which is $B_{2}^{*}$; $[t_{+}, Z_{2}^{*}] = \sum_{b}[v_{1}w_{2}, w_{1}v_{3}] = \sum_{b}v_{3}w_{2} = Z_{1}^{*}$; $[t_{+}, B_{1}^{*}] = 0$ and $[t_{+}, Z_{1}^{*}] = 0$ (no matching pair of indices), and $[t_{\pm}, S^{*}] = 0$ because $t_{\pm}$ exchanges modes $1$ and $2$ inside $v_{2}v_{1}$, which is antisymmetric in them. With $[t_{3}, \cdot\,]$ giving $\pm\tfrac12$ on these, $(B_{1}^{*}, B_{2}^{*})$ and $(Z_{1}^{*}, Z_{2}^{*})$ are doublets and $S$ is a singlet, so $\sum_{\alpha}B_{\alpha}^{*}B_{\alpha}$ and $S^{*}S$ commute with $t_{\pm}$, and so does $H$ (the check confirms $[H, t_{\pm}] = 0$ on every Fock vector of the floors $n_{b} = 1, 2$ at four couplings). $[Z_{2}^{*}, S^{*}] = \sum_{b}[w_{1}v_{3}, v_{2}v_{1}] = \sum_{b}v_{3}v_{2} = B_{2}^{*}$ and $[Z_{1}^{*}, S^{*}] = \sum_{b}v_{3}v_{1} = B_{1}^{*}$; $Z_{\alpha}^{*}$ contains one $v_{3}$ and one $w$ of mode $1$ or $2$, hence the charges.

\emph{(ii).} Two distinct pairs of $\{1, 2, 3\}$ share a mode, so a product of two pair creators of one block contains a $v_{i}(b)$ twice and vanishes (checked in the Clifford algebra). The even vectors of one block are therefore $\Omega_{0}$ and the three pair vectors $\pi_{ij}^{*}\Omega_{0}$, $\pi_{12}^{*} = v_{2}v_{1}$, $\pi_{13}^{*} = v_{3}v_{1}$, $\pi_{23}^{*} = v_{3}v_{2}$, and on this four-dimensional space $\pi_{ij}^{*}$ maps $\Omega_{0}$ to the pair vector and annihilates the others, while $\pi_{ij} = (\pi_{ij}^{*})^{*}$ maps the pair vector back. Even operators of different blocks commute, so the even vectors of the floor form the tensor product of $n_{b}$ copies of this space, and the totals $S^{*} = \sum_{b}\pi_{12}^{*}$, $B_{1}^{*} = \sum_{b}\pi_{13}^{*}$, $B_{2}^{*} = \sum_{b}\pi_{23}^{*}$ act on its symmetric part as $a_{ij}^{\dagger}a_{0}$ in the standard boson description of a symmetric tensor power (bosons $a_{0}$ for an empty block and $a_{ij}$ for a block holding the pair $ij$, $n_{0} + \sum n_{ij} = n_{b}$). Hence $x(n_{S}, n_{1}, n_{2})$ is a nonzero multiple of the symmetric vector with $n_{12} = n_{S}$, $n_{13} = n_{1}$, $n_{23} = n_{2}$, $n_{0} = n_{b} - P$ when $P \le n_{b}$, and vanishes when $P > n_{b}$; and $\pi^{*}\pi$ for one kind acts as $a_{ij}^{\dagger}a_{0}a_{0}^{\dagger}a_{ij} = n_{ij}(n_{0} + 1) = n_{ij}(n_{b} - P + 1)$. The kinetic terms count $N_{\psi} = 2n_{S} + n$ and $N_{\chi} = n$, which gives $E(n_{S}, n)$. This is the stored one-shell band of Proposition \claimref{field-theory.composite-scalars-mean-field-level}(2) (pairs of one kind, $n_{ij}(n_{b} - n_{ij} + 1)$) with three kinds sharing the empty blocks. At fixed $n_{S}$ and $n$, $S^{*}$ commutes with $t_{\pm}$ and the $B_{\alpha}^{*}$ commute with each other and form a doublet, so the span of the $x(n_{S}, n_{1}, n - n_{1})$ is a nonzero image of the symmetric power of a doublet, which is irreducible of isospin $n/2$ and has dimension $n + 1$; the vectors are nonzero for each $n_{1}$, so the span is that irreducible module. The check verifies $Hx = E(n_{S}, n)x$ and $C_{I}x = \tfrac{n}{2}(\tfrac{n}{2} + 1)x$ exactly for every $(n_{S}, n_{1}, n_{2})$ with $P \le n_{b}$, $n_{b} = 1, \ldots, 4$, at four couplings, and that $x$ vanishes for $P = n_{b} + 1$.

\emph{(iii).} $Z_{2}^{*}\Omega_{0} = 0$; $[Z_{2}^{*}, S^{*}] = B_{2}^{*}$ by (i); $[B_{2}^{*}, S^{*}] = 0$; and $[Z_{2}^{*}, B_{2}^{*}] = \sum_{b}[w_{1}v_{3}, v_{3}v_{2}] = 0$ (each term contains $v_{3}(b)$ twice or no contraction). So $Z_{2}^{*}(S^{*})^{m}(B_{2}^{*})^{n}\Omega_{0} = m(S^{*})^{m - 1}(B_{2}^{*})^{n + 1}\Omega_{0}$, which is the ladder relation with $m = P - n$. Per block, $[w_{3}v_{1}, w_{1}v_{3}] = v_{1}w_{1}w_{3}v_{3} - v_{3}w_{3}w_{1}v_{1} = n_{1}(1 - n_{3}) - n_{3}(1 - n_{1}) = n_{1} - n_{3}$, so $[Z_{2}, Z_{2}^{*}] = N_{1} - N_{3}$, and on $x(P - n, 0, n)$, $N_{1} = P - n$, $N_{3} = n$. From (ii), $E(P - n, n) - E(P, 0) = n(K_{\chi} - K_{\psi}) + n(G_{S} - G_{D})(n_{b} - P + 1) = n\omega_{D}$. In a tower level whose planes include these, the kernel bivector $J$ commutes with every plane generator and $J^{*} = -J$, and for any $X$ in the plane algebra $\phi_{X} = X + X^{*}$, $\pi_{X} = J(X^{*} - X)$ are self-adjoint with $[\phi_{X}, \pi_{X}] = 2J[X, X^{*}]$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.kernel-bivector-is-the-unit-of-hermitian-boson-fields-on-the-tower}(ii), (v)); with $X = Z_{2}$ this is $2J(N_{1} - N_{3})$. The defect $2n/P$ is that of the stored collective pair of the quasi-spin (Proposition \claimref{field-theory.how-close-tower-comes-ccr}): here the quasi-spin is the transfer between two kinds of pair, which is exact on the band. The check verifies the ladder relation, the commutator on the ladder, the harmonic energies at four couplings for $n_{b} \le 4$ and every $P$, the charges of $Z_{2}^{*}x$, $[t_{+}, Z_{2}^{*}]x = Z_{1}^{*}x$, and the operator identity $[Z_{2}, Z_{2}^{*}] = N_{1} - N_{3}$ on every Fock vector of $n_{b} = 1, 2$.

\emph{(iv).} At fixed $P$, $E(P - n, n) = E(P, 0) + n\omega_{D}$ for $0 \le n \le P$ is affine, so its minimum over the band is at $n = 0$ for $\omega_{D} > 0$, at $n = P$ for $\omega_{D} < 0$, and no interior $n$ is strictly lowest; at $\omega_{D} = 0$ all are equal. On the doublet band $E(0, P)$ does not depend on $n_{1}$, so the whole isospin-$P/2$ multiplet of (ii) is degenerate; $B_{\alpha}^{*}x(0, n_{1}, n_{2})$ is a nonzero multiple of $x(0, n_{1} + \delta_{\alpha1}, n_{2} + \delta_{\alpha2})$ for $P < n_{b}$, both at energy $E(0, P + 1)$ (not $E(0, P)$). Lowering $n$ from $P$ at fixed $P$ costs $-\omega_{D}$ per step, through $Z_{2}$ on the stretched vector; $Z_{2}$ adds a $\psi_{1}$ and removes a $\chi$, so it changes $N_{\psi} - N_{\chi}$ by $2$, while every $B_{\alpha}$ changes $N_{\psi}$ and $N_{\chi}$ alike and so leaves $N_{\psi} - N_{\chi}$ unbroken. In the sector $(0, P, P)$ no mode $1$ is occupied, so $S^{*}S$ and $B_{1}^{*}B_{1}$ vanish there and $H = (K_{\psi} + K_{\chi})P - G_{D}B_{2}^{*}B_{2}$ is the stored pairing element on the modes $2, 3$ of each block (Proposition \claimref{field-theory.composite-scalars-mean-field-level}(2)), whose band is lowest and whose next level is exactly $G_{D}n_{b}$ higher with none between for $0 < P < n_{b}$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.tower-pairing-gap-at-fixed-charges-is-twice-the-quasiparticle-energy}(i), (ii)). The stretched sector has $t_{3} = -P/2$ and $N_{\psi} = P$, so its levels are exactly those of total isospin $P/2$ at these numbers, the condensate's own isospin. These statements are on the band and in that sector; other sectors at the same numbers are treated in (x). The check verifies the degeneracy and the two pair additions at four couplings for $n_{b} \le 4$, and the sector statement by exact inertia of $H - E$ at $E$ the band value and the band value plus $G_{D}n_{b}$.

\emph{(v).} At $G_{D} = 0$, $H = K_{\psi}N_{\psi} + K_{\chi}N_{\chi} - G_{S}S^{*}S$ and $S^{*}S$ acts on the modes $1, 2$ only; on $V_{P}$ the mode-$3$ particle is a spectator and the modes $1, 2$ are in the sector $(N_{a}, N_{c}) = (P - 1, P)$ of the stored element. By Theorem \claimref{synthesis.tower-pairing-gap-at-fixed-charges-is-twice-the-quasiparticle-energy}(i) its levels are $K(2p + s_{a} + s_{c}) - G_{S}(j + j_{z})(j - j_{z} + 1)$; the largest value of $S^{*}S$ there is $(P - 1)(n_{b} - P + 1)$, at $s_{a} = 0$, $s_{c} = 1$, $p = P - 1$ and top quasi-spin on the $n_{b} - 1$ other blocks. So the lowest level on $V_{P}$ is $E_{\mathrm{th}}$, and $0 \le S^{*}S \le (P - 1)(n_{b} - P + 1)$ on $V_{P}$ for all couplings. $x(P - 1, 0, 1) \in V_{P}$, and by (ii) its energy is $E_{D} = K_{\psi}(2P - 1) + K_{\chi} - (G_{S}(P - 1) + G_{D})(n_{b} - P + 1) = E_{\mathrm{th}} - G_{D}(n_{b} - P + 1)$; subtracting $E(P, 0) = 2K_{\psi}P - G_{S}P(n_{b} - P + 1)$ gives the two differences. The check certifies $E_{\mathrm{th}}$ as the lowest level at $G_{D} = 0$ by exact inertia for $n_{b} \le 4$. The lowest level of $H$ with $N_{\chi} = 0$ and $N = 2P$ is $E(P, 0)$: there $H$ is the stored element on modes $1, 2$, its sector $(P, P)$ has the band lowest (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.tower-pairing-gap-at-fixed-charges-is-twice-the-quasiparticle-energy}(i)), and every isospin multiplet with $N_{1} + N_{2} = 2P$ meets that sector. Likewise every level with $N_{\chi} = 1$, $N = 2P$ has half-integer isospin and so a member in $V_{P}$ or its image under $t_{+}$, which has the same levels; this is why $V_{P}$ decides the doublet gap.

\emph{(vi).} Put $\rho_{bc} = \sum_{i}v_{i}(b)w_{i}(c)$, so $L_{bc} = \rho_{bc} - \rho_{cb}$, $L_{bc}^{*} = -L_{bc}$, and let $\sigma_{bc} = \rho_{bc} + \rho_{cb}$. Normal ordering with the CAR gives, summed over all indices: $C_{3} = 3N - Q$ and $\sum_{b,c}\rho_{bc}\rho_{cb} = n_{b}N + Q$ with the same quartic $Q = \sum v_{i}(b)v_{j}(c)w_{j}(b)w_{i}(c)$, so $C_{3} + \sum_{b,c}\rho_{bc}\rho_{cb} = (n_{b} + 3)N$. Next $\rho_{bc}\rho_{cb} = \tfrac14(\sigma_{bc}^{2} - L_{bc}^{2} + [L_{bc}, \sigma_{bc}])$ and the commutator terms cancel between $(b, c)$ and $(c, b)$, so $\sum_{b,c}\rho_{bc}\rho_{cb} = \tfrac14\sum_{b,c}\sigma_{bc}^{2} + \tfrac12C_{O}$. Finally $\sum_{b,c}\sigma_{bc}^{2} = 2\sum_{b,c}\rho_{bc}\rho_{bc} + 2\sum_{b,c}\rho_{bc}\rho_{cb}$ and, normal ordering again, $\sum_{b,c}\rho_{bc}\rho_{bc} = N + \sum_{i,j}\sum_{b,c}v_{j}(b)v_{i}(b)w_{i}(c)w_{j}(c) = N + 2W$, because $W = \tfrac12\sum_{i \ne j}\pi_{ij}^{*}\pi_{ij}$ summed over blocks is that quartic. Combining, $\tfrac12\sum_{b,c}\rho_{bc}\rho_{cb} = \tfrac12N + W + \tfrac12C_{O}$, so $2W = (n_{b} + 3)N - C_{3} - N - C_{O} = N(n_{b} + 2) - C_{3} - C_{O}$. $C_{O}$ is a sum of $L^{*}L$, hence $C_{O} \ge 0$. The check verifies the identity on every Fock vector of $n_{b} = 1, 2, 3$.

\emph{(vii).} On $V_{P}$ all number operators are constant, so $H = K_{\psi}(2P - 1) + K_{\chi} - (G_{S} - G_{D})S^{*}S - G_{D}W$. With $G_{S} - G_{D} \ge 0$, $S^{*}S \le (P - 1)(n_{b} - P + 1)$ by (v) and $W \le X$, every level is at least $K_{\psi}(2P - 1) + K_{\chi} - (G_{S} - G_{D})(P - 1)(n_{b} - P + 1) - G_{D}X$, which is $E_{D} - G_{D}(X - P(n_{b} - P + 1))$. For the second level, Weyl's inequality for Hermitian $A$ and $B$, $\lambda_{2}(A + B) \ge \lambda_{1}(A) + \lambda_{2}(B)$ (levels in increasing order with multiplicity; from the min-max characterisation), with $A = -(G_{S} - G_{D})S^{*}S$, $\lambda_{1}(A) \ge -(G_{S} - G_{D})(P - 1)(n_{b} - P + 1)$, and $B = -G_{D}W$, $\lambda_{2}(B) = -G_{D}X_{2}$, gives $\lambda_{2}(H) \ge E_{\mathrm{th}} + G_{D}(P - 1)(n_{b} - P + 1) - G_{D}X_{2}$.

\emph{(viii).} By computation. The check builds $W$ on $V_{P}$ for $n_{b} = 2, \ldots, 6$ and every $P$ (dimensions up to $1800$) and counts its eigenvalues above $X$ and above $X_{2}$ by exact congruence diagonalisation of $W - a$ ($a = X$: none above, $a$ an eigenvalue; $a = X_{2}$ when $n_{b} - P + 1 > 3$ or $P = 1$: exactly one above, $a$ an eigenvalue), cross-checked against GA's congruence signature for $n_{b} \le 3$; it confirms $Wx(P - 1, 0, 1) = P(n_{b} - P + 1)x(P - 1, 0, 1)$. With these values (vii) gives: for $n_{b} - P + 1 \ge 3$, $X = P(n_{b} - P + 1)$ and every level is at least $E_{D}$, which is a level; for $n_{b} - P + 1 > 3$, $X_{2} - (P - 1)(n_{b} - P + 1) = 3$; for $n_{b} - P + 1 < 3$, $X - P(n_{b} - P + 1) = P + 2 - n_{b}$. Independently of (vii), the check computes the inertia of $H - E$ on $V_{P}$ for $n_{b} = 2, \ldots, 5$, every $P$, at $K_{\psi} = K_{\chi} = 0$ and three couplings with $G_{S} > G_{D} > 0$: no level below $E_{D}$ when $n_{b} - P + 1 \ge 3$, none below $E_{D} - (P + 2 - n_{b})G_{D}$ otherwise, that value a level at $P = n_{b}$, and exactly one level below $E_{\mathrm{th}} - 3G_{D}$ when $n_{b} - P + 1 > 3$ (below $E_{\mathrm{th}}$ at $P = 1$). The kinetic terms are constant on $V_{P}$, so these hold for all $K_{\psi}, K_{\chi}$. The general formulas are a conjecture; a route to them (argued, not used) is (vi): $C_{O}$ is the quadratic Casimir of the block rotations, and the pairs of a flavour module and a block-rotation module that occur in the Fock space are fixed by a duality between them, under which the band is the block-rotation invariant part, the next value of $C_{O}$ being $2(n_{b} - 2)$; with the least flavour Casimir $2P^{2}$ on the weight $(P - 1, P, 1)$, (vi) gives $W \le P(n_{b} + 2) - P^{2} - (n_{b} - 2) = (P - 1)(n_{b} - P + 1) + 3$ off the band, which is the computed $X_{2}$.

\emph{(ix).} Substituting $G = g/n_{b}$ in $\omega_{D}$ and in $G_{D}(n_{b} - P + 1)$ is exact algebra, and at $P = fn_{b}$ the difference from the limit is $(g_{S} - g_{D})/n_{b}$. By (iii), $[\phi, \pi] = 2(P - 2n)J$ on the ladder, so the rescaled fields have $[\Phi, \Pi] = (1 - 2n/P)J$. The last sentence is (viii) with $G_{D} = g_{D}/n_{b}$ under its conjecture.

\emph{(x).} By computation, exact. For the three couplings listed the check takes the vectors of the sector $(2, 2, 2)$ that are invariant under the permutations of the blocks $2, \ldots, n_{b}$ (block relabellings act on the Fock space with the sign of reordering the creators, and $H$ commutes with them); the orbit sums $s_{i}$ of the Fock basis are orthogonal, and the congruence diagonalisation of $\langle s_{i}, (H - E(P, 0))s_{j}\rangle$ (of dimension $43$) has a negative entry, so by the min-max characterisation $H$ has a level below $E(P, 0)$ in that sector. $E(P, 0)$ is the lowest level with $N_{\chi} = 0$ at $N = 2P$ by (v), confirmed at $n_{b} = 5$ by inertia on $(3, 3, 0)$. Hence the ground state at $N = 2P$ is not the singlet band there although $\omega_{D} > 0$, and the difference of sector minima in (viii) is then not an excitation gap of the ground state. For the doublet-condensed coupling the check computes the inertia of $H - E$ on $(0, P, P)$ and $(1, P - 1, P)$ at $E = E(0, P) + G_{D}n_{b}$ (one and $n_{b}^{2}$ negative entries) and at $E = E(0, P)$ on $(1, P - 1, P)$ (none negative, one zero: the band member); the sector $(1, P - 1, P)$ has $t_{3} = 0$ and holds every isospin from $0$ up, and only one isospin-$P/2$ level lies below the threshold (that of the stretched sector), so the others have isospin below $P/2$.

\emph{Reading (argued).} The result realises the doublet-singlet route named in \claimref{synthesis.rung-field-is-odd-in-the-tower-and-its-modes-carry-no-even-doublet} (step 5): its even doublets are products of a doublet mode and a singlet mode, as clause (3) there requires, and the doublet quantum is of that kind. Where the singlet band is the ground state at fixed $N$ (for $P \le 2$ and $n_{b} = 4, 5$ up to $\omega_{D} = 0$, found in floating point and not part of the proof; not at the couplings of (x)), the ground state is a sector vector of fixed $N_{1}$, $N_{2}$, $N_{3}$, so no doublet has an expectation there and isospin is unbroken: the doublet is a massive composite, not a condensate. At $G_{S} = G_{D}$, $K_{\chi} = K_{\psi}$ the element is invariant under the unitary rotations of the three modes, the $Z_{\alpha}$ are generators, and $\omega_{D} = 0$: the gap measures the explicit breaking of that symmetry, so the doublet is a pseudo-Goldstone doublet and is light only near $\omega_{D} = 0$, that is near $G_{D} = G_{S} + (K_{\chi} - K_{\psi})/(n_{b} - P + 1)$. Past it the doublet condenses on the band, its multiplet is degenerate, and no level of its own isospin lies below pair breaking; the return quanta are gapped doublets, charged under the unbroken $N_{\psi} - N_{\chi}$, so on the band no gapped doublet neutral under that charge accompanies a doublet condensate. Levels of lower isospin do lie below pair breaking, and the band need not hold the ground state (x), so these statements concern the band and the stretched sector. The quantum is even in the tower and the rung field is odd, so it is not the rung field (\claimref{synthesis.rung-field-is-odd-in-the-tower-and-its-modes-carry-no-even-doublet}); the modes carry no momentum or spin, so helicity zero is not shown here. Transport-absent: a finite CAR algebra of tower planes; the unit $J$ is the series'.
\end{proof}
