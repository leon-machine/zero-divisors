The tower's relations \cite[Prop.~4.1]{ButlerT3} (\claimref{tower.ladder-pairs-satisfy-car-vectors}) are the CAR of $k$ modes with $v_i$ the creation and $w_i$ the annihilation operator of mode $i$, each mode's algebra $\Mat_2$ with the two-dimensional Fock space $\{|0\rangle, v_i|0\rangle\}$, one qubit per rung \cite[Thm.~4.2]{ButlerT3} (\claimref{tower.fock-module-one-qubit-per-rung}); the singularity paper's per-mode capacity $\ln 2$ is the maximal entropy of that two-state system; the corank statement counts these pairs. The script realises two pairs in $\Cl(2,4)$ as $v_i = \tfrac12(e_a + e_b)$, $w_i = \tfrac12(e_a - e_b)$ on two orthogonal hyperbolic planes and checks all ten anticommutation relations exactly, the CAR of a Witt basis (\claimref{std-algebra.witt-basis-car}); that no third pair exists is the capacity theorem \cite[Thm.~4.3]{ButlerT3} (\claimref{tower.witt-index-quantum-capacity}).
