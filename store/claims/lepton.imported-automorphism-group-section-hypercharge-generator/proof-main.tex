The automorphisms of the hierarchy, the unitaries $A \otimes B$ of $\C^{2} \otimes \C^{3}$ with $(A, B) \in U(2) \times U(3)$, form $(U(2) \times U(3))/K$ with $K = \{(\lambda I_{2}, \lambda^{-1}I_{3})\}$, and each $K$-coset contains exactly one pair with $\det A \cdot \det B = 1$, so the group is $S(U(2) \times U(3)) \cong (SU(2) \times SU(3) \times U(1))/\mathbb{Z}_{6}$, the $U(1)$ factor acting as $(\lambda^{3}a, \lambda^{-2}b)$. The centre of its Lie algebra is spanned by $\mathsf{y} = (3iI_{2}, -2iI_{3})$; on an irreducible representation $\mathsf{y}$ acts as $iq$ with $q$ the abelian weight, and hypercharge is $Y = q/6$. Irreducible representations of $S(U(2) \times U(3))$ are determined by their $SU(2)$ content, $SU(3)$ content and weight. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thms.~4.1, 4.2, and the definition before Prop.~5.3]{ButlerT12} (\claimref{standard-model.quarks-leptons-two-level-fermion-content}, \claimref{standard-model.global-form-quotient-determined}, \claimref{standard-model.gauge-algebra-minimal-massive-composite}).
