Partial trace over the modes other than $k$ commutes with the pinchings on those modes, so $\tr_{\neq k}\,\EN\rho = E_k(\rho_k) = \rho_k$ when $\rho_k$ is diagonal. Then $C(\rho) - C(\EN\rho) = \bigl[\sum_k S(\rho_k) - S(\rho)\bigr] - \bigl[\sum_k S(\rho_k) - S(\EN\rho)\bigr] = S(\EN\rho) - S(\rho)$, which is the deficit by Theorem \claimref{singularity.deficit-theorem-cost-descent-exactly}.
