Record level. The fits were found by numerical minimisation; the script stores the four coupling sets of the table, recomputes every observable and $\Delta$ from them, prints them as RECORD lines, and checks, as floating-point controls, that each set reproduces the observables and that $\Delta$ is below $15$ in the first row, above $40$ in the second and third, and above $1000$ in the fourth. With real couplings, which cannot produce the CKM phase, no fit of the tuned rows was found with $f_{J} \le \sqrt{4\pi}$. The computation rests on \claimref{standard-model.imported-clebsch-structure-seesaw}, which supplies the Dirac mass matrices at $M_{I}$ and the type-I seesaw, and on \claimref{standard-model.what-angle-asks-neutrino-sector}, which supplies the triplet vev $v_{R}$ of the Majorana matrix $M_{R} = v_{R}f$. The fits also rest on \claimref{standard-model.premise-ew-equal-weight}, which fixes the neutrino's Dirac entry along the heavy family direction, $4w_{u}f_{33} = m_{t}$, held exact in $\Delta$, and on \claimref{standard-model.hyp-s-slot-statistics}, which names the species whose masses are fitted.
