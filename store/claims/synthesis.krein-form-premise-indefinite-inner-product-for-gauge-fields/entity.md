## formal
\emph{Premise (KF), the Krein form for gauge fields.} Units and signature of the store. Let the polarisation modes $a_{\mu} = \partial/\partial z_{\mu}$ of a gauge field act on the palatial polynomial representation (Fact \claimref{field-theory.imported-statistics-no-go-palatial-algebra}) with its positive form $\langle z^{n}, z^{m}\rangle = n!\,\delta_{nm}$, and let ghost modes act on tower floors (Fact \claimref{field-theory.imported-tower-limit}) with their positive trace form. The premise: \emph{the state space of the series' gauge fields, with their ghosts and their regulator fields, carries an indefinite (Krein) inner product $\langle x, y\rangle_{K} = \langle x, \eta y\rangle$, $\eta$ a self-adjoint involution of the positive form (the fundamental symmetry), such that}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(K1)] \emph{(Gupta-Bleuler form.)} on one polarisation quantum $[a(u), a(w)^{\dagger}] = -u\cdot w$ for polarisation vectors $u$, $w$: the one-particle form is the Minkowski metric of the polarisations, invariant under the Lorentz rotors (realised by $\eta = (-1)^{n_{0}}$ on the polynomials);
\item[(K2)] \emph{(Ghosts.)} the ghost and antighost modes of a covariant gauge are tower modes whose form is twisted by the involution exchanging the two modes of a floor, as in Theorem \claimref{synthesis.self-adjoint-brst-charge-needs-a-krein-form-and-the-tower-supplies-the-ghosts}(ii);
\item[(K3)] \emph{(Regulators.)} Pauli-Villars regulator fields of weight $c_{i} < 0$ are states of negative norm for the same form, so that regulated loops may carry weights $\sum_{i}c_{i} = \sum_{i}c_{i}M_{i}^{2} = 0$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-one-loop-integrals-and-pauli-villars-regulators});
\item[(K4)] \emph{(Positive physical space.)} the BRST charge $Q_{B}$ is nilpotent and self-adjoint for $\langle\,,\rangle_{K}$, the physical space is its cohomology $\ker Q_{B}/\operatorname{im}Q_{B}$ at ghost number zero, and the form is positive definite there: the unphysical modes (timelike and longitudinal polarisations, ghosts, antighosts) occur only in zero-norm quartets (Kugo and Ojima, Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-brst-kugo-ojima-strocchi-and-gribov}), and physical amplitudes are taken between physical states, from which the regulator fields decouple as their masses go to infinity.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{What it is for.} One premise covers two standard uses of an indefinite metric that the store's positive representations cannot carry. (a) Covariant gauge fixing: a nonzero self-adjoint nilpotent BRST charge does not exist on a positive space (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.self-adjoint-brst-charge-needs-a-krein-form-and-the-tower-supplies-the-ghosts}(i)), and a local covariant vector potential obeying Maxwell's equations has no positive-metric realisation with nonzero field strength (Strocchi, Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-brst-kugo-ojima-strocchi-and-gribov}); with (KF) the covariant photon propagator $-\gamma_{\mu}\cdot\gamma_{\nu}/k^{2}$ may be used on internal lines and the background Feynman gauge with Faddeev-Popov ghosts may be used in loops. (b) Covariant regularisation: Pauli-Villars fields of negative weight. Where the store's photon (Proposition \claimref{field-theory.elementary-photon-ga}(4)) gives the covariant form only at tree level on conserved currents, (KF) is what licenses it inside loops.

\emph{Results that use it} (each states it as a hypothesis at every level and has an assumes edge to it): one-loop electrodynamics in the spacetime algebra, in a covariant gauge with Pauli-Villars regulators (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.one-loop-qed-in-the-spacetime-algebra}); the one-loop running coefficients in the background Feynman gauge (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.one-loop-running-coefficients-from-spacetime-algebra-spin-traces}); the self-adjoint BRST charge with tower ghosts and its positive cohomology at a null momentum (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.self-adjoint-brst-charge-needs-a-krein-form-and-the-tower-supplies-the-ghosts}, whose clauses (ii) to (v) are computations in the form (K1), (K2) and are read as the series' gauge fixing under (KF)). By the decision of 2 November 2026 the following also carry an assumes edge to it, each stating it as the hypothesis of a clause: the two-loop vacuum polarisation in dimensional regularisation (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.two-loop-qed-in-the-spacetime-algebra-continued-in-dimension-gives-the-second-beta-coefficient}, its covariant internal photon); the free positivity of gluon and massive-vector physical states at every number of quanta (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.quartet-homotopy-makes-gluon-and-massive-vector-physical-states-positive-at-every-number-of-quanta}, clauses (K1), (K2)); the annihilation of an electron-positron pair into muons (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.electron-positron-to-muon-pairs-in-the-spacetime-algebra-lsz-tree-cross-section-and-one-loop-unitarity}, only its charge-renormalisation sentence); the regulator-free vacuum polarisation (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.one-loop-vacuum-polarisation-extends-without-a-regulator-and-ward-preserving-extensions-differ-only-by-the-renormalisation-constants}, only the self-energy and vertex parts of its clause (iii); its vacuum polarisation uses neither (KF) nor a regulator); the running of the rung-field quartics (\claimref{synthesis.rung-field-quartics-run-to-strong-coupling-within-a-few-tev-on-the-transmutation-branch}, its gauge terms); the Krein quartet in the induced Einstein-Hilbert coefficient (\claimref{synthesis.krein-quartet-drops-from-the-induced-einstein-hilbert-coefficient-only-under-a-regulator-that-treats-it-alike}); the gauge parts of the one-loop coefficients of the slot-module result (\claimref{synthesis.slot-module-makes-the-faces-separate-composites-and-leaves-the-generation-number-free}); and the interacting spin-statistics of causal perturbation theory (\claimref{synthesis.interacting-fields-of-causal-perturbation-theory-keep-the-spin-statistics-connection-at-even-ghost-number}). In the locality analysis of the attached free fields the Krein case $c = -1$ is the stated exception in which an anticommuting scalar, the ghost, is local (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.locality-of-the-attached-free-fields-fixes-their-statistics}(2)); that result does not rest on (KF).

\emph{What it does not give.} It does not derive the form from transport or from any stored structure: it is the standard Gupta-Bleuler form, transport-absent. The indefinite twistor form of signature $(2, 2)$ on $\CS$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.positive-structure-is-twistor-form-at-imaginary-time}) is a parallel at the one-particle level of the attachment, not this form. Positivity of the physical space is computed in the store only for the free charge at a null momentum up to three quanta; positivity for every number of quanta is the quartet mechanism (imported), and for the interacting non-abelian theory, with the field-level BRST charge and beyond perturbation theory where Gribov copies arise, it is not established. It supplies no native Lorentz-covariant regulator: the Pauli-Villars fields stay imported fields, and the series' own floors are a frame-dependent cut-off (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.floor-regulator-keeps-the-charge-and-the-central-term-below-the-cut-off}). It says nothing about confinement.

\emph{Note (2 November 2026).} (a) Clause (K3) is not needed for the one-loop vacuum polarisation: by the extension of distributions it extends across the coincident point with no regulator field and no Krein form, to the stored log coefficient (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.one-loop-vacuum-polarisation-extends-without-a-regulator-and-ward-preserving-extensions-differ-only-by-the-renormalisation-constants}(ii)). (K3) is not retired: the self-energy and vertex clauses of the stored one-loop electrodynamics still use Pauli-Villars fields, and their regulator-free extensions are imported, not computed. (b) The one-particle form $-u\cdot w$ of (K1) is restated inside the store: it is the trace form $\langle\bar a b\rangle_{0} = -a\cdot b$ of Clifford conjugation on vectors, minus the store's metric in any Clifford algebra, and Clifford conjugation is the adjoint of the twistor form (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.clifford-conjugation-is-the-twistor-adjoint-and-its-trace-form-is-the-one-particle-gupta-bleuler-form}). This restates and does not derive (K1): that the polarisation and ghost state spaces are quantised with the indefinite form remains this premise. (c) Given (K1) and (K2), the free physical space is positive at every number of quanta for gluons and, in the Feynman-'t Hooft gauge, for massive vectors with their Goldstone modes (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.quartet-homotopy-makes-gluon-and-massive-vector-physical-states-positive-at-every-number-of-quanta}); positivity for the interacting charge stays within (K4), and the covariant BRST construction is perturbative (Gribov copies and the vanishing lattice BRST partition function, Theorem \claimref{synthesis.non-abelian-gauge-fixing-has-gribov-copies-and-the-weak-rotor-lattice-with-doublets-has-a-perimeter-law}).

\emph{Refutation condition.} The premise is false for the series if any of the following is established: (a) for the series' gauge fields the physical space defined in (K4) contains a state of negative norm, or a null state not in $\operatorname{im}Q_{B}$, in a regime where the theory is meant to apply (failure of the quartet mechanism, for example for the interacting non-abelian charge), so that the physical scattering matrix is not unitary; (b) physical amplitudes computed under it depend, after the regulators are removed, on the gauge parameter or on the regulator weights; (c) a derivation from the series' premises that every state space of the series, unphysical modes included, carries a positive form, which (KF) contradicts.

## technical
The store quantises the photon in Coulomb gauge on a positive polynomial representation, and its tower floors carry positive forms. Two standard tools of covariant quantum field theory need an indefinite metric instead: a self-adjoint nilpotent BRST charge, which vanishes on any positive space, and Pauli-Villars regulator fields, which enter loops with negative weights. This premise adopts one indefinite (Krein) inner product for both. On a polarisation quantum it is the Gupta-Bleuler form, the Minkowski metric of the polarisations, which is Lorentz invariant where the positive form singles out a frame; ghost pairs are tower modes with the floor's form twisted by the involution exchanging the pair; regulator fields of negative weight are negative-norm states of the same form. The physical space is the BRST cohomology at ghost number zero and is required to be positive: the unphysical polarisations and the ghosts appear only in zero-norm quartets (Kugo and Ojima), and regulators decouple at infinite mass. Strocchi's theorem says a local covariant vector potential obeying Maxwell's equations cannot live on a positive-metric space with nonzero field strength, so a covariant formulation needs some such form.

Results that rest on it say so: one-loop electrodynamics in the spacetime algebra in a covariant gauge with Pauli-Villars regulators, the one-loop running coefficients in the background Feynman gauge, the self-adjoint BRST charge with tower ghosts, and (from 2 November 2026) the two-loop vacuum polarisation, the free quartet positivity at every number of quanta, the charge renormalisation of electron-positron annihilation, the self-energy and vertex parts of the regulator-free one-loop extensions, the running of the rung-field quartics, the Krein quartet of induced gravity, the gauge parts of the slot-module coefficients and the interacting spin-statistics of causal perturbation theory. Note of 2 November 2026: the Pauli-Villars clause is not needed for the one-loop vacuum polarisation, which extends across the coincident point with no regulator (the clause is kept for the stored self-energy and vertex); the one-particle Gupta-Bleuler form is restated in the store as the trace form of Clifford conjugation, minus the metric, which restates and does not derive the premise; and given the polarisation and ghost clauses the free physical space is positive at every number of quanta. It is not derived from the series (the form is the same in any formalism), it supplies no native covariant regulator, and positivity of the physical space beyond the free charge is imported, not computed. It would be refuted by a negative-norm or stray null state in the physical space of the series' gauge fields (a failure of unitarity), by gauge-parameter or regulator dependence of physical amplitudes, or by a derivation that every state space of the series is positive.

## lay
To describe light and the other force fields in a way that treats space and time alike, physicists use bookkeeping in which some unphysical states have negative or zero length, and remove them again before anything is measured. The same device is used to tame the infinities of quantum corrections, with auxiliary particles that count negatively. The series' quantum spaces all have positive lengths, so it cannot use this bookkeeping as it stands. This premise adopts it: the force fields of the series carry the standard indefinite measure built from the spacetime metric of their polarisations, and only states of positive length remain once the unphysical parts cancel in groups of four. Several results use it, among them the series' calculations of the first and second quantum corrections of electrodynamics, its calculation of how the force strengths change with energy, and its construction of the ghost bookkeeping. One of the two uses, the auxiliary negative particles, turns out not to be needed for the screening of charge by the vacuum, which can be computed without them. It is taken as given, not derived; it would be refuted if the physical states it leaves were found to include ones of negative length.

## one-liner
Premise (KF): the series' gauge fields, ghosts and Pauli-Villars regulators carry one indefinite (Krein) inner product, the Gupta-Bleuler form $[a(u), a(w)^{\dagger}] = -u\cdot w$ on polarisations, with a nilpotent self-adjoint BRST charge whose ghost-number-zero cohomology is positive (quartet mechanism); assumed by one-loop electrodynamics, the running coefficients, the BRST result, the two-loop vacuum polarisation, the quartet positivity and further loop results; not derived (its one-particle form is restated as the trace form of Clifford conjugation), no native covariant regulator, and the Pauli-Villars clause not needed for the one-loop vacuum polarisation (note of 2 November 2026); refuted by negative-norm physical states or gauge-dependent physical amplitudes.
