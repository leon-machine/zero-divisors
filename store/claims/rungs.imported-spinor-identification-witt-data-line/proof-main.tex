The complexified $\Cl(2,4)$ acts irreducibly on $\Sp = \Lambda^{\bullet}W$. There is a canonical isomorphism $V_{\C} \cong \Lambda^{2}\CS$ of $\mathrm{Spin}$-modules under which the quadratic form is $P \mapsto \varepsilon(P \wedge P)$ for a fixed volume functional $\varepsilon$ on $\Lambda^{4}\CS$, and a vector is null exactly when its bivector is decomposable. In the incidence chart $\CS = \Som \oplus \Spi$ of the conformal spinor space (twistor space), a conformal spinor being $Z = (\omega, \pi)$, the lines of $\PCS = \mathbb{P}(\CS)$ skew to the line at infinity $L_{\infty} = \mathbb{P}(\Som)$ are the points of the chart, and the Witt pair $n_{0}, n_{\infty}$ of $V_{\C}$ consists of the bivectors of the two summands, $\Som \wedge \Spi$ being their common orthogonal complement. The real form of the ambient spin group is the conformal $SU(2,2) \cong \mathrm{Spin}(2,4)$, and a line of $\PCS$ is a point of the real conformal compactification exactly when it is null for the Hermitian form $\Sigma$. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Rem.~3.2, Thms.~3.1, 3.4, 4.2, Props.~4.1, 4.5]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.reality-conjugation-twistor-form-factor}, \claimref{correspondence.fock-model-complexified-ascended-algebra}, \claimref{correspondence.skew-lines-splittings-hyperbolic-planes-one}, \claimref{correspondence.spinor-identification}, \claimref{correspondence.incidence-relation-chart}).
