The forty-five elements $v_{i}w_{j}$ ($i \ne j$), $v_{i}v_{j}$, $w_{i}w_{j}$ ($i < j$) and $v_{i}w_{i} - \tfrac12$ are linearly independent (the script computes their rank as $45$) and span the bivectors of the ten, which is $\so(10)$ (\claimref{std-algebra.bivector-lie-algebra}). Invariance under a basis of the Lie algebra is invariance under the connected group, so it suffices to check $B(Xe_{S}, e_{T}) + B(e_{S}, Xe_{T}) = 0$ for the forty-five generators on all $32 \times 32$ basis pairs, which the script does exactly, all $46080$ identities holding. Nondegeneracy and the complement structure are read off the definition, since $\varepsilon(S, S^{c}) = \pm 1$; the degrees $|S|$ and $5 - |S|$ have opposite parity, so the $16$ pairs only with the $\overline{16}$, and the bare pairing of two sixteens vanishes, which the script also confirms on all $16 \times 16$ pairs. This is Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-fock-model-twistor-space}'s pairing with five modes in place of three; the reversal sign $(-1)^{|S|(|S|-1)/2}$ is what makes the pair-creation and pair-annihilation bivectors act skewly.
