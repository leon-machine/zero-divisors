\emph{Step 1 (clause (i)).} At $\alpha = 0$, $\beta = (-v, 0)$ the formulas of \claimref{synthesis.rung-field-coupling-to-its-conjugate-is-the-second-family-matrix} give $M_{d} = -vY$ and $M_{u} = -v\tilde{Y}$, so $Y = -M_{d}/v$ and $\tilde{Y} = -M_{u}/v$; the vacuum value $|\beta| = v$ is $v^{2} = 2\mathsf{N}^{*}$ of \claimref{synthesis.linear-rung-field-has-a-neutral-normal-pair-in-the-scalon-sum}. The rung-$4$ component $\alpha_{4}$ of the $\alpha$ doublet, the normal pair (loc. cit. (2): the directions of $\alpha_{1}$ in its notation), enters $M_{d}$ as $\bar{\alpha}_{4}\tilde{Y}$ and $M_{u}$ as $\alpha_{4}Y$. With canonical real components $\alpha_{4} = \sqrt2\phi$, $\phi = (H + I_{4}A)/\sqrt2$ a canonical $I_{4}$-complex field; in the four-quark algebra the unit is $I_{4}$ as well, acting on the number-valued bilinears $J$, $J^{\dagger}$. For complex symmetric matrices the Takagi forms $M_{q} = U_{q}\hat{M}_{q}U_{q}^{\mathsf T}$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-takagi-factorisation}) diagonalise the mass terms with $d_{L} = U_{d}d'_{L}$, $d_{R} = \bar{U}_{d}d'_{R}$, and likewise for $u$; then $U_{d}^{\dagger}\tilde{Y}\bar{U}_{d} = -U_{d}^{\dagger}U_{u}\hat{M}_{u}U_{u}^{\mathsf T}\bar{U}_{d}/v = -V^{\dagger}\hat{M}_{u}V^{*}/v$ with $V = U_{u}^{\dagger}U_{d}$, the matrix of the charged current of the left-handed quarks, so $|V_{ij}|$ are the measured mixing moduli. The coupling is $-\sqrt2\bar\phi\,\bar{d}_{L}(\tilde{Y})d_{R} + \mathrm{h.c.}$ up to sign, which in the mass basis is $-\kappa\bar\phi\,\bar{d}_{L}\mathcal{A}d_{R}$ with $\mathcal{A} = V^{\dagger}\hat{M}_{u}V^{*}$ and $\kappa = \sqrt2/v$. Since $\hat{M}_{u}$ is diagonal and nondegenerate, Takagi leaves only signs free in $U_{u}$, $U_{d}$, so the phases of $V$ beyond the usual convention are physical; the bounds below hold for all of them. In the up sector the same steps give $V\hat{M}_{d}V^{\mathsf T}$. This is the bidoublet structure $M_{U} = \kappa h + \kappa'e^{-I_{4}\theta}\tilde{h}$, $M_{D} = \kappa'e^{I_{4}\theta}h + \kappa\tilde{h}$ at $\kappa' = 0$ with the roles read as in Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-left-right-bidoublet-two-yukawa-couplings}. The check builds exact unitary matrices by Cayley transforms of Gaussian-rational anti-Hermitian matrices, forms $M_{u}$, $M_{d}$, reads $Y$, $\tilde{Y}$ off the stored formulas at the vacuum and verifies both coupling matrices exactly, and that the down one is symmetric with nonzero off-diagonal entries.

\emph{Step 2 (clause (ii)).} $\mathcal{A}_{jk} = \sum_{i}\bar{V}_{ij}m_{u_{i}}\bar{V}_{ik}$, so $|\mathcal{A}_{jk}| \ge m_{i_{0}}|V_{i_{0}j}||V_{i_{0}k}| - \sum_{i \ne i_{0}}m_{i}|V_{ij}||V_{ik}|$ for any $i_{0}$, whatever the phases. The quark mixing ranges must not come from $B$ mixing, since a tree-level contribution to $\Delta M_{s}$, $\Delta M_{d}$ is what is being tested: the direct values of $|V_{ts}|$, $|V_{td}|$ quoted by \cite{PDG2025} are extracted from the mass differences. The check therefore uses $|V_{ts}| \in [0.0376, 0.0425]$ and $|V_{td}| \in [0.0078, 0.0092]$, about two standard deviations around the values from the tree-level determinations ($|V_{cb}| = 41.1(1.2) \times 10^{-3}$ from semileptonic decays, $|V_{ub}|$, the angle $\gamma$) and three-family unitarity, and widened PDG ranges for the rest: $|V_{tb}| \ge 0.99$, $|V_{cb}| \le 0.0425$, $|V_{cs}| \in [0.96, 0.99]$, $|V_{cd}| \in [0.215, 0.227]$, $|V_{us}| \le 0.227$, $|V_{ub}| \le 0.0045$; running masses at $100$ to $600$ GeV, $m_{t} \in [140, 172]$, $m_{c} \in [0.50, 0.70]$, $m_{u} \le 0.002$ GeV (from memory). \emph{Step 3 (clause (iii), the effective operators).} With $L \supset -(\bar\phi J + \phi J^{\dagger})$, $J = \kappa\bar{d}_{L}\mathcal{A}d_{R}$, write $\phi = (H + I_{4}A)/\sqrt2$: the sources of the real fields are $K_{H} = (J + J^{\dagger})/\sqrt2$ and $K_{A} = I_{4}(J^{\dagger} - J)/\sqrt2$, and integrating out at zero momentum gives $K_{H}^{2}/(2m_{H}^{2}) + K_{A}^{2}/(2m_{A}^{2}) = J^{\dagger}J\,\tfrac{a + b}{2} + (J^{2} + J^{\dagger 2})\tfrac{a - b}{4}$ (checked as an identity of commuting values). The $\Delta F = 2$ part for the pair $(j, k)$: $J^{\dagger}J \supset \kappa^{2}\mathcal{A}_{jk}\bar{\mathcal{A}}_{kj}(\bar{d}_{j}P_{R}d_{k})(\bar{d}_{j}P_{L}d_{k})$, which is $Q_{4}$ of \cite[Eq.~(3)]{Gabbiani1996}, and $J^{2}$, $J^{\dagger 2}$ give $Q_{2}$ and $\tilde{Q}_{2}$ with $\kappa^{2}\mathcal{A}_{jk}^{2}$, $\kappa^{2}\bar{\mathcal{A}}_{kj}^{2}$. With $\mathcal{A}$ symmetric $|\mathcal{A}_{jk}\bar{\mathcal{A}}_{kj}| = |\mathcal{A}_{jk}|^{2}$. The matrix elements of \cite[Eq.~(8)]{Gabbiani1996}, which are $\langle Q\rangle/(2m_{M})$ in the relativistic normalisation and so give $M_{12}$ directly, are $\langle Q_{4}\rangle = (\tfrac{1}{24} + \tfrac14R)m_{M}f_{M}^{2}$ and $\langle Q_{2}\rangle = \langle\tilde{Q}_{2}\rangle = -\tfrac{5}{24}Rm_{M}f_{M}^{2}$, times bag parameters. Hence $|M_{12}^{\phi}| \ge \kappa^{2}|\mathcal{A}_{jk}|^{2}F(a, b)$, $F = \tfrac{a + b}{2}E_{4} - \tfrac{|a - b|}{2}E_{2}$. For $a \ge b$, $F = \tfrac{a}{2}(E_{4} - E_{2}) + \tfrac{b}{2}(E_{4} + E_{2})$ (and symmetrically), which is increasing in $a$ and in $b$ when $E_{4} > E_{2}$. With $a, b \ge a_{0} = 542^{-2}$ GeV$^{-2}$ its minimum is therefore $F(a_{0}, a_{0}) = a_{0}E_{4}$: equal masses at the upper bound are the least constraining case, and the bound $\kappa^{2}|\mathcal{A}_{jk}|^{2}E_{4}/542^{2}$ holds for every splitting. The check verifies the closed form of $F$ and the minimum at sampled $(a, b)$. The ratio $E_{4}/E_{2} = (6R + 1)B_{4}/(5RB_{2})$ exceeds one for the $B$ mesons ($R \approx 1.5$) with $B_{4} \ge 0.85$, $B_{2} \le 0.95$, and is below one for the kaon ($R \approx 25$), where a large split could cancel.
