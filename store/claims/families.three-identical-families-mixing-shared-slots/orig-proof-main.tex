A face is a two-element subset $F \subset \{1, 2, 3\}$ of twistor slots. A permutation $\sigma \in S_{3}$ of the slots acts unitarily on the three-twistor system by relabelling, and the construction of Fact \claimref{families.imported-one-derived-generation-hypothesis-s} is defined on a two-twistor composite in terms of its own two slots and their pairs, with no reference to which slots of the ambient system they are; so $\sigma$ carries the face-$F$ construction, term by term, onto the face-$\sigma(F)$ construction, which is the isomorphism, and since $S_{3}$ acts transitively on two-element subsets the three copies are isomorphic. For the overlap: two distinct two-element subsets of a three-element set intersect in exactly one element, an exact count. The observation-level clause: a transition between the content of faces $F$ and $F'$ is mediated by the operators of the shared slot, so every entry of the mixing matrix is populated (each pair of faces shares a slot), while a double transition (both slots changing) requires leaving the shared slot; no mixing matrix is defined in this paper, and the clause is a reading. The set combinatorics ($\binom{3}{2} = 3$, pairwise intersections of size one, transitivity) are verified by the script.
