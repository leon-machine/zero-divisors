\emph{(i)} With $X_{k} = \sum_{ij}k_{ij}a_{i}^{*}a_{j}$ and the CAR of the null pairs (Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-algebra-boundary-modes-car-algebra}), $[a_{i}^{*}a_{j}, a_{l}^{*}a_{m}] = \delta_{jl}a_{i}^{*}a_{m} - \delta_{im}a_{l}^{*}a_{j}$, so $[X_{k}, X_{k'}] = X_{[k, k']}$. Since $[k, k^{n}] = 0$, $[X_{k}, Q_{n}] = 0$ for every $n$. If $k = \sum_{\alpha}\epsilon_{\alpha}P_{\alpha}$ is its spectral decomposition, the $Q_{n} = \sum_{\alpha}\epsilon_{\alpha}^{n}X_{P_{\alpha}}$ span the eigenmode occupations $X_{P_{\alpha}}$ (Vandermonde), which are therefore conserved. For $k = \operatorname{diag}(\omega_{j})$ the $X_{P_{\alpha}}$ are sums of the $N_{j} = a_{j}^{*}a_{j}$ with equal frequency, and $[X_{k}, N_{j}] = 0$ directly. The check realises the CAR in $\Cl(3,3)$ by null pairs, $a_{j} = \tfrac12(e_{j} + f_{j})$, $a_{j}^{*} = \tfrac12(e_{j} - f_{j})$, $e_{j}^{2} = 1$, $f_{j}^{2} = -1$, and verifies the relations, $[X_{k}, Q_{n}] = 0$ for $n \le 3$ with a generic rational $k$, $[X_{k}, N_{1}] \neq 0$ (site occupations move), $[\sum_{j}\omega_{j}N_{j}, N_{l}] = 0$, all exactly. The products of the $\sigma_{k} = 2N_{k} - 1$ are polynomials in commuting number operators, so they commute with every $N_{j}$, alone or added to $\sum_{j}\omega_{j}N_{j}$, as Proposition \claimref{singularity.number-diagonal-dynamics-models-scrambling-derived} records under (L$_E$); the check verifies $[\sum_{j}\omega_{j}N_{j} + \sigma_{j}\sigma_{k}, N_{l}] = 0$ for all $j < k$ and $l$, and $[(a_{1}^{*}a_{2} + a_{2}^{*}a_{1})N_{3}, N_{1}] = (a_{2}^{*}a_{1} - a_{1}^{*}a_{2})N_{3} \neq 0$.

\emph{(ii)} A $*$-automorphism $\alpha$ with $\alpha(X) = X$ for a conserved $X$ gives $\omega(\alpha(X)) = \omega(X)$, so $\omega\circ\tau_{t}(N_{j}) = \omega(N_{j})$ for the boost dynamics of Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.boundary-mode-algebra-windows-boost-dynamics}. If $\omega\circ\tau_{t}$ converged to $\omega_{I}$ on a window in a sense implying convergence of the expectation of $N_{j}$, the constant sequence $\omega(N_{j})$ would converge to $\omega_{I}(N_{j}) = n_{j}^{I}$ (Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state}(1)), so $\omega(N_{j}) = n_{j}^{I}$. For quasi-free states, a quadratic time evolution is a Bogoliubov unitary $U$ on the one-particle space (Proposition \claimref{synthesis.phase-flow-is-time-rotor-flow-kms-continuation}(iii)), and $C_{ij} = \omega(a_{j}^{*}a_{i})$ goes to $UCU^{*}$; $\operatorname{tr}((UCU^{*})^{m}) = \operatorname{tr}(C^{m})$ and $\operatorname{tr}(k^{n}UCU^{*}) = \operatorname{tr}(k^{n}C)$ when $U$ commutes with $k$. For $U = e^{-I_4tk}$ with $k$ diagonal (the unit of the one-particle space is $I_4$), $(UCU^{*})_{ij} = e^{-I_4(\omega_{i} - \omega_{j})t}C_{ij}$ and the pairing $\omega(a_{i}a_{j})$ acquires $e^{-I_4(\omega_{i} + \omega_{j})t}$. The check replaces $e^{-I_4tk}$ by its Cayley form $(1 - I_4sk)(1 + I_4sk)^{-1}$, which is exactly unitary over $\mathbb{Q}(I_4) = \mathrm{span}_{\mathbb{Q}}\{1, I_4\}$ and commutes with $k$, and verifies on a three-mode state with occupations $(\tfrac12, 0, \tfrac13)$ and nonzero coherences that the diagonal is unchanged over repeated steps and never equals the Fermi-Dirac values $(\tfrac14, \tfrac18, \tfrac1{16})$ (at $e^{\beta\omega_{j}} = 3, 7, 15$), that coherence moduli are kept while their phases move, and that for a generic $k$ the invariants $\operatorname{tr}(k^{n}C)$ and $\operatorname{tr}(C^{m})$ are kept while the site occupations change. That the coherences and pairings average to zero in an infinite window with continuous frequency spectrum (Riemann-Lebesgue), leaving the gauge-invariant state with the conserved occupations, is the standard dephasing of free fermions towards the generalized Gibbs ensemble (Rigol, Dunjko, Yurovsky and Olshanii, 2007; for quasi-free fermions Gluza, Eisert and Farrelly, 2019; both cited from memory); only the finite-window statements are proved here.

\emph{(iii)} In the basis $\ket{00}, \ket{01}, \ket{10}, \ket{11}$ (mode, partner) the pure state $c\ket{00} + s\ket{11}$, $c^{2} + s^{2} = 1$, has reduced density matrix $\operatorname{diag}(c^{2}, s^{2})$ on the mode: no coherence, occupation $s^{2}$, ratio $s^{2}/c^{2}$, which is the Gibbs state of $\omega N$ at $e^{-\beta\omega} = s^{2}/c^{2}$ and has $s^{2} = (1 + e^{\beta\omega})^{-1}$. The check verifies this at three rational points.

\emph{(iv)} Hypothesis (T) is a hypothesis, not a theorem of the store: Lemma \claimref{scrambling.clock-inventory-boundary-modes}(i) shows that the intrinsic (grade-preserving inner) flows are bivector-generated, hence quadratic, and Proposition \claimref{synthesis.phase-flow-is-time-rotor-flow-kms-continuation}(iii) shows that a quadratic $H = H^{*}$ generates a $*$-automorphic Bogoliubov flow, but neither asserts that a window's time evolution is quadratic; the number-diagonal alternative is the class of generators (L$_E$) admits (Proposition \claimref{singularity.number-diagonal-dynamics-models-scrambling-derived}), which includes quartic cross-point products. For gauge-invariant quadratic $H$, (i) and (ii) apply as stated; with pairing terms the same argument runs in the doubled (Nambu) one-particle space, where the flow is a Bogoliubov unitary commuting with the doubled one-particle matrix and conserves the quasi-particle occupations of its eigenmodes. For number-diagonal generators every $N_{j}$ is conserved, so (ii) applies with the $N_{j}$ themselves. In each case the descent state relaxes to $\omega_{I}$ only if the conserved occupations already have their I-state values, which (iii) shows a pure pair state can supply by restriction. A generator that changes some conserved occupation must contain terms off-diagonal in the joint number basis of the conserved modes, that is, terms moving occupation between modes, which is what (L$_E$) excludes. The statement is a no-go for relaxation under (T), not a determination of the descent state.
