Read in Gauge Theory Gravity, the static metric below is the derived metric of the Schwarzschild field in the static gauge and the curvature operator is $\mathcal{R}(B)$ on physical bivectors (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}, clause (iv)). For the static spherically symmetric vacuum metric $f\,dt_s^2 - f^{-1}dr^2 - r^2 d\Omega^2$ with $f = 1 - 2M/r$, the curvature operator on bivectors is diagonal on the coordinate planes of the orthonormal frame, with eigenvalue of one magnitude, $2M/r^3$, on the radial boost plane $\sigma_r$ and its Hodge dual $I\sigma_r$, and eigenvalue of half that magnitude and opposite sign, $-M/r^3$, on the four mixed planes. \emph{Provenance:} the radial-plane eigenvalue from the gauge field alone in \cite[Prop.~2.4(1)]{ButlerT5} (\claimref{singularity.solution-where-divergence-lies}); the mixed- and angular-plane eigenvalues by the Cartan structure equations in the static chart in \cite[App.~A]{ButlerT5}, the two computations agreeing on the invertible region by Fact \claimref{interior.imported-equivalence-einstein-equations}; the same computation in the GTG literature is \cite[Ch.~14]{DoranLasenby2003}.
