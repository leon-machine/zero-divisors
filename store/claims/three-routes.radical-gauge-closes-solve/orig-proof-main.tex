The musical map $\alpha \mapsto G(\alpha, \cdot)$ has kernel $\R k$ and image contained in the vectors killed by pairing with $k$, i.e.\ in $\ker k$ regarded inside the bidual; its rank is three, the dimension of $\ker k$, so the image is exactly $\ker k$: solvability, with ambiguity the kernel. Under $\tilde v \mapsto \tilde v + tk$ the quantity $G(\tilde v, \tilde v)$ is unchanged ($k$ radical) and $\tilde v(u)$ shifts by $t\,k(u) = t$, so the displayed normalisation is met at exactly one $t$.
