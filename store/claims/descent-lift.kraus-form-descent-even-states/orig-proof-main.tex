The mode-wise parity average is $\tfrac12(\rho + \G_{k}\rho\G_{k})$ and the averages commute, so their composite is the stated sum (Fact \claimref{descent-lift.imported-descent-modes-univalence-supplied-class}); it kills every matrix unit $\ket{A}\bra{B}$ with $A \neq B$, since some $\G_{k}$ has opposite signs on $A$ and $B$, and fixes the diagonal ones: the pinching. The Kraus rank is the Choi rank, which the script computes as $4$ on the full two-mode space and $2$ on its even sector, and $4$ on the even sector of three modes. On the even sector $\G_{\nu} = \prod_{k < \nu}\G_{k}$, so the $2^{\nu}$ monomials collapse to $2^{\nu-1}$ distinct operators, each appearing twice. On one mode the even sector is the vacuum line. On the pair, $\G_{0}$ and $\G_{1}$ agree on the even sector ($+1$ on $\ket{00}$, $-1$ on $\ket{11}$), and the script checks the descent of $(\tfrac45)\ket{00} + (\tfrac35)\ket{11}$ is $\operatorname{diag}(\tfrac{16}{25}, \tfrac{9}{25})$ with the coherence $\tfrac{12}{25}$ removed.
