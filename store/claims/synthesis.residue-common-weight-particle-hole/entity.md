## formal
Let the counted boundary modes be $n$ completed pairs $\psi_{1}, \ldots, \psi_{n}$ whose one-particle space $\C^{n}$ carries the principal $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ of Theorem \claimref{scrambling.principality-indifference-sphere}, with $E_{ij} = \psi_{i}^{\dagger}\psi_{j}$ (Proposition \claimref{scrambling.boundary-mode-space-matrix}), $N_{\mathrm{tot}} = \sum_{i}E_{ii}$, $\hat{X} := \sum_{ij}X_{ij}E_{ij}$ for $X \in \Mat_{n}(\C)$, and $\mathcal{A}_{0}$ the algebra generated by the $E_{ij}$. For $0 < q < 1$ let $\rho_{q}$ be the gauge-invariant quasi-free state with symbol $q\,\id$ (Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-quasi-free-states-car-algebra}). Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] (one weight, one occupation) a number-preserving quasi-free generator $\hat{h}$ commutes with every $\hat{J}_{a}$ if and only if $h = \omega\,\id$, so $\hat{h} = \omega N_{\mathrm{tot}}$; and a gauge-invariant quasi-free state invariant under the rotations has symbol $q\,\id$, so it is $\rho_{q}$. With rotation invariance imposed, every counted mode has the same Killing weight $\omega$ and the same occupation, and under the hypotheses of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy} the residue is $\kappa_{c} = 1/(4s(x))$ for the single value $x = 2\pi\omega/\kappa$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.boundary-mode-thermal-entropy}). Without quasi-freeness this fails: the commutant of the rotations on the Fock space is larger than $\operatorname{span}\{\id, N_{\mathrm{tot}}\}$;
\item[(ii)] (the weight is invisible on the gauge-invariant algebra) $N_{\mathrm{tot}}$ is central in $\mathcal{A}_{0}$, so the flow of $\omega N_{\mathrm{tot}}$ restricts to the identity on $\mathcal{A}_{0}$ for every $\omega$, and for every $q$ the restriction of $\rho_{q}$ to $\mathcal{A}_{0}$ is tracial, hence KMS at every $\beta$ for the restricted flow, while $\rho_{q}(N_{\mathrm{tot}}) = nq$ separates them. Neither the boost's action on the gauge-invariant bilinears nor any KMS condition posed on $\mathcal{A}_{0}$ determines $q$; in particular the inference from the trivial restriction to zero weight, and so to $\ln 2$ per mode, is invalid;
\item[(iii)] (particle-hole) let $\varepsilon$ be the $\mathfrak{su}(2)$-invariant bilinear form of the principal representation, symmetric for integer $j$ and symplectic for half-integer $j$, with matrix $\varepsilon_{m,m'} = (-1)^{j - m}\delta_{m,-m'}$ in the standard basis of spin $j = (n-1)/2$, a signed permutation and hence orthogonal. The Bogoliubov automorphism $C(\psi_{i}) = \sum_{k}\varepsilon_{ik}\psi_{k}^{\dagger}$ fixes every $\hat{J}_{a}$, maps $N_{\mathrm{tot}}$ to $n - N_{\mathrm{tot}}$, and satisfies $\rho_{q}\circ C = \rho_{1-q}$; $C^{2}(\psi_{i}) = \sum_{l}(\varepsilon^{2})_{il}\psi_{l}$, so $C$ is an involution for integer $j$ and squares to the parity automorphism $\psi \mapsto -\psi$ for half-integer $j$. So $\rho_{q}$ is $C$-invariant exactly when $q = \tfrac12$, that is exactly at zero common weight; under the hypotheses of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy} and (i), the registered residue $1/(4\ln 2)$ holds if and only if the counted state is invariant under $C$. The direction from $C$-invariance to the registered value needs no quasi-freeness: a rotation-invariant gauge-invariant state has two-point matrix $q\,\id$, and $C$-invariance then forces $q = 1 - q = \tfrac12$, maximally mixed marginals;
\item[(iv)] ((Q) on a principal window) on $n = 2j + 1$ modes the weights $\omega_{k} = \kappa k$, $k = -j, \ldots, j$, of Premise \claimref{singularity.q-joint-quasi-freeness-s-wave} are $n$ distinct values, excluded by (i); they are the spectrum of $\kappa h/2$ for the Cartan element $h$ of the principal triple, a rotation generator, which does not commute with the rotations.
\end{enumerate}
If, further, the counted modes obey Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.boundary-mode-algebra-windows-boost-dynamics}, whose frequencies are strictly positive, (i) and Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy}(ii) give $\kappa_{c} > 1/(4\ln 2)$ strictly at the hole, as clause (iv) of that theorem gives at the cosmological horizon. The hypotheses here (the counted modes are the principal mode space; the counted state and the Killing generator restricted to it are rotation invariant and quasi-free) are stated, not derived; that the counted modes are the fuzzy-sphere modes is the provenance question.

## technical
The calibration residue was re-expressed as the reciprocal of four times the counted modes' mean entropy, with the registered value exactly when every counted mode has zero Killing weight. This result asks what happens if the counted modes are the matrix modes on which the series built its fuzzy sphere, carrying the principal $\mathfrak{su}(2)$, and both the Killing generator and the state respect rotations. Then, by the multiplicity-free decomposition of the matrix algebra, the generator, if quasi-free, is one common weight times the total number, and the state, if quasi-free, has one common occupation. So the residue reduces to one number.

A tempting next step fails. The total number commutes with every gauge-invariant bilinear, so a common weight acts trivially on them; but this is true for every weight, and every common occupation then restricts to a tracial state, which is KMS for the trivial flow at any temperature. The gauge-invariant algebra cannot see the weight. What does fix the registered value is a symmetry of the state: the principal representation is self-dual (through a symmetric form for integer spin, a symplectic one for half-integer spin), so there is a particle-hole map that commutes with the rotations and exchanges occupation $q$ with $1 - q$. The registered residue is exactly the case in which the counted state is particle-hole invariant. The s-wave weights of (Q), placed on a principal window, are a rotation generator's spectrum and violate rotation invariance.

## lay
The series needs to know how much energy each counted boundary mode carries, because a mode holds a full bit of information only when it carries none. Suppose the counted modes form the single round "fuzzy sphere" the series already uses, that nothing singles out a direction, and that the state and the energy assignment are of the simplest kind, built from independent modes (quasi-free). Then every mode must carry the same energy, which reduces the open question to one number. One tempting argument says that number must be zero because ordinary observables cannot see it; that argument fails, since those observables cannot see it whatever its value. What does pin the value at zero is a symmetry: if the state treats filled and empty modes alike, the energy is zero and the registered value follows. The series' existing energy assignment breaks the round symmetry.

## one-liner
On a principal mode space a rotation-invariant quasi-free counted sector has one common weight, invisible on the gauge-invariant bilinears; the registered residue $1/(4\ln 2)$ holds exactly when its state is invariant under the rotation-equivariant particle-hole map.
