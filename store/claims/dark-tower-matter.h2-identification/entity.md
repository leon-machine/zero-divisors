## formal
The gauge algebra of a tower's matter is the automorphism algebra of its minimal massive hierarchy, constituent slots tensor pair slots, as T12's Theorem 4.1 identifies it in dimension four, at observation level \cite[Thm.~4.1, Prop.~4.3]{ButlerT12}.

## technical
H2 identifies the gauge algebra of a tower's matter with the automorphism algebra of its minimal massive hierarchy, the constituent slots tensored with the pair slots. This is how the Standard-Model paper identifies the gauge algebra in dimension four, at observation level; here it is taken as a premise in every tower.

## lay
This is an assumption, not a result: the forces acting on a tower's matter are taken to be the symmetries of its smallest composite that can have mass. Our own tower's forces arise this way in the Standard-Model paper, at the level of matching observation, and the other towers are assumed to follow the same pattern.

## one-liner
Premise H2: a tower's gauge algebra is the automorphism algebra of its minimal massive hierarchy, constituent slots tensor pair slots, as in dimension four at observation level.
