\emph{Setting.} By Proposition \claimref{synthesis.pair-singlet-mass-form-is-floor-plus-face-density}(ii), for every state supported on $\mathcal{S}$, pure or mixed, $H = f\mathbb{1} + G$ with $f = 1 + \tfrac12\langle\hat{D}\rangle$ ($\hat{N} = 2\hat{D}$ on $\mathcal{S}$), $G \ge 0$, and $\mathrm{tr}\,H = 3 + 3\langle\hat{D}\rangle + \langle\hat{D}^{2}\rangle$. Expectations are taken in the normalised state; the pair-number distribution is $p_{D} \ge 0$, $\sum_{D}p_{D} = 1$.

\emph{(i)} Since $G \ge 0$, every eigenvalue of $H$ is at least $f > 0$; in particular $\lambda_{1} \ge f$. Then $(\lambda_{2} + \lambda_{3})/\lambda_{1} = \mathrm{tr}\,H/\lambda_{1} - 1 \le \mathrm{tr}\,H/f - 1 = (3 + 3\langle\hat{D}\rangle + \langle\hat{D}^{2}\rangle)/(1 + \tfrac12\langle\hat{D}\rangle) - 1 = (2 + \tfrac52\langle\hat{D}\rangle + \langle\hat{D}^{2}\rangle)/(1 + \tfrac12\langle\hat{D}\rangle)$, with equality exactly when $\lambda_{1} = f$. In every source-built state the forced eigenvector sits at the floor (loc. cit. (iii)), so there the bound is the stored identity for the heavier pair. The check confirms, on twelve random states with Gaussian-rational coefficients supported on $D \le 3$, the trace formula and $G \ge 0$ (all principal minors of the Hermitian $G$ nonnegative), computing $G$ from the monomial coefficients by the formula of loc. cit. (ii) in the auxiliary-boson form.

\emph{(ii)} Write $a(D) = 2 + \tfrac52 D + D^{2}$ and $b(D) = 1 + \tfrac12 D > 0$, so the bound of (i) is $\sum_{D}p_{D}a(D)/\sum_{D}p_{D}b(D)$. A ratio of two positive linear functionals of a probability vector is at most the largest of the ratios $a(D)/b(D)$ over the support (if $a(D) \le \rho\, b(D)$ for every $D$ in the support, summing with weights $p_{D}$ gives the same inequality for the averages). Now $a(D)/b(D) = 2D + 1 + 2/(D + 2)$, by direct division: $(D^{2} + \tfrac52 D + 2) = (\tfrac12 D + 1)(2D + 1) + 1$, and $1/(1 + \tfrac12 D) = 2/(D + 2)$. Its increment from $D$ to $D + 1$ is $2 - 2/((D + 2)(D + 3)) > 0$, so on $0 \le D \le K$ the largest value is at $D = K$. For the word $\hat\Omega_{1}^{\dagger K}\ket{0}$, $G$ is supported on the first direction ($\hat\Omega_{2}$ and $\hat\Omega_{3}$ annihilate it), so the floor is attained and the support is $\{K\}$: equality. The check verifies the division identity and the monotonicity for $D = 0, \dots, 60$, the inequality on the random states of (i), and equality at $K = 3$ ($\tfrac{37}{5}$).

\emph{(iii)} Under the mass-squared reading (the store's primary reading, as in loc. cit. (iv)) the eigenvalues are proportional to $m_{e}^{2}, m_{\mu}^{2}, m_{\tau}^{2}$ in increasing order, so $(\lambda_{2} + \lambda_{3})/\lambda_{1} = (m_{\mu}^{2} + m_{\tau}^{2})/m_{e}^{2}$. With the stored masses and three stored standard deviations for the muon and the tau, and $10^{-9}$ MeV for the electron (covering its stored value and uncertainty), the check certifies in exact rationals that this ratio lies in $(12131147, 12138498)$, that $2K + 1 + 2/(K + 2)$ is below the lower end at $K = 6065573$ and not below it at $K = 6065574$. These are pole masses, which the store's provisional dictionary assigns to the face mass form. By (ii) and monotonicity, no state supported on $D \le 6065573$ reaches the measured ratio, so the frozen vacuum must give positive weight to some $D \ge 6065574$; by (i) its moments satisfy the stated inequality.

\emph{Scope.} The statement is about states on $\mathcal{S}$, which holds the Gaussian class and the source-built completions; it says nothing about states of the six face modes outside $\mathcal{S}$, and nothing in the $\sqrt{m}$ (circulant) reading, where the masses are not eigenvalues of $H$. No number is fitted: the inputs are the stored identity and the stored masses. It is transport-absent.

\emph{Mass definition (computed in floating point, not part of the check).} At common-scale MS-bar masses, from the pole masses by the one-loop QED relation (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-one-loop-qed-pole-mass-relation}) with $\alpha$ between $1/138$ and $\alpha(M_{Z})$, $(m_{\mu}^{2} + m_{\tau}^{2})/m_{e}^{2}$ is about $1.283 \times 10^{7}$ to $1.288 \times 10^{7}$ and the threshold about $6.41$ to $6.44$ million; the mass-definition dependence is treated in \claimref{synthesis.koide-channel-phase-target-depends-on-mass-definition}. If the selection rule fixes the mass form above the lepton thresholds, the running value is the relevant one; the conclusion that millions of pairs are needed does not depend on the choice.
