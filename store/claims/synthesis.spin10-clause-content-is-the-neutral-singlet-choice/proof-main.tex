\emph{Setting.} Theorem \claimref{standard-model.hyp-s-slot-statistics}(ii) and (v) give the module $V = \Lambda^{\bullet}\C^{5}$ with the two slot modes and three pair modes forming one system of five fermionic modes, the gauge group acting through its bilinears, and the Majorana combinations generating $\Cl(10)$ with $\Spin(10)$ the group of its bivectors (Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-fock-representation-clifford-algebra-bogoliubov}). Theorem \claimref{synthesis.cpt-closure-forces-slot-module-and-chirality}(4) gives the anomaly-free CPT halvings: the chirality is $\pm(-1)^{n + k}$ on every charged component, with a free line in the neutral block. The twelve components $F_{n} \otimes \Lambda^{k}W$ are irreducible and pairwise inequivalent except for $(n, k) = (0, 0)$ and $(2, 3)$, both $(1,1)_{0}$; conjugation pairs $(n, k)$ with $(2 - n, 3 - k)$. The check builds everything on the thirty-two-dimensional Fock space with exact sparse matrices (modes $0, 1$ slots, $2, 3, 4$ pairs), and computes each commutant as the solution space of $[X, g] = 0$ over all generators $g$ of the set, with $X$ supported on pairs of states of equal weight under the set's diagonal generators (which is where any solution lives), by exact elimination.

\emph{(i).} By Schur's lemma the commutant of a completely reducible action is $\bigoplus M_{m_{\rho}}(\C)$ over the isotypes, of dimension $\sum m_{\rho}^{2}$. For the gauge algebra the multiplicities are one on ten components and two on $(1,1)_{0}$, so the dimension is $10 + 4 = 14$; the check finds $14$, and that the only states of zero weight under all four diagonal generators are the empty and the full state (so the charged weight spaces are lines, a control). Under $\uu(5)$ the degrees $\Lambda^{d}\C^{5}$ are irreducible and inequivalent, dimension $6$. Under $\so(10)$ the two parities are the two inequivalent half-spin representations, dimension $2$. The Majoranas generate $\Cl(10)_{\C} = M_{32}(\C)$, dimension $1$. The product $\prod_{k}(a^{\dagger}_{k} + a_{k})(a_{k} - a^{\dagger}_{k}) = \prod_{k}(2N_{k} - 1) = \prod_{k}(-(-1)^{N_{k}}) = -(-1)^{N}$ (the pair factors are even and commute), as the check verifies; so the volume element is the parity up to sign, the stored statement.

\emph{(ii).} A gauge-commuting involution is a sign on each multiplicity-one component and an involution of the neutral block; a CPT halving needs $V_{-} \cong \bar{V}_{+}$, so the signs on conjugate charged components are opposite ($32$ patterns on the five pairs) and the block, self-conjugate, contributes one line to each half, any pair of complementary lines. The check enumerates the $32$ patterns. Commuting with $\uu(5)$ means commuting with the degree projectors: the signs are constant on each degree (degrees $1$ to $4$ carry only charged components, and CPT ties degree $d$ to $5 - d$), which the check finds leaves $4$ charged patterns, and the block involution is diagonal in degree, so the particle line is $\C\Omega$ or $\C\Omega^{\top}$: $8$ halvings. Anomaly freedom, the stored clause (4), fixes the charged signs to $\pm(-1)^{n + k}$: the check finds $2$ of the $4$ patterns, hence $4$ halvings with the neutral choice. An involution commuting with $\so(10)$ is $\alpha P_{\mathrm{even}} + \beta P_{\mathrm{odd}}$ by (i), with $\alpha, \beta = \pm1$; equal signs give $\Gamma = \pm1$, whose halves have dimensions $32$ and $0$, not a halving; so $\Gamma = \pm(-1)^{N}$ (the check enumerates). The vacuum-swapped halving $\Gamma' = (-1)^{N} - 2P_{\Omega} + 2P_{\Omega^{\top}}$ is an involution of trace zero commuting with every $a^{\dagger}_{i}a_{j}$, is anomaly-free (it agrees with $(-1)^{N}$ on every charged component) and does not commute with $a^{\dagger}_{1}a^{\dagger}_{2}$, which carries $\Omega$ to degree two (checked). So the ladder is $32 \times$ (lines) $\to 8 \to 4 \to 2$. The check computes it from the operators: the gauge components as the cyclic submodules generated from the weight vectors (connectivity under the off-diagonal entries of the generators), the conjugate pairing from the negated weight multisets (five pairs and two self-conjugate single states), the $\uu(5)$ components (six, of sizes $1, 5, 10, 10, 5, 1$) and the $\so(10)$ components (two of sixteen), and counts the sign patterns constant on each; removing the hypercharge from the gauge set changes the pairing and the count. Commuting with the total mode number $N$ alone, in place of $\uu(5)$, already splits the neutral block into $\C\Omega$ and $\C\Omega^{\top}$ (they have $N = 0$ and $N = 5$), and with the stored charged chirality gives the same four halvings; only the count $8$ uses $\uu(5)$. The two survivors are conjugate: the swap $S$ of $\Omega$ and $\Omega^{\top}$ (identity on the other thirty states) is an involution, commutes with every gauge generator (both states are gauge singlets of the same weight), and $S(-1)^{N}S = \Gamma'$ (checked); $S$ does not commute with $\so(10)$. So the bit is invisible to every gauge quantum number and is seen only by the pair operators of $\so(10)$, the $B - L$ charged direction of the Majorana element $\Omega \mapsto \Omega^{\top}$.

\emph{(iii).} If $\Gamma\gamma_{p}\Gamma = -\gamma_{p}$ for all $p$ then $\Gamma(-1)^{N}$ commutes with every $\gamma_{p}$ (since $(-1)^{N}$ anticommutes with each), hence with $M_{32}(\C)$ by (i), so it is a scalar, and $\Gamma = c(-1)^{N}$ with $c = \pm1$ from $\Gamma^{2} = 1$. Conversely $\pm(-1)^{N}$ anticommute with each $\gamma_{p}$ (checked for all ten), and the vacuum-swapped $\Gamma'$ fails for $\gamma_{1}$ (checked). The equivalence with the $\Spin(10)$ form: $\Gamma$ commutes with the bivectors iff it commutes with the even subalgebra they generate; a grading of the Clifford system commutes with the even part and anticommutes with the odd part, and conversely an involution commuting with the even part is $\alpha P_{\mathrm{even}} + \beta P_{\mathrm{odd}}$ by (i), which anticommutes with the generators iff $\alpha = -\beta$, the CPT condition. Independence: $\Gamma'$ satisfies clauses (a), (b), (G), the CPT halving, the joint Clifford system (it is an involution on the same module) and anomaly freedom, and differs from $(-1)^{N}$ exactly on the neutral block; so the clause is not a consequence of the others, and its content is the choice of $\Omega$ over $\Omega^{\top}$. The control repeats the counts on the three-mode pair system ($\uu(3)$: $4$; $\so(6)$: $2$; $\gamma_{1}\cdots\gamma_{6} = -(-1)^{N}$), the stored chirality of the pair system in Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-pair-systems-two-chirality-halves}'s language.
