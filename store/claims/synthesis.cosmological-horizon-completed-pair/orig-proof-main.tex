\emph{Step 1 (curvature).} The de Sitter field is the pure dilation $\hb = \sigma$ of the flat background (Lemma \claimref{mass.de-sitter-scale-frozen-point}; derived metric $g = \sigma^{-2}\eta$, conformally flat), and the Weyl tensor is conformally invariant and vanishes for $\eta$, so it vanishes for $g$ \cite{Wald1984}. The Riemann tensor is the Weyl tensor plus the Kulkarni-Nomizu product of the Schouten tensor with the metric, and on the Einstein region the Schouten tensor is $P_{ab} = (\Lambda/6)\,g_{ab}$ (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-frozen-point-geometry}). Hence
\[
R_{abcd} \;=\; \frac{\Lambda}{3}\,\bigl(g_{ac}g_{bd} - g_{ad}g_{bc}\bigr),
\]
which as a map on bivectors is $\Lambda/3$ times the identity in that convention ($-\Lambda/3$ for $R^{a}{}_{bcd} = \partial_{c}\Gamma^{a}_{db} - \cdots$ in signature $(+,-,-,-)$, the convention the check computes in): one eigenvalue of multiplicity six, every bivector an eigenbivector. No plane is distinguished, and the choice of plane falls to the Killing flow $B_{\Lambda}$, that is, to the observer. The check computes the Riemann tensor of $g$ from its Christoffel symbols in exact arithmetic at rational points and confirms $R_{abcd} = -\tfrac{\Lambda}{3}(g_{ac}g_{bd} - g_{ad}g_{bc})$ in that convention.

\emph{Step 2 (one ambient plane).} Write $\hat X = X/\sigma$ on the region $\sigma \neq 0$. Then $\ip{\hat X}{I_{\Lambda}} = 1$ and $Q(\hat X) = 0$, so for every tangent vector $y$, $d\hat X[y]$ is orthogonal to $I_{\Lambda}$ and to $X$; and since $dX[y] = y - \eta(x,y)\,\nInf$ and $\ip{dX[y]}{X} = 0$,
\[
\ip{d\hat X[y]}{d\hat X[y']} \;=\; \sigma^{-2}\ip{dX[y]}{dX[y']} \;=\; \sigma^{-2}\eta(y, y') \;=\; g(y, y').
\]
So $d\hat X$ maps $(T_{x}M, g)$ isometrically onto the four-dimensional space $X^{\perp} \cap I_{\Lambda}^{\perp}$. At $s \in S_{\Lambda}$ one has $\sigma(s) = 1 - \Lambda Q(s)/12 = 2$ and $d\sigma[y] = -(\Lambda/6)\,\eta(s, y)$. For $y = e_{0}$, $\eta(s, e_{0}) = 0$, so $d\hat X[e_{0}] = e_{0}/2$. For $y = s$, $dX[s] = s - Q(s)\nInf$ and $d\sigma[s] = 2$, so
\[
d\hat X[s] \;=\; \tfrac12\bigl(s - Q(s)\nInf\bigr) - \tfrac12 X(s) \;=\; -\tfrac12\nO + \tfrac{3}{\Lambda}\nInf \;=\; \tfrac{3}{\Lambda}\,W .
\]
The tangent directions of $S_{\Lambda}$ at $s$ are the spatial $y$ with $\eta(s, y) = 0$, sent to $y/2$, which is orthogonal to $e_{0}$ and $W$. So the normal plane of the sphere at $s$, spanned by the time and radial directions, is sent to $\mathrm{span}\{e_{0}, W\} = \Pi_{\Lambda}$, independent of $s$. The check confirms these images at rational points of $S_{\Lambda}$ and that the four images span $X(s)^{\perp} \cap I_{\Lambda}^{\perp}$.

\emph{Step 3 (the pair).} $Q(e_{0}) = 1$, and $Q(W) = -\Lambda/3$ (Proposition \claimref{synthesis.de-sitter-static-boost-frozen-point}) gives $Q(e_{W}) = -1$; $e_{0} \perp W$. So $\Pi_{\Lambda}$ is a $(1,1)$ plane and $(e_{0}, e_{W})$ plays the role of $(e_{0}, e_{1})$ in the hyperbolic atom of Lemma \claimref{singularity.pseudo-inverse-identities-hyperbolic-atom}. Directly: $Q(e_{0} \pm e_{W}) = Q(e_{0}) + Q(e_{W}) = 0$; $(e_{0} + e_{W})(e_{0} - e_{W}) + (e_{0} - e_{W})(e_{0} + e_{W}) = 2(Q(e_{0}) - Q(e_{W})) = 4$; and $(e_{0} - e_{W})(e_{0} + e_{W}) = 2 + 2u$, $(e_{0} + e_{W})(e_{0} - e_{W}) = 2 - 2u$, so $v_{\Lambda}^{*}v_{\Lambda} - v_{\Lambda}v_{\Lambda}^{*} = u$. Uniqueness: the null vectors of $\Pi_{\Lambda}$ are the multiples of $e_{0} + e_{W}$ and of $e_{0} - e_{W}$; for $v = a(e_{0} + e_{W})$ the frame adjoint is $\bar a(e_{0} - e_{W})$, and the anticommutator is $4|a|^{2}$, so $|a| = \tfrac12$; for $v = a(e_{0} - e_{W})$ the same computation gives $v^{*}v - vv^{*} = -u$. Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-structure-completed-pair} now applies to the pair with $\sigma = u$: the rotor flow $\exp(\lambda u/2)$ acts by $v \mapsto e^{-\lambda}v$, $v^{*} \mapsto e^{\lambda}v^{*}$, its spectral projectors are the occupation projectors, and $K = u/2 = N - \tfrac12$. Since $B_{\Lambda} = Hu$, $\exp(tB_{\Lambda}/2)$ is that flow at $\lambda = Ht = \kappa_{\Lambda}t$. With $q = e^{\lambda/2}$ the rotor is $\tfrac12(q + q^{-1}) + \tfrac12(q - q^{-1})u$, and it sends $\nO = X(0)$ to a multiple of $X\bigl(\tfrac{2}{H}\tfrac{q^{2} - 1}{q^{2} + 1}e_{0}\bigr)$, a point to the future of the origin for $q > 1$; the check verifies the scalings and this image for rational $q$.

\emph{Step 4 (conformal infinity).} $e_{0}$ and $W$ are orthogonal to $I_{\Lambda}$, so $v_{\Lambda}, v_{\Lambda}^{*} \in I_{\Lambda}^{\perp}$, and they are null. Expanding, $X(\mp\tfrac{2}{H}e_{0}) = \nO \mp \tfrac{2}{H}e_{0} - \tfrac{2}{H^{2}}\nInf$, and $\mp\tfrac{H}{4}$ times it is $\tfrac12 e_{0} \mp \tfrac{H}{4}\nO \pm \tfrac{1}{2H}\nInf$. Since $\Lambda/(6H) = H/2$, this is $\tfrac12\bigl(e_{0} \pm W/H\bigr)$ with $W/H = (\nInf - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO)/H$, which is $v_{\Lambda}$ for the upper sign and $v_{\Lambda}^{*}$ for the lower. At $x = \mp\tfrac{2}{H}e_{0}$, $Q(x) = 4/H^{2} = 12/\Lambda$ and $\sigma = 1 - \Lambda Q/12 = 0$. Proposition \claimref{synthesis.de-sitter-static-boost-frozen-point} lists these two points as the zeros of the Killing velocity off the bifurcation sphere. They are the endpoints of the observer's worldline $x = x_{0}e_{0}$, $|x_{0}| < \sqrt{12/\Lambda}$, the rotor flow of Step 3 carrying the origin towards $x_{0} = 2/H$ as $t \to \infty$. On $S_{\Lambda}$, $\sigma = 2$ by Step 2.

\emph{Step 5 (existence, and the mirror).} For $\Lambda = 0$, $W = \nInf$ is null and orthogonal to $e_{0}$, so the plane of $B_{0}$ carries a degenerate form, with one null direction and no two null vectors pairing to $1$. For $\Lambda < 0$, $Q(W) = -\Lambda/3 > 0$, so the plane of $e_{0}$ and $W$ is definite and has no null vector. Under $Q \mapsto -Q$, $Q(e_{0}) = -1$ and $Q(e_{W}) = +1$, so $\Pi_{\Lambda}$ is still a $(1,1)$ plane and $u^{2} = -Q(e_{0})\,Q(e_{W}) = 1$; $B_{\Lambda}^{2} = \Lambda/3$ is unchanged (Proposition \claimref{synthesis.de-sitter-static-boost-frozen-point}(v)). The frame adjoint now has $e_{0}^{*} = -e_{0}$ and $e_{W}^{*} = e_{W}$, so for $w = \tfrac12(e_{0} - e_{W})$, $w^{*} = -\tfrac12(e_{0} + e_{W})$, and $ww^{*} + w^{*}w = -\tfrac12\bigl(Q(e_{0}) - Q(e_{W})\bigr) = 1$; the products $(e_{0} + e_{W})(e_{0} - e_{W}) = -2 - 2u$ and $(e_{0} - e_{W})(e_{0} + e_{W}) = -2 + 2u$ give $w^{*}w - ww^{*} = u$; and $u(e_{0} - e_{W}) = -(e_{0} - e_{W})$, so the flow contracts $w$, which lies along $i^{+}$ by Step 4, at rapidity $\lambda$.
