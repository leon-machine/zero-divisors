\emph{Step 1 (curvature).} In Gauge Theory Gravity the de Sitter field of the scale is the dilation of the flat background, $\hb^{-1}(a) = a/\sigma$, $\hb(a) = \sigma a$ (Lemma \claimref{mass.de-sitter-scale-frozen-point}; derived metric $g = \sigma^{-2}\eta$), on the region $\sigma \neq 0$, with $\nabla\sigma = -\tfrac{\Lambda}{6}x$. Its rotation-gauge field is fixed by the torsion equation, read on the constant physical frame $\{\gamma_{a}\}$ as $\hb^{T}(\nabla \wedge f^{a}) = \sum_{b}\gamma^{b} \wedge (\omega(\gamma_{b}) \cdot \gamma^{a})$ with $\hb^{T}(f^{a}) = \gamma^{a}$ and $\omega(A) = \Omega(\hb(A))$. Here $f^{a} = \gamma^{a}/\sigma$, so the left side is $\sigma^{2}\,\nabla(\sigma^{-1}) \wedge \gamma^{a} = -\nabla\sigma \wedge \gamma^{a}$, and $\omega(A) = \nabla\sigma \wedge A$ solves it: $\sum_{b}\gamma^{b} \wedge \bigl((\nabla\sigma \wedge \gamma_{b}) \cdot \gamma^{a}\bigr) = \sum_{b}\gamma^{b} \wedge \bigl(\nabla\sigma\,\delta^{a}_{b} - \gamma_{b}\,(\nabla\sigma \cdot \gamma^{a})\bigr) = \gamma^{a} \wedge \nabla\sigma$, the solution being unique. So, with $c = -\Lambda/6$,
\[
\Omega(a) = \omega(a/\sigma) = \frac{c}{\sigma}\,x \wedge a .
\]
The field strength $R(a \wedge b) = a \cdot \nabla\Omega(b) - b \cdot \nabla\Omega(a) + \Omega(a) \times \Omega(b)$ has, with $a \cdot \nabla\sigma = c\,a \cdot x$,
\[
a \cdot \nabla\Omega(b) - b \cdot \nabla\Omega(a) = \frac{2c}{\sigma}\,a \wedge b - \frac{c^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}\bigl((a \cdot x)\,x \wedge b - (b \cdot x)\,x \wedge a\bigr),
\]
and, by $(u \wedge w) \times (y \wedge z) = (w \cdot y)\,u \wedge z - (w \cdot z)\,u \wedge y - (u \cdot y)\,w \wedge z + (u \cdot z)\,w \wedge y$,
\[
\Omega(a) \times \Omega(b) = \frac{c^{2}}{\sigma^{2}}\bigl((a \cdot x)\,x \wedge b - (b \cdot x)\,x \wedge a - Q(x)\,a \wedge b\bigr).
\]
The terms in $x \wedge a$, $x \wedge b$ cancel and $R(a \wedge b) = \tfrac{c}{\sigma^{2}}(2\sigma - cQ(x))\,a \wedge b = \tfrac{2c}{\sigma^{2}}\,a \wedge b$, since $2\sigma - cQ(x) = 2 - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}Q(x) + \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}Q(x) = 2$. With $\hb(a \wedge b) = \sigma^{2}\,a \wedge b$ the store-sign curvature is
\[
\mathcal{R}(B) = -R(\hb(B)) = \frac{\Lambda}{3}\,B
\]
for every physical bivector $B$: one eigenvalue of multiplicity six, $\mathcal{R}(a) = \Lambda a$, $\Sch = \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\mathrm{id}$ (the Schouten map of Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-frozen-point-geometry}) and Weyl part $\mathcal{W} = \mathcal{R} - \mathrm{KN}(\Sch) = 0$. Every bivector is an eigenbivector, so no plane is distinguished, and the choice of plane falls to the Killing flow $B_{\Lambda}$, that is, to the observer. The check solves $\Omega$ from the torsion condition at rational points, confirms $\Omega(a) = -\tfrac{\Lambda}{6\sigma}x \wedge a$ and $\mathcal{R}(B) = \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}B$ exactly, and computes $\mathcal{W} = 0$. \emph{In components} (a reading): $R_{abcd} = \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}(g_{ac}g_{bd} - g_{ad}g_{bc})$ in the convention of the Fact ($P = (\Lambda/6)g$), and by the dictionary's identification $\mathrm{Riem}^{\circ}(u, v, w, z) = (w \wedge z) \cdot \mathcal{R}(u \wedge v)$ it is $-\tfrac{\Lambda}{3}(g_{ac}g_{bd} - g_{ad}g_{bc})$ for $R^{a}{}_{bcd} = \partial_{c}\Gamma^{a}_{db} - \cdots$ in signature $(+,-,-,-)$, which the check also confirms from the derived metric's Christoffel symbols.

\emph{Step 2 (one ambient plane).} Write $\hat X = X/\sigma$ on the region $\sigma \neq 0$. Then $\ip{\hat X}{I_{\Lambda}} = 1$ and $Q(\hat X) = 0$, so for every tangent vector $y$, $d\hat X[y]$ is orthogonal to $I_{\Lambda}$ and to $X$; and since $dX[y] = y - \eta(x,y)\,\nInf$ and $\ip{dX[y]}{X} = 0$,
\[
\ip{d\hat X[y]}{d\hat X[y']} \;=\; \sigma^{-2}\ip{dX[y]}{dX[y']} \;=\; \sigma^{-2}\eta(y, y') \;=\; g(y, y').
\]
So $d\hat X$ maps $(T_{x}M, g)$ isometrically onto the four-dimensional space $X^{\perp} \cap I_{\Lambda}^{\perp}$. At $s \in S_{\Lambda}$ one has $\sigma(s) = 1 - \Lambda Q(s)/12 = 2$ and $d\sigma[y] = -(\Lambda/6)\,\eta(s, y)$. For $y = e_{0}$, $\eta(s, e_{0}) = 0$, so $d\hat X[e_{0}] = e_{0}/2$. For $y = s$, $dX[s] = s - Q(s)\nInf$ and $d\sigma[s] = 2$, so
\[
d\hat X[s] \;=\; \tfrac12\bigl(s - Q(s)\nInf\bigr) - \tfrac12 X(s) \;=\; -\tfrac12\nO + \tfrac{3}{\Lambda}\nInf \;=\; \tfrac{3}{\Lambda}\,W .
\]
The tangent directions of $S_{\Lambda}$ at $s$ are the spatial $y$ with $\eta(s, y) = 0$, sent to $y/2$, which is orthogonal to $e_{0}$ and $W$. So the normal plane of the sphere at $s$, spanned by the time and radial directions, is sent to $\mathrm{span}\{e_{0}, W\} = \Pi_{\Lambda}$, independent of $s$. The check confirms these images at rational points of $S_{\Lambda}$ and that the four images span $X(s)^{\perp} \cap I_{\Lambda}^{\perp}$.

\emph{Step 3 (the pair).} $Q(e_{0}) = 1$, and $Q(W) = -\Lambda/3$ (Proposition \claimref{synthesis.de-sitter-static-boost-frozen-point}) gives $Q(e_{W}) = -1$; $e_{0} \perp W$. So $\Pi_{\Lambda}$ is a $(1,1)$ plane and $(e_{0}, e_{W})$ plays the role of $(e_{0}, e_{1})$ in the hyperbolic atom of Lemma \claimref{singularity.pseudo-inverse-identities-hyperbolic-atom}. Directly: $Q(e_{0} \pm e_{W}) = Q(e_{0}) + Q(e_{W}) = 0$; $(e_{0} + e_{W})(e_{0} - e_{W}) + (e_{0} - e_{W})(e_{0} + e_{W}) = 2(Q(e_{0}) - Q(e_{W})) = 4$; and $(e_{0} - e_{W})(e_{0} + e_{W}) = 2 + 2u$, $(e_{0} + e_{W})(e_{0} - e_{W}) = 2 - 2u$, so $v_{\Lambda}^{*}v_{\Lambda} - v_{\Lambda}v_{\Lambda}^{*} = u$. Uniqueness: the null vectors of $\Pi_{\Lambda}$ are the multiples of $e_{0} + e_{W}$ and of $e_{0} - e_{W}$; for $v = a(e_{0} + e_{W})$, $a \in \mathrm{span}\{1, \hat{I}\}$ (the unit of the boundary-mode algebra) with conjugate $\bar a$, the frame adjoint is $\bar a(e_{0} - e_{W})$, and the anticommutator is $4|a|^{2}$, so $|a| = \tfrac12$; for $v = a(e_{0} - e_{W})$ the same computation gives $v^{*}v - vv^{*} = -u$. Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-structure-completed-pair} now applies to the pair with $\sigma = u$: the rotor flow $\exp(\lambda u/2)$ acts by $v \mapsto e^{-\lambda}v$, $v^{*} \mapsto e^{\lambda}v^{*}$, its spectral projectors are the occupation projectors, and $K = u/2 = N - \tfrac12$. Since $B_{\Lambda} = Hu$, $\exp(tB_{\Lambda}/2)$ is that flow at $\lambda = Ht = \kappa_{\Lambda}t$. With $q = e^{\lambda/2}$ the rotor is $\tfrac12(q + q^{-1}) + \tfrac12(q - q^{-1})u$, and it sends $\nO = X(0)$ to a multiple of $X\bigl(\tfrac{2}{H}\tfrac{q^{2} - 1}{q^{2} + 1}e_{0}\bigr)$, a point to the future of the origin for $q > 1$; the check verifies the scalings and this image for rational $q$.

\emph{Step 4 (conformal infinity).} $e_{0}$ and $W$ are orthogonal to $I_{\Lambda}$, so $v_{\Lambda}, v_{\Lambda}^{*} \in I_{\Lambda}^{\perp}$, and they are null. Expanding, $X(\mp\tfrac{2}{H}e_{0}) = \nO \mp \tfrac{2}{H}e_{0} - \tfrac{2}{H^{2}}\nInf$, and $\mp\tfrac{H}{4}$ times it is $\tfrac12 e_{0} \mp \tfrac{H}{4}\nO \pm \tfrac{1}{2H}\nInf$. Since $\Lambda/(6H) = H/2$, this is $\tfrac12\bigl(e_{0} \pm W/H\bigr)$ with $W/H = (\nInf - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO)/H$, which is $v_{\Lambda}$ for the upper sign and $v_{\Lambda}^{*}$ for the lower. At $x = \mp\tfrac{2}{H}e_{0}$, $Q(x) = 4/H^{2} = 12/\Lambda$ and $\sigma = 1 - \Lambda Q/12 = 0$. Proposition \claimref{synthesis.de-sitter-static-boost-frozen-point} lists these two points as the zeros of the Killing velocity off the bifurcation sphere. They are the endpoints of the observer's worldline $x = x_{0}e_{0}$, $|x_{0}| < \sqrt{12/\Lambda}$, the rotor flow of Step 3 carrying the origin towards $x_{0} = 2/H$ as $t \to \infty$. On $S_{\Lambda}$, $\sigma = 2$ by Step 2.

\emph{Step 5 (existence, and the mirror).} For $\Lambda = 0$, $W = \nInf$ is null and orthogonal to $e_{0}$, so the plane of $B_{0}$ carries a degenerate form, with one null direction and no two null vectors pairing to $1$. For $\Lambda < 0$, $Q(W) = -\Lambda/3 > 0$, so the plane of $e_{0}$ and $W$ is definite and has no null vector. Under $Q \mapsto -Q$, $Q(e_{0}) = -1$ and $Q(e_{W}) = +1$, so $\Pi_{\Lambda}$ is still a $(1,1)$ plane and $u^{2} = -Q(e_{0})\,Q(e_{W}) = 1$; $B_{\Lambda}^{2} = \Lambda/3$ is unchanged (Proposition \claimref{synthesis.de-sitter-static-boost-frozen-point}(v)). The frame adjoint now has $e_{0}^{*} = -e_{0}$ and $e_{W}^{*} = e_{W}$, so for $w = \tfrac12(e_{0} - e_{W})$, $w^{*} = -\tfrac12(e_{0} + e_{W})$, and $ww^{*} + w^{*}w = -\tfrac12\bigl(Q(e_{0}) - Q(e_{W})\bigr) = 1$; the products $(e_{0} + e_{W})(e_{0} - e_{W}) = -2 - 2u$ and $(e_{0} - e_{W})(e_{0} + e_{W}) = -2 + 2u$ give $w^{*}w - ww^{*} = u$; and $u(e_{0} - e_{W}) = -(e_{0} - e_{W})$, so the flow contracts $w$, which lies along $i^{+}$ by Step 4, at rapidity $\lambda$.
