## formal
Through the fourth gravitational-wave transient catalogue (GWTC-4.0, 2025-2026: some two hundred and eighteen compact-binary candidates, with ninety-one confident signals subjected to the dedicated tests of general relativity), the echo searches comprise two families of echo waveform templates and two morphology-agnostic searches for coherent post-merger excess power, applied to the fourth observing run's events alongside all prior ones; they report no statistically significant echo train, and the early tentative claims \cite{Abedi2017} did not survive independent reanalysis \cite{Westerweck2018, GWTC4TGR2026}. The registered prediction of Record \claimref{scrambling.no-echo-prediction-registered} therefore stands consistent with every event so far analysed, across four catalogues and four search methods. \emph{Level:} observational, resting on the cited survey record as of the catalogue date; the confrontation is not closed and cannot be: it becomes more stringent with each catalogue, and the framework remains one confirmed echo train away from refutation.

## technical
Through the fourth gravitational-wave transient catalogue (GWTC-4.0: some 218 compact-binary candidates, 91 confident signals subjected to dedicated tests of general relativity), the echo searches comprise two families of echo templates and two morphology-agnostic searches for coherent post-merger excess power. They report no statistically significant echo train, and early tentative echo claims did not survive independent reanalysis.

The registered no-echo prediction therefore stands consistent with every event analysed so far, across four catalogues and four search methods. The result is observational and dated to the catalogue; the confrontation stays open and becomes more stringent with each catalogue.

## lay
Gravitational-wave detectors have now recorded hundreds of black-hole mergers, and teams have searched the signals for echoes that would betray unusual structure near the horizon. None has been found, and early hints did not survive closer analysis. This agrees with the framework's prediction, though one confirmed echo would still refute it.

## one-liner
Echo searches through GWTC-4.0, four methods over four catalogues, find no significant echo train, consistent with the registered no-echo prediction.
