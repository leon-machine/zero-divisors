(i) A polynomial in commuting projectors is diagonal in their joint eigenbasis; a diagonal matrix with a unique minimum has that basis vector as its unique ground state. A number basis state is a product of single-mode number states, its marginal on any set of modes is the corresponding number state, pure, and it is an eigenvector of each $N_{k}$. A Gibbs state $e^{-\beta K}/Z$ at finite $\beta$ for a single-mode boost $K = \omega(N - \tfrac12)$ is $\operatorname{diag}(1, e^{-\beta\omega})/Z$, of rank two, and a pure marginal has rank one. The script draws random rational diagonal generators on two and three modes with every cut, and for each draw with a unique ground state (at least three such draws in all) confirms the product form, the rank-one idempotent marginals on both sides, the eigenvector property, and, for a single-mode side, the absence of a Gibbs parameter. (ii) The stated $H$ has eigenvalues $0, 0, 1, 1$ on $\ket{00}, \ket{11}, \ket{10}, \ket{01}$; the script checks the ground space and that both named states are annihilated by $H$.
