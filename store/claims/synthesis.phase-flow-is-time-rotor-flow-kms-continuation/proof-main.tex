\emph{(i)} For a bivector $B$ and a vector $x$, $Bx - xB = 2\,B \cdot x$ is a vector, so $\operatorname{ad}_{B}$ preserves $V$. The $*$-operation of Theorem \claimref{tower.structure-tower-c-norms} is an antiautomorphism with $(B^{*})^{*} = B$, and the scalar part is cyclic, $\br{XY}_{0} = \br{YX}_{0}$. Hence
\[
E(\operatorname{ad}_{B}x, y) = \br{Bxy^{*}}_{0} - \br{xBy^{*}}_{0} = \br{xy^{*}B}_{0} - \br{xBy^{*}}_{0} = \br{x\,(B^{*}y - yB^{*})^{*}}_{0} = E(x, \operatorname{ad}_{B^{*}}y),
\]
using $(B^{*}y)^{*} = y^{*}B$ and $(yB^{*})^{*} = By^{*}$. On a frame generator $\br{e_{i}e_{i}^{*}}_{0} = Q(e_{i})\cdot Q(e_{i}) = 1$ and distinct frame generators give $0$, so $E$ is positive definite with the frame orthonormal. For $i < j$, $(e_{i}e_{j})^{*} = e_{j}^{*}e_{i}^{*} = Q(e_{i})Q(e_{j})\,e_{j}e_{i} = -Q(e_{i})Q(e_{j})\,e_{i}e_{j}$, which is $-e_{i}e_{j}$ when the squares agree and $+e_{i}e_{j}$ when they differ; this gives the unique splitting $B = B_{c} + B_{b}$. If $B_{b} = 0$ then $(e^{\lambda B})^{*} = e^{\lambda B^{*}} = e^{-\lambda B}$, so $e^{\lambda B}$ is unitary and $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{\lambda B})$ commutes with $*$; by the adjoint formula $\operatorname{ad}_{B}$ is $E$-antisymmetric, so its spectrum is imaginary and $e^{\lambda\operatorname{ad}_{B}}$ is $E$-orthogonal. Conversely, if $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{\lambda B})$ commutes with $*$ for all $\lambda$, differentiating at $\lambda = 0$ gives $(\operatorname{ad}_{B}X)^{*} = \operatorname{ad}_{B}(X^{*})$, that is $X^{*}(B + B^{*}) = (B + B^{*})X^{*}$ for all $X$, so $B + B^{*} = 2B_{b}$ is central; the centre of $\Cl(p,q)$ is spanned by $1$ and, for $p + q$ odd, the volume element of grade $p + q \ge 3$, so it contains no nonzero bivector and $B_{b} = 0$.

\emph{(ii)} Let $X$ be a clock in the sense of Lemma \claimref{scrambling.clock-inventory-boundary-modes}(ii), so $\operatorname{ad}_{X}$ is semisimple with a real eigenvalue $c \neq 0$ and real eigenvector $x \in V$. If $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{\lambda X})$ were $*$-automorphic for some frame $*$-structure, (i) would make $\operatorname{ad}_{X}$ antisymmetric for that frame's $E$, and $cE(x,x) = E(\operatorname{ad}_{X}x, x) = -E(x, \operatorname{ad}_{X}x) = -cE(x,x)$ with $E(x,x) > 0$ forces $c = 0$, a contradiction. For the atom of $\Cl(1,1)$ with $v = \tfrac12(e_{+} + e_{-})$, $v^{*} = \tfrac12(e_{+} - e_{-})$ the CAR hold and $\sigma = v^{*}v - vv^{*} = e_{+}e_{-}$, $\sigma^{2} = 1$, $\sigma^{*} = \sigma$, $\operatorname{ad}_{\sigma}v = -2v$, $\operatorname{ad}_{\sigma}v^{*} = 2v^{*}$; the rotor $R_{\lambda} = \cosh\tfrac{\lambda}{2} + \sigma\sinh\tfrac{\lambda}{2}$ gives $v \mapsto e^{-\lambda}v$, $v^{*} \mapsto e^{\lambda}v^{*}$, and since $(e^{-\lambda}v)^{*} = e^{-\lambda}v^{*} \neq e^{\lambda}v^{*}$ for $\lambda \neq 0$ it is not a $*$-map (this is the single-plane clause of Proposition \claimref{field-theory.boost-modes}). The script also checks (ii) for clocks obtained by conjugating an atom boost of $\Cl(2,2)$ with boost and rotation rotors (the real eigenvalue survives, the boost part is nonzero, and $\operatorname{ad}_{X}$ is not $E$-antisymmetric), and it checks (i) and (ii) on random rational bivectors of $\Cl(1,1)$, $\Cl(2,2)$, $\Cl(3,3)$, $\Cl(1,3)$, $\Cl(3,1)$, $\Cl(2,4)$ and on the atom at $e^{\lambda/2} = 2$.

\emph{(iii)} The adjoint formula of (i) holds verbatim for $h$ on $V_{\C}$ with $*$ conjugate-linear. If $W$ is gauge-invariant, $[K_{\mathrm{tot}}, W] = 0$, so $e^{it(\kappa K_{\mathrm{tot}} + W)} = e^{it\kappa K_{\mathrm{tot}}}e^{itW}$, and $[K_{\mathrm{tot}}, a_{j}] = -a_{j}$ gives $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{it\kappa K_{\mathrm{tot}}})a_{j} = e^{-i\kappa t}a_{j}$ for every $j$, a common phase. \emph{(iv)} $\alpha_{\lambda}$ is an algebra automorphism with $\alpha_{\lambda}(a_{1}a_{2}) = e^{-2\lambda}a_{1}a_{2}$, $\alpha_{\lambda}(a_{2}^{*}a_{1}^{*}) = e^{2\lambda}a_{2}^{*}a_{1}^{*}$ and $\alpha_{\lambda}(N_{1}N_{2}) = N_{1}N_{2}$. \emph{The reading.} Take a physical time evolution to be a one-parameter group of $*$-automorphisms preserving the state space (the hypothesis of the concluding paragraph). By (i), (ii) and (iv) the rotor flow is not a $*$-automorphism and does not preserve the state space, so it is not the evolution of any state; by (iii) the phase flow and every quadratic perturbation of it are $*$-automorphisms, and the phase flow is the boost dynamics of Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.boundary-mode-algebra-windows-boost-dynamics}; by (v) the rotor flow is its imaginary-parameter continuation and implements the KMS condition at rapidity $\beta$. The growth step of Assumption \claimref{scrambling.spreading-law-chaos-bound-saturated} exponentiates the real weights $\mp 1$ of the boost with a real parameter, which by (ii) is the rotor flow; by (iii) the same generator in real time conserves the $h$-norm of every evolved mode operator and contributes a phase (gauge-invariant couplings) or a detuning (pairing couplings). The rate $\kappa$ in $t_{*} = \kappa^{-1}\ln N$ is therefore the rotor flow's, and its equality with $2\pi T$ is $\beta = 2\pi/\kappa$, the content of (R). The conservation of the $h$-norm is a property of the compact fermionic Bogoliubov group of the CAR windows; nothing here bears on non-quadratic generators, or on bosonic and geometric boosts (for instance the real-time blueshift of Kruskal momenta), which are unitary and grow exponentially in real time.
