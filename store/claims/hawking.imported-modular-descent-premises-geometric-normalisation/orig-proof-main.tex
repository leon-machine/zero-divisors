Let the ascended algebra of a horizon pair carry a state that is pure, invariant under the boost flow of Proposition \claimref{hawking.modular-structure-completed-pair}, and entangling across the pair (cyclic and separating for the one-sided subalgebra). Then the descended state is KMS for the boost flow at \emph{some} inverse temperature $\beta \in \R$ of rapidity, and the hypotheses leave $\beta$ free, its sign included; the Gibbs \emph{form} of the descended state is state-independent. Positivity and the value are supplied by T5's named premises: under (P) univalence and (R) the KMS condition at the geometric period (one modular period equal to one rapidity period $2\pi$, i.e.\ Schwarzschild time $2\pi/\kappa$, the Euclidean period $8\pi M$), the descended state is Fermi-Dirac at $\beta = 2\pi$ of rapidity, which is the physical temperature $\TH = \kappa/2\pi = 1/8\pi M$; the boost plane is the one the shape operator scales by $-2$, and $\kappa = 1/4M$ is proved from the gauge field. Per mode of Schwarzschild frequency $\omega$ the reduced density matrix is the Fermi-Dirac matrix $\mathrm{diag}(1, e^{-\omega/\TH})/Z$. \emph{Provenance:} T5 \cite[Thms.~7.3 and 7.6, Prems.~7.8 and 7.19, Prop.~7.18, Thm.~7.20, Cor.~6.4]{ButlerT5}, with $\kappa = 1/4M$ from the gauge field in \cite[App.~A]{ButlerT5}; premise (R) is proved irreducible there, the temperature being no algebra-intrinsic quantity \cite[Thm.~7.16]{ButlerT5}. The wedge-modular mechanism is Bisognano-Wichmann-Sewell \cite{BisognanoWichmann1976, Sewell1982}, and the thermofield reading is Israel's \cite{Israel1976}.
