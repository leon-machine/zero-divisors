The spinor module of the completed algebra $\Cl(2,4)$ is the conformal spinor space $\CS = \Cl^+(2,4)P$ (twistor space), of dimension $4$ over the complex unit $I_6$ (a \emph{storey} is the series' word for one level of the completion tower, as in \cite{ButlerT9}), the palatial commutation relation $[Z^\mu, \bar Z_\nu] = \hbar\,\delta^\mu_\nu$ on the quadratic (palatial) quantisation of the storey ($\bar Z$ the dual of $Z$ under the reversion form $h$), and, for $M \in \mathfrak{gl}(\CS)$, the symmetrised quadratic $q(M) := \tfrac12\bigl(Z^\mu M_\mu^{\ \nu}\bar Z_\nu + \bar Z_\nu M_\mu^{\ \nu} Z^\mu\bigr)$. (Indices on $\CS$ are written $\mu, \nu$ throughout; $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are reserved for the modular dynamics and its inverse temperature.) Symmetric ordering shifts the distinguished $\mathfrak{u}(1)$ generator $q(\id)$ by $-\hbar/2$ per complex dimension of $\CS$, totalling $-2\hbar$ (equivalently, $q(M) = \sum M_\mu^{\ \nu} Z^\mu \bar Z_\nu - \tfrac{\hbar}{2}\tr M$); on the Clifford storey, the boost generator of each adjoined plane is $N - \tfrac12$, a shift of minus one half per fermionic mode: the two storeys carry the same signed ordering shift. \emph{Provenance:} the correspondence paper \cite[Thm.~3.1, Rem.~3.2, \S 5, Lem.~5.5, Prop.~5.6, Thm.~5.7]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.fock-model-complexified-ascended-algebra}, \claimref{correspondence.clifford-side-ordering-shift}, \claimref{correspondence.symmetrised-quadratics-palatial-algebra}, \claimref{correspondence.shared-core-ordering-dictionary}); the homogeneity consequence is computed inside the proof of its Theorem 5.7 and is promoted to a stated result as Proposition \claimref{hawking.homogeneity-ordering-shift} below.
