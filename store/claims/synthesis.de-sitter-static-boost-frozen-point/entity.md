## formal
In the conventions of Lemma \claimref{mass.de-sitter-scale-frozen-point} ($\ip{\nO}{\nInf} = 1$, $X(x) = \nO + x - \tfrac12 Q(x)\,\nInf$) let $I_{\Lambda} = \nInf + \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO$ be the frozen point and $W = \nInf - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO$, the value at the origin $x=0$ (for $\Lambda\neq0$) of the vector $W = V/Q(V)$ of Lemma \claimref{dark-energy.stelle-west-vector-frozen-point}, so that $\ip{W}{I_{\Lambda}} = 0$ and $Q(W) = -\Lambda/3$. Put $B_{\Lambda} := e_{0}\wedge W = e_{0}W$. Then, for every real $\Lambda$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $B_{\Lambda}$ commutes with $I_{\Lambda}$, so it lies in the stabiliser of the frozen point, and
\[
B_{\Lambda}^{2} \;=\; Q(I_{\Lambda}) \;=\; \frac{\Lambda}{3},
\]
a scalar.
\item[(ii)] Its Killing field $\xi = X \cdot B_{\Lambda}$ is tangent to the null cone, $Q(\xi)$ equals the flat-chart square of the velocity it induces, and $Q(\xi) = \bigl(1 + \tfrac{\Lambda}{12}Q(x)\bigr)^{2} - \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}x_{0}^{2}$. On the slice $x = r e_{1}$, with scale $\sigma = \ip{X}{I_{\Lambda}} = 1 + \tfrac{\Lambda}{12}r^{2}$ and areal radius $R = r/\sigma$, the physical norm is $g(\xi,\xi) = Q(\xi)/\sigma^{2} = 1 - \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}R^{2}$: $B_{\Lambda}$ generates the static time of the observer at $x = 0$, normalised to unit norm there.
\item[(iii)] For $\Lambda > 0$ the static patch has its Killing horizon at $R_{h} = \sqrt{3/\Lambda}$ with surface gravity $\kappa_{\Lambda} = \sqrt{\Lambda/3}$, so
\[
\kappa_{\Lambda}^{2} \;=\; B_{\Lambda}^{2} \;=\; Q(I_{\Lambda}) ;
\]
with $H = \sqrt{\Lambda/3}$, $B_{\Lambda} = H\,u$ where $u = e_{0}e_{W}$, $e_{W} = W/H$, $u^{2} = +1$: the static flow is generated by the unit ambient boost in the plane of $e_{0}$ and the unit Stelle-West direction at the origin $x=0$, at rapidity $H$ per unit Killing time. Its fixed set in the region $\sigma \neq 0$ is the bifurcation sphere $\{x_{0} = 0,\ Q(x) = -12/\Lambda\}$ ($R = R_{h}$), where the induced velocity vanishes and its linearisation is $H$ times the unit boost of the normal $(x_{0}, r)$ plane and zero along the sphere: the horizon's local boost rate is the same $\kappa_{\Lambda} = H$. The Euclidean section of the static patch is smooth at the horizon if and only if imaginary Killing time has period $2\pi/\kappa_{\Lambda} = 2\pi/\sqrt{Q(I_{\Lambda})}$, the counterpart of the $8\pi M$ of Proposition \claimref{singularity.euclidean-section-period}.
\item[(iv)] The sign of the frozen point's norm sorts the static flow: for $\Lambda > 0$ a boost, with a horizon; for $\Lambda = 0$, $B_{0} = e_{0}\nInf \neq 0$ with $B_{0}^{2} = 0$, a null rotation (the Poincar\'e time translation), no horizon and $\kappa = 0$; for $\Lambda < 0$, $B_{\Lambda}^{2} < 0$, a rotation, so the static time closes with period $2\pi/\sqrt{-\Lambda/3}$ and there is no horizon.
\item[(v)] Under reversal of the ambient form, $Q \mapsto -Q$ (the signature $(4,2)$ of the mirror's tractor bundle), $Q(I_{\Lambda})$ changes sign while $B_{\Lambda}^{2}$ does not. The convention-free statement is therefore $\kappa_{\Lambda}^{2} = B_{\Lambda}^{2}$, which reads $Q(I)$ in this world's convention and $-Q(I)'$ in the reversed one.
\end{enumerate}

## technical
Take the frozen point $I_{\Lambda} = \nInf + \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO$ of the mass paper's de Sitter lemma and the dark-energy paper's vector $W = \nInf - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO$, orthogonal to $I_{\Lambda}$ with $Q(W) = -\Lambda/3$. The bivector $B_{\Lambda} = e_{0}W$ commutes with $I_{\Lambda}$ and squares to the scalar $\Lambda/3 = Q(I_{\Lambda})$. Its Killing field $X\cdot B_{\Lambda}$ has physical norm $1 - \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}R^{2}$ on the static slice through the origin, so $B_{\Lambda}$ is the static time translation of de Sitter space. For $\Lambda > 0$ its horizon sits at $R = \sqrt{3/\Lambda}$ with surface gravity $\sqrt{\Lambda/3}$: the surface gravity squared is the frozen point's norm. The generator is $H$ times the unit ambient boost in the plane of $e_{0}$ and the unit Stelle-West direction, and at the bifurcation sphere, where the flow has its fixed points, it linearises to a boost of the normal plane at the same rate $H$. The Euclidean static patch is smooth exactly when imaginary time has period $2\pi/\sqrt{Q(I)}$. For $\Lambda = 0$ the same bivector is a null rotation (no horizon) and for $\Lambda < 0$ a rotation (closed static time). Reversing the form flips the sign of $Q(I)$ but not of $B_{\Lambda}^{2}$, so the invariant identity is $\kappa^{2} = B_{\Lambda}^{2}$.

## lay
In the series the cosmological constant is carried by one fixed vector of the enlarged algebra, the frozen point. This result shows that the time flow seen by an observer in a universe with a positive cosmological constant is a single boost built from that vector, and that the boost's rate, which fixes how strongly the observer's horizon pulls, is exactly the square root of the frozen point's squared length. With no cosmological constant the same construction gives ordinary time translation and no horizon; with a negative one it gives a rotation, and time closes on itself.

## one-liner
The static time of de Sitter space is the bivector $B_{\Lambda} = e_{0}\wedge W$ in the frozen point's stabiliser, with $B_{\Lambda}^{2} = \kappa_{\Lambda}^{2} = Q(I_{\Lambda}) = \Lambda/3$: the cosmological horizon's surface gravity squared is the frozen point's norm.
