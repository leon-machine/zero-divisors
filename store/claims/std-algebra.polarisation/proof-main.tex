Symmetry is immediate from the definition of $B$, which is symmetric in $u$ and $v$. For the diagonal, $B(v, v) = \tfrac12\bigl(Q(2v) - 2Q(v)\bigr) = \tfrac12(4Q(v) - 2Q(v)) = Q(v)$. Conversely, if $B'$ is symmetric bilinear and $Q'(v) = B'(v, v)$, then $Q'(\lambda v) = \lambda^{2} Q'(v)$ and $Q'(u+v) - Q'(u) - Q'(v) = B'(u, v) + B'(v, u) = 2B'(u, v)$, so $Q'$ is a quadratic form with polar form $B'$. The two constructions are inverse to each other by these two computations. The coordinate formula is bilinearity of $B$ applied to $B(x, x)$.
