Each generator squares to a scalar: $I^{2} = \ip{I}{I} = ab$, $c_{4}^{2} = \ip{c_{4}}{c_{4}} = -\lambda$, $c_{5}^{2} = 0$; and they pairwise anticommute, since $I$ lies in the pair space and $c_{4}, c_{5}$ in two different rung planes, mutually orthogonal. A unital associative algebra generated by three pairwise-anticommuting elements with scalar squares is spanned by the $2^{3}$ ordered products of distinct generators, and the script computes that span's dimension as exactly eight on the module (the generic $\lambda = \tfrac17$, $a = 2$, $b = 3$; degenerate choices only lower it). The odd products are the four listed. Colour: the $\su(3)$ generator $v_{1}w_{2}$ has nonzero commutator with $I$ and with $Ic_{4}c_{5}$ (script), because each contains the mode-one operators $v_{1}, w_{1}$ once; $c_{4}, c_{5}$ commute with all of $\su(3)$ (script), and are vectors: $c_{5} = -w_{5}$ has hypercharge $-\tfrac12$, and $c_{4} = -w_{4} + \lambda v_{4}$ is a combination of the hypercharge $-\tfrac12$ and $+\tfrac12$ vectors $w_{4}, v_{4}$, not a hypercharge eigenvector for $\lambda \ne 0$ (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.transported-frozen-point}(i)). The script also computes the $\su(3)$-invariant part of the odd span as a nullspace, finds it to be exactly $\mathrm{span}\{c_{4}, c_{5}\}$ inside the span of the ten mode operators, and finds $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$ outside it and $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$'s mass form outside the span of that part's mass forms. Finally the script adjoins $\sum_{i}v_{i}\wedge w_{i}$ to the eight products and finds the rank rise to nine.
