## formal
Let $I$ be the frozen point, a vector of the first rung's hyperbolic plane inside the pair space $W \oplus \overline{W}$ (in mode terms $I = a\,v_{1} + b\,w_{1}$ with $Q(I) = ab$), and let $c_{4} = -w_{4} + \lambda v_{4}$ and $c_{5} = -w_{5}$ be the rung components of its two lifts (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.transported-frozen-point}, $\lambda = \Lambda/3$). Then the unital associative algebra generated by $\{I, c_{4}, c_{5}\}$ is eight-dimensional, spanned by $1, I, c_{4}, c_{5}, Ic_{4}, Ic_{5}, c_{4}c_{5}, Ic_{4}c_{5}$, its odd part is $\mathrm{span}\{I, c_{4}, c_{5}, Ic_{4}c_{5}\}$, and of these $c_{4}$ and $c_{5}$ are grade one (the $10$) while $I$ and $Ic_{4}c_{5}$ carry a pair-mode index and are not colour singlets. The algebra contains no colour-singlet doublet direction beyond the $10$, hence not $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$; and the pair-sector $u(1)$ bivector $\sum_{i}v_{i}\wedge w_{i}$, the factor $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$ needs, is not in it.

## technical
Let $I = a\,v_{1} + b\,w_{1}$ be the frozen point, with $Q(I) = ab$, and let $c_{4} = -w_{4} + \lambda v_{4}$ and $c_{5} = -w_{5}$ be the rung components of its two lifts, $\lambda = \Lambda/3$. The three generators square to scalars and pairwise anticommute, so the unital associative algebra they generate is eight-dimensional, spanned by $1, I, c_{4}, c_{5}, Ic_{4}, Ic_{5}, c_{4}c_{5}, Ic_{4}c_{5}$.

Its odd part is spanned by $I, c_{4}, c_{5}, Ic_{4}c_{5}$; only $c_{4}$ and $c_{5}$ are colour singlets, and they are grade-one vectors of the $10$. So the algebra contains no colour-singlet doublet direction beyond the $10$, in particular not $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$, and it does not contain the pair-sector bivector $\sum_{i} v_{i} \wedge w_{i}$ that $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$ needs.

## lay
The framework's frozen point and the copies of it carried to higher levels generate a small algebra of operators. This theorem lists that algebra completely and shows it contains only the ordinary Higgs-like directions, not the special direction needed for the factor of three between lepton and quark masses. The missing ingredient is tied to the charge that separates quarks from leptons, which the frozen point never touches.

## one-liner
The algebra generated by the frozen point and its two lifts is eight-dimensional and contains no colour-singlet doublet direction beyond the $10$, hence neither $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$ nor the pair-sector bivector.
