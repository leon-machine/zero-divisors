The components of $\Omega_{I}$ are $\varepsilon(I\wedge e_{k}\wedge e_{l})$, the Hodge dual of $I$, and its Pfaffian is a nonzero multiple of $\varepsilon(I\wedge I) = \ip{I}{I}$; so $\Omega_{I}$ is nondegenerate iff $\ip{I}{I} \neq 0$, which is the non-simplicity of $I$ (Lemma \claimref{mass.de-sitter-scale-frozen-point}(3)). For $A \in \mathfrak{gl}(\T)$ put $\beta_{A}(u,v) := \Omega_{I}(u, Av) = u^{\mathsf T}\Omega_{I}Av$; since $\Omega_{I}^{\mathsf T} = -\Omega_{I}$, $\Omega_{I}(Au,v) = -\beta_{A}(v,u)$, so $\Omega_{I}(Au,v) + \Omega_{I}(u,Av) = \beta_{A}(u,v) - \beta_{A}(v,u)$, and $A$ preserves $\Omega_{I}$ iff $\beta_{A}$, that is the matrix $\Omega_{I}A$, is symmetric. By invariance of the pairing, $\ip{I}{A\cdot(u\wedge v)} = -\ip{A\cdot I}{u\wedge v}$, so $\Omega_{I}(Au,v) + \Omega_{I}(u,Av) = -\Omega_{A\cdot I}(u,v)$: preserving $\Omega_{I}$ is the same as stabilising $I$, and the antisymmetric part of $\beta_{A}$ is $-\tfrac12\Omega_{A\cdot I}$. Symmetry of a $4\times 4$ matrix is six conditions, so the stabiliser is $10$-dimensional; it is $\mathfrak{sp}(\Omega_{I}) \cong \mathfrak{so}(I^{\perp})$, whose elements are traceless. Antisymmetry is ten conditions, leaving the $6$-dimensional space $\{A : \Omega_{I}A \text{ antisymmetric}\}$, on which $A \mapsto A\cdot I$ is injective (an antisymmetric $\beta_{A}$ equal to $-\tfrac12\Omega_{A\cdot I}$ vanishes iff $A\cdot I = 0$, and then $A = 0$ by nondegeneracy), hence a bijection onto $\Lambda^{2}\T$; $\mathfrak{gl}(\T)$ is the direct sum of the two parts. The identity lies in the antisymmetric part ($\beta_{1} = \Omega_{I}$) and has trace $4$, so the trace cuts that part to a $5$-dimensional complement of the stabiliser inside $\mathfrak{sl}(\T)$.

For $U \in I^{\perp}$, $A(U\wedge I)$ is not in the stabiliser, since $R_{U\wedge I}(I) = \ip{U}{I}I - \ip{I}{I}U = -\ip{I}{I}U \neq 0$, and $U \mapsto A(U\wedge I)$ is linear and injective on $I^{\perp}$. Its image lies in the antisymmetric part by equivariance: under the stabiliser $\mathfrak{so}(I^{\perp})$, $I^{\perp}$ is the $5$-dimensional vector representation and the stabiliser itself is the $10$-dimensional adjoint representation, irreducible and non-isomorphic, while $\mathfrak{gl}(\T) = \mathfrak{sp}(\Omega_{I}) \oplus \{\text{antisymmetric part}\}$ is a decomposition into $\mathfrak{so}(I^{\perp})$-submodules (both conditions are invariant under conjugation by the stabiliser); the map $U \mapsto A(U\wedge I)$ is $\mathfrak{so}(I^{\perp})$-equivariant, so by Schur's lemma its component in $\mathfrak{sp}(\Omega_{I})$ vanishes. Since the five images are traceless (all of $A(\cdot)$ is), they span the $5$-dimensional traceless antisymmetric part. The real-form statement is Fact \claimref{mass.imported-frozen-point-tractor-connection}'s stabiliser together with the count $15 - 10 = 5$; the action paper's Proposition 5.4 counts nine broken generators for the pair $(I, V)$, of which the five directions of $I^{\perp}$ are the $I$-half. The script confirms the dimensions $10$, $6$ and $5$ by exact rank at $\Lambda = 3$ and checks directly that the five matrices $A(e_{a}\wedge I_{\Lambda})$ are traceless, independent, and have $\Omega A$ antisymmetric.
