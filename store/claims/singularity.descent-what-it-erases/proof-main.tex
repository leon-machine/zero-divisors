$P^2 = v^*(v v^*)v = v^*(1 - v^* v)v = v^* v - (v^*)^2 v^2 = P$, using \eqref{eq:car} (the CAR of a Witt pair, \claimref{std-algebra.witt-basis-car}) twice and $v^2 = (v^*)^2 = 0$; then $1 - P = vv^*$ is \eqref{eq:car} itself. The four elements $vv^{*}, v, v^{*}, v^{*}v$ span the mode algebra: they are the images of the blades $1, v, v^{*}, vv^{*}$ under an invertible change of basis, and they form a set of matrix units, $vv^{*}\,v = v = v\,v^{*}v$, $v^{*}\,vv^{*} = v^{*} = v^{*}v\,v^{*}$, $v\,v^{*} = vv^{*}$, $v^{*}v = P$, all other products of two of them zero by $v^{2} = (v^{*})^{2} = 0$ (in the defining representation of \claimref{std-algebra.cl11-matrix} they are $E_{11}, E_{12}, E_{21}, E_{22}$). Hence $Pv = 0$, $vP = v$, $Pv^{*} = v^{*}$, $v^{*}P = 0$, so $P x P = d\,P$, and likewise $(1{-}P)\,x\,(1{-}P) = vv^{*}\,x\,vv^{*} = a\,vv^{*}$, which is the displayed action of $E$.
