## formal
The raw representation content matches the fermion table entry by entry

## technical
Refuted. The corrected statement: The disagreements are composites; generators, not states, are the content, and the lepton lies one level higher, under the declared hypothesis of slot statistics.

## lay
A first guess was that the states the composite can occupy line up one for one with the particles of a Standard Model generation. They do not, but the mismatches turn out to be composite, multi-particle states rather than elementary ones. What matches is the set of building blocks, not the full list of states, and the lepton appears one level further up, under the declared slot-statistics hypothesis.
