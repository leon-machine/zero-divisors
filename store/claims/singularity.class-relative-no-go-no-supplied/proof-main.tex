By Fact \claimref{sheaf.function-linearity-tame-constructions} and Lemma \claimref{singularity.function-linear-endomorphisms-are-bundle-maps}, each supplied $a$ is a bundle map, and by Proposition \claimref{singularity.bundle-maps-quantise-without-hopping} its $d\Gamma$ is a multiplication operator; products of the $\sigma(x)$ are joint-diagonal by construction. Both kinds commute with every $N_x$. The pairing clause is an exact matrix computation on two modes (Section \ref{sec:verification}): $v^{*}_x v^{*}_y + v_y v_x$ commutes with $\Gamma = \sigma_x\sigma_y$, has off-diagonal entries in the joint number basis, and fails to commute with $N_x$; it is not of the form $d\Gamma(a)$ for a bundle map $a$, since it changes the total occupation by two.
