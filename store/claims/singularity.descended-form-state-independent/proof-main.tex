By Theorem \claimref{singularity.descent-parity-average} the range of $E_k$ is the even subalgebra, which in the number basis is the diagonal. A diagonal density matrix on a two-dimensional space is $\operatorname{diag}(p_k, 1 - p_k)$, and $\beta_k \mapsto (1 + e^{\beta_k\omega_k})^{-1}$ is a bijection from $\R$ onto $(0,1)$, so every such matrix with $0 < p_k < 1$ is the stated Gibbs matrix for exactly one $\beta_k$; the two boundary values are the limits $\beta_k = \pm\infty$.
