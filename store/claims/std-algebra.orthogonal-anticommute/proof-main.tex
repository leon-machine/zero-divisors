By \claimref{std-algebra.vector-squares}, $uv + vu = 2B(u, v)\,1$. Since $1 \ne 0$ in $\Cl(V, Q)$ by \claimref{std-algebra.blade-basis}, $uv + vu = 0$ exactly when $B(u, v) = 0$. For basis blades, move the generators of $e_J$ leftwards one at a time past those of $e_I$; each transposition of distinct (orthogonal) generators contributes $-1$, the number of transpositions is the number of pairs $i \in I$, $j \in J$ with $i > j$, and each repeated generator contracts to $Q(e_i)$.
