The Cauchy decomposition (Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-cauchy-decomposition-exterior-power}) degree by degree with $A = \C^{2}$, $B = \C^{3}$, restricted to $SU(2) \times SU(3)$, under which the determinant characters are trivial. $\Lambda^{1}(A \otimes B) = A \otimes B = (2,3)$. $\Lambda^{2}(A \otimes B) = S^{2}A \otimes \Lambda^{2}B \,\oplus\, \Lambda^{2}A \otimes S^{2}B = (3, \bar{3}) \oplus (1, 6)$, dimension $9 + 6 = 15 = \binom{6}{2}$, using $\Lambda^{2}\C^{3} \cong \bar{3}$ and $\Lambda^{2}\C^{2} \cong 1$. $\Lambda^{3}(A \otimes B) = S^{3}A \otimes \Lambda^{3}B \,\oplus\, S_{(2,1)}A \otimes S_{(2,1)}B = (4, 1) \oplus (2, 8)$, dimension $4 + 2\cdot 8 = 20 = \binom{6}{3}$; the third Cauchy term $\Lambda^{3}A \otimes S^{3}B$ vanishes because $(1,1,1)$ has three parts and $\dim A = 2$, and $S_{(2,1)}\C^{2} \cong \C^{2}$, $S_{(2,1)}\C^{3} \cong 8$ by the Fact. Degrees $4$ through $6$: $\Lambda^{6-k}(A \otimes B) \cong (\Lambda^{k}(A \otimes B))^{*} \otimes \det$ by the last clause of the Fact, and the dual of $(3, \bar{3}) \oplus (1, 6)$ is $(3, 3) \oplus (1, \bar{6})$, of $(2, 3)$ is $(2, \bar{3})$ (the $2$ being self-dual), as displayed. The case of a single conformal spinor is the exterior algebra of $\C^{3}$ directly (Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-pair-systems-two-chirality-halves}), with $\Lambda^{2}\C^{3} \cong \bar{3}$. The abelian grading is the pair number: the scalar $e^{I_{4}\theta}I_{3}$ of the pair slot ($I_{3}$ the identity) acts on $\Lambda^{k}$ by $e^{I_{4}k\theta}$. The script confirms the graded dimensions ($1, 6, 15, 20, 15, 6, 1$; total $64$), the two $\binom{6}{k}$ splits, and the degree-two and degree-three Cauchy identities as identities of symmetric functions at rational diagonal instances; the identification of the Schur functors with the named irreducibles is the Fact's, not the script's.
