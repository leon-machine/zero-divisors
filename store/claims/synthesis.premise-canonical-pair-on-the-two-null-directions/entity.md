## formal
\emph{Premise (TT), a canonical pair on the two free directions at null momentum, at first order.} Conventions of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.first-order-gravitational-field-has-two-free-directions-at-null-momentum-and-four-constraints}: $\Cl(1,3)$, the gravitational gauge field on flat spacetime (Definition \claimref{obj.synthesis.gtg-flat-spacetime-gauge-fields}) at first order, a symmetric map $h$ of the vectors, and the first-order Einstein map $\mathcal{G}_{k}$ at momentum $k$. By that theorem, at each nonzero null momentum, $Q(k) = 0$, the first-order equations leave exactly two directions free, the transverse trace-free ones $n_{+}$ and $n_{\times}$ (its clause (i)); four of the ten equations are constraints (clause (v)); and the curvatures of the two directions span one line over $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$ (clause (vi)). The premise is: \emph{at first order, at each nonzero null momentum, the two transverse trace-free directions that the first-order equations of the gravitational gauge field leave free carry a canonical pair on polynomials, one mode over $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$ per null momentum, as the stored photon modes do} (Proposition \claimref{field-theory.fock-space-maxwell-modes}(1), (2)). That is, on polynomials with coefficients in $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$ in one variable $z_{k}$ for each nonzero null momentum, $a_{k} = \partial/\partial z_{k}$ and $a_{k}^{*} = z_{k}$, with $[a_{k}, a_{k}^{*}] = 1$ and the form $\langle z^{n}, z^{n'}\rangle = n!\,\delta_{nn'}$; the mode is the line of the first-order curvature of $n_{+}$ over $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$.

\emph{Status and scope.} A postulate of the series from 24 November 2026, adopted by Leon's decision of that date: assumed, not derived. It has the status of the stored photon modes: a pair on polynomials, not an element of a floor of the tower (no floor holds a canonical pair, \claimref{field-theory.no-canonical-commutation-relations-tower}) and not derived from the tower. It is not one of the foundation postulates F1 to F4. Its scope is first order only: from that date the series posits states on the two free directions at first order and nothing more, that is, no quantised geometry beyond first order, no element of the tower and no self-coupling of the mode. It reverses the sentence of the conventions of 10 November 2026 that the series holds no quantum of the gravitational gauge field and posits none (conventions, section ``Gravity and the graviton'', Decision of 24 November 2026). Where stored text says graviton for a quantum of the gravitational field, it names the mode of this premise at first order.

\emph{Grounds.} (1) \emph{The requirement.} The series requires that no signal pass outside the light cone (conventions, the same section, Requirement of 18 November 2026). (2) \emph{A field with no variables of its own fails it, in the stored models.} Proved in an exact lattice scalar model: a field slaved to an operator source fails to commute with the source's later detection at spacelike events, and a free part with canonical commutators restores commutation there to first order (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.field-slaved-to-an-operator-source-does-not-commute-at-spacelike-separation}). Proved in an exact two-dimensional lattice Maxwell model with a Gauss constraint: the static slaved field, the Coulomb one, and the retarded slaved field fail, and neither the constraint nor the Coulomb dressing restores commutation (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.two-dimensional-lattice-maxwell-coulomb-slaved-field-fails-and-string-dressed-detection-commutes}). Proved in the lattice scalar model under that result's hypothesis (D): the failure is a signal of first order in the strength of a coupled device (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.with-a-coupled-device-the-slaved-field-failure-is-a-first-order-signal-and-a-survey-finds-no-stored-bound}). Proved, with the counts computed, in the stored lattice model of the first-order gravitational field itself, with its four constraints: the static slaved field, the Newtonian one, fails; the retarded slaved field fails at events outside the lattice's cone of the detection; and a coupled device then has a signal of first order, under the same hypothesis (D) (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.lattice-model-of-first-order-gravity-slaved-field-dressed-pair-and-the-dipole-obstruction}, clauses (5), (8), (9)). (3) \emph{Sufficiency in a model, at first order.} In that lattice model, with the field's own canonical variables, the detection of a pair of masses in two arrangements about one centre, dressed on the string between them, is an observable, commutes with the curvature at every event outside the lattice's cone, and a coupled device has no effect there at any order (its clauses (4), (8), (9)). This is sufficiency in a model and not a theorem about the continuum field: the model is a periodic cubic lattice with the symmetry of the cube, its own cone (one site a step, twice the speed of its wave), matter at rest with mass density only, and commuting constraints; and that a dressed detection commutes outside its cone once a dressing of compact support exists holds in any local canonical lattice model.

\emph{Limits.} (a) \emph{Not shown necessary.} Commutation at spacelike separation does not single out a canonical pair: for a coupling at a point with no constraint, any medium whose own elements commute with one another outside the cone, and with the matter factor and the unit $J$ of that model, gives it exactly (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.helicity-two-composites-of-light-ray-fermions-on-a-generator-and-off-the-null-plane}, clause (v), proved). The other way named of meeting the requirement is the gravitational field as the response of the stored quantum fields (the Reading of that theorem, argued; the list of ways is not shown complete). For that way the store holds no result: none shows that it meets the requirement with the gravitational constraints in place, and none shows that it fails. (b) \emph{One mass moved alone.} The Hamiltonian constraint of the first-order field is a double divergence, so one mass moved alone between two sites has no dressing by one Weyl operator of compact support and none with curvature confined to a box (the dipole obstruction: clauses (3), (3a) of the lattice model, proved). The obstruction is in the constraint alone, so it does not depend on whether the field has variables of its own (proved there) and, as the Readings of that model and of the composites theorem say, on what the free part is made of; the premise does not remove it. The experiment of Prediction \claimref{pred.gravity-entangles-standard-phase}, one mass on two paths, is of that type: the model gives it a local dressed detection only with its recoil partner included, which is argued and not modelled. (c) \emph{Not derived.} No derivation of the pair from the tower or from the stored fermions is held: on one null generator, with channel weights $\pm\tfrac12$, no fermion pair mode has helicity two (proved), and the lowest four-fermion modes of helicity two are argued not to be a free part of this kind (the same theorem, clause (i) and Reading). (d) \emph{First order only.} Beyond first order the field sources itself and the constraints do not commute; the premise says nothing there. (e) \emph{No state.} The premise posits the pair and its representation on polynomials. It does not select the state of the mode in any gravitational field, and in particular not near a hole. (f) \emph{Normalisation.} The normalisation of the free part of the first-order curvature and the assignment of helicities to its two terms are taken over from the photon in the Reading of the lattice model and are not computed in the store.

\emph{What it gives.} (1) With the premise, the series' account of how the requirement is met at first order is the one the stored lattice model exhibits: the field's own canonical variables on the two directions, with detections dressed by the field, for rearrangements of matter that keep the mass and the centre, within the limits (a) to (f). (2) The word graviton in stored text has a referent, the mode of this premise. (3) The baseline of Prediction \claimref{pred.dimmed-evaporation-bursts}: the ratio of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.dimmed-burst-time-rescaled-copy} is $D = (2 + g + o)/(1 + g)$, the option $g_{\mathrm{h}} = g_{\mathrm{s}} = g$ of clause (viii) of the first-order theorem, provided the state of the pair near a hole is the one regular on the future horizon, as for the other fields; the premise does not address that state. (4) The construction of the stored photon carries over to the two directions (argued in the Reading of the lattice model): the pair is not an element of a floor, the dressing is the exponential of the pair and lies in the completion of the polynomials and not among them, the residue of the first-order interaction on the cone is the positive form of two squares (clause (iv) of the first-order theorem), and in the gauge of a frame with the constraints imposed no indefinite form is needed, while a covariant gauge would need Premise \claimref{synthesis.krein-form-premise-indefinite-inner-product-for-gauge-fields} extended to symmetric maps, which is not stored.

\emph{What it does not change, and does not give.} The registered entangling phase of Prediction \claimref{pred.gravity-entangles-standard-phase} does not depend on it and is unchanged. The first-order processes at non-null momentum, the plasma's production of a gravitationally coupled species among them, need no state of the field (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.gravitational-pair-production-from-the-operator-source-at-first-order}, under its hypothesis (OS)) and do not depend on it. Every proved clause of the results cited above holds without it. It gives no derivation of the field equation: the argument from a mode of helicity two with states to a coupling to the whole conserved stress, cited from memory in the note of the lattice model with its references still to be checked, becomes a route on open problem op.einstein-from-the-field-theory and is not a stored result.

\emph{What would refute or retire it.} (a) \emph{Retired as a postulate} by a stored result that meets the requirement at first order, the gravitational constraints included, with no states on the two directions (for example the field as the response of the stored quantum fields, with the dressing carried by a medium that has no canonical variables); or by a derivation of the pair from the tower or from the stored content, which would make it a theorem. (b) \emph{Refuted} by a conflict with a stored theorem: a proof that a canonical pair on the two directions, with the status of the photon's modes, contradicts a result of the store. None is known; the stored statistics result is not in conflict, the pair being on the same footing as the photon's (argued). (c) \emph{A failure beyond first order.} The premise asserts nothing beyond first order, so no second-order statement contradicts its letter; a proof that the first-order pair is the first order of no account consistent with the series' field equations would leave it without use and retire it. (d) \emph{Observation.} No accessible observation distinguishes it from the standard account, which has the same pair at first order. The two burst ratios of clause (viii) of the first-order theorem differ by $g/(1 + g)$, about $0.02$ at the value $g = 2/98$ quoted there, and the prediction's kill condition, an undimmed burst, does not distinguish them; the entangling phase does not depend on the premise. It is therefore not a prediction and carries no kill condition.

## technical
The series treats gravity as the gauge field of Gauge Theory Gravity on flat spacetime. At first order that field is fixed by its source at every momentum that is not null; at a nonzero null momentum two directions, the transverse trace-free ones, are left free, and they form one mode over $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$, of helicity two. This premise says that, at first order, those two directions carry a canonical pair on polynomials, one mode per null momentum, exactly as the stored photon modes do: annihilation and creation are differentiation and multiplication on polynomials with coefficients in $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$. It was adopted by Leon's decision of 24 November 2026 and is assumed, not derived. It has the photon's status: the pair is not an element of a floor of the tower and is not derived from the tower. Its scope is first order only: no quantised geometry beyond first order, no element of the tower, no self-coupling. It reverses the earlier sentence of the conventions that the series holds no quantum of the gravitational gauge field, and the word graviton in stored text now names this mode.

Grounds. The series requires that no signal pass outside the light cone. In three stored exact models (a lattice scalar field, also with a coupled device; a two-dimensional lattice Maxwell field with its constraint; a lattice model of the first-order gravitational field with its four constraints) a field with no variables of its own fails that. In the lattice model of the first-order field, the field's own canonical variables with a detection dressed by the field meet it for a pair of masses rearranged about one centre: sufficiency in a model, at first order, on a lattice with its own cone.

Limits. It is not shown necessary: without a constraint any medium with causal commutators gives commutation, and the other way named, the gravitational field as the response of the stored quantum fields, has no stored result for or against it. One mass moved alone has no localised dressing (the dipole obstruction), whatever the free part is made of, so the premise does not give a local detection for a single mass on two paths without its recoil partner. The pair is not derived from the tower or from the stored fermions. The premise selects no state and fixes no normalisation.

Consequences. The burst ratio keeps the gravitational term on both sides, $D = (2 + g + o)/(1 + g)$, provided the state of the pair near a hole is the one regular on the future horizon, which the premise does not address. The registered entangling phase and the first-order processes at non-null momentum do not depend on it.

It would be retired by a stored result meeting the requirement at first order with no states on the two directions, or by a derivation of the pair; refuted by a conflict with a stored theorem; left without use by a proof that it is the first order of no consistent account. No accessible observation distinguishes it from the standard account, so it is not a prediction.

Ledger: transport-absent. This is the canonical pair of the free first-order gravitational field of standard physics; the series contributes the decision to posit it, with its scope.

## lay
The series describes gravity as a field on flat spacetime. For weak fields almost all of that field is tied to matter: say where the matter is and the field follows. The exception is two components, the two polarisations of a gravitational wave, which matter does not pin down.

This is an assumption about those two components. It says that, for weak fields, they are quantum variables in their own right, in exactly the way light is in the series: each wave of a given direction and frequency can hold zero, one, two or more quanta. It was adopted by a decision on 24 November 2026. It is assumed, not proved, and it is not built from the series' basic structure, just as the quanta of light are not.

Why assume it. The series requires that nothing signal faster than light. In three exact models, a field that only repeats what its source did breaks that rule. In a model of weak gravity, giving the two components variables of their own, together with a careful way of attaching field to matter, keeps the rule.

What it does not say. It does not show that this is the only way to keep the rule. It says nothing about strong gravity or about a quantum geometry of space and time. It does not remove a known obstacle: a single mass moved alone cannot be given a localised attached field, because nothing moves without something else recoiling. And no experiment within reach could tell this assumption from the standard account of weak quantum gravity, which makes the same one.

Before this decision the series said it held no quantum of gravity at all. It now holds this much and no more.
