## formal
The wall twist is $N^{*}\Sigma \cong (L^{*})^{\otimes 2}$

## technical
Refuted. The corrected statement: The twist is $N^{*}\Sigma \cong L^{\otimes 2}$: the dying line is the square root of the conormal.

## lay
An earlier statement said that the conormal bundle of a wall is the square of the dual of the dying line. That was wrong: it is the square of the dying line itself, so the dying line is a square root of the conormal bundle. The residue computation at the black hole is what refuted it.
