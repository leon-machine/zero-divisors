Compute $M$ in the completion: by the model identity \eqref{eq:nullity}, $M$ is the Gram matrix of the lifted vectors $X(p_i) \in \mathbb{V} \oplus H$, so its rank and signature are those of the ambient form restricted to their span, and it suffices to show the lifts span the whole $(n{+}2)$-dimensional space, of signature $(p{+}1, q{+}1)$. The span contains $\nO = X(0)$; it contains $X(2e_1) - 2X(e_1) + X(0) = (2 - 2)e_1 + (2 - 1)Q(e_1)\nInf = Q(e_1)\,\nInf$ with $Q(e_1) = \pm 1 \neq 0$, hence $\nInf$; hence each $e_i = X(e_i) - X(0) - \tfrac12 Q(e_i)\nInf$. A spanning set of a nondegenerate space has Gram matrix congruent to the form (\claimref{std-algebra.sylvester-inertia}), giving rank $n+2$ and the stated signature.
