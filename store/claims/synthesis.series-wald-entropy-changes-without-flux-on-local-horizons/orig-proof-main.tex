Read in Gauge Theory Gravity, the metric below is the derived metric of the gravitational field, the Riemann tensor and the Gauss equation are read from its field strength, and covariant derivatives are $D$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}, clauses (i), (ii) and (iv)). \emph{Setting.} By Theorem \claimref{synthesis.euler-term-horizon-entropy-shift}(i) the series' Lagrangian is $(R - 2\Lambda + \alpha E_{4})/16\pi$ with $\alpha = -3/(4\Lambda)$; in four dimensions $E_{4}$ is topological, so its field equations are those of Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-macdowell-mansouri-theorem-stelle-west} torsion-free, $G_{ab} + \Lambda g_{ab} = 0$ in vacuum, and Schwarzschild-de Sitter is a solution. The check confirms $\mathrm{Ric} = \Lambda g$ at every test point from the metric. Under (W$_{\mathrm{loc}}$) the entropy of a cut is the Jacobson-Myers functional of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-wald-noether-charge-entropy}(c), with density $s = \tfrac14(1 + 2\alpha R_{\Sigma})$.

\emph{(i)} For a spacelike two-surface with null normals $k, n$, $k\cdot n = -1$, and null second fundamental forms $\chi^{(k)}, \chi^{(n)}$, the Gauss equation (standard, cited from memory) gives $R_{\Sigma} = h^{ac}h^{bd}R_{abcd} + Q(\chi^{(k)}, \chi^{(n)})$, where $Q$ is a sum of terms each bilinear in one $\chi^{(k)}$ and one $\chi^{(n)}$ (explicitly $2\theta_{k}\theta_{n} - 2\chi^{(k)}_{AB}\chi^{(n)AB}$ up to the sign convention for $n$). Where $\chi^{(k)} = 0$, $R_{\Sigma} = h^{ac}h^{bd}R_{abcd} = 2K$, with $K$ the sectional curvature of the tangent plane. Then $s = \tfrac14(1 + 4\alpha K) = \tfrac14(1 - 3K/\Lambda) = -\tfrac{3}{4\Lambda}(K - \Lambda/3)$, and $K - \Lambda/3 = F(e_{2}, e_{3}, e_{2}, e_{3})$ for $F_{abcd} = R_{abcd} - \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}(g_{ac}g_{bd} - g_{ad}g_{bc})$, the curvature of the Cartan connection whose square is the series' action (it vanishes on the de Sitter model, Theorem \claimref{synthesis.euler-term-horizon-entropy-shift}(i)). On de Sitter space $R_{abcd} = \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}(g_{ac}g_{bd} - g_{ad}g_{bc})$, so every spacelike plane has $K = \Lambda/3$ and $s = 0$; the check computes the de Sitter curvature from the metric and the sectional curvatures of four planes, two of them generic, with GA wedge products. On the bifurcation sphere of a Schwarzschild-de Sitter hole both $\chi$ vanish (Killing horizon) and $K = 2M/r_{b}^{3} + \Lambda/3$ (from (ii) below), so $\int s\,dA = 4\pi r_{b}^{2}\cdot(-3M/(2\Lambda r_{b}^{3})) = -6\pi M/(\Lambda r_{b})$, and $f(r_{b}) = 0$, i.e. $6M/r_{b} = 3 - \Lambda r_{b}^{2}$, turns this into $\pi r_{b}^{2} - 3\pi/\Lambda = A/4 - 3\pi\chi/(2\Lambda)$ with $\chi = 2$, the value of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.euler-term-horizon-entropy-shift}(ii). The check confirms this at three rational horizons, with $M$ solved from $f(r_{b}) = 0$.

\emph{(ii) Curvature along radial generators.} In coordinates $(t, r, z = \cos\theta, \phi)$ the metric $-f\,dt^{2} + dr^{2}/f + r^{2}dz^{2}/(1 - z^{2}) + r^{2}(1 - z^{2})\,d\phi^{2}$ is rational. The check expands it in exact truncated Taylor jets about rational points of the static region, forms the Christoffel symbols and the Riemann tensor, and finds: $\mathrm{Ric} = \Lambda g$; in the static orthonormal frame the only components are the pair components $R_{\hat t\hat r\hat t\hat r} = f''/2$, $R_{\hat t\hat A\hat t\hat A} = f'/2r$, $R_{\hat r\hat A\hat r\hat A} = -f'/2r$, $R_{\hat\theta\hat\phi\hat\theta\hat\phi} = (1 - f)/r^{2} = 2M/r^{3} + \Lambda/3$ ($A = \theta, \phi$), with $\partial_{r}(2M/r^{3} + \Lambda/3) = -6M/r^{4}$ read from the jets. For $k_{\pm} = f^{-1}\partial_{t} \pm \partial_{r} = f^{-1/2}(e_{\hat t} \pm e_{\hat r})$: $R(k, A, k, B) = f^{-1}(R_{\hat t\hat A\hat t\hat B} + R_{\hat r\hat A\hat r\hat B} \pm 2R_{\hat t\hat A\hat r\hat B}) = f^{-1}(f'/2r - f'/2r)\delta_{AB} = 0$ for all $r$ (so $k_{\pm}$ is a principal null direction), and $R(k, A, B, C) = 0$ since every component with an odd number of indices in $\{t, r\}$ vanishes. The check confirms both at the test points and at points displaced along the generators, that $k_{\pm}$ is null and affinely geodesic ($k^{a}\partial_{a}k^{b} + \Gamma^{b}_{ac}k^{a}k^{c} = 0$, $dr/d\lambda = \pm 1$), and that the static transverse frame $E_{\theta} = \partial_{z}/r$, $E_{\phi} = \partial_{\phi}/r$ (at $z = 0$) is parallel along $k_{\pm}$.

\emph{Every admissible cut gives the same data on $\gamma$.} Let $\Sigma$ be a cut through $p$ orthogonal to $k$ with $\chi^{(k)}(p) = 0$. Its tangent plane at $p$ lies in $k^{\perp} = \mathrm{span}(k, E_{\theta}, E_{\phi})$ and is spacelike, so it is $\mathrm{span}(E_{\theta} + a k, E_{\phi} + b k)$ for some $a, b$. Propagate $\Sigma$ along its null generators with affine parameter $\lambda$ (any normalisation of $k$ along $\Sigma$, fixed to $k_{\pm}$ at $p$). By the Raychaudhuri and Sachs equations of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-raychaudhuri-null-congruence}, along the generator $\gamma$ through $p$ the second fundamental form obeys $d\chi_{AB}/d\lambda = -\chi_{AC}\chi^{C}{}_{B} - R(k, A, k, B)$ (zero twist); with $\chi(0) = 0$ and $R(k, A, k, B) = 0$ along $\gamma$, uniqueness for this ordinary differential equation gives $\chi^{(k)} = 0$ on all of $\gamma$, so the expansion and shear vanish there and the area element along $\gamma$ is constant. A connecting vector $Z$ of the generators obeys $\nabla_{k}Z = \nabla_{Z}k = \chi^{(k)}(Z) + \omega(Z)k$, with $\omega$ the rotation one-form of the slicing; along $\gamma$ this is $\omega(Z)k$, so the tangent plane of $\Sigma_{\lambda}$ at $\gamma(\lambda)$ is the parallel transport of the plane at $p$ tilted further along $k$. Since $R(k, A, B, C) = 0$ and $R(k, A, k, B) = 0$, the sectional curvature of $\mathrm{span}(E_{\theta} + a k, E_{\phi} + b k)$ equals that of $\mathrm{span}(E_{\theta}, E_{\phi})$ for all $a, b$ (the check confirms this with GA wedge products at three tilts each way); and since $E_{\theta}, E_{\phi}$ are parallel, the latter is $R_{\hat\theta\hat\phi\hat\theta\hat\phi}(r(\lambda)) = 2M/r(\lambda)^{3} + \Lambda/3$ with $r(\lambda) = r_{0} \pm \lambda$. By (i), with $\chi^{(k)} = 0$ on $\gamma$, the density on $\gamma$ is $s(\lambda) = -\tfrac{3}{4\Lambda}\cdot 2M/r(\lambda)^{3} = -3M/(2\Lambda r(\lambda)^{3})$, independent of the cut, of $a, b$, of $\omega$ and of $\chi^{(n)}$.

\emph{The change.} For a pencil of generators about $\gamma$ with cross-section area $a_{0}$ at $\lambda = 0$, $S_{\lambda} = \int s\,dA$ over the cross-section at $\lambda$. As $a_{0} \to 0$ with $\lambda$ fixed, $S_{\lambda}/a_{0} \to s(\lambda)\cdot\exp\int_{0}^{\lambda}\theta\,d\lambda' = s(\lambda)$ by continuity of $s$ and of the expansion. This is the thin-pencil limit of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.einstein-equations-from-c-loc-and-clausius}(H2). So per unit cross-section area $\Delta S = s(0) - s(\lambda_{0}) = -\tfrac{3M}{2\Lambda}(r_{0}^{-3} - (r_{0} \pm \lambda_{0})^{-3})$. With $\lambda_{0} < 0$: outgoing, $r(\lambda_{0}) < r_{0}$ and $\Delta S > 0$; ingoing, $r(\lambda_{0}) > r_{0}$ and $\Delta S < 0$. Expanding, $ds/d\lambda = \alpha\,dK/d\lambda = (-3/4\Lambda)(\mp 6M/r_{0}^{4}) = \pm 9M/(2\Lambda r_{0}^{4})$. The area change is zero (constant area element along $\gamma$), and $\delta Q = \int T_{ab}\chi^{a}k^{b} = 0$ in vacuum. The check evaluates $\Delta S$ against the closed form at three points and two values of $\lambda_{0}$, the signs, and the rate from the jets.

\emph{(iii)} Refutation condition (a) of Premise \claimref{synthesis.c-loc-local-horizon-premise} asks for a class of local horizons whose entropy change is not a universal constant times the area change at leading order. Under (W$_{\mathrm{loc}}$) the radial local horizons of a Schwarzschild-de Sitter hole have $\delta A = 0$ and $\Delta S \neq 0$ at first order, so no $\eta$ works; and the change depends on the curvature at the point through $M/r^{4}$. The Clausius relation (H1) of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.einstein-equations-from-c-loc-and-clausius} requires $\delta Q = T_{\chi}\,\delta S$ at leading order; here $\delta Q = 0$ and $T_{\chi}\Delta S = (\kappa/2\pi)(\pm 9M/(2\Lambda r_{0}^{4}))|\lambda_{0}| + O(\lambda_{0}^{2})$, which is that theorem's step (6) defect for the integrated reading with $k\cdot\nabla s \neq 0$, so (H1) fails at first order. (Under the differences reading of (H1) the density is to be constant; the Jacobson-Myers functional is an integrated functional whose density varies, so it is not of that form either.) An entropy-production reading $dS = \delta Q/T + d_{i}S$, $d_{i}S \ge 0$ (Eling, Guedens and Jacobson, cited from memory) requires $\Delta S \ge 0$ when $\delta Q = 0$, which the ingoing horizon violates. Robustness: a functional differing from the Jacobson-Myers one by terms that vanish where $\chi^{(k)} = 0$ (the extrinsic terms by which the Wald and Jacobson-Myers forms differ off stationary horizons, the Jacobson-Kang-Myers ambiguity, cited from memory) takes the same values on $\gamma$; one differing by functions of the Ricci tensor differs on $\gamma$ by a constant, since $\mathrm{Ric} = \Lambda g$. In either case $\Delta S$ is unchanged. The area part $\tfrac14\,dA$ alone gives $\Delta S = 0 = \delta Q$, consistent with (H1). Scope: the Euler part of the entropy of a cut changes by a total divergence on the cut (the edge term of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.euler-term-horizon-entropy-shift}(ii)); per unit area at $p$ it is the value of that divergence, and over a closed cut tiled by patches the changes cancel. So the failure shown is of the Clausius relation on each open patch, which is how (H1) and (H2) impose it, with the entropy of a patch the integral of the density over the patch. The check reads the curvature from the metric at five points along each generator, integrates the Sachs equation for $\chi^{(k)}$ and the heat $\int(-\lambda)\,8\pi T(k,k)\,d\lambda$ with $8\pi T = \mathrm{Ric} - \Lambda g$ in exact steps, finds both zero, and evaluates $\Delta S$ from the curvature at the endpoints. The general first-order rate on any local patch is filed alongside, synthesis.euler-entropy-first-order-rate-on-local-patches; on radial generators it reduces to the gradient term computed here.

\emph{Conclusion.} (W$_{\mathrm{loc}}$) contradicts (Loc) and (H1). Given (C$_{\mathrm{loc}}$), the entropy functional on local horizons excludes the Euler term. On closed horizons, Theorem \claimref{synthesis.sds-clausius-fixes-area-constant}(v) already excludes its constant shift under the zero-mass normalisation or nonnegativity. Without the shift the de Sitter entropy is $\tfrac14\cdot 4\pi\cdot 3/\Lambda = 3\pi/\Lambda$, and the nucleation exponent is $-1$, the Einstein-Hilbert values in Theorem \claimref{synthesis.euler-term-horizon-entropy-shift}(iii). The Euler term remains in the action, where it does not change the field equations.
