Constancy: the coefficients $\sigma_{3}(r)$ and $\cot\theta/r$ of Lemma \claimref{interior.interior-connection-exactly}'s $\Omega(e_{3})$ do not depend on $\varphi$, and the unit tangent along the line is $e_{3}$ throughout, so $G(s)$ is constant and the linear equation is solved by the exponential. The series: $\tau\sigma_{3} = \tau\,\partial_{\tau}\log r = \tau\sqrt{A}/r$ with $A = 1/r - 1$; writing $\tau = \tfrac23 r^{3/2} S(r)$ with $S(r) = \tfrac32 r^{-3/2}\int_{0}^{r} u^{1/2}(1-u)^{-1/2}\,du = \sum_{k} \tfrac{3}{2}\binom{-1/2}{k}(-1)^{k}\frac{r^{k}}{k + 3/2}$, one has $\tau\sigma_{3} = \tfrac23 S(r)\sqrt{1 - r}$ and $\varepsilon = \tfrac23 \cot\theta\, r^{1/2} S(r)$; the script computes $S = 1 + \tfrac{3}{10} r + \tfrac{9}{56} r^{2} + \tfrac{5}{48} r^{3} + \cdots$ and the product exactly to order eight, reproducing Lemma \claimref{interior.kasner-form-interior}'s $\tfrac{3}{10}$ and Lemma \claimref{interior.exact-parallelism-convergence-rate}'s leading $-\tfrac{2}{15}$ (there written in $R = (3\tau/2)^{2/3} = r + O(r^{2})$), with every printed coefficient of $\delta$ negative. The exponential bound is the identity $e^{X} - e^{Y} = \int_{0}^{1} e^{(1-t)X}(X - Y)e^{tY}\,dt$ (the case of constant generators of the proof of Theorem \claimref{interior.transport-level-convergence-along-tangential-curves}) with $\|X\|, \|Y\| \le \lambda(\tfrac23 + |\delta| + |\varepsilon|) \le \lambda(1 + |\delta| + |\varepsilon|)$, since $\|B_3\| = \|R_{23}\| = 1$. Rates: $r = (3\tau/2)^{2/3}(1 + O(\tau^{2/3}))$ by Lemma \claimref{interior.kasner-form-interior}, so $r = O(\tau^{2/3})$ and $r^{1/2} = O(\tau^{1/3})$. The symmetry statement: the Schwarzschild interior is invariant under $SO(3)$ acting on $S^{2}$ and under $t$-translation, the parallel frame of Lemma \claimref{interior.exact-parallelism-convergence-rate} is carried to itself up to the constant patch rotation, and the boundary data, being limits of invariant quantities in that frame, are invariant functions on $\mathcal{B}$, hence constant. The script also carries one floating-point CONTROL check, marked as such and printing its intermediate ratios, which enters no exact check: the operator-norm distance halves when $r$ is quartered at three latitudes, and a fixed coordinate segment $\Delta\varphi = 0.1$ diverges, confirming the disclaimer rather than contradicting it.
