## formal
\emph{Premise ($\epsilon$), the matter asymmetry as a registered input.} Let $\epsilon$ be the asymmetry per mode of the oriented towers, the strength at which the flag acts on an oriented tower's modes, equal in this tower and the mirror (Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.mirror-tower-copy-one}(v)), so that the asymmetry stored in an oriented tower is the charge $Q = \epsilon N$ (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.stored-asymmetry-charge-not-number}(i)); the asymmetry lives only in this tower and the mirror (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.asymmetry-arrow-counted-fifteenth-refutation}(iii)). The premise: \emph{$\epsilon$ is a free number of the series}, inherited, not generated: it is not computed from the transport structure, from another premise of the register, or from a dynamics of the series, and it is not a function of $N$ (Premise \claimref{synthesis.number-n-registered-free-input}) or of $\xi G$ (Premise \claimref{synthesis.rung-unit-second-free-number}). It is initial data of the descent, the stance of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.asymmetry-arrow-counted-fifteenth-refutation}(iv), registered here as a premise. The charge is named: $Q = \epsilon N$ is the total $B - L$ of the tower's matter, not its baryon number (the paragraph on the naming below). Its value is constrained by the record and is not read off it directly: the measured baryon density, $\Omega_{b}h^{2} = 0.02237 \pm 0.00015$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-planck-2018-lcdm-parameters}), fixes this tower's baryons per unit entropy, the $8.7 \times 10^{-11}$ that the stored results use, and the free datum is the $B - L$ per unit entropy, related to that figure through the equilibrium coefficient of the anomalous weak process, which is imported and applies conditionally (the paragraph on the value below).

\emph{The naming and the value (restated 2026-11-12).} Three objects have been called the charge in the stored statements, and they are different: the residue label $r$ of a tower, which is what the superselection rule is about (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.superselected-hence-dark-exactly-stable}, step 1) and which is constant on a tower's sector; the total $B - L$ of the tower's matter; and its baryon number $n_{B}$, which is what the measured baryon density reads. By the maintainer's decision of 12 November 2026 the inherited charge $Q = \epsilon N$ is the second. The naming is a decision and is not derived; the reason for it is that baryon number is not conserved by the stored content: for one family the $24$ slot-pair generators change $n_{B}$ and carry $B - L$, the mixed weak coefficient is $2$ on $n_{B}$ and $0$ on $B - L$, and the only stored term that changes $B - L$ is the Majorana element (\claimref{synthesis.abelian-charges-of-the-sixteen-and-the-terms-that-break-them}(iii) to (v); \claimref{synthesis.baryon-number-centraliser-in-the-five-mode-algebra}(iii), (iv)).

What then happens to the value (restated 2026-11-16). The record fixes $n_{B}/s = 8.7 \times 10^{-11}$ in this tower. The registered datum is $(B - L)/s$, and the two are related only conditionally: \emph{if} the anomalous weak process (which changes $B + L$ and keeps $B - L$; Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-anomalous-weak-process-changes-b-plus-l-and-keeps-b-minus-l}) acted and reached equilibrium after the descent, then $n_{B} = c\,(B - L)$ with $c$ its equilibrium coefficient, taken where the process freezes out (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-equilibrium-baryon-number-as-a-fraction-of-b-minus-l}), and $(B - L)/s = c^{-1} \times 8.7 \times 10^{-11}$. By the maintainer's decision of 16 November 2026 the process, the coefficient and the freeze-out are imported Facts, and the premise is restated to one light doublet. The coefficient depends on the number of doublets light at the freeze-out and on the Higgs expectation value there. On the level branch of the rung field the second doublet lies at or above about $2.5$ TeV, far above the freeze-out temperature $T_{*} = 131.7 \pm 2.3$ GeV (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-lattice-crossover-and-freeze-out-of-the-anomalous-weak-process}), so one doublet is light at the freeze-out: this is argued, not proved (\claimref{synthesis.conditional-inheritance-of-b-minus-l-and-the-conversion-coefficient-as-a-gram-ratio}(vii)). The two-doublet value $\tfrac{8}{23}$, which the bidoublet of \claimref{standard-model.bidoublet-complex} would give if both of its doublets were in the plasma at the freeze-out, is not the working-branch value. With one doublet $c$ has two ends: $\tfrac{28}{79}$, the symmetric-phase end, and $\tfrac{12}{37}$, the broken-phase end, the limit of a Higgs expectation value large against the temperature (the second Fact, clauses (b), (c)). The freeze-out lies below the crossover at $159 \pm 1$ GeV (the third Fact), so $c$ lies between the two ends, along the interpolation of the second Fact (clause (a)); where between them needs the ratio of the Higgs expectation value to the temperature at the freeze-out, which is not stored. Hence
\[
\tfrac{79}{28} \times 8.7 \times 10^{-11} = 2.45 \times 10^{-10} \;\le\; (B - L)/s \;\le\; \tfrac{37}{12} \times 8.7 \times 10^{-11} = 2.68 \times 10^{-10}
\]
(arithmetic), the lower figure at the symmetric-phase end and the upper at the broken-phase end. The check of Computation \claimref{dark-tower-matter.mirrors-composition} uses $\tfrac{28}{79}$ and so sits at the symmetric-phase end of the bracket, its edge and not its centre; that check is not changed. The bracket is conditional: on the three imported Facts; on one light doublet, which is argued; and on the equilibrium after the descent, which is hypothesis (T) of the cited theorem (a thermal state of the descent reheated above the freeze-out) and is not shown. If the process did not reach equilibrium after the descent, $n_{B}$ is not a function of the datum alone and the record does not fix $(B - L)/s$ through $c$; that case is not excluded by anything stored. That $B - L$ is unchanged between the descent and today is the conditional inheritance of the same theorem (clause (iii)), proved as algebra under its named hypotheses: Premise \claimref{neutrino.r-descent-reheats-below-lower-heavy}, no washout through virtual heavy singlets (which that Premise does not imply), no injection, and no gravitational channel. The washout and the gravitational channel are hypotheses that are not stored, and no rate is computed for either; the completeness of the theorem is over bilinear terms of one family's mode algebra and the three four-forms, and no further.

\emph{Scope.} The premise registers the value. Its equality across this tower and the mirror is Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.mirror-tower-copy-one}(v), not this premise (the former Premise P4, democracy of the asymmetry over the oriented towers, is retired as a postulate: nothing stored used it beyond the mirror). The first route of that clause takes the stance registered here, that $\epsilon$ is a datum of the descent and not of a tower's state, as a stated hypothesis; no assumes edge is drawn for it. That it is one number across space, rather than a field, agrees with Theorem \claimref{synthesis.adiabatic-deposit-modulation-is-a-time-shift}: under the charge reading and the mirror's deposit law the charge per unit entropy must be uniform up to the isocurvature the record allows. It is not the deposit energy per unit charge $\varepsilon$ of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.mirror-temperature-measures-deposit-under-r}, which this premise does not register.

\emph{What it is for.} The charge reading of the stored asymmetry, with the mirror's equal charge $B - L$, takes $\epsilon$ as initial data of the descent (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.stored-asymmetry-charge-not-number}); the dark-to-baryon count uses only its equality across the reversal orbit, not its provenance; on that stance the unexplained numbers of the event are $N$ and $\epsilon$, with the orientation bit a third datum (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.asymmetry-arrow-counted-fifteenth-refutation}(iv)). Registering it makes it a counted input of the register, exchanged for the baryon density of the standard cosmological model on the condition of the paragraph on the naming (the exchange passes through the coefficient $c$). No assumes edge to it is drawn here.

\emph{What it does not give.} It does not explain the value or its sign; it does not say how the descent deposits the charge, nor with what energy per unit charge (the open entries of the pre-descent theory); and it does not decide the trichotomy's classification of $\epsilon$, whose kinematic clause runs for it at observation level and whose dynamical clause does not (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-trichotomy-descents-data}).

\emph{Refutation condition.} The premise is false if $\epsilon$ is produced dynamically by any in-framework calculation (the refutation condition of the stance in Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.asymmetry-arrow-counted-fifteenth-refutation}(iv)); if it is computed from the transport structure or from another premise of the register; or if it is shown to be a function of $N$ or of another registered number. The conditions are unchanged; they are read for the named datum, the $B - L$ per unit entropy. The conversion of an inherited $B - L$ into baryon number by the anomalous weak process is not a production of $\epsilon$ and does not meet the first condition; an in-framework dynamics that produces the $B - L$ does.

\emph{Note (the unit of $\epsilon$, 2026-11-11).} The words ``per mode'' and the formula $Q = \epsilon N$ are the charge reading's own (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.stored-asymmetry-charge-not-number}; Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.mirror-tower-copy-one}(v)), whose argument uses only that $N$ is common to the two towers. They do not give $\epsilon$ a number: with $N = M_{P}/H$, about $10^{61}$, no count of modes makes $8.7 \times 10^{-11}$ the factor (the baryon number inside the de Sitter radius is about $5 \times 10^{78}$ today, and it is not constant, the radius being fixed while the density dilutes). The figure $8.7 \times 10^{-11}$ is this tower's yield $n_{B}/s$ today: the conserved charge per comoving volume, measured with this tower's entropy as the unit of comoving volume. It is the descent's datum only if no entropy is produced in this tower after the descent. The equality along the reversal orbit is an equality of charge per comoving volume, $n_{B}' = n_{B}$; it is not $n_{B}'/s' = n_{B}/s$, which is the thermal reading that clause (iv) of the Proposition rejects (the mirror's own ratio is larger by $\xi^{-3}$, the stored $\eta' = \eta/\xi^{3}$). What the premise registers is unchanged. \emph{Added 2026-11-12.} With the charge named as $B - L$, read this note with $B - L$ in place of baryon number where it speaks of the conserved charge and of the equality along the reversal orbit: the equality is of $B - L$ per comoving volume, and $n_{B}' = n_{B}$ follows from it only if the mirror's ratio of baryon number to $B - L$ equals this tower's, which is not shown.

## technical
The series inherits its matter asymmetry rather than generating it: the descent creates the tower charges and makes one orientation choice, and the size of the asymmetry, $\epsilon$ per mode, so that each oriented tower stores the charge $\epsilon N$, was until now held as a flagged stance inside a proved result: initial data of the descent, refuted by any in-framework calculation that produces it. This premise registers that number as a free input, as the D-term level and $N$ are registered: $\epsilon$ is not derived and is not a function of the other registered numbers. By the maintainer's decision of 12 November 2026 the charge is named as $B - L$, not baryon number: the stored content changes baryon number (the $24$ slot-pair generators) and, among stored terms, changes $B - L$ only through the Majorana element. The measured baryon density fixes about $8.7 \times 10^{-11}$ baryons per unit entropy; the free datum is $B - L$ per unit entropy, which equals that figure divided by the equilibrium coefficient of the anomalous weak process if that process equilibrated after the descent. The process, the coefficient and the freeze-out temperature are imported Facts. On the level branch one doublet is light at the freeze-out (argued, not proved), and the coefficient then lies between $\tfrac{28}{79}$, its symmetric-phase end, and $\tfrac{12}{37}$, its broken-phase end, so the datum lies between $2.45 \times 10^{-10}$ (the symmetric-phase end, where the stored mirror check sits) and $2.68 \times 10^{-10}$ (the broken-phase end), conditional on the equilibrium after the descent, which is not shown. Its equality across this tower and the mirror is a clause of the copy theorem, and its uniformity across space agrees with the stored result that only a time shift of the descent is an adiabatic modulation of the deposit. It is not the deposit energy per unit charge that the mirror's temperature measures, which stays unregistered. It would be refuted by any in-framework dynamical calculation of $\epsilon$, by a derivation from the structure or another premise, or by showing it is a function of another registered number.

## lay
There is more matter than antimatter in the universe. Most theories try to explain how a process in the early universe produced that excess; this framework does not. It takes the size of the excess to have been set when the universe began, as a starting condition, and ties its value to the measured amount of ordinary matter. What is taken as set at the beginning is not the excess of baryons itself but the excess of baryons minus leptons, because later processes can trade baryons against leptons while leaving that difference alone. If the known weak process that does this trading ran to equilibrium, the baryon excess we measure is a fixed fraction of the inherited difference, between 12/37 and 28/79, about a third. The fraction is taken from the literature, and that only one Higgs doublet is light when the process stops is argued here, not proved. This entry records that size as one of the basic numbers put in by hand, beside the huge number N and the number that sets the weak scale. It would be overturned if a calculation within the framework produced the excess.
