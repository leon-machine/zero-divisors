## formal
Let $\nu \geq 2$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] A lift has the form $W\ket{A} = \ket{A} \otimes r_{A}$ on the even basis states $\ket{A}$, with $r_{A} = \sum_{\kappa}(K_{\kappa})_{AA}\ket{r_{\kappa}}$ for orthonormal record states $\ket{r_{\kappa}}$, one per Kraus operator, and every $\ket{r_{\kappa}}$ even. Hence the record must contain $2^{\nu-1}$ orthonormal even states.
\item[(ii)] The even sector of $k$ rungs has dimension $2^{k-1}$. Hence a lift needs $k \geq \nu$ rungs, one per mode, and one rung never suffices.
\item[(iii)] With $\nu$ rungs a lift exists: $W = \sum_{\kappa_{\nu} = 0}\G^{\kappa} \otimes \ket{r_{\kappa}}$, the $\ket{r_{\kappa}}$ any orthonormal basis of the record's even sector; for the pair, $W = 1 \otimes \ket{00} + \G_{0} \otimes \ket{11}$, up to the normalisation $W^{\dagger}W = 2$.
\item[(iv)] The lift factorises as $W = C\,(\,\cdot \otimes \varphi)$ with $\varphi = \sum_{\kappa}\ket{r_{\kappa}}$ and $C = \sum_{\kappa}\G^{\kappa} \otimes \ket{r_{\kappa}}\bra{r_{\kappa}}$. For the pair, $C = 1 - 2P^{R}_{11}N_{0}$, a polynomial in the parity sections, joint-number-diagonal, even, an involution: an element of the supplied class. And $\varphi = \ket{00} + \ket{11}$ is the Thouless state of the record pair with amplitude $1$, a Witt vacuum of a rotated decomposition.
\item[(v)] Any even unitary $U$ realising a lift from the record vacuum has a generator that does not conserve the total number; among the quadratic generators, those of the Bogoliubov algebra $\mathfrak{so}(2(\nu + k))$, that is a generator with a nonzero pairing component. A unitary generated by number-conserving elements (hops within the record, hops between system and record, Cartan elements, joint-number-diagonal couplings) realises no lift. The pairing may sit on the record, as in (iv), or on the system and be carried to the record by hops.
\end{enumerate}

## technical
For $\nu\geq 2$ modes, any lift acts on the system's even basis states as $W\ket{A} = \ket{A}\otimes r_{A}$, with $r_{A} = \sum_{\kappa}(K_{\kappa})_{AA}\ket{r_{\kappa}}$ built from orthonormal even record states, one per Kraus operator, so the record must hold $2^{\nu-1}$ orthonormal even states. Since $k$ rungs have an even sector of dimension $2^{k-1}$, a lift needs $k\geq\nu$ rungs, one per mode, and one rung never suffices. With $\nu$ rungs a lift exists, $W = \sum_{\kappa_{\nu}=0}\G^{\kappa}\otimes\ket{r_{\kappa}}$; for the pair $W = 1\otimes\ket{00} + \G_{0}\otimes\ket{11}$, up to $W^{\dagger}W = 2$.

The lift factorises as $W = C(\,\cdot\otimes\varphi)$ with $\varphi = \sum_{\kappa}\ket{r_{\kappa}}$; for the pair $C = 1 - 2P^{R}_{11}N_{0}$ is an even, joint-number-diagonal involution in the supplied class, and $\varphi = \ket{00} + \ket{11}$ is the Thouless state of amplitude $1$, a Witt vacuum. Any even unitary realising a lift from the record vacuum has a generator that does not conserve total number, and among quadratic generators that means a nonzero pairing component; number-conserving dynamics realises no lift. The pairing may act on the record, or on the system and be carried to the record by hops.

## lay
The descent throws away part of the information in a set of two or more fermionic modes, and one can ask what it costs to keep that information in an extra record instead. Because physical states must have even fermion parity, the record needs one extra mode for every mode of the system, one more in total than a parity-blind count would give. An explicit way to do it exists: prepare the record in a paired state and apply a coupling the framework already supplies. Starting from an empty record, however, preparing that paired state needs a process that changes the particle number, which no number-conserving dynamics can provide.

## one-liner
For $\nu\geq 2$ modes an even lift of the descent needs exactly $\nu$ rungs of record, exists as a supplied-class coupling applied to a paired Thouless record, and cannot be realised from the record vacuum without a generator that fails to conserve number.
