(i) By Fact \claimref{descent-lift.imported-stinespring-kraus-choi} every dilation is $W = \sum_{\kappa}K_{\kappa} \otimes \ket{r_{\kappa}}$ with $K_{\kappa}$ a Kraus family and the $\ket{r_{\kappa}}$ orthonormal, and by Proposition \claimref{descent-lift.kraus-form-descent-even-states} the Kraus operators are diagonal in the number basis, so $W\ket{A} = \ket{A} \otimes r_{A}$. Evenness of $W$ and of $\ket{A}$ force $r_{A}$ even for every $A$; since the matrix $((K_{\kappa})_{AA})$ is invertible (the Kraus operators are linearly independent), each $\ket{r_{\kappa}}$ is a combination of the $r_{A}$ and is even. The record must therefore contain $2^{\nu-1}$ orthonormal even states. (ii) The even basis states of $k$ rungs are the subsets of even size, $2^{k-1}$ of them; the script counts them for $k \leq 4$. (iii) $W$ is even because each $\G^{\kappa}$ and each $\ket{r_{\kappa}}$ is; $W^{\dagger}W = \sum_{\kappa}(\G^{\kappa})^{2} = 2^{\nu-1}$; and the restriction of $W\rho W^{\dagger}$ to the system's algebra is $\sum_{\kappa}\G^{\kappa}\rho\G^{\kappa}$ because the record states are orthonormal and even, which is $2^{\nu-1}\EN(\rho)$ on even $\rho$. The script verifies evenness, the norm and the induced channel on every even matrix unit for $\nu = 2$ (two rungs) and $\nu = 3$ (three rungs), computing the restriction to the system's algebra directly and, for the pair, checking that it agrees with the tensor-product partial trace. (iv) $C(\ket{A} \otimes \varphi) = \sum_{\kappa}\G^{\kappa}\ket{A} \otimes \ket{r_{\kappa}} = W\ket{A}$. For the pair, $\G_{0} = 1 - 2N_{0}$ gives $C = 1 - 2P^{R}_{11}N_{0} = 1 - \tfrac14(1 - \G_{0})(1 - \G_{2})(1 - \G_{3})$; the script checks the polynomial identity, diagonality, commutation with every $N_{k}$ and with the total parity, and $C^{2} = 1$. That $\varphi$ is a Witt vacuum is Fact \claimref{descent-lift.imported-thouless-states-witt-vacua}, checked by the $M^{2} = -N^{4}I$ criterion on the correlation matrix. (v) Let $U$ be generated by number-conserving elements; then $U$ commutes with the total number. By (i) a lift keeps the system in $\ket{A}$ while writing $r_{A}$, so $U(\ket{A} \otimes \ket{0_{R}})$ has total number $|A|$ and system part $\ket{A}$, whence the record part has number $0$: $r_{A} = \ket{0_{R}}$ for every $A$, the $r_{A}$ are not orthogonal, and the induced map keeps the coherence. The script lists ten number-conserving generators, checks each commutes with the total number, and enumerates the joint basis states of the required number and parity: for the pair, both $A = \ket{00}$ and $A = \ket{11}$ admit only the record state $\ket{00}$. A record that reaches $\ket{11}$ from $\ket{00}$ while the system's number is unchanged has changed its own number by two, which, within the record's own quadratic algebra, is the pair creation $a^{\dagger}_{2}a^{\dagger}_{3}$; the script checks that every stabiliser element of the record vacuum (hops, the annihilation, the Cartan elements) annihilates $\ket{00}$ (Fact \claimref{descent-lift.imported-descent-modes-univalence-supplied-class}), and that the rational pairing rotor $(1 - P) + \tfrac35 P + \tfrac45 X$, with $X = a^{\dagger}_{2}a^{\dagger}_{3} - a_{3}a_{2}$ and $P = -X^{2}$ the projection onto the record's even sector $\operatorname{span}\{\ket{00}, \ket{11}\}$ (the element $\tfrac35 + \tfrac45 X$ alone is orthogonal only on that sector), is unitary, even, and carries $\ket{00}$ to $\tfrac35\ket{00} + \tfrac45\ket{11}$, the singularity paper's necessity witness; $\varphi$ is the member of the same family at $\cos\theta = \sin\theta$. The pairing need not act on the record. The script checks that the system's pairing rotor at $\cos\theta = \sin\theta$, unnormalised, $1 + (a^{\dagger}_{0}a^{\dagger}_{1} - a_{1}a_{0})$, conjugated by the two system-record swaps (beam splitters at $(\cos, \sin) = (0, 1)$ between modes $0, 2$ and $1, 3$) and followed by $C$, is an even map inducing $2\EN$ on the even sector: a lift whose only non-number-conserving generator acts on the system. Hops move the pairing between system and record; nothing removes it.
