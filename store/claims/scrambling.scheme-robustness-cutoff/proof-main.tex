Each condition is solved exactly for $r$. Curvature eigenvalue: $r^3 = 2M$, so $r_c = (2M)^{1/3} = 2^{1/3}M^{1/3}$. Kretschmann: $r^6 = 48M^2$, so $r_c = 48^{1/6}M^{1/3}$. Proper time: from the exact interior integral $\tau(r) = \int_0^r \sqrt{u/(2M - u)}\,du = \tfrac{2}{3}\,r^{3/2}(2M)^{-1/2}(1 + O(r/2M))$ (the leading term of the series proved in \cite[Lem.~5.1]{ButlerT6} (\claimref{interior.kasner-form-interior}), rescaled to $2M \neq 1$), setting $\tau = 1$ gives $r_c = (3/2)^{2/3}(2M)^{1/3}(1 + O(r_c/M))$. All three are $M^{1/3}$ times constants $2^{1/3} \approx 1.26$, $48^{1/6} \approx 1.91$, $(3/2)^{2/3}2^{1/3} \approx 1.65$: order one. The script checks the three exponent computations and the constants exactly (as exact powers of rationals).
