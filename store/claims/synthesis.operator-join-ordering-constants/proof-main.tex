Write $x_{i\alpha} := Z^{\alpha}_{i}$ and $y_{i\alpha} := \bar{Z}_{i\alpha}$, so $[x_{i\alpha}, y_{l\beta}] = \hbar\,\delta_{il}\delta_{\alpha\beta}$ and all $x$ commute with all $x$, all $y$ with all $y$. The classical twistor form is $\bar{Z}^{i}\cdot W = y_{i}\cdot W$ and the Gram matrix is $h_{il} = y_{i}\cdot x_{l}$. Normal ordering $:\;:$ moves every $y$ to the right; since $y_{i\alpha}x_{i\alpha} = x_{i\alpha}y_{i\alpha} - \hbar$, the symmetrised product of a pair is $\tfrac12(xy + yx) = xy - \tfrac{\hbar}{2}$.

(1) By Lemma \claimref{correspondence.symmetrised-quadratics-palatial-algebra}(3), the symmetrised and normal-ordered quadratics of $A$ differ by $\tfrac{\hbar}{2}\tr A$ per copy. By Lemma \claimref{mass.chart-form-spin-isomorphism} the generators $A(n_{\infty}\wedge n_{0})$, $A(x\wedge n_{\infty})$, $A(x\wedge n_{0})$ are traceless (the grading element $\operatorname{diag}(-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12,\tfrac12,\tfrac12)$ and two nilpotent off-diagonal blocks), so are all $A(U\wedge I_{\Lambda})$ by linearity, and $\hat{M}_{A} = i\sum_{i}:y_{i}^{\mathsf T}Ax_{i}:$ with no shift. The grip is $\hat{P}$ with $\ip{\hat{P}}{U} = \hat{M}_{U\wedge I_{\Lambda}}$, and $\hat{m}^{2} = \sum_{U,V}G^{UV}\hat{M}_{U\wedge I_{\Lambda}}\hat{M}_{V\wedge I_{\Lambda}}$ over a basis, with $G^{UV}$ symmetric; the contraction is unchanged under exchanging the two factors, so no ordering choice enters. This is the definition of the grip read with operators, in the dictionary of Theorem \claimref{correspondence.shared-core-ordering-dictionary}.

(2) For $f = \sum_{i}y_{i}^{\mathsf T}Fx_{i}$ and $g = \sum_{i}y_{i}^{\mathsf T}Gx_{i}$ (each linear in $y$), moving the $y$ of $f$ past the $x$ of $g$ gives
\[
:f:\,:g: \;=\; :fg: \;-\; \hbar\,:\sum_{k}\partial_{y_{k}}f\;\partial_{x_{k}}g: \;=\; :fg: \;-\; \hbar\sum_{i}:y_{i}^{\mathsf T}GFx_{i}: .
\]
With $F = iA_{U}$, $G = iA_{V}$, $A_{U} := A(U\wedge I_{\Lambda})$, and summing against $G^{UV}$,
\[
\hat{m}^{2} = :m^{2}: + \hbar\sum_{i}:y_{i}^{\mathsf T}\Bigl(\sum_{U,V}G^{UV}A_{V}A_{U}\Bigr)x_{i}: .
\]
The matrix is computed in the basis $n_{\infty}, n_{0}$, $x$-slot, with $\ip{n_{\infty}}{n_{0}} = 1$ and the $x$-slot metric $-\varepsilon\otimes\varepsilon$, its own inverse (Fact \claimref{mass.imported-flat-model-twistor-chart-infinity}, as in the proof of Theorem \claimref{mass.exact-join}). Let $\mathsf{E} = A(n_{\infty}\wedge n_{0})$, $T_{x} = A(x\wedge n_{\infty})$, $K_{x} = A(x\wedge n_{0})$. Then $A_{n_{\infty}} = \lambda\mathsf{E}$ and $A_{n_{0}} = -\mathsf{E}$, contributing $2\cdot\lambda\mathsf{E}\cdot(-\mathsf{E}) = -2\lambda\mathsf{E}^{2} = -\tfrac{\lambda}{2}\,1$. On the $x$-slot $A_{x} = T_{x} + \lambda K_{x}$, and $T_{x}T_{y} = K_{x}K_{y} = 0$, so the contribution is $\lambda\sum G^{xy}(T_{x}K_{y} + K_{x}T_{y}) = \lambda\bigl(\sum G^{xy}b_{x}c_{y}\bigr)\oplus\lambda\bigl(\sum G^{xy}c_{x}b_{y}\bigr)$. With $G^{(ee')(ff')} = -\varepsilon_{ef}\varepsilon_{e'f'}$ and the components of Lemma \claimref{mass.chart-form-spin-isomorphism},
\[
\Bigl(\sum G^{xy}b_{x}c_{y}\,\omega\Bigr)^{a} = \sum -\varepsilon_{ef}\varepsilon_{e'f'}\,\delta^{a}_{e}\delta^{a'}_{e'}\varepsilon_{a'b'}\bigl(-\delta^{c}_{f}\delta^{b'}_{f'}\varepsilon_{cd}\omega^{d}\bigr) = \Bigl(\sum_{a'b'}\varepsilon_{a'b'}^{2}\Bigr)(\varepsilon^{2})_{ad}\,\omega^{d} = -2\,\omega^{a},
\]
and likewise $\sum G^{xy}c_{x}b_{y} = -2$ on $\Spi$. Hence $\sum G^{UV}A_{V}A_{U} = -\tfrac{\lambda}{2} - 2\lambda = -\tfrac52\lambda = -\tfrac54Q(I_{\Lambda})$ times the identity, using $Q(I_{\Lambda}) = 2\lambda = \Lambda/3$ (Lemma \claimref{mass.de-sitter-scale-frozen-point}(3)), and $\sum_{i}:y_{i}^{\mathsf T}x_{i}: = :\tr h:$. This gives (2). Each $\hat{h}_{ii} = \sum_{\alpha}\tfrac12(x_{i\alpha}y_{i\alpha} + y_{i\alpha}x_{i\alpha}) = :h_{ii}: - 2\hbar$, which is the two-$\hbar$ shift of Lemma \claimref{correspondence.symmetrised-quadratics-palatial-algebra}(3), so $:\tr h: = \tr\hat{h} + 2N\hbar$.

(3) Normal ordering is linear, so the classical identity $m^{2} = 2\sum_{i<l}\bar{B}^{il}B^{il} + 2\lambda\bigl(\tfrac12\sum_{i,l}h_{il}h_{li} - \tfrac14(\tr h)^{2}\bigr)$ (Theorem \claimref{mass.exact-join}(2) at $N = 2$, where $C = C_{2}$; and the hypothesis of the general sentence) gives $:m^{2}:$ as the same expression with every product normal-ordered. Re-express each normal-ordered product through symmetric ones.

\emph{Brackets.} $B^{il} = x_{i}^{\mathsf T}\Omega_{\lambda}x_{l}$ with $\Omega_{\lambda} = \operatorname{diag}(\lambda\varepsilon, \varepsilon)$ and $\bar{B}^{il} = y_{i}^{\mathsf T}\Omega'y_{l}$ with $\Omega' = S\Omega_{\lambda}S = \operatorname{diag}(\varepsilon, \lambda\varepsilon)$, $S$ the real symmetric involution of the twistor form ($\bar{x} = Sy$). So $:\bar{B}B: = \hat{B}\hat{B}^{\dagger}$, and for $i \ne l$
\[
[\hat{B}^{il}, \hat{B}^{il\dagger}] = \hbar\Bigl(\sum_{a,c}(\Omega_{\lambda}\Omega'^{\mathsf T})_{ac}\,x_{ia}y_{ic} + \sum_{b,d}(\Omega_{\lambda}^{\mathsf T}\Omega')_{bd}\,y_{ld}x_{lb}\Bigr) = \hbar\lambda\bigl(:h_{ii}: + :h_{ll}: - 4\hbar\bigr) = \hbar\lambda\,(\hat{h}_{ii} + \hat{h}_{ll}),
\]
since $\Omega_{\lambda}\Omega'^{\mathsf T} = \operatorname{diag}(\lambda\varepsilon\varepsilon^{\mathsf T}, \lambda\varepsilon\varepsilon^{\mathsf T}) = \lambda$ and $\Omega_{\lambda}^{\mathsf T}\Omega' = \lambda$ likewise. Hence $2\sum_{i<l}:\bar{B}^{il}B^{il}: = \sum_{i<l}\{\hat{B}^{il}, \hat{B}^{il\dagger}\} + \hbar\lambda(N - 1)\tr\hat{h}$.

\emph{Gram products.} For $i \ne l$, $\hat{h}_{il} = :h_{il}:$ and $\hat{h}_{il}\hat{h}_{li} = \sum_{a,b}x_{la}y_{ia}x_{ib}y_{lb} = :h_{il}h_{li}: - \hbar:h_{ll}:$; summed over ordered pairs, $\sum_{i\ne l}:h_{il}h_{li}: = \sum_{i\ne l}\hat{h}_{il}\hat{h}_{li} + \hbar(N - 1)(\tr\hat{h} + 2N\hbar)$. For a single $f = y^{\mathsf T}x$ summed over components, $:f:\,:f: = :f^{2}: - \hbar:f:$ by the rule in (2), so $:h_{ii}^{2}: = (\hat{h}_{ii} + 2\hbar)^{2} + \hbar(\hat{h}_{ii} + 2\hbar) = \hat{h}_{ii}^{2} + 5\hbar\hat{h}_{ii} + 6\hbar^{2}$, and, the copies commuting, $:(\tr h)^{2}: = (\tr\hat{h} + 2N\hbar)^{2} + \hbar(\tr\hat{h} + 2N\hbar) = (\tr\hat{h})^{2} + (4N + 1)\hbar\tr\hat{h} + (4N^{2} + 2N)\hbar^{2}$. Therefore $\sum_{i,l}:h_{il}h_{li}: = \sum_{i,l}\hat{h}_{il}\hat{h}_{li} + (N + 4)\hbar\tr\hat{h} + (2N^{2} + 4N)\hbar^{2}$ and
\[
2\lambda\Bigl(\tfrac12\sum_{i,l}:h_{il}h_{li}: - \tfrac14:(\tr h)^{2}:\Bigr) = 2\lambda\hat{C}_{N} + \tfrac{7 - 2N}{2}\,\lambda\hbar\tr\hat{h} + 3N\lambda\hbar^{2}.
\]

\emph{Assembly.} $:m^{2}: = \sum_{i<l}\{\hat{B}^{il}, \hat{B}^{il\dagger}\} + 2\lambda\hat{C}_{N} + \bigl(N - 1 + \tfrac{7 - 2N}{2}\bigr)\lambda\hbar\tr\hat{h} + 3N\lambda\hbar^{2}$, and the coefficient of $\lambda\hbar\tr\hat{h}$ is $\tfrac52$. By (2), $\hat{m}^{2} = :m^{2}: - \tfrac52\lambda\hbar(\tr\hat{h} + 2N\hbar)$: the helicity terms cancel and
\[
\hat{m}^{2} = \sum_{i<l}\{\hat{B}^{il}, \hat{B}^{il\dagger}\} + 2\lambda\hat{C}_{N} + (3N - 5N)\lambda\hbar^{2} = \sum_{i<l}\{\hat{B}^{il}, \hat{B}^{il\dagger}\} + \frac{\Lambda}{3}\hat{C}_{N} - \frac{N\Lambda}{3}\hbar^{2},
\]
which at $N = 2$ is (3).

\emph{The Weyl constant.} For comparison with the Weyl quantisation $W$ (symmetrisation of every monomial, imported standard Weyl-Moyal calculus): the Weyl symbol of the product of two symmetrised quadratics $f = \sum_{i}y_{i}^{\mathsf T}Fx_{i}$, $g = \sum_{i}y_{i}^{\mathsf T}Gx_{i}$ is $fg + \tfrac{\hbar}{2}\{f, g\} - \tfrac{N\hbar^{2}}{4}\tr(FG)$. In $\hat{m}^{2}$ the bracket term cancels in the symmetric contraction, and with $F = iA_{U}$, $G = iA_{V}$ the constant is $\tfrac{N\hbar^{2}}{4}\tr\bigl(\sum G^{UV}A_{U}A_{V}\bigr) = \tfrac{N\hbar^{2}}{4}\cdot 4\cdot(-\tfrac52\lambda) = -\tfrac{5N}{2}\lambda\hbar^{2}$, so $\hat{m}^{2} = W(m^{2}) - \tfrac{5N\Lambda}{12}\hbar^{2}$. The constant in (3) is therefore relative to the Jordan-symmetrised products of $\hat{B}$, $\hat{B}^{\dagger}$, $\hat{h}$ and not a convention-free number; the operator $\hat{m}^{2}$ itself is fixed by (1).

(4) At $\lambda = 0$ the correction in (2) and the constants above vanish.

The script confirms these identities by two independent exact routes: the Moyal composition of Weyl symbols (exact for products of quadratics), giving the classical parts, no term of order $\hbar$ and the constants, for $N = 2, 3$ at two values of $\lambda$; and the holomorphic realisation $\bar{Z} = -\hbar\partial_{Z}$ of the palatial algebra on random polynomial test functions at $N = 2$, $\hbar = 1, 3$, for both the normal and the palatial form; together with the tracelessness in (1), the matrix $\sum G^{UV}A_{V}A_{U} = -\tfrac52\lambda$, the commutator $[\hat{B}, \hat{B}^{\dagger}]$, the helicity action of $\hat{h}_{ii}$ on homogeneous functions, and the flat limit.
