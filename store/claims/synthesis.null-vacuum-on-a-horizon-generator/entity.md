## formal
Let $\mathfrak{h} = L^{2}(\R, du)\otimes K$ ($K$ the transverse and internal labels) and $\CAR(\mathfrak{h})$ the continuum mode space of Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.continuum-mode-space} on one null generator with affine parameter $u$, generated by the fields $\psi(u)$ with $\{\psi(u), \psi^{*}(v)\} = \delta(u - v)\otimes\id_{K}$; the unit of $\mathfrak{h}$ is $I_{4}$ and numbers are read in $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$. Let $\nu_{a}(\psi(u)) = \psi(u + a)$ be the null translations, with one-particle generator $P = I_{4}\,d/du$, $\nu_{a}(a(f)) = a(e^{I_{4}aP}f)$, and for $\kappa > 0$ let $\beta_{t}(\psi(u)) = e^{\kappa t/2}\psi(e^{\kappa t}u)$ be the boost about $u = 0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{(The weight and the dilation.)} Among the dilations $\psi(u) \mapsto e^{\lambda w_{0}}\psi(e^{\lambda}u)$ exactly those with $\operatorname{Re}w_{0} = \tfrac12$ preserve the anticommutator, so $\beta_{t}$ is an automorphism; and $\beta_{t}\nu_{a}\beta_{-t} = \nu_{e^{\kappa t}a}$: the boost dilates the null translations at rate $\kappa$.
\item[(ii)] \emph{(The null vacuum.)} A gauge-invariant quasi-free state on $\CAR(\mathfrak{h})$ is a ground state for $\nu$ (invariant, with nonnegative generator in its GNS representation) if and only if its symbol is $\chi_{(-\infty,0)}(P)\otimes\id_{K}$. This state $\omega_{0}$, the null vacuum, has $\omega_{0}(\psi^{*}(v)\psi(u)) = W_{0}(u, v) = \dfrac{I_{4}}{2\pi}\dfrac{1}{u - v + I_{4}0}\otimes\id_{K}$ and $\omega_{0}(\psi(u)\psi^{*}(v)) = -\dfrac{I_{4}}{2\pi}\dfrac{1}{u - v - I_{4}0}\otimes\id_{K}$.
\item[(iii)] \emph{(The factor $2\pi$.)} The subalgebra $\CAR(L^{2}(\R_{+}, du)\otimes K)$ of the modes beyond $u = 0$ is $\beta$-invariant, and the restriction of $\omega_{0}$ to it is the continuum I-state of Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state-continuum} for the one-particle boost generator $h$ at $\beta = 2\pi/\kappa$: on $u^{-1/2 + I_{4}w}$, $h$ acts as $-\kappa w$ and the symbol is $(1 + e^{-2\pi w})^{-1} = (1 + e^{\beta h})^{-1}$. It is jointly KMS on the whole subalgebra and even, and it is the unique $\beta$-KMS state there. The same holds on the past half $\CAR(L^{2}(\R_{-}, du)\otimes K)$ for the boost that is future-directed there, $\beta_{-t}$, with symbol $(1 + e^{2\pi w})^{-1}$ on $|u|^{-1/2 + I_{4}w}$; the reflection $u \mapsto -u$ exchanges the two statements. Positivity fixes the sign (the opposite sign gives $-2\pi/\kappa$), and the one-sided support is needed (on the full generator the boost-continued two-point function has a pole at imaginary rapidity $\pi$, and $\omega_{0}$ there is pure); the period $2\pi$ is the first return of the rotated half-generator $e^{I_{4}\theta}\R_{+}$ to itself.
\end{enumerate}
These are statements about the null vacuum on one generator; they assume no premise. Which state a horizon's modes are in is a separate matter (Premise (N$_{+}$) takes it to be $\omega_{0}$).

## technical
On the continuum of one null generator, with the affine translations along it, a gauge-invariant quasi-free state is a ground state for the translations exactly when it is the filled sea: every negative null-momentum mode occupied, every positive one empty. This null vacuum has the two-point kernel $\tfrac{I_{4}}{2\pi}(u - v + I_{4}0)^{-1}$. The boost about a point of the generator, with the weight $\tfrac12$ that the anticommutator forces, dilates the translations. Restricted to either half of the generator, the null vacuum is the continuum I-state at rapidity period exactly $2\pi$ for the boost that is future-directed on that half: in the Mellin basis $u^{-1/2 + I_{4}w}$, which diagonalises the boost, the compressed sea projection has symbol $(1 + e^{-2\pi w})^{-1}$. Positivity of the null energy fixes the sign of the period, and the restriction to one half is needed: on the whole generator the state is pure. This is the null-plane form of the results of Bisognano-Wichmann, Hislop-Longo and Sewell. It is pure analysis on the generator; whether a horizon's modes are in this state is the content of Premise (N$_{+}$), which this result does not assume.

## lay
Along a single light ray of a horizon, the quantum field has one lowest-energy state for sliding along the ray: every mode with negative momentum along the ray is filled and every mode with positive momentum is empty. Looked at from one side of any point of the ray, that state is exactly thermal, at the temperature corresponding to a period of $2\pi$ in the boost about that point. This result proves these facts about the state itself, without assuming that a horizon is actually in it; that assumption is a separate premise of the series.

## one-liner
On the continuum mode space of a null generator, the gauge-invariant quasi-free ground states of the affine null translations are exactly the filled sea $\chi_{(-\infty,0)}(P)$, $P = I_{4}\,d/du$, with kernel $\tfrac{I_{4}}{2\pi}(u - v + I_{4}0)^{-1}$; the weight-$\tfrac12$ boost dilates the translations, and on either half-generator the null vacuum is the continuum I-state at rapidity period exactly $2\pi$ for the boost future-directed there, with no premise assumed.
