At $\Lambda = 0$ the parallel scale tractor is null, its stabiliser is parabolic (the Poincar\'e-type stabiliser of a null direction), and the rotation to the reductive de Sitter stabiliser happens exactly when $Q(I) \neq 0$; the identity $Q(I)\,Q(V) = -1$ holds for $\Lambda \neq 0$. A quadratic space with a one-dimensional radical has Clifford algebra $\Cl(p,q;1)$ with a nilpotent generator $n$, $n^{2} = 0$, and $n\,\Cl(p,q;1)$ is a two-sided ideal of square zero; the series treats these algebras, their nilpotent ideals and their one-sided completions in the sheaf paper, and a nilpotent generator is the vector-type zero divisor whose resolution is the transport. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Fact~2.1, Thm.~3.1]{ButlerT10} (\claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}, \claimref{dark-energy.imported-frozen-point-geometry}); \cite[Thm.~3.1, Prop.~3.4, Thm.~4.4]{ButlerT4} (\claimref{sheaf.degeneration-sheaf}, \claimref{sheaf.resolution-ideal-theoretically}, \claimref{sheaf.one-sided-completion}); \cite[\S 4]{ButlerT1}.
