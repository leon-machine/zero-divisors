\emph{Setting.} By Premise \claimref{singularity.q-joint-quasi-freeness-s-wave} the array's state is the product of its marginals, and by Theorem \claimref{synthesis.boundary-mode-thermal-entropy} mode $k$ is thermal at $x_{k} = 2\pi\omega_{k}/\kappa = 2\pi(k + \tfrac12)$. The arguments are $\pm(2k + 1)\pi$, $k \geq 0$; measured from the filled sea, a mode above it ($x > 0$) contributes $\omega n(x)$ and a hole below it ($x < 0$) contributes $|\omega|\,n(|x|)$, so $\beta E = 2\sum_{k \geq 0}x_{k}n(x_{k})$ with $x_{k} = (2k + 1)\pi$, and likewise the entropy and the variance are twice the sums over $k \geq 0$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.half-integer-weight-array-carries-half-ln2}, setting).

\emph{(i).} As in the proof of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.half-integer-weight-array-carries-half-ln2}(i), at slope $y$ (occupations at $x_{k} = y(k + \tfrac12)$) the grand-canonical partition function measured from the sea is $Z(y) = \prod_{n \geq 1}(1 + q^{n - 1/2})^{2} = q^{1/24}\vartheta_{3}/\eta$ with $q = e^{-y}$, that is $\ln Z(y) = -y/24 + f(y)$ with $f(y) = \log(\vartheta_{3}/\eta)(iy/2\pi)$. For a product of thermal modes $\beta E = -y\,\partial_{y}\ln Z$ (each mode contributes $-y\partial_{y}\ln(1 + e^{-y(n - 1/2)}) = y(n - \tfrac12)/(e^{y(n - 1/2)} + 1)$). The transformation $\tau \mapsto -1/\tau$ of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-theta-function-identities}(b) gives $f(y) = f(4\pi^{2}/y)$, so $f'(2\pi) = 0$, the step used in the stored proof. Hence at $y = 2\pi$, $\beta E = y/24 - y f'(y) = 2\pi/24 = \pi/12$, and $E = (\pi/12)(\kappa/2\pi) = \kappa/24$. Written out, this is $\sum_{n\ \mathrm{odd}}n/(e^{n\pi} + 1) = 1/24$, a classical identity (cited from memory; the check does not use it). Independently of the identities, the script bounds the sum: $\pi$ by Machin's formula with bracketing partial sums, $e^{-\pi}$ through the Taylor series of $e^{\pi}$ with its remainder, each term $x u/(1 + u)$, $u = e^{-x}$, increasing in $u$ and in $x$, the first $26$ terms bounded below and above, and the tail by $\sum_{n \geq 53,\ \mathrm{odd}}(1 + n\pi)e^{-n\pi}$. It obtains an interval of width below $10^{-56}$ lying within $10^{-55}$ of $\pi/12$, and, for the zero offset ($x = 2\pi k$, $k \geq 1$, twice; the zero mode carries no energy), the interval $(0.02351, 0.02352)$.

\emph{(ii).} Let $h(\epsilon) := \sum_{n \geq 0}e^{-\epsilon(n + 1/2)} = 1/(2\sinh(\epsilon/2))$, so $\sum_{n \geq 0}(n + \tfrac12)e^{-\epsilon(n + 1/2)} = -h'(\epsilon)$. With $2\sinh(\epsilon/2)/\epsilon = \sum_{j\ \mathrm{even}}\epsilon^{j}/((j + 1)!\,2^{j}) = 1 + \epsilon^{2}/24 + \ldots$ one has $\epsilon h(\epsilon) = 1 - \epsilon^{2}/24 + O(\epsilon^{4})$, so $-h'(\epsilon) = \epsilon^{-2} + \tfrac{1}{24} + O(\epsilon^{2})$: the finite part is $\tfrac1{24}$, which is $\zeta(-1, \tfrac12) = -B_{2}(\tfrac12)/2$ with $B_{2}(a) = a^{2} - a + \tfrac16$ (the script inverts the series exactly and compares; for integer weights the same rule gives $\zeta(-1, 1) = -\tfrac1{12}$, a control). The filled sea consists of the modes $k < 0$, with weights $-\kappa(n + \tfrac12)$, $n \geq 0$, so its zeta-regularised energy is $-\kappa/24$ per array. The symmetric generator over the whole array differs from the sea-normal-ordered one by the constant $\sum_{k}\omega_{k}(\theta_{k} - \tfrac12)$ with $\theta_{k} = 1$ below the sea and $0$ above; pairing $k$ with $-k-1$ (particle-hole, $\omega_{-k-1} = -\omega_{k}$) this is $-\sum_{n \geq 0}\kappa(n + \tfrac12)$, regularised to $-\kappa/24$. Hence in the symmetric ordering $\beta E = \pi/12 - 2\pi/24 = 0$ per array; since the entropy does not depend on a constant shift, $\ln Z = S - \beta E = \tfrac12\ln 2$ per array and $Z = 2$ per counted unit, with $E = 0$ and $S = \ln 2$, which are the values of a zero-weight mode ($Z = 2$, $E = 0$, $S = \ln 2$ in either ordering, its weight being zero). The script confirms the cancellation within $10^{-55}$ and $2\ln Z$ within $10^{-54}$ of $\ln 2$ from the bounds of (i) and the exact finite part.

\emph{(iii).} Doubling (i), a counted unit has $\beta E = \pi/6$ and entropy $\ln 2$, so $E/(TS) = \pi/(6\ln 2)$; with $\ln 2 = \sum_{k \geq 1}1/(k2^{k})$ bounded by its partial sum and tail, this lies in $(0.75539, 0.75540)$. Under the hypothesis of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy} with counted units of two arrays, the number of units is $N = A/(4\ln 2)$ with $A = 16\pi M^{2}$, and $T = 1/8\pi M$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.quarter-from-temperature}), so $E_{\mathrm{c}} = N\,(\pi/6)\,T = \frac{16\pi M^{2}}{4\ln 2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{6}\cdot\frac{1}{8\pi M} = \frac{\pi M}{12\ln 2}$; the script evaluates the product at $M = 1, \tfrac32, 7$ with the bounds on $\pi$ and $\ln 2$ and finds $E_{\mathrm{c}}/M \in (0.37769, 0.37770)$, below $\tfrac12$. In Theorem \claimref{synthesis.hole-mass-is-ground-energy-label}(iii), with the hole the counted modes and $H_{M} = E_{0}(M) + \sum_{j}\omega_{j}v_{j}^{*}v_{j}$, one has $E_{0}(M) = M - E$ with $E$ the counted Killing energy measured from the bottom of the spectrum; here $E = E_{\mathrm{c}}$, so $E_{0}(M) = M(1 - \pi/(12\ln 2)) \in (0.62230, 0.62231)\,M$, inside $(M/2, M]$ and below $M$. The bottom of the spectrum of $H_{M}$ does not depend on how its constant is split, so this value is ordering-independent. Writing instead $H_{M} = c + H_{\mathrm{sym}}$ with $H_{\mathrm{sym}}$ the zeta-regularised symmetric generator of the $2N$ arrays, $H_{\mathrm{sym}} = H'_{M} - 2N\kappa/24$ by (ii), so $c = E_{0}(M) + N\kappa/12 = M - E_{\mathrm{c}} + E_{\mathrm{c}} = M$ and $\langle H_{\mathrm{sym}}\rangle = 0$; the Smarr relation $M = 2TS$ is then $\beta M = 2N\ln 2$, the form it takes in the zero-weight case of that theorem's (iii).

\emph{(iv).} For a thermal fermion mode $\beta^{2}\operatorname{Var}(E) = x^{2}n(1 - n) = x^{2}u/(1 + u)^{2}$, $u = e^{-x}$, increasing in $u$ for $u < 1$ and in $x$; summing as in (i) (tail bounded by $\sum (n\pi)^{2}e^{-n\pi}$) gives per array an interval inside $(0.79820, 0.79825)$ and per counted unit $(1.5964, 1.5965)$. A zero-weight mode has $x = 0$ and variance $0$. The two realisations of $\ln 2$ thus agree in $Z$, $S$ and (in the symmetric ordering) $E$ at the Hawking temperature and differ in the second cumulant of the energy.

\emph{Remark.} The torus of the array's partition function has modular parameter $\tau = i\beta\kappa/2\pi$, which is $i$, the fixed point of $\tau \mapsto -1/\tau$, exactly when the real-time recurrence period $2\pi/\kappa$ (up to the parity, Theorem \claimref{synthesis.half-integer-weight-array-carries-half-ln2}, closing paragraph) equals $\beta$. The step $f'(2\pi) = 0$ in (i) is the infinitesimal form of this symmetry, which is why the energy and entropy at $y = 2\pi$ are rational multiples of $\pi$ and of $\ln 2$ for the half offset; at the zero offset the transformation carries the partition function to a different one (stored proof of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.half-integer-weight-array-carries-half-ln2}). $\square$
