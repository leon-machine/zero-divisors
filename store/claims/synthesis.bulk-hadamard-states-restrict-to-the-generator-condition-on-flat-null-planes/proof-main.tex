Units $\hbar = c = 1$. Work in $\Cl(1,3)$ on the flat gravitational field, $\hb = 1$, $\Omega = 0$ (Definition \claimref{obj.synthesis.gtg-flat-spacetime-gauge-fields}); the free Dirac field of mass $m_{0} \ge 0$ is the one of Proposition \claimref{field-theory.second-quantisation}. Matrix forms below are parenthetical readings of left multiplication in $\Cl(1,3)$.

\emph{The plane and its canonical part.} With $k = e_{0} + e_{3}$, $m = e_{0} - e_{3}$, $\ip{k}{m} = 2$, the canonical part of a spinor on $N$ is $\Lambda(m)\psi$, $\Lambda(m) = \tfrac12(1 + e_{0}e_{3}) = \tfrac14 mk$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.canonical-part-of-a-spinor-on-a-null-plane}(i), (ii); for a massive field the free data on $N$ are $\Lambda(m)\psi$ for every mass, its (iv)). It is idempotent and self-adjoint for the Dirac adjoint ($e_{0}\tilde\Lambda e_{0} = \Lambda$), its image is two complex channels (its (iii)), and the check verifies $\Lambda e_{0}\Lambda = 0$ and $\Lambda e_{\mu}e_{0}\Lambda = \ip{e_{\mu}}{k}\Lambda$ for $\mu = 0, \dots, 3$. By linearity
\[
\Lambda(m)\,(p \pm m_{0})\,e_{0}\,\Lambda(m) \;=\; \ip{p}{k}\,\Lambda(m)
\]
for every vector $p$ and every mass: the mass term drops out of the compressed kernels. (Algebraically: $e_{0}\Lambda = \tfrac12 k$ and $\Lambda k = 0$.)

\emph{(ii) The vacuum.} The restriction to $N$ is not a pullback of distributions in H\"ormander's sense: the wave-front set of the vacuum kernel contains the null covectors along the generators, which meet the conormal of $N\times N$. It is defined here by the momentum-space (light-front) computation below, with transverse smearing; the outcome, that the null-plane vacuum does not depend on the mass, is the standard light-front result (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-null-plane-restriction-of-free-fields-is-mass-independent}), reproduced in the store's generator conventions. The bulk vacuum kernels are (standard, matrix reading) $\omega_{m_{0}}(\psi(x)\psi^{\dagger}(x')) = \int\frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}2p_{0}}(p + m_{0})e_{0}\,e^{-ip\cdot(x - x')}$ and $\omega_{m_{0}}(\psi^{\dagger}(x')\psi(x)) = \int\frac{d^{3}p}{(2\pi)^{3}2p_{0}}(p - m_{0})e_{0}\,e^{+ip\cdot(x - x')}$, over the positive-energy shell. Parametrise the shell by $p_{+} = \ip{p}{k}$ and $p_{\perp}$, with $s = p_{\perp}^{2} + m_{0}^{2}$ (Euclidean square): $p_{0} = \tfrac12(p_{+} + s/p_{+})$, $p_{3} = \tfrac12(s/p_{+} - p_{+})$, so $dp_{3}/dp_{+} = -p_{0}/p_{+}$ and $d^{3}p/(2p_{0}) = dp_{+}\,d^{2}p_{\perp}/(2p_{+})$; for $s > 0$, $p_{0} > |p_{3}|$, so $p_{+} > 0$ on the whole positive-energy shell and $p_{+}$ runs over $(0, \infty)$ (for $s = 0$ the set $p_{\perp} = 0$, $m_{0} = 0$ has measure zero). On $N$, $x - x' = (\lambda - \lambda')k + (y - y')$ and $p\cdot(x - x') = (\lambda - \lambda')p_{+} - p_{\perp}\cdot(y - y')$. Compressing by $\Lambda(m)$, the integrand becomes $\tfrac{1}{2p_{+}}\,p_{+}\Lambda = \tfrac12\Lambda$, independent of $m_{0}$ and $p_{\perp}$ (the check verifies the density $\tfrac12$ on rational shell points with and without mass). Hence, with the standard Fourier integrals $\int d^{2}p_{\perp}e^{ip_{\perp}\cdot y} = (2\pi)^{2}\delta^{2}(y)$ and $\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-i\lambda p}dp = -i(\lambda - i0)^{-1}$,
\[
\omega_{m_{0}}\big(\Lambda\psi(x)\,(\Lambda\psi(x'))^{\dagger}\big) = \tfrac12\,\delta^{2}(y - y')\Lambda\cdot\Big(-\tfrac{i}{2\pi}\Big)(\lambda - \lambda' - i0)^{-1},
\]
and the other ordering is $\tfrac12\delta^{2}(y - y')\Lambda\cdot\tfrac{i}{2\pi}(\lambda - \lambda' + i0)^{-1}$. In the stored conventions (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.null-vacuum-on-a-horizon-generator}(ii)) $W_{0}(u, v) = \tfrac{i}{2\pi}(u - v + i0)^{-1}$ and $\delta - W_{0} = -\tfrac{i}{2\pi}(u - v - i0)^{-1}$. So with $\psi_{N} = \sqrt{2}\,\Lambda(m)\psi$ and $u = \lambda$: (i) the anticommutator is $\delta(u - v)\,\delta^{2}(y - y')\Lambda$, the CAR of $\CAR(L^{2}(\R, du)\otimes K)$ with $K = L^{2}(\R^{2}, d^{2}y)\otimes V_{\Lambda}$, $V_{\Lambda}$ the two-channel image of $\Lambda(m)$ on spinors, and (ii) $\omega_{m_{0}}(\psi_{N}^{*}(v)\psi_{N}(u)) = W_{0}(u, v)\otimes\id_{K}$ for every $m_{0} \ge 0$. Equivalently, for each $p_{\perp}$ the occupation symbol in $p_{+}$ is $\theta(p_{+})$, the null vacuum's. A rescaling $\lambda = cu$ multiplies both $\delta$ and $(\lambda - \lambda' \mp i0)^{-1}$ by $1/c$, absorbed by the weight $\tfrac12$ of $\psi_{N}$, so the statement holds in every affine parameter.

\emph{(iii) Hadamard states.} By Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-microlocal-hadamard-condition}, quasi-free Dirac Hadamard states differ by smooth kernels, and on Minkowski space the vacuum of mass $m_{0}$ is one; so a quasi-free Hadamard state $\omega$ of the mass-$m_{0}$ field has $\omega(\psi(x)\psi^{\dagger}(x')) = \omega_{m_{0}}(\psi(x)\psi^{\dagger}(x')) + S(x, x')$ with $S$ smooth, and the other ordering differs by $-S$ (the anticommutator is state independent). Restricting to $N$ and compressing, $\omega(\psi_{N}^{*}(v)\psi_{N}(u))$ has matrix elements $\ip{f}{\,\cdot\,g}_{K}$, for transverse profiles $f, g$ of compact support, equal to those of $W_{0}(u, v)\otimes\id_{K}$ plus $2\int\!\!\int \bar f(y)\Lambda S(\cdot)\Lambda g(y')$, a smooth function of $(u, v)$ (differentiation under the integral over a compact set). So the remainder $R$ is smooth on $J\times J$ for every interval $J$ of every generator, in each $K$-component, and by Theorem \claimref{synthesis.microlocal-condition-on-a-generator-gives-the-local-null-ground-state}(i) the restricted state satisfies the generator's microlocal spectrum condition there; by its (ii) its scaling limit at every point of $N$ is $\omega_{0}$, the local form of (N$_{+}$), with no stationarity, temperature or symmetry used. The restricted state is gauge-invariant and quasi-free because $\omega$ is and the restricted fields are linear in $\psi$. For translation-invariant states the same follows on the symbol side: on the shell $p_{0} = \tfrac12 p_{+} + s/(2p_{+}) \ge p_{+}/2$ (checked), so a bulk occupation remainder decaying faster than every power of $p_{0}$ gives a remainder symbol decaying faster than every power of $p_{+}$ (and, for $s > 0$, also as $p_{+} \to 0^{+}$).

\emph{(iv) Limits.} The ground-state clause of (N$_{+}$) is not reached: the result holds for every Hadamard state, and the stored theorem's affine-KMS states pass the same condition; the thermal states of the bulk field, which are Hadamard (argued, standard), restrict to states that are not the null vacuum. The plane is the local Rindler horizon of the flat field; on a field with nonzero field strength the restriction of the Hadamard parametrix to a null surface is not computed here, and Kay and Wald's analysis of horizon restrictions on Killing horizons is cited from memory only. The light-front reading of the canonical part on a null plane and the mass independence of the restriction are standard (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-null-plane-restriction-of-free-fields-is-mass-independent}); at points of nonzero field strength the conclusion of (iii) is carried by Theorem \claimref{synthesis.hadamard-scaling-limit-premise}, (HS) (formerly a premise), which this proof does not use; the new content is the link to the horizon premises: the field theory's gauge-invariant quasi-free Hadamard states, not a choice of vacuum, give the local form of (N$_{+}$).
