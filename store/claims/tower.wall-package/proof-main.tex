Let $(M, G)$ be a tame (\claimref{sheaf.tameness}) corank-one degeneration in the sense of \cite[Hyp.~2.1]{ButlerT4}, with wall $\Sigma$ and radical line bundle $\mathcal{L}$. Assume the radical line bundle is trivial, $w_1(\mathcal{L}) = 0$, so that collar data exist. Then the one-sided completion of \cite[Thm.~4.4]{ButlerT4} (\claimref{sheaf.one-sided-completion}) is nondegenerate up to and including the wall; the transported reciprocal divides totally there, by \cite[Thm.~4.10]{ButlerT1} (\claimref{transport.totality}); and conservation of degeneracy \cite[Thm.~5.1, Prop.~5.2]{ButlerT4} (\claimref{sheaf.conservation-degeneracy-located}, \claimref{sheaf.conservation-degeneracy-absolute-form}) relocates the degeneration to a shadow wall. Assume further that $\Sigma$ is compact. Then the shadow wall is itself tame for good couplings \cite[Lem.~5.3]{ButlerT4} (\claimref{sheaf.tame-wall-induction}), and the resulting infinite wall sequence adjoins exactly one hyperbolic plane to the ambient fibre form per wall \cite[Rem.~5.4, Cor.~5.5]{ButlerT4} (\claimref{sheaf.infinite-wall-sequence}), each shadow wall's radical line being trivial automatically. \emph{Provenance:} as cited, all in reviewable papers of this series.
