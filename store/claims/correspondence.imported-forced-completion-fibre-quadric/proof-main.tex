Division by a type-N zero divisor in $\Cl(1,3)$ admits no element-level resolution (the transvection no-go), and the minimal completion resolving it is $\Cl(1,3) \hookrightarrow \Cl(2,4)$, unique up to isomorphism; the resolving assignments lie on the quadric of null rays of $\R^{2,4}$, the completion's point space. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thms.~4.2, 4.10, 4.13]{ButlerT1} (\claimref{transport.no-go-level-elements}, \claimref{transport.minimality}, \claimref{transport.totality}); representation-independent minimality \cite[\S4.6]{ButlerT1}.
