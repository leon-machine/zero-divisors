(i) For a nonzero ambient vector $I$ and a composite with conformal moment map $M$, the grip is the ambient vector $P = M(I)$ with $\ip{P}{U} = M_{U \wedge I}$ for all $U$, where $M_{U \wedge I}$ is the value on the composite of the generator $A(U \wedge I) \in \so(2,4)$; the mass-squared relative to $I$ is $m^{2} = Q(P)$. (ii) At $\Lambda = 0$ the frozen point is $I = n_{\infty}$, the $x$-slot part of $P$ is the total momentum and $m^{2}$ is the Poincar\'e Casimir $p^{2}$. (iii) A single conformal spinor (twistor) carries the null momentum $p_{AA'} = \pi_{A}\bar\pi_{A'}$ and is massless; a single dual conformal spinor likewise, through the conjugation of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-fock-model-twistor-space}; and for two conformal spinors $p^{2} = 2|\br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}}|^{2}$, the squared symplectic area of the spinor parts, with $\br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}} = \det(\pi^{i}_{A})$ the determinant over the slot index. (iv) For $\Lambda > 0$, $Q(P) = Q(I)\bigl(C_{2}(\so(2,4)) - C_{2}(\so(1,4))\bigr)$, the difference of the conformal and de Sitter Casimirs evaluated on the composite, with $Q(I) = \Lambda/3 = H^{2}$. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Def.~5.4, Prop.~4.1, Prop.~5.7]{ButlerT11} (\claimref{mass.casimir-form-join}, \claimref{mass.mass-symplectic-area-two-twistor-formula}); proved there, (ii) and the Casimir normalisation at the levels declared there.
