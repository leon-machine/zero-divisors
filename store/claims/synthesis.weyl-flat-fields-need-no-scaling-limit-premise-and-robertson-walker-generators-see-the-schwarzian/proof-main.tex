Units $\hbar = c = 1$; $\Cl(1,3)$ with $e_{0}^{2} = 1$, $e_{i}^{2} = -1$.

\emph{Set-up.} $U$ is globally hyperbolic for the derived metric (hypothesis), so the Hadamard Facts apply on it. On $U$ the field $\hb(b) = b/\varphi$ has $\hb^{-1}(b) = \varphi b$ and derived metric $\varphi^{2}$ times the flat one (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}(i)). That a four-dimensional field with vanishing Weyl curvature is locally of this form in a suitable gauge is the local conformal flatness of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-conformal-covariance-of-massless-fields-and-local-conformal-flatness}(a); the frame $\hb^{-1}(e_{\mu}) = \varphi e_{\mu}$ is the flat one rescaled, so spinor frames are identified without rotation. For the massless Dirac field of Proposition \claimref{field-theory.second-quantisation} the equation is conformally covariant with weight $\tfrac32$: $\psi = \varphi^{-3/2}\chi$ with $\chi$ a solution of the flat massless equation on $U$, and the map preserves the conserved current, hence the CAR (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-conformal-covariance-of-massless-fields-and-local-conformal-flatness}(b)). The two-point kernels of a state of $\psi$ are $\varphi(x)^{-3/2}\varphi(y)^{-3/2}$ times those of the corresponding state of $\chi$; multiplication by a smooth nonvanishing function does not change wave-front sets, and the null geodesics of the derived metric are the flat null lines, affinely reparametrised by $d\tilde\lambda = \varphi^{2}d\lambda$ with unchanged covectors (along $x_{0} + f k$, $k$ null, the geodesic equation of the derived metric, Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}(iii) read in the chart, reduces to $f'' + 2f'^{2}\,\partial_{k}\varphi/\varphi = 0$, solved by $f' = \varphi^{-2}$; the covector $\varphi^{2}f'(k\cdot\,) = (k\cdot\,)$ is the flat one; checked as exact identities at rational times for three polynomial $\varphi$ of $t$, with the non-affine $f' = \varphi^{-1}$ failing), so the sets $C^{\pm}$ of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-microlocal-hadamard-condition} agree and $\omega$ is Hadamard for $\psi$ exactly when the corresponding state is Hadamard for the flat field $\chi$ on $U$.

\emph{(i)} This is the standard scaling-limit property of Hadamard states of free fields, transferred by conformal covariance. By Fact (b) the flat-field state differs from the flat massless vacuum by smooth kernels on $U \times U$, bounded near $(x_{0}, x_{0})$, which the scaling $l^{3}R(x_{0} + lx, x_{0} + ly)$ sends to zero. The vacuum kernel, off the cone the vector-valued $W(z) = z/Q(z)^{2}$ up to a constant, $z = x - y$, is homogeneous of degree $-3$, so $l^{3}\varphi(x_{0} + lx)^{-3/2}\varphi(x_{0} + ly)^{-3/2}W(lz) \to \varphi(x_{0})^{-3}W(z) = W(\varphi(x_{0})z)$: the flat massless vacuum in the coordinates $\varphi(x_{0})(x - x_{0})$. The check verifies $c^{3}W(cz) = W(z)$ for $c = a(t_{0})$ and that the factor $(s(t_{0} + lt_{1})s(t_{0} + lt_{2}))^{-3}$ for $a = s^{2}$ tends to $s(t_{0})^{-6}$ with error at most a constant times $l$.

\emph{(ii)} A null generator in $U$ lies on a flat null plane of the chart, and the restriction of the flat-field state of $\chi$ to that plane has, on every interval $J$ of every generator, kernel $W_{0}(\lambda, \lambda')\otimes\id_{K} + R$ with $R$ smooth in the flat affine parameter (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.bulk-hadamard-states-restrict-to-the-generator-condition-on-flat-null-planes}(ii), (iii), applied on $U$; the result is local in $J$). Write $\tilde\lambda = \Psi(\lambda)$ with $\Psi' = \varphi^{2}$ along the generator. The physical generator field is $c\,\varphi^{-3/2}\chi_{N}$ for a positive function $c$ of $\lambda$. The physical area element $\varphi^{2}d^{2}y$ changes along an expanding generator, so the multiplicity space must be fixed by a choice; with the chart's comoving transverse labels fixed as $K$, the CAR in $\tilde\lambda$, $\delta(\tilde\lambda - \tilde\lambda')\delta^{2}(y - y')$, together with $\delta(\lambda - \lambda') = \Psi'\,\delta(\tilde\lambda - \tilde\lambda')$, gives $c^{2}\varphi^{-3}\Psi' = 1$, that is $c\varphi^{-3/2} = (\Psi')^{-1/2} = (d\lambda/d\tilde\lambda)^{1/2}$: weight $\tfrac12$. The kernel in $\tilde\lambda$ is therefore the weight-$\tfrac12$ pullback of $W_{0} + R$ by the smooth regular map $\Psi^{-1}$. The pulled-back $R$ is smooth; the pulled-back $W_{0}$ differs from the null vacuum of $\tilde\lambda$ by the kernel $\bigl((\Phi'(u)\Phi'(v))^{1/2}(u - v)/(\Phi(u) - \Phi(v)) - 1\bigr)\cdot\tfrac{I_{4}}{2\pi}(u - v)^{-1}$, $\Phi = \Psi^{-1}$, which is smooth: $(\Phi(u) - \Phi(v))/(u - v) = \int_{0}^{1}\Phi'(v + s(u - v))\,ds$ is smooth and positive, the bracket is smooth and vanishes on the diagonal, and by Hadamard's lemma the quotient by $u - v$ is smooth (the bracket is $\tfrac{1}{12}S_{\Phi}(u - v)^{2} + O((u - v)^{3})$, so the kernel's leading term is $\tfrac{I_{4}}{2\pi}\cdot\tfrac{1}{12}S_{\Phi}(u - v)$; the expansion is the stored computation of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.local-normality-at-the-edge-gives-the-null-ground-state}(iii)). So the remainder relative to the null vacuum of $\tilde\lambda$ is smooth on $\Psi(J)\times\Psi(J)$, the generator microlocal condition holds in the physical affine parameter, and by Theorem \claimref{synthesis.microlocal-condition-on-a-generator-gives-the-local-null-ground-state}(ii) the scaling limit at every point of the generator is the null vacuum: the local form of (N$_{+}$). This holds at every point of every null plane of the chart in $U$. A local Rindler horizon at $x_{0}$ is the null surface through a spacelike two-surface element at $x_{0}$ that is expansion-free at $x_{0}$; the chart's null plane with the same tangent at $x_{0}$ has physical expansion proportional to $\partial_{k}\varphi$ there, so the two agree at the leading order, tangent at $x_{0}$, at which (C$_{\mathrm{loc}}$) and the scaling-limit premise (HS) define the horizon, and the scaling limit at $x_{0}$ (the only thing the local form uses) is computed at that order, where it is the null vacuum on the common tangent generator. The statement is not made beyond that order.

\emph{(iii)} For $\varphi = a(t)$ and a null line $x_{0} + \lambda k$ with $k\cdot e_{0} = 1$, $t = t_{0} + \lambda$ and $\Psi' = a(t)^{2}$ along it. The conformal vacuum is the flat vacuum of $\chi$, whose restriction is exactly $W_{0}\otimes\id_{K}$ in $\lambda$ ($R = 0$), so in $\tilde\lambda$ it is the null vacuum plus the Schwarzian kernel above. By the bracket expansion of (ii) its leading term, read in the physical parameter, is $\tfrac{I_{4}}{2\pi}\cdot\tfrac{1}{12}S_{\Phi}(u - v)$, with the Schwarzian of $\Phi = \Psi^{-1}$ given by the inversion rule $S_{\Phi}(\tilde\lambda) = -S_{\Psi}(\lambda)/\Psi'(\lambda)^{2} = -S_{\Psi}/a^{4}$ (standard; computed: for the radiation $\Psi$ and rational $\lambda$ points the squared ratio in $\tilde\lambda$, which is the reciprocal of the one in $\lambda$ at the image points, satisfies $(r^{-2} - 1)/\tilde x^{2} \to -S_{\Psi}/(6a^{4})$ at rate $\tilde x$), so it vanishes exactly where $S_{\Psi}$ does; equivalently the null vacua of $\lambda$ and $\tilde\lambda$ differ by the weight-$\tfrac12$ Schwarzian kernel of $\Psi$, read in either parameter. With $\Psi' = a^{2}$, $\Psi'' = 2aa'$, $\Psi''' = 2a'^{2} + 2aa''$:
\[
S_{\Psi} = \frac{2a'^{2} + 2aa''}{a^{2}} - \frac32\cdot\frac{4a'^{2}}{a^{2}} = \frac{2a''}{a} - \frac{4a'^{2}}{a^{2}}, \qquad \Bigl(\frac1a\Bigr)'' = \frac{2a'^{2} - aa''}{a^{3}}, \qquad S_{\Psi} = -2a\Bigl(\frac1a\Bigr)'' ,
\]
checked at rational times for four polynomial $a$ by computing $\Psi'$, $\Psi''$, $\Psi'''$ from $a^{2}$, and as identities of rational functions for $a = t^{n}$, $-3 \le n \le 3$, and $a = 1/(\alpha t + \beta)$. Hence $S_{\Psi} \equiv 0$ on an interval exactly when $1/a = \alpha t + \beta$ there; then $\Psi = -1/(\alpha(\alpha t + \beta))$ (or $\beta^{-2}t$ for $\alpha = 0$) is a M\"obius map of $\lambda$, the weight-$\tfrac12$ kernel $\tfrac{I_{4}}{2\pi}(u - v + I_{4}0)^{-1}$ is invariant under it ($\Psi'(u)\Psi'(v)(u - v)^{2} = (\Psi(u) - \Psi(v))^{2}$ exactly, checked), and the conformal vacuum is the null vacuum of $\tilde\lambda$, the ground state of the physical affine translations (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.positive-null-energy-gives-r-premises}(i)). In conformal time de Sitter is $a = -1/(Ht)$ ($\alpha = -H$, $\beta = 0$) and the flat field is $a$ constant. For $a = t^{n}$, $a'/a = n/t$ and $a''/a = n(n - 1)/t^{2}$ give $S_{\Psi} = -2n(n + 1)/t^{2}$, zero exactly for $n \in \{0, -1\}$ (computed as rational functions from the derivatives of $a^{2}$ for $-3 \le n \le 3$); radiation ($a \propto t$) gives $-4/t^{2}$ and matter ($a \propto t^{2}$) $-12/t^{2}$. For radiation the check computes the squared ratio $\Psi'(u)\Psi'(v)(u - v)^{2}/(\Psi(u) - \Psi(v))^{2}$ with $\Psi = ((t_{0} + \lambda)^{3} - t_{0}^{3})/3$ on $x = u - v = 10^{-j}$: it differs from $1$, and $(r^{2} - 1)/x^{2} - S_{\Psi}/6$ decreases to zero at rate $x$, so the remainder is the nonzero Schwarzian kernel. Then the conformal vacuum is not the ground state of the physical affine translations on any interval of a radiation- or matter-dominated generator, while it satisfies the local form by (ii).
