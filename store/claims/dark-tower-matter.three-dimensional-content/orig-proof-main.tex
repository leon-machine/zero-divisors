\textit{(i)}\enspace The cells are single-occupation states. The charges $q_{\pm} = (s \pm p)/2$ send
\[
(s, p) = (1, 1) \;\mapsto\; (q_{+}, q_{-}) = (1, 0), \qquad (1, -1) \;\mapsto\; (0, 1) \qquad \text{(F2)},
\]
and the pair reflection $p \mapsto -p$ exchanges them.



\emph{(ii)}  A product of cells adds weights, and each cell has $s + p \in \{-2, 0, 2\}$ (F1).



\emph{(iii)}  A constituent-level state occupies one slot mode and no pair mode, so its weight is $(\pm 1, 0)$, off the lattice $s + p$ even (Remark \ref{rem:S35}). The weights of the transported directions are computed in the supplementary script \file{D3R} (checks H5 to H9): rung directions $(\pm 1, 0)$, pair directions $(0, \pm 1)$, the leftover generator $(0,0)$.



\emph{(iv)}  This is the odd case of T14's Proposition 3.9. A mass term $\bar{A}A$ has weight zero, and a neutral product of cells pairs each cell with its conjugate.
