Let $\mathcal{M}$ be a rain-bounded boundary-tame extension. By Theorem \claimref{sheaf.uniqueness-weighted-module}, $\mathcal{M} = \shW$; and $\shW$ is the maximal rain-bounded extension because rain-boundedness is, by Definition \claimref{obj.sheaf.extensions} and Proposition \claimref{sheaf.rain-duality}(3), exactly the inclusion $\mathcal{M} \subseteq \shW$. The remaining clauses restate Theorems \claimref{sheaf.one-sided-completion-singularity} and \claimref{sheaf.uniqueness-weighted-module} and Example \ref{ex:nullmix}.
