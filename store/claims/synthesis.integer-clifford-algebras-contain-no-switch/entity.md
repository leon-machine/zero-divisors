## formal
For a commutative ring $R$ and $n = p + q \ge 1$ let $\Cl_{R}(p,q)$ be the free $R$-module on the blades $e_{S}$, $S \subseteq \{1, \dots, n\}$, with the product $e_{S}e_{T} = (-1)^{f(S,T)}e_{S \triangle T}$, $f(S,T) = \#\{(i,j) \in S \times T : i > j\} + |S \cap T \cap N|$, $N = \{p + 1, \dots, n\}$ (the algebra of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.division-gives-the-rationals}). A \emph{switch} is a pair $(a, b)$ with $a^{2} = b^{2} = 0$, $ab + ba = 1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $\Cl_{\Z}(p,q)$ has no idempotent other than $0$ and $1$.
\item[(ii)] If $ab + ba = 1$ for some $a, b \in \Cl_{R}(p,q)$, then $2$ is a unit of $R$. In particular $\Cl_{\Z}(p,q)$ contains no switch.
\item[(iii)] If $2$ is a unit of $R$ and $p, q \ge 1$, then $\tfrac12(e_{1} + e_{p+1})$, $\tfrac12(e_{1} - e_{p+1})$ is a switch.
\item[(iv)] Let $M$ be the free $\Z$-module on two states with set $v$ and clear $w$. The homomorphism $\Cl_{\Z}(1,1) \to \mathrm{End}_{\Z}(M)$ with $e_{1} \mapsto v + w$, $e_{2} \mapsto v - w$ is injective, and its image is the integer matrices whose diagonal entries have the same parity and whose off-diagonal entries have the same parity, a subring of index $4$.
\end{enumerate}

## technical
Reduce the integer Clifford algebra modulo $2$: the signs disappear, the algebra becomes the commutative group algebra of the configurations, and squaring becomes the map sending an element to the sum of its coefficients. An idempotent therefore reduces to $0$ or $1$, and an idempotent divisible by $2$ is divisible by every power of $2$, hence zero. In a commutative ring $ab + ba = 2ab$, so an anticommutator equal to $1$ forces $2$ to be invertible. So the integer algebra generated by directions of square $\pm 1$ contains no projection and no set-and-clear pair; they appear exactly when one half is allowed. For one switch the orthonormal algebra sits inside the integer matrices as the sublattice with matching parities, of index $4$.

## lay
Build the algebra of directions using whole numbers only. It can flip a two-state system from one state to the other, but it contains no operation that asks ``is it on?'' and no operation that switches on or switches off. Those appear only once halves are allowed. Starting instead from the switch gives them straight away, with whole numbers.

## one-liner
$\Cl_{\Z}(p,q)$ has no idempotents but $0, 1$ and no pair with $ab + ba = 1$ (that forces $2$ to be a unit, by reduction mod $2$ to the commutative group algebra); with $\tfrac12$ the half-vectors $\tfrac12(e_{1} \pm e_{p+1})$ are a switch, and $\Cl_{\Z}(1,1)$ has index $4$ in the integer matrices of one switch.
