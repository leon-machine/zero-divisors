## formal
Let $\mathcal{R} = H_{4} \oplus H_{5}$ be the rung summand of the transported ambient space of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-tower-commuting-copies-fock-module}, two adjoined hyperbolic planes with frame $e_{a}^{\pm}$, $Q(e_{a}^{\pm}) = \pm 1$, mode vectors $v_{a} = \tfrac12(e_{a}^{-} + e_{a}^{+})$, $w_{a} = \tfrac12(e_{a}^{+} - e_{a}^{-})$ ($a = 4, 5$), so $v_{a}w_{b} + w_{b}v_{a} = \delta_{ab}$, and let $*$ be the tower's conjugate-linear antiautomorphism, on vectors $x^{*} = \theta(\bar{x})$ with $\theta$ fixing $e_{a}^{+}$ and negating $e_{a}^{-}$, so $v_{a}^{*} = w_{a}$. Let $E_{ab} = v_{a}w_{b}$ act on vectors by commutator, and let $\uu(2)$ be the real span of $I_{4}(E_{ab} + E_{ba})$, $E_{ab} - E_{ba}$, $I_{4}E_{aa}$, the slot mixing of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.ten-transported-ambient-space-doublets-are}, acting on $\mathcal{R} \otimes \C = \mathcal{R} \oplus I_{4}\mathcal{R}$ ($\C = \mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$, the unit being the field theory's unit $I_{4}$, which commutes with the rung vectors) by $a\cdot v + b\cdot w \mapsto (Xa)\cdot v + (\bar{X}b)\cdot w$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{(The real summand carries the split form.)} The real span of the $E_{ab}$ is $\gl(2,\R) \subset \so(\mathcal{R}) = \so(2,2)$, acting on the real slice $\mathcal{R}$. The subalgebra of $\uu(2)$ preserving $\mathcal{R}$ is one-dimensional, spanned by $E_{45} - E_{54}$; in particular the compact $U(1)$ generated by the hypercharge does not preserve $\mathcal{R}$, on whose rung vectors the hypercharge acts with the real weights $\pm 3$, as a boost.
\item \emph{(No compact weak connection on the real summand.)} The trace form $\tr(XY)$ of $\so(2,2)$ on $\mathcal{R}$ has signature $(4,2)$, and $\so(2,2)$ contains no subalgebra isomorphic to $\su(2)$. Hence no group of isometries of $(\mathcal{R}, Q)$ is locally $SU(2)$, and no connection on the real rung summand preserving its metric, flat or curved, has holonomy containing an $SU(2)$.
\item \emph{(No positive invariant kinetic form.)} The symmetric bilinear forms on $\mathcal{R}$ invariant under $\gl(2,\R)$, and a fortiori under $\so(2,2)$, are the multiples of the metric, of signature $(2,2)$: a kinetic term $k\,Q(\nabla c)$ for an $\mathcal{R}$-valued field $c$ has two directions of each sign for either sign of $k$. The form $\ip{\theta x}{y}$ is positive definite, and needs $\theta$, that is, the frame.
\item \emph{(The rung unit is the frame's boost.)} Take the lift of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.transported-frozen-point}(i) in the null pair $(n_{0}', n_{\infty}') = (-\lambda w_{4},\, 2\lambda^{-1}v_{4})$, $\lambda > 0$, the proposition's being $\lambda = 1$. Its rung component is $c_{\lambda} = -\lambda w_{4} + \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}\lambda^{-1}v_{4}$, with
\[
Q(c_{\lambda}) = -\frac{\Lambda}{3}\ \text{ for every } \lambda, \qquad \ip{\theta c_{\lambda}}{c_{\lambda}} = \tfrac12\Bigl(\lambda^{2} + \frac{\Lambda^{2}}{9}\lambda^{-2}\Bigr) = \frac{\Lambda}{3} + \tfrac12\Bigl(\lambda - \frac{\Lambda}{3}\lambda^{-1}\Bigr)^{2}.
\]
\item \emph{(Unitary only when balanced.)} The $U(2)$-invariant real forms of $(\mathcal{R} \otimes \C, Q)$ on which $Q$ is real are $\mathcal{R}_{\mu} = \{a\cdot v + \mu\bar{a}\cdot w\}$, $\mu \in \R \setminus \{0\}$, of signature $(4,0)$ for $\mu > 0$ and $(0,4)$ for $\mu < 0$; the tower's $*$ fixes $\mathcal{R}_{1}$. For $\Lambda > 0$ the complex line $\C c_{\lambda}$ meets $\mathcal{R}_{1} \cup \mathcal{R}_{-1}$ if and only if $\lambda^{2} = \Lambda/3$, equivalently $\ip{\theta c_{\lambda}}{c_{\lambda}} = |Q(c_{\lambda})|$, and then $I_{4}c_{\lambda} \in \mathcal{R}_{1}$.
\end{enumerate}

## technical
The two hyperbolic planes that transport adjoins for the rungs form a real space of signature $(2,2)$, and the series' slot mixing, the group the Higgs doublet's representation is read from, acts on it only after complexification. On the real summand itself the slot generators span the split algebra $\gl(2,\R)$; only a one-dimensional rotation of the compact $\uu(2)$ preserves the real summand, and the hypercharge acts there as a boost. Since $\so(2,2)$ has trace form of signature $(4,2)$ and so no room for a compact $\su(2)$, no metric connection of the real rung summand, whatever its curvature, can be the weak gauge field, and the only invariant kinetic form is the metric itself, indefinite. A positive kinetic form and the compact real form on which the weak group acts both need the tower's $*$, that is, the frame. The store does not propose a metric connection of the real summand as the weak connection, the weak group acting on the complexification; clause (2) records why that reading is closed. The frame carries a scale. In the lift of the frozen point, a boost of the null pair leaves the tractor norm $-\Lambda/3$ of the rung component unchanged and changes its $*$-norm, which is $\Lambda/3 + \tfrac12(\lambda - \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}\lambda^{-1})^{2}$; the rung component lies in a $U(2)$-invariant real form, up to a phase, only at the balanced boost $\lambda^{2} = \Lambda/3$, where its $*$-norm is $\Lambda/3$.

## lay
The extra directions that transport adds, where the series finds the Higgs doublet, have a split geometry: two directions count positively and two negatively. The symmetry of the weak force is a rotation-like symmetry that needs an all-positive geometry, so it cannot act on these directions as they stand, and no energy expression built from their own geometry is positive. Both problems are cured by one extra choice the series already makes, a preferred frame. That frame comes with a scale, which the geometry of these directions does not see. On a reading argued in the proof but not proved, that scale is the unexplained ratio of the weak scale to the cosmological scale, and a frame matched to the cosmological scale would make the weak scale as small as the Hubble rate.

## one-liner
The real rung summand is $\R^{2,2}$, on which the slot algebra acts as $\gl(2,\R)$ and $\so(2,2)$ holds no $\su(2)$ and no positive invariant kinetic form; the compact form needs the tower's frame, and the frame's boost is invisible to the rung component's tractor norm $-\Lambda/3$ but sets its $*$-norm $\Lambda/3 + \tfrac12(\lambda - \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}\lambda^{-1})^{2}$, so the rung unit is that boost.
