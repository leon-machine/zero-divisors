\emph{Setting.} Proposition \claimref{synthesis.operator-join-ordering-constants} fixes the oscillators, $[Z^{\alpha}_{i}, \bar{Z}_{l\beta}] = \hbar\,\delta_{il}\delta^{\alpha}_{\beta}$, the bracket $\hat{B} = \br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}} + \lambda[\omega^{1}\omega^{2}]$ with $\br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}} = \epsilon^{A'B'}\pi^{1}_{A'}\pi^{2}_{B'}$ and $[\omega^{1}\omega^{2}] = \epsilon_{AB}\omega^{1A}\omega^{2B}$, its conjugate $\hat{B}^{\dagger}$ (the same polynomial in the $\bar{Z}$, with $\bar{\pi}_{A} = \bar{Z}_{A}$ and $\bar{\omega}^{A'} = \bar{Z}_{A+2}$, as in the check of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.operator-join-seed-has-no-diagonal-matrix-elements}), the symmetrised Gram operators $\hat{h}_{il} = \tfrac12(\bar{Z}_{i}\cdot Z^{l} + Z^{l}\cdot\bar{Z}_{i})$, and the palatial form of clause (3) there, together with the commutator $[\hat{B}, \hat{B}^{\dagger}] = \hbar\lambda(\hat{h}_{11} + \hat{h}_{22})$. By Fact \claimref{mass.imported-palatial-quadratics-homogeneity-charge} and Proposition \claimref{synthesis.operator-join-casimir-shifts}(ii), $\hat{h}_{ii} = 2s_{i}\hbar$ on the helicity-$s_{i}$ module of constituent $i$.

\emph{The positive-energy realisation.} Proposition \claimref{field-theory.twistor-attachment} supplies the one-particle space of each constituent as the oscillator model: a Fock space on which the palatial quadratics act, with the helicity-$s$ module at energies $|s| + 1 + n$ and multiplicities $(n+1)(n + 2|s| + 1)$. The split of the four oscillators into annihilation and creation operators is the one the positive Hermitian structure makes: by Proposition \claimref{synthesis.positive-structure-is-twistor-form-at-imaginary-time}, the positive structure on conformal spinor space is the twistor form $\Sigma$ polarised by a forward-tube point, $h = \Sigma$ on one incidence line and $-\Sigma$ on the conjugate line; the two $\Sigma$-positive directions carry annihilation operators $a_{A}$ with $(a_{A})^{\dagger}$ their $\Sigma$-conjugates, and the two $\Sigma$-negative directions creation operators $b^{\dagger}_{A}$, which is the only assignment for which the canonical relations $[Z, \bar{Z}] = \hbar$ hold with $\bar{Z}$ the adjoint of the $\Sigma$-conjugate component and the vacuum is of positive energy. In the off-diagonal basis of the twistor form ($\bar{Z}\cdot Z = \bar{\pi}_{A}\omega^{A} + \bar{\omega}^{A'}\pi_{A'}$) the $\Sigma$-positive directions are $\omega^{A} + \pi_{A}$ and the negative ones $\omega^{A} - \pi_{A}$ (indices identified by the tube point's time direction), which gives the realisation stated: $\omega^{A} = a_{A} + b^{\dagger}_{A}$, $\pi_{A'} = a_{A} - b^{\dagger}_{A}$, $\bar{\pi}_{A} = a^{\dagger}_{A} - b_{A}$, $\bar{\omega}^{A'} = a^{\dagger}_{A} + b_{A}$, with $[a_{A}, a^{\dagger}_{B}] = [b_{A}, b^{\dagger}_{B}] = \tfrac{\hbar}{2}\delta_{AB}$. That this is the ladder (oscillator) construction of the positive-energy massless representations is standard (cited from memory). The check realises it on polynomials in eight variables, $a_{A} = \partial/\partial x_{A}$, $a^{\dagger}_{A} = x_{A}$, $b_{A} = \partial/\partial y_{A}$, $b^{\dagger}_{A} = y_{A}$, per constituent, with the Fock inner product $\langle x^{m}y^{n}, x^{m}y^{n}\rangle = m!\,n!$ and $\hbar = 2$ so that no square root enters; the algebraic Fock space $\mathcal{F}_{0}$ is the polynomial ring, graded by the total degree, which is the oscillator number $N$.

\emph{(i).} The canonical relations hold with $\hbar$: $[\omega^{A}, \bar{\pi}_{B}] = [a_{A} + b^{\dagger}_{A}, a^{\dagger}_{B} - b_{B}] = \tfrac{\hbar}{2}\delta_{AB} + \tfrac{\hbar}{2}\delta_{AB}$, $[\omega^{A}, \bar{\omega}^{B'}] = \tfrac{\hbar}{2}\delta_{AB} - \tfrac{\hbar}{2}\delta_{AB} = 0$, and likewise for $\pi$ (checked). The adjoint of $\omega^{A} = a_{A} + b^{\dagger}_{A}$ is $a^{\dagger}_{A} + b_{A} = \bar{\omega}^{A'} = \bar{Z}_{\sigma(A)}$ and of $\pi_{A'} = a_{A} - b^{\dagger}_{A}$ it is $\bar{\pi}_{A} = \bar{Z}_{\sigma(A+2)}$, by the definition of annihilation and creation operators; that this split is the attachment's oscillator model, the twistor form being the grading of the central rotation against the Hilbert form, with the stored energies and multiplicities, is proved once, filed alongside, synthesis.attachment-conformal-energy-is-generated-by-z, and is cited, not repeated (the check keeps one sample of the adjoint identity as a consistency test of its own conventions). Since $\hat{B}^{\dagger}$ is the conjugate polynomial, it is the adjoint of $\hat{B}$; $\hat{h}_{il}$ is a sum of $\bar{Z}_{i\alpha}Z^{\alpha}_{l}$ symmetrised, whose adjoint is $\hat{h}_{li}$. Expanding, $\hat{h}_{ii} = \sum_{A}\bigl(\tfrac12\{a^{\dagger}_{A} - b_{A}, a_{A} + b^{\dagger}_{A}\} + \tfrac12\{a^{\dagger}_{A} + b_{A}, a_{A} - b^{\dagger}_{A}\}\bigr) = \sum_{A}(\{a^{\dagger}_{A}, a_{A}\} - \{b_{A}, b^{\dagger}_{A}\}) = \hbar(N_{a,i} - N_{b,i})$ (the cross terms cancel between the two groups and $\{a^{\dagger}, a\} = 2a^{\dagger}a + \tfrac{\hbar}{2}$), and $\hat{h}_{12} = \sum_{\alpha}\bar{Z}_{1\alpha}Z^{\alpha}_{2} = 2\sum_{A}(a^{\dagger}_{1A}a_{2A} - b^{\dagger}_{2A}b_{1A})$ (the factors commute for $i \ne l$), which preserves $N$. So $\hat{h}_{ii}$ has spectrum $\hbar\Z$ and $s_{i} = \tfrac12(N_{a,i} - N_{b,i}) \in \tfrac12\Z$; a sector $(s_{1}, s_{2})$ meets each level $N$ in a finite-dimensional space, on which the $\hat{h}_{il}$ act with $\hat{h}_{12}^{\dagger} = \hat{h}_{21}$, so the internal $U(2)$ acts unitarily and completely reducibly on $\mathcal{F}_{0}$. The check verifies the relations, $\hat{h}_{ii} = \hbar(N_{a,i} - N_{b,i})$ and the degree preservation on all monomials of degree at most two.

\emph{(ii).} $\hat{B}\hat{B}^{\dagger} = \hat{B}^{\dagger}\hat{B} + [\hat{B}, \hat{B}^{\dagger}] = \hat{B}^{\dagger}\hat{B} + \hbar\lambda\tr\hat{h}$ by the stored commutator, which is an identity in the oscillator algebra and so holds in every realisation of the canonical relations. Substituting in the palatial form gives the displayed form of $\hat{m}^{2}$. Put $J_{3} = \tfrac12(\hat{h}_{11} - \hat{h}_{22})$, $J_{+} = \hat{h}_{12}$, $J_{-} = \hat{h}_{21}$. From the canonical relations, $[\hat{h}_{il}, \hat{h}_{mn}] = \hbar(\delta_{lm}\hat{h}_{in} - \delta_{ni}\hat{h}_{ml})$ (the derivation property used in the proof of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.operator-join-seed-has-no-diagonal-matrix-elements}), so $[J_{3}, J_{\pm}] = \pm\hbar J_{\pm}$ and $[J_{+}, J_{-}] = 2\hbar J_{3}$: the standard $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ with $\hbar$ as the unit. Then $\hat{C} = \tfrac12(\hat{h}_{11}^{2} + \hat{h}_{22}^{2} + \hat{h}_{12}\hat{h}_{21} + \hat{h}_{21}\hat{h}_{12}) - \tfrac14(\hat{h}_{11} + \hat{h}_{22})^{2} = J_{3}^{2} + \tfrac12(J_{+}J_{-} + J_{-}J_{+})$, the Casimir, equal to $j(j+1)\hbar^{2}$ on every spin-$j$ isotype of a completely reducible module (a highest-weight vector $v$ with $J_{3}v = j\hbar v$, $J_{+}v = 0$ has $\hat{C}v = (J_{3}^{2} + \hbar J_{3} + J_{-}J_{+})v = j(j+1)\hbar^{2}v$). On the isotype $(s_{1}, s_{2}, j)$, $\tr\hat{h} = 2(s_{1} + s_{2})\hbar$ and $J_{3} = (s_{1} - s_{2})\hbar$, so $j \ge |s_{1} - s_{2}|$ with $j - (s_{1} - s_{2})$ an integer, and $\hbar\lambda\tr\hat{h} + \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}\hat{C} - \tfrac{2\Lambda}{3}\hbar^{2} = \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}\hbar^{2}(s_{1} + s_{2} + j(j+1) - 2)$, using $2\lambda = \Lambda/3$. The check verifies the displayed identity, $[\hat{B}, \hat{B}^{\dagger}] = \hbar\lambda\tr\hat{h}$, $[\hat{h}_{il}, \hat{m}^{2}] = 0$ and $[J_{3}, J_{+}] = \hbar J_{+}$ on all monomials of degree at most two, and on twenty-seven highest-weight monomials $x_{10}^{a}x_{11}^{b}y_{20}^{c}$ (helicities $\tfrac12(a+b)$, $-\tfrac12 c$, spin $\tfrac12(a+b+c)$) that $\hat{C} = j(j+1)\hbar^{2}$ and that $\hat{m}^{2} - 2\hat{B}^{\dagger}\hat{B}$ is the stated constant. For one constituent the same bookkeeping gives the stored $H^{2}(s^{2} - 1)\hbar^{2}$ of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.operator-join-casimir-shifts}; for two the constant vanishes at $(1, 1, 0)$ and at the isotypes listed in (iii).

\emph{(iii).} By (i), for $\psi \in \mathcal{F}_{0}$ in the isotype, $\langle\psi, \hat{B}^{\dagger}\hat{B}\psi\rangle = \langle\hat{B}\psi, \hat{B}\psi\rangle \ge 0$ with equality iff $\hat{B}\psi = 0$, and $\hat{m}^{2}\psi - 2\hat{B}^{\dagger}\hat{B}\psi$ is the constant times $\psi$ by (ii); this gives the inequality and its equality case. The join preserves each isotype (it commutes with every $\hat{h}_{il}$) and $\mathcal{F}_{0}$, so the restricted quadratic form is bounded below by $c := \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}\hbar^{2}(s_{1} + s_{2} + j(j+1) - 2)$ on the dense domain $\mathcal{F}_{0} \cap$ isotype; the spectrum of its Friedrichs extension lies in $[c, \infty)$ (standard, from memory), so for $c > 0$ neither an eigenvector nor a point of the spectrum sits at zero. The arithmetic condition: $c \le 0$ needs $s_{1} + s_{2} + j(j+1) \le 2$ for some admissible $j$, and the least admissible value is $j = |s_{1} - s_{2}|$, for which $s_{1} + s_{2} + |s_{1} - s_{2}|(|s_{1} - s_{2}| + 1) = 2\max(s_{1}, s_{2}) + (s_{1} - s_{2})^{2}$. So $c \le 0$ for some $j$ iff $2\max(s_{1}, s_{2}) + (s_{1} - s_{2})^{2} \le 2$, which forces $\max(s_{1}, s_{2}) \le 1$; the check enumerates both directions over $|2s_{i}| \le 8$. The two readings of the massless locus (kernel of $\hat{B}$ at $c = 0$, an eigenspace of $2\hat{B}^{\dagger}\hat{B}$ at $c < 0$) are immediate from (ii).

\emph{(iv).} Write $[a_{1}a_{2}] = \epsilon^{AB}a_{1A}a_{2B}$ and similarly for the other brackets. Substituting the realisation,
\[
\hat{B} \;=\; (1 + \lambda)\bigl([a_{1}a_{2}] + [b^{\dagger}_{1}b^{\dagger}_{2}]\bigr) \;+\; (\lambda - 1)\bigl([a_{1}b^{\dagger}_{2}] + [b^{\dagger}_{1}a_{2}]\bigr),
\]
since $\br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}} = \epsilon^{AB}(a_{1A} - b^{\dagger}_{1A})(a_{2B} - b^{\dagger}_{2B})$ and $[\omega^{1}\omega^{2}] = \epsilon_{AB}(a_{1A} + b^{\dagger}_{1A})(a_{2B} + b^{\dagger}_{2B})$ (the two $\epsilon$ agree numerically in the check's components). The four terms change the oscillator number $N$ by $-2$, $+2$, $0$, $0$, and $\hat{B}^{\dagger}$ by $+2$ (the term $(1+\lambda)[a^{\dagger}_{1}a^{\dagger}_{2}]$), $-2$, $0$, $0$. So $\hat{B}^{\dagger}\hat{B}$ and $\hat{B}\hat{B}^{\dagger}$ change $N$ by $-4, -2, 0, 2, 4$, and the part raising it by four is, in each, the product of the two $N$-raising terms, $(1+\lambda)^{2}[a^{\dagger}_{1}a^{\dagger}_{2}][b^{\dagger}_{1}b^{\dagger}_{2}]$ (the two brackets commute, so the order does not matter); $\hat{C}$ and the constant preserve $N$ by (i). Hence the $+4$ band of $\hat{m}^{2}$ is $T_{+4} = 2(1+\lambda)^{2}[a^{\dagger}_{1}a^{\dagger}_{2}][b^{\dagger}_{1}b^{\dagger}_{2}]$. In the polynomial model $T_{+4}$ is multiplication by $2(1+\lambda)^{2}(x_{10}x_{21} - x_{11}x_{20})(y_{10}y_{21} - y_{11}y_{20})$, a nonzero polynomial when $\lambda \ne -1$; the polynomial ring is an integral domain, so $T_{+4}$ is injective on $\mathcal{F}_{0}$. Now let $\psi \in \mathcal{F}_{0}$ be nonzero, $\mu \in \C$, and let $\psi_{T}$ be the component of $\psi$ of highest oscillator number $T$. The component of $(\hat{m}^{2} - \mu)\psi$ at number $T + 4$ is $T_{+4}\psi_{T} \ne 0$, since no other band and no multiple of the identity reaches $T + 4$ from numbers at most $T$. So $(\hat{m}^{2} - \mu)\psi \ne 0$: the kernel of $\hat{m}^{2} - \mu$ on $\mathcal{F}_{0}$ is zero for every $\mu$, in every sector (the sectors and isotypes are direct summands of $\mathcal{F}_{0}$, each spanned by monomials, so the same top-component argument applies within each). This uses only $\lambda \ne -1$, so it holds for every $\Lambda \ge 0$, in particular at $\Lambda = 0$, and for $\Lambda < 0$ at every Fock scale except the matched one. The check verifies the band structure and the top band on all monomials of degree at most two, that the top band is nonzero on each of them, and that the kernels of $\hat{m}^{2} - \mu$ on the polynomials of degree at most two are zero for $\mu = 0$ and for the three isotype constants $-\tfrac{\Lambda}{3}\hbar^{2}$, $-\tfrac{2\Lambda}{3}\hbar^{2}$, $-\tfrac{4\Lambda}{3}\hbar^{2}$ of the sectors $(\tfrac12, \tfrac12, 0)$, $(0, 0, 0)$, $(-1, -1, 0)$, together with the kernel of $T_{+4}$. In the polynomial realisation $\bar{Z} = -\hbar\partial$ of the earlier computation, by contrast, the constant is a zero mode: there $\hat{B}^{\dagger}$ is a differential operator, $2\hat{B}^{\dagger}\hat{B}\,1 = 8\lambda = \tfrac{4\Lambda}{3}$ (with $\hbar = 1$) cancels the constant $-\tfrac{4\Lambda}{3}$ of the sector $(-1, -1, 0)$, and the control in the check reproduces this; on the positive-energy modules the sector $(-1, -1)$ exists (two $b$-quanta per constituent) but, by the above, carries no eigenvector of finite energy content when $\Lambda \ge 0$.

\emph{The case $\Lambda < 0$.} The scale $\lambda$ is measured in the Fock scale of the chosen tube point, and for a frozen point of negative $\Lambda$ there is a Fock scale at which $\lambda = -1$ (in general $\lambda s^{4} = -1$ for the scale $s$ of the oscillators). There the pure terms of $\hat{B}$ cancel and $\hat{B} = -2([a_{1}b^{\dagger}_{2}] + [b^{\dagger}_{1}a_{2}])$, which preserves the oscillator number; so does $\hat{m}^{2}$, which then acts on each finite level and has pure point spectrum with an orthonormal eigenbasis. Its kernel on the level $N = 2$ is three-dimensional: the check computes the kernel exactly at $\Lambda = -6$ ($\hbar = 2$) and finds one zero mode in each of the sectors $(\tfrac12, -\tfrac12)$, $(-\tfrac12, \tfrac12)$ and $(0, 0)$, each of internal spin $1$ (so the isotype constant $s_{1} + s_{2} + j(j+1) - 2$ vanishes, consistent with (ii)) and each in $\ker\hat{B}$; the $(0, 0)$ one is $(a^{\dagger}_{1}\cdot b^{\dagger}_{1} - a^{\dagger}_{2}\cdot b^{\dagger}_{2})\Omega$, verified explicitly, and it is not a zero mode at $\Lambda = 3$. At a mismatched scale these zero modes are unitarily equivalent to infinite sums of number states, which is why the top-band argument does not see them. So for $\Lambda < 0$ the join has normalisable zero modes; at level two the three sectors all have $s_{1} + s_{2} = 0$, so no two of them are neighbours in the sense of the seed (differing by $(\tfrac12, \tfrac12)$), and higher levels are not computed here; the series takes $\Lambda > 0$.

\emph{(v) (argued).} The dilation $x \mapsto \rho x$ of the flat chart acts on the conformal model by $n_{0} \mapsto \rho n_{0}$, $n_{\infty} \mapsto \rho^{-1}n_{\infty}$, so $I_{1} = n_{\infty} + n_{0} \mapsto \rho^{-1}(n_{\infty} + \rho^{2}n_{0}) = \rho^{-1}I_{\rho^{2}}$; the grip is linear in the frozen point and the join quadratic, so for $\lambda > 0$, $\hat{m}^{2}_{\lambda} = \lambda\,U\hat{m}^{2}_{1}U^{-1}$ with $U$ the unitary implementing the dilation on $\mathcal{F}$ (an element of $SU(2,2)$ acting linearly on the oscillators and preserving the twistor form is implemented by the metaplectic representation); dilations rescale $\lambda$ by positive factors only, so for $\lambda < 0$ the same argument gives $\hat{m}^{2}_{\lambda} = |\lambda|\,U\hat{m}^{2}_{-1}U^{-1}$, the number-conserving form of the previous paragraph. At $\lambda = 1$ the mixed terms of $\hat{B}$ drop and $\hat{B} = 2([a_{1}a_{2}] + [b^{\dagger}_{1}b^{\dagger}_{2}])$. On the generalised states: for $\lambda = 1$ the exponential $\exp\bigl(\tfrac{2I_{4}}{\hbar}\sum_{i,A}a^{\dagger}_{iA}b^{\dagger}_{iA}\bigr)$ applied to the vacuum, in the sector $(0, 0, 0)$, is formally annihilated by $\hat{B}$ (each $a_{iA}$ acting on it produces $I_{4}b^{\dagger}_{iA}$, so $[a_{1}a_{2}]$ produces $-[b^{\dagger}_{1}b^{\dagger}_{2}]$), and it is not normalisable; it is a generalised eigenvector of the join with eigenvalue $-\tfrac{2\Lambda}{3}\hbar^{2}$, not a massless one. For $\Lambda > 0$, (iv) does not exclude normalisable eigenvectors with infinite energy content; the point-spectrum result filed alongside (synthesis.operator-join-point-spectrum-on-positive-energy-modules), through the unitary $\mathfrak{su}(1,1)$ spanned by $\hat{B}$, $\hat{B}^{\dagger}$ and $\tr\hat{h}$, shows, modulo a cited decomposition theorem, that such eigenvectors exist, that the least eigenvalue is $\tfrac{\Lambda}{3}\hbar^{2}$, and hence that no normalisable zero mode exists; its continuum threshold also empties the three isotypes with vanishing constant that (iii) lists.
