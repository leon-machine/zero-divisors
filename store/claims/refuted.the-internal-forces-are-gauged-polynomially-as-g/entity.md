## formal
The internal forces are gauged polynomially, as gravity is

## technical
Refuted. The corrected statement: The internal curvature-squared invariant is topological; forces are metric-level Yang-Mills, gravity alone is pre-metric.

## lay
It was natural to guess that the forces of particle physics could be described by the same kind of polynomial formula the framework uses for gravity. That guess turned out to be wrong: the analogous quantity for those forces only records global, topological features and produces no equations of motion. The forces instead need ordinary Yang-Mills theory, which depends on the geometry of spacetime, while gravity alone can be written without it.
