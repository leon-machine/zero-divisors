## formal
Indifference forbids uncaused structure: a construction may not carry distinctions nothing in the framework determines. The reading of it as an instrument, that it equates weights along exact symmetry orbits and forbids uncaused selections and never equates inequivalent irreducible components, is the families paper's declared reading; that genuine moduli are data rather than smoothed away is the reading half of that paper's weight-moduli Fact, at observation level there, and its attachment to the instrument is this paper's. It is a structural premise of the series, not a theorem, and is flagged as such wherever this paper uses it; the indexicality clause (that ``why is ours the visible sector'' has no dynamical answer) is the dark-matter paper's premise form of it, scoped there to the reversal orbit. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Principle~5.4]{ButlerT8}; the reading \cite[Fact~2.5]{ButlerT13}, the moduli clause \cite[Fact~2.4]{ButlerT13}, the audit \cite[\S5]{ButlerT13}; the premise form \cite[Premise~2.2]{ButlerT14}.

## technical
Indifference is a structural premise of the series, not a theorem: a construction may not carry distinctions that nothing in the framework determines. This Fact imports it together with the families paper's reading of it as an instrument, which equates weights along exact symmetry orbits, $w(g \cdot c) = w(c)$ for an exact symmetry $g$, forbids uncaused selections, and never equates inequivalent irreducible components.

Two clauses are attached here. The moduli clause says that genuine moduli are data, not something the instrument smooths away; it is the reading half of the weight-moduli Fact and holds at observation level there. The indexicality clause says that the question why ours is the visible sector has no dynamical answer; it is the dark-matter paper's premise form of indifference, scoped there to the reversal orbit. Every use of the instrument in this paper is flagged as resting on the premise.

## lay
The series assumes that nature does not make arbitrary choices: if nothing in the theory tells two things apart, the theory may not treat them differently. In use, this means giving equal weight to things related by an exact symmetry, while never forcing genuinely different things to match. Numbers the theory leaves free are treated as facts to be measured rather than averaged away (a reading held at observation level), and the question of why we find ourselves in our sector rather than its mirror is treated as a matter of where we stand rather than of dynamics. All of this is an assumption of the programme, not a result.

## one-liner
Indifference, a structural premise and not a theorem, equates weights along exact symmetry orbits, leaves genuine moduli as data (an observation-level reading), and treats which of a mirror pair is ours as indexical (P2).
