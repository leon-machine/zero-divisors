By clauses (a) and (b) of Premise \claimref{standard-model.slot-module-hypothesis} the composite space is $V = F \otimes \Lambda^{\bullet}W$, $W$ the pair space of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-pair-systems-two-chirality-halves}, with $(A, B)$ acting as $A \otimes \Lambda^{\bullet}B$ and the hypercharge $Y = (3n - 2k)/6$ of Theorem \claimref{standard-model.global-form-quotient-determined}: exactly the setting of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.cpt-closure-forces-slot-module-and-chirality}.

(ii) and (iv). By clause (G) $F_{0} = \C$ and some $U(2)$-equivariant $a^{\dagger} : \C^{2} \otimes F_{n} \to F_{n+1}$ is onto for every $n$, so $F$ is generated in the sense of that theorem; by clause (CPT) the eigenspaces of the chirality form a CPT halving, so $V$ is isomorphic to its complex conjugate. Theorem \claimref{synthesis.cpt-closure-forces-slot-module-and-chirality}(3) then gives $F \cong \Lambda^{\bullet}\C^{2}$ as a graded $U(2)$-module and $a^{\dagger}(u)a^{\dagger}(u') = -a^{\dagger}(u')a^{\dagger}(u)$ for all $u, u'$, which is (iv); no anomaly condition enters. Then
\[
F \otimes \Lambda^{\bullet}W \;\cong\; \bigoplus_{j,k}\Lambda^{j}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{k}W \;=\; \bigoplus_{r}\Lambda^{r}(\C^{2} \oplus W) \;=\; \Lambda^{\bullet}(\C^{2} \oplus W),
\]
the standard decomposition of the exterior algebra of a direct sum, under which $\Lambda^{\bullet}A \otimes \Lambda^{\bullet}B = \Lambda^{\bullet}(A \oplus B)$ for $(A, B) \in U(2) \times U(3)$ and the component $\Lambda^{j}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{k}W$ is the set of states with $j$ slots and $k$ pairs occupied.

(v) By (ii) the slot module is fermionic, so clause (f) of the premise applies: the slot and pair operators (the slot operators Klein-twisted, $\tilde a_{i} = a_{i}(-1)^{k}$, as clause (f) takes them; \claimref{synthesis.joint-clifford-clause-is-a-klein-convention-on-the-composite-space}) satisfy the canonical anticommutation relations of one system of five modes. Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-fock-representation-clifford-algebra-bogoliubov} with $E = \C^{2} \oplus W$, $n = 5$, then gives that the Majorana combinations generate $\Cl(10)$ acting on $\Lambda^{\bullet}\C^{5}$, its spinor module, with parity grading the chirality of that module; $\Spin(10)$ acts irreducibly on each of $\Lambda^{\mathrm{even}}\C^{5}$ and $\Lambda^{\mathrm{odd}}\C^{5}$, and $U(5)$, hence $S(U(2) \times U(3)) \subset U(5)$, acts as $\Lambda^{\bullet}(g)$ up to a determinant phase.

(iii) and the particle half. By (ii) and (v) the module is $V = \Lambda^{\bullet}\C^{5}$ with the slot creation operators $a^{\dagger}_{1}, a^{\dagger}_{2}$, and by clause (CPT) the chirality $\Gamma$ is a CPT halving commuting with $S(U(2) \times U(3))$. Its particle half is anomaly-free, the consistency condition of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-anomaly-coefficients}. So Theorem \claimref{synthesis.cpt-closure-forces-slot-module-and-chirality}(4) applies: on every isotypic component other than the twice-occurring neutral singlet $(1,1)_{0}$, spanned by $\Omega = \Lambda^{0}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{0}W$ and $\Omega^{\top} = \Lambda^{2}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{3}W$, $V_{+}$ is the sum of the $\Lambda^{j}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{k}W$ with $j + k$ even or the sum of those with $j + k$ odd. Hence $\Gamma = \varepsilon(-1)^{j + k}$ off the neutral block, $\varepsilon = \pm 1$, and $\Gamma$ maps the block $\mathrm{span}\{\Omega, \Omega^{\top}\}$ to itself, an isotypic component of a representation it commutes with. Write $\Gamma\Omega = \alpha\Omega + \beta\Omega^{\top}$ and $\Gamma\Omega^{\top} = \gamma\Omega + \delta\Omega^{\top}$.

By clause (CPT) $\Gamma$ commutes with the Pati-Salam algebra, the bivectors $\gamma_{p}\gamma_{q}$ of clause (f) with both generators among the four slot Majoranas or both among the six pair Majoranas. The two-slot creation operator $x = a^{\dagger}_{1}a^{\dagger}_{2}$ lies in it: each $a^{\dagger}_{i}$ is a combination of the two Majoranas of slot mode $i$, so $x$ is a combination of products of two slot Majoranas, and it has no scalar part since $a^{\dagger}_{1}$ and $a^{\dagger}_{2}$ anticommute and square to zero; likewise its adjoint $x^{\dagger} = a_{2}a_{1}$. Now $x\Omega$ spans $\Lambda^{2}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{0}W = (1,1)_{1}$, a charged state with $j + k = 2$; $x\Omega^{\top} = 0$; $x^{\dagger}\Omega = 0$; and $x^{\dagger}\Omega^{\top}$ spans $\Lambda^{0}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{3}W = (1,1)_{-1}$, a charged state with $j + k = 3$. From $\Gamma x = x\Gamma$ on $\Omega$: $\varepsilon\,x\Omega = \alpha\,x\Omega$, so $\alpha = \varepsilon$; on $\Omega^{\top}$: $0 = \gamma\,x\Omega$, so $\gamma = 0$. From $\Gamma x^{\dagger} = x^{\dagger}\Gamma$ on $\Omega$: $0 = \beta\,x^{\dagger}\Omega^{\top}$, so $\beta = 0$; on $\Omega^{\top}$: $-\varepsilon\,x^{\dagger}\Omega^{\top} = \delta\,x^{\dagger}\Omega^{\top}$, so $\delta = -\varepsilon$. Hence $\Gamma = \varepsilon(-1)^{r}$ on all of $V$, with $r = j + k$ the total mode number. The labelling of clause (CPT) puts $(2,3)_{1/6} = \Lambda^{1}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{1}W$ in $V_{+}$, so $\varepsilon = +1$: the chirality is the parity of the total mode number, as Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-pair-systems-two-chirality-halves} has it for $\Lambda^{\bullet}W$, and the particle half is the sum of the $\Lambda^{j}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{k}W$ with $j + k$ even, with $Y = (3j - 2k)/6$: $(j,k) = (0,0), (0,2), (1,1), (1,3), (2,0), (2,2)$ give $(1,1)_{0}$, $(1,\bar{3})_{-2/3}$, $(2,3)_{1/6}$, $(2,1)_{-1/2}$, $(1,1)_{1}$, $(1,\bar{3})_{1/3}$, the list of (ii).

(i) If the slot quanta are tower elements of one parity $\pi$ and the chirality is the tower grading $(-1)^{\pi j + k}$, comparing with (iii) on $\Lambda^{1}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{0}W$ gives $(-1)^{\pi} = -1$, so $\pi = 1$; Theorem \claimref{synthesis.cpt-closure-forces-slot-module-and-chirality}(4) states the same.

Anomaly freedom. It is used in (iii), as the consistency condition of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-anomaly-coefficients}, and the particle half found satisfies its four conditions. Without it the Pati-Salam requirement would not suffice: the pair-number parity $\pm(-1)^{k}$ is also a CPT halving commuting with $S(U(2) \times U(3))$ and with the Pati-Salam algebra, and its particle half is anomalous; and the vacuum-swapped halving, which agrees with $(-1)^{j + k}$ off the neutral block, is anomaly-free but does not commute with $x$.
