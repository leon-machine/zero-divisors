Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.are-ascents-pair-signed}(ii) and (iii); the family is $I = c\,e_{+} + d\,e_{-}$ with $c^{2} - d^{2} = \Lambda/3$. The ascent's generator in $I^{\perp}$ beyond the Lorentz frame is $d\,e_{+} + c\,e_{-}$, orthogonal to $I$, of square $d^{2} - c^{2} = -\Lambda/3$; at $\Lambda = 0$, where $c = \pm d$, it is $\pm I$, the nilpotent generator. It is this generator whose square the statement gives; $I$ itself has square $+\Lambda/3$.
