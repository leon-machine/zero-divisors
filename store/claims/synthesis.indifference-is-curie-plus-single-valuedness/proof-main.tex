\emph{(1)} For $g \in G_d$, $g\cdot\Phi(d) = \Phi(gd) = \Phi(d)$: the outcome set is $G_d$-invariant. If it is a single assignment $f$, then $g \cdot f = f$ for every $g \in G_d$, where $(g\cdot f)(x) = f(g^{-1}x)$; so $f(g^{-1}x) = f(x)$ and $f$ is constant on orbits. Any element, partition or ordering of $X$ read off from $f$ alone is then fixed by $G_d$, since $f$ is; a selection of one element of a nontrivial orbit, or an ordering of an orbit, is not.

\emph{(2)} If $y \in G_d x$, every invariant $f$ has $f(y) = f(x)$. If $y \notin G_d x$, the indicator $\chi$ of the orbit $G_d x$ is invariant (an orbit is carried to itself) and $\chi(x) = 1 \neq 0 = \chi(y)$. So the common equalities of all invariant assignments are the orbit relations, no more. The invariant assignments are the image of the averaging projection $f \mapsto |G_d|^{-1}\sum_g g\cdot f$, of dimension equal to the number of orbits. A probability on one finite orbit that is invariant is constant on it, hence uniform: this is the push-forward of Haar measure on $G_d$ under an orbit map. The uniform distribution on all of $X$, the maximum of entropy with no constraint, is invariant (permutations preserve it) and assigns equal weight to points of different orbits; so maximum entropy imposes equalities that invariance does not license. At prescribed orbit totals the invariant assignment is unique, the total spread uniformly over each orbit.

\emph{(3)} Let $G_d = \{1, s\}$ swap $X = \{x, y\}$ and $\Phi(d) = \{(1,0), (0,1)\}$, the two selections. Then $s \cdot \Phi(d) = \Phi(d)$, so covariance holds at $d$ (and $\Phi$ extends covariantly to the orbit of $d$ in any $D$), while neither member is invariant. The same holds for the cyclic group on three labels and its three selections. Hence (1) uses single-valuedness essentially, and adding single-valuedness to covariance gives back every clause of the reading in Fact \claimref{families.imported-indifference-instrument} by (1) and (2). Conversely, a single-valued covariant construction always exists on these data (for instance the barycentre of any invariant outcome set, itself invariant), so the two inputs are independent: covariance does not imply single-valuedness, and single-valuedness alone imposes nothing without covariance.

\emph{(4)} By Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.residue-soldered-invariant-not-algebraic-one} the residue $r = p - q \bmod 8$ is an invariant of the soldered algebra, so the towers' data distinguish residues; the reversal $(p,q) \mapsto (q,p)$ sends $r$ to $-r$, and the check computes its orbits on residues from the signatures with $p, q \leq 4$: $\{0\}, \{1,7\}, \{2,6\}, \{3,5\}, \{4\}$. P2 equates along the reversal only. Each equality it draws on $\{2,6\}$ is an instance of (1) for one datum (a weight assignment on the two towers), so it holds under the formalisation exactly when the descent is covariant and single-valued in that datum. For the deposited energy per unit charge, single-valuedness with covariance gives $T' = T$, which Proposition \claimref{dark-tower-matter.mirrors-temperature-p2} records as excluded; so for that datum one of the two fails. The indexical clause admits two readings. As a fact about the observer's position, it is not an output of the descent and leaves the descent single-valued, so it does not accommodate the cold mirror. As a fact about the descent's outcome, it selects one member of a nontrivial orbit, which by (1) no single-valued covariant construction produces, so it is itself a case of many-valuedness, and then (3) shows that the equalities for the other data no longer follow from symmetry and must be supplied datum by datum.

\emph{Scope.} Everything above concerns weight assignments on a finite set with a finite symmetry group. For the use of indifference that selects an $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ structure on the boundary-mode matrix algebra, clause (1) applied pointwise gives only the zero map as a single-valued covariant output, so the principal embedding is not obtained by single-valuedness; it needs a measure principle on the space of structures (filed alongside, synthesis.principality-is-genericity).
