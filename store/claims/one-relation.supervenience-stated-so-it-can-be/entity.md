## formal
An experiencing subject supervenes on a recording structure: a self is a record chain, a bound chain of committed records, nothing more being assumed about what experience is. Status: premise, flagged; Proposition \claimref{one-relation.character-experience-three-record-chain-theorems} is conditional on it and its refutation condition is stated there.

## technical
An experiencing subject supervenes on a recording structure: a self is a record chain, a bound chain of committed records, and nothing more is assumed about what experience is. This is a flagged premise; the record-chain account of the character of experience is conditional on it and states its refutation condition.

## lay
The assumption is that a conscious self is nothing over and above a chain of permanent records. It says nothing about what experience is. It is stated openly so that the conclusions drawn from it can be tested and refuted.

## one-liner
Premise: an experiencing subject is a record chain, a bound chain of committed records.
