## formal
\emph{Premise (FS), the field space of the gravitational action.} Work in $\Cl(2,4)$ in the chart of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.gtg-tractor-gauge-field-reproduces-tractor-connection}, $\nO = \tfrac12(e_{4} + e_{5})$, $\nInf = e_{4} - e_{5}$, $\ip{\nO}{\nInf} = 1$, with canonical tractor $X = \nO$. Let $(\hb, \Omega)$ be a gravitational field on flat spacetime (Definition \claimref{obj.synthesis.gtg-flat-spacetime-gauge-fields}) on its invertible region, with derived metric $g$, and work in the scale $g$ (the physical scale). The \emph{varied gravitational connection} is the $\Cl(2,4)$ gauge field $A$ (a bivector-valued one-form on flat spacetime, $D_{a}V = a \cdot \nabla V + A(a) \times V$) on which the gravitational action is varied; its soldering $a \mapsto A(a) \times X$ is nondegenerate (in the gauges below it is $\hb^{-1}(a)$). The premise: \emph{the varied gravitational connection admits a tractor field $I$ with $D_{a}I = 0$ for every $a$ and $\ip{X}{I} = 1$.} Since $A$ is bivector-valued, $Q(I) = 2\rho$ is then constant; $\Lambda := 6\rho$, so $Q(I) = \Lambda/3$ as for the frozen point (Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}). The value of $\rho$ is not fixed by (FS); the registered input $N$ fixes it. In the sense of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-holonomy-reductions-of-cartan-geometries}(a), $I$ is a holonomy reduction of $A$ to the stabiliser of $I$ (of de Sitter type for $\rho > 0$, anti-de Sitter type for $\rho < 0$); since $\ip{X}{I} = 1$ nowhere vanishes each connected region is a single open curved orbit, modelled on the stabiliser of $I$ with the Lorentz group as structure group (part (b) there and its reading). A special-conformal rotor, which fixes $X$, carries $I$ to the constant $I_{\rho} = \nInf + \rho\,\nO$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.inverse-frame-free-tractor-gauge-field-preserves-a-frozen-point}(vi)); in that gauge $A$ is valued in the stabiliser of $I_{\rho}$ and is $A_{\rho} = \Omega - (\nInf - \rho\,\nO) \wedge \hb^{-1}$ for a unique pair $(\hb, \Omega)$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.frozen-point-field-space-is-the-gtg-pair-and-its-omega-equation-is-the-torsion-equation}(ii)). (FS) is a condition on the connection that is varied, not on a solution: it is imposed for every configuration, Einstein or not, matter included.

\emph{What it is for.} With (FS) the frozen-point MacDowell-Mansouri functional $Q(I)^{-1}\int\epsilon(I, V, F, F)$ (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-macdowell-mansouri-theorem-stelle-west}, Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}) has the frame equation $2(\mathcal{G}(a) + \Lambda a)I = 0$, Einstein's equations with $\Lambda = 6\rho$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.macdowell-mansouri-principle-fixes-the-trace-free-part}), and its $\Omega$-equation is the torsion equation (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.frozen-point-field-space-is-the-gtg-pair-and-its-omega-equation-is-the-torsion-equation}(i)). Without it, the same integrand on the series' transport (the normal tractor connection of a torsion-free field) is a quarter of the Weyl square, with the Bach equations (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.frozen-point-integrand-on-tractor-transport-is-weyl-squared}), and on torsion-free conformal Cartan connections with the special-conformal slot varied it returns that connection and the same Weyl square (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.frozen-point-integrand-gives-einstein-only-with-the-symmetric-slot-frozen}(ii)). So the store's gravitational action is the imported functional plus (FS); what carries Einstein's equations is (FS), not the integrand. The registration condition of the $w = -1$ prediction assumes it (Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.vacuum-region-registration-condition}(iii)).

\emph{Equivalent readings} (each at the strength proved there).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(R1)] \emph{(Assembly from the gauge-theory-gravity pair.)} Under the gauge group of gauge theory gravity (rotations and affine displacements, not the parabolic group), a $\Cl(2,4)$ gauge field assembled from $(\hb, \Omega)$ and no further field (T), covariant (G), soldered (S), and either polynomial with no inverse frame (P) or built without derivatives (O), is $A_{\rho}$ with $\rho$ constant, and conversely every connection obeying (FS) is such a field in the gauge where $I$ is constant (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.inverse-frame-free-tractor-gauge-field-preserves-a-frozen-point}(i), (ii), (vi)); the field space is then exactly the pair $(\hb, \Omega)$ with a constant tractor (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.frozen-point-field-space-is-the-gtg-pair-and-its-omega-equation-is-the-torsion-equation}(ii)). Neither (P) nor (O) suffices without (T).
\item[(R2)] \emph{(The special-conformal slot frozen.)} In the gauge where $I$ is constant, (FS) says that the special-conformal slot is $\rho\,\id$ and the dilation vanishes, with $\rho$ constant (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.parallel-frozen-point-on-matter-regions-is-the-field-space-premise}(i)). As a statement about field equations, on torsion-free fields the frozen-point integrand gives Einstein's equations with $\Lambda = 6\rho$ exactly when the symmetric part of the slot is frozen at $\rho\,\id$, $\rho \neq 0$; the dilation and the antisymmetric slot are inert, so this reading fixes the equations, not the connection in those inert directions (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.frozen-point-integrand-gives-einstein-only-with-the-symmetric-slot-frozen}(iii)-(v)).
\item[(R3)] \emph{(Infinite stiffness.)} Read as a Higgs field on the unreduced connection, the frozen point reduces the connection, for the stored Stelle-West family and any curvature-independent slot potential, only in the infinitely stiff limit of the polynomial stiffness penalty, where its broken components vanish; finite stiffness adds a Weyl square, and a multiplier imposing $DI = 0$ is (FS) written into the action (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.frozen-point-reduces-the-connection-only-when-infinitely-stiff}). Equivalently, the gauge group of the varied field is the stabiliser of $I$, not the parabolic group of the line of $X$.
\item[(R4)] \emph{(Parallel on matter regions in the physical scale.)} In the physical scale the frozen point is parallel for a soldered connection exactly when the slot is $\tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\id$ with no dilation, for every field, matter included; adopting (FS) implies the registration condition of the $w = -1$ prediction, and that condition read kinematically (as fixing which connection carries the frozen point) is (FS) restated; read only of solutions it is weaker (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.parallel-frozen-point-on-matter-regions-is-the-field-space-premise}; forward and kinematic directions only).
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Not supplied by the series' other structures.} \emph{Transport:} for the normal tractor connection, preserving a tractor in the physical scale is the Einstein condition (Theorem \claimref{interior.scale-tractor-leaves-cone}, Theorem \claimref{synthesis.gtg-tractor-gauge-field-reproduces-tractor-connection}(ii)); the series' transport satisfies (T), (G), (S) and violates (P) and (O), in the special-conformal slot only (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.inverse-frame-free-tractor-gauge-field-preserves-a-frozen-point}(iii)), and on shell with matter it differs from $A_{\rho}$ by $4\pi G(T - \tfrac{\tau}{3}g)$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.parallel-frozen-point-on-matter-regions-is-the-field-space-premise}(ii)). \emph{The descent and a fixed frozen point:} a constant frozen point with the connection unrestricted is the unitary gauge of the Higgs reading, which gives conformal gravity at zero stiffness and reduces the connection only when infinitely stiff (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.frozen-point-reduces-the-connection-only-when-infinitely-stiff}); so freezing one $I$, as the descent's single frozen point does (Premise \claimref{dark-matter.p1-single-frozen-point}), does not restrict the connection. \emph{Topology:} sections of every norm and sign exist on every region with a scale, so neither the reduction nor the sign of $\Lambda$ is constrained (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.parallel-frozen-point-on-matter-regions-is-the-field-space-premise}(iv)). \emph{The duality structure:} the duality complex structure acts only on the Lorentz algebra, shared by every field space, and every chirality of the integrand returns the Schouten slot (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.duality-complex-structure-does-not-reach-the-frozen-point}). \emph{The independence of $\hb$ and $\Omega$:} on the reduced field space it decides exactly the torsion equation, not the frozen point (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.frozen-point-field-space-is-the-gtg-pair-and-its-omega-equation-is-the-torsion-equation}(i)). \emph{No inverse frame alone:} (P) admits an independent slot field and every row of the slot lattice (Theorems \claimref{synthesis.inverse-frame-free-tractor-gauge-field-preserves-a-frozen-point}(iv), \claimref{synthesis.frozen-point-integrand-gives-einstein-only-with-the-symmetric-slot-frozen}(v)). Transport-convenient, not transport-essential: the chart, $X$ and the frozen point are the series' objects, and the premise is not derived from them.

\emph{What it does not give.} It does not fix $\rho$ (the registered input $N$ does), the normalisation of the action (Newton's constant), or the spin part of the matter coupling (torsion-free versus Einstein-Cartan). On the reduced field space $\rho$ is constant, so $\Lambda$ is a constant parameter and $w = -1$ is the $\Lambda$CDM equation of state: a confirmed $w = -1$ supports $\Lambda$CDM with a frozen point and is not evidence for (FS) independent of $\Lambda$CDM.

\emph{Refutation condition.} The premise is false if any of the following is established: (a) a confirmed detection of $w \neq -1$ at any epoch, with dark energy read as the norm of the frozen point (Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.vacuum-region-registration-condition}(iii)), since on the reduced field space that norm, $\Lambda/3$, is constant (this refutes $\Lambda$CDM as well, so it does not discriminate (FS) from it); (b) with the imported functional, a confirmed departure of the gravitational field equations from Einstein's with a constant $\Lambda$ in a regime where the action applies, in particular a fourth-order (Weyl-square, Bach-type) term, which the reduced field space does not have and finite stiffness would add; (c) a derivation from the series' premises that the gravitational connection carries no parallel tractor of unit pairing with $X$ off the Einstein region, for example that its special-conformal slot is an independent field or the Schouten map, so that the frozen-point integrand gives the Bach equations rather than Einstein's.

## technical
The store reaches Einstein's equations from the frozen-point MacDowell-Mansouri functional only when it is varied over a particular field space, and no route through the series derives that field space; this premise records it. In invariant form: the $\Cl(2,4)$ gauge field that is varied, with nondegenerate soldering, admits a parallel tractor $I$ whose pairing with the canonical tractor is one, that is, in the physical scale of the gravitational field. Its norm $Q(I) = 2\rho$ is then constant and gives $\Lambda = 6\rho$; in the language of Cap, Gover and Hammerl it is a holonomy reduction of the connection to the stabiliser of $I$, de Sitter or anti-de Sitter type, and the whole region is one open curved orbit. In the gauge where $I$ is the constant $\nInf + \rho\nO$ the connection is $\Omega - (\nInf - \rho\nO) \wedge \hb^{-1}$ for a unique gauge-theory-gravity pair. With it the functional's frame equation is Einstein's with that $\Lambda$ and its $\Omega$-equation the torsion equation; without it, on the series' transport or with the special-conformal slot free, the same integrand is the Weyl square and gives the Bach equations.

Four readings are proved equivalent at their stated strengths: building the connection from $(\hb, \Omega)$ alone, covariantly and soldered, with no inverse frame or no derivatives; the special-conformal slot frozen at $\rho$ times the identity (as a statement about the field equations, only its symmetric part matters); the frozen point infinitely stiff, so that the gauge group of the varied field is the stabiliser of $I$ rather than the parabolic group; and the frozen point parallel on matter regions in the physical scale (forward and kinematic directions). Transport does not supply it (for the normal tractor connection preserving $I$ is the Einstein condition), nor does a fixed frozen point with the connection unrestricted, topology, the duality complex structure, the independence of $\hb$ and $\Omega$, or the no-inverse-frame hypothesis alone. It leaves $\Lambda$'s value, Newton's constant and the spin part of the matter coupling open, and with it $w = -1$ is the $\Lambda$CDM equation of state, not separate evidence. It would be refuted by a confirmed $w \neq -1$, by a confirmed departure from Einstein's equations with constant $\Lambda$ (for example a Weyl-square term) where the action applies, or by a derivation that the gravitational connection has no such parallel tractor off the Einstein region.

## lay
The series borrows a rule, an action, from which the equations of gravity follow, and that rule gives Einstein's equations only if one decides in advance which shapes of the gravitational field are allowed to vary: those that keep one special fixed direction, the frozen point, unchanged everywhere, measured in the actual scale of the spacetime. Without that decision the same rule gives a different theory, conformal gravity. Several attempts to derive the decision from the series' own structures failed; each turned out to be the same assumption in another form. So it is recorded as an assumption of its own. It explains why the cosmological constant is a fixed number, but for the same reason a measurement that dark energy never changes would not count as separate evidence for it. It would be refuted if dark energy were found to change, or if gravity were found to deviate from Einstein's equations where the rule applies.

## one-liner
Premise (FS): the varied gravitational connection, a $\Cl(2,4)$ gauge field with nondegenerate soldering, admits a parallel tractor $I$ with $\ip{X}{I} = 1$ (a holonomy reduction to the stabiliser of $I$, $Q(I) = \Lambda/3$ constant); with it the frozen-point functional gives Einstein's equations with $\Lambda = 6\rho$, without it the same integrand is the Weyl square; equivalent readings: assembly from $(\hb, \Omega)$ with no inverse frame or no derivatives, the slot frozen at $\rho\,\id$, infinite stiffness, the frozen point parallel on matter regions; not supplied by transport, a fixed frozen point, topology, the duality structure or the independence of $\Omega$; refuted by a confirmed $w \neq -1$ or a departure from Einstein's equations with constant $\Lambda$.
