\emph{Conventions.} The modes $c_{x,k}$ of distinct channels and levels are distinct tower planes, so $\{c_{\alpha}, c^{*}_{\beta}\} = \delta_{\alpha\beta}$ and all other anticommutators vanish (Fact \claimref{field-theory.imported-tower-limit}); the check realises them by the Jordan-Wigner signs and verifies the Clifford relations of the planes $e = c^{*} + c$, $f = c^{*} - c$. The unit $J$ commutes with every mode, so $J(M)_{m}$ is linear in $M$ over $\mathrm{span}\{1, J\}$ and $J(M)_{m}^{*} = J(M^{*})_{-m}$, with $M^{*}$ the transpose with $J \mapsto -J$. By Theorem \claimref{synthesis.null-plane-photon-data-and-the-pair-currents-of-the-generators}(iv) and linearity, on every window $[J(M)_{m}, J(M')_{l}]$ is the exact window commutator (the two restricted sums), and on vectors edge-clean at depth $\max(|m|, |l|)$
\[
[J(M)_{m}, J(M')_{l}] = J([M, M'])_{m+l} + m\operatorname{tr}(MM')\,\delta_{m+l,0}, \tag{1}
\]
since $\sum_{x,y,z,u}M_{xy}M'_{zu}(\delta_{yz}j^{xu} - \delta_{xu}j^{zy} + m\delta_{xu}\delta_{yz}\delta_{m+l,0}) = J(MM') - J(M'M) + m\operatorname{tr}(MM')\delta_{m+l,0}$.

\emph{(i)} The bilinears $a^{*}_{a}a_{b}$ conserve the mode number, so they preserve each degree of $\Lambda^{\bullet}\C^{5}$ and hence $V_{+}$; this is the action of $S(U(2) \times U(3))$ on $V$ in Theorem \claimref{synthesis.cpt-closure-forces-slot-module-and-chirality}, whose algebra is that of the composite (Theorem \claimref{standard-model.gauge-algebra-minimal-massive-composite}). The CAR give $[a^{*}_{a}a_{b}, a^{*}_{c}a_{d}] = \delta_{bc}a^{*}_{a}a_{d} - \delta_{ad}a^{*}_{c}a_{b}$, so $\rho$ is a homomorphism; $(a^{*}_{a}a_{b})^{*} = a^{*}_{b}a_{a}$ and the occupation basis is orthonormal, so $\rho(X^{*}) = \rho(X)^{*}$. The diagonal entry of $\rho(E_{aa})$ at a state is its occupation of mode $a$, and $a$ is occupied in $8$ of the $16$ even states, so $\operatorname{tr}\rho(X) = 8\operatorname{tr}X$; $\mathfrak{g}$ is traceless ($\tfrac12 + \tfrac12 - 3\cdot\tfrac13 = 0$ for $Y$). For $X^{*} = -X$, $\rho(X)$ is anti-Hermitian and its exponential is a unitary channel matrix; $J(\rho X)_{m} = \sum\rho(X)_{xy}j^{xy}_{m}$ is a combination of pair currents. The module's numbers are read in $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.slot-module-light-ray-arrays-are-weyl-components}); identifying the occupation basis with the channels carries them to the operators' unit $J$, a matching of units, as for the channel weights in the stored pair currents. By Theorem \claimref{synthesis.slot-module-light-ray-arrays-are-weyl-components} the generation is read as left-handed Weyl fields valued in $V_{+}$, one channel per component, and by Theorem \claimref{synthesis.light-ray-good-part-of-a-spinor-is-two-chirality-arrays}(iv) the rotation about the generator acts on a channel of one chirality by one phase $z$; the mode rotation $U = \prod_{x,k}(1 + (z - 1)n_{x,k})$ multiplies $c^{*}_{x,k}c_{y,l}$ by $z\bar z = 1$ (the computation of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.null-plane-photon-data-and-the-pair-currents-of-the-generators}(iv) with equal weights), so every pair current is invariant. The hypercharges follow from $Y = (3n - 2k)/6$ on the states of degree $0$ ($n = k = 0$), $2$ ($(n, k) = (2, 0), (1, 1), (0, 2)$, multiplicities $1, 6, 3$) and $4$ ($(2, 2), (1, 3)$, multiplicities $3, 2$).

\emph{(ii)} $\operatorname{tr}_{V_{+}}(a^{*}_{a}a_{b}a^{*}_{c}a_{d})$ vanishes unless the operator is diagonal: $a = b$, $c = d$ gives the number of even states containing $a$ and $c$, $8$ if $a = c$ and $4$ otherwise (the even subsets of the other three modes, completed); $a = d \ne b = c$ gives $\operatorname{tr}(n_{a}(1 - n_{b})) = 4$. Hence $\operatorname{tr}(\rho X\rho X') = 8\sum_{a}X_{aa}X'_{aa} + 4\sum_{a \ne c}X_{aa}X'_{cc} + 4\sum_{a \ne b}X_{ab}X'_{ba} = 4\operatorname{tr}(XX') + 4\operatorname{tr}X\operatorname{tr}X'$. On $\mathfrak{g}$ the second term vanishes and (1) gives the displayed relation. On $\su(3)$ and $\su(2)$ the $\C^{5}$ trace is the trace of the fundamental, so both levels are $4$. $\operatorname{tr}_{5}Y^{2} = 2\cdot\tfrac14 + 3\cdot\tfrac19 = \tfrac56$, so $\operatorname{tr}_{16}\rho(Y)^{2} = \tfrac{10}{3}$. Different factors have $\operatorname{tr}_{5}(XX') = 0$ (disjoint blocks, or a traceless block element against a multiple of the identity on that block), and $\operatorname{tr}\rho(X) = 0$, so the central terms between factors and with $N = J(1)$ vanish, and the commutators of the currents of commuting generators vanish by (1).

\emph{(iii), the band.} On the domain $\mathcal{D}$ of finite excitations of the band of the null vacuum in every channel (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.fluctuation-forms-of-the-tower-and-density-wave-bosons-of-the-null-vacuum}(vi), Theorem \claimref{synthesis.null-vacuum-on-a-horizon-generator}(ii)), with zero modes counted from the sea and $H$ the sea-normal-ordered $H_{\infty}$ of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.exact-window-sugawara-identity-and-luttinger-normal-modes-of-tower-density-waves}(iii) summed over the channels (finite on each vector of $\mathcal{D}$, and $[H_{\infty}, j^{xy}_{m}] = -mj^{xy}_{m}$ there, each term moving a fermion by $m$ levels), (1) holds on every vector: a vector $\psi \in \mathcal{D}$ agrees with the sea outside a finite set of levels, and as in the proof of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.exact-window-sugawara-identity-and-luttinger-normal-modes-of-tower-density-waves}(iii) the operators involved act on $\psi$ as the window operators of a window around it, at whose edges $\psi$ is clean at any given depth. Let $\mathfrak{f}$ be one factor, $S = \sum_{q \in \Z}{:}J(M^{A})_{-q}J(M_{A})_{q}{:}$ (the positive index on the right; by symmetry of $\sum_{A}M^{A}\otimes M_{A}$ this is $2\sum_{q \ge 1}J^{A}_{-q}J_{A,q} + J^{A}_{0}J_{A,0}$), and $X \in \mathfrak{f}$, $m \ge 1$. By (1),
\[
[J^{A}_{-q}J_{A,q}, X_{m}] = J^{A}_{-q}J([M_{A}, X])_{q+m} + J([M^{A}, X])_{m-q}J_{A,q} - m\operatorname{tr}(M^{A}X)\,\delta_{qm}J_{A,q},
\]
and $\sum_{A}\operatorname{tr}(M^{A}X)M_{A} = X$, so the central terms of $2\sum_{q \ge 1}$ give $-2mX_{m}$. Put $P_{a,b} = \sum_{A}J(M^{A})_{a}J([M_{A}, X])_{b}$; invariance of the Casimir tensor, $\sum_{A}([M^{A}, X]\otimes M_{A} + M^{A}\otimes[M_{A}, X]) = 0$, gives $\sum_{A}J([M^{A}, X])_{a}J_{A,b} = -P_{a,b}$, and with its symmetry $\sum_{A}M^{A}\otimes M_{A} = \sum_{A}M_{A}\otimes M^{A}$ also $P_{b,a} = -\sum_{A}J([M_{A}, X])_{b}J(M^{A})_{a}$; so $P_{a,b} + P_{b,a} = \sum_{A}[J(M^{A})_{a}, J([M_{A}, X])_{b}] = J(\sum_{A}[M^{A}, [M_{A}, X]])_{a+b} = \lambda_{\mathfrak{f}}X_{a+b}$ for $a + b \ge 1$ by (1), with no central term. The noncentral terms are $2\sum_{q \ge 1}(P_{-q,q+m} - P_{m-q,q}) + P_{0,m} - P_{m,0}$, all sums finite on $\psi$. In the second sum the terms $q > m$ are the terms of the first, so what remains is $-2\sum_{q=1}^{m}P_{m-q,q} + P_{0,m} - P_{m,0} = -\sum_{q=1}^{m-1}(P_{m-q,q} + P_{q,m-q}) - (P_{0,m} + P_{m,0}) = -m\lambda_{\mathfrak{f}}X_{m}$. Hence $[S, X_{m}] = -(2 + \lambda_{\mathfrak{f}})mX_{m}$; For $m \le -1$, $S$ is self-adjoint (computed in a self-adjoint basis) and $[S, X_{m}] = -[S, (X^{*})_{-m}]^{*} = -(2 + \lambda_{\mathfrak{f}})mX_{m}$; for $m = 0$ there is no central term and the noncentral terms are $2\sum_{q \ge 1}(P_{-q,q} - P_{-q,q}) + P_{0,0} - P_{0,0} = 0$. So $[H_{\mathfrak{f}}, J(\rho X)_{m}] = -mJ(\rho X)_{m}$. For $X'$ in another factor or $X' = 1$, $[M_{A}, X'] = 0$ and $\operatorname{tr}(M_{A}X') = 0$ by (ii), so $[S, X'_{m}] = 0$. Finally $[H, j^{xy}_{m}] = -mj^{xy}_{m}$ exactly (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.null-plane-photon-data-and-the-pair-currents-of-the-generators}(v), $H$ differing from the level operator by $\tfrac12\hat N$, which commutes with pair currents), so $[H_{c}, J(\rho X)_{m}] = -m J - (-m J) - 0 = 0$, and likewise for $N_{m}$. The values $\lambda_{\su(3)} = \tfrac32$, $\lambda_{\su(2)} = 1$ are computed exactly from the trace-dual bases ($\lambda$ is $2h/k$ in terms of the dual Coxeter number and the level, which are not used).

\emph{(iii), windows.} Call a window vector $j$-local if it equals $\Omega_{s}$ outside levels $s - j, \dots, s + j - 1$. On a $j$-local vector, $J(M)_{q}$ with $q \ge 1$ vanishes for $q \ge 2j$ (a fermion must drop onto an empty level, and both lie in the band of width $2j$) and otherwise gives a $j$-local vector, and $J(M)_{-q}$ gives a $(j + q)$-local one; a window operator $J(M)_{q}$ agrees with the band operator on a $j$-local vector when $|q| \le \min(s, L - s) - j$ (the omitted terms would move a fermion across an edge of the window), and the zero modes differ by $s\operatorname{tr}M$, which vanishes for the gauge factors and for $N$ only shifts $H_{N}$ by a multiple of $N_{0}$, which commutes with every pair current. For a $K$-local $\psi$ and $|m| \le K$, $J(\rho X)_{m}\psi$ is $(K + |m|)$-local, $H_{\mathfrak{f}}$ applied to a $j$-local vector uses only $q \le 2j - 1$ and produces $(3j - 1)$-local vectors, so all operators in the relations act as on $\mathcal{D}$ when $\min(s, L - s) \ge 3(K + |m|)$, and the relations hold. The check verifies them on $\Omega_{6}$ and on $J(E_{1,8})_{-1}\Omega_{6}$ in a window of $12$ levels for one generator of each factor, $m = \pm1$, and exhibits the failure on $c^{*}_{x,3}c_{x,1}\Omega_{2}$ in a window of $4$ levels.

\emph{(iv)} Take a basis $t_{a}$ of $\mathfrak{f}$ with $t_{a}^{*} = t_{a}$ and $\operatorname{tr}(t_{a}t_{b}) = \delta_{ab}$ (the trace form is positive on self-adjoint matrices); the vector $v = \sum_{a}J(t_{a})_{-1}J(t_{a})_{-1}\Omega_{s} = \sum_{A}J(M^{A})_{-1}J(M_{A})_{-1}\Omega_{s}$ does not depend on the basis. For $s \ge 2$, $L - s \ge 2$, the vectors $\Omega_{s}$ and $J_{-1}\Omega_{s}$ are edge-clean at depth $1$, $J(M)_{1}\Omega_{s} = 0$ and $J(M)_{0}\Omega_{s} = s\operatorname{tr}(M)\Omega_{s}$. By (1), $J(t_{b})_{1}v = \sum_{a}\bigl(J([t_{b}, t_{a}])_{0} + \delta_{ab}\bigr)J(t_{a})_{-1}\Omega_{s} + \sum_{a}J(t_{a})_{-1}\delta_{ab}\Omega_{s} = J\bigl(\textstyle\sum_{a}[[t_{b}, t_{a}], t_{a}]\bigr)_{-1}\Omega_{s} + 2J(t_{b})_{-1}\Omega_{s} = (2 + \lambda_{\mathfrak{f}})J(t_{b})_{-1}\Omega_{s}$, and $\|J(t_{b})_{-1}\Omega_{s}\|^{2} = \operatorname{tr}t_{b}^{2} = 1$. So $\|v\|^{2} = \sum_{b}\ip{J(t_{b})_{-1}\Omega_{s}}{J(t_{b})_{1}v} = (2 + \lambda_{\mathfrak{f}})\dim\mathfrak{f}$ and $\|L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{-2}\Omega_{s}\|^{2} = \dim\mathfrak{f}/(2 + \lambda_{\mathfrak{f}})$. For $t_{b}$ in another factor the same computation gives $J(t_{b})_{1}v = 0$ (commuting, trace-orthogonal), hence orthogonality. For the whole $\uu(16)$, with $\sum_{x,y}[[t, E_{yx}], E_{xy}] = 2nt - 2\operatorname{tr}(t)1$, $J(t)_{1}L_{-2}\Omega_{s} = \tfrac{1}{2(n + 1)}\bigl((2n + 2)J(t)_{-1} - 2\operatorname{tr}(t)N_{-1} + 2\operatorname{tr}(t)N_{-1}\bigr)\Omega_{s} = J(t)_{-1}\Omega_{s}$ for every $t$, so $\ip{L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{-2}\Omega_{s}}{L_{-2}\Omega_{s}} = \|L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{-2}\Omega_{s}\|^{2}$, and the decomposition $\gl(16) = \su(16) \oplus \C 1$, with $\lambda_{\su(16)} = 32$ from $\sum_{x,y}[E_{yx}, [E_{xy}, X]] = 2nX - 2\operatorname{tr}(X)1$, gives $\|L_{-2}\Omega_{s}\|^{2} = \tfrac{255}{34} + \tfrac12 = 8$ (with $\sum_{x,y}E_{yx}\otimes E_{xy}$ equal to the $\su(16)$ Casimir tensor plus $\tfrac{1}{16}1\otimes 1$, $L_{-2}\Omega_{s}$ is the $\su(16)$ vector, coefficient $\tfrac{1}{34}$, plus $\tfrac{1}{34}\cdot\tfrac{17}{16}N_{-1}N_{-1}\Omega_{s} = L^{N}_{-2}\Omega_{s}$). The remainder is orthogonal to each factor's vector and has norm squared $8 - \tfrac{16}{7} - 1 - \tfrac12 - \tfrac12 = \tfrac{26}{7}$. The check computes all these vectors exactly in a window of four levels with $s = 2$.
