\emph{Conventions.} Write $(n_o, n_\infty)$ for a local frame of $\Lambda^* \oplus \Lambda$ dual under the hyperbolic pairing, $H(n_o, n_\infty) = 1$, obtained from a unit section $\lambda_0$ of $\Lambda$ (fibre metric) and its dual $\xi_0$: $n_o = \xi_0$, $n_\infty = \lambda_0$. The store's normalisation of the adjoined plane differs by the sign of $H$, which only changes $F = 1 - 2\mu\nu\beta^2 s$ into $1 + 2\mu\nu\beta^2 s$. Since $s = 1/\lambda + O(1) \to -\infty$ from the far side (the dying eigenvalue $\lambda$ is negative there), a far-side zero of $F$ needs $\mu\nu < 0$ in the normalisation used here and $\mu\nu > 0$ in the store's; ``good coupling'' below means $|\mu\nu| < 1/(2S)$ with that sign. Every other statement is insensitive to the normalisation.

\emph{(1) Local form and well-definedness.} Write $u = e \otimes \lambda_0$ locally, $e$ a vector field. Then $\kappa(\alpha, n_o) = \nu\beta\,\alpha(e)$ and $\kappa(\alpha, n_\infty) = \mu\beta\,\alpha(e)$, which is the coupling of \claimref{obj.sheaf.collar-data-coupling} with transversal $e$; in the frame $(T^*M\text{-frame}, n_o, n_\infty)$
\[
h = \begin{pmatrix} G & \beta\nu e & \beta\mu e \\ \beta\nu e^{\mathsf T} & 0 & 1 \\ \beta\mu e^{\mathsf T} & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
The other unit frame is $-\lambda_0$, which sends $e \mapsto -e$, $n_o \mapsto -n_o$, $n_\infty \mapsto -n_\infty$, and $D h(e) D = h(-e)$ for $D = 1 \oplus (-1) \oplus (-1)$ (checked): the form is frame-independent, so $h$ is a well-defined symmetric form on $T_\Lambda$. The condition $\langle k, u\rangle = 1$ reads $k_{\mathrm{loc}}(e) = 1$ for the local section $k_{\mathrm{loc}}$ of $\shL$ paired with $\lambda_0$; such $u$ exist near $\Sig$ because $k : \Lambda|_\Sig \to T^*M|_\Sig$ is injective (extend over the collar by the product structure, then choose $u$ by a partition of unity, the condition being affine). By the Schur complement,
\[
\det h = \det G \cdot \det\!\begin{pmatrix} -\beta^2\nu^2 s & 1 - \beta^2\mu\nu s \\ 1 - \beta^2\mu\nu s & -\beta^2\mu^2 s\end{pmatrix} = -\det G\,(1 - 2\mu\nu\beta^2 s), \qquad s = e^{\mathsf T}G^{-1}e,
\]
checked exactly on fixed rational data. $s$ is quadratic in $e$, so it is frame-independent, a function. Lemma \claimref{sheaf.tame-wall-induction} and its ingredients (the blow-up expansion $s = 1/\lambda + O(1)$ of \claimref{sheaf.determinant-identity-blow-up-expansion}(2), the monotone zone, the regular level set $\{F = 0\}$, the rank of the adjugate, Jacobi's formula) are pointwise or local along transversals, and use only $\det h$, $s$ and the adjugate, all frame-independent here; so for good couplings $\Sig'$ is a smooth closed hypersurface in the monotone zone, a graph over $\Sig$ along the collar lines, and $(T_\Lambda, h)$ is a tame corank-one degeneration along it.

\emph{(2) The radical line.} Let $x = (\alpha, a, b)$ with $h x = 0$. The $T^*M$ rows give $G\alpha + \beta(\nu a + \mu b)e = 0$, so $\alpha = -c\,G^{-1}e$ with $c = \beta(\nu a + \mu b)$; the $n_o$ and $n_\infty$ rows give $b = -\beta\nu\,e^{\mathsf T}\alpha = \beta\nu s c$ and $a = \beta\mu s c$; consistency is $c = 2\mu\nu\beta^2 s\,c$, i.e.\ $F c = 0$. On $\Sig'$, $F = 0$ and the radical is the line $c\,(-G^{-1}e, \beta\mu s, \beta\nu s)$, $c \in \R$, checked exactly at a zero of $F$ (with corank exactly one). Its $T^*M$ component $-cG^{-1}e$ vanishes only for $c = 0$, as $G$ is nondegenerate off $\Sig$ and $e \neq 0$. Invariantly, $c$ multiplies $\xi_0$: the radical is $\{(-G^{-1}u(\xi), \beta\mu s\,\xi^\sharp, \beta\nu s\,\xi) : \xi \in \Lambda^*\}$, where $u(\xi) \in TM$ is the contraction and $\xi^\sharp$ the metric dual, and the frame change $\lambda_0 \mapsto -\lambda_0$ sends the local kernel vector $K(e)$ to $K(-e) = -DK(e)$ (checked), the same line with its $T^*M$ component reversed together with the frame. Thus $\xi \mapsto$ (that vector) is a bundle isomorphism $\Lambda^*|_{\Sig'} \to \shL'$. Real line bundles are self-dual (a fibre metric), so $\shL' \cong \Lambda|_{\Sig'}$. Along the collar lines $\Sig' \to \Sig$ is a diffeomorphism homotopic, through the slices of the product collar, to the identity, and $\Lambda|_{\Sig'}$ corresponds to $\Lambda|_\Sig \cong \shL$ (homotopy invariance of pullback); by Fact \claimref{sheaf.imported-real-line-bundles}, $w_1(\shL') = w_1(\shL)$ under this identification.

\emph{(3) Iteration.} The physical side of $\Sig'$ is $\Mp$ together with the part of the collar between $\Sig$ and $\Sig'$, and $\Lambda$ is defined there and restricts on $\Sig'$ to a bundle isomorphic to $\shL'$ by (2). So $\Lambda$ extends $\shL'$ over the physical side of $\Sig'$, the hypothesis of the twisted construction holds for $(T_\Lambda, h, \Sig')$, and the construction applies again with the same $\Lambda$, producing $\Sig''$ inside a collar of $\Sig'$ contained in a product neighbourhood $\Sig \times [-a, 1]$, where $\Lambda$ is the pullback of $\shL$. By induction every $\shL^{(j)} \cong \Lambda|_{\Sig^{(j)}}$ and $w_1(\shL^{(j)}) = w_1(\shL)$. Each stage adds one hyperbolic plane, so the physical-side signature rises by $(1,1)$ per wall as in \claimref{sheaf.infinite-wall-sequence}, the clock coordinates shift by the rung and the mismatch across each wall stays $2 \in \mathbb{Z}_8$ (each wall is tame, \claimref{synthesis.wall-class-one-rung-obstruction}(1)). Hence $W(\Sig^{(j)})$ is constant. The untwisted case is $\Lambda$ trivial, where (2) shows every $\shL^{(j)}$ trivial.

\emph{(4) The torus wall.} On $T^2 \times \R$ with angles $\varphi, \psi$ and $t \in \R$, let $(c, \sigma) = (\cos\tfrac\varphi2, \sin\tfrac\varphi2)$, $k = c\,d\psi + \sigma\,dt$, $m = -\sigma\,d\psi + c\,dt$, and $G = -\partial_\varphi^2 + t\,K^2 + M^2$ with $K, M$ the dual vector fields. $G$ is invariant under $(c,\sigma) \mapsto (-c,-\sigma)$, the effect of $\varphi \mapsto \varphi + 2\pi$, so it is a smooth cometric on $T^2 \times \R$, while $k$ and $K$ change sign: the line $\shL = \operatorname{span}(k)|_{t=0}$ turns through $\pi$ in the $(d\psi, dt)$ plane around the $\varphi$-circle, a Moebius band, so $w_1(\shL) \neq 0$ (Fact \claimref{sheaf.imported-real-line-bundles}). At $t = 0$ the radical of $G$ is $\operatorname{span}(k)$ (rank two, $Gk = 0$, checked at rational points of the circle $(c,\sigma) = ((1-x^2)/(1+x^2), 2x/(1+x^2))$); $G(k,k) = t$ has $dt \neq 0$, so the degeneration is first order, and (T1) to (T3) of \claimref{sheaf.tameness} hold; $N^*\Sig = \operatorname{span}(dt)$ is trivial and the signatures are $(2,1)$ for $t > 0$ and $(1,2)$ for $t < 0$. By \claimref{synthesis.wall-class-one-rung-obstruction}(3)-(4) no untwisted one-rung completion restricting to $G$ along the wall exists. Take $\Lambda = \operatorname{span}(k)$ over all of $M$ (so $\Lambda|_\Sig = \shL$), $u = K \otimes k$ in the twisted sense (locally $e = K$, $k(K) = 1$) and $\beta \equiv 1$. Then $s = G^{-1}(K, K) = 1/t$, $\det h = -\det G\,(1 - 2\mu\nu/t) = t - 2\mu\nu$ (checked at rational points, with $\mu\nu = -\tfrac13$): $h$ is nondegenerate at $\Sig$, with value $-2\mu\nu \neq 0$, and on the physical side (the twisted analogue of \claimref{sheaf.one-sided-completion}), and degenerates exactly on $\{t = 2\mu\nu\}$, on the far side when $\mu\nu < 0$. There the kernel's $T^*M$ component is $-G^{-1}K = -k/t$ (checked), so $\shL'$ projects onto $\operatorname{span}(k)$ at $t = 2\mu\nu$, again turning through $\pi$: $w_1(\shL') \neq 0$, as (2) predicts. The example uses $\beta \equiv 1$ on the slab containing both walls; the cut-off away from it is the good-coupling step of Lemma \claimref{sheaf.tame-wall-induction} and does not touch the computation.
