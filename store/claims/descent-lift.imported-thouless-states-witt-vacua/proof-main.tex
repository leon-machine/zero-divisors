For an antisymmetric matrix $A$ the state $\exp(\sum_{i<j}A_{ij}a^{\dagger}_{i}a^{\dagger}_{j})\ket{0}$ is the vacuum of the Witt decomposition obtained from the standard one by the corresponding Bogoliubov rotation; on two modes it is $\ket{00} + A_{12}\ket{11}$, and every even state vector of at most three modes is a Witt vacuum (pure, by Cartan's theorem), of this exponential form exactly when its overlap with $\ket{0}$ is nonzero ($\ket{11}$ on two modes is a Witt vacuum not of this form). \emph{Provenance:} \cite{Thouless1960}; Cartan's theorem as imported by the laboratory paper \cite[Fact~2.10]{ButlerT16} (\claimref{one-relation.imported-cartans-purity-theorem-thouless-form}).
