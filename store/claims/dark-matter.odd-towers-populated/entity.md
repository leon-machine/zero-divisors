## formal
\emph{Premise (Pop), the population of the odd towers.} The descent deposited matter in each of the four odd towers: the two three-dimensional towers (residues $1$ and $7$) and the two five-dimensional towers (residues $3$ and $5$), at their representatives under Premise \claimref{dark-matter.p3-nearest-dimension-representatives}.

\emph{It is inferred from the number it helps fit.} Nothing in the series reaches the odd towers' population: Premise \claimref{dark-matter.p2-indifference} does not equate them with this tower, the series gives them algebras and not contents, and what the descent deposited in each belongs to the pre-descent theory. The only support is the record read inside the framework (Observation \claimref{dark-matter.which-towers-are-populated}): with the mirror's share exactly one, empty odd towers would give a dark-to-baryon ratio of $1$ against the observed $5.364 \pm 0.065$, and the framework has no other dark candidate, so the observed remainder $4.364$ is the evidence that they hold matter. That remainder is the same number the unit-ball closed form, which sums over these four towers, is then compared with. The premise therefore adds no support to the fit that is independent of the fitted number.

\emph{Scope.} This tower is populated by observation and the mirror by the copy along the reversal orbit (Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.mirror-tower-copy-one}); neither is part of this premise. The premise says that the odd towers hold matter, not how much: the amount is Premises \claimref{dark-matter.r1-equal-mass-per-quantum} to \claimref{dark-matter.r3-one-cell-per-unshared-direction}. No result outside the fitted total depends on it: the mirror share, the superselection, the parity theorem and the arrow are the same if the odd towers are empty.

\emph{Refutation condition.} The premise is false if the pre-descent theory, once written, deposits nothing in an odd tower, or if the dark matter is shown to be other than the towers' matter; without it the framework's ratio is $1$.

## technical
The unit-ball closed form sums over the mirror and the four odd towers, so it needs the odd towers to hold matter. Nothing in the series gives that: indifference does not reach them, and the series assigns them algebras, not contents. The stored support is an inference from the record: with the mirror's share one, empty odd towers would give a ratio of $1$ against the observed $5.364$, so the remainder $4.364$ is read as their matter. That remainder is also what the closed form is compared with, so the premise is inferred from the number it helps fit and adds no independent support. It was held as text (the population observation, and a presupposition of R2); this entry registers it as a premise so that the total shows it among its conditions.

## lay
The formula for the amount of dark matter assumes that four of the hidden sectors actually contain matter. The series has no argument for that except the measurement itself: if they were empty, the dark matter would equal the ordinary matter, and it is about five times larger. So the assumption is read off from the very number the formula is meant to account for, and cannot count as separate evidence for it. This entry records it as an assumption in its own right.
