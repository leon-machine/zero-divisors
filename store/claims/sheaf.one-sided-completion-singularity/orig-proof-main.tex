The determinant identity of Lemma \claimref{sheaf.determinant-identity-blow-up-expansion} holds verbatim: $\det h = \det G \cdot (-(1 + 2\mu\nu\beta_b^2 s))$ with bump $\beta_b$ and $s = G^{-1}(e,e) = (r - m^2)/(m^2 r)$ exactly, $e$ evaluation at the normalised transversal $u = \partial_t/m$. (The normalisation matters: the unnormalised choice $u = \partial_t$ has $k(u) = m$, and with it the ladder anticommutator comes out $m$, not $1$, for $2M \neq 1$; the script checks the normalised construction at two masses.) The sign structure is the mirror of the interior-wall case: here the dying eigenvalue is negative on the physical side ($g_k = -r < 0$), so the ascent of the physical side's algebra is selected by $\mu\nu < 0$; the wall Gram then has signature $(2,4)$ at $E$, the opposite sign giving $(3,3)$. For nondegeneracy: inside the horizon $s < 0$, so with $\mu\nu < 0$ the factor $F = 1 + 2\mu\nu\beta_b^2 s \geq 1$ wherever the bump is supported, and outside the bump $h = G \perp H$ with $G$ nondegenerate. Since $F$ never vanishes on $\Mt$, no shadow wall forms: the zero set of $F$ would require $s > 0$ within the coupling's support, which the choice of collar excludes. The subalgebra and ladder-pair statements are Corollary \claimref{sheaf.localised-transport} verbatim; the CAR identity is verified on all $64$ states of the ambient fibre at $E$.
