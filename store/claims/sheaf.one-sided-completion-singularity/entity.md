## formal
Adjoin the trivial hyperbolic plane $(\nO, \nInf)$ to $\shW$ and couple it to the dying direction by the construction of Definition \claimref{obj.sheaf.collar-data-coupling}, taking the normalised transversal $u = \partial_t/m$ (so that $k(u) = 1$, as the definition requires), coupling constants satisfying $\mu\nu < 0$, and bump supported inside the horizon. Then the ambient form $h$ is nondegenerate of constant signature $(2,4)$ at every point of $\Mt$, the boundary $E$ included; the subalgebra generated by $\shW$ is canonically the degeneration sheaf; and the ladder pair
\[
k^* = \frac{\nO}{2\nu}, \qquad k k^* + k^* k = 1, \qquad k^2 = (k^*)^2 = 0
\]
exists exactly over $E$. The ascent $\Cl(1,3) \to \Cl(2,4)$, i.e.\ $\Mat_2(\HH) \to \Mat_4(\HH)$, is thereby performed at the Schwarzschild singularity, localised on $E \cong \R \times S^2$. Moreover no shadow wall forms anywhere in the manifold.

## technical
Adjoin the hyperbolic plane $(\nO, \nInf)$ to the weighted module $\shW$ and couple it to the dying direction by the collar construction, with normalised transversal $u = \partial_t/m$ (so that $k(u) = 1$), coupling constants with $\mu\nu < 0$, and bump supported inside the horizon. Here the dying eigenvalue is negative on the physical side and $s < 0$ inside the horizon, so the determinant factor satisfies $1 + 2\mu\nu\beta_b^2 s \geq 1$ wherever the bump is supported; hence the ambient form $h$ is nondegenerate of constant signature $(2,4)$ at every point of $\Mt$, the boundary $E$ included, and no shadow wall forms anywhere.

The subalgebra generated by $\shW$ is canonically the degeneration sheaf, and the ladder pair $k^* = \nO/(2\nu)$, with $k k^* + k^* k = 1$ and $Q(k) = Q(k^*) = 0$, exists exactly over $E$. This performs the ascent $\Cl(1,3) \to \Cl(2,4)$, that is $\Mat_2(\HH) \to \Mat_4(\HH)$, at the Schwarzschild singularity, localised on $E \cong \R \times S^2$.

## lay
The completion that repairs a degenerating geometry can be carried out at the central singularity of a Schwarzschild black hole, once that singularity has been opened up into a boundary. Coupling two extra dimensions to the dying direction, with the sign of the coupling chosen to suit the black-hole interior and the coupling switched on only inside the horizon, gives a geometry that is non-degenerate everywhere, and no new degeneracy appears anywhere else. The fermion-like pairing then lives exactly on the opened-up singularity, which has the shape of a line times a sphere.

## one-liner
At the blown-up Schwarzschild singularity, coupling an adjoined hyperbolic plane to the dying direction with $\mu\nu < 0$ and bump inside the horizon gives an ambient form of constant signature $(2,4)$ everywhere, boundary included, with no shadow wall and a ladder pair existing exactly over the boundary.
