For a bivector $u \wedge v$ and a vector $w$, $(u \wedge v) \times w = (u \wedge v) \cdot w = u\,(v \cdot w) - v\,(u \cdot w)$. With $V = \sigma\nInf + \mu + \rho\nO$, $Q(\nO) = Q(\nInf) = 0$, $\nO \cdot \nInf = 1$ and $A$, $\Sch(A)$ orthogonal to $\nO$, $\nInf$:
\[
-(\nInf \wedge A) \cdot V = -\nInf\,(A \cdot \mu) + A\,\rho, \qquad (\nO \wedge \Sch(A)) \cdot V = \nO\,(\Sch(A) \cdot \mu) - \Sch(A)\,\sigma ,
\]
and $\Omega(a) \times V = \Omega(a) \times \mu$ since $\Omega(a)$ is a spacetime bivector. Adding $a \cdot \nabla V$ gives the slots of (i); with $D_{a}\mu = a \cdot \nabla\mu + \Omega(a) \times \mu$ the Levi-Civita derivative of $\mu$ in the physical frame (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}(ii)), these are the slots of the connection of Fact \claimref{interior.imported-tractor-connection}, the index-form terms $\mu_{a}$, $g_{ab}\rho$, $\Sch_{ab}\sigma$, $\Sch_{a}{}^{b}\mu_{b}$ reading $\mu \cdot A$, $\rho A$, $\sigma\Sch(A)$, $\Sch(A) \cdot \mu$. A bivector acts by an infinitesimal rotation, so $V \cdot (\Omega_{T}(a) \times V) = 0$ and $Q$ is preserved.

For (ii), $a \cdot \nabla I = (a \cdot \nabla\rho)\,\nO$ and the two displays give $\Omega_{T}(a) \times I = \rho A - \Sch(A)$; the $\nO$ slot and the spacetime slot vanish for all $a$ exactly when $\rho$ is constant and $\Sch = \rho\,\mathrm{id}$, and then $\mathcal{R}(a) = 2\Sch(a) + \tfrac16 a\mathcal{R}$; taking the trace, $\mathcal{R} = 24\rho$, so $\mathcal{R}(a) = 2\rho a + 4\rho a = 6\rho a$; set $\Lambda = 6\rho$, so that $\mathcal{R}(a) = \Lambda a$, as in Theorem \claimref{interior.scale-tractor-leaves-cone}; $Q(I) = 2\rho = \Lambda/3$.

For (iii), $a \cdot \nabla V = -\tfrac{\Lambda}{6}(a \cdot x)\nInf - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}a$ and $-(\nInf \wedge a) \cdot V = \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}(a \cdot x)\nInf + \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}a$, which cancel; $Q(V) = 2\sigma\tfrac{\Lambda}{6} + \tfrac{\Lambda^{2}}{36}Q(x) = \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}$; at $x = 0$, $V = \nInf + \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO$, the frozen point of Lemma \claimref{mass.de-sitter-scale-frozen-point}.

For (iv), the field strength of $\Omega_{T}$ is $R_{T}(a \wedge b) = a \cdot \nabla\Omega_{T}(b) - b \cdot \nabla\Omega_{T}(a) + \Omega_{T}(a) \times \Omega_{T}(b)$. Collecting the terms with $\Omega$ alone gives $R(a \wedge b)$, whose covariant form is $-\mathcal{R}(A \wedge B)$ in the store's sign; the terms linear in $\Theta$ combine with $\Omega$ into covariant derivatives of the frame (the torsion, along $\nInf$) and of $\Sch$ (the Cotton term, along $\nO$); the remaining term is $\Theta(A) \times \Theta(B)$. Its $\nInf \wedge \nO$ part has coefficient $A \cdot \Sch(B) - B \cdot \Sch(A) = 0$, the products $(\nInf \wedge A) \times (\nInf \wedge B)$ and $(\nO \wedge \Sch(A)) \times (\nO \wedge \Sch(B))$ vanish because $\nInf$ and $\nO$ are null, and the spacetime part is $\Sch(A) \wedge B + A \wedge \Sch(B)$. So the spacetime block is $-(\mathcal{R}(A \wedge B) - \Sch(A) \wedge B - A \wedge \Sch(B)) = -\mathcal{W}(A \wedge B)$ by Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}(v). On de Sitter space $\mathcal{R}(B) = \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}B$ and $\Sch = \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\mathrm{id}$, so it vanishes. The four checks verify (i) to (iv) in exact arithmetic on fixed rational data, the general curvature operators of (iv) being sums of a Kulkarni-Nomizu part and a type-D Weyl part.


For (v), with $\Sch = \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\mathrm{id}$, $\Theta(A) = -\nInf \wedge A + \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO \wedge A = A \wedge (\nInf - \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO) = A \wedge W$. With $I = \nInf + \tfrac{\Lambda}{6}\nO$ and $V = \nO - \tfrac{3}{\Lambda}I$ (Lemma \claimref{dark-energy.stelle-west-vector-frozen-point}), $Q(V) = -\tfrac{6}{\Lambda} + \tfrac{9}{\Lambda^{2}}\cdot\tfrac{\Lambda}{3} = -\tfrac{3}{\Lambda}$ and $V/Q(V) = W$. Then $(A \wedge W) \cdot V = A\,(W \cdot V) - W\,(A \cdot V) = A$, $(A \wedge W) \cdot I = A\,(W \cdot I) = 0$, and $(A \wedge W) \times (B \wedge W) = -Q(W)\,A \wedge B = \tfrac{\Lambda}{3}A \wedge B$; since $W = -V/\ell^{2}$, $\Omega_{T}(a) = \Omega(a) - \hb^{-1}(a) \wedge V/\ell^{2}$, and its Lorentz block is $-\mathcal{R}(A \wedge B) + \ell^{-2}A \wedge B$.
