A product and quotient of rational multiples of integer powers of $\pi$ is one, which is the closure the script checks on the rule's three factors. $1/(4\ln 2) = q\pi^k$ with $q$ rational gives $\pi^k\ln 2 = 1/(4q)$ rational; at $k = 0$ this says $\ln 2$ is rational, contradicting its transcendence (Hermite-Lindemann, an import, not scripted). The residues of the natural rules are Proposition \claimref{scrambling.candidate-principles-their-yields}'s yields divided by $A$: $16\pi\,\SBH/A = 4\pi$ and $A/A = 1$.
