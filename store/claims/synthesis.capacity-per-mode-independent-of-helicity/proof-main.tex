\emph{(i)} By Proposition \claimref{scrambling.pair-mode-qubit-one-object} a boundary mode is one ladder pair $(v, w)$, whose algebra is $\Mat_{2}$ acting on the two-dimensional Fock space $\{\ket{0}, v\ket{0}\}$; the script realises the pair in $\Cl(1,1)$ as $v = \tfrac12(e_{+} + e_{-})$, $w = \tfrac12(e_{+} - e_{-})$ and checks the CAR, that $wv$ is an idempotent of rank one, and that the left ideal $\Cl(1,1)\,wv$ has dimension $2$. The von Neumann entropy of a density on $\C^{2}$ is at most $\ln 2$, attained only at the maximally mixed state. This bound is a property of the mode's state space and does not refer to what the mode registers. In the Fock reading each helicity channel is empty or occupied, so two helicity channels have four joint occupation states; a capacity $2\ln 2$ is the maximal entropy of that four-dimensional state space, that is of two ladder pairs, which by the same Proposition are two boundary modes.

\emph{(ii)} Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy}(i) gives, under its hypothesis $\sum_{j \in J}s(x_{j}) = A/4$, the residue $\kappa_{c} = |J|/A = 1/(4\langle s\rangle)$ with $\langle s\rangle$ the mean entropy per counted ladder pair. Partition $J$ into units of $h$ pairs, $|J| = h\,|U|$; the total entropy is the same sum, so the mean per unit is $\langle s\rangle_{h} = h\langle s\rangle$ and $\kappa_{c}^{(h)} = |U|/A = 1/(4\langle s\rangle_{h}) = \kappa_{c}/h$. Since $s(x_{j}) \le \ln 2$ with equality iff $x_{j} = 0$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy}(ii)), $\kappa_{c}^{(h)} \ge 1/(4h\ln 2)$ with equality iff every pair is maximally mixed, a condition on the pairs that does not involve $h$. The number of pairs per Planck area per modular period is $\kappa_{c}$ in both cases. The mode density $\nu$ of Theorem \claimref{scrambling.mode-count} counts fermionic modes per Planck cell, that is ladder pairs, so it is unchanged. The script checks the bookkeeping exactly for $h = 1, 2$ on random rational per-pair entropies in units of $\ln 2$.

\emph{(iii)} In the capacity convention and under the hypothesis of (ii), Proposition \claimref{scrambling.residue-each-convention}(ii) is the capacity-convention value per boundary mode, $1/(4\ln 2)$; its clause (iv) excludes $1/(8\ln 2)$ by appeal to the helicity lift. By (i) the capacity per boundary mode is $\ln 2$ from Proposition \claimref{scrambling.pair-mode-qubit-one-object} alone, and by (ii) $1/(8\ln 2)$ is the same ladder-pair count per unit of two pairs. Neither step uses joint J3, which enters only in identifying which ladder pairs belong to which channel $(\omega, \ell, m, s)$ of a species.
