## formal
(i) The completion of an increasing union of full matrix algebras $\Mat_{2^k}(\C)$ along unital embeddings, in its unique C*-norm, is the UHF algebra of type $2^\infty$, unique up to *-isomorphism. \emph{Provenance:} \cite{Glimm1960}. (ii) Any two C*-algebras generated by elements satisfying the canonical anticommutation relations over a given complex mode space, with $*$ matching the pairing, are canonically *-isomorphic; the CAR algebra over $\ell^2$ is *-isomorphic to the $2^\infty$ UHF algebra. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thm.~5.2.5]{BratteliRobinson1997}.

## technical
(i) Completing an increasing union of full matrix algebras $\Mat_{2^k}(\C)$ along unital embeddings, in its unique C*-norm, gives the UHF algebra of type $2^\infty$, unique up to *-isomorphism (Glimm).

(ii) Any two C*-algebras generated by elements satisfying the canonical anticommutation relations over the same complex mode space, with $*$ matching the pairing, are canonically *-isomorphic, and the CAR algebra over $\ell^2$ is *-isomorphic to the $2^\infty$ UHF algebra (Bratteli and Robinson).

## lay
Gluing together ever larger matrix algebras, doubling in size each time, and completing gives one specific infinite-dimensional algebra, whatever unital gluing maps are used. Separately, the algebra generated by fermion creation and annihilation operators is determined by their relations alone, and for countably many modes it is that same algebra. Both are standard results imported from the operator-algebra literature.

## one-liner
The completion of a unital chain of matrix algebras $\Mat_{2^k}(\C)$ is the unique $2^\infty$ UHF algebra, and the CAR algebra over $\ell^2$ is canonically determined by its relations and isomorphic to it.
