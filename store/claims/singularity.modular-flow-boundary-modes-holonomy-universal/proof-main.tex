(i) In the synchronous form the $t$-line at fixed $r$ has unit tangent $e_1$ and the connection on it is $\sigma_1 B_1$ per unit proper length $\sqrt{A}\,dt$ of the line, with $\sigma_1 = \partial_\tau\log\sqrt A = \tfrac12 (A'/A)\,dr/d\tau = \tfrac12 A'/\sqrt{A}$, since $dr/d\tau = +\sqrt A$ in the orientation of Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-interior-collar-kasner-form}, where $\tau$ increases with $r$ (and $\sigma_1 < 0$ there, $A$ decreasing in $r$, matching the sign of $\tau\sigma_1 \to -\tfrac13$); per unit Killing time the rapidity rate is therefore $\sigma_1\sqrt A = \tfrac12 A'(r)$, of magnitude $-\tfrac12 A'(r)$, which for $A = 1/r - 1$ is $1/(2r^2)$: the script verifies $-\tfrac12 A' = 1/(2r^2)$ as an identity in $\mathbb{Q}(r)$, its value $\tfrac12$ at $r = 1$, and the values $2, 8, 32$ at $r = \tfrac12, \tfrac14, \tfrac18$. That $\kappa = 1/4M = \tfrac12$ in these units is Proposition \claimref{singularity.plane-rate}, and that the modular flow is the boost of this plane at the geometric period is Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-structure-completed-pair} with Premise \claimref{singularity.r-regularity-fixed-locus}. (ii) With $\tau = \tfrac23 r^{3/2} S(r)$, $S = 1 + \tfrac{3}{10}r + \tfrac{9}{56}r^2 + \cdots$ the series of Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-interior-collar-kasner-form}, $\tau |A'|/(2\sqrt A) = \tfrac23 r^{3/2} S \cdot \tfrac{1}{2r^2} \cdot \sqrt{r/(1-r)} = \tfrac13 S(r)(1 - r)^{-1/2}$; the script forms the product of exact truncated series and reads off $\tfrac13 + \tfrac{4}{15}r$, whose constant term is $|p_1|$ (Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-interior-collar-kasner-form}: $\tau\sigma_1 \to -\tfrac13$, the sign being that of $A' < 0$), and likewise the sphere rate $\tau\sigma_2 = \tau\sqrt{A}/r = \tfrac23 S(r)(1 - r)^{1/2} = \tfrac23 - \tfrac{2}{15}r + \cdots$, whose constant term is $p_2$; the script then divides $2\pi$ by each constant term, obtaining $6\pi$ and $3\pi$ from the series themselves. The conditional clause is the definition of the KMS condition: a KMS state of the flow generated by a boost of rapidity rate $\rho$ per unit parameter has inverse temperature $2\pi/\rho$ in that parameter (Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-structure-completed-pair}, Premise \claimref{singularity.r-regularity-fixed-locus}, applied along the $t$-line), and the rate at the exceptional set in Hubble-normalised time is $|p_1|$. (iii) The commutator is computed blade-exactly in $\Cl(1,3)$. If a faithful normal state is KMS at $\beta \neq 0$ for a flow $\alpha_t$ then its modular group is $\alpha_{-\beta t}$, and the modular group is unique (Tomita-Takesaki theory, \cite[Sec.~5.3]{BR2}; Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-general-form} imports the state-side consequences of the same theory), so a second flow $\alpha'_t$ with the state KMS at $\beta' \neq 0$ would satisfy $\alpha_{-\beta t} = \alpha'_{-\beta' t}$ for all $t$, making the generators proportional, which the commutator excludes for the pairs among $B_1$, $B_2$, $B_3$.
