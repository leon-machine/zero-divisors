\emph{Setting.} As in Proposition \claimref{synthesis.koide-channel-phase-is-no-symmetry-angle}, the projected coherent face vector of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.coherent-face-vector-real-slices} is read as the $\sqrt{m}$ vector; at the Koide depth $\lambda^{2} = 2$ (the null condition of the equal-coefficient member of Proposition \claimref{families.koide-nullity-condition-one-member-invariant}) it is $p_{k} = 1 + \sqrt2\cos(\delta + \tfrac{2\pi k}{3})$ up to a common factor, and $r(\delta) = (p_{2}/p_{1})^{2}$ increases strictly from $1$ to $\infty$ on $[0, \tfrac{\pi}{12})$ (proved there). The imported input is the one-loop QED relation between the pole mass and the $\overline{\mathrm{MS}}$ mass of a lepton of unit charge, $m^{\mathrm{pole}} = \bar{m}(\mu)\bigl(1 + a(1 + \tfrac32\ln(\mu/\bar{m}))\bigr)$, $a = \alpha/\pi$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-one-loop-qed-pole-mass-relation}; the form used by \cite{Sumino2009}, eq. (6) of \cite{Sumino2009b}). Nothing else is imported; the numbers are the stored pole masses and the stored $\alpha^{-1}(M_{Z}) = 127.951$.

\emph{(i)} For two leptons at a common $\mu$, write $A = 1 + \tfrac32(\ln\mu - \ln m_{j})$, $B = 1 + \tfrac32(\ln\mu - \ln m_{i})$. Then $\bar{m}_{i}/\bar{m}_{j} = (m_{i}/m_{j})(1 + aA)/(1 + aB) = (m_{i}/m_{j})\bigl(1 + a(A - B) + O(a^{2})\bigr)$ and $A - B = \tfrac32\ln(m_{i}/m_{j})$: the $\mu$-dependence and the constant cancel, and with $\kappa = \tfrac32 a = 3\alpha/(2\pi)$, $\bar{r} = r(1 + \kappa\ln r)$ to first order. Equivalently $\bar{m}_{i} \propto m_{i}(1 + \kappa\ln m_{i})$ with a common factor that no ratio and no value of $Q$ sees. At $\mu = \bar{m}_{i}$ for each lepton the logarithm vanishes and every factor is $1 + a$, so ratios of own-scale masses are pole ratios at this order. (The difference between $\bar{m}$ and $m$ inside the logarithm is second order.) The check confirms the coefficient algebra on three rational instances of the logarithms and that the second-order coefficient does not vanish, so the statement is first-order only.

\emph{(ii)} $\bar{r}$ increases with $r$ and with $\kappa$ (as $\ln r > 0$), so over the stored window $r \in (r_{\min}, r_{\max})$ of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.koide-channel-phase-is-no-symmetry-angle} and $\alpha \in [\tfrac{1}{138}, \alpha(M_{Z})]$ its extremes are at the corners. With $r(\delta)$ evaluated as in the stored check (alternating Taylor polynomials for $\sin$, $\cos$; $\sqrt2$, $\sqrt3$ by integer square roots; outward rounding), $r(0.22254) < \bar{r}_{\min}$ and $r(0.22258) > \bar{r}_{\max}$, and neither survives one step of $10^{-5}$ inwards; $0.22258 < \tfrac{\pi}{12}$. \emph{Consequence.} The proved statements of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.koide-channel-phase-is-no-symmetry-angle} stand as statements about pole masses. What changes is their reach: a rule that fixes the face mass form at the frozen point fixes it at a scale far above the lepton thresholds, where (i)-(ii) put its target at $\bar{\delta}$ in the first-order band, more than $10^{5}$ pole-window widths from $\delta_{*}$, and higher orders move it by about $10^{-5}$ (computed in floating point: the running masses at $M_{Z}$ of Xing, Zhang and Zhou, 2008, cited from memory, $0.48657$, $102.718$, $1746.24$ MeV, give $m_{\mu}/m_{e} = 211.11$ and $\delta = 0.22259$, just outside the first-order band). This does not touch (iii) or (v). And by (iv) exact Koide at a common scale contradicts exact Koide at the pole masses at first order, so either the series' masses are pole masses by premise, or a mechanism must cancel the $\kappa\ln m_{i}$ term (the family-gauge cancellation of \cite{Sumino2009} is one such), or the measured pole-mass Koide is not what the face mass form predicts. Under the mass-squared reading, which the store takes as primary and in which the channel phase does not enter the spectrum of source-built completions, (ii), (iii) and (v) have no counterpart; (i) and (iv) carry over unchanged, as statements about the mass ratios and Koide's quotient. The dictionary in force is pole masses, provisionally; under it the stored statements stand. The result is transport-absent: QED at one loop, the stored masses and the stored parametrisation.
