In the Jordan-Wigner representation $\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma_x)$ fixes $v_y$ for $y \neq x$ and negates it at $y = x$, $\sigma_x$ being single-site; hence every generator of every mode lies in some other point's big fibre, $J(F)$ contains them all, and words in them span the full $F$-mode algebra (verified by exact rank on two modes, Section \ref{sec:verification}). Isotony is the inclusion of the $F$-mode algebra in the $F'$-mode algebra, which carries big fibres to big fibres. The last clause is $v_y \in B_x$ for $x \neq y$ with $\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma_y)(v_y) = -v_y$.
