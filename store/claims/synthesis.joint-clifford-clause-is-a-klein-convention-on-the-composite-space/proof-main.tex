\emph{Notation.} Numbers are read in $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$ (conventions); $D$ is the slot doublet, $F = \Lambda^{\bullet}D$, $V = F \otimes \Lambda^{\bullet}W$; $a_{i} = a(u_{i}) \otimes 1$ ($u_{1}, u_{2}$ an orthonormal basis of $D$, $a^{\dagger}(u) = u \wedge$), $b_{p} = 1 \otimes b(t_{p})$ ($t_{1}, t_{2}, t_{3}$ an orthonormal basis of $W$), $(-1)^{k} = 1 \otimes (-1)^{\deg}$ the pair parity, $(-1)^{N} = (-1)^{n}(-1)^{k}$, and $\tilde a_{i} = a_{i}(-1)^{k}$. Products of operators on a tensor product: $(X \otimes Y)(X' \otimes Y') = XX' \otimes YY'$, so operators on different factors commute.

\emph{Step 1 (realisation in geometric algebra).} Take the floor $\Cl(5,5)$ of \claimref{synthesis.even-slots-need-a-spectator-generator}: generators $e_{k}$ ($e_{k}^{2} = 1$), $f_{k}$ ($f_{k}^{2} = -1$), $k = 0, \dots, 4$, null pairs $v_{k} = \tfrac12(e_{k} + f_{k})$, $w_{k} = \tfrac12(e_{k} - f_{k})$ with $v_{k}w_{l} + w_{l}v_{k} = \delta_{kl}$ and all other anticommutators zero, modes $0, 1$ the slots and $2, 3, 4$ the pairs, vacuum the null blade $\Omega = w_{0}\cdots w_{4}$. The $32$ states $v_{K}v_{J}\Omega$ ($v_{K}$ an ordered product of pair creators, $v_{J}$ of slot creators) are independent and span the Fock ideal; identify $v_{K}v_{J}\Omega$ with $u_{J} \otimes t_{K}$. A pair vector, acting from the left, passes no slot vector, so it acts as $1 \otimes b$; a slot vector passes the $k$ pair creators of $v_{K}$, so it acts as $a \otimes (-1)^{k}$: the vectors $v_{i}, w_{i}$ ($i = 0, 1$) are the twisted $\tilde a^{\dagger}_{i}, \tilde a_{i}$, and the untwisted ones are $v_{i}\Gamma_{W}$, $w_{i}\Gamma_{W}$ with $\Gamma_{W} = \prod_{p}(1 - 2v_{p}w_{p})$, the pair parity, as in clause (iii) of that result; the device is the commuting-copies twist of \claimref{tower.commuting-copies} read backwards. Since $2v_{p}w_{p} = \tfrac12(e_{p} + f_{p})(e_{p} - f_{p}) = 1 - e_{p}f_{p}$, $1 - 2v_{p}w_{p} = e_{p}f_{p}$ and $\Gamma_{W} = e_{2}f_{2}e_{3}f_{3}e_{4}f_{4}$ is a single blade, $\Gamma_{W}^{2} = 1$, commuting with the slot vectors and anticommuting with the six pair vectors. Over $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$, $I_{4}$ commuting with all ten generators, $\Cl(5,5)$ spans $\mathrm{End}(V)$.

\emph{Step 2 ((i) and (ii)).} $\Gamma_{W}$ commutes with $a_{i}$, $a_{i}^{\dagger}$ and squares to $1$, so $\tilde a_{i}\tilde a_{j}^{\dagger} + \tilde a_{j}^{\dagger}\tilde a_{i} = a_{i}a_{j}^{\dagger} + a_{j}^{\dagger}a_{i} = \delta_{ij}$ and likewise for the other anticommutators. $A \otimes \Lambda^{\bullet}B$ preserves the pair degree, so it commutes with $(-1)^{k}$ and $\tilde a_{i}$ transforms as $a_{i}$ does. On $F_{n} \otimes \Lambda^{k}W$, $\tilde a_{i}^{\dagger} = (-1)^{k}a_{i}^{\dagger}$, so the creation maps have the same images and clause (G) holds for one set exactly when it holds for the other. The $a_{i}$ act on the first factor and the $b_{p}$ on the second, so they commute; $(-1)^{k}$ anticommutes with $b_{p}$ and $b_{p}^{\dagger}$, so $\tilde a_{i}b_{p} = -b_{p}\tilde a_{i}$ (in $\Cl(5,5)$: distinct vectors anticommute). Hence $\tilde a_{1}, \tilde a_{2}, b_{1}, b_{2}, b_{3}$ satisfy the canonical anticommutation relations of one system of five modes, and the real Majorana combinations $\tilde a + \tilde a^{\dagger}$, $\tilde a - \tilde a^{\dagger}$, $b + b^{\dagger}$, $b - b^{\dagger}$ are the ten vectors $e_{k}$, $f_{k}$, generating $\Cl(5,5)$; its span over $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$ is the $\Cl(10)$ of clause (f), with no imaginary unit needed for the Majorana of square $-1$.

\emph{Step 3 ((iii)).} For two slot operators $x, y$, $(x\Gamma_{W})(y\Gamma_{W}) = xy\,\Gamma_{W}^{2} = xy$; so every slot-slot bilinear, in particular the $\binom{4}{2} = 6$ slot Majorana bivectors, is the same element in both conventions. The pair operators are not twisted, so the $\binom{6}{2} = 15$ pair Majorana bivectors are the same too: the $21$ split-preserving bivectors of clause (CPT), the Pati-Salam algebra (\claimref{standard-model.automorphisms-preserve-slot-pair-split-are}, cited for the name only), do not depend on the convention. $\Gamma_{W}$ commutes with every slot-slot and pair-pair bilinear, the generators of $\uu(2) \oplus \uu(3)$ and the pair creation and annihilation bilinears among them. The grading $(-1)^{N} = \prod_{k=0}^{4}e_{k}f_{k}$ anticommutes with every one of the ten vectors and commutes with $\Gamma_{W}$, so it anticommutes with $a_{i}$, $\tilde a_{i}$ and $b_{p}$ alike: the grading form of clause (CPT) does not depend on the convention either.

\emph{Step 4 ((iv), uniqueness).} Let $K$ commute with every $a_{i}$, $a_{i}^{\dagger}$, and let $a_{i}K$ anticommute with every $b_{p}$, $b_{p}^{\dagger}$ and satisfy the canonical anticommutation relations. For a pair operator $\beta$, $a_{i}K\beta + \beta a_{i}K = a_{i}(K\beta + \beta K)$, and likewise with $a_{i}^{\dagger}$; adding $a_{i}^{\dagger}$ times the first to $a_{i}$ times the second gives $K\beta + \beta K = 0$. So $K(-1)^{k}$ commutes with every pair operator and every slot operator. The slot operators generate $\mathrm{End}(F) \otimes 1$ and the pair operators $1 \otimes \mathrm{End}(\Lambda^{\bullet}W)$ (the Fock representations of the canonical anticommutation relations are irreducible), which together generate $\mathrm{End}(V)$; so $K(-1)^{k}$ is a number $c \in \mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$, and the relation $(a_{i}K)(a_{i}K)^{\dagger} + (a_{i}K)^{\dagger}(a_{i}K) = |c|^{2} = 1$ makes $c$ a unit; $c = \pm 1$ when $K$ is an involution or a real element of the floor. A unit $c$ is a phase that the centre of the slot $U(2)$ already supplies. In the realisation the same follows by counting blades with the commutation form of \claimref{synthesis.blade-square-signs-f2-quadratic-form}(ii): blades $e_{S}$, $e_{T}$ anticommute exactly when $b(S, T) = |S||T| + |S \cap T| = 1 \pmod 2$; the conditions are diagonal in the blade basis, and with $P = \{e_{2}, e_{3}, e_{4}, f_{2}, f_{3}, f_{4}\}$, $b(S, \{x\}) = |S| + [x \in S] = 1$ for all $x \in P$ forces $S \supseteq P$ with $|S|$ even or $S \cap P = \emptyset$ with $|S|$ odd, and commuting with the untwisted slot Majoranas $e_{S'}$, $S' = \{y\} \cup P$, forces $[y \in S] = |S| + |S \cap P| \pmod 2$ for the four slot generators $y$; the first case gives $S = P$, the second would need all four slot generators in $S$ with $|S| = 4$ odd. So $K \in \mathrm{span}\{\Gamma_{W}\}$.

\emph{Step 5 (the reading; (v)).} By Steps 2 to 4, on $V$ the operators required by clause (f) exist whenever $F \cong \Lambda^{\bullet}D$, namely the Klein-twisted slot operators, unique up to a unit; every operator the clause produces ($\Cl(10)$, $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$, the $24$ cross bivectors) is an operator on $V$ built from them, and everything clause (CPT) asks of the convention is convention-free. The $24$ cross bivectors do depend on the convention (only the twisted operators give $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$); the residual sign $c = -1$ acts on them by $\mathrm{Ad}$ of the slot parity $(-1)^{n} = e_{0}f_{0}e_{1}f_{1}$ (of reverse norm $1$, the central element $-1$ of the slot $U(2)$, an element of $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$), which negates the slot Majoranas and fixes the pair ones, so the $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ of clause (f) is canonical as a subgroup. So on $V$ clause (f) is a choice of convention with no content of its own. It keeps content only relative to an embedding of slot operators given in advance, for instance as tower elements, where the question is whether the given operators are the twisted or the untwisted ones; this is the setting of \claimref{synthesis.even-slots-need-a-spectator-generator}(iii), which already makes the replacement $s_{i} \mapsto s_{i}\Gamma_{W}$, and it is not addressed here. The checks verify Steps 1 to 4 exactly in $\Cl(5,5)$, including the commutation form against the products for all $1024$ blades.
