A pair $(M,G)$, $G$ a smooth symmetric form on a bundle over $M$, is a tame (\claimref{sheaf.tameness}) corank-one degeneration if (T1) the degenerate locus $\Sigma$ is a smooth closed hypersurface, (T2) the corank is exactly one on $\Sigma$ with the radical lines a smooth line bundle spanned locally by $k$, and (T3) the dying eigenvalue crosses zero at nonzero speed along every transversal, invariantly: the section $\delta(k \otimes k) = dg_{k}|_{\Sigma}$ of $\operatorname{Hom}(L^{\otimes 2}, N^{*}\Sigma)$ is nowhere zero. The signatures on the two sides differ by one unit, $(p,q)$ where the dying eigenvalue is positive against $(p-1, q+1)$. Rescaling the dying direction by $t^{-1/2}$ along a transversal gives a continuous family of nondegenerate algebras through the wall whose special fibre, the exceptional algebra, is the Clifford algebra of the surviving form orthogonal to the rate of degeneration, of signature $(p,q)$ on the side approached. The inverse form has a canonical simple pole at the wall whose residue is $\delta^{-1}$, giving $N^{*}\Sigma \cong L^{\otimes 2}$: the dying line is a square root of the conormal bundle and the adjoined partner line a square root of the normal bundle. Conservation, absolute form: for a tame wall with both sides nonempty, any symmetric form on any bundle containing the original, standard outside a neighbourhood of the wall in the sense of being $G \perp K$ there with $K$ nondegenerate of the \emph{same} signature on both exterior regions, degenerates somewhere; the constancy of $\operatorname{sig} K$ across the wall is necessary, a completion allowed to switch $K$'s signature being able to match the two sides. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Hyp.~2.1, Lem.~2.2, Rem.~2.3, Prop.~6.1, Thm.~6.2, Thm.~5.1]{ButlerT4} (\claimref{sheaf.metrics-residue-square-root-twist}, \claimref{sheaf.invariant-form-t3}, \claimref{sheaf.exceptional-algebra-deformation-normal-cone}, \claimref{sheaf.conservation-degeneracy-absolute-form}).
