The script checks the induced map on the even sector's four matrix units for both, and the failure of evenness for both, by evaluating $W\G_{S}$ against $(\G_{S}\otimes\G_{R})W$ on the even basis. That no even one-rung lift exists is Theorem \claimref{descent-lift.lift-costs-one-rung-per-mode}(i) and (ii): a lift needs two orthonormal even record states and one rung has one. That parity is the only obstruction is shown by the two-rung occupation copy $P_{00}\psi \otimes \ket{00} + P_{11}\psi \otimes \ket{11}$, which is even and is the lift of Theorem \claimref{descent-lift.lift-costs-one-rung-per-mode}(iii) written in the record basis $\ket{00} \pm \ket{11}$: $W = P_{00} \otimes (\ket{00} + \ket{11}) + P_{11} \otimes (\ket{00} - \ket{11})$; the script checks the identity, the evenness and the induced channel.
