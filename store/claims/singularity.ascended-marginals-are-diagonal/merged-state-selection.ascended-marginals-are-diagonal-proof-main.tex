$v_k$ and $v_k^{*}$ are odd, so $\alpha(v_k) = -v_k$ and (P, \claimref{singularity.p-univalence}) gives $\omega(v_k) = \omega(\alpha(v_k)) = -\omega(v_k)$, hence $\omega(v_k) = 0$; likewise for $v_k^{*}$. The off-diagonal entries of the marginal $\rho_k$ in the number basis are exactly $\omega(v_k)$ and $\omega(v_k^{*})$, since $v_k$ and $v_k^{*}$ are the two off-diagonal matrix units of the mode. Diagonality is then the coincidence with $E_k(\rho_k)$, and Theorem \claimref{state-selection.descended-form-state-independent} supplies the Gibbs form. The argument uses no finiteness and applies verbatim to the CAR algebra over $E$. For the model: its global states are supported on configurations with $R_k = L_k$ for every $k$ \cite[Lem.~12.1]{ButlerT5} (\claimref{singularity.parity-protection}), whose total occupation is $2\sum_k b_k$, hence even; a state supported on a single parity sector is even.
