Read in Gauge Theory Gravity: the metric $g$ below is the derived metric of the gravitational field $(\hb, \Omega)$ on flat spacetime, the manifold is the invertible region, and the tractor connection is the flat-space $\Cl(2,4)$ gauge field of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.gtg-tractor-gauge-field-reproduces-tractor-connection}; the index computation below is its reading in the splitting of $g$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-gtg-dictionary}).

(i) The conformal class carries a canonical connection on the rank-six tractor bundle whose expression in a scale $g$ is
\[
\nabla_a(\sigma, \mu_b, \rho) = \bigl(\nabla_a\sigma - \mu_a,\;\; \nabla_a\mu_b + g_{ab}\rho - \Sch_{ab}\sigma,\;\; \nabla_a\rho + \Sch_a{}^b\mu_b\bigr),
\]
with $\Sch$ the Schouten tensor. (ii) The conformal class likewise carries the classical bundle of conformal spinors (the \emph{local twistor} bundle), fibrewise the conformal spinor space $\CS = \Cl^+(2,4)P$, a $4$-dimensional module over the complex unit $I_6$, with the scale splitting $Z = (\omega^B, \pi_{B'})$ into the halves $\Som \oplus \Spi$, and its transport, the conformal spinor connection (local twistor transport),
\[
\nabla^{\mathrm{LT}}_a(\omega^B, \pi_{B'}) \;=\; \bigl(\nabla_a \omega^B + \varepsilon_A{}^B\,\pi_{A'}I_6,\;\; \nabla_a\pi_{B'} + \Sch_{AA'BB'}\,\omega^B I_6\bigr),
\]
in the index conventions of \cite[Vol.~2, Ch.~6]{PenroseRindler86}, where the complex unit of $\CS$, the source's imaginary unit, is right multiplication by the conformal pseudoscalar $I_6$; under conformal rescaling $\omega$ is invariant and $\pi$ shifts, so the $\pi$-plane $\{\omega = 0\}$ (the kernel in $\CS$ of the point's null vector) is the canonical subbundle. \emph{Provenance:} (i) \cite{BEG1994, CurryGover2018}, in the conventions of \cite[Fact 4.1, eq.~(3)]{ButlerT6} (\claimref{interior.imported-tractor-connection}); (ii) \cite{PenroseMacCallum73, Dighton74}; the statement that (ii) is the spin representation of (i), that is, that the conformal spinor connection is the tractor connection acting on $\CS$, is standard in the tractor literature \cite{BEG1994, CurryGover2018}, and is \emph{proved} rather than imported below, since the proof is short in the present framework.
