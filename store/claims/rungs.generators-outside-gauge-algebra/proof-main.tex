Here the imaginary unit is $I_{4}$, which commutes with every mode operator (on the five-mode Fock module numbers, $I_{4}$ among them, act as scalars; as Clifford elements $I_{4}$ commutes with the rung vectors, and the pair vectors of $V_{\C}$ are complexified by $I_{7}$, which is not used here). The Majorana generators $\gamma_{2i} = a_{i} + a_{i}^{\dagger}$, $\gamma_{2i+1} = I_{4}(a_{i} - a_{i}^{\dagger})$ of the five modes (by \claimref{rungs.hyp-r-rung-reading} or \claimref{standard-model.hyp-s-slot-statistics}) satisfy the Clifford relations, and the forty-five bilinears $\gamma_{p}\gamma_{q}$, $p < q$, span $\mathfrak{so}(10)$ (Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-fock-representation-bogoliubov-group}). The bilinears from one mode, $\gamma_{2i}\gamma_{2i+1} = I_{4}(2a_{i}^{\dagger}a_{i} - 1)$, five of them, and from two modes $i \neq j$ the two combinations $a_{i}^{\dagger}a_{j} - a_{j}^{\dagger}a_{i}$ and $I_{4}(a_{i}^{\dagger}a_{j} + a_{j}^{\dagger}a_{i})$, twenty of them, commute with the total number operator and span $\uu(5)$, of dimension twenty-five; the remaining two combinations from each pair of modes, $a_{i}a_{j} \pm a_{i}^{\dagger}a_{j}^{\dagger}$ up to factors of $I_{4}$, twenty of them, change the mode number by two. Of the twenty-five, those with $i, j$ both rungs or both pairs commute with the rung and pair numbers separately: $5 + 2 + 6 = 13$, spanning $\uu(2) \oplus \uu(3)$; the other twelve, with one rung index and one pair index, move an excitation across the split. The script computes the two centralisers as exact kernels, of dimensions $25$ and $13$, and exhibits the spanning sets.
