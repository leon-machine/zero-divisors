## formal
\emph{Theorem (HS), the Hadamard scaling limit (version 4; versions 1 to 3 were a premise, note below).} Units $\hbar = c = 1$. Let $(\hb, \Omega)$ be a gravitational field on four-dimensional flat spacetime (Definition \claimref{obj.synthesis.gtg-flat-spacetime-gauge-fields}) whose derived metric $g$ is smooth and globally hyperbolic on a region $U$ of its invertible region, $x_{0} \in U$ any point (the field strength there arbitrary), in a gauge with $\hb(x_{0}) = 1$. Let $\psi$ be the free Dirac field of mass $m_{0} \ge 0$ on it (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-dimock-dirac-car-algebra-on-a-gravitational-field}) and $\omega$ a gauge-invariant quasi-free Hadamard state (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-microlocal-hadamard-condition}(b); Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-dirac-hadamard-form-propagates-and-adiabatic-states-are-hadamard}(a)). Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a local Rindler horizon through $x_{0}$ (in the sense of Premise \claimref{synthesis.c-loc-local-horizon-premise}, whose assertions are not used), or any piece through $x_{0}$ of a null hypersurface generated by affinely parametrised null geodesics $u \mapsto X(u, y)$, $x_{0} = X(u_{0}, y_{0})$, taken small enough to lie in a geodesically and causally convex neighbourhood with distinct generators spacelike separated; with the canonical projector $\Lambda$, area density $a$, canonical generator field $\psi_{\mathcal{H}} = \sqrt{2a}\,\Lambda\psi$ and multiplicity space $K = L^{2}(\R^{2}, d^{2}y)\otimes V_{\Lambda}$ of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.field-strength-cancels-from-the-leading-null-singularity-of-hadamard-dirac-states-on-null-hypersurfaces}, and $W_{0}(u, u') = \tfrac{i}{2\pi}(u - u' + i0)^{-1}$ the null vacuum. Then, for every $m_{0} \ge 0$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] \emph{(Bulk scaling limit.)} The scaling limit of $\omega$ at $x_{0}$ is the vacuum $\omega_{\mathrm{fl}}$ of the massless Dirac field on the flat field: $l^{3}\omega(\psi(x_{0} + ly)\psi^{\dagger}(x_{0} + ly')) \to i\nabla D_{+}(y - y')e_{0}$ as $l \to 0^{+}$, the other ordering likewise, whatever the Weyl and Ricci field strength at $x_{0}$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.hadamard-dirac-states-scale-to-the-flat-massless-vacuum-at-every-point-of-a-gravitational-field}(iii)).
\item[(b)] \emph{(Generator scaling limit.)} The generator field obeys the canonical anticommutation relations $\{\psi_{\mathcal{H}}(u, y), \psi_{\mathcal{H}}(u', y')^{*}\} = \delta(u - u')\,\delta^{2}(y - y')\,\Lambda$, and the restriction of $\omega$ to the generators through the flux trace, $\psi_{\mathcal{H}}(F) = \psi(\iota_{\mathcal{H}}F)$, has two-point function $W^{+}_{\mathcal{H},\omega} = W_{0}\otimes\id_{K} + r + D^{\omega}_{0,0}$, where for each pair of transverse profiles $r(u, u')$ is a bounded function, bounded uniformly for profiles bounded in $C^{4}$ with support in a fixed compact set, and $D^{\omega}_{0,0}$ is smooth. Hence $\lambda^{\varpi}(W^{+}_{\mathcal{H},\omega} - W_{0}\otimes\id_{K})(u_{0} + \lambda x, u_{0} + \lambda x') \to 0$ in $\mathcal{D}'$ for every $\varpi > 0$: the generator scaling limit of $\omega$ at $x_{0}$ is the null vacuum $\omega_{0}$, on both orderings.
\item[(c)] \emph{(The $\epsilon$-regularised restriction.)} The restriction of the Dirac Hadamard parametrix to the generators as the $\epsilon \to 0$ limit of the transverse-smeared, $i0$-regularised kernel, the form in which versions 1 to 3 posed the premise, is $W_{0}\otimes\id_{K}$ plus a bounded kernel, defined for a chosen time function and modulo smooth kernels (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.restricted-dirac-hadamard-parametrix-on-a-null-hypersurface-is-the-null-vacuum-plus-a-bounded-kernel}). It differs from the flux-trace restriction of the parametrix, $W^{+}_{\mathcal{H},\omega} - D^{\omega}_{0,0}$ (the same for every such state), by a bounded kernel that contains a smooth term depending on the time function, nonzero when the derivative of the time function along the generators varies along them; they agree modulo that kernel, not identically. Both have generator scaling degree at most $0$, so the clause of version 3 holds with every $\varpi \in (0, 1)$.
\item[(d)] \emph{(Scaling and restriction commute; the local form of (N$_{+}$).)} The generator scaling limit of (b) is the restriction of the bulk scaling limit of (a) to the null plane through $x_{0}$ tangent to $\mathcal{H}$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.bulk-hadamard-states-restrict-to-the-generator-condition-on-flat-null-planes}(ii) at $m_{0} = 0$), which was clause (b) of version 2. So every gauge-invariant quasi-free Hadamard state of the Dirac field, of any mass, has the local form of (N$_{+}$) (Premise \claimref{synthesis.n-plus-null-ground-state}, local form) at every point of every local Rindler horizon of every gravitational field in this scope.
\end{enumerate}
The proof assembles Theorems \claimref{synthesis.restricted-dirac-hadamard-parametrix-on-a-null-hypersurface-is-the-null-vacuum-plus-a-bounded-kernel}, \claimref{synthesis.dirac-states-restrict-to-null-hypersurfaces-through-the-flux-contraction-and-are-thin-layer-limits-under-the-generator-car} ((i), (iii-a), (iii-b)), \claimref{synthesis.dirac-causal-propagator-between-nearby-null-leaves-tends-to-the-generator-car-on-every-geodesic-null-hypersurface} ((i) to (iii)), \claimref{synthesis.field-strength-cancels-from-the-leading-null-singularity-of-hadamard-dirac-states-on-null-hypersurfaces}(i) and \claimref{synthesis.hadamard-dirac-states-scale-to-the-flat-massless-vacuum-at-every-point-of-a-gravitational-field}. Nothing in it uses the field equations, vanishing expansion or shear, or the value of the field strength.

\emph{What remains cited.} Proved given the imported Facts (within them, the parts their notes mark as cited from memory: the Lichnerowicz identification of the connection of the squared Dirac operator, the four-dimensional leading Hadamard coefficient, and the bundle case of the four-dimensional form of the fundamental solutions, which was checked against the source for scalar and vector fields) and, inside the proofs of the assembled results, standard inputs cited from memory and not imported: the existence of a local null foliation containing a small piece of a geodesic null hypersurface; the Morse lemma with parameters (H\"ormander, Vol.~III, App.~C, lemma number not checked) and the pullback of distributions (H\"ormander, Vol.~I, Thm.~8.2.4); the achronality of local Rindler horizons, which gives the spacelike separation of distinct generators there; the divergence theorem on a compact region with Lipschitz boundary.

\emph{What it does not give.} (1) Not the ground-state clause of (N$_{+}$): every Hadamard state passes, the thermal states of the field included, and on a generator the affine-KMS states have the same scaling limit (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.microlocal-condition-on-a-generator-gives-the-local-null-ground-state}(iv)); a global datum, such as stationarity at the edge, is still needed. (2) Not (Loc), the entropy clause of Premise \claimref{synthesis.c-loc-local-horizon-premise}. (3) Not which state: the series' states being Hadamard is Premise \claimref{synthesis.hadamard-deposit-premise}, (HD), with the propagation of the Hadamard form (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-dirac-hadamard-form-propagates-and-adiabatic-states-are-hadamard}(b)); no locally covariant choice of state exists (Hollands and Wald; Fewster and Verch), and the attachment's tractor transport supplies no state where the Weyl curvature is nonzero (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.attachment-transport-is-flat-only-where-weyl-vanishes}). (4) Not the generator microlocal condition on intervals (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.microlocal-condition-on-a-generator-gives-the-local-null-ground-state}(i)), only the scaling limit at each point. (5) Not the identity of the two restrictions: they agree modulo a bounded time-function-dependent kernel. (6) Not fields other than the free Dirac field. (7) The passage from the local form of (N$_{+}$) to the period of (R$_{\mathrm{loc}}$) where the field strength is nonzero stays the stored leading-order argument (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.positive-null-energy-gives-r-premises}), argued.

\emph{Relation to the open premise of 1 November.} That the series' states satisfy the Hadamard condition on horizon generators was recorded as an open premise (scope note on Premise \claimref{synthesis.n-plus-null-ground-state}). For the use the store makes of it, the local form of (N$_{+}$) at every point of every local Rindler horizon, it now rests on the bulk statement that the series' states are gauge-invariant quasi-free Hadamard states ((HD) and the propagation of the Hadamard form) and on this theorem, with no further premise.

\emph{Note (history: the premise, versions 1 to 3).} Version 1 (1 November 2026, adopted by Leon's decision as a premise at Tier C) asserted, at a point $x_{0}$ of a gravitational field with nonzero field strength, in a gauge with $\hb(x_{0}) = 1$ and $\Omega(x_{0}) = 0$, of every gauge-invariant quasi-free Hadamard state $\omega$ of the Dirac field: (a) that its scaled two-point kernels, on both orderings, converge as distributions to those of the flat massless vacuum $\omega_{\mathrm{fl}}$; and (b) that the state of its modes on the generators of every local Rindler horizon through $x_{0}$ has at $x_{0}$ the scaling limit $W_{\mathrm{fl},N}$, the restriction of $\omega_{\mathrm{fl}}$ to the tangent null plane $N$ (scaling and restriction commute). Its refutation condition named a curvature term at the order of the null-vacuum singularity. Version 2 (1 November) added a note that on Weyl-flat fields the conclusion is a theorem for the massless field at the leading order at which the premise is posed (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.weyl-flat-fields-need-no-scaling-limit-premise-and-robertson-walker-generators-see-the-schwarzian}(i), (ii)), so that the content was the Weyl-curved and massive cases. Version 3 (2 November) retired clause (a), which had become Theorem \claimref{synthesis.hadamard-dirac-states-scale-to-the-flat-massless-vacuum-at-every-point-of-a-gravitational-field}, and restated the premise as a statement about the field: the restriction of the Dirac Hadamard parametrix to the generators, as the $\epsilon \to 0$ limit of the transverse-smeared, $i0$-regularised kernel, minus $W_{0}\otimes\id_{K}$, has generator scaling degree below one at $x_{0}$, uniformly in compactly supported transverse profiles; the passage to states was argued, given the characteristic-data embedding (cited from memory) and the agreement of the two restrictions (not shown); its refutation condition was a restricted parametrix with a remainder as singular as the null vacuum. Version 4 (this one): the remainder bound of version 3 is proved, with degree at most $0$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.restricted-dirac-hadamard-parametrix-on-a-null-hypersurface-is-the-null-vacuum-plus-a-bounded-kernel}); the embedding is the flux contraction (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.dirac-states-restrict-to-null-hypersurfaces-through-the-flux-contraction-and-are-thin-layer-limits-under-the-generator-car}(i)); the agreement of the restrictions holds modulo a bounded time-function-dependent kernel (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.dirac-causal-propagator-between-nearby-null-leaves-tends-to-the-generator-car-on-every-geodesic-null-hypersurface}(iii)(b)); so (HS) is a theorem, and clause (b) of version 2 holds for every mass, at every point, whatever the field strength, with $\Omega(x_{0}) = 0$ not needed. The refutation conditions of versions 1 to 3 are void: each negation contradicts a proved result of the chain. No stored result stated any version as a hypothesis, so no assumes edge pointed to it.

## technical
This was the premise (HS) of the series; it is now a theorem. It says that every reasonable state of the Dirac field (gauge-invariant, quasi-free and Hadamard) looks like empty space at short distances near any point of a gravitational field, both in the bulk and along the light rays (generators) of every local Rindler horizon through the point, which is the local form of the null ground-state premise (N$_{+}$). The bulk part is a standard theorem of Sahlmann and Verch, redone in the store. The light-ray part follows from three results: the flux of the conserved Dirac current through a null sheet is at most the norm, which defines the light-ray field inside the field algebra and makes any two Hadamard states differ there by smooth kernels; the causal propagator restricted between the sheet and a nearby one tends to the canonical delta kernel, the only correction cancelling by the half-density law, which gives the canonical anticommutation relations on the light rays; and the Hadamard parametrix restricted to the light rays, either through thin layers or with the standard regularisation, is the null vacuum plus a bounded kernel, the logarithms cancelling by the same identity. A bounded kernel scales away, so every such state has the null vacuum as its scaling limit on the light rays, for every mass and whatever the field strength, given the imported standard results and a few standard inputs cited from memory (a local null foliation, the Morse lemma with parameters, the pullback theorem). The regularised restriction depends on the chosen time function by a smooth kernel, so the two restrictions agree up to a bounded kernel, not exactly. It does not give the ground-state clause of (N$_{+}$), since thermal states pass too, nor the entropy clause (Loc), nor which state the world is in: for the series' states it applies given that they are Hadamard, which is the separate premise (HD). It is stated for the free Dirac field only.

## lay
Physicists require that any reasonable quantum state, looked at closely enough, resembles empty space. The series had assumed this along the light rays of the small horizons used to derive the equations of gravity, wherever gravity is present. It is now proved, for the electron field of any mass, given standard results from the literature: the flow of electric charge through a sheet of light can never exceed the total, signals travel at or below light speed, and two growing corrections cancel because the area of the sheet and the focusing of its light rays are tied together. So every reasonable state looks like empty space along those light rays at short distances, which is what the series' horizon temperature needs locally. It does not pick out which state the world is in, and it does not by itself give the full horizon assumption, since warm states pass the test too.

## one-liner
Theorem (HS), formerly a premise: for every gauge-invariant quasi-free Hadamard state of the Dirac field of any mass on a gravitational field, at every point of every local Rindler horizon, the bulk scaling limit is the flat massless vacuum and, given the imported Facts and standard inputs cited from memory (local null foliation, Morse lemma with parameters), the restriction to the generators through the flux trace obeys the generator CAR and is $W_{0}\otimes\id_{K}$ plus a bounded and a smooth kernel, so its generator scaling limit is the null vacuum (the epsilon-regularised restriction agrees modulo a bounded time-function-dependent kernel); hence the local form of (N$_{+}$) there, not its ground-state clause, not (Loc), not which state.
