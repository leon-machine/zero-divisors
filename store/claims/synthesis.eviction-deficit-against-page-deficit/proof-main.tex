(i) By Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-page-average-entropy-subsystem}, $\langle S_{A}\rangle = \sum_{k=n+1}^{mn}k^{-1} - (m-1)/(2n)$. Since $1/x$ is decreasing, $\int_{n+1}^{mn+1}dx/x < \sum_{k=n+1}^{mn}k^{-1} < \int_{n}^{mn}dx/x = \ln m$. The right inequality gives $\delta_{\mathrm{P}} = \ln m - \langle S_{A}\rangle > (m-1)/(2n)$. The left gives $\delta_{\mathrm{P}} < \ln m - \ln\frac{mn + 1}{n + 1} + \frac{m - 1}{2n} = \ln\frac{m(n+1)}{mn + 1} + \frac{m - 1}{2n}$, and $\ln y < y - 1$ with $y = m(n+1)/(mn+1)$ gives $\ln y < (m - 1)/(mn + 1)$. Finally $(m-1)/(2n) < \tfrac12$ and $(m - 1)/(mn + 1) < 1/n \le \tfrac12$, so $\delta_{\mathrm{P}} < 1$. At $m = n$ the bounds give $\delta_{\mathrm{P}}(m, m) \in ((m-1)/(2m), (m-1)/(2m) + (m-1)/(m^{2} + 1))$, inside $(\tfrac25, \tfrac12 + \tfrac1m)$ for $m \ge 5$; for $m = 4$ the exact value, $\delta_{\mathrm{P}}(4, 4) = 2\ln 2 - \tfrac{664789}{720720} = 0.4639$, is computed. With $\dim L = m = 2^{w}$ and $\dim R = n = 2^{r}$, $r \ge w$ after the crossing, the lower bound $(2^{w} - 1)/2^{r+1}$ halves with each increment of $r$. The script encloses $\ln 2$ between rational partial sums of $\sum_{j}1/(j2^{j})$ with the tail bound $1/((N+1)2^{N})$, $N = 60$, and verifies both bounds, positivity, the bound $1$, and $\delta_{\mathrm{P}} > \tfrac25$ at the crossing, for $w = 1, \ldots, 5$ and $r = w, \ldots, w + 3$.

(ii) A zero-weight member has $x_{k} = 0$, occupation $\tfrac12$, and $h_{k} = s(0) = \ln 2$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.boundary-mode-thermal-entropy}); so every term of $\delta_{\mathrm{ev}}$ vanishes and, by Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}(4), $S_{R} = C$ on the falling branch, which with clause (3) is $S_{R} = \min(\Sth, C)$ at every stage. By (i) the Page-typical value is $C - \delta_{\mathrm{P}}$ with $\delta_{\mathrm{P}} > 0$ whenever $\dim L \ge 2$.

(iii) A member of weight $x$ has occupation $p = 1/(e^{x} + 1)$ and $\ln 2 - h = \rel{p}{\tfrac12}$, the relative entropy of the binary distribution $(p, 1 - p)$ to the uniform one, since $\ln 2 - H(p) = \sum p_{i}\ln(p_{i}/\tfrac12)$. Pinsker's inequality for two-point distributions in nats (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-pinsker-inequality}) gives $\rel{p}{\tfrac12} \ge 2(p - \tfrac12)^{2}$, and $\tfrac12 - 1/(e^{x} + 1) = \tfrac12\tanh(x/2)$, so each member with $|x| \ge x_{*}$ contributes at least $\tfrac12\tanh^{2}(x_{*}/2)$ and $\delta_{\mathrm{ev}} \ge \tfrac12|W(t)|\tanh^{2}(x_{*}/2)$. For (Q)'s weights, $x = 2\pi|k| \ge 2\pi > \tfrac{157}{25}$ and $e^{157/25} > 500$ (a Taylor partial sum, all terms positive), so $p < \tfrac1{501}$ and Pinsker alone gives more than $2(\tfrac{499}{1002})^{2} > \tfrac{49}{100}$ per member, which needs three members to pass $1$. A sharper bound gives two: $H$ is increasing on $(0, \tfrac12]$, and $H(p) = p\ln(1/p) + (1 - p)\ln(1/(1 - p)) \le p\ln(1/p) + p$ because $\ln(1/(1-p)) \le p/(1-p)$; so for $p < \tfrac1{501}$, $H(p) < \tfrac1{501}(\ln 501 + 1) < \tfrac{8}{501}$, using $e^{7} > 501$ (Taylor partial sum). With $\ln 2 > \tfrac{6931}{10000}$ (rational partial sum), each member with $k \neq 0$ contributes $\ln 2 - h > \tfrac{6931}{10000} - \tfrac{8}{501} > \tfrac12$, so two members give more than $1 > \delta_{\mathrm{P}}$. (Computed in floating point, not used: the exact per-member value at $|k| = 1$ is $\ln 2 - s(2\pi) = 0.6796$.) The script verifies this arithmetic exactly.

For the closing statement: under uniform zero weight $\delta_{\mathrm{ev}} = 0 < \delta_{\mathrm{P}}$ at every stage with $\dim L \ge 2$; under weights bounded below by $x_{*}$, $\delta_{\mathrm{ev}}$ exceeds $1 > \delta_{\mathrm{P}}$ once $|W(t)| > 2/\tanh^{2}(x_{*}/2)$, and for (Q)'s weights once two members of nonzero weight are present. Nothing is claimed for small windows with nonzero weights or for mixed weights. The leading-order agreement at zero weight is (ii) together with the $\min(\Sth, C)$ of Fact \claimref{singularity.assembled-page-curve-up-typicality}. That replica-wormhole computations in a two-dimensional gravity model with end-of-the-world branes reproduce Page's typical-state spectrum, and so its deficit, is the motivation for reading the typical branch as the island side; it is cited from memory (Penington, Shenker, Stanford and Yang, 2019) and is not used in the proof.
