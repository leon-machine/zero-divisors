(i) \emph{The abstract tensor product.} By Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-page-average-entropy-subsystem}, $\langle S_{A}\rangle = \sum_{k=n+1}^{mn}k^{-1} - (m-1)/(2n)$. Since $1/x$ is decreasing, $\int_{n+1}^{mn+1}dx/x < \sum_{k=n+1}^{mn}k^{-1} < \int_{n}^{mn}dx/x = \ln m$. The right inequality gives $\delta_{\mathrm{P}} = \ln m - \langle S_{A}\rangle > (m-1)/(2n)$. The left gives $\delta_{\mathrm{P}} < \ln m - \ln\frac{mn + 1}{n + 1} + \frac{m - 1}{2n} = \ln\frac{m(n+1)}{mn + 1} + \frac{m - 1}{2n}$, and $\ln y < y - 1$ with $y = m(n+1)/(mn+1)$ gives $\ln y < (m - 1)/(mn + 1)$. Finally $(m-1)/(2n) < \tfrac12$ and $(m - 1)/(mn + 1) < 1/n \le \tfrac12$, so $\delta_{\mathrm{P}} < 1$. At $m = n$ the bounds give $\delta_{\mathrm{P}}(m, m) \in ((m-1)/(2m), (m-1)/(2m) + (m-1)/(m^{2} + 1))$, inside $(\tfrac25, \tfrac12 + \tfrac1m)$ for $m \ge 5$; for $m = 4$ the exact value, $\delta_{\mathrm{P}}(4, 4) = 2\ln 2 - \tfrac{664789}{720720} = 0.4639$, is computed. With $\dim L = m = 2^{w}$ and $\dim R = n = 2^{r}$, $r \ge w$ after the crossing, the lower bound $(2^{w} - 1)/2^{r+1}$ halves with each increment of $r$. The script encloses $\ln 2$ between rational partial sums of $\sum_{j}1/(j2^{j})$ with the tail bound $1/((N+1)2^{N})$, $N = 60$, and verifies both bounds, positivity, the bound $1$, and $\delta_{\mathrm{P}} > \tfrac25$ at the crossing, for $w = 1, \ldots, 5$ and $r = w, \ldots, w + 3$.

\emph{The parity sector.} The argument is that of clause (vii) of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.mode-split-of-the-clifford-algebra-and-its-klein-twist-to-the-tensor-product}, repeated here from Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-page-average-entropy-subsystem} because that theorem cites this one and is not a premise. The sector is $H_{+} \oplus H_{-}$, each summand the tensor product of a space of dimension $m/2$ with one of dimension $n/2$; put $k_{0} = mn/4$. A unit vector is $\sqrt{t}\,\psi_{+} + \sqrt{1-t}\,\psi_{-}$ with unit $\psi_{\pm} \in H_{\pm}$. The two factors of dimension $n/2$ are orthogonal, so the partial trace of a cross term vanishes, $\rho_{A} = t\rho_{+} \oplus (1-t)\rho_{-}$ and $S(\rho_{A}) = h(t) + tS(\rho_{+}) + (1-t)S(\rho_{-})$, $h(t) = -t\ln t - (1-t)\ln(1-t)$. Under the invariant measure, which is the law of a normalised Gaussian vector (background, not stored), $t$ follows the Beta law with parameters $(k_{0}, k_{0})$ and is independent of $\psi_{+}$ and $\psi_{-}$, which are independent and invariantly distributed on their summands. Hence $\langle S_{A}\rangle_{\mathrm{sector}} = \langle h(t)\rangle + \langle S\rangle_{m/2,\,n/2}$, the last term given by the Fact (zero for $m = 2$). For the Beta law $\langle t\ln t\rangle = -\tfrac12\sum_{k=k_{0}+1}^{2k_{0}}1/k$ (the derivative at $s = 0$ of $\langle t^{1+s}\rangle$, a ratio of Gamma functions), so $\langle h(t)\rangle = \sum_{k=mn/4+1}^{mn/2}1/k$; adding the Fact's $\sum_{k=n/2+1}^{mn/4}1/k - (m/2 - 1)/n$ gives the formula. For the bounds, convexity of $1/x$ gives $1/k < \int_{k-1/2}^{k+1/2}dx/x$ and $\int_{k-1}^{k}dx/x < \tfrac12(\tfrac{1}{k-1} + \tfrac1k)$; summing over $n/2 < k \le mn/2$, $\ln m - \tfrac{m-1}{mn} < \sum 1/k < \ln\tfrac{mn+1}{n+1}$, so $\delta_{\mathrm{sec}} > \tfrac{m-2}{2n} + \ln\bigl(1 + \tfrac{m-1}{mn+1}\bigr)$, which with $\ln(1+u) > 2u/(2+u)$ is the stated lower bound, and $\delta_{\mathrm{sec}} < \tfrac{m-2}{2n} + \tfrac{m-1}{mn} = \tfrac{m}{2n} - \tfrac{1}{mn}$, below $\tfrac12$ because $m \le n$. The difference $\Delta = \delta_{\mathrm{P}} - \delta_{\mathrm{sec}}$ is a difference of finite harmonic sums plus $\tfrac{1}{2n}$, so it is rational: with $a_{N} = \sum_{k=N+1}^{2N}1/k$, $\Delta = \tfrac{1}{2n} - (a_{mn/2} - a_{n/2})$, and $a_{N+1} - a_{N} = 1/((2N+1)(2N+2))$ lies strictly between $\tfrac12(\tfrac{1}{2N+1} - \tfrac{1}{2N+3})$ and $\tfrac12(\tfrac{1}{2N} - \tfrac{1}{2N+2})$; summing over $n/2 \le N < mn/2$ gives $\tfrac12(\tfrac{1}{n+1} - \tfrac{1}{mn+1}) < a_{mn/2} - a_{n/2} < \tfrac12(\tfrac1n - \tfrac{1}{mn})$, the stated bounds on $\Delta$. The sector lower bound exceeds $(m-1)/(2n)$ exactly when $n(2m - 4) > m + 1$, true for $m \ge 4$; for $m = 2$ the upper bound is $\tfrac1n - \tfrac{1}{2n} = \tfrac{1}{2n} = (m-1)/(2n)$, so the sector deficit is below it. At $m = n$ the upper bound is $\tfrac12 - m^{-2}$, and the lower one is $\tfrac14 + \tfrac{6}{37}$ at $m = 4$, $\tfrac38 + \tfrac{14}{137}$ at $m = 8$ and at least $\tfrac12 - \tfrac1m \ge \tfrac{7}{16}$ from $m = 16$, all above $\tfrac25$. The script evaluates the formula, the block decomposition, $\Delta$ and all the bounds for $w = 1, \ldots, 5$ and $r = w, \ldots, w + 3$, with the values $\tfrac12$, $\tfrac{7}{12}$ and $\tfrac{271}{280}$ at $(m, n) = (2, 2)$, $(2, 4)$, $(4, 4)$ and $\Delta(4, 4) = \tfrac{6553}{144144}$.

(ii) A zero-weight member has $x_{k} = 0$, occupation $\tfrac12$, and $h_{k} = s(0) = \ln 2$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.boundary-mode-thermal-entropy}); so every term of $\delta_{\mathrm{ev}}$ vanishes and, by Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}(4), $S_{R} = C$ on the falling branch, which with clause (3) is $S_{R} = \min(\Sth, C)$ at every stage. By (i) the Page-typical value is $C - \delta_{\mathrm{P}}$ with $\delta_{\mathrm{P}} > 0$ whenever $\dim L \ge 2$, and on a parity sector $C - \delta_{\mathrm{sec}}$ with $\delta_{\mathrm{sec}} > 0$.

(iii) A member of weight $x$ has occupation $p = 1/(e^{x} + 1)$ and $\ln 2 - h = \rel{p}{\tfrac12}$, the relative entropy of the binary distribution $(p, 1 - p)$ to the uniform one, since $\ln 2 - H(p) = \sum p_{i}\ln(p_{i}/\tfrac12)$. Pinsker's inequality for two-point distributions in nats (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-pinsker-inequality}) gives $\rel{p}{\tfrac12} \ge 2(p - \tfrac12)^{2}$, and $\tfrac12 - 1/(e^{x} + 1) = \tfrac12\tanh(x/2)$, so each member with $|x| \ge x_{*}$ contributes at least $\tfrac12\tanh^{2}(x_{*}/2)$ and $\delta_{\mathrm{ev}} \ge \tfrac12|W(t)|\tanh^{2}(x_{*}/2)$. For (Q)'s weights, $x = 2\pi|k| \ge 2\pi > \tfrac{157}{25}$ and $e^{157/25} > 500$ (a Taylor partial sum, all terms positive), so $p < \tfrac1{501}$ and Pinsker alone gives more than $2(\tfrac{499}{1002})^{2} > \tfrac{49}{100}$ per member, which needs three members to pass $1$. A sharper bound gives two: $H$ is increasing on $(0, \tfrac12]$, and $H(p) = p\ln(1/p) + (1 - p)\ln(1/(1 - p)) \le p\ln(1/p) + p$ because $\ln(1/(1-p)) \le p/(1-p)$; so for $p < \tfrac1{501}$, $H(p) < \tfrac1{501}(\ln 501 + 1) < \tfrac{8}{501}$, using $e^{7} > 501$ (Taylor partial sum). With $\ln 2 > \tfrac{6931}{10000}$ (rational partial sum), each member with $k \neq 0$ contributes $\ln 2 - h > \tfrac{6931}{10000} - \tfrac{8}{501} > \tfrac12$, so two members give more than $1 > \delta_{\mathrm{P}}$. (Computed in floating point, not used: the exact per-member value at $|k| = 1$ is $\ln 2 - s(2\pi) = 0.6796$.) The script verifies this arithmetic exactly.

For the closing statement: under uniform zero weight $\delta_{\mathrm{ev}} = 0 < \delta_{\mathrm{P}}$ at every stage with $\dim L \ge 2$; under weights bounded below by $x_{*}$, $\delta_{\mathrm{ev}}$ exceeds $1 > \delta_{\mathrm{P}}$ once $|W(t)| > 2/\tanh^{2}(x_{*}/2)$, and for (Q)'s weights once two members of nonzero weight are present. The same holds with $\delta_{\mathrm{sec}}$ in place of $\delta_{\mathrm{P}}$, because $0 < \delta_{\mathrm{sec}} < \tfrac12 < 1$ by (i). Nothing is claimed for small windows with nonzero weights or for mixed weights. The leading-order agreement at zero weight is (ii) together with the $\min(\Sth, C)$ of Fact \claimref{singularity.assembled-page-curve-up-typicality}. That replica-wormhole computations in a two-dimensional gravity model with end-of-the-world branes reproduce Page's typical-state spectrum, and so its deficit, is the motivation for reading the typical branch as the island side; it is cited from memory (Penington, Shenker, Stanford and Yang, 2019) and is not used in the proof.
