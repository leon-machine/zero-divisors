Each $E_k(x) = \tfrac12(x + \sigma_k x \sigma_k)$ is a conditional expectation onto the $\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma_k)$-fixed subalgebra. The automorphism is inner and local, which is what removes any graded-tensor subtlety from the argument: in a Jordan-Wigner representation the string factors of $v_k$ and $v_k^{*}$ cancel in the product $\sigma_k = v_k^{*}v_k - v_k v_k^{*}$, which is single-site, so the fermionic mode-$k$ grading coincides with the spin-picture one and $\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma_k)$ is an automorphism of the full mode algebra. The uniqueness argument of Theorem \claimref{singularity.descent-parity-average} runs verbatim on the full mode algebra: a conditional expectation $F$ onto that subalgebra satisfies $\sigma_k F(x)\sigma_k = F(\sigma_k x \sigma_k)$ by bimodularity ($\sigma_k$ lying in the range), so $F$ annihilates the $\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma_k)$-anti-fixed part and equals $E_k$ on the direct sum. The $\sigma_k$ commute, so the $E_k$ commute; their composite in any order is the group average over $\mathbb{Z}_2^{N}$, which is the pinching $\EN$ of Definition \claimref{obj.singularity.multi-mode-descent}; and the range of a composite of commuting conditional expectations is the intersection of the ranges: an element fixed by every $\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma_k)$ is diagonal in the joint number basis, and conversely. Strict containment for two or more modes is witnessed by elements of the kind $X_{R_1}X_{L_1}Z_{L_2}$, total-parity even and not joint-diagonal. The script verifies the commutation, the composite-equals-pinching identity, and the idempotence, on generic rational data.
