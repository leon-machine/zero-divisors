By Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-tower-commuting-copies-fock-module}, $T_{2} \cong \Cl(2,4) \otimes \mathrm{M}_{4}(\R)$, so $T_{2} \otimes \C \cong \mathrm{M}_{8}(\C) \otimes \mathrm{M}_{4}(\C) = \mathrm{M}_{32}(\C)$, whose irreducible module is unique up to isomorphism and is the Fock module of any five anticommuting mode pairs; the three pairs of $W$ with $\overline{W}$ and the two rung pairs $(v_{a}, w_{a})$ are such a set, and their Fock module is $\Lambda^{\bullet}(W \oplus R_{2}) = \Lambda^{\bullet}W \otimes \Lambda^{\bullet}R_{2}$ by the direct-sum formula for exterior algebras \cite[App.~B.2]{FultonHarris1991} (\claimref{standard-model.imported-cauchy-decomposition-exterior-power}). Realise the mode operators in the Jordan-Wigner form on the occupation basis, modes $1, 2, 3$ of $W$ before modes $4, 5$ of $R_{2}$: the creation and annihilation operators of mode $i$ carry the sign $(-1)^{\#\{\text{occupied modes below } i\}}$. For $i \le 3$ that sign involves pair modes only, so the operator is $x_{W} \otimes 1$; for $i \in \{4, 5\}$ it involves all three pair modes, giving $(-1)^{N_{W}} \otimes y_{R}$. This is (1) for vectors, and products of two vectors of $V_{\C}$ are products of operators of the form $x_{W} \otimes 1$, hence $X_{W} \otimes 1$; the even ground subalgebra is spanned by such products. (2): $(x_{W} \otimes 1)((-1)^{N_{W}} \otimes y_{R}) = -((-1)^{N_{W}} \otimes y_{R})(x_{W} \otimes 1)$ since a vector of $V_{\C}$ changes the pair number by one and so anticommutes with $(-1)^{N_{W}}$; two rung vectors give $((-1)^{N_{W}})^{2} \otimes Y_{R} = 1 \otimes Y_{R}$. (3) follows: operators of the forms $X_{W} \otimes 1$ and $1 \otimes Y_{R}$ commute, and $N_{R}$ and the $v_{a}w_{b}$ are of the second form. The untwisted bivectors used here are the twisted ones of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-tower-commuting-copies-fock-module} up to sign, since $x\omega_{1}\omega_{2}\,y\omega_{1}\omega_{2} = \pm xy\,(\omega_{1}\omega_{2})^{2} = \pm xy$, so (3) is also the statement that $A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ commute, read on the even part. The script builds the $32$-dimensional module exactly and checks: the Clifford relations of the five pairs; that every one of the fifteen bivectors of $V_{\C}$ has the block form $X_{W} \otimes 1_{4}$ (the same $8 \times 8$ block on each of the four rung states, no element between rung states); that every vector of $V_{\C}$ has that form and no rung vector does; that vectors of the two kinds anticommute; and that every bivector of $V_{\C}$ commutes with $N_{R}$, with the four $v_{a}w_{b}$, and with every bivector of the rung plane.
