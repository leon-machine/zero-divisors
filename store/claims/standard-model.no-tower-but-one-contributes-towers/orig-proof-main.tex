By Proposition \claimref{standard-model.connection-forced}, this tower's gauge fields are connections on this tower's hierarchy bundle $\mathcal{H}$, and a field is charged under them, that is, has a nonzero index under any of the three factors, exactly when it carries a component of $\mathcal{H}$: the index of a representation is $\tr(\rho(X)^{2})$ for a normalised generator $X$, and $\rho(X) = 0$ on any field on which the connection acts trivially. By Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-sectors-superselection-mirror-copy}, a field of another tower is a section of that tower's soldered bundle and acts within that tower's sector; it carries no component of this tower's $\mathcal{H}$, since $\mathcal{H}$ is built from this tower's soldering (the hierarchy fibre is the two-twistor ladder of this tower's conformal completion, Theorem \claimref{standard-model.gauge-algebra-minimal-massive-composite}), and a component of $\mathcal{H}$ would make the minimal coupling $\bar{\phi}\,\mathcal{A}\,\phi$ a non-gravitational interaction between the two towers, which Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-sectors-superselection-mirror-copy} excludes: the only inter-tower coupling is gravitational. Hence $\rho(X) = 0$ on $\phi$ for every generator $X$ of the derived algebra, all three indices vanish, and by Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-coefficient-sum-indices} the contribution of $\phi$ to each $b_{i}$ is zero, whatever its statistics and multiplicity and at whatever scale it is light. The script checks the arithmetic of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-coefficient-sum-indices} on a field with all indices zero, for both statistics. The sum over the seven other towers is a sum of zeros.
