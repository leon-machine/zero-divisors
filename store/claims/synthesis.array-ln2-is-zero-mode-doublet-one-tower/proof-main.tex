Throughout, (Q) is assumed as in Theorem \claimref{singularity.bosonisation-bridge-s-wave-sector}: weights $\omega_{k} = \kappa k$, sea level at $k = 0$, Gibbs state of $\sum_{k}\kappa k:N_{k}:$ at $\beta = 2\pi/\kappa$. A configuration with finitely many particles above the sea and holes in it has charge $Q = \#\{\text{occupied } k \ge 0\} - \#\{\text{empty } k < 0\}$ and energy $E = \sum_{\text{occupied } k \ge 0}k + \sum_{\text{empty } k < 0}|k|$, which is the eigenvalue of $\sum_{k}k:N_{k}:$; its Gibbs weight is $q^{E}$ with $q = e^{-\beta\kappa} = e^{-2\pi}$.

(i) Lowest energy at fixed charge: for $Q > 0$ fill $k = 0, \ldots, Q - 1$, energy $\sum_{k=0}^{Q-1}k = Q(Q-1)/2$; for $Q < 0$ empty $k = -1, \ldots, Q$, energy $\sum_{j=1}^{|Q|}j = |Q|(|Q| + 1)/2 = Q(Q-1)/2$. The generating function of all configurations is $(1 + z)\prod_{k \ge 1}(1 + zq^{k})(1 + z^{-1}q^{k})$, with $z$ counting charge; by the Jacobi triple product identity (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-jacobi-triple-product}, its second rewritten form; the script also verifies it on windows) it equals $\sum_{Q \in \Z}z^{Q}q^{Q(Q-1)/2}\prod_{m \ge 1}(1 - q^{m})^{-1}$. Reading off the coefficient of $z^{Q}q^{E}$ gives $p(E - Q(Q-1)/2)$. This is the charge decomposition of the boson-fermion correspondence that the bridge cites for its charge-zero isomorphism (Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-s-wave-bosonic-sector}): the factor $\prod(1 - q^{m})^{-1}$ is the partition function of one oscillator tower, one oscillator of energy $m$ per $m \ge 1$, created by the $a_{-m}$. The script checks the count exactly on the window $k = -6, \ldots, 6$ for every $E \le 6$, where no configuration of energy $E \le 6$ is cut off by the window, against $p$ computed from $\prod(1 - q^{m})^{-1}$. Since the Gibbs weight $q^{E} = q^{Q(Q-1)/2}\cdot q^{E - Q(Q-1)/2}$ factorises over the decomposition, the Gibbs state is the product of $P(Q) \propto q^{Q(Q-1)/2}$ and the Gibbs state of the tower, and entropy is additive over a product: $S_{\mathrm{array}} = S_{\mathrm{charge}} + S_{\mathrm{osc}}$, where each oscillator of energy $m\kappa$ contributes the Bose entropy $s_{\mathrm{B}}(2\pi m)$.

(ii) $Q(Q-1)/2 = 0$ exactly for $Q \in \{0, 1\}$ and is at least $1$ otherwise, so the zero-energy configurations are the sea and the sea with $k = 0$ filled; the script lists them. $P(0) = P(1)$ is the largest charge probability and is below $\tfrac12$, so $S_{\mathrm{charge}} \ge -\ln P(0) > \ln 2$. In Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy}(vi) the array's $\ln 2$ is the entropy of the zero-weight mode $k = 0$, whose two occupations are exactly these two charge sectors. With weights $\kappa(k + \tfrac12)$ the lowest energy at charge $Q$ is $\sum_{k=0}^{Q-1}(k + \tfrac12) = Q^{2}/2$ for $Q > 0$ and likewise for $Q < 0$, the generating function becomes $\sum_{Q}z^{Q}q^{Q^{2}/2}\prod_{m}(1 - q^{m})^{-1}$ (the first rewritten form of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-jacobi-triple-product}), the tower factor is unchanged, and $Q^{2}/2 = 0$ only at $Q = 0$; the script checks these counts on the window $k = -6, \ldots, 5$ in units of $\kappa/2$.

(iii) Each $\psi_{k}^{\dagger}\psi_{l}$ changes $N_{\mathrm{tot}}$ by zero, so $[N_{\mathrm{tot}}, a_{m}] = 0$ and every current preserves $Q$; $a_{0}$, normal ordered at the sea, is $Q$ itself, diagonal on the doublet with eigenvalues $0$ and $1$; on the finite window this is the operator identity checked on Jordan-Wigner matrices for five sites, and it is consistent with the exact edge identity of Proposition \claimref{hawking.current-commutator-exactly-edge-split}, all of whose terms are number operators. The doublet states have charges $0$ and $1$, so every current, and every element of the algebra the currents generate, has zero matrix element between them, and for $m \neq 0$ the current acts on the tower factor only; the script checks this for $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}$ and their adjoints, checks that the charge separates the two states without connecting them, and shows that $\psi_{0}^{\dagger}$ connects them. Hence any state or flux built from the currents (the Bose-Einstein occupancy of the bridge's clause (3), and the current boson that Joint J3 of Proposition \claimref{hawking.helicity-lift-joints-itemised} lifts to the field) sees only the tower factor and its entropy $S_{\mathrm{osc}}$.

(iv) At charge $0$ the level counts are $p(E)$, the dimension of the tower's energy-$E$ subspace; $p(1) = 1$ means one oscillator at weight $\kappa$, and by (i) one at each weight $m\kappa$. A massless field's partial wave at fixed $(\ell, m)$ with both helicities has two independent oscillators at each frequency (standard free-field Fock structure, imported, not proved in the series), so its level counts are the coefficients of $\prod(1 - q^{m})^{-2}$, namely $1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 36, 65$, which exceed $p(E)$ at every $E \ge 1$, already at $E = 1$. A single array's current sector therefore cannot carry both helicities of one partial wave; under J3 it carries one channel. This is consistent with Theorem \claimref{synthesis.capacity-per-mode-independent-of-helicity}, which fixes the capacity per ladder pair without J3; here the capacity per array is located in the charge sector, which J3 does not touch.
