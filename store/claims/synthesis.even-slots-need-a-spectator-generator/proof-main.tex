(i) This covariance lemma is also clause (i) of the anomaly route filed alongside (synthesis.slot-parity-decides-hypothesis-s); it is reproved here because (ii) uses it. (ii) A blade $e_{S}$ commutes with a generator not in $S$ iff $|S|$ is even, and with one in $S$ iff $|S|$ is odd. So a blade commutes with all six pair generators $e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3}, f_{1}, f_{2}, f_{3}$ iff either $S$ avoids them all and $|S|$ is even, or $S$ contains them all and $|S|$ is odd: $8 + 8 = 16$ blades, spanning $P'$; the even ones are the $8$ even blades in the slot generators $e_{4}, e_{5}, f_{4}, f_{5}$, the even part of $\Cl(2,2)$, closed under products.
