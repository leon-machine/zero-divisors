## formal
Under Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-frozen-point}, write $\Cl(I^{\perp})$ for the Clifford bundle of the rank-five bundle $I^{\perp}$, and let $e_{5} = V/|V|$ be the unit Stelle-West direction, so that the Lorentz frame $e_{1}, \ldots, e_{4}$ of $\{I,V\}^{\perp}$ and $e_{5}$ together generate $\Cl(I^{\perp})$ fibrewise. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] for $\Lambda > 0$, $\Cl(I^{\perp}) \cong \Cl(1,4)$, $e_{5}^{2} = -1$, and the translation bivectors $e_{\mu}e_{5}$ of the MacDowell-Mansouri connection generate this world's shadow $\Cl(1,3)$;
\item[(ii)] for $\Lambda < 0$, $\Cl(I^{\perp}) \cong \Cl(2,3)$, $e_{5}^{2} = +1$, and the translation bivectors generate a copy of the mirror's abstract algebra $\Cl(3,1)$, while this world's shadow is the subalgebra generated by the Lorentz frame (what this copy is and is not: Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.belongs-tower} and Corollary \claimref{odd-extensions.two-readings-which-which}).
\end{enumerate}
In either case the fibrewise algebra $\Cl(I^{\perp})$ is one of the two extensions of Corollary \claimref{odd-extensions.four-extensions-oriented-pair}, and the Poincar\'e contraction $\ell \to \infty$ leaves the Clifford type of the translation algebra unchanged.

## technical
Under the imported frozen-point geometry, let $e_{5} = V/|V|$ be the unit Stelle-West direction, which together with the Lorentz frame $e_{1},\ldots,e_{4}$ of $\{I,V\}^{\perp}$ generates $\Cl(I^{\perp})$ fibrewise. Since $Q(V) = -3/\Lambda$, $e_{5}^{2} = -1$ for $\Lambda > 0$ and $e_{5}^{2} = +1$ for $\Lambda < 0$, so $\Cl(I^{\perp})$ is $\Cl(1,4)$ or $\Cl(2,3)$, the space or time extension of $\Cl(1,3)$.

The translation part $e^{\mu}\wedge V/\ell$ of the MacDowell-Mansouri connection has Clifford image the bivectors $e_{\mu}e_{5}$, up to the scalar $1/\ell$. For $\Lambda > 0$ they generate this world's shadow $\Cl(1,3)$; for $\Lambda < 0$ they generate a copy of the mirror's abstract algebra $\Cl(3,1)$, while this world's shadow is generated by the Lorentz frame. The Poincar\'e contraction $\ell \to \infty$ only rescales the generators, so it leaves the Clifford type of the translation algebra unchanged.

## lay
Gravity's translation generators, the ones that move things through spacetime, can be placed inside an algebra built from the frozen point. Whether the cosmological constant is positive or negative decides which algebra that is. For a positive value, as observed, the translations generate an algebra of the same kind as our own spacetime's; for a negative value they would generate one of the same kind as the mirror world's. This is a statement about the structure of the algebra only.

## one-liner
The sign of $\Lambda$ decides whether the frozen point's translation bivectors $e_{\mu}e_{5}$ generate this world's $\Cl(1,3)$ inside $\Cl(1,4)$ or, for $\Lambda < 0$, a copy of the mirror's $\Cl(3,1)$ inside $\Cl(2,3)$.
