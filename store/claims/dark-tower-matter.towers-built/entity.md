\emph{Note (the third and fourth choices).} ``Strong coupling with an unprotected light mass'' is the requirement read at the particle model, the nucleon's mass and metre spacing. On E and R3 alone the cell's mass cancels from the survival and cluster conditions, and the requirement is a floor on the three-dimensional coupling, $e^{2}$ above $6.3$ to $8.7 \times 10^{7}$ GeV $\times \sqrt{d/d_{b}}$, with the cell's mass anywhere well below it (Proposition \claimref{synthesis.sheet-relic-bounds-under-e-fix-the-coupling-not-the-cells-mass}). Likewise ``collisionless five-dimensional relics'' is, on E and R3 alone, the one inequality $E_{s}m_{c}^{2}(d/d_{b}) \ge 9.5 \times 10^{15}\ \mathrm{GeV}^{3}$ in those towers' coupling scale, charged mass and depth, which at a GeV is a coupling scale above $10^{16}$ GeV and at a coupling scale of $10^{11}$ GeV is a charged mass above about $300$ GeV (Proposition \claimref{synthesis.five-dimensional-plasma-exposure-is-one-inequality-in-coupling-mass-and-depth}). Both remain choices the series does not make; the conclusion of this observation rests on the mirror's rows, which do not involve the quanta, and is unchanged.

## technical
Observation level. The radiation bound $\Delta\Neff < 0.3$ is met on every branch except a hot mirror ($\xi = 1$, giving $7.45$) and a five-dimensional sector decoupled at a GeV (about $670$). Cluster mergers pass the simulation runs' mass-to-light test only if the mirror is the only colliding component, and even then the subcluster's own mass falls by more than the $23$ percent bound; that needs both three-dimensional towers in phase (T) at strong coupling with an unprotected light mass and both five-dimensional towers collisionless; charged relics with zero modes in both five-dimensional towers give a colliding fraction of $50.2$ percent, and in one $34.4$ percent, above the $31.6$ percent run that already loses about half the subcluster's central mass, and the extrapolation from fluids to a plasma is unproved. The mirror bounds are marginal at best and need an exception to P2. Neither E nor the particle model has a mechanism in the odd towers, whose quanta have bare masses; equal masses hold only for the mirror.

No configuration of the five towers as built is compatible with every test at face value. The least exposed one needs four choices the series does not make (a P2 exception, boosted mirror feedback, strong coupling with an unprotected light mass, and collisionless five-dimensional relics), and even then it stays $1.24$ above Roux and Cline's limit. The P2 exception is one at P2's version-1 reach; indifference as scoped leaves the mirror's temperature free, and the cold mirror then needs a non-equilibrium descent. The third choice is the particle model's reading: on E and R3 alone it is a floor on the three-dimensional coupling, about $10^{8}$ GeV at metre spacing, and not a light mass.
