By (L) and (U) the global state is pure on $H_{L(t)} \otimes H_{R(t)}$ at every stage, so $S_R = S_L$ (equal marginal spectra of a pure state), and by (C) $\dim H_{L(t)} = 2^{|W(t)|}$, so $S_L \leq C(t)$ is the dimension bound.

For (1), consider the joint number basis of all the qubits ever injected, labelled $R_k, L_k$. By (I) the state at injection of pair $k$ is the previous state tensored with $c_k|0_{R_k}0_{L_k}\rangle + s_k|1_{R_k}1_{L_k}\rangle$, so its support lies in the configurations with $R_k = L_k$ for every injected $k$ and its amplitude on such a configuration is $\pm\prod_k c^{(k)}_{b_k}$, with $c^{(k)}_0 = c_k$, $c^{(k)}_1 = s_k$. By (L$_E$) every subsequent operation is either a diagonal unitary on the window, which multiplies each configuration by a phase and preserves both the support and every squared amplitude, or an eviction, which relabels a qubit from $L$ to $R$ and touches no amplitude; the radiation quanta are free. So at every stage the support constraint $R_k = L_k$ holds and the squared amplitude of each configuration is $\prod_k (c^{(k)}_{b_k})^{2}$. Now trace out the radiation. Every $R_k$ is in the radiation (the emitted quanta never return), so two configurations that agree on the radiation agree on every $L_k$ with $k \in W(t)$ by the support constraint: $\rho_L$ has no off-diagonal entries. Its diagonal entry at the window configuration $(b_k)_{k \in W(t)}$ is the total probability of the global configurations extending it, which is $\prod_{k \in W(t)}(c^{(k)}_{b_k})^{2}$ times the sum over the other pairs' configurations, and that sum is $1$. This is the product state displayed, and its entropy is $\sum_{k \in W(t)} H(c_k^{2}) = \sum_{k \in W(t)} h_k$.

(2) $W(t)$ is a subset of the injected pairs and every $h_k \geq 0$, so $\sum_{W(t)} h_k \leq \sum_{k \leq t} h_k = \Sth(t)$; and $\sum_{W(t)} h_k \leq |W(t)|\ln 2 = C(t)$ since each $h_k \leq \ln 2$. At complete evaporation $W = \varnothing$ and the sum is empty. (3) is (1) with $W(t)$ the full set of injected pairs. (4) is (1) rewritten as $|W|\ln 2 - \sum_{W}(\ln 2 - h_k)$, with equality in $S_R \leq C$ exactly when every term of the shortfall vanishes; in the idealisation the shortfall is identically zero, so $S_R = C$ whenever an eviction has occurred and $S_R = \Sth$ before, which is $\min(\Sth, C)$ in both regimes. The script verifies (1) on the thermal model at every stage, from the exact spectrum of $\rho_L$, as an identity of formal logarithms; the strict inequality of (4) at every stage with a nonempty window, by rigorous rational bounds; and (4)'s exact saturation on an idealised run simulated on exact density matrices along a decreasing capacity schedule.
