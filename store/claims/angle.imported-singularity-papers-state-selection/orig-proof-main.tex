For the horizon pair, a boost-invariant state has the form $\cos\theta\,\ket{00} + \sin\theta\,\ket{11}$; its one-sided marginal is $\operatorname{diag}(\cos^{2}\theta, \sin^{2}\theta)$, the Gibbs state for the number operator at $\tan^{2}\theta = e^{-\beta\omega}$; the sample point $e^{-\omega/T} = \tfrac{9}{16}$ has $(\cos\theta, \sin\theta) = (\tfrac45, \tfrac35)$. Every automorphism of the mode factor intertwining the boost flow acts on $\beta$ through $\{\pm 1\}$ only. The surface gravity is $\kappa = 1/4M$, the Euclidean section is smooth at the bifurcation sphere exactly for the period $2\pi/\kappa$, and (R) imposes KMS at that period mode by mode.
