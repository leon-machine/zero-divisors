$C$, the coupling of \claimref{descent-lift.lift-costs-one-rung-per-mode}(iv), acts on the two branches as $1$ and as $\G_{0}$, so the induced map is $\tfrac12(\rho + \G_{0}\rho\G_{0})$; the joint output is a mixture of two orthogonal pure states with equal weights. The one-rung version is the same computation on the eight-dimensional space: the record is a mixture of the two parities, which the univalence rule allows, and its coupling is again a polynomial in the parity sections. The script forms both joint density matrices and computes their restrictions, their purities and, for the one-rung case, the commutation with the total parity.
