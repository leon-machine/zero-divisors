\emph{Setting.} The window and its boost dynamics are those of Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.boundary-mode-algebra-windows-boost-dynamics}. On the window, $\rho_{I} = e^{-\beta H_{F}}/Z_{F}$ (Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state}), and Theorem \claimref{synthesis.boundary-mode-first-law-of-entanglement}(ii) gives, for every state $\rho$ of $F$,
\[
S(\rho) - \beta\Tr(\rho H_{F}) = \ln Z_{F} - S(\rho\,\|\,\rho_{I}). \tag{$*$}
\]
The same identity holds for any finite system and any Gibbs state $\sigma = e^{-\beta K}/Z$: $S(\rho\,\|\,\sigma) = \Tr\rho\ln\rho - \Tr\rho\ln\sigma = -S(\rho) + \beta\Tr(\rho K) + \ln Z$.

\emph{(1)} The CAR give $[a_{i}^{*}a_{j}, a_{k}^{*}a_{l}] = \delta_{jk}a_{i}^{*}a_{l} - \delta_{il}a_{k}^{*}a_{j}$, so with $d\Gamma(A) = \sum_{ij}A_{ij}a_{i}^{*}a_{j}$,
\[
[d\Gamma(A), d\Gamma(B)] = \sum_{i,l}(AB)_{il}a_{i}^{*}a_{l} - \sum_{k,j}(BA)_{kj}a_{k}^{*}a_{j} = d\Gamma([A, B]).
\]
The map $d\Gamma$ is injective on one-particle operators, since $d\Gamma(A)a_{j}^{*}|0\rangle = \sum_{i}A_{ij}a_{i}^{*}|0\rangle$. With $H_{F} \otimes 1 + 1 \otimes d\Gamma(k) = d\Gamma(w \oplus k)$ on the joint Fock space (Theorem \claimref{field-theory.second-quantisation}(3) for the span$\{1, I_{4}\}$ part), $[d\Gamma(v), d\Gamma(w \oplus k)] = d\Gamma([v, w \oplus k])$ vanishes if and only if $[v, w \oplus k] = 0$, and then every $U = \exp(-I_{4}t\,d\Gamma(v))$ satisfies (J). In a basis of boost eigenmodes with weights $\omega_{i}$, $[v, \mathrm{diag}(\omega)]_{ij} = v_{ij}(\omega_{j} - \omega_{i})$, so the condition is that $v$ joins only modes of equal weight.

\emph{(2)} Apply $(*)$ to $\rho_{F}'$ and to $\rho_{I}$ (for which the relative entropy vanishes) and subtract: $\Delta S_{F} = \beta\,\Delta\langle H_{F}\rangle - S(\rho_{F}'\,\|\,\rho_{I})$, where $\Delta\langle H_{F}\rangle = \Tr(\rho_{F}'H_{F}) - \Tr(\rho_{I}H_{F})$. Under (J) the expectation of $H_{F}\otimes 1 + 1\otimes d\Gamma(k)$ is the same in $\rho'$ and in $\rho_{I}\otimes\rho_{m}$, and the expectation of $H_{F}\otimes 1$ (of $1\otimes d\Gamma(k)$) depends only on the window (matter) marginal, so $\Delta\langle H_{F}\rangle = \delta Q$. The relative entropy is nonnegative and vanishes only at $\rho_{F}' = \rho_{I}$ (Klein's inequality; Theorem \claimref{synthesis.boundary-mode-first-law-of-entanglement}(ii)), and along a differentiable family $\rho_{t}$ with $\rho_{0} = \rho_{I}$ its first derivative at $t = 0$ is $\Tr(\dot\rho\ln\rho_{I}) - \Tr(\dot\rho\ln\rho_{I}) + \Tr\dot\rho = 0$, so it is of second order. Hence $\Delta S_{F} = \beta\,\delta Q$ at first order and $\Delta S_{F} \le \beta\,\delta Q$, with $\beta = 1/T_{\chi}$, $T_{\chi} = \kappa/2\pi$. This is the standard first-law identity of a Gibbs state combined with (J).

\emph{(3)} The joint entropy is invariant under $U$ and additive on the product $\rho_{I}\otimes\rho_{m}$; after the process $S(\rho') = S(\rho_{F}') + S(\rho_{m}') - I(F{:}m)_{\rho'}$. So $\Delta S_{F} = -\Delta S_{m} + I(F{:}m)_{\rho'}$. The identity below $(*)$ for $\sigma_{m} = e^{-\beta d\Gamma(k)}/Z_{m}$ gives $\Delta S_{m} = \beta\big(\Tr(\rho_{m}'d\Gamma(k)) - \Tr(\rho_{m}d\Gamma(k))\big) - \big(S(\rho_{m}'\,\|\,\sigma_{m}) - S(\rho_{m}\,\|\,\sigma_{m})\big) = -\beta\,\delta Q - \Delta D_{m}$. Hence $\Delta S_{F} = \beta\,\delta Q + I(F{:}m)_{\rho'} + \Delta D_{m}$, with no use of (J). This is the standard entropy-production identity of quantum thermodynamics for a system coupled unitarily to a thermal state (Esposito, Lindenberg and Van den Broeck 2010; Reeb and Wolf 2014; cited from memory), with the window and the matter in the roles stated. The modular Hamiltonian of $\sigma_{m}$ is $-\ln\sigma_{m} = \beta\,d\Gamma(k) + \ln Z_{m}$: the matter's boost charge. Under (J), comparing with (2): $-S(\rho_{F}'\,\|\,\rho_{I}) = I(F{:}m)_{\rho'} + \Delta D_{m}$, which is the stated balance. (It is also the invariance of $S(\rho\,\|\,\rho_{I}\otimes\sigma_{m})$ under a $U$ that commutes with $\rho_{I}\otimes\sigma_{m}$, split by the chain rule $S(\rho'\,\|\,\rho_{I}\otimes\sigma_{m}) = S(\rho_{F}'\,\|\,\rho_{I}) + S(\rho_{m}'\,\|\,\sigma_{m}) + I(F{:}m)_{\rho'}$.)

\emph{(4)} Take one window mode $b$ of weight $\omega_{F}$ at its Gibbs occupation $n_{I} = (1 + r_{F})^{-1}$, $r_{F} = e^{\beta\omega_{F}}$, and one matter mode $a$ of weight $\omega_{m}$ at occupation $p \neq n_{I}$, in the diagonal product state, and the number-conserving hopping $U$ that rotates $(a, b)$ by $\begin{pmatrix} c & -s\\ s & c\end{pmatrix}$ on the one-particle space (and is the identity on the empty and doubly occupied states, the determinant being $1$). On the occupation basis the one-particle block $\mathrm{diag}(p(1 - n_{I}), (1 - p)n_{I})$ is rotated and the doubly occupied weight $pn_{I}$ is unchanged, so $n_{b}' = s^{2}p + c^{2}n_{I} = n_{I} + \delta n$ and $n_{a}' = p - \delta n$ with $\delta n = s^{2}(p - n_{I})$. The window's marginal is diagonal, so $\Delta S_{F} = h(n_{I} + \delta n) - h(n_{I})$ with $h(q) = -q\ln q - (1 - q)\ln(1 - q)$, whose first-order term is $h'(n_{I})\,\delta n = \ln\frac{1 - n_{I}}{n_{I}}\,\delta n = \beta\omega_{F}\,\delta n$, while $\beta\,\delta Q = \beta\omega_{m}(p - n_{a}') = \beta\omega_{m}\,\delta n$. For $\omega_{m} \neq \omega_{F}$ these differ at first order in $s^{2}$, while the window's state before the process and its first law $(*)$ are as in (2). The coupling $v$ joins modes of different weight, so by (1) it violates (J).

\emph{(5)} The windows of Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.boundary-mode-algebra-windows-boost-dynamics} have positive weights; zero weight is the limit at which the registered residue sits when each counted unit is one mode, admitted here as such (under (Q) version 3 the residue is also realised by two arrays at nonzero weight, outside this clause). If $\omega_{j} = 0$ on $F$ then $H_{F} = 0$ and (J) says $U$ commutes with $1\otimes d\Gamma(k)$, so $\delta Q = 0$; then (2) gives $\Delta S_{F} = -S(\rho_{F}'\,\|\,\rho_{I}) \le 0$, with $\rho_{I}$ the tracial state of entropy $\ln 2$ per mode (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.boundary-mode-thermal-entropy} at $\omega = 0$), the maximum, which is the zero-weight case of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy} in which the residue takes its registered value. Without (J), (4) with $\omega_{F} = 0$ shows the window's entropy has zero first-order change while the matter loses $\omega_{m}\,\delta n$. Either way a fixed zero-weight window cannot satisfy $\Delta S_{F} = \beta\,\delta Q$ with $\delta Q \ne 0$ at first order.

\emph{(6)} For $\psi$ smooth with support in the patch, integration by parts gives $-\int v\bar\psi'\psi\,dv = \int\bar\psi\,(v\psi)'\,dv = \int\bar\psi\psi\,dv + \int v\bar\psi\psi'\,dv$, so $\int v(\bar\psi\psi' - \bar\psi'\psi)\,dv = 2\ip{\psi}{(v\partial_{v} + \tfrac12)\psi}$, and multiplying by $\tfrac{I_{4}}{2}\kappa$ gives the claim. The operator $I_{4}(v\partial_{v} + \tfrac12)$ is symmetric, since $(v\partial_{v})^{*} = -v\partial_{v} - 1$. With the unit $I_{4}$ (Theorem \claimref{field-theory.second-quantisation}(4)), its second quantisation is the boost charge of the chiral field on the generator, and its expectation is the boost flux $\int T_{ab}\chi^{a}k^{b}\,d\lambda$ of (H1) per generator. For a general field the identification of $d\Gamma(k)$ with $\int T_{ab}\chi^{a}d\Sigma^{b}$ is the Noether charge of the Killing field $\chi$, cited from memory and not proved here.

\emph{(7)} On $u^{a}$, $I_{4}(u\partial_{u} + \tfrac12)u^{a} = I_{4}(a + \tfrac12)u^{a}$, which for $a = -\tfrac12 + I_{4}w$ is $-w\,u^{a}$; so $h$ is the generator of the dilation $\psi(u) \mapsto e^{\kappa t/2}\psi(e^{\kappa t}u)$ with the Mellin eigenvalues $-\kappa w$, and the continuum I-state of Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state-continuum} for this $h$ has symbol $(1 + e^{\beta h})^{-1}$. For modes $f, g$ vanishing at $u = 0$ (and at the far end of the patch) the integration by parts of (6), applied to $\bar f g$, gives $\int\kappa u\,\tfrac{I_{4}}{2}(\bar f g' - \bar f' g)\,du = \ip{f}{hg}$. Expanding $\psi(u) = \sum_{i}e_{i}(u)a_{i}$ in an orthonormal basis of such modes, $\int\chi^{u}T_{uu}\,du = \sum_{ij}\ip{e_{i}}{he_{j}}a_{i}^{*}a_{j} = d\Gamma(h)$ as quadratic forms on the finite-particle domain (with the unit $I_{4}$, the span$\{1, I_{4}\}$ part of $\Psi^{*}h\Psi$, Theorem \claimref{field-theory.second-quantisation}(3)). In the I-state the expectation of $d\Gamma(h)$ is not finite, and $\delta Q$ is the difference of expectations, finite for perturbations of finite boost energy. If the window's modes and the crossing matter are one field, the operator in (J) is $d\Gamma(h)$ alone and (J) is the statement that a unitary commuting with $d\Gamma(h)$ conserves it, while $\delta Q$, the boost energy deposited, is $\Delta\langle d\Gamma(h)\rangle$ by definition; so no separate conservation hypothesis remains. This proves (J) as an identity of quadratic forms. The first law on this carrier is not the window theorem: $h$ has purely continuous spectrum and no finite-dimensional invariant subspace, so the restriction of the I-state to a finite subspace with projection $P$ has symbol $P(1 + e^{\beta h})^{-1}P$, which is not the Gibbs symbol of $PhP$, and Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state-continuum}(1) gives the window theorem only for pure point spectrum; replacing $h$ by a point-spectrum discretisation $h_{d}$ makes (2) available but $d\Gamma(h_{d})$ is then no longer $\int\chi^{u}T_{uu}\,du$, so (J) stops being an identity exactly where (2) applies. The continuum statement is supplied by Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-null-plane-modular-hamiltonian-first-law}: Araki's relative-entropy identity $S(\rho\,\|\,\omega) = \Delta\langle\beta\,d\Gamma(h)\rangle - \Delta S$ for the half-generator algebra, read for entropy differences on a type III algebra, with the null-plane modular Hamiltonian $\beta\,d\Gamma(h) = 2\pi\int u\,T_{uu}\,du$ (at $\kappa$ normalised as here, since $\beta\kappa = 2\pi$; the Fact's $2\pi K(0)$ on one generator). The restriction of the I-state to the half-generator is KMS at $\beta$ for $h$ (Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state-continuum}), so its modular Hamiltonian is $\beta\,d\Gamma(h)$, and clause (iii) of the Fact gives $\Delta S_{F} = \beta\,\Delta\langle d\Gamma(h)\rangle - S(\rho_{F}'\,\|\,\rho_{I}) = \beta\,\delta Q - S(\rho_{F}'\,\|\,\rho_{I})$ with $\delta Q = \Delta\langle d\Gamma(h)\rangle$ as above: this is (2) on the continuum, for entropy differences, given the imported Fact. If the modes at the edge were shared by its two sheets, whose future boosts are $\chi$ and $-\chi$, the KMS conditions at $\beta$ for $h$ and for $-h$ hold together only for $h = 0$ on each window, and (5) applies.

\emph{Consequence.} In the patch of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.einstein-equations-from-c-loc-and-clausius}, $\delta Q = \tfrac{\kappa}{2}T(k,k)\lambda_{0}^{2} + O(\lambda_{0}^{3})$ per unit area; if $\rho_{F}' - \rho_{I} = O(\delta Q)$ the defect of (2) is $O(\lambda_{0}^{4})$, so (2) is the balance (H1) requires at order $\lambda_{0}^{2}$, once the window's entropy change is read as $\eta\,\delta A$.
