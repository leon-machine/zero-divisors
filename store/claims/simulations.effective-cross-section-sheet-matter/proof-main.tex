The in-sheet collision rate is $n_{2}\lambda v$, and $n_{2}\lambda v = (n_{2}/s)(\lambda s)v = n_{3}\sigma_{\mathrm{eff}}v$. By R3 (\claimref{dark-matter.r3-one-cell-per-unshared-direction}) the sheets are one unit apart in the mean, comoving \cite[Prem.~7.4]{ButlerT14}, and under R1 (\claimref{dark-matter.r1-equal-mass-per-quantum}) and R2 (\claimref{dark-matter.r2-one-quantum-per-unit-ball}) that unit is the common cell radius $d_{b}$, this world's baryon spacing. A region compressed by $\Delta$ in volume has its comoving lengths reduced by $\Delta$ along one direction, by $\Delta^{1/3}$ along each for isotropic compression, or not at all along the normal if the compression is in the plane; the physical spacing carries the further factor $1/(1+z)$.
