The in-sheet collision rate is $n_{2}\lambda v$, and $n_{2}\lambda v = (n_{2}/s)(\lambda s)v = n_{3}\sigma_{\mathrm{eff}}v$. By R3 the sheets are $d_{b}$ apart in the mean, comoving \cite[Prem.~7.4]{ButlerT14}. A region compressed by $\Delta$ in volume has its comoving lengths reduced by $\Delta$ along one direction, by $\Delta^{1/3}$ along each for isotropic compression, or not at all along the normal if the compression is in the plane; the physical spacing carries the further factor $1/(1+z)$.
