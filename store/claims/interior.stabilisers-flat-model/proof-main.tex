Apply \eqref{eq:bivectoraction}, the action of bivectors on vectors (\claimref{std-algebra.bivector-lie-algebra}), to $\nInf$ term by term. $(\nInf \wedge \nO)(\nInf) = \ip{\nO}{\nInf}\nInf - Q(\nInf)\nO = \nInf \neq 0$: the grading element scales $\nInf$ and so stabilises the ray but not the vector. For $C \in \Lambda^2 W$: both pairings of $\nInf$ against $W$ vanish, so $C(\nInf) = 0$. For $w \in W$: $(\nInf \wedge w)(\nInf) = \ip{w}{\nInf}\nInf - Q(\nInf)w = 0$, while $(\nO \wedge w)(\nInf) = \ip{w}{\nInf}\nO - \ip{\nO}{\nInf}w = -w \neq 0$. Summing dimensions: $6 + 4 = 10$ for the vector, $11$ for the ray; the Levi decomposition is read off the direct sum. For (3): on a point tractor, $(\nInf \wedge w)\bigl(X(x)\bigr) = \ip{w}{X(x)}\,\nInf - \ip{\nInf}{X(x)}\,w$; here $\ip{\nInf}{X(x)} = 1$ (the pairing of $(0,0,1)$ with $(1, x, -\tfrac12 Q(x))$ through the hyperbolic slot, triples listing the $\nO$, $W$ and $\nInf$ components) and $\ip{w}{X(x)} = \eta(w, x)$, so with $N := \nInf \wedge w$ acting on the point tractor: $N(X(x)) = \eta(w, x)\,\nInf - w$, then $N^2(X(x)) = -Q(w)\,\nInf$ (using $N(\nInf) = 0$ and $N(w) = Q(w)\,\nInf$) and $N^3(X(x)) = 0$, so the exponential terminates exactly:
\[
e^{tN} X(x) \;=\; \bigl(1,\; x - tw,\; -\tfrac12Q(x) + t\,\eta(w,x) - \tfrac{t^2}{2}Q(w)\bigr) \;=\; X(x - tw),
\]
the last slot being the completed square $-\tfrac12\eta(x - tw,\, x - tw)$: the flow is exactly the translation flow of the point space, with no projective factor, and the verification script confirms the identity at rational samples. Nilpotency: the image of $(\nInf \wedge w)$ lies in $\operatorname{span}(\nInf, w)$, the image of that span lies in $\R \nInf$ (since $(\nInf\wedge w)(w) = Q(w)\nInf$ and $(\nInf\wedge w)(\nInf) = 0$), and $\nInf$ is annihilated. The identification with the transvections of \cite{ButlerT1} is the statement that both are the maps $1 + \nInf \wedge w$ fixing the null vector $\nInf$ and shearing its orthogonal complement into it.
