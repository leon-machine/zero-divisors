The sums over the Weyl fermions of one derived generation: $\sum T_{3}^{2} = 3 \cdot 2 \cdot \tfrac14 + 2 \cdot \tfrac14 = \tfrac32 + \tfrac12 = 2$ (quark doublet with colour multiplicity, lepton doublet; the singlets have $T_{3} = 0$); $\sum Y^{2} = 6 \cdot \tfrac{1}{36} + 2 \cdot \tfrac14 + 3 \cdot \tfrac49 + 3 \cdot \tfrac19 + 1 + 0 = \tfrac16 + \tfrac12 + \tfrac43 + \tfrac13 + 1 = \tfrac{10}{3}$ (doublets, then $u^{c}, d^{c}, e^{c}, \nu^{c}$); and the cross term $2\sum T_{3}Y$ vanishes because each doublet's $T_{3}$ values sum to zero at fixed $Y$. Hence $\sum Q^{2} = 2 + \tfrac{10}{3} = \tfrac{16}{3}$ and the ratio is $\tfrac38$. Under Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-one-loop-running-trace-ratio}(b) the ratio over any representation of the unifying group is $\sin^{2}\theta_{W}$ at the unification scale; that one generation of this content, with the hypercharge-zero singlet, is a representation of a simple group containing $S(U(2) \times U(3))$ is Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor}(1) with Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-fock-representation-clifford-algebra-bogoliubov} (the $16$ of $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$, whose restriction to $SU(5) \supset S(U(2) \times U(3))$ is $\bar{5} \oplus 10 \oplus 1$), and the ratio computed here is the same over $\bar{5} \oplus 10$, since the singlet contributes nothing to either sum. Without the lepton: $\sum T_{3}^{2} = \tfrac32$, $\sum Y^{2} = \tfrac{10}{3} - \tfrac12 = \tfrac{17}{6}$, $\sum Q^{2} = \tfrac{13}{3}$, ratio $\tfrac{9}{26}$. Over the fibre alone: $\sum T_{3}^{2} = \tfrac32$, $\sum Y^{2} = 6 \cdot \tfrac{1}{36} = \tfrac16$, ratio $\tfrac{3/2}{5/3} = \tfrac{9}{10}$. Over the two doublets: $\sum T_{3}^{2} = 2$, $\sum Y^{2} = \tfrac16 + \tfrac12 = \tfrac23$, ratio $\tfrac{2}{8/3} = \tfrac34$. Every sum is verified exactly by the script.
