Every $10$ enters $M_{d}$ and $M_{e}$, and $M_{u}$ and $M_{\nu}$, with the same coefficient (\claimref{standard-model.imported-clebsch-structure-seesaw}), so it cancels in each difference, and the $126$ leaves $1 - (-3) = 4$ times its contribution. Under (EW, \claimref{standard-model.premise-ew-equal-weight}), $4w_{u}f = m_{t}f/f_{33}$, and $f/f_{33} = (M_{d} - M_{e})/(M_{d} - M_{e})_{33}$ by the first identity. The script checks both identities, and their failure once a $120$ is added, on rational matrices with two $10$s. The argument holds given \claimref{standard-model.hyp-s-slot-statistics}, which names the species whose mass matrices enter.
