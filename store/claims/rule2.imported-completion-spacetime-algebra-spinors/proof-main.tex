The forced completion of the spacetime algebra is $\Cl(1,3) \hookrightarrow \Cl(2,4)$, and the complexified $\Cl(2,4)$ acts irreducibly on the Fock space of three fermionic ladder pairs, whose chirality halves are the conformal spinor space $\CS$ (Penrose's twistor space; in the real algebra it is the minimal left ideal $\Cl^+(2,4)P$, whose complex unit is $I_6$) and its conjugate dual. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thms.~4.2 and 4.13]{ButlerT1} (\claimref{transport.no-go-level-elements}, \claimref{transport.minimality}); \cite[Thms.~3.1 and 3.4]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.fock-model-complexified-ascended-algebra}, \claimref{correspondence.spinor-identification}).
