Every automorphism of a matrix factor is inner, $\theta = \operatorname{Ad}(U)$. (1) Commutation gives $Ue^{JtK}U^{*} = c(t)e^{JtK}$ with $c(t)$ in the field $\mathrm{span}\{1, J\}$ and $|c(t)| = 1$; taking determinants over that field, $c(t)^{2} = 1$, and $c$ is continuous with $c(0) = 1$, so $c \equiv 1$ and $U$ commutes with $K$. As $K = \operatorname{diag}(-\tfrac12, \tfrac12)$ is nondegenerate, $U$ is diagonal and $\operatorname{Ad}(U)$ fixes every diagonal matrix, in particular $e^{-\beta K}/Z$. (2) Flow reversal gives $UKU^{*} = -K$, so $U$ exchanges the spectral projectors of $K$ and $Ue^{-\beta K}U^{*} = e^{+\beta K}$. (3) The reparametrised flow is $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{Jt(cK)})$, generated by $cK$, and by Theorem \claimref{singularity.kms-uniqueness-factor} its $\beta'$-KMS state is $e^{-\beta' cK}/Z$, which equals $e^{-\beta K}/Z$ exactly when $\beta' = \beta/c$. (4) By Theorem \claimref{singularity.kms-uniqueness-factor} the KMS states are exactly the Gibbs states, and an automorphism intertwining the flow either commutes with it or reverses it (it conjugates $K$ to $\pm K$ up to a constant, the spectrum $\{-\tfrac12, \tfrac12\}$ of $K$ leaving no other option), so by (1) and (2) it acts on the parameter through $\{\pm 1\}$ only. That is the whole mathematical content of the clause; the sentence about what a selecting condition must refer to is its reading, and clause (3) is the reason the reading is exact: $\beta$ is the ratio of the state's modular parameter to the flow's parameter, and the second of these is a choice the algebra does not make. The script verifies clauses (1) to (3) on rational data.
