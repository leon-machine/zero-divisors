Every automorphism of a matrix factor is inner, $\theta = \operatorname{Ad}(U)$. (1) Commutation with $\alpha_t$ gives $U e^{itK}U^{*} = c(t)\,e^{itK}$ with $c(t)$ a scalar of modulus one; taking determinants, $c(t)^{2} = 1$, and $c$ is continuous with $c(0) = 1$, so $c \equiv 1$ and $U$ commutes with $e^{itK}$ for every $t$, hence with $K$. Since $K = \operatorname{diag}(-\tfrac12, \tfrac12)$ is nondegenerate, $U$ is diagonal, so $\operatorname{Ad}(U)$ fixes every diagonal matrix, in particular $e^{-\beta K}/Z$. (2) Flow reversal gives $UKU^{*} = -K$, so $U$ exchanges the two spectral projectors of $K$; then $U e^{-\beta K}U^{*} = e^{+\beta K}$, which is the $(-\beta)$-Gibbs matrix. (3) By Theorem \claimref{state-selection.kms-uniqueness-factor} the KMS states are exactly the Gibbs states, and by (1) and (2) the automorphism group acts on the parameter $\beta$ through the group $\{\pm 1\}$ only. A condition stated purely in terms of $\Aa$ and $\alpha_t$ is invariant under every automorphism intertwining the data, hence cannot distinguish $\beta$ from $\beta'$ with $|\beta| = |\beta'|$ and, having no other resources, cannot select a value at all.
