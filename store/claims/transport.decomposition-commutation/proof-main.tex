The blades of $B_\gamma$ are the blades of $A$ together with the blades of $A$ times $\nu$ (ordering $\nu$ last), giving the direct sum; the count $2^{n+1} = 2^n + 2^n$ confirms it, and holds for $\gamma = 0$ as well \cite{Chevalley1997} (\claimref{std-algebra.blade-basis}). For the commutation rule: $\nu$ anticommutes with every generator $e_i$ of $A$ (orthogonality, \claimref{std-algebra.orthogonal-anticommute}), so for a blade $e_I$ of grade $k$, $\nu\, e_I = (-1)^k e_I\, \nu = \hat{e_I}\,\nu$, and the rule extends linearly. This is the commutation rule \eqref{eq:commutation} (\claimref{primer.commutation-rule}) applied to the adjoined generator. Invertibility: $\nu \cdot \gamma\nu = \gamma \nu^2 = \gamma^2 = 1$ for $\gamma = \pm 1$.
