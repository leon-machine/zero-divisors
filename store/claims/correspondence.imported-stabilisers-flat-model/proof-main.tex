In $\Lambda^2 V \cong \mathfrak{so}(2,4)$, the stabiliser of the null vector $\nInf$ is $\Lambda^2 W_{1,3} \oplus (\nInf\wedge W_{1,3}) \cong \mathfrak{so}(1,3)\ltimes\R^4$, the Poincar\'e algebra; the stabiliser of the ray $\R^+\nInf$ adds the grading element $\nInf\wedge\nO$ and is the eleven-dimensional maximal parabolic; the nilradical acts on the point space as the translations and consists of the two-step nilpotent transvections of the series' no-go theorem. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Prop.~4.3]{ButlerT6} (\claimref{interior.stabilisers-flat-model}); the transvection identification \cite[Thm.~4.2]{ButlerT1} (\claimref{transport.no-go-level-elements}).
