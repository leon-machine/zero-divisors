## formal
In $\Cl(2,4)$ with $\nz = \tfrac12(e_{+} + e_{-})$, $\ninf = e_{+} - e_{-}$, $\ip{\nz}{\ninf} = 1$, the point embedding $X(x) = \nz + x - \tfrac12 Q(x)\ninf$ is null, and the vacua at $x$ are the conformal spinors (twistors) whose isotropic three-space contains $X(x)$, a projective line of $\PCS$; two points are null-separated exactly when their lines meet. On the flat model the tractor connection is the ambient derivative, so its parallel transport is trivial, and the motion of the point's null vector $X(x)$ under a displacement is generated by the translations $\{e_a \wedge \ninf\}$, the four-dimensional nilradical of the ten-dimensional stabiliser $\so(1,3) \ltimes \R^{4}$ of $\ninf$ in $\so(2,4)$, the Poincar\'e algebra. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Fact~2.3, Prop.~4.1(2)]{ButlerT16}; the incidence theorem and the stabiliser \cite[Thm.~4.2, Fact~4.3]{ButlerT9}; the tractor connection \cite{BEG1994}.

## technical
In $\Cl(2,4)$ with $n_0 = \tfrac12(e_+ + e_-)$, $n_\infty = e_+ - e_-$ and $\ip{n_0}{n_\infty} = 1$, the point embedding $X(x) = n_0 + x - \tfrac12 Q(x)\,n_\infty$ is null. The vacua at $x$ are the conformal spinors whose isotropic three-space contains $X(x)$, forming a projective line, and two points are null-separated exactly when their lines meet.

On the flat model the tractor connection is the ambient derivative, so its parallel transport is trivial, and displacements move $X(x)$ through the translations $e_a \wedge n_\infty$, the four-dimensional nilradical of the stabiliser $\so(1,3) \ltimes \R^4$ of $n_\infty$ in $\so(2,4)$, the Poincar\'e algebra.

## lay
This restates the flat-space picture of the conformal spinors used in the pairing note. Each spacetime point is a light-like vector in a six-dimensional space and corresponds to a line of vacuum states, and light-like separated points have lines that meet. In flat space, moving a point is a translation, one of the Poincar\'e symmetries that fix the point at infinity.

## one-liner
Imported: on the flat model each point is a null vector whose vacua form a projective line, lines meet exactly for null-separated points, and displacements act through the Poincar\'e translations.
