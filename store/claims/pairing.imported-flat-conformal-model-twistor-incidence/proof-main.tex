In $\Cl(2,4)$ with $\nz = \tfrac12(e_{+} + e_{-})$, $\ninf = e_{+} - e_{-}$, $\ip{\nz}{\ninf} = 1$, the point embedding $X(x) = \nz + x - \tfrac12 Q(x)\ninf$ is null, and the vacua at $x$ are the conformal spinors (twistors) whose isotropic three-space contains $X(x)$, a projective line of $\PCS$; two points are null-separated exactly when their lines meet. On the flat model the tractor connection is the ambient derivative, so its parallel transport is trivial, and the motion of the point's null vector $X(x)$ under a displacement is generated by the translations $\{e_a \wedge \ninf\}$, the four-dimensional nilradical of the ten-dimensional stabiliser $\so(1,3) \ltimes \R^{4}$ of $\ninf$ in $\so(2,4)$, the Poincar\'e algebra. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Fact~2.3, Prop.~4.1(2)]{ButlerT16} (\claimref{one-relation.imported-pure-spinors-incidence-chart}, \claimref{one-relation.spacetime-manifold-vacuum-lines}); the incidence theorem and the stabiliser \cite[Thm.~4.2, Fact~4.3]{ButlerT9} (\claimref{correspondence.imported-stabilisers-flat-model}, \claimref{correspondence.incidence-relation-chart}); the tractor connection \cite{BEG1994}.
