Thermality at the Hawking temperature is a single-mode theorem, the mode being the ladder pair of the ascent, one per point of the singular boundary; its exactness holds under the singularity paper's declared premises (P, \claimref{singularity.p-univalence}) and (R, \claimref{singularity.r-regularity-fixed-locus}), and the value of $T_{H}$ follows from (R). The entropy paper's clock inventory of the singularity's boundary modes gives their clocks the period $\beta = 8\pi M$, the flow's Euclidean period converted at the horizon rate; that it is the state's modular period is premise (R) again (\claimref{singularity.r-regularity-fixed-locus}), which no statement at the singular boundary can supply (\claimref{singularity.why-calibration-cannot-be-internal}). The descent is a one-way conditional expectation. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Thm.~7.3, Rem.~7.33]{ButlerT5} (\claimref{singularity.modular-descent}), the ladder pair per point being \cite[Thm.~8.12]{ButlerT4} (\claimref{sheaf.one-sided-completion-singularity}); \cite[Lem.~4.2]{ButlerT8} (\claimref{scrambling.clock-inventory-boundary-modes}); \cite[Prop.~7.5, Thm.~7.6]{ButlerT1} (\claimref{transport.pinching-kernel}, \claimref{transport.door-one-way}) via \cite[Fact~5.9]{ButlerT5} (\claimref{singularity.imported-descent-one-way-conditional-expectation}).
