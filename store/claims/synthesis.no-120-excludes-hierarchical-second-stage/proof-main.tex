\emph{(i)} By Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-clebsch-structure-seesaw}, under Hypothesis (S) (\claimref{standard-model.hyp-s-slot-statistics}), the Dirac matrices are $M_{e} = \sum_{a}v_{d}^{a}Y_{a} - 3w_{d}f + c_{e}h$ and $M_{\nu} = \sum_{a}v_{u}^{a}Y_{a} - 3w_{u}f + c_{\nu}h$, where the $10$ and $126$ Yukawa matrices $Y_{a}$, $f$ are symmetric in the family indices and the $120$'s $h$ is antisymmetric (the standard symmetry of these couplings, which the Fact's decomposition carries). With no $120$, $c_{e} = c_{\nu} = 0$ and both are symmetric. In the textures of the sum rule, $M = s(\J + \delta(E + X))$ with $\J$ and $E$ symmetric, so $X = ((M/s) - \J)/\delta - E$ is symmetric exactly when $M$ is, and its antisymmetric part is that of $M$ divided by $s\delta$, which is $c h/(s\delta)$. By clause (i) of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.solar-angle-light-plane-sum-rule}, symmetric $X_{s}$ gives $y_{s} = x_{s}$ (one type). The check builds $M_{e}$ from two rational symmetric $10$ couplings and a rational symmetric $126$ coupling and finds it and its $X$ symmetric with equal off-diagonal compression entries ($\hat{b}_{e}^{\mathsf{T}}(E + X)\hat{b}_{o} = \hat{b}_{o}^{\mathsf{T}}(E + X)\hat{b}_{e}$ before normalisation), and with an antisymmetric $h$ added finds the antisymmetric part of $X$ equal to $c h/(d\delta)$ and the two entries different.

\emph{(ii)} With both $X_{s}$ symmetric, clause (iii) of the sum rule gives $\Delta \le \arctan\sqrt{m_{e}/m_{\mu}} + \arctan(m_{1}/m_{2})^{1/4}$, with $|U_{e1}| = \cos\Delta$ at this order. The global fit's three-sigma edges give $|U_{e1}|^{2} \le 0.711$, as in clause (iv) of that Proposition, so $\tan^{2}\Delta \ge (1 - 0.711)/0.711$ and $\tan\Delta \ge T_{0} = 0.6375$ (the check verifies $T_{0}^{2} \le 0.289/0.711$). Put $a = 0.06955$; at the PDG masses $a^{2} \ge m_{e}/m_{\mu}$, so $\arctan\sqrt{m_{e}/m_{\mu}} \le \arctan a$. Then $\arctan(m_{1}/m_{2})^{1/4} \ge \Delta - \arctan a \ge \arctan T_{0} - \arctan a$, and since $T_{0} > a$ and $\tan$ increases on $(-\tfrac{\pi}{2}, \tfrac{\pi}{2})$, $(m_{1}/m_{2})^{1/4} \ge (T_{0} - a)/(1 + T_{0}a) =: b$. In exact rationals $0.5438 < b < 0.5439$ and $b^{4} > 0.0874$, which exceeds $0.01$ by a factor above $8.7$.

\emph{(iii)} By (ii), at this direction and order, (H) together with the measured $\theta_{12}$ excludes the case in which both second stages are of one type, and by (i) that case includes the absence of a $120$ (in both sectors). So (H) needs $c_{e} \neq 0$ or $c_{\nu} \neq 0$. Proposition \claimref{standard-model.neutrino-dirac-matrix-charged-fermions} fixes $M_{\nu}$ by the charged fermions under no $120$, and states that with a $120$ the two differences gain the terms $(c_{d} - c_{e})h$ and $(c_{u} - c_{\nu})h$. How much is lost: $M_{d} - M_{e} = 4w_{d}f + (c_{d} - c_{e})h$ has symmetric part $4w_{d}f$ and antisymmetric part $(c_{d} - c_{e})h$, so $f$, and $h$ up to scale (when $c_{d} \neq c_{e}$), are read off the charged fermions; $c_{u}h$ is the antisymmetric part of $M_{u}$, and (EW) fixes $w_{u}$; so $M_{\nu} = M_{u} - 4w_{u}f - (c_{u} - c_{\nu})h$ is fixed up to the single coefficient $c_{\nu}$. At $c_{\nu} = 0$ the antisymmetric stage can sit in the charged-lepton sector while $M_{\nu}$ stays determined, so the incompatibility is with the elimination as stated, not with every version of it.
