\emph{Setting and finiteness.} The modes $c_{x,k}$ ($x$ a channel, $k \in \Z$ a level) are tower modes, $\{c_{\alpha}, c^{*}_{\beta}\} = \delta_{\alpha\beta}$ and all other anticommutators zero (Fact \claimref{field-theory.imported-tower-limit}); $\mathcal{D}$ is the domain of finite excitations of the band of the null vacuum in every channel (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.exact-window-sugawara-identity-and-luttinger-normal-modes-of-tower-density-waves}(iii), as used in Theorem \claimref{synthesis.standard-model-currents-of-the-sixteen-channels-levels-and-window-sugawara-split}(iii)): a vector agrees with the sea $\Omega$ (levels $k < 0$ filled) outside finitely many modes. The check realises $\mathcal{D}$ exactly, ordering modes from the top so that every fermion sign is a finite count, and confirms the relations of the tower planes $e = c^{*} + c$, $f = c^{*} - c$ there. On $\mathcal{D}$ the energy $H = L_{0}$, counted from the sea, is a sum of positive half-integers over the flipped modes, so $H \ge 0$; since $[H, J(M)_{q}] = -qJ(M)_{q}$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.null-plane-photon-data-and-the-pair-currents-of-the-generators}(v)), $J(M)_{q}\psi = 0$ when $q$ exceeds the energy $E(\psi)$ of $\psi$. Hence in $L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n}\psi$ only terms with both indices in $[n - E(\psi), E(\psi)]$ survive, and every operator of the statement maps $\mathcal{D}$ to itself by a finite sum. On $\mathcal{D}$ the current commutator holds exactly,
\[
[J(M)_{a}, J(M')_{b}] = J([M, M'])_{a+b} + a\operatorname{tr}(MM')\,\delta_{a+b,0}, \tag{1}
\]
(Theorem \claimref{synthesis.standard-model-currents-of-the-sixteen-channels-levels-and-window-sugawara-split}, proof of (iii), the band), and for $\mathfrak{f}$ one of the factors, with $d = \dim\mathfrak{f}$, the trace-dual bases give $\sum_{A}\operatorname{tr}(M^{A}M_{A}) = d$, $\sum_{A}[M^{A}, M_{A}] = 0$ (the Casimir tensor $\sum_{A}M^{A}\otimes M_{A}$ is symmetric), $\sum_{A}\operatorname{tr}(M^{A}X)M_{A} = X$, invariance $\sum_{A}([M^{A}, X]\otimes M_{A} + M^{A}\otimes[M_{A}, X]) = 0$ and $\sum_{A}[M^{A}, [M_{A}, X]] = \lambda_{\mathfrak{f}}X$, with $\lambda = \tfrac32, 1, 0, 0$ (stored (iii)). Write $X_{a} = J(\rho X)_{a}$, $T(a, b) = \sum_{A}J(M^{A})_{a}J(M_{A})_{b}$ and $U(a, b) = \sum_{A}J(M^{A})_{a}J([M_{A}, X])_{b}$. By (1) and the identities, $T(a, b) - T(b, a) = a\,d\,\delta_{a+b,0}$ and, as in the stored proof, $U(a, b) + U(b, a) = \lambda X_{a+b}$, $\sum_{A}J([M^{A}, X])_{a}J(M_{A})_{b} = -U(a, b)$. With $S_{n} = (2 + \lambda)L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n}$, the normal ordering gives $S_{n} = \sum_{q}T(n - q, q)$ termwise for $n \ne 0$ and $S_{0} = \sum_{q}T(-q, q) + \sum_{q < 0}q\,d$; on a fixed $\psi$ every sum below runs over $|q| \le \Lambda$ with $\Lambda$ large, outside which all terms of both sides vanish on $\psi$ (the constant in $S_{0}$ is then a finite constant and commutes with everything).

\emph{(i), the shift.} By (1), $[T(a, b), X_{m}] = U(a, b + m) - U(a + m, b) + b\,\delta_{b,-m}X_{a} + a\,\delta_{a,-m}X_{b}$. Summing over $|q| \le \Lambda$ with $a = n - q$, $b = q$, the two central terms give $-2mX_{m+n}$, and the $U$-sums telescope: for $m > 0$ what survives is $\sum_{q=\Lambda+1}^{\Lambda+m}U(n + m - q, q) - \sum_{q=-\Lambda}^{-\Lambda+m-1}U(n + m - q, q)$. On $\psi$ the first sum vanishes (its right factor has index above $E(\psi)$); in the second, $U(n + m - q, q) = \lambda X_{n+m} - U(q, n + m - q)$ and the last term vanishes on $\psi$ for the same reason, so the second sum is $m\lambda X_{n+m}\psi$. For $m < 0$ the same telescoping leaves $|m|$ terms at the lower end with the opposite sign, again $-m\lambda X_{n+m}$, and $m = 0$ leaves nothing. So $[S_{n}, X_{m}] = -(2 + \lambda)mX_{m+n}$, that is $[L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n}, X_{m}] = -mX_{m+n}$, for every $n$ (the stored case is $n = 0$). For $X'$ in another factor or $X' = 1$, $[M_{A}, \rho X'] = 0$ and $\operatorname{tr}(M_{A}\rho X') = 0$ (stored (ii): the factors commute and are trace-orthogonal, and traceless), so $[S_{n}, X'_{m}] = 0$. For the band energy, $[L_{n}, c^{*}_{x,l}] = (l + \tfrac12 - \tfrac{n}{2})c^{*}_{x,l-n}$ and $[L_{n}, c_{y,l}] = -(l + \tfrac12 + \tfrac{n}{2})c_{y,l+n}$, so after the shift $k \mapsto k + n$ in the first sum the coefficients of $c^{*}_{x,k}c_{y,k+m+n}$ add to $-m$: $[L_{n}, j^{xy}_{m}] = -mj^{xy}_{m+n}$; the boundary terms of the cutoff are deep-sea occupation numbers, a scalar, which vanishes because the weights $k + \tfrac12 + \tfrac{n}{2}$ sum to zero over the $n$ levels below the Fermi level that a hop of length $n$ crosses (for $m = -n > 0$: $\sum_{l=0}^{n-1}(l - \tfrac{n}{2} + \tfrac12) = 0$).

\emph{(ii), the Virasoro relations.} By (i), $[L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{m}, T(a, b)] = -a\,T(a + m, b) - b\,T(a, b + m)$. Summing over $|q| \le \Lambda$ with $a = n - q$, $b = q$ and shifting $q \mapsto q - m$ in the second sum, the common range $[-\Lambda + m, \Lambda]$ (for $m > 0$) carries $\big(-(n - q) - (q - m)\big)T(n + m - q, q) = (m - n)T(n + m - q, q)$; of the leftover terms, those with $q \in [\Lambda + 1, \Lambda + m]$ vanish on $\psi$, and those with $q \in [-\Lambda, -\Lambda + m - 1]$ equal $-(n - q)T(n + m - q, q) = -(n - q)\big(T(q, n + m - q) + (n + m - q)d\,\delta_{n+m,0}\big)$, whose first part vanishes on $\psi$. For $n + m \ne 0$ the common range is $S_{n+m}\psi$ and the result is $[L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{m}, S_{n}] = (m - n)S_{m+n}$. For $n = -m$, rewriting $\sum_{q=-\Lambda+m}^{\Lambda}T(-q, q)$ through the normal ordering of $S_{0}$ leaves the constant
\[
2md\Big(-\sum_{q=-\Lambda}^{-1}q + \sum_{q=-\Lambda}^{-\Lambda+m-1}q\Big) - d\sum_{q=-\Lambda}^{-\Lambda+m-1}q(q + m) = \frac{d\,(m^{3} - m)}{6},
\]
in which the terms in $\Lambda^{2}$ and $\Lambda$ cancel; $m < 0$ follows by antisymmetry. Dividing by $2 + \lambda$, $[L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{m}, L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n}] = (m - n)L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{m+n} + \tfrac{c_{\mathfrak{f}}}{12}(m^{3} - m)\delta_{m+n,0}$ with $c_{\mathfrak{f}} = 2d/(2 + \lambda_{\mathfrak{f}})$: $c_{\su(3)} = 16/\tfrac72 = \tfrac{32}{7}$, $c_{\su(2)} = 6/3 = 2$, $c_{Y} = c_{N} = 1$. For the band energy, the bilinear commutator $[c^{*}_{a}c_{b}, c^{*}_{c}c_{d}] = \delta_{bc}c^{*}_{a}c_{d} - \delta_{ad}c^{*}_{c}c_{b}$ gives $(m - n)L_{m+n}$ for $m + n \ne 0$, and for $m + n = 0$ the same up to a scalar (deep-sea occupation numbers); the scalar is the vacuum value $\langle\Omega, [L_{m}, L_{-m}]\Omega\rangle = \|L_{-m}\Omega\|^{2}$ ($L_{m}\Omega = 0$ for $m \ge 0$), and $L_{-m}\Omega = \sum_{x}\sum_{l=0}^{m-1}(l + \tfrac12 - \tfrac{m}{2})c^{*}_{x,l}c_{x,l-m}\Omega$ has norm $16\sum_{l=0}^{m-1}(l + \tfrac12 - \tfrac{m}{2})^{2} = \tfrac{16}{12}(m^{3} - m)$: $c = 16$. Adjoints: $J(M)_{m}^{*} = J(M^{*})_{-m}$, and the Casimir tensor is invariant under $M \mapsto M^{*}$ on both factors (computed in a basis closed under $^{*}$), so $(L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n})^{*} = L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{-n}$; $L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n}\Omega = 0$ for $n \ge -1$ since every normal-ordered term then has a right factor of index $\ge 1$, or is $J_{-1}J_{0}$ or $J_{0}J_{0}$, and $J(M)_{0}\Omega = 0$. The inner product of $\mathcal{D}$ is positive definite, so these are unitary representations.

\emph{(iii), the remainder.} By (i), $L^{c}_{n} = L_{n} - \sum_{\mathfrak{f}}L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n}$ commutes with every $X_{m}$ ($X$ in a factor) and with $N_{m}$: the band energy shifts every pair current, and each factor's part shifts its own currents and commutes with the others'. On each vector, $L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n}$ is a finite sum of products of currents of $\mathfrak{f}$, applied in turn; so $[L^{c}_{m}, L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n}] = 0$, and for $\mathfrak{f} \ne \mathfrak{f}'$, $[L^{\mathfrak{f}'}_{m}, L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n}] = 0$. Hence $[L_{m}, L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n}] = [L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{m}, L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n}] = [L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{m}, L_{n}]$ and
\[
[L^{c}_{m}, L^{c}_{n}] = [L_{m}, L_{n}] - \sum_{\mathfrak{f}}[L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{m}, L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{n}] = (m - n)L^{c}_{m+n} + \frac{16 - \sum_{\mathfrak{f}}c_{\mathfrak{f}}}{12}(m^{3} - m)\delta_{m+n,0},
\]
with $16 - \tfrac{32}{7} - 2 - 1 - 1 = \tfrac{52}{7}$, and $(L^{c}_{n})^{*} = L^{c}_{-n}$.

\emph{(iv), the $\so(10)$ form.} The $45$ bilinears of the five modes form a traceless family on the sixteen channels, trace-orthogonal to $1$, with $\lambda_{\so(10)} = 4$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.window-energy-of-one-generation-is-exactly-the-so10-sugawara-energy}(iii)); its currents commute with $N_{m}$ by (1). Steps (i) to (iii) apply to the family $\{\so(10), N\}$ with no change, so $\Delta_{n} = L_{n} - L^{\so(10)}_{n} - L^{N}_{n}$ is a Virasoro algebra on $\mathcal{D}$ of central charge $16 - 15 - 1 = 0$. The stored identity $H = H_{\so(10)} + H_{N}$ holds on every window and every vector; a vector of $\mathcal{D}$ and the operators of $\Delta_{0}$ acting on it agree with window vectors and window operators of a window around it, at whose edges it is clean (the localisation argument of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.standard-model-currents-of-the-sixteen-channels-levels-and-window-sugawara-split}(iii), windows), with the window energy $H_{W} = H + sN_{0} + 8s^{2}$ and $H_{N,W} = H_{N} + sN_{0} + 8s^{2}$ for a sea of depth $s$ and the $\so(10)$ zero modes unchanged (traceless); so $\Delta_{0} = 0$ on $\mathcal{D}$, which the check also confirms directly. Then $-n\Delta_{n} = [\Delta_{0}, \Delta_{n}] = 0$, so $\Delta_{n} = 0$ for every $n$: $L_{n} = L^{\so(10)}_{n} + L^{N}_{n}$, and $L^{c}_{n} = L^{\so(10)}_{n} - L^{\su(3)}_{n} - L^{\su(2)}_{n} - L^{Y}_{n}$, the gauge algebra acting inside $\so(10)$ through the number-conserving bilinears.

\emph{(v).} The check computes all of this exactly on $\mathcal{D}$: the plane relations on $1692$ relations of a three-channel band; the band energy's Virasoro relations at $c = 16$ (and their failure at $c = 15$ and with the weight $k + \tfrac{n}{2}$) and the shift of pair currents; the shifts of each factor's currents ($80$ cases, failing with $\lambda + 1$); the factors' Virasoro relations ($48$ cases, failing with $c + 1$ and with $\lambda + 1$); the remainder's relations at $c = \tfrac{52}{7}$ and its commutation with the currents and the parts; $L_{n} = L^{\so(10)}_{n} + L^{N}_{n}$ for $n = -2, \dots, 2$ on three vectors (failing with the $25$ number-conserving bilinears alone); the vacuum norms $\|L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{-2}\Omega\|^{2} = \tfrac{16}{7}, 1, \tfrac12, \tfrac12$, $\|L^{\mathfrak{f}}_{-3}\Omega\|^{2} = \tfrac{64}{7}, 4, 2, 2$, the remainder's $\tfrac{26}{7}$, $\tfrac{104}{7}$, and the adjoint relations on sample vectors.

\emph{What is standard.} The Sugawara construction, its Virasoro relations with $c = k\dim\mathfrak{g}/(k + h^{\vee})$ and the coset construction are standard (prior art, not premises: see the extraction note); here they are derived from the stored band current algebra, with $\lambda$ in place of $2h^{\vee}/k$, and checked exactly on the sixteen channels of one generation.
