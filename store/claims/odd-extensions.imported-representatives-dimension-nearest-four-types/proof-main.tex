At the dimension nearest four the residues are represented by the shadows $(1,3)$ at $r = 6$, $(3,1)$ at $r = 2$, $(2,2)$ at $r = 0$, $(0,4)$ and $(4,0)$ at $r = 4$, $(2,1)$ at $r = 1$, $(1,2)$ at $r = 7$, $(4,1)$ at $r = 3$ and $(1,4)$ at $r = 5$; the residues $0$ and $4$ are sterile and the other six admit matter. The real spinor type is a function of the residue alone: $\Cl(1,3) \cong \Mat(2,\HH)$ (quaternionic, one module) and $\Cl(3,1) \cong \Mat(4,\R)$ (real, one module); in particular the two are not isomorphic as real algebras. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[Prop.~3.1, Thm.~3.4]{ButlerT14} (\claimref{dark-matter.towers-are-residues-single-clock-filter}, \claimref{dark-matter.type-residue-what-each-tower-can}).
