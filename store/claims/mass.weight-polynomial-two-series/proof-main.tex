(1) $w(w+3) = 0$ iff $w \in \{0,-3\}$, and $w(w+3)$ is invariant under $w \mapsto -3-w$. (2) At $w = 1 - n/2$, $w + n - 1 = n/2$ and $-w(w+n-1) = (n/2)(n/2 - 1) = n(n-2)/4$; for $n = 4$ this is $2$. Directly from the operator: at this weight the top two slots of $D\sigma$ vanish, $I \cdot D\sigma = -(\Box\sigma - wJ\sigma) = -(\Box\sigma + J\sigma)$, and $J = 2\Lambda/3$ gives $m^{2} = 2\Lambda/3$, which is the classical value of the conformal coupling $\tfrac16 R$ on de Sitter space in these conventions; this is the convention-independent reference for the sign of \claimref{mass.mass-weight-dictionary}. (3) Complete the square: $-w(w+3) = \tfrac94 - (w+\tfrac32)^{2}$, which is nonnegative iff $|w + \tfrac32| \le \tfrac32$ iff $w \in [-3,0]$; for instance $w = 1$ gives $m^{2} = -4H^{2}$, outside both series of Fact \claimref{mass.imported-unitary-representations-de-sitter-group}, which also names the series in (3) and (4). (4) Solve the quadratic $w^{2} + 3w + m^{2}/H^{2} = 0$; its discriminant $9 - 4m^{2}/H^{2}$ is negative exactly when $m > 3H/2$. The expansion of $\nu = (m/H)\sqrt{1 - \tfrac94 H^{2}/m^{2}}$ is checked by the script as an exact truncated power series in $\epsilon := H/m$: $\sqrt{1 - \tfrac94\epsilon^{2}} = 1 - \tfrac98\epsilon^{2} - \tfrac{81}{128}\epsilon^{4} + \cdots$. The script checks the two massless roots and the shadow symmetry $m^{2}(w) = m^{2}(-3-w)$ on instances, the closed form $\tfrac{n(n-2)}{4}Q(I)$ at $w = 1 - n/2$ for four values of $n$, the completed square as a polynomial identity together with the sign of $m^{2}$ at real weights inside and outside $[-3,0]$, and item (4) in Gaussian rationals.
