Every member of $A$ has its partner outside $A$ ($L_k \in W$ for the new quantum, $R_j \in R_{<}$ for an evicted $L_j$), so by the support constraint $\rho_A$ is diagonal with the product distribution of its members, and $S(\rho_A) = h_k + \sum_j h_j$. By purity, $S(\rho_{R_<})$ before the event equals $S_L$ before it, and it is unchanged by the event (a unitary on the complement of $R_<$ and the adjunction of a product factor leave $\rho_{R_<}$ fixed); $S(\rho_{AR_<})$ after the event is $S_L$ after it. Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}(1) gives $S_L^{\mathrm{after}} - S_L^{\mathrm{before}} = h_k - \sum_j h_j$, so $I(A{:}R_<) = S(\rho_A) + S_L^{\mathrm{before}} - S_L^{\mathrm{after}} = 2\sum_j h_j$. The per-qubit statement is the same computation with $A$ replaced by the single $L_j$. The script computes $S(\rho_A)$, $S(\rho_{R_<})$ and $S(\rho_{AR_<})$ from exact spectra at every event of the model's history and verifies the identity.
