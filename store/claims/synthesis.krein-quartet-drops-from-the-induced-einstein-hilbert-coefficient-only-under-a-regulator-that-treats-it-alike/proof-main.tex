\emph{Weights.} By Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-sakharov-induced-gravity-coefficients}(a), (b), a Laplace-type operator $D = -(\nabla^{2} + E)$ on a bundle of rank $r$ over a closed Riemannian four-manifold has heat trace $\mathrm{Tr}\,e^{-tD} = (4\pi t)^{-2}\bigl(\int r + t\int\mathrm{tr}(E + R/6) + O(t^{2})\bigr)$, and a term $\tfrac{\sigma}{2}\log\det D$ of the one-loop effective action, regulated by a heat-kernel (proper-time) cut-off $t \ge \epsilon$, contributes to the induced Einstein-Hilbert term in proportion to $\sigma\int\mathrm{tr}(E + R/6)\,\epsilon^{-1}$. We call $\sigma\,\mathrm{tr}(E + R/6)/(R/6)$ the \emph{weight} of the term: $1$ for a minimally coupled real scalar ($\sigma = 1$, $E = 0$), $1 - 6\xi_{R}$ for a real scalar of curvature coupling $\xi_{R}$ (we write $\xi_{R}$ for the curvature coupling; $\xi$ is the D-term level elsewhere in the store), and its $t^{-2}$ analogue $\sigma r$ the volume (quartic) weight. For a two-component fermion the Lichnerowicz term is $E = \tfrac12\sum_{a<b}e_{a}e_{b}\,\mathcal{R}(e_{a}\wedge e_{b})$ acting by left multiplication; on a field with $\mathcal{R}(B) = \kappa B$ on a set of planes the check computes it in $\Cl(4,0)$ as $-R/4$, so with $\sigma = -1$ on two components the weight is $-2(R/6 - R/4)/(R/6) = 1$. The Ricci map is $\Ric(a) = \sum_{b}e_{b}\cdot\mathcal{R}(e_{b}\wedge a)$; the check computes $\Ric = (0, 2, 2, 2)$ in a frame $(e_{0}, e_{i})$ with $R = 6$ on the Einstein static field $\mathbb{R}\times S^{3}$ of unit radius, and $\Ric = 3$, $R = 12$ on the unit four-sphere.

\emph{The vector and its quartet.} Write the gauge field as a one-form $A$ with field strength $dA$, gauge-fixed covariantly by the term $\tfrac{1}{2\alpha}(\delta A)^{2}$ ($\delta$ the codifferential; in Lorentzian signature the Nakanishi-Lautrup family, whose member $\alpha = 1$ has the Gupta-Bleuler commutator $[a(u), a(w)^{\dagger}] = -u\cdot w$ of clause (K1) of Premise \claimref{synthesis.krein-form-premise-indefinite-inner-product-for-gauge-fields}), with the Faddeev-Popov ghost and antighost, whose operator is $\delta d = \Delta_{0}$ on functions and which enter as $-\log\det\Delta_{0}$ (two real scalars of fermionic sign, weight $-2$). The quadratic operator on one-forms is $P_{\alpha} = \delta d + \alpha^{-1}d\delta = \Delta_{1} + (\alpha^{-1} - 1)d\delta$, with $\Delta_{1} = d\delta + \delta d$ the Hodge Laplacian; by the Weitzenb\"ock formula $\Delta_{1} = \nabla^{*}\nabla + \Ric$, so $E = -\Ric$ and $\mathrm{tr}(E + R/6) = -R + 4R/6 = -R/3$: the weight of $\tfrac12\log\det\Delta_{1}$ is $-2$, and with the ghosts the Feynman-gauge ($\alpha = 1$) vector has weight $-4$, the value of the stored count (\claimref{synthesis.attachment-modules-give-sakharov-weights-cancelling-at-three-separate-generations}(ii)). The unphysical quartet is the time component (in the static case) or the exact part of $A$, its partner, the ghost and the antighost; by clause (K4) of the Krein premise its states carry zero norm in the physical space (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-brst-kugo-ojima-strocchi-and-gribov}).

\emph{(i) Energy cut-off on the Einstein static field (under the Krein premise).} In the Feynman gauge on $\mathbb{R}\times S^{3}$ the field equation is $(\partial_{t}^{2} + \Delta)A = 0$ componentwise: since $e_{0}$ is parallel and $\Ric(e_{0}) = 0$ (computed), $A_{0}$ obeys the minimal scalar equation with $\Delta = \Delta_{0}$ on $S^{3}$, and the spatial part obeys it with the Hodge Laplacian $\Delta_{1}$ of $S^{3}$. The scalar harmonics of degree $l$ (restrictions of harmonic homogeneous polynomials of degree $l$ on $\mathbb{R}^{4}$) have $\Delta_{0} = l(l+2)$ and multiplicity $\binom{l+3}{3} - \binom{l+1}{3} = (l+1)^{2}$ (computed for $l < 40$; in general by the same binomial identity). The Hodge decomposition of one-forms on $S^{3}$ (no harmonic one-forms) is exact $\oplus$ coexact, and $\Delta_{1}d = d\Delta_{0}$, with $d$ injective on nonconstant functions; so the exact one-forms have the eigenvalues $l(l+2)$, $l \ge 1$, with multiplicity $(l+1)^{2}$. The ghost and antighost obey the minimal scalar equation. So all four members of the quartet have frequencies $\omega_{l} = \sqrt{l(l+2)}$, with multiplicities $(l+1)^{2}$ ($A_{0}$, $l \ge 0$), $(l+1)^{2}$ (exact part, $l \ge 1$) and $2(l+1)^{2}$ (ghosts, $l \ge 0$). A bosonic mode of either norm has zero-point energy $+\omega/2$: for the time component, quantised with $[a_{0}, a_{0}^{\dagger}] = -1$ as clause (K1) of the Krein premise requires, the symmetrised Hamiltonian $-\tfrac{\omega}{2}(a_{0}a_{0}^{\dagger} + a_{0}^{\dagger}a_{0}) = \omega N_{0} + \tfrac{\omega}{2}$ with $N_{0} = -a_{0}^{\dagger}a_{0}$; a real Grassmann mode has $-\omega/2$. The signed count at level $l$ is therefore $(l+1)^{2} + (l+1)^{2} - 2(l+1)^{2} = 0$ for $l \ge 1$ and $1 - 2 = -1$ at $l = 0$, where $\omega_{0} = 0$ (computed for $l < 40$, and identically from the multiplicities). For every cut-off function, $\tfrac12\sum\omega f(\omega/L)$ over the quartet is zero: the quartet drops level by level and the vector's zero-point sum is that of the coexact one-forms. These have $\Delta_{1} = (l+1)^{2}$, $l \ge 1$, and multiplicity $2l(l+2)$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-ikeda-taniguchi-spectra-of-the-hodge-laplacian-on-spheres}), which is $2(k^{2} - 1)$ at $k = l + 1$, the attachment's helicity $\pm 1$ modules of the stored count; the check confirms that the $S^{3}$ spectra reproduce the heat-kernel densities, $\tfrac{\sqrt{\pi}}{4}t^{-3/2}(1 + t)$ for $\Delta_{0}$ and $\tfrac{\sqrt{\pi}}{4}t^{-3/2}(3 - 3t)$ for $\Delta_{1}$ (with $\mathrm{tr}(E + R/6) = -6 + 3 = -3$ from the computed Ricci map), using $\int_{0}^{\infty}k^{2j}e^{-tk^{2}}\,dk = \Gamma(j + \tfrac12)/(2t^{j + 1/2})$ and the Euler-Maclaurin formula for an even summand (whose odd derivatives vanish at $0$, so the sum and the integral have the same polar part). So under an energy cut-off the vector's weight is $-4$ and comes from its two helicities alone. The energy cut-off and the heat-kernel cut-off give the same weights for the other fields: on an ultrastatic field the four-dimensional operator is $-\partial_{t}^{2}$ plus a Laplace-type spatial operator $D_{3}$, the frequencies are $\sqrt{D_{3}}$, and the curvature-linear heat coefficient of $D_{3}$ is $\mathrm{tr}(E + R_{3}/6)$ with $R_{3} = R$ there, so the $t^{-1}$ polar coefficient of the energy trace (the dictionary of \claimref{synthesis.attachment-modules-give-sakharov-weights-cancelling-at-three-separate-generations}(ii)) is the four-dimensional weight: $1$ per Weyl fermion and $1 - 6\xi_{R}$ per scalar. For $\alpha \neq 1$ the time and longitudinal components mix and have dipole solutions, but every frequency is still $\omega_{l}$; that the quartet then also drops is argued, not computed.

\emph{(ii) Heat-kernel cut-off on the gauge-fixed operators (Euclidean reading).} The computation is a Euclidean reading, outside the flat background on which the gravitational field lives: the weights it defines are the coefficients of the local curvature-linear density, which do not depend on the global field used to read them. On a closed Riemannian four-manifold, Hodge theory gives $\Omega^{1} = d\Omega^{0}\oplus\delta\Omega^{2}\oplus\mathcal{H}^{1}$, $P_{\alpha}$ preserves the decomposition, $P_{\alpha} = \Delta_{1}$ on coclosed forms, and $P_{\alpha}\,d\phi = \alpha^{-1}d\delta d\phi = \alpha^{-1}d\Delta_{0}\phi$. So the spectrum of $P_{\alpha}$ on exact forms is the nonzero spectrum of $\Delta_{0}$ divided by $\alpha$ (the flat momentum-space form, $P_{\alpha}(k)a = Q(k)a - (1 - \alpha^{-1})(k\cdot a)k$ with eigenvalue $Q(k)/\alpha$ on $k$ and $Q(k)$ on $k^{\perp}$, is checked in $\Cl(4,0)$), and the coclosed part of $\Delta_{1}$ has heat trace $\mathrm{Tr}\,e^{-t\Delta_{1}} - \mathrm{Tr}'e^{-t\Delta_{0}}$ (finitely many harmonic and zero modes contribute no pole). With the cut-off $t \ge \epsilon$ applied to $P_{\alpha}$ and to the ghost operator, the polar coefficients are additive: the coclosed part has $t^{-1}$ weight $-2 - 1 = -3$ and $t^{-2}$ weight $4 - 1 = 3$; $\mathrm{Tr}'e^{-t\Delta_{0}/\alpha}$ is $\mathrm{Tr}'e^{-t'\Delta_{0}}$ at $t' = t/\alpha$, so its $t^{-1}$ and $t^{-2}$ coefficients are multiplied by $\alpha$ and $\alpha^{2}$, weights $\alpha$ and $\alpha^{2}$; the ghosts give $-2$ and $-2$. Hence the vector's weight is $-3 + \alpha - 2 = \alpha - 5$ and its quartic weight is $3 + \alpha^{2} - 2 = 1 + \alpha^{2}$. The value $-4$ (and $2$) holds only at $\alpha = 1$; in the Landau limit the longitudinal modes become infinitely heavy and the weight is $-5$. On the unit four-sphere the check computes every term exactly from the spectra: $\Delta_{0} = l(l+3)$ with multiplicity $(l+1)(l+2)(2l+3)/6$ (checked against the harmonic-polynomial count for $l < 30$) and coexact $\Delta_{1} = (l+1)(l+2)$ with multiplicity $l(l+3)(2l+3)/2$, $l \ge 1$ (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-ikeda-taniguchi-spectra-of-the-hodge-laplacian-on-spheres}); with $\nu = l + \tfrac32$ both are odd polynomials in $\nu$ times $e^{-t\nu^{2}}$ on the half-integer grid, whose polar parts are those of the integrals, $\int_{0}^{\infty}\nu^{2j+1}e^{-t\nu^{2}}d\nu = j!/(2t^{j+1})$ (midpoint Euler-Maclaurin; the endpoint corrections are polynomial in $t$). They give $\tfrac16t^{-2} + \tfrac13t^{-1}$ and $\tfrac12t^{-2} - t^{-1}$, and the heat-kernel densities $\tfrac16(1 + 2t)t^{-2}$ for $\Delta_{0}$ and $\tfrac16(4 - 4t)t^{-2}$ for $\Delta_{1}$ (volume $8\pi^{2}/3$, $\mathrm{tr}(E + R/6) = -12 + 8 = -4$ from the computed Ricci map) are reproduced exactly; the weights $\alpha - 5$ and $1 + \alpha^{2}$ follow for $\alpha = 0, \tfrac12, 1, 2, 3$.

\emph{(iii) The gauge-invariant reading.} The product $\det_{T}(\Delta_{1})^{-1/2}\det{}'(\Delta_{0})^{1/2}$, which carries no gauge parameter (it is the result of (ii) at $\alpha = 1$, where the longitudinal factor and one ghost factor cancel), regulated by one cut-off, has weight $-3 - 1 = -4$ and quartic weight $3 - 1 = 2$, the two helicities.

\emph{(iv) The series' content.} The connection-forced algebra has dimension $8 + 3 + 1 = 12$ (Theorem \claimref{standard-model.connection-forced}), each generation sixteen Weyl components (\claimref{synthesis.slot-module-light-ray-arrays-are-weyl-components}), three generations read as separate slot modules as in the stored count, and the rung field is two elementary doublets of hypercharge $\tfrac12$ (\claimref{synthesis.linear-rung-field-has-a-neutral-normal-pair-in-the-scalon-sum}), eight real scalars, each of weight $1 - 6\xi_{R}$ by Fact (a). Summing, $B = 48 + 12\,w_{V} + 8(1 - 6\xi_{R})$ with $w_{V} = -4$ under the energy cut-off (by (i)) and $w_{V} = \alpha - 5$ under the heat-kernel cut-off on the gauge-fixed operators (by (ii)): $B = 8(1 - 6\xi_{R})$, respectively $B = 12(\alpha - 1) + 8(1 - 6\xi_{R})$. The check evaluates the sum for several $\alpha$ and $\xi_{R}$: it is $0$ at $\xi_{R} = \tfrac16$ for the energy cut-off and for $\alpha = 1$, $-12$ at $\xi_{R} = \tfrac16$ in the Landau gauge, $8$ at $\xi_{R} = 0$, $\alpha = 1$, and zero also at $(\alpha, \xi_{R}) = (\tfrac13, 0)$.

\emph{Reading (argued).} Under clause (K4) of the Krein premise the quartet has zero norm in the physical space (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-brst-kugo-ojima-strocchi-and-gribov}). The heat-kernel cut-off on $P_{\alpha}$ treats the longitudinal mode, whose eigenvalue is rescaled by $\alpha^{-1}$, differently from the ghosts, whose operator is not, so the quartet that (K4) removes from the physical space still contributes $\alpha - 1$ per vector to a power-divergent coefficient; a regulator applied alike to the four members (the energy cut-off, or any cut-off at $\alpha = 1$) removes it. The dependence of power-divergent terms on the gauge parameter is standard in kind; the weight $\alpha - 5$ is computed here. Schemes without a dimensionful cut-off (dimensional continuation, or the locally covariant renormalisation of the stress tensor) induce no such term for massless fields at all (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-sakharov-induced-gravity-coefficients}(d)). So the induced Einstein-Hilbert coefficient of the series' content is not fixed by its physical content alone: it is fixed by the content together with the regulator and, for a covariant cut-off, the gauge. Under the store's Krein premise, whose clause (K1) is the Gupta-Bleuler value $\alpha = 1$, the vector weight is $-4$ and the stored cancellation holds at $\xi_{R} = \tfrac16$; at $\xi_{R} = 0$ the content induces $B = 8$. The result does not use transport (transport-absent).
