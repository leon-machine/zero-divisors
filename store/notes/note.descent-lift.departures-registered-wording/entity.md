## formal
Five, recorded in one place. (1) The registration framed the count on the full Fock space, where the single-mode descent has Kraus rank $2$ and the $\nu$-mode descent rank $2^{\nu}$, and predicted a minimal record of $\nu + 1$ rungs (P1). On the physical, even states, which is where (P) places the framework and where the note is stated, the ranks are $1$ and $2^{\nu-1}$ and the minimal record is $\nu$ rungs; the single mode has nothing to lift, and the pair is the base case. The full-space count $\nu + 1$ is correct for the descent of unphysical coherences and is not the note's result. (2) K1 was framed for one mode, where on even states there is nothing to lift; its test, that the even record states must be orthogonal and one rung has one, applies unchanged to the pair, and Corollary \claimref{descent-lift.parity-alone-forces-second-rung} records that both one-rung maps do induce the descent and fail by parity alone. (3) K4, P4, P5 and P6 were stated for a single mode $c\ket{0} + s\ket{1}$, which is not an even state; the note states them for the pair $c\ket{00} + s\ket{11}$, where every number predicted holds. (4) Claim D, K2 and P2 as registered place the pairing rotation on the record. Theorem \claimref{descent-lift.lift-costs-one-rung-per-mode}(v) shows it may be placed on the system instead and carried to the record by hops; the clause that survives is that no number-conserving dynamics lifts, and the lift of Theorem \claimref{descent-lift.lift-costs-one-rung-per-mode}(iv) places the pairing on the record as registered. (5) P7 as registered named the ledger entry transport-convenient; the grade found is transport-absent, for the reason given below.
