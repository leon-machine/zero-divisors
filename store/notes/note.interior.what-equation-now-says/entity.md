## formal
In the flat model the parallel null tractor is the ideal point $\nInf$ of Proposition \claimref{interior.stabilisers-flat-model}: the point at infinity. Theorem \claimref{interior.covariant-form-vacuum-equations} therefore reads: \emph{the field equation of vacuum gravity literally asserts the covariant constancy of a distinguished point at infinity}. This is a statement not about metric components but about a preferred ideal point of the fibre quadric, parallel-transported without change over the whole of $M^\circ$. The space it lies in, the point space of the completion, is precisely where \cite[Thm.~4.10]{ButlerT1} located the resolution of division by zero, and precisely the structure that the no-go theorem showed cannot be replaced by any element-level classical assignment. The ascent is not notation added to the physics: the physics, in vacuum, is an assertion within the ascended description, and the familiar metric form of the equations is what that assertion looks like after a scale is chosen. The points of contact with established physics, kept in view as the series' practice requires: the bundle $\mathcal{T}$ is the standard conformal tractor bundle (Thomas; Bailey-Eastwood-Gover), the equivalence ``Einstein $\Leftrightarrow$ parallel scale tractor'' is the almost-Einstein characterisation of Gover \cite{Gover2010}, and the spinor module of the fibre algebra $\Cl(2,4)$, the conformal spinor space $\CS$, is Penrose's twistor space, whose development belongs to the series' correspondence paper.
