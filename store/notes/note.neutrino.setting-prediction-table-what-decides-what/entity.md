## formal
The paper's stances, each with the measurement that refutes it. \emph{The sum.} A robust prior-independent $\mnu < 0.057$ eV within $w = -1$, across likelihoods and free of the lensing anomaly, refutes the joint prediction: the neutrino sector and the frozen-point cosmology cannot both survive. Live now, in successive baryon-acoustic and microwave releases \cite{DESI2025, HouWangZhaoZhang2026}. \emph{The ordering.} A confirmed inverted ordering refutes Proposition \claimref{neutrino.rank-one-seesaw-ordering-sum-double} outright; long-baseline and atmospheric data decide it, and cosmology already constrains inverted ordering most tightly. \emph{The lightest mass.} A determination of $m_{1}$ above about $1$ meV refutes Premise (H) as stated and moves the sum off the floor by more than the prediction's own margin; above about $4$ meV (certainly above $4.6$ meV, across the whole splitting bracket) the sum exceeds the $0.064$ eV bound already quoted, and a sum determined meaningfully above the floor, at $0.075$ eV say, refutes the prediction as decisively as one below it; neutrinoless double-beta decay at next-generation sensitivity refutes the rank-one structure itself. \emph{The phase.} A measured nontrivial $\delta_{CP}$ with normal ordering established refutes the real-breaking assumption of Proposition \claimref{neutrino.leptogenesis-suppressed-alignment-reheating-premise-new}(3) and reopens the leptogenesis question it closed; the current joint analysis leaves it allowed under normal ordering and excluded under inverted \cite{T2KNOvA2025}. \emph{The orientation.} No completion of the orientation can exclude the identification, which is flag-covariant (Fact \claimref{neutrino.imported-ceiling-under-hypothesis-u-arrow}); there is no refutation condition here. The first of these may be the fastest-moving falsifier in the series, which is why this paper is written and dated now.
