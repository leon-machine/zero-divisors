## formal
Three groups appear in nature and three groups appear on the ladder, but the derivation does not proceed by matching lists. The chain is: mass begins at two constituents (a theorem of the mass paper); a constituent carries three pairs (a theorem of T09, the twistor paper, on the conformal spinor space, with the three tied to dimension four); so the smallest composite that can have mass is $\C^{2} \otimes \C^{3}$; and the forces are the freedom to rotate its internal structure without changing what it is. Each link carries its own level, itemised where it occurs, and the final identification of the automorphism group with the observed gauge group is at observation level and says so. What makes the derivation falsifiable rather than numerological is Theorem \claimref{standard-model.global-form-quotient-determined}(iii): the global form is forced along with the algebra, and the $\mathbb{Z}_{6}$ lattice it imposes is an exclusion statement current experiments can violate.
