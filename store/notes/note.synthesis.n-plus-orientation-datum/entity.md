## formal
Premise (N$_{+}$) (\claimref{synthesis.n-plus-null-ground-state}) assumes the orientation of the field's unit $I_{4}$ relative to the time orientation, which decides between the null vacuum and the filled positive-energy sea. Three facts now locate that datum. First, with the unit $I_{4}$ every massless mode's helicity is locked to its frequency sign, every map commuting with the Lorentz rotors is $I_{4}$-linear, and among the discrete maps of the massless field the frequency sign flips exactly under reversal of spatial orientation; Hestenes' charge conjugation flips the chirality of massless even spinors and not the frequency sign, and a CP map flips the frequency sign and not the chirality (\claimref{synthesis.massless-frequency-sign-relative-to-i4-is-not-the-chirality}). So the datum is odd under P and under CP, it is not that chirality, and no CP-invariant interaction fixes it. Second, on the Witt flags of $\R^{2,4}$ adapted to the conformal energy, the energy sign of a flag's vacuum and the stored flag bit flip together on each pair of conjugate flags (\claimref{synthesis.z-adapted-witt-flags-energy-sign-follows-the-flag-bit}), so read as the energy sign of the flag's vacuum the datum is the descent's flag bit (\claimref{dark-matter.premise-flag-as-datum}) up to the descent's kinematic sign on the four-space orthogonal to $Z$. Third, on every field the Hadamard deposit premise reaches, the datum is carried by that premise, through results that rest on no postulate (\claimref{synthesis.hadamard-scaling-limit-premise}). What is not shown is that the generators' modes inherit the descent's flag; on the eternal hole and eternal de Sitter, which no descent reaches, the datum stays assumed.
