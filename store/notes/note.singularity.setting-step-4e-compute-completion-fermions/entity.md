## formal
The chain's pairs are fermionic; the directly derived spectrum is Fermi-Dirac. The observed Hawking flux is dominated by photons, whose occupancy is Bose-Einstein. The bridge is bosonisation, and its setting is not imported: the boundary modes form a one-dimensional fermion array with the boost as its clock, which is exactly the setting of the boson-fermion correspondence \cite{PressleySegal1986}, met from the physics side by the standard s-wave reduction of near-horizon field theory \cite{RW2005}. \emph{Scope declaration, once for the section:} every statement here concerns the s-wave-reduced sector; the four-dimensional lift is a point of contact at the end, flagged as assembly. One matter of notation belongs in the declaration. The paper carries three index sets for the boundary modes: the points of $E \cong \R \times S^{2}$ (Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-one-sided-completion-singularity}), the $\mathbb{Z}$-array of this section with weights $\omega_k = \kappa k$, and the Planck cells of the stretched singularity (Step 6a). Axiom (C1) names the identification of the first with the third; the identification of the first with the second, the aggregation of $E$'s sphere factor into a single s-wave array with a filled sea, is likewise an identification and not a theorem, made here as part of the s-wave reduction and flagged as such.
