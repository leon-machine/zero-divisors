## formal
By Proposition \claimref{standard-model.equal-weight-module}(2), ``the Dirac elements commute with the condensate'' says no more than ``the condensate removes the neutrino's Dirac entry'', so it does not derive that removal. The paper uses it as a declared premise, and only along the heavy family direction. \emph{Premise (EW):} in a family basis in which the symmetric part of $M_{u}$ is diagonal with $(M_{u})_{33} = m_{t}$, $(M_{\nu})_{33} = 0$; since $h$ has no diagonal, this is $4w_{u}f_{33} = m_{t}$. Applied in every family direction the premise would make $M_{u}$ proportional to $f$ and every light neutrino massless, so it cannot hold beyond the heavy direction.
