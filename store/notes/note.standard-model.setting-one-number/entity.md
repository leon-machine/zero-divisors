## formal
The computation, in units $\hbar = c = 1$ with $G = M_{P}^{-2}$ and $H^{2} = \Lambda/3$: $G\Lambda = 3H^{2}/M_{P}^{2} = 3N^{-2}$. The gravitational coupling of matter at energy $E$ is $GE^{2} = (E/M_{P})^{2}$, and against a dimension-one current coupling (the dimension count of the action paper's weakness remark, \cite[Rem.~5.3]{ButlerT10}, at its own assembly level) the suppression at $E = H$ is $(H/M_{P})^{2} = N^{-2}$. The hierarchy: by Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-frozen-point-dictionary}, $\operatorname{Im} w = \nu \approx m/H = (m/M_{P}) \cdot (M_{P}/H) = (m/M_{P})\,N$, the approximation $\nu \approx m/H$ being exact up to $O(H/m)$ from $m^{2} = (\tfrac94 + \nu^{2})H^{2}$. The script checks all three as monomial identities in the two scales.
