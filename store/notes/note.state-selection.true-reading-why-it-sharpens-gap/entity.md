## formal
Theorem \claimref{singularity.descended-form-state-independent} should be read as deflationary, not triumphant. For a single fermionic mode, ``thermal'' is not a discriminating property: the state space of one mode, after the descent that the chain performs anyway, is one-dimensional, and every point of it is a Gibbs state. It follows that no part of the physical content of Step 4d lies in the \emph{shape} of the reduced density matrix. All of it lies in the number $\beta_k$, in the claim that $\beta_k$ is the same for all modes, and in the claim that its value is $2\pi/\kappa$. The gap is therefore not ``which class of states'', as the status line ``up to a state'' suggests; it is ``which point of a line, and why the same point at every mode''. Everything that follows is about that line.

This is a sharpening of the gap and not a closing of it, and it is worth being explicit that the sharpening cuts against a certain kind of rhetorical comfort: one cannot claim the Fermi-Dirac \emph{form} as a prediction of the chain, because the descent manufactures that form from any input whatsoever. What the chain can claim is the temperature, and only under the premises named below.
