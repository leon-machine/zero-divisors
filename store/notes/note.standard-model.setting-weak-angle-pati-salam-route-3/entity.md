## formal
Proposition \claimref{standard-model.space-symmetric-doublet-mass-forms-two} counts the symmetric doublet forms and is unaffected: these forms are antisymmetric, which is why they give no mass to a single family \cite[Prop.~8.1(iii)]{ButlerT13}. Like $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$, each contains the pair-sector current $B - L$, so the question Corollary \claimref{standard-model.coefficient-cannot-come-frozen-point} raises, how the pair sector enters the mass mechanism, applies to it unchanged.
