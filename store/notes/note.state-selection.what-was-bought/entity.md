## formal
Theorem \claimref{singularity.ascended-marginals-are-diagonal} replaces hypothesis (ii), a symmetry assumption about the state, with (P), a statement about which elements of the ledger are observable. It is strictly weaker: (ii) constrains the whole state, (P) constrains one complex number per mode. It is also more robust, and this matters for the chain: (ii) is an equilibrium condition, and the evaporating hole is not in equilibrium, while (P) holds throughout an arbitrary non-stationary history. Section \ref{sec:correlations} shows that this robustness is not a luxury but a necessity, because the exact equilibrium condition is provably too strong for what Steps 5 and 6b need.
