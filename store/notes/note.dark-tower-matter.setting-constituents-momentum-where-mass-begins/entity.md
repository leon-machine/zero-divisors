## formal
The commutant of the spin action is the commutant of the even algebra, since the bivectors generate $\Cl^{0}$ as an algebra; so U1's field and the field of this definition agree. The constructions are explicit: $e_{t}e_{a}$ squares to $+1$, and $\Cl^{0}(V)f$ has real dimension $2$, $4$, $8$ in dimensions three, four, five, which is minimal (Theorem \claimref{dark-tower-matter.constituents-field}); $\hf$ is a product of two or three commuting factors $\tfrac{1}{2}(1 + b)$ with $b$ an even blade of square $+1$. The script builds both by a search over commuting even blades (Part A).
