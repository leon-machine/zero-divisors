## formal
Everything in Proposition \claimref{lepton.content-constituent-space} rests on (C), and (C) is not a theorem. What makes it plausible is Fact \ref{fact:ladder}'s form, $Z^{i} \mapsto U^{i}{}_{j}Z^{j}$, which on bare conformal spinors (bare twistors) is the action of $A$ on a slot vector once; (C) extends that form to excited conformal spinors, and the extension is the premise. Nothing in (C) makes a slot a fermionic mode, and in $\Con$ every pair belongs to the conformal spinor in the slot; so (C) does not pay the second cost T12 lists for (S), a composite state space that does not record which pairs belong to which slot. It pays nothing for the composite because it says nothing about the composite.
