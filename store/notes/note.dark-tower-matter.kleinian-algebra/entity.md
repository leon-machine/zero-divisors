## formal
At $r = 0$ the constituent field is $\R$ (Theorem \claimref{dark-tower-matter.constituents-field}), and $\Cl^{0}(2,2) \cong \Mat_{2}(\R) \oplus \Mat_{2}(\R)$ has two inequivalent spinor ideals. There is no time direction, so no adjoint and no norm clause: a momentum bilinear in one element of each ideal is preserved by acting with $U$ on the elements of the first ideal and $U^{-T}$ on those of the second, for every $U \in GL(n,\R)$ (supplementary \file{STE}, checks C4, C5). Under H2 with this slot group the gauge algebra is the split form $\sll(2,\R) \oplus \sll(3,\R) \oplus \gl(1,\R)$, of dimension $12$, whose simple factors have indefinite Killing forms, signatures $(2,1)$ and $(5,3)$ (E5): there is no positive gauge-kinetic energy. T14's ``Kleinian copy of the Standard Model'' \cite[Cor.~3.11]{ButlerT14} needs a compact weak factor, which does not preserve that bilinear. Assembly level.
