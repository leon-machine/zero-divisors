## formal
The averaging in \eqref{eq:projector} is an approximation: it stands in for the interaction of quanta on differently oriented sheets, which T14 does not specify (Premise S2). The runs with all sheets parallel (Section \ref{sec:ics}) do not use the average. With the normals along the line of sight (\texttt{rule\_perp}) the subcluster fails as with random sheets; with the normals along the merger axis (\texttt{rule\_par}) the fluid slides through along the axis and the subcluster keeps a lensing peak but half its mass (Section \ref{sec:results}).
