## formal
Theorem \claimref{transport.one-generator-minimal-minimal-completions-classified} sharpens the disanalogy with Rule 1 into its correct form. In Rule 1 the signature increment $(+1,+1)$ is unique outright (Theorem \claimref{transport.minimality}). In Rule 2 the \emph{algebraic} completion is likewise unique (there is one fused algebra, $\Mat_2(\Cl^0(p,q))$), but its geometric realisations are two, and no fusion-internal criterion distinguishes them: the sign $\gamma$ is a genuine geometric datum with no algebraic counterpart. The clock positions differ ($d + 1$ and $d - 1$ mod 8, i.e.\ residues $2$ and $0$, or $6$ and $4$), yet the classification table of Theorem \claimref{transport.classification-real-clifford-algebras} is symmetric about the split residues (positions $0$ and $2$ are both real type, $4$ and $6$ both quaternionic), which is the table-level reflection of the isomorphism proved intrinsically in Theorem \claimref{transport.rule-2-ideal-fusion-sharp-form}(3). A canonical choice of $\gamma$, if one is ever wanted, must therefore be imported from structure outside the algebra (an orientation of the ambient geometry), and we record the fact here at the level of algebra only: \emph{the fusion is forced, the sign is free}.
