## formal
Sections \ref{sec:setup} and \ref{sec:degeneration}, and the completion of Section \ref{sec:ambient}, use nothing about $T^*M$ beyond its being a vector bundle with a smooth symmetric form: Hypotheses \claimref{sheaf.tameness}, Lemma \claimref{sheaf.invariant-form-t3}, the blade basis, the degeneration sheaf and the coupling construction all apply verbatim to a pair $(E, h)$ with $E$ any real vector bundle of rank $m$, producing Clifford sheaves of rank $2^m$ and one-sided completions of rank $2^{m+2}$. Lemma \claimref{sheaf.tame-wall-induction} needs exactly this generality: the first iteration supplies the induction with the ambient pair $(T, h)$, of rank $n+2$, rather than a cometric.
