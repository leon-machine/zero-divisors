## formal
The asymmetry of transport (total on the way up, lossy on the way down) is now made exact in the matrix picture of Theorem \claimref{transport.rule-1-morita-trivial}. Throughout, $P = f^*f$ and $1 - P = ff^*$ are the \emph{descent projectors}, the diagonal matrix units; they are the spectral projections $\tfrac12(1 \pm \sigma)$ of the involution $\sigma = f^*f - ff^*$, $\sigma^2 = 1$, canonically attached to the completed pair.
