## formal
This section uses imported physics (gauge theory in three and five dimensions, \claimref{rule2.imported-parity-anomaly} and \claimref{rule2.imported-five-dimensional-chern-simons-shift}, and the published dark-plasma bounds) and decimal estimates, labelled; every number is reproduced by Part K of the script or by the named supplementary check. Its decimal estimates take T14's particle model (Fact \claimref{dark-tower-matter.imported-unit-ball-rule}): the quanta's mass is the nucleon's and $d$ is this world's baryon spacing, $d_{b} \approx 0.98$ m. E alone fixes the mass density and leaves both the number of quanta and $d$ free, so a statement below that uses a number density or the value of $d$ is conditional on that model. The five-dimensional statements of Computation \claimref{dark-tower-matter.five-dimensional-towers}(i), (ii) and (iv) need only $LT \gg 1$; the radiation caps of (iii) scale with $LT$.
