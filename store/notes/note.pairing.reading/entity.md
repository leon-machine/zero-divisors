## formal
By Fact \claimref{pairing.imported-null-flags} the vacuum ray is the null direction and the orbit of vacua is the celestial sphere; Proposition \claimref{pairing.stabiliser-parabolic-algebra-null-direction} is the statement, in the series' vocabulary, that the stabiliser of a point of the celestial sphere in the Lorentz algebra is the parabolic subalgebra of that null direction, and that the tangent of the sphere at $\ell$ is $\ell^{\perp}/\ell \otimes \C$, the transverse plane. What is the note's own is the functional $\pv$ in the Clifford conventions of the series and the next proposition, which says what $\pv$ is on the Fock module.
