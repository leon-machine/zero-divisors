## formal
At observation level, and flagged as such: minimal coupling is on this reading a holonomy constraint rather than an ansatz, the gauge fields of the Standard Model algebra being exactly the freedom to rotate the pair hierarchy independently from point to point. The gauging of the internal factors is Yang-Mills, not MacDowell-Mansouri, by Fact \ref{fact:twokinds}: the internal forces are conformal-level, gravity alone is pre-metric, and the $\theta$-terms are what survives the singularity. The coupling constants $g_{i}$ are normalisations this proposition does not determine; the numerical layer of Section \ref{sec:running} takes them up. Under Hypothesis (S) the hierarchy bundle would have fibre $\Lambda^{\bullet}(\C^{2} \oplus W)$ and the split-and-number-preserving structure group $U(2) \times U(3)$, with the thirteenth generator settled in Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor}(3); the proposition as stated is the tensor state space's.
