## formal
The ambient signature at rung two is $(4,6)$, whose spin group is the noncompact $\mathrm{Spin}(4,6)$; the group in Theorem \claimref{rungs.rung-spinor-module-mixings}(3) is the compact $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$. They are two real forms of one complex group sharing the module, and the compact one is the real form fixed by the $*$-structure of the tower (Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-tower-one-qubit-per-rung}), under which the ladder pairs are mutually adjoint, $v_{j}^{*} = w_{j}$: that adjointness is the Hermitian structure of Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-fock-representation-bogoliubov-group}, and the Bogoliubov group preserving it is the compact one. The $*$-structure is defined once the tower's frames are fixed, as T3 fixes them, so "fixed by" means fixed given the frames, a choice of maximal compact subgroup made once for the whole series rather than a consequence of the relations alone. The pattern is already present one rung down, where the ambient real form is the conformal $SU(2,2) \cong \mathrm{Spin}(2,4)$ (Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-spinor-identification-witt-data-line}) while the pair-mixing group is the unitary group $U(3)$ of $W$ for the same adjointness, inside the compact three-mode Bogoliubov group. A grand-unification group must be compact, and the tower supplies the compact form for the same reason it supplies $U(3)$.
