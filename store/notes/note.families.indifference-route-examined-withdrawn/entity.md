## formal
The natural candidate explanation of the angle is an equal split of the vacuum norm between the $S_{3}$ singlet and doublet by indifference, giving $\cos^{2}\theta = \tfrac12$, exactly Koide. The reading of Fact \claimref{families.imported-indifference-instrument} closes this route: the instrument equates weights along symmetry orbits and forbids uncaused selections; it does not equate norms between inequivalent irreducible components, which no symmetry relates. Applied to face space it says what Proposition \claimref{families.alignment-computed-thirteenth-refutation-democracy-one} already extracted: the uncaused vacuum lies wholly in the singlet. The route is withdrawn, and what survives is a measurement: the physical $\sqrt{m}$ vector splits its norm between singlet and doublet as $941.55 : 941.55$, equality to seven parts in $10^{6}$, an order-one, exactly tuned, \emph{caused} breaking whose cause is the open target. The breaking is small in $m$ and large in $\sqrt{m}$, compatible because masses are squares.
