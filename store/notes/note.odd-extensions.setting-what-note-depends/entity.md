## formal
The note makes no prediction; no observation can refute its identifications, and the only observational input it uses is the sign of $\Lambda$, which selects which branch describes this universe (Remark \claimref{note.odd-extensions.what-says-what-it-does-not}). What it claims is a set of identifications, each proved in the note from imported results, and this section says which imported result each identification depends on and what falls with it.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(D1)] \emph{The frozen point.} Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.are-ascents-pair-signed}, Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.translation-bivectors-frozen-point} and all of Sections \ref{sec:soldering}, \ref{sec:run}, \ref{sec:doors} and \ref{sec:lambdawall} rest on the dark-energy paper's frozen point (Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-frozen-point}): the parallel scale tractor of norm $\Lambda/3$ with the Stelle-West vector as the fifth direction of $I^{\perp}$. Without it the algebra of Section \ref{sec:pair} is a statement about an arbitrary vector of the rung's hyperbolic plane with no physical referent.
\item[(D2)] \emph{The soldered residue.} Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.belongs-tower} and Corollaries \claimref{odd-extensions.two-readings-which-which} and \claimref{odd-extensions.not-coupling-between-towers} rest on the dark-matter paper's definition of the residue as the signature of the tangent image (Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-copy-theorem-soldered-residue}). A definition admitting unsoldered directions into the residue would reopen both questions the section closes and, by the same stroke, the exactness of the dark-matter paper's superselection.
\item[(D3)] \emph{The copy theorem's inputs.} Corollary \claimref{odd-extensions.copy-theorems-blind-spot} and Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.two-towers-one-curved-universe}(ii) rest on every input of the copy theorem being a spin-group object (Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-copy-theorem-soldered-residue}), and Remark \claimref{note.odd-extensions.one-operator-sees-difference}'s conclusion that the mirror sector is observationally identical to this one rests on Fact \ref{fact:goldstone}: a derived quantity depending on the bare fifth generator would need a physical coupling to the pure-gauge direction, which that Fact excludes.
\item[(D4)] \emph{The Goldstone proposition.} Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.boost-dilation-ratio-gauge}(iii) rests on the dark-energy paper's $(I,V)$ sector being pure gauge (Fact \ref{fact:goldstone}); a dynamical fixing of the dilation gauge would make the ratio $b/a$ physical.
\item[(D5)] \emph{The residue of a conformal class.} Remark \claimref{note.odd-extensions.holographic-coincidence}'s statement that the boundary of $\mathrm{AdS}_{4}$ has residue $7$ and lies in a tower distinct from the bulk's rests on Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-conformal-completion}, on Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-towers-clock}, and on the signature of a conformal class being well defined. A definition of the residue requiring a chosen scale would withdraw it, and with it the remark's statement of the difference between the two frameworks.
\item[(D6)] \emph{The sheaf paper's hypotheses.} Section \ref{sec:lambdawall} rests on the sheaf paper's tameness conditions and conservation theorem as stated (Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-tame-walls-exceptional-algebra-residue}); its one observation is about the hypothesis of that theorem and stands or falls with the theorem's statement.
\end{enumerate}
Sections \ref{sec:single} to \ref{sec:clock} prove their algebra in the note, importing Facts \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-representatives-dimension-nearest-four-types} and \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-towers-clock} for it; their identifications with the frozen point, in Propositions \claimref{odd-extensions.translation-bivectors-frozen-point} and \claimref{odd-extensions.four-extensions-are-four-odd-towers}, rest on Facts \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-conformal-completion} and \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-frozen-point} as (D1) records, and the copy theorem's inputs in Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.twistor-algebra}(iii), with the superselection read in Remarks \claimref{note.odd-extensions.what-says-what-it-does-not} and \claimref{note.odd-extensions.no-mechanism}, on Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-copy-theorem-soldered-residue}.
