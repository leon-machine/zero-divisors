## formal
Every computational identity above is verified by the deposited script \texttt{verify\_three\_routes.py}, in exact rational arithmetic against the series' shared library \texttt{GA.py}, with negative controls. The coverage: the two-mode computations behind Theorem \claimref{three-routes.join-disqualified-wrong-stalks} ($\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma_x)$ fixing $v_y$, $\operatorname{Ad}(\sigma_y)$ negating it, the meet equal to the joint diagonal and to the range of the pinching, the join containing every generator); the sheaf axioms on a three-point model of the even subbundle (locality, unique gluing from an overlapping cover, the join's stalk defect exhibited); the full Section \ref{sec:nogo} block on the Schwarzschild boundary data of Fact \claimref{three-routes.imported-boundary-gram-ladder} at two rational masses (radical membership $Gk = 0$, the solve of Lemma \claimref{three-routes.radical-gauge-closes-solve} with its normalisation and its uniqueness, the intertwiner identity $\Phi^{\mathsf{T}} h_u \Phi = h_{u+v}$ for two independent $v$, the ladder relations in both completions, unipotence, the automorphism identity $\theta^{\mathsf{T}} h_u \theta = h_u$ with $\theta \neq \id$ and $\theta(k) = k$, the translation action on two candidates, and a wrong-normalisation control that breaks the intertwiner); and the Section \ref{sec:hopping} block (the finite support lemma as the exact statement that commuting with the point projectors forces block diagonality, the hopping matrix failing it, $d\Gamma$ of a block map conserving both occupations, $d\Gamma$ of a hop moving $(0,1)$ to $(1,0)$, and the controlled-$Z$ generator conserving both). The script exits nonzero on any failure; no floating point is used anywhere.
