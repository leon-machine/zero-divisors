## formal
Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.two-roots-are-chord-arc-quarter} reads the rule as ``our world sources from the five-dimensional tower's matter between the equator and one pole, counted flat,'' and two contrasts are in play. Chord against the sphere's own measure, whose $\cos^{4}$ weighting gives $\tfrac{3\pi}{8}$ in Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.two-reductions}(i), is I with S: one pole rather than two is S (Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.what-s-uses-what-it-does}), counting flat is I. Chord against arc, the two roots of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.total-function-thickness}(iii), R2 decides: its cells are unit balls of the tower's flat space, whose measure along the added direction is the chord.
