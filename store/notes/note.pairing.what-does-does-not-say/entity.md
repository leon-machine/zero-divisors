## formal
Proposition \claimref{pairing.displacement-pairs-point-mode-vacuum-parallel} is the incidence relation of the conformal spinor space (twistor incidence) in the vocabulary of pairing classes, an import with a reading: the point mode is annihilated by the point and created by $\ninf$, moving the point moves its vacuum, and the amount is the displacement's null components. It is stated for the flat model, where the laboratory paper states it, and not for the singular boundary, where the completion attaches an independent ladder pair at each point of the exceptional set and the tractor connection on the collar is not constructed in the series. Whether the transport along the singular boundary pairs the boundary mode at a point with the fibre modes there, as the flat model's translation pairs the point mode, is a successor problem, and this note does not claim it.
