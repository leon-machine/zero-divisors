## formal
Theorem \claimref{standard-model.gauge-algebra-minimal-massive-composite}'s rule takes the automorphisms of $\C^{2} \otimes \C^{3}$ that preserve the partition. Proposition \claimref{standard-model.automorphisms-preserve-slot-pair-split-are} applies it to the canonical anticommutation relations of the five-mode state space and preserves the slot/pair split there without preserving the mode number. The reading is canonical, since Pati-Salam is exactly the stabiliser of the rung parity, but it is a reading: the rule as stated for the tensor hierarchy does not select it, and the paper registers it as such. Pati-Salam unification, with lepton number as a fourth colour, is Pati and Salam's \cite{PatiSalam1974}. What the framework adds is the reason for this group: it is what the composite's own slot/pair split preserves, with the slot mixing as the weak $\su(2)$ and the full or empty pair system as the fourth colour.
