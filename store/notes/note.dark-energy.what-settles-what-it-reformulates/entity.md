## formal
Two things are settled. The symmetric form $M$ of Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.walls-free-sector} is the free sector of a wall of the soldering form, the degeneration dual to the sheaf paper's, and it is not the completion's sector at the sheaf paper's wall; the claim that the sheaf paper's wall is a wall of the soldering form is withdrawn. And the completion's ladder pair does appear in the dynamics at the sheaf paper's wall, but as gauge, not as a field: the wall opens exactly one new gauge generator per point, the dilation of the dying line, and that generator is the boost of the ladder plane $\mathrm{span}(k, k^*)$. The half-power weight that the sheaf paper's residue theorem assigns to $k$ ($\mathcal{L}^{\otimes 2} \cong N^*\Sigma$) is a particular gauge choice for this generator, the one that makes the exceptional algebra nondegenerate. What is reformulated is the dynamical question: whether the equations demand the completion becomes, at the sheaf paper's wall, the question of how the MacDowell-Mansouri datum fails to extend, and Section \ref{subsec:kasner} answers it for the Schwarzschild wall: the datum's connection has a simple pole, and the residues of that pole are the weights from which the completion is built.
