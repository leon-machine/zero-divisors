## formal
The theorem is a statement about \emph{vector-level} completions: nondegenerate quadratic spaces extending $(W,B)$. It does not assert that $\Cl(p{+}r,q{+}r)$ is minimal among all associative algebras receiving $\Cl(p,q,r)$; that stronger, representation-independent statement is the subject of the sharpenings of \cite[\S4.6]{ButlerT1}, proved there for a nondegenerate base, where the completion adjoins a single hyperbolic plane, by conformal Gram methods. It also does not promise that individual degenerate \emph{elements} acquire inverses in the completion: they cannot, since a square-zero element is invertible in no unital extension \cite[Lem.~2.1]{ButlerT1}. What the completion delivers is stated ideal-theoretically in Proposition \claimref{conformal.nilpotent-ideal-becomes-unit-ideal}.
