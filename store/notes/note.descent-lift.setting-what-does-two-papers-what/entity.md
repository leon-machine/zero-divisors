## formal
\textbf{One fact.} The root paper proves that the descent is one-way at the level of algebras: no homomorphism returns. The singularity paper premises that the completion supplies no generator off-diagonal in the joint number basis, and its own envelope corollary shows that one pairing rotation is what the return of its boundary modes needs. Proposition \claimref{descent-lift.within-supplied-dynamics-descent-has-no} joins the two: inside the dynamics the framework supplies, the descent cannot be lifted, and the missing ingredient is a pairing rotation, on the adjoined rungs or on the modes themselves. The one-way door and the pairing exclusion are the same statement seen from the two sides of the descent.
