## formal
Every factor of Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.mirror-share-assembled} is a ratio between two towers, so the share is one whatever content $X$ the Standard-Model chain produces from Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-inputs-standard-model-chain}, correct or not: the reversal carries $X$ to $X$. It is exposed instead to that list's completeness and to P2: a non-invariant input off the list breaks the copy, priced by Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.mirror-share-one-how-fragile}(ii). The record's test is refutation item 6.
