## formal
If the missing statement of Remark \claimref{note.rungs.where-leaves-r} is not supplied, the series' own rule for a structural premise that resists derivation is to register it at Tier C, with its predictions and its kill conditions priced. The registration, in the form T12 uses for its stances:

\begin{quote}
\emph{Registered premise (S), equivalently (R).} The internal state space of the minimal massive composite is the five-mode Fock space $\Lambda^{\bullet}(W \oplus \C^{2})$, the two-rung transport of the twistor completion, with the gauge group below the intermediate scale acting through Theorem \claimref{rungs.rung-spinor-module-mixings}(2) on its determinant-one part (the thirteenth generator, anomaly-free in the action the state space carries, being broken at that scale, Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-results-t12-used-registration}) and chirality the parity of the mode number.
\end{quote}

\noindent\emph{What it predicts.} (i) Exactly one generation per composite, the sixteen of $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$: quark and lepton doublets, four singlets, the fourth at hypercharge zero (Fact \ref{fact:sixteen}); that the composite carries no further chiral state is the registration's own reading of "the state space is this Fock space", not a clause of Fact \ref{fact:sixteen}. (ii) The $\mathbb{Z}_{6}$ charge lattice of T12 Theorem 4.2; this is unconditional in T12 and is not a test of (S), and is listed because (S) must respect it and does (Fact \ref{fact:sixteen}). (iii) Unification into $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ with $\sin^{2}\theta_{W} = 3/8$ at the unification scale, hence, with the desert dead (Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-results-t12-used-registration}), a change in the running between the weak and unification scales: partner-like content, or an intermediate symmetry such as T12's Pati-Salam route, on which $\sin^{2}\theta_{W}$ is an input. (iv) Baryon and lepton number violation by the thirty-two generators of $\mathfrak{so}(10)$ outside $\uu(2) \oplus \uu(3)$ (Proposition \claimref{rungs.generators-outside-gauge-algebra}): twelve that move an excitation between a rung and a pair (the leptoquark directions of $\uu(5)$), and twenty that create or annihilate two modes at once.

\noindent\emph{What kills it.} (a) A confirmed chiral fermion outside the sixteen per generation, or a fourth colour singlet with nonzero hypercharge (the anomaly conditions would then admit a family, Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-results-t12-used-registration}). (b) A fractionally charged colour singlet, off the lattice; this kills the exclusion stance of T12 Theorem 4.2 whether or not (S) holds, so it is a kill shared with T12, not one that discriminates (S). (c) A measured $\sin^{2}\theta_{W}$ at a scale where the three couplings can be shown, with the observed spectrum plus any partner content compatible with the sixteen, not to meet: the two-loop run with thresholds, T12 successor problem (2). (d) A proton lifetime bound incompatible with any $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ breaking scale allowed by (iii); the framework does not yet fix that scale, so this condition is priced as inherited from the $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ literature rather than sharpened. (e) Internally: a proof that the composite's internal algebra closes on the three pair modes alone, with the slots as labels (the tensor ledger reading of Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-ladder-automorphism-group-no-lepton}), which would refute (R) directly and leave (S) without its only structural reading.

\noindent\emph{Level.} A structural premise with priced kill conditions is Tier C by the series' definition; T12 is declared Tier B, and if (S) is registered rather than derived the sections that rest on it (T12 Proposition 5.3(2), Theorem 5.5(1), the consistency clause of (3), the last clause of Proposition 5.6, Proposition 5.8, Remark 5.9, and Proposition 6.1's values $3/8$ and $3/4$, which are taken over a generation containing the lepton; without the lepton the ratio is $9/26$) carry Tier C and the abstract should say so. The sections that do not rest on it, the algebra, the global form, the converse, the quark doublet, the singlet spectrum as a computation on the single-twistor Fock space, the uniqueness arithmetic given a charge list, the trace ratios as arithmetic over any tabulated content with $9/10$ over the fibre, and the run, stay at Tier B.
