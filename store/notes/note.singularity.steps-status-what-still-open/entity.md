## formal
Collecting: hypothesis (i) is Theorem \claimref{singularity.purity-output-partner-commutant}; hypothesis (ii) is not needed for the form at all (Theorem \claimref{singularity.descended-form-state-independent}), and its per-mode content follows from (P) (Theorem \claimref{singularity.ascended-marginals-are-diagonal}); hypothesis (iii) follows from (R) (Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-discharge-hypothesis-iii}, whose gluing condition is stated by evaluation and does not presuppose a modular group); and the value of $\TH$ follows from (R) with the period computed in Proposition \claimref{singularity.euclidean-section-period}, and provably from nothing internal (Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-rigidity-temperature-not-algebra-intrinsic}, Proposition \claimref{singularity.why-calibration-cannot-be-internal}). The exact equilibrium condition (G) is not a premise of this step: it is examined and found too strong for the evaporating boundary modes (Theorem \claimref{singularity.exact-equilibrium-over-determines-boundary-modes}), the class the chain uses is Corollary \claimref{singularity.chains-state-class-stability}'s, and the one place the chain later adopts the (G) member is Step 4e's (Q), for the equilibrium reading of the Planck sector alone. The status line in Table \ref{tab:chain} is accordingly \emph{proved from (P), (R)} rather than \emph{up to a state}, where (R) is the KMS condition at the geometric period, imposed mode by mode; the premises of the later steps, (Q) in Step 4e, (C1) to (C3) in Step 6a and (L), (U), (C), (I), (L$_E$) in Step 6b, are named where those steps are proved.

Four things remain open and are recorded as such. (a) (R) is an import, and Proposition \claimref{singularity.why-calibration-cannot-be-internal} shows it cannot be removed by strengthening the framework's statements at the singular boundary; the open route is the spectrum-condition restatement of Remark \claimref{note.singularity.cost-r-dictionary}, which belongs with T9 \cite{ButlerT9}. (b) (L$_E$) is a statement about which generators the localisation theorems supply; the class-relative no-go is Theorem \claimref{singularity.class-relative-no-go-no-supplied} (Appendix \ref{app:threeroutes}): no supplied generator hops, the supplied class being the second quantisations of function-linear maps of the mode line bundle and the products of the parity sections, an ultralocal class of multiplication operators. What would settle the premise outright is Remark \claimref{note.singularity.declared-residual-size}'s residual question, whether the physical dynamics on $E$ carries a differential or a pairing term; the necessity witness of Corollary \claimref{singularity.envelope-endpoint-why-boundary-mode-locality} is itself a pairing rotation, which the theorem's criterion does not exclude; Proposition \claimref{singularity.l-stabiliser-tangent} reads the two residual types in $\mathfrak{so}(2\nu)$. (c) Theorems \claimref{singularity.kms-uniqueness-factor}, \claimref{singularity.rate-rigidity-temperature-not-algebra-intrinsic} and \claimref{singularity.purity-output-partner-commutant} and Proposition \claimref{singularity.two-kms-functionals-sign} are proved in the matrix factor, which is where this step works, and their multi-mode applications, Corollary \claimref{singularity.universality-temperature-under-g} and Theorem \claimref{singularity.exact-equilibrium-over-determines-boundary-modes}, extend them to finite collections of modes factor by factor, and where Section \ref{sec:photons} invokes them on the $\mathbb{Z}$-array the reading is through finite mode subsets, on which every quantity involved is defined, no global entropy being claimed on the infinite array; in the field-theoretic setting the horizon algebra is a type III$_1$ factor, the Gibbs form is unavailable, and the KMS condition must be used directly, in the simplex form of Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-general-form}, with \cite{KayWald1991} as the template. (d) Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-discharge-hypothesis-iii} treats the sphere directions of $E$ as inert, correct for Schwarzschild by symmetry; the corresponding statement over a rotating horizon, where $\kappa$ is still constant but the Killing flow is not the static one, is untouched, and belongs with the Kerr programme of \cite{ButlerT4, ButlerT6}.
