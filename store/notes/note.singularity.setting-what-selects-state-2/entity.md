## formal
The chain is entitled to (P) for a reason it supplies itself: the boundary modes form a CAR algebra, one pair per point of $E$ (Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-one-sided-completion-singularity}; the tower limit \cite[Thm.~5.6]{ButlerT3} is its algebraic model), its grading is the Clifford grading of Definition \claimref{obj.singularity.grading-parity-automorphism}, and by Theorem \claimref{singularity.descent-parity-average} its even part is, mode by mode, exactly the metric-level algebra (the multi-mode metric level is the joint-diagonal algebra, whose licence is Proposition \claimref{singularity.p-reduced-metric-level-reading-many}, one parity per point rather than the product of all of them). What (P) asserts is that the odd sector carries no expectation values, which by that theorem is the same as asserting that the ascended and descended readings agree \emph{mode by mode}. That is a much weaker demand than invariance of the whole state under the boost flow, and it is not a choice of state at all: it is a statement about which elements of the boundary mode space are observables.
