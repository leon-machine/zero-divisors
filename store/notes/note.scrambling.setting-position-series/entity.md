## formal
This paper is T8 of the series. \emph{It relies on seven predecessors:} the primer T0 \cite{ButlerT0} and the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} for the completed-pair language, the transport principle, and the standard of proof; the tower paper T3 \cite{ButlerT3} for capacity as Witt index (Fact \ref{fact:capacity}) and for the iteration corollary the no-attic route runs on (Assembly \claimref{scrambling.no-attic-route}); the sheaf paper T4 \cite{ButlerT4} for the wall results that localise one ladder pair per point of the blown-up singular locus, corank one under the forced weights (Fact \ref{fact:capacity}); the singularity paper T5 \cite{ButlerT5} for the counting axioms of the boundary modes, the causal epoch, the eviction dynamics, and the geometric normalisation (Facts \ref{fact:ledger} and \claimref{scrambling.imported-modes-modular-inventory}); the geometry paper T6 \cite{ButlerT6} for the curvature scale, the interior proper-time series, and the weighted completion, closed at Schwarzschild, on whose exceptional set the calibration this paper carries is to be read (Facts \ref{fact:curvature} and \ref{fact:capacity}, Remark \claimref{note.scrambling.density-factor-stated-full}); and the spectrum paper T7 \cite{ButlerT7} for the mode's modular inventory and the quasi-free calculus behind the I-state checks (Fact \claimref{scrambling.imported-modes-modular-inventory}, Proposition \claimref{scrambling.consistency-car-construction-i-states-insensitivity}).

\emph{Papers that rely on this one:} the singularity paper T5 \cite{ButlerT5} conditions its counting step on the epoch and density that this paper derives, quantifies, and converts into the single prediction $\nu = 1/16\pi$ in the 1-nat convention, $\nu = 1/(16\pi\ln 2)$ in T5's own capacity convention; the action paper T10 \cite{ButlerT10} imports the sphere theorem (Theorem \claimref{scrambling.principality-indifference-sphere}), with its indifference premise attached, and shares the sphere's orthogonality no-go (Proposition \claimref{scrambling.consistency-car-construction-i-states-insensitivity}); and the capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17} tabulates this paper's claims at their tiers.
