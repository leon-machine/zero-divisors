## formal
A limit statement must say which completion is meant: here no hidden choice is made. On the algebraic limit the C*-norm is unique (Lemma \claimref{tower.structure-tower-c-norms}: each stage finite-dimensional, UHF at the top), so ``the completed tower'' is unambiguous once complexified; the real algebraic limit itself is Theorem \claimref{tower.algebraic-limit-even-charge}, with no completion needed. What is genuinely a choice is the complexification, made because the CAR algebra and its Fock adjointness are complex notions; the real limit remembers strictly more (its real K-theory), which is part of what Question \claimref{q.tower.odd-charge} is about.
