## formal
Two readings of the sheet family are excluded, one by symmetry and one by the record. A fixed missing direction gives no clustering along it for $42\%$ of all matter, against the statistical isotropy of the matter power spectrum and the microwave background at the level of a few percent, and it is the uncaused selection P2 refuses. Orientations varying below observed scales give the isotropic average $g = \tfrac{2}{3}$, and that the spectra exclude directly. Computed with a Boltzmann code (Figure \ref{fig:boltzmann}; CLASS \cite{CLASS2011}, version 3.4, Planck 2018 parameters otherwise, the three-dimensional towers as a separate pressureless species responding at $g$ with the stress of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.reduced-response-gravity-needs-supporting-stress} carried so that the field equations stay consistent; the patch, inputs and outputs are deposited with the verification materials as a record), $49.7\%$ of the dark matter at $g = \tfrac{2}{3}$ gives $\sigma_{8} = 0.36$ against $0.81$, the lensing-potential spectrum at $28\%$ of the standard model's, and the temperature spectrum $17\%$ high at $\ell = 100$, against measurements at the percent level; the floor's $40.5\%$ and the exchanged assignment's $31.6\%$ give $\sigma_{8} = 0.42$ and $0.49$; and with half the dark matter in the towers a deficit $1 - g$ above about half a percent is excluded by the lensing amplitude alone. Both readings need the stress the series does not have. The reading it does have, $g = 1$, returns in the same code the standard model's temperature, polarisation, lensing and matter power spectra to four decimal places for the rule's composition (the towers as a separate species at full response), so at linear order the rule's dark sector is the standard model's. Within collapsed structures the three-dimensional towers differ from cold dark matter only by what the sheets add beyond collisionless gravity (Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.halo-built-collisionless-sheets-cold-dark}): the self-interaction of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.radiation-audit-what-theorem-closes-what}, which is the fraction test of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.total-function-thickness}(v), with the cross-section read per sheet (at composite strength a fluid, Section \ref{subsec:simulated}), and, on the deposit branch only, an in-plane free-streaming cutoff. None of this bears on the value $5.364$. The Boltzmann integration is floating point and is recorded, not checked by the script.
