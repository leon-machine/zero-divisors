## formal
Five, in decreasing order of urgency. (1) \emph{Produce or refute $\nu = 1/(16\pi\ln 2)$:} derive the residue $\kappa_c = 1/(4\ln 2)$, one boundary mode per Bekenstein-Mukhanov area quantum of horizon per modular period, from the structure of the boundary modes themselves, or refute (IC); the corank is settled at one \cite[\S 8.9]{ButlerT4}, the analysis of the geometry paper's weighted limit is closed at Schwarzschild \cite[\S 5.5]{ButlerT6}, exactness narrows the normalisation to two cutoff-free scales, $r_h$ or $r_* = M$ (Proposition \claimref{scrambling.exactness-excludes-cutoff-dependence}), one tick per period is (IC) read as a counting rule, and no recount with a rational residue ($k = 0$) reaches $\kappa_c$, nor, conditionally on the expected algebraic independence of $\pi$ and $\ln 2$, any recount with $k \neq 0$ (Proposition \claimref{scrambling.no-recount-reaches-quarter}); Corollary \claimref{scrambling.integer-holographic-form-identification} states the target in its sharpest form ($N_{\mathrm{epoch}} = \SBH$), and Proposition \claimref{scrambling.per-slice-verification-two-failed-aggregations}'s failed aggregations mark two excluded routes. (2) \emph{The Kerr ring count:} repeat Section \ref{sec:count} over the ring's codimension-two, duality-fibred exceptional structure \cite[Conj.~6.6]{ButlerT6}, which will scale differently with mass and spin, testing the count against the known angular-momentum dependence of Kerr entropy. (3) \emph{Chaotic saturation beyond free-fermion spreading:} upgrade Assembly \claimref{scrambling.spreading-law-chaos-bound-saturated} from operator-spreading to genuine out-of-time-order chaos, or show that the quadratic tier of the boundary modes is where the framework's claim ends. (4) \emph{The slice-to-epoch aggregation:} a first-principles rule taking Proposition \claimref{scrambling.per-slice-verification-two-failed-aggregations}(1)'s slice count to the epoch count, replacing the two failed naive rules. (5) \emph{Catalogue currency:} the confrontation of Record \claimref{scrambling.catalogue-confronted} is with a moving record, and every new observing run is a fresh test; the stance is standing, not archival.
