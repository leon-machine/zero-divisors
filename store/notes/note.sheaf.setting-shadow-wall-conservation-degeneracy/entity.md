## formal
The one-sided completion is one-sided by necessity, and the necessity has two layers. The first is absolute: a pure signature obstruction that rules out \emph{every} completion agreeing with the standard form away from the wall, whatever its construction. The second is quantitative: within the coupling ansatz, the determinant factor $F$ of Remark \claimref{note.sheaf.only-product-coupling-constants-matters} locates the displaced degeneration exactly, and for good couplings the displaced wall is itself tame, so the construction applies to its own output.
