## formal
Proposition \claimref{rungs.ladder-group-rung-mixing-group-one} is exact, and it is the one place where the tower and the Standard-Model paper's objects are literally the same thing: the ladder's $U(n)$, derived from momentum and helicity preservation, and the tower's rung-mixing group, derived from the completion, agree on $\C^{n} \otimes \CS$, $\CS$ the conformal spinor space (twistor space). But the identification is on that sector only. The ladder theorem says nothing about the other sectors ($j \neq 1$, or $k$ odd), and the tower says nothing about momentum. To say that the composite's state space is the whole module is a hypothesis, stated next.
