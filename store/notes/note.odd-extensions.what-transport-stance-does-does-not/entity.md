## formal
The programme reads a zero divisor as a signpost rather than a prohibition, and Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.are-ascents-pair-signed}(iii) puts $\Lambda = 0$ on the wall. It is tempting to read the stance as saying that a universe does not sit on the wall, hence $\Lambda \neq 0$. Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.transport-constrains-frozen-point-only-through} is what the stance actually delivers: the transport off the wall is the passage from $\Cl(1,3;1)$ to the rung, which is the same rung for every $\Lambda$, and it says nothing about where in the rung's new plane the frozen point lies. The dark-energy paper's finding that the norm is a parameter of the laws under the action as written is the dynamical form of the same statement, and this note does not improve on it.
