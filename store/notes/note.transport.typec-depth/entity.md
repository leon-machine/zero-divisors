## formal
The proof just given settles a point worth recording: for $n$ odd the pseudoscalar is central, so \emph{no inner automorphism} of $\Cl(p,q)$ exchanges the two ideals; the exchanging symmetry is the grade involution, which is outer on the split algebra, and its realisation by an inner automorphism is available only after the one-generator ascent of Rule 2, where a new generator anticommuting with $\omega$ exists. This sharpens the sense in which Rule 2 is a \emph{repair}: the ascent does not merely contain the fused algebra, it renders inner the very symmetry whose absence defined the obstruction. The observation will be part of the minimality argument in Section \ref{sec:rules}.
