## formal
The procedure is run end to end, on the case that motivated the programme, in the series' gravitational paper \cite{ButlerT5}, whose chain of steps mirrors Table \ref{tab:algorithm} row for row. It is worth carrying the outline through the paper as a running example. In it: the failure is located at the centre of the Schwarzschild solution, where the light cones fold onto a single direction (Step 1); the degenerating direction is null and the algebra $\Cl(1,3)$ is simple, with $d \equiv 6 \bmod 8$, so the diagnosis is type N and type C is unavailable (Step 2, by Theorem \claimref{transport.existence-classification-type-c} and Example \claimref{note.transport.cl-1-3-physical-case-n}); Rule 1 gives $\Cl(1,3) \to \Cl(2,4)$, the conformal algebra of spacetime, forced (Step 3); the transported division is total there (Step 4); and the descent is lossy in exactly the way Step 5 states, which is where that paper's physical content begins. The example is worth keeping in view for one reason in particular: the answer to the forbidden division is not a number in spacetime, and the algorithm says so in advance rather than after the fact.
