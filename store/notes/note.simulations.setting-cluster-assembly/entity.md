## formal
Eight progenitors with $M_{200} = 2 \times 10^{14}\ \Msun$ and $c = 5$, $4000$ particles each, are placed at random inside $1.5$ Mpc, at least $700$ kpc apart, with random velocities of dispersion $150$ km/s, and the whole is set at rest at the origin. They collapse and merge into one cluster, $2.3 \times 10^{15}\ \Msun$ in all with $M_{200} \approx 2.0 \times 10^{15}\ \Msun$ at the end, followed to $t = 2.0$ ($1.96$ Gyr). Each composition in Table \ref{tab:halo} is run with two random seeds, $11$ and $23$; \texttt{rule1} is run once, with seed $11$ (projected ellipticity $0.17$, $0.72$ of CDM's with the same seed), and is not tabulated.
