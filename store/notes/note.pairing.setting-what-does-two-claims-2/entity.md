## formal
\textbf{Verdict on Claim G1.} Not closed. K1.1 triggers: the two frames share a vacuum (Proposition \claimref{pairing.two-frames-share-vacuum-selected-transport}(i)). K1.2 triggers: the class of the one transport the framework selects is zero (Proposition \claimref{pairing.two-frames-share-vacuum-selected-transport}(ii)), and no single holonomy carries a mode weight. K1.3 triggers by the singularity paper's own Proposition 7.21(3): the Hawking pairing needs the Euclidean rotor, whose generator is not in the real algebra, while every holonomy is a real rotor. The registered claim ``some path or loop'' was, as worded, satisfiable by drawing a loop of any prescribed area for each mode; Proposition \claimref{pairing.two-frames-share-vacuum-selected-transport}(iii) shows what such a family of loops would have to do, one rotor per mode, and nothing in the framework selects one, its mode weights being the s-wave weights of an imported premise \cite[Prem.~8.1]{ButlerT5} and its only selected transport the $t$-line. Two limits of this verdict are recorded. The representation of a Hawking state on the two modes of one fibre in (iii) is an analogy this note constructs, not the singularity paper's pairing, which is between a boundary mode and its partner across points. And the route by which the mode weight does enter a transport, the Killing flow continued through the Euclidean period, is exactly K1.3 and is not a holonomy of the real connection. P1.1 and P1.2 held; the regularity premise remains where the singularity paper put it.
