## formal
Proposition \claimref{correspondence.local-twistor-transport-ascended-spinor-connection}(3) proves the block form, the uniqueness of the summands up to two constants whose product is invariant, and the value of that invariant on the Clifford side. The remaining step, that Penrose's composite $-\varepsilon_A{}^B\mathrm{P}_{CC'DA'}\omega^D$ equals $\tfrac12 c(e_a)c(\mathrm{P}(e_c))$ under Levi-equivariant identifications, is the statement that the tensor written $\mathrm{P}_{ab}$ in \cite{PenroseRindler86} (there $\Phi_{ab} - \Lambda g_{ab}$) is the Schouten tensor of Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-tractor-connection-local-twistor-transport}(i) with a definite sign fixed by their curvature conventions. The paper imports this identification rather than re-deriving the curvature conventions of \cite{PenroseRindler86}; the algebraic side is proved and script-checked, and nothing later depends on the sign.
