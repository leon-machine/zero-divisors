## formal
\textbf{Halo shapes.} The assembled cluster's centre is found by shrinking spheres on all mass. Its three-dimensional axis ratios come from the iterative reduced inertia tensor, $I_{ab} = \sum m\,x_{a}x_{b}/r_{\mathrm{ell}}^{2}$ over the ellipsoid of the current shape, inside $300$ and $1000$ kpc. The projected ellipticity $1 - b/a$ is the same construction in two dimensions inside a projected $500$ kpc, averaged over the three coordinate projections; this is the quantity of Fact \claimref{simulations.imported-cluster-shapes}.
