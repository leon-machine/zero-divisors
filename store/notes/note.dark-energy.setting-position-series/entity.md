## formal
This paper is T10 of the series. \emph{It relies on eight papers:} the primer T0 \cite{ButlerT0} for the Clifford and bivector language; the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} for the standard of proof and the Fact (imported) discipline every result here is held to; the sheaf paper T4 \cite{ButlerT4} for the continuation of the ambient structure across a tame wall and the corank-one radical (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-walls-sphere-theorem}); the singularity paper T5 \cite{ButlerT5} for the s-wave current algebra and, under the singularity paper's premise (Q), its thermal state (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-s-wave-current-algebra}); the geometry paper T6 \cite{ButlerT6} for the frozen point, the tractor connection and the exterior exactness (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-frozen-point-geometry}, Assembly \claimref{dark-energy.lift-inherits-no-go-steps-itemised}); the Hawking paper T7 \cite{ButlerT7} for the finite-window quantification of the current algebra; the entropy paper T8 \cite{ButlerT8} for the sphere theorem that carries the sphere step (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-walls-sphere-theorem}); and, later in the series, the standard-model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} for the rescaling argument that the norm is not kinematic (Fact \ref{fact:notkinematic}), with no circularity, since that argument uses only the conformal class and the pair structure.

\emph{Papers that rely on this one:} the mass paper T11 \cite{ButlerT11} imports the action theorem (Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}) for its gravity assembly; the standard-model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} shares the two-kinds theorem (Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.gravity-pre-metric-forces-degenerate-exactly}), uses the action's single coupling, and reads its classification's initial-data clause against Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.under-action-written-norm-parameter-laws}; the dark-matter paper T14 \cite{ButlerT14} imports the acoustic no-go and the $w = -1$ registration (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.vacuum-region-registration-condition}); the entropy paper T8 \cite{ButlerT8} and the Hawking paper T7 \cite{ButlerT7} refer the engineering no-go and the lift's dynamics to this paper; and the capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17} tabulates this paper's claims at their tiers.
