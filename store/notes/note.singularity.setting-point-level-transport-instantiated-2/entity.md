## formal
What Rule 1 installs instead of a value is the pair \eqref{eq:car} itself: the canonical anticommutation relations of one fermionic mode. Nothing commuting satisfies these relations; objects obeying them are irreducibly operators on a two-state space. By the localisation of \cite[Cor.~4.5]{ButlerT4}, in the form the Schwarzschild composite gives it at the black hole \cite[Thm.~8.12]{ButlerT4} (reproduced verbatim, hypotheses included, as Facts \ref{fact:schwtame} and \claimref{singularity.imported-one-sided-completion-singularity} in Appendix \ref{app:imports}), exactly one such mode is at each point of the blown-up singular locus $E \cong \R \times S^2$, and nowhere else in the physical closure: the composite's corank-one theorem \cite[Thm.~8.7]{ButlerT4} makes the count one pair per point, not one per dying coordinate direction. Quantisation is demanded locally, exactly where the classical description divides by zero, and is output rather than input: no quantisation procedure is imposed anywhere; the strength of ``demanded'' is uniform: barred on the approach by Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-no-c-number-repair} and at the boundary itself by Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-no-equivariant-value-wall}.
