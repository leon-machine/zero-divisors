## formal
The chain must still supply the reader one sentence of locality, and here it is. The localisation theorem \cite[Thm.~4.4, Cor.~4.5]{ButlerT4}, in the form the Schwarzschild composite gives it at the black hole \cite[Thms 8.7, 8.12]{ButlerT4}, attaches the completed modes, and with them the descent, to the degenerate locus: $\EN$ is the map from the ascended boundary mode space of the singularity to its metric-level shadow, and nothing in the physical closure applies $\EN$ to the exterior radiation, whose observed single-quantum thermality is the coincidence of Proposition \claimref{singularity.pinching-against-partial-trace-scope-coincidence}(i), entanglement seen one mode at a time. Applying $\EN$ to the radiation regardless is, however, a perfectly well-defined operation, and it is precisely what it means to read the radiation at the $\Cl(1,3)$ level: keep the occupation records, erase the coherences, every mode at once. Theorem \claimref{singularity.deficit-theorem-cost-descent-exactly} quantifies that reading exactly: it costs $\rel{\rho}{\EN\rho}$, which by Corollary \claimref{singularity.deficit-carried-quanta-correlations} is exactly the inter-quanta correlation content. One conflation must be avoided here, and the model is what prevents it: the deficit measures how \emph{much} coherent content the metric-level reading destroys, and it is identical in the model's scrambled and unscrambled histories, the accumulated phases squaring away in the diagonal (Proposition \claimref{singularity.two-levels-finite-model}); the \emph{message} is written in which coherences carry it, the dressed correlators of Proposition \claimref{singularity.purity-correlation-transfer-recovery}(3), a pattern that moves phase, not entropy, and to which the deficit is provably insensitive. What descent destroys is the sector where the information lies; what the deficit measures is the size of that destruction, never its content. Hawking's information loss is thereby derived rather than merely undergone, as a \emph{quantity}: it is the deficit of the wrong level, in closed form, the content itself residing in the destroyed sector's pattern, which no function of the deficit recovers. Two more locations must be stated plainly. First, the warrant: reading $\EN$, rather than the single average over $\operatorname{Ad}(\Gamma)$, as the metric-level map is Proposition \claimref{singularity.p-reduced-metric-level-reading-many} below, the composite of the pointwise descents, its definitional antecedent quantified there; under the total-parity grading alone the average fixes every even-total state outright, the model's radiation state included, and the two-level discrepancy would be identically zero. Second, the cost's location: in the rate model the window marginal is joint-diagonal at every stage (Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}(1)), hence a fixed point of $\EN$, so the reading the closure does perform, of the boundary modes, costs nothing; the entire nonzero deficit of Section \ref{subsec:descentapplied} is the cost of the counterfactual reading, of the radiation, at the metric level. Section \ref{subsec:descentapplied} exhibits both in exact numbers.
