## formal
The ledger asked for a physical system whose natural algebra carries a central splitting. The note has given two, and the theorem that they are instances of: the five-dimensional Kaluza-Klein algebra of this world and the planar algebra of the mirror's convention, each split, each with its ideals the two inequivalent spinor representations that physics has always known about, each with its outer exchange the parity that physics has always known to be anomalous, each with its Rule 2 repair the doubled fermion that physics has always used to restore the symmetry. In the five-dimensional case the repair is the conformal algebra of the series' own root paper, and Theorem \claimref{rule2.where-obstruction-sits-commuting-square} says that this is not a coincidence of the example but the general shape: for the physical lineage, the hyperbolic plane that Rule 1 adjoins is a Kaluza-Klein direction plus the direction that repairs it. Rule 2 has been exercised physically in the sense that the obstruction, the exchange, and the repair are each carried by a measured or standard physical structure, imported here as Facts.

What has not been exercised is dynamics. The note does not derive that a sixth direction exists, nor that the fifth one does; it shows that if a fifth is adjoined the algebra obstructs, and that the repair is the direction the series already had. The forgotten bit is read, not decided: whether the parity of the fifth direction becomes a boost or a rotation is a geometric datum, and the series' choice of $\Cl(2,4)$ rests on the root paper's minimality argument, not on anything here. The planar system exercises the fusion at the level of representation theory and of an induced term in an effective action; it does not make the third direction physical, which in graphene it is not. And the note has used the series' convention where it needed to: the Kaluza-Klein algebra splits in the time-positive form, the planar and eleven-dimensional algebras in the time-negative one, and the complexified statement of Corollary \claimref{rule2.odd-lorentzian-dimension-exactly-one-real} is the convention-free residue.

The row's new state, for the capstone's ledger: owned by this note; the kinematic exercise delivered; remaining, a system in which the fused direction is forced dynamically rather than by the representation theory of the fermions, which is the question of what physical principle selects the sign of $\nu^2$.
