## formal
Definition \claimref{obj.sheaf.variable-order-algebra-strict-diagonal-data} imposes the strict ordering $p_1 < 0 < p_2 \leq p_3$. Mixmaster behaviour is precisely the failure of that ordering: along a bounce the exponents reorder. This subsection settles what a collision can be and what the wall does at one, on the Kasner constraint variety $\sum p_i = \sum p_i^2 = 1$, entirely at the algebraic level; the dynamics of an actual bounce stays with \cite{ButlerT6}.
