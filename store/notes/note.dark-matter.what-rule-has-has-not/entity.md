## formal
One rule is applied identically to every tower, our world entering once, as $V_{3}$, in every comparison, so every share is a multiple of the one $\Omega_{b}$ gravity measures; there is no distinction between towers we contain and towers that contain us, and no observer in another tower would compute a different $\rho_{r}/\rho_{\mathrm{us}}$, because the rule is Lemma \claimref{dark-matter.projection-every-towers-content-dilutes-ones} with the thickness R3 states. What the rule does not have is a reason for R1 or R2: R1 is consistent with the series' mass formulas (Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.r1-against-series-mass-formulas}) but not derived; R2 asks every tower to be filled at the same spacing, which for this tower is set by baryon number, a charge the odd towers do not carry (Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.flag-bit-exists-exactly-tower-dimension}). R3 is a length, fixed at one cell radius; Section \ref{subsec:free} gives a mechanism that returns that value and costs it. The rule is not the coupling rule of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.no-coupling-rule-odd-towers-follows}: it says nothing about the odd towers' scales, lightest states or number-changing rates, only how much of their matter, whatever it is, sources our metric per unit of our volume.
