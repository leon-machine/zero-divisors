## formal
The chain's slogan is that the metric level keeps the occupation records and loses the coherences. Theorem \claimref{singularity.descent-parity-average} says something sharper and more structural: \emph{the metric level is the parity even level}, and the ladder sector that Rule 1 adjoined is exactly the odd sector. The one-way character of the descent \cite[Fact 5.9]{ButlerT5} is then not a separate fact to be imported but the statement that a $\mathbb{Z}_2$ average cannot be undone: the even subalgebra retains no trace of the odd part it averaged away. It also explains, at a glance, why the descent is the \emph{unique} trace-compatible conditional expectation onto the commutant of $P$: it is the unique one compatible with the grading, and the grading is not a choice but the Clifford algebra's own.
