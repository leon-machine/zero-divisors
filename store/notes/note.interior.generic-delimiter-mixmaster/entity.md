## formal
The theorem's scope is the admissible (Einstein velocity-dominated) class, which contains the Schwarzschild and Schwarzschild-de Sitter interiors and the vacuum Kasner models with $p_i \neq 0$, and the scope boundary is a result, not a caveat. For generic oscillatory (BKL, mixmaster) singularities the Hubble-normalised frame undergoes infinitely many Kasner-axis rotations, so parallel transport of the frame has no limit at all, and no theorem of the present form can hold framewise: the admissibility integral of Definition \claimref{obj.interior.weighted-blow-up-hubble-normalisation-admissible} diverges. \emph{Provenance for the generic case:} the oscillatory approach is \cite{BKL1970}, its billiard formulation \cite{DHN2003}; velocity-dominated asymptotics in the sense used here descend from \cite{ELS1972, IsenbergMoncrief1990}. The correct generic statement must be formulated on the quotient by the Kasner-axis dynamics, the cosmological billiard, and formulating a billiard-invariant weighted limit theorem is posed in Section \ref{sec:scope} as a successor problem. The delimiter locates exactly where the present method ends and names the invariant object that should replace the frame.
