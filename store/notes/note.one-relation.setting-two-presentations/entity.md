## formal
\textbf{The relation in words.} Multiply two directions in either order, add the results, and what comes back is a number rather than a direction: that is the whole of $\{e_{a}, e_{b}\} = 2g_{ab}\mathbf{1}$. Taken on one direction with itself it says that a displacement squared is its own interval, which along a timelike direction is what a clock carried there reads. The same relation decides which directions have zero interval, because $g$ is what says so, and in the plane spanned by the time direction and any one spatial direction there are two such: the opposite light rays $n_{\pm} = \tfrac12(e_{0} \pm e_{1})$, for which the relation gives $n_{\pm}^{2} = 0$ and $\{n_{+}, n_{-}\} = 1$, the real null pair of the proof above. Those are the relations of a fermionic ladder pair, so $n_{+}^{2} = 0$ is at once the statement that a light ray has no length and the statement that no state holds two identical fermions. The two rays are not yet a creation operator and an annihilation operator, because nothing in the relation says which is which. Both are future directed, so a time orientation does not separate them; what does is the orientation of the plane they span, the sign of the bivector $\sigma = e_{0}e_{1}$, since $n_{-}n_{+} = \tfrac12(1 + \sigma)$ and $n_{+}n_{-} = \tfrac12(1 - \sigma)$ are the two occupation projectors and choosing the sign of $\sigma$ is choosing which of them counts as occupied. Reversing $e_{0}$ alone, or $e_{1}$ alone, swaps the roles; reversing both leaves them fixed. This is the sense in which the flag of Fact \claimref{one-relation.imported-flag-arrow-superselection} is a $\mathbb{Z}_{2}$ orientation and not a frame vector, and $\sigma$ is the boost generator whose rotor flow rescales the pair in Lemma \claimref{one-relation.third-constant-conversion-constant-between-charts}(iii) and which the singularity paper reads as its clock \cite{ButlerT5}. Once the sign is chosen the plane carries the kinematics of one fermionic mode: a two-state system with exclusion built in, and nothing more than that. Dynamics, a Hamiltonian, and the complex structure the usual Fock space needs are not in the relation and are not claimed here; over $\R$ the plane's algebra is $\Cl(1,1) \cong \Mat(2, \R)$, the real form of the one-mode CAR. The script checks the two projector identities, $\sigma n_{\pm} = \mp n_{\pm}$, and the three sign reversals.
