## formal
Each factor of the $126$ direction exists in the series: the rung vector is the Standard-Model paper's transported frozen point \cite[Prop.~9.27]{ButlerT12}, the slot area bivector $v_{5}\wedge w_{5}$ is the object whose expectation the grip computes, the symplectic area of the slot doublet \cite[Thm.~9.11]{ButlerT12}, and $\sum v_{i}\wedge w_{i}$ is the pair-number generator, the $B - L$ current. The product is not derived in the series as a coupling, and Fact \claimref{families.imported-tree-relations-family-no-go}(ii), (iii) say that neither the frozen point with its lifts nor the mass bracket with its composites produces it: the bracket direction couples through the $120$ or the $126$ \cite[Prop.~9.15(b)]{ButlerT12}, but along the $e^{c}$ direction, of hypercharge $+1$ (the script reads $Y(e^{c}) = 1$), and a mass form $B(\psi, T\varphi)$ conserving hypercharge needs $Y(T) = -(Y(\psi) + Y(\varphi)) = \pm\tfrac12$ on the Yukawa pairs, so a direction of charge one is not a neutral vev, and its neutral composites with the rung vectors are rung-sector objects insensitive to $B - L$. The weight spectrum therefore cannot be addressed until the dynamics of the rung coupling is known, as the levels of the results say, but the obstruction now has a definite form: one grade-five term, three factors, all present, and the missing coupling is specifically that of the rung vector to the pair-sector $\uu(1)$ current, which is what the Standard-Model paper's colour label carries and what Hypothesis (S) would have to produce. The face-family conjecture of Section \ref{sec:faces} and the condensation of Section \ref{sec:condensation} supply the family matrix $F$; they cannot supply the factor, and this section says why.
