## formal
The tree relations of Corollary \claimref{standard-model.one-vev-vector-relations-breaking-pattern}, $m_{\nu} = m_{u}$ and $m_{e} = m_{d}$, are the relations of a single $10$, and the observed second family needs the factor three of Georgi and Jarlskog at the unification scale \cite{GeorgiJarlskog1979}. The families paper T13 \cite{ButlerT13} locates that factor in the module, in the operator $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$ of Fact \ref{fact:gj} at the end of this section, imported there with its provenance: with $B - L := 1 - \tfrac23 N_{W}$ and $\chi := -(-1)^{N_{R}}$ the rung parity, $T_{\mathrm{GJ}} = w_{4}(B - L)\chi$ is pure grade five, its mass form is the $126$ component, and its Clebsches have ratio $|y_{e}/y_{d}| = 3$, Georgi-Jarlskog's. The script recomputes the closed form, the grade and the Clebsches before using them. Three theorems then say, on the one-generation module, that the family structure cannot supply the factor, that the frozen point and its lifts cannot, and that the mass bracket cannot either; for an odd element $T$ of the Clifford algebra of the ten the \emph{mass form} is $Y_{T}(\psi, \varphi) = B(\psi, T\varphi)$ on the sixteen, symmetrised.
