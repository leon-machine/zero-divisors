## formal
Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.point-infinity-has-no-physical-fluctuation} treats $\Lambda$ as a number fixed before variation, the value of the Stelle-West constraints. The Standard-Model paper \cite[Prop.~7.6]{ButlerT12} classifies the same number, in the form $N = M_P/H$ with $H^2 = Q(I) = \Lambda/3$ on the de Sitter region (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-frozen-point-geometry} for the norm, Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector} for its identification with the Stelle-West value), in four clauses. Clause (1) proves that $N$ is not kinematic: under $\Lambda \to s^2\Lambda$ at fixed $M_P$, that is $I \to sI$ with the conformal class unchanged, $N \to N/s$ while every discrete invariant that paper derives is unchanged, so $N$ does not factor through the kinematic isomorphism class (Fact \ref{fact:notkinematic} below). Clause (2) imports from this paper that it is not dynamical, the norm being fixed by a constraint and not evolved: its \cite[Fact~2.10]{ButlerT12} cites Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.point-infinity-has-no-physical-fluctuation} and Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.vacuum-region-registration-condition}(i). Clause (3) concludes that $N$ is initial data carried by the boundary at the origin, conditionally on three things: on the three alternatives kinematic, dynamical, initial data being exhaustive, on the dating observation \cite[Obs.~7.4]{ButlerT12} that locates the boundary, and on the promotion decision its clause (4) names. Clause (4) imports from this subsection what follows. This subsection records, at the level of the action that belongs to this paper, that the three alternatives are not exhaustive, that the missing alternative is exactly where the action as written puts $N$, and what modification of the action would move it.
