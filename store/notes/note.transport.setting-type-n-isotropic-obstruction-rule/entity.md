## formal
This is the hard case, and it takes the longest section of the paper. The plan: first the question, can the non-invertible element itself be repaired in the next ascent, and the theorem that closes it, with its cohomological sharpening (\S\ref{sec:nogo}, \S\ref{subsec:cohomology}); then the completion in which the repair succeeds at the level of points, and the totality theorem (\S\ref{sec:model}, \S\ref{sec:totality}); then the two minimality theorems that make the completion forced (\S\ref{sec:minimality}, \S\ref{subsec:chartmin}); then the coordinate normal form and the cost of descent (\S\ref{sec:swap}); and finally two consistency checks against classical mathematics (\S\ref{sec:checks}).
