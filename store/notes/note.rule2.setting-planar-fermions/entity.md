## formal
The second system is the oldest home of the parity anomaly. Take generators $e_x, e_y$ of square $+1$ and $e_t$ of square $-1$: the algebra $\Cl(2,1)$ of a $2{+}1$-dimensional spacetime with time of square $-1$, which is the mirror's labelling in the series' convention (Corollary \claimref{rule2.odd-lorentzian-dimension-exactly-one-real} at $n = 3$: the time-positive form $\Cl(1,2)$ is complex type).
