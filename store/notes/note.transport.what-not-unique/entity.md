## formal
Uniqueness in Theorem \claimref{transport.minimality} is uniqueness of the adjoined \emph{space} $\mathbb{W}$ (up to isometry), hence of the completed algebra up to isomorphism. The lift $f$ itself is not unique: for any $c \neq 0$, $f_c(x) = c\,\nO + \tfrac{1}{2c} Q(x)\,\nInf$ satisfies the same nullity condition. What is forced is the space in which the lift must lie, not how it is parametrised.
