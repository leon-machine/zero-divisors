## formal
This note is a companion to T17 of the series, the capstone \cite{ButlerT17}, and closes Direction 7 of its forward ledger. Every algebraic input is the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1}: the classification and the centre (its Theorem 2.5 and Lemmas 2.6, 2.7), the existence and outerness of type C (Theorem 3.6), the Rule 2 machinery (Proposition 5.2, Lemmas 5.3 and 5.4, Theorems 5.5 and 5.7, Remark 5.8), Rule 1's Morita triviality (Theorem 7.1), and the parking of the clock (Proposition 7.4); the note's Lemma \claimref{rule2.even-subalgebra-one-dimensional-extension} is the one standard fact it proves for itself. Theorem \claimref{rule2.where-obstruction-sits-commuting-square} is the note's contribution to T1's own subject: the two rules, compared there as amplifications of the whole algebra and of an even half, coincide on every one-dimensional extension of an even-dimensional algebra, where the even half is the base. The naming of $\Cl(2,4)$ as the conformal completion and of its spinors as twistors is the root paper's and the twistor paper T9's \cite{ButlerT9}. The residue set $\{2, 6\}$ is the dark-matter paper T14's \cite{ButlerT14}, whose reading of the signature convention as indexical is what Corollary \claimref{rule2.odd-lorentzian-dimension-exactly-one-real} needs. The primer T0 \cite{ButlerT0} carries the classification table in the form the note quotes. Nothing downstream depends on this note; the capstone's ledger row is its only consumer.
