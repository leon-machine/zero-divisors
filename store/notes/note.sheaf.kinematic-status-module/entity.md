## formal
At the singular boundary the raw cotangent module is not a carrier: it admits no bounded extension across $E$ (the order-one structure fails the minimality clause of Proposition \claimref{sheaf.basic-properties-operational-characterisation}), and by Theorem \claimref{sheaf.uniqueness-weighted-module} the rain-bounded boundary-tame extension is unique. The positive identification is two-tier: what the infalling observer measures finitely is exactly the exceptional module, the nondegenerate rain-dual carrier; its $u$-smooth shadow is $\shW$, the tame carrier of the degeneration sheaf; and the chain's own radial dyad lies in the former with constant unit components. The exceptional algebra of Proposition \claimref{sheaf.exceptional-algebra-black-hole} is, literally, the Clifford algebra of what the rain measures. What remains interpretive is confined to the operator and state level of \cite{ButlerT5}'s chain, and is recorded in Section \ref{subsec:schwscope}.
