## formal
T06 works with the gauge field of the Schwarzschild solution in the Newtonian (Painlev\'e-Gullstrand) gauge exactly as T05 sets it up (\claimref{note.singularity.setting-schwarzschild-gauge-field-connection-where}): the identity minus $\beta$ times the projector $P$, which squares to zero. T06 calls this one-parameter family the unipotent ray and reads it as a trajectory in the gauge group (\claimref{interior.gauge-trajectory-unipotent-ray}).
