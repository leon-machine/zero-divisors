## formal
T5's capacity statement (the Page-curve computation) \emph{cites} the count of Section \ref{sec:count} and the area-law condition under test; any derivation of the epoch routed through the Page-curve or eviction computation therefore proves the prediction from itself, and is excluded. Admissible derivations of $\nu = 1/16\pi$ in the 1-nat convention, $\nu = 1/(16\pi\ln 2)$ in the capacity convention (Remark \claimref{note.scrambling.density-factor-stated-full}), must come from the structure of the boundary modes themselves (a calibration principle on the exceptional set of the weighted completion, Section \ref{subsec:calibration}, or the slice-to-epoch aggregation of Section \ref{sec:sphere}); the fence is part of what makes the prediction falsifiable, and the failed direct aggregations recorded in Section \ref{sec:sphere} show that it excludes real candidates.
