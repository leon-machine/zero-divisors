## formal
The theorem's content is that under boundary-mode locality the falling branch is not a statistical effect and not the outcome of a scrambling mechanism: it is the window's contents, read off. Typicality is not used anywhere, and neither is any assumption about where correlations are deposited, because under (L$_E$) nothing the window does can move a marginal. What the theorem does not deliver, for any mode the chain admits, is exact saturation of the envelope: maximal entanglement is $c_k = s_k$, which by the thermofield angle $\tan^{2}\theta_k = e^{-\omega_k/\TH}$ is the zero-frequency case $\omega_k = 0$; the linear assignment of Premise \claimref{singularity.q-joint-quasi-freeness-s-wave} supplies exactly one such mode, the sea level, so saturation, which needs every window member at zero frequency, is unavailable to any window of two or more members; at every nonzero weight $h_k < \ln 2$ strictly, and the falling branch runs strictly inside the capacity. What this paper claims without typicality is therefore the envelope, the exact rising branch, the quantified falling branch $S_R = \sum_{W} h_k$ and the endpoint, and not the saturated Page curve, which remains available on exactly two routes: typicality (Fact \claimref{singularity.assembled-page-curve-up-typicality}), or the zero-frequency idealisation. Section \ref{sec:model} exhibits the shortfall in numbers, and Proposition \claimref{singularity.models-entropy-sequence-exactly} states the conclusion.
