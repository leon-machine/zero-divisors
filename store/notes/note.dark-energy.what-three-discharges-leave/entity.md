## formal
The residue statement of Assembly \claimref{dark-energy.forcing-half-schwarzschild-wall-residue-sense} now holds with $\Lambda$ (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.cosmological-constant-does-not-change-residue}), with a finite renormalised action whose only ambiguity is a scheme constant (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.wall-counterterm}), and pointwise for the diagonal variable-exponent class (Lemma \claimref{dark-energy.variable-exponents-residue-pointwise-corrections-are}), where the sheaf paper's variable-order structure \cite[\S 9]{ButlerT4} is the Gram-level counterpart. What is not done: the subleading momentum constraints of the variable-exponent class, which decide which exponent fields $p_i(x)$ the equations admit, and the non-diagonal class, where the frame directions themselves rotate as the wall is approached; both are the dynamical half of the sheaf paper's connection-level matching \cite[Rem.~8.28]{ButlerT4}, and the logarithm in Lemma \claimref{dark-energy.variable-exponents-residue-pointwise-corrections-are} is the first sign of the variable-order structure at connection level.
