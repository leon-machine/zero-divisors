## formal
A \emph{completion} of $(W,B)$ is a quadratic space $(\widetilde W, Q)$ with $W \subseteq \widetilde W$ and $Q|_W = B$ and $Q$ nondegenerate; it is \emph{minimal} if no completion has smaller dimension. Every completion induces a unital algebra embedding $\Cl(W,B) \hookrightarrow \Cl(\widetilde W, Q)$: the inclusion of generators satisfies the Clifford relation, so the universal property gives the algebra map, and the map is injective because a basis of $W$ extends to a basis of $\widetilde W$, whereupon Fact \claimref{conformal.blade-basis-persists-under-degeneracy} sends the blades of $\Cl(W,B)$ to blades of $\Cl(\widetilde W, Q)$ in the extended basis, which are linearly independent.
