\emph{Step 4d after this note. The status line ``up to a state'' becomes ``proved from (P) and (R)''.}

The proposed status line for the chain table of \cite[Table 1]{ButlerT5} is therefore \emph{proved from (P), (R)} rather than \emph{up to a state}, with the two premises named in the row's own text, in exactly the manner Steps 6a and 6b already name (C1) to (C3) and (U), (C), (F). That is not a cosmetic change: it moves Step 4d from the class of results that await a state to the class of results that hold given premises the reader can inspect, which is the standard the rest of the chain is held to.

What has been relocated rather than removed is this. The chain's residual state dependence is now entirely in the correlations, and the correlations are the business of Step 6b, where the rate model already prices them by (U), (C) and (I). Theorem \claimref{singularity.exact-equilibrium-over-determines-boundary-modes} adds one thing to that pricing which was not previously visible: the correlations cannot be obtained from any equilibrium principle whatsoever, so no sharpening of Step 4d will ever supply them, and the search for a state-selection principle that delivers both thermality and the Page curve is a search for something that provably does not exist in the equilibrium category. Steps 4d and 6b are not two instalments of one problem; they are two different problems, and this note closes the first at the price of showing that the second cannot be closed the same way.
