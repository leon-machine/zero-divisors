## formal
First, statistics: the chain's pairs are fermionic, so the directly produced spectrum is Fermi-Dirac, correct for the fermionic sector of Hawking emission; the bosonic Planck sector is Step 4e's task and is derived, not assumed. Second, coherence with the count: at $\omega \sim \TH$ the thermal entropy per fermionic mode is of order $\ln 2$, one bit, exactly the per-mode capacity that converts Step 6a's mode count $N_{\mathrm{6a}}$ into the boundary-mode capacity $C = N_{\mathrm{6a}}\ln 2$ of Proposition \claimref{singularity.area-law-test}: the thermodynamics and the counting cohere. Third, the role of the hyperbolic atom is now complete: $\Cl(1,1)$, the unique self-complete unit of transport (Lemma \claimref{singularity.pseudo-inverse-identities-hyperbolic-atom}), is the horizon's boost plane, matched to the curvature-selected plane by Corollary \claimref{singularity.matching-steps-4b-4c-join-equation}, and its intrinsic structure, the unique rotor flow with its spectral projectors, is the modular structure of black hole thermodynamics. The atom that needs no ascent is the one that carries the temperature.
