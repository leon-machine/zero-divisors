## formal
Algebra-level theorems, proved here: rigidity (Theorems \claimref{tower.commuting-copies}, \claimref{tower.rigidity-clock-frozen}), the CAR relations and Fock modules (Proposition \claimref{tower.ladder-pairs-satisfy-car-vectors}, Theorem \claimref{tower.fock-module-one-qubit-per-rung}), capacity (Theorem \claimref{tower.witt-index-quantum-capacity}), the even-charge limit (Theorems \claimref{tower.algebraic-limit-even-charge}, \claimref{tower.car-limit-even-charge}). Imported theorems: the single step and its minimality \cite{ButlerT1}; the wall sequence \cite{ButlerT4}. Open, stated as such: the odd-charge limit (Question \claimref{q.tower.odd-charge}). Physical readings, flagged as readings: ``one qubit per rung'' as a statement about horizon or singularity modes (the algebra supplies the register; identifying its qubits with counted physical modes is the task of the series' counting papers); ``iterated transport is second quantisation'' as a slogan (the theorem is the tensor factorisation with CAR; the slogan adds an interpretive claim about what quantisation is that the mathematics neither needs nor decides).
