## formal
Observation \claimref{standard-model.fits-record-level} does not derive the weak angle. It shows that $\sin^{2}\theta_{W} = 0.2312$ is consistent with every fermion mass and mixing on this route, and that consistency without tuning needs the second family's factor $3$ not to come from $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$ acting on that family. The series takes that route and registers it as a choice: the factor comes through mixing with the third family, which the grade-three direction of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.grade-three-georgi-jarlskog-direction} supplies through an antisymmetric coupling between the second and third families. That coupling is not derived (successor problem (8)). The lepton mixing angles are fitted here, not derived; the neutrino paper carries that question.
