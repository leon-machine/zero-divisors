## formal
Proposition \claimref{correspondence.local-twistor-transport-ascended-spinor-connection} completes the identification begun in \cite{ButlerT6}: the ambient bundle of the completion is the standard conformal tractor bundle, and its spin bundle, with the induced connection, is the classical local twistor bundle, up to the one sign convention of Remark \claimref{note.correspondence.one-imported-convention-local-twistor-identification}. The series' chain of results is thus: division fails (type N) $\Rightarrow$ ascent to $\Cl(2,4)$ forced \cite{ButlerT1} $\Rightarrow$ vacuum equations are formulated in the completion as a parallel ideal point \cite{ButlerT6} $\Rightarrow$ the spinors of the completion are the conformal spinors (Penrose's twistors), and the parallel ideal point is the constant simple infinity bivector of $\CS$ (the infinity twistor) (Corollary \claimref{correspondence.one-statement-three-languages}). Each formalism was invented independently (tractors: Thomas, Bailey-Eastwood-Gover; twistors: Penrose); the completion derives both from one division problem.
