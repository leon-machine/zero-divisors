## formal
The case against: the families paper supplies a direction and states that the magnitude was never its physical content, each sector carrying its own normalisation; and this paper needs the second-order breaking to depress the down-type and lepton eigenvalues relative to the up sector by $m_{t}/m_{b}$, where the families paper describes that breaking as what lifts the two light families. What survives: (U) is used only to value a scale, carried as a range whose ends are the two readings of (U) itself; nothing proved here depends on where in the range the ceiling lies, and the fragility bound of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.mirror-share-one-how-fragile}(ii) is quoted across the whole range. If (U) fails the ceiling is unvalued, not wrong: the rung scale becomes a free datum of the pre-descent theory, successor problem (2).
