## formal
$\Lambda^{\mathrm{even}}\C^{5}$ restricted to $S(U(2) \times U(3)) \subset SU(5) \subset \mathrm{Spin}(10)$ being one Standard Model generation with a right-handed neutrino is the exterior-algebra presentation of grand unification expounded by Baez and Huerta \cite{BaezHuerta2010}, and Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor} arrives at it with the slot space of the conformal spinors (the twistor-slot space) as the $\C^{2}$ summand, the pair space $W$ as the $\C^{3}$ summand, and the duality of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.chirality-two-halves-spinor-module-conjugation} as its Hodge star. What Hypothesis (S) would yield: the group $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ is then the framework's own, the automorphism group of the composite's state space in the same sense in which $S(U(2) \times U(3))$ is the automorphism group of the two-level partition, and the hypothesis of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-one-loop-running-trace-ratio}(b) is discharged with $G_{U} = \mathrm{Spin}(10)$ and the derived generation as its $16$, so that $3/8$ becomes a derived value rather than an inherited one. What it would cost, stated before Proposition \claimref{standard-model.quarks-leptons-two-level-fermion-content}: the automorphisms preserving the split and the mode number are $U(2) \times U(3)$, thirteen-dimensional, so the automorphism rule that gave twelve generators in Theorem \claimref{standard-model.gauge-algebra-minimal-massive-composite} gives thirteen under (S); the extra abelian generator is anomaly-free in the action the generation carries (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor}(3)), so the rule returns thirteen generators and the thirteenth must be broken, which the right-handed neutrino's Majorana mass does (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.majorana-element}); and the five-mode state space does not record which pairs belong to which slot, so the two-level hierarchy of Section \ref{sec:algebra} is not a substructure of it. Deciding (S) is successor problem (6), and it is the decision on which the lepton, the singlet listing as part of one object, the unification group and the values $3/8$ and $3/4$ all turn.
