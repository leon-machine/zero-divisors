## formal
Fix $c \neq 0$ and let $\mathcal{M}_c \subset \shW$ be generated by $(u\rho,\ k + c\rho,\ \mu_1,\ \mu_2)$, with $k = \rho + m\,\dtPG$ the radical section. Then $\mathcal{M}_c$ is a strict submodule (its boundary evaluation has rank three), it satisfies (T2$\partial$), and its dying \emph{entry} is
\[
G(u\rho,\,u\rho) \;=\; r\,(m^2 - r) :
\]
first order in $r$, with nonvanishing coefficient. Nevertheless its dying \emph{eigenvalue} is second order: $\det A = mu$, so $\det G_{\mathcal{M}_c} = -m^2 r^2$ exactly, while the nonvanishing $3\times 3$ minor at the boundary equals $c^2 m^2 \neq 0$: the off-diagonal entries of order $u$ reduce the first-order diagonal entry to a second-order eigenvalue. So $\mathcal{M}_c$ is corank one with a first-order dying entry and is \emph{not} $\shW$: the entry formulation of (T3$\partial$) would make Theorem \claimref{sheaf.uniqueness-weighted-module} false, and the eigenvalue formulation is the correct one. All displayed identities are verified exactly (Section \ref{sec:verification}).
