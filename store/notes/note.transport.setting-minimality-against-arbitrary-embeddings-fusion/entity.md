## formal
Theorem \claimref{transport.one-generator-minimal-minimal-completions-classified} quantifies over Clifford algebras of extensions of the quadratic space, the same normalisation used for Rule 1 in Section \ref{sec:minimality}. For Rule 1 the stronger, representation-independent statement was Theorem \claimref{transport.representation-independent-minimality-completion}, settled on the ambient quadric. For Rule 2 the corresponding question is settled by module theory instead, and the answer is stronger still: the fused algebra is minimal among \emph{all} simple real algebras, Clifford or not, into which the split algebra embeds unitally.
