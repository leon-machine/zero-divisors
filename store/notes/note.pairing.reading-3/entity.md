## formal
Proposition \claimref{pairing.curvature-which-loops-move-vacuum} is the component form of Fact \claimref{pairing.imported-weyl-scalars-petrov-type-schwarzschild}'s $\Psi_{2}$ through Lemma \claimref{pairing.loop-classes-are-weyl-components}: the loops that move the framework's Schwarzschild vacuum are exactly those carrying $\Psi_{2}$, and the null cone of $\ell$ carries none because $\ell$ is a repeated principal null direction. The framework's radial vacuum is therefore not only the one selected by the extreme curvature eigenvalue and the sign of the square; it is the one whose null cone the holonomy leaves fixed. This is an import with a reading.
