## formal
A single common threshold for all superpartners is the crudest model of a supersymmetric spectrum; the known result that a degenerate spectrum needs a few TeV to meet at $\alpha_{s} = 0.118$ is reproduced here, not discovered, and the actual thresholds, split as in Section \ref{subsec:twothresholds}, move $M_{U}$ and $\alpha_{U}$ by amounts the two-threshold theorem quantifies at one loop. What the proposition settles within this paper is narrower and exact in its logic: Remark \claimref{note.standard-model.sensitivity-one-loop-decides-nothing-about} left one row of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.table-exact} undecided because its one-loop gap equalled the two-loop scale, and at two loops that row is admissible. The other rows' conclusions were ten times outside the two-loop scale and stand.
