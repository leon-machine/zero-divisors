## formal
Proposition \claimref{pairing.pairing-class-transverse-derivative-null-direction} is elementary once Proposition \claimref{pairing.stabiliser-parabolic-algebra-null-direction} is in hand, and its content is a dictionary: by Fact \claimref{pairing.imported-spin-coefficients-null-tetrad} the components of the pairing class on the null tetrad are, up to normalisation and sign convention, the spin coefficients $\kappa, \tau, \sigma, \rho$, so the pairing generator of the framework's vacuum is the transverse deformation of the vacuum's null congruence, the optical scalars of Sachs and Newman-Penrose: the vacuum is parallel along its own integral curve if $\ell$ is geodesic ($\kappa = 0$), along $n$ if $\tau = 0$, and along every transverse real direction if the congruence has neither expansion, twist nor shear ($\rho = \sigma = 0$). Under the series' rule this is an import with a framework reading. The reading is this. The stabiliser part of the connection, eight of the twelve spin coefficients, rescales $\ell$, rotates $m$ in its plane and tilts $n$ and $\mb$ by null rotations about $\ell$; it is what the singularity paper's hops, annihilations and Cartan elements do, and it cannot move the vacuum, and so cannot create entanglement from it (off the vacuum a hop does entangle, as that paper's own script exhibits \cite[Rem.~13.5]{ButlerT5}). The pairing part, the other four, tilts $\ell$ and is what the framework had said it did not supply. It supplies it wherever the congruence of its chosen null direction is not parallel.
