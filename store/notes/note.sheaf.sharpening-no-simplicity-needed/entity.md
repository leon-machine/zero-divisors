## formal
Proposition \claimref{sheaf.resolution-ideal-theoretically} can also be proved by invoking simplicity of the completed algebra, which is available in the physical case ($\Cl(2,4)$ is simple) but fails in the split signatures $p - q \equiv 1 \pmod 4$, where the completed algebra has proper two-sided ideals. The hyperbolic-partner argument above is signature-free: it produces the unit inside the ideal directly, so the statement holds for \emph{every} nondegenerate completion of every corank-one fibre, split cases included. This matters for the iteration of Section \ref{sec:wall}, where the wall sequence visits ascents of varying signature and no control of the Wedderburn type along the sequence should be needed, or is.
