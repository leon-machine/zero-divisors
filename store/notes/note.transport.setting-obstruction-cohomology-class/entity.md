## formal
Remark \claimref{note.transport.locality-robustness-obstruction} observed that the no-go is local: only the stabiliser of a single null vector is used. That locality has an exact formulation, which we now give. It identifies the obstruction as a cohomology class, makes the enlargement-independence of Theorem \claimref{transport.no-go-level-elements} structural rather than a quantifier, and exhibits the hyperbolic atom as the class's vanishing locus.

We keep the setting of the proof of Theorem \claimref{transport.no-go-level-elements}, relaxed by one dimension so that the vanishing case is included rather than excluded: $(\mathbb{V}, Q)$ nondegenerate of mixed signature with $n \geq 2$; a null vector $v$ with hyperbolic partner $w_0$ ($v \cdot w_0 = \tfrac12$); $U = \{v, w_0\}^\perp \cap \mathbb{V}$, of dimension $n - 2 \geq 0$; an arbitrary extension $\mathbb{V}' \supseteq \mathbb{V}$ of quadratic spaces, split as $\mathbb{V}' = H_0 \perp U'$ with $U' = U \perp \mathbb{V}^\perp$; and the normal form of pseudo-inverses established in Step 1 there: the set
\[
S(v) \;=\; \{\, \sigma \in \Cl(\mathbb{V}') : v\sigma v = v \,\}
\;=\; \{\, w_0 + \alpha + v\beta + vw_0\delta \;:\; \alpha, \beta, \delta \in \Cl(U') \,\}.
\]
