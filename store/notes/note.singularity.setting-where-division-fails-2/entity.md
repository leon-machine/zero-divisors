## formal
Figure \ref{fig:fold} displays the folding. The section's propositions locate the division exactly: nowhere at $r > 0$ (Propositions \claimref{singularity.unipotent-ray} and \claimref{singularity.solution-where-divergence-lies}(3): the field solves the equations, invertibly and unimodularly), unavoidably at $r = 0$ (Propositions \claimref{singularity.degeneration-exactly} and \claimref{singularity.non-removability-invariant-argument}), and through a direction that is null for the limit form (Proposition \claimref{singularity.degeneration-exactly}(3)). That last fact is the input the series' taxonomy needs, and this is the algorithm's Step 1 discharged \cite[\S 8.1, Step 1]{ButlerT1}.
