## formal
This note is a companion to T12 of the series, alongside a companion note on the lepton, which asks whether the lepton is derivable inside the two-level hierarchy and answers no. \emph{It relies on:} the tower paper T3 \cite{ButlerT3} for the rungs, the Fock module and the Witt index (Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-tower-one-qubit-per-rung}), and the transport principle paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} for the totality and minimality of the completion (Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-totality-minimality-completion}); the twistor paper T9 \cite{ButlerT9} for the spinor identification, the Witt data and the line at infinity (Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-spinor-identification-witt-data-line}); the mass paper T11 \cite{ButlerT11} for the two-twistor mass formula (Fact \claimref{rungs.imported-mass-symplectic-area}); and the Standard-Model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} for the ladder, the automorphism group, Hypothesis (S), the derived generation, the constituent hypothesis of its successor problem (6), and the results priced in Section \ref{sec:fallback} (Facts \claimref{rungs.imported-ladder-automorphism-group-no-lepton}, \claimref{rungs.imported-fock-representation-bogoliubov-group}, \ref{fact:sixteen}, \claimref{rungs.imported-results-t12-used-registration}). \emph{Papers that would rely on it:} T12, if (R) replaces (S) as the premise or is derived; the tower paper T3, if the missing statement of Remark \claimref{note.rungs.where-leaves-r} is taken up there, where it belongs; and the capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17}, which would record (S) as a registered premise with the kill conditions of Section \ref{sec:fallback}.
