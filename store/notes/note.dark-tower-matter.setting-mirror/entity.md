## formal
So the mirror's constituents are complex, as ours are (Theorem \claimref{dark-tower-matter.constituents-field}): its real (Majorana) ground spinors do not make them real. The isomorphism sends the mirror's field unit $I_{4}'$ to ours and its conformal unit $I_{6}'$ to minus ours. If each tower's constituent unit is taken to be its own oriented conformal pseudoscalar, the mirror's constituent unit and field unit therefore have the opposite relative sign to ours, and the mirror is the parity image of this tower. That reading is interpretive and depends on the identification of the transported plane, which with one sign reversed would flip the sign of $I_{6}'$'s image. Nothing gravitational distinguishes the two.
