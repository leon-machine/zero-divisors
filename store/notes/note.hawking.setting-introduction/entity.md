## formal
T5 of this series \cite{ButlerT5} computes a six-step chain from the Schwarzschild gauge field to the Page curve. Two of its links are deliberately minimal, and say so: single-mode thermality is proved from two named premises, (P) univalence and (R) the KMS condition at the geometric period, with the occupancy derived from the gluing identity \cite[Prems.~7.8 and 7.19, Thm.~7.20]{ButlerT5}; and the bosonic Planck sector is derived in the s-wave-reduced theory under a joint quasi-freeness premise \cite[Prem.~8.1]{ButlerT5}. Between that core and the physical Hawking spectrum stand four questions, and this paper answers them at their correct levels.

\emph{Which state?} Section \ref{sec:state} proves the I-state theorem, the I-state being the series' name for the framework's distinguished equilibrium state $\omega_I$: among the quasi-free states of the CAR algebra of the boundary modes there is exactly one that is KMS for the geometry's boost at the Hawking temperature, and the KMS identity forces its two-point data outright, the pairing to zero and the symbol to Fermi-Dirac (Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state}). T5 derives the single-mode occupancy the same way; the increment here is operator-level and simultaneous across all modes, with uniqueness inside the quasi-free class proved, and uniqueness beyond it imported with provenance. What is genuinely open is the \emph{selection} of the dynamics from a geometric axiom, and Section \ref{sec:state} poses that open problem precisely, in the terms T5's own selection analysis fixes.

\emph{Which algebra carries the mode continuum?} Section \ref{sec:continuum} extends the single-mode statements over the tower's CAR limit \cite{ButlerT3}: the finite-window statements are exact theorems, the inductive-limit results are imported as Facts with provenance, and the result is free-fermion thermal field theory as the algebra carrying the near-horizon boundary modes.

\emph{How does the two-dimensional core become four-dimensional radiation?} Section \ref{sec:lift} assembles the lift with its joints itemised: helicity is the charge of the twistor storey (one level of the completion tower), and the Penrose homogeneity $-2s-2$ is proved from the ordering shift of the twistor paper \cite[Thm.~5.7]{ButlerT9} in Proposition \claimref{hawking.homogeneity-ordering-shift} (the computation appears inside that theorem's proof; the proposition promotes it to a stated result); greybody factors are inherited verbatim, because the series' exterior description coincides with general relativity on the invertible region at $\Lambda = 0$ \cite{ButlerT6}, so the flux prediction equals Hawking's, filtering included.

\emph{What does the finite boundary mode space change?} Section \ref{sec:seam} proves that the deviations of the finite boundary mode space from the infinite current algebra are edge-supported, with the exact edge identity and quantified tails: the corrections are area-suppressed at their proven rates, and the resulting $\ell_P^2/A$ correction is stated as a prediction. The Weinberg-Witten triage against the composite-graviton reading is recorded there as this paper's own claim.
