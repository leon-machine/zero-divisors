## formal
The case against: every residue has a causal member once the dimension is free (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.towers-are-residues-single-clock-filter}(i)), so the count five belongs to one window, and P3 picks it. What survives: P3 reads P1 (one metric) as a minimality condition, each tower as close to the visible four as its residue allows, and it is the only natural window returning the two-sterile answer; clause (iii) records what every other window gives, so the dependence is exposed. What does not survive: any claim that the fixed-point coincidence $\{0, 4\} = \{r : r \equiv -r\}$ is structural; it belongs to the window. A derivation of tower dimension from the descent (the wall's dimension in the sheaf paper is where it would come from \cite{ButlerT4}) would retire P3.
