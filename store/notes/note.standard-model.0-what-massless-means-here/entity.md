## formal
Two clarifications. First, at $\Lambda > 0$ Theorem \claimref{standard-model.moment-map-grip-mass-are-insensitive} holds unchanged, the frozen point still being a vector of $V$, and Theorem \claimref{standard-model.grip-mass-two-rung-module} becomes: the grip mass of a slot-singlet state is the single-component value of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-grip-area-casimir-form}(iv), $H^{2}$ times a difference of Casimirs of the conformal spinor representation (the twistor representation), a number of order $H^{2}$; so ``massless'' reads ``at the Hubble scale'', which for the purposes of the running between $M_{Z}$ and $M_{U}$ is the same thing. Second, the grip mass of a state is the mass of the composite whose state it is, the object the mass paper defines; reading the sectors of the module as the species of one generation is Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor}'s reading under (S), and the theorem inherits that reading and adds none of its own. What it says in that reading is structural and exact: the framework's mass mechanism produces mass only where a state has two slot components to misalign, and the $SU(2)$-singlet species have one.
