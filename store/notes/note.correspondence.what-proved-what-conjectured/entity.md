## formal
The conjecture's \emph{algebraic} half is a theorem. Quadratic ambiguities are central and carry nothing; the first tensorial slot is quartic and is $\mathfrak{so}(2,4)$-valued (Lemma \claimref{correspondence.ordering-filtration}); the ASD sector closes under ordering discrepancies and never sources the googly slot (Proposition \claimref{correspondence.chirality-split-ordering-insensitivity-asd-sector}). These are exactly the consistency conditions clause (a) needs, and they were not automatic: they depended on the chirality split commuting, which is special to four dimensions. What remains open is the \emph{analytic} half: the palatial construction itself, i.e.\ a deformation in which clause (b)'s quartic tensor demonstrably reproduces prescribed self-dual curvature. That construction is the googly problem in palatial form and is beyond this paper; the conjecture registers the precise expectation, and Proposition \claimref{correspondence.chirality-split-ordering-insensitivity-asd-sector} is its first falsifiable check, passed.
