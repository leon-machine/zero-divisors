## formal
Clause (1) is the classical statement that in six ambient dimensions every chiral spinor is pure (Cartan, Chevalley \cite{Chevalley54}); the annihilator planes of spinors in the conformal spinor space $\CS$ are one ruling family of maximal isotropic subspaces, in twistor language the $\alpha$-planes of complexified compactified Minkowski space, and the $\CSm$-family are the $\beta$-planes \cite{Penrose1967, PenroseRindler86}. The transport reading adds the physical origin: the three pairs of clause (2) are ladder pairs of exactly the kind the completion produces (Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-ladder-pairs-fock-modules-over}), and the tower paper \cite[\S 4]{ButlerT3} shows each further ascent adjoins one more pair, doubling the half-spinor space: the tower is a qubit register, and the conformal spinor space (twistor space) is the chirality half of its three-qubit floor. \emph{First quantisation, in this precise sense, is the Chevalley functor from ambient directions to Fock states; it is forced at the singular locus by the no-go theorem} \cite[Thm.~4.2]{ButlerT1}.
