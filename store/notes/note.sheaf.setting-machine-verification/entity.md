## formal
Every computational identity in this paper has been checked by computer, in exact rational arithmetic, in the manner of the verifications deposited with \cite{ButlerT1}. The script \texttt{verify\_T04.py}, deposited with this paper alongside the series' shared exact-arithmetic library \texttt{GA.py}, \cite{ButlerVerifyT04}, performs two hundred and ninety-six checks and reports each. Three exact computational routines are implemented directly: the basis-blade Clifford product over the rationals with degenerate generators; an exterior-algebra model of the Clifford action for \emph{arbitrary} rational Gram matrices, non-diagonal and degenerate included, whose faithfulness is the symbol-map triangularity in the proof of Lemma \claimref{sheaf.blade-basis-degenerate-forms-included}; and a rational-function routine in a formal collar parameter $\tau$, with exact polynomial GCD reduction, so that identities are verified as identities of rational functions rather than at samples. The coverage: the blade-basis independence of Lemma \claimref{sheaf.blade-basis-degenerate-forms-included} for a degenerate non-orthogonal Gram matrix, together with the Clifford relation of the exterior model on every basis state; the full degeneration-sheaf package of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.degeneration-sheaf} at the physical wall fibre (anticommutation, $ka = \hat a k$, $\dim J = 8$ by exact rank, $J^2 = 0$ over all $64$ ideal products, and the quotient's structure constants against $\Cl(0,3)$); the unit-ideal identity of Proposition \claimref{sheaf.resolution-ideal-theoretically} in $\Cl(1,1)$ and in the spacetime ascent $\Cl(2,4)$; the wall Gram of Lemma \claimref{sheaf.wall-gram} (block determinant $-2\mu\nu$, both block signatures, the orthocomplement vector $w$, and the full wall signatures for $(1,3)$ and $(2,1)$ under both coupling signs); the determinant identity of Lemma \claimref{sheaf.determinant-identity-blow-up-expansion} as an exact rational-function identity in $\tau$, with nonzero $b(\tau)$, $\tau$-dependent tangential block and two bump values, plus the blow-up expansion $(\tau s)(0) = 1/\lambda'(0)$; the one-sided completion sampled at exact rational collar points ($F \geq 1$, signature $(2,4)$); the localised ladder pair of Corollary \claimref{sheaf.localised-transport} verified \emph{on} the wall in the full coupled six-dimensional Gram via the exterior model ($c(k)c(k^*) + c(k^*)c(k) = \mathrm{id}$ on all $64$ states); the located conservation of Proposition \claimref{sheaf.conservation-degeneracy-located} with the shadow wall found at the exact rational point $\tau' = -2\mu\nu$, its crossing simple, its corank exactly one, and the signatures $(2,4)$ and $(1,5)$ on the two sides; the rescaled Gram limits, complement-independence and $c^2$ weight of Proposition \claimref{sheaf.exceptional-algebra-deformation-normal-cone} as polynomials in $\sqrt t$; and the residue of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.metrics-residue-square-root-twist}, all sixteen entries of $\tau G^{-1}$ at $\tau = 0$ with $b \neq 0$, the scale-freeness under $k \mapsto 2k$, and the weight $a$ under $\tau \mapsto a\tau$.

The Schwarzschild composite of Section \ref{sec:schwarzschild} is covered by seventy further checks, all identities in the radial variable being verified as identities of rational functions in $u = \sqrt r$ (exact polynomial GCD reduction), not at samples, with the mass entering as $2M = m^2$. The mass coverage is stated per block: the weighted Gram is derived at two masses; the completion block, normalised transversal included, runs \emph{in full} at two masses ($2M = 1$ and $2M = 4$), which is what fixes the $k(u) = 1$ normalisation of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.one-sided-completion-singularity}; the remaining blocks run at $2M = 1$. The coverage: the weighted Gram of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.schwarzschild-degeneration-boundary-tame} derived from first principles (the Painlev\'e-Gullstrand cometric in the raw coordinate coframe, inverted exactly), its polynomiality, $\det G = -r$ and $g_k = -r$ as exact identities; corank, radical and signatures at the boundary and at interior, horizon and exterior sample points; the Schwarzschild-frame transition, its $G$-orthogonality, the radical's Killing-time identification and the rain identity; the wall-fibre package of Proposition \claimref{sheaf.wall-fibre-black-hole} in the exterior model ($\dim J = 8$, $J^2 = 0$ over all $64$ products, quotient signature $(1,2)$); the determinant identity for the coupled ambient form at three couplings, the wall signatures $(2,4)$ and $(3,3)$ under the two coupling signs, $F \geq 1$ inside the horizon, the constant-determinant identity $\det h = m^2$ of the global coupling $\mu\nu = -\tfrac{m^2}{2}$ with signature $(2,4)$ at five sample radii, and the ladder pair on all $64$ ambient states; the exceptional Gram $\mathrm{diag}(m^2,-1,-1,-1)$, the residue $r\,G^{-1} \to -k \otimes k$ with its rank-one property, and the proper-time normalisation of $\rho$; and the weight-uniqueness search of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.weights-are-forced-are-kasner-exponents}, exhaustive over the stated grid, with the Kasner conversion and sum rules.

A further one hundred and four checks cover Sections \ref{subsec:uniqueness} through \ref{subsec:stability}. Uniqueness: the submodule $\mathcal{M}_c$ of Example \claimref{note.sheaf.eigenvalue-essential}, with its strictness (boundary rank three), corank, first-order dying entry, and second-order dying eigenvalue, including $\det G_{\mathcal{M}_c} = -m^2r^2 = (\det A)^2\det G_{\shW}$ as rational-function identities; and the null-mixing extension $\mathcal{M}'$ of Example \claimref{note.sheaf.rain-boundedness-essential-null-mixing-extension}, its exact null mixing $G(\alpha,\alpha) = G(\alpha,\beta) = 0$ and $\det G_{\mathcal{M}'} = -r/4$ at two rational sample points, its boundary Gram of rank three with radical on the $\beta$-slot, and the nonzero nondegenerate-block minor certifying the first-order dying eigenvalue. The rain frame: exact orthonormality of the infalling frame at every radius, the full duality pairing matrix, the exact Minkowski Gram of the exceptional coframe, the norm identity on the frame and a mixed element, the divergence of the excluded coordinate forms, the transition $\rho = \sqrt r(k/\sqrt r) - m\,\dtPG$ with determinant $-\sqrt r$, the nullity and constant unit components of the dyad $n_\pm$, the convention-pinning identity $\eta(La,Lb) = G(a,b)$ on all coordinate pairs, and the gauge field carrying the flat dyad onto $\pm n_\pm$, all at two masses. The matching: the six-dimensional bracket closure of the exceptional bivector algebra, the $\eta$-antisymmetry and characteristic polynomial $\lambda^4 - \lambda^2$ of the weight bivector at two rational Kasner points, $K^2 = \tfrac14$ on all $64$ ambient states, the rationalised frame identities of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.matching-connection-level} at two masses, and the rate series re-derived independently as exact power series in $\sqrt r$: the $\tau(r)$ series, the four printed constants, the trace rule, and the decay of the normalised sphere term. The anisotropic class: the full package of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.variable-weight-boundary-tameness-completion-forced} at the three rational Kasner points $u = 1, 2, 3$ of the classical parametrisation $(p_1,p_2,p_3) = (-u, 1+u, u(1+u))/(1+u+u^2)$ \cite{Kasner1921}, sum rules through completion signatures and pointwise weight forcing, with the ladder pair on all $64$ boundary states; and the stability theorem's congruence executed explicitly at $u = 2$ with a concrete relatively admissible perturbation, plus the sharpness example in both modes.

The final forty-seven checks cover Sections \ref{subsec:smoothstructure} and \ref{subsec:collisions}. The smooth structure: a self-contained formal calculus of terms $f(x)\,\tau^{e(x)}(\log\tau)^k$ with polynomial coefficients and exponents over $\mathbb{Q}$, on which the script checks ring closure, commutativity and associativity, both derivative formulas of Lemma \claimref{sheaf.log-obstruction} with their exact coefficients, the log-free constant-exponent case, the log-degree filtration through two tangential derivatives and along a five-letter derivative word, $\tau\partial_\tau\log\tau = 1$, the vanishing of $[\partial_x, \tau\partial_\tau]$, both Leibniz rules, exponent-set preservation under both operations, the ratio invariance under quasi-homogeneous rescaling, and the separation and strict monotonicity of the Kasner ratio $-p_1/p_2 = u/(1+u)$. The collisions: the equal-pair factorisation $6s^2-4s = 2s(3s-2)$ with its two census triples verified against both constraints, the zero-forcing factorisation $2p_2(p_2-1)$, the edge curve $q(v)$'s two constraint identities in $v$, its Taub value, its $u=2$ and axis-exchanged $u=3$ values, the ordering change across $v=0$ at three samples per side, the joint second-order vanishing identity $q_1q_2(1+v+v^2)^2 = -v^2(1+v)$, the Kasner-map identity $p(1/u) = (p_1,p_3,p_2)(u)$ at four samples with its $u=1$ fixed point, the dying-order maximum identity and its Taub limit, the Schwarzschild consistency $\varrho^3 = \tau^2 = \tfrac49 r^3(1+O(r))$ as an exact series, the nondegenerate Taub edge Gram at two unit choices, the complete relabelled chamber package at $q(-\tfrac14)$ through completion signatures and both weight-forcing solves, the edge coincidence of the two weight assignments, and the failed gluing's two negative transition exponents. Section \ref{sec:transversal} is covered by thirty-four further checks, thirty-two on the Schwarzschild boundary data at two masses and two on model data: the radical membership $Gk = 0$; the solve of Lemma \claimref{sheaf.solve-closed-radical-gauge}, its normalisation met at the computed gauge and violated at a second one, and the gauge independence of $G(\cdot, \tilde v)$; the intertwiner identity $\Phi^{\mathsf{T}} h_u \Phi = h_{u+v}$ for two independent $v$ at each mass, with unipotence and with a wrong-normalisation control that violates it; the cocycle $\Phi^u_v \circ \Phi^{u+v}_w = \Phi^u_{v+w}$, with $\tilde w$ normalised against $u + v$; at Clifford level, in the exterior model on the coupled Gram over all $64$ states, that $\Phi_v(k^*) = k^* + \tfrac12\tilde v$ is a ladder partner of $k$ and that $\Phi_v(\sigma) = \sigma + \tilde v k$; the ladder relations in both completions; the isometry identity for $\hat g$; the automorphism identity $\theta^{\mathsf{T}} h_u \theta = h_u$ with $\theta \neq \id$ and $\theta(k) = k$, for two directions $\lambda$ spanning radial and angular parts of $\ker k$; the contragredient $(\theta|_{T^*M})^{-\mathsf{T}}$ computed from $\theta$ by exact inversion and found equal to $\id - \lambda \otimes k$, then applied to three candidates, each translated by $-\lambda$ and none fixed; the fixed set of $\theta$, computed as a kernel, of dimension four and $h_u$-orthogonal to $k$ for both $\lambda$, so that no $\theta$-fixed ladder partner exists; the intertwiner at the opposite coupling sign $\mu\nu > 0$; and, for Remark \claimref{note.sheaf.scope-what-discharges}, the off-wall model in which the two ambient forms have different signatures at one collar point and different ranks at another. No floating point is used anywhere; the script exits nonzero on any failure, and all two hundred and ninety-six checks pass.
