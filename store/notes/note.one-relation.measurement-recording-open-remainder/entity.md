## formal
What the framework can say, under Premise \claimref{one-relation.recording-irreversible-stated-so-it-can}: after a measurement, interference between flag sectors is as inaccessible as inter-tower coherence, the superselection mechanism of the dark-towers paper operating in miniature, at laboratory scale (Fact \claimref{one-relation.imported-flag-arrow-superselection}); the step from unwritten records to inaccessible interference is the decoherence argument \cite{Zeh1970, JoosZeh1985}, and it is the first gap Proposition \claimref{one-relation.record-chain-votes-measurement-without-collapse} names and states with its cost. What it cannot yet say: why one outcome obtains rather than all persisting in parallel record chains. The recording is compatible with both readings empirically, and no present instrument distinguishes them. What the framework's own principles nonetheless say is Proposition \claimref{one-relation.record-chain-votes-measurement-without-collapse}.
