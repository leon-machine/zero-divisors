## formal
This paper is T3 of the series. It relies on two others. From the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} it imports the single transport step as matrix amplification with the twisted diagonal (Fact \ref{fact:step}), the minimality and uniqueness of the step (Remark \claimref{note.tower.why-tower}), the type-C diagnosis (Fact \ref{fact:typeC}), and the totality theorem used in the assembly of Section \ref{sec:reading}. From the sheaf paper T4 \cite{ButlerT4} it imports the wall package (Fact \claimref{tower.wall-package}), which converts the tower from a kinematic possibility into the geometrically forced ladder of the far side.

Downstream, the tower is used by the physical papers. T5 \cite{ButlerT5} computes its transport chain inside the algebra this paper certifies as canonical, with the capacity count exact; the mode continuum of T7 \cite{ButlerT7} and the Fock-module dictionary of T9 \cite{ButlerT9} build on the CAR limit and the one-qubit-per-rung theorems; and the matter papers T12 \cite{ButlerT12} and T14 \cite{ButlerT14} use the eight parallel towers and the frozen clock as the framework of their constructions.
