## formal
From here on we identify $V_\C$ with $\Lambda^2\T$ through the isomorphism $\Psi$ of Theorem \claimref{correspondence.spinor-identification} and write the quadratic form as $\ip{P}{P'} = \varepsilon(P \wedge P')$ without further comment. This section locates the entire chart structure of the completion inside the line geometry of $\PT$: the adjoined Witt pair is a pair of skew lines, the affine chart is the space of lines skew to one of them, and the chart map is Penrose's incidence relation.
