## formal
Two further results are proved here rather than imported; both are used in the body (Proposition \claimref{singularity.p-reduced-metric-level-reading-many}'s antecedent paragraph and Premise \claimref{singularity.l-boundary-mode-locality}) and both were developed alongside Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-no-equivariant-value-wall}. Throughout, the collar constants and bump are fixed and the completed mode bundle $\Aa$ of Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-one-sided-completion-singularity} is in place: its existence as an untwisted bundle over $E$ is exactly the standing hypothesis $w_1(\shL) = 0$, met here by $k = d\Phi$. Given $\Aa$, the ladder $(k, k^{*})$ and the parity $\sigma := k^{*}k - kk^{*}$ are global sections over $E$, and $\Aa_x \cong \Mat_2$ denotes the mode factor at $x \in E$ with parity automorphism $\alpha_x := \operatorname{Ad}(\sigma(x))$. Since $\sigma$ is quadratic in the ladder, $\sigma$, $\alpha_x$, the idempotent $\tfrac12(\id + \alpha_x)$ and everything built from them are insensitive to the sign of the ladder, which is what $w_1$ measures; nothing in this appendix depends on the standing hypothesis beyond the existence of $\Aa$ itself.
