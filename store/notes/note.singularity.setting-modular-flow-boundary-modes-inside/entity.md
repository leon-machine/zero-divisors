## formal
Two results that the chain's later readers supply are recorded here at their own level. The first follows the modular flow of Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-structure-completed-pair} from the horizon, where Proposition \claimref{singularity.plane-rate} fixes its rate, into the interior, where the geometry paper's weighted limit gives the collar an exact frame: the flow is the holonomy along the $t$-lines of the exceptional set, and the inverse temperature its Hubble-normalised rate would fix, for any KMS state of that flow on the collar, is a universal number, $6\pi$; whether such a state exists is left open. The second reads Premise \claimref{singularity.l-boundary-mode-locality} against the Lie algebra of the boundary mode space: the half of (L$_E$) that Theorem \claimref{singularity.class-relative-no-go-no-supplied} proves within the supplied class lies in the stabiliser of the vacuum, and of the half it premises, the pairing part is exactly the tangent space of the vacuum's orbit, the only part that can move a Witt decomposition, while the differential part of Remark \claimref{note.singularity.declared-residual-size} stays in the stabiliser.
