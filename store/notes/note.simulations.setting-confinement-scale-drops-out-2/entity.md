## formal
\textbf{The five-dimensional towers and the mirror.} A five-dimensional tower's matter counts through one cell radius $L$ of its extra direction, so its effective cross-section is $\sigma_{4}/L$ with $\sigma_{4} \sim \lambda^{3}$; in general $\sigma_{\mathrm{eff}} = \sigma_{D}\,d_{b}^{\,3-D}$. For $\lambda = 0.21$ to $2$ fm and $L = d_{b}/(1+z)$ this is $7 \times 10^{-20}$ to $6 \times 10^{-17}\ \cmg$: collisionless by geometry. The mirror's figure is that of Fact \claimref{simulations.imported-mirror}, $5 \times 10^{7}$ to $2 \times 10^{8}\ \cmg$: a fluid like our gas. Figure \ref{fig:sigma} shows the three.
