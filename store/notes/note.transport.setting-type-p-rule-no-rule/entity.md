## formal
Do non-central idempotents demand an ascent? The answer is that type P demands nothing, and the reason is a one-line identity whose \emph{absence} is precisely what made type N hard. We state the repair theorem in the exact form of the no-go of Section \ref{sec:nogo}, so that the two answers can be compared clause by clause; then we develop the structure theory that shows the triviality is a theorem about the idempotent variety, not an evasion of it.
