## formal
The rung of this world is $\Cl(2,4)$, obtained from $\Cl(1,3)$ by adjoining a hyperbolic pair, one generator of each sign, whose null combinations are the zero divisors the transport resolves (Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-towers-clock}, Definition \claimref{obj.odd-extensions.single-extension}). The frozen point of the dark-energy paper is a vector in that rung, and its norm is the cosmological constant. This section shows that $I$ and the Stelle-West vector $V$ are the ascent's pair, signed by $\Lambda$, and that the odd extensions of Section \ref{sec:single} are the intermediate algebras of this world's own ascent, with the degenerate case at $\Lambda = 0$ the wall of the sheaf paper.
