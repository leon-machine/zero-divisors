## formal
The pairing argument fixes which way the isomorphism runs, and the direction is easy to state the wrong way round, so it is worth recording. A nondegenerate pairing between $\shL^* \otimes \shL^*$ and $N^*\Sig$ identifies $N^*\Sig$ with $(\shL^* \otimes \shL^*)^* = \shL^{\otimes 2}$, and not with $(\shL^*)^{\otimes 2}$. The identifications, proved above and forced already by Lemma \claimref{sheaf.invariant-form-t3} (where $\delta : \shL^{\otimes 2} \to N^*\Sig$ lands in the conormal bundle by construction), are therefore: the \emph{dying line} squares to the conormal bundle, and the \emph{partner line} squares to the normal bundle. Everything downstream is stated in that form: the half-power rescaling $k \mapsto k/\sqrt t$, the weightlessness of the normalisation $kk^* + k^*k = 1$ (the two half-weights cancel), and the square-root form of the residue identity.
