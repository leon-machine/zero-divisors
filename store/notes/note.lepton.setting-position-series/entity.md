## formal
This note is a companion to T12 of the series and belongs with its Section 5. \emph{It relies on:} the Standard-Model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} for the hierarchy, the ladder theorem, the automorphism group and its section, the hypercharge generator, the tensor ledger and its no-lepton clause, the free sector and the anomaly Fact (Facts \ref{fact:ladder} to \claimref{lepton.imported-free-sector-anomaly-conditions}); the twistor paper T9 \cite{ButlerT9} for the Fock model and the pure-spinor theorem behind Fact \claimref{lepton.imported-twistor-state-pair-system-excitations}; and the dark-matter paper T14 \cite{ButlerT14} for the orientation bit that Proposition \claimref{lepton.relative-handedness-slotted-free-sectors} identifies. \emph{Papers that would rely on it:} T12 itself, if (C) is adopted in place of (S), and its companion note on the transport tower, which restates (S) as a hypothesis about the tower and prices (S) at Tier C; and through T12 the families paper T13 \cite{ButlerT13} and the neutrino paper T15 \cite{ButlerT15}, whose imports of the content would then rest on (C) rather than on (S).
