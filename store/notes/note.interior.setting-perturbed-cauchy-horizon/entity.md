## formal
Exact Kerr was never the hard part of the test. Since Kerr is Ricci-flat, Theorem \claimref{interior.covariant-form-vacuum-equations} applies verbatim on the whole vacuum region, inner horizons included: the parallel null tractor exists globally, and exact Kerr passes the covariance test outright. The physical question is what survives perturbation, because the Cauchy horizon is known to be unstable. The established results are imported with provenance and then read.
