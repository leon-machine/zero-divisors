## formal
The correspondence paper proves that a conformal spinor (a twistor) is a state of the completion's three ladder pairs, a pure spinor, and that each conformal spinor determines a Fock vacuum \cite[Thm.~5.3]{ButlerT9}. Under that identification the bracket $\br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}}$ is the overlap datum of the two constituents' vacua, and Proposition \claimref{mass.mass-symplectic-area-two-twistor-formula} acquires its structural reading: a composite is massless exactly when its constituents agree on a vacuum, and mass is the area of their disagreement. Mass, in this reading, is not a quantity intrinsic to one constituent but a relation: a misalignment within the composite, on the conformal spinor side; a grip on infinity, on the tractor side. That these are the same number is the join of the next section. The two-twistor description of massive particles is classical twistor theory \cite{PenroseRindler1986, Hughston1979}, read here on conformal spinors; what the series adds is the identification of the ambient objects on both sides of the pairing, which is what lets the join be computed rather than postulated.
