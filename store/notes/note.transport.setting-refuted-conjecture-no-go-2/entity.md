## formal
(The phrase \emph{minimal ascent} anticipates Theorem \claimref{transport.minimality}, which is logically independent of this section and proves the completion $\Cl(p{+}1,q{+}1)$ minimal and unique; the conjecture is quoted in the form in which it was originally posed.)

The conjecture is false, not merely in the minimal ascent but in every ascent however large, as soon as $\dim \mathbb{V} \geq 3$. The refutation is the first main result of this paper.
