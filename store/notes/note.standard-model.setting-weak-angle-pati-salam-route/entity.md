## formal
Remark \claimref{note.standard-model.what-chain-decides-what-it-leaves} leaves $\sin^{2}\theta_{W}$ as an input that fixes $M_{I}$. This section asks what that input demands of the fermion masses, and what the module offers in return. Everything here assumes Hypothesis (S). The couplings are those of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-spinor-bilinears}: Yukawa matrices of $10$s and of one $126$, symmetric in the family indices, and of one $120$, antisymmetric in them.
