## formal
Two comments locate the result within the series. First, the parabolic structures of this paper are singularity phenomena, not infinity phenomena: the unipotent ray of Proposition \claimref{interior.gauge-trajectory-unipotent-ray} and the Levi boundary holonomy of Theorem \claimref{interior.weighted-limit-theorem} concern the approach to $r = 0$, and Section \ref{sec:approach} proves ($\Lambda$-robustness, Theorem \claimref{interior.schwarzschild-de-sitter-weighted-limit}) that they survive $\Lambda \neq 0$ unchanged: under the weighted blow-up the Weyl data is order one while the $\Lambda$ terms carry positive weight and vanish at the boundary. Second, the rotation of the stabiliser is itself series data: the passage from a parabolic to a reductive stabiliser is exactly the passage from the obstructed situation of the no-go theorem (null direction, transvections, no canonical complement) to the unobstructed one (non-null direction, orthogonal complement splits); the cosmological constant is, in this precise sense, the parameter that removes the obstruction at infinity while leaving the obstruction at the singularity intact.
