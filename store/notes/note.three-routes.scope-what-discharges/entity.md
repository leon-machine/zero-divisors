## formal
The theorems are pointwise on $\Sig$ with every formula rational in the collar data, hence smooth in the point, and so hold over every open piece of $\Sig$; the collar constants $\mu, \nu$ and the bump are held fixed, their variation being a rescaling of the adjoined plane recorded in \cite[\S 4]{ButlerT4} and orthogonal to the question. Within that scope, the open item of T5 Fact 5.4's scope paragraph is discharged: the mechanism of \cite[Thm.~4.2]{ButlerT1} is not merely portable but ported, the affine candidate space and the fixed-point-free unipotent action both realised at the degenerate point, with the completion's own transversal-independence (Theorem \claimref{three-routes.change-transversal-canonical-isomorphism-completions}) supplying the equivariance that the sketch could only name. What remains for T4 is transcription: restating Theorems \claimref{three-routes.change-transversal-canonical-isomorphism-completions} and \claimref{three-routes.no-equivariant-value-boundary} as statements about its sheaves, for which every ingredient above is already sheaf-ready. Upon that transcription, T5's abstract sentence on classical repair may drop its scope qualifier.
