## formal
Note the division of labour between the two transports of the ascent. The inclusion $\Cl(p,q) \subseteq \Cl(p{+}1,q{+}1)$ transports \emph{elements}, and fixes nothing: a null $v$ remains null and, by Theorem \claimref{transport.no-go-level-elements}, remains without equivariant repair in the enlarged algebra. The map $X$ transports \emph{points}: it is quadratic, preserves no products, and is not an algebra map; and it is on points that the repair succeeds.
