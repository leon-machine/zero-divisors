## formal
The framework's Schwarzschild vacuum is the radial one: the extreme curvature eigenvalue distinguishes the pair of the radial plane and its dual, the sign of the square selects the radial member (Fact \claimref{pairing.imported-schwarzschild-connection-curvature}), the flag bit picks the outgoing or the ingoing null direction in it, and the chirality bit picks $m$. The computations below take $\ell$ outgoing, and Proposition \claimref{pairing.regular-frame-class-vanishes-horizon-changes} records what the ingoing choice gives instead; they are exact in the field $\Q(F)$, $F = \sqrt f$, $r = 2M/(1 - F^{2})$, at $M = \tfrac12$ (the singularity paper's device for keeping every coefficient rational), and in $\Q(\beta)$, $r = 2M/\beta^{2}$, for the regular frame.
