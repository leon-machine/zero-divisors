## formal
This paper is T14c of the series, a companion to the dark-matter paper T14 \cite{ButlerT14}, downstream of its Theorem 3.4 (types by residue), Proposition 4.1 (superselection), Theorem 4.3 (the mirror copy), Theorem 3.10 (no orientation bit in odd dimension) and its unit-ball rule on Premises E and R3 (Section 7 of T14 Version 2.1). It runs the construction of the Standard-Model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} in each tower, using the mass formula of the mass paper T11 \cite{ButlerT11}, the rungs and canonical relations of the tower paper T3 \cite{ButlerT3}, and, for comparison only, the families of T13 \cite{ButlerT13} and the neutrino sector of T15 \cite{ButlerT15}. Its physical tests take the simulations of the companion note T14b \cite{ButlerT14b} as given; the companion note T14a \cite{ButlerT14a} is upstream of nothing here. The null-cone statement for signature $(2,2)$ is proved here (Proposition \claimref{dark-tower-matter.sterile-kinematics}); the foundations paper T16 proves the corresponding statement for the timelike sets \cite{ButlerT16}. The capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17} records T14's predictions; Section \ref{sec:refute} lists what this note adds to them.
