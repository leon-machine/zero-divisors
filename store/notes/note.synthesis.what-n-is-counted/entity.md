## formal
Observation \claimref{standard-model.what}(i) rests on an indication that no paper of the series performed: that the boundary-mode count of the entropy paper, applied to the cosmological horizon, gives an entropy of order $N^{2}$ and makes $N$ the square root of the pair capacity. The application is now performed (Theorems \claimref{synthesis.de-sitter-entropy-pi-n-squared} and \claimref{synthesis.de-sitter-mode-count}). The entropy part is exact and needs no counting rule: $S_{\Lambda} = \pi N^{2}$. The capacity part holds under one hypothesis, that the cosmological horizon's modes are counted by the black hole's rule, and then $N = \sqrt{C/\pi}$, so the observation's reading is right up to the factor $\sqrt{\pi}$. The observations that build on (i), Observations \claimref{standard-model.why-large}, \claimref{standard-model.one-number-explain} and Conjecture \claimref{standard-model.largeness-number-universes-boundary-modes}, therefore rest on a computed count and one named hypothesis, the very application T12 left unperformed, rather than on an indication; their further claims (the tower reading of largeness, the dating of the descent) are untouched, except that where they make $N$ the number of the universe's boundary modes or put its boundary-mode space at order $10^{60}$, the count gives about $\pi N^{2}/\ln 2 \approx 4.8 \times 10^{122}$ modes, of which $N$ is the square root up to $\sqrt{\pi/\ln 2}$ (store/conventions.md, known limit 5). With $H = \sqrt{\Lambda/3}$, the reading the dictionary fixes, $N = M_{P}/H$ is about $1.0 \times 10^{61}$ and $S_{\Lambda}$ about $3.3 \times 10^{122}$; T12's ``of order $10^{60}$'' is the same number, low by about a factor of ten.
