## formal
The prior work is acknowledged once. The Koide relation is Koide's \cite{Koide1982}; its $45^{\circ}$ geometric reading is Foot's \cite{Foot1994}; the running-stability problem and its family-gauge resolution are Sumino's \cite{Sumino2009}; the Cabibbo square-root relation $\theta_{C} \approx \sqrt{m_{d}/m_{s}}$ is Gatto, Sartori and Tonin's \cite{GST1968}, its nearest-neighbour texture Fritzsch's \cite{Fritzsch1978}, and the democratic mass matrix Harari, Haut and Weyers' \cite{HHW1978}; the Gaussian-state technology (Takagi factorisation, Bogoliubov transformations, Wick's theorem for squeezed vacua) is textbook and is cited at Fact \claimref{families.imported-squeezed-vacuum-bosonic-pairing}. The relation itself is a forty-year-old empirical fact and no discovery of this paper. What this paper claims as new is the framework reading: the face-space geometry and the nullity reformulation at its declared level, the operator-level derivation of the rank-one-plus-shift skeleton from the Gaussian freezing, the sector-wide rigidity and closure theorems, the classification of the displaced-Gaussian class at its declared level, and the conversion of the relation, within the present theory, into a precision measurement of the frozen vacuum rather than a derived theorem. The condensation chain (the fixed bracket, the democratic alignment, the thirteenth refutation) is stated here at the series' standard with its levels declared.
