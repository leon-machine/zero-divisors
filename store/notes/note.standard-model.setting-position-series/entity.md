## formal
This paper is T12 of the series. \emph{It relies on eight predecessors, and on three later papers for imports its last sections name:} the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} for the standard of proof and the Fact (imported) discipline; the tower paper T3 \cite{ButlerT3} for the iterated pair structure and the rung (Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-pair-systems-two-chirality-halves}, Observation \claimref{standard-model.why-large}); the geometry paper T6 \cite{ButlerT6} for the frozen point, its norm and the tractor bundle (Facts \claimref{standard-model.imported-frozen-point-dictionary}, \claimref{standard-model.imported-tractor-bundle-spinor-bundle-their}); the Hawking paper T7 \cite{ButlerT7} for the derivation of the homogeneity $-2s-2$ (Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-twistor-charge-helicity}); the entropy paper T8 \cite{ButlerT8} for the boundary-mode-counting method that Observation \claimref{standard-model.what} borrows; the twistor paper T9 \cite{ButlerT9} for the Fock model, the chirality halves, the pure-spinor theorem, local twistor transport and the ordering shift (Facts \claimref{standard-model.imported-pair-systems-two-chirality-halves}, \claimref{standard-model.imported-twistor-charge-helicity}, \claimref{standard-model.imported-tractor-bundle-spinor-bundle-their}); the action paper T10 \cite{ButlerT10} for the two-kinds theorem, shared with this paper, the action, the registration $w = -1$ and the rigidity of the norm (Facts \ref{fact:twokinds}, \claimref{standard-model.imported-norm-frozen-point-not-field}); and the mass paper T11 \cite{ButlerT11} for mass-begins-at-two and the dictionary with its complex weights (Facts \ref{fact:mass}, \claimref{standard-model.imported-frozen-point-dictionary}); the tower paper T3 and the twistor paper T9 again for the two-rung module and the Fock model with its pairing (Facts \claimref{standard-model.imported-tower-commuting-copies-fock-module}, \claimref{standard-model.imported-fock-model-twistor-space}), and the mass paper again for the grip (Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-grip-area-casimir-form}); the action paper T10 again for the fourth alternative (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.trichotomy-what-kind-number}(4)); the families paper T13 \cite{ButlerT13} for the bound on the bracket's magnitude and for the Georgi-Jarlskog direction with its Clebsch (Facts \claimref{standard-model.imported-frozen-point-grip-thirteenth-refutation}, \ref{fact:gj}), and for that direction's up-type twin (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.equal-weight-module}); the dark-matter paper T14 \cite{ButlerT14} for the sector decomposition and the mirror copy (Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-sectors-superselection-mirror-copy}); and the neutrino paper T15 \cite{ButlerT15} for the neutrino's Dirac mass (Fact \ref{fact:neutrino}).

\emph{Papers that rely on this one:} the families paper T13 \cite{ButlerT13} imports the ladder and the mass bracket's transformation law (Propositions \claimref{standard-model.internal-symmetry-ladder}, \claimref{standard-model.mass-bracket-charged-singlet}), the algebra and content of Sections \ref{sec:algebra} and \ref{sec:content}, the trichotomy (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.trichotomy-what-kind-number}), the three-twistor bound of Remark \claimref{note.standard-model.sixth-refutation-ladder-not-gauge-group}, at the observation level that remark declares, and the module with its pairing, Yukawa pattern and family no-go (Lemma \claimref{standard-model.invariant-pairing-five-modes}, Theorems \claimref{standard-model.yukawa-pattern-two-rung-module}, \claimref{standard-model.family-no-go-vev-cannot-separate}), and, for its order of operations, the grade-three direction and the route the series registers for the second family's factor (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.grade-three-georgi-jarlskog-direction}, Remark \claimref{note.standard-model.what-section-settles-route-series-takes}); the dark-matter paper T14 \cite{ButlerT14} imports the chirality proposition and the trichotomy (Propositions \claimref{standard-model.chirality-two-halves-spinor-module-conjugation}, \claimref{standard-model.trichotomy-what-kind-number}), carries the orientation (successor problem (4)), and receives the answer to its partner-sector question, that no tower but this one contributes and the mirror inherits the desert (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.no-tower-but-one-contributes-towers}, Corollary \claimref{standard-model.under-s-mirror-inherits-desert}); the neutrino paper T15 \cite{ButlerT15} imports the Fock decomposition, the content, the hypercharge theorem and the global form (Propositions \claimref{standard-model.raw-representation-content-state-space}, \claimref{standard-model.quarks-leptons-two-level-fermion-content}, Theorems \claimref{standard-model.hypercharge-global-form-pair-counting-consistency}, \claimref{standard-model.global-form-quotient-determined}), and, for its valuation of the heavy scale, the unification scales of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.spectrum-run} and of the Pati-Salam chain with the requirement on the neutrino sector (Propositions \claimref{standard-model.chain}, \claimref{standard-model.what-angle-asks-neutrino-sector}); the foundations paper T16 \cite{ButlerT16} imports the forced connection (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.connection-forced}); and the capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17} tabulates this paper's claims at their tiers and carries the refutation narrative the remarks of this paper point to.
