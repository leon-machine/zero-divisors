## formal
As the series' practice requires, the points of contact are stated where they occur. The principal plane pair of Fact \claimref{interior.imported-kerr-curvature} is the eigenplane structure of a type-D Weyl operator (Petrov); the Killing spinor is the standard generator of Kerr's hidden symmetry (Carter's constant, symmetry operators for wave equations); and the identification of Proposition \claimref{interior.complex-scale-inverse-cube-killing-spinor} places the framework's conformal scale inside Penrose's twistor theory, which in geometric algebra is the theory of the conformal spinor space $\CS$ of $\Cl(2,4)$; its systematic development for the series (ascended spinors, incidence, the palatial bridge) belongs to the correspondence paper T9 \cite{ButlerT9}. Nothing in Parts one and two depended on these identifications; Part three's point is that the framework's internal objects coincide with them exactly.
