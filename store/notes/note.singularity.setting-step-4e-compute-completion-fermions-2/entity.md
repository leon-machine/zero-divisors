## formal
(Q) is a genuine fourth premise, and it must be recorded that it stands in explicit tension with the principal content of Steps 5 and 6b: for the given marginals the quasi-free state is the product member of Corollary \claimref{singularity.chains-state-class-stability}'s class, the unique member satisfying (G), and Theorem \claimref{singularity.exact-equilibrium-over-determines-boundary-modes} rejects exactly that member as carrying none of the correlations those steps need. What (Q) yields is therefore the \emph{equilibrium reading} of the flux: the Planck sector below is a statement about the stationary idealisation of the boundary modes, not about the evaporating hole, for whose state the exact formula fails, the sum acquiring connected inter-mode correlators that (P) and (R) leave free (Corollary \claimref{singularity.chains-state-class-stability}(3)). One consequence of the rate model belongs here rather than being discovered against it: under (I) and (L$_E$) the window marginal is exactly this product at every stage (Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}(1)), so for the model's mode space the product clause of (Q) holds as a theorem (the weight assignment, the stationarity and the $\mathbb{Z}$-array remain (Q)'s own), and the tension is relocated rather than dissolved: the product form empties the \emph{internal} correlations of the boundary modes, and the correlations Steps 5 and 6b depend on are carried across the window/radiation cut, to which Theorem \claimref{singularity.exact-equilibrium-over-determines-boundary-modes}'s rejection does not apply. Table \ref{tab:chain}'s status line for the step carries the premise.
