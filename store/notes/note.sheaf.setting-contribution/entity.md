## formal
The paper proves eight clusters of results, at the standard of \cite{ButlerT1} (Tier A of the series: complete proofs, each theorem carrying its own hypotheses).

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The degeneration sheaf} (Section \ref{sec:degeneration}). For a cometric of corank one along a tame hypersurface, the Clifford sheaf $\shA = \Cl(T^*M, G)$ is locally free of constant rank $2^n$ (Proposition \claimref{sheaf.local-freeness-constant-rank}, valid for arbitrary degeneracy); over the degenerate locus each fibre carries the canonical square-zero two-sided ideal generated by the radical line, with quotient the Clifford algebra of the induced nondegenerate form, and these ideals assemble into a coherent sheaf supported exactly on $\Sig$ (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.degeneration-sheaf}). Resolution is characterised ideal-theoretically: in any fibrewise nondegenerate Clifford completion the radical generates the \emph{unit} ideal (Proposition \claimref{sheaf.resolution-ideal-theoretically}); completion turns an infinitesimal into a dimension. The proof given needs no simplicity hypothesis, the hyperbolic partner exhibiting the unit directly, so the statement holds in the split signatures as well.
\item \textbf{One-sided completion} (Section \ref{sec:ambient}). For walls with trivial radical line bundle (the twisted case is posed as a successor problem), a rank-$(n{+}2)$ ambient bundle with a coupled form $h$, equal to $G \perp H$ outside a collar of $\Sig$, is nondegenerate of constant signature $(p{+}1,q{+}1)$ on the closed physical side; the subalgebra generated by $T^*M$ is canonically the degeneration sheaf, globally; and the localised ladder pair exists exactly over $\Sig$.
\item \textbf{The shadow wall} (Section \ref{sec:wall}). Conservation of degeneracy in two forms. Absolute: \emph{no} completion whatever that is standard outside a neighbourhood of the wall is nondegenerate everywhere, by a pure signature obstruction (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.conservation-degeneracy-absolute-form}), which is stronger than any ansatz-level statement. Located: within the coupling ansatz the degeneration lies exactly on the zero set of the determinant factor $F$, displaced to the unphysical side, with the signature passing from $(p{+}1,q{+}1)$ to $(p,q{+}2)$; for good couplings the shadow wall is itself tame and the infinite wall sequence iterates unconditionally (Lemma \claimref{sheaf.tame-wall-induction}, Corollary \claimref{sheaf.infinite-wall-sequence}). The cohomological wall-class question, and its relation to the conservation conjecture of \cite[\S7.4]{ButlerT1}, is posed precisely.
\item \textbf{The exceptional algebra and the residue} (Section \ref{sec:exceptional}). The $\sqrt{t}$-rescaled Clifford family through a tame wall converges to a nondegenerate special fibre built from normal-cone data, independent of all choices up to canonical isomorphism; the inverse cometric has a canonical simple pole at the wall whose residue is exactly the inverse of the invariant transversality datum of (T3), identifying $N^*\Sig \cong \shL^{\otimes 2}$: the dying line is a canonical square root of the conormal bundle, the partner line of the normal bundle. The identification fixes the direction of the twist (Remark \claimref{note.sheaf.direction-induced-twist}).
\item \textbf{Topology, dictionary and scope} (Section \ref{sec:dictionary}). A topological corollary of the residue theorem: tame walls are always two-sided, since $N^*\Sig \cong \shL^{\otimes 2}$ makes the normal bundle's $w_1$ zero; the construction's global choices are itemised with their exact obstruction classes ($w_1(\shL)$ the only genuine invariant); the wall class is posed as a precise conjecture whose coarse half Theorem \claimref{sheaf.conservation-degeneracy-absolute-form} already proves; and the relation to tractor bundles and the Fefferman-Graham ambient metric is developed at assembly level and flagged as such, with the anisotropic, higher-codimension and BKL-weight questions stated as successor problems, the first instance of which the next item executes.
\item \textbf{The Schwarzschild composite} (Section \ref{sec:schwarzschild}). The two failure modes that keep the Schwarzschild singularity outside the tame class, codimension three and anisotropy, are cleared together for the exact solution: the real oriented blow-up of the worldline absorbs the angular rates tautologically, and on a canonically weighted coframe module the residual degeneration is boundary-tame of corank one, with the Killing-time direction as its radical (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.schwarzschild-degeneration-boundary-tame}). The one-sided completion then ascends $\Cl(1,3) \to \Cl(2,4)$ on the whole blown-up manifold with the ladder pair supported exactly on the singular boundary and no shadow wall (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.one-sided-completion-singularity}); the weights that achieve this are forced, once the geometrically determined dying direction is named, and equal the Kasner exponents (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.weights-are-forced-are-kasner-exponents}), settling the Kasner-weights conjecture for Schwarzschild in the diagonal class.
\item \textbf{Uniqueness, the rain frame, the matching, and the anisotropic class} (Sections \ref{subsec:uniqueness}-\ref{subsec:weld}, \ref{sec:anisotropic}). The weighted module is the \emph{unique} rain-bounded locally free extension of the interior cotangent module that is boundary tame, by a determinant argument whose hypothesis must be read on the dying eigenvalue, not the dying entry, and whose anchoring class cannot be dropped: two explicit modules separate the readings (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.uniqueness-weighted-module}, Examples \claimref{note.sheaf.eigenvalue-essential} and \claimref{note.sheaf.rain-boundedness-essential-null-mixing-extension}). Physically, the exceptional coframe is exactly the dual coframe of the infalling orthonormal frame, with exact Minkowski Gram at every radius, and the gauge field carries the flat radial null dyad onto a physical dyad with constant unit components in it (Propositions \claimref{sheaf.rain-duality}, \claimref{sheaf.chains-radial-dyad-rain-finite}): the weighted module is the $u$-smooth shadow of what the falling observer measures finitely. The weighted limit of \cite{ButlerT6} is then matched to the present construction, at the fibre and at the connection level: its adapted frame consists of exceptional-module sections, its rates are re-derived here exactly, its limit is the weight one-form of the exceptional bundle, and its surviving matrix is a unit boost precisely by the second Kasner relation (Proposition \claimref{sheaf.matching-fibre}, Theorem \claimref{sheaf.matching-connection-level}). Finally the whole package extends to position-dependent Kasner exponents, over a variable-order function algebra, stably under the relative error structure that velocity-dominated asymptotics deliver, with sharpness exhibited at relative order zero (Theorems \claimref{sheaf.variable-weight-boundary-tameness-completion-forced}, \claimref{sheaf.stability-reduction-strict-diagonal-case}); the smooth structure of that algebra is characterised exactly, variable-order polyhomogeneous with unavoidable logarithms measured by the derivative of the exponents (Theorems \claimref{sheaf.differential-closure-filtration-conormality}, \claimref{sheaf.exponent-ratios-are-invariant}); and the mixmaster boundary is closed at the algebraic level by the collision dichotomy: positive-pair collisions pass through the Schwarzschild triple and are interior to the theory, while ordering changes force Taub edges on which the wall ends and across which no weighted module continues (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.edge-wall}, Remark \claimref{note.sheaf.mixmaster-wall-complex}).
\item \textbf{Transversal independence, and the no-value theorem at the wall} (Section \ref{sec:transversal}). The coupling uses a transversal, and the dependence is resolved exactly: a closed-form unipotent intertwiner identifies the completions built on any two transversals, the identifications compose (a cocycle), and the completion at the wall is canonically transversal-independent, preserving the form, the grading and the degeneration subalgebra though neither the frame nor the ladder pair (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.transversal-independence-wall}). On the same mechanism, the wall admits no equivariant classical value for the dying direction and no canonical ladder partner: the automorphisms of the completed structure translate the candidates freely (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.no-equivariant-value-wall}), so an analogue of the root paper's no-repair theorem holds at the wall itself, where that theorem's hypotheses fail. This is the boundary no-go the singularity paper imports verbatim.
\end{enumerate}
