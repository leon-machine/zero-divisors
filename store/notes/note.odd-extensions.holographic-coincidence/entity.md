## formal
Writing, as here, $\Cl(2,4)$ for the Clifford algebra of the flat space in which $\mathrm{AdS}_{5}$ is the hyperboloid, and likewise for the other hyperboloids: the rung is the algebra of $\mathrm{AdS}_{5}$, whose isometry group $SO(2,4)$ is the conformal group of this world; the intermediate $\Cl(2,3)$ is the algebra of $\mathrm{AdS}_{4}$ and, by Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.four-extensions-are-four-odd-towers}, the conformal ambient of the three-dimensional residue-$7$ shadow, the group theory of $\mathrm{AdS}_{4}/\mathrm{CFT}_{3}$; the de Sitter intermediate $\Cl(1,4)$ is the ambient of the Euclidean three-dimensional shadow $(0,3)$, also of residue $5$, the group theory of $\mathrm{dS}/\mathrm{CFT}$. All of this is the standard fact that the isometry group of $\mathrm{AdS}_{d+1}$ is the conformal group in $d$ dimensions, which any conformal field theory in $d$ dimensions shares whether or not a bulk exists; the note adds only the residues. The coincidence does expose a difference in what two frameworks say about one pair of algebras. Holography takes the bulk and its boundary to be one physics in two descriptions. The series takes the residue of a sector to be the signature of its soldered space (Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.belongs-tower}), which a conformal class carries as well as a metric, so the boundary of $\mathrm{AdS}_{4}$ has residue $7$ and lies, by Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-towers-clock}, in a tower distinct from the bulk's; whether a conformal class with no chosen scale is also a superselected sector, Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-copy-theorem-soldered-residue} speaking of the one metric, is a separate question and is not settled here; and the series' superselection is a statement about operators soldered to one metric, which a holographic dictionary is not. Whether that last difference means the two frameworks are speaking about different things, or that one of them is wrong about this pair, is not argued anywhere in the series and is not argued here; the remark records the question and claims nothing about the dynamical content of holography, to which the series has no counterpart.
