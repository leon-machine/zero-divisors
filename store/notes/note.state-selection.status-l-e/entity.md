## formal
(L$_E$) is a premise, not a theorem, and it is the true location of a genuine assumption: it says that the framework supplies no hopping generator such as $v_j^{*}v_k$ between distinct points of $E$. That is a statement about what the localisation theorems provide, and they do provide the ladder pair pointwise; it is not a proof that no such coupling can arise from a dynamics not yet written down. Recorded as a programme item in Section \ref{sec:open}. What Proposition \claimref{singularity.number-diagonal-dynamics-models-scrambling-derived} buys, if (L$_E$) holds, is worth the exposure: it converts the model's most questionable-looking choice, that the scrambling is phase-type, into the general case, and with it upgrades \cite[Lem.~12.1]{ButlerT5} from a statement about a particular circuit to a statement about the ledger.
