## formal
The synthesis does not by itself reach the laboratory. Nonrelativistic quantum mechanics postulates three things the framework must either source or concede: a preferred time split, an imaginary unit, and a mass term. Of these the section sources one outright (the mass, at the level its Fact holds), sources half of the first (the time orientation, under the Standard-Model paper's Hypothesis (S)), declares the other half of the first, the rest frame, unsourced, and proves that the second was not a separate postulate at all: given the law, the imaginary unit is the rest frame read through it (Lemma \claimref{one-relation.one-datum-not-two-imaginary-unit}). It begins with the statement that is proved outright, and returns after the descent to the three lemmas that sharpen it.
