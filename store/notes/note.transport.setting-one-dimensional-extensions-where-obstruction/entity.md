## formal
The physical algebras the series works with are even-dimensional and simple. Adjoining one direction makes them odd-dimensional, where the volume element is central and the type-C question arises. This subsection places the obstruction exactly among the one-dimensional extensions of an even-dimensional algebra, identifies its ideals, and shows that its Rule 2 repair by a generator of the opposite sign is the Rule 1 ascent of Section \ref{sec:typeN}: the two rules, compared in Remark \claimref{note.transport.2-pattern-two-rules-compared} as amplifications of the whole algebra and of an even half, coincide on every such extension, where the even half is the base. Throughout, write $c_u(x) = u x u^{-1}$ for conjugation by an invertible $u$.
