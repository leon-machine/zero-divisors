## formal
Four, recorded in one place. (1) P2.3 was registered as ``nonzero pairing class in the $n$-$m$ planes''; computed, the class of the complex loop $(n, m)$ is zero and the nonzero one is $(n, \mb)$ (Proposition \claimref{pairing.curvature-which-loops-move-vacuum}). The registration meant the real planes $n \wedge e_{2}$, $n \wedge e_{3}$, which do carry the class, but as worded the prediction was refuted, and it is so recorded. (2) P2.1 asks for proportionality to $\sqrt f/r$ and for a sign change across the horizon; no single frame delivers both, the first being established in the static frame (Proposition \claimref{pairing.static-frame-only-expansion-survives}) and the second in the regular one (Proposition \claimref{pairing.regular-frame-class-vanishes-horizon-changes}), and the two are tied by the covariance factor. (3) G1's sample point was registered as the singularity paper's necessity witness $(\tfrac35, \tfrac45)$; the computation uses its thermal sample point $e^{-\omega/T} = \tfrac{9}{16}$, $\tan\theta = \tfrac34$, for which the rotated null direction is rational; nothing depends on which. (4) The consequence drawn from K1.2 in the verdict below, that a holonomy reproducing the family mode by mode would need a loop selected by the mode, is not part of the registered condition; K1.1 and K1.2 as registered give the verdict on their own.
