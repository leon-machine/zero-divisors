## formal
At observation level, and flagged as such: if the pair mixing $U(3)$ were the colour group acting on the same ambient vector the frozen point lies in, Proposition \claimref{standard-model.frozen-point-pair-mixing} would say that mass breaks colour to a $U(2)$, which is false of the world. This paper does not make that identification: Proposition \claimref{standard-model.connection-forced} has the gauge algebra acting on a hierarchy bundle $\mathcal{H}$ of rank six, tensored with the local twistor bundle $\mathcal{S}$ on which the conformal connection acts, so the colour $SU(3)$ of Theorem \claimref{standard-model.gauge-algebra-minimal-massive-composite} acts on a factor to which the frozen point is insensitive. Section \ref{subsec:flags} shows that the identification is not available in the first place: the pair mixing of the twistor's own Fock space is neither a spacetime symmetry nor an internal one, so it cannot be colour, and the apparent tension is a confusion of two copies of $\C^{3}$. Nothing in Section \ref{subsec:blind} depends on it: Theorems \claimref{standard-model.moment-map-grip-mass-are-insensitive} and \claimref{standard-model.grip-mass-two-rung-module} use only that the frozen point lies in $V$ and that $\so(V_{\C})$ acts on the pair factor, which is true in either reading.
