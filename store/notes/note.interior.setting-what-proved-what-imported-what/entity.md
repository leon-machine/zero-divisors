## formal
\emph{Proved in this paper, at the standard of \cite{ButlerT1}:} the unipotent-ray proposition and the exact scale decoupling (Propositions \claimref{interior.gauge-trajectory-unipotent-ray}, \claimref{interior.exact-scale-decoupling}); the elementary tractor lemmas and the entire flat-model dictionary (Lemma \claimref{interior.metric-parallelism-trace-schouten-condition}, Propositions \claimref{interior.stabilisers-flat-model}, \claimref{interior.non-null-stabilisers-section-they-act}); the vacuum theorem, the $\Lambda$ theorem with its internal sign-fixing, and the stabiliser rotation (Theorems \claimref{interior.covariant-form-vacuum-equations}, \claimref{interior.scale-tractor-leaves-cone}, Lemma \claimref{interior.sign-fixed-internally}, Proposition \claimref{interior.stabiliser-rotates-parabolic-reductive}); the Kasner lemmas with explicit rates (Lemmas \claimref{interior.kasner-form-interior}, \claimref{interior.exact-parallelism-convergence-rate}, \claimref{interior.interior-connection-exactly}); the boost-generation lemma (Lemma \claimref{interior.boosts-generate-exactly-levi-factor}); the weighted limit theorem and its Schwarzschild-de Sitter extension (Theorems \claimref{interior.weighted-limit-theorem}, \claimref{interior.schwarzschild-de-sitter-weighted-limit}), with the boundary holonomy algebra in the values-level sense fixed by Definition \claimref{obj.interior.weighted-blow-up-hubble-normalisation-admissible}; the transport-level convergence theorem along tangential curves for the whole admissible class and its Schwarzschild $\varphi$-line corollary with rate (Theorem \claimref{interior.transport-level-convergence-along-tangential-curves}, Corollary \claimref{interior.schwarzschild-lines-exactly}); the Kerr-Schild unipotency and complex-factorisation propositions (Propositions \claimref{interior.unipotency-across-kerr-family}, \claimref{interior.complex-factorisation-location-ring}); and the elementary halves of Propositions \claimref{interior.complex-scale-inverse-cube-killing-spinor} and \claimref{interior.filtration-cauchy-horizon-read-literature}.

\emph{Imported as Facts with provenance:} the GTG-Einstein equivalence and the two curvature computations (Facts \claimref{interior.imported-equivalence-einstein-equations}, \claimref{interior.imported-schwarzschild-curvature-spectrum}, \claimref{interior.imported-kerr-curvature}); the tractor connection (Fact \claimref{interior.imported-tractor-connection}); the Kerr-Schild form (Fact \claimref{interior.imported-kerr-schild-form-kerr-gauge}); the Walker-Penrose spinor (Fact \claimref{interior.imported-walker-penrose-killing-spinor}); the Cauchy-horizon results (Fact \claimref{interior.imported-mass-inflation-stability}). Each is used only through its stated content.

\emph{Scoped and open:} the weighted limit theorem's scope is the admissible (Einstein velocity-dominated) class of Definition \claimref{obj.interior.weighted-blow-up-hubble-normalisation-admissible}, with the mixmaster delimiter of Remark \claimref{note.interior.generic-delimiter-mixmaster} marking the provable boundary; the duality-monodromy conjecture (Conjecture \claimref{interior.duality-monodromy-fibration-over-ring}) is posed, not proved; and the limits of the Kerr conclusion are itemised in Remark \claimref{note.interior.conclusion-two-cautions}.
