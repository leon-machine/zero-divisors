## formal
An idempotent $e \notin \{0,1\}$ is a genuine invertibility obstruction: $e(1-e) = 0$ exhibits it as a zero divisor, and it has no inverse in any unital extension (if $ex = 1$ then $e = e(ex) = e^2 x = ex = 1$). The transport question of Section \ref{sec:nogo}, transposed verbatim from the null cone to the idempotent variety, is therefore well posed: does some extension of the algebra carry an equivariant assignment of von Neumann pseudo-inverses to the idempotents?
