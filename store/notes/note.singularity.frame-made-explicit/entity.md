## formal
Which frame carries these statements deserves one sentence each, since the $\hb$-image frame is exactly what degenerates. The bivector $\sigma_r$ is defined in the orthonormal frame of the solution, and Theorem \claimref{singularity.curvature-gauge-field-exact-shape-decoupling}'s invariance display shows the radial \emph{plane} is the same whether computed in the flat background frame or the physical one: the plane is frame-robust precisely where the argument needs it, and no choice is introduced unstated. The sign of $\sigma_r$ (equivalently, which null leg of the plane is called outgoing) is an orientation convention; every appearance of $\sigma_r$ in this paper is either quadratic in it or explicitly sign-convention-flagged, and the verification script fixes one convention and checks every identity in it. The conversion of Proposition \claimref{singularity.plane-rate}, $2\pi$ of rapidity equal to $8\pi M$ of Killing time, is a statement about the Killing flow's boost in this plane; it is the only place in the chain where geometry is converted into a number carrying units, and Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-descent} is where that number becomes the temperature. One more frame fact, imported, settles the question whether the s-wave structure might exist only in coordinates that degenerate: the radial null dyad of this plane, read as covectors, is carried by the gauge field onto forms with \emph{constant unit} components in the infalling orthonormal frame, at every radius up to the wall \cite[Prop.~8.23, Rem.~8.24]{ButlerT4}.
The structure Steps 4d and 4e run on is exactly finite in the frame of the observer who actually crosses, even though the individual coordinate legs diverge.
