## formal
The bosonic sector's lift contains a graviton candidate (homogeneities $-6, +2$ in Assembly \claimref{hawking.helicity-lift-joints-itemised}), and a composite graviton must answer \cite{WeinbergWitten1980}: a theory with a Lorentz-covariant conserved stress tensor admits no massless spin-2 particle coupling to it. The evasion, recorded here as this paper's own claim and at Tier C: the theorem's hypotheses require the stress tensor and the spin-2 state to be defined on one and the same spacetime, and on the composite reading they are not; the covariant stress tensor of the boundary modes is defined in the two-dimensional reduced theory at the degenerate locus, the graviton candidate is a collective mode of the emergent four-dimensional description, and the hypotheses fail exactly where they fail for boundary gravitons in holography. The evidential triage, stated because the series states its own claims at their level: by \cite{Weinberg1964}, \emph{any} massless spin-2, composite or fundamental, couples universally to stress-energy, so the equivalence principle and the universal vertex are common ground and do not discriminate; what is distinctive to the composite reading is the ultraviolet behaviour and the $\ell_P^2/A$ correction of Remark \claimref{note.hawking.p-2-correction-interpreted}, and the discriminating experiments, if any, are at the scale of that correction. Below the Planck scale no available experiment discriminates the composite reading from the fundamental one, so the reading is at present untestable; this paper says so rather than presenting the absence of a refutation as support. The engineering no-go and its ontology-independence are assigned to the action paper T10 \cite{ButlerT10}.
