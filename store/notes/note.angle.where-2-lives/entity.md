## formal
Proposition \claimref{angle.value-parameter-dynamics} is the finite-model form of the singularity paper's rate rigidity: a hop selects an entangled state and a finite $\beta$, but the value of $\beta$ is whatever the couplings make it. The number $2\pi$ enters only through Fact \claimref{angle.imported-theorems-deliver-their-hypotheses}, whose abstract form needs a half-sided translation group with positive generator on the mode algebra: infinitely many modes, the translation acting by shifting them, the state its ground state, and the algebra of one side mapped into itself by the translations. A ${\mathbb{Z}}$-array with a hopping term and a filled sea is the discrete shadow of that structure; the theorem itself is a statement about its continuum limit and its analytic continuation, which exact arithmetic does not reach. The note reaches the necessity of the hop and stops there.
