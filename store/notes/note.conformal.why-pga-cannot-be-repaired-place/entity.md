## formal
A frequently asked question in the applied community is whether PGA's non-invertible elements (anything with a factor in the ideal $J$) could be given inverses by a clever convention. Proposition \claimref{conformal.nilpotent-ideal-becomes-unit-ideal} says what is possible and what is not: no extension makes a radical element invertible (its square is zero and stays zero), but the minimal completion makes the ideal it generates the whole algebra, so the obstruction is lifted at the level of ideals: what annihilated in PGA generates $1$ in CGA, through the identity $\rho\varphi + \varphi\rho = 2$ and not through an inverse of $\rho$. The repair is a transport, not an inverse: the same reading this series gives division by zero throughout \cite{ButlerT1}. And the minimality clause fixes the cost: exactly one hyperbolic plane per radical dimension.
