## formal
Facts \claimref{correspondence.imported-nonlinear-graviton} and Proposition \claimref{correspondence.no-real-lorentzian-vacuum-half-flat} together are the boundary of the correspondence: the nonlinear graviton globalises the completion's point space in the Euclidean and complexified sectors, where half-flatness is a nontrivial condition, and \emph{never} for a real Lorentzian black hole. Encoding the full, two-sided Einstein equations in data on the projective spinor space $\PCS$ (twistor data) is the googly problem, open in every formalism, including this one. What the transport framework does capture of the Lorentzian black hole's structure on the conformal spinor space (its twistorial nature) is the type-D structure: the Kerr conformal scale is the inverse cube of the Walker-Penrose Killing spinor \cite[Prop.~6.8]{ButlerT6}, a statement at the level of Section \ref{sec:incidence}'s dictionary, not of Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-nonlinear-graviton}'s globalisation.
