## formal
The step of Theorem \claimref{scrambling.epoch-modular-period-two-unknowns-become} is the framework's version of a familiar holographic practice: when a quantity is conditional on a regulator, derive the regulator from the system's own modular structure. The coherence checks are threefold: the causal epoch of Fact \ref{fact:ledger} is $O(M)$ with an identified logarithm, matching the modular period's scale; the count's rate is set by the horizon's own temperature, $dN/dt = \nu/\TH$; and the epoch three-volume comes out as $\beta^2\lP$, modular up to a single Planck factor. None of the three is a proof of (IC), and the third is the weakest, its power of $\lP$ the residue of geometry; together they are why the premise is worth flagging rather than discarding.
