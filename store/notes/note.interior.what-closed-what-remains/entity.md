## formal
Of the two halves of the edge-regularity problem, the transport-level half is closed by Theorem \claimref{interior.transport-level-convergence-along-tangential-curves} for every admissible metric, in the only reading in which a transport-level statement can hold (fixed Hubble-normalised length), and the $\varphi$-line clause is closed at Schwarzschild with its rate by Corollary \claimref{interior.schwarzschild-lines-exactly}. The tangential-regularity half is closed at Schwarzschild by symmetry and remains open in general: it asks for tangential derivatives of $E_{\tau}$, which admissibility does not control. The billiard-invariant limit of Remark \claimref{note.interior.generic-delimiter-mixmaster} is the generic-singularity extension and does not bear on any statement made at Schwarzschild.
