## formal
Propositions \claimref{hawking.current-commutator-exactly-edge-split} and \claimref{hawking.bose-einstein-occupancy-quantified-window-corrections} together are the quantitative form of T5's note on the level of its assembly joint. The charge-zero Fock isomorphism is exact for the infinite chiral fermion; for the physical boundary mode space of $L \sim A/\ell_P^2$ modes, every deviation is edge-supported (Proposition \claimref{hawking.current-commutator-exactly-edge-split}), and thermal expectations of the deviations carry explicit tails (Proposition \claimref{hawking.bose-einstein-occupancy-quantified-window-corrections}(3)). The corrections therefore vanish as the area grows, at a rate set by the window geometry: the relative scale of generic finite-size effects is read dimensionally as $1/L = \ell_P^2/A$ (for the discretisation of mode sums and the Sugawara normalisation; proving that scaling per named observable is successor problem 4), while edge-clean bulk correlators at fixed $m$ are exponentially smaller, at the proven rates. The quantum-gravity corrections are area-suppressed and vanish where general relativity is well tested; any new physics in this derivation would appear in the $\ell_P^2/A$ correction, and that correction is a prediction of the derivation rather than a defect in it.
