## formal
This paper is T14a of the series, a companion to the dark-matter paper T14 \cite{ButlerT14}, downstream of its Theorem 3.4 (types by residue), Theorem 3.5 (the soldered residue), Proposition 4.1 (superselection) and Theorem 4.3 (the mirror copy), and it says where that copy theorem stops: at the real form. It uses the tower paper T3 \cite{ButlerT3} for the clock, the transport paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} for why a single generator is not a transport, the twistor paper T9 \cite{ButlerT9} and the Standard-Model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} for the conformal completion and the twistor module, and the dark-energy paper T10 \cite{ButlerT10} for the frozen point, whose rank-five bundle $I^{\perp}$ is the physical occurrence of the single extension. A sibling companion note to T14 constructs the odd-dimensional twistor spaces and the odd towers' content under the reading in which all five generators are soldered; this note is upstream of nothing there and is consistent with it, the two reading the same odd algebras under different solderings, which Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.belongs-tower} assigns to different towers. Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.are-ascents-pair-signed} is downstream of T10's Theorem 3.1 and gives its parabolic-to-reductive rotation an algebraic form (Remark \claimref{note.odd-extensions.what-adds-dark-energy-paper}); Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.transport-constrains-frozen-point-only-through} runs the completion theorem of the projective-to-conformal paper T2 \cite{ButlerT2} on the wall of Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.are-ascents-pair-signed}(iii) and finds the cosmological constant to be the one modulus the transport leaves free, which the capstone should record beside T10's parameter-of-the-laws finding; Section \ref{sec:doors} adds that the frame's components are gauge by T10's Goldstone proposition and that the sign of $\Lambda$ does not select the visible tower. Section \ref{sec:lambdawall} runs the sheaf paper T4 \cite{ButlerT4} on the family over the $\Lambda$ line and finds it a tame wall completed in the class the conservation theorem excludes (Corollary \claimref{odd-extensions.two-classes-completion}), which T4's successor-problem list should carry. The capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17} should carry Corollary \claimref{odd-extensions.copy-theorems-blind-spot} as the statement of what the copy theorem does and does not identify above the shadow, Corollary \claimref{odd-extensions.cosmological-constant-deforms-wall} beside T10's entry, and Corollary \claimref{odd-extensions.not-coupling-between-towers} beside the odd towers' missing coupling rule, which this note leaves as missing.
