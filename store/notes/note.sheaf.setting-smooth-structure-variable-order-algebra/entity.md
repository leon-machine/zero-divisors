## formal
Definition \claimref{obj.sheaf.variable-order-algebra-strict-diagonal-data} introduced $\mathcal{A}$ by fiat, and it is natural to ask whether it is the function ring of a variable-order polyhomogeneous structure in the sense of \cite{Melrose1993}. This subsection answers the question. The answer is yes, at the cost of logarithms, and the cost is provably unavoidable: the algebra is stable under the boundary calculus in the radial direction, fails stability in the tangential directions by an exactly computable logarithmic term, and no admissible change of collar presentation removes the failure unless the exponent ratios are constant.
