## formal
Every computational identity in this paper, chain, gauge sector, state selection and model alike, has been checked by computer in exact rational arithmetic, in the manner of the verifications deposited with the earlier papers of the series \cite{ButlerT1, ButlerT3, ButlerT4}. The script \texttt{verify\_T05.py}, deposited with this paper alongside the series' shared exact-arithmetic library \texttt{GA.py} \cite{ButlerVerifyT05}, performs two hundred and thirty-three checks in nine kinds of exact arithmetic: the basis-blade Clifford product over the rationals, which also carries the point-level transport of Section \ref{subsec:lift} in $\Cl(2,4)$ with the vector slot represented; rational-function arithmetic over $\mathbb{Q}(r)$ for the Schwarzschild fibre, in which the scalar identities of the point-level transport (the null direction, the dual norms, T04's Gram and its dual) are verified as identities rather than at samples; arithmetic for the field $\mathbb{Q}(r)[\beta]$ with $\beta^2 = 2M/r$, in which the gauge sector is verified as identities rather than at samples; fermionic Fock-space arithmetic on a finite site array for the current algebra; a multi-qubit simulator with rational amplitudes (controlled-$Z$ scrambling contributes only signs) for the finite-mode model; rational-function arithmetic in the radial variable for the appendix, so that the curvature identities are verified as identities of rational functions; formal-logarithm arithmetic, in which the von Neumann entropy of a rational spectrum is an exact vector over the primes, identities between entropies are dictionary equalities, and inequalities are decided by rigorous rational bounds on the logarithms of the primes involved; exact truncated power series over the rationals, for the Hubble-normalised rates of Section \ref{sec:flowinside}; and Gaussian-rational arithmetic on the Jordan-Wigner Fock module, for the $\mathfrak{so}(2\nu)$ computations of the same section.
