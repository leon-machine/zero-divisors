## formal
The three lemmas that follow sharpen steps (2) and (3) of Proposition \claimref{one-relation.schr-odinger-slow-shadow} at proved level and do not touch step (4). Throughout, $\psi$ takes values in the even subalgebra $\Cl^{+}(1,3)$, Hestenes' spinor space, the general first-order law with a constant right factor is $\nabla\psi = m\,\psi\,C$, and left rotors and the pullback commute with any constant right factor, so covariance constrains nothing on the right; Lemma \claimref{one-relation.vector-derivative-covariant-first-order-operator} has fixed the left side.
