## formal
Premise (M) is valued, not derived. With $m_{3} \in (49.4, 50.5)$ meV, the scalar seesaw of Proposition \claimref{neutrino.rank-one-seesaw-ordering-sum-double}(0) gives $M_{3} = m_{D}^{2}/m_{3}$, and the Dirac eigenvalue is fixed only by the dark-matter paper's Hypothesis (U) (Fact \claimref{neutrino.imported-ceiling-under-hypothesis-u-arrow}): $M_{3} \approx 6 \times 10^{14}$ GeV if the neutrino's Dirac mass is retained unbroken like the top's ($m_{t} \in (172.28, 172.86)$ GeV, the PDG 2024 $172.57 \pm 0.29$ \cite{PDG2024}), $3.5 \times 10^{11}$ GeV if it is split like the bottom's ($m_{b} \in (4.13, 4.23)$ GeV \cite{PDG2024}), the two differing by $(m_{t}/m_{b})^{2} \approx 1.7 \times 10^{3}$, with a further order of magnitude of running systematic named there. The dark-matter paper's rung scale is the same quotient under the same hypothesis from the same datum, so the two papers agree by construction and not by test; deriving $M$ from the framework's own dynamics, which would turn the agreement into a check, is successor problem (3). $M_{3}$ lies below a unification scale taken at $M_{U} = 2 \times 10^{16}$ GeV by a factor of $33$ to $34$ at the top of the range and about $5.7 \times 10^{4}$ at the bottom (script-bracketed at that $M_{U}$; the factor scales with it). That $M_{U}$ is the Standard-Model paper's \emph{conditional} branch, the meeting of the couplings with partner-type intermediate content that it does not derive. Its other branch, an intermediate Pati-Salam symmetry from the split between slots and pairs, meets at $M_{U} \approx 3.4 \times 10^{16}$ GeV, above $M_{3}$ at either end, with an intermediate scale $M_{I} \approx 1.9 \times 10^{10}$ GeV \cite[Prop.~10.10]{ButlerT12}. On the derived content alone the couplings do not meet: the unification condition with the Standard-Model coefficients puts $M_{U}$ near $7 \times 10^{14}$ GeV, at which the factor at the top of the range is $1.16$ to $1.19$ to two places (script-bracketed at $(1.159, 1.192)$), while the pairwise meetings themselves fall at about $10^{13}$, $2 \times 10^{14}$ and $10^{17}$ GeV \cite[Prop.~6.2]{ButlerT12}; the lowest of these is at this paper's own $\alpha \approx 9 \times 10^{12}$ GeV at the top of Hypothesis (U) (Remark \claimref{note.neutrino.branch-m-3}; at the bottom, $\alpha \approx 5.1 \times 10^{9}$ GeV, it lies three orders above), the ceiling Premise (R) imposes on the descent, so the consistency consequence of Proposition \claimref{neutrino.leptogenesis-suppressed-alignment-reheating-premise-new}(3) is confined to the two branches on which the couplings meet and is claimed only there. On the Pati-Salam branch the measured weak angle fixes the triplet vev, $v_{R} \approx 4.2 \times 10^{10}$ GeV, and with it the Dirac mass along the heavy direction, $m_{D} = 1.44\sqrt{f_{J}}$ GeV for a Majorana coupling $f_{J}$ there \cite[Prop.~11.2]{ButlerT12}: the bottom-like end of the range above, since a top-like $m_{D}$ would need $f_{J}$ near $4000$. This paper keeps both ends, its valuation resting on Hypothesis (U) and not on that branch; the series' Pati-Salam route selects the bottom one. On that branch $M_{I}$ lies between the two values of $\alpha$, above the bottom one and below the top one, so whether Premise (R) keeps the descent below the intermediate scale depends on the end.
