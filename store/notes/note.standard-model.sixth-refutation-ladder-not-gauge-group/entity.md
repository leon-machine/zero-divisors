## formal
The natural conjecture, that the ladder of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.internal-symmetry-ladder} is the Standard Model gauge group, fails structurally, and for the sixth time in the series (the capstone keeps the count \cite{ButlerT17}) the failure makes the question more precise. The ladder's three groups act on \emph{different sectors}, indexed by constituent number, whereas $SU(3) \times SU(2) \times U(1)$ acts on all matter simultaneously as a direct product. What survives the refutation is the architecture: a fundamental matter unit built of up to three conformal spinors, each itself the vacuum of three pairs (Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-pair-systems-two-chirality-halves}), carries a two-level hierarchy of pairs, and the corrected conjecture is that the physical gauge group is the automorphism group of that hierarchy: the mixings respecting both levels of the partition. Section \ref{sec:algebra} proves the corrected conjecture's algebra and determines its global form; the representation content is Section \ref{sec:content}'s. Everything in this remark past the two propositions is at observation level, and is flagged as such.
