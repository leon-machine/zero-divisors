## formal
Fact \claimref{sheaf.weighted-limit}'s admissibility fixes \emph{constant} Kasner exponents, so Theorem \claimref{sheaf.matching-connection-level} matches the routes for the constant-exponent admissible class, Schwarzschild included. The variable-exponent generalisation of the weighted limit theorem, the connection-level analogue of Section \ref{sec:anisotropic}, is the single remaining ingredient of a fully matched singular boundary; its Gram-level half is Section \ref{sec:anisotropic}, and its connection-level half is a Duhamel argument in the variable-order structure, expected but not attempted here.
