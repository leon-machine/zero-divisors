## formal
The foundations paper proves the causal criterion $\min(p, q) = 1$, a definition in Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.towers-are-residues-single-clock-filter}, as a theorem about the cone: $\{Q > 0\}$ and $\{Q < 0\}$ have the components of $S^{p-1}$ and $S^{q-1}$, so a future/past split exists exactly in the causal signatures, while $(2,2)$ has an invariant ratio and no arrow \cite[Lem.~4.4(iv)]{ButlerT16}. This does not close the fork of Corollary \claimref{dark-matter.which-towers-can-carry-arrow}, sterility being about population; it prices it. Under Hypothesis (S) the descent's bit is read twice, as the asymmetry sign and as the arrow (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.orientation-arrow-seventeenth-refutation}), and the foundations paper proves the two readings inseparable at $\Cl(1,3)$ level under the same (S) \cite[Cor.~5.7]{ButlerT16}. On the $(2,2)$ fork the bit exists (Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.flag-bit-exists-exactly-tower-dimension}) but the arrow does not, so the Kleinian Standard Model would be oriented but timeless: a sharper, algebraic cost than the imported two-time instability.
