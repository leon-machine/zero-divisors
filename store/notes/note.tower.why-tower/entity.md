## formal
Rule 1 is the only rule that iterates: by \cite[Prop.~7.4]{ButlerT1} the fusion rule occurs at most once along any transport history and a pure Rule-1 lineage stays fusion-free, so the minimal repeated completion is exactly Definition \claimref{obj.tower.tower}. Minimality and uniqueness at each step are \cite[Thm.~4.13]{ButlerT1}, so the tower is the canonical iterated repair, not one choice among many.
