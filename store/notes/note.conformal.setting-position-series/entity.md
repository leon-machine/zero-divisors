## formal
This paper is T2 of the series. It relies on two papers of the series. From the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} it takes the element-level lemma that a square-zero element is invertible in no unital extension, the type-N reading of a radical direction and the taxonomy frame of Remark \claimref{note.conformal.relation-transport-rules}, and the representation-independent sharpenings whose scope Remark \claimref{note.conformal.what-minimality-promises-what-it-does} delimits. From the sheaf paper T4 \cite{ButlerT4} it takes the blade-basis lemma for degenerate forms (Fact \claimref{conformal.blade-basis-persists-under-degeneracy}), together with the corank-one square-zero ideal and resolution mechanism of which Proposition \claimref{conformal.nilpotent-ideal-becomes-unit-ideal} is the finite-dimensional, any-corank instance.

No later paper of the series depends on this note's theorems: it is the applied-audience presentation of results whose general versions are proved in T1 \cite{ButlerT1} and T4 \cite{ButlerT4}, and the status table of the capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17} records it on that basis.
