## formal
This paper is T15 of the series. \emph{It relies on six predecessors:} the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} for the transport principle and the series' method; the dark-energy paper T10 \cite{ButlerT10} for the registered $w = -1$ with its condition (Fact \ref{fact:wminusone}); the mass paper T11 \cite{ButlerT11} for the exact join, with its massless locus in place of a floor, and the bracket's dimensions (Fact \ref{fact:join}); the Standard-Model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} for the derived content, the hypercharge per grade, the mass bracket's quantum numbers, the unification scale on its conditional branch and the unconditional pairwise meetings (\cite[Prop.~6.2]{ButlerT12}, at that paper's assembly level, cited inline in Remark \claimref{note.neutrino.valuation-m-both-ends-hypothesis-u}), the Pati-Salam chain's scales and the requirement it places on the neutrino sector (\cite[Props.~10.10, 11.2]{ButlerT12}, at record level, inline in the same remark), and Hypothesis (S), on which this paper's doublet content depends and which it records as open (Facts \claimref{neutrino.imported-derived-content-singlets}, \claimref{neutrino.imported-mass-bracket-quantum-numbers}); the families paper T13 \cite{ButlerT13} for the indifference instrument, the faces, the democratic alignment and its registered open problem (Fact \claimref{neutrino.imported-indifference-faces-democratic-alignment-rank}, Observation 7.1 there); and the dark-matter paper T14 \cite{ButlerT14} for the ceiling range under Hypothesis (U), the arrow as a declared stance with Premise P4, the flag covariance, and the reheating assumption (Fact \claimref{neutrino.imported-ceiling-under-hypothesis-u-arrow}).

\emph{Papers that rely on this one:} the dark-matter paper T14 \cite{ButlerT14} cites the sixteenth refutation (Proposition \claimref{neutrino.seesaw-what-gauge-invariance-proves-what}(1), whose scope is the cosmological term of the deformed bracket, not a floor) and the flag covariance of the $\nu_{R}$ identification, $\nu_{R}$ being the hypercharge-zero singlet at grade zero of T12's listing (Proposition \claimref{neutrino.empty-twistor}); its seeding appendix's reheating is read here at the lower heavy scale (Premise (R)), about two to five orders below the top of the range across which that appendix quotes its numbers, so the numbers quoted at the top of that range do not apply on this paper's branch; the mass paper T11 \cite{ButlerT11} refers the massless locus of its join to this paper; the families paper T13 \cite{ButlerT13} refers to this paper the neutrino exemption from its Koide relation, whose mechanism (seesaw-composed masses) holds under Premise (M); the capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17} tabulates this paper's claims at their tiers, carries the sixteenth refutation in its refutation table at the scope of Proposition \claimref{neutrino.seesaw-what-gauge-invariance-proves-what}(1), numbers the framework's stances (the joint prediction fourth, the phase fifth), and maintains the series-wide table of falsifiable claims of which Section \ref{sec:bets} is the neutrino sector's entry; what it takes from this paper is the refutation at that scope (the cosmological term, not a floor), the seesaw as premised under (M) rather than derived, the empty twistor at grade zero, the branch of Remark \claimref{note.neutrino.branch-m-3}, and Premises (M), (H), (R) with the results that depend on each.
