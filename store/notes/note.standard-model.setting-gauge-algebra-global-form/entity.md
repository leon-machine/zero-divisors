## formal
The corrected conjecture of Remark \claimref{note.standard-model.sixth-refutation-ladder-not-gauge-group} names a group: the automorphisms of the two-level hierarchy. This section computes it, in Lie algebra and in global form, and finds the Standard Model's, exactly and in the smallest of its global forms. Throughout, an \emph{automorphism of the hierarchy} $\C^{m} \otimes \C^{k}$ is a unitary of the product that preserves the tensor factorisation, acting as $A \otimes B$ with $(A, B) \in U(m) \times U(k)$; this is the definition of respecting both levels of the partition (twistor slots and pair slots), and it is also, for $m \neq k$ and $m, k \ge 2$, the full symmetry of the decomposable states, by Lemma \claimref{standard-model.segre-lemma-automorphisms-preserving-decomposable-tensors}; for $m = k$ the swap that lemma notes is excluded here by the requirement that twistor slots and pair slots are distinguished levels of the partition.
