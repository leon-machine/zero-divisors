## formal
\textbf{Film 1: the merger, three ways.} \file{merger\_three\_ways.mp4} ($16$ s, $1920 \times 1080$), titled for a general audience ``Galaxy clusters in collision: testing three ideas about dark matter'', shows CDM, reading (W) with random normals and reading (C) side by side from approach to $1.27$ Gyr, labelled ``Standard cold dark matter: none of it collides'', ``Unit-ball rule: mirror and 3D-tower matter collide'' and ``Unit-ball rule: only mirror matter collides''.

The subtitle states that collisions are within a tower's own matter and that different towers interact only through gravity (Fact \ref{fact:stable}). Collisionless matter appears only in the total-mass contours, so the CDM panel has no orange or green layer and the right-hand panel no green one; the film's legend calls the sheet fluid ``colliding 3D-tower matter''. A panel beside the chart explains the Bullet-like separation.

Below the maps a chart, with its own legend, follows the subcluster's own mass (Figure \ref{fig:retention}), with a cursor at the current time. The film runs without pauses; reading (C)'s Bullet-like separation, $1.17$ Gyr, falls at $15$ s. The camera is centred between the two clusters of the CDM run and its width follows their separation; positions are interpolated linearly between snapshots at three frames per interval.
