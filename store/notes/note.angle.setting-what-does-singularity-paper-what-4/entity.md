## formal
\textbf{What it does not do.} It does not derive $2\pi$; Remark \claimref{note.angle.where-2-lives} says where the number lives and why the finite model cannot reach it. It does not say whether nature's dynamics on the singularity paper's boundary carries a hop; that remains the residual, now stated once. It does not move the modes: the singularity paper attaches them to the exceptional set and calibrates at the horizon, and Proposition 7.25 of that paper stands. And it does not touch the vacuum of the spinor fibre at a point, the null direction a Witt decomposition of the fibre selects: the selection rule that exists, positivity of energy, is a property of a generator acting across the cut, and no datum at one point constrains a correlation between two.
