## formal
The theorem completes the argument announced in Remark \claimref{note.interior.parabolic-structural-reason}. The stabiliser of the covariantly constant ideal point is the parabolic with Levi factor $\mathfrak{so}(1,3)$ and nilradical $\R^4$ (Proposition \claimref{interior.stabilisers-flat-model}); the weighted limit gives nonzero limiting values in the boost part of the Levi factor, which generates it, and zero in the nilradical, which consists of the very shear directions whose transvection action obstructed the element-level completion in \cite[Thm.~4.2]{ButlerT1}. Three appearances of one structure are now related: the parabolic obstruction of the no-go theorem, the unipotent gauge trajectory of Proposition \claimref{interior.gauge-trajectory-unipotent-ray}, and the Levi boundary holonomy of Theorem \claimref{interior.weighted-limit-theorem}. At the singularity the limiting connection lies purely in the Levi factor of the stabiliser of a frozen ideal point.
