## formal
\textbf{Topology.} Since Section \ref{sec:totality} makes claims about limits and continuity, we fix the topology once. Give $\mathbb{V} \oplus H \cong \R^{n+2}$ its standard topology, the projective space $P(\mathbb{V} \oplus H) = (\mathbb{V} \oplus H \setminus \{0\})/\R^{\times}$ the quotient topology, and $\mathcal{Q} \subset P(\mathbb{V} \oplus H)$ the subspace topology. Real projective space is compact and Hausdorff; the condition $N^2 = 0$ is well defined on rays (it scales by $\lambda^2$) and cuts out a closed subset; hence $\mathcal{Q}$ is compact and Hausdorff. The quotient map $\pi : N \mapsto [N]$ is continuous on nonzero vectors, and the lift $x \mapsto X(x)$ is polynomial, so $x \mapsto [X(x)]$ is continuous $\mathbb{V} \to \mathcal{Q}$. (The read-out map of Lemma \claimref{transport.descent-read-out-map}, which runs in the opposite direction, is written $\dsc$ and should not be confused with $\pi$.)
