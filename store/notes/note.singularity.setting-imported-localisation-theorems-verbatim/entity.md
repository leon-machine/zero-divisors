## formal
The two theorems of T4 \cite{ButlerT4} that carry the localisation weight at Steps 2, 3, 4a, 6a and 6b, the no-value theorem at the wall used at Step 3, and the function-linearity principle used by Appendix \ref{app:threeroutes}, are reproduced here in full, hypotheses included, so that this paper can be refereed without the sheaf paper on the desk. The notation is that of T4 \cite{ButlerT4}, aligned with this paper's where the two differ: $\shW$ is the weighted coframe module on the real oriented blow-up $\Mt$ of the singular worldline, $E \cong \R \times S^2$ its boundary, $m = \sqrt{2M}$, $\rho$ the weighted radial coframe element, $(\nO, \nInf)$ the adjoined hyperbolic plane, and T4's $dt$ and $dt_{\mathrm{S}}$ are written $dt_{\mathrm{PG}}$ and $dt_s$ here. The proofs of Facts \ref{fact:schwtame} and \claimref{singularity.imported-one-sided-completion-singularity}, and the machine verification of every computational identity in them (seventy checks, exact rational functions in $u = \sqrt r$), are Section 8 of T4 \cite{ButlerT4}; Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-no-equivariant-value-wall} is proved in its Section 10 (thirty-four checks on the same boundary data), and Fact \ref{fact:functionlinear} in its Section 9. Two further alignments concern Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-no-equivariant-value-wall}: that paper's wall $\Sigma$ is the boundary $E$ here, and its coframe module $T^*M$ at the wall point is the weighted module $\shW$ at a point of $E$, on whose frame Gram, the matrix of Fact \ref{fact:schwtame}, the machine checks in fact run. The coupling constant $\nu$ of Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-one-sided-completion-singularity} is T4's and is unrelated to the cell density $\nu$ of Axiom (C2).
