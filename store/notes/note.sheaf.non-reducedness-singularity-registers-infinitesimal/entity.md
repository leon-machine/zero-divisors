## formal
The interpretation deserves emphasis, because it is the central idea of the paper. In algebraic geometry, the special fibre of a degenerating family typically acquires nilpotents: the scheme becomes non-reduced, and the nilpotents record infinitesimal directions that no longer correspond to actual points \cite[Ch.~II]{Hartshorne1977}. Theorem \claimref{sheaf.degeneration-sheaf} exhibits the same phenomenon literally rather than by analogy: the direction along which the metric degenerated survives in the singular fibre only as an infinitesimal, an element all of whose products through the entire algebra square to zero. And $\shJ$ is the direct analogue of the object a blow-up is performed along: a canonical sheaf of ideals supported on the singular locus \cite[\S II.7]{Hartshorne1977}, transcribed from the structure sheaf to the Clifford sheaf. The ideal-theoretic characterisation of what resolution must accomplish is available in one line, and is the next proposition.
