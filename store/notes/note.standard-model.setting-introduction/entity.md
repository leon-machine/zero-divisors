## formal
The mass paper of this series proves that mass first exists at two twistor constituents: a single twistor is null, and a two-twistor composite's mass is set by the symplectic area of its constituents' misalignment \cite{ButlerT11}. This paper asks what symmetries such composites carry, and finds that the question answers most of particle physics. The internal mixings of an $n$-twistor system form exactly $U(n)$; the minimal \emph{massive} hierarchy, two twistors of three ladder pairs each, is $\C^{2} \otimes \C^{3}$; and the automorphisms of that tensor structure have Lie algebra $\su(2) \oplus \su(3) \oplus \uu(1)$, dimension $4 + 9 - 1 = 12$: the Standard Model's, exactly. The global group comes out $S(U(2) \times U(3))$, the full $\mathbb{Z}_{6}$ quotient, the smallest of the Standard Model's global forms; and conversely (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.four-dimensions-forces-converse}(2)) the Standard Model algebra is the automorphism algebra of a two-factor hierarchy only for a two-by-three one. The fermion content is the quark doublet at the pair level, unconditionally, as the cells of the hierarchy's own state space, and the lepton doublet one level higher in the hierarchy under one declared hypothesis, that the twistor slots are fermionic modes like the pairs; without it the state space contains no lepton at all, and the paper proves that too. Under that hypothesis the object carrying the states has a thirteen-dimensional automorphism algebra, one abelian generator more than the twelve; in the action the state space carries, the extra generator is anomaly-free, the $U(1)_{X}$ of $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$, and the right-handed neutrino's Majorana mass breaks it (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor}(3)): the twelve are what survives below that scale. Colour neutrality of the lepton is then the exact statement that a lepton is a complete twistor, its pair system full, while a quark is a fractional one. Hypercharge is the weight of the global form's own abelian generator, and pair counting reads it off: one pair has weight one unit, so the quark doublet is at $Y = 1/6$; the single-twistor Fock space, listed by one handedness, gives the four singlets at $Y = 0, -2/3, 1/3, 1$ (a computation on that Fock space, which is not a subspace of the tensor state space; under the hypothesis it is the state space's slot-free row); and under the slot-statistics hypothesis a slot with its pair system full has weight minus three, so the lepton doublet is at $Y = -1/2$. Three anomaly conditions then hold as identities, the fourth confirms the orientation, and, given that content, they admit no other spectrum. Chirality is the handedness of the two halves of the single-twistor spinor module $\Lambda^{\bullet}W$, and under the hypothesis the parity of the five-mode state space, whose even half is the generation as the half-spinor of $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor}). The connection is forced, not chosen. And the numerical layer compresses. The mixing angle at the unification scale, over that generation, is $\sin^{2}\theta_{W} = 3/8$, a value that rests on the hypothesis with the lepton (without the lepton the ratio is $9/26$); the one-loop run from the scale where the couplings meet (the structural scale, $M_{U}$ below) refutes the desert and demands that the running between the scales change, either by partner-like content or by a gauge symmetry enlarged at an intermediate scale, and the composite's split between slots and pairs supplies the second (Section \ref{sec:split}); and the framework's three smallness problems are powers of the single largeness $N = M_{P}/H$, which the paper's closing trichotomy classifies: under the action as written, with the frozen point's norm fixed before variation, $N$ is a parameter of the laws (an import from the action paper); under the promotion of that norm to a Lagrange-multiplier field, a decision the series records open, it is initial data of the descent, a state-count fixing the universe's own boundary-mode count (about $\pi N^{2}/\ln 2$) rather than a coupling. The exhaustiveness of the alternatives is not proved.

Seven of this paper's results began as natural conjectures that failed and were refuted into better questions, and each failure is carried as a result that makes precise the question the next result answers. The refutation narrative itself belongs to the capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17}, which numbers these seven as the sixth, seventh, eighth, tenth, eleventh, twelfth and fourteenth of the series; here the results are stated neutrally and the remarks say which conjecture each one refuted. The paper is Tier B for its unconditional results: the algebra, the quark doublet, the singlet spectrum, the anomaly arithmetic given a content, the run and the compressions are proved or derived at the series' standard from named premises; the identification of the derived structures with the observed forces is declared at observation level wherever it occurs; and the registered stances (the $\mathbb{Z}_{6}$ charge lattice, the refutation of the desert) are flagged with their falsification conditions. The results that rest on the slot-statistics hypothesis, the lepton doublet, the common handedness of doublets and singlets, the $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ spinor and the values $3/8$ and $3/4$, are proved from that hypothesis at the same standard and carry its level, that of a declared and underived structural premise: until the hypothesis is derived or registered with refutation conditions (successor problem (6)), those results are conditional, and the list of levels in Section \ref{sec:ledger} sets them apart.
