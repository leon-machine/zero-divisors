## formal
Each part of the paper relies on named prior art, and the boundaries are declared here once. \emph{Gauge theory gravity} is due to Lasenby, Doran and Gull \cite{LDG1998}, with the textbook treatment in \cite{DoranLasenby2003}; the field equations, the Newtonian (Painlev\'e-Gullstrand) gauge, and the Kerr-Schild forms used below are theirs, and the curvature computations we import are so marked. \emph{Tractor calculus} goes back to Thomas and was formalised by Bailey, Eastwood and Gover \cite{BEG1994}, with the modern treatment in Curry-Gover \cite{CurryGover2018}; the almost-Einstein characterisation (Einstein metrics as parallel scale tractors) is Gover's \cite{Gover2010}, and Theorem \claimref{interior.covariant-form-vacuum-equations} is that characterisation put to a new use: the reading of the GTG vacuum equations as the covariant constancy of an ideal point of the series' completion, and the identification of the parabolic stabiliser structure with the obstruction structure of \cite{ButlerT1}, are the contributions claimed, not tractor calculus itself. The \emph{Kasner} form of the Schwarzschild interior is classical (Kantowski-Sachs), and the velocity-dominated class descends from Eardley-Liang-Sachs \cite{ELS1972} and Isenberg-Moncrief \cite{IsenbergMoncrief1990}; BKL oscillations and the billiard description are \cite{BKL1970, DHN2003}; the weighted limit theorem and its holonomy identification are new. On the Kerr side, the Kerr-Schild form is \cite{KerrSchild1965}, the Killing spinor is Walker-Penrose \cite{WalkerPenrose1970} with Carter's constant \cite{Carter1968}; mass inflation is Poisson-Israel \cite{PoissonIsrael1990}, the $C^0$ stability of the Kerr Cauchy horizon with generic curvature blow-up is Dafermos-Luk \cite{DafermosLuk2017}, and the finiteness of tidal distortion is Ori \cite{Ori1991}. What part three adds is the reading: the location of the framework's own criteria inside these results, and the conclusion they jointly support about the ascent.
