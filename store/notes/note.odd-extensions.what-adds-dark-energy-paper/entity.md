## formal
The dark-energy paper proves the parabolic-to-reductive rotation of the stabiliser at $\Lambda \neq 0$ at the level of groups. Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.are-ascents-pair-signed} is the same rotation at the level of the Clifford algebra: the null direction of the parabolic case is the nilpotent generator of a degenerate algebra, and the reductive case is that generator given a sign. That the tangent image keeps signature $(1,3)$ whatever $\Lambda$ does is Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.are-ascents-pair-signed}(iv) read through Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-copy-theorem-soldered-residue}.
