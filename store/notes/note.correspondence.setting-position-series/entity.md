## formal
This paper is T9 of the series. \emph{It relies on four predecessors:} the primer T0 \cite{ButlerT0} for the Clifford conventions (Section \ref{sec:setting}); the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} for the forced completion, its fibre quadric, the real ladder pairs and the transvection no-go (Facts \claimref{correspondence.imported-forced-completion-fibre-quadric}, \claimref{correspondence.imported-ladder-pairs-fock-modules-over}, \claimref{correspondence.imported-stabilisers-flat-model}); the tower paper T3 \cite{ButlerT3} for the iterated ascent whose rungs are the ladder pairs of Theorem \claimref{correspondence.twistors-are-states-three-ladder-pairs} (Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-ladder-pairs-fock-modules-over}, Remark \claimref{note.correspondence.point-contact-planes-tower}); the interior paper T6 \cite{ButlerT6} for the flat-model stabilisers, the scale tractor and the vacuum equations, the tractor connection, and the Kerr Killing spinor (Facts \claimref{correspondence.imported-stabilisers-flat-model}, \claimref{correspondence.imported-vacuum-equations-scale-tractor}, \claimref{correspondence.imported-tractor-connection-local-twistor-transport}, Remark \claimref{note.correspondence.boundary-stated-plainly}). No result is imported from the spectrum paper T7 \cite{ButlerT7}, which is named in successor problem (4) as the paper that proves its homogeneity half from this paper's ordering shift; the sheaf paper T4 \cite{ButlerT4} is named in the introduction and in successor problem (3), and the singularity paper T5 \cite{ButlerT5} as context.

\emph{Papers that rely on this one:} the singularity paper T5 \cite{ButlerT5} assigns the twistor restatement of its spectrum condition to this paper's setting; the interior paper T6 \cite{ButlerT6} cites the twistor reading of the completion's spinor module as a point of contact; the spectrum paper T7 \cite{ButlerT7} imports the ordering shift of Theorem \claimref{correspondence.shared-core-ordering-dictionary}(4); the action paper T10 \cite{ButlerT10} imports nothing from this paper: the summand decomposition of Proposition \claimref{correspondence.local-twistor-transport-ascended-spinor-connection}(1) reappears there, in the frozen point's scale, as its own Lemma 2.5(iii), proved directly; the mass paper T11 \cite{ButlerT11} imports the pure-spinor identification of Theorem \claimref{correspondence.twistors-are-states-three-ladder-pairs}, the chart normalisation of Theorem \claimref{correspondence.incidence-relation-chart}, and the palatial quadratics of Lemma \claimref{correspondence.symmetrised-quadratics-palatial-algebra}; the standard-model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} imports the spinor identification, the pure-spinor theorem and the shared core (Theorems \claimref{correspondence.spinor-identification}, \claimref{correspondence.twistors-are-states-three-ladder-pairs}, \claimref{correspondence.shared-core-ordering-dictionary}); the dark-matter paper T14 \cite{ButlerT14} imports the Witt-flag reading of the ladder pairs; the synthesis paper T16 \cite{ButlerT16} imports the point embedding and the Fock-vacuum reading; and the capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17} tabulates this paper's claims at their tiers.
