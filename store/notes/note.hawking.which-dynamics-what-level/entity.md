## formal
The one-particle Hamiltonian $h$ was supplied by the geometry: the Schwarzschild frequency of each boundary mode, with the temperature's coefficient fixed by the surface gravity (Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-modular-descent-premises-geometric-normalisation}); in rapidity the weights are $\omega/\kappa$ at modular temperature $2\pi$. Two notes on scope. First, the greybody filtering of Section \ref{sec:lift} does \emph{not} deform $h$ or the KMS structure: transmission through the exterior barrier is a property of the propagation from the horizon to infinity, not of the horizon algebra; conflating the two is what the interface premise (I) of Assembly \claimref{hawking.greybody-inheritance-under-interface-premise} guards against, and it is named in that assembly's statement. Second, the identification of the mode set (which $(\omega, \ell, m)$ channels, with which degeneracies) is a modelling input from the standard near-horizon reduction; the theorems of this section are exact for whatever separable $(\mathfrak{h}, h)$ that input delivers; the algebra supplies the theorems and the physics supplies the input.
