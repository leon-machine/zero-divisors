## formal
The theorem is elementary, and its point is exactly that: the transport-level statement along tangential curves of fixed Hubble-normalised length needs nothing beyond what admissibility already supplies. Control of the model transport's conjugation of the comparison error, weight by weight, would be needed for a comparison over segments of fixed \emph{coordinate} length, whose Hubble-normalised length diverges as $\tau^{p_{i} - 1}$ and along which both transports individually diverge; that comparison is disclaimed in the proof of Theorem \claimref{interior.weighted-limit-theorem} and no claim is made about it here either.
