## formal
In the ascended algebra the complex capacity is three, and the third mode is the point itself: the vacuum at $x$ is a twistor whose isotropic three-space contains $X(x)$ (Fact \claimref{pairing.imported-flat-conformal-model-twistor-incidence}). The framework's vacuum field in the flat model is
\[
W(x) = \operatorname{span}\{\,X(x),\ \ell(x),\ m(x)\,\}, \qquad \ell(x) = \ell - \ip{x}{\ell}\,\ninf, \quad m(x) = m - \ip{x}{m}\,\ninf ,
\]
the lifts of the null tetrad orthogonal to $X(x)$, and the three pairing classes of a bivector $B$ relative to $W(x)$ are $\ip{B\cdot X}{\ell(x)}$, $\ip{B\cdot X}{m(x)}$, $\ip{B\cdot\ell(x)}{m(x)}$, one for each pair of modes.
