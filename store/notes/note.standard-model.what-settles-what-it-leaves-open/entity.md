## formal
Corollary \claimref{standard-model.coefficient-cannot-come-frozen-point} asked whether the bracket's composites with the rung vectors land on $T_{\mathrm{GJ}}$ with a fixed coefficient. They land on the $10$, all of them, with the fixed coefficient $-\tfrac12$: the bracket is a rung-sector object and the rung sector is insensitive to $B - L$. So the second term of the mass mechanism, the one the families need, cannot be built from the frozen point, its lifts, the bracket, or any polynomial in them. What would build it is the pair-sector $\uu(1)$ current $\sum_{i} v_{i} \wedge w_{i}$, whose coupling to the doublet is what this paper's colour label carries and what Hypothesis (S) would have to produce. The matter programme's next computable object is therefore not derived in the series, and this paper records that as a theorem rather than as an expectation; successor problem (3) reads with that theorem as its starting point.
