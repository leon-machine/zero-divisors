## formal
Two consequences. First, the routing question is settled: Proposition \claimref{standard-model.spectrum-run} asks whether the pair and tower structure, ``eight towers derived'', supplies the demanded content, and the dark-matter paper carries the same as its successor problem (5). The towers do not. The answer does not depend on the odd towers' content, on their coupling rule, which the dark-matter paper shows not to be derivable \cite[Prop.~A.4]{ButlerT14}, or on their scales: it depends only on superselection, and it holds for any content whatever in the other seven sectors. What remains of the routing is the pair structure of this tower, taken up in Section \ref{subsec:tower}. Second, the one coupling the towers do share, the metric, contributes to the running of a gauge coupling only through graviton loops, which are suppressed by $(\mu/M_{P})^{2}$ and are not part of the one-loop gauge $\beta$-function at all; at the meeting scales of Section \ref{subsec:price}, some two to five orders below $M_{P}$, this is at most a part in $10^{4}$, below the record's precision. The gravitational channel is therefore not an exception to Proposition \claimref{standard-model.no-tower-but-one-contributes-towers}; it is quoted at assembly level, as the framework's gravitational sector is not quantised in this series.
