## formal
The structural family is the one that recurs throughout the series. Unipotent, null-direction-shearing maps are exactly what obstructed the element-level completion in the no-go theorem of \cite[Thm.~4.2]{ButlerT1}: the stabiliser of a null direction contains parabolic null rotations, and their fixed-point-free shear excludes every candidate partner. Proposition \claimref{interior.gauge-trajectory-unipotent-ray} says the dynamical approach to the singularity proceeds along a parabolic orbit in the gauge group: the gauge field is, at every radius, a finite null-type shear of the identity, and the singularity is the divergence of the shear parameter. The obstruction of the kinematic theory and the trajectory of the dynamical solution belong to the same conjugacy structure. In Section \ref{sec:approach} this observation is promoted to a theorem: the boundary holonomy of the weighted limit is exactly the Levi factor of the parabolic, with no component in the shearing nilradical.
