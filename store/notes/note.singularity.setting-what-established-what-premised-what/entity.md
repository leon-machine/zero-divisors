## formal
The chain's concessions are argued where they arise, which scatters them; this section is the one place they stand together, so that the front matter has a single statement to agree with.

\emph{Established, unconditionally or from the imported Facts alone.} The gauge sector of Section \ref{sec:locate}: the unipotent ray, the connection bivector in closed form, the exact degeneration, non-removability by the Kretschmann invariant. The shape decoupling $\mathcal{R} = -(M/r^{3})S$ and the matching's two equations (Theorem \claimref{singularity.curvature-gauge-field-exact-shape-decoupling}, Corollary \claimref{singularity.matching-steps-4b-4c-join-equation}), with the matching's residual transport step stated in the corollary. The parity-average identity and the outright uniqueness of the conditional expectation (Theorem \claimref{singularity.descent-parity-average}). Single-mode modular descent (Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-descent}), the over-determination theorem (Theorem \claimref{singularity.exact-equilibrium-over-determines-boundary-modes}), rate rigidity (Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-rigidity-temperature-not-algebra-intrinsic}), and the canonical purification (Theorem \claimref{singularity.purity-output-partner-commutant}), all in the matrix factor and its finite tensor powers. The deficit theorem and its correlation identity (Theorem \claimref{singularity.deficit-theorem-cost-descent-exactly}, Corollary \claimref{singularity.deficit-carried-quanta-correlations}), as statements about the map $\EN$. The exact edge-operator current algebra (Theorem \claimref{singularity.bosonisation-bridge-s-wave-sector}(1)). The causal-epoch integral (Lemma \claimref{singularity.causal-epoch-computed-exactly}). From Appendix \ref{app:threeroutes}: that the sheaf assembly of the pointwise metric levels is a sheaf whose finite truncations are $\operatorname{range}(\EN)$ and the net assembly a net whose finite truncations are the full mode algebras (Theorems \claimref{singularity.finite-truncation-meet-big-fibres-equals}, \claimref{singularity.net-assembly-net-it-everything}, given the completed mode bundle of Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-one-sided-completion-singularity}, hence under its standing hypothesis $w_1(\shL) = 0$), and that no generator in the supplied class hops (Theorem \claimref{singularity.class-relative-no-go-no-supplied}, from the imported Fact \ref{fact:functionlinear}); and the boundary no-value theorem, imported verbatim as Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-no-equivariant-value-wall}. And the finite-mode model of Section \ref{sec:model}, every number exact. Two much-used results are placed deliberately between the lists: Corollary \claimref{singularity.chains-state-class-stability}, a theorem from (P), (R) and, for its stability clause, (L$_E$), and Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}, whose window identity with the envelope, the exact rising branch, the quantified falling branch and the endpoint is a theorem from its five named inputs. The dynamical input Section \ref{sec:page} uses is (L$_E$)'s \emph{full} diagonality: the half of (L$_E$) that Appendix \ref{app:threeroutes} proves, no hopping within the supplied class, does not carry the envelope alone, since Corollary \claimref{singularity.envelope-endpoint-why-boundary-mode-locality}'s necessity witness is a pairing rotation that hops nothing. Each result is exactly as strong as its premises, and both belong to what the premises yield rather than to the unconditional list. Two further results belong to this list. The arithmetic of Proposition \claimref{singularity.modular-flow-boundary-modes-holonomy-universal}, from the geometry paper's collar alone: the $t$-line holonomy rate $M/r^2$ per Killing time, its Hubble-normalised limit $|p_1| = \tfrac13$ and the number $2\pi/|p_1| = 6\pi$, and the non-proportionality of the three limiting boosts; its identification of that holonomy with the modular flow of the boundary modes rests on Premise \claimref{singularity.r-regularity-fixed-locus}, as clause (i) states, and whether any state carries $6\pi$ as a temperature is in the Open list below. And the stabiliser-tangent split of Proposition \claimref{singularity.l-stabiliser-tangent}, an exact statement about $\mathfrak{so}(2\nu)$ on the Fock space, which places the pairing part of the premised half of (L$_E$) in the tangent of the vacuum orbit and its differential part in the stabiliser.

\emph{Premised, named where each is used.} (P) univalence (Premise \claimref{singularity.p-univalence}) and (R) the KMS condition at the geometric period (Premise \claimref{singularity.r-regularity-fixed-locus}), in Step 4d; the metric-level reading $\EN$ on many modes, licensed by Proposition \claimref{singularity.p-reduced-metric-level-reading-many} with its definitional antecedent, the sheaf assembly of Appendix \ref{app:threeroutes} (Step 5); (Q) joint quasi-freeness with the s-wave weights (Premise \claimref{singularity.q-joint-quasi-freeness-s-wave}), the equilibrium reading of the Planck sector, in Step 4e, in its recorded tension with Steps 5 and 6b; (C1) to (C3) in Step 6a (Axioms \claimref{singularity.c1-curvature-cutoff}, \claimref{singularity.c2-cell-density}, \claimref{singularity.c3-counted-epoch}); and, in Step 6b, (L) with its exhaustiveness postulate, (U), (C), (I) with its freshness clause (Premise \claimref{singularity.rate-models-kinematic-inputs-l-u}), and (L$_E$), stated as Premise \claimref{singularity.l-boundary-mode-locality} in Step 4d and used in Step 6b as full diagonality in the joint number basis with the never-return clause: its no-hopping half is a theorem within the supplied class (Theorem \claimref{singularity.class-relative-no-go-no-supplied}), its exclusion of pairing terms and of unsupplied generators is a premise, and it is the premised part, not the proved part, that Step 6b's envelope turns on (Corollary \claimref{singularity.envelope-endpoint-why-boundary-mode-locality}). The imported localisation theorems that carry Steps 2 through 4a are reproduced verbatim as Facts \ref{fact:schwtame} and \claimref{singularity.imported-one-sided-completion-singularity}.

\emph{Open, and stated as such.} Whether the KMS state of the boundary modes on the completed pair extends to a KMS state on the algebra of $t$-line holonomies of the whole collar, which would carry the universal Hubble-normalised inverse temperature $6\pi$ of Proposition \claimref{singularity.modular-flow-boundary-modes-holonomy-universal}(ii) (the obstruction, if any, is the running of the rate before its limit), so that the universal $6\pi$ is a temperature of something only if this is settled; and the $SO(3)$-invariant state on the sphere holonomies of the exceptional set with its two-point function, the one place a spectrum could enter (Remark \claimref{note.singularity.where-spectrum-would-lie}). The derivation of (R), shown to lie outside the framework's reach (Proposition \claimref{singularity.why-calibration-cannot-be-internal}); of (P$_{\mathrm{loc}}$) only the definitional antecedent remains, quantified at Proposition \claimref{singularity.p-reduced-metric-level-reading-many} and restated by Appendix \ref{app:threeroutes} as the choice of the sheaf assembly over the net assembly, which the appendix quantifies on both sides and does not decide. (L)'s exhaustiveness, the no-remnant hypothesis in its correct location. The identifications joining the three mode index sets (Section \ref{sec:photons}'s declaration; Axiom (C1)). Saturation of the falling branch, available only through typicality or the one-mode zero-frequency idealisation. The separation the model enforces between the deficit, a size, and the message, a pattern the deficit cannot see. The type III$_1$ setting, and Kerr. The islands dissonance of Remark \claimref{note.singularity.island-dictionary-one-dissonance}, which the body calls the sharpest open question the chain faces: whether the extremal-surface derivations and the eviction mechanism are two descriptions of one thing. The matching's residual transport step (Corollary \claimref{singularity.matching-steps-4b-4c-join-equation}). Of the hopping no-go (Remark \claimref{note.singularity.steps-status-what-still-open}(b)) the class-relative form is proved (Appendix \ref{app:threeroutes}), for a class of multiplication operators; what remains is whether the dynamics on $E$ carries a differential or a pairing term. The four-dimensional lift of the s-wave sector (Remark \claimref{note.singularity.four-dimensional-lift-flagged}). And the horizon smoothness that eviction presupposes (Remark \claimref{note.singularity.monogamy-firewalls-small-corrections-theorem}). The chain's conclusion survives at exactly this scope: the division transported rather than forbidden, the temperature from named premises, the two-level discrepancy an equation; what the premises yield is quantified above, and what they do not yield, this section records.
