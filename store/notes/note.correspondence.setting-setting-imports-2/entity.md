## formal
Two dimension counts we use, $\dim_\R \Cl(2,4) = 2^6 = 64$ and $\dim_\C \Cl(2,4)_\C = 64$ for the complexification $\Cl(2,4)_\C = \Cl(2,4)\otimes_\R\C \cong \Cl_6(\C)$, the second of which is checked in the verification script (the first follows from it, since complexification doubles the real dimension); Remark \claimref{note.correspondence.dimensions-real-form} records where they place the algebra in the standard tables. Two letters are reused with the tractor literature's meanings wherever a tractor is written in its scale splitting (Facts \claimref{correspondence.imported-vacuum-equations-scale-tractor} and \claimref{correspondence.imported-tractor-connection-local-twistor-transport}, Corollary \claimref{correspondence.one-statement-three-languages}, Proposition \claimref{correspondence.local-twistor-transport-ascended-spinor-connection}): inside the tractor triple $(\sigma, \mu, \rho)$, $\sigma$ and $\rho$ are the top and bottom slots, and $\rho_g$ is the bottom slot of the scale tractor; outside a triple, $\rho$ is the spinor action and $\sigma$ the pairing of Proposition \claimref{correspondence.reality-conjugation-twistor-form-factor}, and $\sigma_a$ with a direction index is the soldering summand of Proposition \claimref{correspondence.local-twistor-transport-ascended-spinor-connection}. Throughout, ``twistor space'' means the module $\T$ constructed in Theorem \claimref{correspondence.spinor-identification}; no prior twistor formalism is assumed, and the contact with Penrose's conventions is made in Section \ref{sec:incidence}. We fix once an orthonormal basis $a_1, a_2$ (norm $+1$), $b_1, \dots, b_4$ (norm $-1$) of $V$, with $\nO, \nInf$ in the span of $a_1, b_1$. The verification script works with $C(v) := c(v)/\sqrt2$, for which $\{C(u), C(v)\} = \ip{u}{v}$; every identity below is stated for $c$ and checked for $C$, the two differing only by powers of $\sqrt2$.
