## formal
Three things. (i) Where the field theory's one-particle structure comes from: the palatial storey, not the tower (Proposition \claimref{field-theory.twistor-attachment}); the tower supplies the CAR and nothing about spacetime (Proposition \claimref{field-theory.ground-does-not-attach-modes}). (ii) Why the canonical commutation relations must be adjoined: the tower's own bosons are $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ quasi-spins (Proposition \claimref{field-theory.how-close-tower-comes-ccr}), the field-theory form of T09's statistics no-go. (iii) What one complex unit fixes: with the unit $I_4$, positive frequency is positive helicity for every massless field, so the opposite helicities and chiralities enter conjugated in one stroke (Propositions \claimref{field-theory.second-quantisation}(4), \claimref{field-theory.fock-space-maxwell-modes}(3)). The dictionary of Proposition \claimref{field-theory.boost-modes}, the boost on the modes as the KMS-strip map, is transport-convenient: its mathematics is that of any hyperbolic plane, its use the series'.
