## formal
In the quantum-extremal-surface derivations \cite{Penington2020, AEMM2019} the falling branch arises when an interior island joins the computation of the radiation's entropy, contributing its area over $4G$. Here the same role is played by the singularity's boundary mode space, which by Proposition \claimref{singularity.area-law-test} has exactly the right size. The dictionary: island $\leftrightarrow$ partner modes; extremal-surface area term $\leftrightarrow$ stretched-singularity capacity; saddle exchange $\leftrightarrow$ the crossing of the two counts. One dissonance is recorded: in the islands derivation the exterior semiclassical theory computes its own correction, whereas here the correction is carried by the ascended boundary modes. Whether these are two descriptions of one mechanism or genuinely different claims is the sharpest open question the chain faces; the eviction theorem below converts it from an unresolved tension into a programme.
