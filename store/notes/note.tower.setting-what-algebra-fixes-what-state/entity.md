## formal
The tower theorems are kinematic: they fix the algebraic setting, not the physics within it. Fixed by the algebra alone: the mode count and its capacity bound (Theorem \claimref{tower.witt-index-quantum-capacity}); the Fock module and its qubit-per-rung doubling (Theorem \claimref{tower.fock-module-one-qubit-per-rung}); the matrix-unit description of ascent and descent, including the one-way pinching of \cite[\S7.3]{ButlerT1}; and the CAR limit as the algebra in which a mode continuum lies (Theorem \claimref{tower.car-limit-even-charge}). Not fixed by the algebra: which state the field algebra carries. Thermality of a mode, entanglement across a ladder pair, the thermofield-double structure of a horizon pair, and everything downstream of a choice of vacuum are properties of states on the CAR factor, constrained but not selected by the kinematics established here. That division is deliberate: T5 \cite{ButlerT5} computes a specific transport chain in a specific state and shows where its information goes; this paper certifies that the algebra it computes in is the canonical one, with its capacity count exact. The reading of modular theory on a single mode, and its extension over the limit as free-fermion thermal field theory, is T5's task; here it is a reference, not a claim.
