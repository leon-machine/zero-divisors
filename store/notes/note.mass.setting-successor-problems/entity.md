## formal
Five. (1) \emph{The operator join:} quantise \eqref{eq:grip} with the palatial ordering and compute the ordering constants; the ordering dictionary of the twistor paper is the method. (2) \emph{A de Sitter chart:} the join runs in the flat chart with the deformation carried by $I_{\Lambda}$; a chart adapted to the non-simple frozen point, in which $B_{\Lambda}$ is a single determinant, would make the $SO(1,4)$ covariance of \eqref{eq:grip} manifest rather than proved, a chart shared with the geometry paper's $\Lambda$-extension \cite{ButlerT6}. (3) \emph{The weight assignments:} the dictionary maps the observed spectrum to imaginary weights of order $10^{38}$ in Hubble units (about $3 \times 10^{38}$ for the electron); deriving them is the hierarchy problem restated in the framework's terms, and the condensation programme's second-order breaking is the named route \cite{ButlerT13}. (4) \emph{The massless locus and the seed:} decide whether the condensation equation's seed is supported on the locus of Theorem \claimref{mass.exact-join}(5) or arises from the ordering constants of (1) \cite{ButlerT13}. (5) \emph{Many-twistor composites:} for $N$ constituents the grip $P = M(I_{\Lambda})$ is defined verbatim and $P = P_{\infty} + \lambda P_{0}$ still holds, so $Q(P) = Q(p) + 2\lambda(p\cdot k - d^{2}) + \lambda^{2}k\cdot k$; the component formulas of the proof of Theorem \claimref{mass.exact-join} are written for sums over $i, l$ and extend to $Q(p) = 2\sum_{i<l}|\br{\pi^{i}\pi^{l}}|^{2}$, $Q(k) = 2\sum_{i<l}|[\omega^{i}\omega^{l}]|^{2}$, $p\cdot k = \sum_{i,l}|t_{il}|^{2}$ and $d = \mathrm{Im}\tr t$ (not proved or scripted here beyond $N = 2$); the comparison with $2\sum_{i<l}|B^{il}_{\Lambda}|^{2}$ and the identification of the remainder as an internal $\mathfrak{u}(N)$ invariant is the counting the standard-model paper's content derivation needs \cite{ButlerT12}.
