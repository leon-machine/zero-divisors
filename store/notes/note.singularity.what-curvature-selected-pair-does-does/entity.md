## formal
Corollary \claimref{singularity.matching-steps-4b-4c-join-equation} uses the pair $\{\sigma_r, I\sigma_r\}$ to identify the modular bivector by equation, and that use stands: $\pm\sigma_r$ are the members with square $+1$. What the pair does \emph{not} contain is the Euclidean partner of the boost. The rotation whose period fixes the temperature lies in the complexified radial plane, and its $2\pi$ is the periodicity of the Euclidean section (Proposition \claimref{singularity.euclidean-section-period}), a fact about the smooth structure at the bifurcation sphere, not about the eigenplanes of the shape operator. The chain's contribution to the premise is therefore not a location for it inside the curvature but three more modest things: the value of the period, computed from the section (Proposition \claimref{singularity.euclidean-section-period}); the sign of the statistics, located in the algebra (Proposition \claimref{singularity.two-kms-functionals-sign}); and the proof that the premise cannot be replaced by anything internal (Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-rigidity-temperature-not-algebra-intrinsic}, Proposition \claimref{singularity.why-calibration-cannot-be-internal}).
