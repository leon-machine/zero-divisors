## formal
This paper is T14 of the series. \emph{It relies on twelve predecessors, and on two later papers:}
\begin{itemize} 
\item the taxonomy paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} for the clock and the standard of proof;
\item the tower paper T3 \cite{ButlerT3} for the eight residues and the rungs (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-eight-towers-mod-8-clock});
\item the sheaf paper T4 \cite{ButlerT4} for the wall displacement (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.superselected-hence-dark-exactly-stable}(2), under its hypotheses);
\item the singularity paper T5 \cite{ButlerT5} for the ascend-then-descend structure and its exhaustiveness clause (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.parallel-boundary-mode-spaces-one-wall}, Fact \ref{fact:noremnant});
\item the geometry paper T6 \cite{ButlerT6} for the single $\Lambda$ (Premise \claimref{dark-matter.p1-single-frozen-point}) and, with T10, the de Sitter radius (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-frozen-point-de-sitter-radius});
\item the entropy paper T8 \cite{ButlerT8} for the indifference instrument (Premise \claimref{dark-matter.p2-indifference});
\item the twistor paper T9 \cite{ButlerT9} for the three-pair Witt decomposition (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-derived-generation-flag}), whose reading as the descent's flag is this paper's;
\item the dark-energy paper T10 \cite{ButlerT10} for the acoustic no-go and the frozen norm (Facts \claimref{dark-matter.imported-gravity-only-coupling}, \ref{fact:frozen});
\item the mass paper T11 \cite{ButlerT11} for the two-constituent mass formula whose normalisation Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.r1-against-series-mass-formulas} compares;
\item the Standard-Model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} for the derived content whose chain Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.mirror-tower-copy-one} reruns (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-inputs-standard-model-chain}), for the trichotomy (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-trichotomy-descents-data}), for the listing choice under Hypothesis (S), and for the closure of the partner question (successor problem (5));
\item the Hawking paper T7 \cite{ButlerT7} for the greybody assembly and the imported flux behind P5 and Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.parallel-boundary-mode-spaces-one-wall};
\item the companion note T14b \cite{ButlerT14b} for the simulations of the composition in galaxy clusters and in the Milky Way (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-composition-simulated});
\item the foundations paper T16 \cite{ButlerT16} for the connectedness criterion and the one-bit corollary that restate the fork's cost (Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.forks-cost-restated-causal-criterion-theorem});
\item the families paper T13 \cite{ButlerT13} for the audit of indifference and the within-sector alignment (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.ceiling-valued-inherited-success});
\item and, outside the series, the record's graviton-production rate (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-massless-graviton-production-rate}), Schwinger discharge (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-schwinger-discharge-charged-hole}), the induced equations on a hypersurface (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-induced-equations-hypersurface}), the linear perturbation equations (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-linear-momentum-equation-traceless-field}) and the short-range test of the inverse-square law (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-inverse-square-law-short-range}).
\end{itemize}

\end{itemize}
