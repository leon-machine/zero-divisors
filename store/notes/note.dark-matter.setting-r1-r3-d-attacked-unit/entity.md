## formal
The case against R1 (equal mass per quantum): the odd towers' quanta are composites of sectors whose confinement scales the series does not derive (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.no-coupling-rule-odd-towers-follows}), so nothing fixes their mass to ours; a factor two either way in a quantum's mass is a factor two in that tower's share, and no closed form survives it. What survives: Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.r1-against-series-mass-formulas} shows the series' mass formulas, compared in one normalisation, do not contradict R1, and the closed form's agreement is evidence for R1 at exactly the strength of Observation \claimref{dark-matter.status-rule}(ii), one candidate in fourteen.

The case against R2 (one quantum per unit ball): this tower's filling is set by baryon number, a conserved charge the odd towers cannot carry (Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.flag-bit-exists-exactly-tower-dimension}), so R2 asks a symmetric sector to be filled at a spacing a charge sets elsewhere; and its co-expanding reading (Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.extra-direction-must-co-expand}) adds a comoving metre-scale direction to what must exist. What survives: the de Sitter reading replaces R2 by D at the one length the series has (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.democracy-de-sitter-radius-unit-ball}), at the price of a thickness the geometry then gets wrong.

The case against R3 (one cell radius per unshared direction, one side): it is the one number the whole relation turns on, the geometry's two computations of it give $\tfrac{3\pi}{8}$ and $\tfrac{\pi}{4}$ (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.two-reductions}), and no gravitational measurement can reach it (Corollary \claimref{dark-matter.thickness-not-gravitational-quantity}). The contact mechanism that returns it (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.contact-mechanism-returns-r3}) is open to the same attack one clause at a time: contact and indivisibility are a hard-ball picture that nothing in the series gives an odd tower's quanta, and under contact the tower's matter beyond one cell radius sources nothing (Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.scope-mechanism}); the side is postulated, the sign of $\omega$ being a label with no reason to become a direction of sourcing (Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.what-s-uses-what-it-does}); the contact count does not compose across two nestings; and reciprocity is the equal-thickness assumption restated. What survives: each clause has a computed alternative that the observation excludes (Table \ref{tab:excludes}), so the mechanism's choices are exposed rather than hidden; and the family's floor, $5.255$, holds whatever becomes of R3.

The case against D: it is P2 applied to inequivalent towers, exactly the equation P2's audit refuses (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.no-coupling-rule-odd-towers-follows}(c)). What survives: nothing in Section \ref{sec:rule} rests on D; it is named as the one route by which the series' own length could enter, and it does not reach the observed value.
