## formal
The Standard-Model paper derives the tree relations $m_{\nu} = m_{u}$, $m_{e} = m_{d}$ from a vev in the rung directions of the $10$ and proves three no-go theorems on what can dress them \cite[Cor.~9.25, Thms.~9.31, 9.33, 9.35]{ButlerT12}. This section locates in the module the one direction that carries the second family's factor on a single family, records the grade-three direction that can carry it between families, and fixes the order of operations of the matter programme: the two-term structure first, the family breaking second, the order the capstone's table of open directions records \cite[Table~4]{ButlerT17}. Throughout, the module is the Standard-Model paper's two-rung module \cite[Def.~9.8]{ButlerT12}, realised on $\Lambda^{\bullet}\C^{5}$ with $v_{a}$ acting by wedge and $w_{a}$ by contraction, modes $1, 2, 3$ the pairs and $4, 5$ the rungs; $N_{W}$ and $N_{R}$ are the pair and rung number operators; the sixteen is the even subspace, and its species reading, $\nu^{c}, e^{c}, Q, L, u^{c}, d^{c}$ at (rungs, pairs) $= (0,0), (2,0), (1,1), (1,3), (0,2), (2,2)$, is the Standard-Model paper's under Hypothesis (S) \cite[Prop.~5.8]{ButlerT12}, so that every statement below naming a species carries (S); $B$ is the $\so(10)$-invariant bilinear pairing \cite[Lem.~9.23]{ButlerT12}; and for an odd element $T$ of the Clifford algebra of the ten, the \emph{mass form} is $Y_{T}(\psi, \varphi) = B(\psi, T\varphi)$ on the sixteen. The operator $B - L := 1 - \tfrac23 N_{W}$ takes the values $(1, 1, \tfrac13, -1, -\tfrac13, -\tfrac13)$ on $(\nu^{c}, e^{c}, Q, L, u^{c}, d^{c})$: $B - L$ is an affine function of the pair number (script). The decomposition of $16 \otimes 16$ and the dictionary between grades and $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ representations are stated as Fact \claimref{families.imported-decomposition-grade-dictionary} at the end of the section.
