## formal
The grip of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-frozen-point-grip-thirteenth-refutation} is a sesquilinear moment, $\ip{\Psi}{A\Psi}$ for a Hermitian product form, and a rung component of the frozen point enters it through mixed bivectors that change slot and pair number by one each, joining $Q$ to all four singlets and $L$ to $u^{c}$ and $d^{c}$ only: not the Yukawa pattern. The mass term of a Weyl fermion is a bilinear, $\psi\psi$, and the object that couples two sixteens to a vector is the invariant bilinear $B(\psi, x\varphi)$ of Lemma \claimref{standard-model.invariant-pairing-five-modes}, through which the same rung component joins exactly the Yukawa pairs. The two are different objects and answer different questions: the grip gives the mass of a composite state, the pairing the mass term between two species. A rung component is Higgs-like in the second sense and not in the first, and the second is the sense in which the Standard Model's Higgs is a Higgs.
