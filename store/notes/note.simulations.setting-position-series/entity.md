## formal
This paper is T14b of the series, a companion to the dark-matter paper T14 \cite{ButlerT14}. It is downstream of T14's Proposition 4.1 (superselection), Theorem 4.3 and Proposition 4.6 (the mirror), Proposition 6.2 (the radiation audit and its self-interaction statement) and Section 7 (the unit-ball rule, its premises R1 to R3, the thickness family and the moving sheets), and it tests the composition T14's Section 7 fixes against the record. Remark \claimref{note.simulations.what-premise-c-needs} refers to the Standard-Model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} for the gauge charges of the four-dimensional minimal massive composite. It proves nothing about the series' algebra and changes no derived result of T14; it says which of T14's physical statements the simulations and the Galaxy model support and which they do not (Section \ref{sec:costs}). The capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17} should carry Table \ref{tab:glance} beside T14's ledger entry, Premise C as the condition under which the rule's composition meets the cluster record, and the two measurements of Section \ref{sec:galaxy} that would decide the mirror's helium branch.
