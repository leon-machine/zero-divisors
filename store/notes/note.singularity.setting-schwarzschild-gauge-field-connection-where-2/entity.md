## formal
The metric \eqref{eq:metricdef} that \eqref{eq:hbar} defines is computed blade-exactly in Appendix \ref{app:metric}: it is the Painlev\'e-Gullstrand line element $ds^2 = dt^2 - (dr + \beta\,dt)^2 - r^2 d\Omega^2$, with orthonormal coframe
\begin{equation}
\theta^0 = dt, \qquad \theta^1 = dr + \beta\,dt, \qquad \theta^2 = r\,d\vartheta, \qquad \theta^3 = r\sin\vartheta\,d\varphi .
\end{equation}
The next proposition computes the second gauge field, which turns out to be the carrier of the divergence.
