## formal
The result refines Corollary \claimref{standard-model.direction-demanded-content-doublet-heavy} into a statement about the gluino at the level of the four blocks of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.four-supersymmetric-splits}: among the splittings that keep the gauginos, the higgsinos, the second doublet and the sfermions each on one side, the gluino must be at the heavier threshold, and once it is there the admissible family interpolates between the near-degenerate spectrum and a split one with the electroweak-inos at $10^{9}$ GeV. Splittings by species are not covered: with the twelve species-level components (three gauginos, two higgsinos, the second doublet, six sfermion species) there are $4096$ splittings, and the script records that $296$ of those with the gluino at the lighter threshold admit a segment, the light set (wino, gluino, three squark doublets), $\Dl = (\tfrac{1}{10}, \tfrac{17}{6}, 3)$ against $\Dl' = (\tfrac{12}{5}, \tfrac43, 1)$, among them; this is a recorded observation about the enumeration, and no statement about species-level splittings is made here. That the near-degenerate end is at $f = 1$ exactly is the one-loop miss of $1.2$ percent seen from the two-threshold side: it is removed by raising the gluino, sfermions and second doublet by a factor $1.34$.
