\emph{The mechanism's four clauses and the nearest alternative to each. The section weighting counts a cut cell by the measure of its section relative to the maximal section, one normalisation among several: a unit $D$-ball cut at height $h$ has section measure $V_{D-1}(1 - h^{2})^{(D-1)/2}$, and relative to $V_{D-1}$ these weights integrate over both sides to $V_{D}/V_{D-1}$, which for $D = 4$ is $\tfrac{3\pi}{8}$, the $\cos^{4}$ factor of the zero-mode reduction (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.two-reductions}(i)); that reduction is the five-dimensional count with I and S withdrawn. With C withdrawn the sourcing rule reverts to the homogeneous integral over a length $c$, which is Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.total-function-thickness}. The list is the paper's, not exhaustive; other cell conventions and other normalisations of a partial count are not on it.}
