## formal
This paper is T7 of the series. \emph{It relies on six predecessors:} the primer T0 \cite{ButlerT0} and the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} for the completed-pair language and the transport principle; the tower paper T3 \cite{ButlerT3} for the CAR limit that makes the boundary modes an algebra (Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-algebra-boundary-modes-car-algebra}); the singularity paper T5 \cite{ButlerT5} for modular descent under its premises (P) and (R), the bosonisation bridge under (Q), and the surface gravity $\kappa = 1/4M$ (Facts \claimref{hawking.imported-modular-descent-premises-geometric-normalisation} and \claimref{hawking.imported-s-wave-bosonic-sector}); the interior paper T6 \cite{ButlerT6} for the exterior exactness that greybody inheritance rests on (Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-exterior-exactly-general-relativity}); and the twistor paper T9 \cite{ButlerT9} for the ordering shift from which Proposition \claimref{hawking.homogeneity-ordering-shift} is promoted.

\emph{Papers that rely on this one:} the entropy paper T8 \cite{ButlerT8} imports the single-mode modular structure, the quasi-free calculus, and the finite-window identities; the action paper T10 \cite{ButlerT10} imports the finite-window quantification of the current algebra; the standard-model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} imports the helicity identification of Proposition \claimref{hawking.homogeneity-ordering-shift}; and the capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17} tabulates this paper's claims at their tiers.
