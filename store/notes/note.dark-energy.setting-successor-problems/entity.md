## formal
Five, the first two the most precisely posed. (1) \emph{The forcing half beyond the diagonal class:} Assembly \claimref{dark-energy.forcing-half-schwarzschild-wall-residue-sense} with Propositions \claimref{dark-energy.cosmological-constant-does-not-change-residue}-\claimref{dark-energy.wall-counterterm} and Lemma \claimref{dark-energy.variable-exponents-residue-pointwise-corrections-are} answers it in the residue sense for the diagonal Kasner class with $\Lambda$ and variable exponents. Analyse the subleading momentum constraints, which select the admissible exponent fields $p_i(x)$, and the non-diagonal class, where the frame rotates as the wall is approached; this is the dynamical half of the sheaf paper's connection-level matching, and the logarithm of Lemma \claimref{dark-energy.variable-exponents-residue-pointwise-corrections-are} is where the variable-order structure enters. (2) \emph{The dual wall:} Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.walls-free-sector}'s symmetric form $M$ belongs to walls of the soldering form, metrics on tangent vectors with a radical. Decide whether such walls occur in the series' geometries (the unweighted sphere directions of the Schwarzschild interior are of this type before the sheaf paper's weights absorb them) and, where they do, whether $M$ carries a boundary mode space; the gauge generator the sheaf paper's wall opens, the ladder boost of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.sheaf-papers-wall-soldering-variables}(iii), is the object to compare it with. (3) \emph{Matter coupling at $\Sigma$, and the frozen point off the vacuum region:} extend the polynomial functional by matter in a form that remains defined across the locus, determine what the wall does to minimal coupling, and define the frozen point on a matter-filled region in a way that keeps it parallel, which Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.vacuum-region-registration-condition}(iii) needs and Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.vacuum-region-registration-condition}(ii) shows the naive scale tractor does not supply. (4) \emph{The Kerr version of the no-go:} the ring's codimension-two exceptional structure replaces the wall; the orthogonality argument needs the rotating current algebra, and the duality monodromy of the geometry paper will enter. (5) \emph{The smallness of $\Lambda$, and the promotion decision:} Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}(2) gives the form of the action for $\Lambda \neq 0$, not a value and not smallness, and the numerical layer is the declared open problem of the matter papers. Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.under-action-written-norm-parameter-laws} places the question exactly: under the action as written $\Lambda$ indexes the theory, and ``why this $\Lambda$'' is a question within one theory only if a modification of the known class of Remark \claimref{note.dark-energy.what-clause-3-requires-what-it}, the Henneaux-Teitelboim promotion being the one used there, is adopted. Decide the promotion; if adopted, recompute the Goldstone count of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.point-infinity-has-no-physical-fluctuation} with the norm mode and its conjugate time, and name the measure over $\Lambda$ on which the Standard-Model paper's entropic and null-endpoint forces act.
