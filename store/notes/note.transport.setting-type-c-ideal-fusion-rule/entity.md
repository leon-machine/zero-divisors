## formal
Section \ref{sec:typeN} settled type N: no element-level repair in any ascent, a total repair at the level of points, and a minimal completion forced in two independent senses. This section settles type C, where the answer is different in form. The obstruction is not a direction without a partner but a splitting of the algebra into two ideals that no inner symmetry can exchange (Remark \claimref{note.transport.typec-depth}); its repair costs one dimension rather than two, is delivered at the level of elements rather than points, and is unique up to isomorphism though not up to isometry.
