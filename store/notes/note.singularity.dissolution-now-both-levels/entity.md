## formal
Table \ref{tab:model} and Proposition \claimref{singularity.two-levels-finite-model} are the paradox in miniature, every entry exact. In the completion: purity, the Page shape, a recoverable message. In the original space: exact thermality, monotone entropy, no message, and no trace that a message was ever present. Same history, two levels, and the map between them is the explicit dephasing $\EN$, whose cost is the theorem $\rel{\rho}{\EN\rho} = C(\rho) - C(\EN\rho)$. What the metric level retains is exactly what survives $\EN$, populations and per-pair records; what it cannot see is exactly what carries the information. Thermality is what the projection records in place of what it cannot carry (Remark \claimref{note.singularity.thermality-records-what-projection-cannot-see}); the deficit theorem gives the total, carried by inter-quanta correlations and insensitive to their pattern; and the model exhibits it in exact arithmetic.
