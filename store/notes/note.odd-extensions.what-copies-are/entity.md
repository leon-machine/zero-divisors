## formal
Sections \ref{sec:single} to \ref{sec:rung} are about this world's own ascent. The copy of the mirror's algebra that Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.translation-bivectors-frozen-point}(ii) finds on the translation bivectors for $\Lambda < 0$ is not the mirror tower, whose vectors are soldered; it is the reason the two oriented towers' intermediate algebras are non-isomorphic (Corollary \claimref{odd-extensions.copy-theorems-blind-spot}), and that non-isomorphism is a statement about the two towers' own ascents under their own solderings, unaffected by this section. Likewise the odd towers' algebras that Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.four-extensions-are-four-odd-towers} finds among the four extensions are their abstract algebras only.
