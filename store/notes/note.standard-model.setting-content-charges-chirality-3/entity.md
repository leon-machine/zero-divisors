## formal
At observation level, and flagged as such: the weak coupling is chiral because the automorphisms act on states of definite chirality, and under (S) every state of the composite has one, the parity of its mode number; which parity is called left-handed is the identification of the one-handed listing of Theorem \claimref{standard-model.hypercharge-global-form-pair-counting-consistency} with the Standard Model's left-handed convention, and it is the same choice as the orientation. That twistor charge is helicity (Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-twistor-charge-helicity}) is what ties the parity of the single-twistor sector to spacetime handedness; the extension of that tie to the five-mode parity of (S) is part of what (S) asserts.
