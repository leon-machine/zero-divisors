## formal
$v = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(e_1 + e_2)$, $w = \tfrac{1}{2\sqrt2}(e_1 - e_2)$ form a hyperbolic pair; $e = wv = \tfrac12(1 + e_1e_2)$ is the annihilating idempotent of Example \claimref{note.transport.hyperbolic-partner-supplies-annihilating-idempotent}, non-central; the algebra is $\Mat_2(\R)$ ($d = 0$), simple, and every zero divisor is N/P. This is the hyperbolic atom of Remark \claimref{note.transport.hyperbolic-atom} below, the one mixed signature where the element-level choice of $w$ is canonical.
