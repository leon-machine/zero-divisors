## formal
Two normalisations enter $\nu$, and their statuses differ. The corank is settled: under the weights the geometry forces, the anisotropic Schwarzschild singularity carries exactly one ladder pair per point of the blown-up singular locus \cite[\S 8.9]{ButlerT4}, the resolution T5 records in passing the count to this paper \cite[\S 10]{ButlerT5}. What remains open is the \emph{cell calibration}: how many Planck cells of slice volume one localised pair accounts for, the conversion from per-point to per-cell. The weighted completion supplies the geometry at the exceptional set on which that conversion is to be read (Fact \ref{fact:capacity}), and its analysis is closed at Schwarzschild, so no analytic step of the geometry programme stands between it and $\nu$; what blocks the calibration is one dimensionless number, the residue $\kappa_c$ of Section \ref{subsec:calibration}, and deriving it is this paper's task, carried as its first successor problem (Section \ref{sec:scope}). The $M$-scaling of Theorem \claimref{scrambling.mode-count} is independent of it, the coefficient is not, and Section \ref{sec:epoch} converts the requirement into that single number. Separately, the per-mode entropy normalisation is $\ln 2$ for a maximally mixed fermionic mode: T5 compares the capacity $C = N\ln 2$, not the bare count, with $\SBH$, so the area-law condition of Proposition \claimref{scrambling.area-law-test} reads $\nu\Delta t = M/2$ in the 1-nat convention used here and $\nu\Delta t = M/(2\ln 2)$ in T5's capacity convention, a factor of $\ln 2$ (31 per cent) that is stated rather than absorbed into an approximation sign.
