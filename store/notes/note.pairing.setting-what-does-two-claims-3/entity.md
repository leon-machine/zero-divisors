## formal
\textbf{Prior art and the two ledgers.} Proposition \claimref{pairing.stabiliser-parabolic-algebra-null-direction} in its geometric content is the parabolic stabiliser of a point of the celestial sphere; Proposition \claimref{pairing.pairing-functional-pair-creation-amplitude} is the Thouless matrix element; Proposition \claimref{pairing.pairing-class-transverse-derivative-null-direction} is the Newman-Penrose definition of the spin coefficients; Propositions \claimref{pairing.static-frame-only-expansion-survives} and \claimref{pairing.regular-frame-class-vanishes-horizon-changes} are the Schwarzschild spin coefficients, standard since Chandrasekhar; Proposition \claimref{pairing.curvature-which-loops-move-vacuum} and Lemma \claimref{pairing.loop-classes-are-weyl-components} are $\Psi_{2} = -M/r^{3}$; Proposition \claimref{pairing.displacement-pairs-point-mode-vacuum-parallel} is twistor incidence. What the note proves for itself is the functional $\pv$ in the series' conventions, its identity with the pair-creation amplitude, the identification of the singularity paper's witness as a rotation of the null direction (Corollary \claimref{pairing.singularity-papers-witness-rotation-null-direction}), the exact Schwarzschild classes in both frames with the horizon zero and the sign change, the curvature classes, and the three statements of Proposition \claimref{pairing.two-frames-share-vacuum-selected-transport}. Under the two-ledger discipline every result here is transport-absent: Sections \ref{sec:stabiliser} to \ref{sec:ascended} would stand verbatim with no transport principle, the pre-ascent reading using no ascent and the ascended reading reproducing incidence; what the framework supplies is the labelling of which null direction is its vacuum, and that labelling rests on the curvature pair, the sign of the square and the flag bit. The negative result is likewise transport-absent: the framework's transport does not produce the Hawking pairing. The note's contribution is a dictionary, not a derivation, and it is offered as one.
