## formal
Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.are-ascents-pair-signed} gives a one-parameter family of intermediate algebras, nondegenerate for $\Lambda \neq 0$ and degenerate at $\Lambda = 0$. The sheaf paper has a theory of exactly such families: tame corank-one degenerations, their exceptional algebras, the residue of the metric at the wall, and a conservation law that says the degeneracy of a wall in spacetime can be displaced but never destroyed. This section runs that theory on the $\Lambda$ line. The family is a tame wall in the sheaf paper's sense, and the rung completes it on both sides, which the conservation law forbids for the completions it covers; the family lies in the case the conservation theorem's proof excludes by hypothesis, a complement whose signature switches across the wall, and that case is generic to any corank-one family of hyperplanes in a fixed nondegenerate space. The section's content is the identification of the dark-energy paper's frozen-point geometry with the sheaf paper's tame structure, and the placement of the $\Lambda$ family in that excluded case.
