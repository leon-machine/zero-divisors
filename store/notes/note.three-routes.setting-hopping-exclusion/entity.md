## formal
T5's Premise 7.30, ledger locality, has two halves; the diagonality half was argued there from what the localisation theorems fail to supply, and T5's Remark 7.33(b) records the gap: ``a no-go on inter-point hopping over $E$ would settle it.'' This section proves the class-relative form of that no-go, and is explicit about which class. The result upgrades the premise's status from \emph{unsupplied} to \emph{excluded within the class of supplied generators}; the residue, stated in Remark \claimref{note.three-routes.true-residual-differential-dynamics}, is a genuine dynamical question and not bookkeeping.
