## formal
(1) Pair creation, antiparticle modes and Compton scattering in the coupled system. (2) Gauge invariance beyond current conservation, and Lorentz covariance of the Coulomb-gauge construction beyond tree level. (3) Loops and renormalisation. (4) Non-abelian gauge fields, which bring the axial anomaly into play. (5) The composite scalar beyond mean field: the Goldstone pole, a covariant regularisation, the sigma mode, and whether the contact term of Proposition \claimref{field-theory.contact-interactions-tower} has an attractive scalar channel. (6) The map from the palatial ladder module's conformal-energy basis to the plane-wave Fock space, which would turn the analogy of Proposition \claimref{field-theory.fock-space-maxwell-modes}(1) into a construction. (7) The terminology conflict recorded in the first run: T03 calls the tower second quantisation and T09 calls the palatial storey second quantisation; on the attachment found here the palatial storey supplies the one-particle space and the tower's CAR over it is second quantisation, which is T03's usage. (8) Whether the Higgs is composite, which the tower supports at mean-field level, or elementary: a modelling decision. (9) The relative sign of the twistor's complex unit and the field's, which now carries T12's orientation, and a check of the conventions of T12's Fact on twistor charge and helicity against T09 and T07. (10) For the gravity prediction: retardation and the radiative sector, non-rigid bodies, and the shared spinor factor of two masses in one tower.
