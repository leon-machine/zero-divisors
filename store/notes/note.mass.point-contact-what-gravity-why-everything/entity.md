## formal
Assembling this paper with the action paper gives the following account, each claim at its declared level. First: mass is coupling to the frozen point, at theorem level for fields (Theorem \claimref{mass.mass-weight-dictionary}) and, for composites, by Definition \claimref{obj.mass.grip-mass-composite} with the closed form of Theorem \claimref{mass.exact-join}; and gravity's action is the MacDowell-Mansouri functional of the frozen point (Fact \ref{fact:action}, whose action clause rests on an identity imported at assembly level). So matter and gravity are two relations to the same object: a massive field couples to $I$; gravity is the dynamics of $I$. Second, a structural reading of the equivalence principle: every mass term in the theory is a coupling to the one frozen point whose dynamics is the gravitational field, so the universality of gravitation, that gravitational and inertial mass coincide and everything falls alike, reads as the statement that the theory contains a single scale-giving object. Fields differ in how strongly they couple to $I$ (their weight), never in what they couple to. This is a reading, not a theorem: it rests on the two theorem-level ends and on the uniqueness of the scale-giving object, and it is entered in the levels of the results below at that level. Third, imported from the action paper at the levels it assigns: the action paper leaves the graviton's ontology open and states its engineering conclusion on either reading; on the reading in which the graviton is a collective mode of the boundary mode space, the acoustic no-go gives that conclusion at the mixed level of its inputs, one step conditional on the entropy paper's premise \cite[\S\S5--6]{ButlerT10}. Last, at the level of flagged speculation: a massive body is a multi-spinor composite (a composite of many conformal spinors), misaligned within (Proposition \claimref{mass.mass-symplectic-area-two-twistor-formula}) and coupled to $I$ through its grip (Definition \claimref{obj.mass.grip-mass-composite}, Theorem \claimref{mass.exact-join}), and its gravitational field is the response of the boundary mode space to that disturbance. In one sentence: gravity, at the quantum level, is the collective dynamics of the frozen point, coupling to which defines every mass, and everything falls the same way because there is only one point at infinity to couple to.
