## formal
Six, the first the registered open problem. (1) \emph{The non-Gaussian completion:} produce the $207$ from caused structure, under the relabelling-signature refutation condition; this is the family programme's terminal open item and the paper's declared open problem, and by Corollary \claimref{families.order-operations-matter-programme} it is the second step, acting on whatever two-term structure the mass mechanism supplies. (2) \emph{The Gram-square structure:} derive that the full three-family Yukawa form is the square of a face-space map, promoted from convenience to necessity by the order-one angle; Proposition \claimref{families.alignment-computed-thirteenth-refutation-democracy-one}(3) and the whole of Section \ref{sec:gaussian} assume it. (3) \emph{The second-order breaking's modulus:} the free phase $\delta = 0.222$ encodes $m_{\mu}/m_{e}$ and is the genuine $S_{2} \to 1$ stage; its computation depends on (1) and, by Corollary \claimref{families.order-operations-matter-programme}, on the coefficient of the $126$ direction relative to the $10$, which the breaking acts on and cannot supply. (4) \emph{The real structure of face space:} find, or refute, a real structure on the complexified face space under which the bracket form $C$ has signature $(1,2)$ on the physical slice, which would promote the light-cone reading of Proposition \claimref{families.koide-nullity-condition-one-member-invariant} from a choice to a consequence. (5) \emph{The dressing:} compute the strong dressing that spoils the quark relation, converting the selectivity account into a derivation. (6) \emph{The absolute scale, in three pieces, and the pair-sector current:} Proposition \claimref{families.why-kinematic-attacks-had-fail} dissolves the absolute scale into $N$, the rung unit and the Yukawa pattern, the first two assigned to the Standard-Model paper's successors (5) and (1) and kinematically not functions of each other. What this paper must still supply is the third: the weight spectrum's pattern, whose next computable object is no longer the bracket composite, closed in the negative by Fact \claimref{families.imported-tree-relations-family-no-go}(iii), but the coupling of the rung vector to the pair-sector $\uu(1)$ current in the mass mechanism (Remark \claimref{note.families.what-series-holds-what-bracket-cannot}), shared with the Standard-Model paper's weight-assignment successor \cite{ButlerT12}; the second-order breaking of (3) then acts on the two-term structure it produces. On the route that paper registers the same question takes a second form: the antisymmetric coupling of the grade-three direction between the second and third families, its successor problem (8), which a family structure would have to supply (Fact \claimref{families.imported-grade-three-direction-between-families}).
