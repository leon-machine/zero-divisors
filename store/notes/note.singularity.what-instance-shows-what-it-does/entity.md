## formal
The computation is the algorithm's own diagnosis, made concrete: in the completion the division never fails, and what fails is the return to the original space. The answer to ``divide by the dying direction'' exists as a point of $\mathcal{Q}_p$ and is not a vector of spacetime, so no assignment of a classical partner value can be read off it. That is the point-level counterpart of the element-level no-go recorded next as Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-no-c-number-repair}, and the two together are what force the repair to be delivered as operators: the value has left the space, and the algebra keeps only the ladder pair \eqref{eq:car} that records it.
