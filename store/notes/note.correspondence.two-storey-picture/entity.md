## formal
The corrected relationship is a proved structure, drawn in Figure \ref{fig:twostorey}. The Clifford (fermionic) quantisation turns ambient directions into conformal spinor (twistor) \emph{states}: first quantisation, forced at the singular locus (Remark \claimref{note.correspondence.point-contact-planes-tower}). The Weyl (bosonic) quantisation of the palatial programme turns conformal spinor states into the noncommutative algebra of quantised conformal spinors: second quantisation, one storey up, and provably not the first storey under another name (Proposition \claimref{correspondence.statistics-no-go}). The conformal algebra $\mathfrak{so}(2,4)$ is the invariant core of both storeys (Theorem \claimref{correspondence.shared-core-ordering-dictionary}), and the ordering shift of a half per dimension recurs at each. What survives of the naive conjecture is its corrected form: the framework's operator-valued completion and Penrose's noncommutative holomorphic geometry are opposite ends of one tower rather than of one identification, meeting at the conformal algebra. Read against the tower paper \cite{ButlerT3}, the completion's ladder pairs supply the states on which the palatial storey acts.
