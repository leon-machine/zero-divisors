## formal
In this tower $x = \rev{\psi}_{1}\psi_{2}$ lies in the two-dimensional space $(1-f)\Cl^{0}f$, a copy of $\C$, and Theorem \claimref{dark-tower-matter.where-mass-begins}(i) is T11's two-constituent mass formula \cite[Prop.~4.1]{ButlerT11} written in the even algebra, the pairing $x$ playing the role of the bracket. In five dimensions the law is the Fierz identity $j^{2} = (\bar{\psi}\psi)^{2}$ of a single Dirac spinor; the geometric statement is that the four-vector part of $\rev{\phi}\phi$ survives the idempotent.
