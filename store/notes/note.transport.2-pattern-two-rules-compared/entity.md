## formal
Both minimal ascents turn out to be $2 \times 2$ matrix amplifications, of the whole algebra for type N and of its even half for type C (Section \ref{sec:clock} proves the first and reads both as the transport's own bookkeeping). The comparison exposes the cost asymmetry precisely: Rule 1 adds two dimensions and preserves the Morita class, $d$ fixed; Rule 2 adds one dimension, halves the coefficient algebra to its even part, and moves $d$ by $\pm 1$.
