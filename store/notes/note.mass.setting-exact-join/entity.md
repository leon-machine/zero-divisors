## formal
Theorem \claimref{mass.mass-weight-dictionary} and Proposition \claimref{mass.mass-symplectic-area-two-twistor-formula} both compute a mass. This section shows that the flat-space formula is the squared pairing of the composite against the frozen point, extends it to $\Lambda \neq 0$ exactly, and then reads the composite's weight off the dictionary. The plan: find the frozen point of de Sitter space in the flat chart and fix what the chart's normalisation does and does not decide (Lemma \claimref{mass.de-sitter-scale-frozen-point}, Remark \claimref{note.mass.what-nullity-fixes}); write the conformal generators in the chart (Lemma \claimref{mass.chart-form-spin-isomorphism}); define the grip of a composite on the frozen point, which is its de Sitter momentum, and prove it is a real vector (Definition \claimref{obj.mass.grip-mass-composite}, Lemma \claimref{mass.grip-real}); compute its squared length in closed form (Theorem \claimref{mass.exact-join}); recognise the result as a difference of Casimirs (Proposition \claimref{mass.casimir-form-join}) and identify the five broken generators (Lemma \claimref{mass.five-broken-generators}); and read off the composite's weight (Proposition \claimref{mass.weight-composite}).
