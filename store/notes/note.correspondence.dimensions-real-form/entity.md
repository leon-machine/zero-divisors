## formal
Over the reals, $\Cl(2,4) \cong \Mat_4(\HH)$ and $\Cl^0(2,4) \cong \Mat_4(\C)$; the paper never uses these classification statements as inputs (the complexified Fock model carries all the structure), but they locate the construction in the standard tables, and the counts $64 = 4^2\cdot 4$ and $32 = 4^2 \cdot 2$ are the ones that make them possible. What the real structure of $V \subset V_\C$ adds is an antilinear conjugation $\kappa$ on the module, constructed explicitly and shown unique up to scale in Proposition \claimref{correspondence.reality-conjugation-twistor-form-factor}, which pairs the conformal spinor space $\CS$ with its opposite chirality $\CSm$: this is the statement that the opposite chirality is the conjugate of the conformal spinor space (dual twistors are conjugate twistors), and it induces on $\CS$ the Hermitian form of signature $(2,2)$ preserved by $\mathrm{Spin}(2,4) \cong SU(2,2)$. The signature is computed in the incidence section, where the $(\omega, \pi)$ splitting makes it a two-line count (Proposition \claimref{correspondence.reality-conjugation-twistor-form-factor}(4)). \emph{Point of contact (per the series' practice):} $\CS$ with its $SU(2,2)$ structure is exactly Penrose's twistor space \cite{Penrose1967, PenroseRindler86}, and the conjugation pairing $\CS \leftrightarrow \CSm$ is his twistor-dual-twistor conjugation.
