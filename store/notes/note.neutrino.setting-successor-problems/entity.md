## formal
Five. (1) \emph{The PMNS numbers:} all three angles are second-order effects here, depending on the same breaking that the CKM numbers depend on, and therefore on the families paper's second-order programme and its registered open problem \cite[Prop.~4.3(4), Obs.~7.1]{ButlerT13}; the large angles are the most demanding requirement on that breaking. (2) \emph{The two light masses and $\alpha$ at second order:} compute how far the two stages of the breaking lift $m_{2}$ and $m_{1}$, and what fixes $\alpha$, which would decide Premise (H), test the relation of Remark \claimref{note.neutrino.branch-m-3}, and convert the double-beta values into a single prediction; on the Standard-Model paper's Pati-Salam route the question becomes whether the family structure that fits the charged fermions there gives this branch. (3) \emph{Deriving $M$:} identify the framework's object that realises the neutral bilinear (Fact \claimref{neutrino.imported-mass-bracket-quantum-numbers} says nothing about it: the bracket is computed there only on slot states) and obtain its scale from its own dynamics rather than from the observed masses under Hypothesis (U), discharging Premise (M), deciding Premise (R), and turning the agreement with the dark-matter paper's rung scale from a construction into a check. (4) \emph{The degenerate pair and the non-thermal injection:} compute the split and widths of $N_{\perp}$ after the breaking, and bound the injected asymmetry ($\delta^{2}\sin\varphi$ from $N_{u'}$, $O(\sin\varphi)$ from the pair) from the descent's own dynamics rather than from the real-breaking assumption, which would convert the fifth stance from conditional to derived. (5) \emph{Dataset currency:} this paper's numbers are date-stamped by construction; the prediction table above is the object to refresh, and the capstone maintains the series-wide version.
