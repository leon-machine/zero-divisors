## formal
Degeneracy is conserved: it can be pushed off the physical region, never annihilated globally. This is the first geometric instance of the conservation principle conjectured at the fibre level in \cite[Conj.~7.9]{ButlerT1} (obstructions are moved, never destroyed), and the sharpest open question this paper poses is its measure: to construct a cohomological invariant on $\Sig$, presumably a relative class built from the mod-8 residues of the two competing ascents on the two sides of the wall, whose nonvanishing forces the shadow wall's existence, and whose behaviour under the iteration of Lemma \claimref{sheaf.tame-wall-induction} advances the clock of \cite[\S7]{ButlerT1}. The precise formulation is given in Section \ref{sec:dictionary} among the successor problems.
