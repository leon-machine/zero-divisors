## formal
Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.walls-free-sector} is a theorem about a soldering form with a radical: $g_{\mu\nu} = \eta_{ab}e^a_\mu e^b_\nu$ degenerate, the metric on tangent vectors acquiring a null direction. The sheaf paper's tame wall is a degeneration of the \emph{cometric}, the form on covectors, and the two are not the same degeneration. The following proposition records exactly how they differ, what the sheaf paper's wall looks like in soldering variables, and which piece of the completion the wall's extra freedom is.
