## formal
Together, Theorems \claimref{sheaf.variable-weight-boundary-tameness-completion-forced} and \claimref{sheaf.stability-reduction-strict-diagonal-case} close the sheaf-theoretic half of the anisotropic successor problem for the full relatively admissible diagonal class; for exact Schwarzschild the Gram errors against the frozen Kasner model are $O(r)$ relative (Fact \claimref{sheaf.weighted-limit}'s rates), well inside it. Uniqueness transfers in the qualified form of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.uniqueness-weighted-module}: within a fixed variable-order structure, the determinant argument (with $r$ replaced by the canonical defining element) shows the weighted module contains no second boundary-tame extension; an analogue of the rain-frame anchoring class is not pursued here, and Example \claimref{note.sheaf.rain-boundedness-essential-null-mixing-extension}'s mechanism is available in the variable-order setting too. Sections \ref{subsec:smoothstructure} and \ref{subsec:collisions} complete the algebraic side: the smooth structure of $\mathcal{A}$ (answered, with unavoidable logarithms) and the mixmaster boundary (the collision dichotomy and the edge of the wall). What is deliberately not claimed is recorded in Section \ref{subsec:schwscope}, and it is analysis alone: the dictionary with \cite{ButlerT6}'s admissibility for genuine solutions, and the dynamical traversal of the edges.
