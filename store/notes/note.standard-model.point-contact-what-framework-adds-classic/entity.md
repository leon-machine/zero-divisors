## formal
The numbers in Proposition \claimref{standard-model.spectrum-run} are the grand-unification literature's, and the paper claims no novelty in them. What the framework changes is the epistemic status of one endpoint. In $SU(5)$ the value $3/8$ rests on embedding the observed content into an assumed group; here the content is derived, the lepton under (S), so what is inherited is the content and what remains assumed is either (S), which supplies the simple group through Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor}, or, without (S), the simple group itself, the hypothesis of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-one-loop-running-trace-ratio}(b): the trace ratio is derived on one side and rests on a declared premise on the other, and the paper says so. The desert's refutation is, in the literature, an argument against minimal models; here it is the failure of a natural conjecture about the framework's own derived content (the capstone \cite{ButlerT17} records it as the series' fourteenth refutation), and its precise form, that the running between the scales must change, becomes a structural prediction: partner-like content, which the tower sector must either supply or be refuted by, or the intermediate symmetry of Section \ref{sec:split}. The dark-matter paper T14 \cite{ButlerT14} registers exactly that question as an open successor problem of its own.
