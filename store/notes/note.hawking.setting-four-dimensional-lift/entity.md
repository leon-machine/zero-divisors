## formal
The results so far are stated for the boundary modes, in the effectively two-dimensional near-horizon theory whose unit is the hyperbolic atom. The physical spectrum is four-dimensional radiation sorted by helicity and angular momentum and filtered by the exterior geometry. This section assembles the lift. It is the paper's principal assembly section, its Tier B, and the helicity assembly's joints are itemised by name so that each carries its own provenance: one joint is a classical theorem, one is a theorem of this series completed in Proposition \claimref{hawking.homogeneity-ordering-shift} below, and one is modelling, flagged as such; the greybody assembly itemises three more and adds one named premise, (I), its only non-theorem joint. What the lift does and does not claim is bounded in Remark \claimref{note.hawking.what-lift-does-does-not-claim}.
