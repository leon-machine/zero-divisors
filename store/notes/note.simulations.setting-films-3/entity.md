## formal
\textbf{Film 2: the Bullet-like moment.} \file{bullet\_moment.mp4} ($20$ s, $1600 \times 900$) uses the snapshot of reading (C)'s run in Table \ref{tab:merger} at $t = 1.20$ ($1.17$ Gyr). It adds the layers one at a time (galaxies, our gas, mirror gas, total mass) with a sentence on each, then marks the subcluster's galaxy peak, its mass peak, its gas peak and the main cluster's mass peak, and lists the simulated values beside the observed ones of Fact \claimref{simulations.imported-bullet-cluster}: separation $729$ kpc against $720$; mass peak $5$ kpc from the galaxies against about $42$ kpc, within $1\sigma$; gas $355$ kpc behind against an $8\sigma$ offset; mass-to-light $0.82 \pm 0.17$ against $0.84 \pm 0.07$ (the film shows the change since $t = 0$ with its $21$ percent sampling error, Section \ref{sec:measure}); contrast $4.7$, with CDM's $7.5$ for reference.
