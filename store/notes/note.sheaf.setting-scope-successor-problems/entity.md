## formal
The tame class (T1)-(T3) is the boundary of Sections \ref{sec:setup} through \ref{sec:exceptional}, and each failure mode is either executed in Section \ref{sec:schwarzschild} or assigned to a named successor. \textbf{Anisotropic degeneration}: when different directions degenerate at different rates, the single rescaling $k \mapsto k/\sqrt t$ must be replaced by direction-dependent weights; the conjecture that the correct weights are the Kasner exponents of the BKL limit, and that the exceptional algebra of a black-hole singularity is the Clifford algebra of its Kasner form, is the \emph{Kasner-weights conjecture} of this programme. Section \ref{sec:schwarzschild} settles it for exact Schwarzschild in the diagonal class, where the weights are forced (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.weights-are-forced-are-kasner-exponents}), and Section \ref{sec:anisotropic} closes the diagonal class with position-dependent exponents at the algebraic level, stably under relatively admissible errors; what remains is the analytic dictionary with the admissibility class of \cite{ButlerT6}. \textbf{Higher codimension}: the Schwarzschild worldline has codimension three; hypersurface methods apply only after a geometric blow-up replacing the worldline by its normal sphere bundle. Section \ref{sec:schwarzschild} carries out the composite (geometric blow-up, then tame completion on the exceptional boundary) for exact Schwarzschild. For Kerr, whose ring has codimension two, the degeneration is of a genuinely new kind: preliminary computation by the methods of Section \ref{sec:schwarzschild} indicates that on the oblate blow-up the mass divergence concentrates as a rank-one null pulse along the lightlike ring generator, winding as the normal-circle cosine and vanishing on the two disc directions, so that fixed-power weights fail, the variable-order methods of Section \ref{sec:anisotropic} together with a focal double cover appears forced, and the winding-three duality monodromy conjectured in \cite{ButlerT6} appears as an integer winding on the oblate exceptional circle; the Kerr composite itself remains open. \textbf{Edge regularity}: where a tame wall meets a boundary or corner, the collar constructions need uniform control not provided here. \textbf{The twisted completion}: for walls with non-orientable dying direction, $w_1(\shL) \neq 0$, the coupling construction requires the $\shL$-twisted ambient plane $\shL \oplus \shL^*$ in place of $\underline{\R}^2$ (Section \ref{subsec:choices}, item 2); the twisted variant of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.one-sided-completion} is expected to be routine but is not carried out here. \textbf{The wall class}: Conjecture \claimref{sheaf.wall-class}. Within the tame class, nothing is left conditional: every statement of Sections \ref{sec:degeneration} through \ref{sec:exceptional} is proved, the boundary extension of Section \ref{sec:schwarzschild} is proved for exact Schwarzschild, and the variable-weight extension of Section \ref{sec:anisotropic} is proved for the relatively admissible diagonal class.
