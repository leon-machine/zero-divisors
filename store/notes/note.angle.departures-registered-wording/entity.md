## formal
Three. (1) K3 and P3 as registered wrote the image of $c\ket{10} + s\ket{01}$ under the particle-hole map as $c\ket{00} + s\ket{11}$, up to sign. The image is $s\ket{00} + c\ket{11}$: $V$ inverts the right occupation, sending $\ket{01}$ to $\ket{00}$ and $\ket{10}$ to $\ket{11}$, and fixes $a_{0}$, so the left marginal is preserved and the amplitudes exchange places; at the sample point the image is the singularity paper's pair $\tfrac45\ket{00} + \tfrac35\ket{11}$ rather than its transpose, and the sign is that of the convention $\ket{11} = a^{\dagger}_{0}a^{\dagger}_{1}\ket{00}$. K3 is read as held because its substance, that the image is the thermofield pair with the same marginal and the hop generator becomes a pairing generator, holds. (2) K5 as registered attributed the chiral half-line case to Hislop--Longo; the note cites Hislop--Longo for the free massless field and the conformal-net theorem for the chiral case, and states the abstract form as Borchers' theorem, which is the form the route needs. (3) Proposition \claimref{angle.wedge-reflection-translations-are-ascent}(i)'s rescaling of the null generators and Remark \claimref{note.angle.one-term-two-vacua}'s exchange in the ascent are outside the registration.
