## formal
\textbf{Prior art, and the two ledgers.} The dilation, the Kraus form and the minimal environment are Stinespring, Kraus and Choi; the parity constraint is the univalence rule in its operational form; the Thouless state and the Witt vacuum are imports. What the note proves for itself is the even-state Kraus rank $2^{\nu-1}$, the count of one rung per mode with the second rung forced by parity, the explicit lift and its factorisation into a supplied-class coupling and a pairing rotation, the identification of that rotation with the singularity paper's witness family, the number-conservation obstruction and its indifference to where the pairing acts, the stored-coherence and partial-lift computations, and Proposition \claimref{descent-lift.within-supplied-dynamics-descent-has-no}. Under the two-ledger discipline the result is transport-absent: the rungs are where the record lives and the count matches the tower's one mode per rung, but the lift exists for any $\nu$ fermionic modes adjoined by any means, the univalence rule and Stinespring's theorem fix the count without the $(1,1)$ increment, and the transport principle does not force the lift (K5). The ascent supplies the vocabulary in which the record is counted, not a step the argument needs; and Proposition \claimref{descent-lift.pairing-creator-under-transport-algorithm} shows that the creator from which the one generator the argument needs is built is one the principle classifies as costing nothing.
