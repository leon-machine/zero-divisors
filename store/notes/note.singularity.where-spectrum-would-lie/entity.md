## formal
The proposition fixes the thermal direction and does not fix the sphere: the KMS condition for the $B_1$ flow determines no state on the holonomies along the $\theta$- and $\varphi$-lines of the exceptional set, but it is not silent about them either, since the $B_1$ flow moves the angular subalgebra ($[B_1, B_2] = -R_{12}$ lies outside the span of $B_2$, $B_3$, $R_{23}$), so a state on the algebra the three boosts generate that is KMS for $B_1$ is constrained on the sphere holonomies through that action, and which $SO(3)$-invariant states meet the constraint is not settled here. That is where a spectrum, in the sense of a two-point function on the exceptional set, would have to come from, and it is recorded as a successor problem in Section \ref{sec:conclusion}, beside the question whether the KMS state of the boundary modes on the completed pair extends to a KMS state at $\beta_{\mathrm{H}} = 6\pi$ on the algebra of $t$-line holonomies of the whole collar, whose obstruction, if any, is the $O(\tau^{2/3})$ running of the rate before its limit.
