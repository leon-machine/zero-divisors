## formal
\textbf{Verdict.} K1 to K4 do not trigger, in the corrected framing: the two-rung count for the pair and the one-rung-per-mode count in general (Theorem \claimref{descent-lift.lift-costs-one-rung-per-mode}(ii)); the necessity of a generator that fails to conserve number, a pairing among quadratic ones (Theorem \claimref{descent-lift.lift-costs-one-rung-per-mode}(v)); the coupling in the supplied class (Theorem \claimref{descent-lift.lift-costs-one-rung-per-mode}(iv)); the stored coherence with the pair's Schmidt coefficients (Corollary \claimref{descent-lift.classical-shared-bit-reduced-view-pure}). K5 triggers, as registered: nothing in the root paper's transport principle forces a map to be lifted, so the step from ``the descent is one-way'' to ``the descent should be lifted'' is a reading of the principle, not a consequence of it. Of the predictions, P3 held as registered; P1, P4, P5 and P6 held with the framing corrected as Remark \claimref{note.descent-lift.departures-registered-wording} records; P2 held as to the two quanta the record must gain and not as to where the pairing acts (item (4) of the remark); P7 held as to K5 and not as to the registered grade (item (5)).
