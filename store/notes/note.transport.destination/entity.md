## formal
The geometric content of (3), for a null vector $v$: the value of ``division by $v$'' is an ideal point at null infinity \emph{determined by} $v$: it lies over the null direction of $v$, at a position fixed by the scale of $v$. The value remembers more than the direction alone: rescaling $v$ moves it, $[\,2v + \tfrac12\nInf\,] \neq [\,v + \tfrac12\nInf\,]$ (in the coordinates of Proposition \claimref{transport.swap-rescale-normal-form}, these are distinct points of the same ideal stratum). The answer to the forbidden division exists and is canonical; it merely does not lie in the original space. Together with Theorem \claimref{transport.no-go-level-elements}, this is the precise sense in which the ascent performs ``division by zero without an inverse operation'': the element remains non-invertible; the space of points acquires a value for the division.
