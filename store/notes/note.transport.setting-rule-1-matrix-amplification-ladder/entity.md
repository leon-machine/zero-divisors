## formal
Fix the completion $B = \Cl(p{+}1, q{+}1) = \Cl(\mathbb{V} \perp H)$ of Rule 1, with $H$ spanned by a hyperbolic pair normalised as in Section \ref{subsec:null}: null vectors $f, f^*$ with $f \cdot f^* = \tfrac12$, so that
\[
f^2 = (f^*)^2 = 0, \qquad ff^* + f^*f = 1, \qquad ff^*f = f, \quad f^*ff^* = f^* .
\]
(The conformal model's pair of Section \ref{sec:model} is the same plane in a different normalisation: $\nO \cdot \nInf = -1$, so $f = \nO$ and $f^* = -\tfrac12\nInf$ is a hyperbolic pair in the present sense.) Every vector of $H$ anticommutes with every vector of $\mathbb{V}$, so (as in Lemma \claimref{transport.decomposition-commutation}) $f x = \hat{x} f$ and $f^* x = \hat{x} f^*$ for $x \in A = \Cl(p,q)$, and the \emph{even} elements of $\Cl(H)$ (in particular $ff^*$ and $f^*f$) commute with all of $A$. The blade basis gives the Peirce-ready decomposition
\[
B \;=\; A \,\oplus\, Af \,\oplus\, Af^* \,\oplus\, Aff^* .
\]
