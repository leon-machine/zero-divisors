## formal
The de Sitter algebra of this world is two copies of this world, and the anti-de Sitter algebra of this world is this world merged with its mirror, with the mirror sitting on the momenta. This is a statement about the fibrewise Clifford algebra of $I^{\perp}$, which Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-frozen-point} supplies; it is not a statement that a negative cosmological constant would turn matter into mirror matter. The tower charge is soldered and superselected (Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-copy-theorem-soldered-residue}), and Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.every-path-mirror-crosses-two-time} below shows that no path of sign changes at fixed dimension four connects the two oriented towers without passing through a sterile signature. What the proposition does establish is that the sign of $\Lambda$ selects which of two non-isomorphic algebras the frozen point's translation generators live in, and that only one of them contains the mirror. The observed cosmological constant is positive \cite{Planck2018}, so the algebra realised in this universe is the doubled $\Cl(1,4)$ with no copy of the mirror in it; the mirror on the momenta is a statement about the other sign. The same source gives the present Hubble rate as exceeding the asymptotic rate $\sqrt{\Lambda/3}$ of Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.exceptional-algebra-residue-completion-both-sides}(ii) by the factor $\Omega_{\Lambda}^{-1/2}$.
