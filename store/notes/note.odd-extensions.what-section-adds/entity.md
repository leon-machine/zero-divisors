## formal
That the family of intermediate algebras in $\Lambda$ is a tame wall at all is generic (Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.exceptional-algebra-residue-completion-both-sides}(iii)). What is specific to the cosmological constant is which objects of the dark-energy paper play the tame structure's roles: the frozen point's null direction is the radical, the Stelle-West line is the dying direction and the Stelle-West norm is the residue; the completion on both sides that the rung provides is the generic one for a family of hyperplanes and is recorded as an instance of the case the conservation theorem excludes, not as a property of $\Lambda$. None of this fixes $\Lambda$, its sign or its magnitude: the family is parametrised by $\Lambda$ and the theory describes the family.
