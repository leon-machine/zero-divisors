## formal
\cite[Rem.~7.4]{ButlerT5} records that the natural state satisfying (i) to (iii) is the thermofield double, Israel's reading of the eternal black hole \cite{Israel1976}. Theorem \claimref{state-selection.partner-purification-are-canonical} makes the word ``natural'' precise: the thermofield double is not one state among those satisfying the hypotheses, it is the canonical purification of the one-sided state, and the partner is not an extra system but the commutant. In the chain's own terms, the adjoined ladder's ``other end'' is produced by the modular conjugation of the state, so the ascent supplies the algebra and the state supplies the partner.
