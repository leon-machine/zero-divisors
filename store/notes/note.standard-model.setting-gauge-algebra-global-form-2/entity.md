## formal
Two readings, at observation level and flagged as such. The natural completion of Remark \claimref{note.standard-model.sixth-refutation-ladder-not-gauge-group}'s corrected conjecture, that the maximal hierarchy $\C^{3} \otimes \C^{3}$ carries the gauge group, fails on the seventeen; the framework's own correction, that mass begins at two, selects $\C^{2} \otimes \C^{3}$; the capstone \cite[Table~1]{ButlerT17} records this refutation. And the algebra of the observed forces is, on this reading, the automorphism algebra of the smallest composite that can have mass.
