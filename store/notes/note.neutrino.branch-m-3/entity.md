## formal
The measured $m_{2}/m_{3} \approx 0.17$ and Lemma \claimref{neutrino.breaking-relation} leave two branches. With $\alpha$ of order $M_{3}$ the Dirac breaking would need $\delta$ of order one, which is not a perturbation and would void the second-order language and the analogy behind Premise (H). With $\alpha \ll M_{3}$ the breaking stays a correction. The size of $\delta$ is calibrated against the paper's own texture: a charged-lepton matrix $c(\J + \delta_{c}e_{3}e_{3}^{\mathsf{T}})$ reproduces $m_{\mu}/m_{\tau} \approx 0.0595$ \cite{PDG2024} at $\delta_{c} = 0.286$ by exact singular values (the leading-order relation is $m_{\mu}/m_{\tau} = \tfrac29\delta_{c}$), and if the Dirac neutrino breaking is of the same size in the same direction, an assumption made for the instance, then the measured $m_{2}/m_{3}$ is met at $M_{3}/\alpha \approx 66$ by exact diagonalisation (the perturbative root; the exact ratio is not monotone in $M_{3}/\alpha$, peaking near $0.24$ at about $176$, and a second root past the peak lies where the lemma's remainders dominate), that is $\alpha/M_{3} \approx 1.5 \times 10^{-2}$; $\delta$ and $M_{3}/\alpha$ are the two parameters of a fitted instance, not a test, and $M_{3}/\alpha$ scales as $\delta^{-2}$. This paper takes the second branch, and it is the one the Gram-square reading supplies: $M_{R} \propto \J$ at leading order means $\alpha = 0$ there, and $\alpha$ arises at the order of the breaking of the Majorana sector, $\alpha = \delta' M_{3}$, so that the measured ratio is a relation between the two breakings: at leading order $\delta^{2}\lambda_{A}/(9\delta')$, which the exact diagonalisation reduces to the measured $0.17$ at $\delta' = 1/66 \approx 1.5 \times 10^{-2}$ for $\delta = 0.286$ (the leading-order form would put $\delta'$ at $0.024$, an overestimate by more than a half at these sizes); a relation the second-order programme must satisfy and that this paper records rather than derives. Two consequences follow from the branch: the pair $N_{\perp}$ is near $\alpha \approx 9 \times 10^{12}$ GeV at the top of Hypothesis (U) (and near $5.1 \times 10^{9}$ GeV at its bottom), nearly two orders ($\log_{10}66 = 1.8$) below $M_{3}$; and the comparison of $0.17$ with the charged-sector ratios is not like for like, since the neutrino ratio carries the seesaw factor $M_{3}/\alpha$ that the charged ratios do not. The same lemma says what Premise (H) asks of the direction: the first term carries both $m_{2}$ and $m_{1}$, with $m_{1}/m_{2} = \lambda'_{A}/\lambda_{A}$ the eigenvalue ratio of $\Pi A\Pi A^{\mathsf{T}}\Pi$, so (H) is the statement that the first stage of the breaking is effectively rank one on $u'^{\perp}$, as the $S_{3} \to S_{2}$ direction $e_{3}e_{3}^{\mathsf{T}}$ is exactly ($\lambda'_{A} = 0$), and as a generic direction is not. What fixes $\alpha$ and the direction is successor problem (2). On the Standard-Model paper's Pati-Salam route the Majorana matrix is $v_{R}f$, with $f$ the $126$ coupling the charged fermions share \cite[Fact~11.1, Prop.~11.5]{ButlerT12}, so there the texture is not free; that paper's fits reach the measured masses with textures of their own, the untuned one with $m_{1}$ inside Premise (H) \cite[Obs.~11.7]{ButlerT12}, and whether the branch taken here is among them is not settled.
