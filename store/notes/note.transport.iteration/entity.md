## formal
Iterating Theorem \claimref{transport.rule-1-morita-trivial} gives $\Cl(p{+}k, q{+}k) \cong \Mat_{2^k}(\Cl(p,q))$: the tower over any base is Morita-stationary from its first step. The structural consequences of the iterated statement (tower rigidity, the one-mode-per-rung reading, and the direct limit) are the subject of the series' tower paper \cite{ButlerT3} and are not developed here.
