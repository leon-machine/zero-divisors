## formal
The interpretation the conjecture below rests on: a curvature operator is an $\mathfrak{so}$-valued function of $\mathfrak{so}$-arguments (the bivector-valued function of bivectors $R(B)$ of \cite{ButlerT6}), and Lemma \claimref{correspondence.ordering-filtration} says that the ordering discrepancy $q(M)q(N) \mapsto \hbar\,q([M,N])$ of two $\mathfrak{so}$-elements is the first slot in the palatial algebra with that shape. This is a reading of the lemma, not part of it.
