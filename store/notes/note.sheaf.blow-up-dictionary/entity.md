## formal
Proposition \claimref{sheaf.exceptional-algebra-deformation-normal-cone} is the deformation to the normal cone \cite[Ch.~5]{Fulton1998} transcribed into Clifford algebra: the exceptional algebra over $x \in \Sig$ is built from the surviving tangential form together with the normal derivative of the degeneration, normal-cone data alone, exactly as the exceptional divisor of a blow-up replaces each point of the centre by its normal directions. The substitution $k \mapsto k/\sqrt{t}$ is the blow-up chart; the ambient description of Section \ref{sec:ambient} and the rescaled description here are its two standard charts; and Theorem \claimref{sheaf.metrics-residue-square-root-twist} identifies the tautological bundle of the correspondence: not $\shL^* \otimes N^*\Sig$ but the square-root identity. Since $\shL^{\otimes 2} \cong N^*\Sig$ says that $k$ carries the weight of a half-power of a defining function, the rescaling is exactly the normalisation of a conormal square root by the inverse square root of the defining function: the adjoined dimension is constructed tautologically from the dying direction and the geometry of its degeneration.
