## formal
The standard alternatives sit on the line as follows. $\beta_k = \infty$ for all $k$: the Boulware analogue, ladder unoccupied, no radiation, zero deficit, excluded by (R). $\beta_k = 2\pi/\kappa$ for all $k$: the Hartle-Hawking and Unruh analogue, the GMC state, selected by (R). $\beta_k$ finite but not constant in $k$: no geometric modular flow by Corollary \claimref{singularity.universality-temperature-under-g}, so a state for which the modular clock is not the Killing boost at all. $\beta_k = 0$: the tracial state, infinite temperature, excluded with the other boundary case. The chain's contribution to the dictionary is that the entries are points of an explicitly parametrised line rather than incommensurable constructions.
