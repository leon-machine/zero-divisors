## formal
Theorem \claimref{scrambling.epoch-modular-period-two-unknowns-become} leaves one number, and Fact \ref{fact:capacity} places it at the exceptional set of the weighted completion. The geometry paper's weighted limit holds at transport level along every tangential curve of fixed Hubble-normalised length for the whole admissible class (the metrics of \cite[Def.~5.5]{ButlerT6}, whose Kasner-type exponents satisfy $\sum_i p_i^2 = 1$, as Schwarzschild's $(-\tfrac13, \tfrac23, \tfrac23)$ do), and at Schwarzschild the $\varphi$-lines converge with rate $\tau^{1/3}$ and the boundary data are regular by symmetry \cite[\S 5.5]{ButlerT6}; the prediction is stated at Schwarzschild, where no billiard arises. So no analytic obstacle remains between the framework and $\nu$; what remains is a calibration principle, carried by this paper (Remark \claimref{note.scrambling.density-factor-stated-full}), and the results below say what it must produce: the required thinning factorises; exactness excludes the cutoff from the count and narrows the normalisation to two cutoff-free scales, the horizon $r_h$ and $r_* = M$; one tick per modular period is (IC) read as a counting rule; and the residue is Bekenstein-Hawking's quarter, which in the one convention that compares an entropy with an entropy is $1/(4\ln 2)$, unreachable by any counting rule whose residue is rational ($k = 0$ in Proposition \claimref{scrambling.no-recount-reaches-quarter}) and, conditionally on the expected algebraic independence of $\pi$ and $\ln 2$, by any counting rule at all.
