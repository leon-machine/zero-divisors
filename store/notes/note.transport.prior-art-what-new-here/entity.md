## formal
The isomorphism $\Cl(p{+}1,q{+}1) \cong \Mat_2(\Cl(p,q))$ is classical: it is the algebraic form of the $(1,1)$-periodicity of Clifford algebras \cite{ABS1964, Porteous1995, Lounesto2001}, usually obtained from $\Cl(p{+}1,q{+}1) \cong \Cl(p,q)\,\hat{\otimes}\,\Cl(1,1)$ and $\Cl(1,1) \cong \Mat_2(\R)$. What we claim as expository contribution is the presentation: the matrix units \emph{are} the transported ladder pair and its occupation projectors, so the periodicity isomorphism is not auxiliary to the transport principle but is the transport principle's own bookkeeping: the ascent adjoins exactly one row and column's worth of new structure, and the relations $f^2 = 0$, $ff^* + f^*f = 1$ are the matrix-unit relations of that adjunction. The parallel with Rule 2 is now exact (Remark \claimref{note.transport.2-pattern-two-rules-compared}): both rules amplify by $\Mat_2$, Rule 1 over the whole base, Rule 2 over its even half, and in both cases the matrix units are built from the completion's own canonical elements: the ladder pair for N, the central projectors plus the adjoined generator for C.
