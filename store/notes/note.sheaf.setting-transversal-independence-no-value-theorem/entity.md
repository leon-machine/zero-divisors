## formal
The coupling of Definition \claimref{obj.sheaf.collar-data-coupling} uses a transversal, and two questions were left open: whether the completion depends on the choice, and whether the wall admits an equivariant classical value for the dying direction at all, the no-repair theorem of \cite[Thm.~4.2]{ButlerT1} being proved where the ambient form is nondegenerate and the dying direction has a hyperbolic partner. Both are settled here, at the wall, with the collar constants $\mu, \nu$ and the bump held fixed. Throughout, computations are at a point of $\Sig$ on the inner collar, where $\beta = 1$ and the Gram $G$ of the coframe module has corank one with radical $\R k$; $\alpha$ ranges over covectors, $u, v, \lambda$ over vectors, and $\alpha(v)$ is the pairing. One term is fixed before anything is compared. The \emph{completed structure} at the point is the triple $(T, h_u, k)$: the ambient module with its form and the dying direction, carrying the $\mathbb{Z}_2$-grading of its Clifford algebra and the subalgebra generated by the coframe module (the fibre of the degeneration sheaf). The named frame $(\nO, \nInf)$ of Definition \claimref{obj.sheaf.collar-data-coupling} and the ladder partner $k^* = \nO/2\nu$ of Corollary \claimref{sheaf.localised-transport} are choices made inside that structure, not part of it; had the frame been retained as structure, the transversal would be recoverable from it, $\alpha(u) = 2h_u(\alpha, k^*)$ for $\alpha \in T^*M$, and Theorem \claimref{sheaf.no-equivariant-value-wall} would have nothing to say. What the theorem says is that neither choice is canonical. Every identity below is verified in exact rational arithmetic on the Schwarzschild data of Section \ref{sec:schwarzschild} at two masses (Section \ref{sec:verification}).
