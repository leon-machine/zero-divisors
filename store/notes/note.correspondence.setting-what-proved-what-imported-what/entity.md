## formal
\emph{Proved in this paper, at the standard of \cite{ButlerT1}:} the Fock model theorem and the chirality split (Theorem \claimref{correspondence.fock-model-complexified-ascended-algebra}); the Chevalley pairing with its four sign clauses (Lemma \claimref{correspondence.chevalley-pairing}); the spinor identification $V_\C \cong \Lambda^2\T$ with the norm correspondence and the Klein-quadric clause (Theorem \claimref{correspondence.spinor-identification}); the skew-lines/splitting/hyperbolic-plane equivalence (Proposition \claimref{correspondence.skew-lines-splittings-hyperbolic-planes-one}); the incidence theorem with the term-by-term chart identification and coverage (Theorem \claimref{correspondence.incidence-relation-chart}); the reality proposition: the conjugation, the Hermitian twistor form, the $\Sigma$-nullity of real incidence lines, the $(2,2)$ signature and the factor $i$ (Proposition \claimref{correspondence.reality-conjugation-twistor-form-factor}); the chiral planes and the summand spaces (Lemma \claimref{correspondence.chiral-planes-summand-spaces}); the three-language dictionary (Corollary \claimref{correspondence.one-statement-three-languages}); the tractor-connection decomposition and the local twistor identification up to the one imported convention of Remark \claimref{note.correspondence.one-imported-convention-local-twistor-identification} (Proposition \claimref{correspondence.local-twistor-transport-ascended-spinor-connection}); the statistics no-go (Proposition \claimref{correspondence.statistics-no-go}); the spin representation isomorphism (Proposition \claimref{correspondence.spin-representation}); the purity and Fock-vacuum theorem (Theorem \claimref{correspondence.twistors-are-states-three-ladder-pairs}); the palatial quadratic algebra, the Clifford ordering shift, and the shared-core theorem (Lemma \claimref{correspondence.symmetrised-quadratics-palatial-algebra}, Proposition \claimref{correspondence.clifford-side-ordering-shift}, Theorem \claimref{correspondence.shared-core-ordering-dictionary}); the Lorentzian duality lemma and the half-flat no-go (Lemma \claimref{correspondence.lorentzian-duality-complex-structure}, Proposition \claimref{correspondence.no-real-lorentzian-vacuum-half-flat}); the ordering filtration and the ASD ordering insensitivity (Lemma \claimref{correspondence.ordering-filtration}, Proposition \claimref{correspondence.chirality-split-ordering-insensitivity-asd-sector}).

\emph{Imported as Facts with provenance:} the forced completion and the real ladder results (Facts \claimref{correspondence.imported-forced-completion-fibre-quadric}, \claimref{correspondence.imported-ladder-pairs-fock-modules-over}); the flat-model stabilisers (Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-stabilisers-flat-model}); the vacuum/scale-tractor equivalences (Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-vacuum-equations-scale-tractor}); the tractor connection and the classical local twistor formula (Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-tractor-connection-local-twistor-transport}); the nonlinear graviton (Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-nonlinear-graviton}); the Weyl-star commutation (Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-weyl-commutes-duality}); Weyl's theorem (Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-weyls-theorem}); complete reducibility for $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\C)$ (Fact \claimref{correspondence.imported-complete-reducibility}); the sign convention relating Penrose and Rindler's $\mathrm{P}_{ab}$ to the Schouten tensor (Remark \claimref{note.correspondence.one-imported-convention-local-twistor-identification}).

\emph{Conjectural, and declared as such:} the googly localisation (Conjecture \claimref{correspondence.googly-localisation-quartic-order}), whose ordering-insensitivity consistency conditions are proved (Proposition \claimref{correspondence.chirality-split-ordering-insensitivity-asd-sector}) and whose analytic half is open.
