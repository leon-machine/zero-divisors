## formal
T5's Proposition 9.6 proves that the composite of the pointwise descents is the pinching $\EN$ and that its range is the joint-diagonal algebra; what it leaves as an antecedent is that the metric-level reading of a \emph{region} of the ledger is the simultaneous performance of the pointwise descents. This section shows that the antecedent is the statement that the metric level is \emph{local}: the pointwise metric levels assemble into a genuine sheaf on $\Sig$, that sheaf's sections are the compatible families, its finite truncations are exactly $\operatorname{range}(\EN)$, and the rival reading is disqualified on sheaf-theoretic grounds alone, not by preference. One point deserves emphasis before the definitions: the risk named at the outset, that the pointwise even algebras might fail to glue, resolves positively, and the obstruction that would have prevented gluing is precisely the standing hypothesis the completion already carries. (Status now, 7 November 2026: the standing hypothesis $w_1(\shL) = 0$ is needed for the untwisted ladder bundle; $\mathfrak{M}$ itself, built from $\operatorname{Ad}\sigma$, glues on a twisted ladder bundle as well and does not see the twist, Proposition \claimref{three-routes.it-sheaf-risk-resolves-into-standing}.)
