## formal
First, \emph{state selection}: Proposition \claimref{one-relation.two-presentations} identifies the kinematic algebra, and the physical state remains the flagged remainder of the thermality theorem; a selected state incompatible with the incidence-line structure of Proposition \claimref{one-relation.spacetime-manifold-vacuum-lines}, or one requiring more than the two real or three complex modes that the single ascent the conformal completion licenses, to $\Cl(2,4)$, supplies (Fact \claimref{one-relation.imported-pure-spinors-incidence-chart}, Proposition \claimref{one-relation.two-capacities-three-consequences}), would break the vacuum-line reading. Second, \emph{the laboratory problem}: whether ordinary nonrelativistic quantum mechanics is recoverable as excitation dynamics above a vacuum chosen on a regular point's incidence line, with the Hamiltonian descending from the boost clock (the boost generator of the horizon pair, the singularity paper's clock) in the appropriate limit, is settled by no numbered result here; Proposition \claimref{one-relation.spacetime-manifold-vacuum-lines}(4) claims ubiquity only. Sections \ref{sec:lab}-\ref{sec:experience} carry that computation out at the levels they declare, and the claim is quantum mechanics everywhere, not only at interfaces. What the laboratory leaves as its remainder, after the sharpening of Lemmas \claimref{one-relation.form-law-forced-square} to \claimref{one-relation.one-datum-not-two-imaginary-unit}, is not the form of the law, which is forced, but why the evolution parameter is the frame's time and which frame: the frame cannot be sourced from the law, since the frozen point is $SO(1,4)$-invariant and any theorem selecting a $\gamma_{0}$ would break that; it is state data or observer data, and sourcing it is thermal time plus state selection, the geometric modular action the capstone carries as the open problem together with successor (2) here, and it is named and not opened. A third item, at programme level: if the earliest correlations are sourced by the descent, the primordial spectrum is the shape-shadow of a fibre state projected through the freezing, a computation whose output would confront the measured spectral index and would be a further registered stance; it is named in the successors and assigned to the capstone \cite{ButlerT17}.
