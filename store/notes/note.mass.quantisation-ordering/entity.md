## formal
Everything in this section is classical: $P$ and $m^{2}$ are functions on the phase space of two conformal spinors, and $\hbar$ below is the formal parameter of Section \ref{sec:setting}. The quantisation of the generators is the palatial one of Fact \claimref{mass.imported-palatial-quadratics-homogeneity-charge}, symmetrised quadratics with $[q(M), q(M')] = \hbar q([M,M'])$, and \eqref{eq:grip} is then an identity between quartic operators up to ordering terms of order $\hbar$ times quadratics and $\hbar^{2}$ times constants; the correspondence paper's ordering dictionary \cite[Thm.~5.7]{ButlerT9} is the method for computing them. The operator form of \eqref{eq:grip}, with its ordering constants, is the first successor problem of Section \ref{sec:gravity}. Nothing in the classical identity depends on the choice, and the flat-space limit of the operator identity is the classical Poincar\'e Casimir of the two-spinor system, which is standard.
