## formal
The singularity paper's residual question asks whether the physical dynamics on the boundary carries a differential term or a pairing term, the two kinds of generator its supplied class lacks. Proposition \claimref{angle.hop-sea-filled-pairing} says the question has one answer, not two: a hop between a mode and its partner, with the partner's sea filled, is a pairing between the mode and the partner's hole, and the ground state of the one is the thermofield pair of the other. That a number-conserving generator applied to the vacuum keeps the pair in its vacuum, while a hop applied to a one-particle state produces the thermofield marginal, is the same fact read in the two vacua: what the hop needs is the filled sea the s-wave array of Step 4e supplies, and what the pairing needs is the vacuum. The particle-hole map $V$ is the Fock form of a symmetry of the ascent itself: in the two-rung ascent $\Cl(2,2)$ with ladder pairs $f_{k} = \tfrac12(e_{k} + e_{k+2})$, $f_{k}^{*} = \tfrac12(e_{k} - e_{k+2})$, the reflection $e_{4} \mapsto -e_{4}$ is an automorphism that fixes $f_{1}$, carries $f_{2}$ to $f_{2}^{*}$, and carries the hop $f_{1}^{*}f_{2}$ to the pairing $f_{1}^{*}f_{2}^{*}$ (script, Part E).
