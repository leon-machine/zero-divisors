## formal
\textbf{Consequences for the owning papers.} For the root paper: Theorem 7.6 can carry a clause, and Proposition \claimref{descent-lift.pairing-creator-under-transport-algorithm}(i) records a point for it to settle: Step 2 diagnoses a product of two adjoined null vectors by its grade, as type P, while Theorem 3.9(2) refines it to type N by the origin of its annihilating idempotent; the two branches agree that no element is returned, and the root paper should say which diagnosis governs such products. The pinching's kernel, the ladder sector, is recoverable by an even isometry into $\nu$ further rungs, one per pinched mode, so the door is one-way for algebra homomorphisms and for the dynamics the completion supplies, and two-way for the tower if a pairing rotation is admitted. For the singularity paper: Premise 7.30's pairing clause now has a second characterisation beside Proposition 13.4's tangent: among quadratic generators the pairing terms are exactly those whose absence makes the descent irreversible, and Corollary 11.6's witness rotation is the record preparation of the descent's lift at amplitude $\tfrac43$, which overshoots (Corollary \claimref{descent-lift.partial-lifts-witness-overshoots}); the complete lift is its member at amplitude $1$. Successor problems: whether the framework's own tower selects the amplitude $1$, that is, whether anything in the ascent prefers the maximally paired record; whether the lift composes along the tower, the record of the record costing $\nu$ further rungs each time; and the angle, which this note leaves where it found it.
