## formal
\textbf{What the note finds.}
\begin{itemize}
\item At composite strength the three-dimensional towers' matter is a fluid within its sheets, and its effective cross-section per mass in a cluster is $10^{8}$ to $10^{13}\ \cmg$ (Proposition \claimref{simulations.effective-cross-section-sheet-matter}, Table \ref{tab:sidm}). The confinement scale does not matter (Corollary \claimref{simulations.confinement-scale-does-not-matter}); the series does not supply it, since no coupling rule for the odd towers follows from the algebra \cite[Prop.~A.4]{ButlerT14}.
\item In a Bullet-like merger, reading (W) loses the subcluster from the lensing map (unless every sheet's normal lies along the merger axis); reading (C) keeps it, with its mass peak on its galaxies and its gas behind (Section \ref{sec:results}). T14's mixed count, which exchanges the two odd shares, is run alongside as a comparison and behaves as (W).
\item In an assembled cluster, (W) gives about $0.4$ of the cold-dark-matter (CDM) ellipticity, below CDM in both seeds, and (C) about $0.8$, overlapping it.
\item In the Milky Way, the mirror's proton-only branch is excluded; its helium branch, with helium-star feedback, fits the room at the Sun under some normalisations of the mirror's gas and survives in the bulge only if the bulge holds over twice the mirror's proportional share of its dark mass (Section \ref{sec:galaxy}).
\end{itemize}
