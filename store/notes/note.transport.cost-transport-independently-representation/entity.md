## formal
Theorems \claimref{transport.representation-independent-minimality-completion} and \claimref{transport.embedding-independent-minimality-fusion} together make the picture uniform across the two rules, and in both cases stronger than the quadratic-space normalisations under which the rules were first proved minimal. For type N, what an ambient algebra must supply is a quadric large enough to host the \emph{points}, and the conformal Gram matrix quantifies it exactly. For type C, what it must supply is a \emph{module} large enough to carry both ideals faithfully with the commutant the fusion requires, and the module count quantifies that exactly, against arbitrary simple real algebras rather than Clifford targets alone. In both cases the cost is independent of the embedding, and in both cases the minimal environment is the one the transport rule constructs.
