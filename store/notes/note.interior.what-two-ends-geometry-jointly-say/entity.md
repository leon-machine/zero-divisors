## formal
The frozen point governs both ends of the geometry, in opposite algebraic ways. At infinity, its stabiliser is rotated from parabolic to reductive by $\Lambda$ (Proposition \claimref{interior.stabiliser-rotates-parabolic-reductive}): the obstruction structure at infinity is removed by the cosmological constant. At the singularity, the approach gives nonzero limiting values in the boost part of the Levi factor, which generates it, and zero in the nilradical, for $\Lambda = 0$ and $\Lambda \neq 0$ alike (Theorems \claimref{interior.weighted-limit-theorem}, \claimref{interior.schwarzschild-de-sitter-weighted-limit}): the obstruction structure at the singularity is intact, and the weighted limit resolves it by transporting the description, not by removing the obstruction. This division is the geometric instance of the series' one-way door: what is obstructed is never repaired in place, and what survives is the conformal, ascended data.
