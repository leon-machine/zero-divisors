## formal
This is the linearise-then-normalise pattern of classical mathematics: M\"obius transformations $z \mapsto (az+b)/(cz+d)$ rendered total as linear maps on homogeneous pairs $(z,1)$ with the unnormalisable pairs $(\cdot\,,0)$ serving as the point at infinity; the homogeneous coordinates of projective geometry; the deferred ``perspective divide'' of computer graphics. All nonlinearity and all division are deferred to a single optional read-out, whose refusal is information (the answer is ideal), not failure. What Theorems \claimref{transport.no-go-level-elements}, \claimref{transport.totality} and \claimref{transport.minimality} add to the classical picture is a mandate: for the geometric reciprocal of a quadratic space, this pattern is not one available representation among many but the \emph{unique minimal} totalisation, realised on the null cone exactly two dimensions up, with the element-level alternative provably closed.
