## formal
The two remaining charts are T14's results, shown here for completeness. Figure \ref{fig:Tc} is the total against the thickness: the observed value is met at $c = 1$ and $c = \pi/2$, where the thickness was set, and the floor $5.255$, the family's one prediction independent of $c$, sits $1.7\sigma$ below Planck's value with lensing \cite{Planck2018}. A measured value clearly below $5.255$ would exclude the whole family. Figure \ref{fig:neff} is the radiation: with the dark sectors heated only through gravity the extra radiation is below $10^{-7}$ \cite[Props.~6.2 and 6.3]{ButlerT14}, so $N_{\mathrm{eff}}$ stays at the Standard Model's $3.044$, against the measured $2.99 \pm 0.17$.
