## formal
Write $\T = \Sc \oplus \Sc'$ with bases $e_{1}, e_{2}$ of $\Sc$ and $e_{3}, e_{4}$ of $\Sc'$, so that $n_{\infty} = e_{1}\wedge e_{2}$, $n_{0} = e_{3}\wedge e_{4}$, the $x$-slot is spanned by the mixed bivectors $e_{a}\wedge e_{a'}$ ($a \in \{1,2\}$, $a' \in \{3,4\}$), and $\varepsilon(e_{1}\wedge e_{2}\wedge e_{3}\wedge e_{4}) = 1$, which gives $\ip{n_{0}}{n_{\infty}} = +1$. A twistor is $Z = \omega + \pi = \omega^{a}e_{a} + \pi^{a'}e_{a'}$. Let $\varepsilon_{ab}$ and $\varepsilon_{a'b'}$ be the antisymmetric symbols with $\varepsilon_{12} = \varepsilon_{34} = 1$, so that $e_{a}\wedge e_{b} = \varepsilon_{ab}\,n_{\infty}$, $e_{a'}\wedge e_{b'} = \varepsilon_{a'b'}\,n_{0}$, and the brackets are the determinants $\br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}} := \varepsilon_{a'b'}\pi_{1}^{a'}\pi_{2}^{b'}$ and $[\omega^{1}\omega^{2}] := \varepsilon_{ab}\omega_{1}^{a}\omega_{2}^{b}$. For the ambient vector $U \wedge V$ let $R_{U\wedge V}$ be the rotation $W \mapsto \ip{U}{W}V - \ip{V}{W}U$ of $\Lambda^{2}\T$, and let $A(U \wedge V) \in \mathfrak{sl}(\T)$ be its preimage under the isomorphism of Fact \claimref{mass.imported-flat-model-twistor-chart-infinity}. Matrices act on column vectors in the basis $(e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3}, e_{4})$, in block form according to $\Sc \oplus \Sc'$.
