## formal
Throughout this section $A = \Cl(p,q)$ with $p - q \equiv 1 \pmod 4$, so $n = p + q$ is odd, $\omega$ is central with $\omega^2 = +1$, and $A = \varepsilon_+ A \oplus \varepsilon_- A$ splits along the central projectors $\varepsilon_\pm = \tfrac12(1 \pm \omega)$ (Theorem \claimref{transport.existence-classification-type-c}). We consider the Clifford algebras of one-dimensional extensions of the quadratic space: $\mathbb{V}' = \mathbb{V} \perp \langle \nu \rangle$ with $\nu$ orthogonal to $\mathbb{V}$ and $\nu^2 = \gamma \in \{+1, -1, 0\}$ (a nonzero square is rescaled to $\pm 1$; $\gamma = 0$ is the degenerate, null-generator case, which Theorem \claimref{transport.one-generator-minimal-minimal-completions-classified} shows never fuses). Write $B_\gamma = \Cl(\mathbb{V}', Q')$; for $\gamma = +1$ this is $\Cl(p{+}1, q)$, for $\gamma = -1$ it is $\Cl(p, q{+}1)$. Since $Q$ is nondegenerate on $\mathbb{V}$, the blade basis gives a canonical unital inclusion $A \subseteq B_\gamma$ in every case, degenerate included \cite{Chevalley1997}.
