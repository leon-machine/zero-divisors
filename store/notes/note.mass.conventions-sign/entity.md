## formal
The dictionary is written in the conventions of Fact \claimref{mass.imported-frozen-point-tractor-connection}, where $Q(I) = +\Lambda/3$, and there it carries a minus sign in the invariant form. The physical content is convention-independent: $m^{2} = \Delta(3-\Delta)H^{2}$ with $\Delta = -w$ is the de Sitter mass formula of Fact \claimref{mass.imported-unitary-representations-de-sitter-group}, and the conformally coupled scalar of Corollary \claimref{mass.weight-polynomial-two-series}(2) fixes it. The sign in the invariant form and the sign of the norm are tied together by the computation of Theorem \claimref{mass.mass-weight-dictionary}, and neither carries content on its own. \cite[Rem.~4.9]{ButlerT6} records that the norm reads $+\Lambda$ in the present conventions, and why the opposite sign could not carry the de Sitter stabiliser; this paper's displays are all in the present conventions. What no convention changes is the statement that a positive mass above $3H/2$ requires a complex weight.
