the tower paper T3 \cite{ButlerT3} for the ladder and for the capacity theorem this paper imports and sharpens (Facts \claimref{one-relation.imported-completion-ladder}, \ref{fact:capacity}); the sheaf paper T4 \cite{ButlerT4} for the localisation theorems read in Proposition \claimref{one-relation.spacetime-manifold-vacuum-lines}(4); the singularity paper T5 \cite{ButlerT5} for the single-mode thermality theorem under its premises, its thermofield angle (Remark \claimref{note.one-relation.where-exhaustiveness-first-fails-4-quadric}), and for carrying the localisation theorems in Schwarzschild form (Fact \claimref{one-relation.imported-single-mode-thermality-modular-period}); the twistor paper T9 \cite{ButlerT9} for the Fock model, the pure-spinor theorem and the incidence chart on which Proposition \claimref{one-relation.spacetime-manifold-vacuum-lines}(2) rests, and for the ordering shift (Fact \claimref{one-relation.imported-pure-spinors-incidence-chart}, Proposition \claimref{one-relation.overlap-table-sorted});
