## formal
The three couplings $g_{1}, g_{2}, g_{3}$ are the normalisations Proposition \claimref{standard-model.connection-forced} left free. At observation level, and flagged as such, the framework suggests a reason they should meet: all three factors act on one fibre. But this is not a proof, and the paper does not offer it as one. The invariant symmetric forms on $\su(2) \oplus \su(3) \oplus \uu(1)$ form a three-parameter family, one scale per summand, so ``one trace normalisation'' on the fibre picks one member of the family and does not privilege it; the trace form of $\C^{2} \otimes \C^{3}$ itself, restricted to the algebra, gives $\sum T_{3}^{2}/\sum Q^{2} = 9/10$ over the fibre, unconditionally, and $3/4$ over the two doublets alone, which needs Hypothesis (S) for the lepton doublet (both computed in the proof below), neither of them the unification value. What forces a normalisation is a simple unifying group, Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-one-loop-running-trace-ratio}(b), whose existence the framework derives only under Hypothesis (S), where Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor}(2) supplies $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$; without (S), Theorem \claimref{standard-model.global-form-quotient-determined}(i) says only that the global form is the one such an embedding requires. The unification hypothesis is therefore imported here at assembly level, with the note that (S) would discharge it, and the trace ratio is computed over the content of Theorem \claimref{standard-model.hypercharge-global-form-pair-counting-consistency}.
