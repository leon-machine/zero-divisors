## formal
The boundary mode space of Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.boundary-mode-algebra-windows-boost-dynamics} has countably many modes because the boundary modes, the ladder pairs of the blown-up singular boundary, one per point, are aggregated over the s-wave channels into the countable array of Theorem \claimref{singularity.bosonisation-bridge-s-wave-sector}. The tower of iterated completions is the algebra's model, not the source of the count: its limit is the CAR algebra with the adjoined null pairs as modes (Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-algebra-boundary-modes-car-algebra}), while on the physical closure one ascent completes the description (Corollary \claimref{tower.answers-iteration-question}). The physical spectrum is emitted into a continuum of frequencies and a tower of angular channels, and this section carries the state theorem across that idealisation with the levels declared: what is exact, what is an inductive-limit result, and what is a modelling identification are separated.
