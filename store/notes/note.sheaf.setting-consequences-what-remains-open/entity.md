## formal
\emph{The localisation import of the singularity paper.} The singularity paper \cite{ButlerT5} imports this paper's localisation for a case that failed the tame hypotheses. For exact Schwarzschild this section supplies the missing structure: the ascent and the ladder pair are theorem-backed \emph{at the black hole}, localised on $E \cong \R \times S^2$, in the boundary-tame weighted sense defined here. Separately, the corank ambiguity behind the count of boundary modes at the singularity (corank $1$, $2$ or $3$ depending on rescaling weight) is resolved structurally: with the weights of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.weights-are-forced-are-kasner-exponents}, forced once the dying direction is read off the geometry, the corank is exactly one, so there is one ladder pair per point of $E$, not one per dying coordinate direction. What this section does \emph{not} do is compute the quantitative content of the boundary mode space: the epoch and count analysis belongs to \cite{ButlerT8}, and the aggregation over the sphere factor of $E$ is exactly the slice-to-epoch question assigned to that paper.

\emph{The conservation principle.} The constant-determinant global completion of Remark \claimref{note.sheaf.conservation-degeneracy-boundary-sharp-example} is the first explicit example exhibiting the sharpness of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.conservation-degeneracy-absolute-form}'s standardness hypothesis, and the boundary case completes the trichotomy: interior wall, shadow wall; boundary wall, no wall; non-standard completion, wall at infinity.

\emph{What remains open.} Four problems, at the boundary of what this paper's methods reach. \textbf{The analysis of the anisotropic class}: Section \ref{sec:anisotropic} closes the diagonal class at the algebraic level, position-dependent exponents included, stably under relatively admissible errors, with the smooth structure of the function algebra characterised in Section \ref{subsec:smoothstructure} and the collision boundary settled in Section \ref{subsec:collisions}; what it does not supply is analysis: the dictionary identifying the connection-level admissibility of \cite{ButlerT6} with Gram-level relative admissibility for genuine velocity-dominated solutions, and the dynamical traversal of the Taub edges, the bounce and era maps themselves, both of which lie in \cite{ButlerT6}'s admissibility class. \textbf{Kerr}: the ring has codimension two and, unlike every case treated here, a lightlike dying direction (see Section \ref{subsec:scope}); the Kerr composite, on a twisted variable-order module over a focal double cover, is the genuine successor problem, and its geometry is assigned to \cite{ButlerT6}. \textbf{The operator and state level}: Section \ref{subsec:rainframe} answers the kinematic question of what $\shW$ is, the $u$-smooth shadow of what the infalling observer measures finitely; whether the fermionic modes and modular structures of the boundary mode space of \cite{ButlerT5}'s chain, built on the dyad shown there to be rain-finite, define states on the completion at the wall is a question for \cite{ButlerT5}'s framework. \textbf{The variable-exponent weighted limit}: Section \ref{subsec:weld} matches the weighted limit of \cite{ButlerT6} to the present construction at the fibre and at the connection level; extending the matching to position-dependent exponents is the question posed precisely in Remark \claimref{note.sheaf.what-remains-matching}.
