## formal
This paper is T6 of the series. It consumes four predecessors. From the primer T0 \cite{ButlerT0} it takes the working language of geometric algebra; from the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} the zero-divisor taxonomy, the no-go theorem whose transvections reappear here as the nilradical of the frozen point's stabiliser, and the completion that fixes the ambient algebra $\Cl(2,4)$; from the sheaf paper T4 \cite{ButlerT4} the wall-level theory of degeneration whose codimension-two extension Conjecture \claimref{interior.duality-monodromy-fibration-over-ring} names; and from the singularity paper T5 \cite{ButlerT5} the curvature computation imported as Fact \claimref{interior.imported-schwarzschild-curvature-spectrum} and the six-step chain whose three geometric links this paper closes. It supplies, downstream: the Hawking paper T7 \cite{ButlerT7} imports the GTG equivalence and the vacuum theorem (Fact \claimref{interior.imported-equivalence-einstein-equations}, Theorem \claimref{interior.covariant-form-vacuum-equations}); the entropy paper T8 \cite{ButlerT8} builds on the Kasner form of the interior (Lemma \claimref{interior.kasner-form-interior}), the weighted-limit results of Section \ref{sec:approach}, and the duality-monodromy conjecture (Conjecture \claimref{interior.duality-monodromy-fibration-over-ring}); the twistor paper T9 \cite{ButlerT9} develops the flat-model dictionary of Section \ref{sec:tractor}, the vacuum and $\Lambda$ theorems, and the Killing-spinor identification (Proposition \claimref{interior.complex-scale-inverse-cube-killing-spinor}); and the dark-energy paper T10 \cite{ButlerT10} takes the frozen point, its norm $\Lambda/3$, and the parabolic-to-reductive stabiliser rotation (Theorems \claimref{interior.covariant-form-vacuum-equations}, \claimref{interior.scale-tractor-leaves-cone}, Proposition \claimref{interior.stabiliser-rotates-parabolic-reductive}) as its kinematic base.
