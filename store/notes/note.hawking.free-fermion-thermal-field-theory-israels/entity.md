## formal
Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-thermal-representation} is the continuum instance of T5's single-mode picture: the doubling is the completed pair structure mode by mode, the GNS vector is the thermofield double, and the modular flow is the boost. ``Free-fermion thermal field theory as the algebra carrying the mode continuum'' is now a theorem-backed description: the algebra is $\CAR(\mathfrak{h})$ (Fact \claimref{hawking.imported-algebra-boundary-modes-car-algebra} plus Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.continuum-mode-space}), the state is the I-state (Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state-continuum}), and the representation it generates is the standard thermal one. \emph{Point of contact:} Israel's thermofield reading of the eternal black hole \cite{Israel1976} is exactly this representation with the second copy read as the far wedge; T5's eviction dynamics runs inside it.
