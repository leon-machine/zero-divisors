## formal
What remains is one binary choice. The doublets are the $\T'$ constituents; the free sector $\Sp$ of Fact \claimref{lepton.imported-free-sector-anomaly-conditions} has its own halves; and the anomaly conditions require every fermion to be listed in one handedness. Which half of the free sector is listed with the $\T'$ half of the slot sector is not decided by Hypothesis (C).
