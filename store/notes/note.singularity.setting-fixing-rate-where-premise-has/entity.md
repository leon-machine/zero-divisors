## formal
Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-rigidity-temperature-not-algebra-intrinsic}(3) says what the missing datum is: a conversion of the flow parameter into geometric time. The geometry supplies one, and supplies it at a definite place. Wick rotation of the exterior region turns the Killing boost into a rotation about the bifurcation sphere, and the section is a smooth manifold there for exactly one period of imaginary Killing time. That period is computed first; the premise then says that the state's correlation functions lie on the section.
