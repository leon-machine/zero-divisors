## formal
Propositions \claimref{scrambling.exact-factorisation-thinning} to \claimref{scrambling.no-recount-reaches-quarter} reduce the calibration to the form
\[
N_{\mathrm{epoch}} \;=\; \kappa_c \times (\text{horizon-normalised sphere cells}) \times (\text{one tick per modular period}),
\]
with the normalisation narrowed to two cutoff-free scales by exactness ($r_h$, or $r_* = M$, Proposition \claimref{scrambling.exactness-excludes-cutoff-dependence}), one tick per period being (IC) read as a counting rule, and $\kappa_c$ the residue. The residue is not an artefact of the counting conventions: the chain from tower to count carries no hidden factor of two (Proposition \claimref{scrambling.pair-mode-qubit-one-object}); the helicity clause is closed at the tier of the lift's modelling joint J3 (Proposition \claimref{scrambling.residue-each-convention}(iv), Fact \claimref{scrambling.imported-collars-coframe-exceptional-set-helicity}), not at theorem tier; and in the one convention with physical content the residue is $1/(4\ln 2)$, which no recount of the exceptional set with a rational residue ($k = 0$) produces, and, conditionally on the expected algebraic independence of $\pi$ and $\ln 2$, no recount with $k \neq 0$ either (Proposition \claimref{scrambling.no-recount-reaches-quarter}). The prediction's declared form is therefore: \emph{the boundary mode space registers one mode per Bekenstein-Mukhanov area quantum of horizon per modular period}, $\nu = 1/(16\pi\ln 2)$ in the capacity convention, and deriving that is equivalent to deriving Bekenstein-Hawking's quarter. Arithmetically $\kappa_c = \tfrac14$ in the 1-nat convention is horizon normalisation replaced by normalisation at $r_* = r_h/2 = M$, since $4\pi M^2 = \SBH$; whether that radius means anything is not decided here. The $16\pi$ of Theorem \claimref{scrambling.epoch-modular-period-two-unknowns-become} stands as the convention-dependent form of the same statement.
