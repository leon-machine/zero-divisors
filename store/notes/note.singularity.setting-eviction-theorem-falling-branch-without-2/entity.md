## formal
To these add (L$_E$) \emph{boundary-mode locality}, in the form the rate model needs: the window's internal dynamics is diagonal in the window's joint number basis (Premise \claimref{singularity.l-boundary-mode-locality} with Proposition \claimref{singularity.number-diagonal-dynamics-models-scrambling-derived}; diagonal, not merely number conserving, since a parity-preserving hopping changes the marginals), and, a clause the premise does not contain and which is stated here as part of the rate model, the emitted quanta, once in the radiation, are free and never return to the window. Both halves are used in the proof: a returning quantum or a non-diagonal window unitary breaks the identity below, the second by the example in Corollary \claimref{singularity.envelope-endpoint-why-boundary-mode-locality}. No further assumption about where the window places correlations is needed: (L$_E$) already fixes every window marginal, and that is what the theorem below depends on.
