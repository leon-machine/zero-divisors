## formal
Proposition \claimref{singularity.degeneration-exactly} used the uniform rescaling weight $\beta^{-2}$, and under it the limit form has corank three. The choice of weight is a genuine choice, and it is not an open one: the Schwarzschild composite of the sheaf paper settles it. There, after the real blow-up of the worldline (which absorbs the two angular rates as the exceptional divisor's own tautological rescaling), the rescaling weights are \emph{forced}, per the geometrically named dying slot, and equal the Kasner exponents $(-\tfrac13, \tfrac23, \tfrac23)$ \cite[Thm.~8.16]{ButlerT4}; the weighted coframe module carrying them is unique in the rain-bounded class \cite[Thm.~8.18]{ButlerT4}; and in that module the degeneration is boundary-tame of corank exactly \emph{one}, with radical generated by $k = \sqrt r\,dr + \sqrt{2M}\,dt_{\mathrm{PG}}$, conormal to the infalling congruence, whose $G$-length is $-r$ exactly at every radius, the Killing-time covector $dt_s$ spanning the radical line only on $E$ itself \cite[Thm.~8.7]{ButlerT4}. The corank-three limit of Proposition \claimref{singularity.degeneration-exactly} is the unweighted, uniform-rescaling shadow of that structure (the two forms rescale different objects, the metric on vectors here, the cometric on the weighted coframes there, so there is no contradiction; the forced answer is the weighted one). Where the answer bites is Step 6a: Axiom \claimref{singularity.c2-cell-density}'s cell density carries a corank factor, and that factor is fixed: one ladder pair per point of the blown-up singular boundary \cite[\S 8.9]{ButlerT4}, so the corank factor in $\nu$ is one, and what remains open in $\nu$ is the cell calibration and the epoch, assigned to the counting paper (T8). Nothing in Steps 1 through 4e depends on any of this.
