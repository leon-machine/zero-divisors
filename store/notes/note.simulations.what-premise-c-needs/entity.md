## formal
Premise C is not derived, and it is not T14's reading. What it needs is a statement about the three-dimensional towers' couplings: whether their abelian charges are gauge charges, as the Standard-Model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} makes the charges of the four-dimensional minimal massive composite, and with what strength their lightest stable state interacts. The series fixes neither \cite[Prop.~A.4]{ButlerT14}. The note tests reading (C) as stated and records this as its main cost (Section \ref{sec:costs}).
