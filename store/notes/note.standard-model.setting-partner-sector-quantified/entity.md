## formal
Proposition \claimref{standard-model.spectrum-run} states that the framework's $3/8$ survives experiment only if the running between the weak scale and the structural scale changes, by partner-like content or by an intermediate gauge symmetry, and names the pair and tower structure as the place to look for the first; the dark-matter paper carries the same as an open successor problem of its own, and Section \ref{sec:split} takes up the second. This section takes up the first, in three exact steps and two at record level, and closes with the proton condition that every candidate must also meet. Whether the towers can supply the content is a question about superselection, answered exactly and in the negative. What the one-loop run demands of whatever supplies it is a question about three linear equations, answered exactly as a criterion any candidate spectrum can be held against, at one threshold and then at two. And whether the row that one loop cannot decide, the supersymmetric content, is decided at two loops is answered at record level. Notation: $a_{i} = \alpha_{i}^{-1}$ in the normalisation of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-one-loop-running-trace-ratio}(a), whose one-loop running is \cite{GeorgiQuinnWeinberg1974}, the one-loop coefficients $b_{i}$, representations written $(r_{2}, r_{3})_{Y}$; $M_{Z}$ is the weak scale, $M_{U}$ the meeting scale, and $M_{I}$ a threshold between them. Every result of this section whose coefficients are those of the derived content with its lepton doublet rests on Hypothesis (S), which supplies the lepton doublet (Theorem \claimref{standard-model.hypercharge-global-form-pair-counting-consistency}(1)); each such result says so in its statement, and the results that hold for any coefficients (Lemma \claimref{standard-model.complete-multiplets-shift-all-three-coefficients}, Theorems \claimref{standard-model.one-threshold-exact-criterion} and \claimref{standard-model.two-threshold-criterion}) are unconditional. The record values are those of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.spectrum-run}, $\alpha_{\mathrm{em}}^{-1}(M_{Z}) = 128$, $\alpha_{s}(M_{Z}) = 0.118$, $\sin^{2}\theta_{W}(M_{Z}) = 0.2312$; the record's central values in the same scheme are $127.951$, $0.1180$ and $0.23122$, with uncertainties in the last digit or beyond \cite{PDG2024}. Three further imports carry the argument.
