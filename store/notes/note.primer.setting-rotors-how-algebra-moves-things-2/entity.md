## formal
\textbf{Where the series uses this.} The \emph{null rotations}, rotors of the form $1 + vu$ with $v$ null and $u$ orthogonal to $v$, are the source of the obstruction in T1's no-go theorem: they shear every candidate pseudo-inverse of $v$. The boost of Example \claimref{note.primer.boost-what-it-does-null-direction} becomes, in T5 and T7, the modular flow whose temperature is the Hawking temperature.
