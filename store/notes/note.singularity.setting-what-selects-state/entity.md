## formal
Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-descent} is conditional on three hypotheses, and the status line of Table \ref{tab:chain} would read \emph{up to a state} because of them. This subsection shows that the first is an output rather than an input, that the second is not needed for the form at all and that its per-mode content follows from a statement about observables, that what remains is exactly one real number per mode, that this number cannot be fixed inside the algebra, and that one named premise fixes it, mode by mode, and with it the third hypothesis. The central step is Theorem \claimref{singularity.descent-parity-average}: the descent is the parity average, so the question ``which states descend thermally'' is a question about a $\mathbb{Z}_2$ grading, and gradings are easily supplied.
