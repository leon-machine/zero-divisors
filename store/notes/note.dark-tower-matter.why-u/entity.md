## formal
(U1) and (U2) are how this tower does it: T12's constituent is one chirality half of the ascended spinor module, which is spin-irreducible \cite[Fact~2.2]{ButlerT12}; its $\C^{2}$ is two constituents and its $U(2)$ their momentum ladder; and T12 takes both factors' groups unitary. (U3) is this paper's rule, not T12's. T12 builds its pairs in the complexification of the conformal space \cite[Fact~2.2, Lem.~2.12]{ButlerT12}. In this tower the two readings coincide: the even algebra $\Cl^{0}(2,4) \cong \Mat_{4}(\C)$ \cite[Rem.~3.2]{ButlerT9} has centre spanned by $1$ and the pseudoscalar $I_{6}$, so the commutant of the spin action on the constituent ideal is $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{6}\}$, and any complex unit commuting with it, the complexification's included, acts there as $\pm I_{6}$. The readings part only where the field is $\HH$, in the five-dimensional towers, and there they give different gauge algebras (Remark \claimref{note.dark-tower-matter.branches-break-u-what-they-give}): U3 is a choice, made because the series works in the real algebra. A further rule, the commutant of the full Clifford module read on its irreducible module, reproduces this tower too, but would make the mirror's constituents real and contradict T14's copy theorem; U1 does not.
