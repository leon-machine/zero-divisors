## formal
From now on fix the real Witt pair $\nO, \nInf \in V$ of Section \ref{sec:setting} and let $(\Som, \Spi)$ be the corresponding splitting: $\Spi$ is the plane of $N_0 = \Psi(\nO)$ (the \emph{origin line} $L_0$) and $\Som$ the plane of $N_\infty = \Psi(\nInf)$ (the \emph{infinity line} $L_\infty$). In the Fock model with $\nO = w_1$, $\nInf = w_1'$ these are the planes computed in Example \claimref{note.correspondence.isomorphism-witt-pair}.
