## formal
This paper is T5 of the series. This paper relies on: T1 \cite{ButlerT1} for the no-go theorem, the minimal completion, the zero-divisor taxonomy, the pinching and one-way theorems, and the six-step transport algorithm that Table \ref{tab:algmap} instantiates; T3 \cite{ButlerT3} for the tower and its CAR limit, the frozen clock at $d \equiv 6$ that stops the iteration at Step 6a, and the closure-completeness corollary that (L)'s exhaustiveness clause aims at; T4 \cite{ButlerT4} for the localisation theorems reproduced in Appendix \ref{app:imports}, which fix the weight of Proposition \claimref{singularity.degeneration-exactly}, place the ladder pair on $E$, and discharge the boundary case of the no-repair import (Facts \ref{fact:schwtame}, \claimref{singularity.imported-one-sided-completion-singularity} and \claimref{singularity.imported-no-equivariant-value-wall}); and T6 \cite{ButlerT6} for the interior collar and its $t$-line holonomy, which Section \ref{sec:flowinside} identifies with the modular flow of the boundary modes inside. The sheaf paper T4 in turn reads this paper's Schwarzschild data (Propositions \claimref{singularity.unipotent-ray} to \claimref{singularity.degeneration-exactly} and Appendix \ref{app:schw}) as its worked composite; the two papers are cited in both directions and neither proof depends on the other's conclusion.

Papers that rely on this one: the interior paper T6 \cite{ButlerT6} imports the curvature eigenvalues and the Cartan conventions of Appendix \ref{app:schw}, the rescaled-limit collapse of Proposition \claimref{singularity.degeneration-exactly}, and the type-N reading of Proposition \claimref{singularity.type-n-only-type-n}; the Hawking paper T7 \cite{ButlerT7} imports the single-mode thermality of Section \ref{sec:thermal} (Theorems \claimref{singularity.modular-descent} and \claimref{singularity.descended-form-state-independent}, premises (P) and (R), the gluing occupancy of Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-discharge-hypothesis-iii}), the rate-rigidity Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-rigidity-temperature-not-algebra-intrinsic}, and the state class of Corollary \claimref{singularity.chains-state-class-stability}; the entropy paper T8 \cite{ButlerT8} imports the counting axioms and eviction dynamics of Sections \ref{sec:capacity} and \ref{sec:page}, the geometric normalisation of Appendix \ref{app:schw}, the atom's Euclidean period from Proposition \claimref{singularity.euclidean-rotor-where-it-lies}, and the capacity comparison of Proposition \claimref{singularity.area-law-test}; the twistor paper T9 \cite{ButlerT9} takes the chain end to end as the instance whose completion it develops, and receives the spectrum-condition restatement of Remark \claimref{note.singularity.cost-r-dictionary}; the dark-energy paper T10 \cite{ButlerT10} imports the s-wave current algebra of Section \ref{sec:photons} under premise (Q) (Premise \claimref{singularity.q-joint-quasi-freeness-s-wave}); the dark-matter paper T14 \cite{ButlerT14} imports the deficit theorem (Theorem \claimref{singularity.deficit-theorem-cost-descent-exactly}) as an entropy statement, the exhaustiveness and purity clauses (L) and (U) of Premise \claimref{singularity.rate-models-kinematic-inputs-l-u}, and the ascend-then-descend structure run tower by tower; the laboratory paper T16 \cite{ButlerT16} imports the modular period $\beta = 8\pi M$ with the descent as a one-way conditional expectation (Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-descent}, Remark \claimref{note.singularity.steps-status-what-still-open}), the thermofield angle of Lemma \claimref{singularity.schmidt-forcing-proved-outright}, and the verbatim imports of Appendix \ref{app:imports}; and the programme paper T17 \cite{ButlerT17} carries this paper's status row, its check count and its premises in the series ledger.
