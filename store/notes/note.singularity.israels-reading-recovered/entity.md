## formal
The natural state satisfying (i)-(iii) is the thermofield double taken across the two ends of the completed pair, which is Israel's 1976 reading of the eternal black hole \cite{Israel1976}, now expressed in the chain's own terms: the global vacuum is entanglement between the element and its adjoined partner, and Hawking radiation is what descent sees of that entanglement. The slogan of Step 4a (the exterior radiation as the shadow of the return map) is hereby made quantitative: the shadow is the partial trace, identified mode by mode with the pinching $E$ by Proposition \claimref{singularity.pinching-against-partial-trace-scope-coincidence}(i) (the coincidence scoped precisely in Step 5, which this identification anticipates), and its output is thermal at exactly $\TH$. What the identification deliberately does not extend to is the full boundary mode space, where the two maps differ (Proposition \claimref{singularity.pinching-against-partial-trace-scope-coincidence}(ii)) and the computation is Theorem \claimref{singularity.deficit-theorem-cost-descent-exactly}'s. The word ``natural'' is made precise in Section \ref{subsec:selection}: by Theorem \claimref{singularity.purity-output-partner-commutant} the thermofield double is not one state among those satisfying the hypotheses but the canonical purification of the one-sided state, the partner being the commutant produced by the modular conjugation.
