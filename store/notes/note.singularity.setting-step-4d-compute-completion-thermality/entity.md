## formal
Temperature is a property of a state, and a fixed projector is state-independent: the projection alone provably cannot select a temperature, and this paper does not pretend otherwise. What the descent structure supplies, exactly, is the complete operator structure of thermality: the modular Hamiltonian ($K = N - \tfrac12$, Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-structure-completed-pair}), its spectral projectors (the descent projectors), the localisation plane and the rate (Proposition \claimref{singularity.plane-rate}), and the uniqueness of the flow. The theorem of Section \ref{subsec:scaffolding} is then that any state meeting three physically standard conditions descends to the Hawking thermal matrix, exactly. Section \ref{subsec:selection} takes the next question: what selects the state. The answer is that two of the three conditions are theorems, the third is an output, and the whole residue is a single real number whose value is provably not fixable inside the algebra and is fixed by one named premise. One notational warning: from here to the end of Step 6b, $\beta$ is an inverse temperature; the Painlev\'e-Gullstrand velocity $\beta(r)$ of Section \ref{sec:locate} reappears only in the appendix and in the verification section.
