## formal
Write $d := p - q \bmod 8$ for the clock position of the physical side's fibre algebra \cite[\S7]{ButlerT1}. Across $\Sig$ the metric signature passes to $(p{-}1, q{+}1)$, of clock position $d - 2$; the completion ascends both by $(+1,+1)$, which fixes clock position; and each iteration of Lemma \claimref{sheaf.tame-wall-induction} produces a new wall again separating clock positions differing by $2$. The data of a tame wall thus includes the locally constant function
\[
w(\Sig) \;:=\; \bigl(d,\, d-2\bigr) \in H^0\bigl(\Sig;\, \mathbb{Z}_8 \times \mathbb{Z}_8\bigr),
\]
the ordered pair of clock positions of the two competing ascents.
