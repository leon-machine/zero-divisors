## formal
Three measured couplings fix $M_{I}$, $M_{U}$ and $\alpha_{U}$, so on this route $\sin^{2}\theta_{W}$ is an input that fixes $M_{I}$, not a prediction. The route passes the tests it can be held to: $M_{I} < M_{U}$, and the proton condition, which the chain with the $(15,2,2)$ meets by a factor of six in $M_{U}$. The $(15,2,2)$ is the Pati-Salam home of the $126$'s electroweak doublets (Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-pati-salam-representation-theory-survival}(a)), and so of the Georgi-Jarlskog direction of Fact \ref{fact:gj}: if that direction is to contribute to a mass at the weak scale, a $(15,2,2)$ doublet must be light, and by Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-pati-salam-representation-theory-survival}(b) the multiplet then sits at $M_{I}$, which is the chain with the $(15,2,2)$. The vev along $T$ sets both $M_{I}$, through the mass of the $\su(2)_{R}$ gauge bosons, and the right-handed neutrino's Majorana mass (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.majorana-element}). A derived relation in the neutrino sector would therefore fix $M_{I}$ and turn the input into a prediction; this is successor problem (7). Left-right symmetric unification with left-right parity broken at the unification scale is Chang, Mohapatra and Parida's \cite{ChangMohapatraParida1984}; the chains are of the class surveyed by Bertolini, Di Luzio and Malinsky \cite{BertoliniDiLuzioMalinsky2009}.
