## formal
The strict positivity $\delta > 0$ is necessary, not technical. Take the mixed error $\Xi_{12} = c\,\varrho^{1/2}$ on the dying-nondegenerate slot pair, relative order exactly zero. The Schur correction is then of order $\varrho$ and adds to the leading unit: in the verified example the choice $c = 2$ keeps the eigenvalue at first order but flips its leading unit from $-1$ to $+3$, changing the sign structure of the wall, while the choice $c = 1$ cancels the leading term entirely and the dying eigenvalue jumps to the \emph{square} of the defining order. At relative order zero the first-order structure is coefficient-dependent, hence not stable; below it, Theorem \claimref{sheaf.stability-reduction-strict-diagonal-case} says it is unaffected.
