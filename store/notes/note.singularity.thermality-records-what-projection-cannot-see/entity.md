## formal
The lemma is now also a special case: by Theorem \claimref{singularity.ascended-marginals-are-diagonal} every even state of the boundary modes has diagonal single-mode marginals, and the model's states are even, so the protection is not a property of the phase-type scrambling but of fermion parity, and Proposition \claimref{singularity.number-diagonal-dynamics-models-scrambling-derived} shows that under boundary-mode locality the scrambling could not have been anything but phase type. Within the model the lemma is the model's instance of Theorem \claimref{singularity.descended-form-state-independent}: no encoding performed by the (phase-type) internal dynamics can be visible to any single-quantum measurement, exactly. One quantum at a time, the radiation carries nothing, and this is a theorem of the encoding, not an approximation: thermality is what the projection records in place of what it cannot see. The choice of \emph{diagonal} scrambling is what makes the statement exact rather than approximate, and it is the exact match to the chain, where single-mode thermality is likewise exact (Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-descent}) rather than emergent.
