## formal
Five problems are named for the series' open directions, in decreasing order of urgency for the programme. (1) \emph{The quantitative transport theorem at the Cauchy horizon:} establish or refute integrability of the tractor connection along causal curves crossing the perturbed Cauchy horizon; either outcome sharpens Proposition \claimref{interior.filtration-cauchy-horizon-read-literature} into a statement about the completion itself. (2) \emph{The billiard-invariant weighted limit:} formulate and prove the analogue of Theorem \claimref{interior.weighted-limit-theorem} on the quotient by Kasner-axis dynamics, where the generic mixmaster approach lies; the admissibility integral of Definition \claimref{obj.interior.weighted-blow-up-hubble-normalisation-admissible} says exactly what fails framewise. (3) \emph{The ring blow-up:} extend the wall theory of \cite{ButlerT4} to codimension two and prove or refute Conjecture \claimref{interior.duality-monodromy-fibration-over-ring}; the winding number three of Proposition \claimref{interior.complex-scale-inverse-cube-killing-spinor} is the first invariant any such theory must reproduce. (4) \emph{Edge regularity in general:} for a general admissible metric, control the tangential derivatives of the error $E_{\tau}$ on compact sets of the exceptional set, which admissibility does not include; this is the only analysis left in the edge problem, since the transport-level comparison along all tangential curves of fixed Hubble-normalised length is Theorem \claimref{interior.transport-level-convergence-along-tangential-curves} for the whole admissible class and the tangential regularity holds at Schwarzschild by symmetry (Corollary \claimref{interior.schwarzschild-lines-exactly}). (5) \emph{Carter's constant inside GTG:} derive the quadratic first integral directly from the scale identification of Proposition \claimref{interior.complex-scale-inverse-cube-killing-spinor} within the gauge-theoretic formalism, completing the connection the identification begins. The weighted completion of the wall program in its generic-singularity extension depends on (2) and on (4); the density constant $\nu$ of the entropy paper is stated for Schwarzschild, inside the admissible class with constant exponents and no bounces, where (2) does not arise and (4) is closed, so what that prediction still waits on is the calibration of the count, not analysis here.
