## formal
\textbf{Levels.} Proposition \claimref{simulations.effective-cross-section-sheet-matter}, Corollary \claimref{simulations.confinement-scale-does-not-matter} and Lemma \claimref{simulations.averaged-pair-operator} are proved here; Computation \claimref{simulations.cluster-bound-two-dimensions} is a numerical evaluation of an imported result. Everything that rests on simulation or on a galaxy-formation model is at observation level: it compares models with one another and with published measurements, and its costs are listed in Section \ref{sec:costs}. The runs are idealised (about $80{,}000$ particles per merger and $32{,}000$ per assembled cluster, one merger geometry run once per composition, no cosmological surroundings), and they are suited to comparing compositions, not to precision prediction.
