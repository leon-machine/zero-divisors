## formal
The condition covers the gauge-mediated channel only. In supersymmetric content the partners of the colour triplets mediate proton decay through operators of dimension five, whose rate depends on the triplets' masses and on flavour structure this paper does not compute; Proposition \claimref{standard-model.proton-condition-candidates}(2) and (3) say nothing about that channel.
