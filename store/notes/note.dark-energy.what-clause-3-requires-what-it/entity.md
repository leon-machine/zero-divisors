## formal
The Standard-Model paper's dating observation reads $N$ as fixed at the origin and constant since, which is the (I) reading: a constant of integration. Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.under-action-written-norm-parameter-laws} says the action as written does not deliver that reading; it delivers (P). The two are reconciled by a known class of modifications of the action (Henneaux-Teitelboim \cite{HenneauxTeitelboim1989}; Brown-Teitelboim \cite{BrownTeitelboim1988}; Kaloper-Padilla sequestering \cite{KaloperPadilla2014}), of which the Henneaux-Teitelboim promotion is the one used here (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-henneaux-teitelboim-promotion}): promote the Stelle-West norm from a fixed number to the value of a Lagrange-multiplier field whose constancy is an equation of motion, so that the norm mode of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.under-action-written-norm-parameter-laws} acquires the equation $d\Lambda = 0$ and $\Lambda$ becomes a label of the solution rather than of the theory. This is a modification of the action, carrying a conjugate cosmic-time variable with it, and it is a decision for the series, not a theorem of this paper; it is recorded as open, with both branches stated, and the Standard-Model paper's classification is to be read with its clause (3) resting on it. Two things follow whichever way the decision goes. First, the entropic and null-endpoint forces that the classification's discussion names act on a measure over $\Lambda$, and a measure over the values of a parameter of the laws is a measure over theories, while a measure over initial data is a measure within one theory; the descent measure the series does not possess is one or the other, and which one is the decision just named. Second, the rescaling $\Lambda \to s^2\Lambda$ maps $\mathcal{S}_\Lambda$ to $\mathcal{S}_{s^2\Lambda}$ isomorphically: under (P) it is an isomorphism between different theories, under (I) a symmetry of one theory's solution space, and only in the second case is ``why this $\Lambda$'' a question the theory can be asked to answer. If the promotion is adopted, the Goldstone count of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.point-infinity-has-no-physical-fluctuation} is to be recomputed with the norm mode and its conjugate time as dynamical variables, a ten-dimensional constrained space with one physical direction; if it is rejected, the count stands and the dating observation is withdrawn from initial-data status. Successor problem (5) reads: the smallness of $\Lambda$ cannot be addressed until this decision is made, and then until the measure is available.
