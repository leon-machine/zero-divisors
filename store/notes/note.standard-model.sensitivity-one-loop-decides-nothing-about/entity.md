## formal
Two-loop running and threshold corrections for content of this kind shift the effective $\sin^{2}\theta_{W}$ at the level of a few times $10^{-4}$ \cite{Amaldi1991, DimopoulosRabyWilczek1981}; at the record point $dR/d\sin^{2}\theta_{W} = -18.1$, so a shift of $3 \times 10^{-4}$ moves $R$ by $0.0054$, which is the whole of the supersymmetric content's gap $7/5 - R = 0.0055$. The one-loop table therefore neither admits nor excludes that content, and the one-loop table draws no conclusion about it; Section \ref{subsec:twoloop} does, at record level, since the exact-rational discipline of Theorem \claimref{standard-model.one-threshold-exact-criterion} does not survive the two-loop terms, which are not rational in the inputs. The conclusions the table does deliver are those whose gaps exceed the two-loop scale by an order of magnitude: the gauginos ($r_{\Dl} - R = 0.48$), the higgsinos ($0.16$), a second doublet ($0.41$) and the gaugino-higgsino spectrum ($0.084$) fall short, and gluino-type content moves the ratio in the wrong direction, by margins ten to eighty times the shift. The record's central values $(127.951, 0.23122, 0.1180)$ in place of the rounded inputs move $R$ by $1.5 \times 10^{-4}$ and change no conclusion; the script checks both. The gaugino-higgsino row is the light content of a split spectrum with its scalars at or above $M_{U}$; the split spectra of the literature \cite{GiudiceRomanino2004, ArkaniHamedDimopoulos2005} place the scalars between the two scales, a two-threshold problem Section \ref{subsec:twothresholds} quantifies at one loop, and rest their unification on two-loop running.
