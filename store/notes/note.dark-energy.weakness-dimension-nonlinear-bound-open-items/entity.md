## formal
Four consequences, each at its level. \emph{Structural:} coupling to a dimension-two operator is irrelevant, suppressed at energy $E$ by $(E/\Lambda_{\mathrm{cut}})^2$ relative to dimension-one couplings, which is exactly the form $GE^2$ with the cutoff at the Planck scale: weakness is a consequence of compositeness, the collective mode being one dimension above the currents. This supplies the weakness's functional form, not its number; the smallness problem stands. \emph{Consistency:} the first nonvanishing vertex is cubic, $\langle TJJ\rangle \neq 0$, two currents fusing to the stress tensor: translated out of boundary-mode language this is the Gertsenshtein process \cite{Gertsenshtein1962}, already present in classical general relativity with its known and unobservably small efficiency; that the current algebra reproduces the correct leading vertex is a check passed, and that it produces nothing stronger is the conclusion on engineering. \emph{The coherence loophole, closed by the hypothesis:} a background with $\langle J\rangle \neq 0$ breaks the charge-conjugation symmetry that Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.acoustic-no-go-one-way-grip} assumes, linearises the quadratic, and seems to offer a first-order channel; but the cross term is precisely the background's own stress-energy, which is what the broken symmetry admits: enhancement by coherence is general relativity's own gravitomagnetism, not a new coupling, and by reciprocity the same orthogonality that forbids a source forbids an enhanced detector. \emph{The open items, named so the no-go can be tested where they stand:} (i) the proposition is stated in the s-wave reduction, and the four-dimensional lift's inheritance must be established, not asserted; (ii) if the second variation of Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}'s functional carries a component along fluctuations of $I$ itself, the argument must extend to that sector. Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.point-infinity-has-no-physical-fluctuation} closes (ii); Assembly \claimref{dark-energy.lift-inherits-no-go-steps-itemised} addresses (i) at its level.
