## formal
Every computational identity asserted in this paper has been verified in exact arithmetic by the script \texttt{verify\_T12.py} \cite{ButlerVerifyT12}, which imports the series' shared library \texttt{GA.py}: exact rationals, Gaussian rationals with exact linear algebra over them, and arbitrary-precision integer arithmetic; the one field the library does not provide, the cyclotomic field $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{6})$, is realised in the script in a few dozen lines. No floating point is used anywhere except in the lines marked RECORD (the GeV conversions, the proton lifetimes, and the two-loop integrations of Propositions \claimref{standard-model.supersymmetric-row-two-loops} and \claimref{standard-model.chain}, printed as records rather than checked) and in the checks marked CONTROL; two hundred and forty-three checks, all passing (two hundred and thirty-four exact, nine floating-point controls), organised in thirteen parts keyed to statement tags.

\emph{Part A: the ladder.} The polarisation step of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.internal-symmetry-ladder} at $n = 3$: the nine rank-one constraints $v^{\dagger}Mv = 0$ span the nine-dimensional real space of Hermitian forms on $\C^{3}$, so only $M = 0$ survives; and the nine products $\pi^{j}\bar\pi^{k}$ of an explicit three-twistor configuration have rank four, not nine, the dependence the proof must avoid (the $n = 2$ configuration has rank four of four). Exact rational unitaries (one real, one complex) are confirmed to preserve the total momentum; and the mass bracket is confirmed to transform by $\det(U)$ exactly on both, and to carry abelian weight $6$, hypercharge $+1$, under the generator of Section \ref{sec:content} (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.mass-bracket-charged-singlet}).

\emph{Part B: the algebra and the global form.} The action of an explicit $13$-generator basis of $\uu(2) \oplus \uu(3)$ on $\C^{2} \otimes \C^{3}$ has image of rank exactly $12$ by exact Gaussian-rational rank (the diagonal-phase kernel), and $\uu(3) \oplus \uu(3)$ on $\C^{3} \otimes \C^{3}$ has image rank $17$: the two dimension claims of Theorem \claimref{standard-model.gauge-algebra-minimal-massive-composite}. The coset rescaling law $\lambda^{2}\lambda^{-3} = \lambda^{-1}$ is confirmed on a unit-modulus Gaussian rational. In $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{6})$: $\zeta_{6}$ has order exactly six, the second isomorphism $(a, b, \lambda) \mapsto (\lambda^{3}a, \lambda^{-2}b)$ evaluated on each of the six elements $(\zeta^{-3j}I_{2}, \zeta^{2j}I_{3}, \zeta^{j})$ returns the identity with $a$, $b$ of determinant one, and evaluated on $(I_{2}, I_{3}, \zeta)$ does not (that nothing else lies in the kernel is the determinant argument of the proof); and the lattice residue $2t + 3\delta + q \bmod 6$ vanishes on every representation of Section \ref{sec:content}, the hypercharge-zero singlet included, and does not vanish on a colour singlet of hypercharge $1/3$. The pair count $(n+2)/2$ equals three at $n = 4$ and at no other even $n$ in the tested range, and the algebra dimension $((n+2)/2)^{2} + 3$ equals twelve only there (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.four-dimensions-forces-converse}(1)); $m^{2} + k^{2} - 1 = 12$ has exactly the solutions $\{2, 3\}$ for $1 \le m, k \le 12$ (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.four-dimensions-forces-converse}(2)).

\emph{Part C: content and charges.} The graded dimensions $1, 6, 15, 20, 15, 6, 1$ and both Cauchy splits of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.raw-representation-content-state-space} are confirmed (the splits from the Schur-functor dimensions), together with the degree-two and degree-three Cauchy identities as identities of symmetric functions at rational diagonal instances; and, exhaustively over the ten partitions of the two-by-three box with the Schur-functor dimensions from the hook-content formula, the summands total $64$, no summand is $(2,1)$ (the two-dimensional $S_{\lambda}(\C^{2})$ occur at $\lambda = (1), (2,1), (3,2)$, whose conjugates give $3, 8, \bar{3}$), the $SU(3)$ singlets occur only at $\lambda = (), (3), (3,3)$, and no summand has a one-dimensional $S_{\lambda}(\C^{2})$ against a three-dimensional $S_{\lambda'}(\C^{3})$, so neither $(1,3)$ nor $(1,\bar{3})$ occurs in any degree; every cell has weight $+1$ under the generator, so the state space has no state of negative weight, against the single-twistor weights $0, -2, -4, -6$. The hypercharge generator $\mathsf{y} = (3iI_{2}, -2iI_{3})$ is built as an exact matrix on $\C^{2} \otimes \C^{3}$, on $\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{0}$ and $\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{3}$, and on each $\Lambda^{k}\C^{3}$, and acts as the scalar $iq$ with $q = 1$; $3$ and $-3$; $0, -2, -4, -6$ (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.quarks-leptons-two-level-fermion-content}). The negative clause of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.quarks-leptons-two-level-fermion-content}(2): five unipotent images of a filled row of the tensor state space, expanded in the wedge basis of $\Lambda^{3}\C^{6}$, have rank exactly four, and the Lie-algebra generator of the unipotent applied three times to the filled row is $6\,e_{01} \wedge e_{02} \wedge e_{03} \neq 0$ (four times, zero), so the four-dimensional span is a single Jordan block, irreducible: the row-filling states are the quadruplet $(4,1)$ and not $2 \oplus 2$. The $[SU(2)]^{2}U(1)$ coefficient is $0$ for the listing that keeps the full twistor and $1$ for the listing that keeps the empty one (Theorem \claimref{standard-model.hypercharge-global-form-pair-counting-consistency}(1)); the one-handed listing gives the singlet hypercharges $0, -\tfrac23, \tfrac13, 1$ (Theorem \claimref{standard-model.hypercharge-global-form-pair-counting-consistency}(2)); all four anomaly sums vanish on the derived spectrum, and without the lepton doublet they are $(0, \tfrac12, 1, \tfrac14)$; the uniqueness arithmetic is reproduced line by line (the forced $Y_{u} + Y_{d} = -\tfrac13$ and $Y_{e} = 1$, the cubic sum $-\tfrac{7}{27}$, the product $-\tfrac29$, the roots $\{+\tfrac13, -\tfrac23\}$ with unit discriminant); the exhibited second solution with a free fourth singlet, together with the members $t = 0, 1, -1, 2, \tfrac57$ of the family $Y + t(B-L)$, unrescaled and, where $Y_{Q} \neq 0$, rescaled to $Y_{Q} = \tfrac16$ (at $t = -\tfrac12$ the member has $Y_{Q} = 0$ and still solves), is confirmed to satisfy all four conditions; and conjugating the two doublets alone is confirmed to give $[SU(3)]^{2}U(1) = -\tfrac23$ and cubic $+\tfrac49$, so both hypotheses of Theorem \claimref{standard-model.hypercharge-global-form-pair-counting-consistency}(3) are seen to be necessary. The hypercharge quantum $1/6$ and the ratio $Y_{L} = -3Y_{Q}$ are read off the weights. The duality of Proposition \claimref{standard-model.chirality-two-halves-spinor-module-conjugation}: $\Lambda^{3-k}W \otimes \det^{-1}$ has weight $+2k$ for $k = 0, \ldots, 3$ and $(\C^{2})^{*} = \C^{2} \otimes \det(A)^{-1}$ has weight $-3$, the full-twistor doublet's. And Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor}(1): the generator is built on all twelve components $\Lambda^{j}\C^{2} \otimes \Lambda^{k}\C^{3}$, the even part has dimension sixteen and its (content, hypercharge) multiset is exactly the derived generation's, the odd part's is the conjugate multiset, with $(2,1)_{1/2}$ at $(1,0)$ and the slot factor $(1,1)_{1}$ at the even component $(2,0)$, and the Hodge star $(j,k) \mapsto (2-j, 3-k)$ negates the weight on all twelve components. Proposition \claimref{standard-model.under-s-composites-state-space-spinor}(3): for every integer pair $(\alpha, \beta) \in [-6, 6]^{2}$ the three linear anomaly sums of the generator $\alpha j + \beta k$ over the even half are $2(2\alpha + 3\beta)$, $2(2\alpha + 3\beta)$, $8(2\alpha + 3\beta)$, vanishing exactly at the multiples of $(3, -2)$, where the cubic sum vanishes too; the total mode number gives $(10, 10, 40, 400)$ and the kernel generator $(-2, -2, -8, -32)$.

\emph{Part D: unification.} The trace sums $\sum T_{3}^{2} = 2$, $\sum Y^{2} = \tfrac{10}{3}$, the vanishing cross term, and the ratio $\tfrac38$ over the derived generation, together with $\tfrac{9}{10}$ over the fibre, $\tfrac34$ over the doublets, and $\tfrac{9}{26}$ over the content without the lepton, the lepton contributing exactly $\tfrac12$ to each sum (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.weak-angle-over-derived-generation}); the closed-form one-loop angle evaluated exactly, $\tfrac{78761}{379488}$ for the Standard Model coefficients and $\tfrac{6539}{28320}$ for the supersymmetric ones, with $L > 0$ in both cases, the partner meeting point higher, and the partner value over twenty times closer to the measured $0.2312$ as an exact inequality; and the three pairwise meeting values $L_{12}, L_{13}, L_{23}$ for the Standard Model coefficients, exact rationals confirmed pairwise distinct (Proposition \claimref{standard-model.spectrum-run}).
