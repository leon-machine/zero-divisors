## formal
The horizon is where the pairing generator of the outgoing vacuum vanishes in every direction. Along $\ell$ and along $n$ it vanishes at every radius, the congruence being geodesic and $\tau = 0$ throughout Schwarzschild; what singles out the horizon is the transverse class, which vanishes there and nowhere else because the expansion of the horizon generators is zero, a Killing-horizon fact (Fact \claimref{pairing.imported-spin-coefficients-null-tetrad}) that Proposition \claimref{pairing.regular-frame-class-vanishes-horizon-changes} exhibits exactly. In the trapped region the class has the opposite sign: transport tilts the outgoing null direction the other way. None of this uses the ascent; it is the pre-ascent geometry of the null direction the descent selects, and it is where the singularity paper's Step 4d premise is calibrated, at the fixed locus of the boost \cite[Prop.~7.25]{ButlerT5}. The evidence for the horizon statement is the regular-frame computation of Proposition \claimref{pairing.regular-frame-class-vanishes-horizon-changes}, not the static one: the static frame degenerates on the horizon and the rescaling between the two frames vanishes there, so Proposition \claimref{pairing.pairing-class-transverse-derivative-null-direction}'s invariance does not carry the static result across; that the static class $-\sqrt f/r$ also vanishes is consistent, not evidential.
