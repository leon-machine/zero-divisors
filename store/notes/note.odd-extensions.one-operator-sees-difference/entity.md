## formal
An operator linear in $e_{5}$ alone, a term $\bar\psi e_{5}\psi$, is the one place the doubling would show: $e_{5}$ is the real pseudoscalar in one tower and the pseudoscalar times a central complex unit in the other, which in the physicists' notation is $\gamma_{5}$ against $i\gamma_{5}$, an anti-Hermitian against a Hermitian bilinear. But $e_{5}$ is the unit Stelle-West direction, unsoldered by Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.belongs-tower}(i) and pure gauge by Fact \ref{fact:goldstone}; so either a coupling to it can be physical, in which case Fact \ref{fact:goldstone} is being read too strongly in Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.boost-dilation-ratio-gauge}(iii), or it cannot, in which case the mirror sector is observationally identical to this one at every level the series reaches. The note takes the second reading, consistently with Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.boost-dilation-ratio-gauge}, and records the operator as the one place the first reading would show; the Hermiticity statement depends on a Dirac-representation convention the note does not fix.
