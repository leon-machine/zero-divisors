## formal
Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.transport-constrains-frozen-point-only-through}(iii) says the frozen point's two components in the canonical null frame are related by a symmetry of the completion. This section adds that the symmetry is a gauge symmetry of the dark-energy paper's action, so the components are unphysical and not merely symmetry-related; and it shows that the sign of $\Lambda$, though it acts differently on the two oriented towers, does not select which of them is the visible one.
