## formal
Five problems are named. (1) \emph{The analytic googly half:} construct the palatial deformation of Conjecture \claimref{correspondence.googly-localisation-quartic-order}(b), or refute the localisation; Proposition \claimref{correspondence.chirality-split-ordering-insensitivity-asd-sector} is the consistency check any construction must pass. (2) \emph{The quantised incidence relation:} promote $\omega = I_6\hat x\pi$ (Penrose's $\omega = ix\pi$) to an operator equation in $\mathcal{P}$ (the incidence line as a subspace annihilated by operator combinations of the quantised twistor), and determine what replaces the chart of Theorem \claimref{correspondence.incidence-relation-chart} one storey up. (3) \emph{Local twistors over the wall:} extend Proposition \claimref{correspondence.local-twistor-transport-ascended-spinor-connection} across the degenerate walls of \cite{ButlerT4}, where the tractor bundle persists but the scale splitting fails; the wall sequence should act on the spin bundle by the completion's own ladder operators. (4) \emph{Massless fields:} develop the Penrose transform in the series' language. The homogeneity $-2s-2$ is stated and proved from the ordering shift of Theorem \claimref{correspondence.shared-core-ordering-dictionary}(4) in the spectrum paper T7 \cite[Prop.~5.2]{ButlerT7}; what remains open is the transform itself in the series' language. (5) \emph{The real-form dictionary in full:} the $\mathbb{PN}$ hypersurface as the whole real conformal compactification, the two open regions $\PT^\pm$ where $\Sigma$ is definite, and the two ruling families over the reals, completing Proposition \claimref{correspondence.reality-conjugation-twistor-form-factor} beyond the affine chart.
