## formal
When this list was made, on 10 November 2026, the conventions (section ``Gravity and the graviton'') stated the series' position as: gravity is the classical gauge field of Gauge Theory Gravity, the series holds no quantum of that field, and where stored text says graviton it names a helicity-2 channel of the classical field, a counting convention, or prior art. By Leon's decision of 24 November 2026 the second of these is reversed at first order and no further: the series posits a canonical pair on the two free directions of the first-order field at each nonzero null momentum (Premise \claimref{synthesis.premise-canonical-pair-on-the-two-null-directions}, called (TT)), with no quantised geometry beyond first order, no element of the tower and no self-coupling. Where stored text says graviton for a quantum of the gravitational field, it now names the mode of (TT) at first order, one mode over $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$ per null momentum, of helicity two (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.first-order-gravitational-field-has-two-free-directions-at-null-momentum-and-four-constraints}, clause (vi)); the three readings stay for the texts they were written for.
