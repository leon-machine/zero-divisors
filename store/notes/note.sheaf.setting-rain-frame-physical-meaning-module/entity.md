## formal
The weighted module has, so far, been characterised metrically (bounded norm) and structurally (uniqueness). It also has an operational characterisation, and the structure behind it is a duality with the exceptional regime. Let $(V, e_{\hat r}, e_{\hat\theta}, e_{\hat\phi})$ be the infalling orthonormal frame of the Painlev\'e-Gullstrand form of Fact \claimref{sheaf.gauge-field-cometric}: $V = \partial_t - \beta\partial_r$ the rain four-velocity, $e_{\hat r} = \partial_r$, and the unit sphere directions; its exact orthonormality at every radius is itself one of the machine checks.
