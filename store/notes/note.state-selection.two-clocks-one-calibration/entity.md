## formal
Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-rigidity-temperature-not-algebra-intrinsic} is the note's no-go, and it is a constructive one: it says exactly what kind of input is needed and how much. The inverse temperature is the ratio of two clocks, the modular clock of the state and the geometric clock of the Killing boost, and a ratio of clocks is meaningless until one of them is calibrated. The chain calibrates exactly once, in \cite[Prop.~6.2]{ButlerT5}: an interval of $2\pi$ in rapidity is $2\pi/\kappa = 8\pi M$ of Schwarzschild time. Everything numerical in Step 4d passes through that one conversion, as the paper already says, and Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-rigidity-temperature-not-algebra-intrinsic} now proves that no rearrangement of the algebra can remove the need for it. The residual sign in (2) is not new either: it is the orientation of the boost, the labelling of outgoing against ingoing, which \cite[Rem.~6.5]{ButlerT5} already flags as the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ attached to the weights of $v$ and $v^{*}$. The rigidity theorem identifies that $\mathbb{Z}_2$ as the entire automorphism-theoretic freedom in the temperature.
