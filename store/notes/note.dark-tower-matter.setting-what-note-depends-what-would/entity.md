## formal
\emph{Proved here, at proof level} (the two mass laws of Theorem \claimref{dark-tower-matter.where-mass-begins} by exact symbolic verification)\emph{:} Theorem \claimref{dark-tower-matter.constituents-field}, Lemma \claimref{dark-tower-matter.adjoint-norm}, Theorems \claimref{dark-tower-matter.where-mass-begins}, \claimref{dark-tower-matter.ladder}, \claimref{dark-tower-matter.third-pairs-unit-five-dimensions} and \claimref{dark-tower-matter.mirror-algebra}, Proposition \claimref{dark-tower-matter.pairs}, Proposition \claimref{dark-tower-matter.residue-seven-unit}(i) to (iii) up to its last clause, Proposition \claimref{dark-tower-matter.sterile-kinematics}, and the algebra in Theorem \claimref{dark-tower-matter.gauge-algebras} and Propositions \claimref{dark-tower-matter.three-dimensional-content}, \claimref{dark-tower-matter.five-dimensional-content}, \claimref{dark-tower-matter.anomalies}.

\emph{Assembly, under named premises:} that the algebras of Theorem \claimref{dark-tower-matter.gauge-algebras} are gauge algebras (H2), that a constituent's momentum is $j$ of its spinor part off dimension four (H1), the constituent-level content (S), the transposition rule (U), including the compactness of the pair group (U2), which is assumed and not derived, and Proposition \claimref{dark-tower-matter.mirrors-temperature-p2}, Observations \claimref{dark-tower-matter.three-dimensional-phases}, \claimref{dark-tower-matter.r1-has-no-mechanism-odd-towers}, \claimref{dark-tower-matter.population}, Remark \claimref{note.dark-tower-matter.kleinian-algebra}.

\emph{Decimal:} Computations \claimref{dark-tower-matter.mirrors-composition}, \claimref{dark-tower-matter.survival-sheets}, \claimref{dark-tower-matter.five-dimensional-towers}, Observation \claimref{dark-tower-matter.towers-built}.

\emph{Interpretive:} the mirror as parity image; the spin $0$ of the dressed three-dimensional cells.

\emph{What would refute the constructions.} A spin-irreducible constituent ideal whose spin commutant differs from Theorem \claimref{dark-tower-matter.constituents-field}'s; a nonzero element of $S$ in dimension three or four with $\rev{\psi}\psi \neq 0$; an element of $\HH$ outside $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{7}\}$ at $r = 3$ commuting with the $I_{7}$-built pair algebra. Each is a finite computation the script performs.

\emph{What would refute the physics.} A thin dark disk or bulge component excluding a mirror of $0.995\,\Omega_{b}$ in the helium interval with helium-star feedback refutes T14's mirror; a demonstration that dark plasma instabilities transfer bulk momentum in cluster mergers, with gauge zero modes in the five-dimensional towers, refutes the composition unless their relic is the neutral species; a measured $\Delta\Neff$ excess attributable to a dark sector would place a five-dimensional tower above $0.085$ of the photon temperature, outside T14's estimates.

\emph{Successor problems.} (1) Whether the cold mirror is a stated exception to P2, and where in T14's interval $\xi$ lies. (2) A footing for E's common length and equal mass per ball in the odd towers, where neither E nor the particle model has a mechanism; and, for T14, its Section 8's reading of the odd towers' quanta as confined composites, which Observation \claimref{dark-tower-matter.r1-has-no-mechanism-odd-towers} contradicts. (3) The boundary data of the five-dimensional towers' fifth direction (zero modes or not) and their population mechanism. (4) The three-dimensional phase and coupling. (5) Hypothesis (S) off dimension four, the same missing theorem as in this tower. (6) The family count in the odd towers, where the maximal-composite bound of T13 \cite[Conj.~3.1]{ButlerT13} does not transpose. (7) The $r = 5$ unit: datum or field. (8) Whether the five-dimensional pair unit is internal (U3) or external, which decides between $\uu(3)$ and the Standard-Model algebra in both towers. (9) The $SU(3)$ and mixed Chern-Simons levels of the five-dimensional content.
