## formal
The case against: indifference is a selection rule on initial data, exactly where the trichotomy locates the framework's freedom, and might forbid the very contingency initial data is. The response is the instrument's audit, inherited from the families paper: in every use in a proof indifference equates along exact symmetry orbits and refuses selections with no distinguished member, and never legislates the \emph{values} of genuine moduli ($N$, $\epsilon$, the bit), which it classifies as data; it constrains the \emph{shape} of the pre-descent state, not its \emph{coordinates}. Here P2 counts both irreducible modules where the centre splits (Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.mode-counts-two-modules-which-one}(ii)); it does not equate the asymmetries of towers off the reversal orbit, and it does not sterilise $r = 0$ by refusing a flag there, which would refuse every residue (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.flag-insensitive-residue-no-flag-level}). P2 is a principle with a record of successful use (the families' democracy, the fuzzy sphere's principality), not a theorem; its failure mode is refutation item 1.
