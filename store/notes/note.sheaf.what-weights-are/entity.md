## formal
In proper time $\tau_{\mathrm{p}} \propto r^{3/2}$ the frame of Proposition \claimref{sheaf.basic-properties-operational-characterisation} reads: $\rho \propto d\tau_{\mathrm{p}}$ at $E$ (weight $0$), the angular generators carry weight $\tau_{\mathrm{p}}^{2/3}$, and the dying generator (below) rescales by $\tau_{\mathrm{p}}^{-1/3}$ in the exceptional regime. These are the Kasner exponents of Fact \claimref{sheaf.kasner-data-interior}; their uniqueness is Theorem \claimref{sheaf.weights-are-forced-are-kasner-exponents}. The angular weights are exactly the blow-up's tautological rescaling: the normal sphere at parameter $r$ has radius $r$, so the unit-sphere coframe is $r\,d\theta$, $r\sin\theta\,d\phi$. This is the sense in which the blow-up absorbs the angular anisotropy: those two rates were never Kasner data, they were the exceptional divisor's own geometry.
