## formal
One structural difference from the cosmological walls of Example \claimref{note.sheaf.physical-fibre} is worth recording: there the quotient was the split algebra $\Cl(0,3) \cong \HH \oplus \HH$; here it is the simple algebra $\Mat_2(\mathbb{C})$. The wall fibre's reduced algebra carries no central idempotents, so no type-C obstruction coexists with the type-N one at the black hole. The completion demanded is the type-N rule in both cases.
