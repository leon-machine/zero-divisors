## formal
Theorem \claimref{singularity.ascended-marginals-are-diagonal} replaces a symmetry assumption about the state by a statement about which elements are observable. The replacement is strictly weaker: hypothesis (ii) constrains the whole state, (P) constrains one complex number per mode. It is also more robust, and Theorem \claimref{singularity.exact-equilibrium-over-determines-boundary-modes} below shows that the robustness is necessary, not merely convenient: hypothesis (ii) is an equilibrium condition, the evaporating hole is not in equilibrium, and the exact equilibrium condition turns out to be too strong for what Steps 5 and 6b need.
