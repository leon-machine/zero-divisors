## formal
This section fixes notation and assembles the facts the proofs use, under a strict division of labour with the companion primer \cite{ButlerT0}. The \emph{language} belongs to the primer: the geometric product, blades, involutions, versors and the groups they generate, the classification, and the structural lemmas about them, which are restated below with their proofs imported from the primer's toolkit \cite[Section 9]{ButlerT0}, each statement carrying its number there (the classification theorem alone is not reproved in the toolkit, which states it and cites the classical sources for its induction). The \emph{failure of division} belongs to this paper: every fact about zero divisors, null vectors as obstructions, pseudo-inverses and idempotents is stated and proved here, because that failure is the paper's subject; the one deliberate exception is the splitting lemma (Lemma \claimref{transport.splitting-along-hyperbolic-plane}), a basis fact about hyperbolic planes rather than about failure, whose proof stays in the toolkit. The definitions specific to this paper (extension, equivariance) are likewise stated here and belong to this paper.
