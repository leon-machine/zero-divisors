## formal
Theorem \claimref{transport.minimality} quantifies over null-lift completions of the quadratic space, with the normalisation $X(x) = f(x) + x$ that makes the counting meaningful. One can ask more: could $\Cl(p,q)$, embedded \emph{exotically} inside some smaller Clifford algebra, find its resolution there? Exotic unital embeddings exist (Example \claimref{note.transport.exotic-embedding-inertness}), so the question has content. The answer is that hosting a resolution is a property of the ambient \emph{quadric}, not of the embedding, and the following theorem settles it by a rank computation. It is the sharpest form of the minimality half of the trichotomy, and it discharges the scope question that the normalisation of Definition \claimref{obj.transport.null-lift-completion} leaves open.
