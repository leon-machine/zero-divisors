## formal
This paper is T4 of the series. It uses two predecessors and supplies four successors. It uses the language and fibre computations of the primer T0 \cite{ButlerT0} and the fibre theory of the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1}: the Witt completion, the zero-divisor taxonomy with its transport rules, and the minimality theorem, all used here one fibre at a time. It supplies, downstream: the localised transport (Corollary \claimref{sheaf.localised-transport}) is the structure the singularity paper T5 \cite{ButlerT5} imports when it applies the transport principle at the black hole, and Section \ref{sec:schwarzschild} extends that import from tame walls to the Schwarzschild boundary itself; the wall package of Sections \ref{sec:ambient} and \ref{sec:wall} is what the iteration corollary of the tower paper T3 \cite{ButlerT3} climbs; the support statement, that the ladder pair is supported exactly on the singular locus, is the geometric input behind the no-attic and no-echo arguments of the entropy paper T8 \cite{ButlerT8}; and the tractor-level route to the same singularity, reached here at sheaf level, is the subject of the interior paper T6 \cite{ButlerT6}, whose weighted limit Section \ref{subsec:weld} identifies as a connection on this paper's exceptional bundle, and whose admissibility class is assigned the analytic half of the anisotropic theory that Section \ref{sec:anisotropic} closes at the algebraic level. Two further exports go to the singularity paper alone: the no-value theorem at the wall (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.no-equivariant-value-wall}), which it reproduces verbatim as its boundary no-go, and the function-linearity principle (Proposition \claimref{sheaf.function-linearity-tame-constructions}), the class-membership input of its hopping exclusion \cite[Facts B.3, B.4]{ButlerT5}.
