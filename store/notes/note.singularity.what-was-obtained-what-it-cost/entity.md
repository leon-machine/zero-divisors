## formal
Typicality is absent from what the model claims, and the axiom count is correspondingly small: the envelope, the exact rising branch, the quantified falling branch, the turnover and the endpoint follow mechanically from the window identity, saturation of the falling branch remaining typicality's alone (Fact \claimref{singularity.assembled-page-curve-up-typicality}; the one-mode zero-frequency idealisation aside), the envelope and endpoint needing no typicality and no placement rule (Corollary \claimref{singularity.envelope-endpoint-why-boundary-mode-locality}), and the no-attic axiom (A) is a proposition of the model rather than an assumption, its physical force consolidated into the exhaustiveness of (L), which is the no-remnant hypothesis in its correct location: exactly the islands-side choice in the dissonance of Remark \claimref{note.singularity.island-dictionary-one-dissonance}, with its derivation programme aimed at the closure-completeness of the tower \cite[Cor.~6.2]{ButlerT3}. What remains assumed: boundary-mode locality (L$_E$) with its never-return clause (its no-hopping half a theorem within the class of supplied generators, Appendix \ref{app:threeroutes}), which is what makes the falling branch mechanical (the capacity crossing is the rate-model shadow of the extremal-surface exchange); (I)'s freshness clause, the rate model's own; the capacity postulates (C1)-(C3) of Step 6a feeding (C); the standing hypothesis (U); and, for the Planck sector alone, the joint quasi-freeness (Q) of Step 4e, in its recorded tension with Steps 5 and 6b; the state of Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-descent} is not a standing hypothesis but is fixed by (P) and (R) (Corollary \claimref{singularity.chains-state-class-stability}), and (L)'s exhaustiveness clause remains the conjectural input. Every premise in the chain is stated, named and quantified; and the two-level discrepancy itself is quantified separately, once and exactly, by Theorem \claimref{singularity.deficit-theorem-cost-descent-exactly}. The division of tasks with the sheaf paper is likewise exact: its Schwarzschild composite proves the kinematics (the carrier module, its uniqueness, the rain-finiteness of the chain's dyad, the localised ladder pair) and poses back to this paper the operator and state level \cite[\S 8.9]{ButlerT4}; that residue is discharged in Section \ref{subsec:selection}, down to the single import (R).
