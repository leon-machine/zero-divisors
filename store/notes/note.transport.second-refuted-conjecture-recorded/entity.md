## formal
The natural conjecture is that the two minimal completions are ``in general non-isomorphic,'' reading the distinct clock positions $d \pm 1$ as distinct algebras. The computation refutes this: the matrix-unit construction of Lemma \claimref{transport.matrix-units-peirce-isomorphism} is independent of $\gamma$, and the two ascents are isomorphic always. The conjecture and its refutation are both recorded, in the practice of this paper's Section \ref{sec:nogo}, where the founding conjecture of the programme is refuted in the same way: the surviving true statement (geometric twoness, algebraic oneness) is sharper than either the conjecture or its naive negation.
