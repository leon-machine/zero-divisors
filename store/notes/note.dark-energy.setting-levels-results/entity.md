## formal
\emph{Proved in this paper, at the standard of the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1}:} the Stelle-West lemma, with the norm $-3/\Lambda$, the soldering identity $\nabla V = \nabla X = e$, the connection decomposition and the bracket sign (Lemma \claimref{dark-energy.stelle-west-vector-frozen-point}); the reduction, the Stelle-West datum and the scalar form of the functional (Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}(i)-(iii)); the polynomiality and the pole with the vanishing interior variation of the Gauss-Bonnet term (Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}(1)-(2), the expansion itself imported); the wall's free sector, with both ranks, the identification of the freed connection as two symmetric tensors, the propagation statement, and, on the flat model, the Hamiltonian component (Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.walls-free-sector}); the sheaf paper's wall in soldering variables, the seven-dimensional structure algebra it opens, and the identification of its one new generator with the boost of the ladder pair (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.sheaf-papers-wall-soldering-variables}(i)-(iii)); the frozen-point datum at a Kasner wall, its pole, residue conditions and action densities (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-datum-kasner-wall}); the $\Lambda$-independence of the residue (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.cosmological-constant-does-not-change-residue}); the wall counterterm and the renormalised action (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.wall-counterterm}); the pointwise residue for variable exponents (Lemma \claimref{dark-energy.variable-exponents-residue-pointwise-corrections-are}); the $w$-rigidity on the Einstein region and the norm $R/12$ off it (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.vacuum-region-registration-condition}(i)-(ii)); the two-kinds theorem for the two routes named, with the conformal invariance of the two-form star (Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.gravity-pre-metric-forces-degenerate-exactly}); the symmetry orthogonality (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.acoustic-no-go-one-way-grip}); and the Goldstone counting with its bijectivity for the pair $(I,V)$, together with the invariance of the integrand $\epsilon(I,V,F,F)$ under the ambient gauge group acting on all three arguments (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.point-infinity-has-no-physical-fluctuation}); the exhaustiveness of the four alternatives and the non-exhaustiveness of the three (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.four-alternatives-are-exhaustive-three-are}); and the norm as a parameter of the laws under the action as written, with the free-$\Lambda$ count and its one norm mode (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.under-action-written-norm-parameter-laws}, its ``not (K)'' clause imported as Fact \ref{fact:notkinematic}).

\emph{Imported as Facts with provenance:} the frozen point's geometry and the tractor connection (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-frozen-point-geometry}); the weighted limit at the Schwarzschild singularity and its matching, values-level (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-weighted-limit-schwarzschild-singularity-matching}); the MacDowell-Mansouri expansion and Euler-Lagrange equations (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-macdowell-mansouri-theorem-stelle-west}, imported at assembly level and declared as such); the s-wave current algebra and, under the singularity paper's premise (Q), its thermal state (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-s-wave-current-algebra}); the walls and, conditional on the entropy paper's Indifference premise, the sphere theorem (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-walls-sphere-theorem}); the CFT inputs, the Sugawara clause throughout Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.acoustic-no-go-one-way-grip} and the orthogonality clause for its vacuum sharpening (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-conformal-orthogonality-sugawara-construction}); the rescaling argument that the norm is not kinematic, used in Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.under-action-written-norm-parameter-laws}(ii) (Fact \ref{fact:notkinematic}); and the Henneaux-Teitelboim promotion, used by Remark \claimref{note.dark-energy.what-clause-3-requires-what-it} (Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-henneaux-teitelboim-promotion}).

\emph{Assembly, steps itemised:} the forcing half at the Schwarzschild wall in the residue sense (Assembly \claimref{dark-energy.forcing-half-schwarzschild-wall-residue-sense}); the lift inheritance (Assembly \claimref{dark-energy.lift-inherits-no-go-steps-itemised}, step (2) at assembly level and step (4) conditional on the entropy paper's Indifference premise); the engineering conclusion (Assembly \claimref{dark-energy.conclusion-does-not-depend-ontology-level}); the identification of the functional after the Goldstone gauge fixing of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.point-infinity-has-no-physical-fluctuation} with the MacDowell-Mansouri functional, through Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-macdowell-mansouri-theorem-stelle-west} (the scalar form of the functional itself, Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}(iii), is proved); and the dichotomy of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.sheaf-papers-wall-soldering-variables}(iv), a statement of cases each proved, with the case that obtains at the sheaf paper's own tame wall open; on the diagonal Bianchi I class the datum's pole is computed by Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-datum-kasner-wall} and transported to the Schwarzschild and Schwarzschild-de Sitter interiors by Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-weighted-limit-schwarzschild-singularity-matching}.

\emph{Readings and registrations, flagged:} the identification of dark energy with the norm of the frozen point, conditional off the vacuum region on successor (3), with its observational confrontation date-stamped (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.vacuum-region-registration-condition}(iii)); the reading of the $\Lambda \neq 0$ boundary as the boundary of the curvature-squared form (Remark \claimref{note.dark-energy.point-contact-why-form-not-value}); and the promotion decision, adopt or reject the unimodular mechanism, left open with both branches stated (Remark \claimref{note.dark-energy.what-clause-3-requires-what-it}).

\emph{Withdrawn, so that they are not cited:} the claim that the components of the frame equation along the radical vanish identically at a wall (they do not, Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.walls-free-sector}(a)); and the claim that the sheaf paper's wall is a wall of the soldering form (it is the dual degeneration, Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.sheaf-papers-wall-soldering-variables}(i)).
