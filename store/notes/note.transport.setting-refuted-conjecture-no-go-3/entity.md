## formal
The refutation redirects rather than terminates: what cannot be repaired is the element; what can be, as the remainder of this paper shows, is the \emph{space of points} in which the element's division is asked. The revised statement, no longer a conjecture, is proved as Theorems \claimref{transport.totality} and \claimref{transport.minimality} below: division by zero promotes not the element but the space the element belongs to, and does so uniquely, minimally, and totally.
