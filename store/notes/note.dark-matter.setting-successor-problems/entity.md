## formal
Six open and one closed, numbered as in the capstone's inventory; its (0), the charge-saturated hole, is discharged by P5 and Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.parallel-boundary-mode-spaces-one-wall}.

(1) \emph{The odd towers' content:} the odd-dimensional Fock ideals, their mass formulas and gauge algebras, not used in any proof here, are to be established at their proved levels; the parity theorem and the seeding floor are this paper's (Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.flag-bit-exists-exactly-tower-dimension}, Appendix \ref{app:seeding}).

(2) \emph{The pre-descent theory:} P1's residue, with two more entries the count depends on, the descent's reheating temperature, which the temperature floor is a function of, and the energy with which the descent deposits each tower's charge, which sets the temperature above the floor (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.stored-asymmetry-charge-not-number}).

(3) \emph{The coupling rule:} the relation between an odd tower's dimensionful coupling and this tower's at the ceiling, which nothing derived in the series supplies; it alone stands between Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.population-count-one-share-half-percent}(iii) and a computed contribution from the odd towers, and the unit-ball rule constrains it (the odd towers' densities per unit of our volume) without supplying it. Two questions travel with it as conditions on the programme: whether a tower can be populated at all without the flag bit, and whether any consistent value of the odd towers' relic densities supplies the remainder. One signpost, conditional on two antecedents this paper does not assert: if the remainder is the odd towers' and they are relics of the gravitational seeding of Appendix \ref{app:seeding} rather than of the deposit, the yield's $T_{\mathrm{desc}}^{3}$ scaling and the requirement that the produced quanta be no heavier than the bath put $T_{\mathrm{desc}}$ above about $2 \times 10^{12}$ GeV, excluding the lower end of the ceiling range, and the odd towers' lightest stable state near $10^{5}$ GeV at its top.

(4) \emph{The burst dimming factor:} promote the late-evaporation counting of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.parallel-boundary-mode-spaces-one-wall}, unconditional on the charge regime, from a remark to a spectrum prediction for burst searches, once (1) supplies the degrees of freedom.

(5) \emph{The partner question, closed in the negative} in the Standard-Model paper: under the sector decomposition no field of another tower carries a component of this tower's hierarchy bundle, so every other tower contributes zero to this tower's one-loop coefficients at every scale, and the mirror's running, a copy of ours, carries a copy of whatever resolves the desert here \cite[Prop.~8.5, Cor.~8.6]{ButlerT12}. The zero rests on the sector decomposition and on that paper's theorem that this tower's gauge fields are connections on its hierarchy bundle \cite[Prop.~5.7]{ButlerT12}, and holds for any content in the other sectors without Hypothesis (S); the mirror coefficients hold under (S), as Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.mirror-tower-copy-one}(i) does. What remains is this tower's pair structure, which that paper takes up.

(6) \emph{The window:} derive the tower dimension per residue from the descent and retire P3; note that the odd residues' exclusion from the asymmetric share is window-free (Theorem \claimref{dark-matter.flag-bit-exists-exactly-tower-dimension}(i)), while the even residues' inclusion is P3's, so a derived window moves the asymmetric share only by admitting an even causal residue at a dimension divisible by four.

(7) \emph{The thickness:} everything in the unit-ball rule turns on R3, one cell radius of the direction we cannot see, on one side. The contact mechanism of Section \ref{subsec:free} returns that value on four clauses chosen with it known, its contact count does not compose across two nestings, and it makes the tower's matter beyond one cell inert to gravity (Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.scope-mechanism}); the two standard reductions give $\tfrac{3\pi}{8}$ and $\tfrac{\pi}{4}$ (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.two-reductions}); and gravity cannot measure the quantity (Corollary \claimref{dark-matter.thickness-not-gravitational-quantity}). What would settle it is a rule for the descent's deposit: the one such rule P2 licenses, equal filling per rung on the shared wall, gives every share one and $5.000$, so the rule needs democracy in each tower's own cells, which is R2 and R3 together. With it travel a reason for equal filling (a conserved number a symmetric sector can carry, fixed at the descent in every tower alike, or a democratic principle at the level of the descent measure) and, for the three-dimensional towers, a derivation of the sheets' displacement from the towers' own dynamics rather than from momentum conservation alone (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.reduced-response-gravity-needs-supporting-stress}), and the galaxy-cluster bounds on self-interaction re-expressed for a cross-section per sheet (Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.halo-built-collisionless-sheets-cold-dark}), which the companion note T14b has done. At composite strength the self-interacting fraction, a half at $c = 1$, a third at $\tfrac{\pi}{2}$, forty percent at the floor (Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.total-function-thickness}(v)), fails the Bullet Cluster and the shapes of galaxy clusters whatever becomes of the total (Section \ref{subsec:simulated}); what remains open is a derivation of the odd towers' couplings that would make their matter collisionless, or not.
