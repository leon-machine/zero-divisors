## formal
The case against: the singularity paper's purity premise, written (U$_{\mathrm{T5}}$) here to keep it apart from Hypothesis (U) \cite[Premise~11.3(U)]{ButlerT5}, extends to every conserved quantum number of the infalling state, so the boundary mode space must return baryon number and Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.parallel-boundary-mode-spaces-one-wall}(iv) is false. What survives: (U$_{\mathrm{T5}}$) is purity of the state on $H_{L} \otimes H_{R}$, which says nothing about a global $\mathrm{U}(1)$ the Hamiltonian need not commute with at the wall, where the algebra degenerates and the rung $\mathrm{U}(1)$ of Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.where-flag-defined-what-it-carries} (argued, not proved) has no reason to survive the ascent; and, decisively, the alternative makes every stellar hole in our tower a remnant or a non-Hawking emitter, against the imported flux. Read the other way, Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.parallel-boundary-mode-spaces-one-wall} is a constraint on the series: exact return of global charge would force the Hawking paper's interface premise or the singularity paper's exhaustiveness to fail in the saturated regime. If the series ever promotes exact return to a theorem, P5 reverses and Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.parallel-boundary-mode-spaces-one-wall} with it; the count and the shares are insensitive to holes and do not move.
