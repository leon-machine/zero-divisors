## formal
T5's Fact 5.4 records that the no-c-number-repair theorem of \cite[Thm.~4.2]{ButlerT1} is proved where the ambient form is nondegenerate and the dying direction has a hyperbolic partner, and that at the boundary itself, where the partner is exactly what is missing, only the weaker statement was available: no equivariant value had been \emph{exhibited}. This section closes the gap: none \emph{exists}. The candidate objects are fixed first, then the two lemmas, then the two theorems. Everything is at a point of $\Sig$, on the inner collar where $\beta = 1$, with $(W, G, k)$ as in Fact \claimref{three-routes.imported-boundary-gram-ladder}; ``vector'' means an element of the dual of $W$, and for a vector $v$ and covector $\alpha$ we write $\alpha(v)$ for the pairing.
