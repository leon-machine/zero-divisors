## formal
This section contains the paper's new computation: an exactly solvable model, small enough that every claim is a finite calculation in exact arithmetic, in which the chain's conceptual endpoint can be followed step by step, at both levels. The model is a toy and is offered as one; its role is to exhibit, not to derive. What it exhibits, exactly rather than approximately: every single radiated quantum is thermal; the joint radiation state ends pure; the information is carried by inter-quanta correlations invisible to any single-quantum measurement; the entropy of the radiation rises exactly along $\Sth$, falls under the eviction mechanism of Theorem \claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}, and returns to zero, with the falling branch strictly inside the envelope by exactly the per-quantum shortfall the theorem quantifies; and, in Section \ref{subsec:descentapplied}, the descent of Definition \claimref{obj.singularity.multi-mode-descent} is applied to the radiation outright, so that the metric-level reading of the same history is also computed rather than described.
