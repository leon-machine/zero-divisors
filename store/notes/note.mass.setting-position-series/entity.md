## formal
This paper is T11 of the series. \emph{It relies on five predecessors:} the root paper T1 \cite{ButlerT1} for the standard of proof and the Fact (imported) discipline; the geometry paper T6 \cite{ButlerT6} for the frozen point, the tractor connection, the norm $\Lambda/3$ and its convention note (Fact \claimref{mass.imported-frozen-point-tractor-connection}); the twistor paper T9 \cite{ButlerT9} for the twistor chart, the incidence theorem, the spin isomorphism, the infinity twistor, the Fock-vacuum theorem and the palatial quadratics (Facts \claimref{mass.imported-flat-model-twistor-chart-infinity} and \claimref{mass.imported-palatial-quadratics-homogeneity-charge}, Remark \claimref{note.mass.point-contact-angle-between-two-vacua}); and the action paper T10 \cite{ButlerT10} for the action theorem, the Goldstone count and the engineering conclusion (Fact \ref{fact:action}, Lemma \claimref{mass.five-broken-generators}, Remark \claimref{note.mass.point-contact-what-gravity-why-everything}). The fifth is the primer T0 \cite{ButlerT0}, which supplies the bivector language throughout.

\emph{Papers that rely on this one:} the standard-model paper T12 \cite{ButlerT12} imports the frozen point and its norm (Fact \claimref{mass.imported-frozen-point-tractor-connection}), the dictionary and its weight polynomial (Theorem \claimref{mass.mass-weight-dictionary}, Corollary \claimref{mass.weight-polynomial-two-series}), the area formula (Proposition \claimref{mass.mass-symplectic-area-two-twistor-formula}, mass begins at two constituents), the de Sitter scale (Lemma \claimref{mass.de-sitter-scale-frozen-point}), the grip (Definition \claimref{obj.mass.grip-mass-composite}), the exact join (Theorem \claimref{mass.exact-join}) and the Casimir form of the join (Proposition \claimref{mass.casimir-form-join}), and takes up the internal symmetry whose Casimir is the correction term of Theorem \claimref{mass.exact-join}; the families paper T13 \cite{ButlerT13} carries the condensation programme to which the massless locus of Theorem \claimref{mass.exact-join}(5) and successor problems (3) and (4) are routed, and settles the question of Remark \claimref{note.mass.what-join-says} outright, the seed being the source term rather than a point of that locus \cite[Props.~4.1-4.2]{ButlerT13}; the neutrino paper T15 \cite{ButlerT15} is routed the massless locus of Theorem \claimref{mass.exact-join}(5); the foundations paper T16 \cite{ButlerT16} imports what nullity fixes (Remark \claimref{note.mass.what-nullity-fixes}) and the grip (Definition \claimref{obj.mass.grip-mass-composite}, Theorem \claimref{mass.exact-join}); and the capstone T17 \cite{ButlerT17} tabulates this paper's claims at their tiers.
