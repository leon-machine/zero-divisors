## formal
Proposition \claimref{sheaf.basic-properties-operational-characterisation} characterised $\shW$ operationally and fixed the quasihomogeneous order as the minimal one admitting a smooth frame. What it left open is whether some \emph{other} locally free extension of the interior cotangent module could carry a boundary-tame structure of its own. Within the rain-bounded class the answer is no, and the proof is short. Two explicit examples delimit the theorem exactly: first-order vanishing must be read on the dying eigenvalue, not the dying entry (Example \claimref{note.sheaf.eigenvalue-essential}), and the rain-bounded hypothesis cannot be dropped, because null mixing produces genuinely different boundary-tame extensions outside it (Example \claimref{note.sheaf.rain-boundedness-essential-null-mixing-extension}).
