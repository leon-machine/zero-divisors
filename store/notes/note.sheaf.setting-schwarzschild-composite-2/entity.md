## formal
\noindent\textbf{Conventions.} Units $G = c = 1$; to keep the arithmetic exact we write $2M = m^2$ and work in the variable $u := \sqrt{r}$, so that $\beta = m/u$ is rational in $u$. The defining function of the singular boundary is $r = u^2$; ``first order in $r$'' reads ``second order in $u$''. Signature convention $({+},{-},{-},{-})$. Throughout this section $E$ denotes the singular boundary (not the auxiliary bundle of Theorem \claimref{sheaf.conservation-degeneracy-absolute-form}); the coupling constants $\mu, \nu$ of Definition \claimref{obj.sheaf.collar-data-coupling} always appear as the product $\mu\nu$, while the angular coframe elements are the subscripted $\mu_1, \mu_2$. One conormal convention, used throughout: on $\Mt_u$ the smooth defining function of $E$ is $u$, and $dr = 2u\,du$ vanishes on $E$ as a $u$-smooth one-form; the boundary data of the tame theory are graded by $r$, so the conormal line in which the transversality datum and the residue lie is the $r$-conormal $N^*_r E := (N^*_u E)^{\otimes 2}$, spanned by the class of $dr$, matching the first-order-in-$r$ clause of (T3$\partial$).
