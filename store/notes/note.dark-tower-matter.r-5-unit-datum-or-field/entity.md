## formal
If the unit $u$ of Theorem \claimref{dark-tower-matter.third-pairs-unit-five-dimensions}(iii) is a constant of the tower, every choice gives an isomorphic tower and nothing observable distinguishes it from $r = 3$; this is how this tower treats its own unit, which the algebra fixes up to sign. If $u$ varies over spacetime, the tower carries a sigma model on $S^{2}$ with two massless five-dimensional scalars, which moves the radiation bound of Computation \claimref{dark-tower-matter.five-dimensional-towers}(iii) from $0.085$ to $0.084$. The series supplies no kinetic term for $u$; the first reading is this tower's.
