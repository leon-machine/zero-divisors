## formal
The algorithm's last instruction is Step 6: iterate, or stop \cite[\S 8.1, Step 6]{ButlerT1}. It is discharged before any counting begins: the ascent $\Cl(1,3) \to \Cl(2,4)$ fixes the transport charge, the mod-8 clock stops at $d \equiv 6$, and by \cite[Thm.~3.6]{ButlerT3} no floor of the tower over the completed algebra ever develops a further central obstruction: the iteration is Morita-stationary and nothing structural remains to repair. What the boundary mode space at which the iteration stops \emph{holds} is another matter, and it is the task of this step and the next: Step 6a counts the capacity of the boundary modes, and Step 6b runs the two-level entropy computation on it.

This step counts the modes available for entanglement, and its physical content is not derivable from the algebra: it enters through three postulates, stated here as named axioms so that the count itself is a theorem and every premise is visible. Of their derivation programme, the corank half is discharged: the forced weighted completion of \cite[\S 8]{ButlerT4} fixes one ladder pair per point of the blown-up singular boundary (Remark \claimref{note.singularity.corank-limit-weighted-answer}). What remains is the quantitative half, the cell calibration, the aggregation over the sphere factor of $E$, and the epoch from the modular period, assigned to the series' counting paper T8 \cite{ButlerT8}, with the connection-level geometry in the interior paper T6 \cite{ButlerT6}. Nothing in Steps 1 through 5 depends on this section.
