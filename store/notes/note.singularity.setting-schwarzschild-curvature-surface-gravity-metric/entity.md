## formal
This appendix computes the curvature spectrum and the surface gravity in the metric picture, by the Cartan structure equations in the static chart, where the computation is shortest. It plays two roles. It supplies the mixed- and angular-plane eigenvalues that the abelian radial computation of Proposition \claimref{singularity.solution-where-divergence-lies}(1) does not reach, completing the input to Theorem \claimref{singularity.curvature-gauge-field-exact-shape-decoupling}; and it is the independent cross-check, in a second formalism, of the gauge-theoretic Section \ref{sec:locate}: on the invertible region the two pictures agree by Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-equivalence-einstein-equations}, so any disagreement anywhere would falsify one of them. The computations are classical differential geometry, included in full per the series' standard of proof; the verification script checks every derivative identity and eigenvalue exactly, with $f$ handled as a rational function.
