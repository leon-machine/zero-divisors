## formal
Together with Fact \claimref{odd-extensions.imported-copy-theorem-soldered-residue} this closes the question of a physical passage between the oriented towers within the series: the tower charge is superselected by the one metric, the only algebra in which both towers are present identifies them by the complex unit and not by any automorphism (Theorem \claimref{odd-extensions.mirror-inside-complexified-extension-absent-doubled}(iv)), and the only continuous route through signatures at dimension four runs through a signature with no causal structure. The two towers share gravity, and by Proposition \claimref{odd-extensions.translation-bivectors-frozen-point} the sign of $\Lambda$; that is the whole of the shared channel.
