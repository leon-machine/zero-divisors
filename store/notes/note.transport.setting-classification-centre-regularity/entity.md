## formal
The taxonomy of Section \ref{sec:taxonomy} rests on three classical inputs, recorded here. The first is the structure theory of the real Clifford algebras, together with the centre and pseudoscalar computations behind it, imported from the primer's toolkit because they are structure theory, the language's side of the division of labour. The second is the regularity lemma, proved here: it is a statement about zero divisors, this paper's subject, and we have not found it stated in the Clifford literature in the form used.
