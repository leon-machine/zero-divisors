## formal
\textbf{One item, not two.} The singularity paper leaves two things open at the horizon: (a) whether (R) can be traded for a spectrum condition in the ascent's own algebra, and (b) whether the physical dynamics on the boundary carries a differential or a pairing term. Propositions \claimref{angle.within-supplied-class-spectrum-condition-selects} to \claimref{angle.hop-sea-filled-pairing} join them. Within the supplied class the spectrum condition selects a product state or nothing; a hop across the cut can select an entangled state, and the example does, at the sample point; a hop with the sea filled and a pairing from the vacuum are one term in two vacua. So (a) is answered affirmatively only if the boundary dynamics carries a generator outside the supplied class, which is the residual (b) declares; the paper's residual names the two kinds, and the example uses a hop across the cut. The trade on offer is conditional: (R), regularity at the fixed locus, can be replaced by the premise that the boundary dynamics carries a kinetic term with positive energy under the translations the ascent installs, together with the remaining hypotheses of Fact \claimref{angle.imported-theorems-deliver-their-hypotheses}, the half-sided inclusion of the one-sided algebra, the cyclic and separating property of the state, an action of the installed translations on the modes themselves, and the identification of the modular flow with the Killing boost, none of which this note verifies for the boundary modes; under those, the value $2\pi$ is the imported theorem's. That is one physical premise, a kinetic term, in place of one regularity premise, plus a theorem with hypotheses still to be met. It is not a derivation of the temperature; it is a relocation of the import to a statement about the dynamics rather than about the state, which is the relocation the paper asked for.
