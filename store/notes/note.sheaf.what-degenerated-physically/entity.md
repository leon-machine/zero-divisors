## formal
The radical at $E$ is the Killing-time direction $\dtS$ (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.schwarzschild-degeneration-boundary-tame}(4)); equivalently, at every radius, the exact covector $d(mt + \tfrac23 r^{3/2})$ conormal to the infalling congruence (Theorem \claimref{sheaf.schwarzschild-degeneration-boundary-tame}(5)). Its $G$-length is $-r$: spacelike everywhere inside, going null exactly at the singularity. The direction that the metric loses at $r = 0$ is the one the rain can never cross. On the vector side this is the stretching Kasner direction $p_1 = -\tfrac13$: dying covector and stretching vector are the same exponent seen on the two sides.
