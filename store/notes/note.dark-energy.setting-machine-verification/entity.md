## formal
The computational identities asserted in this paper are verified in exact arithmetic by the script \texttt{verify\_T10.py} \cite{ButlerVerifyT10}, which imports the series' shared library \texttt{GA.py}: rational matrix algebra over $\mathbb{Q}$, exact rank and congruence-signature routines, and rational functions in a formal variable. No floating point is used anywhere; ninety checks, all passing, organised in five parts keyed to statement tags; the fifth, on Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.under-action-written-norm-parameter-laws}, computes the free-$\Lambda$ constraint count, the norm mode against the gauge images, and its identification with the tangent of the rescaling family, at the rational instance the Goldstone checks use, with $d(N^2)/d\Lambda$ as a rational-function identity. One check confirms that the withdrawn radical-vanishing claim fails, and two negative controls confirm that refuted alternative readings fail (the one-argument functional, the opposite tangential sign); the checks of Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.gravity-pre-metric-forces-degenerate-exactly}(1) are the trace identities its proof uses, algebraic identities rather than tests, and are labelled so in the script.

\emph{Part A: the frozen point and the Stelle-West vector.} The scale-tractor norm $Q(I) = \Lambda/3$ is confirmed as an identity of rational functions in a formal $\Lambda$, with $I = (1, 0, 0, 0, 0, \Lambda/6)$ in the flat model $\mathbb{R}^{2,4}$ carrying the form \eqref{eq:tractormetric} with $g = \eta$; the normalisation $1/Q(I) = 3/\Lambda$ is confirmed to have a simple pole at $\Lambda = 0$ and nowhere else; the norm identity is also confirmed at the rational instance $\Lambda = 3$. For Lemma \claimref{dark-energy.stelle-west-vector-frozen-point}: with $X = (0,0,0,0,0,1)$ and $V = X - (3/\Lambda)I$ as rational-function vectors, $Q(X) = 0$, $\langle X, I\rangle = 1$, $\langle V, I\rangle = 0$ and $Q(V) = -3/\Lambda$ are confirmed as identities; the model connection bivector $e_a\wedge W$ with $W = V/Q(V) = (1,0,0,0,0,-\Lambda/6)$ is confirmed to annihilate $I$, to send $X$ and $V$ to $e_a$, and to agree with the display \eqref{eq:conn} on every basis tractor; the bracket $[e_a\wedge W, e_b\wedge W] = (\Lambda/3)\,e_a\wedge e_b$ is confirmed as a matrix identity in formal $\Lambda$; and the slot count of Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}(iii) is confirmed by computing, at $\Lambda = 3$ and for generic integer bivectors, that $\epsilon(I, \cdot, B_1, B_2)$ is a nonzero fibre vector (an integrand with one vector slot open is not a scalar) while $\epsilon(I, V, B_1, B_2) = \epsilon(I, X, B_1, B_2)$ is a scalar. At the rational instance $\Lambda = 3$ the image of all fifteen basis bivectors of $\mathfrak{so}(2,4)$ on $I$ has rank exactly $5$, every image vector is orthogonal to $I$, and $I^{\perp}$ has dimension exactly $5$ with induced form of signature $(1,4)$ by exact congruence, and at $\Lambda = -3$ of signature $(2,3)$, the anti-de Sitter branch; the induced form on $\{I,V\}^\perp$ is confirmed to have signature $(1,3)$ at both values; the first two facts are the stabiliser dimension $10$ and the de Sitter signature of Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-frozen-point-geometry}; for Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.point-infinity-has-no-physical-fluctuation} the joint action matrix of the fifteen basis bivectors on the pair $(I, V)$ is confirmed to have rank exactly $9$ (stabiliser of dimension $6$), the constrained fluctuation space to have dimension $9$, the one-argument failure to occur as displayed: $\epsilon(I, \xi\cdot X, B_1, B_2) = -\epsilon(I, V, B_1, B_2) \neq 0$ for $\xi = I\wedge V$; and the invariance $\epsilon(gI, gV, gB_1, gB_2) = \epsilon(I, V, B_1, B_2)$ to hold for three rational elements of $SO(2,4)$ (a boost, a rotation, and their product), each confirmed an isometry on all basis pairs. For the field-space clause of Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}(iii), $[I\wedge u, I\wedge v] = -Q(I)\,u\wedge v$ is confirmed as a matrix identity for $u, v \in I^\perp$ and $\epsilon(I, V, u\wedge v, B_1)$ confirmed nonzero: the $I$-leg components of an unconstrained connection would enter the Lorentz block quadratically.

\emph{Part B: the wall's free sector.} In the normal form $e = \mathrm{diag}(1,1,1,0)$ the connection equation is assembled as an exact $24\times 24$ integer matrix on the torsion components and its rank is confirmed to be $18$, with exactly $3$ identically vanishing rows, and $24$ on the invertible normal form; the frame equation is assembled as a $16\times 36$ matrix on the curvature components and its rank is confirmed to be $16$ in both cases, with no identically vanishing row. The kernel of the wall connection equation is confirmed to consist of torsion components with no $dx^3$ leg and no frame index $3$. At the connection level the equation is assembled as a $24\times 24$ matrix on the components of $\omega$ and its rank confirmed to be $12$, with the kernel confirmed to be exactly the symmetric parts of $K$ and $M$ of Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.walls-free-sector}(b); the principal symbol of the frame equation is assembled in each of the four coordinate directions and its rank on the free sector confirmed to be $0$ on $M$ in every direction, $6$ on $K$ in the radial direction and $3$ on $K$ in each tangential direction; the full quadratic frame equation is evaluated on random elements of the free sector and confirmed to vanish in all components except $E_3(\partial_0,\partial_1,\partial_2)$, where it is confirmed to equal $\sigma_2'(M) - \sigma_2'(K)$ exactly with no cross term, and the cosmological coefficient $-6$ is confirmed. The torsion-level counts, the rank $12$, the symbol ranks and the single surviving component are confirmed for the Riemannian and null normal forms with identical counts (the null form in the frame adapted to its dying direction); the symmetric $K \oplus M$ split, the identity $\sigma_2'(M) - \sigma_2'(K)$ with vanishing cross term and the cosmological coefficient are confirmed for the Riemannian form as well, with the cosmological coefficient $+6$ there, and at the null form the surviving pair $E_0 = -E_1$ is confirmed to carry the cosmological term with coefficient $\pm 6$. The Bianchi-series check also confirms the quoted coefficients $h_{i,1} = (2, \tfrac12, \tfrac12)$ and $h_{i,2} = (-\tfrac{33}{20}, -\tfrac{3}{40}, -\tfrac{3}{40})$ at the Schwarzschild exponents with $\Lambda = 3$. For Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.sheaf-papers-wall-soldering-variables}: the stabiliser algebra of the degenerate internal cometric $\mathrm{diag}(0,\eta_3)$ in $\mathfrak{gl}(4)$ is confirmed to have dimension $7$, with no rotation of the dying direction into the surviving ones and with the dilation present; the ambient form on $(k, m_i, k^*, w)$ is confirmed to have signature $(2,4)$; the subalgebra of $\mathfrak{so}(2,4)$ preserving the degenerate four-plane is confirmed to have dimension $8$ with a restriction of rank $7$; and the lift of the dilation is solved for exactly and confirmed to be $k \mapsto k$, $k^* \mapsto -k^*$ with everything else fixed. For Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-datum-kasner-wall}, with $\tau = s^N$ so that every power is integral: at the two rational Kasner points $(-\tfrac13,\tfrac23,\tfrac23)$ and $(-\tfrac27,\tfrac37,\tfrac67)$ the connection $\omega^{i0} = (p_i/\tau)e^i$ is confirmed torsion-free, its curvature confirmed to be the displayed double pole, the Ricci tensor confirmed to vanish identically, and the Gauss-Bonnet density confirmed to be $-16p_1p_2p_3\tau^{-3}$ ($\tfrac{64}{27}\tau^{-3}$ for Schwarzschild); at the non-Kasner point $(\tfrac15,\tfrac15,\tfrac25)$ the Ricci formulas of (ii) are confirmed as rational-function identities with nonzero value; at the excluded Milne point $(1,0,0)$ every curvature coefficient, the Ricci tensor and the Gauss-Bonnet density are confirmed to vanish. For Lemma \claimref{dark-energy.variable-exponents-residue-pointwise-corrections-are}, the displayed connection is confirmed torsion-free as an identity of the exterior algebra with the structure coefficients replaced by independent rational placeholders, and the connection with the opposite tangential sign is confirmed not to be. For Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.wall-counterterm}, the Chern-Simons form $Q$ of the Kasner connection is assembled as an exact three-form and $dQ = \epsilon_{abcd}R^{ab}\wedge R^{cd}$ confirmed identically, and the same identity is confirmed on a generic $\mathfrak{so}(1,3)$ connection with all six components nonzero and legs in all four coordinate directions, where the quartic step $\epsilon(\omega^2,\omega^2) = 0$ is exercised, and its slice component confirmed to be $8p_1p_2p_3\tau^{-2}$ at both Kasner points. For Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.cosmological-constant-does-not-change-residue}, the Bianchi I series with $\Lambda = 3$ is solved through order $\tau^5$ at the Schwarzschild exponents, the first coefficient confirmed to be $\Lambda(1-p_i)/2$, and the residuals of the evolution equations and of the constraint confirmed to vanish through order $\tau^4$, at two Kasner points and two values of $\Lambda$; the unit-boost reading of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-datum-kasner-wall}(ii) is confirmed by forming $B_w^2$ on the boost plane exactly. The withdrawn radical-vanishing claim is confirmed to fail: for an explicit integer curvature-type two-form, eleven of the twelve components $E_a(\partial_3, \cdot, \cdot)$ carrying the radical leg, and three of the four cokernel components $E_3$, are nonzero.

\emph{Part C: the two kinds, and $w$.} Trace cyclicity and the derivation identity $\operatorname{tr}([X,Y]Z) + \operatorname{tr}(Y[X,Z]) = 0$ are confirmed on exact integer matrices, and $\operatorname{tr}(\alpha\wedge\beta) = \operatorname{tr}(\beta\wedge\alpha)$ for matrix-valued two-forms on an exact instance: the algebraic content of Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.gravity-pre-metric-forces-degenerate-exactly}(1). For Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.vacuum-region-registration-condition}(ii), the norm of $I_g = (1, 0, J/4)$ is confirmed to be $J/2$ as a rational-function identity in formal $J$, and the $\mu$-slot of $\nabla I_g$, formed from \eqref{eq:conn} with a symbolic Schouten tensor, to be the trace-free Schouten tensor on an exact non-Einstein instance, and all three slots to vanish on an Einstein one; the $\rho$ slot is confirmed to be $\tfrac14\partial_aJ$ by forming $\nabla_a I_g$ from \eqref{eq:conn} itself on a Schouten tensor whose entries are rational functions of a coordinate $s$ (the slot derivatives taken by the rational-function class, the algebraic part by \eqref{eq:conn}), nonzero on that instance, where $J$ is not constant, and zero on an Einstein instance.

\emph{Part D: symmetry orthogonality.} On a six-mode fermionic window with exact rational occupancies, particle modes $k = 1,2,3$ and antiparticle modes $k = -1,-2,-3$, with energy $H = \sum |k| N_k$ and charge $Q_c = \sum_{k>0}N_k - \sum_{k<0}N_k$: the covariance $\langle HQ_c\rangle - \langle H\rangle\langle Q_c\rangle$ is confirmed to vanish exactly in the charge-conjugation-symmetric state and to be exactly nonzero at nonzero chemical potential. (Dictionary: the window's antiparticle mode $-k$ with occupancy $n^{\mathrm{anti}}_{k}$ is the hole at $-k$ of the singularity paper's array, $n^{\mathrm{array}}_{-k} = 1 - n^{\mathrm{anti}}_k$, so the window's criterion $n^{\mathrm{anti}}_k = n_k$ is the array criterion $n_{-k} = 1 - n_k$ of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.acoustic-no-go-one-way-grip}.) For the charged case, the vanishing of $\langle H\,\psi^\dagger_a\psi_b\rangle$ in a diagonal state is an identity (an operator that changes the occupation has no diagonal matrix element) and is labelled so in the script; the content is the negative control, that in a pure state superposing two charge sectors the same overlap is exactly nonzero, so the $U(1)$ hypothesis of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.acoustic-no-go-one-way-grip} is seen to be necessary on this window. The window is a free-fermion proxy for the current algebra, with $H$ as the working substitute for $L_0$; the identification with the algebra of Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-s-wave-current-algebra} is not tested.

\emph{Part E: the norm as a parameter of the laws.} For Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.under-action-written-norm-parameter-laws}(ii), with $M_P = 1$: $N^2 = 3/\Lambda$ is built as a rational function of a formal $\Lambda$ and its derivative confirmed to equal $-3/\Lambda^2$ and to be nonzero; the same rational function is evaluated at $\Lambda = 3$ and $\Lambda = 12$ and the two values confirmed unequal, with ratio $4 = s^2$. For the free case, at the rational instance $\Lambda = 3$ of the Goldstone checks: the three linearised constraints on the thirteen components $(\delta I, \delta V, \delta\Lambda)$ are confirmed to have rank $3$; the fifteen gauge images extended by a zero $\delta\Lambda$-component are confirmed to have rank $9$; the displayed norm mode is confirmed to satisfy all three free constraints and to violate the two fixed-$\Lambda$ constraints that involve a norm; the gauge images together with the norm mode are confirmed to have rank $10$, the whole constrained space; and the rescaling family $(sI, V/s, s^2\Lambda)$ is built as a vector of rational functions in a formal $s$, its componentwise $s$-derivative evaluated at $s = 1$ and confirmed to equal $2\Lambda$ times the norm mode, and its members confirmed to satisfy the three constraints at $s^2\Lambda$ as identities in $s$. The rank-$10$ check tests that the mode has a component outside the gauge span; that the mode and the images lie inside the constrained space is what the constraint checks confirm separately.

What the script does not check: the variational manipulations of Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-macdowell-mansouri-theorem-stelle-west} (imported at assembly level), the continuum limits behind Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-s-wave-current-algebra} and Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-conformal-orthogonality-sugawara-construction}, the weighted limit of Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-weighted-limit-schwarzschild-singularity-matching}, the assembly steps of Section \ref{sec:grip}, the across-the-wall regularity statement of Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}(1), the half-power pole of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.sheaf-papers-wall-soldering-variables}(i), the conformal invariance of the two-form star in Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.gravity-pre-metric-forces-degenerate-exactly}(2) and its clause (3), the boundary-term and frame statements of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.wall-counterterm}(iii), the order counting of Lemma \claimref{dark-energy.variable-exponents-residue-pointwise-corrections-are} beyond the connection identity, the vanishing interior variation of the Gauss-Bonnet term in Theorem \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}(2), the charge-conjugation criterion of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.acoustic-no-go-one-way-grip} (its window instance is the dictionary stated above), the Einstein-metric identity $-4R_{ab}R^{ab} + R^2 = 0$ behind Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-datum-kasner-wall}(i), the charge-zero Fock clause of Fact \claimref{dark-energy.imported-s-wave-current-algebra}, which no result here uses, and the observational registration of Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.vacuum-region-registration-condition}(iii), which is a date-stamped confrontation, not a computation.
