## formal
$N_{\mathrm{eff}} = 3.044$, the Standard-Model effective number of relativistic neutrino species after neutrino decoupling, from the precision calculation of Bennett et al.

## lay
How many neutrino species the early universe's expansion counts, slightly above three because neutrinos were still warmed when electrons and positrons annihilated.

## one-liner
$N_{\mathrm{eff}} = 3.044$ in the Standard Model.
