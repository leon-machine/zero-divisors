## formal
$|V_{us}| = 0.22501 \pm 0.00068$, the magnitude of the up-strange element of the quark mixing matrix from the global fit \cite{PDG2024}; the direct determination from kaon decays is $0.22431 \pm 0.00085$.

## lay
How strongly the up quark couples to the strange quark in weak decays, the size of the Cabibbo mixing between the first two quark families.
