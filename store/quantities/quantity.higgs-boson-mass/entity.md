## formal
$m_{h} = 125.20 \pm 0.11$ GeV, the mass of the Higgs boson as averaged by the Particle Data Group \cite{PDG2024}.

pending

## lay
The mass of the Higgs boson, the particle of the field that gives the weak bosons and the charged fermions their masses, about 133 times the mass of a proton.

## one-liner
$m_{h} = 125.20$ GeV.
