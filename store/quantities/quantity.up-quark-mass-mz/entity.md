## formal
$m_{u}(M_{Z}) = 1.27^{+0.50}_{-0.42}$ MeV, the $\overline{\mathrm{MS}}$ up-quark mass run to $\mu = M_{Z}$ \cite{XingZhangZhou2008}.

## technical
pending

## lay
The mass of the up quark as it appears at the energy of the Z boson, after the running of quark masses with energy.

## one-liner
$m_{u}(M_{Z}) = 1.27$ MeV.
