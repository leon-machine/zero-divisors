## formal
$m_{s}(M_{Z}) = 55^{+16}_{-15}$ MeV, the $\overline{\mathrm{MS}}$ strange-quark mass run to $\mu = M_{Z}$ \cite{XingZhangZhou2008}.

pending

## lay
The mass of the strange quark as it appears at the energy of the Z boson, after the running of quark masses with energy.

## one-liner
$m_{s}(M_{Z}) = 55$ MeV.
