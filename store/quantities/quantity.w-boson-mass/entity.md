## formal
$M_{W} = 80.3692 \pm 0.0133$ GeV, the mass of the $W$ boson as averaged by the Particle Data Group, without the CDF II measurement \cite{PDG2024}.

pending

## lay
The mass of the W boson, the electrically charged carrier of the weak force, about 86 times the mass of a proton.

## one-liner
$M_{W} = 80.37$ GeV.
