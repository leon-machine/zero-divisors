\section{Introduction}

\phantomsection\label{c:note.neutrino.setting-introduction}
The neutrino was the largest gap in the framework's derived content. This paper addresses it using only parts already present in the series, and ends by registering the framework's most precise and most exposed prediction: this is, by design and by declaration, the series' fastest-falsifiable paper, published while the prediction is still open.

The chain. The Standard-Model paper T12's derived content always contained a fourth colour singlet: the single-twistor sector carries \emph{two} singlets, at grades zero and three of $\Lambda^{\bullet}\C^{3}$, and the observed comparison column always had two. The right-handed neutrino is the grade-zero state, the \emph{empty twistor}, the pair system with no rung occupied, whose hypercharge the weight rule computes as zero. Anomaly cancellation could not have fixed it: with a fourth singlet of free hypercharge and $Y_{Q} \neq 0$ the anomaly-free spectra are exactly the two-dimensional span of the derived spectrum and $B - L$, a fact we prove in closed form, so the zero is a computation of the content derivation and not a consistency condition, and the Standard Model's ambivalence about $\nu_{R}$ is the ambivalence of a bottom-up construction that has only anomalies to go on. The natural small-mass mechanism is refuted first (the sixteenth refutation): the cosmological term of the mass paper T11's deformed bracket lies below the flat term by $\Lambda l^{2}/6$ for a composite of extent $l$, and can dominate only at horizon size. Gauge invariance then singles out the Majorana bilinear of the total singlet as the one fermion mass term that carries no charge and needs no condensate, an elementary fact proved outright and using nothing from the framework; whether the framework's own mass bracket, which T12 computes only on slot states and about which it says nothing for slot-free states, realises that term, and at what scale $M$, is a premise this paper names rather than derives, and with it the light masses follow by block inversion. Indifference makes the Majorana matrix $S_{3}$-invariant, which we show means $\alpha\mathbf{1} + \beta\J$ with $\J$ the all-ones matrix and, in the Gram-square reading, $\alpha = 0$ at leading order: the democratic vector is an eigenvector, and the seesaw of the democratic Dirac matrix is rank one at leading order and scalar on the democratic block, $m_{3} = m_{D}^{2}/M_{3}$, whatever $\alpha$ is. Three numbers follow: the ordering is normal (inverted excluded by rank plus small corrections, not by fit); the sum is bounded below by the oscillation floor and is at it, $\mnu \approx 0.059$ eV, if the second stage of the breaking is hierarchical; and the effective double-beta mass is one of two values, $1.5$ or $3.7$ meV, if the breaking is real. These are consequences of $m_{1} \approx 0$ and real phases, shared with any model having those two features; what the framework adds is the structure that produces them. The sum is at the exact centre of the sharpest tension in current cosmology, and the framework cannot use the community's standard way of relaxing the bound (a varying dark-energy equation of state) because it predicts $w = -1$: the two stances are one joint prediction. Finally, the heavy states that make the masses are the textbook mechanism of leptogenesis, and that mechanism is suppressed in three steps (alignment, a reheating premise at the lower heavy scale, phases), at the cost of a fifth registered stance: $\delta_{CP} \in \{0, \pi\}$.

\begin{postulate}[Hypothesis (U): equal rank-one eigenvalues at the ceiling]\label{c:dark-matter.hyp-u-equal-rank-one-eigenvalues}
\emph{Hypothesis (U)}, that the rank-one eigenvalues of the different sectors coincide at the ceiling, so that the neutrino's Dirac eigenvalue $m_{D}$ there is the common leading-order value, which the top retains and the second-order breaking splits for the down-type and charged-lepton sectors.
\end{postulate}

\begin{postulate}[CPT covariance of the kinematic structures and of every action]\label{c:dark-matter.premise-cpt-covariance}
\emph{CPT covariance.} CPT conjugation is an isomorphism of every kinematic structure and leaves every action functional invariant (T14, Proposition 5.2, its first premise: ``checked in the proof for the metric, the completion, the pairing and the action; premised for the imported gauge sector and for structures later papers add'').

\emph{The conjugation.} CPT conjugation $\phi$ is the composite of grade involution, reversal and the flag conjugate exchanging the two families of maximal isotropic subspaces (Definition \emph{The Witt flag} [T14, Def 2.10]). Because of the reversal, $\phi$ is an anti-automorphism of the Clifford algebra.

\emph{Convention (field action).} $\phi$ acts on fields as a symmetry: on a vector field by its vector part, and on a connection $A$ through the Lie algebra automorphism it induces, $A \mapsto -\phi(A)$, so that curvature is carried by that automorphism. In this convention $\phi$ acts on fields as $C = \mathrm{Ad}(I_{6}) \circ \kappa$, with $\kappa$ complex conjugation on $\Cl(2,4) \otimes \C$: its vector part $-1$ is the rotor $I_{6}$ of the identity component, and the flag conjugate that preserves the frozen point's action is complex conjugation, a real-linear one reversing that action (\emph{On the conformal ambient, the field action of CPT conjugation is the pseudoscalar rotor times complex conjugation}). So CPT conjugation is antilinear on the complexified ambient and on the Fock module, of Wigner's antiunitary type (argued there, not proved).

\emph{Action clause.} For every action functional $S_{\mu}$ of Definition \emph{Four ways a number enters a theory of this shape} [T10, Def 5.6], the gauge couplings among its parameters $\mu$ included, $S_{\mu} \circ C = S_{\mu}$; in particular $C$ does not move $\mu$.

\emph{What is checked and what is premised.} Checked: the fibre metric, the completion, the pairing on the Fock module, and the frozen point's action, which with every functional of real tensor fields invariant under constant $SO_{0}(2,4)$ satisfies the action clause in this convention by the cited result's clause (5). Premised: the imported gauge sector, spinor and fermion fields, and structures later papers add. For the gauge sector the standard CPT theorem for local Lorentz-invariant actions concerns the antiunitary CPT operator of quantum field theory; that this conjugation realises that operator is argued, not proved, so the gauge sector's invariance ``is imported and carried as a premise''.
\end{postulate}

\begin{postulate}[The face identification, as a working hypothesis]\label{c:families.hyp-face-identification}
Working hypothesis: the three generations are the three two-spinor faces of the maximal three-spinor system (three conformal spinors; the paper's three-twistor system). This is the identification stated as Conjecture \emph{Three generations as the faces of the maximal composite} [T13, Conj 3.1], which is not proved; the results that rely on the face identification take it as given and hold only under it.
\end{postulate}

\begin{postulate}[H: the second stage is hierarchical]\label{c:neutrino.h-second-stage-hierarchical}
The breaking $S_{3} \to S_{2} \to 1$ lifts $m_{1}$ by a factor small compared with the factor that lifts $m_{2}$: $m_{1}/m_{2} \lesssim 0.01$, that is $m_{1} \lesssim 0.1$ meV. The charged sectors supply the analogy at $m_{e}/m_{\mu} \approx 0.005$ and $m_{u}/m_{c} \approx 0.002$ \cite{PDG2024}; by Lemma \ref{c:neutrino.breaking-relation} the light masses go as the \emph{squared} singular values of the compressed breaking where the charged masses go as the singular values themselves, so a shared direction would give $m_{1}/m_{2} \approx (m_{e}/m_{\mu})^{2}$, far below what (H) needs, and if the directions are not shared the analogy is only that hierarchies are the rule. Under (H) the sum is the floor to within about $0.1$ meV ($0.1007$ meV at the premise's boundary, the residue being $m_{1}$ plus the small lift of $m_{2}$ and $m_{3}$), and the double-beta values of (3) hold to within $0.1$ meV; without it the sum is a lower bound, with $m_{1}$ free up to order $m_{2}$, where it would reach roughly $0.07$ eV.
\end{postulate}

\begin{postulate}[M: the framework realises the Majorana term, at a scale $M \gg v$]\label{c:neutrino.m-framework-realises-majorana-term-scale}
The neutral bilinear of (2) is realised by the framework, is nonzero, and its scale $M$ lies far above $v$. What (2) proves is that nothing forbids the term; gauge invariance says nothing about whether it forms or about its magnitude ($M = 0$, Dirac neutrinos; $M \sim v$; $M \sim$ eV are all consistent with it), and the framework's own mass bracket, computed only on slot states, undefined for excited composites, and of unsettled relation to the order parameter (Fact \ref{c:neutrino.imported-mass-bracket-quantum-numbers}), has not been identified with it, nor shown to differ from it. This paper takes (M) as a premise, values $M$ from the observed masses in Remark \ref{c:note.neutrino.valuation-m-both-ends-hypothesis-u}, and names its derivation as a successor problem.
\end{postulate}

\begin{postulate}[R: the descent reheats below the lower heavy scale]\label{c:neutrino.r-descent-reheats-below-lower-heavy}
$T_{\mathrm{desc}} \le \alpha = \min(\alpha, M_{3})$, with $\alpha$ on the branch of Remark \ref{c:note.neutrino.branch-m-3}. This is a reading of the dark-matter paper's own assumption (Fact \ref{c:neutrino.imported-ceiling-under-hypothesis-u-arrow}: $T_{\mathrm{desc}}$ at the ceiling or below it) at "below"; under it no heavy state ever thermalised, the washout of $N_{u'}$ is Boltzmann-suppressed, the pair $N_{\perp}$ was never produced thermally, and the descent's $B - L$ is untouched. The natural justification, that the frozen phase's description dissolves above the rung scale, identifies $M_{3}$ with that scale, which is Hypothesis (U) and the valuation of Remark \ref{c:note.neutrino.valuation-m-both-ends-hypothesis-u}, not a theorem; and (R) at $\alpha$ asks more of the descent than that, since $\alpha$ lies nearly two orders below $M_{3}$ on the branch taken. A derivation of $M$ and of $\alpha$ (successor problems (2) and (3)) would decide it.
\end{postulate}

\begin{postulate}[The real-breaking assumption]\label{c:neutrino.real-breaking-assumption}

\end{postulate}

\begin{lemma}[$S_{3}$-invariant symmetric matrices]\label{c:neutrino.invariant-symmetric-matrices}
Let $\J$ be the $3 \times 3$ all-ones matrix and $u' = (1,1,1)$. A real symmetric $3 \times 3$ matrix $S$ satisfies $PSP^{\mathsf{T}} = S$ for every permutation matrix $P$ if and only if $S = \alpha\mathbf{1} + \beta\J$ for real $\alpha, \beta$. Such an $S$ has $u'$ as an eigenvector with eigenvalue $M_{3} := \alpha + 3\beta$, the plane $u'^{\perp}$ as an eigenspace with eigenvalue $\alpha$, $\det S = \alpha^{2}(\alpha + 3\beta)$, hence is invertible if and only if $\alpha \neq 0$ and $\alpha \neq -3\beta$, with $S^{-1} = \alpha^{-1}\mathbf{1} - \beta\,\alpha^{-1}(\alpha + 3\beta)^{-1}\J$. Writing $\Pi = \mathbf{1} - \J/3$ for the projector on $u'^{\perp}$, $S = \alpha\Pi + M_{3}\J/3$, and $S^{-1} = \alpha^{-1}\Pi + M_{3}^{-1}\J/3$ when both are nonzero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Invariance under the transposition $(12)$ gives $S_{11} = S_{22}$ and $S_{13} = S_{23}$; under $(23)$, $S_{22} = S_{33}$ and $S_{12} = S_{13}$; so all diagonal entries equal some $\alpha + \beta$ and all off-diagonal entries equal some $\beta$, that is $S = \alpha\mathbf{1} + \beta\J$; conversely $\mathbf{1}$ and $\J$ are fixed by every $P$. Since $\J u' = 3u'$ and $\J v = 0$ for $v \perp u'$, the spectrum is as stated and $\det S = \alpha^{2}(\alpha + 3\beta)$. The inverse follows from $\J^{2} = 3\J$: $(\alpha\mathbf{1} + \beta\J)(\alpha^{-1}\mathbf{1} + \gamma\J) = \mathbf{1} + (\alpha\gamma + \beta\alpha^{-1} + 3\beta\gamma)\J$, which is $\mathbf{1}$ for $\gamma = -\beta/(\alpha(\alpha + 3\beta))$. The projector form is $\Pi\J = 0$, $\Pi^{2} = \Pi$, $(\J/3)^{2} = \J/3$. The script verifies the characterisation on positive and negative instances over all six permutations, the spectrum, the determinant with both singular loci, the inverse against the adjugate, and the projector form on rational instances.

\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The breaking relation]\label{c:neutrino.breaking-relation}
Let $\mD = d(\J + \delta A)$ with $A$ a real $3 \times 3$ matrix (its scale is fixed by the reference direction $e_{3}e_{3}^{\mathsf{T}}$, the $S_{3} \to S_{2}$ direction, for which $\|\Pi A\Pi\| = \tfrac23$ in the operator norm and $\lambda_{A} = \tfrac49$ below; $\delta$ is quoted relative to that direction, as the instances state, and $\delta^{2}\lambda_{A}$ is invariant under a rescaling of $A$), $\delta$ a real breaking parameter, and $M_{R} = \alpha\Pi + M_{3}\J/3$ with $\alpha, M_{3} \neq 0$. Then, exactly,
\[
\mD M_{R}^{-1}\mD^{\mathsf{T}} \;=\; \frac{d^{2}\delta^{2}}{\alpha}\,A\,\Pi\,A^{\mathsf{T}} \;+\; \frac{d^{2}}{3M_{3}}\,q\,q^{\mathsf{T}}, \qquad q = (\J + \delta A)\,u' = 3u' + \delta A u' .
\]
Consequently the second and third light masses are, to leading order in $\delta$, $(d^{2}\delta^{2}/\alpha)$ times the two eigenvalues $\lambda_{A} \ge \lambda'_{A}$ of the compression $\Pi A\Pi A^{\mathsf{T}}\Pi$ of the first term to $u'^{\perp}$ (not of $A\Pi A^{\mathsf{T}}$ itself, whose range is not orthogonal to $u'$), so $m_{1}/m_{2} = \lambda'_{A}/\lambda_{A}$ at this order, and
\[
\frac{m_{2}}{m_{3}} \;=\; \frac{\delta^{2}\lambda_{A}}{9}\,\frac{M_{3}}{\alpha}\,\Bigl(1 + O(\delta) + O\bigl(\delta^{2}M_{3}/\alpha\bigr)\Bigr):
\]
no breaking of the Majorana sector alone lifts $m_{2}$ (since $\J X \J = (\textstyle\sum_{ij}X_{ij})\J$ for every $X$), the Dirac breaking enters squared, and the seesaw enhances it by $M_{3}/\alpha$. The second remainder is $O(m_{2}/m_{3})$, a function of the answer itself, and does not vanish as $\delta \to 0$ at fixed physical ratio; at the sizes of interest the two together are not small (the formula overestimates the exact ratio by more than a half at the instance of Remark \ref{c:note.neutrino.branch-m-3}), so the instances are diagonalised exactly rather than read from the formula.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
$M_{R}^{-1} = \alpha^{-1}\Pi + M_{3}^{-1}\J/3$ by Lemma \ref{c:neutrino.invariant-symmetric-matrices}. Expand $\mD M_{R}^{-1}\mD^{\mathsf{T}} = d^{2}(\J + \delta A)(\alpha^{-1}\Pi + M_{3}^{-1}\J/3)(\J + \delta A^{\mathsf{T}})$. Since $\J\Pi = \Pi\J = 0$, the $\Pi$ term leaves only $\alpha^{-1}\delta^{2}A\Pi A^{\mathsf{T}}$; since $\J = u'u'^{\mathsf{T}}$, the $\J/3$ term is $M_{3}^{-1}\tfrac13\,(\J + \delta A)u'u'^{\mathsf{T}}(\J + \delta A^{\mathsf{T}}) = M_{3}^{-1}\tfrac13\,qq^{\mathsf{T}}$. The identity $\J X\J = (\sum X_{ij})\J$ is $u'^{\mathsf{T}}Xu' = \sum_{ij}X_{ij}$. The second term is rank one along $q = 3u' + O(\delta)$ with eigenvalue $d^{2}|q|^{2}/(3M_{3}) = 9d^{2}/M_{3}\,(1 + O(\delta))$; at fixed $\alpha$ and $M_{3}$ the first term is $O(\delta^{2})$ and the second $O(1)$, so to leading order in $\delta$ the second dominates, the top eigenvector of the sum is $q + O(\delta^{2}M_{3}/\alpha)$, and the remaining two eigenvalues are those of the first term compressed to $q^{\perp} = u'^{\perp} + O(\delta)$: that is, $(d^{2}\delta^{2}/\alpha)$ times the eigenvalues of $\Pi A\Pi A^{\mathsf{T}}\Pi$. (The range of $A\Pi A^{\mathsf{T}}$ is $A(u'^{\perp})$, which is not orthogonal to $u'$ unless $A^{\mathsf{T}}u' \parallel u'$, so the eigenvalues of the first term itself are not the light masses; for $A = e_{3}e_{3}^{\mathsf{T}}$ the two differ by the factor $\tfrac23$ exactly.) The script verifies the exact identity on rational instances of $(\alpha, \beta, d, \delta, A)$; for $A = e_{3}e_{3}^{\mathsf{T}}$ (the $S_{3} \to S_{2}$ direction, $\lambda_{A} = \tfrac49$, $\lambda'_{A} = 0$) it diagonalises the light matrix exactly, the characteristic polynomial factoring as $\lambda$ times a quadratic, and certifies $m_{2}/m_{3} \in (0.168, 0.172)$ at $\delta = 0.286$ and $M_{3}/\alpha = 66$ (the instance of Remark \ref{c:note.neutrino.branch-m-3}), against $0.267$ from the leading-order formula there; and, at the small-breaking end, that the exact ratio at $\delta = 0.006$ is within four percent of the $\lambda_{A} = \tfrac49$ formula and about two thirds of the $A\Pi A^{\mathsf{T}}$ one.

\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The branch: $\alpha \ll M_{3}$]\label{c:note.neutrino.branch-m-3}

\end{remark}
