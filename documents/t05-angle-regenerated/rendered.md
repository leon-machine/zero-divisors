# What Fixes the Angle: The spectrum-condition route to the Hawking temperature, run in the ascent's own algebra: Companion note to T5: within the supplied dynamics positivity of energy selects the Boulware state; a hop across the horizon is what the route needs, and the hop with the sea filled is the pairing (regenerated from the store)

**Abstract.** The singularity paper fixes the Hawking temperature by a premise, regularity at the fixed locus of the Killing boost, proves that nothing inside the mode algebra replaces it, and records one route to a better premise: positivity of the energy of the boundary modes for a generator the ascent installs, from which the Bisognano--Wichmann mechanism derives the factor $2\pi$ rather than assuming it. This note runs that route as far as the algebra goes, in exact arithmetic, with kill conditions fixed in advance. The geometric half of the mechanism is in the ascent: the boost rotor at imaginary rapidity $i\pi$ is the reflection through the bifurcation surface, and the conformal completion installs the translations as null rotations fixing the point at infinity. The selective half is not. Within the supplied dynamics every generator is diagonal in the joint number basis, so a unique ground state is a product across every cut, Gibbs at no finite inverse temperature: the spectrum condition selects a Boulware-type state or nothing. A hop across the cut changes this: the generator $7N_{0} - 12(a^{\dagger}_{0}a_{1} + a^{\dagger}_{1}a_{0})$ has a unique entangled ground state whose one-sided marginal is the Gibbs state at the singularity paper's sample point, and the particle-hole map on one mode carries it to that paper's thermofield pair. The route therefore survives only with the kinetic term the singularity paper's locality premise excludes, its two open items becoming one; the value $2\pi$ remains the imported theorem's, being a ratio of couplings in the finite model.

## What we assume

**Postulate 1 ((L$_E$): boundary-mode locality, as descent covariance on finite windows).** \emph{Premise (L$_{E}$), boundary-mode locality.} On every finite window of the boundary modes, $v_{1}, \dots, v_{n}$ satisfying the CAR, the internal dynamics $\alpha_{t}$ of the boundary modes commutes with the descent at all times:
\[
\alpha_{t}\circ\mathcal{E} \;=\; \mathcal{E}\circ\alpha_{t} \quad \text{for all } t, \qquad \mathcal{E} = E_{1}\circ\cdots\circ E_{n}, \quad E_{k}(x) = N_{k}xN_{k} + (1 - N_{k})x(1 - N_{k}),
\]
$\mathcal{E}$ the descent of the window (Proposition \claimref{singularity.descent-what-it-erases}).

\emph{The version 1 wording, equivalent.} By Theorem \claimref{synthesis.boundary-mode-locality-is-descent-covariance}, on every finite window this holds if and only if the dynamics is generated by elements of the abelian algebra $\mathcal{D}$ that the $\sigma_{k} = 2N_{k} - 1$ generate, diagonal in the joint number basis: the internal dynamics is generated by the geometric data the framework supplies on $E$, the boost bivector field $x \mapsto \sigma_{r}(x)$ and its products. Cross-point products such as $\sigma_{j}\sigma_{k}$ correlate phases without moving occupation; hopping $v_{j}^{*}v_{k} + v_{k}^{*}v_{j}$, pairing $v_{j}^{*}v_{k}^{*} + v_{k}v_{j}$ and every other generator with a component outside $\mathcal{D}$ are excluded by the one clause. Within the class of generators the framework supplies, the exclusion of hopping is a theorem (Theorem \claimref{singularity.class-relative-no-go-no-supplied}); what the premise adds is the exclusion of unsupplied generators, pairing terms and the possible differential dynamics, which Proposition \claimref{singularity.l-stabiliser-tangent} places in the tangent of the vacuum orbit and in its stabiliser.

\emph{What it forbids.} By Theorem \claimref{synthesis.number-diagonal-dynamics-spreads-nothing}, every dynamics the premise admits carries an evolved mode operator only to the modes directly coupled to it, and every local commutator and out-of-time-order correlator is almost periodic and bounded: the premise forbids exponential real-time commutator growth. The real-time spreading law $t_{*} = \kappa^{-1}\ln N$ of Proposition \claimref{scrambling.spreading-law-chaos-bound-saturated} is therefore not realised under it, and the scrambling time is a modular-time statement: the growth $e^{\kappa t}$ is a rate of the rotor flow, the KMS-strip continuation of the phase flow that is the windows' time evolution (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.phase-flow-is-time-rotor-flow-kms-continuation}). The premise is kept over fast real-time scrambling; the two cannot both hold.

**Postulate 2 ((Q): joint quasi-freeness, with the s-wave weights at the half-integer offset).** In the stationary idealisation of the boundary modes, and for this step alone, the joint state of the mode array is taken to be the gauge-invariant quasi-free state with the marginal data that (P) and (R) (Premise \claimref{singularity.r-regularity-fixed-locus}) fix unconditionally (Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-discharge-hypothesis-iii}, Corollary \claimref{singularity.chains-state-class-stability}): writing $\psi_k$ for the fermion modes of the array of Theorem \claimref{singularity.bosonisation-bridge-s-wave-sector}, its two-point function is $\langle \psi^\dagger_k \psi_l \rangle = \delta_{kl}\,n_k$, and every higher correlation function factorises by Wick's theorem, so that all connected correlators vanish and the joint state is KMS at $\beta = 2\pi/\kappa$ for the joint modular flow. The mode weights are evenly spaced with the array's sea level midway between two sites,
\[
\omega_k = \kappa\bigl(k + \tfrac12\bigr), \qquad k \in \Z ,
\]
so that no mode has zero weight, the weights are symmetric under the particle-hole relabelling $k \mapsto -1-k$, and the Killing flow at one modular period $2\pi/\kappa$ is the parity automorphism $\psi \mapsto -\psi$.

\emph{Why this offset.} The weights $\omega_k$ of Definition \claimref{obj.singularity.geometric-modular-condition-g} are fixed nowhere else in the framework: given (P), (R) and the quasi-free clause, an affine law $\omega_k = ck + d$ is what the currents' being normal modes of the boost flow requires, particle-hole symmetry leaves exactly two offset classes, $d = 0$ and $d = c/2$, and the slope is the value $\beta c = 2\pi$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.q-splits-maximum-entropy-affine-weights}(ii), (iv)). A field of boost weight $\tfrac12$ changes sign under a full turn of the regular Euclidean section, and $\kappa(k + \tfrac12)$ is the offset at which the array's Killing flow at one modular period carries that sign (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.half-integer-weight-array-carries-half-ln2}). The reading is a decision, not a derivation: the Euclidean-period sign $R_{2\pi} = -1$ (Proposition \claimref{state-selection.where-fermi-dirac-comes}) holds in a KMS state of the array at either offset, so identifying the array's real-time recurrence at one period with it is part of the reading.

**Postulate 3 ((R): regularity at the fixed locus).** For every mode $k$ and every pair $a, b$ of elements of its ladder $\{v_k, v_k^{*}\}$, the correlation function $F_{a,b}(t) := \omega\bigl(a\,\alpha_t(b)\bigr)$ of the Killing flow $\alpha_t = \operatorname{Ad}(e^{it\omega_k K_k})$, an entire function of $t$, continues in imaginary Killing time to a single-valued function on the regular Euclidean section of the Schwarzschild gravitational field on flat spacetime (Proposition \claimref{singularity.euclidean-section-period}; Definition \claimref{obj.synthesis.gtg-flat-spacetime-gauge-fields}): its two orderings are the two sides of the cut, and they meet after one period,
\[
F_{a,b}\bigl(t + i\,p\bigr) \;=\; \omega\bigl(\alpha_t(b)\,a\bigr), \qquad p = \frac{2\pi}{\kappa} .
\]
In words: each single-mode marginal is KMS for the Killing flow at exactly the period the Euclidean section's smoothness at its tip dictates. The regularity is local, posed at the tip of the Euclidean section (a polar chart about the tip of the Wick-rotated flat background); for a state that is KMS for the Killing flow, this is also the period at which it is regular across the horizon locus in the Painlev\'e-Gullstrand chart.

\emph{Note (continuum restatement).} Posed on the continuum of the horizon's null generators (Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.continuum-mode-space}), the content of (R) for the eternal (Kruskal) hole follows from Premise \claimref{synthesis.n-plus-null-ground-state}: by Theorem \claimref{synthesis.positive-null-energy-gives-r-premises}(iii) the generators' modes are then jointly KMS for the Killing flow at $\beta = 8\pi M$. The wording above, on the ladders $\{v_{k}, v_{k}^{*}\}$ with a phase-acting flow, is a discretisation of that statement, recovered only for modes of vanishing Mellin width; (N$_{+}$) itself cannot be posed on point spectrum. A hole formed by collapse is not covered by (N$_{+}$), and (R) remains a premise. (The whole generator of the eternal horizon, which that case of (N$_{+}$) uses, lies beyond one flat chart; see that premise.)

## Setting, and what is imported



**setting  (Setting, and what is imported).** The singularity paper obtains the Hawking temperature in three moves. Modular descent gives the form: a pure, boost-invariant, entangling state of a horizon pair descends to a Gibbs state for the boost at some inverse temperature $\beta$ in rapidity \cite[Thm.~7.3]{ButlerT5}. Rate rigidity shows $\beta$ is a ratio of two clocks, the state's modular clock and the boost's geometric clock, and that no automorphism of the mode algebra fixes it \cite[Thm.~7.16]{ButlerT5}. Premise (R) then fixes it: each single-mode marginal is KMS for the Killing flow at the period $2\pi/\kappa$ that the smoothness of the Euclidean section dictates \cite[Prop.~7.18, Prem.~7.19]{ButlerT5}, whence $T = \kappa/2\pi = 1/8\pi M$. The paper proves that (R) cannot be internal: the locus where the calibration happens, the bifurcation surface, is one where division does not fail, so the transport principle makes no statement there \cite[Prop.~7.25]{ButlerT5}. It lists the standard forms of the same import, Euclidean regularity, regularity on the future horizon, nonsingularity of the quasi-free state, and the short-distance condition near the horizon, and it records one route to a better-placed premise: the Bisognano--Wichmann theorem does not assume the factor $2\pi$ but derives it from a spectrum condition, positivity of the energy, together with covariance and locality; the ascent to $\Cl(2,4)$ installs translations in the parabolic stabiliser of the point at infinity; so the premise could be restated as positivity of the energy of the boundary modes with respect to a generator the ascent itself installed. Transcribing the theorem, the paper says, needs an action of the relevant subgroup on the boundary modes, a wedge and locality structure, and analyticity of the state, each of which could fail \cite[Rem.~7.26, Rem.~7.33(a)]{ButlerT5}.

This note runs that route as far as exact arithmetic reaches, and no further. What it can test is whether, within the dynamics the singularity paper supplies, a spectrum condition selects a state at all, and what the selected state is; what the ascent contributes to the geometric half of the mechanism; and what a generator outside the supplied class does. What it cannot test is the analytic half, the theorem that turns positivity of energy into the number $2\pi$, which is a statement about infinitely many modes and analytic continuation and is imported below with its hypotheses. The kill conditions of the section ``The claim, and the kill conditions fixed before computation'' were fixed before the computation, after reading the passages of the singularity paper just cited.

Vocabulary, for a reader of the singularity paper: the \emph{supplied class} is the set of generators its completion provides for the boundary-mode dynamics, the second quantisations of function-linear maps of the mode line bundle and the products of the parity sections, all diagonal in the joint number basis, so that no supplied generator moves occupation from one point to another \cite[Prem.~7.30, Thm.~C.8]{ButlerT5}; a \emph{hop} is a generator $a^{\dagger}_{x}a_{y} + a^{\dagger}_{y}a_{x}$ between two modes, the second quantisation of a first-order differential operator, which the supplied class does not contain and whose presence in nature the paper declares as its residual question \cite[Rem.~7.33(b), Rem.~C.9]{ButlerT5}; a \emph{cut} divides a set of modes into two sides, the two ends of a horizon pair; a state is \emph{boost-KMS at $\beta$} on a side if its marginal there is $e^{-\beta K}/Z$ for the boost generator $K$ restricted to that side; the \emph{spectrum condition} for a generator $H$ is that the state is the unique ground state of $H$; and the \emph{Boulware} member of the singularity paper's dictionary is the product state with pure marginals, $\beta = \infty$ \cite[Rem.~7.26]{ButlerT5}.

\item[(ii)] At boost rapidity $2\eta = i\pi$, that is $\eta = i\pi/2$, the rotor is $iB$, with $(iB)\widetilde{(iB)} = B^{2} = 1$, and its action $x \mapsto BxB$ is $-1$ on the boost plane and $+1$ transverse to it: the reflection through the bifurcation surface. \item[(iii)] In the completion $\Cl(2,4)$, for $a$ in the base, $T_{a} = 1 + \tfrac12\nInf a$ is an even element with inverse $1 - \tfrac12\nInf a$, fixes $\nInf$, is the null rotation $R_{u}$ of Fact \claimref{angle.imported-conformal-model-null-rotations} with $v = \nInf$ and $u = \tfrac12 a$, and carries $X(x)$ to $X(x + a)$. The base's boost rotor, read in the completion, fixes $\nO$ and $\nInf$ and carries $X(x)$ to $X(Rx\widetilde{R})$.
\end{enumerate}

*Proof.* All clauses are identities in the blade basis, checked by the script (Parts A and B) on rational vectors. For (iii): $(\nInf a)^{2} = -\nInf^{2}a^{2} = 0$ since $a$ anticommutes with $\nInf$, so $T_{a}$ is invertible with the stated inverse and is the null rotation $R_{u}$ of \claimref{angle.imported-conformal-model-null-rotations} with $u = \tfrac12 a$. ## The selective half, in the algebra





**Theorem 8 (Within the supplied class the spectrum condition selects Boulware, or nothing).** Let $H$ be a generator in the supplied class on $\nu$ modes, a polynomial in the number operators, and let a cut divide the modes into two sides.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $H$ is diagonal in the joint number basis. If its ground state is unique, that state is a number basis state: a product across the cut, with a pure marginal on each side, an eigenvector of every $N_{k}$ and hence of every boost generator $\sum_{k}\omega_{k}(N_{k} - \tfrac12)$, and Gibbs on a side at no finite $\beta$: a Boulware-type state, a product with pure marginals.
\item[(ii)] If the ground state is not unique, the ground space may contain entangled states, but the spectrum condition selects none of them: for $H = (N_{0} - N_{1})^{2} = N_{0} + N_{1} - 2N_{0}N_{1}$ on the pair, the ground space is $\operatorname{span}\{\ket{00}, \ket{11}\}$, which contains the thermofield pair $\tfrac45\ket{00} + \tfrac35\ket{11}$ and the product state $\ket{00}$ alike.
\end{enumerate}
Within the supplied class, therefore, positivity of the energy selects a Boulware-type state or selects no state, and Claim G is dead at the state-selection step.

*Proof.* (i) By Theorem \claimref{singularity.class-relative-no-go-no-supplied} the supplied class is ultralocal, every generator in it a multiplication operator in the joint number basis, so a polynomial in the commuting projectors $N_{k}$. A polynomial in commuting projectors is diagonal in their joint eigenbasis; a diagonal matrix with a unique minimum has that basis vector as its unique ground state. A number basis state is a product of single-mode number states, its marginal on any set of modes is the corresponding number state, pure, and it is an eigenvector of each $N_{k}$. A Gibbs state $e^{-\beta K}/Z$ at finite $\beta$ for a single-mode boost $K = \omega(N - \tfrac12)$ is $\operatorname{diag}(1, e^{-\beta\omega})/Z$, of rank two, and a pure marginal has rank one. The script draws random rational diagonal generators on two and three modes with every cut, and for each draw with a unique ground state (at least three such draws in all) confirms the product form, the rank-one idempotent marginals on both sides, the eigenvector property, and, for a single-mode side, the absence of a Gibbs parameter. (ii) The stated $H$ has eigenvalues $0, 0, 1, 1$ on $\ket{00}, \ket{11}, \ket{10}, \ket{01}$; the script checks the ground space and that both named states are annihilated by $H$.




**Theorem 9 (A hop across the cut).** On two modes, let $H = 7N_{0} - 12(a^{\dagger}_{0}a_{1} + a^{\dagger}_{1}a_{0})$, the second quantisation of the one-particle operator $\begin{psmallmatrix} 7 & -12 \\ -12 & 0\end{psmallmatrix}$, which is not in the supplied class.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $H$ is Hermitian and conserves the total number; its spectrum is $0$ on $\ket{00}$, $7$ on $\ket{11}$, and $16, -9$ on the one-particle sector, so its ground state is unique: $\psi = \tfrac35\ket{10} + \tfrac45\ket{01}$ with eigenvalue $-9$.
\item[(ii)] $\psi$ is an odd vector, hence of definite parity, so the state it defines is even (it vanishes on the odd sector) and is physical under the univalence rule, and entangled across the cut: its left marginal is $\operatorname{diag}(\tfrac{16}{25}, \tfrac{9}{25})$, the Gibbs state for $N_{0}$ at $e^{-\omega/T} = \tfrac{9}{16}$, the singularity paper's sample point, and its right marginal is $\operatorname{diag}(\tfrac{9}{25}, \tfrac{16}{25})$.
\item[(iii)] $\psi$ is an eigenvector of $N_{0} + N_{1}$ and not of $N_{0} - N_{1}$: it is invariant under the boost flow in which the two modes have equal frequency, and not under the number-difference boost of the vacuum form.
\end{enumerate}

*Proof.* The one-particle block has trace $7$ and determinant $-144$, eigenvalues $16$ and $-9$, and the eigenvector for $-9$ satisfies $16u = 12v$, giving $(u, v) = (\tfrac35, \tfrac45)$. The script checks the block, the sector energies, the eigenvector, the norm, the parity, both marginals by restriction to the mode's algebra, and the two eigenvector statements. The generator lies outside the supplied class because every supplied generator commutes with every $N_{x}$ (Theorem \claimref{singularity.class-relative-no-go-no-supplied}), while $[N_{0}, a^{\dagger}_{0}a_{1} + a^{\dagger}_{1}a_{0}] = a^{\dagger}_{0}a_{1} - a^{\dagger}_{1}a_{0} \neq 0$.




**Theorem 10 (The hop with the sea filled is the pairing).** Let $V = (a_{1} + a^{\dagger}_{1})\Gamma_{0}$ on the pair, with $\Gamma_{0} = (-1)^{N_{0}}$ and $\ket{11} = a^{\dagger}_{0}a^{\dagger}_{1}\ket{00}$. Then $V$ is unitary and Hermitian, $V^{2} = 1$, conjugation by $V$ fixes $a_{0}$ and exchanges $a_{1}$ with $a^{\dagger}_{1}$ (the particle-hole automorphism of the right mode), and:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $V\psi = \tfrac45\ket{00} + \tfrac35\ket{11}$, exactly the singularity paper's thermofield pair at the sample point, an even state with the same left marginal $\operatorname{diag}(\tfrac{16}{25}, \tfrac{9}{25})$;
\item[(ii)] $VHV^{-1} = 7N_{0} - 12(a^{\dagger}_{0}a^{\dagger}_{1} + a_{1}a_{0})$, a pairing generator with the same spectrum, whose unique ground state is $V\psi$;
\item[(iii)] $V(N_{0} + N_{1} - 1)V^{-1} = N_{0} - N_{1}$: the equal-frequency boost of the hop form becomes the number-difference boost of the vacuum form.
\end{enumerate}

*Proof.* $(a_{1} + a^{\dagger}_{1})$ is a Majorana operator, Hermitian with square one, and conjugation by it sends $a_{1}$ to $a^{\dagger}_{1}$ and $a_{0}$ to $-a_{0}$; the factor $\Gamma_{0}$ restores the sign of $a_{0}$ and commutes with the right mode. The script checks the unitarity, the automorphism on the four ladder operators, the image of $\psi$, its parity and marginal, the conjugated generator against the pairing generator entry by entry, its ground state, and the conjugated boost. Here $\psi$ and $H$ are the state and generator of Proposition \claimref{angle.hop-across-cut}; conjugation by the unitary $V$ preserves the spectrum, so $VHV^{-1}$ has that proposition's spectrum and its unique ground state is the image $V\psi$ of the unique ground state there.


**Remark  (One term in two vacua).** The singularity paper's residual question asks whether the physical dynamics on the boundary carries a differential term or a pairing term, the two kinds of generator its supplied class lacks. Proposition \claimref{angle.hop-sea-filled-pairing} says the question has one answer, not two: a hop between a mode and its partner, with the partner's sea filled, is a pairing between the mode and the partner's hole, and the ground state of the one is the thermofield pair of the other. That a number-conserving generator applied to the vacuum keeps the pair in its vacuum, while a hop applied to a one-particle state produces the thermofield marginal, is the same fact read in the two vacua: what the hop needs is the filled sea the s-wave array of Step 4e supplies, and what the pairing needs is the vacuum. The particle-hole map $V$ is the Fock form of a symmetry of the ascent itself: in the two-rung ascent $\Cl(2,2)$ with ladder pairs $f_{k} = \tfrac12(e_{k} + e_{k+2})$, $f_{k}^{*} = \tfrac12(e_{k} - e_{k+2})$, the reflection $e_{4} \mapsto -e_{4}$ is an automorphism that fixes $f_{1}$, carries $f_{2}$ to $f_{2}^{*}$, and carries the hop $f_{1}^{*}f_{2}$ to the pairing $f_{1}^{*}f_{2}^{*}$ (script, Part E).



**Theorem 11 (The value is a parameter of the dynamics).** For every rational point $(c, s)$ of the unit circle with $cs \neq 0$ and every rational $t$ with $tcs > 0$, the hop generator $H = \varepsilon N_{0} - t(a^{\dagger}_{0}a_{1} + a^{\dagger}_{1}a_{0})$ with $\varepsilon/t = (s^{2} - c^{2})/(cs)$ has $c\ket{10} + s\ket{01}$ as its unique ground state, with eigenvalue $-tc/s$, and left marginal Gibbs for $N_{0}$ at $q = c^{2}/s^{2}$. The Gibbs parameter of the marginal, and with it $\beta\omega = -\log q$, is determined by the ratio $\varepsilon/t$ of the couplings together with the sign of $t$ (by the ratio alone for $t > 0$): $c/s$ is the root of $r^{2} + (\varepsilon/t)r - 1 = 0$ with the sign of $t$.

*Proof.* The one-particle block $\begin{psmallmatrix} \varepsilon & -t \\ -t & 0\end{psmallmatrix}$ has $(c, s)$ as an eigenvector with eigenvalue $\lambda = -tc/s$ exactly when $\varepsilon c - ts = \lambda c$, i.e.\ $\varepsilon = t(s^{2} - c^{2})/(cs)$; the other one-particle eigenvalue is $\varepsilon - \lambda$, and the three gaps, to it and to the sector energies $0$ and $\varepsilon$, are $ts/c + tc/s$, $tc/s$ and $ts/c$, all positive exactly when $tcs > 0$. The script confirms the eigenvector, the gaps and the marginal at seven points, $(\tfrac35, \tfrac45)$, $(\tfrac{5}{13}, \tfrac{12}{13})$, $(\tfrac{8}{17}, \tfrac{15}{17})$, $(\tfrac{7}{25}, \tfrac{24}{25})$, $(\tfrac{24}{25}, \tfrac{7}{25})$ and $(-\tfrac35, -\tfrac45)$ at $t = 60$ and $(\tfrac35, -\tfrac45)$ at $t = -60$. Given $\varepsilon/t$, the ratio $c/s$ is the root of $r^{2} + (\varepsilon/t)r - 1 = 0$ with the sign of $t$ (the two roots have product $-1$) and $q$ is its square.


**Remark  (Where $2\pi$ lives).** Proposition \claimref{angle.value-parameter-dynamics} is the finite-model form of the singularity paper's rate rigidity: a hop selects an entangled state and a finite $\beta$, but the value of $\beta$ is whatever the couplings make it. \]
so that near $\varrho = 0$ the $(\varrho, \tau)$ plane carries the metric $d\varrho^{2} + \kappa^{2}\varrho^{2}d\tau^{2} + O(\varrho^{4})\,d\tau^{2}$. If $\tau$ is periodic with period $p$, the section is a smooth Riemannian manifold at $\varrho = 0$ (the tip of the section, the two-sphere where the Euclidean Killing flow has its fixed points, the Euclidean counterpart of the bifurcation sphere) if and only if
\[
\kappa\,p \;=\; 2\pi, \qquad \text{i.e.} \qquad p \;=\; \frac{2\pi}{\kappa} \;=\; 8\pi M ;
\]
for any other period the origin of the $(\varrho, \tau)$ plane is a conical point of total angle $\kappa p$. The period of imaginary Killing time is therefore fixed by the gravitational field, at the horizon locus, and it is the conversion factor of Proposition \claimref{singularity.plane-rate} read in imaginary time. The regularity condition is local: it lives in a polar chart about the tip of the Wick-rotated flat background; the Lorentzian bifurcation surface itself lies beyond one flat chart and is not used.

**Fact (imported) 15 (Modular descent).** Let the ascended algebra of the horizon pair carry any state that is (i) pure, (ii) invariant under the boost flow generated by $\sigma$, and (iii) entangling across the two ends of the pair (cyclic and separating for the one-sided subalgebra). Then the descended state, the partial trace over the partner, which for such a state coincides with the restriction under the pinching $E$ of Proposition \claimref{singularity.descent-what-it-erases} (Proposition \claimref{singularity.pinching-against-partial-trace-scope-coincidence}(i); the coincidence is exactly single-mode, Proposition \claimref{singularity.pinching-against-partial-trace-scope-coincidence}(ii)), is KMS for the phase flow $\operatorname{Ad}(e^{it\sigma/2})$ of $\sigma$ at some inverse temperature $\beta \in \R$ in rapidity, and the hypotheses leave $\beta$ free, its sign included: nothing in (i) to (iii) forces $\beta > 0$ (the descended form of Theorem \claimref{singularity.descended-form-state-independent} allows the closed range $[-\infty, +\infty]$, and hypothesis (iii) removes exactly the endpoints), and positivity arrives with (R). If the modular parameter is normalised to the geometric period, one modular period being one period $2\pi/\kappa$ of rapidity-time (the Bisognano-Wichmann normalisation, which the section ``What selects the state'' names as premise (R) and proves irreducible), then $\beta = 2\pi$ in rapidity and, by Proposition \claimref{singularity.plane-rate}, the physical temperature is
\[
T \;=\; \frac{\kappa}{2\pi} \;=\; \frac{1}{8\pi M} \;=\; \TH ,
\]
exactly the Hawking temperature. For the CAR pair, the reduced density matrix per mode of frequency $\omega$ is then the Fermi-Dirac matrix $\operatorname{diag}(1, e^{-\omega/T})/Z$ with occupancy $1/(e^{\omega/T} + 1)$. The hypotheses are sufficient, not necessary: a state violating (ii) or (iii) need not descend thermally, and the projection cannot make it do so, but some such states do (the proof exhibits one), and Theorem \claimref{singularity.descended-form-state-independent} explains why no hypothesis on the state can be necessary for the \emph{form}.

*Provenance:* Transport Lab claim singularity.modular-descent, version 5 (T05 Thm 7.3).

**Fact (imported) 16 (Rate rigidity: the temperature is not algebra intrinsic).** Let $\alpha_t = \operatorname{Ad}(e^{itK})$ be the boost flow of the single-mode algebra $\Aa$, let $\theta$ be a $*$-automorphism of $\Aa$, and let $\omega_\beta$ denote the $\beta$-KMS state of $\alpha$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item If $\theta$ commutes with the flow then it fixes every Gibbs state of $K$: $\omega_\beta \circ \theta = \omega_\beta$ for every $\beta$.
\item If $\theta$ reverses the flow, $\theta\circ\alpha_t = \alpha_{-t}\circ\theta$, then $\omega_\beta\circ\theta = \omega_{-\beta}$.
\item Under a constant reparametrisation of the flow, $\alpha_t \mapsto \alpha_{ct}$ with $c > 0$, the $\beta$-KMS state of $\alpha$ is the $(\beta/c)$-KMS state of the reparametrised flow: $\omega_\beta$ is unchanged and its inverse temperature is rescaled.
\item Hence no automorphism intertwining the flow carries $\omega_\beta$ to $\omega_{\beta'}$ for $|\beta'| \neq |\beta|$: $|\beta|$ is an invariant of the pair (state, parametrised flow), and the automorphism group acts on the line of KMS states with orbits $\{\beta, -\beta\}$, so that line carries no automorphism-theoretic structure beyond the orientation flip of (2). A condition that singles out one point of the line is therefore either a stipulation of the state itself or a reference to data outside the pair $(\Aa, \alpha)$; by (3), the value of $|\beta|$ is meaningful only relative to a parametrisation of the flow, and it is that parametrisation, the conversion of the flow parameter into geometric time, that the algebra does not supply.
\end{enumerate}

*Provenance:* Transport Lab claim singularity.rate-rigidity-temperature-not-algebra-intrinsic, version 4 (T05 Thm 7.16).

**Fact (imported) 17 (Schmidt forcing, proved outright).** Realise the boost on the doubled system by the one-parameter unitary group $e^{i t G}$, $G = N \otimes 1 - 1 \otimes N$ (the pair carries the flow of Theorem \claimref{singularity.modular-structure-completed-pair}, the partner the mirrored flow). If a unit vector $\Omega \in \C^2 \otimes \C^2$ is invariant up to phase under this group and is cyclic and separating for the pair factor, then
\[
\Omega \;=\; \cos\theta\,|00\rangle + \sin\theta\,|11\rangle \qquad \text{with } \cos\theta\sin\theta \neq 0 ,
\]
up to a global phase and a phase convention on $|11\rangle$: the Schmidt basis is the number basis.

**Fact (imported) 18 (The group of null rotations).** Let $v$ be a null vector with hyperbolic partner $w$ and $U = \{v, w\}^{\perp}$ the nondegenerate complement of the plane they span. For $u \in U$ set $R_u = 1 + vu$. Then $R_u R_{u'} = R_{u + u'}$, so
\[
N_v \;=\; \{\, R_u : u \in U \,\}
\]
is an abelian subgroup of $\Spin^0(p,q)$, and $u \mapsto R_u$ is an isomorphism of groups $(U, +) \cong N_v$. Each $R_u$ fixes $v$. Moreover the assignment is equivariant for the Levi factor: every rotor $g$ in the even subalgebra of $\Cl(U)$ fixes $v$ and satisfies $g\, R_u\, g^{-1} = R_{g u g^{-1}}$, the conjugation on the right being the vector action of $\Spin(U)$ on $U$.

*Provenance:* Transport Lab claim transport.group-null-rotations, version 4 (T01 Lem 4.6).

**Fact (imported) 19 (Totality).** Let $(\mathbb{V}, Q)$ be nondegenerate, with the completion $\Cl(p{+}1,q{+}1)$ and quadric $\mathcal{Q}$ of the section ``The completion: the conformal model''. Define $\iota : \mathcal{Q} \to \mathcal{Q}$ by $\iota[N] = [\,e_+ N e_+\,]$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\iota$ is well defined, total and continuous on $\mathcal{Q}$, and an involution.
\item For every $x$ with $Q(x) \neq 0$, \; $\iota[X(x)] = [X(x/Q(x))]$: on invertible vectors $\iota$ is the geometric reciprocal $\rho$.
\item For every $x_0$ with $Q(x_0) = 0$, that is, a null vector or the origin, $\iota[X(x_0)] = [\, x_0 + \tfrac12 \nInf \,]$, an ideal point (for $x_0 = 0$, the classical point at infinity $[\nInf]$), and this value is the limit of $\iota[X(x)]$ along every path of invertible $x \to x_0$. Consequently $\iota$ is the unique continuous map $\mathcal{Q} \to \mathcal{Q}$ agreeing with the lifted reciprocal $[X(x)] \mapsto [X(\rho(x))]$ on the lifted invertible locus.
\end{enumerate}

*Provenance:* Transport Lab claim transport.totality, version 3 (T01 Thm 4.10).
