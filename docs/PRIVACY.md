# Privacy notice: reviews

Version 1, 16 November 2026.

## Who runs the review service

The Physics Graph and its review service are run by Leon Butler, as an individual, as a
non-commercial project. He is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact:
the privacy address given on the review page.

## What is collected

- **Your ORCID iD and name**, from ORCID when you sign in.
- **ORCID's signed sign-in token** (an id_token), so that anyone can check your review is
  genuine.
- **Your review**: verdict, scope, depth, where a problem is and what it is, declared
  conflicts, and any note.
- **Evidence of qualification**, read once from your public ORCID record when you submit (a
  current employment added by an organisation, or works with DOIs) and, for each DOI, its
  subject in OpenAlex.
- **Your contact email**, which you give on the form.
- **Server logs**: IP address, time and the page requested.

The access token ORCID issues at sign-in is discarded at once and never stored.

## Why, and on what basis

The legal basis is **legitimate interests**: a public, verifiable record of who checked which
result, which is the purpose of the service. You choose to review under your own name, the data
is limited to what ORCID already shows publicly plus what you write, and you are told this
before you sign in. Your contact email is used, on the same basis, only for reminders and
notices about your own reviews and objections.

## Who can see it

- **The public** sees your name, iD, review and qualification evidence, on the Physics Graph
  and in the open review log.
- **GitHub** (United States) hosts the Physics Graph and the open review log.
- **The hosting and email providers** of the review service process your data on its behalf.
- **ORCID** handles your sign-in. **OpenAlex** receives only the DOIs of your works, never
  anything from the review form.

Your contact email and the server logs are never published or shared.

## Transfers outside the UK

GitHub, and any provider outside the UK, receive data under the UK's arrangements for
international transfers (the UK extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the
provider is certified, or the UK's international data transfer addendum).

## How long it is kept

- Your review, name, iD, token and qualification evidence: for as long as the record exists,
  unless you ask for your identity to be removed.
- Your contact email: deleted 30 days after your last open review or objection ends.
- Server logs: 30 days.

## Your rights

- **Withdraw a review** at any time from its result page; the withdrawal is shown beside it.
- **Remove your identity**: ask, and within one month your name, iD, token and qualification
  evidence are removed from the open review log and the site. The review stays as "a review by
  a reviewer whose identity was removed on request" and no longer counts. Its text is removed
  too if you ask or if it identifies you. Copies outside the project's control (forks, mirrors)
  are named in the reply. Being named on a Zenodo release is optional and cannot be undone once
  a release is published.
- **Access** everything held about you, including your contact email.
- **Correct** your name at ORCID. A review is never edited: withdraw it and submit a new one.
- **Ask for a person to look at** the automated qualification decision.
- **Complain** to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Cookies

The only cookie is a session cookie used to complete the ORCID sign-in. It is strictly
necessary, and there is no analytics or tracking on the review pages.
