# Reviewer terms

Version 1, 16 November 2026. By submitting a review you accept these terms. The version you
accept is part of your signed record, so ORCID's signature also seals which terms you agreed to.

1. **Your review is published.** Your name, ORCID iD, review, and the evidence used to decide
   whether it counts appear publicly on the Physics Graph and in its open review log.
2. **It is lasting, with two exceptions.** You can withdraw it, and the withdrawal is shown
   beside it. You can ask for your identity to be removed, as the privacy notice explains.
3. **Licence.** You license your review text under CC BY 4.0, the licence of the Physics
   Graph's content.
4. **Honesty.** The review is your own; you have declared any conflict of interest; its scope
   and depth say truthfully what you checked.
5. **About the work.** Reviews concern results, not people. Abusive, defamatory or unlawful
   text is removed from public view; the record keeps its hash, the date and the reason, so the
   removal is visible.
6. **Display.** The maintainer may decline to show a review on a result page, with a published
   reason. It stays in the open review log.
7. **Email.** Your contact email is used only for reminders and notices about your own
   reviews, and is never published or shared.
8. **Zenodo credit** is optional, and permanent once a release is published.
9. **ORCID** is used to confirm your identity. It is not affiliated with the Physics Graph and
   does not endorse reviews.
10. **General.** No payment; the service is provided as is; these terms may change, but each
    review stays under the version it was submitted under; the law of England and Wales
    applies.
