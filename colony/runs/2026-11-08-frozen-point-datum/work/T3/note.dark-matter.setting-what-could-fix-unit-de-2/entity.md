## formal
By Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-frozen-point-de-sitter-radius}, our world with $\Lambda = 3H^{2}$, $H > 0$, is de Sitter space of radius $\ell = 1/H$ at the level of the frozen point, and Euclidean de Sitter space $\dS_{4}$ is $\Sph^{4}$ of radius $\ell$, the boundary of $\B^{5}$ of radius $\ell$. The towers share one gravity, and $\Lambda$ is a parameter of the laws, so every tower has the same $\Lambda$ and its own Euclidean de Sitter sphere $\Sph^{n(r)}$ of the same radius $\ell$; the nesting of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.nesting}(iii) is the nesting of these spheres by equators. So the objects of the rule's sphere form (Remark \claimref{note.dark-matter.equivalent-forms-assignment-not}), and the common unit R2 needs, are already in the series, with the unit the de Sitter radius rather than a microscopic spacing.
