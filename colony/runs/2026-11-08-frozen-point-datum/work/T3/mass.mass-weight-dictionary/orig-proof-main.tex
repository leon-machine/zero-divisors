Pair $I = (1, 0, \rho_{I})$ with $D\sigma$ in the metric \eqref{eq:tractormetric}. The middle slots contribute nothing since $I$ has $\mu = 0$, leaving the two hyperbolic cross terms:
\[
I \cdot D\sigma = 1 \cdot \bigl(-(\Box\sigma - wJ\sigma)\bigr) + \rho_{I} \cdot w(n+2w-2)\,\sigma .
\]
Substituting $\rho_{I} = J/n$ from Fact \claimref{mass.imported-frozen-point-tractor-connection} and collecting the $\sigma$-terms:
\[
I \cdot D\sigma = -\Box\sigma + wJ\sigma + \frac{J}{n}\,w(n+2w-2)\,\sigma
= -\Box\sigma + \frac{wJ}{n}\bigl[n + (n+2w-2)\bigr]\sigma
= -\Box\sigma + \frac{2wJ}{n}(n+w-1)\,\sigma .
\]
Setting this to zero gives $\Box\sigma = \tfrac{2wJ}{n}(n+w-1)\,\sigma$, that is $(\Box + m^{2})\sigma = 0$ with
\[
m^{2} = -\frac{2J}{n}\,w(n+w-1) = -\,w(w+n-1)\,\ip{I}{I},
\]
the last step by $\ip{I}{I} = 2J/n$ (Fact \claimref{mass.imported-frozen-point-tractor-connection}). In $n = 4$ with $J = 2\Lambda/3$: $m^{2} = -w(w+3)\Lambda/3$. The script forms the pairing slot by slot from the two Facts, with $\sigma$ and $\Box\sigma$ as formal coordinates, and reads $m^{2}$ off the resulting Klein-Gordon equation, on a rational grid in $(w, n, J)$ and as an identity of rational functions in a formal $\Lambda$; as a consequence, the opposite sign fails the same pairing wherever $m^{2} \neq 0$, which the script also records.
