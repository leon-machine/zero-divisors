(3) Let $\begin{psmallmatrix} x \\ y \end{psmallmatrix}$ be an eigenvector with eigenvalue $\mu$: $\mD y = \mu x$ and $\mD^{\mathsf{T}}x + M_{R}y = \mu y$. For $\mu$ of order $\|\mD\|^{2}/\|M_{R}\|$ the second equation gives $y = -(M_{R} - \mu)^{-1}\mD^{\mathsf{T}}x = -M_{R}^{-1}\mD^{\mathsf{T}}x + O(\mu\|M_{R}\|^{-2})$, and substituting, $\mu x = -\mD M_{R}^{-1}\mD^{\mathsf{T}}x$ to leading order; the light block is $\mD\,(-M_{R}^{-1})\,\mD^{\mathsf{T}}$, a congruence of a symmetric matrix, hence symmetric. The script assembles the block matrix and checks the exact elimination identity $\begin{psmallmatrix} 0 & \mD \\ \mD^{\mathsf{T}} & M_{R}\end{psmallmatrix}\begin{psmallmatrix} x \\ -M_{R}^{-1}\mD^{\mathsf{T}}x\end{psmallmatrix} = \begin{psmallmatrix} -\mD M_{R}^{-1}\mD^{\mathsf{T}}x \\ 0\end{psmallmatrix}$ on rational instances; in the scalar case the eigenvalues of $\begin{psmallmatrix} 0 & m_{D} \\ m_{D} & M_{R}\end{psmallmatrix}$ are $\tfrac12(M_{R} \pm \sqrt{M_{R}^{2} + 4m_{D}^{2}})$, and the script certifies by exact square-root brackets that the small one lies within $m_{D}^{4}/M_{R}^{3}$ of $-m_{D}^{2}/M_{R}$.
