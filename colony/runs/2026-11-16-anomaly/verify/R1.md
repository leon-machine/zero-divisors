## 1. synthesis.colour-angle-fixed-by-no-stored-conjugation-and-relaxed-by-no-stored-field

**Verdict: OVERSTATED.** What is proved (the true statement):
- (a) Theta is fixed by the stored C in both stored field-action conventions, given that C acts on the colour index by a Lie-algebra automorphism or anti-automorphism. The store does not state that: the premise leaves the gauge sector premised.
- (b) Theta is reversed by real-linear flag conjugates that preserve the four-space and reverse its orientation. Among maps acting by the outermorphism of a vector isometry, the only ones that reverse Theta are those with det = -1.
- (c) There is an even-algebra isomorphism to the mirror signature that carries Theta with its sign. Another one carries it with the opposite sign.
- (d) On the covariant coupling with Y and Y-tilde both invertible, no phase symmetry of the quarks and the two rung doublets has a colour coefficient.
- (e) The rung field's potential has no shift symmetry that survives the Yukawa terms.

"No stored field relaxes the angle" is argued: it covers the rung field's eight components, and the list of fields is not proved complete. The entity's own Reading says this, but the title, the one-liner and the lay text state it without qualification under status proved.

**Recomputed:**
- `r1_theta_ga.py`, in Cl(2,4) with spacetime on e0, e2, e3, e4:
  - Theta(C F) = Theta(F), and phi(<FF>)_4 = <FF>_4 in the whole-integrand convention too. So Theta is invariant in both conventions of clause (5), unlike the frozen-point integrand, which changes sign between them (I reproduced -8 against +8).
  - The reflection in e1, e3, e5 reverses Theta.
  - Duality F -> I_4 F reverses Theta.
  - sigma o Ad(gamma_0) is a multiplicative isomorphism Cl+(1,3) -> Cl+(3,1) with I_4 -> -I'_4, and it carries Theta with the opposite sign.
- `r1_phase.py`: my own kernel of the invariance conditions. I computed:
  - a generic complex-symmetric invertible pair: a3 = 0;
  - Y = 1 with Y-tilde = E12 + E23: two generators, a3 = -+3 with q1 - q2 = +-1, matching -(2j - n)(q1 - q2) at j = 3.
- Exhaustive tests of clause (v) found no violation:
  - 704 symmetric 2 x 2 pairs over {0, 1, i} with p not identically zero (56 with a3 != 0);
  - 27 diagonal 3 x 3 pairs.
- Perturbations of the check (all caught):
  - a non-monomial counter-model;
  - the unsigned signature map;
  - slot charge -2 changed to -1;
  - a singular Y-tilde in the "invertible" check.

**A. Sign statements.**
- The stored premise (premise-cpt-covariance v4) defines phi as grade involution, reversal and the flag conjugate, and by its convention acts on fields as C = Ad(I_6) o kappa. C fixes every even element of Cl(2,4), so it fixes F and I_4. Invariance of Theta holds as stored, and robustly (both conventions).
- One step is not stored. "C acts on the colour index by an automorphism of su(3)" is an added hypothesis: the premise and clause (5) of the CPT theorem leave "the imported gauge sector" premised. It is harmless, because any automorphism or anti-automorphism preserves the trace form, but it must be stated as a hypothesis, not read off the stored entity.
- "Only orientation-reversing maps reverse it" is a theorem only within outermorphisms of isometries (formal (i) says this correctly). As written in the one-liner and technical text it is false. Counterexample: the duality map F -> I_4 F (the Hodge star) reverses Theta and is induced by no vector map. It is not a symmetry of the Yang-Mills term, so the conclusion survives, but the sentence must be restricted.
- "Fixed by no stored conjugation" is a proof over a named list, not a search: C, real-linear flag conjugates, and the signature reversal via sigma, plus the automorphism lemma. The list is incomplete. It omits:
  - c -> c* (lift-locus (5));
  - left-right parity D = gamma_5 gamma_9 (standard-model.left-right-parity-what-breaks-it);
  - omega (order-four entity);
  - the set/clear exchange (flag-conjugate-antilinear...);
  - the tower-top identification x_i -> y_i e_1 (synthesis.tower-top-pair-forgets-reversal);
  - the rung-boost-at-imaginary-rapidity Ad(e^{phi I_4 b}), which moves spacetime vectors (linear-rung-field (1)).

  All of these act on colour by automorphisms, or commute with spacetime bivectors, so they fix Theta. The conclusion stands, but the list should be named and these added.
- Mirror sign. (iii) proves that one isomorphism, sigma, carries I_4 to I'_4. The store's copy theorem fixes no identification, and sigma o Ad(gamma_0) is an equally good isomorphism that reverses the sign. So "carried to the mirror with its sign" and "the common ceiling premise equates the two towers' angles" hold only up to a sign set by a choice of identification that the store does not make.

**B. Colour coefficient.**
- The trace argument of (iv) is correct; I redid it by hand. Anti-Hermiticity of K_Phi gives k21 + conj(k12) = 0, and the two traced equations give S = -n I_4 (q1 - q2), then its negative.
- The monomial argument of (v) is correct, and my exhaustive search found no exception.
- Counterexample attempt. With the physical vacuum on the lift locus (alpha beta = -H^2), Y = 1 and Y-tilde = E12 + E23 give:
  - all six quark masses nonzero (det M_u = 1/1000, det M_d = -1000 in my units);
  - [H_u, H_d] != 0, so nontrivial mixing;
  - a phase symmetry with a3 = 3.

  The masses are suppressed by powers of H (product |c|^2 H^{2n}, as (v) says), so the cone condition is the physically right one. But the lay sentence "provided no quark is massless" should read "provided both family matrices are invertible (no quark massless at the cone's family point)".
- Other scalars. Extra stored fields can only add invariance conditions, so they cannot restore a colour coefficient. There are two exceptions the proof does not exclude:
  - a further field with the doublets' gauge charges, which enlarges U(2) and breaks the trace argument;
  - further coloured fermions (KSVZ-type).

  The order-four entity's omega and the 210 vev are internal or gauge and carry no colour coefficient: omega shifts the angle by 2 pi per family, consistent with omega lying in Spin(10).
- So "relaxed by no stored field" is proved for the rung coupling and argued for the store.

**C. Scalar sector.** Verified against linear-rung-field v5 (2):
- With k4 to k9 = 0, the axial phase composed with hypercharge fixes alpha = 0, beta = (-lambda, 0) and rotates alpha_1. So the normal pair is one complex field with m_H = m_A (r = 0).

- "W(N) unconstrained" is correct: nothing in (iv) to (viii) involves W.
- The 10^-13 estimate assumes |t| ~ m_t/m_b. For complex-symmetric family matrices I find that plausible (|t| ~ 40 to 50). It is an order-of-magnitude estimate, and the proposal labels it so.

**D. Flag and stage phase.**
- "The flag is not the only CP-odd datum" is a correct reading of the store: the stored Cabibbo result's phi is a free parameter of the family terms that C (adjoint reading) does not move. It contradicts the open problem's "why" text, not a stored claim.
- Proposals 1 and 2 are complementary: 1 leaves the determinant phase undone, and 2 does it. They agree on the entrywise reading (Theta -> Theta*).
- Store-level tension, not a defect of either proposal: the rung entities read phases in span{1, I_4}, the Fock-module unit of the premises is I_7, and C fixes I_4. So C is linear with respect to the unit in which the mass phase is read.

**Literature.**

- Abel et al. 2020 and Bhattacharya et al. 2021: the proposal says it checked them; I did not recheck them.

**Transport.** Transport-absent, and the proposal says so.

**Exact fixes.**
