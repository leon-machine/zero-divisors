## The choice
Three problems under one idea: the anomaly of a charge of the sixteen, that is, the pairing of an abelian or
discrete charge with the pseudoscalar part of a field square (already stored for the triangle), and its global
form on a closed five-manifold.

- op.strong-cp: the colour pairing. Which stored charges and fields shift the angle, whether the determinant
  phase is fixed, whether anything relaxes it.
- op.matter-asymmetry-origin: the weak pairing. The anomalous weak process as a selection rule from the stored
  mixed weak coefficient, the equilibrium coefficient c, the conditional inheritance theorem, and the relaxing
  angle shared with op.strong-cp.
- op.order-four-anomaly-and-sixteen: the global form. The fixed-point count of the pseudoscalar on real
  projective five-space.

op.graviton-rate-as-classical-field-process was the fourth candidate: equally tractable, but it shares nothing
with the other three.
