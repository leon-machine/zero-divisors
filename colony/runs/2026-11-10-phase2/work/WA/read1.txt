===== synthesis.clausius-is-window-first-law-plus-boost-charge-conservation ['version: 3', 'status: proved']

---

## formal
Units $\hbar = c = 1$. Let $F$ be a window of the boundary-mode algebra with its boost dynamics (Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.boundary-mode-algebra-windows-boost-dynamics}), $H_{F} = \sum_{j \in F}\omega_{j}N_{j}$ in the time of a boost field $\chi$ of surface gravity $\kappa$, $\beta = 2\pi/\kappa$, and $\rho_{I}$ the restriction to $F$ of the state of Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state}, the 
Gibbs state of $\beta H_{F}$ (the state that the temperature clause (R$_{\mathrm{loc}}$) of the local-horizon premise prescribes for a patch's window before matter crosses). Let the matter be a second-quantised field over a finite-dimensional one-particle space $\mathfrak{h}_{m}$ (finitely many matter modes crossing the patch) with self-adjoint one-particle boost generator $k$, so that its boost 
charge is $d\Gamma(k)$, the $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$ part of $\Psi^{*}k\Psi$ (Theorem \claimref{field-theory.second-quantisation}(3)). A \emph{process} is a unitary $U$ on the joint Fock space applied to $\rho_{I} \otimes \rho_{m}$, $\rho_{m}$ any matter state; write $\rho'$ for the result, $\rho_{F}'$ and $\rho_{m}'$ for its marginals, $\delta Q := \Tr(\rho_{m}\,d\Gamma(k)) - 
\Tr(\rho_{m}'\,d\Gamma(k))$ for the boost energy the matter loses, $\Delta S_{F} := S(\rho_{F}') - S(\rho_{I})$, and $S(\cdot\,\|\,\cdot)$ for relative entropy. Consider the hypothesis
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(J)] \emph{(Boost-charge conservation.)} $U$ commutes with $H_{F}\otimes 1 + 1\otimes d\Gamma(k)$.
\end{enumerate}
Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{(Conservation is a one-particle condition.)} $[d\Gamma(a), d\Gamma(b)] = d\Gamma([a, b])$, so a quadratic coupling $d\Gamma(v)$ on $\mathfrak{h}_{F} \oplus \mathfrak{h}_{m}$, with $H_{F} = d\Gamma(w)$, conserves the total boost charge if and only if $[v, w \oplus k] = 0$: it couples only modes of equal boost weight.
\item \emph{(Clausius from the first law.)} Under (J), $\Delta S_{F} = \beta\,\delta Q - S(\rho_{F}'\,\|\,\rho_{I})$ exactly. The defect is nonnegative, vanishes only for $\rho_{F}' = \rho_{I}$, and is of second order in $\rho_{F}' - \rho_{I}$. Hence $\Delta S_{F} = \delta Q/T_{\chi}$, $T_{\chi} = \kappa/2\pi$, at first order in the change of the window's state, and $\Delta S_{F} \le \delta 
Q/T_{\chi}$ always. (This is the standard first-law identity of a Gibbs state, combined with (J).)
\item \emph{(The matter's modular Hamiltonian reading.)} For every process, with or without (J), $\Delta S_{F} = \beta\,\delta Q + I(F{:}m)_{\rho'} + S(\rho_{m}'\,\|\,\sigma_{m}) - S(\rho_{m}\,\|\,\sigma_{m})$, where $\sigma_{m}$ is the Gibbs state of $\beta\,d\Gamma(k)$, whose modular Hamiltonian is $\beta\,d\Gamma(k)$ up to a constant (the boost-KMS state of the matter on the patch, when it 
exists), and $I(F{:}m)_{\rho'}$ is the mutual information created. Under (J) the defects balance: $S(\rho_{F}'\,\|\,\rho_{I}) + I(F{:}m)_{\rho'} = S(\rho_{m}\,\|\,\sigma_{m}) - S(\rho_{m}'\,\|\,\sigma_{m})$. (This is the standard entropy-production identity of a system coupled unitarily to a thermal state, with the window and the matter in the roles stated.)
\item \emph{(Without (J) the relation fails.)} For a window mode of weight $\omega_{F}$ coupled by number-conserving hopping to a matter mode of weight $\omega_{m} \neq \omega_{F}$, the first-order coefficients of $\Delta S_{F}$ and $\beta\,\delta Q$ are $\beta\omega_{F}\,\delta n$ and $\beta\omega_{m}\,\delta n$, $\delta n \neq 0$ the occupation transferred: the window's first law and its state 
hold, and (H1) fails. So (J) does not follow from the window's state and its first law.
\item \emph{(Zero weight.)} If every mode of $F$ has zero weight (a limit outside the windows of the Definition, whose weights are positive, admitted here because one realisation of the registered residue sits there), the case in which each counted unit is one mode carrying $\ln 2$ and the residue takes its registered value (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.residue-from-mode-entropy}; under the weight 
clause of version 3 of Premise (Q), $\omega_{k} = \kappa(k + \tfrac12)$, the registered value is also realised by two arrays per counted unit at nonzero weight, to which this clause does not apply), then $H_{F} = 0$, under (J) $\delta Q = 0$ for every process, and $\Delta S_{F} = -S(\rho_{F}'\,\|\,\rho_{I}) \le 0$: a fixed window of zero-weight modes absorbs no boost energy and its entropy can 
only fall. On that reading a nonzero heat can raise the horizon entropy only by changing the window's mode set, not through the first law of a fixed window.
\item \emph{(The boost charge on a generator.)} On a horizon generator with affine parameter $v$ and $\chi = \kappa v\,\partial_{v}$, a chiral mode $\psi(v)$ supported in the patch, with $T_{vv} = \tfrac{I_{4}}{2}(\bar\psi\psi' - \bar\psi'\psi)$ (the mode space's complex unit is the field theory's unit $I_{4}$, numbers read in $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$), has $\int \chi^{v}T_{vv}\,dv = 
\kappa\ip{\psi}{I_{4}(v\partial_{v} + \tfrac12)\psi}$: the boost flux through the generator is the expectation of the one-particle generator of dilations of $v$, whose second quantisation is the boost charge $d\Gamma(k)$ above.
\item \emph{(The continuum carrier: (J) as an identity; the first law there given the imported Fact.)} Let the patch's modes on a generator be the continuum mode space (Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.continuum-mode-space}) over $L^{2}(\R_{+}, du)\otimes K$, the half of the generator beyond the edge with affine parameter $u$ and $\chi = \kappa u\,\partial_{u}$, the boost acting as $\psi(u) 
\mapsto e^{\kappa t/2}\psi(e^{\kappa t}u)$ with one-particle generator $h = \kappa\,I_{4}(u\partial_{u} + \tfrac12)$ (eigenvalue $-\kappa w$ on $u^{-1/2 + I_{4}w}$), in the continuum I-state at $\beta$ (Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state-continuum}). If the patch's modes and the matter crossing the patch are one field on these generators, then by the polarised form of (6) the window's boost 
Hamiltonian and the matter's boost charge are one quadratic form, $d\Gamma(h) = \int\chi^{u}T_{uu}\,du$, on modes vanishing at the edge, so (J) holds identically (proved). That the first law (2) then holds for that field's entropy differences on the continuum follows given the imported Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-null-plane-modular-hamiltonian-first-law}: $h$ has purely continuous spectrum, 
so the continuum carrier has no Gibbs windows of the Definition (and a point-spectrum discretisation replaces $h$ by an operator whose second quantisation is no longer the boost flux), and the statement is Araki's relative-entropy form with the null-plane modular Hamiltonian $\beta\,d\Gamma(h) = 2\pi\int u\,T_{uu}\,du$, which by that Fact gives $\Delta S_{F} = \beta\,\delta Q - 
S(\rho_{F}'\,\|\,\rho_{I})$ for entropy differences, hence (2), on the type III algebra of the half-generator. This is the series' form of the known statement that the null-plane modular Hamiltonian is the boost charge of the field on the horizon. On modes shared by both sheets of an edge the temperature clause for both sheets forces zero weight, and then (5) applies instead.
\end{enumerate}
\emph{Consequence for the Einstein derivation.} If the patch's window is in the state above before matter crosses, (J) holds, the window's state changes at the order of the heat (so the defect of (2) is of order $\delta Q^{2}$, that is $\lambda_{0}^{4}$ in the patch of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.einstein-equations-from-c-loc-and-clausius}, two orders beyond the balance), and the window's entropy 
change is the horizon's, $\eta\,\delta A$, then the Clausius hypothesis (H1) of that theorem holds at the order it is used, with $\delta Q$ the boost flux of the matter. The temperature clause is used here as a condition on the window's state before the crossing: by (2) and Klein's inequality the window's state after a process with $\delta Q \neq 0$ is not $\rho_{I}$, so the clause read as a 
condition at every instant on a fixed window is incompatible with (2).

gravitational field ($\hb = 1$, $\Omega = 0$; Definition \claimref{obj.synthesis.gtg-flat-spacetime-gauge-fields}). Let $\xi$ be its boost (the Killing flow in (a), (b); the local boost in (c), with time measured in rapidity) and $\kappa$ its surface gravity: $\kappa = 1/(4M)$, $H$, $1$. On each generator let $u$ be the affine parameter with $u = 0$ on the bifurcation (edge) surface, defined on 
the whole generator $u \in \R$ of the extended solution, and normalised so that $\xi = \kappa u\,\partial_{u}$ on $\mathcal{H}$. Let $\mathfrak{h} = L^{2}(\R, du)\otimes K$ ($K$ the transverse and internal labels) and $\CAR(\mathfrak{h})$ the continuum mode space of Definition \claimref{obj.hawking.continuum-mode-space}, generated by the fields $\psi(u)$ with $\{\psi(u), \psi^{*}(v)\} = \delta(u 
- v)\otimes\id_{K}$, carrying the null translations $\nu_{a}(\psi(u)) = \psi(u + a)$ and the boost $\beta_{t}(\psi(u)) = e^{\kappa t/2}\psi(e^{\kappa t}u)$ (the weight $\tfrac12$ is the one for which $\beta_{t}$ preserves the anticommutator). Hypothesis (N$_{+}$), Premise \claimref{synthesis.n-plus-null-ground-state}: the state $\omega$ is gauge-invariant and quasi-free (assumed, not derived), 
invariant under $\nu$, and the generator of $\nu$ in its GNS representation is positive.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{(The null vacuum.)} Under (N$_{+}$) the symbol of $\omega$ is $\chi_{(-\infty,0)}(P)\otimes\id_{K}$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.null-vacuum-on-a-horizon-generator}(ii)), where $P = I_{4}\,d/du$ is the one-particle generator of $\nu$, $\nu_{a}(a(f)) = a(e^{I_{4}aP}f)$ (the unit of $\mathfrak{h}$ is $I_{4}$; numbers are read in $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{4}\}$): 
$\omega(\psi^{*}(v)\psi(u)) = \dfrac{I_{4}}{2\pi}\dfrac{1}{u - v + I_{4}0}\otimes\id_{K}$. No other gauge-invariant quasi-free state satisfies (N$_{+}$).
\item[(ii)] \emph{(The factor $2\pi$.)} The subalgebra $\CAR(L^{2}(\R_{+}, du)\otimes K)$ of the modes beyond the edge is $\beta$-invariant, and the restriction of $\omega$ to it is the continuum I-state of Theorem \claimref{hawking.i-state-continuum} for the one-particle boost generator $h$ at $\beta = 2\pi/\kappa$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.null-vacuum-on-a-horizon-generator}(iii)): on 
$u^{-1/2 + I_{4}w}$, $h$ acts as $-\kappa w$ and the symbol is $(1 + e^{-2\pi w})^{-1} = (1 + e^{\beta h})^{-1}$. It is jointly KMS on the whole subalgebra and even, and it is the unique $\beta$-KMS state there. The same holds on the past half $\CAR(L^{2}(\R_{-}, du)\otimes K)$ for the boost that is future-directed there, $\beta_{-t}$, with symbol $(1 + e^{2\pi w})^{-1}$ on $|u|^{-1/2 + I_{4}w}$; 
the reflection $u \mapsto -u$ exchanges the two statements. Positivity fixes the sign (the opposite sign gives $-2\pi/\kappa$), and the one-sided support is needed (on the full generator the boost-continued two-point function has a pole at imaginary rapidity $\pi$, and the restriction of $\omega$ there is pure); the period $2\pi$ is the first return of the rotated half-generator 
$e^{I_{4}\theta}\R_{+}$ to itself.
\item[(iii)] \emph{(The continuum restatements follow.)} Hence: on (a), the modes of the generators are jointly KMS for the Killing flow at $\beta = 8\pi M$, Fermi-Dirac with density $(1 + e^{2\pi\varpi/\kappa})^{-1}$ in Killing frequency $\varpi$: the continuum restatement of (R) (Premise \claimref{singularity.r-regularity-fixed-locus}) for the eternal hole, (N$_{+}$) being there a 
Hartle-Hawking-type regularity condition on the whole Kruskal generator; a hole formed by collapse is not covered (its horizon generators have no $u < 0$ half, and its physical state is not a ground state for $\nu$ on the horizon). On (b), the same at $\beta = 2\pi/H$: the continuum restatement of (R$_{\Lambda}$) (Premise \claimref{synthesis.r-lambda-regularity-cosmological-horizon}). On (c), on 
the past half of the generators, which is the half the premise's patch uses, joint KMS for the future-directed local boost at rapidity period $2\pi$ on the patch's whole mode algebra: the continuum restatement of (R$_{\mathrm{loc}}$). In each case the state is jointly KMS and even: the joint KMS part of (Q) (Premise \claimref{singularity.q-joint-quasi-freeness-s-wave}); its quasi-freeness is a 
hypothesis of (N$_{+}$), and its weight clause ($\omega_{k} = \kappa(k + \tfrac12)$ in version 3 of (Q), $\kappa k$ in version 2) is not given.
\item[(iv)] \emph{(Only on the continuum.)} (N$_{+}$) needs a nontrivial strongly continuous translation group dilated by the boost at rate $\kappa$; by Theorem \claimref{synthesis.point-spectrum-boost-admits-no-half-sided-translations} there is none when the boost acts by phases on a generating set of modes, so (N$_{+}$) cannot be posed on the windows, the countable mode space or the array of 
(Q). The stored wordings of (R), (R$_{\Lambda}$) and (R$_{\mathrm{loc}}$), on point-spectrum mode sets with phase-acting flows, are discretisations of the statements in (iii): the boost has no eigenvectors on the continuum, a normalisable mode $f$ has occupation $\int|\hat f(w)|^{2}m(w)\,dw$, and the ladder values $m(w_{j})$ are recovered only for modes of vanishing Mellin width.
\item[(v)] \emph{(Positivity from the spectrum condition.)} If a representation of the spacetime translations has spectrum in the closed forward cone, every future null translation has a positive generator: $\ip{\ell}{p} \ge 0$ for $p$ in the closed forward cone and $\ell$ future null, with equality only on the ray of $\ell$. In the conformal model $\Cl(2,4)$ the null translation $1 + 
\tfrac{\alpha}{2}\ell\nInf$ along a generator, the boost and the dilation (both unit boosts) satisfy the $ax{+}b$ relation of the Borchers form of Fact \claimref{angle.imported-theorems-deliver-their-hypotheses}, the boost and the dilation agreeing on the generator and differing off it.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Beyond one flat chart.} The whole generator of the eternal horizon (case (a)) and of the de Sitter cosmological horizon (case (b)), through its bifurcation surface, is global and is not carried by a single gravitational field $(\hb, \Omega)$ on flat spacetime; the statements above that hold on the invertible region are in GTG, and the whole generator, through its bifurcation surface, is 
read on the manifold assembled from the derived metric's charts.

## formal
\emph{Premise (N$_{+}$).} Units $\hbar = c = 1$. Let $\mathcal{H}$ be one of: (a) the future event horizon of the eternal (Kruskal-extended) Schwarzschild solution; (b) the future cosmological horizon of the static patch of de Sitter space; (c) a local Rindler horizon $\mathcal{H}_{p,\ell}$ of Premise \claimref{synthesis.c-loc-local-horizon-premise} in Minkowski space, flat spacetime with 
vanishing gravitational field ($\hb = 1$, $\Omega = 0$; Definition \claimref{obj.synthesis.gtg-flat-spacetime-gauge-fields}). On each generator of $\mathcal{H}$ let $u$ be the affine parameter, with $u = 0$ on the bifurcation (edge) surface and $u \in \R$ the whole generator of the extended solution. The modes of $\mathcal{H}$ are the continuum mode space of Definition 
\claimref{obj.hawking.continuum-mode-space}, one carrier per light ray: $\CAR(L^{2}(\R, du)\otimes K)$, $K$ the transverse and internal labels, generated by fields $\psi(u)$ with $\{\psi(u), \psi^{*}(v)\} = \delta(u - v)\otimes\id_{K}$, carrying the affine null translations $\nu_{a}(\psi(u)) = \psi(u + a)$, which act on the fields by parallel transport along the generator, the zero flat 
connection on $K$: no internal rotation accompanies the translation. Then the state $\omega$ of these modes is gauge-invariant and quasi-free, it is invariant under $\nu$, and the generator of $\nu$ in its GNS representation is positive: $\omega$ is a ground state for the affine null translations along the generators. It contains no temperature, period or Euclidean section.

\emph{The two data the premise supplies.} (1) The lift. The clause names the lift of the affine translations to the fields, by parallel transport. This excludes the translation-invariant Fermi seas $\chi_{(-\infty, 0)}(P - \mu)$, $\mu \ne 0$ a constant Hermitian operator on $K$ (the cited Theorem's $\kappa$; here $\kappa$ is the surface gravity): they are local gauge transforms of the null 
vacuum, with its local algebras and modular objects, locally normal to it and generator-Hadamard, and they are ground states only of the lift with flat connection $\mu\,du$, not of $\nu$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.modular-data-on-a-generator-fix-neither-the-translation-lift-nor-the-sign-of-i}(ii), (iv)). (2) The orientation of the unit $I_{4}$. The positivity clause refers to the imaginary unit 
of the field algebra, $I_{4}$: the filled positive-energy sea $\chi_{(0, \infty)}(P)$, the time reverse of the null vacuum and the image of it under complex conjugation of the Majorana fields, has the same local algebras and the same net-level modular data and is a ground state only for the reversed translations (Theorem 
\claimref{synthesis.modular-data-on-a-generator-fix-neither-the-translation-lift-nor-the-sign-of-i}(iii), (iv)). Which of the two is the state of the light rays, the orientation of the imaginary unit relative to the time orientation, is a datum that (N$_{+}$) assumes; the series does not fix it.

\emph{Local form.} On a local Rindler horizon $\mathcal{H}_{p,\ell}$ of a gravitational field $(\hb, \Omega)$ on flat spacetime with nonvanishing field strength, the premise is read at leading order in the size of the patch: in a gauge with $\hb(x_0) = 1$ and $\Omega(x_0) = 0$ at the point $x_0$ of $p$ (the reading of a normal neighbourhood), the state of the modes on the generators is a ground 
state for the null translations of the background along $\ell$, a physically null direction at $x_0$. That this is the leading-order form of (c) is argued, not derived.

\emph{Scope.} In case (a) the premise is a Hartle-Hawking-type regularity condition on the whole Kruskal generator: it describes the eternal hole in equilibrium. A hole formed by collapse is not covered by case (a): its horizon generators have no $u < 0$ half, and its physical state is not a ground state for $\nu$ on the horizon. It is covered by the premise's local form read on the other sheet, 
given the reduction of the outgoing sector to one chiral CAR field per light ray (the postulate J3, Premise \claimref{hawking.joint-j3-s-wave-reduction}): posed in its short-distance form (on a generator, the ground-state singularity plus a remainder bounded near the point, a class the same in every regular parameter for the weight-$\tfrac12$ kernel) on the ingoing sheet through each point of the 
future horizon, whose generator has both halves, it gives the late-time state of the near-horizon exterior outgoing modes as the continuum I-state at $\beta = 2\pi/\kappa$; the condition is sufficient, and states outside the class can have the same limit (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.collapsing-hole-flux-from-ground-state-on-the-ingoing-sheet}). In the collapse of a null shell the vacuum before 
the collapse is in that class, and among states KMS for the Killing translations at late times finite null energy across the future horizon holds only at $\kappa/2\pi$ (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.collapse-in-vacuum-regular-at-future-horizon-selects-hawking-temperature}). Hawking's flux at infinity follows from that late-time state under Premise 
\claimref{hawking.premise-i-interface-identification} with the greybody passage; the greybody factors themselves and collapse geometries other than the spherical null shell are not covered. Not part of this premise: the weight clause of Premise \claimref{singularity.q-joint-quasi-freeness-s-wave}, $\omega_{k} = \kappa(k + \tfrac12)$ in its version 3, which stays a separate hypothesis (the 
half-integer offset is read from the modes being spinor fields of boost weight $\tfrac12$ in the state (N$_{+}$) selects, a reading and not a consequence). \emph{Scope note (local normality).} Within stationary gauge-invariant quasi-free states, (N$_{+}$) is equivalent to local normality of the state to the null vacuum on the algebra of an interval of each generator containing the bifurcation 
surface (Theorem \claimref{synthesis.local-normality-at-the-edge-gives-the-null-ground-state}(iv)), the analogue for that generator algebra of the local normality principle of algebraic quantum field theory, which is stated for the algebras of spacetime regions (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-haag-local-normality}). The folium form: local normality to the null vacuum on every interval of the generator closed by its Moebius covariance, the arcs through the point at infinity included, puts a pure gauge-invariant

\emph{What it delivers.} By Theorem \claimref{synthesis.positive-null-energy-gives-r-premises}, (N$_{+}$) fixes $\omega$ as the filled sea $\chi_{(-\infty,0)}(P)$, $P = I_{4}\,d/du$, and its restriction to either side of the edge is the continuum I-state at rapidity period $2\pi$ for the boost future-directed on that side. ## formal
Assume (P) (Premise \claimref{singularity.p-univalence}) and (R) (Premise \claimref{singularity.r-regularity-fixed-locus}), so that the boundary modes of a Schwarzschild hole of mass $M$ are thermal at $T(M) = \kappa/2\pi = 1/8\pi M$ (Theorem \claimref{singularity.rate-discharge-hypothesis-iii}, Proposition \claimref{singularity.plane-rate}). Suppose, as Hawking did, that the hole is a 
thermodynamic system along the Schwarzschild family at this temperature: its internal energy is $M$ (not the boundary modes' Killing energy, which is below $TS = M/2$), its entropy $S(M)$ obeys the Clausius relation $dS = dM/T$, and $S \to 0$ as $M \to 0$. Then
\[
S(M) \;=\; \int_{0}^{M} 8\pi M'\,dM' \;=\; 4\pi M^{2} \;=\; \frac{A}{4}, \qquad A = 16\pi M^{2},
\]
the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy with its quarter, and no calibration enters. Equivalently, along the family $dM = (\kappa/8\pi)\,dA$ identically, so $dS = dA/4$. The quarter is fixed by the period of (R), $2\pi/\kappa$, and by nothing else: had (R) fixed a period $p$ in place of $2\pi/\kappa$, the entropy would be $S = (p\kappa/2\pi)\,A/4$.
