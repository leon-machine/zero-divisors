## Verdict on the three targets
Born rule: under (P), a state sharp on an atom is the scalar-part functional, so the weights of projectors
self-adjoint for the split follow. This is not a derivation of the Born rule: the non-contextuality assumption
(linearity of values across non-commuting elements) is relocated into (P), sharpness is assumed, and physical states
are not shown to be sharp. (P) is not derived from the stored measurement premises; the parity average creates no
positivity. Classical mathematics (Gleason-type, Busch, Kochen-Specker, Meyer) placed in the series' algebra.
Number N: negative, as the plan expected. The counts 3, 8, 256, 2^256 are the iterated Fock construction, not a step
of the proved ascent (one ascent from three pairs gives four pairs); a second rule gives 3, 4, 8, 128, 2^127; nothing
stored selects a rule (argued). Two exact models move N and keep every listed stored relation; "nothing stored fixes
N" is argued on a search whose limits are listed (28 of 40 postulates by symbol search only; notes not searched).
Window entropy: the count compares the array's own Gibbs entropy with A/4 and is internally consistent; the
continuum's entropy never enters. The array is the twist one-half fibre of the light-ray continuum, not a
Killing-invariant subsystem; the stored energy equalities are one identity holding only at one thermal period. The
counterfactual residue 3/(4 pi) per unit of two components is recorded as not adopted. pred.boundary-mode-density is
unchanged and its kill condition is not met.
