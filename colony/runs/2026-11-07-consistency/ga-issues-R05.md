# GA issues for the GA rewriters, from reader R05 (subject dark-matter)

Entities of R05's list that are in GA_OWNED.txt (eight). R05 edited none of them. Each item: id, issue, proposed fix. Items 1 and 7 also carry a non-language issue for Leon (repeated in consistency-R05.md).

1. `dark-matter.mirror-tower-copy-one` (proof, "Hodge star"): $(\star F)_{ab} = \tfrac12\sqrt{|g|}\,\epsilon_{abcd}g^{ce}g^{df}F_{ef}$ in tensor components. GA form: for a bivector field $F$ the dual is $F I_4$ (or $I_4 F$, equal on bivectors), $F \wedge \star F$ is $\langle F \tilde F\rangle_0$ times the volume and $F \wedge F$ is $\langle F F\rangle_4$; reversal $(p,q) \mapsto (q,p)$ sends $I_4' \mapsto +I_4$ on the even algebra (`dark-tower-matter.mirror-algebra`(ii)), which is the GA statement of "even in $g$". Also `$m^{2} = 2|\br{\pi^{1}\pi^{2}}|^{2}$` uses a modulus of a complex bracket; the store writes the twistor bracket's numbers in span{1, I_4} (or right multiplication by $I_6$ for twistor components, ruling R3): say which. Non-language (for Leon, not for the rewriters): the T14c caveat says the cold mirror is "an exception to P2 that this theorem does not state"; P2 is now version 2 (scope "labels, not states", which leaves $\xi$ free), so the caveat describes the version-1 reach.
2. `dark-matter.imported-induced-equations-hypersurface`: the bracket $\tfrac{2\kappa_5^2}{3}[T_{\mu\nu} + (T_{ab}n^an^b - \tfrac14 T)g_{\mu\nu}]$ and $G_{ab}$ in components. GA form: on physical vectors of the hypersurface, the bulk term is $\tfrac{2\kappa_5^2}{3}\bigl[P(T(P(a))) + (n\cdot T(n) - \tfrac14\partial_b\cdot T(b))\,P(a)\bigr]$ with $P$ the projection orthogonal to $n$ and $\mathcal{G}$ the five-dimensional Einstein map; source form to `extraction.source_formal`. Signature: the source's $(-,+,+,+,+)$ is kept and named; keep it named.
3. `dark-matter.imported-linear-momentum-equation-traceless-field`: $ds^2$ line element, $\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0$. The scalar perturbation equations themselves ($\theta' = \dots$, $k^2(\phi - \psi) = \dots$) are scalar mathematics and need nothing; only the conservation law ($D\cdot T(a)$ form, `\hb^T(\partial_a)\cdot D_a T`) and the line element (a labelled reading) need rewriting.
4. `dark-matter.no-coupling-rule-odd-towers-follows`: proof (a) scales "$\int F\wedge\star F$" with "the two inverse metrics in $\star$".
