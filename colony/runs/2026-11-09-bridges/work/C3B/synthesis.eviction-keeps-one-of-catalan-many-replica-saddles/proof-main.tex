\emph{(i).} At zero weight every window member is maximally mixed on its qubit, and the window is a product (\claimref{singularity.eviction-window-thermal-product-radiation-entropy}); the radiation purifies the window, so $\rho_{R}$ has the spectrum of the window's state: flat, of rank $2^{|W|}$, $|W| = \min(j, c)$, as in clause (ii) of \claimref{synthesis.eviction-keeps-one-replica-saddle}. Hence $\operatorname{tr}\rho_{R}^{n} = 2^{-(n-1)\min(j,c)}$, the larger of $2^{-(n-1)j}$ and $2^{-(n-1)c}$. In the Pauli algebra $\Cl(3,0)$ a member's marginal is $\rho = \tfrac12$ (trace $2\langle\cdot\rangle_{0}$), so $\operatorname{tr}\rho^{n} = 2^{1-n}$ and the product over $m$ members gives $2^{m(1-n)}$; the check computes these powers in GA, and also shows that a member of nonzero weight $\rho = \tfrac12(1 + r e_{3})$ has $\operatorname{tr}\rho^{2} = (1+r^{2})/2$, $\operatorname{tr}\rho^{3} = (1 + 3r^{2})/4$, so the flat-spectrum statement is special to zero weight.

\emph{(ii).} For $\sigma \in S_{n}$, $n - \#(\sigma)$ is the least number of transpositions whose product is $\sigma$, so $(n - \#(\sigma)) + (n - \#(\sigma^{-1}\eta)) \geq n - \#(\eta) = n - 1$, i.e.\ $\#(\sigma) + \#(\sigma^{-1}\eta) \leq n + 1$; equality holds exactly for the permutations on a geodesic from the identity to $\eta$ in the Cayley graph, which are the non-crossing permutations, counted by the Catalan numbers (Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-non-crossing-permutations-are-geodesic}, Biane). The check enumerates $S_{n}$ for $n \leq 7$ and finds the bound and exactly $1, 2, 5, 14, 42, 132, 429$ permutations attaining it. In the replica computation the $n$-th moment of $\rho_{R}$ is a sum over such permutations, the boundary conditions of the radiation joining the replicas by $\sigma$ and those of the black hole by $\sigma^{-1}\eta$; at $n = 2$ this is the two-term sum $1/k + Z_{2}/Z_{1}^{2}$ of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-replica-wormhole-two-saddle-purity} (identity and $\eta$), and its planar part in the microcanonical ensemble is Page's spectrum (the same Fact, Sec.\ 2.5.1). With $k = e^{S} = d$, a permutation contributes $d^{\#(\sigma) + \#(\sigma^{-1}\eta) - 2n}$, so the planar ones give $C_{n}d^{1-n}$ and the rest are smaller by at least $d^{-2}$. (Reading: that the general-$n$ replica sum has this permutation form is the standard structure of the end-of-the-world-brane model, from memory; the check uses only the combinatorics and the Haar computation (iii).)

\emph{(iii).} First the abstract tensor product. For a pure state $\psi$ drawn from the unitarily invariant measure on $\C^{D}$, $\langle \psi^{\otimes n}\psi^{*\otimes n}\rangle$ is invariant under $U^{\otimes n}$ and supported on the symmetric power, hence (Schur's lemma, the symmetric power being irreducible; Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-haar-moments-of-random-pure-state}) equal to $\sum_{\sigma}P_{\sigma}/\prod_{l<n}(D + l)$ with $P_{\sigma}$ the permutation of the tensor factors. With $D = d_{A}d_{B}$ and $\operatorname{tr}\rho_{A}^{n} = \operatorname{tr}\bigl(\psi^{\otimes n}\psi^{*\otimes n}(P^{A}_{\eta} \otimes 1_{B})\bigr)$, the trace of $P_{\sigma}(P^{A}_{\eta}\otimes 1)$ factorises as $d_{A}^{\#(\sigma^{-1}\eta)}d_{B}^{\#(\sigma)}$. At $n = 2$ this gives $d_{A}d_{B}(d_{A} + d_{B})/(d_{A}d_{B}(d_{A}d_{B} + 1))$, the value of \claimref{synthesis.eviction-keeps-one-replica-saddle}(iii), which the check confirms at five dimension pairs. At $d_{A} = d_{B} = d$ the numerator is a polynomial in $d$ of degree $n + 1$ with leading coefficient $C_{n}$ by (ii), and the denominator $\prod_{l<n}(d^{2} + l)$ is monic of degree $2n$; so $d^{n-1}\langle\operatorname{tr}\rho^{n}\rangle \to C_{n}$. Since every term is positive and the denominator exceeds $d^{2n}$, $\langle\operatorname{tr}\rho^{n}\rangle < C_{n}d^{1-n} + (n! - C_{n})d^{-n-1}$; the check verifies $d^{1-n} < \langle\operatorname{tr}\rho^{n}\rangle < C_{n}d^{1-n}$ exactly for $n = 2, 3, 4$ and $d = 2, 4, 8$ (for instance $4/5$ at $n = 2$, $d = 2$ and $7/10$ at $n = 3$, $d = 2$).

Now the even states. Let the modes be null pairs of a Clifford algebra with generators $e_{k}, f_{k}$, $Q(e_{k}) = 1$, $Q(f_{k}) = -1$, mode parity $p_{k} = f_{k}e_{k}$, $A$ and $B$ complementary sets of $c'$ modes with sub-Clifford algebras $\Cl(A)$, $\Cl(B)$, $\Gamma_{A}$, $\Gamma_{B}$ the products of their $p_{k}$ and $\Gamma = \Gamma_{A}\Gamma_{B}$. The generators of $\Cl(B)$ multiplied by $\Gamma_{A}$ keep their squares, anticommute with each other and commute with $\Cl(A)$, and generate an algebra $B'$ with $\Cl(A) \otimes B'$ the whole algebra as an ordinary tensor product (Theorem \claimref{tower.commuting-copies}, ground algebra $\Cl(B)$, the planes of $A$). Blades being orthogonal for $\langle xy\rangle_{0}$, the projection onto the blades of $\Cl(A)$ times $d$ is the partial trace of this tensor product over $B'$; so the reduction of the statement is a partial trace and $\operatorname{tr}\rho^{n} = \operatorname{tr}\bigl(\psi^{\otimes n}\psi^{*\otimes n}(P^{A}_{\eta} \otimes 1)\bigr)$ as before. (The projection onto $\Cl(B)$ agrees with the other partial trace on even elements, the two differing only on odd blades, so for an even pure state the two reductions have the same moments.) A pure even state has its vector in an eigenspace of the involution $\Gamma = \Gamma_{A} \otimes \Gamma_{B}$, a parity sector, of dimension $d^{2}/2$. The Fact applied to the sector gives $\langle \psi^{\otimes n}\psi^{*\otimes n}\rangle = \sum_{\sigma}P_{\sigma}\Pi^{\otimes n}/\prod_{l<n}(d^{2}/2 + l)$ with $\Pi = \tfrac12(1 \pm \Gamma)$, and $\Pi^{\otimes n} = 2^{-n}\sum_{U}(\pm 1)^{|U|}\Gamma_{(U)}$ over the subsets $U$ of the $n$ copies. The trace of $\Gamma_{(U)}P_{\sigma}(P^{A}_{\eta} \otimes 1)$ factorises over the cycles of $\tau = \sigma^{-1}\eta$ on the $A$ side and of $\sigma$ on the $B$ side, a cycle $z$ contributing the trace of $\Gamma_{A}^{|U \cap z|}$ or $\Gamma_{B}^{|U \cap z|}$, which is $d$ if $|U \cap z|$ is even and $0$ otherwise. The $U$ meeting every cycle of $\sigma$ and of $\tau$ evenly are the common zeros in $\mathbb{F}_{2}^{n}$ of the indicators of the cycles; a linear relation among the indicators equates a union of cycles of $\sigma$ with a union of cycles of $\tau$, a set invariant under $\sigma$, $\tau$ and so under $\eta = \sigma\tau$, hence empty or everything, so the indicators span a space of dimension $\#(\sigma) + \#(\tau) - 1$ and there are $2^{n+1-\#(\sigma)-\#(\tau)}$ such $U$, each of even size. The trace on the sector is therefore $2^{-n}2^{n+1-\#(\sigma)-\#(\tau)}d^{\#(\sigma)+\#(\tau)} = 2(d/2)^{\#(\sigma)+\#(\tau)}$, which gives the first formula. With $\#(\sigma) + \#(\tau) = n + 1 - 2g$ ($g$ is an integer since the signs of $\sigma$ and $\tau$ multiply to that of $\eta$, and $g \ge 0$ by (ii)) a term is $2(d/2)^{n+1-2g}/(d^{2}/2)^{n} = 4^{g}d^{1-n-2g}$ before the factor $\prod_{l}(1 + 2l/d^{2})^{-1}$, which gives the second formula and, with $N_{n,0} = C_{n}$ from (ii), the limit. At $n = 2$: $N_{2,0} = 2$, so the value is $2d^{-1}/(1 + 2d^{-2}) = 2d/(d^{2} + 2)$. The lower bound holds because $\rho$ is a state on $d$ dimensions, with equality only for a flat spectrum; the upper bound is verified in the stated range and not proved in general. The check counts the sector traces by enumeration at small sizes and evaluates both forms for $n \le 6$, $d \le 32$.

\emph{(iv).} By (i) to (iii), at the crossing $S_{n}(\text{eviction}) = m\ln 2$ exactly and $S_{n}(\text{planar}) = m\ln 2 - \ln C_{n}/(n-1)$ to leading order, the Haar value agreeing with the planar one as $d \to \infty$. Monotonicity: $C_{n+1}^{\,n-1} > C_{n}^{\,n}$ and $C_{n} < 4^{n-1}$, checked for $2 \leq n \leq 9$; in general $C_{n+1}/C_{n} = 2(2n+1)/(n+2)$ increases to $4$, so $\ln C_n$ is convex in $n$ with $\ln C_{1} = 0$ and slope tending to $\ln 4$, which gives both. For the continuation, $\ln C_{n} = \ln\Gamma(2n+1) - \ln\Gamma(n+1) - \ln\Gamma(n+2)$ vanishes at $n = 1$, so $\lim_{n\to 1}\ln C_{n}/(n-1) = 2\psi(3) - \psi(2) - \psi(3) = \psi(3) - \psi(2) = H_{2} - H_{1} = \tfrac12$. Page's average at equal dimensions is $\ln m - \tfrac12$ to leading order (Fact \claimref{singularity.imported-page-average-entropy-subsystem}), so the von Neumann gap between eviction (at capacity) and Page at the crossing is $\tfrac12$, as stored in \claimref{synthesis.eviction-deficit-against-page-deficit}; the Catalan continuation reproduces it. This is a consistency check of the continuation, not a derivation: the continuation of a sequence off the integers is not unique without growth conditions, and the stored value comes from Page's formula. The Gamma-function continuation is nevertheless the natural one (classical, from memory): the $C_{n}$ are the moments of the Marchenko-Pastur law at ratio one, the large-dimension limit of Page's spectrum at equal dimensions, and $\ln C_{n}/(n-1)$ continued through the moment integral is that law's Renyi family, whose $n \to 1$ limit is its von Neumann value; so the half nat is the planar von Neumann limit.
