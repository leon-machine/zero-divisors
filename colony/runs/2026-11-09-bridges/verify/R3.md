## 2. synthesis.mode-split-of-the-clifford-algebra-and-its-klein-twist-to-the-tensor-product

Verdict: SOUND WITH FIXES (three overstated wordings, listed as fixes 1 to 3).

What I verified.

  `r3_modesplit.py`): the blade projection E_A satisfies E_A(y x y') = y E_A(x) y', preserves the scalar part, and is
  positive (20 random positive elements per split; the proof's argument is also correct, and (iii) makes it a partial
  trace, hence completely positive). d_B E_A equals the literal partial trace for A = first modes on a random
  matrix. For a generic pure state the moments of d_B E_A(rho) and of d_A E_B'(rho) agree and those of d_A E_B(rho)
