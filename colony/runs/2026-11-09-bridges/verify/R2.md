## 1. synthesis.quarter-turn-rotors-of-the-even-subalgebra-form-a-state-design-of-order-three

Verdict: SOUND WITH FIXES.

What was checked.
- Proof steps (setting, (i) to (v)) read line by line. Transitivity on nonzero classes and on ordered commuting
  pairs is proved for every N by explicit transvections (no appeal to Witt's theorem; the only thing assumed is
  nondegeneracy of b on V, which is clause (iii) of signed-directions@2, even case, plus the full set being the
  radical on even subsets). The dependent case d' = d + c' is handled. The two identities of a state, the sign-flip
  argument, the R_e argument for anticommuting triples (ss' = -1) and the count (D^2/2 - 2) kappa = (D - 2)/(D + 1)
  are correct. (v): (D + m^2)/(m(D + 1)) = (m + n)/(mn + 1) with D = mn, correct.
- Premises at pinned versions: signed-directions@2 (ii), (iii) say what is used; imported-haar-moments@2 states the
  symmetriser formula for every n; imported-haar-average-purity@2 states (m + n)/(mn + 1). Current versions equal
  the pins.
- Own route (own1.py, own2.py, own5.py): |Gamma| = 24, 24, 11520 in Cl(1,3), Cl(3,1), Cl(6,0); on my own generic
  rational states (all 3, resp. all 15 coordinates nonzero) the group average of every monomial of degree at most
  three, over ALL 680 multisets of classes in Cl(6,0) (the proposal's check samples the triples), equals the closed
  values. Independent second route: frame potentials of the orbit of a blade state (6 and 60 states) are 1/2, 1/3,
  1/4 and 1/4, 1/10, 1/20 at t = 1, 2, 3 (the design values 1/C(D+t-1,t)) and 5/24 against 1/5, 1/32 against 1/35 at
  t = 4. On a generic state the fourth moment of x_c is state dependent and not 3/((D+1)(D+3)). So "order three,
  nothing at four" is exact.
- Perturbations (all fail as they should): third moment denominator (D+1)(D+3); second moment 1/(D+2); generator
  1 + h_c in place of 1 + I h_c (KeyError: lands off the units); purity target 4/6.
- Literature: Kueng and Gross, "Qubit stabilizer states are complex projective 3-designs" (arXiv:1510.02767), Zhu,
  "Multiqubit Clifford groups are unitary 3-designs" (arXiv:1510.02619) verified to exist with those titles on the
  web; Webb (2016) not opened. The result is classical in content (as the entity says); the tag classical-theorem
  is present.

Defects.
(a) "Rotor" is wrong from eight generators on. In Cl(6,0), Cl(1,3), Cl(3,1) every k = I h_c is a bivector and R_c is
a rotor. In Cl(7,1) (an example named in the formal statement) the 63 generators have k of grade 2 (21), grade 6 (7)
and grade 4 (35); for the 35 of grade 4, k~ = k, so R_c R_c~ = (1 + k)^2 = 2k is not a scalar: these are not rotors in
any sense, and no R_c with k of grade above two is a versor (it sends a vector inside k to a blade of grade
|k| - 1). The 28 generators with R R~ scalar give class orbits 28 + 35 and the 21 bivector rotors give 7 + 21 + 35
(own3.py). In Cl(10,0): k of grade 2 (45) and 6 (210); all have R R~ = 2 but the 210 are not versors; bivector rotors
alone give 45 + 210 (confirmed). So the sentence of the note "the real form in which every generator is a rotor" is
false in Cl(7,1) and misleading in Cl(10,0); the lay sentence "quarter turns in the planes of the algebra" is true
only up to six generators; the title says "rotors".
(b) Scope missing from the title: I^2 = -1 and "all blades of square -1, not bivectors alone".
(c) Clause (i) is headed "proved" but the orders 24 and 11520 are computed (the proof says so). They can be proved:
the kernel of Gamma -> Sp(2N-2, 2) is the sign flips {conjugation by h_u}, of order 2^(2N-2), and the image is
generated by all transvections, so |Gamma| = 2^(2N-2) |Sp(2N-2, 2)| = 4*6, 16*720; "the image is all of Sp" is the
classical generation theorem and would have to be flagged as such.
(d) The adjoint in (iv) is described loosely ("the reverse composed with the sign that makes every h_c
self-adjoint"); in Cl(1,3) the reverse sends h_c to -h_c, so the phrase does not define a map.
(e) Clause (iv) is weaker than what the proof gives. Because the Gamma-average of every monomial of degree at most
three is the same for every state, ANY probability measure on states that is invariant under Gamma has these
moments; the unitarily invariant measure is one. So for D a power of two the Haar moments to order three are
derived, not merely matched, and the comparison with the imported Fact becomes a cross-check. This is what makes
"the stored comparison can drop its import" true (see fix 6), and it is in line with the rule that results are
derived and literature is prior art.

Fixes.
1. Title: "Quarter turns about the blades of square -1 of an even subalgebra with I^2 = -1 form a state design of
   order three" (or similar: no "rotors" unqualified, I^2 = -1 present).
2. Formal, clause (i): rename R_c "quarter-turn elements" (R_c = 1 + k, k = I h_c a blade with k^2 = -1) and replace

   Cl(3,1), Cl(6,0)). For 2N >= 8 the generators include blades of grade 4 or 6, which are not versors, and for

   planes when there are at most six generators)". Extraction note: delete "and the real form in which every
   generator is a rotor"; say "every generator is a real element 1 + k of the algebra, a rotor for 2N <= 6".
4. Clause (i): label the orders "computed" or add the order formula with its classical input flagged (defect c).
5. Proof (iv): define the adjoint as the real-linear map fixing every h_c and negating every I h_c; it is an
   anti-automorphism because h_c h_d = +-h_(c+d) for commuting and +-I h_(c+d) for anticommuting units, and
