## What the postulate says, in brief
- The premise, in D2's words; the construction is the stored photon's (differentiation and multiplication on
  polynomials over span{1, I_4}, one variable per nonzero null momentum).
- Status: the photon's (a pair on polynomials, not an element of a floor, not derived from the tower); not a
  foundation postulate; first order only; no quantised geometry beyond first order, no self-coupling.
- Grounds: the requirement of 18 November; failure of a field with no variables of its own in three stored models
  (the scalar one also with a coupled device); sufficiency in the lattice model of the first-order field, labelled
  as sufficiency in a model with that model's limits (cubic symmetry, the lattice's own cone, matter at rest,
  commuting constraints).
- Limits: not shown necessary (the other way named has no stored result for or against it); the dipole
  obstruction for one mass moved alone; not derived; no state selected; normalisation and helicity assignment not
  computed.
- Refutation clause: retired by a stored result meeting the requirement at first order with no states on the two
  directions, or by a derivation of the pair; refuted by a conflict with a stored theorem (none known); left
  without use by a proof that it is the first order of no consistent account; no accessible observation
  distinguishes it from the standard account, so it is not a prediction and carries no kill condition.
- Ledger: transport-absent (stated at the technical level and in the note; the build derives `ledger: absent`).
