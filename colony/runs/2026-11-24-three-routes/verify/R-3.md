## 2. synthesis.local-records-give-the-chsh-bound-...-keeps-them, clause (iii) only

**Verdict: SOUND WITH FIXES.** The extension to m >= 2 is proved; the fixes are wording at the restated levels
(field Q and the no-signalling/marginal condition missing) and one overstatement of what the check exercises.

What I checked.
- What is taken from the stored theorem: clause (i), A with dagger = Cl_Q(m,m) = Mat_{2^m}(Q) with transpose
  (A is the span of the blades in e_j, f_j, j <= m, closed under product and dagger, by the signed-directions basis;
  the proof asserts this in one phrase); clause (iii), each E -> mu(EF) (an additive weight on ALL projectors of A,
  since E ranges over all self-adjoint idempotents of A) is sum rho^F_ij E_ij, rho^F symmetric, for 2^m >= 4. For
  m = 2 the separate citation of the two-mode theorem is redundant (size four is covered) but harmless.
- Proof steps: rho^F PSD on Q^N from mu(Pi_r F) >= 0, so L_F(s^2) >= 0 (sum over nonzero columns); projectors span
  self-adjoint elements, so the marginal conditions give L_{F+} + L_{F-} = L_1 = L_{G+} + L_{G-} on self-adjoint
  elements (S^2, D^2, 1 are self-adjoint); (L_{F+} - L_{F-})(A_i) = sum ab mu(E_{i,a}F_b) = c_i0; the four terms sum
  to L_1(S^2 + D^2) + 2t^2 - 2tV = 4 + 2t^2 - 2tV; t = V/2 in Q gives V^2 <= 8, strict since V is rational. Complete;

## Overlaps and conflicts
Both items rest on the same stored size-four theorem and use it the same way (clause (i) for the identification,
clause (iii) on a full matrix algebra of size >= 4). No conflict. The CHSH clause (iii) applies to party 1's whole
algebra (all its projectors, including parity-odd ones); item 1 shows that from three modes on the even projectors

parity-homogeneous). No merge needed; each should stand.
