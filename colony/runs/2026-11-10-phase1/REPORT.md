# Run 2026-11-10-phase1: report

Phase 1 of the theory action plan of 10 November: entanglement from the foundation, and the Kerr entropy test. Method:
two clusters, four attackers (two pairs), a bridge-hunter, two blind referees, one fix round, one maintainer agent,
close-out. Eight results accepted: five SOUND WITH FIXES as filed; three restated after overstated wording (the
positivity counter-model at two levels; both Kerr results in title, one-liner and lay level) and accepted by Leon
individually. No result is wrong. One new postulate, one new open problem.

## Verdict on the two targets
Entanglement: the foundation postulates F1 to F3 give the operator identity behind Tsirelson's bound; they do not give
the bound. The bound needs positivity of values, a premise the foundation does not contain: a normalised integer-valued
functional on the integer Clifford algebra gives valid no-signalling probabilities and CHSH value 4. Leon adopted
positivity as postulate (P). Every result here is classical mathematics (Tsirelson, Landau, Popescu-Rohrlich,
Toner-Verstraete) placed in the series' algebra; no novelty is claimed beyond that placement and a denominator law.
Kerr: not a test. A horizon placement of the count gives A/4 at every spin by construction (the surface gravity
cancels); the literal curvature-cutoff placement fails at both ends of the spin range. The boundary-mode density
prediction's kill condition is neither met nor probed.

contradiction; unrecorded until now. The attainment question over the rationals collapsed (rational data give rational
values). The Born weights from (P) and sharpness on a primitive idempotent: argued for one state, a phase 2 candidate.
