Read in Gauge Theory Gravity, the metric used is the derived metric $g(b,c) = \hb^{-1}(b)\cdot\hb^{-1}(c) = b\cdot c - 2H(b\cdot k)(c\cdot k)$ of the Kerr-Schild gauge field of Fact \claimref{interior.imported-kerr-schild-form-kerr-gauge} (using $Q(k) = 0$), written in components in the signature $(-,+,+,+)$, and induced metrics are Gram matrices of physical tangent vectors. All entries are rational in $(M, a, r, u)$.

\emph{(i)} The determinant lemma, clause (2) of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.no-spin-independent-modulus-level-set-gives-the-area-law-exactly-on-slow-kerr}, gives for any surface $r = R(u)$ invariant under the two Killing flows, with tangent vectors $\partial_t + \Omega\partial_\phi$, $\partial_u + R'\partial_r$, $\partial_\phi$, the Gram determinant $-\Sigma\,[\Delta(R) + (1-u^2)R'^2]$ in this signature, for every constant $\Omega$ (and $+\Sigma[\ldots]$ in the store's product). At $R' = 0$ it is $-\Delta\Sigma$. (For $R' = 0$ it also follows in two lines: $g_{uu} = \Sigma/(1-u^2)$, $g_{tu} = g_{\phi u} = 0$, and the $(t,\phi)$ block $\eta_b + 2H\,k_b\otimes k_b$, $\eta_b = \mathrm{diag}(-1, P(1-u^2))$, $k_b = (-1, a(1-u^2))$, has determinant $D = \det\eta_b\,(1 + 2H\,k_b\cdot\eta_b^{-1}k_b) = -(1-u^2)(P - 2H\Sigma) = -(1-u^2)\Delta$ by the rank-one rule, unchanged by $\partial_t \to \partial_t + \Omega\partial_\phi$.) The volume element per unit Killing parameter is $\sqrt{|\Delta|\Sigma}\,du\,d\phi$, and integrating over $\phi \in [0,2\pi)$ and $u \in [-1,1]$ gives $V'(r)$. The sign of $-\Delta\Sigma$ gives the causal character (in this signature a positive Gram determinant is a spacelike three-surface). At $a = 0$, $\Sigma = r^2$, $I = 2r$, $\Delta = r^2(1 - 2M/r)$. For the store's product: with $\Omega = a/P$, $g(K_\Omega, K_\Omega) = g_{tt} + 2\Omega g_{t\phi} + \Omega^2g_{\phi\phi} = -\Delta\Sigma/P^2$ in components by direct evaluation, a rational identity in $(M, a, r, u)$ that the check confirms at rational points, so $Q(K_\Omega) = \Delta\Sigma/P^2$ there. The check confirms the determinant at rational points with three angular velocities each, and at one point in $\Cl(1,3)$ with the store's product, where it is $+\Delta\Sigma$.

\emph{(ii)} The inverse metric is $\eta^{-1} - 2H\,k^\sharp\otimes k^\sharp$ with $k^\sharp$ the $\eta$-raised $k$, whose $u$ component vanishes and whose $r$ component is $-1$; so $g^{ru} = 0$ and $g^{rr} = P/\Sigma - 2H = \Delta/\Sigma$. The gradient of $r$ therefore has no $u$ component, its lines run at fixed $u$, and the proper length along them is $\int dr/\sqrt{|g^{rr}|} = \int\sqrt{\Sigma/|\Delta|}\,dr$. Near $r_+$ write $\delta = |r - r_+|$: $|\Delta| = \delta\,(2s \mp \delta)$ and $\Sigma = \Sigma_+(u)(1 + O(\delta))$ with $\Sigma_+ = r_+^2 + a^2u^2 > 0$, so $\ell_+ = \sqrt{\Sigma_+/2s}\int_0^{\delta}x^{-1/2}(1 + O(x))\,dx = 2\sqrt{\Sigma_+\delta/2s}\,(1 + O(\delta))$ and $w = \sqrt{2s\,\delta\,\Sigma_+}\,(1 + O(\delta))$. Hence $w/(P_+\ell_+) \to 2s/(2P_+) = (r_+ - r_-)/(2P_+) = \kappa_+$, in which $\Sigma_+(u)$ has cancelled: the limit is the same at every latitude. The same computation at $r_-$, where $|\Delta| = \delta(2s \mp \delta)$ again and $\Sigma_- = r_-^2 + a^2u^2 > 0$ for $a > 0$, gives $\kappa_- = (r_+ - r_-)/(2P_-)$. That $P_\pm\,du\,d\phi$ is the area element of the cross-section: on $r = r_\pm$ at fixed $t$ the Gram determinant of $(\partial_u, \partial_\phi)$ is $g_{uu}g_{\phi\phi}$ with $g_{\phi\phi} = P(1-u^2) + 2Ha^2(1-u^2)^2$, so $g_{uu}g_{\phi\phi} = P\Sigma + 2Mr\,a^2(1-u^2)$, and at $\Delta = 0$, where $2Mr = P$, this is $P(\Sigma + a^2 - a^2u^2) = P^2$. The check verifies the constant term of the polynomial $|\Delta|\Sigma/\delta$ in $\delta$ against $\kappa_\pm^2P_\pm^2\cdot 4\Sigma_\pm/2s$ at four latitudes for five holes, and $P_\pm = 2Mr_\pm$.

\emph{(iii)} $\mathcal{S}_{\rho_0}$ is $r = r_+ + \delta(u)$ (or $r_+ - \delta(u)$) with $\ell_+ = \rho_0$, so $\delta(u) = \rho_0^2\,2s/(4\Sigma_+(u))\,(1 + O(\delta))$. By the determinant lemma cited in (i), with $R = r_+ \pm \delta(u)$ and $R' = \pm\delta'$, the Gram determinant on $\mathcal{S}_{\rho_0}$ is exactly $-\Sigma\,[\Delta(R) + (1-u^2)\delta'^2]$, which is the integrand of the statement. Here $\delta' = O(\delta)$, since $\delta$ is a smooth multiple of $1/\Sigma_+(u)$, so $(1-u^2)\delta'^2 = O(\delta^2)$ against $|\Delta| = \delta(2s \mp \delta)$ of order $\delta$: the determinant is $-\Delta\Sigma\,(1 + O(\delta))$. Hence $V'_{\rho_0} = \int\!\!\int w\,(1 + O(\delta))\,du\,d\phi = \kappa_+\rho_0\int\!\!\int P_+\,du\,d\phi\,(1 + O(\delta)) = \kappa_+\rho_0A_+(1 + O(\delta))$ by (ii). All lengths scale with $M$ at fixed $a/M$, and $\delta/M = O(\rho_0^2/M^2)$ at fixed $a/M < 1$, which gives the limit and the order $\rho_0^2/M^2$ of the remainder. \emph{The coefficient at $a = 0$, outside.} With $r = 2M + x$: $\ell = \int_0^x\sqrt{(2M + y)/y}\,dy = 2\sqrt{2Mx}\,(1 + x/12M + O(x^2/M^2))$ and the rate is $4\pi r^2\sqrt{x/r} = 4\pi(2M)^{3/2}\sqrt{x}\,(1 + 3x/4M + \ldots)$. Setting $\ell = \rho_0$ gives $\sqrt{x} = \rho_0(2\sqrt{2M})^{-1}(1 - x/12M)$, so the rate is $4\pi M\rho_0\,(1 + 2x/3M)$ with $x = \rho_0^2/8M$: $\kappa\rho_0A\,(1 + \rho_0^2/12M^2)$, as $\kappa\rho_0A = 4\pi M\rho_0$. Inside, $x \to -x$ in the two expansions reverses the sign. For $a > 0$ the coefficient is computed numerically from the exact integrand (solving $\ell_+ = \rho_0$ at each latitude) and is not proved. The count is $\nu V'_{\rho_0}\beta_+$ by the rule of Proposition \claimref{singularity.stretched-singularity-count} with the epoch of Theorem \claimref{scrambling.epoch-modular-period-two-unknowns-become}; $\kappa_+$ cancels, and $2\pi\nu\rho_0 = \tfrac14$ at $\nu\rho_0 = 1/8\pi$. The entropy $A_+/4$ compared with is that of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-kerr-surface-gravity-entropy}. The identification of the modes of (SH) with those of hypothesis (H) is part of the hypothesis; Theorem \claimref{synthesis.c-loc-is-a-second-placement-of-the-count}(iii) is what excludes adding them (two independent sets of density $\tfrac14$ give $\tfrac12$ per unit area and $G_{\mathrm{cl}} = 2G$).

\emph{(iv)} On $r = \mathrm{const}$ the Gram determinant of (i), taken with $\Omega = \Omega_H$, is $g_{uu}\bigl(g(K,K)\,g_{\phi\phi} - g(K,\partial_\phi)^2\bigr)$ in components. On the locus $g(K,K) = 0$ and $g(K, \partial_\phi) = 0$ at $\Omega = \Omega_H = a/P_+$, by direct evaluation of the components ($g(K,K) = 0$ fails at $\Omega = -a/P_+$, so the sense of rotation is that of the entered $k$; with $D = 0$ and $g(K,K) = 0$ the vanishing of $g(K,\partial_\phi)$ also follows from $D = g(K,K)g_{\phi\phi} - g(K,\partial_\phi)^2$; the check asserts all three), hence $g(K,\partial_\phi) = O(\delta)$ nearby and $|Q(K)| = w^2/(g_{uu}g_{\phi\phi})\,(1 + O(\delta)) = (w/P_+)^2(1 + O(\delta))$. So (ii) says $\sqrt{|Q(K)|} = \kappa_+\ell_+(1 + o(1))$ with $\kappa_+$ independent of $u$: the norm-gradient form of the surface gravity of Fact \claimref{synthesis.imported-kerr-surface-gravity-entropy} (classically equivalent to $(K\cdot D)K = \kappa_+K$ for a Killing horizon; used here as a reading, and verified directly by (ii) for this field with the Fact's value of $\kappa_+$), together with its constancy on the horizon. The count of (iii) uses nothing else about the hole, so no value of $(M, a)$ with $a < M$ could give a ratio to $A_+/4$ other than $8\pi\nu\rho_0$ at leading order.

\emph{(v)} With $\beta_+ = 2\pi/\kappa_+ = 4\pi Mr_+/s$ and $A_+/4 = 2\pi Mr_+$: rate $8\pi\nu M$ gives $N = 32\pi^2\nu M^2r_+/s = 2\pi M^2r_+/s$, ratio $M/s$; rate $\nu\beta_+$ gives $N = \beta_+^2/16\pi = \pi M^2r_+^2/s^2$, ratio $Mr_+/2s^2$; rate $2\nu\kappa_+A_+$ gives $4\pi\nu A_+ = A_+/4$. With $e = a/M$, $\sigma = s/M$, $1 - \sigma = e^2/(1+\sigma)$: $M/s - 1 = e^2/(\sigma(1+\sigma))$ and $Mr_+/2s^2 - 1 = (1-\sigma)(1+2\sigma)/2\sigma^2 = e^2(1+2\sigma)/(2\sigma^2(1+\sigma))$, with coefficients $\tfrac12$ and $\tfrac34$ at $\sigma = 1$; both ratios diverge as $\sigma \to 0$. \emph{(vi)} For $a > 0$, $\kappa_\pm A_\pm = 4\pi P_\pm(r_+ - r_-)/(2P_\pm) = 4\pi s$, and the inner-locus statement is (ii) and the argument of (iii) at $r_-$ on the side $r > r_-$. The expansion $\Sigma = \Sigma_-(1 + O(\delta))$ used there needs $\delta \ll r_-$ at the equator, where $\Sigma_- = r_-^2$; with $\delta = \rho_0^2\,2s/4\Sigma_-$ this is $\rho_0^2M \ll r_-^3$ up to a constant, which fails as $a \to 0$ at fixed $\rho_0$. In that limit the surface leaves the neighbourhood of the locus and tends to the level set of proper time $\rho_0$ from the singular locus of the Schwarzschild interior, whose volume rate at leading order is $\mu_E\rho_0 = 12\pi M\rho_0$ with the measure of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.exceptional-boundary-measure-one-calibration}(ii), and $12\pi M\rho_0 = 3\kappa\rho_0A$ at $a = 0$; the approach of the Kerr rate to this value is computed numerically (ratio $1.00002$, $1.01$, $1.32$, $2.06$ at $a = 300, 100, 30, 10$ for $M = 500$, $\rho_0 = 2$), not proved. For a surface $r = \mathrm{const}$, $N = \nu V'\beta_+ = \tfrac{1}{16\pi}\cdot2\pi\sqrt{|\Delta|}\,I\cdot4\pi Mr_+/s$, and $N = 2\pi Mr_+$ exactly when $\sqrt{|\Delta|}\,I = 4s$. On $(0, 2M)$ the function $r^3(2M - r)$ rises to its maximum $27M^4/16$ at $r = 3M/2$ and then falls to zero, so it takes the value $4M^2$ exactly twice when $27M^4/16 > 4M^2$, i.e.\ $M^2 > 64/27$; on $(2M, \infty)$, $r^3(r - 2M)$ increases from $0$ without bound and takes it once. At $r = r_c$, $r_c^3 = 2M$: $r_c^3(2M - r_c) - 4M^2 = -r_c^4 < 0$, and at $r_c(1 + x)$, $x = r_c^{-2}$, it is $r_c^6[(1+x)^3(1 - x - x^2) - 1] = r_c^6\,x\,(2 - x - 5x^2 - 4x^3 - x^4)$, which is positive for $x \le \tfrac14$; so the first root lies in $(r_c, r_c + 1/r_c)$ for $r_c \ge 2$. Near the horizon, $r = 2M \mp d$ gives $(2M \mp d)^3d = 4M^2$, $d = \tfrac{1}{2M}(1 + O(M^{-2}))$, and by (ii) with $\Sigma_+ = 4M^2$, $2s = 2M$ the proper distance is $2\sqrt{4M^2d/2M} = 2\sqrt{2Md} = 2$ at leading order.
