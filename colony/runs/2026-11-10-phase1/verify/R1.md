silently: where it is needed (base (v) last sentence, parity (v)) it is stated as an extra hypothesis. Correct as is.

C2. The adjoint. "dagger = blade inverse" is well defined and an anti-automorphism in every signature, given the
generators. In signature (p,0) it is the reverse and in (0,q) the Clifford conjugate, both independent of the
orthonormal frame. In indefinite signature it depends on the split of the vectors into the positive and the negative
generators: a boost changes the adjoint, the observables and the states. No proposal says this (no text matching
"frame-dependent" exists in the six). Required sentence in the formal level of every proposal that works in
indefinite signature (all six): "The adjoint is defined by the chosen generators. In definite signature it does not
depend on the orthonormal frame; in indefinite signature it depends on the split into generators of square 1 and of
square -1, so observables, states and (P) are relative to that split, and each statement holds for every fixed
split." For the signature list the conclusion is frame-independent (a change of frame is an automorphism carrying
configurations to configurations); say so there.

synthesis.mode-split-of-the-clifford-algebra-and-its-klein-twist-to-the-tensor-product@1. Its past cone has 113
