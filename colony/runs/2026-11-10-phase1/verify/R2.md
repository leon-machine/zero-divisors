## 1. synthesis.stretched-horizon-count-on-kerr-gives-the-area-law-at-every-spin

## 2. synthesis.no-spin-independent-curvature-level-set-carries-the-count-to-slow-kerr

### Verdict: SOUND WITH FIXES

Decisive reason. For the class stated in the formal level (level sets of the modulus M|z|^{-3}, rho <= M/3,
0 < a <= 4 rho/5, exact equality at zero and nonzero spin) every step is correct and I reproduced every number. The
title, one-liner and lay level do not carry that scope, and no level states the other half of what the two-sided
bound proves: when a/rho -> 0 the count agrees with A_+/4 at leading order, the standard at which the Schwarzschild
count itself holds.

### What I recomputed

- Determinant. On any surface r = R(u), det h = -Sigma (Delta + (1 - u^2) R'^2), symbolically, for symbolic Omega
  (`own_symbolic.py` B2); on |z| = rho with R' = -a^2 u/R this is rho^2 B(u) (B3). Also at an exact point in the
  store's product with GA.py (sign reversed, as expected).
- Proved or computed. The 25-point sample is not decisive on its own: the difference is a rational function of
  (M, a, rho, the angle, Omega) and no degree bound or basis argument is given, so the sample supports "computed".
  The text's proof is valid (I confirmed that the Kerr-Schild chart differs from Boyer-Lindquist by shifts of t and
  phi that depend on r only, so the restriction to the surface is a unit-Jacobian shear), but it rests on the
  Boyer-Lindquist block form, which is not in the store and is quoted with no provenance. Fix 4 gives a proof
  inside the Kerr-Schild gauge with no import; with it "proved" stands and the sample is a consistency check.
- Bounds, by hand: R(rho - R) = R a^2u^2/(rho + R) <= a^2; a^4u^2(1-u^2)/R^2 <= a^4/(4R^2) <= (4/9)a^2;
  (13/9)/(3/5) = 65/27 <= 5/2; delta <= 8/25; (1 - x/4)^4 - (1 - x) = x^2(3/8 - x/16 + x^2/256);
  eps^2/144 <= eps/225; 1/9 < 1/6 - 1/225. All correct. r_- <= a^2/M <= (16/75) rho. M >= 8 suffices for
  rho_c <= M/3 (the exact threshold is M^2 >= 54).
- Numerics (`own_numeric.py` (4)), V'(M,a,rho)/V'(M,0,rho) inside the stated bounds at all eleven points, including
  the corners rho = M/3, a = 4 rho/5 (0.8662, bounds 0.5349 and 0.9467) and M = 8 on the Planck level set (0.8709).
  At a = 4 rho_c/5 the actual ratio Q(M,a)/Q(M,0) is 0.900 (M = 8), 0.918 (M = 32), 0.932 (M = 500), 0.9357
  (M = 10^6), tending to G(16/25) = 0.93574. The stated bound 71/75 = 0.9467 is correct and not sharp.
- The deficit at large M. Sound for fixed a/rho: the bound is (1 - eps/12)/sqrt(1 - eps rho^2/M^2), and on the
  Planck level set rho_c/M -> 0, so the ratio is at most 71/75 + o(1) at a = 4 rho_c/5 for all large M; the actual
  deficit is 6.4 per cent in the limit. It does vanish as a/rho -> 0: the result's own lower bound gives a deficit

  Schwarzschild count's own miss 1 - sqrt(1 - rho_c/2M) = 3.1e-5 at the stored density. So the miss is a
  leading-order miss only for a comparable to rho.
