independent surveys each exclude (w0, wa) = (-1, 0) at five sigma (shared CMB prior allowed; published frequentist

combinations. Under the stored premises the series has no fallback (argued, on a search): the promotion keeps

contained: the prediction, the field-space premise and the claim that the stored action is the law of gravity fall;
Standard Model, mass and dark-matter-ratio results do not.
Primordial spectrum: no mechanism, kept as the series' position (Leon's decision). No stored mode equation gives an
index within three standard deviations of the measured one without a chosen number; one stored row is red but far
too red (index -1). The survey is argued, not a no-go theorem: five stored index results are outside the ledger.
Dark-matter premises: none derived outright. The closed form 5.364 rests on eight postulates (R1, R2, R3, P1, P2, P3
and two newly registered conditions), not on the subject's whole list; the total remains a fit, two shares against
one measured number. One postulate (P4) retired; H1 weakened by a uniqueness result; several premises marked as
definition or model specification without leaving the postulate count.
Quantum field theory: the protraction identity is now a stored theorem; a Ward-obeying pair of one-loop extensions
is exhibited on the Euclidean section (the import for time-ordered products in signature (1,3) stays); the
curved-field freedom is five local covariant terms of which one is transverse, conditional on existence, which is
neither proved nor imported. A scope note records what a full theory would need.
