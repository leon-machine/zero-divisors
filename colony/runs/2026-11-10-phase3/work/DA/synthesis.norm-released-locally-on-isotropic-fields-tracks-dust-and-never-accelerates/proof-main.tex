\emph{(ii), the reduction.} Let $G$ be the group generated by the rotations of the background about the $e_{0}$ axis, each combined with the same rotor acting as a rotation gauge transformation, by one reflection $e_{1} \mapsto -e_{1}$ combined in the same way, and by the spatial translations.
