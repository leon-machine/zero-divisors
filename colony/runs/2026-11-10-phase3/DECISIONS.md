# Decisions by Leon, run 2026-11-10-phase3

First round (10 November, at the start of the run).

1. Dark energy. The series counts w = -1 as refuted when two independent surveys each exclude (w0, wa) = (-1, 0) at
   five sigma. Register this before the next data release.
2. Gaps. Add open-problem entries for strong CP and for the matter asymmetry. No entry for a quantised graviton: the
   position of the series is that the graviton does not exist. Nothing in this run is to treat quantising the
   gravitational gauge fields as a gap or a goal; where the store counts a graviton (joint J1 kinematics, the spin-2
   channel clause of J3, the additive counting readings), report the places and do not extend them.

Second round (10 November, while the referees read).

3. Primordial spectrum: decide after the referee reports (keep "no mechanism" or propose a premise).
4. The w = -1 rule: each of the two surveys may use the shared CMB prior; the collaboration's published frequentist
   distance from (w0, wa) = (-1, 0) decides, with Bayesian evidence recorded beside it; the registration says plainly
   that the rule is conservative.
5. Dark-matter ratio: register both unregistered conditions of the closed form as postulate entities (the
   single-ideal clause; the population of the odd towers, with the statement that it is inferred from the number it
   helps fit).

Third round (10 November).

6. The imported massless-graviton production rate and its dependants (31 entities, pred.no-dark-radiation,
   pred.no-indirect-dark-signals, the dimmed-burst baseline): flag now, derive later. Mark the Fact and its dependants
   as resting on a process the series does not hold, and open a problem: derive the rate as radiation of the classical
   gauge field sourced by matter, or show it vanishes. No prediction is changed in this run.
7. State the series' position on the graviton once, in store/conventions.md: gravity is the classical gauge field of
   Gauge Theory Gravity; "graviton" in stored text names a helicity-2 channel or prior art, not a quantum of the
   field. Draft for Leon's wording.
8. Primordial spectrum (after the referee): keep "no mechanism". The spectrum stays free initial data; the open
   problem records what was examined, labelled argued, and the rule that a future index match counts only with a
   second number.

Fourth round (10 November, before the maintainer pass).

9. R3's unit of thickness: each tower's own cell radius (the store's literal wording and the convention of the
   thickness function). R2 therefore also supplies R3's unit and stays a premise of the total; the unit-ball
   reduction is recorded as holding only in the fixed-common-length reading, which is not adopted.
10. Dark-matter re-typings, apply all: retire P4 as a postulate (repoint its assumes edges to the stored theorem that
    proves the instance used, after giving that theorem's clause its actual premises); re-type U as a definition and
    the six simulation premises as model specification with S2 and C as exclusive readings; stop counting D with R1
    to R3 (D with R1 and R2 forces one quantum per de Sitter ball; D belongs to the de Sitter reading); split P1
    into its three clauses. The flag premise is left alone.
11. Field-space premise: add assumes edges on the action results that state field equations with matter, so the cone
    shows what a confirmed w different from -1 would take.
12. Conventions text, as drafted: "Gravity in this series is the classical gauge field of Gauge Theory Gravity. The
    series holds no quantum of that field: the graviton does not exist here. Where stored text says graviton it names
    a helicity-2 channel of the classical field, a counting convention, or prior art; no stored result is to be read
    as resting on a quantised gravitational field, and results that import such a process are flagged."
