## Pre-registered kill condition (written before any computation)

match the measured value. Then the result is a no-go over the audited list and the series needs a new premise
(Leon's decision). A red index obtained by choosing a parameter counts as a fit; the parameter and its stored
status are named. A red index along some axes only (spread between axes) counts as not matching.

(b) be red by exact arithmetic on the Bessel order (nu^2 > 9/4 as a rational inequality), (c) have no free
exponent or coupling. Failing any of the three, it is listed as killed with the failing clause.
