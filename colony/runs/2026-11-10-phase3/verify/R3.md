### The proof, Steps 2 to 5

- Step 2: `sum_a (d_v - d_u) S(u) g_a S(v) = S(u)(delta - mS)(v) - (delta - mS)(u) S(v)`; the mass terms cancel;

- Step 3: `(nabla + m)` annihilates `G = S * (nS) - (n.w) S`; for m > 0 a tempered solution of
  `(-nabla^2 + m^2) G = 0` is zero; for m = 0, G is harmonic, tempered and homogeneous of degree -2, hence zero.
