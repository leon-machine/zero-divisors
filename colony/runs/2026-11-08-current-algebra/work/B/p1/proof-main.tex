\emph{Preliminaries.} By Theorem \claimref{std-algebra.witt-basis-car} the modes $a^{*}_{x,k} = \tfrac12(e_{x,k} + f_{x,k})$, $a_{x,k} = \tfrac12(e_{x,k} - f_{x,k})$ satisfy $\{a_{x,k}, a^{*}_{y,l}\} = \delta_{xy}\delta_{kl}$, $\{a_{x,k}, a_{y,l}\} = 0$, and the adjoint $*$ (the antiautomorphism fixing the generators of square $+1$ and negating those of square $-1$) has $(a_{x,k})^{*} = a^{*}_{x,k}$. The kernel bivector $J$ commutes with every mode, $J^{2} = -1$, $J^{*} = -J$, so $\mathrm{span}\{1, J\}$ is a field of scalars for the mode algebra, as in \claimref{synthesis.helicity-one-pair-modes-on-a-null-generator-are-quasi-spins}; for a matrix $A$ on the one-particle index set, $\hat A = \sum A_{(x,k)(y,l)}a^{*}_{x,k}a_{y,l}$ satisfies $[\hat A, \hat B] = \widehat{[A, B]}$, and $\rho_{q}(M) = \widehat{S_{q} \otimes M}$ with $S_{q}$ the partial shift of the window (the proof of (i) there). The five-mode module and its halvings are those of \claimref{synthesis.spin10-clause-content-is-the-neutral-singlet-choice}, and a halving's sixteen occupation states label the sixteen channels of a light ray, its Weyl components (\claimref{synthesis.slot-module-light-ray-arrays-are-weyl-components}(ii)); on it the same bilinear rule gives $[\hat t, \hat s] = \widehat{[t, s]}$.

\emph{(i).} The operator $\hat t$ conserves the number of slots and the number of pairs when $t$ is block diagonal on slots and pairs, which every element of $\mathfrak{g}$ is; so $\hat t$ preserves each $V_{n,k}$, hence each component of a halving, and $\rho_{+}(T)$ is block diagonal on the components of $V_{+}$. It is anti-Hermitian since $\hat t^{*} = \widehat{t^{*}} = -\hat t$ for the positive Hermitian form in which the occupation states are orthonormal. A gauge current is $\rho_{q}(\rho_{+}(T)) = \sum M_{xy}a^{*}_{x,k}a_{y,k+q}$: only creator-annihilator products occur, so it is a pair current, and the real Lie algebra of the $\rho_{0}(M)$ with $M$ anti-Hermitian over $\mathrm{span}\{1, J\}$ is $\uu(16)$; the bilinears $a_{x}a_{y}$, $a^{*}_{x}a^{*}_{y}$, which together with the pair currents span $\so(32)$, do not occur. For the partner blocks, the exterior product pairs $V_{n,k}$ with $V_{2-n,3-k}$ into the top degree: $P(\alpha, \beta)a^{\dagger}_{1}\cdots a^{\dagger}_{5}\Omega = \alpha\wedge\beta$. The bases $a^{\dagger}_{S}\Omega$ and $b_{R} = \epsilon(R)a^{\dagger}_{R^{c}}\Omega$ are dual for $P$: $a^{\dagger}_{S}a^{\dagger}_{R^{c}}\Omega = 0$ unless $S = R$, and $a^{\dagger}_{S}\epsilon(S)a^{\dagger}_{S^{c}}\Omega = \epsilon(S)^{2}a^{\dagger}_{1}\cdots a^{\dagger}_{5}\Omega$. The operator $\hat t$ is the derivation of the exterior algebra induced by $t$, so $\hat t(\alpha\wedge\beta) = \hat t\alpha\wedge\beta + \alpha\wedge\hat t\beta$, and on the top degree $\hat t$ is multiplication by $\operatorname{tr}t = 0$. Hence $P(\hat t\alpha, \beta) + P(\alpha, \hat t\beta) = 0$, and in dual bases the matrix of $\hat t$ on $V_{2-n,3-k}$ is $-M^{T}$. On an irreducible component the trace of a semisimple generator vanishes ($\su(2) \oplus \su(3)$ is its own derived algebra), and the hypercharge generator acts on $V_{n,k}$ as the scalar $Y_{n,k}J$.

\emph{(ii).} The images of the generators under $\varphi_{C}$ are, up to sign, a permutation of the generators, with the same squares, so they anticommute pairwise and $\varphi_{C}$ extends to an automorphism of the window Clifford algebra (its universal property). A vector $u$ with $Q(u) \ne 0$ acts by the reflection $z \mapsto -uzu^{-1}$, which maps $f_{x,k}$ to $-f_{x,k}$ for $u = f_{x,k}$, exchanges $e_{x,k}$ and $e_{x,L-1-k}$ for $u = e_{x,k} - e_{x,L-1-k}$ ($Q(u) = 2$, $z - 2(z\cdot u)u/Q(u)$), exchanges $f_{x,k}$ and $f_{x,L-1-k}$ for $u = f_{x,k} - f_{x,L-1-k}$ ($Q(u) = -2$), and fixes every generator orthogonal to $u$. The product $u_{x}$ has $2L$ factors, an even number, so the signs cancel and $\operatorname{Ad}(u_{x})$ is the composition of these reflections, which is $\varphi_{\{x\}}$ on the generators; $u_{x}\tilde u_{x} = \prod Q = (-1)^{L}2^{L/2}(-2)^{L/2}$ is a nonzero scalar. For distinct channels the $u_{x}$ involve disjoint generators, so $\varphi_{C} = \operatorname{Ad}(\prod_{x \in C}u_{x})$. It commutes with $*$ because it maps generators of square $+1$ to generators of square $+1$ and generators of square $-1$ to $\pm$ generators of square $-1$. Then $\varphi_{C}(a^{*}_{x,k}) = \tfrac12(e_{x,L-1-k} - f_{x,L-1-k}) = a_{x,L-1-k}$ and $\varphi_{C}(a_{x,k}) = a^{*}_{x,L-1-k}$, so $\varphi_{C}(n_{x,k}) = a_{x,L-1-k}a^{*}_{x,L-1-k} = 1 - n_{x,L-1-k}$. With $c = (L - 1)/2$,
\[
\varphi_{C}\Bigl(\sum_{k}(k - c)n_{x,k}\Bigr) = \sum_{k}(k - c) - \sum_{k}(k - c)n_{x,L-1-k} = 0 + \sum_{j}(j - c)n_{x,j},
\]
since $L - 1 - j - c = c - j$: $H$, the window energy with the half-integer weights of \claimref{synthesis.exact-window-sugawara-identity-and-luttinger-normal-modes-of-tower-density-waves}(i), (iii) (shifted by the constant $M^{2}/2$ there, $L = 2M$), is fixed. A vector with $n_{x,k} = 1$ for $k < d$ and $n_{x,k} = 0$ for $k \ge L - d$ goes to one with the same property, so the edge-clean vectors at each depth, and for $d = L/2$ the sea, are preserved. For the currents, $M = \rho_{+}(T)$ is block diagonal on components and $C$ is a union of components, so $M_{xy} = 0$ when exactly one of $x, y$ lies in $C$. For $x, y \in C$,
\[
\varphi_{C}(a^{*}_{x,k}a_{y,k+q}) = a_{x,L-1-k}a^{*}_{y,L-1-k-q} = -a^{*}_{y,k'}a_{x,k'+q} + \delta_{xy}\delta_{q0}, \qquad k' = L - 1 - k - q,
\]
and $k, k + q$ run over the window exactly when $k', k' + q$ do. Summing with $M_{xy}$ gives $\rho_{q}$ of the matrix $-M^{T}$ on $C$ plus $\delta_{q0}L\operatorname{tr}_{C}M$; by (i), $-M^{T}$ on $C$ is the matrix of $\hat t$ on the partner components in the partner bases, that is $\rho_{+}^{C}(T)$ there, and outside $C$ nothing changes. The constant vanishes on $\su(2) \oplus \su(3)$ by (i). Finally $\varphi_{C}(\sum_{k}w_{x}n_{x,k}) = w_{x}(L - \sum_{k}n_{x,k})$ for $x \in C$, which gives the reversed weights and the phase.

\emph{(iii).} $\varphi_{C}$ is an automorphism, so it carries every identity among the currents (the exact window commutators of \claimref{synthesis.helicity-one-pair-modes-on-a-null-generator-are-quasi-spins}(i) with their edge terms, the values on the edge-clean vectors it preserves) to the corresponding identity for $V_{+}^{C}$, and it fixes $H$ and the sea. Every gauge-commuting halving is obtained from the halving $(-1)^{n+k}$ by reversing a set of whole charged components (a sign pattern) and choosing a line of the neutral block, whose channel carries the zero matrix; so all $32$ sign patterns, with any neutral line, are related by some $\varphi_{C}$. The trace form: $\operatorname{tr}(MM')$ is unchanged when a block $M$, $M'$ is replaced by $-M^{T}$, $-M'^{T}$, so it is the same for all halvings, and on the halving $(-1)^{n+k}$ it equals $4\operatorname{tr}_{5}(tt')$ for each of the twelve basis generators and their pairs (computed). The anomaly coefficients are sums over the weights of $V_{+}$: $\sum Y$, $\sum Y^{3}$, $\sum YT_{3}^{2}$, $\sum YH_{3}^{2}$ (the four conditions of Fact \claimref{standard-model.imported-anomaly-coefficients}, the second and third weighted by the multiplicity of the other factor through the sum over weights) and the colour cubic $\sum H_{8}^{3}$, $H_{8} = \mathrm{diag}(1, 1, -2)$ on the pairs; each is odd under replacing a component by its partner, whose weights are the negatives. Over the $32$ sign patterns exactly two give zero for all five, and they are complementary; they are the parity halvings $\pm(-1)^{n+k}$ on the charged components, as Theorem \claimref{synthesis.cpt-closure-forces-slot-module-and-chirality}(4) states (computed). The hypercharge constant: the sea fills $L/2$ levels of every channel, so $\rho_{0}(\rho_{+}(JY)) = \sum_{x,k}Y_{x}J\,n_{x,k}$ acts on it as $\tfrac{L}{2}\operatorname{tr}_{V_{+}}Y\,J$; since $\varphi_{C}$ maps the sea of $V_{+}$ to the sea of $V_{+}^{C}$ and $\operatorname{tr}_{V_{+}^{C}}Y = \operatorname{tr}_{V_{+}}Y - 2\operatorname{tr}_{C}Y$, the constant $L\operatorname{tr}_{C}Y\,J$ of (ii) is the difference of the two seas' hypercharges. By \claimref{synthesis.helicity-one-pair-modes-on-a-null-generator-are-quasi-spins}(i) with $M = \rho_{+}(JY)$ and $M' = 1$ (the number current, in $\uu(16)$ but not in $\mathfrak{g}$), $[\rho_{q}(M), \rho_{q}(1)^{*}]$ acts on edge-clean vectors as $q\operatorname{tr}(M) + \rho_{0}([M, 1]) = q\operatorname{tr}_{V_{+}}Y\,J$. Both are the mixed gravitational coefficient $\operatorname{tr}_{V_{+}}Y = \pm 1 \pm 1 \pm 1 \pm 1 \pm 2$ (the contributions $\dim V_{n,k}Y_{n,k}$ of $V_{1,0}$, $V_{2,0}$, $V_{0,1}$, $V_{1,1}$, $V_{2,1}$ are $1, 1, -1, 1, 2$, each sign reversed with the component), which is zero exactly when the sign of the $2$ is opposite to three of the four $\pm1$: $8$ of the $32$ patterns (computed); it does not vanish on the other $24$, so these are distinguished from the anomaly-free ones by the sea's hypercharge. Two halvings with $\operatorname{tr}Y = 0$ differ by a $C$ with $\operatorname{tr}_{C}Y = 0$, so there $\varphi_{C}$ maps currents, energy and sea exactly, and the $8$ contain the $2$ anomaly-free patterns and $6$ anomalous ones. If the channels of $V_{+}$ carry one weight $w_{0}$, then after $\varphi_{C}$ the channels of $C$ carry $-w_{0}$ and the opposite charges, so the weighted traces $\sum_{x}(w_{x}/w_{0})Y_{x}$ (linear) and $\sum_{x}(w_{x}/w_{0})Y_{x}^{3}$ and the other cubic ones are unchanged, while every unweighted trace quadratic in $\mathfrak{g}$ is the same for all halvings, as shown.

\emph{(iv).} On the halving $(-1)^{n+k}$ (one generation: $Q$, $u^{c}$, $d^{c}$, $L$, $e^{c}$ and the neutral singlet), $\operatorname{tr}T_{3}^{2} = 4\cdot 2\cdot\tfrac14 = 2$ (four doublets), $\operatorname{tr}Y^{2} = 6\cdot\tfrac{1}{36} + 3\cdot\tfrac49 + 3\cdot\tfrac19 + 2\cdot\tfrac14 + 1 + 0 = \tfrac{10}{3}$, $\operatorname{tr}T_{3}Y = 0$ (each doublet has one $Y$), so $\operatorname{tr}Q^{2} = 2 + \tfrac{10}{3} = \tfrac{16}{3}$. On the five modes $\operatorname{tr}_{5}T_{3}^{2} = \tfrac12$ and $\operatorname{tr}_{5}Y^{2} = 2\cdot\tfrac14 + 3\cdot\tfrac19 = \tfrac56$, ratios $4$ and $4$, as (iii) gives for every generator. The charges $6Y$ are the integers $0, -4, 1, -3, 6, 2$ with multiplicities $1, 3, 6, 2, 1, 3$, greatest common divisor $1$ and squares summing to $120$; the charges $3Q$ are integers with squares summing to $9\cdot\tfrac{16}{3} = 48$. Rescaling multiplies a quadratic trace by $\lambda^{2}$, and the ratios $2 : \tfrac{10}{3} : \tfrac{16}{3} = 3 : 5 : 8$ do not change when $Y$ is fixed by $Q = T_{3} + Y$.

\emph{Checks.} The exact check verifies (i) for the twelve basis generators on all five conjugate pairs (the partner blocks, anti-Hermiticity, block diagonality on all $32$ states, the traces on components); (ii) on a window $L = 4$ with five channels (the components $V_{1,0}$ and $V_{0,1}$) for three choices of $C$: the images square correctly, $\operatorname{Ad}(u_{x})$ equals $\varphi_{C}$ on the generators of a channel and fixes another, $u_{x}\tilde u_{x} = 16$, the number operators and $H$, and the identity for all twelve generators and $q = 0, \dots, 3$; (iii) the $12 \times 12$ trace form $4\operatorname{tr}_{5}$ on all $32$ sign patterns, the traces $2$, $2$, $\tfrac{10}{3}$, four doublets, and exactly two anomaly-free patterns, complementary, of uniform parity, with zero colour cubic; and, separately, $\operatorname{tr}_{V_{+}}Y$ over the $32$ patterns (values $0, \pm2, \pm4, \pm6$, zero for $8$, the $2$ anomaly-free among them), the constant $L\operatorname{tr}_{C}Y\,J$ as the difference of the seas' hypercharges $\tfrac{L}{2}\operatorname{tr}Y$ for every pair of patterns differing on one component, and on a window $L = 2$ with the three channels of $V_{1,0}$ and $V_{2,0}$ ($\operatorname{tr}Y = 2$) the sea's hypercharge and the $Y$-$N$ central term on the sea, both $\operatorname{tr}Y\,J$ (times $L/2 = 1$); (iv) the numbers $2$, $\tfrac{10}{3}$, $\tfrac{16}{3}$, the ratios, $120$, $48$, the greatest common divisor and the scaling.
