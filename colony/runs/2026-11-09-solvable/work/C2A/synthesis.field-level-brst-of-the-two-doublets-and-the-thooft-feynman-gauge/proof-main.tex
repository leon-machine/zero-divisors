\emph{Setting.} The jet algebra, the total derivatives and the odd derivation $s$ on $A^{a}_{\mu}$, $c^{a}$, $\bar c^{a}$, $B^{a}$ are those of Theorem \claimref{synthesis.field-level-brst-differential-of-the-derived-gauge-fields-is-nilpotent}, with the gauge algebra of Theorem \claimref{standard-model.gauge-algebra-minimal-massive-composite} realised there by bivectors of $\Cl(9,0)$; here the even symbols $h^{i}_{J}$ ($i = 1, \dots, 8$, $J$ a multi-index) are added, with $\partial_\mu h^{i}_{J} = h^{i}_{J+\mu}$, and $s$ is extended by $s h^{i} = -g\big(\rho(c)(v + h)\big)^{i}$, $s(h^{i}_{J}) = \partial_{J}(sh^{i})$, the vacuum components $v^{i}$ being constants ($\partial_\mu v = 0$, $sv = 0$). The two doublets are the scalar sector of \claimref{synthesis.linear-rung-field-has-a-neutral-normal-pair-in-the-scalon-sum} (eight real components, two hypercharge-$\tfrac12$ doublets), as in \claimref{synthesis.quartet-homotopy-makes-gluon-and-massive-vector-physical-states-positive-at-every-number-of-quanta}(v).

\emph{(i).} Left and right multiplications in $\Cl(3,0)$ commute (associativity), and left multiplication by the bivectors $T_{1}, T_{2}, T_{3}$ represents their commutator algebra, so $\rho$ is a homomorphism on $\su(2) \oplus \uu(1)$; colour acts by $0$, and colour commutes with the rest, so $\rho$ is a homomorphism on all twelve generators for every $y$ ($T_{12}$ is central). For a bivector $T$, $\widetilde{T} = -T$, so $\langle(T\phi)^{\sim}\psi\rangle_{0} = -\langle\widetilde{\phi}T\psi\rangle_{0}$ and, by cyclicity of the scalar part, $\langle(\phi T)^{\sim}\psi\rangle_{0} = -\langle T\widetilde{\phi}\psi\rangle_{0} = -\langle\widetilde{\phi}\,\psi T\rangle_{0}$: both actions are antisymmetric for $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$, which is the Euclidean form on the components. The check computes the twelve $8 \times 8$ matrices from GA products and confirms $[\rho_{a}, \rho_{b}] = f_{ab}{}^{c}\rho_{c}$ and antisymmetry for $y = \tfrac12$ and $y = \tfrac13$; hypercharge acting on the left instead gives four failures of the homomorphism property (it no longer commutes with $\su(2)$).

\emph{(ii).} $s^{2} = \tfrac12[s, s]$ is an even derivation commuting with $\partial_\mu$, so it vanishes once it vanishes on the underived symbols; on $A$, $c$, $\bar c$, $B$ it vanishes by the stored theorem. On $h$, with $\phi = v + h$ and $s(c^{a}X) = (sc^{a})X - c^{a}sX$ for even $X$,
\[
s^{2}h = -g\,\rho(sc)\phi + g\,\rho(c^{a}T_{a})\,s\phi = \tfrac{g^{2}}{2}f_{bc}{}^{a}c^{b}c^{c}\rho_{a}\phi - g^{2}c^{a}c^{b}\rho_{a}\rho_{b}\phi = \tfrac{g^{2}}{2}c^{b}c^{c}\big(f_{bc}{}^{a}\rho_{a} - [\rho_{b}, \rho_{c}]\big)\phi = 0,
\]
using $c^{a}c^{b} = -c^{b}c^{a}$ and that $\rho$ is a representation. Only the representation property and $sv = \partial v = 0$ enter, so no condition on $v$ is needed. The check confirms $s^{2} = 0$ on the $40$ generators $h^{i}_{J}$ with $|J| \le 1$, as polynomials in $g$ and eight formal vacuum components, and on the $72$ underived gauge, ghost and antighost generators; with $sh = +g\rho(c)\phi$ all $40$ fail.

\emph{(iii).} $s(\partial_\mu h) = \partial_\mu(s\phi) = -g\rho(\partial_\mu c)\phi - g\rho(c)\partial_\mu h$ and $s(g\rho(A_\mu)\phi) = g\rho(\partial_\mu c + g[A_\mu, c])\phi - g^{2}\rho(A_\mu)\rho(c)\phi$ ($A_\mu$ even), whose sum is $-g\rho(c)\big(\partial_\mu h + g\rho(A_\mu)\phi\big)$: $s(D_\mu\phi) = -g\rho(c)D_\mu\phi$. For even $x$, $y$ transforming as $sx = -g\rho(c)x$, $s\langle x, My\rangle = -g\,c^{a}(\langle\rho_{a}x, My\rangle + \langle x, M\rho_{a}y\rangle) = -g\,c^{a}\langle x, [M, \rho_{a}]y\rangle$ by antisymmetry; this vanishes for $M = 1$ (so $s\langle D_\mu\phi, D^{\mu}\phi\rangle = 0$) and for every $M$ commuting with $\rho$, among them the identity on either doublet, the identification of the two doublets, and right multiplication by $e_{12}$ between them. So the four invariants and every polynomial in them are $s$-closed, and the Yang-Mills density of the stored theorem plus $\tfrac12\langle D_\mu\phi, D^{\mu}\phi\rangle - V$, for any such potential $V$, is $s$-invariant. Right multiplication by $e_{01}$ does not commute with right multiplication by $e_{12}$, and left multiplication by $e_{12}$ does not commute with $\su(2)$; the check finds both bilinears not $s$-closed.

\emph{(iv).} $s\bar c^{a} = B^{a}$, $s(\nabla\cdot A^{b}) = \nabla\cdot(Dc)^{b}$, $sB = 0$ and $s\langle\rho_{a}v, h\rangle = \langle\rho_{a}v, sh\rangle$ give the displayed expansion, and $s(s\Psi_{\xi}) = 0$ by (ii). Solving $\partial(s\Psi_{\xi})/\partial B^{a} = 0$ gives $B = -F/\xi$ and the value $-\tfrac{1}{2\xi}\kappa_{ab}F^{a}F^{b}$. With $F^{a} = \nabla\cdot A^{a} - \xi g\kappa^{ab}\langle\rho_{b}v, h\rangle$, its part bilinear in $A$ and $h$ is $g(\nabla\cdot A^{a})\langle\rho_{a}v, h\rangle$; the part of $\tfrac12\langle D_\mu\phi, D^{\mu}\phi\rangle$ bilinear in $A$ and $h$ is $g A^{a\mu}\langle\partial_\mu h, \rho_{a}v\rangle$; since the form is symmetric, the sum is $g\,\partial_\mu(A^{a\mu}\langle\rho_{a}v, h\rangle)$, for every $v$ and every $\xi$. The check confirms this monomial by monomial ($256$ monomials, formal $v$, $\xi = 1$ and $3$), that the total derivative has vanishing Euler derivatives in every $A^{a}_{\mu}$, and that with $+\xi g$ in $F^{a}$ the remainder has nonzero Euler derivatives, so it is not a total derivative.

\emph{(v).} The terms quadratic in the fields without derivatives are $\tfrac12g^{2}\Gamma_{ab}A^{a}\cdot A^{b}$ (from $\tfrac12\langle g\rho(A)v, g\rho(A)v\rangle$), $-\tfrac{\xi}{2}g^{2}\langle\rho_{a}v, h\rangle\kappa^{ab}\langle\rho_{b}v, h\rangle = -\tfrac{\xi}{2}g^{2}\langle h, W\kappa^{-1}W^{\mathsf T}h\rangle$ and $-\xi g^{2}\Gamma_{ab}\bar c^{a}c^{b}$, with kinetic forms $-\tfrac14\kappa_{ab}\langle F^{a}F^{b}\rangle_{0}$, $\tfrac12\langle\partial h, \partial h\rangle$ and $-\kappa_{ab}\bar c^{a}\Box c^{b}$; so the squared masses are the eigenvalues of $g^{2}\kappa^{-1}\Gamma$, $\xi g^{2}W\kappa^{-1}W^{\mathsf T}$ and $\xi g^{2}\kappa^{-1}\Gamma$. If $\kappa^{-1}W^{\mathsf T}Wu = \mu u$ with $\mu \ne 0$ then $Wu \ne 0$ and $W\kappa^{-1}W^{\mathsf T}(Wu) = \mu Wu$, and conversely, so the nonzero spectra agree with multiplicity, and at $\xi = 1$ the vector, Goldstone and ghost masses coincide. For the neutral aligned vacuum the Goldstone map has rank $3$, the stored count. On the block $\{T_{1}, T_{12}\}$, $\Gamma = (v_{1}^{2} + v_{2}^{2})\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1/4 & y/2\\ y/2 & y^{2}\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and $\kappa = \mathrm{diag}(\tfrac14, \tfrac34)$, so $\kappa^{-1}\Gamma$ has eigenvalues $0$ and $(v_{1}^{2} + v_{2}^{2})(1 + \tfrac43y^{2})$; on $T_{2}$, $T_{3}$ it is $v_{1}^{2} + v_{2}^{2}$. At $v_{1} = 1$, $v_{2} = 2$, $y = \tfrac12$ this is $5, 5, \tfrac{20}{3}$, which the check reads off the computed Lagrangian and confirms by the characteristic polynomials of $\kappa^{-1}\Gamma$ ($12 \times 12$) and $W\kappa^{-1}W^{\mathsf T}$ ($8 \times 8$). At $\xi = 1$ these are the one-particle data of the stored quartet result (iii): each massive vector, its Goldstone mode and its ghost pair share one mass, the on-shell condition $\beta^{2} = Q(p)$ of the free charge there.
