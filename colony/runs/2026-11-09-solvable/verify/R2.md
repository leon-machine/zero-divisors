## Method note (independence proofs)

of their own cones (listed above with the cone files). In 4, 5 and 7 the omitted postulates do not mention the moved quantity,
so the omission is a presentation defect; in 3 the omitted Premise U fails in the second model at residues 1 and 7 and changes
the result.
