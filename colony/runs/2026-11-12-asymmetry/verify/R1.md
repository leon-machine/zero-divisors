## G. The stored claim `synthesis.cpt-conjugation-pseudoscalar-rotor-times-complex-conjugation@4`, clause (3)

**There is a defect.** The last sentence of (3), "The complex-linear Witt exchange `v_k <-> w_k` does not
preserve R^{2,4}", is false as a general statement. It is true only for the particular, non-unitary
normalisation used in the proof: `w_k = e_{2k-2} - I_7 e_{2k-1}`, `v_k = conj(w_k)/B(w_k, conj w_k)` with
`B = 2, -2, -2`, for which `e_0 -> (5/4) e_0 - (3/4) I_7 e_1` (reproduced exactly).

General fact (derived by hand, checked on the two normalisations below): for a Witt basis `w_k` of W with dual basis `v_k` in conj(W), the exchange
commutes with complex conjugation exactly when the Hermitian Gram matrix `c_{jk} = 2 B(conj w_j, w_k)` satisfies
`c conj(c) = 1`; in particular whenever `v_k = +- conj(w_k)`. Rescale `w'_k = ((1 + I_7)/2) w_k` (so
`|factor|^2 = 1/2`), `v'_k` its dual: then `v'_k = +- conj(w'_k)` (signs +, -, -), `B(w'_k, v'_k) = 1` as
before, and the exchange `w'_k <-> v'_k` is the real map `e_0 <-> e_1`, `e_2 <-> -e_3`, `e_4 <-> -e_5`: it
preserves R^{2,4}, is an isometry, and has determinant -1 (computed exactly over the Gaussian rationals in
`r1_recompute.py`). With a real factor `1/sqrt 2` the exchange is the reflection `e_1 -> -e_1`, `e_2 -> -e_2`,
`e_4 -> -e_4`. The proof's own instance, "the reflection in e_1, e_3, e_5, which carries each w_k to
conj(w_k)", is such a real, complex-linearly extended exchange of W with conj(W) (it is `w_k <-> B_k v_k`),
which is the internal contradiction that was flagged.

Nothing downstream breaks: the first sentence of (3) (every real isometry with g(W) = conj(W) has determinant
-1) and the last sentence of (4) (no realisation is both real-linear and orientation-preserving) are untouched,
and the corrected sentence is an instance of them.

Exact correction of the last sentence of (3):
"The complex-linear Witt exchange `v_k <-> w_k` preserves R^{2,4} exactly when the Witt basis is normalised so
that the Gram matrix c of the `w_k` against their conjugates satisfies `c conj(c) = 1`, in particular when
`v_k = +- conj(w_k)`; it is then a real isometry of determinant -1, a product of three reflections, an instance
of the first sentence. For other normalisations, such as `w_k = e_{2k-2} - I_7 e_{2k-1}` with
`v_k = conj(w_k)/B(w_k, conj w_k)`, it does not preserve R^{2,4}."
and of the matching sentence of the proof: replace "it does not preserve R^{2,4}" by "so in this normalisation
it does not preserve R^{2,4}; after the rescaling `w_k -> ((1 + I_7)/2) w_k` the exchange is the real map
`e_0 <-> e_1`, `e_2 <-> -e_3`, `e_4 <-> -e_5` of determinant -1". This is a corrected entity (version 5, kind
correction), with the Gaussian-rational computation as evidence.

Consequence for proposal 1: its Reading should not say that the exchange "sees no real form" on the strength of
this clause; see fix 6 of section 1.
