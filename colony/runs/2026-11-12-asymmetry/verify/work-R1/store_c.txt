TITLE: The orientation is the arrow: the seventeenth refutation
FORMAL: The Standard-Model paper left one absolute bit undetermined, the particle-antiparticle orientation, expected to be fixed by the charge-zero condition. The expectation is refuted by a $\mathbb{Z}_{2}$ covariance argument parallel to the trichotomy's rescaling one, on three premises: that CPT conjugation $C$ is an isomorphism of every kinematic structure and leaves every action functional $S_{\mu}$ of Definition \claimref{obj.dark-energy.four-ways-number-enters-theory-shape} invariant, $S_{\mu}\circ C = S_{\mu}$, the gauge couplings included (\claimref{dark-matter.premise-cpt-covariance}; checked in the proof for the metric, the completion, the pairing and the action; premised for the imported gauge sector and for structures later papers add); Hypothesis (S), under which the Standard-Model paper's chirality proposition, the source of the relative-versus-absolute split, is proved (Fact \claimref{dark-matter.imported-derived-generation-flag}); and that the orientation flag is a datum of each solution, the sign of its complex structure relative to the Fock module's unit planted by the descent and not part of the kinematic structure (\claimref{dark-matter.premise-flag-as-datum}; were it kinematic, the bit would be constant on every solution space, hence dynamically fixed within each of two isomorphic theories, and the conclusion below would fail). Here $C$ is the composite of grade involution, reversal and the flag conjugate, an anti-automorphism; acting on fields as a symmetry does (vectors by its vector part, connections through the induced Lie algebra automorphism), it is $\mathrm{Ad}(I_{6}) \circ \kappa$, with $\kappa$ complex conjugation, so it is antilinear (\claimref{synthesis.cpt-conjugation-pseudoscalar-rotor-times-complex-conjugation}).

Anything the structures determine is conjugation-invariant, and the orientation is not. The charge-zero condition fixes all \emph{relative} structure \cite[Prop.~5.6]{ButlerT12}; under that reading the absolute bit is not kinematic, and since conjugation maps each solution space onto itself and exchanges the two orientations, the bit takes both values on every nonempty solution space: it is initial data in the sense of clause (I) of Definition \claimref{obj.dark-energy.four-ways-number-enters-theory-shape}, and so neither dynamically fixed nor a parameter of the laws (Proposition \claimref{dark-energy.four-alternatives-are-exhaustive-three-are}). No exhaustiveness of the kinematic, dynamical and initial-data alternatives is used. It is the Witt flag of Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.asymmetry-arrow-counted-fifteenth-refutation}: the open orientation of the content derivation and the arrow of time are one choice, made once.

The descent measure carries two numbers and one bit. The neutrino sector supplies no orientation refutation condition: the identification of $\nu_{R}$ with the hypercharge-zero singlet at grade zero is flag-covariant (the conjugate flag exchanges the singlet grades zero and three), so no completion of the orientation could exclude it \cite{ButlerT15}.

This is the seventeenth refutation (the sixteenth is the neutrino paper's \cite[Prop.~4.1(1)]{ButlerT15}), in the pattern of the thirteenth and the trichotomy: what the framework cannot determine, it can prove it cannot determine, and locate.

##### dark-matter.premise-cpt-covariance | version 3 | status postulate | kind claim
TITLE: CPT covariance of the kinematic structures and of every action
FORMAL: \emph{CPT covariance.} CPT conjugation is an isomorphism of every kinematic structure and leaves every action functional invariant (T14, Proposition 5.2, its first premise: ``checked in the proof for the metric, the completion, the pairing and the action; premised for the imported gauge sector and for structures later papers add'').

\emph{The conjugation.} CPT conjugation $\phi$ is the composite of grade involution, reversal and the flag conjugate exchanging the two families of maximal isotropic subspaces (Definition \claimref{obj.dark-matter.witt-flag}). Because of the reversal, $\phi$ is an anti-automorphism of the Clifford algebra.

\emph{Convention (field action).} $\phi$ acts on fields as a symmetry: on a vector field by its vector part, and on a connection $A$ through the Lie algebra automorphism it induces, $A \mapsto -\phi(A)$, so that curvature is carried by that automorphism. In this convention $\phi$ acts on fields as $C = \mathrm{Ad}(I_{6}) \circ \kappa$, with $\kappa$ complex conjugation on $\Cl(2,4) \otimes \C$: its vector part $-1$ is the rotor $I_{6}$ of the identity component, and the flag conjugate that preserves the frozen point's action is complex conjugation, a real-linear one reversing that action (\claimref{synthesis.cpt-conjugation-pseudoscalar-rotor-times-complex-conjugation}). So CPT conjugation is antilinear on the complexified ambient and on the Fock module, of Wigner's antiunitary type (argued there, not proved).

\emph{Action clause.} For every action functional $S_{\mu}$ of Definition \claimref{obj.dark-energy.four-ways-number-enters-theory-shape}, the gauge couplings among its parameters $\mu$ included, $S_{\mu} \circ C = S_{\mu}$; in particular $C$ does not move $\mu$.

\emph{What is checked and what is premised.} Checked: the fibre metric, the completion, the pairing on the Fock module, and the frozen point's action, which with every functional of real tensor fields invariant under constant $SO_{0}(2,4)$ satisfies the action clause in this convention by the cited result's clause (5). Premised: the imported gauge sector, spinor and fermion fields, and structures later papers add. For the gauge sector the standard CPT theorem for local Lorentz-invariant actions concerns the antiunitary CPT operator of quantum field theory, and this conjugation is not that operator (argued): it fixes every bivector of $\Cl(2,4)$ ($I_{6}$ commutes with the even algebra), so every conformal generator and in particular every translation, and it is antilinear, so on a Hilbert space that carries the translations it reverses their generator and with it the energy, whereas the CPT operator of quantum field theory reverses the translations and keeps the energy positive. The gauge sector's invariance under this conjugation is therefore not supplied by the CPT theorem; it ``is imported and carried as a premise''.

##### dark-matter.premise-flag-as-datum | version 4 | status postulate | kind claim
TITLE: The flag as datum: the sign of the Witt flag is configuration data
FORMAL: \emph{The flag as datum.} The descent's flag $W$ (Definition \claimref{obj.dark-matter.witt-flag}) is the $+I_7$ eigenspace of an orthogonal complex structure $J$ on $\R^{2,4}$, extended to $\R^{2,4} \oplus I_7\R^{2,4}$, and its conjugate $\overline W$ is the $+I_7$ eigenspace of $-J$; here $I_7 = I_6e$, the central pseudoscalar of the odd extension $\Cl(3,4) = \Cl(2,4)_{\C}$, is the scalar unit of the complexification and of the Fock module (\claimref{std-algebra.complexification-is-the-odd-extension}), and $J$ is a map on vectors, not an element identified with it (\claimref{synthesis.cpt-conjugation-pseudoscalar-rotor-times-complex-conjugation}, clause (2)). That is, in the terms of Definition \claimref{obj.dark-energy.four-ways-number-enters-theory-shape}, a configuration $x$ and the configuration with the opposite sign of $J$ have isomorphic kinematic structures and lie in the configuration space of the same family of action functionals $S_{\mu}$; the orientation bit $q(x) = \pm 1$ is a function of the configuration, not of the theory.

\emph{What it is for.} With CPT covariance (\claimref{dark-matter.premise-cpt-covariance}), whose conjugation exchanges the two signs, it places the orientation bit in clause (I) of the Definition, taking both values on every nonempty solution space, rather than in (K) or (D). Without it the conclusion fails in the way those results state: were the sign kinematic, the bit would be constant on every solution space of the theory carrying it, and the two orientations would be two isomorphic theories rather than two solutions of one.

\emph{What would refute it.} A structure of the framework, or a term of one of its action functionals, that distinguishes $J$ from $-J$ relative to the Fock module's $I_7$ and is fixed by the descent's kinematics rather than chosen by a solution: for example a proof that the pairing on the Fock module, the completion or the frozen point's data carry a preferred sign of $J$. Such a result would make the orientation bit kinematic or dynamically fixed. No observation tests the premise directly; it is a modelling choice about where the bit lives.

TITLE: The two families are the two chiralities
FORMAL: In even dimension $2m$:
\begin{enumerate}

##### synthesis.cpt-conjugation-pseudoscalar-rotor-times-complex-conjugation | version 4 | status proved | kind claim
TITLE: CPT conjugation as the pseudoscalar rotor times complex conjugation
FORMAL: Let $\R^{2,4}$ carry the conformal ambient metric, $\Cl(2,4)$ its Clifford algebra with $e_0^2 = e_1^2 = 1$ and $e_2^2 = \dots = e_5^2 = -1$, $I_6 = e_0 e_1 \cdots e_5$ its unit pseudoscalar, and $\Cl(2,4) \otimes \C = \Cl(3,4) = \Cl(2,4) \oplus \Cl(2,4)I_7$ its complexification, whose complex unit is the central pseudoscalar $I_7 = I_6e$ of the odd extension ($e$ orthogonal to $\R^{2,4}$, $Q(e) = +1$; Theorem \claimref{std-algebra.complexification-is-the-odd-extension}); $\kappa$ is complex conjugation, the automorphism induced by $e \mapsto -e$, which fixes $\Cl(2,4)$ and sends $I_7$ to $-I_7$. $I_7$ is the complex unit throughout; $I_6$ is the real pseudoscalar of $\Cl(2,4)$, which acts on pure spinors with eigenvalues $\pm I_7$, and the two are not identified. Let $W \subset \C^6 = \R^{2,4} \oplus I_7\R^{2,4}$ be a maximal isotropic subspace with $W \cap \overline W = 0$ (the generic Witt flag of Definition \claimref{obj.dark-matter.witt-flag}), and $\psi_W$ its pure spinor.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The composite of grade involution and reversal is an anti-automorphism of $\Cl(2,4)$, not an automorphism, and acts on vectors as $-1$. Grade involution equals $\mathrm{Ad}(I_6)$, and $I_6 = (e_0e_1)(e_2e_3)(e_4e_5)$ is a rotor of the identity component: each factor squares to $-1$ and is $\exp(\pi B_k/2)$, and $I_6 \widetilde{I_6} = 1$. So the vector action $-1$ lies in $SO_0(2,4)$. In $\Cl(1,3)$, by contrast, $I_4\widetilde{I_4} = -1$.
\item $W$ is the $+I_7$ eigenspace of an orthogonal complex structure $J$ on $\R^{2,4}$, $W \oplus \overline W = \C^6$, and $I_6\psi_{W} = \pm I_7\,\psi_{W}$, $I_6 \psi_{\overline W} = \mp I_7\,\psi_{\overline W}$. So $\overline W$ lies in the other family, and complex conjugation is a flag conjugate. In $\Cl(3,3)$, $I_6^2 = +1$ and conjugation preserves the family.
\item Every real-linear isometry $g$ of $\R^{2,4}$ with $g(W) = \overline W$ has $\det g = -1$, so that $\epsilon(gI, gV, gF, gF) = -\epsilon(I, V, F, F)$ for vectors $I, V$ and bivectors $F$, $g$ acting on bivectors by its outermorphism. The complex-linear Witt exchange $v_k \leftrightarrow w_k$ does not preserve $\R^{2,4}$.
\item $C := \mathrm{Ad}(I_6) \circ \kappa$ is an antilinear involutive automorphism of $\Cl(2,4) \otimes \C$ that is $-1$ on $\R^{2,4}$, fixes the metric, $\epsilon$ and $I_6$, and carries $\psi_W$ to a multiple of $\psi_{\overline W}$. With the flag conjugate realised by $\kappa$, the stored composite $\phi$ (grade involution, reversal, flag conjugate) is reversal composed with $C$: an antilinear anti-automorphism. No realisation of the flag conjugate on $\R^{2,4}$ is both real-linear and orientation-preserving.
\item \emph{Convention.} A (possibly anti-) automorphism $\phi$ acts on fields as a symmetry: on a vector field by its vector part, and on a connection $A$ through the Lie algebra automorphism it induces, $A \mapsto -\phi(A)$ when $\phi$ is an anti-automorphism, so that curvature transforms by the same automorphism; for the stored composite this is the action of $C$. In this convention every action functional of real tensor (multivector) fields on which $C$ acts through its vector part, invariant under constant $SO_0(2,4)$ transformations, in particular the frozen point's $Q(I)^{-1}\int \epsilon(I, V, F, F)$ (\claimref{dark-energy.frozen-point-makes-stelle-west-vector}), satisfies $S \circ \phi = S$, while with any real-linear flag conjugate in place of $\kappa$ the same action changes sign. Spinor fields are not covered. If instead $\phi$ is applied to the Clifford integrand $IVFF$ as a whole and the top-grade coefficient taken, both sign statements invert (with $\kappa$ the integrand changes sign, with a real-linear flag conjugate it is preserved), so the conclusion of (5) depends on the convention.
\end{enumerate}
