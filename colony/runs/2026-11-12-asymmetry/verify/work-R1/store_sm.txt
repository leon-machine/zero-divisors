Nothing else here depends on the choice: the Pati-Salam bivectors, the grading and the two-slot operator $a^{\dagger}_{1}a^{\dagger}_{2} = \tilde a^{\dagger}_{1}\tilde a^{\dagger}_{2}$ are the same in both conventions.

##### standard-model.imported-anomaly-coefficients | version 1 | status imported | kind claim
TITLE: Imported: the anomaly coefficients
FORMAL: For a gauge group with a $U(1)$ factor acting on left-handed Weyl fermions, with the $U(1)$ charges $Y$, the four anomaly-cancellation conditions are: $[SU(3)]^{2}U(1)$, the sum of $Y$ over the colour triplets and antitriplets (each weighted by its $SU(2)$ multiplicity) vanishes; $[SU(2)]^{2}U(1)$, the sum of $Y$ over the weak doublets (each weighted by its colour multiplicity) vanishes; $U(1)^{3}$, the sum of $Y^{3}$ over all Weyl fermions vanishes; and mixed gravitational, the sum of $Y$ over all Weyl fermions vanishes. A right-handed Weyl fermion of charge $Y$ enters the conditions as a left-handed one of charge $-Y$ in the conjugate representation. The four conditions are necessary for the consistency of the gauge theory, and a failure of any of them is not lifted by spontaneous breaking: an anomalous $U(1)$ cannot be gauged whether or not it is broken. \emph{Provenance:} \cite[\S 22.4]{WeinbergQFT2}.
