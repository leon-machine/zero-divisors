## The questions, in order
Q1. The bit. In the switch, set v and clear w satisfy v^2 = w^2 = 0, vw + wv = 1. Is the exchange of set and clear an
    automorphism of every structure the store builds on it (the hyperbolic plane, the tower, the Fock module, the
    descent, the stored laws)? Is the flag bit of the descent this same exchange, exactly, or a different bit? F1
    names the two states "nothing" and "something": does the foundation, in either order of construction (ladder or
    switch), distinguish them in a way that survives to the descent, or is the distinction lost (a label)? State and
    prove what holds: either "the orientation is exchanged by an automorphism of all stored structure and so cannot
    be derived" (its sign is initial data by theorem, with premises named), or exhibit the stored structure that
    breaks the exchange.
Q2. The no-go for dynamics. The asymmetry is stored as a charge Q = epsilon N of the descent. Decide which operator
    that charge is (the tower charge of the superselection rule, baryon number, B - L, or something else) and whether
    the stored statements about it are about one operator. Then characterise the stored dynamics after the descent
    as a class defined algebraically (for example: generated by elements of even parity, or by the stored gauge
    algebra and the stored mass and Yukawa terms), and prove, for the class, that the charge per unit entropy is or
    is not changed. A proof over a class gives the completeness the problem asks for; a list does not. Name every
    premise used (CPT covariance, the flag as datum, Premise (R), real breaking, Hypothesis (S), others). The stored
    chain has terms that violate baryon or lepton number (the Majorana terms, the slot-pair proton channel of
    standard-model.proton-decay-only-through-slot-pair, anomalous electroweak processes): each must fall inside the
    class and be handled by the proof, or be named as the exception with a bound or a stated dependence.
Q3. The magnitude. With the sign a label (if Q1 says so) and the charge conserved (if Q2 says so), is the magnitude
    of epsilon fixed, bounded or left free by the foundation and the counts the store holds? A value obtained by
    choosing among stored numbers after seeing the measured one is a fit: report the look-elsewhere count. The
    registered refutation conditions of synthesis.matter-asymmetry-registered-free-input apply.
