## formal
Work in $\Cl(2,4)$ with the frame of Proposition \claimref{synthesis.positive-conformal-energy-gives-positive-null-translations}: $e_{0}^{2} = \nu^{2} = 1$, $e_{1}^{2} = e_{2}^{2} = e_{3}^{2} = e_{-}^{2} = -1$, $Z = e_{0}\nu$ (the conformal energy, $Z = P_{e_{0}} + e_{0}\nO$), $I_{4} = e_{0}e_{1}e_{2}e_{3}$, $E = \nu e_{-}$, $I_{6} = I_{4}E$; the conformal spinors' unit is right multiplication $R_{I_{6}}$, which commutes with every left multiplication. For $s = (s_{0}, s_{1}, s_{2}) \in \{1, -1\}^{3}$ let $J_{s}$ be the orthogonal complex structure $J_{s}e_{0} = s_{0}\nu$, $J_{s}e_{1} = s_{1}e_{2}$, $J_{s}e_{3} = s_{2}e_{-}$ ($J_{s}^{2} = -1$); these are the eight orthogonal complex structures preserving the planes $(e_{0}, \nu)$, $(e_{1}, e_{2})$, $(e_{3}, e_{-})$. Their Witt flags $W_{s}$ (Definition \claimref{obj.dark-matter.witt-flag}) act on the module through the unit as $w_{1} = L_{e_{0}} - s_{0}L_{\nu}R_{I_{6}}$, $w_{2} = L_{e_{1}} - s_{1}L_{e_{2}}R_{I_{6}}$, $w_{3} = L_{e_{3}} - s_{2}L_{e_{-}}R_{I_{6}}$, and a pure spinor (Fock vacuum) of $W_{s}$ is a nonzero $\psi \in \Cl(2,4)$ with $w_{a}\psi = 0$ for all $a$, equivalently $v\psi = (J_{s}v)\psi I_{6}$ for every vector $v$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{(The flags; proved.)} The $w_{a}$ are nilpotent and pairwise anticommute; the pure spinors of $W_{s}$ span an $8$-dimensional real space, generated by any one of them under right multiplication by $\Cl^{+}(2,4)$, so in a minimal left ideal stable under $R_{I_{6}}$ the pure spinor is unique up to $\mathrm{span}\{1, I_{6}\}$.
\item[(ii)] \emph{(The conformal energy sees only $s_{0}$; proved.)} $Z\psi = -s_{0}\,\psi I_{6}$ on every pure spinor of $W_{s}$: the conformal energy acts on the flag's vacuum as $-s_{0}$ times the conformal spinors' unit. In the normalisation of \claimref{synthesis.attachment-conformal-energy-is-generated-by-z}(i) ($Z$ is minus the unit on the positive plane $T_{+}$ of the twistor form) the vacuum lies in $T_{+}$ exactly when $s_{0} = 1$; $s_{0}$ is the orientation of $J_{s}$ on the plane of $Z$, $e_{0}\wedge J_{s}e_{0} = s_{0}Z$.
\item[(iii)] \emph{(The family is the product; proved.)} $I_{6}\psi = -s_{0}s_{1}s_{2}\,\psi I_{6}$ on every pure spinor of $W_{s}$: the vacuum lies in the even half $\CS$ when $s_{0}s_{1}s_{2} = -1$ and in the odd half $\CSm$ when $s_{0}s_{1}s_{2} = 1$, so the family of $W_{s}$ (the chirality of its pure spinor, Proposition \claimref{dark-matter.two-families-are-two-chiralities}) is $-s_{0}\cdot s_{1}s_{2}$, with $s_{1}s_{2}$ the orientation of $J_{s}$ on the four-space orthogonal to $Z$, $e_{1}\wedge J_{s}e_{1}\wedge e_{3}\wedge J_{s}e_{3} = s_{1}s_{2}\,e_{1}e_{2}e_{3}e_{-}$. The conjugate flag $W_{-s}$ is in the other family. (With the opposite orientation of $I_{6}$ every label $s$ becomes $-s$; the statements keep their form.)
\item[(iv)] \emph{(Independence; proved.)} Each of the four pairs (energy sign $s_{0}$, family) occurs for exactly two of the eight flags. The energy sign of a flag's vacuum and its family bit, the stored orientation bit of Premise \claimref{dark-matter.premise-flag-as-datum}, are independent; one determines the other exactly on a set of flags with fixed orientation $s_{1}s_{2}$ on the four-space orthogonal to $Z$.
\item[(v)] \emph{(Reading; argued.)} So if the orientation datum of Premise \claimref{synthesis.n-plus-null-ground-state} is read as the conformal-energy sign of a flag's vacuum (through \claimref{synthesis.positive-conformal-energy-gives-positive-null-translations}(ii)), it is a second orientation bit of the flag, not the stored family bit. Whether the descent's flag is $Z$-adapted is not decided.
\end{enumerate}
Transport-essential as the ledger derives it (the frame, $Z$ and the unit are the completion's); the algebra is elementary.

## technical
The descent plants a Witt flag, a choice of which half of each null pair annihilates the Fock vacuum, and its family, the chirality of that vacuum, is stored as configuration data. The premise (N$_{+}$) leaves a different-looking sign open: the orientation of the field's unit relative to time, which decides the sign of the null translations. Positive conformal energy $Z = e_{0}\nu$ gives positive null translations, so one may ask whether the energy sign of a flag's vacuum is its family. For the eight flags adapted to the three planes $(e_{0}, \nu)$, $(e_{1}, e_{2})$, $(e_{3}, e_{-})$ it is not. Written on the module with the conformal spinors' unit $R_{I_{6}}$, a flag is three anticommuting nilpotent operators $L_{x} - s L_{y}R_{I_{6}}$, and its vacuum satisfies $v\psi = (J_{s}v)\psi I_{6}$. The conformal energy acts on it as $-s_{0}R_{I_{6}}$, a sign set by the orientation of the flag on the plane of $Z$ alone, while left multiplication by $I_{6} = I_{4}E$ acts as $-s_{0}s_{1}s_{2}R_{I_{6}}$, so the family is the product of all three orientations: even vacuum (the half $\CS$) for $s_{0}s_{1}s_{2} = -1$. Each combination of energy sign and family occurs twice; the two agree as functions only once the orientation of the flag on the four-space orthogonal to $Z$ is fixed. Read as the energy sign of a flag's vacuum, the (N$_{+}$) datum is therefore a second orientation bit, not the stored one (argued); whether the descent's flag is of this kind is left open. Transport-essential as the ledger derives it.

## lay
Two different orientation choices appear in the series. One is planted when the theory descends to spacetime: a choice, made for every pair of directions at once, of which half of each pair empties the vacuum; its overall handedness is stored as a single bit. The other decides which way energy runs along light rays near a horizon. For a natural family of eight such choices, the sign of the energy is set by one of the three pairs alone, while the stored handedness is the product over all three. So knowing one bit does not tell you the other; they are tied only if the orientation of the remaining two pairs is fixed by something else.

## one-liner
For the eight Witt flags adapted to the planes $(e_{0}, \nu)$, $(e_{1}, e_{2})$, $(e_{3}, e_{-})$, written on the module with the unit $R_{I_{6}}$, the conformal energy acts on the flag's vacuum as $-s_{0}R_{I_{6}}$ while $I_{6} = I_{4}E$ acts as $-s_{0}s_{1}s_{2}R_{I_{6}}$, so the energy sign and the stored family bit are independent, tied only by the orientation $s_{1}s_{2}$ on the four-space orthogonal to $Z$.
