## Judgement calls (for Leon)
1. **Boundary modes with n > 1 use J.** This matches G1/G2 and the coordinator's ruling on the phase-flow theorem. Reading numbers in span{1, J} is a field reading. J = e_2 e_3 commutes with modes only if they sit in the radial plane or in tower planes orthogonal to e_2, e_3, and the store does not fix where n abstract modes live. An exact alternative is the odd-extension unit I_{2n+1} of Cl(n,n). I drafted scrambling and l-stabiliser with that unit, then switched to J for consistency.
2. **Complexifications of arbitrary-ground towers use a tensor-factor scalar.** This covers plane-grading (iv)-(v) and tower.structure-tower-c-norms. J cannot be used: it does not commute with a spacetime ground. U3 cannot be used either: it is floor by floor, does not pass to the limit, and fails for odd n. I wrote the scalar of the abstract complexification (U1) as 1 (x) I_4 to avoid confusion with an I_4 element of a floor. The same reading is used for the abstract fields of std-algebra.complexification and complex-clifford-algebra.
3. **Wick continuation of the line-element rapidity in local-boost-temperature uses I_5.** This follows G1's ruling on singularity.euclidean-section-period. The KMS strip of the same entity uses J. The two units are stated as distinct, and only the real period 2 pi is shared.
4. **Imported Fritzsch and theta Facts use I_4 for physical and modular-form numbers.** The source's form is in source_formal.
