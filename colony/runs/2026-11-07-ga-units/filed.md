## 1. synthesis.massless-frequency-sign-relative-to-i4-is-not-the-chirality
- **Title:** "Massless frequency sign relative to $I_{4}$: helicity-locked, not the chirality" (79 characters).
- **open_problem:** op.r-premises-external.
- **Statement:** with the field unit I4 on even spinors:
  - (i) on S_k, L_{B_k} = -1 and L_{D_k} = -L_{I4} with D_k = I4 B_k. Every massless mode e^{-s I4 k.x} psi0 has helicity s/2 relative to I4, for either chirality.
  - (ii) L_{I4} = -Gamma R_{I sigma3}, where Gamma psi = I4 psi I sigma3.
  - (iii) Every real-linear map of Cl+(1,3) that commutes with the left Lorentz rotors is a right multiplication, so it is I4-linear.
  - (iv) Consider the maps a psi(Lambda^{-1}x) b that keep the massless equation (classified: a in {1, I4} for 1 and PT, a in {g0, g0 I4} for P and T). For these, s' = s(-1)^{|a|} sgn(g0 . Lambda k), which flips exactly for P and PT. The chirality transforms as Gamma' = (-1)^{|a|} c(b) Gamma. Charge conjugation psi sigma1 flips the chirality only; a CP map flips the frequency sign only.
  - (v) The consequence, labelled argued: the (N+) orientation datum is odd under spatial-orientation reversal and under CP, and it is not the doublets' chirality.
- **Checks:** 4 functions, 28 executed checks.

## 2. synthesis.z-adapted-witt-flags-energy-sign-and-family-are-independent
- **Title:** "Conformal energy sign and family of the $Z$-adapted Witt flags are independent bits" (83 characters).
- **open_problem:** op.r-premises-external.
- **Statement:** the setting is the eight orthogonal complex structures J_s that preserve the planes (e0, nu), (e1, e2), (e3, e-). Their flags are written on the module as w_a = L_x - s_a L_y R_{I6}, so the pure spinors are the psi with v psi = (J_s v) psi I6. Then:
  - Z psi = -s0 psi I6. The pure spinor lies in T+ of the twistor form exactly when s0 = 1.
  - I6 psi = -s0 s1 s2 psi I6, so the pure spinor is in CS (even) exactly when s0 s1 s2 = -1.
  - The family is -s0 times the orientation s1 s2 of J_s on the four-space orthogonal to Z.
  - Each (energy sign, family) pair occurs for exactly two flags. So the energy sign and the stored family bit are independent; each fixes the other only on a set of flags with fixed s1 s2.
  - The (N+) reading is labelled argued.
- **Checks:** 3 functions, 13 executed checks. They include the 8-dimensional pure-spinor space of each flag, generated by one element, and the positive-form sign.
- **Uses:** synthesis.positive-conformal-energy-gives-positive-null-translations@6 (frame, Z = P_e0 + e0 n0, and its clause (ii) for the reading) and dark-matter.two-families-are-two-chiralities@6.
- **Ledger:** essential, as derived through the completion's frame and Z. The text says the algebra itself is elementary.
- **Weaker than planned, or changed:**
  - **The family sign is -s0 s1 s2, not s0 s1 s2.** This uses the store's I6 = I4 E with E = nu e-. The survey's script took the generators in a different order, which amounts to I6 = -I4 E. This sign is a convention: reversing I6 relabels every flag s as -s. The independence does not depend on it.

## 3. synthesis.fock-slot-module-is-cpt-closed-only-over-one-doublet
- **Title:** "A Fock slot module is CPT-closed only as the exterior algebra of one doublet" (75 characters).
- **open_problem:** op.hypothesis-s.
- **Statement:**
  - F_1 is D (x) M with dim M = b. This follows from the grading alone.
  - For the exterior Fock module, Lambda^2(D (x) M) = C(b,2) F_(2,0) + C(b+1,2) F_(1,1). CPT closure (clause (1) of synthesis.cpt-closure-forces-slot-module-and-chirality) therefore holds exactly for b = 1. For b >= 2 the module has weak spin one; for b = 0 it has no two-slot state.
  - The symmetric Fock module is never CPT-closed. For b >= 1 it is infinite-dimensional and also has weak spin one.
  - So the Fock clause plus CPT closure gives F = Lambda D: (G), a unique vacuum and exclusion. The statistics and b = 1 are derived. The Fock clause is a hypothesis of the statement, not a postulate.
  - The doublet is a module over span{1, I4}.
- **Checks:** 3 functions, 12 executed checks:
  - the Lambda^2(V (x) W) identity for dimensions 1..3;
  - the content and the closure criterion for b = 1..4;
  - the symmetric dimensions for n <= 6;
  - a null-pair realisation in Cl(2b, 2b) giving the weak Casimir on the two-slot states for b = 1 and 2 (triplet on 3 states, singlets on 3).
- **Uses:** synthesis.cpt-closure-forces-slot-module-and-chirality@1.
- **Ledger:** absent.
- **Weaker than planned:** nothing. The survey's kill condition, a stored result that needs a non-Fock generated slot module, rests on the survey's search only, not a proof, and the note says so.

## 4. synthesis.joint-clifford-clause-is-a-klein-convention-on-the-composite-space

- **open_problem:** op.hypothesis-s.
- **Statement:** on V = Lambda D (x) Lambda W:

  - They anticommute with the pair operators, where the untwisted ones commute.
