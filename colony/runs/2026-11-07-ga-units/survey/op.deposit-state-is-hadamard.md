## 1. The residual

(HD) (synthesis.hadamard-deposit-premise, postulate, no edges) is still adopted, not derived. For the massless Dirac field on a radiation-type collar, the store reduces it to one condition: the deposit's occupation profile over the conformal vacuum must have every moment finite (synthesis.deposit-over-the-conformal-vacuum-is-hadamard-exactly-when-every-occupation-moment-is-finite). The data the store already bounds (number, energy spread, shot noise) do not decide it. Four candidate selectors are ruled out: the conditional expectation (the identity on its range), smoothness at the wall (synthesis.radical-normal-tame-wall-is-a-regular-initial-surface-whose-smoothness-selects-no-state), the spin product (synthesis.spin-product-state-of-the-massless-dirac-field-is-odd-under-chirality-and-never-hadamard) and the tracial state. That wall result itself leaves two routes unexamined: "the boundary modes the wall carries" and "any selection by the Riemannian far side". So two things are missing: a series principle that forces a tail decaying faster than any power, and a series reason why the modes nobody deposited sit in the vacuum and not at occupation one half.

## 2. Leads, best first

**L1. The Riemannian far side, continued through the wall's smooth coordinate, selects the conformal vacuum. The branch of the continuation is the orientation datum.** Subjects: walls, descent, dark-matter.
- Ids: synthesis.radical-normal-tame-wall-is-a-regular-initial-surface-whose-smoothness-selects-no-state (items i, iv: the physical side is the flat half τ = 2√t > 0, the far side is Riemannian with τ_E = 2√(−t), and for the massless Dirac field ψ = t^{-3/4}ψ_flat); dark-energy.imported-walls-sphere-theorem (the Clifford sheaf continues across the wall); dark-matter.premise-flag-as-datum and one-relation.imported-flag-arrow-superselection (the orientation bit is planted by the descent); odd-extensions.mirror-inside-complexified-extension-absent-doubled (the Wick rotation is complex-linear).
- What it supplies. Computed (exact, Fractions, scratch hd/screen.py): a massless mode analytic in t continues term by term. Its even part cos kτ goes to cosh kτ_E. Its odd part sin kτ goes to ±i sinh kτ_E, the sign being the branch of √t. So the far-side mode that decays, e^{-kτ_E}, continues to e^{∓ikτ}: exactly the positive-frequency or the negative-frequency mode for the conformal time. Argued: if every mode is required to stay bounded on the far side, the result is the conformal vacuum (Hadamard) for one branch and the filled sea for the other. A real continuation (no branch chosen) gives standing waves. That ties to the stored result that real states have occupation ½ (synthesis.incidence-vacua-thermofield-real-capacity (i)). The selection works through holomorphy in t, which complements item (ii) of the wall result (smoothness in t selects nothing) rather than contradicting it. The Dirac spinor module continues because the sheaf continues: the far side carries Cl(0,4), and both Cl(1,3) and Cl(0,4) are M_2(H) (classification, memory). So the Dirac field is defined on the far side.
- What remains. (a) The Dirac computation: with the t^{-3/4} weight, the continuation carries the phase e^{∓3πi/4}, and one must check that the bounded far-side solutions of the Euclidean Dirac operator match onto Hardy-polarised Lorentzian data. (b) The occupation. An infinite far side gives the vacuum and zero occupation, which is Hadamard but cold. A hot deposit is needed, because a radiation-type descent is driven by the deposit's own radiation density (argued, Friedmann), and that needs a far side of finite Euclidean width L, giving KMS at β = 2L. The only finite width in store is the far-side shadow wall (sheaf.conservation-degeneracy-located). Its position depends on the collar constants μ, ν (three-routes.imported-collar-data-coupling), so it is probably not canonical.
- Grade: PLAUSIBLE for selecting the reference vacuum; WEAK for the temperature. Kill condition (pre-registered): L1 dies if the bounded far-side Dirac solutions, continued through t, are not exactly the positive-energy subspace for either branch. The thermal version dies if the proper distance from Σ to the shadow wall changes under a change of collar data. Ledger: transport-essential if it survives, since it uses the wall, the one locus where division fails (contrast singularity.why-calibration-cannot-be-internal, where the temperature is calibrated at an invertible locus).

**L2. A finite deposit over the flag vacuum. The tower's floors are conformal-energy cut-offs.** Subjects: quantum-fields, dark-matter, dark-energy.
- Ids: field-theory.twistor-attachment ("the tower's floors are energy cut-offs, not regions of spacetime"); dark-matter.p2-indifference ("the deposited mode set is declared finite"); synthesis.attachment-conformal-energy-is-generated-by-z (Z grades the twistor form into the Hilbert form; the positive part is the conformal vacuum's polarisation); synthesis.hadamard-condition-leaves-the-deposit-spectrum-free-on-bounded-bands (the scalar analogue).
- What it supplies. Argued: a deposit that occupies finitely many modes of a finite floor, each a finite-Z-energy and therefore analytic mode, differs from the attachment's Fock vacuum by a finite-rank smooth kernel, so it is Hadamard by the imported microlocal criterion. With homogeneity this is the bounded band, and every moment is finite.
- What remains. (a) Leon must decide that P2's finiteness declaration covers the field modes of the cosmological deposit. In P2 it was introduced only to keep temperature from being a sector label. (b) A homogeneous hot universe in infinite space is not globally finite, so the declaration must be local: finitely many modes per window below a floor. That is a UV end k_UV, the datum op.descent-state-and-spectrum asks for. (c) The undeposited modes must sit in the flag's vacuum, which needs the orientation datum (obstruction O1). A thermal deposit, with its exponential tail, violates strict finiteness but is still Hadamard.
- Grade: STRONG as a conditional derivation (the steps exist in store). The premise it needs is a scope decision, not a theorem. Kill condition: L2 fails if the attachment's floor N, read on a flat collar, does not bound comoving momentum, that is if Z-floors contain arbitrarily high-k flat modes in a bounded region. Ledger: convenient.

**L3. A KMS or maximum-entropy deposit for the collar's conformal-time flow.** Subjects: thermality, entropy, descent.
- Ids: synthesis.q-splits-maximum-entropy-affine-weights (the quasi-free clause of (Q) is maximum entropy at fixed marginals, which is equivalent to (G)); singularity.universality-temperature-under-g; synthesis.boundary-mode-first-law-of-entanglement (unique entropy maximiser at fixed modular energy); field-theory.path-integral-inside-tower (thermal trace, period imported); singularity.modular-descent (KMS at a free β).
- What it supplies. Argued: a maximum-entropy deposit under a finite set of moment constraints, at least one of positive power, has n = (1 + e^{poly(p)})^{-1}. The leading coefficient must be positive, so the tail is exponential and every moment is finite. A KMS state at any β for ∂_η (equivalently a Gibbs state of Z on a closed collar) is Fermi-Dirac, hence Hadamard. On a collar there is no fixed locus for ∂_η, so no (R)-type premise forces the period. This matches (HD) leaving two scales free.
- What remains. The series' maximum-entropy principle acts only at marginals that (R) has already fixed, and it needs a fixed locus. A new premise, (G) for the collar's conformal Killing flow, would be required. Maximum entropy with no constraint gives the tracial state, so the principle must name an energy.
- Grade: PLAUSIBLE. Kill condition: L3 fails if no series flow on the collar is the boost of a completed pair. By O2 the wall's own pair boost is gauge, so the candidate must come from elsewhere (Z is elliptic, Z² = −1, not a pair boost). Ledger: convenient.

## 3. Obstructions and independence evidence

- **O1. Indifference selects the tracial state.** Proved in store: real states fill only the real capacity, with occupation ½ on definite blocks (synthesis.incidence-vacua-thermofield-real-capacity). The orientation of i is a datum, not kinematic (dark-matter.premise-flag-as-datum; the edge-normality run's decision on (N+)). Argued: the only state on the tower's CAR limit that needs no choice is the trace (memory: unique trace on the CAR algebra). So any selection that makes no undetermined distinction (scrambling.indifference-structural-premise-series) gives the non-Hadamard state. (HD) therefore needs the orientation bit, the same datum (N+) assumes.
- **O2. Modular descent at the wall's pair deposits a trace.** Proved: the one gauge generator the wall opens is the ladder pair's boost (dark-energy.sheaf-papers-wall-soldering-variables (iii)), and no ladder partner is canonical (sheaf.no-equivariant-value-wall). Argued: a state that is KMS for a flow acting trivially on gauge-invariant observables is a trace there. Its one-particle operator is E = (1−n̄)P₋ + nP₊ with n, n̄ constant, and that is Hadamard for no constants other than the vacuum's. The horizon mechanism (a physical Killing boost) does not transplant to the wall.
- **O3. Every finite-moment criterion is too weak.** Shot noise (stored); finite local entropy (argued: for the stored tail n = (1+p/T)^{-6}, the entropy density ∫p²s(n)dp converges); the Friedmann radiation density (one moment); local quasi-equivalence (Powers-Størmer and Verch, imported in store: Hadamard implies it, but the tail above passes it and is not Hadamard). Entropy counting, the area law and the C2 cell density all exclude the tracial state and none excludes power tails.
- **O4. Conformal invariance.** Argued (Parker, memory): a conformally flat descent produces no massless Dirac quanta. So for the massless case, (HD) is equivalent to the inherited state being Hadamard in the conformal frame, which moves the question to op.pre-descent-theory and to the Kasner (P2) rule (synthesis.pre-descent-state-carried-into-descended-phase-premise). Only the Milne and isotropic Kasner points carry the conformal vacuum through (synthesis.kasner-field-is-weyl-flat-only-at-the-milne-and-isotropic-points).
- **O5. Symmetry selects only the vacuum.** Argued: the gauge-invariant quasi-free states invariant under the conformal group, and so under the frozen point's stabiliser, are exactly the constant-occupation family of O2. Adding Hadamard leaves only the vacuum, so a hot deposit is never stabiliser-invariant. synthesis.frozen-point-geometry-reads-descent-as-vacuum assumes Hadamard and cannot supply it.
