### Points the brief asked to settle
1. **Chain map: proved for all n.** The (p,q) component of d Delta(e_k) - Delta(d e_k), p + q = k - 1, involves only

   these exist for every p, q >= 0. So there are exactly four cases, not "cases up to k <= 5 including whether p or q
   is zero". With T = sum_{r<j} g^r (x) g^j, N the norm, they are, in Z[G x G]:
   (even, even) (1 (x) g)(g (x) 1 - 1) + (1 (x) g - 1) = g (x) g - 1;
   (odd, even) N (x) 1 - T (1 (x) g - 1) = (sum_a g^a (x) g^a)(1 (x) g);
   (even, odd) T (g (x) 1 - 1) + 1 (x) N = sum_a g^a (x) g^a;
   (odd, odd) N (x) 1 - 1 (x) N = (g (x) g - 1) T.
   I verified all four for m = 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8. The finite check supports the general statement, and the label
   "proved" is correct, but the proof should display these four identities.
2. **Cup coefficient 6: derived**, as the number of terms of Delta_{p,q} (binomial(4,2)), not pattern-read. With the
   Koszul sign the odd-odd value is -6; nothing claimed mod 2 or mod 4 changes.
3. **(G) is an import under derive-not-import.** Exactly what is assumed: (a) cellular chains of a regular cell
   structure compute singular (co)homology; (b) invariant cochains compute the quotient for a free cellular action;

4. **K-ring.** Both halves are proved given (K1). (K1) supplies: ring structure, the multiplicative filtration, the

3. **No degree bound.** Vanishing on three lines theta = const and two lines r = const does not make a two-variable

   homogeneous of degree -1 in (s, c); R_1 is of total derivative order two, so the degree in c is at most 2 (the
   substitution S'' -> (c/s) S' - L S uses both derivatives). So each coefficient lies in the span of 1/s, c/s^2,
   c^2/s^3, and three angles with distinct cotangents decide it. The filed angles have cot = 4/3, 12/5, -8/15.
   Together with degree at most one in L (two values of L per angle suffice).

5. **Title.** "leave the generation number free" reads as an independence result; the content shows that these two
