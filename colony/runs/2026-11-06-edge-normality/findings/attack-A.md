## Literature verified (WebFetch of arXiv:math-ph/9909003, Buchholz, Florig, Summers, Lett. Math. Phys. 49 (1999) 337-350)
- CGMA (Minkowski): (a) W -> R(W) order-preserving bijection, (b) cyclic/separating on intersections,

- Modular Stability: Delta_W^{it} lies in the group generated by the J's.
- Spectrum condition is DERIVED, but only from CGMA + Modular Stability (Prop. 3.6, citing Florig's

## Routes
1. CGMA on the generator (lead 1). Delivers: the ground-state clause from the modular conjugations of the
   half-line algebras alone, if J acts elementwise as the charge-conjugating reflection. Needs: J_a at a
   set of cuts with dense differences; the natural-cone positivity of J (automatic for a modular J).
   Fails if: algebra-level action suffices for nothing (it does fail: Fermi-level shifts), or positivity
   needs Delta.
2. Witt-pair polarisation (lead 3). Delivers at most the orientation sign. Needs a map from the pointwise
   Witt pair to a basis projection. Fails because any polarisation fixed pointwise commutes with
   multiplication operators, hence is a multiplication operator e(u): its state is a product over points,
   locally disjoint from the null vacuum (not Hadamard). The Hardy projection chi(P<0) commutes with no
   nonconstant multiplication operator. So route 2 can supply only the sign, not the projection (argued, short).
3. Own route: twisted reflection positivity (Osterwalder-Schrader type) on the null line, no modular theory:
   invariance under the charge-conjugating reflection about cuts with dense differences, plus positivity of
   -i eta omega(B(x) B(R_a x)) for x supported beyond the cut. Delivers stationarity + charge symmetry + a
   sign condition on the occupation. Fails to give purity: translation-KMS states at every temperature pass
   (computed: their Loewner function is psi(beta a/2pi + 1/2), operator monotone).

## Choice: Route 1, with Route 3 as its positivity engine
Reason: route 3 is exactly the part of CGMA that survives without twisted Haag duality, so doing route 1
splits cleanly into (twisted duality => purity) + (route 3 => sign of occupation) and shows where the thermal
states are excluded. Route 2 is cheap to close and is recorded only as an argued remark.

## Established (labels)
Setting: one generator, GIQF state with symbol T, GNS (pi, H, Omega), grading Theta, Klein twist
Z = (1 + i Theta)/(1 + i), R_a the half-line algebra beyond a, rho_a the reflection about a.
CGMA_eta at a cut (eta = +-1): Omega cyclic separating for R_a and J_a pi(psi(f)) J_a = eta Z pi(psi*(rho_a f)) Z*
for f in h_a (the elementwise, field-level form; J only, no Delta, no KMS, no temperature).

Route 1 (chosen):
- (proved, given Powers-Stormer factor property) one cut: CGMA_eta gives twisted Haag duality
  R_a' = Z R_a^c Z*, hence the GNS representation is irreducible, hence T is a projection (pure).
  All mixed states (affine-KMS at every T, half-thermal products) fail CGMA at every cut. [B has the same
  purity statement at net level, in its findings; it is a step of my theorem, not filed separately.]
- (proved) state-level identity rho_a (1 - T) rho_a = T at each cut (from J fixing Omega and acting as the
  charge-conjugating reflection); at cuts whose differences generate a dense subgroup this gives
  T = theta(P), theta(-p) = 1 - theta(p). Stationarity comes OUT.
- (proved) natural-cone positivity <B(x)Omega, J B(x)Omega> >= 0 (from S = J Delta^{1/2} only) is the twisted
  null reflection positivity -i eta omega(B(x) B(rho_a x)) >= 0; on exponentials e^{-a u} its Gram kernel is
  int_0^inf w(p) kappa_p(a,b) dp, w = -eta(1 - 2 theta(p)), kappa_p(a,b) = (g_p(a) - g_p(b))/(b - a),
  g_p(a) = p/(a^2+p^2): a LOEWNER kernel of H(a) = -int w (g_p(a) - g_p(1)) dp.
- (proved, given Loewner's theorem, cited from memory; Yafaev Thm 4.7 is an alternative) the Pick continuation
  of H has Im H(iy + 0) = (pi/2) w(y), so w >= 0 a.e.: theta(p) <= 1/2 for p > 0 (eta = -1).
- (proved) with purity: theta = chi(p < 0) (eta = -1, the null vacuum of (N+)) or chi(p > 0) (eta = +1, the
  time reverse). Vacuum Loewner function H = ln a; Gram on u^m e^{-u} = m! n!/(m+n+1) (exact).
- (imported) converse: omega_0 satisfies CGMA_{-1} at every cut (Bisognano-Wichmann for the chiral Fermi
  field; one-particle identity argued by continuing the boost to rapidity i pi).
- Sharpness: (proved) two commensurable cuts insufficient (period-2 local gauge function, exact);
  (argued, numerics + digamma Pick property) translation-KMS states pass reflection invariance and the
  positivity (Loewner function psi(beta a/2pi + 1/2)), failing only purity; (proved, exact) pure
  stationary charge-symmetric swapped-band state S = (-inf,-2) u (0,2) is locally normal and Hadamard but its
  form is -3/5 on e^{-u} and 3/20 on e^{-4u}: indefinite, fails CGMA; (proved) net-level form insufficient
  (local gauge orbit, Fermi seas; B's proposal owns this, I keep only a remark).
- Kill conditions: K1 not triggered (exact indefiniteness for the band state; mixed states fail purity);
  K2 not triggered (positivity uses only S = J Delta^{1/2}; no modular group, no KMS); K3 triggered in the
  sense anticipated: the algebra-level form does NOT suffice (so the elementwise form is essential).
- Is CGMA thermal input in disguise? No temperature, period or flow in the hypothesis; the thermal states are
  excluded (by twisted duality, i.e. purity), not smuggled in. But it is a modular hypothesis, equivalent
  within this class to the stored KMS form and to (N+): a restatement, not a derivation from an adopted
  principle. Agrees with B's reading ("spectral, not thermal, in disguise").

Route 2 (Witt pair, lead 3; argued, short): a polarisation fixed pointwise commutes with all multiplication
operators, so it is a multiplication operator e(u); its GIQF state is a product over points, locally disjoint
from the null vacuum, while chi(P < 0) commutes with no nonconstant multiplication operator. So route 2 can
supply at most the sign eta (which member annihilates), not the Hardy polarisation. Not filed.

Route 3 (twisted reflection positivity without modular theory; computed/argued): reflection invariance at
dense cuts + positivity at one cut <=> theta charge-symmetric with operator-monotone Loewner function; this class
contains every translation-KMS state, so purity (route 1's twisted duality) is exactly what selects the ground
state. Recorded as clause (vi)(b) of the proposal (argued).
