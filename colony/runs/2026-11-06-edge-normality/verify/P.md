## 2. synthesis.modular-data-on-a-generator-fix-neither-the-translation-lift-nor-the-sign-of-i

**Verdict: OVERSTATED** (title, one-liner and lay); the formal clauses are sound with the fixes below.

True weaker statement, every level: net-level modular data (the local von Neumann algebras, their modular
operators and conjugations as operators, and every condition on the net and the vector) fix neither the lift of
the affine translations to the fields nor the orientation of i; the field-level (elementwise) action of the
modular conjugations does fix the lift, and carries the orientation as its phase (c = -i versus +i).

### Proof steps
- Step 1 (net-level identity): correct and elementary. alpha_m preserves every A(O) and commutes with the
  grading, so the GNS space, vector, net and all modular objects coincide. The listed conditions (BS-form CGMA,
  geometric modular groups on the net, modular stability, twisted duality, a positive implementer of the net
  translations) are all net-level, so all hold. Correct.
- Step 2 (Fermi seas): symbol chi_(-inf,0)(P - kappa) re-derived; pure, tau-invariant; on bounded O the
  compressed difference is +- a positive trace-class operator (trace k L dim K/2pi), so Fact (c) then (a) give
  local normality; the kernel difference (1/2pi) int_0^k e^{-ip(u - v)} dp is entire, which by
  synthesis.microlocal-condition-on-a-generator-gives-the-local-null-ground-state@4 (i) is the generator
  microlocal condition. Not a ground state for tau: negative-energy holes, unbounded below. All correct.
  Ground state on the gauge-invariant subalgebra for kappa = k id: correct (alpha_m tau_t alpha_m^{-1} = tau_t
  followed by a gauge rotation, trivial on A^G). Densities k/2pi and k^2/4pi correct.
  **Sign slip**: "for kappa with eigenvalues +-k ... it is a ground state for tau_t followed by the internal
  rotation e^{it kappa}". omega_kappa = omega_0 o alpha_m is the ground state of alpha_m^{-1} tau_t alpha_m,
  which is a(f) -> a(e^{-it kappa} f(. - t)); the one-particle generator is P - kappa, whose ground-state symbol
  is chi(P < kappa). With e^{+it kappa} the generator is P + kappa and the ground state chi(P < -kappa), a
  different state. The same slip sits in the last sentence of Step 2.
- Step 3 (the sign): theta(a(f)) = a*(f) is an antilinear automorphism commuting with tau, grading, gauge;
  the conjugate state has symbol 1 - E_0 (re-derived). The c -> conj(c) statement: I re-derived it with Theta
  the grading: the modular conjugation of the untwisted complement algebra is Theta J (since (Z*)^2 = Theta),
  which turns the phase c into -c = conj(c) for c = +-i. So (iii) is correct IF Theta is the grading. The
  proposal calls Theta "the Klein twist"; with Theta = Z the stated form J a(f) J = Theta a*(c f o r_a) is not
  even of the right shape (the vacuum relation is J psi(f) J = eta Z psi*(rho f) Z* = i eta Theta psi*(rho f)).
  Fix 5.
- Explicit phases (argued): the Hankel kernels h = -+ i/(2 pi t) re-derived. The continuum reading of (vi) needs

  number), not (-1)^{n0}: with r = 2 the formula with (-1)^{n0} fails positivity of J_c S and the one with +1
  passes; with r = 1 the reverse. Under (vi)'s own hypothesis (Omega cyclic and separating on the full space)

  Gamma_Omega = (-1)^r Gamma, the parity normalised to fix Omega, which becomes Theta (Theta Omega = Omega); then

- Step 4 (independence): correct and close to tautological once Step 1 is noted: if a G1-invariant property holds
  for omega_0 it holds for omega_kappa. The definitions of G1 and G2 are precise enough for a theorem; the brief's

  The Cl(1,3) part: the identities (I_4 B = J, transverse rotor fixes n and B, transverse reflection reverses J

## Overlaps and conflicts

- **Local gauge transforms.** P1 Step 8(a), (d) and P2 (i), (ii) prove the same net-level identity (a local gauge
  transform of omega_0 has the vacuum's net and modular objects) and use the same Fermi-level states
  (P1: g = e^{iku}, symbol chi_(-inf,k)(P); P2: m = e^{iu kappa}, symbol chi(P < kappa); conventions agree).
  Recommended split: P2 holds the net-level identity, the G1/G2 independence, the A^G observation and (v); P1
  holds the field-level positive result, its converse and sharpness (vi)(a), (c), and cites P2 (i) for 8(d)
  instead of reproving it.
- **Sign conventions.** P1's eta and P2's phase c are related by c = i eta, provided P2's Theta is the grading
  (P1's Theta), not the Klein twist (P1's Z). Both give the vacuum eta = -1, c = -i, and the time reverse
  (filled sea) eta = +1, c = +i: consistent, after P2's fix 5.
- **Band states.** P1 (vi)(c) (S = (-inf,-2) u (0,2)) and P2 (iii) (S = (-inf,-1) u (0,1)) are the same example
  scaled by 2; keep it in P1, where it is computed exactly with its local normality.
- **Phase derivations.** Both derive the vacuum's phase by an argued route (P1 Step 7, analytic continuation; P2
  (iii), continuum polar part). P1 Step 4 fixes the same sign by a proved and checked natural-cone positivity;
  P2 should cite it.
- **Framing conflict.** P2's title says modular data fix neither datum; P1 shows that the field-level form of the
  same modular data fixes the lift and expresses the sign as eta. No mathematical contradiction once P2 says
  "net-level" (fix 1), and P2's extraction note should say the elementwise form fixes the lift rather than
  presupposing it (fix 9).
- **Not in conflict with the store.** The Fermi seas are translation-stationary but not boost-stationary, so they
  do not contradict synthesis.local-normality-at-the-edge-gives-the-null-ground-state (stationarity there is for
  the boost).

## What each proposal does to the open problems

**op.r-premises-external.**
- P1: gives what the problem statement asks in its second disjunct, a form of (N+) free of stationarity, and the
  first one that reaches the ground-state clause (the stored folium form does not), within gauge-invariant
  quasi-free states on one generator, with no thermal input in the hypothesis. It does not meet the success
  criterion, because its principle (field-level geometric modular action at a dense set of cuts, with
  eta = -1) is not a principle of the series. The equivalence with (N+) needs the imported converse. Effect:
  record it as an equivalent form of (N+); the problem stays "reformulated", now as "derive field-level
  geometric modular action of the half-line conjugations from the series".
- P2: a partial no-go. No principle invariant under G1 (net-level conditions) or G2 (antilinear automorphisms
  commuting with the translations) implies the ground-state clause. It does not reach the success criterion's
  no-go, since P1's condition can be stated and is invariant under neither. Effect: closes the net-level modular
  routes (Buchholz-Summers CGMA, modular stability, twisted duality, positive net translations) and names the
  two data any derivation must supply. Its A^G observation (the Fermi seas are ground states on the
  gauge-invariant observables) means the ground-state clause must stay posed on the field algebra; worth a line
  on (N+) (decision for Leon).

**op.null-positivity-curved-horizons, item (ii).**
- P1: supplies "another condition on the state of the generators" from which positivity follows, with no
  stationarity and no conformal symmetry needed, so it can be posed on the eternal hole's generators. It is not
  derived there, and the orientation of the positivity enters as the choice eta = -1. Item (ii) stays open;
  the positivity is restated as field-level CGMA_{-1} at dense cuts.
- P2: shows no net-level condition on the generator algebra can give the positivity (the Fermi seas share all of
  them). It does not settle (ii) and does not make positivity a permanent part of the premise.

Neither proposal settles either problem. P1 restates (N+) (and item (ii)) as an equivalent modular condition;
P2 bounds the class of principles that could derive it.
