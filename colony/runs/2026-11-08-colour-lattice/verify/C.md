## A: centre flux, Polyakov lines, glueball tube

Checked and confirmed.
- E: E^2 = E = E~, <E>_0 = 1/4, P_k E = -I E, J E = -3 I E. Expanding, E = (1/8)(2 + IJ - sum_{j<k} P_j P_k) has grades 0 and 4 only, so E and IE are independent and exp(aI)E = +-E forces sin a = 0; the step "RE = E forces exp(3 phi I/2) = +-1, so det = 1" is right for genuine rotors. J-commutation of colour rotors (via the kernel of B -> BE, orthogonal to J) is right. The vector-level statement f(Omega_R) = cos phi Omega_R + sin phi Omega_I for J-determinant cos phi + sin phi j agrees with the quarter-turn computation (det -j sends Omega_R to -Omega_I).
- Character: on T_theta with sum theta_k = 0, 4c1c2c3 = 1 + sum cos theta_k and -4 s1s2s3 = sum sin theta_k (product-to-sum, verified by hand); w = (1/3) Re Tr, weight exp(beta w) is Wilson's with N = 3, beta = 6/g^2, consistent with Fact (i) (beta = 2N/g^2, checked there against Denbleyker et al.) and with the stored SU(2) normalisation (weight exp(beta <U>_0) = exp((beta/2) Re Tr), beta = 4/g^2). 1 - w = (4/3)(1 - <U>_0) immediate.

- Centre flux (iv)-(v): U_l -> z U_l preserves Haar and multiplies each term by omega^(net charge through l); so surviving terms carry a Z3 two-chain n with boundary minus the observable's chain. The projection onto the plane of C is a cellular chain map; the plane has no finite Z3 two-cycles; so each interior position carries a plaquette with n_p != 0, order >= A. The order-A identification (heights equal by edge cancellation, connectedness) is right. Hidden problem found: the definition. The formal says "A colour rotor is an even element R of the block with R R~ = 1 and R E = E". In six dimensions R R~ = 1 does not make R a rotor. Counterexample (recompute_C.py (4)): with cos(phi/2) = 3/5, R = exp(-3 phi I/2) exp(-phi J/2) is rational, even, R R~ = 1, R E = E, but R e3 R~ has grades 1 and 5. ## B1: a finite subgroup shares the expansion through link degree three
