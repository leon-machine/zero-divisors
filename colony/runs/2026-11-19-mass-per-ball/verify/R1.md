### 1. dark-matter.e-equal-mass-per-unit-ball (v1, postulate): SOUND WITH FIXES

The law rho_r V_{D_r} d^{D_r} = mu is stated correctly, and "R1 and R2 in the length d give E, E gives neither" is
Theorem (i) of the mass-per-ball audit, correctly cited. Fixes:
- (a) "Refutation condition": "E with R3 is refuted exactly when the closed form is" is true in one direction
  only. E with R3 implies the closed form, so refuting the closed form refutes E with R3. The converse fails:
  the closed form can hold while E with R3 fails, for example with s different from t, or with the two members
  of an odd pair at unequal densities whose shares sum to 8/3. Replace with: "Observation can refute E with R3
  only through the closed form: by a ratio that excludes (11pi + 16)/3pi, or by a composition other than the
  shares V_3/V_D. E with R3 is strictly stronger than the closed form (unit-ball rule (iii)), but the extra
  content (the two thicknesses are equal, the per-tower equalities) is not observed."

  simulations.effective-cross-section-sheet-matter take the thickness to be d_b of about 0.98 m. Each assumes R3
  only, with no R1, R2 or E edge (see F). Either draw the edges (the maintainer's call) or write "rely on R1 and R2
  for that (two of them hold it only as text)".

  alone. E at d and R2 at d_b with d not equal to d_b are consistent, with masses m_r = mu (d_b/d)^{D_r}.
  The same slip appears in the status rule (viii) and the open-problem paragraph ("only through R2"). Say
  "through R1 and R2".
- (d) "For the mirror ... E is equal density, which the population count gives separately": this is exact only
  on the proton-only branch. On the burning branch the count gives 0.9948, so E fails there by about 1/200
  (mass-per-ball theorem (iii)). Add "on the proton-only branch".
- (e) Lay: "This assumption says it in the weakest form that works" is an overclaim. E on the reversal pair is
  redundant, because the population count gives it (theorem (iii)), and the total sees only the sum within each
  odd pair. Replace with "in a weaker form than before".
- (f) "E does not say what d is, and the total does not depend on it" is true. But by rule (iii), once E and R3
  hold, d is fixed as rho_2 V_2/(rho_3 V_3) by the unobserved densities. Better: "E does not fix d independently
