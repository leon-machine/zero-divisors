## 3. synthesis.exchangeable-deposits-carry-two-numbers-and-no-spectral-shape

**Verdict: OVERSTATED.** Clauses (i) to (v) are correct as stated. The "Consequently" paragraph of the formal,

### Counter-model
Exchangeable $C = (v - c)1 + cU$, point profiles $g_i = V_c\delta(x - x_i)$ at points that are not a lattice. Then
$\mathrm{Var}\,\phi(f) = V_c^2[(v - c)\sum_if(x_i)^2 + c(\sum_if(x_i))^2]$ and the second term is $c$ times the placement's own
structure factor $|\sum_ie^{I_4k\cdot x_i}|^2$, which is supported at $k = 0$ only for a lattice. For $N$ large and a generic
placement $\sum_if(x_i)^2 \approx N/2$ for every mode, so per unit volume $P(k) \approx V_c[(v - c) + c\,S_{\rm pl}(k)]$ with
$S_{\rm pl}(k) = N^{-1}|\sum_ie^{I_4k\cdot x_i}|^2$; $c$ may be as large as $v$, so for a clustered or otherwise correlated
placement $P$ has the placement's shape. Smallest explicit case: $N = 2$, cells at $x = 0$ and $x = L/4$ on a circle
of length $L$; the modes $\cos(2\pi x/L)$ and $\cos(4\pi x/L)$ have the same slice norm and $f = (1, 0)$, $f = (1, -1)$,
variances $v$ and $2(v - c)$: their ratio depends on $c$, so the state's second number reaches a nonzero wave
vector. So the slice spectrum of an exchangeable deposit can carry any shape the placement has, through the $c$
term. Formal (ii) is still right

## The physics, in one paragraph: is the slicing gap real?

Yes. To first order the constraint part of the field equation on one slice of a perturbed flat Robertson-Walker
field ties three things, the slice's curvature, its expansion perturbation and the matter's contrast
(${}^3R = 6H^2(\delta - 2\delta_H)$), plus the momentum constraint for the velocity; the slice data $(\psi, \delta, u)$ are
free, and the growing-mode amplitude in the radiation era is an exact linear function of them that I re-derived
independently and checked on both solutions in four slicings. Conventional cosmology never faces the choice
because the slice data are inherited from an earlier metric evolution (inflation, or causal processes with
local energy-momentum conservation that force compensation), which fixes the growing mode. A deposit laid down in
one event with no earlier metric stage has nothing that fixes $\delta_H$, and I found no stored premise or result
that does (the time-shift result's separate-universe slice is local to its own hypothesis; reading (ii) of the
dating's join supplies an earlier metric stage but no junction rule, and $H$ jumps there; regularity at a
radiation origin, which is not stored, would remove the decaying mode but not choose the slice). So the stored
"order-one" reading is a hypothesis, (FR), and the gap is real. It does not open a mechanism: a white deposit
reads as index $-1$, $+1$ or $+3$ according to the slice data, all far from $-0.035$; the only scale-invariant row
is (UE) with a structure factor linear in $k$ (a pure stationary filled sea), which is short by more than $10^{17}$
in the window and is not what the stored state rules deposit. What changes is the reasons: "far below $A_s$" holds
only on flat slices; under (UE) the thermal deposit is excluded by its index and, high in the window, by its size,
and if (UE) were adopted it would impose a rough ceiling near $10^9$ GeV on the descent from the largest scales.
Physically, (UE) makes the curvature the elliptic potential of the matter excess, of infinite range, so a premise
of locality (each patch's geometry set by its own deposit, the separate-universe reading) would select (LC) and
restore the stored verdicts; that premise is a decision the store has not made.
