## B. Cross-links found

1. Operator source, static to general. field-theory.operator-newtonian-coupling fixes the normalisation of the
   first-order exchange at every non-null momentum; with clause (iii) of the stored first-order theorem
   (synthesis.weyl-flat-variations-...) the field at non-null momentum is fixed by its source. Together they give
   the production rate with no quantum of the field (target B).
2. Curvature coupling. The scalar coefficients of that rate depend on (1 - 6 xi)^2 of the bath's and the produced
   scalars: the datum of op.curvature-coupling-across-subjects enters the dark towers' heating.
3. Null momentum. The same stored theorem says two directions of the field at null momentum are not fixed by the
   source. That is where the free part of target C lives, where the burst baseline's term g lives, and what
   op.einstein-from-the-field-theory has to say something about.
4. Clifford multiplication and the wall class. The family "multiplication by the position vector" degenerates
   exactly at the zero divisors; whether its degeneration can be removed is whether the module carries one more
   anticommuting generator, a question the stored classification answers with period eight. This is the
   conjecture's "class built from the residues modulo eight", in a different place from where it was looked for
   (target A), and it bears on op.wall-class-invariant beyond the tame case.
5. Planck-scale localisation. The notes of 12 October say the slaved field's failure is unobservable only under a
   Planck-length floor on localisation, "not derived here". The store's cutoff premises (C1), (C2) are statements
   of that kind. Whether they supply the floor is not examined; it is the natural first attack item if the series
   keeps the field without quanta.
6. Trace anomaly. The stored remark that the quantum trace of the massless Dirac stress is nonzero at first order
   (clause (v) of the first-order theorem) means the fermion channel of the rate acquires a trace part at the next
   order; not computed.
