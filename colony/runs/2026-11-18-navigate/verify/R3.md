## The energy convention (M1's open item)

The energy a creation event deposits is E1 + E2 of the incoming pair. In the rate integral (theorem clause (v)) the
weight is f1 f2 E1 E2 dE1 dE2 dcos times the angular integral of |M|^2, which is proportional to s^2 = 4 E1^2 E2^2 (1 -
cos)^2: so each leg is weighted by E^3 f(E), independently. The mean of E with weight E^3 f(E):
- Bose leg: Gamma(5) zeta(5)/(Gamma(4) zeta(4)) T = 4 zeta(5)/zeta(4) T = 3.832 T;
- Fermi leg: (30/7) zeta(5)/zeta(4) T = 4.106 T.
So the mean energy per event is 8 zeta(5)/zeta(4) T = 7.664 T for a Bose initial pair and (60/7) zeta(5)/zeta(4) T =
8.212 T for a Fermi pair. Weighting by the bath's channels (boson-initial share 0.59 to 0.62 of each produced species'
rate; r3_energy.py): 7.873 T per event for a produced real scalar, 7.890 T for a chiral fermion or a vector, 7.889 T for
the mirror's content and for nine vectors. That is about 3.94 T per produced quantum.

With s/rho = 4/(3T), the energy fraction is (4/3)(E_event/T) Y = 10.52 Y, against the stored 4Y (3T per event) and the
alternative 8Y (3T per quantum). So 8Y is the closer of the two (low by a factor 1.31, against 2.63 for 4Y), and neither is
right. Both stored counts are lower bounds, so "the stated value is a floor on either count" stays true.

What the floors become (energy fraction 10.52 Y; ratio factor (10.52/4)^(1/4) = 1.273 over the stated floors):
- mirror floor 6.6e-4 (stated 5.2e-4); nine-vector floor 8.1e-4 (stated 6.4e-4);
- eta' at the mirror's floor 2.1 (stated 4.4; still above one; the crossover coefficient falls to about 1.5, 2.7 times the
  mirror's 0.568);
- Delta N_eff at the floors 1.4e-12 (mirror) and 1.7e-11 (nine vectors): above the bounds 1e-12 and 1e-11 now stated in
  coefficients (iv); "far too small to detect" stands;
- radiation audit margin at the mirror's floor 2.1e11 (still eleven orders);
- under (R): mirror floor 3.1e-5 (above the stated 2.5e-5), 98 times below the helium edge (stated more than 120); nine
  vectors 3.8e-5 (stated below 3.0e-5); the floor's share of xi^4 at the helium edge 1.1e-8 under (R) (stated less than
  1e-8) and 2.4e-3 without (R) (stated less than 9.1e-4); "the floor drops out" stands;
- the shortfall and the Pati-Salam and untuned-fit results count quanta, not energy, and do not change.

Caveats on this computation: it assumes the produced energy thermalises within the tower (as the stored floor does),
neglects the change of g between production and thermalisation, and uses the theorem's s^2 angular weight, which is exact
for every channel at this order (all nine W_ij multiply s^4/s^2). It is a computation, not stored; if it is adopted, the
seeding proposition's "energy 3T per event" becomes "the mean energy per event, 8 zeta(5)/zeta(4) T to (60/7) zeta(5)/zeta(4)
T, 7.9 T for the Standard-Model bath", the energy fraction 10.5 Y, and the prefactor 9.6e-4 becomes 1.23e-3; every
dependant above moves by the factors listed.

**Recommendation.** Adopt the computed energy (it is derivable in the store from the theorem's clause (v) and two zeta
values), or, if the convention is kept, make every "below" statement that is an upper bound built from the floor
(coefficients (iv), mirror-temperature (i) and (iii)) say "on the stored convention of 3T per event", since at the computed
energy each of those round bounds fails by a factor up to 2.7 while no conclusion changes. This is the maintainer's
decision; as the store stands now, those bounds are true only as conventions.
