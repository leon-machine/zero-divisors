### The cited Bott / Atiyah-Bott-Shapiro statement

Nothing proved depends on it: (8)'s removable half uses only the table and uniqueness; the non-removable cases proved
are m = 1 and m = 2 via (1) and (5). The Reading and the lay use it interpretively ("the clock is in the codimension").

Is it correctly stated? The obstruction to a model completion (bounded U) is rigorous by (4): h_+ trivial over R^m forces
G_+ (+) (K_0)_+ trivial on a sphere S^{m-1} in the exterior, i.e. [G_+] = 0 in reduced KO(S^{m-1}) (any W_0, so stable).
Reduced KO(S^{m-1}) is nonzero exactly for m - 1 = 0, 1, 2, 4 mod 8, i.e. m = 1, 2, 3, 5 mod 8 (Bott), and by
Atiyah-Bott-Shapiro the +1 eigenbundle of x.E on the least module represents a nonzero class exactly when the least module
does not extend to Cl(m+1,0), which is the same set of m. Checks against known cases: m = 2 Moebius; m = 3 the Hopf line
over S^2 (w_2 nonzero); m = 5 the quaternionic Hopf bundle over S^4; m = 9 the generator of reduced KO(S^8) = Z. So the
statement "non-removable by a model completion exactly for m = 1, 2, 3, 5 mod 8" is right (my confirmation is also from
memory). But the proposal words it as "not removable for m = 3, 5 mod 8 is the periodicity theorem": the proved cases are
m = 1 and m = 2 only, not m = 1, 2 mod 8 with m >= 9, so the cited part must be "m = 1, 2, 3, 5 mod 8 other than m = 1, 2".
The same wording is in the extraction note ("m = 3 and 5 modulo 8").
