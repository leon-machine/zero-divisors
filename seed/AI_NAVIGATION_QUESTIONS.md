# Questions about the Transport Lab

Answer each question from the store, through the tools only. For every
answer give the entity ids you relied on.

1. Which result says that the maximal number of fermionic mode pairs realisable by vectors of a Clifford algebra is an invariant of the quadratic form, and what is that number for signature (p, q)?
2. What signature increment does the minimal completion adjoin, and which result establishes it? Is that result machine-checked, or supported by proof text only?
3. How many results in the store rest, directly or indirectly, on Hypothesis (S)?
4. The store holds a live prediction for the sum of the neutrino masses. What value does it predict, and which postulates does the prediction hold given?
5. Which results does the I-state result use directly, and is it machine-checked?
6. What is the imaginary unit of the field theory, what is the complex unit of the conformal spinor space (the papers' twistor space), and why are the two kept distinct?
7. Does the field theory's imaginary unit commute with the mode operators of the atom used in the gravitational-singularity paper (T05)? Why or why not?
8. The dark-tower-matter note (T14c) has a Premise U, and the dark-matter paper (T14) has a Hypothesis (U). Are they the same hypothesis? Say what each states.
9. The store records a refuted claim that conformal spinors (twistors) are the ladder operators. Which result refutes it?
10. Name three results that build directly on the minimality result of question 2.
11. Give three results tagged "modular-theory", from at least two different subjects.
12. In the root paper (T01), how is a point x of the base space lifted into the completion? Give the formula.
13. In the field-theory note "One Complex Unit", is the photon elementary or composite, and is that choice forced?
14. Which of the store's results is the most built upon (the largest number of results resting on it, directly or indirectly), as far as you can determine?
15. Find one result that is declared proved but whose proof text never cites one of the premises its derivation lists, and name the uncited premise.
